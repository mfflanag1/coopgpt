# Source and priority status — 7 September 2026

The mathematical target is resolved by a conventional proof: a fixed three-sector family has best whole-core simultaneous protection 1/(2q+3), and the graph classification extends to all core selections. This is a new deduction within this project from its existing family. Historical novelty remains unconfirmed. No paper purchase, paid consultation, or outreach was performed.

## Direct antecedent to the question: the bilateral half-optimal core

Marina Núñez and Carles Rafels, *Bargained stable allocations in assignment markets*, [free Barcelona Economics Working Paper 153](https://www.bse.eu/sites/default/files/working_paper_pdfs/153.pdf). The inspected original PDF is dated 28 December 2004; an aggregator's different year is not used to date this copy. Read scope: title, abstract, introduction, Definition 6 and its surrounding argument (PDF pages 19–20), and Section 6's nonactive-agent extension (pages 32–34). They explicitly study stable allocations paying every agent at least half of their maximum core payoff and establish nonemptiness in the bilateral setting. Thus neither simultaneous protection nor its bilateral half guarantee is new. Our proposed contribution is the vanishing best fraction in strict three-sector nonnegative pair-additive markets and the graph-wide classification. This source does not itself state that result in the inspected passages. No claim of a full-paper implication audit is made.

## Structural and model precedents

[Atay–Núñez 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10479-019-03256-5), particularly Theorem 1, Corollary 1 and Proposition 2, supplies tree decomposition, a tree/cycle strong-balancedness characterization, and sector-optimal core points in a model with productive partial-sector coalitions. Its model distinction was checked in the preceding audit. Acyclicity and the existence of simultaneous sector maxima are established themes. Empty-core counterexamples do not imply our impossibility conditional on nonempty core. The mathematical transfer to all selectors here uses the project's actual strict-core proof, not an unproved identification of these two models.

[Martínez-de-Albéniz–Rafels–Ybern 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dam.2025.10.006), free full text recovered in the preceding audit, gives extrema and their simultaneous same-sector attainment under supermodularity and local monotonicity. Averaging one sector-maximizing point per sector would give a 1/K guarantee in that class. Our theorem does not assume those hypotheses. The present simplex witness shows a failure of simultaneous protection in the broader pair-additive class. The prior audit records a natural-order supermodularity violation, not an all-relabeling certificate.

[Vazirani, arXiv:2402.11437v6](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.11437v6), studies bilateral leximin and other fair core selections. The present impossibility optimizes over the entire core; it does not hinge on a specific selector. No new selector definition is being claimed.
