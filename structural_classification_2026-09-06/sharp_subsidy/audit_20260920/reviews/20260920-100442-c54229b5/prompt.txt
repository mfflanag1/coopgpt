7: ## 1. Replication preserves the original entire core and subsidy

12: 
13: Proof. Repeat an optimal perfect matching D times to obtain worth D*T. Repeat any original core vector; it covers every copied team and has total D*T. Therefore the new grand worth is D*T and the repeated vector is in the new core.
14: 
15: For any point X in the new core, all teams in the repeated optimal matching are tight. Fix two copies i^a,i^b of the same player. This proves the asserted entire-core identity and ideal identity, with no recomputation under subsidy.
