This is a bounded priority check, not confirmation of novelty. No purchases or outreach.

1. Bachrach et al., The Cost of Stability in Coalitional Games (2009), original full text https://arxiv.org/html/0907.4385v1, Section 3, LP* and Theorem 3.5 proof. The paper already formulates minimum external grand-coalition payments as a covering LP and writes its fractional-packing dual. Those principles, and complementary/slack bookkeeping, are established ingredients. Our frozen floors H_i/3 and the particular three-sector pair-additive bound are additional restrictions; the inspected argument is not a priority claim for those restrictions. Original full text re-opened Sept20.
