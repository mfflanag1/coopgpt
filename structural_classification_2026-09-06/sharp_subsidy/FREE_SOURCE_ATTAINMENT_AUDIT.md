## Core extensions: a previously uncompleted original comparison now checked

Camelia Bejan and Juan Camilo Gómez, *Core Extensions for Non-balanced TU-Games*, author manuscript dated August 1, 2008; published in International Journal of Game Theory 38 (2009), 3-16. [Free author original](https://faculty.washington.edu/cameliab/research/core.pdf); [author publication record](https://faculty.washington.edu/jcgomez/).

Section 2, manuscript pp. 5-6, defines an expansion that changes only grand-coalition worth and minimizes the expansion needed for a nonempty core. It then uses individual taxes to return to an efficient allocation of the original total. Its claims are tax-allocation inputs, not frozen coordinate maxima of the original core. This confirms that minimal grand-coalition expansion and the associated optimization framework are established ingredients. Our extra original-ideal floors identify a restricted problem; that distinction alone does not establish novelty. Status: original definitions and intervention comparison complete.

## Core covers: utopia is not automatically our H

The k-core-cover construction bounds coalition payoffs using utopia and minimal-rights games. Those objects must not be silently substituted for attained maxima over our original three-sector core. The inspected definitions and bilateral application do not prove the desired frozen-floor subsidy inequality. Status: this original's definitions and stated application compared; no exhaustive claim about the surrounding literature.
