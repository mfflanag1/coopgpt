# Free-source comparison for the core-compatibility improvement

Audit date: 2026-09-08 UTC. This is a bounded comparison of nearby originals,
not an exhaustive novelty confirmation. No source purchase or outreach occurred.

## Established ingredients and the proposed contribution

The new proof combines original-core attainment witnesses, selective diagonal
cloning, component restriction, weighted incidence counting, and a finite
three-label deletion certificate. LP duality, payoff bounds from core constraints,
equal treatment of copied players, and threshold integration are not claimed
as new individually. The candidate contribution is their quantitative combination
in the master inequality and the uniform frozen-ideal subsidy bound in
CORE_COMPATIBILITY_UPPER.md. Priority for that combination remains unconfirmed.

## Primary-source comparisons

1. Bachrach et al., *The Cost of Stability in Coalitional Games* (2009),
   [author arXiv original](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.4385).
   Its abstract defines stabilization by an external supplement to a coalition
   and studies bounds and algorithms. The grand-supplement optimization is an
   established framework. The present proof concerns a specified class of
   three-sector markets with floors from separate original-core maxima; the
   inspected source does not itself establish our quantitative bound.

2. Núñez Lugilde, Estévez-Fernández and Sánchez-Rodríguez, *Priority coalitional
   games and claims problems* (2024),
   [free accepted original](https://www.investigo.biblioteca.uvigo.es/xmlui/bitstream/11093/11185/4/2024_coalitional_games.pdf).
   The exact original comparison is already retained in
   PRIORITY_ORIGINAL_COMPARISON.md: Definition 2 and Lemma 2 (PDF page 7) and
   Definitions 3-4/Lemma 8 (PDF pages 10-11). Weak-priority transformations can
   assemble the protected singleton floors used here. Those transformations
   are therefore established ingredients; their balancedness hypothesis does
   not assert that floors taken from different core allocations are jointly
   feasible. The current bound is not a new priority-game definition.
   A search resurfaced the VU Version-of-Record PDF address, but opening it
   returned HTTP403. The accepted original remains the inspected version;
   no completed published-version comparison is asserted.

3. Sánchez-Rodríguez, Borm, Estévez-Fernández, Fiestras-Janeiro and Mosquera,
   *Characterizing the core via k-core covers*, Tinbergen discussion paper
   2013-177/II, [free institutional original](https://papers.tinbergen.nl/13177.pdf).
   PDF page 5 defines the utopia payoff by marginal contribution; Section 5
   and Theorem 5.1 (PDF pages 20-21) concern two-sided assignment games and
   2-compromise stability. This differs from the actual attainable H_i used
   here, which must not be replaced by a marginal-contribution upper bound.
   Its general core-cover machinery is relevant background, but the inspected
   theorem is not our three-sector frozen-floor subsidy inequality.

4. El Obadi, Miquel and Núñez, *Assignment markets with capacitated middlemen:
   core and competitive equilibria* (published 25 August 2026),
   [open-access original](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00182-026-01007-y).
   The abstract and introduction specify buyer-seller profit independent of
   middleman identity, with capacitated multiple partnerships. Our fixed model
   instead has unit participation on all three sides and three general
   nonnegative pair matrices. Its core-nonemptiness result does not supply
   the present floor-protection bound by a direct identification of models.

## Search limits and outstanding priority risks

Fresh searches combined multisided assignment, core cover, utopia, subsidy,
coordinate maxima and priority-game terms. No matching quantitative theorem
was verified in these inspected sources. Absence from a small search is not
proof of novelty. Quint, Lucas and Stuart's previously unavailable originals
have not been purchased or silently counted as compared. The existing
accepted-manuscript/published-version distinctions remain in force.

The evidence supports presenting a new internally verified proof in this
project, with its exact model and dependencies. It does not justify calling
it the first result of its kind, a sharp constant, or a field-level breakthrough.
Specialist assessment of both significance and priority remains desirable.
