1: # Selective diagonal subdivision gives vanishing protection on C4
2: 
3: Candidate conventional proof. Entire strict core; no productive partial-sector coalitions. No priority claim.

5: ## Definitions and theorem
6: 
7: Every sector has the same finite size m. Only full four-sector teams produce output, with value the sum of the four specified nonnegative edge matrices on ab,bc,cd,ad. Any coalition's worth is its maximum weight integral matching of contained full teams. The entire core is equivalently the set of individually nonnegative full-team covers with sum equal to grand worth T. The equivalence follows by summing team covers over coalition matchings and using nonnegativity for unmatched players. Full leximin maximizes the ascending sorted vector over this entire core, retaining every labeled coordinate. H_i is the maximum of the same coordinate over that entire core.
8: 
9: For every integer q>=1 there is such a C4 market with m=3q+1, all pair entries in {0,1,2}, T=10q+1, and a strictly positive core point, for which
10:     x_(c,L0)=1/[4(q+1)],  H_(c,L0)=1.
11: Thus c(C4)=0. This settles the remaining four-sector graph type, conditional on verification of this proof. It does not claim novelty or field-level significance.

13: ## A selective subdivision lemma
14: 
15: Start with a strict three-sector market with team value A(a,b)+C(b,c)+B(a,c), nonnegative matrices, nonempty core, and an optimal perfect diagonal matching of value T0. Let D_j>=max_a B(a,j). Add sector d of the same size and use
16:     w'(a,b,c,d)=A(a,b)+C(b,c)+B(a,d)+D_c*1_(c=d).
17: The last term equals D_d*1_(c=d). For every old core point z, the point (z_a,z_b,z_c,D_d) is a new full-team cover. Indeed:
18: * c=d: its sum is at least A+C+B(a,c)+D_c, exactly the required expression.
19: * c!=d: its sum is at least A+C+B(a,c)+D_d >= A+C+B(a,d), since B(a,c)>=0 and D_d>=B(a,d).
20: Its total is T0+sum_j D_j, attained by the diagonal full matching. Therefore this is the exact integral worth and the displayed cover is an entire-core point.
21: 
22: More generally, from any old core point z and any t>=0 such that z_i>=t/3 for every old player, form
23:     p_a=z_a-t/3, p_b=z_b-t/3, p_c=z_c-t/3,
24:     p_d=D_d+t.
25: This is nonnegative, has the same total, and each full team's sum is unchanged from the previous lift. Hence it is in the new entire core. If z_i>=tau throughout, choosing t=3tau/4 gives every coordinate of p at least t.
26: 
27: This lemma permits D_j=0 at zero B-columns. It is different from the retired uniform-positive-bonus construction: no uniform positive new-payoff floor is forced by D at those labels.
