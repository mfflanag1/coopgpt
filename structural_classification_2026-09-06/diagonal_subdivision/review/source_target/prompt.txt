1: # A uniform bound for the diagonal-bonus subdivision route
2: 
3: Conventional no-go lemma, reviewed by Claude with no correctness defect, for a specific C4 construction. It is not a bound on all C4 markets.

5: ## Construction and hypotheses
6: 
7: Let a strict three-sector source market of equal size m have pair matrices A(a,b), B(a,c), C(b,c), all nonnegative, positive grand worth T computed by integral matching, and nonempty entire core. Only full teams produce value; arbitrary coalition worth is the maximum weight of a matching of full teams contained in it. In particular pairs have zero worth, while larger coalitions can have positive worth. Nonnegative values and equal sizes allow completion of an optimal matching without loss; relabel an optimal perfect full-team matching to the diagonal. Let
8: 
9:     D=max_j [A(j,j)+B(j,j)+C(j,j)].
10: 
11: Choose M>0 such that B(a,d)-B(a,c)<=M for every a,c,d. Form a strict four-sector C4 market on edges ab,bc,cd,ad with matrices A,C,M*identity,B respectively. Thus
12: 
13:     w'(a,b,c,d)=A(a,b)+C(b,c)+B(a,d)+M*1_{c=d}.
14: 
15: This is the usual attempt to replace the old ac edge by a path through a duplicate-index sector d. The hypothesis on M is sufficient for the following bound; no assertion is made when it is dropped.
16: 
17: ## Exact worth and a uniform floor
18: 
19: For any source-core payoff z=(z_a,z_b,z_c), define extended payoffs
20: 
21:     p_a=z_a+M/4, p_b=z_b+M/4, p_c=z_c+M/4,
22:     p_d=M/4 for every new player.
23: 
24: Their total is T+mM. For any full four-team, the source core inequality gives
25: 
26:     p_a+p_b+p_c+p_d
27:       >= A(a,b)+C(b,c)+B(a,c)+M
28:       >= A(a,b)+C(b,c)+B(a,d)+M*1_{c=d}.
29: 
30: When c=d the last comparison is equality. When c!=d it follows from the defining bound on M. Thus p is a nonnegative full-team cover. The diagonal integral matching has worth T+mM, so weak duality proves the exact grand worth is T+mM and p is an entire-core point.
31: 
32: In particular the extended first optimal floor is at least M/4, and every coordinate of full leximin x' is at least M/4, including all later stages. No identification of p with full leximin is needed.
33: 
34: Every core point makes each team of the diagonal optimal matching tight. Individual nonnegativity therefore bounds every whole-core ideal by D+M. Hence every player with positive ideal satisfies
35: 
36:     x'_i/H'_i >= M/[4(D+M)].
37: 
38: Across a family, this is a positive uniform bound whenever D/M is bounded above. An upper bound on M is not necessary. In fact every ideal here is positive because it is at least M/4.
