A LOGARITHMIC TRUTHFUL COALITION-COVERING MECHANISM ON EVERY FINITE DAG
7 October2026. Proof independently derived by coordinator and Codex reviewer; Fable audit
completed without mathematical findings. Strengthened exact verification passed.

THEOREM AND MODEL
Fix a finite directed acyclic s-t graph, with one fixed original owner per
edge and arbitrary nonnegative real capacity reports c. Owners may only
underreport; utility is payment, without operating costs. Let T(c) be grand
maxflow and v_c(S) the maxflow using coalition S. Let K be the number of
inclusion-minimal structural s-t cuts, fixed by the topology, not the reports.
There is a deterministic nonnegative payment rule p satisfying, at EVERY
profile and for every owner and coalition:
 (i) p_e(t,c_-e) is nondecreasing in t;
 (ii) sum_{e in S}p_e(c)>=v_c(S);
 (iii) sum_e p_e(c)<=(exp(1)+o(1)) T(c) log K as K tends to infinity.
The rule uses exact finite expectations, not realized random payments.
No computational-efficiency claim, historical novelty confirmation, or
field-level breakthrough claim is made. This improves the project's known
general K-only existence bound from K to O(log K). The established glove
lower bound is (1-o(1))log K/log log K; a log log K factor remains unresolved.

More explicitly, fix ANY q in(0,1). The mechanism below obeys, for every
lambda>0 and every K>=1,
 sum p <= T * (log K+lambda+1)
              / [q*(-log(q+(1-q)exp(-lambda)))].        (1)
Use the single fixed q=exp(-1) for (iii). Only the analysis parameter lambda
depends on K; the mechanism's noise law remains fixed for all topologies.
If there is no structural s-t path, set all payments zero and exclude that
trivial topology from the definition of K. If T=0, the same rule gives zero.

1. EFFECTIVE CAPACITIES AND MASKS
Let D_h(c) be the largest flow through h among all feasible s-t flows of any
total, not just maximum flows. This maximum is attained. DAG path decomposition
gives 0<=D_h(c)<=min(c_h,T(c)). The map D_h is nondecreasing in each report.
Every feasible flow for c is bounded coordinatewise by D(c), and D(c)<=c,
so the two capacity vectors define the same feasible-flow set. More strongly,
for ANY edge mask b in{0,1}^E, the capacity vectors c*b and D(c)*b define
the same feasible-flow set: any feasible masked raw flow is feasible in c,
and hence bounded by D(c); the reverse inclusion is immediate.
In particular, F(D(c)*b)=F(c*b), including all coalitions and zero masks.

Fix a report-independent total priority order on the K structural cuts.
Sample iid Bernoulli B_h with Pr(B_h=0)=q and Pr(B_h=1)=1-q. Select W(c,B)
as the first minimum-scoring structural cut for scores D_h(c)B_h.
Structural minimal cuts suffice to attain every nonnegative-capacity mincut.
Define the auxiliary covering rule and the proposed mechanism by
 z_e(c)=D_e(c)Pr(e in W(c,B)),
 p_e(c)=D_e(c)Pr(e in W(c,B) | B_e=0).                 (2)
This is a finite, everywhere-defined, deterministic expectation rule.

2. THE STRICT SUPPORT LEMMA
Fix c_-e, a positive high report u>0, and any0<t<=u. Write D_h(s) for the
effective capacity when c_e=s. Then
 D_h(t)>=D_h(0)+(t/u)(D_h(u)-D_h(0)).                   (3)
Indeed mix a flow attaining D_h(0) and a flow attaining D_h(u), with weight
t/u on the latter. Every other capacity is fixed; flow through e in the mix
is at most (t/u)u=t. The mixed flow is feasible at report t and proves (3).
Monotonicity also gives D_h(0)<=D_h(t)<=D_h(u). Consequently
 D_h(t)>D_h(0) iff D_h(u)>D_h(0), for EVERY0<t<=u.       (4)
Thus a cap that increases at any positive report has already increased
strictly at every positive report, however small. No differentiability or
nondegeneracy assumption is used.

3. CONDITIONING ON THE OWNER'S ZERO MASK REMOVES OWN-REPORT DEPENDENCE
Fix a mask b with b_e=0. Let V=F(c*b); it is independent of c_e because e
is masked out. By Section1, at every report s>=0,
 min_C sum_{h in C}D_h(s)b_h=V.                         (5)
At report0 every cut has score at least V. At a positive report t, a cut C
has score exactly V if and only if BOTH
 (a) sum_{h in C}D_h(0)b_h=V;
 (b) D_h(t)=D_h(0) for every open h in C.
This follows because all score increments are nonnegative. By (4), condition
(b) is the same for every positive t: compare any pair using their maximum u.
The ENTIRE family of minimizing structural cuts is therefore independent of
the positive own report. Its first member in the fixed priority order is also
independent. Hence a_e(c_-e)=Pr(e in W | B_e=0) is constant on c_e>0.
For positive own reports, p_e(c)=a_e(c_-e)D_e(c) is nondecreasing. At c_e=0,
D_e=0 and p_e=0, so the endpoint also obeys monotonicity. This proves (i),
with all ties, zero other reports, and arbitrary fixed owner identities allowed.

4. COALITION COVERAGE AND THE POINTWISE SANDWICH
At fixed c and B_-e, changing B_e from1 to0 lowers every e-containing cut
score by exactly D_e and leaves all other cut scores unchanged. If D_e>0,
an e-containing cut selected before this change remains the selected member
of the containing class and becomes strictly cheaper than every excluding
competitor. Thus conditional membership probabilities satisfy a_0>=a_1.
If D_e=0 the desired payment inequalities are trivial. Equation(2) gives
 z_e=D_e[q a_0+(1-q)a_1], p_e=D_e a_0,
 so z_e<=p_e<=z_e/q.                                  (6)
Every structural cut C satisfies
 sum_{e in C intersect S}D_e(c)>=v_c(S),                (7)
because D preserves coalition flow values and C cuts the coalition subgraph.
Taking the expectation of (7) for W proves z(S)>=v_c(S), hence p(S)>=v_c(S).
This proves (ii). It also shows p is a truthful pointwise majorant of the
BERNOULLI base rule, and its least own-report envelope y^Bern satisfies
 y_e^Bern<=p_e<=z_e^Bern/q.                             (8)
This does not contradict the polynomial obstruction for strictly positive
noise: Bernoulli's positive atom at zero is precisely the excluded hypothesis.

5. LOGARITHMIC BUDGET, INDEPENDENT OF OWNER COUNT
Assume T>0, put a_e=D_e/T in[0,1], and rho_C=sum_{e in C}a_e. An original
minimum-capacity structural cut A has D(A)=T: D(A)<=c(A)=T and every cut
has D-capacity>=T. Its masked score is at most T. Hence the selected W has
normalized masked score sum_{e in W}a_eB_e<=1, in every realization.

For lambda>0 put alpha=-log(q+(1-q)exp(-lambda))>0. The function
 f(x)=log(q+(1-q)exp(-lambda x))
is convex on[0,1], with f(0)=0 and f(1)=-alpha, so f(x)<=-alpha x there.
For any fixed cut C, exponential Markov and independence yield
 Pr(sum_C a_eB_e<=1)
 <=exp(lambda) product_C [q+(1-q)exp(-lambda a_e)]
 <=exp(lambda-alpha rho_C).                           (9)
If rho_W>s, some cut with rho_C>s has masked score<=1. A union over the K
structural cuts therefore gives
 Pr(rho_W>s)<=min(1,K exp(lambda-alpha s)).             (10)
Integrating this tail from0 to infinity, split at s0=(log K+lambda)/alpha:
 E rho_W<=s0+1/alpha=(log K+lambda+1)/alpha.            (11)
Since sum z=T E rho_W and sum p<=sum z/q, (11) proves (1).
At T=0, every D_e=0, so p=0 as already stated.

Take q=exp(-1) and lambda=log(2+log K)>0, valid for every K>=1. Then
 q alpha=exp(-1)*[1-log(1+(exp(1)-1)/(2+log K))],
which tends to exp(-1), while log K+lambda+1=(1+o(1))log K. This proves
(iii). The chosen denominator is positive even at K=1 because
1+(exp(1)-1)/2=(exp(1)+1)/2<exp(1).
The coefficient exp(1) is an upper-bound coefficient for THIS proof, not an
optimality statement. Indeed maximizing q log(1/q) gives exp(-1); improving
the particular domination/tail analysis or changing the mechanism may improve it.

6. VERIFICATION, ESTABLISHED INGREDIENTS, AND REMAINING QUESTIONS
Established ingredients: maxflow/mincut, path decomposition, convex flow
mixtures, exponential Markov/Chernoff bounds, and tail integration. The proposed
contribution is the conditioned-zero cut selection, its exact own-report
invariance, and the resulting owner-independent logarithmic subsidy bound.
The exact rational verifier checks masked-flow preservation, whole minimizing
families, strict-cap support, cut-membership domination, every coalition, and
selected positive unilateral reports on small graph families. It is a check
of implementation/algebra and corner cases, not proof by finite extrapolation.
Unresolved: sharp general K-order between log K/log log K and log K; efficient
exact computation; specialist review and priority. The method's extension to
general binary resource-packing games is being checked separately.

APPENDIX: THE PREVIOUS GENERAL MARGINAL BOUND
For context define m_e(c)=T(c)-F(c with e deleted). It is nonnegative and
own-report nondecreasing because the deletion value does not depend on c_e.
For any original minimum-capacity structural cut C, deleting e in C gives
F(c with e deleted)<=T-c_e; deleting e-containing path flows from a maximum
flow loses at most c_e, giving the reverse inequality. Thus m_e=c_e on C,
and m(S)>=c(C intersect S)>=v_c(S). For ANY maximum flow f, the same path
deletion argument gives m_e<=f_e. DAG path decomposition implies sum m<=LT,
where L is the longest s-t path length. Finally L<=K: for each edge e of a
fixed length-L path, a vertex cut separating that path's prefix through the
tail of e from its remaining vertices meets the path exactly in e; a minimal
subcut still contains e. These L subcuts are distinct. Thus KT was an
elementary general K-only existence bound, independently of the envelope.
