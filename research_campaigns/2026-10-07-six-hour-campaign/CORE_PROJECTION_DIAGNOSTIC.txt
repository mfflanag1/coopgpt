1. FIXED OWNERS, CORE, AND PROJECTION CONVENTION

There are four fixed owners, A={0,1}, B={2,3}. For a capacity vector c, let
v_c(S)=min(sum_{i in S intersect A}c_i, sum_{j in S intersect B}c_j).
The original efficient core is
 C(c)={x in R^4: sum_i x_i=T=v_c(N), sum_{i in S}x_i>=v_c(S) for every S}.
Singleton inequalities imply x_i>=0. Define the marginal/utopia vector
 U_i(c)=T-v_c(N\{i}). The audited rule is the unique Euclidean projection
of U(c) onto C(c), equivalently the minimizer of (1/2)||x-U||_2^2.

Low report L=(1/2,1,1,1); high report H=(1/2,1,2,1). Only owner 2 increases
its own report. Owners, technology, and all other reports remain fixed.

2. LINEAR PRODUCTION EXAMPLE

At L, T=3/2 and U=(1/2,1,1/2,1/2). Its projection onto the efficiency
hyperplane subtracts (sum U-T)/4=1/4 from every coordinate, giving
 xL=(1/4,3/4,1/4,1/4).
It belongs to the full efficient core. Empty/single-side coalitions have value
zero. The four mixed pairs have values 1/2,1/2,1,1 and matching payments.
The triples {0,1,j} have value 1 and payment 5/4; {0,2,3} has value 1/2
and payment 3/4; {1,2,3} has value 1 and payment 5/4. The grand coalition
has value and payment 3/2. Thus every one of the 16 inequalities holds.
Since xL minimizes squared distance on the entire efficiency hyperplane,
it minimizes it on the contained core as well; strict convexity gives uniqueness.

At H, T=3/2. IMPORTANT: the high marginal vector is
 U(H)=(1/2,1,1/2,0), not the high core allocation.
For any high core allocation x, coalition {0,1,2} has value 3/2, so x3<=0.
Singleton coverage gives x3=0. The coalitions {0,3} and {1,3} then force
x0>=1/2 and x1>=1. Efficiency and nonnegativity force
 C(H)={(1/2,1,0,0)}.
That vector covers every coalition: the A-side capacity sum bounds coalition
production from above. Owner 2's projected payment falls from 1/4 to 0.

Exact KKT convention. Write cover constraints as
 g_S(x)=v(S)-sum_{i in S}x_i<=0,
with nonnegative multipliers mu_S and efficiency multiplier lambda. Stationarity
is x_i-U_i+lambda-sum_{S containing i}mu_S=0. At L use lambda=1/4 and every
mu=0. At H use lambda=1 and mu_{012}=mu_{03}=mu_{13}=1/2, every other mu=0.
All nonzero cover multipliers are attached to binding constraints. These are
exact feasible KKT certificates for the stated strictly convex projection.

3. FOUR UNIQUE RESOURCE TYPES AND FOUR FIXED ACTIVITIES

Give owner i the unique resource type i in amount c_i. There are four
activities y02,y03,y12,y13>=0, each consuming one unit of its A resource
and one unit of its B resource, with profit one. Resource constraints are
 y02+y03<=c0, y12+y13<=c1, y02+y12<=c2, y03+y13<=c3.
The maximum total activity is min(c0+c1,c2+c3): either pool gives the upper
bound, and greedy bipartite transportation attains it. Setting capacities of
nonmembers to zero realizes exactly the preceding characteristic game. No
public resource, grouped owner, owner cloning, or changing ownership is used.
The embedded verifier gives primal and dual certificates for all 32 coalition
LPs across L and H.

Scope distinction for comparing with a source. At both L and H the unique
optimal resource-price dual vector is (1,1,0,0). Indeed write a=min(p0,p1),
b=min(p2,p3). Pair feasibility gives a+b>=1 and nonnegativity gives a,b>=0.
With aggregate A=3/2 and aggregate B>3/2, the dual objective is at least
A*a+B*b >= A+(B-A)*b, with equality at the optimum only if b=0,a=1. Since
all four capacities are positive, equality also forces both A prices to be a
and both B prices to be b. Hence resource-price-generated owner payments are
already the singleton (1/2,1,0,0) at the LOW report. This example concerns the
full original efficient core, and does not refute a rule whose target is that
smaller dual-price-generated allocation set.

4. STRICTLY INCREASING, STRICTLY CONCAVE UTILITY EXTENSION

Keep the same four owners, resources, activities, constraints, and reports.
Replace each activity's linear profit by u(t)=10t/(10+t), t>=0, and maximize
sum_{ij}u(yij). This u is globally strictly increasing and strictly concave:
 u(0)=0, u'(t)=100/(10+t)^2>0, u''(t)=-200/(10+t)^3<0.
Let v now be this nonlinear packing optimum. This extension is not asserted
to be homogeneous. Define
 a=2u(1/4)=20/41, b=2u(1/2)=20/21, T=a+b=1240/861.
At both reports, splitting each A row equally between the two B columns is
feasible (each B column uses 3/4) and is optimal by concavity within each row.

At L, deletion values are (b,a,b,b), so U(L)=(a,b,a,a). The efficiency
hyperplane projection is
 xL=(a/2,b-a/2,a/2,a/2).
It is nonnegative and satisfies every core inequality:
 - empty/single-side coalitions have value zero;
 - pairs {0,j} have value u(1/2)<=2u(1/4)=a and payment a;
 - pairs {1,j} have value u(1)<=2u(1/2)=b and payment b;
 - triples {0,1,j} have value b and payment b+a/2;
 - triple {0,2,3} has value a and payment 3a/2;
 - triple {1,2,3} has value b and payment b+a/2;
 - the grand coalition has value and payment T.
Therefore this is the unique full-core projection. A KKT certificate is
lambda=a/2, all cover multipliers zero.

At H, the value of {0,1,2} is u(1/2)+u(1). Put
 delta=T-u(1/2)-u(1)=20/41+10/21-10/11=520/9471>0.
The high marginal vector is (a,b,a,delta). Every efficient core allocation x
satisfies x3<=delta by coalition {0,1,2}. The coalitions {0,3} and {1,3}
give x0+x3>=u(1/2) and x1+x3>=u(1). Summing these and using efficiency yields
 x2<=delta+x3<=2delta=1040/9471.
The high core is nonempty: (a,b,0,0) covers every coalition. More generally,
for any coalition the utility contributed by each present A row is at most
2u(c_i/2), so its total value is at most the allocated a/b sum.
Consequently the high Euclidean projection exists uniquely, regardless of
its uncomputed coordinates, and must pay owner 2 at most 2delta. Its low
payment is a/2=u(1/4)=10/41, and
 10/41-2delta=1270/9471>0.
Thus the same full-efficient-core projection rule violates own monotonicity
under globally strictly concave, strictly increasing separable activity utility.
