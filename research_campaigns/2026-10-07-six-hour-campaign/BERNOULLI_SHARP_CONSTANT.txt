THE LEADING COEFFICIENT exp(1) IS SHARP FOR THE CONDITIONED-ZERO BERNOULLI FAMILY
7 October2026. Coordinator and independent Codex derivations; separate audit
pending. This is a mechanism-class result, NOT a general mechanism lower bound.

Let p^q be the mechanism in BERNOULLI_LOG_BOUND.txt, for any q in(0,1).
For directed multigraphs with K structural minimal cuts,
 inf_{q in(0,1)} sup_{G,c:T>0} p^q(E)/T
   =(exp(1)+o(1)) log K.                              (1)
In particular q may depend arbitrarily on K. The infimum is over ONE common
Bernoulli parameter per graph size, not mixtures or owner-dependent parameters.
The lower bounds work for every fixed cut priority and use strictly positive
reports and fixed original owners. Simple-DAG conversion is not asserted here.

For any fixed profile the exact identity is
 p^q(E)=(1/q) E[D(W)-sum_{e in W}D_e B_e].             (2)
It follows by summing p_e=(D_e/q)E[(1-B_e)1{e in W}].

A. SMALL q
Take K serial pools, each two owners of capacity1/2. All D equal reports,T1.
The selected cut has closed D-mass1 when some pool is allclosed,1/2 when no
pool is allclosed but some owner isclosed, and0 when all areopen. Thus
 p^q(E)=[1-(1/2)(1-q^2)^K-(1/2)(1-q)^{2K}]/q
 >=[1-(1-q)^{2K}]/(2q)>=K/(1+2Kq).                  (3)
The last inequality follows from (1-q)^{-2K}>=(1+q)^{2K}>=1+2Kq.
This rule's cost tends to K as q tends tozero; rare closure is not cheap.

B. HEAVY POOLS WITH A CONCENTRATED BOTTLENECK
Put N=K-1,L=log N>=16. For q>=L^{-2}, let h=log(1/q)<=2log L and set
 m=floor((L-sqrt(L))/h), R=ceil(sqrt(L)/h).
There are N serial pools of m unit-capacity owners, followed by one pool
of R owners each report1/R. T1; every effective cap equals its report.
There are exactly K structural cuts, regardless of m or R.
On the event that the final cut score ispositive and at least one heavy
pool is allclosed, every selected cut is an allclosed heavy pool, and its
closed D-mass is m. Independence and(2) therefore give
 p^q(E)>=(m/q)(1-q^R)[1-(1-q^m)^N].                  (4)
The parameters imply
 Nq^m>=exp(sqrt L), q^R<=exp(-sqrt L),
 m>=(L-sqrt L-2log L)/h.
Since q log(1/q)<=1/exp(1), (4) gives the uniform lower bound
 p^q(E)>=exp(1)*(L-sqrt L-2log L)
          *(1-exp(-sqrt L))*(1-exp(-exp(sqrt L))).     (5)
All owner counts are finite for every q<1, however close to1. The numerator
is positive and m>=2 when L>=16.

If instead q<L^{-2}, (3), L^2<=K and L>=16 give
 p^q(E)>=K/(1+2K/L^2)>=L^2/3>=exp(1)L.              (6)
Equations(5)-(6) prove a lower bound (exp(1)-o(1))log K UNIFORMLY in q.
The upper follows by the one fixed q=exp(-1) in BERNOULLI_LOG_BOUND.txt.

CAREFUL INTERPRETATION
The parallel edges are distinct original owners, fixed before reports; no
owner splitting is a strategic operation. Pool cardinalities can grow without
limit. This optimizes the Bernoulli-parameter family only. It neither rules
out other truthful covering rules nor closes the general loglog K gap.
