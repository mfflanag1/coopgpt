1. A NATURAL TRUTHFUL MAJORANT
Use the model of THEOREM.txt in the preceding monotone-envelope campaign.
For each owner e and independent uniforms U define
 A_e^0=min_(C contains e) sum_(h in C\e) D_h(c_e=0,c_-e) U_h,
 B_e^c=min_(C excludes e) sum_(h in C) D_h(c) U_h,
 p_e(c)=E min(D_e(c),(B_e^c-A_e^0)_+).
If there is no excluding cut put B_e^c=infinity and p_e=D_e.
The minimum over containing cuts exists: every edge belongs to a minimal cut,
as follows by retaining an s-t path through that edge and blocking its complement.

A_e^0 is independent of c_e. Both B_e^c and D_e(c) are nondecreasing in c_e.
Thus p_e is nonnegative and own-report monotone on all real capacity profiles.
Because A_e^0<=A_e^c, the conditional integration formula for z gives p>=z.
Hence p covers every coalition and, by monotonicity, p>=y. On TTSP graphs,
co-cut independence gives A_e^0=A_e^c and p=z=y. These correct properties do
not imply a useful general budget. The candidate below disproves that hope.

2. UNBOUNDED FIXED-OWNER NETWORK FAMILY
Replace the edge s->A of a Wheatstone graph by a k-edge serial path X with
distinct fixed owners x_1,...,x_k, all capacities1. Add b:s->B capacity beta,
and a:A->t, g:A->B, h:B->t, all capacities1, where 0<beta<1.
There are k+4 fixed owners. No identity change occurs within any game or report.
Every edge lies on an s-t path; the three path types are X+a, b+h, X+g+h.

Minimal cuts containing a serial owner are exactly {x_i,b} and {x_i,h}.
A minimal cut without a serial owner must contain a and block both b+h and
g+h, giving {a,h} and {a,b,g}. Thus K=2k+2. All effective capacities equal
the stated reports. T=1+beta, attained by one unit on X+a and beta on b+h.
The source cut proves the matching upper bound.

Deleting any x_i kills X. Only the path b+h survives, so
 D_b^0=D_h^0=beta and all other D^0 are zero.
Consequently A_i^0=beta min(U_b,U_h). Every excluding cut has score at least
 m=min(beta U_b,U_h): cuts {x_j,b} or {a,b,g} include b, and cuts {x_j,h}
or {a,h} include h. Therefore, since 0<=m-A_i^0<=beta<1,
 p_i >= E[m-A_i^0]
     = (beta/2-beta^2/6)-beta/3
     = beta(1-beta)/6.                                      (1)
The expectation uses E min(beta U,V)=integral_0^beta (1-t/beta)(1-t)dt and
E min(U,V)=1/3 for two independent uniforms.

At beta=1/2, sum_e p_e>=k/24=(K-2)/48. Dividing by T=3/2 gives
 sum_e p_e/T >= (K-2)/72.                                 (2)
Thus this truthful covering majorant has no O(log K/log log K) budget.

The coefficient is asymptotically exact. For k>=2 put M=min_(j!=i) U_j.
The excluding family includes {x_j,b} and {x_j,h}, so m<=B_i^c<=m+M.
Positive-part and truncation are 1-Lipschitz, and E M=1/k. Hence
 beta(1-beta)/6 <=p_i<=beta(1-beta)/6+1/k.
The four other owners have total p at most3+beta. It follows that
 k beta(1-beta)/6 <=sum_e p_e<=k beta(1-beta)/6+4+beta.
For beta=1/2 this is k/24+O(1), or asymptotically K/72 after division by T.

3. THE ACTUAL ENVELOPE STAYS BOUNDED HERE
If x_i reports t in [0,1], every serial effective cap becomes t. Also
 D_a=D_g=t, D_b=beta, D_h=min(1,beta+t).
For t>0, if a selected cut contains x_i then replacing x_i by any x_j in the
same cut type shows U_i<=U_j. This event has probability1/k. At t=0 its payment
is zero. Thus z_i(t)<=t/k<=1/k, y_i<=1/k, and sum_(i in X)y_i<=1.
The remaining four envelope coordinates are at most their current effective
caps, whose sum is3+beta. Therefore sum_e y_e<=4+beta=9/2 uniformly in k.
No envelope lower bound or impossibility for all truthful mechanisms follows.

4. WHY THIS ATTACK STOPS
The hybrid calculation lowers h's cap in containing cuts as though x_i were
deleted, while retaining current caps in excluding cuts on the other serial
owners. That artificial mismatch is counted independently for all k owners.
Actual unilateral reports lower all serial caps together, preserving the
1/k competition. Any further relaxation must retain this shared competition.
Do not spend another campaign wave tuning this failed hybrid formula.
