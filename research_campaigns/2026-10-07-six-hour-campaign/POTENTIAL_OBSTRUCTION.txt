ROUTE C/A: A TRUTHFUL POTENTIAL REPAIR AND AN EXACT LINEAR-COST OBSTRUCTION
7 October 2026. Coordinator candidate; independent audit pending.

1. A NATURAL ALTERNATIVE THAT REALLY IS TRUTHFUL AND COVERS COALITIONS
Let U have independent Uniform[0,1] coordinates. Define
 Psi(c)=E F(D(c)*U),
where F is maxflow and D is the effective-capacity normalization. All capacities
are nondecreasing in every report, so Psi is nondecreasing. For c^0 obtained by
setting only owner e's report to zero, define
 p_e(c)=2[Psi(c)-Psi(c^0)].
This is nonnegative and nondecreasing in c_e, because its second term does not
depend on c_e. It is also a pointwise majorant of the base z, and hence of y.

Proof of domination. For fixed U_-e, set
 g(u)=F(D_-e(c)*U_-e, u D_e(c))-F(D_-e(c)*U_-e,0).
The maxflow is a minimum of affine cut scores, hence g is concave on [0,1],
nonnegative, with g(0)=0. Thus g(u)>=u g(1), and E_Ue g(U_e)>=g(1)/2.
Coordinatewise D_h(c^0)<=D_h(c), with D_e(c^0)=0, implies
 Psi(c^0)<=E F(D_-e(c)*U_-e,0).
Subtract and integrate: Psi(c)-Psi(c^0)>=z_e(c)/2, where z_e=E g(1).
Consequently p>=z and p covers every coalition. Since p is own-report monotone,
the least-majorant property of y gives p>=y. This uses fixed original owners.

2. WHY THE PROMISING COLLECTIVE BOUND IS FALSE
An attempted bound was sum_e[Psi(c)-Psi(c^0)]<=sum_e z_e(c), which would have
implied the desired 2U(K)T result. It is false, even in the already safe TTSP
class. Small numerical samples did not show an aggregate violation; they did
show coordinatewise violations. The following exact family supersedes that
exploration as an obstruction to this proposed collective inequality.

Take a branch A consisting of k serial edges, each report 1, in parallel with
a single edge b of capacity beta=1/2. Put a final single edge r of capacity 1
in series after that parallel composition. Every edge has its own fixed owner.
There are k+2 owners, k+2 vertices (including the junction and final terminal),
K=k+1 minimal structural cuts, L=k+1, and T=1. The cuts are {r} and
{a_j,b}, j=1,...,k. Parallel edges are allowed at k=1; the family is simple
for k>=2. D_a=1 for all A edges, D_b=beta, and D_r=1.

Write A_k=min(U_1,...,U_k), B=beta U_b and V=U_r. Then
 Psi(c)=E min(A_k+B,V).
When ANY A-edge owner is erased, the whole A branch carries no flow. Effective
caps on B and r both become beta, and all A caps become zero. Therefore
 Psi(c^0)=E min(beta U_b,beta U_r)=beta/3.
On the other hand, since A_k>=0,
 Psi(c)>=E min(beta U_b,U_r)=beta/2-beta^2/6.
Every one of the k A owners has a potential deletion marginal at least
 beta(1-beta)/6=1/24.
Thus sum_e p_e>=k/12, while T=1 and K=k+1. This linear lower bound rules out
an O(U(K)) budget for p. It is an obstruction to THIS potential repair only.

In fact A_k tends to zero almost surely and bounded convergence gives
Psi(c)->5/24. Removing b leaves k+1 serial uniform edges, so Psi(c with b=0)
=1/(k+2); removing r gives zero. Hence the exact total is
 sum_e p_e=2[(k+2)Psi(c)-k/6-1/(k+2)] ~ k/12 ~ K/12.
The original envelope on this TTSP graph equals z. Since the possible selected
cut weights are 1 and 3/2, its total is at most 3/2 for EVERY k at this profile.
Thus the potential repair can overpay its intended envelope by a linear factor.
Neither the general sharp envelope conjecture nor the sharp minimax conjecture
is disproved by this example.

3. OPTIONAL EXACT INTEGRAL FORMULAS FOR REPRODUCTION
For any 0<beta<=1 let phi_k=E min(A_k+beta U,V). With X=1-A_k having density
k x^(k-1), conditional expectation is 1/2-x^3/(6 beta) for x<=beta and
1/2-x^2/2+beta x/2-beta^2/6 for x>=beta. Integrating gives
 phi_k=beta^k/2-k beta^(k+2)/(6(k+3))
       +(1/2-beta^2/6)(1-beta^k)
       -k(1-beta^(k+2))/(2(k+2))
       +k beta(1-beta^(k+1))/(2(k+1)).
The probability q_k that a cut {a_j,b} wins is
 q_k=k beta^(k+1)/(2(k+2))
     +k(1-beta^(k+1))/(k+1)-beta(1-beta^k)/2.
The base total is exactly 1+beta q_k; each A edge receives q_k/k.
Both identities admit an independent rational CDF-algebra check.

4. ROUTE DECISION
Reject this potential's deletion marginals as a sharp general repair, even
though its incentive and coverage properties are valid. The mechanism reacts
to deletion-induced changes of normalization on a shared downstream edge, once
for EVERY series edge in the long branch. Its base random flow was already
insensitive to that branch when k was large. This repeats the same induced loss
across many owners. Any new potential must prevent this repeated charge.
No novelty or field-level significance claim is made for these elementary
mechanism and obstruction observations. They are an exact no-go for this route.
