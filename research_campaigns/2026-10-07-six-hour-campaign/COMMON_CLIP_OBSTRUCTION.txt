This closes an alternative normalization, not the actual-envelope question.

Candidate mechanism: a_e(c)=min(c_e,T(c)); select a minimum structural cut
under independent a_e U_e and pay w_e=a_e Pr(e is in that cut). Common clipping
preserves every coalition flow and satisfies the same U(K) budget argument as
effective clipping, since a_e<=T and an original minimum cut still sums to T.
The missing incentive property fails already on a ten-owner, four-cut DAG.

1. FIXED NETWORK
Use vertices s,A,B,t. Owners are x:s->A with report1; z:A->t with report3;
g:A->B with report delta=1/10; focal h:B->t with varying report h; and N=6
parallel owners Y_i:s->B each report1/N. Compare h0=3/4 with h1=1.
The four structural minimal cuts are
 {x} union Y; {x,h}; {z,h}; {z,g} union Y.
Hence T(h)=min(2,1+h) on the interval and the common clipped caps are
 a_x=1, a_z=1+h, a_g=delta, a_h=h, a_Yi=1/N.
All ten owners stay fixed and all edges are on s-t paths; longest path is3.

2. CONDITIONAL PAYMENT FORMULA
Write X=U_x, Z=(1+h)U_z, G=delta U_g, and Y=N^(-1)sum_i U_i.
The cheapest h-containing intercept is min(X,Z). The cheapest h-excluding
cut is Y+min(X,Z+G). The elementary identity
 min(X,Z+G)-min(X,Z)=min(G,(X-Z)_+)
therefore gives
 w_h(h)=E min(h,Y+W_T), W_T=min(delta U_g,(U_x-T U_z)_+), T=1+h. (1)
Y is independent of W_T. Since T>=1, the latter has survival function
 Pr(W_T>r)=(1-r/delta)(1-r)^2/(2T), 0<=r<=delta,
and zero survival for r>delta. In particular
 E W_T=(delta/4-delta^2/6+delta^3/24)/T=187/(8000T).    (2)
As h increases, the varying common cap of z lowers this bonus. For sufficiently
concentrated Y, the increased focal cap h does not offset that decrease.

3. EXACT TEN-OWNER CERTIFICATE
Let
 F_N(u)=N^N/N! sum_{j=0}^N (-1)^j binom(N,j)(u-j/N)_+^N,
 L_N(u)=N^N/(N+1)! sum_{j=0}^N (-1)^j binom(N,j)(u-j/N)_+^(N+1).
These are respectively Pr(Y<=u) and E(u-Y)_+ for the uniform average Y.
Symmetry Y=distribution 1-Y gives
 E min(h,Y)=1/2-L_N(1-h).
Integrating the bonus in(1) yields the finite rational formula
 w_h(h)=1/2-L_N(1-h)
       +integral_0^delta [(1-r/delta)(1-r)^2/(2T)] F_N(h-r) dr. (3)
Every integral is a polynomial on rational intervals. At N=6, direct exact
integration, independently checked using the density and atom of W_T, gives
 w_h(3/4)-w_h(1)=587847649425223/914457600000000000>0.    (4)
Thus common-grand-value clipping violates own-report monotonicity. The proof
is finite and exact; the discovery QMC scans are not used to certify(4).

for z or y, and does not refute a sharp budget for either monotone envelope.
