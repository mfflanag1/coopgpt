FREE ORIGINALS CHECKED DURING THE SIX-HOUR CAMPAIGN
7 October 2026. These are bounded source checks, not confirmation of novelty.

Korenblit and Levit, The st-Connectedness Problem for a Fibonacci Graph (2002).
https://www.wseas.us/e-library/conferences/poland2002/papers/446-139.pdf
Original PDF inspected, especially Theorem3 (printed pp.3-4): number of
inclusion-minimal cuts is floor(n²/4). This is precisely the count used for
the Fibonacci lower family in CUT_HEIGHT.txt and is established prior work.

Korenblit and Levit, On a Reliability Polynomial of Fibonacci and Some Other
Graphs (2003 proceedings PDF).
https://www.wseas.us/e-library/conferences/digest2003/papers/459-144.pdf
Original PDF inspected. It defines the same Fibonacci graph, cites the above
count, and reproduces the cut-size distribution from their earlier On Sizes
of Mincuts paper. Search-index title was misleading; the PDF's actual title is
the reliability-polynomial title. Cut size and maximum path intersection h(C)
are different statistics. Neither the h moment nor the universal interval
injection was located in these two papers; absence here does not prove novelty.

Korenblit and Levit, Nested Graphs (2006), DOI10.1016/j.endm.2006.06.016.
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220082606_Nested_Graphs
Author-uploaded original text was accessible in search results. Theorem2.14
characterizes graphs with n-1 minimal cuts as nested and relates them to
series/parallel composition. This is adjacent prior structure, not a new
campaign theorem. Full publisher PDF and exact priority for cut-height bound
remain unconfirmed. No purchase or outreach performed.
