MAIN OUTCOME
The original repair target is false, but changing the mechanism produces the
desired sharp general bound. The strongest result is now a theorem for all
finite nonnegative min-linear production technologies, substantially beyond
weighted multi-glove games and series-parallel networks. Both the number of
owners and the network/technology complexity can grow without bound.

For fixed technology f(c)=min_(w in V) w.c, |V|=K, fixed owners report whole
nonnegative resource bundles b_i. Put T=f(sum_i b_i). There is a deterministic
payment rule that, at EVERY real report profile:
  - pays nonnegative amounts;
  - gives every coalition at least what it can produce by itself;
  - never increases an owner's payment when that owner hides resources;
  - has total payment at most (1+o(1))T log K/loglog K.
The guarantee is uniform in the owner count and resource dimension. It applies
to nonnegative resource-production LPs with fixed positive activity profits
and no zero-resource positive-profit activity; dual vertices may be fractional.
For single-commodity networks K is the structural minimal-cut count.

The existing weighted report-transition lower supplies the matching leading
constant one for the general class. A new shared-fan construction transfers
that sharp lower to SIMPLE directed acyclic graphs, treating every auxiliary
edge owner as strategic. The sharp statement is for worst cases with at most
K structural cuts, with a matching constructed sequence of exact cut counts.
It does not silently pad to every exact K. Naive edge subdivision would fail:
full subdivision into pairs of separately owned serial edges instead permits
a truthful efficient rule, which is proved and checked separately.

Payment factor and subsidy are different quantities. If total payment is at
most C*T, the additional external subsidy is at most (C-1)*T. All factors in
this report bound TOTAL payments. Utilities here are receipts with no operating
costs, and reports are binding componentwise underreports. No arbitrary private
cost, overreporting, false-name, or group-strategyproof guarantee is asserted.

WHY THE SUCCESSFUL CONSTRUCTION WORKS
An owner is rewarded partly using a supporting price selected with that whole
owner's report omitted, and partly through the production added by including
that owner. Each selected price contribution is capped at T. Both terms are
monotone in the owner's entire bundle, and their combination covers coalitions.
Averaging over a short range of independent deletion probabilities lets the
marginal term integrate into a controlled boundary contribution. A Chernoff
bound over the fixed supporting forms supplies logK/loglogK with leading one.
The cap is essential: an exact two-owner, two-form example makes the uncapped
version unbounded even at K=2, although that example has an efficient rule.

Precise finite bound for any integer j>=2:
  C_j(K)=j+(log K+2log j+1)/log[j^3/(j^2+j-1)].
Taking j=max(2,ceil(log K/[log(2+log K)]^2)) gives the stated asymptotic.
Adding the classical grand-minus-complement marginal divided by K protects
each owner's original-core maximum divided by K at additional cost at most T.
Thus the earlier fairness floor can be retained without changing leading one.

TWO FURTHER GENERAL ADVANCES
First, the construction extends to EVERY finite-valued monotone, concave,
degree-one homogeneous production function in finite dimension, including
functions discontinuous at the boundary. Relative supporting forms are
extended by infinity outside their support face and clipped before payments
are formed. All selected-ON terms remain finite. At a fixed report profile
at most 2^n forms can be selected, giving a total-payment upper bound
  (log 2+o(1))T n/log n.
This is not claimed sharp in n. The imported hierarchy, with explicit owner-
count conversion, gives a lower of (1-o(1))log n/loglog n. An elementary dT
fallback is available in resource dimension d. The concave dimension-only
rate also remains open; a d lower for NONCONCAVE technologies does not close it.

Second, for rationally encoded finite technologies the theorem has an
algorithmic implementation route.
An exact rational mask sampler gives a finite rule with exact incentives for
every seed. Uniform convergence yields one frozen seed covering every coalition
at every real report profile with an explicit confidence and polynomial sample
bound. An exact rational arrangement/LP procedure can certify a seed across
ALL real profiles, including every tie face. Repeated sampling and certification
is Las Vegas preprocessing: every returned rule is correct, but preprocessing
can be exponential. Once certified, evaluation uses polynomially many ordinary
LP calls in the stated rational input/sample parameters. This is not a practical
sample-size guarantee or polynomial-time derandomization.

WHAT FAILED, AND WHY THAT MATTERS
The actual own-report monotone envelope of the original continuous-noise rule
has a polynomial-in-K lower obstruction, even on simple DAGs. Every truthful
pointwise majorant of that rule inherits the obstruction. This resolves the
original attempt's stopping question rather than merely failing to find a proof.
Its general upper was improved to K*T, but its exact growth rate remains open.

Further examined routes have explicit limits: uniform Shapley can badly miss
coalition coverage; a full uniform mixture of the conditioned-OFF term alone
costs Theta(K); one fixed Bernoulli probability achieves only leading e*logK
within that rule class. Fixed-noise classifications, clipping failures, path-
star limits and exact small-profile certificates are archived in ROUTES.txt.
None is stated as an impossibility against the successful hybrid or against
all truthful mechanisms.
