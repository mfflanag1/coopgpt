EXACT ALL-REAL-PROFILE CERTIFICATION AND SEARCH FOR A FINITE MASK SEED
7 October 2026. Conventional proof; independent cross-model audit completed.

VERDICT
The proposed certificate is valid. It gives an exact terminating decision
procedure for one seed, and—combined with the uniform good-seed theorem—a
Las Vegas preprocessing algorithm that eventually returns a certified seed.
The key requirements are (i) rational technology/weights/budget threshold,
(ii) all tie faces, and (iii) FROZEN face formulas when optimizing over a face
closure. Efficient derandomization is not established.

There is no hidden incompatibility between the posterior-mean finite sampler
and the uniform theorem. Although theta_|S| depends on the full mask, including
the sampled own bit, it does not depend on reports. Conditional expectation
preserves the exact mean; the weights remain in [0,1]; and the growth argument
treats theta_|S| as a fixed scalar for each observation. None of those arguments
requires theta to be independent of S.

1. FIX THE EXACT INPUT

Let n,d>=1 and V=(w^1,...,w^K) be an ordered, COMPLETE finite list of rational
vectors in Q_+^d, with K>=1. Define
  F(c)=min_v w^v.c,
  v_b(U)=F(sum_{i in U}b_i),     T(b)=v_b(I),
where owner reports b_i range over ALL of R_+^d and I={1,...,n}.
The priority on V is fixed independently of reports.

The seed consists of N>=1 masks S_1,...,S_N. The finite-law coefficients
theta_0,...,theta_n are supplied rationals in [0,1], and eta>=0 and Cbar are
supplied rationals. For the posterior sampler they are the exact theta_k in
SAMPLING_COROLLARY.txt, and eta>0 in the uniform theorem. The checker itself
does not require that the seed was randomly drawn.

For mask S and owner i, let
  W^-_{i,S}(b)=first argmin_v w^v.sum_{h in S\{i}}b_h,
  A_{i,S}(b)=min(W^-_{i,S}(b).b_i,T(b)),
  Delta_{i,S}(b)=v_b(S union {i})-v_b(S\{i}),
  Xtilde_i(S,b)=theta_|S| A_{i,S}(b)+(1-theta_|S|)Delta_{i,S}(b).
The frozen-seed payment is
  P_i(b)=N^{-1}sum_l Xtilde_i(S_l,b)+(eta/n)T(b).

The exact checker decides whether BOTH of the following hold:
  sum_{i in U} P_i(b)>=v_b(U) for every U subseteq I and every real b>=0;
  sum_i P_i(b)<=Cbar*T(b) for every real b>=0.                       (1)
It is sound and complete for (1), not merely a sufficient finite test.
Universal own-vector monotonicity follows separately for every seed from the
mechanism formula and need not be re-certified by this checker.

If technology is supplied by a rational production LP rather than an explicit
V, one may first enumerate its dual vertices exactly. For Q={w>=0:A^T w>=r},
enumerate all linearly independent active d-tuples, solve them rationally,
retain feasible solutions, and deduplicate. Under the stated bounded packing
model this gives all vertices. This enumeration is a preprocessing cost, not
an assumed polynomial-time vertex-counting algorithm.

2. HOMOGENEOUS NORMALIZATION AND THE ARRANGEMENT

Every payment, coalition value, and budget slack in (1) is positively
homogeneous of degree one in the entire report vector. Scaling preserves
every score tie, fixed-priority winner, and cap branch. The zero report vector
has zero payments and values. Thus it suffices to check the compact simplex
  Delta={b in R_+^{p}:sum_{i,h} b_{ih}=1},      p=nd.                (2)
Normalize by total report mass, NOT by T. Nonzero profiles with T=0 remain
inside the domain and cause no division-by-zero issue.

For every coalition U and vertex v, define the rational homogeneous form
  L_{U,v}(b)=w^v.sum_{i in U} b_i.
Make an arrangement with the following rational homogeneous hyperplanes:
  L_{U,v}-L_{U,t}=0       for all U subseteq I and v<t;
  w^v.b_i-L_{I,t}=0       for all i and all v,t.
Omit identically zero forms if desired. The number H satisfies
  H<=2^n binom(K,2)+nK^2.                                         (3)
The first family fixes the entire minimizing family and priority winner for
EVERY coalition, including every sampled off/on coalition and the grand
coalition. The second fixes all possible cap branches. No report-dependent
sampling coefficients occur.

If the optional zero-reporter cushion eta*T/n * 1{b_i!=0} is used instead,
add the n forms sum_h b_{ih}=0. On the nonnegative domain these identify
zero bundles exactly; the rest of the proof is unchanged.

3. EXACT STRICT-FACE FEASIBILITY

Write the retained arrangement forms as ell_1,...,ell_H. For each ternary
sign pattern sigma in {-1,0,+1}^H define its relatively strict region
  C_sigma={b in Delta:
      ell_h(b)=0 when sigma_h=0,
      sigma_h ell_h(b)>0 when sigma_h!=0}.
These regions partition Delta. A region may lie on a proper coordinate face
of the nonnegative simplex; that creates no difficulty.

Let P_sigma be the relaxed polytope obtained by replacing each strict >0
with >=0. Determine whether C_sigma is nonempty using this rational LP:
  maximize tau,
  b in Delta,
  ell_h(b)=0                         if sigma_h=0,
  sigma_h ell_h(b)>=tau              if sigma_h!=0,
  0<=tau<=1.                                                      (4)
If the LP is infeasible or its optimum is zero, discard the pattern.
If its optimum is positive, retain it and a rational feasible witness z_sigma
with tau>0. If there are no strict constraints, (4) has optimum 1 exactly
when its equality-constrained simplex is nonempty, so this case is covered.

The criterion is exact: a point satisfying finitely many strict rational
inequalities has a positive minimum margin; conversely tau>0 enforces them.
LP (4) has rational data and a compact feasible set. Feasible optima and
positive-margin witnesses can be represented exactly by rational vectors.

The tiny exact checker uses an equivalent vertex-average criterion: enumerate
all vertices of P_sigma and average them. Every signed strict form is already
nonnegative on P_sigma. It is positive somewhere iff it is positive at some
vertex. Thus all strict forms can simultaneously be positive iff their values
at the average of ALL vertices are positive. This average supplies the needed
strict witness; the margin LP is one implementation, not a requirement.

IMPORTANT CLOSURE LEMMA. For every retained pattern,
  closure(C_sigma)=P_sigma.                                      (5)
To prove the nontrivial inclusion, take x in P_sigma and the strict witness
z_sigma. For every 0<t<=1, b_t=(1-t)x+t z_sigma belongs to Delta, satisfies
the equality signs, and satisfies every strict sign, since
  sigma_h ell_h(b_t)>=(t) sigma_h ell_h(z_sigma)>0.
Thus b_t is in C_sigma and tends to x. This proof handles simplex boundaries,
lower-dimensional faces, degeneracy, and arbitrary rational hyperplane scales.

4. FROZEN LINEAR FORMULAS AND THE DECISION LPS

Evaluate the coalition winners and cap branches at z_sigma using exact
arithmetic. Their identities are the same throughout C_sigma, with equality
ties resolved by the prescribed priority. Hence, on C_sigma, each coalition
value is a fixed rational linear form V_{U,sigma}, T is V_{I,sigma}, and each
seed payment P_i is a fixed rational linear form P_{i,sigma}.

For every U subseteq I form the linear coverage slack
  G_{U,sigma}=sum_{i in U}P_{i,sigma}-V_{U,sigma},
and form the linear budget slack
  G_{budget,sigma}=Cbar*V_{I,sigma}-sum_i P_{i,sigma}.
For each of these finitely many slacks solve the exact rational LP
  minimize G(b) subject to b in P_sigma.                         (6)
Accept the seed iff every optimum in (6) is nonnegative, for every retained
pattern and every coverage/budget slack.

The objective in (6) is the FROZEN FACE FORMULA evaluated on the closure.
At a boundary point outside C_sigma it need not equal the actual payment
computed using the boundary's different tie-resolved winner. Re-running the
mechanism at closure vertices and ignoring their originating face would not
be a valid substitute. All lower-dimensional tie regions are checked under
their own actual formulas as separate retained patterns.

Why the closure LP is nevertheless equivalent: a linear form is continuous,
so it is nonnegative on C_sigma iff it is nonnegative on closure(C_sigma).
Equation (5) identifies that closure with the LP polytope. Thus (6) has a
negative optimum iff the original inequality fails somewhere in C_sigma.

An explicit rational violating profile can also be returned. Let x be a
rational closure optimizer with G(x)<0 and let z=z_sigma. Choose a sufficiently
small positive rational t, for example
  t=min(1/2, -G(x)/(2[1+|G(x)|+|G(z)|])).
Then b_t=(1-t)x+t z is a rational point in C_sigma and G(b_t)<0. Its actual
mechanism branch is exactly the frozen branch, so it is a genuine violation.
Checking the other tie faces is essential for completeness, not an optional
generic-position assumption.

T=0 requires no exceptional optimization. Every coalition value then vanishes
by monotonicity, each capped term is zero, every marginal is zero, and every
payment is zero. On a retained face closure, its selected value forms remain
valid minimizing forms; a cap branch using w.b_i still obeys w.b_i<=T there.
Consequently the frozen payment forms also vanish at closure points with T=0.
No spurious strict violation is created by such a point.

Combining normalization, exhaustive face partition, and (5)-(6) proves the
checker is sound and complete for all real report profiles.

5. WHAT AN EXACT FINITE CERTIFICATE CAN CONTAIN

A verifiable certificate can include:
  - an exhaustive face-enumeration tree;
  - positive rational margin witnesses for retained branches;
  - exact infeasibility or upper-bound-zero certificates for discarded
    margin branches;
  - the frozen winner/cap identities and rational linear formulas at leaves;
  - rational LP dual certificates establishing nonnegative lower bounds for
    every slack in (6).
These use only rational equalities and inequalities. Standard LP duality
provides the dual certificates for feasible bounded rational LPs. One can
instead enumerate vertices of each compact P_sigma exactly and evaluate the
frozen forms there, retaining the originating-face labels. Either method is
finite. Certificate checking is polynomial in the explicit certificate length;
that length can be exponential in the original problem parameters.

A smaller SUFFICIENT certificate is possible if desired. On the same faces,
the exact mean mechanism M_i is a rational linear form because the full mask
law has only 2^n masks and rational probabilities/weights. Certify P_i>=M_i for
each i, plus the budget inequality. This uses n+1 objective checks per face
instead of 2^n+1. It is not complete for coverage itself, but every seed in the
uniform concentration event satisfies this stronger condition, so the search
termination argument below still applies.

6. HONEST PREPROCESSING COMPLEXITY

Blind enumeration of all 3^H sign assignments is finite but may be DOUBLY
exponential in n because H already contains 2^n. It is unnecessary.

Enumerate only realizable signs incrementally. Start with Delta; insert one
hyperplane at a time. For each retained prefix try its three sign extensions
and apply the margin LP to the prefix forms. Inductively every nonempty
region is kept, and every point of Delta has its sign path represented.

The number of nonempty ternary sign faces of h hyperplanes in R^p is at most
  sum_{k=0}^p 2^k binom(h,k) <= (1+2h)^p.
Therefore at most 3H(1+2H)^p margin LPs suffice. At the end at most
(1+2H)^p faces require at most 2^n+1 slack LPs each. Every LP has p or p+1
variables and O(p+H) constraints. Coefficients have bit length polynomial in
the explicit rational technology, seed, eta, Cbar, and finite-law weights.

Using exact rational LP algorithms, or even active-set enumeration on these
compact polytopes, gives a conservative total bound of the form
  (2^n+1)(1+H)^{O(nd)} * poly(N,n,d,K,L),                          (7)
where L bounds the relevant rational input bit lengths. In particular, the
combinatorial factor is singly exponential in a polynomial of n,d,log K:
  2^{O(nd[n+log(K+1)+log(n+1)]+n)}.
This is not a polynomial preprocessing claim. The cost of enumerating V from
a compact LP input is additional; it too has a finite active-set bound.
Arrangement geometry, face witnesses, and closure constraints depend only on
technology and can be reused across candidate seeds.

7. CERTIFIED SEED SEARCH AND SUBSEQUENT EVALUATION

Choose 0<eta<=1, j, a sample size N meeting the uniform theorem, and 0<delta<1,
all independently of reports. The restriction eta<=1 belongs to the quoted
uniform sample bound; the seed-agnostic checker itself permits any eta>=0.
Supply a CERTIFIED rational bound
  Cbar >= B_j(K)+2eta
(or use a certified upper bound on K). A rational bound avoids inserting
transcendental coefficients into the exact LP tests. If needed, an arbitrarily
small additional budget slack permits a rational Cbar certified by rigorous
logarithm enclosures.

Repeat:
  1. Draw a fresh length-N iid seed from the exact finite mask law.
  2. Run the exact all-profile checker.
  3. Reject on a genuine violation; output the seed and certificate on success.

The uniform theorem says each trial is accepted with probability at least
1-delta: all good concentration seeds satisfy both (1) inequalities. Trials
are independent. Thus termination is almost sure, expected trials are at most
1/(1-delta), and every returned seed is correct, regardless of the randomness.
Taking delta=1/2 makes the expected number of verification runs at most two.
Exact finite-law fair-bit sampling has expected polynomial cost; its rejection
loops also terminate almost surely. Combining these facts with (7) gives
finite expected, generally exponential, preprocessing. There is no finite
worst-case bound on the number of independent random trials.

Alternatively enumerate the finite seed space deterministically. Existence
of a good seed implies eventual success, after at most 2^(nN) candidate
sequences. This is a finite exhaustive construction, not efficient
derandomization. A smaller Cbar lacking the stated guarantee can still be
tested exactly, but the probability theorem would no longer guarantee search
termination or the expected-trial bound.

Preprocessing and certification use the technology and ALL-profile conditions,
not the actual owners' reports. Freezing the accepted seed consequently
preserves the already established universal individual incentives.

Subsequent evaluation need not revisit the exponential certificate. It uses
at most 1+N(n+1) canonical value/minimizer queries. For a compact rational
production LP with lexicographic priority on dual vertices, each canonical
query needs at most d+1 exact LP calls, giving at most
  1+N(n+1)(d+1)
ordinary LP calls. This is polynomial evaluation for rationally encoded
reports in the displayed sample/input parameters. An arbitrary supplied
priority over an explicit V is evaluable by scanning V, polynomial in its
explicit size K; it does not automatically admit the same compact-LP oracle.
For all real reports the certified mechanism is mathematically well-defined;
bit-model running time is asserted only for finite rational encodings.

The result upgrades random good-seed existence to an exact, always-correct
output after potentially enormous preprocessing. It does not establish
polynomial preprocessing, a practical certificate size, or an efficient
deterministic construction of a good seed.
