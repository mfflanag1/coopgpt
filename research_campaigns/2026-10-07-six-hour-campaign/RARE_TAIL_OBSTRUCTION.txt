SEPARATE RARE-TAIL NOISE FAMILY: EXACT OMEGA(sqrt(K)) ENVELOPE OBSTRUCTION
7 October 2026. This concerns the strictly-positive-noise base-cut envelope,
NOT the conditioned-zero Bernoulli mechanism or its uniform-q mixture.

For every integer K>=4, let q=K^(-1/2), choose any 0<epsilon<=1/8, and use
iid noise X with law
 (1-q) delta_1 + q Uniform(0,epsilon).
The noise is positive almost surely, bounded, and nondegenerate. Epsilon
may depend on K and may tend to zero arbitrarily fast.

Use K serial pools of two fixed original owners each. At the base profile
each pool has capacities (3/4,1/4), so T=1 and all effective capacities D
equal reports. The base payment is z_e=D_e Pr(e in the selected minimum
cut under edge scores D_h X_h); ties use any fixed priority. Its least
own-report monotone envelope is y_e(c)=sup_{0<=t<=c_e} z_e(t,c_-e).

For each pool separately, lower its 1/4 owner to 1/8. This profile has
grand flow 7/8, and every raw capacity remains at most 3/4, so D still equals
the report vector. Consider the event that the companion 3/4 owner's noise
comes from the low component, the focal owner's noise equals one, and no
other pool has both noises from the low component. Its probability is
 q(1-q)(1-q^2)^(K-1).
On this event the focal pool's score is at most
 1/8+(3/4)epsilon <=7/32<1/4.
Every competing pool has an owner whose noise equals one, and hence score
at least 1/4. The focal pool is the unique minimum cut, regardless of cut
priority. Its lowered owner's payment is at least one eighth of the event
probability. Transporting this value by the envelope to the base and summing
over the K focal owners yields
 sum_e y_e(c) >= (K/8) q(1-q)(1-q^2)^(K-1)
              >= sqrt(K)/64.                            (1)
Indeed 1-q>=1/2 and (1-1/K)^(K-1)>=exp(-1)>1/4.
No perfect-square assumption, limiting approximation, or favorable tie is
used. This disproves the proposed escape via this particular K-dependent
rare-tail family; it does not prove a dichotomy for every K-dependent law.

The motivating scalar variance heuristic alone is insufficient: for this
law Var(X)=q(1-q)(1-epsilon/2)^2+q epsilon^2/12, of order q, while certain
fixed bridge-module mean gaps can be at most m q epsilon. Thus variance
does not lower-bound the bridge gap uniformly over shrinking epsilon. The
different two-owner-pool event above supplies the growing obstruction.
