A SHARED RANDOM CUT GIVES THE ACTUAL MONOTONE ENVELOPE A K*T BUDGET
7 October2026. Coordinator and independent Codex proof checks complete;
opposite-model audit completed; no invalid step found. This improves this rule's bound, not the best

THEOREM
In the fixed-edge-owner DAG model of CONTRACT.txt, with arbitrary nonnegative
real capacity reports, let K be the number of inclusion-minimal structural
s-t cuts and let T(c) be grand maxflow. Let D_e(c) be maximum feasible edge
flow (any total), z_e(c)=D_e(c)Pr(e belongs to the selected random minimum cut
under independent D_h(c)U_h), and y_e(c)=sup_{0<=t<=c_e}z_e(t,c_-e).
The selection minimizes over exactly the K structural minimal cuts, with a
fixed cut tie order. This includes zero-capacity profiles without ambiguity.
Then y is nonnegative, own-report truthful under binding underreports,
coalition-covering, and
 sum_e y_e(c)<=K*T(c).                                   (1)
More precisely, writing d_C=sum_{h in C}D_h(c), for T>0,
 y_e(c)<=T*D_e(c)*sum_{C containing e}1/d_C.              (2)
All owners and the graph are fixed during each unilateral comparison.

1. ELEMENTARY UNIFORM COMPARISON
For nonnegative finite coefficient vectors a,b and independent U_i uniform
on[0,1], put A=sum a_i,B=sum b_i. Then
 A*Pr(sum a_iU_i<=sum b_iU_i)<=B.                        (3)
If A<=B, probability<=1 proves it, including A=B and identical vectors.
If A>B, put N=sum(b_i-a_i)_+, W=sum|a_i-b_i|=A-B+2N.
Reflect each U_i whose coefficient a_i-b_i is negative. Independence and the
uniform laws are preserved; the event becomes
 sum |a_i-b_i|V_i<=N.
Here W>0 and 0<=N<W/2. A positive weighted sum of independent uniforms has
a symmetric unimodal density about W/2. Its CDF F is convex on[0,W/2], with
F(0)=0 and F(W/2)=1/2. The chord bound gives F(N)<=N/W. Since N<=B,
 N/(A-B+2N)<=B/(A+B),
and therefore A*Pr<=AB/(A+B)<=B. This includes B=0. Zero weights are omitted.

For completeness, uniform convolution preserves symmetric unimodality here
without any external theorem. Center the variables. If f is even and
nonincreasing on[0,infinity), its convolution with a centered uniform density
on[-r,r] is even and has derivative [f(x+r)-f(x-r)]/(2r)<=0 for x>=0,
almost everywhere, because |x+r|>=|x-r|. The convolution is absolutely
continuous, so the derivative proves monotonicity. Induct from one uniform.
This also establishes the lower-half CDF convexity used above.

2. ALL UNILATERAL PROFILES SHARE ONE ORIGINAL RANDOM BENCHMARK
Fix c with T>0 and write D=D(c). Flow preservation under effective capping
implies d_C>=T for every structural cut and permits a fixed minimum cut A
with d_A=T. Define original-profile random scores
 Z_C=sum_{h in C}D_hU_h.
Take e with D_e>0. Reports t>=D_e leave every feasible flow unchanged, so
the envelope may be restricted to0<=t<=D_e. The case t=0 pays0. For t>0 set
x=t/D_e and c^t=(t,c_-e). For every edge h,
 xD_h(c)<=D_h(c^t)<=D_h(c), and D_e(c^t)=t.               (4)
The upper bound is feasible-set inclusion. To prove the lower one, take any
flow attaining D_h(c) and scale it by x. Its e-coordinate is at most xD_e=t
and every other coordinate stays feasible. To get equality for h=e, use a
flow attaining D_e. This proof scales flows, not owners or their identities.

Couple all profiles through the same independent uniforms. If the selected
minimum cut at c^t is C, then
 xZ_C<=score_{c^t}(C)<=score_{c^t}(A)<=Z_A.               (5)
Apply(3) to a_h=xD_h*1_{h in C}, b_h=D_h*1_{h in A}.
Their totals are x d_C and T. Weak event inclusion in(5) gives
 x d_C*Pr(W^{c^t}=C)<=T,
 t*Pr(W^{c^t}=C)<=T D_e/d_C.                            (6)
No independence between cuts is assumed: overlap is handled by subtracting
coefficients in(3). Selection ties are harmless because(5) is weak.

3. SUM BEFORE TAKING THE OWN SUPREMUM
For every t in the allowed range,
 z_e(c^t)=sum_{C containing e}t*Pr(W^{c^t}=C)
          <=T D_e sum_{C containing e}1/d_C.
The right-hand side is independent of t, proving(2). Summing over owners,
 sum_e y_e<=T sum_C [sum_{e in C}D_e]/d_C=KT.
Zero-D owners pay0. If T=0 then all D vanish on a DAG, so all payments vanish.
Truthfulness follows because the own-report supremum domain expands with
the report; coalition coverage follows from y>=z and the existing cut-based
coverage proof. All these statements apply on the entire report domain.

4. EXACT SCOPE OF THE IMPROVEMENT
This supersedes the earlier 2H(G)T envelope accounting, including its campaign
improvement H=O(K^(3/2)). The cut-height theorem remains true and sharp for H,
but no longer supplies the best bound on this particular envelope.
A different grand-deletion marginal mechanism already has budget L*T<=K*T.
Thus(1) does not improve the general mechanism-existence bound. Its value is
a substantially simpler bound on the mechanism targeted by this campaign,
using a shared random benchmark instead of bounding every score by T.
The aggregate obstruction in AGGREGATE_ENVELOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt rules out
the O(U(K)) target for this envelope. Its exact worst-case growth between that
lower bound and K remains open. GENERAL_PRODUCTION_THEOREM.txt obtains the
sharp general payment bound through a different mechanism. Neither result
by itself confirms historical novelty or recognized field-level significance.
