7 October 2026. Conventional proof, coordinator checked; focused cross-model audit found no invalid step.
No novelty claim. This is an obstruction to a proof strategy, NOT an aggregate
subsidy lower bound and NOT an impossibility for other truthful mechanisms.

Definitions are those of BASE_FLOW_DOMINATION.txt. For effective capacities D,
z_e=D_e Pr(e belongs to the selected minimum random cut), with independent

THEOREM. There is no universal finite C for either coordinatewise assertion
 y_e(c)<=C z_e(c), or y_e(c)<=C r_e(c).
This holds on finite DAGs with longest path4 and minimal-cut height at most2.
Owners are fixed throughout each report comparison. Structural cut count grows.

1. GRAPH AND EFFECTIVE CAPACITIES
For integer m>=1 use vertices s,A,B_1,...,B_m,q,t and these distinct owners:
 x:s->A (the focal report),
 y_i:s->B_i with capacity epsilon=1/10,
 g_i:A->B_i and h_i:B_i->q, each with capacity2,
 k parallel Z_j:A->q, each with capacity2,
 N parallel P_j:s->q, each capacity(1-2b)/N,
 N parallel R_j:q->t, each capacity1/N.
Take 0<b<=1/100, k>=1, and compare x0=1/10 with x1=9/10.
Indeed all focal reports 0<=x<=9/10 have total maxflow T=1: the direct P
bundle supplies1-2b, and the y_i branches supply at least2b to q. The R bundle
is the unit bottleneck. The individual effective caps are exactly
 D_x=x, D_yi=epsilon, D_gi=D_Zj=x, D_hi=x+epsilon,
 D_Pj=(1-2b)/N, D_Rj=1/N.                              (1)
Each equality follows by sending the specified amount on paths through that
edge, together with the evident prefix/suffix bottleneck upper bound.

Writing a bundle symbol for all its edges, the structural minimal cuts are:
 (a) R;
 (b) P union{x} union one choice of {y_i} or {h_i} for every i;

Every minimal cut comes from q outside the source side (and either A outside
or A inside), or is R. The displayed cuts are minimal: each edge has an s-t
path meeting its cut only at that edge. They are distinct, so
 K=2^(m+1)+1, L=4, and H=sum_C h(C)=3*2^m.               (2)
In (b), all but the all-y cut have height2; all other cuts have height1.
The number of owners is 1+3m+k+2N, fixed between the two profiles.

2. REDUCING THE FOCAL PAYMENT TO A SIMPLEX INTEGRAL
Let P,R,Z also denote their independent random capacity score sums. Put
 B=R-P, A_i=min(epsilon U_i,(x+epsilon)V_i), A=sum_i A_i.
Then EB=b and Var(B)=[1+(1-2b)^2]/(12N)<=1/(6N).
The minimum containing-cut intercept is P+A. The minimum excluding-cut
score is min(R, P+Z+sum_i min((x+epsilon)V_i,epsilon U_i+x W_i)).
Integrating out the focal uniform yields exactly
 z_x=E min(x,[min(B-A,
          Z+sum_i min(x W_i,((x+epsilon)V_i-epsilon U_i)_+))]_+).  (3)
On Z>=1 the second argument cannot bind, since B-A<=R<=1. Hence with
 J_x(s)=E_A min(x,(s-A)_+),
 |z_x-J_x(b)|<= E|B-b|+Pr(Z<1)
              <=1/sqrt(6N)+10^k/k!.                     (4)
The first step uses that the clamp is 1-Lipschitz in s. The truncation error
is at most its probability, because 0<=payment<=x<=1. The Z lower-tail
bound is the simplex-volume bound for x sum_j U_j, with x>=1/10.

For 0<=a<=epsilon the density of A_i is
 f_x(a)=alpha_x-beta_x a,
 alpha_x=10+1/(x+epsilon), beta_x=20/(x+epsilon).
When 0<b<=min(epsilon,x), no upper density breakpoints enter and
 J_x(b)=integral_{a_i>=0,sum a_i<=b}(b-sum a_i) product_i f_x(a_i) da.
The unweighted simplex integral is I=b^(m+1)/(m+1)!.
For exact finite checks the polynomial identity is
 J_x(b)=sum_{j=0}^m binom(m,j) alpha_x^(m-j)(-beta_x)^j
                         b^(m+j+1)/(m+j+1)!.            (5)

3. EXPLICIT FINITE PARAMETERS AND DIVERGENCE
Set
 b=1/(100m), Q=11^m b^(m+1)/(m+1)!,
 N=ceil(16/Q^2), k=2 ceil(8/Q).
At low x0, each density on the integration simplex is >=15-1/m, so
 J_low(b)>=(15-1/m)^m I>=(14/15)15^m I.                 (6)
At high x1, each density is at most11 and at least11-1/(5m), whence
 (54/55)Q<=J_high(b)<=Q.                                (7)
The lower estimates use (1-u/m)^m>=1-u.
Our N gives1/sqrt(6N)<=Q/8. Also Q<=11/20000<0.0011, hence k>=800.
For even k>=800, k!>=(k/2)^(k/2), and therefore
 10^k/k!<=(200/k)^(k/2)<=2^(-k)<=1/k<=Q/16.
Consequently (4) is at most3Q/16<=Q/4 at either profile. In particular
 z_high >=(54/55-1/4)Q=161Q/220>0.
Because x0 is an admissible underreport at the high profile,
 y_x(c_high)/z_x(c_high)
 >= [(14/15)(15/11)^m-1/4]/(5/4)
 = (56/75)(15/11)^m-1/5 ->infinity.                     (8)
Since r_high<=2z_high, y_x/r_high is unbounded too. All parameters are finite
for each m, all changes are focal underreports, and no owner is introduced
or removed during an incentive comparison.

Formula(5) gives

This K=17, 200060-owner graph directly gives y_x>2z_x>=r_x.

5. SCOPE AND RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE
