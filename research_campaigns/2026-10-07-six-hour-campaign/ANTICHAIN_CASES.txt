ACTUAL ENVELOPE BUDGETS FOR TWO INCOMPARABLE OWNERS AND PARALLEL BUNDLES
7 October 2026. Conventional proof, coordinator checked; focused independent audit found no invalid step.
These are structural lemmas for the original effective-capacity envelope y.
They do not establish the full antichain conjecture or sharp aggregate budget.

Use the fixed DAG, fixed owners, arbitrary nonnegative capacity reports, effective
caps D, base random-cut payments z and own-report envelope y from CONTRACT.txt.
T denotes the ORIGINAL grand maxflow. A path-antichain A is a set of edges such
that no s-t path contains two of them. In particular every path containing one
of its edges avoids all other edges of A. All flows can be decomposed into paths.

1. RIVAL-FLOW BOUND
For e in A let R_{-e} be the maximum total flow using ONLY s-t paths which meet
A\{e}. This is a finite path-flow LP, and every such path avoids e. Consequently
R_{-e} is unchanged by any own underreport of e and satisfies0<=R_{-e}<=T.
Then
 y_e(c)<=min(D_e(c), max(T-R_{-e},T/2)).                 (1)

Proof. Fix an arbitrary underreport t and write c^t and d=D_e(c^t). A flow
attaining R_{-e} is still feasible under c^t, avoids e, and remains feasible under
the effective caps D(c^t). Every structural cut C containing e must block it;
therefore D(c^t)(C\{e})>=R_{-e}. If d>T-R_{-e}, every such containing cut has
deterministic effective-capacity sum greater than T. The minimum effective cut
has capacity T(c^t)<=T, so at least one minimum cut C0 excludes e. With A_t the
minimum random containing-cut intercept and B_t the minimum excluding-cut score,
the conditional formula and nonnegativity imply
 z_e(c^t)=E min(d,(B_t-A_t)_+)<=E B_t
         <=E sum_{h in C0}D_h(c^t)U_h=T(c^t)/2<=T/2.
If d<=T-R_{-e}, simply z_e(c^t)<=d<=T-R_{-e}. This proves the maximum bound
uniformly in t. The independent bound y_e<=D_e(c) completes(1).

2. EVERY TWO-OWNER ANTICHAIN IS SAFE
For any two path-incomparable owners e,f,
 y_e(c)+y_f(c)<=T(c).                                  (2)

The effective cap D_f can be attained by retaining only paths through f from
an attaining flow. These paths avoid e, so R_{-e}>=D_f. Hence(1) gives
 y_e<=max(T-D_f,T/2), and symmetrically with e,f exchanged.
If D_f<=T/2 then y_e<=T-D_f and y_f<=D_f. If only D_e<=T/2, exchange names.
If both exceed T/2, each envelope is<=T/2. All zero and equality cases are
included. This proves(2) for arbitrary interacting surroundings, not only TTSP.

3. EVERY PARALLEL-EDGE BUNDLE IS SAFE
Let X be the complete set of parallel owners from u to v. Put
 q=sum_{i in X}c_i,
 B=min(maxflow(s,u),maxflow(v,t)),
omitting a prefix/suffix bound whose terminals coincide. If both are omitted
put B=infinity. These prefix/suffix flows are independent of all X reports;
their path regions are disjoint because G is acyclic. Thus
 D_i=min(c_i,B), and the maximum total flow through X is min(q,B)<=T.
We claim
 sum_{i in X}y_i(c)<=min(q,B).                           (3)

If q<=B, sum y_i<=sum c_i=q. If B=0 all D_i and payments vanish. Thus assume
0<B<q. Replace the parallel bundle temporarily by one aggregate capacity q
when considering feasible flows on external edges. This aggregation preserves
exactly their feasible flow projections: total bundle flow can always be split
among its original owners according to their capacities. Every such total flow
is at most B. Therefore all EXTERNAL effective caps depend on the bundle only
through q and are constant whenever q>=B. Each other bundle owner's effective
cap is min(c_j,B), independently of i's report.

Every structural minimal cut contains either the entire parallel bundle or none
(use its source-side boundary representation). Thus at the current profile
 z_i=D_i p, with one common cut-inclusion probability p. Write W=sum_i D_i>0.
BASE_FLOW_DOMINATION.txt gives a single feasible flow dominating all base z;
its total through X is at most B, hence Wp<=B.

For a fixed owner i put s_i=q-c_i and delta_i=(B-s_i)_+. At every underreport
t>=delta_i, total bundle capacity stays>=B. All effective caps other than i's
remain fixed, and the usual conditional clamp formula makes its base payment
nondecreasing in t. Thus these underreports cannot exceed z_i(c). For t<delta_i,
the payment is at most t<=delta_i. We conclude y_i<=max(z_i,delta_i).
If delta_i=0, the first term is<=BD_i/W. If delta_i>0, all other c_j<=s_i<B,
so W=D_i+s_i and D_i>=B-s_i=delta_i. Consequently
 delta_i W=(B-s_i)(D_i+s_i)<=B D_i.
Both terms in the maximum are<=BD_i/W; summing proves(3).
For a subset of parallel owners, apply the whole-bundle bound and also the
individual bounds y_i<=c_i, obtaining min(sum_subset c_i,B).

4. EXACT LIMIT OF THIS ADVANCE
Any counterexample to sum_{e in A}y_e<=T requires at least three positive
path-incomparable owners, not all belonging to one parallel bundle, with
sum_{e in A}D_e>T. For three or more owners, (1) can leave each payment bounded
only by T/2. A common feasible flow dominating all separately optimized payments
has not been constructed. The above results do not prove such a flow exists.
No historical-priority claim is made.
