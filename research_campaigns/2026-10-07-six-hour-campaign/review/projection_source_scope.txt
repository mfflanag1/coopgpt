INDEPENDENT MATHEMATICAL CHECK OF COORDINATOR'S LINEAR EXAMPLE

For four resources with recipe pairs 02,03,12,13 and unit linear profits,
V_c(S)=min(sum_{i in S intersect {0,1}}c_i,
           sum_{i in S intersect {2,3}}c_i).

At c=(1/2,1,1,1), T=3/2 and the marginal vector is
v=(1/2,1,1/2,1/2). The core is exactly
  x(w)=(1/2-w, 1-w, w, w), 0 <= w <= 1/2.
Indeed, the complementary pairs (02,13) and (03,12) each have coalition
values summing to T; hence their core constraints bind. All remaining
coalition inequalities hold throughout this segment. The squared distance
to v equals 2w^2 + 2(w-1/2)^2, uniquely minimized at w=1/4.

After only c_2 increases to 2, T remains 3/2. Coalition 012 now has value
T, so core efficiency and x_3>=0 force x_3=0. Coalition constraints for 03
and 13 force x_0>=1/2 and x_1>=1; hence the core is the singleton
(1/2,1,0,0). Owner 2's projected receipt falls from 1/4 to 0.

Thus the linear production instance rigorously refutes own-capacity
monotonicity of Euclidean marginal-contribution core projection. Attribution
as a counterexample inside ABV's stated full domain remains conditional on
the unresolved admissible-utility assumption. Do not assert this final scope
claim from the displayed Problem 1 alone.
