OWNER-INDEPENDENT PERSONAL CEILINGS: TRUTHFUL, SMALL BUDGET, UNBOUNDED COVERAGE LOSS
7 October2026. Conventional proof, independently derived/checked by coordinator;
focused cross-model audit found no invalid step. This is a mechanism-class obstruction, not
an impossibility for truthful subsidy mechanisms in general.

1. CANDIDATE AND VALID PROPERTIES
For owner e define B_e=min_{C not containing e} c(C), with B_e=infinity if
no structural minimal cut avoids e. This equals the limiting maxflow when
c_e tends to infinity and is independent of e's report. Put
 a_h^(e)=min(c_h,B_e), interpreting min(c_h,infinity)=c_h,
 p_e(c)=a_e^(e) Pr(e belongs to the minimum cut under a^(e)*U).
Each owner's expectation is deterministic; owners and the network stay fixed.
The random multipliers are independent uniform variables, with fixed tie order.

Own-report monotonicity is immediate from the conditional clamp formula:
all a_h^(e) for h!=e are fixed, and the focal cap min(c_e,B_e) is nondecreasing.
At a fixed profile with T>0, every B_e>=T. If B_e=T, p_e agrees with the
common-grand-flow clipped base w_e. If B_e>T, every deterministic minimum cut
contains e. All such exceptional owners therefore lie in one common minimum
cut C*, have c_e<=T, and satisfy sum_exceptional p_e<=c(C*)=T.
The remaining payments are a subset of the nonnegative w vector, whose budget
is<=U(K)T. Consequently
 sum_e p_e(c)<=(U(K)+1)T.                                 (1)
At T=0, any clipped coalition game has zero maxflow and all the conditional
marginals vanish. Thus zero profiles cause no exception.
This proof is useful despite failure of the coverage condition below.

2. FINITE TTSP COUNTERFAMILY
Fix integer q>=4 and define
 m=q^2, delta=1/q^2, N=m! q^m.
The initial two-terminal component has two parallel branches: one owner e
of capacity delta, and a path comprising owner a of capacity1-delta followed
by m owners h_1,...,h_m, each of capacity m. After this component put N
components in series, each a bundle of m parallel unit-capacity owners b_jk.
All edges and owners are fixed for this network. Its structural minimal cuts
are exactly
 {e,a}, {e,h_i} (1<=i<=m), and C_j={b_j1,...,b_jm} (1<=j<=N).
Therefore
 T=1, K=N+m+1, vertices=m+N+2, owners=m+2+Nm, L=m+1+N.
The personal ceilings are
 B_e=B_a=m, B_hi=B_bjk=1.                                 (2)
For e only the bundle cuts avoid it. For a the alternatives have costs m or
m+delta. The unit cut{e,a} avoids every h_i and every bundle owner.

Choose coalition S consisting of e and one fixed original owner from every
bundle. It forms a single s-t path, so v_c(S)=delta.

3. EXACT COALITION PAYMENT AND PROBABILITY BOUNDS
Use one common uniform sample space to express the expectations. Define
 Y=min_{1<=i<=m} U_hi,
 Z=min_{1<=j<=N} sum_{k=1}^m U_jk,
 X_r=delta U_e+min((1-delta)U_a,rY).
The focal owner e uses ceiling m; every coalition bundle owner uses ceiling1.
All cuts and scores have continuous nondegenerate distributions, so ties have
probability zero. Since every winning bundle contains exactly one S owner,
 p(S)=delta Pr(X_m<Z)+Pr(Z<X_1).                          (3)

For 0<=z<=1, the m-uniform sum CDF is z^m/m!. Our N gives
 Pr(Z<1/(2q))<=N*(1/(2q))^m/m!=2^-m,
 Pr(Z>2/q)=(1-(2/q)^m/m!)^N<=1/(1+2^m).                 (4)
The second inequality follows from (1-x)^N<=1/(1+Nx), for0<=x<1,
by Bernoulli applied to (1-x)^(-N), or by (1-x)^(-1)>=1+x.

If X_m<Z<=2/q, then either (1-delta)U_a<2/q or mY<2/q.
A union bound on the m uniforms therefore yields
 Pr(X_m<Z)<=2q/(q^2-1)+2/q+1/(1+2^m).                   (5)
Also X_1<=delta+Y. Set t=1/(2q)-delta=(q-2)/(2q^2)>0.
If Z>=1/(2q) and Y<=t, then X_1<=Z. Thus
 Pr(Z<X_1)<=2^-m+(1-t)^m.                               (6)
Combining(3)-(6) and dividing by delta gives the wholly rational bound
 p(S)/v_c(S)<=R_q:=
 2q/(q^2-1)+2/q+1/(1+2^(q^2))
 +q^2[2^(-q^2)+(1-(q-2)/(2q^2))^(q^2)].                 (7)
Since (1-t)^m<=exp(-mt)=exp(1-q/2),
 R_q=(4+o(1))/q ->0.                                    (8)
No universal constant multiplying p restores coalition coverage on all DAGs,
or even on all TTSP graphs. In particular the exact rational verifier gives
 R_16<1/2, R_32<1/4, R_64<1/8.
These are finite explicit networks, even though their owner counts are enormous.

4. RELATION TO CUT COUNT AND SCOPE
Stirling gives log K~3q^2 log q and log log K~2 log q, hence
 log K/log log K~(3/2)q^2.
Thus this rule's necessary coverage multiplier is
 Omega(sqrt(log K/log log K)).
This does not improve the existing general truthful-mechanism lower bound.
In fact these are TTSP networks, where the original effective-capacity rule
already achieves the optimal asymptotic order. The obstruction explains why
owner-by-owner normalizations lose the common cut mixture needed for coverage,
even when their truthfulness and total-payment bound are easy to prove.
No historical novelty or specialist endorsement is claimed.
