DEFINITIONS
D_e(c) is the largest flow through e among all feasible s-t flows, of any total.
W is a minimum structural cut for iid scores D_e(c)U_e, U_e uniform[0,1],
using a fixed tie order. The payments and own-report envelope are exactly
 z_e(c)=D_e(c)Pr(e in W), y_e(c)=sup_{0<=t<=c_e}z_e(t,c_-e).

THEOREM
There is an explicit family of fixed-edge-owner DAGs and strictly positive
capacity profiles, with grand value T=1 and growing structural cut count K,
on which the actual own-report envelope y of the effective-capacity
continuous-Banzhaf rule satisfies
 sum_e y_e = Omega(K^(1/401)/log K).                       (1)
There is also an explicit SIMPLE-DAG family with
 sum_e y_e = Omega(K^(1/19201)/log K).                     (2)
Both bounds exceed every fixed multiple of U(K)~log K/log log K.
Thus this envelope, and EVERY own-report-monotone pointwise majorant of z,
fails the sharp-order budget target. This is NOT a lower bound for arbitrary
truthful coalition-covering mechanisms. The general optimal-mechanism question
remains open. No historical novelty or field-level breakthrough is claimed.

2. EXACT CUT COUNT AND EFFECTIVE CAPACITIES
One module has exactly2^{m+1} structural minimal cuts:
 (i) e_j plus one of y_{j,i},h_{j,i} for every i;
 (ii) all Z_{j,l}, plus either h_{j,i} or both y_{j,i},g_{j,i} for every i.
If e is cut, Z and g edges are redundant and each direct y-h path needs one
cut edge. If e is not cut, all direct e-Z paths require every Z; each branch
then requires h or both y,g. Each listed edge has an unblocked-path witness
if removed from the cut. This proves necessity, sufficiency, and minimality.
A structural minimal cut of a series composition lies in one component:
otherwise every component has a surviving path and concatenation survives.
The final bundle contributes one cut. Consequently
 K=N2^{m+1}+1=2^{401r+1}+1.                              (3)

With focal report q in {1,t}, a module's raw maxflow is exactly a+q, by its
source cut and the feasible routing of q along e-Z and all y flows along h.
Every module therefore retains capacity at least a>1. The final bundle fixes
global T=1 at the original profile and every tested underreport.
Prefix/suffix maxflow bounds give, within the module whose focal report is q,
 D_e=D_g=D_Z=q, D_y=a/m, D_h=min(1,q+a/m).                 (4)
Indeed the prefix to A has value q; a local prefix to B_i has value q+a/m.
Any preceding-module truncation is above1 and therefore irrelevant to D_h.
Suffix flow from A or B_i is1, limited by the final bundle. Each R owner retains its
reported effective capacity. All other modules' effective capacities stay
unchanged, since the altered module still has raw flow>a>1.
Thus high modules have D_h=1; the lowered module has D_h=(a+b)/m.

 E min(uU,vV)=u/2-u^2/(6v),                              (5)
by integrating (1-s/u)(1-s/v) from0 to u.

5. WHY THIS STOPS THE CURRENT APPROACH

The lower bounds therefore block every such repair, not just a particular
algorithm used to compute the envelope. They do NOT apply to mechanisms
