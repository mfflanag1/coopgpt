FIXED RANDOMIZATION LAWS CANNOT REPAIR THE ENVELOPE
7 October 2026. Candidate extension independently derived by Codex agents;
coordinator checked constants and flow formulas. Fable proof audit completed
without substantive findings; review/fixed_noise_review.md records its scope.

MODEL AND CLAIM
Fix a nonconstant probability law X with 0<X<=1 almost surely. The law does
not change with the graph, capacities, number of owners, or structural cuts.
For a fixed edge-owned DAG and report profile c, D_e(c) is the largest flow
through e among all feasible s-t flows, of any total. Independently sample
one X_e per original owner and select a minimum structural cut W for scores
D_e(c)X_e, using any fixed tie order. Define
 z_e^X(c)=D_e(c) Pr(e in W),
 y_e^X(c)=sup_{0<=t<=c_e} z_e^X(t,c_-e).
There are constants eta_X>0 and A_X>0 and fixed-owner simple DAGs with
growing structural cut count K, for all sufficiently large family parameters
r>=r0(X), and profiles with grand value T=1 such that
 sum_e y_e^X >= A_X K^{eta_X}/log K.
Every all-profile own-report-monotone pointwise majorant of z^X has the
same lower bound. Thus no single fixed law in this class gives an O(U(K))
envelope budget, U(K)~log K/log log K. This is a method obstruction, not a
general mechanism impossibility. The law may have atoms; tie-breaking is
irrelevant on the strict-separation event used below. No novelty claim.

PARAMETERS, ALL FINITE AND LAW-DEPENDENT
Let mu=EX, nu=E min(X,X'), and d=mu-nu. For independent copies,
 nu=mu-(1/2)E|X-X'|, so 0<nu<mu and d>0.
Put a=(mu+nu)/(2nu)>1, b=d/(4mu)>0, delta=d/40>0.
The low mean lambda=E min(aX,(a+b)X') obeys
 lambda<=a nu+b mu=mu-d/4=mu-10delta.                  (1)
The pointwise increment of min(aX,aX') when its second argument increases
by bX' is at most bX'. This proves the inequality.
The high mean H_m=E min(aX,mX') increases to a mu, by strict positivity
and dominated convergence. Since a mu-mu=mu d/(2nu)>d/2=20delta,
choose an integer m0 with H_m>=mu+10delta for all m>=m0.
Choose positive integers C,M and then B satisfying
 C >= (mu+10delta)/(b mu),   M >= delta^{-2},
 gamma=min{32delta^2/a^2, 32delta^2/(C b^2)},
 B >= max{1,m0,a+b,b/delta,2/gamma}.
For integer r>=4 put N=2^r, m=Br, k=Cm, and t=b/m.

MULTIGRAPH CONSTRUCTION AND EFFECTIVE CAPS
Use the modules of AGGREGATE_ENVELOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt: N in series, each
with focal e:s->A of report1; m edges y_i:s->B_i of capacity a/m;
g_i:A->B_i and h_i:B_i->exit of capacity2; and k parallel Z_l:A->exit
of capacity2. Finish with M parallel R owners of capacity1/M each.
All original owners remain fixed in every comparison. For each j separately
lower only its e_j to t. Raw module flow with focal report q in {1,t} is
a+q>1. Indeed all y-flow fits through h (a/m<=1), and q fits through Z.
Global T=1; neither prefix nor suffix flow below1 arises in other modules.
Thus
 D_e=D_g=D_Z=q, D_y=a/m, D_h=min(1,q+a/m), D_R=1/M.
For h this uses the final suffix bound1: a raw prefix may be truncated by
an earlier module but that earlier module has value>1, so cannot affect D_h.
This avoids requiring a raw prefix equality beyond the relevant range.
Because m>=a+b, low D_h=(a+b)/m. Every other module's effective caps are
unchanged under the separate focal underreport. The structural cut count is
 K=N2^{m+1}+1=2^{(B+1)r+1}+1.                          (2)

STRICT CUT COMPETITION
In one module the minimum containing-cut score is qX_e+A_q, where
 A_q=sum_i min((a/m)X_{y_i},D_{h_i}X_{h_i}).
Every excluding-cut score is at least Z_q=q sum_l X_{Z_l}.
At the high report E A_high=H_m>=mu+10delta.
At the low report E A_low=lambda<=mu-10delta.
The independent A summands have squared-range sum a^2/m. Hoeffding yields
 Pr(A_high<=mu+6delta)<=exp(-gamma m),
 Pr(A_low>=mu-6delta)<=exp(-gamma m).                  (3)
Low Z has mean Cb mu>=mu+10delta and squared-range sum Cb^2/m, hence
 Pr(Z_low<=mu+6delta)<=exp(-gamma m).                 (4)
High Z stochastically dominates low Z. The final score Q is the average
of M iid X copies. Since Var(X)<=1/4, Chebyshev gives
 Pr(|Q-mu|>2delta)<=1/(16M delta^2)<=1/16.              (5)
Fix one focal underreport. A union over all N relevant A variables and N
Z variables costs at most 2N exp(-gamma m). Each N exp(-gamma m) is at most
2^r exp(-2r)<=2^{-r}<=1/16. With probability at least13/16, therefore,
the low containing score is at most mu-6delta+t<=mu-5delta, the final cut
scores at least mu-2delta, and every other/excluding module cut scores
above mu+6delta. Every minimum cut contains the lowered focal owner.
Consequently y_{e_j}(c)>=t/2 and, at the ONE common original profile,
 sum_e y_e(c)>=Nb/(2m)=b2^r/(2Br).                    (6)
Combining (2) and (6) proves the claim with eta=1/(B+1) for multigraphs.

SIMPLE DAGS WITH THE SAME ORIGINAL NOISE LAW
Apply the parameter selection above afresh to the law V=min(X_1,X_2).
It is positive almost surely and nonconstant: its survival function is
Pr(V>x)=Pr(X>x)^2. Subdivide each y,h,Z,R edge into a private two-edge
path, both original owners having the unsplit capacity. Keep e,g unsplit.
The resulting DAG is simple. Scores of the subdivided paths use independent
V copies. The primitive noise of every owner is still the ORIGINAL X.
The proof requires only X_e<=1 for the focal edge and X_g>=0 for g; all
mean and concentration comparisons now use V and its chosen constants.
Every original cut lifts by choosing one serial copy of each subdivided
edge. The structural cut count is exactly
 K=N4^m(1+2^{Cm})+2^M
  =2^{(1+2B)r}+2^{[1+(C+2)B]r}+2^M.                  (7)
For all sufficiently large r this lies between 2^{pr} and 3*2^{pr},
p=1+(C+2)B. Equation (6) gives eta=1/p>0 as claimed.

SCOPE AND A CONSTANT-COST ALTERNATIVE
No continuity of X, of the envelope, or attainment of its supremum is used.
Strict inequalities separate cut classes even when there are within-class
ties. The result does not yet cover atom-zero laws, or laws depending on K.
The constants, exponent, and onset are law-dependent, not uniform in X.
The final fixed cut has M owners (one serial copy per path in the simple
variant). The fixed-cut clipped rule in FIXED_CUT_BASELINE.txt covers all
coalitions truthfully with budget <=MT on these same topologies, at every
profile. Thus the bad envelope is unboundedly worse than a constant-budget
alternative on the constructed family; it is not an intrinsic subsidy lower
bound. Maxflow/mincut, bounded convergence, Hoeffding and Chebyshev are the
established ingredients. The proposed new ingredient is serial amplification
of report-dependent effective-capacity competition. Priority is unconfirmed.
