LEADING-ONE WEIGHTED-HIERARCHY LOWER BOUND ON SIMPLE EDGE-OWNED DAGS
7 October 2026. Structural cuts mean inclusion-minimal source-sink edge cuts. Bounded derivation by personal_ceiling; coordinator review

RESULT AND QUANTIFIERS
Let b(G) be the infimum, over all nonnegative own-report-monotone payment
rules on a fixed graph G that cover every coalition at every nonnegative
capacity profile, of their worst total-payment/grand-flow ratio. There is
a sequence of finite SIMPLE DAGs G_d with one distinct fixed original
owner per edge, and structural-cut counts K_d, such that
 b(G_d) >= (1-o(1)) log K_d / log log K_d.                 (1)
All report profiles needed for the certificate are strictly positive.

More generally, if b_simple_at_most(K)=sup_{G simple, cuts(G)<=K} b(G), then
 liminf_{K->infinity} b_simple_at_most(K) log log K/log K >=1. (2)
This is an AT MOST K statement. No exact-cut-count padding is asserted.
Together with a valid leading-one upper bound on all DAGs, (2) would give
the sharp leading constant for this natural simple-DAG complexity budget.
The candidate hybrid upper is being reviewed separately; it is not used here.

The construction is a shared-fan replacement for each parallel-edge pool.
All auxiliary arcs are strategic owners. Their compensation is explicitly
accounted for, and every transported inequality changes ONE actual owner's
report. The argument does not aggregate private owner identities.

1. WHY ORDINARY TWO-OWNED-EDGE SUBDIVISION CANNOT WORK
This is a separate exact obstruction to the naive reduction.

Replace every edge e of ANY original graph by a two-edge path, with reports
(a_e,b_e) belonging to two distinct owners. Put d_e=min(a_e,b_e). Choose any
deterministic core allocation Q(d) for the original edge game, for example
capacity allocation on a fixed-priority minimum cut. It need not be truthful.
Pay Q_e(d) entirely to the higher-report member of the pair, splitting it
equally at equality; pay its lower-report member zero.

For a fixed report of the mate, an owner's payment is zero below its mate's
report, half Q_e at equality, and Q_e above it. Above the mate, d_e and all
other virtual capacities are independent of the owner's report. Thus its
payment is nondecreasing in its own report, even if Q itself is not truthful.
At zero virtual capacity Q_e=0 (in particular for the chosen mincut rule),
so zero-capacity ties cause no difficulty.

For a coalition S of the subdivided owners, let S* contain exactly the
original edges whose BOTH members belong to S. Its coalition flow is exactly
v_d(S*). Moreover p(S)>=sum_{e in S*}Q_e(d)>=v_d(S*). Total payment is T.
Thus every fully two-edge-subdivided network has an efficient truthful
covering rule. Restricting to equal paired reports and adding the pair's
payments therefore cannot preserve a nontrivial lower bound.

2. SIMPLE SHARED-FAN MODULE
Fix integers d>=1 and m>d. Choose an integer R>=1 and H=m^2. A module with
terminals a,b has four internal vertex layers:
 left fan vertices L_1,...,L_R;
 input vertices u_1,...,u_m;
 output vertices v_1,...,v_m;
 right fan vertices W_1,...,W_R.
Its edges are exactly
 a->L_i; L_i->u_j; u_j->v_j; v_j->W_l; W_l->b,
for all indicated i,j,l, with only matching middle edges u_j->v_j.
There are 2R+2m internal vertices and 2Rm+2R+m edges. Every edge has its own
fixed original owner. The m middle edges are called variable owners, and
all other edges auxiliary owners. The graph is simple and acyclic.

Take k>=2 copies in series, identifying only consecutive terminal vertices.
There are no parallel arcs and no additional connector owner between copies.
Hold every auxiliary report at the strictly positive value H. Variable
reports will lie in {1,m}. A type-h module has h high variables of report m
and m-h low variables of report 1, with 0<=h<=d.

Every selected subset J of a module's variable edges can be supported by
choosing ONE left fan i and ONE right fan l and taking exactly these owners:
 a->L_i, all L_i->u_j with j in J, all variables j in J,
 all v_j->W_l with j in J, and W_l->b.
This coalition has module flow sum_{j in J} c_j. The middle-edge cut bounds
it above; sending c_j along each chosen branch achieves it, since their
total is at most m^2=H. This also proves the full module flow is its variable
total, m+(m-1)h. The full series graph therefore has
 T=m+(m-1) min_{1<=i<=k} H_i.                            (3)

Choose the left and right fans independently and uniformly among the R
possibilities. For ANY fixed J, every particular auxiliary edge is included
with probability at most 1/R. Source/final fan edges have probability 1/R;
the other auxiliary edges have that probability if their variable is in J,
and zero otherwise. This bound is independent of all auxiliary payments.

3. VALID FINITE SYMMETRIZATION
Average any eligible rule over permutations of the k identical modules and
over permutations of the m variable branches within each module, relabeling
the incident auxiliary owners along with their branches. These operations
preserve every coalition flow for every profile. Branch permutations are
module isomorphisms; permuting entire two-terminal modules in a series
composition preserves the minimum of their individual coalition flows.
They are permutations of the finite original owner set, not splitting or
merging owners. Relabeling and averaging preserve own-report monotonicity,
nonnegativity, all coalition constraints, and the same budget factor B.

At a histogram n=(n_0,...,n_d), sum n_h=k, a present low variable in a type-h
module consequently has a common payment x_h(n), and a high variable has
payment z_h(n). Values for absent types are set to zero. Let
 Y(n)=sum_h n_h[(m-h)x_h(n)+h z_h(n)]
be the total VARIABLE-owner budget, and Q(n) the total AUXILIARY-owner budget.
The two sets of owners are disjoint, so Y+Q is the entire payment budget.

4. TWO ACTUAL COALITION FAMILIES, INCLUDING ALL AUXILIARY PAYMENTS
At any histogram n choose one low variable in every module. This is possible
because m>d. Supply their selected branches with the random fans from
section 2. Each resulting actual coalition has worth one. Average its
coverage inequality over these finite fan choices. Its variable-payment
sum is sum_h n_h x_h; its expected auxiliary-payment sum is at most Q/R.
Nonnegativity therefore gives
 sum_h n_h x_h + Q/R >=1.                               (4)

For the second coalition, put q0=m-d+1. In every type-h module with h<d,
choose q0 low variables; there are enough. In each type-d module choose one
high variable. Supply them with independent uniform fans as before. Its
worth is q0 if a type below d is present, and m otherwise. Thus
 q0 sum_{h<d}n_h x_h + n_d z_d + Q/R >=q0.               (5)

Multiply (4) by m-d and (5) by d. With
 A=(d+1)m-d^2,
the result is
 A <= A sum_{h<d}n_h x_h
      +n_d[(m-d)x_d+d z_d] +(m/R) Q.                    (6)
This is the original weighted-hierarchy coalition inequality with an explicit
auxiliary-owner term. No auxiliary owner was declared public or free.

5. WEIGHTED-PROFILE TRANSPORT CHANGES ONLY VARIABLE OWNERS
Fix alpha>0 and put
 lambda=A/alpha, Z=sum_{h=0}^d lambda^h/h!,
 p_h=lambda^h/(h! Z).
Draw the k module types independently with probabilities p_h as an averaging
device over profiles; write their multinomial histogram weights as mu(n).

For h<d, increasing one actual low VARIABLE report from 1 to m changes its
module from type h to type h+1 and leaves EVERY other report, including all
auxiliary reports H, unchanged. If n'=n-e_h+e_{h+1}, own-monotonicity gives
 x_h(n)<=z_{h+1}(n').
The exact multinomial identity
 mu(n)n_h=(p_h/p_{h+1})mu(n')n'_{h+1}
then gives E[n_h x_h]<=(p_h/p_{h+1}) E[n_{h+1}z_{h+1}].
Since A p_h/p_{h+1}=alpha(h+1), averaging (6) yields
 A <= alpha E[sum_{r=1}^d r n_r z_r]
      +E[n_d((m-d)x_d+d z_d)] +(m/R)E Q
   <= (1+alpha)E Y +(m/R)E Q
   <= max(1+alpha,m/R) B E T.                           (7)
The penultimate line uses only subsets of the VARIABLE-owner budget Y.
The last line accounts for the disjoint auxiliary budget Q. In particular,
taking R=m preserves the original coefficient 1+alpha exactly; there is
no need to add m/R to that coefficient or to make m/R vanish.

By (3), the same tail-sum formula as in the original hierarchy gives
 E T = m+(m-1) sum_{r=1}^d [sum_{h=r}^d p_h]^k
     <= m[1+d exp(-k/Z)].                               (8)
Thus the finite, all-rules lower bound on this SIMPLE graph is
 B >= A / {max(1+alpha,m/R) E T}.                        (9)
No unchanged owner's payment is transported between profiles.

6. CUT COUNT AND LEADING-CONSTANT CHOICE
Let C_{m,R} be the number of minimal structural cuts of one module. Each
structural minimal cut is an s-side cut for some subset of its internal
vertices, so
 1<=C_{m,R}<=2^(2R+2m).
A minimal cut of the series composition lies entirely in one module: if
every module retains an a-b path, those paths concatenate, while once one
module is disconnected every edge removed elsewhere is extraneous. Hence
the total number of structural cuts is EXACTLY
 K=k C_{m,R}.                                          (10)

For d>=2 choose
 g=ceil(sqrt(log(d+1))), m=dg, R=m, alpha=1/g,
 ell=ceil(log(d+1)), lambda=A g,
 k_d=ceil(2 ell Z).
Here g>=2, m>d, g->infinity, and g=o(log d). Notice that the multiplier in
k_d is ell of order log d, NOT g: this ensures the required tail error tends
to zero even though g grows slowly. From k_d/Z>=2ell and (8)-(9),
 B >= [d+1-d/g] / [(1+1/g)(1+d exp(-2ell))]
   = (1-o(1))d.                                        (11)

Also lambda=(d+1)d g^2-d^2 g>d, so the terms lambda^h/h! increase through d.
Thus lambda^d/d! <= Z <= (d+1)lambda^d/d!, and Stirling gives
 log lambda=(2+o(1))log d,
 log Z=d log lambda-log(d!)+O(log d)=(1+o(1))d log d,
 log k_d=(1+o(1))d log d.
Since R=m=dg, (10) gives
 log k_d <=log K_d<=log k_d+4m log 2
                         =(1+o(1))d log d,             (12)
because m=o(d log d). Equations (11)-(12) prove (1).

For any fixed epsilon in (0,1) and all sufficiently large external budgets K,
choose d=floor((1-epsilon)log K/log log K). Equation (12) implies K_d<=K
eventually. Equation (11) supplies the lower coefficient at least 1-epsilon
for b_simple_at_most(K). Letting epsilon decrease to zero proves (2).
Adding serial padding owners is neither needed nor claimed valid.

7. CHECKS, LIMITATIONS, AND AUDIT TARGETS
Independent exact integer maxflow checks were run for k=2, (m,d,R)=(2,1,2)
and (3,2,3), over all type profiles and all left/right fan choices for both
coalition families. All 1,586 coalition-flow checks passed, and every
auxiliary inclusion count was at most its number of fan choices divided
by R. Direct structural-cut enumeration of small modules gave
 C_{1,1}=5, C_{2,2}=39, C_{3,3}=689,
consistent with the upper bound 2^(4m). These finite checks are supplementary;
sections 2-6 provide the proof for unbounded parameters.

Review should especially check: preservation of all-owner game values under
module/branch symmetrization; coalition fan support and probability bound;
the disjoint Y/Q budget accounting in (7); and the slowly growing g with
the separate ell multiplier in k_d. No exact-K theorem, computational claim,
historical priority, or general upper-bound validation is asserted here.
