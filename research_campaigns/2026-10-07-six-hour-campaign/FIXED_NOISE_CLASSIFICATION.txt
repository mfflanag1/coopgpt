Use the definitions of AGGREGATE_ENVELOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt, replacing Uniform

RESULTS (WORST CASE OVER FIXED-OWNER DAG TOPOLOGIES AND PROFILES)

* X=c>0 almost surely: exact worst-case factor K on directed multigraphs;

* X in{0,c}, both probabilities positive: Theta_q(1+log K), with

These statements concern this repair class, not optimal arbitrary mechanisms.

1. EVERY NONZERO LAW HAS AN O_X(K) ENVELOPE BOUND
Let mu=EX>0 and A_mu=max{2/mu,2/(exp(1)mu^2)}. Then
 sum y<=A_mu K T.                                    (1)
For T>0 fix a cut C, w_h=D_h/T<=1, d=sum_C w_h=D(C)/T>=1,
and S=sum_C w_h X_h. At an own underreport t=xD_e,0<x<=1,
homothetic scaling of feasible flows gives D_h(c^t)>=xD_h(c). Selection of C
implies its new random score is at most T, by comparison with an original
minimum-capacity cut. Hence xS<=1. Put u=1/x>=1.
If u>=mu d/2, then (d/u)Pr(S<=u)<=2/mu. Otherwise Hoeffding, using
sum_C w_h^2<=d, gives Pr(S<=u)<=exp(-mu^2 d/2), and therefore
 (d/u)Pr(S<=u)<=d exp(-mu^2d/2)<=2/(exp(1)mu^2).
Consequently
 t Pr(W(c^t)=C)<=A_mu T D_e/D(C).
Sum over cuts containing e before taking the own supremum, then over owners;
each cut contributes A_mu T. Reports above D_e leave the entire feasible-flow
set and hence all effective capacities unchanged; report0 payszero. This proves
(1), including atoms and ties. At T0 every effective cap vanishes.

2. POSITIVE DETERMINISTIC NOISE
Scaling all scores by c>0 has no effect. The same homothety proof now has
selection implying xD(C)<=T deterministically, so the constant in(1) is1.
For equality use K serial pools of two parallel owners, each report1/2.
T1. Lowering one owner to t in(0,1/2) leaves all other effective caps1/2,
makes its pool uniquely cheapest and pays that owner t. Every envelope is
1/2 and sum y=K. Supremum attainment is not needed.
For simple DAGs use N serial diamonds, each two private two-edge paths with
every owner report1/2. K=4N. The first-priority local cut in each diamond has
two owners. Lowering either of these two separately makes this module uniquely
cheapest, preserves equality of the serial copies on each path, and selects
that same local cut. Each selected owner's envelope is1/2, yielding sum y>=N.

3. BERNOULLI LOGARITHMIC UPPER AND LOWER
Scaling the nonzero support point to1 leaves W unchanged. The conditioned-
zero mechanism in BERNOULLI_LOG_BOUND.txt proves
 y_e<=p_e<=z_e/q, sum p=O_q((1+log K)T).                (2)
For a lower bound take N=ceil(q^{-m}) serial pools of m unit-capacity owners,
followed by a singleton unit-capacity bottleneck. T1 and every D_e=1.
On the event that the singleton is open and some heavy pool entirely closed,
every minimum cut is a heavy all-zero cut with unmasked D-weight m. Therefore
 sum z=E D(W)>=(1-q)[1-(1-q^m)^N]m
 >=(1-q)(1-exp(-1))m=Omega_q(log K), K=N+1.
For simple DAGs subdivide every heavy edge into a private two-edge path,
leaving the singleton final edge unchanged. A path's minimum score iszero
with probability q'=1-(1-q)^2. Set N=ceil((q')^{-m}); each winning heavy
cut still has m unit-effective-cap owners, and K=N2^m+1. Again m=Theta_q(log K).

4. AN ATOM AT ZERO DOES NOT RESCUE NONCONSTANT POSITIVE NOISE
Let s=Pr(X>0)>0, mu=EX, nu=E min(X,X'), and assume the conditional positive
law is nonconstant. Conditioning on both copies being positive yields
nu<mu*s. Put d=mu-nu/s>0 and
 a=(1/s+mu/nu)/2>1, b=d/(4mu), delta=d/40.
Then
 E min(aX,(a+b)X')<=a nu+b mu=mu-10delta,
 H_m=E min(aX,mX') increases to a mu*s>mu+20delta.
The high limit has the factor s because X'=0 forces the minimum tozero;
omitting that factor would be wrong. Choose finite m0 with H_m>=mu+10delta
for m>=m0. Choose integers
 C>=(mu+10delta)/(b mu), M>=delta^{-2},
 gamma=min{32delta^2/a^2,32delta^2/(C b^2)},
 B>=max{1,m0,a+b,b/delta,2/gamma}.
Use exactly the graph, effective caps and cut classes of FIXED_NOISE_OBSTRUCTION:
N=2^r,m=Br,k=Cm,r>=4, final M owners totaling1, focal low reportt=b/m.
Raw module value a+q>1 ensures fixed globalT1 and unchanged other modules.
The low/high mean separation, Hoeffding and Chebyshev calculations are now

yielding exponent1/(B+1)>0. The parameters are fixed by the law, not by K.
For simple DAGs apply the constants afresh to V=min(X1,X2), then subdivide

For a pure Bernoulli law nu=mu*s; the above mean gap vanishes. The O(log K)
upper in(2) shows this is a genuine structural distinction, not merely an

5. IDENTICALLY ZERO NOISE
Take two serial pools of m parallel owners. The fixed cut priority selects
one pool regardless of reports. Give every owner in that pool report1 and
every owner in the other pool report1/m. Then T1, each chosen owner hasD1,
and every random score is always0. Hence z=y on the chosen cut and sum y=m,
with K2. This uses the report-independent priority assumption explicitly.
For a simple-DAG lower, use two pools of m private two-edge paths. K=2^{m+1}.
The selected fixed cut lies in one pool; give both copies of every path there
report1 and both copies in the other pool1/m. Selected owners again haveD1,
so sum y=m=Omega(log K). No fixed-K simple-DAG failure is asserted.
