NO CONSTANT SHAPLEY COVERAGE EVEN WHEN EVERY SERIAL POOL IS BALANCED
7 October2026. Conventional proof checked by coordinator; independent audit
completed; no invalid step found. This is a rule-specific obstruction, not a lower bound for all mechanisms.

1. FAMILY AND CLAIM
Fix integers M>=2,L>=2. Let a=1/(4M^2), q_n=2M(n+1)-1 and K_n=L^{q_n}.
For each n=1,...,M place K_n pools of type n in series. A type-n pool contains
one distinguished owner of capacity a and n outside owners of capacity
b_n=(1-a)/n. Every pool has total1, so T=1 and every structural minimal cut
has capacity1. There are K=sum K_n cuts and sum(n+1)K_n fixed original owners.
Let S contain the distinguished owner of EVERY pool. Its coalition value is a.
The graph is a directed serial composition of parallel-edge bundles; no owner
is cloned or changed during the evaluation or any report comparison.

We prove, writing Sh for the usual Shapley value,
 2/(M+1) <= Sh(S)/a <= 2/(M+1)+E(M,L),                    (1)
where
 E(M,L)=(M-1)L^{-1/[M(M+1)]}+L^{-1/(2M)}
        +(M-1)L^{-2M}
        +[M(M-1)/2](2^{M+1}+1)L^{-1/2}.                 (2)
Consequently the ratio tends to2/(M+1) as L tends to infinity at fixed M.
Letting M grow disproves every positive constant coverage guarantee.

2. THRESHOLD DECOMPOSITION AND PIVOTAL POOLS
For every coalition X, write
 v(X)=integral_0^1 g_theta(X)dtheta,
 g_theta(X)=1{each pool's X-capacity is at least theta}.
All games here are finite, so the finite Shapley sum commutes with this integral.
Use independent uniform arrival times to generate the random owner order.
Each pool completes its threshold at an order statistic of its own priorities.
Different pools have independent continuous completion times. The last pool
to complete supplies the unique global pivotal owner, almost surely.

The distinguished owner in a type-n pool can matter only on the bands
 I_{n,j}=(j*b_n,j*b_n+a), j=0,...,n.                      (3)
On this band the requirement is exactly j+1 of the n+1 owners: with the small
owner, j outsiders suffice; without it, j+1 outsiders are necessary. All owner
labels are thus interchangeable in this Boolean game, despite unequal weights.
On the gaps the distinguished owner is a dummy. Endpoint bands (0,a) and
(1-a,1) coincide for every type. Other bands lie strictly between them.
Band endpoints form a finite set and do not affect the integrals.

3. EVERY INTERIOR BAND HAS A DOMINATING OUTSIDER-ONLY TYPE
For I_{n,j}, 1<=j<n, put d=n-j, r=d+1, and m=ceil(n/d)-1. Then1<=m<n and
 m*d<n<=(m+1)*d,
 j/n-(m-1)/m=(n-m*d)/(m*n)>=1/M^2.
Since a<(1-a)/M^2, the whole band lies strictly above (m-1)b_m+a and below
1-a. It is therefore inside the type-m gap requiring ALL m outsiders, while
its distinguished owner is a dummy. (The upper inclusion also uses a<b_n.)

A type-n pool completes at the (j+1)th order statistic of n+1 uniforms. For
0<delta<1, at least r priorities must exceed1-delta for completion to be late:
 Pr(tau_n>1-delta)<=2^{n+1}delta^r.
The competing type-m pool completes at the maximum of m outsider priorities.
Its multiplicity exponent wins because
 Q=q_m*r-q_n=2M[(m+1)d+m-n]-d>=2M*m-d>=M+1.              (4)
Set beta=(q_n/r+q_m)/2 and delta=L^{-beta}. A union bound over type-n copies gives
 Pr(max type-n completion>1-delta)<=2^{n+1}L^{-Q/2}.
Independence across type-m pools gives
 Pr(all type-m complete<=1-delta)
 =(1-delta)^{m K_m}<=exp(-m L^{Q/(2r)}).
If the global latest pool has type n, one of these two events must occur.
Since r<=M and Q>=M+1, and exp(-x)<=1/x for x>0, its probability is at most
 (2^{M+1}+1)L^{-1/2}.                                   (5)
The probability the pivotal owner belongs to S and has type n is no larger.
There are M(M-1)/2 interior bands, each of length a. Integrating (5) and summing
gives the last term in (2). Overlaps only overcount and cause no problem.

4. LOWER ENDPOINT
On (0,a), type n is OR of its n+1 owners. Its completion is their minimum,
with late tail delta^{n+1}. Define alpha_n=q_n/(n+1)=2M-1/(n+1), and take
 beta=(alpha_M+alpha_{M-1})/2, delta=L^{-beta}.
For each n<M,
 q_n-beta(n+1)<=-1/[M(M+1)],
so the chance any lower-type completion exceeds1-delta is at most
 (M-1)L^{-1/[M(M+1)]}.
All type-M completions precede1-delta with probability at most
 exp(-L^{q_M-beta(M+1)})=exp(-L^{1/(2M)})<=L^{-1/(2M)}.
Thus type M supplies the latest pool except for the first two error terms.
Conditional on that pool having type n, its first-arriving owner label is
uniform among its n+1 labels, independently of its completion time and the
other pools. The S-pivotal probability is therefore at most1/(M+1) plus
these errors, and at least1/(M+1). It converges to1/(M+1).

5. UPPER ENDPOINT
On (1-a,1), every owner of every pool is required: the whole threshold game
is unanimity. The S share is exactly
 R=sum_n K_n / sum_n (n+1)K_n >=1/(M+1).
It is at most1/(M+1)+(M-1)L^{-2M}, since K_n/K_M<=L^{-2M} for n<M;
indeed subtracting1/(M+1) yields
 sum_{n<M}(M-n)K_n / [(M+1)sum_n(n+1)K_n],
which is bounded by sum_{n<M}K_n/K_M.
Integrating over the two endpoint intervals, each of length a, and adding
the nonnegative interior contribution proves both sides of (1).

6. FULLY EXPLICIT PARAMETERS
For M=4,L=10^40,a=1/64, q=(15,23,31,39). The upper bound is
 2/5+3/100+1/100000+3/10^320+198/10^20<431/1000<1/2.
The pool counts are10^600,10^920,10^1240,10^1560. This enormous but finite
exact witness needs no numerical integration or approximate probabilities.

For one explicit growing diagonal put
 R_M=4(M+1)M(2^{M+1}+1), L_M=R_M^{2M(M+1)}.
The four error terms in (2) are respectively bounded by
 (M-1)/R_M^2, 1/R_M, (M-1)/R_M^2,
 [M(M-1)/2](2^{M+1}+1)/R_M^2,
each at most1/[4(M+1)]. Hence Sh(S)/v(S)<=3/(M+1)->0.

7. INTERPRETATION
This closes the proposed constant-factor Shapley route even on balanced
serial pools. The simpler unbalanced two-path family in SHAPLEY_OBSTRUCTION
already rules out the desired logarithmic guarantee for general networks.
Neither result refutes the existing truthful continuous Banzhaf rule on TTSP,
nor supplies a lower bound applying to every truthful mechanism. Exact
balanced min-game noncore examples were already known; historical priority
of this quantitative family has not been established. No external review
or manuscript submission is claimed.
