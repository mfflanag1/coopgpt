UNIFORM-q MIXTURE OF CONDITIONED-ZERO PAYMENTS HAS THETA(K) WORST ORDER
7 October 2026. Exact proof and small rational checks by personal_ceiling.
Scope: finite directed multigraphs, fixed owners, nonnegative reports, a fixed
report-independent total priority on structural cuts. Zero reports are used.
No lower bound on arbitrary mechanisms is claimed.

RULE AND PERMUTATION IDENTITY
At a profile c let D_e be the largest feasible s-t flow through e, T the
grand flow, and K the number of structural minimal cuts. Independently set
B_e=0 with probability q and B_e=1 otherwise. Let W be the first minimum
structural cut for scores D_e B_e. The conditioned-zero payment is
 p_e^q=D_e Pr(e in W | B_e=0).
Its uniform mixture is pbar_e=integral_0^1 p_e^q dq.

Give owners independent uniform arrival times U_e in (0,1). Immediately
before owner e arrives, select the first minimum cut for the D-weight of
already arrived owners, and charge D_e if that cut contains e. Conditional
on U_e=t, each other owner has independently arrived with probability t,
and e is absent. Integrating over t, with q=1-t, proves that the expected
charge vector is exactly pbar. Random times have a uniform permutation;
there is no tie in arrival times almost surely.

LINEAR LOWER BOUND, ROBUST TO EVERY FIXED CUT PRIORITY
Fix integers K>=2 and B>=3. Use K serial pools, each having
 M=B^(K-1)
parallel edges with distinct fixed original owners. The only minimal
structural cuts are the K complete pools. After reading the rule's fixed
cut priority, enumerate the pools in that priority as i=1,...,K. In pool i
activate m_i=B^(K-i) owners with capacity one and give the remaining owners
capacity zero. This is a report profile on one fixed equal-pool topology;
the owner identities and structural cuts do not change.

The last-priority pool has one active owner. Hence T=1. Every active owner
can carry a unit flow by selecting one active edge in every other pool, so
D_e=c_e for every owner. Zero-capacity owners can be omitted from the
arrival calculation: their positions affect no cut score or payment, and
the relative order of active owners is still uniform.

Let tau_i be the first arrival time among the m_i active owners in pool i.
If tau_i>max_{j<i}tau_j, all earlier-priority cuts have strictly positive
score immediately before tau_i, while cut i has score zero. It is the
selected zero-score cut, and its arriving owner is charged one. Therefore
 pbar(E) >= sum_i Pr(tau_i>max_{j<i}tau_j).
For j<i, the earliest owner among the two pools is uniform among their
m_i+m_j active owners. Consequently
 Pr(tau_i<tau_j)=m_i/(m_i+m_j)<=B^{-(i-j)}.
A union bound now proves the exact lower bound
 pbar(E) >= K - sum_{i=1}^K sum_{d=1}^{i-1} B^{-d}
          >= K(1-1/(B-1)).                              (1)
B=3 gives K/2; allowing B to grow makes the lower coefficient approach one.
The argument counts only charges at zero-score cuts. Any later charges,
including those created by positive-score ties, are nonnegative and may be
discarded. Thus arbitrary fixed tie priority is fully accounted for.

DETERMINISTIC 2KT UPPER BOUND
For ANY profile and ANY owner permutation, allocate each charge to the
selected cut that caused it. At every such selection its current revealed
score is at most T, because a fixed minimum D-capacity cut has full score T.
At the last positively charged arrival for a particular cut, adding the
new owner increases that cut's revealed score by at most D_e<=T. All charges
ever allocated to that cut are distinct nonnegative revealed increments
included in its score immediately after this last charge. Their sum is
therefore at most 2T. Cut reappearances cause no gap in this argument.
Summing over the K cuts gives total charge<=2KT for every permutation.
Taking expectations proves pbar(E)<=2KT. Together with (1), worst order is
Theta(K), not O(log K).

The upper argument also applies to K binary packing dual vertices provided
each owner's single-step dual loss lies in [0,T] and some full dual objective
equals T. It makes no additional claim for nonbinary dual coefficients.

EXACT CHECKS AND CORRECTION RECORD
The accompanying verifier uses rational arithmetic to sum over every
predecessor subset. It also compares direct permutation sums on small cases.
Unit active pool sizes:
 (2,1): pbar(E)=7/3;
 (3,1): pbar(E)=9/4;
 (9,3,1): pbar(E)=2193/715.
For the last example the first-arrival-only lower bound is 161/65.
Singleton pools (a K-edge path) recover H_K exactly.

An earlier exploratory message described 1+m/(m+1) as the exact budget of
an m-unit heavy pool followed by an R-owner tiny pool of total capacity one,
with heavy-first priority. That omitted a possible extra unit charge when
the tiny pool is fully revealed and the heavy score is exactly one. For
m>=2 the correct exact expression is
 1+m/(m+1) + m! (R+1)!/(m+R)!.
In particular R=1 gives 2+1/(m+1), and R->infinity gives the former expression
only as a limit. For m=1 the exact value is 3/2. This correction has no
effect on the scalable lower proof, which uses only zero-score events.

REPRESENTATION AND SCOPE
K counts structural cuts before zero reports, not only cuts left after
deleting zero-capacity owners. The topology has exactly K cuts and K M
owners. It is a directed acyclic multigraph; ordinary subdivision changes
the structural-cut count and is not claimed to preserve this theorem for
simple DAGs. The rule remains truthful and coalition covering; its uniform
q mixture simply has a linear worst-case budget.

Owner-count caveat: n=K B^(K-1) in the lower construction. The result is
linear in structural-cut count K; it does not rule out O(log n) in owners.
