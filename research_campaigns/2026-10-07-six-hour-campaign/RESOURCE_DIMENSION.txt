RESOURCE-DIMENSION FALLBACK AND ITS LIMITS
7 October 2026. Bounded independent check by personal_ceiling.
Scope: exact elementary fallback, sharp obstruction without concavity,
technology-aware refinement, and an explicitly imported min-linear lower
baseline. No new sharp dimension theorem for concave technologies is claimed.

1. MODEL AND UNIVERSAL NORMALIZED-SHARE RULE
There are n fixed owners and d fixed resource coordinates. Owner i reports
b_i in R_+^d and may only decrease its own bundle componentwise. Let
c=sum_i b_i. Assume f:R_+^d->R_+ is finite-valued, coordinatewise monotone,
and positively homogeneous of degree one. Concavity is NOT assumed.
Set T=f(c) and v(S)=f(sum_{i in S}b_i). Finite homogeneity implies f(0)=0.

Define 0/0=0 in the following normalized shares:
 rho_i(b)=max_{1<=e<=d} b_ie/c_e,
 P_i(b)=T rho_i(b).                                     (1)
Then (1) is nonnegative, own-vector-monotone, and covers every coalition.
Its total payment obeys the pointwise bound
 sum_i P_i <= min(n,d_+(c)) T <= d T,                    (2)
where d_+(c) is the number of positive aggregate resource coordinates.

Own-vector monotonicity. Holding every other report fixed, write
b_ie/c_e=b_ie/(b_ie+c_-i,e). This is nondecreasing in b_ie. If the other
aggregate is zero, its value is zero at b_ie=0 and one at every positive
b_ie, which is still nondecreasing. Increasing the entire own vector
componentwise therefore increases rho_i weakly. It also increases T weakly.
Both factors are nonnegative, so their product is nondecreasing. This proves
the exact underreport incentive condition, including zero boundaries.

Coverage. Each coordinate satisfies b_ie<=rho_i c_e, also when c_e=0.
For a coalition S put r_S=sum_{i in S}rho_i. Then c(S)<=r_S c, and
 v(S)<=f(r_S c)=r_S T=sum_{i in S}P_i.
When r_S=0 this uses f(0)=0; when T=0 coverage also follows immediately
from monotonicity and 0<=c(S)<=c. No differentiability, support prices,
continuity, or concavity was used.

Budget. Each rho_i<=1, so their sum is at most n. Also
 sum_i rho_i <=sum_{e:c_e>0}sum_i b_ie/c_e=d_+(c).
If c=0 both sides of the payment guarantee are zero.

2. d IS EXACTLY OPTIMAL IN THE STATED NONCONCAVE CLASS
For every d take f(c)=max_e c_e and d owners b_i equal to the d unit vectors.
Then T=1 and v({i})=1 for every owner. Singleton coverage alone forces
sum_i P_i>=d, independently of truthfulness or computational restrictions.
Thus the optimal universal factor over ALL finite monotone homogeneous
technologies is exactly d, for every resource dimension d.

The obstruction is not an artifact of zeros. Give owner i amount one in its
own coordinate and epsilon>0 in every other coordinate. Every singleton is
still worth one, while T=1+(d-1)epsilon. The necessary factor is at least
d/[1+(d-1)epsilon], tending to d as epsilon decreases. For epsilon=d^(-2),
it grows as (1-o(1))d with strictly positive bundles.

This f is convex and is not min-linear for d>=2: it is not superadditive.
Therefore section 2 does NOT give a lower bound for the concave/min-linear
class. That distinction is essential.

3. TWO EXACT REFINEMENTS FOR NONNEGATIVE MIN-LINEAR f
Suppose f(c)=min_{w in V} w dot c for a fixed finite nonempty set V of
nonnegative vectors. The dimension fallback can be refined without changing
its d-only worst guarantee.

(a) Marginal/utopia vector. Put
 M_i=f(c)-f(c-b_i).
The second term is independent of i's report, so M_i is own-vector-monotone.
Choose an optimal w at c and use the core allocation a_i=w dot b_i. Its
coalition-complement inequality yields
 a_i<=T-v(N\{i})=M_i.
Consequently M covers all coalitions. Further,
 c-b_i >= (1-rho_i)c
implies f(c-b_i)>=(1-rho_i)T, and hence
 0<=M_i<=T rho_i.
Thus the standard marginal rule is already bounded by the new elementary
dimension fallback. This is consistent with the project's earlier marginal
flow baseline, and is not claimed as a new mechanism.

(b) Technology-aware maximum normalized dual share. Define
 theta_i=max_{w in V} (w dot b_i)/(w dot c), with 0/0=0,
 Q_i=T theta_i.                                        (3)
Every ratio is of the form a/(a+A), with A=w dot c_-i fixed and a=w dot b_i
nondecreasing in the own vector. Thus theta_i and Q_i are own-vector-monotone.
If T>0 and w* is optimal at c, then for every S,
 f(c(S))<=w* dot c(S)
         =T sum_{i in S}(w* dot b_i)/(w* dot c)
         <=sum_{i in S}Q_i.
For T=0 all payments and coalition values are zero. Moreover each normalized
dual share is a weighted average of the coordinate shares b_ie/c_e, so
theta_i<=rho_i. Thus Q is a pointwise improvement of (1), with the same dT
bound. It is exactly efficient for a single linear form, since its normalized
shares then sum to one whenever T>0. This is a simple refinement, not a
sublinear-in-d theorem.

If the forms in V belong to the nonnegative cone of r fixed nonnegative
vectors a_1,...,a_r, the same weighted-average argument bounds theta_i by
max_l (a_l dot b_i)/(a_l dot c), and sum_i theta_i<=r. This merely expresses
the fallback in nonnegative projected resource coordinates; it does not
establish a bound from ordinary signed linear rank alone.

The d-only bound can be tight for rule (1) even when a much better rule exists:
with f(c)=sum_e c_e and one unit-basis owner per resource, (1) costs dT,
while paying each owner's linear contribution costs exactly T. Similarly,
the marginal vector costs dT for f(c)=min_e c_e at the unit-basis profile,
although that profile has an efficient core allocation. These are method
limitations, not lower bounds against all truthful mechanisms.

4. ESTABLISHED GROWING LOWER FOR THE MIN-LINEAR DIMENSION PROBLEM
The existing weighted-glove hierarchy already supplies an unbounded
dimension-only obstruction. This section is a reformulation of an established
project result, not a new lower construction.

Use D resource types and production f(c)=min_{1<=j<=D}c_j. Give each resource
type m separate fixed owners whose bundles are confined to that coordinate.
The total owner count is Dm but RESOURCE DIMENSION IS D. Any mechanism for
arbitrary owner bundles restricts to a valid scalar-own-report mechanism on
these axis-supported bundles.

For integers h>=1,m>h, alpha>0, define
 A=(h+1)m-h^2, lambda=A/alpha,
 Z=sum_{r=0}^h lambda^r/r!, p_r=lambda^r/(r!Z).
The established weighted-profile hierarchy proves, for any D>=2,
 B >= A / ((1+alpha) E T),
 E T=m+(m-1)sum_{r=1}^h (sum_{s=r}^h p_s)^D
     <=m[1+h exp(-D/Z)].                               (4)
The finite certificate uses reports 1 and m, two actual coalition families,
and only a changing original owner's monotonicity comparison. Resource
aggregation does not merge owner identities or add any incentive assumption.

For h>=2, q=ceil(log(h+1)), m=hq, alpha=1/q, and D_h=ceil(2qZ), equation (4) gives
 B >= [h+1-h/q]/[(1+1/q)(1+h exp(-2q))]=(1-o(1))h,
while log D_h=(1+o(1))h log h. The all-D version of the same proof yields
 B_dimension(D) >= (1-o(1)) log D/log log D.              (5)
Thus no constant, or o(log D/log log D), dimension-only guarantee can hold
even for the single-product Leontief technology and axis-supported owners.

The baseline source is
 research_campaigns/2026-10-06-leading-constant/SHARP_THEOREM.txt
and the weighted-hierarchy subsection of
 paper5/truthful_cooperation/manuscript.tex.
The present bounded investigation did not improve (5), and did not prove a
sublinear dimension-only upper bound for arbitrary nonnegative min-linear f.
The gap between this imported lower and the elementary d upper remains open
in this note. The separate sharp K-complexity theorem can be used alongside
the dimension rule by selecting a mechanism in advance from the fixed values
of d and K; coordinatewise minima of different payment rules are not asserted
to preserve coalition coverage.

5. PRIMARY-SOURCE CHECK AND TERMINOLOGY
The statistic max_e b_ie/c_e is the familiar dominant share from the
multi-resource-allocation literature. A primary source is Ali Ghodsi et al.,
"Dominant Resource Fairness: Fair Allocation of Multiple Resource Types,"
NSDI 2011, section 4:
 https://www.usenix.org/conference/nsdi11/dominant-resource-fairness-fair-allocation-multiple-resource-types
 https://static.usenix.org/event/nsdi11/tech/full_papers/Ghodsi.pdf
That paper allocates resources to users reporting requirements; its own
strategy-proofness theorem concerns that different model. Its terminology
does not establish historical priority for payments (1), coalition coverage,
or the present underreport-only supplier model. No novelty inference is made.
