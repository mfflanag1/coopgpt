LOCAL-RANK AND SIMULTANEOUS-PROFILE REDUCTION FOR A MINIMAL ENVELOPE COUNTEREXAMPLE
7 October2026. Coordinator checked conventional proof; independent audit completed, no invalid step found.
This narrows the unresolved actual-envelope antichain problem. It does not solve it.

1. LOCAL THROUGH-ANTICHAIN RANK
For a path-antichain A, let G_A be the subgraph consisting of edges on paths
which meet A. Every s-t path of G_A meets A. Indeed each such edge is either
in A, before an A-edge, or after an A-edge. No vertex can be both after one
A-edge and before another, since this would create a path containing two
A-edges (or a directed cycle). A path cannot pass from the before region to
the after region on an edge outside A: that edge's membership in an A-path
would force its head to be before an A-edge or its tail to be after one.
Every vertex lies before (including a tail) or after (including a head) an
A-edge; s is before-only and t after-only. Thus every s-t path must cross
between the disjoint regions, and this crossing occurs through A.

Define R(B), B subset A, as maximum total flow on paths meeting B. Equivalently
it is maxflow in G_B. Each such path avoids A\B. Values are attained and
remain unchanged when reports in A\B are reduced. R({e})=D_e of the full graph.
Effective-capacity normalization preserves all restricted-subgraph flows,
because these are also feasible full-network flows.

The rival-flow bound in ANTICHAIN_CASES.txt improves to
 y_e<=min(D_e, max(R(A)-R(A\{e}), R(A)/2)).              (1)
Proof: at an own underreport, every cut of G_A containing e has its non-e
capacity at least R(A\{e}). Above d_e>R(A)-R(A\{e}), a deterministic minimum
cut C0 of G_A excludes e and has D-capacity<=R(A). It need not cut the FULL
network, but it blocks every e-containing path. For any maximum full-network
flow, remove its e-containing path flows. The endpoint maxflow marginal from
adding e is at most their total, which is at most the random capacity of C0.
Therefore the conditional endpoint marginal defining z_e has expectation at
most R(A)/2. The other case has payment<=d_e<=R(A)-R(A\{e}). Taking the own
supremum gives(1). No claim B_e(random full excluding-cut score)<=C0 is needed.

The pair proof now yields y_e+y_f<=R({e,f}) for any two incomparable owners.
For a three-owner A, if any pair B has R(B)<=R(A)/2, then (1) for its remaining
owner and the local pair bound give sum_A y<=R(A).

2. STRUCTURE OF ANY INCLUSION-MINIMAL COUNTEREXAMPLE
Suppose A is an inclusion-minimal antichain with Y(A):=sum_A y_e>R(A).
Every proper B subset A obeys Y(B)<=R(B); singletons and pairs cannot be A.
For every e in A, write delta_e=R(A)-R(A\{e}). Then
 y_e>delta_e,                                           (2)
because Y(A)>R(A) and Y(A\{e})<=R(A\{e}). Any report t_e witnessing its
envelope satisfies t_e>=z_e(c^{t_e})=y_e>delta_e (attainment is already proved;
near-maximizers would suffice with strict margins). In particular c_e>delta_e.

The single-coordinate maxflow hinge on G_A is
 R_A(c^t)=min(R(A),R(A\{e})+t), 0<=t<=c_e.
Thus each witnessing underreport preserves R(A). Also no original minimum cut
of G_A can contain e: a minimum cut containing it would have deletion value
R(A)-c_e, whereas the hinge forces c_e=delta_e. Consequently ANY original
minimum cut C* of G_A is disjoint from the entire set A.

3. A COMMON JOINT PROFILE REALLY EXISTS
Set every report e in A simultaneously to y_e and keep all other reports.
For a cut C of G_A let B=C intersect A. A flow of value R(A\B) on paths
meeting A\B avoids every B-edge and is blocked by C\B. Hence the unchanged
non-A capacity of C is at least R(A\B). If B is nonempty, its new cut sum is
 >=Y(B)+R(A\B)>=Y(A)>R(A),
where the middle inequality uses minimality for the proper set A\B (including
the empty set). If B is empty, its unchanged sum is>=R(A). The disjoint C*
still has capacity exactly R(A). Thus the JOINT y-profile has through-A value
exactly R(A), and every cut meeting A has strictly larger capacity.
The same is true for simultaneous witnessing reports t_e>=y_e by monotonicity.

At each of these profiles, a through-A maximum flow saturates every edge of C*.
Because C* is disjoint from A, their reports are unchanged. Therefore each full-
network effective cap on C* is exactly its original report c_h, at the original,
individual-witness and simultaneous-witness profiles. Its random score
 S*=sum_{h in C*} c_h U_h
is one fixed barrier, independent of U_A, with mean R(A)/2. It bounds the
endpoint marginal through every e, not necessarily the FULL minimum cut score.

4. THE EXACT UNRESOLVED STEP
If one could compare each separate optimizing payment to its coordinate at
the joint profile in the favorable direction, the base-flow antichain bound
would contradict Y(A)>R(A). That comparison is NOT proved. Constant through-A
value and a common fixed disjoint cut do not by themselves imply the necessary
cross-substitutes property; CONSTANT_VALUE_CROSS_OBSTRUCTION.txt disproves
that shortcut. The extra self-consistency of the joint y-targets remains unused.
No field-level or historical-novelty claim is made.
