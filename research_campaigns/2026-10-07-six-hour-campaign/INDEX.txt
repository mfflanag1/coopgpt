PREFERRED PROOF PACKAGE
1. GENERAL_PRODUCTION_THEOREM.txt
   Sharp leading-one logK/loglogK upper for finite nonnegative min-linear
   production, fixed owners, arbitrary overlapping resource bundles, all profiles.
   Includes general nonnegative production-LP interpretation and uncapped failure.
   review/general_production_{review.md,adjudication.txt};
   verification/general_production.py and general_production_result.json.
2. SIMPLE_DAG_SHARP_LOWER.txt
   Matching simple-DAG lower via shared fans; all auxiliary owners strategic.
   Worst-case at-most-K scope; exact constructed sequence. Full subdivision
   instead admits an efficient truthful rule. Review/checker share this prefix.
3. OWNER_COUNT_EXTENSION.txt
   Every finite monotone concave homogeneous production function, even at
   discontinuous boundary faces: O(n/logn) owner bound, full bundle incentives.
   Finite-K refinement min(K,2^n); optional original-core fairness floors.
   review/owner_extension_*; verification/concave_production.py.
4. OWNER_COUNT_LOWER.txt
   Imported hierarchy translated to (1-o(1))logn/loglogn lower for all large n.
   Parameter conversion and zero-owner padding, not a new lower construction.
   review/owner_lower_*; verification/owner_count_lower.py.
5. RESOURCE_DIMENSION.txt
   Elementary dT fallback and refinements. A matching d lower is for the
   NONCONCAVE class only. The concave dimension-only gap remains open.
   Covered by owner_extension review; verification/resource_dimension.py.

ALGORITHMIC CONSEQUENCES
SAMPLING_COROLLARY.txt: exact finite rational mask law; pointwise high-probability
coverage, every-seed exact incentives, oracle count and bit-complexity scope.
UNIFORM_SAMPLING_THEOREM.txt: one frozen seed good simultaneously at every real
profile, with explicit pseudodimension/sample bound, including tie faces.
SEED_CERTIFICATION_THEOREM.txt: exact all-profile rational arrangement/LP checker
and Las Vegas seed search; exponential preprocessing, polynomial later evaluation
for rational reports with the stated canonical oracle or explicit form list.
Reviews: review/sampling_* and review/seed_certificate_*.
Checkers: verification/sampling.py and verification/seed_certificate.py.
The tiny seed checker exhausts all real profiles for its listed configurations;
other finite grids do not provide infinite-domain certification.

REPRODUCTION
Use a full checkout of https://github.com/mfflanag1/coopgpt, Python3.9+ standard
library, and preserve repository layout. No optimization solver or paid source
is needed for these verification commands. From repository root:
  python3 -B -O research_campaigns/2026-10-07-six-hour-campaign/verification/run_all.py
Optional --output /path/to/record.json also saves the complete replay record.
The runner invokes 26 bounded exact verifiers in separate processes, with
always-active checks, actual commands, output, dependency hashes, and timings.
Some scripts import prior campaign helpers and the original sharp certificate
checker; the runner checks their presence. A single script copied alone may fail.
Working replay and final clean-archive replay are labeled separately in
verification/. The clean-archive record is authoritative for the final package.
See verification/README.txt for dependencies and coverage limits.

STATUS LIMITS
Conventional proofs and model audits are not formal proof-assistant verification.
Finite certificates do not establish asymptotics by themselves. The main results
remain research supplements: the open specialist brief and existing manuscript
were not edited during this campaign. No human outreach occurred.
