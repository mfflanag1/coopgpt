SHAPLEY PAYMENTS CAN MISS COALITION COVERAGE BY A POLYNOMIAL CUT FACTOR
7 October2026. Conventional proof and independent exact computation checked;
cross-model audit completed; no invalid step found. No claim of historical novelty.

1. CANDIDATE AND MODEL
For a fixed directed network with one original owner per edge, let phi_e(c)
be the Shapley value of its capacity-induced flow game. The model permits only
binding underreports and has no operating costs, as specified in CONTRACT.txt.
Payments are nonnegative
and sum to grand flow T. For each predecessor set R not containing e, the
marginal F(R+e)-F(R) is nondecreasing in c_e, so phi is own-report truthful
on all nonnegative real profiles. The difficulty is coalition coverage.
An all-profile constant multiplier depending only on the fixed topology would
preserve truthfulness, if that multiplier provided adequate coverage.

2. SIMPLE TWO-PATH FAMILY
Put a unit-capacity veto edge e from s to u, followed by two internally disjoint
u-t paths of lengths n and l, with every edge capacity1 and a distinct owner.
For n,l>=2 this is a simple DAG. There are n+l+1 owners, n+l+1 vertices, and
K=1+n*l inclusion-minimal structural cuts: the veto singleton and all pairs
of one edge from each path. T=1. Coalition S contains the veto and every owner
of the length-n path, hence v(S)=1.

Generate the uniform random order by independent uniform arrival times. The
veto is pivotal at time t precisely if at least one path is already complete:
 phi_e=integral_0^1 [t^n+t^l-t^(n+l)]dt
      =1/(n+1)+1/(l+1)-1/(n+l+1).
For an owner of the length-l path to be pivotal, the veto and its other l-1
path edges must have arrived, while the length-n path must be incomplete.
Its Shapley share is
 integral_0^1 t*t^(l-1)*(1-t^n)dt
 =1/(l+1)-1/(n+l+1).
By efficiency, subtracting the l such outsider shares yields exactly
 phi(S)=1/(l+1)+l/(n+l+1).                              (1)

Set n=l^2. Then K=l^3+1 and
 phi(S)<2/l,  v(S)/phi(S)>l/2=(K-1)^(1/3)/2.             (2)
Thus no absolute constant rescaling covers all coalitions. More strongly,
no multiplier C*U(K), U(K)~log K/log log K, suffices. This already fails on
unit-capacity simple TTSP networks, so neither general Wheatstone interactions
nor large unequal reports cause the obstruction. All owners remain fixed.

3. SMALL EXACT WITNESS
Take n=9,l=3. There are13 owners,13 vertices,K28,T1. Exact Shapley shares:
 veto71/260; each of9 long-path owners3/130;
 each of3 short-path owners9/52.
They sum to1, whereas phi(S)=25/52<1/2, deficit1/52 below half coverage.
Independent subset enumeration and the arrival-time formulas agree exactly.

4. SEPARATE BALANCED-POOL CALIBRATION
Even requiring every serial pool to have equal total capacity does not put
Shapley in the core. Three pools (1,1,1),(1,1,1),(1,2) all total3. The first
six owners each receive9/28, the last unit owner7/20, the weight2 owner101/140.
A coalition taking one unit from each pool has value1 and payment139/140.
Hand integration: for the last unit owner at priority u, its pivotal probability
is (1-u)[1-(1-u)^3]^2+u*u^6, integrating to7/20. For the weight2 owner define
p1=1-(1-u)^3,p2=3u^2-2u^3,p3=u^3. Its expected marginal is
(1-u)(p1^2+p2^2)+u(p2^2+p3^2), integrating to101/140. Symmetry and efficiency
give the other six shares. Exact subset and permutation checks independently
recover this vector. This small witness does not refute a factor2 bound on
balanced serial pools; that separate gate remains under investigation.

5. SCOPE AND SOURCES
The Shapley definition, random-priority representation, and basic network
counterexample method are classical. Hoffmann-Sudholter2005 section5 already
gives a balanced minimum-of-three-additive-games noncore example; see SOURCES.
Its arbitrary overlapping additive rows are not automatically a singleton-edge
network realization. We do not claim priority for the family or asymptotic.
This is a rule-specific obstruction. It does not lower-bound the optimal subsidy
of all truthful mechanisms, and it does not refute the actual own-report
envelope y. The latter has a sharp logarithmic budget on these TTSP graphs.
