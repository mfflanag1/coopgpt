SERIAL SUBDIVISION AND A LIMIT TO SERIES-COMPOSITION BOUNDS
7 October 2026. Conventional proofs, coordinator checked; independent audit found no invalid step.
Scope: exclusions and structural facts, not a sharp general subsidy result.

1. SERIAL SUBDIVISION DOES NOT AMPLIFY ITS OWN BLOCK'S ENVELOPE
Replace any edge of a fixed DAG by a path X of k distinct fixed owners, with
no attachments at its internal vertices. Reports on the new owners may be
unequal. Let q=min_(i in X)c_i and keep all external reports fixed.
All feasible flows use the same amount on every member of X, so their common
effective cap d is determined by q. All external effective caps depend on X
only through q. A minimal structural cut contains either no X edge or exactly
one X edge; replacing that edge by any other member preserves the cut family.

At bottleneck report q', all X caps equal d(q'). If d(q')>0, a selected cut
containing X must choose the member with smallest U_i. The argmin is uniform
on X and independent of V=min_i U_i and all external uniforms. Let
 Z_X(q')=d(q') Pr(the selected cut contains X).
Then every member's base payment is Z_X(q')/k. Every owner's allowed unilateral
reports attain exactly q' in [0,q]; reports yielding q'>=q leave feasible flows
unchanged. Hence, even for unequal reports,
 y_i(c)=(1/k) sup_(0<=q'<=q) Z_X(q'),
 sum_(i in X)y_i(c)=sup Z_X(q')<=d(q)<=T(c).                (1)
The collapsed edge's multiplier V has Beta(1,k) law. This is NOT the original
edge's uniform-noise envelope, and does not prove neutrality of arbitrary series
composition. It is a fixed-owner calculation on each expanded graph.

2. CONSTANT/MAX COMPOSITION IS FALSE EVEN FOR TTSP MODULES
For integer m>=2 put r=4^m*m! and M=16r. Each of r identical modules consists
of two serial parallel bundles: a light bundle of M edges, each capacity1/M,
and a heavy bundle of m edges, each capacity1. Standalone T_i=1,K_i=2. Compose
the r modules in series. Then T=1, K=2r, and all effective caps equal reports.

Write L_i=(1/M)sum_(j=1)^M U_ij and H_i=sum_(j=1)^m V_ij. All cut scores across
modules are independent. Every minimal cut is exactly one entire bundle.
For a light owner underreporting t in [0,1/M], the bottleneck stays at least
(M-1)/M>=1/M, so all other owners in its containing cut retain cap1/M. For a
heavy owner reporting t in [0,1], its bundle total m-1+t>=1, so other owners
in its containing cut retain cap1. In either case the conditional containing
intercept A is fixed; focal D and excluding threshold B are nondecreasing.
The conditional formula proves z is own-monotone on every relevant interval,
so y=z at these profiles, standalone and after series composition.

For one standalone module, total payment is1+(m-1)Pr(H_i<L_i), which is at
most1+(m-1)/m!<=2 because L_i<=1 and Pr(H_i<=1)=1/m!.

In the series composition, Pr(H_i<=1/4)=1/(4^m*m!)=1/r. Hence some heavy cut
has score<=1/4 with probability 1-(1-1/r)^r>=1/2. Since E L_i=1/2 and
Var(L_i)=1/(12M), Chebyshev gives Pr(L_i<1/4)<=4/(3M). The union bound over
r light cuts is at most1/12. With probability at least5/12 a heavy cut wins.
Therefore the global payment total is at least5m/12 although every local
total is at most2. No constant multiple of the largest local envelope budget
controls arbitrary series composition. Here m~log K/log log K, so this is
consistent with, and structurally related to, the already proved glove scale;
it is not a stronger general lower bound.

3. EXACT BOOKKEEPING FOR GENERAL SERIES MODULES
For arbitrary modules G_i in series, with local maxflows T_i and effective caps
d_(i,e), global T=min_i T_i and D_e=min(d_(i,e),T). The cut family is a disjoint
union, giving K=sum_i K_i. Under a report change inside module i, all external
module caps change only through T(t): min(d_(j,h),T(t)). This one-dimensional
coupling may be useful, but no sublinear composition upper bound is proved here.
