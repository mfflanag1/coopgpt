Alternative short proof and contribution audit -- candidate, 3 October 2026
This is a coordinator simplification of UNIT_FLOW_BOUND.txt, checked in final opposite-model review (scope in review/final/ADJUDICATION.txt).
It identifies how much of the structural mechanism is classical cut theory.

Use paragraph1 of UNIT_FLOW_BOUND: in a unit-capacity game H_e is 0 or1, and H_e=1
exactly for arcs in some minimum cut. No full core=Owen theorem is needed.
For any two minimum-cut source sides U,V, directed cut capacity is submodular:
c(delta+(U))+c(delta+(V)) >= c(delta+(U intersect V))+c(delta+(U union V)).
Every term on the right is >=T. Equality therefore holds and both new cuts are
minimum. Finiteness gives minimum-cut source sides U_min=intersection of all
minimum-cut source sides and U_max=union of them. This is a classical lattice
argument, not a claimed contribution.

Every source arc e=(s,u) with H_e=1 crosses some minimum cut, so u is absent
from at least one such source side and hence from U_min. Thus the U_min cut
attains ALL source-arc ideals at once. Every sink arc e=(u,t) with H_e=1 has u
in at least one minimum-cut source side and hence in U_max. The U_max cut
attains ALL sink-arc ideals at once. Direct s-t arcs can belong to both.

Let B be positive-ideal arcs outside these two cuts. They are strictly internal:
neither leaving s nor entering t. Fix an integral max flow of T arc-disjoint
paths. H_e<=f_e means every member of B is used. Every s-t path has at most one
strictly internal arc because its length is <=3. Hence |B|<=T. The third-cut
construction of paragraph5 of UNIT_FLOW_BOUND gives s_(1/3)<=(T-1)/3, with the
same r=0 exception yielding0. This bypasses the detailed residual rerouting
argument while proving the same theorem on the declared class.

Audit implication: binary unit-game ideals, cut submodularity/lattice, and
simultaneous endpoint attainment follow from established flow theory. The
proposed result is the sharp frozen-entire-core subsidy bound and its family,
not discovery of these ingredients. The longer residual proof is retained as an alternative written argument.
Its fixed-case rerouting checks exercise only two-arc paths and simple parallel
reroutes, not the delicate late crossing on a three-arc path. They provide limited
corroboration; the theorem now relies on the shorter lattice proof. The longer
argument's complexity should not be presented as evidence of new depth. The alternative proof also shows why the missing general-capacity
step is genuinely about the ENTIRE core rather than new cut uncrossing.

Optional conditional observation, not an additional campaign target:
If source and sink ideals can each be attained in one original-core point,
then their average protects endpoint ideals at H/2. Internal ideals total at
most T, by H<=f and length<=3. This proves C2 by clipping at H/3. If any internal
ideal is positive, average those two witnesses and a core point attaining that
internal ideal instead: all endpoints and that arc are already protected at
H/3, giving subsidy <=(sum_internal H - H_e)/3 < T/3. If no internal ideal is
positive the subsidy is0. This is an elementary implication of the unresolved
joint-attainment lemma, not a proof of it or a significance claim.
