What changed mathematically
1. Sharp UNIT-capacity transfer: for an arc-player DAG with all s-t paths of
length<=3 and T>0, original entire-core maxima H frozen before intervention,
  s_(1/3) <= (T-1)/3,
attained by the chain family for every integer T>=1. The all-parameter chain family proves the
constant 1/3 is a supremum. This addresses a different coalition model, and its
positive subsidies exclude a vacuous universal-zero problem. Conventional proof,
scoped opposite-model checks and exact certificates support it. Priority remains
unresolved; the shorter cut-lattice proof shows substantial established machinery.
It is a credible small theorem candidate, not by itself the requested breakthrough.

2. Four-sector aggregation survives: s_(1/4)<T/3 in the stated pair-additive
model. The calculation extracts tight blocks, same-sector range budgets and
common residual mass. K=4 sharpness remains unproved. This is a routine extension;
the four-cycle zero-matrix embedding supplies no separate application. No extra
sharpness search was opened because the transfer had the stronger research case.

3. Full rational-capacity C2 is UNKNOWN. A cut-only construction fails even on
capacities(2,2,1), although that game needs zero subsidy. Five selected unequal-
capacity cases have exact entire-core primal-dual certificates and satisfy C2;
their feasible endpoint witnesses do not establish the general lemma. Splitting an owner into independent unit players
changes the benchmark and is not a valid reduction.

4. A precise failed shortcut: conditional source submodularity does not generally
imply joint attainment of individual core maxima. An exact analytic counterexample
survives even total balancedness and the requirement that every positive coalition
needs a source-group member. The obstruction is an overlapping weighted coalition
family, invisible to single deletion/marginal bounds. The original five-player
version has opposite-model QA; final opposite-model review also found no error
in the coordinator's six-player strengthening and source-submodularity proof. Neither is claimed
as a flow example. The six-player bases have four members, whereas minimal
positive coalitions in our flow class have at most three, so that game is outside
the class. Non-minimal flow coalitions may still carry the same overlap pattern.
A flow-specific repair remains to be found; path switching is only an attack idea.

5. Published-problem route NO-GO on current tool fit. A04/A05 have the same
individual extrema but different leximin points, so extrema alone do not give
the Vazirani geometry characterization. The branching/MST backup uses different
Owen/cost-share objects and no connecting lemma was found. The bounded scout is
closed; these are not refutations of either published problem.

Why conditional rather than GO
The class-specific theorem and exact obstruction identify a real next attack,
but they do not establish general-capacity compatibility or historical novelty.
A longer investment is warranted only after a non-cut flow lemma or exact flow
obstruction changes the proof state. The copied follow-on goal is therefore a
bounded mechanism test with an early stop, not another automatic forty-hour run.
A source finding that makes the unit result routine must reduce its contribution
credit; it must not be hidden by adding more examples or theorem variants.
