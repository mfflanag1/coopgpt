S9. Fang--Fleischer--Li--Sun, Algorithms for core stability, core largeness,
exactness, and extendability of flow games, Frontiers of Mathematics in China
5(1):47-63 (2010; article accepted2009).
https://nas.iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/~jianli/paper/flowgame_fcs.pdf
Read: introduction; section2.3 Theorem2/Corollary3; Lemma4 and Lemma11; references.
Established baseline: unit-flow core is the convex hull of minimum-cut indicators;
zero-ideal arcs are characterized by deletion/min-cut absence. The paper attributes
these facts to earlier Kalai--Zemel and Deng et al. results. These are not our
contributions. Its stability/exactness results concern other core properties.
General-capacity core-membership hardness is cited to Fang--Zhu--Cai--Deng(2001),
but that original reduction was not read and is not asserted for hop length3.
Corollary3's printed path-inequality statement must retain efficiency: our proof
uses total T explicitly and never infers core membership from path cover alone.

S11. Garg--Kumar--Rudra--Verma, Coalitional games on graphs: core structure,
substitutes and frugality (EC2003 original).
https://cse.buffalo.edu/faculty/atri/papers/game-theory/ec03.pdf
Read: model/definitions, section4's coordinatewise lattice construction and
section5's network applications. This is a procurement model with a distinguished
auctioneer absorbing residual payoff, not an arc-player max-flow profit game.
Merging all downstream owners into that auctioneer would remove coalition
constraints. Hence its substitutes/lattice statement does not prove our proposed
source joint-attainment lemma without a lifting theorem. This is a checked
competing explanation, not an adopted extension or another named-problem scout.
