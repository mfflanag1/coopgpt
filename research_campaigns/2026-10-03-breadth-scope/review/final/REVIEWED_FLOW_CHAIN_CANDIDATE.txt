Coordinator flow family, derived independently while Fable analyzed C2.
Subsequently independently rederived and model-reviewed. See UNIT_FLOW_BOUND.txt
for the matching sharp unit-capacity upper bound; historical priority is open.

For n>=1 take vertices s,L_1,...,L_n,R_1,...,R_n,t and unit-capacity arcs
a_i=sL_i, b_i=L_iR_i, c_i=R_it, plus d_i=L_iR_(i+1), i=1,...,n-1.
All s-t paths have exactly three arcs. Players are arcs. Diagonal paths give
flow n; the source cut gives upper n. Thus T=n.

Any player's maximum core payoff is at most its flow in any maximum flow,
by the deletion-marginal argument. The diagonal maximum flow gives H_(d_i)=0
and H_(a_i),H_(b_i),H_(c_i)<=1. The source and sink cut allocations attain 1
at each a_i and c_i. For b_i take cut S_i={s,L_i,...,L_n,R_(i+1),...,R_n}.
Its forward boundary is {a_j:j<i}, b_i, {c_j:j>i}, of total capacity n.
No d_j leaves S_i. Paying 1 to boundary arcs and zero otherwise is a core
allocation: every coalition's flow is at most the capacity of its arcs in this
cut. Hence all 3n diagonal-path arcs have H=1.

At floors H/3, the cross paths {a_i,d_i,c_(i+1)} are player-disjoint. Their
union coalition has worth n-1 and contains 2(n-1) positive-H arcs. Outside it
remain n+2 positive-H arcs. Therefore protected total >= n-1+(n+2)/3,
so subsidy >=(n-1)/3. For n=1 the grand coalition gives subsidy >=0.

For a matching upper allocation pay 1/3 to each a_i,b_i,c_i, add 1/3 to
a_i for i<n, and pay zero to d_i. Every diagonal path has payment >=1;
every cross path has payment 1. Every coalition's integral unit-capacity
maximum flow decomposes into arc-disjoint paths, so summing their payment
inequalities covers its value. The total is n+(n-1)/3. Consequently
s_(1/3)=(n-1)/3 and s/T=(n-1)/(3n)->1/3.

This gives positive subsidy already at n=2 and equality in the separate proved
unit-capacity bound (T-1)/3. The chain argument alone is a lower-family calculation;
the original unequal-capacity T/3 conjecture remains unresolved. Unit
capacities are used critically in the path-to-all-coalition implication here.
No such implication is assumed for the general unequal-capacity class.
