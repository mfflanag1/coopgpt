Four-sector baseline -- coordinator derivation, 3 October 2026
Conventional proof candidate; final independent review and source comparison
pending. This is a routine extension, excluded from the significance gate.
The proof is written for K>=3 to expose the coefficient mechanism; K=4 is C1.

Use the approved strict full-team nonnegative pair-additive model, K equal sectors,
positive integral worth T, nonempty entire original core. Complete an optimal
matching to a perfect one and label its blocks j. Let d_j be its team value,
u_(a,j) the sum of its diagonal pair values incident with player (a,j), and
H_(a,j) the separately attained original core maxima. Nonnegativity and tight
optimal blocks give 0<=H_(a,j)<=d_j, sum_j d_j=T, sum_(a,j)u_(a,j)=2T.

The same-sector range holds unchanged: at any core point x, replace player (a,j)
in its diagonal team by (a,h). The replacement team's worth is >=d_j-u_(a,j),
since retained pairs contribute that amount and all new pair values are >=0.
Core coverage and diagonal tightness imply x_(a,j)-x_(a,h)<=u_(a,j).
This holds also for equal labels and zero diagonal values.

For an arbitrary team e with labels j_a and distinct sectors b,c, subtract the
diagonal equality at label j_b from its core covering inequality. Bound the K-2
remaining coordinate differences by their incident budgets. Then choose a core
point maximizing the subtracted coordinate x_(c,j_b). This gives
 H_(c,j_c)-H_(c,j_b) >= v(e)-d_(j_b)-sum_(a not b,c)u_(a,j_a).
No joint maximization is assumed. Summing over K(K-1) ordered pairs (b,c):
 K(K-1)v(e) <= (K-1)sum_a d_(j_a)
            +(K-1)(K-2)sum_a u_(a,j_a)
            +K sum_a H_(a,j_a)-sum_a H^Sigma_(j_a),
where H^Sigma_j=sum_a H_(a,j). All labels retain their multiplicities.

For any fractional partial full-team packing lambda let p_i be its load,
q_i=1-p_i, W=sum lambda_e v(e), S=sum q_i H_i and V=KW+S. Each sector has
residual mass k=n-sum lambda_e. Set beta=sum q_i u_i, C2=sum q_i^2 u_i and
E=sum_(a,j)(sum_(b!=a)q_(b,j))(d_j-H_(a,j))>=0.
Summing the team comparison, then adding (K-1)S, gives an H coefficient
 Kp_(a,j)+(K-1)q_(a,j)-sum_b p_(b,j)=sum_(b!=a)q_(b,j).
Substitute H_(a,j)=d_j-(d_j-H_(a,j)) and use sum u=2T to obtain
             V <= (3K-4)T-(K-2)beta-E/(K-1).                 (A)

At a core witness attaining H_i, weighted fractional coverage and efficiency
imply sum q_h x_h<=T-W. In i's sector the range bound gives
 kH_i <= T-W+(k-q_i)u_i.
Multiply by q_i and sum over i; sum_i q_i=Kk. If k>0,
             V <= KT+beta-C2/k.                            (B)
The witnesses may differ because the final inequalities concern fixed H_i.
For k=0, all q vanish and W<=T, so V<=KT.

Use weights 1/(K-1) on (A) and (K-2)/(K-1) on (B):
 V <= [K+2(K-2)/(K-1)]T - E/(K-1)^2 - (K-2)C2/[(K-1)k].
If C2>0 this is strict. If C2=0, nonnegativity gives beta=0 and (B) gives V<=KT,
strictly below the displayed leading bound for K>=3,T>0. The compact fractional
packing polytope attains its linear maximum. LP duality after y=H/K+z therefore
proves
             s_(1/K) < 2(K-2)T/[K(K-1)].
At K=4, s_(1/4)<T/3. Sparse sector graphs inherit the result by zero pair matrices.
This establishes no K=4 sharpness family and counts as no new structural breadth.

For an integral partial matching only, C2=beta and k is an integer. For k>=1,
weight (A) by (k-1)/[k(K-1)-1] and (B) by k(K-2)/[k(K-1)-1]. Then its dual
obstruction is <=2(K-2)(k-1)T/[K(k(K-1)-1)]. At K=4 this is (k-1)T/(3k-1).
For k=0 it is <=0. This is an upper bound on an integral obstruction, not by
itself an upper bound on the full fractional subsidy. No sharpness asserted.

MECHANISM EXTRACTION.
The two derived bounds follow from separately checkable primitives: a tight
optimal block partition with one player in each sector; common residual mass;
nonnegative payoffs; and directed same-sector range budgets u_i. A finite-game
certificate of these range bounds can be checked independently of the desired
subsidy conclusion. With total budget B=sum u_i instead of 2T, the same argument
gives s_(1/K)<=(K-2)B/[K(K-1)]. Pair-additivity supplies B=2T through a genuine
replacement-team calculation. Merely positing (A) and (B) would hide that step.
For arbitrary full K-team values, trivial budgets u_(a,j)=d_j give B=KT, so the
same calculation yields s_(1/K)<(K-2)T/(K-1); its priority is unchecked and this
is not the selected transfer claim. Whether a different game class admits useful
small exchange budgets and a common residual partition remains a real obligation.
