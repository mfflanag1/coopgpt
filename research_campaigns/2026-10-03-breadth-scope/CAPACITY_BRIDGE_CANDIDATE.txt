Unresolved bridge within C2: source/sink joint attainment
Coordinator hypothesis, not a theorem or literature claim.

For an arbitrary finite arc-player flow game, is there an original-core point
attaining every source-incident arc maximum simultaneously, and separately a
core point attaining every sink-incident arc maximum simultaneously? DAGs with
maximum s-t path length three are the selected test class. Ignore arcs on no
s-t path only after proving their H is zero; do not alter player identities.

If yes in this class, average the two witnesses. The result dominates H/2 on
all source/sink arcs, hence the required H/3 there. Every remaining arc occurs
strictly internally on a path. A fixed maximum flow decomposes into paths with
at most one strictly internal arc, so sum_internal H<=sum_internal f<=T.
Clipping the averaged core point at H/3 thus adds at most T/3, proving C2.
This is a concrete missing lemma. Positing it without a new argument is not
progress: the bounded probes must seek a counterexample as well as witnesses.

Potential mechanism: with all other arcs fixed, max-flow as a function of the
subset of source arcs is submodular. That fact alone has not been proved to
imply simultaneous whole-core attainment; conditioning on arbitrary other-player
coalitions can obstruct naive lattice arguments. Need an actual lifting/uncrossing
proof or a counterexample, not an inference from marginal-sum bounds.
