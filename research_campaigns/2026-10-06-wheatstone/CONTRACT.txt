INTERACTING WHEATSTONE BRIDGES — RESEARCH CONTRACT
6 October 2026

Model unchanged: a fixed finite acyclic directed s-t network, one fixed original
owner per edge; arbitrary nonnegative real capacity reports; deterministic
nonnegative payments covering every max-flow coalition and nondecreasing in
the reporting owner's own capacity. Binding underreports, no use costs.
Both network size and owner count may grow across the target class.

Target: extend the effective-capacity rule beyond TTSP, or prove an exact
obstruction distinguishing failure of a sufficient lemma from failure of the
rule. An impossibility claim for all mechanisms requires a separate valid
weighted report-transition certificate. Known depth-L marginal payments bound
the total by L*T, so do not seek divergence at fixed path length.

Five-edge calibration graph: s->u (x), s->v (y), u->t (z), v->t (w), u->v (h).
Structural minimal cuts: {x,y}, {z,w}, {y,z,h}, {x,w}.
Maxflow=min(x+y,z+w,y+z+h,x+w).
Effective capacities (maximum feasible edge flow, any total) are
 D_x=min(x,z+min(h,w)); D_y=min(y,w); D_z=min(z,x);
 D_w=min(w,y+min(x,h)); D_h=min(h,x,w).
An exact co-cut failure already occurs: x and w share cut {x,w}, but D_w
can grow with x. This alone DOES NOT prove that the payment rule is nonmonotone.

The rule is D_e times the probability edge e belongs to a minimum structural
cut under independent capacities D_i U_i, U_i uniform[0,1]. Equivalently it is
the absolute continuous Banzhaf endpoint integral after effective capping.
Value preservation, nonnegativity and the cut-count U(K) budget extend to DAGs
by effective capacity; the open obligation is own-report monotonicity.

Parallel routes: exact/symbolic bridge analysis; exploratory coupled-uniform
search followed by exact witness certification; structural sufficient condition
or obstruction for growing chains/overlapping bridge networks. Save every
promoted claim with exact model, proof, reproducible certificate and scope.
No manuscript changes until a result survives independent audit. No novelty
or field-breakthrough claim from a finite computation. No human outreach.
