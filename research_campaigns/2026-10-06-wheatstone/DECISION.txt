WHEATSTONE ATTEMPT: PRECISE BOUNDARY AND NEXT MATHEMATICAL GATE
6 October2026

RESULTS
1. The effective-capacity continuous-Banzhaf rule is truthful at every profile
   on the five-edge bridge and on H_k with arbitrary parallel z owners. The
   all-k positive class has FOUR cuts and fixed four-vertex structure; it is
   not a general theorem for increasingly complicated bridge interactions.
2. Adding an outer parallel bypass and then a downstream parallel cut breaks
   this same rule. The exact17-owner certificate extends to every k>=10.
   Safety of individual modules is therefore insufficient: this rule's
   truthfulness is not closed under series/parallel composition.
3. Replacing parallel edges by distinct two-edge paths gives simple DAGs with
   k+7 vertices and2k+9 owners, for every k>=14. A21-vertex37-owner case has
   a strictly positive certified underreporting gain >5.9*10^-6 at grand value1.
   This gain is a rational LOWER BOUND, not an approximate point estimate.
4. Coverage, value preservation and the general U(K) budget remain true; the
   failing obligation is the focal owner's incentive across actual reports.
   The earlier TTSP theorem is unaffected. This is an obstruction to the
   specified rule, not to every possible truthful mechanism or subsidy bound.

STOPPING DECISION
The unchanged effective-capacity rule cannot solve the unrestricted problem.
Further random searches for its universal monotonicity would revisit a disproved
statement. The exact obstruction meets this attempt's negative-result gate.
No new growing lower bound for all eligible mechanisms has been established.
Human review, historical novelty and field-level significance remain open.

RECOMMENDED NEXT TARGET (PROPOSED, NOT STARTED AS A NEW GOAL)
Study the own-report monotone envelope of the base payment z:
 y_e(c)=sup_(0<=t<=c_e) z_e(t,c_-e).
This is nonnegative, own-report monotone by nesting of the supremum domains,
and coalition-covering because y_e(c)>=z_e(c). It is finite since
z_e(t,c_-e)<=D_e(t,c_-e)<=D_e(c)<=T(c). These are elementary facts, not
the required new result. They yield only the trivial |E|*T budget.

The hard gate is a bound sum_e y_e(c)<=C*U(K)*T(c) with an absolute C and no
owner-count restriction, or an exact family showing why no such bound holds.
The coordinate maxima occur at DIFFERENT unilateral profiles: the old
pointwise U(K) bound cannot simply be summed across them. A new budget or
weighted report-transition argument is essential. Fixed-family constructions
or additional numerical nonviolations do not meet that gate.
