WHAT STILL HOLDS ON EVERY FIXED FINITE DAG
6 October2026. Elementary reductions; not claimed new.

Assume every edge lies on an s-t path, and one fixed owner per edge. Define
D_e(c) as the maximum feasible s-t flow through edge e, allowing any total.
All reports are arbitrary nonnegative finite capacities. Because a DAG has no
circulations, edge flow is at most total flow, so0<=D_e<=T. Also D_e<=c_e and
D_e is nondecreasing in every report by inclusion of feasible flow sets.

Every coalition flow is a grand-network feasible flow. Therefore simultaneously
capping all edges at their D_e preserves the ENTIRE feasible flow set of every
coalition: one inclusion uses the definition of D_e; the other uses D<=c.
In particular all coalition values and the grand value T are preserved.

For edge e=(a,b), a useful independent DAG formula is
 D_e=min(c_e,maxflow(s,a),maxflow(b,t)),
omitting a prefix/suffix bound if its terminals coincide. Any s->a path and
b->t path are disjoint: a shared vertex would create a directed cycle through
a->b. Scaled prefix/suffix maximum flows can thus be joined through e, proving
attainability of the displayed minimum. The reverse upper bounds are immediate.
This is the graph-based formula used by the exact verifier.

Let C range over the K structural minimal cuts. For ANY selected cut C*, the
vector D_e 1(e in C*) covers all coalitions, since C* meets every coalition's
s-t path and maxflow/mincut bounds its value by D(C* intersect S). Selecting
a minimum cut under random capacities D_e U_e and averaging therefore gives
nonnegative coalition-covering deterministic payments.

For T>0 let a_e=D_e/T<=1 and rho_C=sum_(e in C)a_e. Some cut has rho=1.
The winning random cut W has random score<=1. For each fixed C, independence
inside that cut and the uniform Laplace bound give
 Pr(sum_(e in C)a_e U_e<=1)<=(e/rho_C)^rho_C.
A union bound gives Pr(rho_W>s)<=min(1,K(e/s)^s) for s>=e. Tail integration
therefore yields total payment<=U(K)T, with U defined in the prior TTSP theorem.
This proof requires no independence between different cuts. T=0 gives D=0.

These statements survive on the augmented overlapping-bridge graph at K=5.
What FAILS there is own-report monotonicity of the averaged payment. Thus these
coverage/budget statements alone are not a truthful mechanism theorem.

AN ELIGIBLE BASELINE REMAINS AVAILABLE
For any flow game with nonempty core, M_e(c)=T-v_c(E without e) is nonnegative
and own-report monotone. A minimum-cut allocation gives a core vector a. Every
core coordinate obeys a_e<=M_e, so the vector M covers every coalition.
For a cycle-free maximum flow f, delete all path flows using e to see
v(E without e)>=T-f_e; thus M_e<=f_e. If all s-t paths have length at most L,
sum_e M_e<=sum_e f_e<=L*T. The displayed obstruction family has L=4, hence it
still admits truthful payments with factor at most4 by this baseline.
The failure of the tested rule is not divergence or impossibility for all rules.
