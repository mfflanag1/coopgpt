PARALLEL-BUNDLED WHEATSTONE NETWORKS: POSITIVE RESULT AND COMPOSITION FAILURE
6 October2026. Independently audited conventional proof; no priority claim.

Let H_k have terminals s,q, vertices s,u,v,q and fixed original edges
 x:s->u, y:s->v, z_1,...,z_k:u->q, w:v->q, h:u->v.
Each capacity is any nonnegative real number. There are k+4 original owners;
k is arbitrary. Exactly four structural minimal cuts are
 {x,y}, {z_1,...,z_k,w}, {y,z_1,...,z_k,h}, {x,w}.

THEOREM: effective-capacity continuous-Banzhaf payments are nonnegative,
coalition-covering and own-report monotone at every profile on H_k, for all k.
Their total payment is <=U(4)*T by the established cut-count tail argument.
Thus this parallel-bundled bridge is a positive unbounded-owner class outside
TTSP, with fixed four-vertex structure and four structural cuts. This is a four-cut class, not an unrestricted-
cut-count theorem or an assertion of an optimal constant.

Write C=sum_j z_j. The effective capacities are
 d_x=min(x,C+min(h,w)); d_y=min(y,w); d_zj=min(z_j,x);
 d_w=min(w,y+min(x,h)); d_h=min(h,x,w).
These follow from the maximum feasible flow through the specified edge; all
are nondecreasing in every report. All-coalition preservation follows because
every feasible coalition flow is a feasible grand-network flow and hence
respects these coordinatewise maxima. The reverse inclusion uses d<=c.

Let X=d_x U_x, Y=d_y U_y, Z=sum_j d_zj U_zj, W=d_w U_w, H=d_h U_h,
with independent uniform U variables. For focal edge e integrate out only U_e.
The conditional payment integrands are:
 q_x=min(d_x, Z+min((W-Y)_+,H));
 q_w=min(d_w, Y+min((X-Z)_+,H));
 q_y=min(d_y, (W-min((X-Z)_+,H))_+);
 q_zj=min(d_zj, (X-Z_minus_j-min((W-Y)_+,H))_+);
 q_h=min(d_h, (X-Z)_+, (W-Y)_+).
In the z_j formula, Z_minus_j=sum_(ell!=j) d_zell U_zell, excluding the focal
uniform. No other formula involves the focal uniform. These are identities
from the four cuts: q_e=min(d_e,(B_e-A_e)_+), with A_e the least score of a
containing cut after removing e and B_e the least excluding-cut score.
For z_j explicitly, A_e=Z_minus_j+min(W,Y+H), B_e=X+min(Y,W), whose difference
is X-Z_minus_j-min((W-Y)_+,H). The remaining formulas reduce to the independently
checked five-edge identities, with the aggregate Z replacing the single z score.

Own monotonicity for ALL real profiles, holding U_-e and original other reports:
 x: d_x,Z,W,H can increase; Y is fixed. All moving arguments are positive.
 w: d_w,Y,X,H can increase; Z is fixed. All moving arguments are positive.
 y: only d_y and W can increase; X,Z,H are fixed.
 z_j: only d_zj and X can increase; Z_minus_j,W,Y,H are fixed.
 h: d_h,X,W can increase; Y and Z are fixed.
Thus each q_e is nondecreasing in its own original report; averaging proves
the claim. Zero capacities and ties are handled by the outer min with d_e and
the continuous U_e integration; no transformed owner or coordinated deviation.

Coverage: for every selected cut C*, the vector d_e 1(e in C*) covers every
coalition's flow; take expectation. Since d_e<=T, the prior Laplace/union-bound
proof uses K=4 even with overlapping cuts. Nothing new is claimed about that
tail bound or the continuous Banzhaf functional.
