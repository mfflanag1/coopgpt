EFFECTIVE-CAPACITY PAYMENTS FAIL ON OVERLAPPING WHEATSTONE NETWORKS
Conventional proof and rational certificate, 6 October 2026.
Independently audited conventional proof; exact rational checks passed.
No impossibility claim for all mechanisms, formal verification or human review.

THEOREM
For every integer k>=10 there is a fixed edge-owned directed acyclic network
with k+7 original owners and two strictly positive capacity profiles differing
only in one owner's report, such that the effective-capacity continuous-Banzhaf
rule strictly decreases that owner's payment when its report increases.
Both grand maxflows equal1. The decrease is at least
 delta=553/136800000 - (10/9)^10/10!
      =97726081746439/30050502681578400000 >0.
This refutes extension of THAT RULE to arbitrary DAGs. It does not refute the
existence of other truthful subsidized mechanisms, or the earlier TTSP theorem.

1. FIXED GRAPH AND REPORTS
Vertices s,u,v,q,t. Edges/owners:
 x:s->u; y:s->v; z_1,...,z_k:u->q (distinct parallel original edges);
 w:v->q; h:u->v; p:s->q; r_1,r_2:q->t.
Each five-edge subgraph (x,y,z_j,w,h) is a Wheatstone bridge. These k bridges
share four edges and interact with the outer bypass p and downstream cut r.
Every owner is fixed throughout its two-profile comparison. Varying k indexes
different fixed networks, not strategic splitting/cloning during a comparison.
All edges are on an s-t path; the graph is acyclic; path length is at most4.

Set epsilon=1/20, x0=9/10, x1=19/20, y=epsilon,
 z_j=w=h=p=2, and r_1=r_2=1/2.
Only x changes between the two profiles. There are exactly five inclusion-
minimal structural cuts, writing Z for the SET of all k z edges:
 {x,y,p}, {Z,w,p}, {y,Z,h,p}, {x,w,p}, {r_1,r_2}.                  (1)
Here {Z,w,p} means Z union {w,p}. Consequently the grand maxflow is1 at
both profiles (the downstream cut binds, and p alone supplies at least1).
Owner count grows without bound even though this family's cut count is5.

2. EFFECTIVE CAPACITIES
For any x in[x0,x1], the maximum feasible flow through each edge, allowing any
total feasible flow, is
 D_x=x, D_y=epsilon, D_zj=x, D_w=x+epsilon,
 D_h=x, D_p=1, D_r1=D_r2=1/2.                                  (2)
Upper bounds follow from the incoming x cut, the incoming x+y cut at v,
individual reports, and the downstream cut. They are attainable: direct
z_j routes carry x; the route x,h,w carries x; combining it with y carries
x+epsilon<=1 through w; p supports total1 and either downstream edge's1/2.
Every such construction respects the fixed original capacities. Thus (2)
uses actual effective capacities, not the rejected global cap min(c_e,T).

3. EXACT CONDITIONAL PAYMENT
All following uniforms are independent uniform[0,1]. Write
 Y=epsilon U_y, W=(x+epsilon)U_w, H=xU_h,
 Z=x sum_(j=1)^k U_zj, P=U_p, R=(U_r1+U_r2)/2,
 A=min(Y,W).
Integrating out U_x in the minimum-cut rule gives the exact deterministic
payment z_x(x)=E[q_k(x)] with
 q_k(x)=min(x, ( min(Z+min(H,(W-Y)_+), R-P-A) )_+).               (3)
Indeed the minimum containing-cut score excluding x is P+min(Y,W), and the
minimum excluding-cut score is min(P+Z+min(W,Y+H),R); subtract and integrate
the affine term xU_x. This argument handles the fixed cut family and all ties
in the same way as the previous endpoint formula.

4. IDEAL COMPARISON WITH A CERTIFIED ERROR
Define q_infinity(x)=min(x,(R-P-A)_+) and I(x)=E[q_infinity(x)].
Then q_k<=q_infinity always. Whenever Z>=1, the first entry in the minimum
in(3) is >=1 while R-P-A<=1, so equality holds. Since both q's lie in[0,1],
 I(x)-Pr(Z<1) <= z_x(x) <= I(x).                               (4)
An enclosing nonnegative simplex in R^k gives
 Pr(Z<1) <= 1/(k! x^k).                                       (5)
This is an UPPER bound, not an equality: the simplex may extend beyond the
unit cube. Its k-dimensional volume is (1/x)^k/k!. No probabilistic limit
or unquantified concentration approximation is used.

5. EXACT IDEAL INTEGRALS
For b=x+epsilon, A=min(epsilon U,bV) has support[0,epsilon] and moments
 M_0(b)=1,
 M_j(b)=epsilon^j/(j+1)
       -j epsilon^(j+1)/((j+1)(j+2)b), j>=1.                    (6)
This follows by integrating j a^(j-1)(1-a/epsilon)(1-a/b).
For t>=epsilon define
 P4(t,b)=sum_(j=0)^4 binom(4,j)(-1)^j t^(4-j) M_j(b).
This equals E[(t-A)^4]. At x0,x1, the exact integral is
 I(x)=[P4(1,b)-2P4(1/2,b)-P4(1-x,b)]/6.                        (7)
Derivation: R-P has the same distribution as S-1, where S is the sum of
independent uniforms of widths1/2,1/2,1. Reflection and the simplex
inclusion-exclusion formula give
 E[(R-P-a)_+]=[(1-a)^4-2(1/2-a)^4]/6 for0<=a<=epsilon.
For x in{x0,x1}, 0<=1-x-a<=1/10<1/2, so
 E[(R-P-a-x)_+]=(1-x-a)^4/6.
Subtract these two positive-part expectations and then average A to obtain(7).

Exact rational substitution yields
 I(x0)-I(x1)=553/136800000 >0.                                 (8)
From(4)-(5), with x0=9/10,
 z_x(x0)-z_x(x1) >=553/136800000-(10/9)^k/k!.
At k=10 this equals the positive delta in the theorem. For k>=10 the error
bound decreases, because its ratio at k+1 versus k is(10/9)/(k+1)<1.
Thus every k>=10 has the same strictly positive certified payment drop.

6. WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
At true capacity19/20 the reporting owner benefits by reporting9/10; all other
original reports remain fixed, and both profiles have value1. Coverage and the
U(5) budget argument of the effective-capacity rule survive, but truthfulness
fails. Consequently preserving coalition values and bounding each effective
capacity by T are insufficient to extend the mechanism beyond its proved class.
The bare five-edge Wheatstone bridge DOES admit the rule at every profile, by
the separately checked conditional formulas. Interaction with other flow routes
is essential to this displayed obstruction; no assertion of minimality is made.

Known monotone marginal-contribution payments still provide a factor at most4
on this depth4 family. Hence this cannot be used as a growing lower bound on
optimal subsidies or an impossibility result for all truthful mechanisms.
No priority or field-breakthrough claim is made.
