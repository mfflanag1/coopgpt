TWO-POOL EXTENSION ACHIEVED IN THE FROZEN BINARY DOMAIN
2026-10-05

The requested extension of the existing finite-family construction is proved:
allow TWO pools to contain any mixture of 1/r and 1 reports, with every label and
every order of changes included. The new assignment retains total cost <=2T and
matches the old zero/one-altered payments exactly. The original H_i/k floor is
available with cost <=3T. These constants do not depend on k or r.

The new ingredient is a mixture that assigns the more advanced pool weight
(1/2)*product(a_s/b_{s+1}) across the gap between the two counts. A changed owner's
increase in its component payment compensates exactly for the reduction in its
component weight. The opposite direction, ties, and return to the one-pool
boundary are checked separately. Coalition coverage comes from covers of
auxiliary profiles in which all owners outside one altered pool are unit.

An independent attempt correctly rejected the naive minimum-pool selector with
an exact switching counterexample. It did not produce the final construction;
the coordinator derived the product rule independently. The final theorem and
verifier receive separate model reviews, with exact labeled checks recorded.

This rules out divergent lower bounds supported ONLY on the current two-level,
two-altered-pool family: an explicit bounded feasible payment assignment exists.
It does not establish a constant all-report mechanism. The next mathematical
uncertainty is compatibility beyond this family: three changing pools or extra
report levels. Any proposed unrestricted lower bound must use profiles outside
the family proved feasible here, rather than repeat these interacting chains.

The value 2 is sharp for rules required to keep the old boundary, which itself
approaches 2 as r grows. No minimax sharpness over freely chosen boundaries or
sharp fairness-inclusive factor is asserted. Novelty remains unconfirmed.
