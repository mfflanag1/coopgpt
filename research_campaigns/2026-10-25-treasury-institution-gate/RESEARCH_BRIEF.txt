Institutional discovery gate: a bounded treasury allocation pilot
25 October 2026. Design and source audit; no institution has agreed to participate.

DECISION
The public-record check identifies a credible institutional category, but does not supply an operational contract or the joint risk data needed to evaluate our allocation rules. Keep the application parked at the data/contract gate. Do not rerun the failed synthetic Treasury book or substitute a public-bank VaR table for unit-level exposures.

PUBLIC REFERENCE
JPMorgan Chase's 2025 report documents centralized Treasury/CIO risk management, funds transfer pricing that transfers primary interest-rate and liquidity risk, separate equity allocation, and a risk-management VaR table. Sources and locations are in SOURCE_RECORD.json. This is a reference institution, not a partner or a claim that its practices need improvement. Its published metrics are not our earlier Gaussian ES experiment. No named person has been approached.

PROPOSED DECISION — TO BE RATIFIED, NOT ASSERTED AS BANK POLICY
At a scheduled internal risk review, can a fixed set of books in ONE legal/accounting perimeter be assigned a common, explicitly defined market-risk-capacity budget without violating their approved stand-alone risk-charge benchmarks or exceeding permitted internal credits?

The smallest credible scope is 3–8 existing books within a treasury or market-risk perimeter, a common valuation basis/horizon/currency, and an identifiable risk manager with authority to compare allocations. The number is a practical enumeration limit for a first pilot, not a theorem's quantifier or a claim about an actual bank's organization. Do not merge public reportable segments, legal subsidiaries or risk-factor categories into pretend players.

WHY THIS CHANGES THE MODEL
1. Existing funds-transfer payments and risk ownership must be reconciled first. A hedging benefit may already be priced internally. A new credit cannot simply be added on top. Compare fully reconciled ledgers on the same budget and exposure perimeter; no double counting of internal back-to-back trades.
2. Economic capital, regulatory equity, funding expense, liquidity charges, market-risk capacity and accounting net interest income are distinct quantities. Select ONE for the primary question. A published funding-pricing rule is not automatically an appropriate baseline for an ES-capacity allocation.
3. Fixed-book coalition comparisons are managerial counterfactuals. Do not assume desks can withdraw, form a legal coalition, change reports or receive cash transfers. An appeal guarantee needs explicit managerial approval. If unsupported, call it a normative diagnostic and do not claim cooperative stability in the institution.
4. The existing internal rule is the primary comparator. Identify its risk functional, model/scenario version, valuation basis and information cutoff, and obtain its evaluation on the SAME scenarios/risk budget for a rule-only comparison. If that evaluation is unavailable, do not attribute an end-to-end difference to the allocation rule. Include Euler only if the chosen risk functional and its derivative/subgradient convention make it well-defined. The two savings selectors are secondary diagnostics until the sponsor explains why absolute-dollar egalitarianism matters to an actual decision.
5. An allocation-only exercise cannot reduce aggregate risk when positions/scenarios stay fixed. A welfare or useful-output claim requires an observed decision mechanism, implementation cost and opportunity-value evidence. Descriptive reallocations remain descriptive.

MINIMUM CONTRACT (CURRENTLY UNOBSERVED)
- Sponsor and independent model/risk reviewer; authority to set the selected budget and approve a study.
- Fixed book IDs and legal/accounting boundary, including central hedge ownership before/after existing transfers.
- Current rule, exact charge definitions, budget total, review dates, and approved risk functional/horizon.
- Eligibility of coalition appeals, meaning of the stand-alone counterfactual, and treatment of costs/constraints that persist on hypothetical separation.
- Credit/negative-charge permissions and maximum total credit, stated in the SAME units as the primary allocation. Cash conversion requires an observed rental rate/time basis; no guessed lambda.
- The decision affected: one specified risk-limit review, approved hedge choice, funded activity or documented charge appeal. It must be named by the sponsor, not invented by us.

STAGED EVIDENCE PLAN
Stage 0: written contract/data fit. No performance optimization. Pass only after the items above and DATA_REQUIREMENTS.json are available and reconciled. A single authorized snapshot is enough to inspect algebraic compatibility, NOT to establish empirical usefulness or calibrate tails.
Stage 1: diagnostic pilot. Freeze the roster and one versioned scenario-loss matrix at each decision date. Compute every eligible coalition under the approved functional and the same scenarios. Compare existing charges, Euler when applicable, and the two secondary selectors. Measure constraint violations, efficiency/budget reconciliation, required credits and stability under sponsor-approved stress sets. No causal or superiority claim from allocation differences.
Stage 2: empirical/decision test. Before outcomes, agree on a decision-relevant minimum improvement and evaluation horizon, use an untouched or prospective set, and justify sample size for the selected tail probability/dependence and decision frequency. No arbitrary claim that 252 observations, 5% disagreement or a fixed number of months is sufficient. Evaluate the risk model independently of the allocation rule and retain null results.

STOPPING CONDITIONS
No approved decision or no compatible data: data-insufficient, parked.
No coalition appeal right: retain only a clearly labeled normative allocation diagnostic, if useful to sponsor; otherwise stop.
Current rule uses a different budget/perimeter, risk measure, scenario/model version or valuation basis and cannot be evaluated on the common specification: a rule-only comparison is unidentified; stop rather than rescale silently. Changing the risk model and the allocation rule together is a separate end-to-end intervention, requiring its own predeclared design and interpretation.
Nonadditive valuation/default/netting/collateral interactions change coalition losses: request a coalition valuation oracle or validated rule; do not sum book losses blindly.
Credit restrictions and coalition guarantees conflict: return exact infeasibility and the minimum relaxations under the approved model; do not invent a subsidy or a withdrawal right.
Only accounting reassignment with no decision consequence: stop at descriptive evidence; no pooling-gain claim.
