MATHEMATICAL CONTENT RETAINED
1. Technology: all minimal binary zero-sum circuits of size k+1 in
   F_2^[1+(k-1)M], with unit profits and unit inputs. Exact arity r=k+1.
2. Explicit supported-circuit proof: every nonempty displayed zero-sum subset
   has size kt+(t mod 2), so exactly the complete one-branch circuits have
   size k+1. Full packing dual prices root and every zero-capacity row at one.
3. Full-cube institution has exactly n=1+kM actual owners and
   d=2^[1+(k-1)M]-1 rows; every owner has its fixed full real unit cube.
4. Explicit seed permutations and v-to-w_1 basis replacement; explicit active
   branch permutations, within-branch permutations and e_(j,1)-to-w_j maps.
   All dormant high reports are retained. The maps are coordinate permutations
   of real reports, not binary linear transformations of real quantities.
5. Arbitrary-payment recurrence: a_m=min(s-1,k(m-1)),
   (a_m+1)q_m >= a_m q_(m-1). The same actual low owner alone restores;
   selected old owners are unchanged. Its old payment is dropped only by
   nonnegativity. Coverage gives q_1>=1/(k+1), including when k+1>s.
6. Exact finite factor L_(k,M,s)=1+k(M-1)P/(k+1), where
   P=product_(m=2)^M a_m/(a_m+1). The explicit nonnegative rational
   certificate is included, including its M=1 boundary.
7. If k(M-1)+1<=s, both k Q/(k+1) and
   1+k(M-1)Q/[(k+1)M] are lower bounds, with
   Q=product_(m=2)^M km/[k(m-1)+1].
8. For s>=2, exact certificate maximizers M=s,s+1 and formula B_(k,s);
   exact gamma identity and fixed-k exponent (k-1)/k with its leading constant.
   A separate elementary inequality gives the all-s bound including s=1.
9. Pure realization: one actual unit owner per row, with scalar real domain
   [0,1] along its own fixed coordinate. Only the JOINT GL action is used;
   no unrestricted pure-owner permutation is assumed. Setwise preservation of
   active rows implies preservation of every extra zero owner's report.
   Exact counts n=d=2^[1+(k-1)M]-1 remain separate from item 3.
10. The s=6, k=2 exhibit gives 493/243 with 127 rows, and either 13 fully
    eligible owners or 127 pure owners.

  appendix does not claim the displayed labeled C_M must itself be costly.

  lower or simultaneous pure-and-small-owner realization is claimed.

  editorial omission, not a mathematical rejection of the accepted corollary.

  barriers because none is needed for these lower-bound proofs.
