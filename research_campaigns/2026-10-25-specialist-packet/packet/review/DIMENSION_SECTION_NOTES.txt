Quantifiers and boundary conventions
  * D_d and D_d^{OUT} are supremums across technologies and finite fixed
    owner/endowment models of their infimal all-profile funding factors.
    No attainment of the individual-model infima is assumed.
  * Every real report in each fixed finite box is in the domain. Every
    coalition is protected, including unchanged members of a deviating
    coalition. No original-mechanism anonymity, regularity, price structure,
    scale invariance, or measurability is assumed in either lower bound.
  * The upper class includes finite-valued monotone concave homogeneous
    functions with downward discontinuities between support faces. Supporting
    gradients are extended to boundary bundles by the concavity segment
    inequality, not boundary continuity. Integrals start at positive epsilon
    and discard a nonnegative endpoint. Finite TCWM controls initial face
    activation. Log-diagonal smoothing has relative bounds at every face.
  * The OUT reduction is deliberately specialized to min_r, the continuous
    lower witness. On zero-value faces all its price payments are set to zero;
    coverage itself handles an OUT comparison with a zero-value low profile.
    We do not claim the more general price-reduction theorem for discontinuous
    technologies.
  * In the finite TCWM lower, zero-report owners at positive output retain a
    payment variable w>=0. In the count reduction, inactive payments are
    dropped only where their nonnegativity weakens a necessary inequality.
    Neither proof inserts a dummy-zero axiom.
  * Compactness is across distinct exogenous finite models. Each original
    model has fixed owners, eligibility, and unit report intervals. The final
    contradiction says that some finite such model forbids every factor below
    the endpoint; it is not a claim about a literal infinite-owner mechanism.
