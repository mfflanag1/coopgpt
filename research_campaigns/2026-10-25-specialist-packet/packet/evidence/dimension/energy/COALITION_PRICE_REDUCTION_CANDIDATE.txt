AGGREGATE-PRICE EXISTENCE EQUIVALENCE FOR UNBOUNDED FIXED-OWNER COALITION MODELS
17 October 2026. Internally audited structural theorem: root and independent
Fable review 011328 found no correctness defects. External specialist review
and historical novelty comparison remain outstanding.
Version2 includes a boundary completion using the SAME global count array on
every support face. It does not apply to individual IC alone. Version1, which
proved only positive-orthant necessity, is frozen separately.

MODEL
Fix a finite resource dimension d and a finite, continuous, nonnegative,
coordinatewise nondecreasing, degree-one homogeneous production function
F:R_+^d->R_+. Coalition worth is F of its aggregate owned bundle. Actual owners
and their eligibility remain fixed in each model. Suppose every finite owner
model admits nonnegative coalition-covering payments with uniform total funding
at most kappa F, and either:
 (TCWM) no coalition increases its total payments by bindingly withholding
 its members' resources; or
 (OUT) its payment loss also covers F of the coalition's withheld bundle.
The same kappa works for arbitrary owner counts; the rules themselves need not
be the same across counts, anonymous, continuous, homogeneous, or linear.

EXISTENCE EQUIVALENCE
There exists a nonnegative resource-price vector p(c) on R_+^d such that:
 1. p(lambda c)=p(c) for every lambda>0;
 2. on the relative interior of each nonzero support face, its active prices
    are locally Lipschitz; absent-coordinate prices are set to zero;
 3. F(c)<=c dot p(c)<=kappa F(c);
 4. b dot p(c)>=F(b) for every b>=0 supported on the support of c;
 5. the aggregate-price payments y_i=b_i dot p(sum_j b_j) satisfy the SAME
    TCWM or OUT coalition inequalities on ALL nonnegative real profiles,
    including changes of the active support and arbitrary mixed actual owners.
Thus any impossibility for such prices, even just on the positive orthant, is an
unrestricted-mechanism impossibility for the original universal owner-count
problem. Conversely such a price rule directly supplies a mechanism for every
finite fixed-owner model with the same F and incentive requirement. This proves
existence equivalence with no funding loss. Neither boundary continuity across
different faces nor regularity of the original mechanisms is claimed.
The construction does not alter identities during any deviation.

PROOF A: COUNTS AND COMPACTNESS
For each N take N pure-role owners for each of the d resources, each with
capacity bound1. Average a candidate rule within the N owners of each role.
At a binary profile let m_j<=N be the number of active resource-j owners and
pi_j^(N)(m) their common active payment. Zero-type coordinates are assigned zero
when there are no active owners of that type. Nonnegative inactive payments
are retained in the real rule, but may be dropped in necessary inequalities.

For integer vectors0<=u<=r<=m, the necessary inequalities are
 sum_j m_j pi_j(m)<=kappa F(m),
 sum_j r_j pi_j(m)>=F(r),
 sum_j r_j pi_j(m)>=sum_j(r_j-u_j)pi_j(m-u)+D(u),          (1)
where D(u)=0 for TCWM and D(u)=F(u) for OUT. The last comparison withholds u_j
whole active owners of each type inside a coalition containing r_j such owners.
Its newly inactive members' payments at the reduced profile are nonnegative;
dropping them gives(1). Every comparison uses the same actual owners.

For m_j>0, each coordinate pi_j(m) lies in the finite interval
[0,kappa F(m)/m_j]. Take a diagonal N->infinity subsequence on the countably
many coordinates. This yields a nonempty compact convex set C of abstract
arrays satisfying(1) for every finite integer count vector. Compactness is
coordinatewise diagonal compactness; no common bound across all count vectors
is needed. For absent types set the unused pi_j(m) coordinate to zero.

PROOF B: INTEGER DILATION AND PUBLIC SCALE INVARIANCE
For each positive integer k, define(T_k pi)(m)=pi(km). Applying(1) with all
counts multiplied by k and dividing by k proves T_k maps C into C, using
degree-one homogeneity of F and D. These linear operators commute.

Average T_k over k=prod_{j=1}^L p_j^{e_j},0<=e_j<L, for the first L primes.
For each fixed prime p_j, shifting its exponent telescopes, so the coordinate
difference between this average and its T_{p_j} image is at most
[kappa F(m)/m_v]/L at coordinate(m,v). A coordinatewise subsequential limit
is fixed by every prime and hence every integer dilation. This is the same
elementary averaging used in UNRESTRICTED_OUTSIDE_SHARP_CANDIDATE.txt.

For rational c>0 define p(c)=pi(Lc), for any common integer denominator L.
Dilation invariance makes this independent of L and homogeneous under rational
positive scaling. Multiplying rational capacities, coalition holdings and
withheld amounts by a common denominator transfers(1) exactly to price
payments on all rational profiles. At this point no regularity is assumed.

PROOF C: GENERAL FINITE-COMPARISON LIPSCHITZ BOUND
Let c,c'>0 be rational vectors and lambda=min_j c_j/c'_j, so lambda c'<=c.
Fix a resource v. At low aggregate lambda c', choose a coalition holding ALL
lambda c'_v units of resource v and no other resources. Give that coalition
exactly the added bundle d=c-lambda c' to reach the high aggregate c. Its high
holdings are c_v of resource v and d_j of every other resource. TCWM, which
is also implied by OUT, yields
 lambda c'_v p_v(c')
 <=c_v p_v(c)+sum_{j!=v}(c_j-lambda c'_j)p_j(c),          (2)
using rational homogeneity p(lambda c')=p(c'). Thus
 p_v(c')-p_v(c)<= [sum_j(c_j-lambda c'_j)p_j(c)]/(lambda c'_v).
Interchanging c and c' gives the reverse bound.

On any compact box[a,b]^d with a>0, individual prices satisfy
p_j(c)<=kappa max_{[a,b]^d}F/a. Also |1-lambda|<=||c-c'||_infinity/a,
c_j-lambda c'_j<=||c-c'||_infinity(1+b/a), and lambda c'_v>=a^2/b.
Consequently(2) and its reverse give a finite box-dependent Lipschitz constant
for each p_v on the rational points of the box. Therefore p has a unique locally
Lipschitz extension to R_{++}^d. Rational homogeneity, budget, coalition coverage
and same-interior TCWM/OUT inequalities pass to real profiles by continuity of
p and F. Coalition holdings may themselves have zero coordinates.

For arbitrary b>=0 choose a small positive scalar s with sb<=c. Coalition
coverage at holdings sb gives sb dot p(c)>=F(sb)=sF(b). Dividing by s yields
the global linear-majorant property on the positive orthant. Taking b=c gives
its lower budget inequality.

PROOF D: ALL SUPPORT FACES AND CROSS-FACE COMPARISONS
The ONE global array from ProofB already contains count vectors with any zero
pattern and all inequalities between such vectors. For each nonempty support
I, restrict to rational vectors positive exactly on I. Repeat ProofC using only
coordinates in I and F restricted to that face. Its proof supplies a unique
continuous, locally Lipschitz extension of the active prices to the face's
relative interior. Set inactive-coordinate prices to zero, and p(0)=0. There
are finitely many faces. Homogeneity holds on each face and hence globally;
the zero profile has F(0)=0 by finite degree-one homogeneity. If F vanishes on
a face, the budget forces every active price to zero there.

It is important that these face functions extend restrictions of the SAME
array; no independent favorable choice of mechanisms on different faces occurs.
To verify an arbitrary real cross-face TCWM or OUT comparison, write the
aggregate outside resources as o>=0, the coalition's retained resources as r>=0,
and its withheld resources as d>=0. Thus high aggregate c=o+r+d and low
aggregate c'=o+r. The desired inequality is
       (r+d) dot p(o+r+d) >= r dot p(o+r)+D(d).           (3)
Approximate o,r,d jointly by nonnegative rational vectors, preserving the exact
zero/positive pattern of EACH of the three vectors. High and low aggregate
support patterns then remain EXACTLY fixed throughout the approximation. The
rational form of(3) follows from array(1). Relative-interior continuity on each
of the two faces, together with continuity of F, passes it to the real limit.
If a compared aggregate is zero, its component vectors are identically zero
and its payment is identically zero, so the same argument applies. There is
no need to show that prices converge across a changing face.

For coverage, approximate coalition and outsider aggregates with their patterns
fixed in the same manner. For the budget, approximate each aggregate within
its support face. Both inequalities pass to the real profile. If b is supported
on I=supp(c), a small multiple sb is coordinatewise at most c, so coalition
coverage and homogeneity again give b dot p(c)>=F(b). No majorant for bundles
using absent resources is needed or asserted at that profile.

Finally define actual owner payments y_i=b_i dot p(sum_j b_j). Their sum is
c dot p(c), and coalition totals depend only on their aggregate bundles.
For any real underreport by any coalition of mixed owners, its actual outside,
retained and withheld aggregate vectors are o,r,d from(3), so that inequality
proves exactly the required coalition condition. Coalition coverage and the
funding bound likewise transfer directly. There is no change to any owner's
identity or allowed resources, and no strategic split/merge assumption. This
completes the all-profile existence reduction and its immediate converse.

WHY THIS DOES NOT APPLY TO INDIVIDUAL INCENTIVES
Count dilation turns a unit-owner deviation into a deviation by k actual owners.
The proof that T_k preserves(1) therefore uses coalition protection. Individual
IC alone does not license that scaled coalition comparison. No implication for
the original individual-IC owner/dimension gap follows from this reduction.

BOUNDARY AND HISTORICAL LIMITS
The proof constructs an all-profile rule from a globally compatible count array;
it does not say that an arbitrary interior price system can be filled in on
faces independently. Boundary continuity is unnecessary for the finite
inequality argument and is not claimed. The result is an existence theorem,
not an algorithm or an effective finite-owner lower-bound certificate.
Compactness and averaging are established mathematics; the historical status
of this resource-incentive reduction requires original-source review. It should
not be advertised as a historically new general method before that comparison.
