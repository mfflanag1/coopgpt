For integer k>=2 (arity r=k+1) and M>=1, let
  s0=k(M-1)+1, n=1+kM,
  Q_(k,M)=product_(m=2)^M [km/(k(m-1)+1)].
Then the same arbitrary-payment contract admits a finite fixed institution
forcing
  kappa >= [k/(k+1)] Q_(k,M),
and terminal coverage strengthens this to
  kappa >= 1 + [k(M-1)/((k+1)M)] Q_(k,M).
Consequently K_r(s)=Omega_r(s^((r-2)/(r-1))).

CONSTRUCTION
Let V=F2^{1+(k-1)M}, with basis v and e_(j,l) for j=1,...,M,
l=1,...,k-1. Rows are all nonzero vectors. Activities are all minimal binary
linear circuits of size exactly k+1: distinct rows whose sum is0 and no proper
nonempty subset sums to0. Each has unit profit and consumes1 of each row.
The technology has arity exactly r. GL(V) preserves it.

Take n=1+kM actual owners, all with fixed full unit-cube report domains, and
average an arbitrary rule over GL(V) times owner permutations as in the parent
proof. Root p reports v. Each of the k-1 fixed high owners of branch j reports
its basis row e_(j,l). Low owner l_j reports
  w_j=v+sum_l e_(j,l)
when j<=m and reports0 when j>m. Denote this C_m. Actual owner identities never
change across comparisons; only l_m restores from0 to its fixed row.

EXACT SUPPORTED-ACTIVITY CLASSIFICATION
Consider any nonempty zero-sum subset of the displayed possible row set
{v} union {e_(j,l)} union {w_j}. Let J be the set of low rows it contains,
with t=|J|. Each corresponding high basis coordinate occurs in no other low
row, so cancellation forces exactly all k-1 high rows of every branch in J,
and none from other branches. Root v occurs exactly when t is odd. Therefore
the subset has cardinality
  tk + (t mod2).
No nonempty dependency has t=0. For k>=2, cardinality k+1 occurs precisely at
t=1; for t>=2 it is at least2k>k+1 except k=1, which is excluded. Thus the
only supported size-(k+1) circuits are the complete single branches
  {v,w_j,e_(j,1),...,e_(j,k-1)}
for j<=m. Each is minimal. All production uses root capacity1, and one branch
at level1 is feasible, so F(C_m)=1. Full dual: price1 on root and all currently
zero-capacity rows,0 on other positive rows. It covers every full-tech column
and has objective1.

SEED AND ACTIVE ORBITS
At C_1 all k+1 members of its one supported circuit form one payment orbit.
Permuting the basis vectors v,e_(1,1),...,e_(1,k-1) fixes their sum w_1 and
all inactive high basis rows. Mapping v to w_1 while fixing every high basis
row is invertible and exchanges v,w_1. These transformations preserve the
entire profile and connect all seed owners. Coverage gives each at least
1/(k+1), hence active private total T_1>=k/(k+1).

At C_m, permutations of active branch blocks and permutations of the high
basis within a branch preserve the profile. In addition, mapping e_(j,1) to
w_j while fixing v and every other basis vector is invertible and exchanges
e_(j,1),w_j. Therefore all km active private owners share q_m and T_m=km q_m.

Protect l_m together with all k(m-1) old active private owners. This fixed
coalition has k(m-1)+1<=s0 members. Its old payment is at least T_(m-1);
its new payment is [k(m-1)+1]q_m. Thus
  T_m >= [km/(k(m-1)+1)] T_(m-1).
Iterate and use the terminal budget to prove the first bound. For the stronger
bound, final branch coverage gives root payment z plus k q_M at least1;
budget is at least z+kM q_M, giving1+k(M-1)q_M.

RATE WITH AN EXPLICIT ELEMENTARY CONSTANT
Put alpha=(k-1)/k. Then the product factor is m/(m-alpha). Since
  m/(m-alpha) >= 1+alpha/m >= (1+1/m)^alpha,
where the second inequality is concavity of x^alpha, multiplication gives
  Q_(k,M) >= ((M+1)/2)^alpha.
For integer s>=1 choose M=floor((s-1)/k)+1. Then s0<=s and M>=s/k, so
  K_(k+1)(s) >= [k/(k+1)] (s/(2k))^alpha.
The exact product also equals
  Gamma(M+1) Gamma(1+1/k) / Gamma(M+1/k),
but the gamma asymptotic is not needed for the rate.
