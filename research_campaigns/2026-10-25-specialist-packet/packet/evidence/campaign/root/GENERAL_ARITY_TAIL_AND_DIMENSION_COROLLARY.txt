GENERAL-ARITY TAIL AND JOINT-PARAMETER COROLLARY
Root derivation, 18 October2026. Routine consequences of the binary-circuit
construction; not separate results/papers or an improved arity3 exponent.
General-arity parent and arity3 tail have survived opposite-model review.
This combined corollary is pending its own final independent check.

Fix k=r-1>=2, s>=2 and M>=1. The parent's binary circuit construction has
n=1+kM fixed fully eligible owners, d=2^{1+(k-1)M}-1 resource rows, unit profits,
and arity exactly r. At profile C_m the km active private owners share payment
q_m, production is1, and seed coverage gives q_1>=1/(k+1).

Choose a_m=min(s-1,k(m-1)) old active owners plus the same actual newly
restoring owner. TCWM gives (a_m+1)q_m>=a_m q_(m-1). Together with terminal
branch coverage z+kq_M>=1, the budget is at least
 B_(k,M,s)=1+[k(M-1)/(k+1)]P_(k,M,s),
 P_(k,M,s)=product_(m=2)^M a_m/(a_m+1).                 (1)
The selection need not contain entire private branches. All old active owners
have equal payments at both relevant profiles. This is a fixed-owner lower
for arbitrary admissible payments after the same legitimate finite average.

OPTIMAL LENGTH FOR THIS CERTIFICATE
Put h=ceil(s/k), alpha=(s-1)/s, and
 Q_(k,h)=product_(m=2)^h km/[k(m-1)+1].
Then P_(k,h,s)=Q_(k,h)/h, and for M>=h,
 P_(k,M,s)=[Q_(k,h)/h] alpha^(M-h).
Before that point, for M>1 the successive ratio of (M-1)P is
 k M^2/[(M-1)(kM+1)]>1: numerator minus denominator is
 (k-1)M+1>0. The initial M=1 increment is0 and M=2 is positive.
In the tail, for M>=h and M>1 the ratio is M alpha/(M-1), greater than1
for M<s, equal to1 for M=s, and smaller than1 for M>s. Since h<=s,
the two global maxima are M=s and M=s+1, with identical value
 B_(k,s)=1+[k(s-1)Q_(k,h)/((k+1)h)] alpha^(s-h).        (2)

For fixed k and s tending to infinity, the exact product has
 Q_(k,h)=Gamma(h+1)Gamma(1+1/k)/Gamma(h+1/k)
        ~Gamma(1+1/k)h^{(k-1)/k}.
Consequently B_(k,s)-1 is asymptotic to
 [k/(k+1)] Gamma(1+1/k) k^(1/k) exp(-(k-1)/k)
 times s^{(k-1)/k}. At k=2 this is exactly the arity3 constant
 sqrt(2*pi)/(3*sqrt(e)), with B_(2,6)=493/243.
This sharpens only the constructed certificate's constant. It is not the
optimal general funding constant or a mechanism upper bound.

JOINT OWNER/ROW/COALITION COUNTS
Define K_r^{<=}(n,d,s) as the worst optimal funding factor over admissible
institutions with at most n owners, at most d resource rows and arity at most r.
The definition is only used here to state the parameter dependence cleanly;
the main campaign still studies unrestricted growing n,d.

Whenever M>=1 satisfies
 1+kM<=n,   2^{1+(k-1)M}-1<=d,
formula(1) is a lower for K_r^{<=}(n,d,s), with no need to change owner labels
or to impose dummy-zero payments. One may maximize(1) over these allowed M.
The unconstrained recurrence already gives the simpler exact corollary when
also k(M-1)+1<=s:
 K_r^{<=}(n,d,s)>=1+[k(M-1)/((k+1)M)]Q_(k,M).
For fixed r and sufficiently large parameters this implies
 K_r^{<=}(n,d,s)=Omega_r(min{s,n,log(d+1)}^{(r-2)/(r-1)}).
An exact admissible choice is the minimum of
 floor((n-1)/k), floor((log2(d+1)-1)/(k-1)), ceil(s/k),
provided that minimum is at least1. The logarithm expresses the actual
exponential resource cost of this construction; it must not be omitted.

PURE-OWNER VARIANT
Assign one actual owner to each nonzero vector row and use the joint resource/
owner GL action. The same proof and tail hold, but now n=d=2^{1+(k-1)M}-1.
For that variant the corresponding lower is only
 Omega_r(min{s,log(n+1),log(d+1)}^{(r-2)/(r-1)}).
Do not combine this pure-ownership conclusion with the small-owner count of
the fully eligible construction. Both variants have genuine fixed owners and
all real report boxes; the counts, not the incentive contract, differ.
