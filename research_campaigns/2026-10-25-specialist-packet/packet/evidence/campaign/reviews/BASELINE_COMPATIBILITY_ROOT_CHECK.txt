CONTRACT COMPATIBILITY
Both results fix actual owners, technology, eligibility and endowment boxes
within an institution. Both allow every real binding coordinate underreport.
Both require nonnegative payments, coverage for EVERY coalition, and total
funding relative to production at that report. Only incentive protection is
truncated at coalition size s; protected members may leave reports unchanged.
Neither result requires dummy-zero payments or an additional marginal floor.
The upper additionally provides the full outside-production increment, hence
TCWM. The lower uses TCWM alone, hence also constrains full OUT. Thus writing
K3(s) <= K3_OUT(s) between the lower and upper is legitimate.

WHY THE INHERITED UPPER IS VALID UNDER THESE QUANTIFIERS
For p=1/s define m_i as the p-Bernoulli expected whole-owner marginal and pay
y_i=p^(1-r)m_i. The resource-supplier construction supports each optimal
activity by positive actual-owner hyperedges of size at most r. It is an
analytic decomposition only: no strategic owners are split or cloned.

At a retained set R, an optimal dual gives the subset marginal lower
sum_(i in T) U_i(R)>=F(b_T) for every T subset R. The activity-support
deletion argument gives sum_(i in R) U_i(R)<=rF(b_R). Positive rank-r
support also gives E F(b_(S intersect R))>=p^r F(b_S) for every S.
Consequently coverage holds for ALL S. Concavity and homogeneity give
E F(b_R)<=F(p b_N)=pF(b_N), yielding total funding at most
r p^(1-r)F(b_N)=r s^(r-1)F(b_N).

For a protected coalition C, the coefficient of F(b_A) in sum_(i in C)m_i
has the sign of |A intersect C|-p|C|. Every affected A intersects C, so
this coefficient is nonnegative when |C|<=s. Superadditivity gives
F(b_A)-F(b_A-d_A)>=F(d_A). Unaffected A have d_A=0, including the terms
whose coefficients can be negative. Summing leaves the SAME n-owner rule
at the withheld-bundle profile d. The already proved full coverage there
gives the required full OUT increment. This includes partial, simultaneous
multicoordinate restorations and passive protected members.

For s=1, p=1 is handled by the grand marginal directly; singular powers in
the coefficient formula are not used. At zero production all budgeted
payments vanish, and the construction remains valid. Zero-profit activities
can be discarded; profitable input-free activities are excluded by contract.
