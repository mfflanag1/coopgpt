The model fixes actual resource owners and allows partial withholding within
their original report boxes. Payments cover every coalition's stand-alone
production. The incentive condition protects the aggregate payment of coalitions of at
most s owners, including members whose reports do not change; coverage still
applies to every coalition. A stronger version also covers production from
the withheld resources. A funding factor K bounds total payments by K times
realized output, so the supplement is at most (K-1) times output.

The draft proposes sharp resource-dimension funding bounds and an
Omega(sqrt(s)) lower for arbitrary payment rules in arity-three packing
production, against an O(s^2) upper. It also includes an all-profile construction
with factor 1+1/sqrt(2) on unit-profit four-resource paths and their complete
blow-ups, allowing fixed mixed owners. The general sparse gap remains open.

I would particularly value your advice on:
