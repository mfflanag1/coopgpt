1. EXACT MULTILATERAL MODEL IS ESTABLISHED
Moretti and Norde, A note on weighted multi-glove games (2021), Definition 3.1,
Theorem 3.2, and final weight-domain remark (printed PDF pp. 3-4 and 11).
https://d-nb.info/1240670206/34
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00355-021-01337-8
Coordinator retrieved the complete primary article. Its model is exactly a
partition of original owners by good type, with coalition value the minimum
of weighted totals over types. The text initially uses positive integer weights,
then explicitly extends results to positive real weights and discusses zero-weight
null players. Its bottleneck-pool vector proves total balancedness. It studies
supermodularity, population monotonic allocation schemes, and Shapley values in
specified subclasses, rather than the displayed private-capacity subsidized rule.
We claim no new game model, nonempty-core theorem or generic capping principle.
The earlier two-sided Moretti-Norde source alone did not establish k-type priority;
this directly inspected paper closes that specific model-comparison gap.

2. CONTINUOUS BANZHAF FUNCTIONAL IS ESTABLISHED
Sascha Kurz, Measuring voting power in convex policy spaces (2013), Definition
6.11, printed p. 17. The unversioned PDF identifies itself as arXiv v1.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.5936
Coordinator read the definition directly. BANZHAF_IDENTIFICATION.txt proves that
our raw owner-threshold payment equals T times its absolute continuous Banzhaf
index, applied to the normalized capped OWNER-coordinate minimum game. The
fairness top-up is a further operation. The expectation formula itself is therefore
not claimed as new. Priority of the joint reporting/coverage/fairness theorem,
its sharp stage-asymptotic bound, and restricted-class separation is unresolved.
A citation-network audit of continuous values in resource-sharing is still needed.

3. STRATEGIC CAPACITY MODEL AND QUALITATIVE CONFLICT ARE ESTABLISHED
Huang, Mei, Yang and Cao, Mechanism Design in Max-Flows (2025), Sections 2.1-2.2,
Example 1, Proposition 1, Definition 2 and Theorem 1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1
Their formal graph is acyclic, with one source/sink and every edge on a source-
sink path, one owner per edge, positive capacities, underreports, and no use costs.
The formal report domain is positive though deviations include zero. They require
efficient payments and establish a conflict with core selection; Shapley and their
minimal-cut rule provide truthful alternatives. Our k-pool game is the explicit
specialization to serial parallel-edge bundles. Neither efficiency nor their
other guarantees imply our subsidized coverage across profiles. Our zero-capacity
boundary proof is explicit. No current-open or priority claim follows from this
comparison, and later citing literature was not exhaustively traced.

4. SET CROSS-MONOTONIC PROFIT SHARING IS A DIFFERENT STRONGER REQUIREMENT
Immorlica, Mahdian and Mirrokni, Limitations of cross-monotonic cost-sharing
schemes, author PDF, Definition 3.3 and Theorem 3.6 in Section 3.5.
https://immorlica.com/pubs/costshare.pdf
Coordinator directly inspected the original. It requires a profit-sharing scheme
for all participating-agent sets, with every retained agent gaining as the set
grows. Its maximum-flow lower bound grows with owner count even for a two-layer
unit network. The theorem concerns population changes, not only the payment of
the owner changing a capacity report. Thus its lower bound does not contradict
our fixed-owner own-report mechanism. Converting its alpha budget factor to
subsidy alpha-1 is an interpretation, not its terminology. No unproved transfer
of that theorem to Huang-style CM is used here.

5. FIXED-GAME STABILIZATION IS ESTABLISHED
Bachrach et al., Bounds on the Cost of Stabilizing a Cooperative Game, JAIR 63
(2018), Definitions 1-2 and Observation 5.
https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/11270/26463/20899
Coordinator retrieved the original and inspected the definitions. These are
pointwise subsidy requirements for a fixed game; our games already have nonempty
cores, so their pointwise cost of stability is zero. Our positive requirements
come from one report-monotone rule across profiles. Their general n and sqrt(n)
bounds do not supply this stage-scaling theorem. The logarithmic cost-sharing
result elsewhere in that article has a different direction of inequalities and
normalization; it is not a logarithmic upper bound on our profit subsidy.

6. THRESHOLD FLOW STABILIZATION IS A DIFFERENT CHARACTERISTIC FUNCTION
Resnick, Bachrach, Meir and Rosenschein, The Cost of Stability in Network Flow
Games (2009), abstract and Section 2.3.
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/CoS-NFG-MFCS.pdf
Coordinator retrieved original text in this continuing session. Coalition worth
is binary, depending on reaching a flow threshold, rather than the amount of
produced flow. No strategic report or original-core H/k theorem is supplied by
that definition. Its results cannot be imported by changing the name of the game.
