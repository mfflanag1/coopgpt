EXACT OWNER-THRESHOLD PROBABILITIES
For positive normalized widths a_1,...,a_m, the sum of independent uniforms on
[0,a_i] has CDF
 F(t)=[1/(m! product a_i)] sum_{S subset [m]}(-1)^|S|(t-a(S))_+^m.
The density is its derivative on each subset-sum interval. A short pool has
support within [0,1], so the winning probability for pool j is exactly
 integral_0^1 f_j(t) product_{l!=j}(1-F_l(t)) dt.
The verifier enumerates signed knots, multiplies rational polynomials and
integrates exactly. Zero widths are omitted from integration, but owners are
not removed from payments or original coalition inequalities. No simulation.
