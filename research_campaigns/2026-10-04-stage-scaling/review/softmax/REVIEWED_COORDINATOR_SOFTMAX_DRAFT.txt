OWN REPORT, LONG REGION: hold all other reports fixed, own pool total x+a>=B,
where B is the least total among the other pools. Then T=B. Write
m=min(x,B), alpha=sum_{same-pool others}min(c_l,B), so C_j=alpha+m.
Other weights fixed. On m<B, derivative of m p_j wrt m is
 p_j[1-(m/B)(1-p_j)]>=0. Saturation makes the payment constant for m>=B.

OWN REPORT, SHORT REGION: 0<x+a<=B, T=x+a, rho_j=1, own payment=x/Z.
For every other pool l, rho_l=sum_{i in N_l}min(c_i/T,1), with
 0>=rho_l'>=-rho_l/T
on each smooth interval. Thus
 y_i'=p_j[1+x sum_{l!=j}p_l rho_l']
       >=p_j[1-sum_{l!=j}p_l rho_l].
The bracket is nonnegative iff
 sum_{l!=j}(rho_l-1)exp(-(rho_l-1))<=1.
Each summand is at most 1/e. Therefore it holds for k<=3, since 2/e<1.
Continuous clipping kinks and matching formulas at T=B complete monotonicity.
At T=0, all values and payments are zero; later payments are nonnegative.

BUDGET: sum_i y_i/T=1+F, where F=sum_j u_j exp(-u_j)/sum_j exp(-u_j).
Choose an index s with u_s=0. Let b>0 solve b exp(b)=(k-1)/e. For any u>=0,
 (u-b)exp(-u)<=exp(-1-b), with maximum at u=1+b.
Summing over j!=s, sum_j(u_j-b)exp(-u_j)<=-b+(k-1)exp(-1-b)=0.
Hence F<=b. Equality when u_s=0 and all remaining u_j=1+b. This is feasible:
one unit owner in pool s; in each other pool choose three equal owners with
capacity (2+b)/3<1 for k=3. Then C_j=2+b and no owner is clipped.
This is budget optimality FOR THIS RULE ONLY.
