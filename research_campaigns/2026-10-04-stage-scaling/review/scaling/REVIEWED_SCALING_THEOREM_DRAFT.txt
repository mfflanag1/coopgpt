COORDINATOR SYNTHESIS -- CANDIDATE, PENDING QA AND EXACT CHECKS
Random owner thresholds: a sublinear all-stage mechanism and its sharp order.
Based on independent delegated construction plus coordinator tail refinement.

MODEL AND RULE
Use CONTRACT.txt: k>=2 pools, original fixed owners, c_i>=0, T=min_j A_j,
d_i=min(c_i,T), D_j=sum_{i in N_j}d_i, rho_j=D_j/T when T>0.
M_i=T-v(N without i) bounds original H_i from above. Draw independent U_i uniform
on [0,1] only as an analysis device, let X_j=sum_{i in N_j}U_i d_i,
p_j=P(X_j<X_l for every l!=j), and pay
 y_i=max(d_i p_j, M_i/k).
These are deterministic expected-value payments, not realized randomized payments.
Set y=0 at T=0. Each nonzero-T pool has a positive-capacity owner, so X_j is
continuous, independent across pools; ties have probability zero and sum p_j=1.

CLAIM A: COVERAGE, MONOTONICITY AND ORIGINAL-CORE FLOOR
Capping preserves every coalition value: min(c(X),T)=min(d(X),T), v(S)<=T,
and the globally short pool has d(N_j)=T. Hence v(S)=min_j d(S intersect N_j).
The base payments d_i p_j cover every coalition as a convex combination of its
capped pool totals. The max operation only increases payments.

M_i is nondecreasing in own report: v(N without i) does not depend on it and
T does. For the base payment, fix owner i in pool j, report x, other own-pool
capacity a, and least other pool total B. If B=0 all payments vanish. Otherwise
T(x)=min(x+a,B), d_i(x)=min(x,B). Write
 R=sum_{h in N_j without i} U_h min(c_h,T(x)),
 Y(x)=min_{l!=j}sum_{h in N_l}U_h min(c_h,T(x)).
R is INDEPENDENT of x: if a<=B, T(x)>=a>=each other own capacity; if a>B,
T(x)=B throughout. Y(x) is pointwise nondecreasing. Conditional on U_{-i},
 d_i P(U_i d_i<Y-R | U_{-i})=min(d_i,(Y-R)_+).
This also holds at d_i=0 with both sides zero. Its expectation is d_i p_j and
is nondecreasing in x. Taking max with M_i/k preserves monotonicity everywhere,
including clipping kinks, bottleneck changes and zero reports.
Finally any original-core allocation has x_i<=T-v(N without i)=M_i, so H_i<=M_i.

CLAIM B: FAIRNESS TOP-UP COST AT MOST T
In each pool j let delta=A_j-T>=0. Then M_i=(c_i-delta)_+.
If r>=1 terms are positive, their sum is at most A_j-r delta<=T; if r=0 the
sum is zero. Thus sum_i M_i/k<=T. The max operation increases total payment by
at most T. This proves an additive budget bound, not that full T is always needed.

CLAIM C: OWNER-COUNT-INDEPENDENT TAIL
Normalize d_i by T, so a_i in [0,1] and sum_{i in N_j}a_i=rho_j. Let W be the
almost surely unique winning pool, so the normalized base total payment is
sum_j p_j rho_j=E[rho_W]. A short pool s has X_s/T<=1, hence
 p_j <= P(sum_{i in N_j}a_i U_i<=1).
For t>0 and a in [0,1], Jensen applied to the concave function z^a gives
 E exp(-taU) <= [E exp(-tU)]^a = [(1-exp(-t))/t]^a <= t^(-a).
Independence and exponential Markov therefore give
 P(sum a_i U_i<=1) <= exp(t)t^(-rho_j).
Taking t=rho_j gives p_j<=(e/rho_j)^(rho_j).
For r>=e, the function (e/r)^r is decreasing, so
 P(rho_W>r)<=min(1, k(e/r)^r).
Let R_k>e be the unique solution R_k log(R_k/e)=log k. Then
k(e/R_k)^(R_k)=1. For r>=R_k, log[k(e/r)^r] has derivative -log r<=-log R_k,
so P(rho_W>r)<=exp(-(r-R_k)log R_k). Tail integration yields
 E[rho_W]<=R_k+1/log R_k.
Adding Claim B, the rule has
 sum_i y_i <= [1+R_k+1/log R_k]T,
 beta <= R_k+1/log R_k = (1+o(1)) log k/log log k.
The last equivalence follows directly from R log(R/e)=log k (or Lambert W).
There is no polynomial-time evaluation claim: the present exact verifier uses
piecewise polynomial integration with exponentially many subset-sum knots.

CLAIM D: THE GROWTH ORDER IS SHARP FOR THIS RULE, NOT FOR ALL MECHANISMS
Take one unit owner in pool 1, and r unit owners in each of pools 2,...,k, r>=2.
T=1. On 0<=u<=1 the CDF of the sum of r independent uniform [0,1] variables
is u^r/r!: volume of an r-simplex. Thus
 p_1=integral_0^1 (1-u^r/r!)^(k-1) du.
The base subsidy is (r-1)(1-p_1); the fairness top-up cannot reduce it.
For every delta in (0,1),
 p_1<=delta+exp(-(k-1)delta^r/r!).
Fix epsilon in (0,1), L=log k, and r=floor((1-epsilon)L/log L). For sufficiently
large k, r>=2, r!<=r^r and r log r<=(1-epsilon)L. Also r log delta=o(L).
Therefore log((k-1)delta^r/r!)>=epsilon L+o(L), which tends to infinity.
Consequently limsup p_1<=delta for every fixed delta>0, and p_1 tends to zero.
The worst-case subsidy of this particular rule is at least
 (1-epsilon+o(1))log k/log log k.
Together with Claim C, and then epsilon down to zero, its worst-case ratio is
 (1+o(1))log k/log log k.
The unrestricted optimum may still be uniformly bounded. This construction
cannot answer that question positively, even though it improves the known upper
scaling within this project from linear to sublinear.

CLAIM E: WHY CAPPED POOL-TOTAL PRICES CANNOT MATCH THIS ORDER
Restrict to y_i=d_i p_j(rho_1,...,rho_k), with nonnegative prices independent of
within-pool distribution (labels and fixed owner counts may enter). Prices need
not sum to one; coalition coverage and own-report monotonicity are assumed.
Let k=q^2 and each pool have n owners, n divisible by q. Let B=1+beta be a
uniform budget factor. At the profile where every capacity is 1/n, rho=(1,...,1),
coverage gives sum p_j>=1 and budget sum p_j<=B, so some p_j<=B/k.
Now retain these same fixed owners, put capacity x in one owner of that pool j
and zero in its other owners; other pools keep n owners of capacity 1/n.
At x=1 the ratio vector is again all ones. At x=1/q, the j pool is short and
other rho values equal 1/x. By own monotonicity x p_j(low)<=p_j(high)<=B/k.
A coalition of the one positive j owner and n/q owners from every other pool
has value x and payment x sum_l p_l(low), so sum_l p_l(low)>=1.
Total payment at this low profile is x p_j + sum_{l!=j}p_l,
which is >=1-(1-x)p_j >=1-(1-x)B/(kx), and is at most Bx.
Hence B>=1/[x+(1-x)/(kx)]=k/(2 sqrt(k)-1)>sqrt(k)/2.
Thus this restricted class requires Omega(sqrt(k)) budget factor. The random
owner-threshold rule escapes by using within-pool capacity distributions.
This no-go permits zero capacities, as the frozen contract does. It makes no
claim for classes requiring all capacities to be strictly positive without
continuity assumptions or a separate argument.

STATUS
Claims A, B and the mechanism were independently proposed by Fable with a looser
Hoeffding O(log k) bound. Claims C and D sharpen that analysis here. Claim E is
the independently proposed obstruction with its exact denominator retained.
All remain pending independent review and exact finite stress checks. Novelty
is unconfirmed. Branching networks, costs, merging/splitting and overreports are
outside scope. Do not promote draft status to theorem until verification gates pass.
