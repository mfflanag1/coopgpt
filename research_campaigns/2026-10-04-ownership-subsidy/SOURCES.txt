1. Huang, Mei, Yang, Cao (2025), Mechanism Design in Max-Flows, Section3,
   Proposition1, and Example1.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1
   The original directly establishes Shapley truthfulness for capacity-owned flow
   games in the underreport-only model and the qualitative failure of truthful
   efficient core selection. These ingredients are credited, not claimed here.
   Our candidate contribution is the sharp5/6 coalition factor for the specified
   weighted2x2 pooling class, its scalar subsidy implication and original-core
   fairness bound. The inspected proposition does not state those quantitative
   constants. This local comparison is not an exhaustive search of follow-ups.

2. Hoffmann and Sudholter (2007), The Shapley value of exact assignment games,
   original article uploaded by author Peter Sudholter; read Section2 exactness
   definition and Section3 theorem/discussion, including Proposition3.5.
   https://www.researchgate.net/publication/24058925_The_Shapley_value_of_exact_assignment_games
   They give Shapley core-membership results for exact assignment games and a
   four-player assignment characterization. Our arbitrary-capacity pooling game
   need not be a one-to-one assignment game or exact. Concrete distinction at
   (2,1|1,1): all cross-pair values are1, but coalition{1,3,4} has value2; the
   one-to-one assignment game with those pair values gives that triple only1.
   Its efficient core is(0,0,1,1); coalition{2,3,4} has worth1 but receives2 in
   every core point, violating exactness. These comparisons are coordinator
   deductions from definitions, not claims made by that paper about our rule.
   No full citation-network audit was performed.
