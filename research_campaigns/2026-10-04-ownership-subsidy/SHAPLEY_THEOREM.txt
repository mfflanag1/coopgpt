A 20% SUBSIDY RULE FOR FOUR CAPACITY OWNERS -- 2026-10-04
Conventional proof, independently derived by Fable and coordinator; Codex QA
completed with no material error. Exact checks passed. Not formally verified.
No novelty claim. Ordinary Shapley value and its flow truthfulness are established.

MODEL AND MAIN THEOREM
Four labeled owners have capacities (a,b|c,d)>=0, left{1,2}, right{3,4}.
v(S)=min(sum of left capacities in S,sum of right capacities in S).
T=v(N). The original efficient core C has x>=0, x(N)=T, x(S)>=v(S).
H_i=max{x_i:x in C}; this benchmark is not recomputed after a subsidy.
Let phi be the ordinary individual Shapley value, averaged over all24 orders.
Define y_i=(6/5)phi_i at every report profile.

Then y>=0, y(S)>=v(S) for ALL coalitions, y_i is nondecreasing in its own
capacity, y_i>=(3/5)H_i, and sum y=(6/5)T. In particular beta=1/5 meets H/3.
At T=0 every coalition value and payment is0. This is truthful under binding
underreports, no operating costs, utility equal to payment. No overreport,
false-name, coordinated-report, cost or arbitrary-owner-count guarantee follows.
The original minimum-pool cut vector proves C is nonempty; C is compact.

NOTATION AND SHAPLEY FORMULA
For a cross pair set m_ij=min(c_i,c_j). Set
 w_1=min(b,c+d), w_2=min(a,c+d), w_3=min(a+b,d), w_4=min(a+b,c).
Thus w_i=v(N\{i}). The usual subset formula gives
 phi_1=[m13+m14+(w4-m23)+(w3-m24)+w2]/12+(T-w1)/4.
The other coordinates follow by within-pool swaps and exchanging the two pools.
Shapley nonnegativity follows from v being monotone; efficiency follows by
summing the telescoping marginal contributions in each of24 orders.

LEMMA1. SHARP COALITION FACTOR: phi(S)>=(5/6)v(S).
Empty sets, singletons and within-pool pairs have zero value. Grand-coalition
coverage follows from efficiency. It remains to check cross pairs and triples.

Cross pair S={2,3}: summing the two Shapley formulas gives
 6(phi_2+phi_3)=m23+w4+w1-m14+3T-w2-w3.
The desired inequality is equivalent to G>=4m23, where
 G=3T+w1+w4-w2-w3-m14.
Exchanging sides via(1<->4,2<->3) fixes this coalition, so assume a+b<=c+d.
Then T=a+b, w2=a, w1=b. Using w3<=a+b and m14<=a gives
 G=2a+4b+w4-w3-m14 >=3b+min(a+b,c) >=4min(b,c)=4m23.
Every cross pair is obtained by a within-pool relabeling.

Triple S={1,2,3}: by efficiency phi(S)=T-phi4. Substitution gives the equivalent
inequality K>=0, where
 K=9T-7w4-w1-w2-w3-m14-m24+m13+m23.
Put L=a+b and R=c+d. The following cases cover every nonnegative profile:
(i) L<=R, c>=L: K=[L-min(L,d)]+[a-min(a,d)]+[b-min(b,d)]>=0.
(ii) L<=R, c<L: bounding w3,m14,m24 by L,a,b respectively gives
 K>=6(L-c)+[min(a,c)+min(b,c)-c]>=0.
The bracket is nonnegative because a+b>=c.
(iii) L>R: T=c+d, w4=c, w3=d. For x=a,b use
 min(x,c+d)<=min(x,c)+d, and bound m14,m24 by d. This gives
 K>=2c+4d>=0.
The other triples follow by relabeling. Ties are covered by case(i) or(ii);
no division or positive-capacity assumption was used.

Sharpness: at (2,1,1,1), phi=(7/12,1/4,7/12,7/12). For S={2,3},
v(S)=1 and phi(S)=5/6. All capacities are strictly positive. Therefore6/5 is
the smallest CONSTANT scalar multiplier of Shapley that covers all coalitions
on this domain. This does NOT establish optimality among all mechanisms.

LEMMA2. phi_i>=H_i/2 (indeed at least half its removal marginal).
For any x in the original efficient core, x_i=T-x(N\{i})<=T-w_i.
Write M_i=T-w_i, so H_i<=M_i. For i=1 define
 f(z)=min(a+b,z)-min(b,z)=min(a,max(0,z-b)).
Then M_1=f(c+d) and the bracket in the displayed phi_1 formula is
 B=min(a,c)+min(a,d)+min(a,c+d)+f(c)+f(d).
The function f is increasing,1-Lipschitz and has range[0,a]. Consequently
 f(c+d)-f(d)<=min(a,c), f(c+d)-f(c)<=min(a,d),
and min(a,c+d)>=f(c+d). Adding gives B>=3M_1, so
 phi_1=B/12+M_1/4>=M_1/2>=H_1/2.
Relabeling proves every coordinate. This bound can be strict: at(2,1,1,1),
H_1=0 but M_1=1. At(1,1,1,1), H_1=1 and phi_1=1/2, so1/2 is sharp.
The proof never substitutes the removal marginal for the definition of H.

LEMMA3. OWN-CAPACITY MONOTONICITY.
For a predecessor set U not containing i, v(U) is independent of c_i, and
v(U union{i}) is nondecreasing in c_i. Every Shapley marginal and their fixed
weighted average are nondecreasing. Multiplication by the CONSTANT6/5 preserves
this property. This established argument is also Proposition1 of Huang et al2025.
A profile-dependent normalization would need a new monotonicity proof.

CONCLUSION AND BOUNDARY
Lemma1 gives coalition coverage for y=(6/5)phi, Lemma2 gives the3H/5 floor,
and Lemma3 gives the required underreport-only truthfulness. Efficiency yields
budget6T/5. Thus the four-owner minimax interval narrows from[1/8,1/2] to[1/8,1/5].
The1/8 lower bound is the prior five-profile calibration and is not improved here.
The20% bound does not improve the arbitrary-owner-count50% theorem without a
separate proof for those larger games. This is progress on the frozen live cell,
not a solution of general depth-3 networks or a field-level breakthrough claim.
