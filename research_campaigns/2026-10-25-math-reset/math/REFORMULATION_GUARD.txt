2. Elementary equivalence guard. For activity arity at most r,
    L/r <= J(L) <= L.
Every one-layer certificate obeys L_piece <= r D_piece by the existing rank charging lemma in inputs/ONE_RESTORATION_LAYER_BARRIER.txt (all coverage rows, isolated profiles, and mixed owners allowed), so summing proves the lower inequality. For the upper inequality, choose any legal positive-output profile p with f=F(B_p)>0 and put grand-coalition coverage lambda_(p,N)=L/f, budget nu_p=L/f, with no comparisons and all other rows zero. Every owner coefficient is nu_p. This is a feasible zero-comparison one-layer certificate with objective L and budget L. Such a profile exists if L>0; if L=0 the zero certificate works. Thus J(L)/D lies between R/r and R. Fixed owners and all coverage requirements are unchanged; this is a dual certificate replacement, not a new primal rule.

Consequently a uniform O(s^alpha) bound on completely unconstrained one-layer replacement cost is equivalent, up to the constant r, to the original O(s^alpha) funding bound. Merely naming a cheap replacement lemma is not a new attack or progress. A useful theorem must specify a transformation or a controlled intermediate invariant and prove its cost from inputs independently of the desired bound.
