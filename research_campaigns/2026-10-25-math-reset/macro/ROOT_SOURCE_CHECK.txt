Decision criterion:
Do not launch a public prediction campaign without archived issue-dated commitment variables connected to compatible later outcomes, enough distinct matured forecast events for a defensible evaluation, and a specific contribution beyond official accuracy reports and existing studies. To claim investment value, also fix capacity lead times and asymmetric early/late investment costs before evaluating decisions. Without those inputs, retain a conditional study design and park empirical expansion. This is a bounded source-access conclusion, not a claim that the needed data exist nowhere.
