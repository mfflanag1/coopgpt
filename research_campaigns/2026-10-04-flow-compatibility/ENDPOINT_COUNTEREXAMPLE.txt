DEFINITIONS.
For the player set N and coalition values v, T=v(N), define
 C={x>=0: x(N)=T, x(A)>=v(A) for every coalition A subset N}.
The frozen ideal is H_e=max_{x in C}x_e. A source-ideal point is one x in C
with x_e=H_e for EVERY source arc e; sink-ideal is defined analogously.
 s_(1/3)=min{y(N)-T: y>=H/3, y(A)>=v(A) for every coalition A}.
The minimum-cut allocation below proves core nonemptiness directly in this game.

NETWORK AND CLAIM.
There are vertices s,u,t and twelve distinct arc players. Five parallel source
arcs s->u have the following capacities; seven parallel sink arcs u->t follow:
  source player   P   A    G    M    B
  capacity        2  12   46   62   75     (total197)
  sink player     Q   R    D    E    F    L    N
  capacity        6  10   16   22   24   56   62  (total196).
All players own exactly one arc. The network is acyclic, all s-t paths have
length2, all capacities are positive integers, and T=196. A coalition with
source set S and sink set R0 has value min(c(S),c(R0)). Parallel arcs were
already permitted in the frozen campaign and its two-hop bundle fixtures.

The ORIGINAL ENTIRE-core ideals are
  H_source=(1,6,23,31,38),      sum99;
  H_sink=(6,10,16,22,24,56,62), sum196.
But max_{x in C} x(all sources)=98. Hence all source ideals cannot be attained
jointly. All sink ideals can be attained jointly. Moreover s_(1/3)=0 for this
same game, so this counterexample refutes the proposed endpoint mechanism only;
it does NOT refute the general C2 subsidy bound.

BASE WEIGHTS AND TWO CORE WITNESSES.
Define baseline source weights a=(1,6,23,31,37), indexed(P,A,G,M,B), and baseline
sink weights b=(3,5,8,11,12,28,31), indexed(Q,R,D,E,F,L,N). Both sums are98.
Source capacity is2*a except B, whose capacity is2*37+1. Sink capacity is2*b.
Let U pay(a;b), and V pay(1,5,23,31,38;b). Both are nonnegative with total196.
Thus V differs from U by transferring one unit from source A to source B.

We first prove a subset-sum fact. Let C0 be the ten players other than A,B.
Their baseline weights are(1,3,5,8,11,12,23,28,31,31), totaling153.
No subset of these weights has sum61 (therefore none has sum92 either).
Proof: set D0=(1,3,5,8,11,12), total40. D0 has no subset of sum2,7,10:
for2 only1 fits; for7 only1,3,5 fit and give0,1,3,4,5,6,8,9; for10 only
1,3,5,8 fit, and either use8 leaving2, or use1,3,5 of total9.
A sum61 uses at most one31. If it uses31, the remainder30 may use28 leaving2,
or23 leaving7, or neither leaving a D0 subset30 whose complement is10. All
are impossible (using both28 and23 exceeds30). Without31, using28 and23 leaves10;
using28 alone leaves33 with D0 complement7; using23 alone leaves38 with D0
complement2; using neither cannot reach61 because D0 totals40. QED.

Consequently, every subset of ALL baseline player weights summing98 contains
A and B either both or neither. A without B would require a C0 subset92;
B without A would require a C0 subset61, both impossible.

ALL-COALITION VERIFICATION OF U,V.
For arbitrary source set S and sink set R0 write d=a(S)-b(R0), an integer,
and delta=1_(B in S). The worth is min(2*a(S)+delta,2*b(R0)).
If d<=-1 the worth equals2*a(S)+delta. U's slack is-d-delta>=0.
If d>=0 the worth equals2*b(R0). U's slack is d>=0.
Therefore U is in the entire core.
V's payment differs by1_(B in S)-1_(A in S). If d<=-1 its slack is
 -d-1_(A in S)>=0; if d>=1 its slack is d+1_(B in S)-1_(A in S)>=0.
If d=0, the subset S union(all sinks minus R0) has baseline weight98. By the
subset-sum fact, A and B have equal membership in S, so V's slack is0.
These cases cover EVERY coalition, including empty/source-only/sink-only ones.
Hence V is in the entire core as well.

EXACT UPPER CERTIFICATES FOR ANY CORE POINT x.
For source i define z_i=c_i-x_i; for sink j define t_j=x_j. Efficiency gives
 sum_sources z - sum_sinks t =197-196=1.                              (E)
For source P, deletion leaves source capacity195 and sink capacity196, so
x_P<=196-195=1 and z_P>=1. All other source z_i are nonnegative by the same
complement argument; sink t_j>=0 by core nonnegativity.
Let theta be the combined vector with theta_i=z_i on sources and theta_j=t_j
on sinks. For compact notation write
 p=theta_P, q=theta_Q, r=theta_R, d0=theta_D, e=theta_E, f=theta_F,
 g=theta_G, l=theta_L, m=theta_M, n=theta_N,
 alpha=theta_A, beta=theta_B.
In particular p>=1. The symbol d0 here is a coordinate, distinct from the
integer coalition difference d in the preceding witness proof.

TIED COALITIONS FORCE LINEAR IDENTITIES.
Whenever a(S)=b(R0), both(S,R0) and its complement have worth equal to their
sink capacity. Those two worths sum196, so efficiency forces both constraints
tight. Thus z(S)-t(R0)=1_(B in S).
For any C0 subset K of baseline weight55, the baseline weight of{A,B} union K
is98. The preceding equality and(E) imply
 theta(K)=k0:=p+g+m,
because the source side itself is{A,B,P,G,M}. Use the following eight weight55
subsets (each can be verified directly from the displayed baseline weights):
  PGM, PGN, PEFM, PQDFM, RDEM, PQGL, PQREFG, PDEFG.
Equating their theta-payments to k0 yields respectively
 m=n; g=e+f; e=q+d0; p+f=r+d0; m=q+l;
 m=q+r+g; m=d0+g.
(The first listed subset PGM defines k0.) It follows by substitution that
 d0=q+r, e=2q+r, f=q+2r-p, g=3q+3r-p,
 l=3q+4r-p, m=n=4q+4r-p, k0=7q+7r-p.                 (I)
The theta-sum on C0 is -4p+19q+20r. Combining(I) with(E) gives
 alpha+beta=1-2p+5q+6r,                                  (II)
 sum_sources z=1-3p+12q+13r.                             (III)

TWO STRICT COALITIONS GIVE q,r LOWER BOUNDS.
If a subset W of ALL baseline players has weight97, set S=W intersect sources
and R0=(all sinks) minus W. Then a(S)-b(R0)=-1. Its actual source capacity is
strictly less than its sink capacity, even if B is present. The core inequality
therefore gives z(S)<=t(R0), or equivalently theta(W)<=sum_sinks t.
For any C0 subset K of baseline weight54, take W={A,B} union K. By(E),
 theta(K)<=k0-1.                                         (L)
Use the two weight54 subsets
 K1={M,E,D,Q,P}, K2={L,F,D,R,P}.
By(I), theta(K1)=8q+6r and theta(K2)=5q+8r-p. Inequality(L) becomes
 r-q>=p+1,              r<=2q-1.
Consequently
 q>=p+2,              r>=2p+3,              p>=1.         (B)

INDIVIDUAL SOURCE IDEALS.
The C0 subset {M,L,P} has baseline weight60. The full subset
W={B,M,L,P} therefore has baseline weight97. Its strict-coalition inequality
is beta+theta({M,L,P})<=sum_sinks t=alpha+beta+k0-1.
Since theta({M,L,P})=k0+r by(I), this implies
 alpha>=r+1>=2p+4>=6.                                   (A)
The C0 subset {M,N} has baseline weight62. The full subset
W={A} union(C0 minus{M,N}) has baseline weight6+(153-62)=97. Its inequality
is alpha+theta(C0)-m-n<=sum_sinks t=alpha+beta+k0-1, giving
 beta>=-p+4q+5r+1>=13p+24>=37.                           (C)
Also(I),(B) give g>=8p+15>=23 and m>=11p+20>=31.
Together with p>=1 these prove
 x_source <= (2-1,12-6,46-23,62-31,75-37)=(1,6,23,31,38).
U attains the first four bounds, and V attains the fifth. Thus these are the
ORIGINAL ENTIRE-core ideals, not merely deletion bounds or Owen-set maxima.
For every sink j, deleting it leaves flow196-c_j, so H_j<=c_j. The all-sink
cut allocation(0_sources,c_sinks) is core and attains all those bounds.

SOURCE SUPPORT AND FAILED JOINT ATTAINMENT.
By(III),(B),
 sum_sources z >=1-3p+12(p+2)+13(2p+3)=35p+64>=99.
Hence every core point has source total at most197-99=98. U and V both attain98.
But the source ideals total99, giving an exact support gap1. Even the two source
ideals A,B are incompatible: (II),(B) imply alpha+beta>=15p+29>=44, so
 x_A+x_B <=12+75-44=43,
whereas H_A+H_B=6+38=44. U and V attain that pair-support bound43 separately.

THE SUBSIDY CONJECTURE SURVIVES THIS EXAMPLE.
U pays(1,6,23,31,37) to sources, dominating(1,6,23,31,38)/3. U pays each sink
half its capacity, dominating H_sink/3. Since U is already efficient and core,
s_(1/3)<=0. The grand-coalition inequality implies s_(1/3)>=0. Thus s_(1/3)=0.
