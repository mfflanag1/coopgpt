AN ACTUAL FLOW EXAMPLE OBSTRUCTING COORDINATEWISE-MAX CLOSURE
Hand-derived by independent Fable attempt; coordinator full verification below.
2026-10-04. No solver or new finite search used. This is an analytical witness,
not an additional member of the eight-instance discovery census.

Network: three parallel source arcs s->u with capacities l=(4,4,3), and two
parallel sink arcs u->t with capacities p=(5,5). One player per arc; DAG;
every s-t path has length2. T=10. Order payoffs as (source y; sink x).
Claim: the source projection of the entire core is not closed under coordinatewise
maximum, although all source ideals ARE jointly attained in this example.

Exact core points:
 U: y=(3,2,1), x=(2,2).
 V: y=(2,3,1), x=(2,2).
 Z: y=(3,3,2), x=(1,1).
Here is a complete coalition certificate for U and Z. For each source subset A,
write L=l(A), Y=U_y(A), W=Z_y(A). A coalition with k sink players has worth
min(L,5k), k=0,1,2. At U its payoff is Y+2k; at Z it is W+k. The table lists
all source subsets and the necessary lower bounds on Y and W for k=1,2.

 A     L   Y   W   min(L,5)-2  min(L,10)-4   min(L,5)-1  min(L,10)-2
 empty 0   0   0       -2          -4           -1          -2
 1     4   3   3        2           0            3           2
 2     4   2   3        2           0            3           2
 3     3   1   2        1          -1            2           1
 12    8   5   6        3           4            4           6
 13    7   4   5        3           3            4           5
 23    7   3   5        3           3            4           5
 123  11   6   8        3           6            4           8

In every row Y dominates its two bounds and W dominates its two bounds.
Coalitions with k=0 have value0 and nonnegative payoff. Sink players are identical
in capacities/payoffs, so k=1 covers both choices. This checks all32 coalitions
(21 have positive value), not just path coalitions. All displayed points are
nonnegative with total10. V follows by the exact symmetry exchanging sources1,2.

Their source-coordinate maximum is J=(3,3,1). If it lifted to any core point,
efficiency would force x_1+x_2=3. But the coalition consisting of source3 and
sink j has value min(3,5)=3, forcing x_j+1>=3, i.e. x_j>=2 for j=1,2. These
constraints contradict x_1+x_2=3. Therefore J has no core lift.

The source coordinate deletion bounds are (3,3,2): removing each source leaves
source capacity7,7,8 respectively, versus total sink capacity10. Z attains all
these bounds, so these are precisely H_source. Sink deletion bounds are5 each,
jointly attained at source payoffs0 and sink payoffs(5,5). Thus the example
refutes ONLY the stronger max-closure assertion, not endpoint compatibility,
and certainly not C2. The extra adjustment from J to Z must increase source3.

Mechanism isolated: two individually feasible source-payoff vectors can require
a third source coordinate to rise when seeking a common dominating core point.
The unit/many-to-one salary lattice cannot simply be asserted for the general
flow source projection. At this stage a directedness theorem (existence of some common
upper point, possibly larger than the coordinatewise maximum) was still missing.
It is now refuted in general by the separate ENDPOINT_COUNTEREXAMPLE.txt.

Additional exact bundle identity from the same attempt. Let P be the short side
with capacities p summing T and L the long side with capacities l summing T+d.
Write core payoff (x on P,y on L), deficit z=p-x, omega=(l,-p), and h=(y,-z).
Then the full core is equivalently described by
 h(N)=0; min(omega(A),0)<=h(A)<=(omega(A)-d)^+ for all A.
The lower inequality is the original coalition constraint after substitution;
the upper inequality is its complementary-coalition form. Thus whenever
 l(S)-d<=p(W)<=l(S), every core point obeys y(S)=z(W).
This window equality is useful exact structure, not a solution of joint maxima.
The independent bounded bundle attempt itself returned UNKNOWN. The subsequent
coordinator construction in ENDPOINT_COUNTEREXAMPLE.txt refutes the general
long-side bundle assertion; this smaller max-closure example does not.

Framing clarification after review: this is an obstruction WITHIN the flow
class, not a claim that non-closure is exclusive to flow games. It does not
challenge the worker-salary lattice used in the one-sided quota theorem: that
lattice is on a different projection. The required general property is existence
of some common upper core point (directedness), weaker than coordinatewise-max
closure. The reviewer suggested a stronger non-closure statement even inside
the proved class, but its scalar one-firm illustration does not establish that
statement; it is not adopted here.
