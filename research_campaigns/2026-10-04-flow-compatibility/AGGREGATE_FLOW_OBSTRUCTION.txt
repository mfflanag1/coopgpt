WHY THE AGGREGATE-CAPACITY RELAXATION DOES NOT PROVE COMPATIBILITY
Coordinator exact derivation, 2026-10-04. Uses the ALREADY registered G01;
no new instance or optimizer run. This is a proof-route obstruction, not a
counterexample to endpoint compatibility.

For a family of coalitions A with nonnegative weights lambda_A, assume every
non-source arc has total coalition coverage at most1. Let w_i be total coverage
of source i and V=sum lambda_A v(A). Pick a maximum flow for each coalition.
Their weighted sum is a flow whose non-source arc usage is at most original
capacity, and whose source i usage is at most c_i*w_i. Thus V is at most the
ordinary maxflow with scaled source capacities. This relaxation is valid but
forgets the fact that a capacity owner must be activated as a whole in each
coalition. We test its adequacy against an existing exact core certificate.

G01 is the TWO-HOP bundle with vertices s,u,t and precisely five arc players:
(s,u,3), (s,u,3), (u,t,2), (u,t,2), (u,t,1), in that order.
There are no middle players or further routing restrictions. Its source
capacities are(3,3), sink capacities(2,2,1), T=5, source ideals(1,1),
and core point z=(1,1;1,1,1), all independently certified in DISCOVERY.json.
Set both source coverages to w_i=9/10 and non-source coverages<=1. Summing the
core inequalities z(A)>=v(A) against lambda gives, for EVERY such family,
  V <= (9/10)*1+(9/10)*1+1+1+1 = 24/5.

But the scaled-capacity relaxation has source capacities(27/10,27/10), with
sinks(2,2,1). Its maximum flow is5: use5/2 on each source and(2,2,1) on sinks.
At u, incoming flow5 equals outgoing flow2+2+1=5, so this explicit vector
is feasible; both source loads5/2 are below27/10. The sink cut is the matching
upper certificate. Hence the relaxation returns
5 >24/5, the actual weighted-coalition upper bound. In this same instance the
endpoint ideal-support inequality would require
  V + sum_i(1-w_i)H_i <= T,
namely V<=24/5. The capacity relaxation alone cannot supply it.

Even capping each source by its maximum achievable flow when it is the only
source does not help: each G01 source alone supplies3, its existing capacity.
A proposed repair must retain information about coalition activation beyond
plain aggregate arc capacity. Defining that information by the entire reduced
core merely restates the target and is not counted as progress.
