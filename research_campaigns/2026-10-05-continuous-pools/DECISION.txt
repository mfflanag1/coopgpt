POSITIVE CONTINUOUS EXTENSION; STOP THIS BOUNDED CAMPAIGN
2026-10-05

The binary-report restriction is removed for the requested two-altered-pool
family. An explicit real-report rule covers all coalitions, is own-monotone,
and meets the original H_i/k floor at total payment <=8T on [1/r,1]. The base
rule costs <=7T. These are TOTAL PAYMENT factors, not subsidy percentages.
They are uniform in the number k of pools, owners r, and report-grid mesh.
The rule also allows capacities in [0,1] at factors 8 and 9 respectively.
No limit from finite grids is needed: the continuous formula is proved directly.

Tradeoff: this is a new rule, with explicitly revised binary boundary payments.
The old rule retains its better base2/fair3 guarantee on binary reports. We do
not prove the old boundary impossible to preserve, nor a sharp constant here.
A loose absolute bound resolves this campaign's existence question; optimizing
it is not necessary to establish that intermediate reports pose no obstruction.

Key mechanism: weight each owner's proportional share by the total deficit of
its OTHER pool members. That weight stays fixed when the owner changes its
report, making own-monotonicity transparent. Uniform payments cover coalitions
when total flow is large relative to r; payments within a minimum-total pool
cover them when flow is small. Only altered pools incur the additional term.

Evidence: conventional all-parameter proof; independent high-effort Fable proof
review with no defects found; a separate verifier review; exact full labeled
checks on fifteen finite grids (including refinements and zero reports).
The code review found an overstated negative-control count; genuine corrupted
payment injections now exercise the rejection paths and pass. This is model
review and reproducible arithmetic, not formal verification or human review.
Freely available originals were inspected; historical novelty is unconfirmed.

Generalization: for any fixed m altered pools, base <=(2m+3)T and fair
<=(2m+4)T on [1/r,1]. Therefore simply adding a third or fourth altered pool
will not establish an unbounded budget lower bound in this family.

Precise stopping point: the same rule pays exactly (3+4k/3)T when r=3 and
all k pools have all reports 1/3. Its dependence on the altered-pool count is
real, not merely slack in the proof. This is a failure of THIS rule only.
The unrestricted constant-versus-divergence question remains open.

Recommended next mathematical direction: remove dependence on the number of
altered pools. A constructive route must share these additional payments
without destroying own-monotonicity or returning to the blocked pool-price
class. A lower-bound route must couple profiles with an unbounded number of
altered pools (or another feature outside this normalized equal-size family).
No new campaign or outreach is launched by this recommendation.
