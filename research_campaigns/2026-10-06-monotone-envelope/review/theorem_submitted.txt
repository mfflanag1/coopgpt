THE OWN-REPORT ENVELOPE ON ALL FINITE DIRECTED ACYCLIC NETWORKS
Proof candidate, 6 October 2026. Independent audit pending.

1. MODEL AND PRECISE CLAIM
G is a finite directed acyclic s-t network with at least one s-t path; every
edge lies on an s-t path. Parallel edges are permitted and retain distinct
fixed original owners. Each owner controls exactly one edge. Capacities c are
arbitrary finite nonnegative reals. Coalition S has value v_c(S), its maximum
s-t flow; T(c)=v_c(E). Reports may only understate capacity, and utility is
payment, with no operating cost. No group, identity, split/merge or overreport
incentive claim is made.

Let C be the report-independent family of inclusion-minimal structural s-t
edge cutsets, K=|C|. Let L be the largest number of edges in an s-t path.
For a structural cut C, let h(C) be the largest number of its edges lying on
one s-t path, and H(G)=sum_(C in C) h(C). These are structural parameters.
They obey H<=KL<=K^2.

For any profile define D_e(c)=max_f f_e over all feasible s-t flows, permitting
any total. Use independent uniform U_e in [0,1], and a fixed order to select
a minimum structural cut W for capacities D_e(c)U_e. Define deterministic
base payments z_e(c)=D_e(c) Pr(e in W), and their own-report envelopes
 y_e(c)=sup_(0<=t<=c_e) z_e(t,c_-e).

CLAIM. At every real report profile y is nonnegative, covers every coalition,
and is nondecreasing in each owner's own report with others fixed. Moreover
 sum_e y_e(c) <= 2 H(G) T(c) <= 2 K L T(c) <= 2 K^2 T(c).       (1)
Thus external subsidy, total payments minus T, is at most (2H-1)T.
This is an owner-independent bound for THIS envelope on ALL finite DAGs.
It is much weaker than the still unproved target C*U(K)T with absolute C and
U(K)~log K/log log K. It does not improve the known L*T budget of the different
marginal-contribution mechanism. No sharpness or historical novelty is claimed.

2. FLOW NORMALIZATION AND ENVELOPE REGULARITY
All feasible flows decompose into nonnegative s-t path flows because G is a
DAG. Hence 0<=D_e<=min(c_e,T). The maximum exists by compactness. Every feasible
coalition flow is a feasible grand-network flow, so capping all edges at their
D_e simultaneously preserves every coalition's ENTIRE feasible flow set.
Consequently v_D(S)=v_c(S), and maxflow(D)=T.

D_h is nondecreasing in every report. For c^t=(t,c_-e), 0<=t<=c_e,
 D_e(c^t)=min(t,D_e(c)).                                      (2)
For the lower bound retain only e-containing paths of a flow attaining D_e,
then scale to total min(t,D_e); every edge flow in it is at most that total.
The reverse bound follows from t and feasible-set inclusion. The same argument
retaining h-containing paths proves the useful, but unused in (1), inequality
 D_h(c^t)>=min(t,D_h(c)) for every h.                          (3)
If t>=D_e(c), all original feasible flows already satisfy f_e<=t. Thus their
feasible set, every D_h, and z are unchanged. The envelope may restrict its
search to 0<=t<=D_e(c).

The rule is well defined even at ties or zero capacities. More explicitly,
for fixed U_-e let A be the minimum cut score excluding the e term among
cuts containing e, and B the minimum score among cuts not containing e,
where B=+infinity if that family is empty. Conditional integration in U_e gives
 z_e(c)=E[min(D_e(c),(B-A)_+)].                               (4)
Here A and B depend on c through all D, unlike in the safe TTSP argument.
When D_e>0 equality of containing/excluding scores has probability zero in
U_e; when D_e=0 both sides are zero. If B=infinity the integrand is D_e.

Each D_h is continuous in c: for h=(a,b),
 D_h=min(c_h,maxflow(s,a),maxflow(b,t)),
with coincident-terminal bounds omitted. Prefix and suffix paths cannot share
a vertex in a DAG; scaled maximum flows can be joined through h. Each maxflow
is a minimum of finitely many cut sums and hence continuous. Formula (4) and
dominated convergence make z_e continuous. Writing t=r*c_e with r in [0,1]
shows the envelope is a maximum over a fixed compact interval of a continuous
function. It is continuous, finite, and the supremum is attained. Attainment
and continuity are not needed for the budget comparison below.

Nonnegativity is immediate, and y>=z because t=c_e is admissible. For any
selected structural cut and any S, D(S intersect W)>=v_D(S)=v_c(S), by the
cut bound in the coalition network. Averaging proves coverage for z and y.
Own-report monotonicity of y follows from nested intervals [0,c_e], with
the same fixed other reports. At T=0 all D, z and y are zero.

3. PATH-INCOMPARABLE OWNERS HAVE INDEPENDENT EFFECTIVE CAPS
Say e precedes h if there is an s-t path containing e before h. On distinct
edges this is a strict partial order: concatenating the intervening paths
in a DAG cannot create repetition; every edge lies on an s-t path. A chain
can be put on one s-t path by the same concatenation argument.

If e and h are incomparable, no s-t path contains both. In computing D_h,
take any maximizing flow and discard its paths not containing h. The retained
flow still attains D_h and uses no e. It is feasible for EVERY report on e,
including zero. Conversely any flow under the changed e report can be treated
in the same way. Therefore D_h is independent of c_e, for all report vectors.

Restrict this edge order to a cut C. Assign each e in C its longest-chain
rank within C (ending at e). Edges of one rank are incomparable. The ranks
partition C into h(C) nonempty antichains A; the equality with the path-based
definition of h(C) follows from the preceding concatenation observation.
For a focal e in such an A, all D_h for h in A without e remain fixed under
every own-report change of e. Edges outside A can change; no independence
claim is made about them.

4. A UNIFORM-SUM BUDGET LEMMA
For independent uniforms and nonnegative weights a_i with a=sum_i a_i,
put X=sum_i a_i U_i. For every b>=0,
 a*Pr(X<=b)<=2b.                                            (5)
If a=0 this is immediate. If a<=2b it follows from probability<=1. Suppose
a>2b. Omit zero weights. The density of X is symmetric about a/2 and
nondecreasing from 0 to a/2, so its CDF F is convex there, with F(0)=0 and
F(a/2)=1/2. The chord bound gives F(b)<=b/a; in fact aF(b)<=b in this case.

Here is a direct justification of the density property. A centered uniform
density is even and nonincreasing on [0,infinity). If f has these properties,
convolution with a centered uniform of width w gives
 g(x)=(1/w) integral_(x-w/2)^(x+w/2) f(v)dv.
It is even, and for x>=0 its a.e. derivative is
 (f(x+w/2)-f(x-w/2))/w<=0,
since |x+w/2|>=|x-w/2|. Induct on the number of positive weights, then translate
the centered sum by a/2. This also covers a single positive uniform weight.
There are no atoms for a>0. This elementary probability lemma is not claimed new.

5. THE KEY COMPARISON USES ONE COMMON PROFILE
Fix c with T>0, one cut C, and one antichain A in its partition. For focal
e in A, take ANY allowed t, and let W^t be the selected cut at c^t. Its score
is at most T(c^t)<=T: a minimum cut for D(c^t) has deterministic sum T(c^t),
and its random score can only be smaller since every U<=1.

Put d=D_e(c^t)<=D_e(c) and S_-e=sum_(h in A without e) D_h(c)U_h. Independence
of effective caps from section 3 is essential here. If W^t=C, dropping the
other nonnegative terms of its score gives S_-e+d U_e<=T. Consequently
 d Pr(W^t=C)
 <= d Pr(S_-e+d U_e<=T)
  = E[min(d,(T-S_-e)_+)]
 <= E[min(D_e(c),(T-S_-e)_+)]
  = D_e(c) Pr(sum_(h in A) D_h(c)U_h<=T).                    (6)
For d=0 or D_e(c)=0 use zero on the corresponding side; (6) still holds.
All quantities on the last line use the SAME original profile c and are
independent of t. The event on the last line is common to every e in A.

For fixed e, z_e(c^t)=sum_(C contains e) D_e(c^t)Pr(W^t=C).
Use (6) in every term BEFORE taking the supremum. There are finitely many
cuts; their antichain partitions are fixed by the graph. Then sum over e
and group by (C,A), obtaining
 sum_e y_e(c)
 <= sum_C sum_(A in its partition)
      [sum_(e in A)D_e(c)] Pr(sum_(h in A)D_h(c)U_h<=T)
 <= 2 sum_C h(C) T = 2H(G)T,                               (7)
by (5). No base budgets from different unilateral profiles were combined.
This is the point at which the possible number of owners disappears.

6. REDUCING THE STRUCTURAL BOUND TO CUT COUNT
Clearly h(C)<=L. Also L<=K. To prove the latter, take any s-t path P. For
each e in P the set (E without P) union {e} cuts every s-t path, because after
its removal only P without e remains. Choose an inclusion-minimal cut C_e
inside it. Since E without P does not cut P, C_e must contain e, and
C_e intersect P={e}. Different e give distinct structural cuts. Therefore
|P|<=K. Apply this to a longest path to obtain H<=KL<=K^2, completing (1).

7. CALIBRATION AND WHAT REMAINS OPEN
On TTSP graphs z is already own-report monotone by the earlier co-cut theorem,
so y=z and the sharper U(K) budget survives exactly. In particular K=1 gives
efficient payments. On the previously certified augmented Wheatstone example,
y_x(x_high,c_-x)>=z_x(x_low,c_-x)>z_x(x_high,c_-x); the problematic underreport
is necessarily repaired. The two envelope values need not coincide; their
monotonic ordering follows from the definition for ALL reports.

The coarse proof discards (i) score contributions outside one antichain and
(ii) competition between different cuts. Replacing the sum over cuts by the
earlier small-ball tail argument would require a single selected random cut
at a common profile; the separate own-report suprema do not supply that object.
No obstruction to a C*U(K) bound has been proved. The sharp target therefore
remains open, even if the present cut-count-only bound is verified.

Because y is the pointwise LEAST own-report-monotone majorant of z (with others
fixed), any own-report-monotone rule p satisfying p>=z everywhere also satisfies
p>=y everywhere: p_e(c)>=p_e(t,c_-e)>=z_e(t,c_-e), then take sup. Thus an eventual
large lower bound for y would obstruct every pointwise-majorant repair of z,
not all truthful coalition-covering mechanisms. This is an elementary envelope
fact, not a new impossibility result.

The classical mincut/flow facts, elementary monotone-envelope facts, uniform
convolution lemma, and prior continuous Banzhaf functional are established
ingredients. The proposed contribution is the antichain comparison (6)-(7)
for this effective-capacity payment envelope. Historical novelty, formal
verification and human specialist review remain unestablished.
