OWN-REPORT MONOTONE ENVELOPE — RESEARCH CONTRACT
6 October2026

Fixed finite directed acyclic s-t network, every edge on an s-t path, one
fixed original owner per edge. Arbitrary nonnegative real capacity reports.
v_c(S)=coalition maxflow, T=v_c(E). K is the number of inclusion-minimal
structural s-t edge cutsets, independent of reports. All network and owner
sizes may grow. Binding capacity underreports, no operating costs.

D_e(c)=maximum feasible s-t flow through e, at any feasible total flow.
z_e(c)=D_e(c)*Pr[e belongs to the selected minimum cut for capacities D_h U_h],
with independent uniform U_h and a fixed tie order. Equivalently use the
absolute continuous Banzhaf endpoint integral after this normalization.
The base z covers every coalition and costs <=U(K)T for all DAGs, but it is
not own-report monotone in general (proved Wheatstone counterexample).

Target y_e(c)=sup_(0<=t<=c_e) z_e(t,c_-e).
Nonnegativity, own-report monotonicity and coalition coverage are immediate.
Finite because z_e(t,c_-e)<=D_e(t,c_-e)<=D_e(c)<=T(c). The open obligation is
a budget bound depending ONLY on K, independent of original owner count.
Primary ambitious gate: C*U(K)T with absolute C, where U(K) is asymptotic
to log K/log log K. A weaker universal bound must be labeled as weaker;
a finite-family bound or unverified null search is not sufficient.

Two-sided outcome: a conventional general theorem with independent audit and
exact checks, or an exact obstruction to an explicitly stated proposed bound.
Suprema occur at different actual unilateral report profiles. Do not sum their
separate profile budget inequalities as if they held at one common profile.
No comparisons of unchanged owners' payments, changing owner identities,
strategic cloning, or assumption that grand flow stays constant.

Calibration: y=z on the previously proved TTSP class; on the Wheatstone
counterexample, the envelope must remove the observed underreporting gain.
Known marginal payments have factor at most longest s-t path length; do not
mistake an elementary baseline mechanism for a bound on this envelope.
Novelty, human review and formal validation are not assumed. No outreach or
manuscript changes during proof discovery. A proposed next target is not an
authorization to silently run an additional multi-hour campaign.
