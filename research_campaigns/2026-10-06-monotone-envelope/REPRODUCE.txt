Run from the repository root, using Python 3 and only its standard library:

python3 research_campaigns/2026-10-06-monotone-envelope/verification/verify.py

The verifier is self-contained and can also run after copying it elsewhere.
It prints JSON. Use --output /tmp/envelope-check.json to save another record.
No package installation, network access, numerical solver, or random sampling
of payment probabilities is needed. Random profile choices use a fixed seed;
all arithmetic and conditional integrals are exact rational values.

203 graph cases: all relevant forward-edge simple DAGs on 2-5 labelled vertices
(isolated internal vertices permitted), one parallel-edge bridge, and 24 fixed-
seed six-vertex DAG cases. There are 609 capacity profiles, including all zero
and all unit. Smaller coalition sets are exhaustive; larger ones use fixed-seed
subsets. Uniform CDFs use exact inclusion-exclusion; independently integrated
stop-loss formulas verify the group identity. Main conditional domination is
checked at rational uniform coordinates, including endpoints/ties.

The all-parameter proof is THEOREM.txt. These finite checks do not prove its
universal quantifiers, compute every continuous envelope, establish the sharp
asymptotic target, or certify novelty. The previous Wheatstone rational gain
is recalculated only for calibration; its full proof is in the prior campaign.

MANIFEST.json stores SHA-256 for all files in this directory except itself.
