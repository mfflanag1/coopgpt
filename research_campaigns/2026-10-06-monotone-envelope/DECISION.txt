OUTCOME AND NEXT MATHEMATICAL GATE
6 October 2026

PROVED IN THIS ATTEMPT
The own-report envelope y of the effective-capacity cut/Banzhaf rule is an
all-profile truthful, coalition-covering mechanism on every fixed finite DAG,
with one fixed owner per edge and arbitrary nonnegative real capacity reports.
Its total payment is at most 2H(G)T<=2KLT<=2K^2T, independent of owner count.
The proof allows owner count, cut count and network size to grow, including
interacting Wheatstone structures. K counts structural minimal cutsets.

The new budget argument uses antichains of edges inside each structural cut.
Changing one antichain edge's report cannot change the effective capacities of
the others. Conditional integration then dominates every unilateral profile
by quantities at the same original profile. This resolves the cross-profile
summation problem for a coarse bound.

VALIDATION
Independent deep Fable proof audit found no invalid step; minor explanations
were added and checked by the coordinator. Exact standard-library checks cover
203 graphs and 609 profiles, including zeros, parallel edges, and overlapping
cuts. These finite checks support implementation and algebra; the conventional
argument supplies the theorem for all graphs/profiles. See review/ADJUDICATION.txt.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
The ambitious C*U(K)T envelope bound, U(K)~log K/log log K, is unresolved.
No counterexample to that bound was found or certified. The theorem is weaker
than the known L*T marginal-contribution mechanism as an existence/budget
result, so it should not be represented as a better general subsidy mechanism
or a field-level breakthrough. It settles the literal owner-independent
boundedness question for this particular monotone envelope.

EXACT DIAGNOSTIC
PROOF_LIMIT.txt shows that the intermediate sum of light-cut probabilities
overpays by a factor K on a simple K-edge path whose actual envelope is
efficient. It also shows the scalar uniform-sum constant 2 is optimal. Thus
improving scalar concentration alone cannot bring this proof to C*U(K).
This is a proved obstruction to the relaxation, not to the envelope.
