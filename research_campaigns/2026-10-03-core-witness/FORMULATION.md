# Fixed-support formulation and obligations

Only full triples are productive; coalition worth is the maximum integral disjoint-triple sum. Let m>=1, P a sector-wise player-disjoint partial matching on labels 0..m-1, U its unmatched players. Allowed pair positions E are the union of pairs of diagonal triples and P. Variables w_e>=0, with diagonal total1. For each i in U, x^i>=0 has total1 and covers every full triple with worth equal to its supported pair sum. Maximize J=w(P)+sum_{i in U}x^i_i/3-1.

A witness exists whenever U is nonempty. Then diagonal matching and the total-one cover force grand worth1 and a nonempty original core. Since nonnegative coordinates sum1, every coordinate is at most1. Each allowed edge lies in a full triple covered by any witness, so every w_e<=1. Thus the feasible set is compact. It is nonempty by placing all weight on diagonal pairs and splitting each diagonal pair entry between that pair's two endpoints. For fixed admissible weights, witnesses are independent and each positively weighted coordinate can reach its core maximum; hence the LP maximum equals the all-weight supremum of J(M,P) on the named support. Covering full triples and singleton nonnegativity covers every integral coalition matching.

Edge case: U empty (P perfect) needs one additional core witness, even though it is absent from the objective. Otherwise diagonal normalization alone would not certify diagonal optimality or nonempty core. Our live registry has |P|<m, but the new generic generator must handle this case or reject it explicitly. Empty P is allowed for calibration with all players unmatched.

For dual certificates, use max c*z with c=3 times the unshifted objective, inequalities A*z<=0 (team weight minus witness payoff), equalities B*z=1 (diagonal total then each witness total), and box0<=z<=1. A certificate has lambda>=0, free nu, with residual r=c-A^T lambda-B^T nu. The bound is (sum nu+sum max(r_j,0))/3-1. This residual correction follows valid variable upper bounds and can be checked exactly even after numerical dual rationalization. It is an upper bound, not necessarily optimal. A primal witness establishes a lower bound; equality proves a fixed-support optimum. An exact feasible J>1/3 suffices to refute S3=1/3 via the fixed-P subsidy lower bound. A fixed-support upper does not upper-bound other packings in that same market.

The solver may omit triples of zero supported worth; the independent checker reconstructs all triples. The generator and checker must not share layout or coefficient-building code. Unknown, timeout, incomplete inventory and failed rational reconstruction remain unresolved. All malformed-data checks must survive python -O.

For any protected allocation y, summing the disjoint P-team inequalities and the frozen floors on U gives total y >= w(P)+sum_U H_i/3. Hence s>=J.
