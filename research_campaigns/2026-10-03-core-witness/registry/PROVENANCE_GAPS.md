# Retained-history provenance limits

The frozen supports are not equivalent to any support recoverable from the frozen retained-artifact inventory. This is not an absolute never-tried claim. Earlier best-only numerical logs and alternating searches did not retain every intermediate support; absent states cannot be reconstructed. The plan's stronger “genuinely untested” requirement is therefore unresolved in that literal sense and must not be silently certified.

The scan recursively reads JSON, JSONL, parseable complete JSON logfile lines, and matching archive members under sharp_subsidy. It records source bytes and SHA-256 values, schema paths, and accepted market-size inference. It does not execute discovery scripts. Python-source-only parameterized support generators and truncated/non-JSON log fragments are outside extraction; nine ZIP containers are hashed even when they hold only scripts. The probe records carry H, so their size is reconstructed from len(H)/3. Auxiliary deletion packings are omitted; weighted dual/packing fields are retained only when their positive support is an actual player-disjoint matching. Skipped recognized support fields are listed in the inventory.

All 34 CYCLIC_FRONTIER records and transformed copies must be rejected by the exact sector-coloured multigraph isomorphism filter. Canonical forms for the selected supports are generated by a separate exhaustive row/sector permutation construction. Fixed safe patterns are rejected through the inventory, the at-most-three-leaves theorem and uniform-template count theorem are applied to cyclic and repeated-label supports, and all incidence trees are conservatively excluded. This does not establish completeness of the collection of proven safe classes.

The deterministic generator inspects a bounded BFS pool and 500 seventh-team augmentations and selects at most six supports. If two r=7 candidates survive, four r=6 and two r=7 supports are selected, with one of each held out. Neither LP objectives nor numerical feasibility results are used for candidate generation or ranking. The final two positions are held out before solves. Registry files refuse overwrite; any later change requires a new explicitly versioned registry and disclosure.
