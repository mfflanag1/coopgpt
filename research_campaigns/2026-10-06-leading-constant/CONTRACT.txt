EXACT TARGET
For each k>=2 let B_k be the supremum over all finite fixed k-pool ownership
partitions of the infimum all-profile payment factor, over deterministic,
nonnegative coalition-covering rules whose original owners have nondecreasing
payments in their own nonnegative real capacity reports. Ownership is fixed
within a rule, but owner counts and pool counts both grow across instances.
Binding underreports, utility equal to payment, no use costs; no proportional,
anonymity, population, splitting/merging, or group-report axioms.

Known: (1/2-o(1)) log k/log log k <= B_k <= (1+o(1)) log k/log log k.
Find the sharp leading coefficient, or first prove its limit exists. Do not
assume a limit or either endpoint is correct. An intermediate theorem must
strictly improve a general asymptotic bound, or give a structural lemma with
an explicit demonstrated implication for this problem. A bounded-family
construction or finite-factor refinement alone does not meet that gate.
