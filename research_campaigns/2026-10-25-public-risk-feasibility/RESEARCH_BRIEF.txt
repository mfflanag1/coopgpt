WHAT IS ACTUALLY OBSERVABLE
Form N-PORT contains fund/series identifiers, reference dates, net assets, holdings, and paired interest-rate metrics for five maturity nodes. The development examples have USD currency entries. Compare the two reported metrics within one fund and node; preserve signs. A positive number must not automatically be interpreted as a gain on an upward rate move. Neither reported metric is an independently validated realization of a shock. The two values alone do not establish true convexity, mispricing, a reporting violation or forecasting error.

For fund f and node k, define r_fk = DV100_fk - 100 DV01_fk.
Report r in filed units, abs(r)/(100 abs(DV01)) when the denominator is nonzero, and 10000 abs(r)/NAV when the units are compatible USD amounts. Also report max_k abs(r) and sum_k abs(r). The latter is a vector-discrepancy summary, NOT a joint or parallel-shock loss. Node responses do not identify nonlinear interactions between simultaneous curve moves. No fund-to-fund cash netting, loan, coalition, withdrawal right or sponsor is inferred.

EXECUTED DEVELOPMENT CHECK
Two conveniently discoverable filings were inspected before designing the proposed panel gate. They are development examples, not a random sample or holdout. The dates and fund types differ; no cross-fund performance comparison is made.

1. US Treasury 12 Month Bill ETF, series S000077173, reference date 2025-11-30. NAV 302757037.95. USD DV01 at 3m/1y is 135.696611 / 29417.927858; DV100 is 13569.661160 / 2941792.785855; the other three nodes are zero. The sum of absolute node residuals is 0.000115 filed dollars, effectively immaterial at fund scale. This is not proof the valuation is linear: the disclosed large-shock metric could itself be a scaled approximation. The small residual is consistent with upstream numerical/reporting precision, but trailing decimal zeros are not evidence of the model's true precision.

2. Gladstone Alternative Income Fund, CIK 0002026098, reference date 2024-12-31. NAV 20237340.35. At 3m, reported DV01=10 and DV100=550; all other nodes are zero. The arithmetic residual is -450: 45% of the scaled small-shock number, but only about 0.22236 basis points of NAV (0.0022236%). A large relative discrepancy alone is a poor materiality screen. Its cause is unresolved. The filing's series ID is blank; do not invent one. This standalone fund is outside the proposed series-ID primary panel anyway.

The first example is a transparent manual transcription from the SEC-rendered original, with exact URL and fields. The second is parsed independently from a preserved issuer-hosted filing, SHA256 recorded in raw/gladstone_access.json. verify_examples.py checks the parsed fields against the recorded transcription, verifies assets minus liabilities equals NAV, and computes both decimal and rational arithmetic through separate paths in the same verifier. Fixed expected-output assertions are regression checks, not an independent valuation model. No full panel was obtained.

PRIORITY AND OVERLAP
The SEC's 2026 proposed rule expressly discusses the paired measures as information about convexity. It also documents reporting-currency inconsistency. We use it as evidence about measurement and prior motivation, not a statement that its proposals became law. Historical filing semantics are checked against the reference form and actual filings.

Choi, Kim and Randall's Hidden Duration already studies fixed-income funds' interest-rate derivatives and exposures. The freely accessible December 2024 manuscript uses N-PORT with CRSP/Morningstar; the March 2026 SSRN abstract indicates a revised study. Only the older full manuscript and newer abstract were inspected in this bounded pass; the latest full paper remains an explicit priority-comparison item. A search returning no exact phrase would not establish novelty.

The IMF October 2025 GFSR Annex 1.4 is a relevant existing stress benchmark. Its fund sample and flow shocks combine N-PORT and Lipper. Its derivative shocks are linearized and derivative-specific; portfolio-wide N-PORT metrics cannot be substituted for margin calls. It already distinguishes gross forced sales from purchases by other funds. A public-only replacement would be an adapted replication, not a full replication and not a newly discovered aggregation insight.
