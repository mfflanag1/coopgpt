M100 APPLICATION FEASIBILITY GATE — 21 OCTOBER 2026 UTC

Decision

Stop this M100 reserve-performance route at the feasibility gate. No adequate
historical measurement/state contract was established, and the checked readers
cannot acquire even the fixed June candidate within the remaining byte cap.
This does not reject the economic idea or prove that no alternative data source
or extraction technology can work. It rules out proceeding to a reserve/pooling
claim from this evidence and acquisition plan.

What this gate added

1. Replayed the exact acquisition plan independently. The complete-column-chunk
   plan needs 107,625,469 NEW response-body bytes, versus 6,393,615 remaining.
   Timestamp chunks alone cost 33,114,069. Omitting all four extra state columns
   saves only 81,037. These costs are conditional on the retained metadata and
   on reading the declared columns/chunks, not universal information bounds.

2. Inspected the already retained footers through a structural projection.
   All 4,176 column chunks across the three members declare one data page each,
   including all 3,429 selected chunks. No standard ColumnIndex or OffsetIndex
   is advertised. Thus a complete-data-page reader has no smaller page partition
   within these chunks. Custom partial compressed-stream decoding, external
   indexes, a separately validated representation, or a server-produced extract
   remain outside this result. Page headers and observation values were not read.

3. Verified 325 cached tar headers, with an unbroken ordinary-header chain to
   the first zero terminator: 159 regular members. No Logics or Schneider member
   occurs in that chain. The archive is 5,222,973,440 bytes; the first zero header
   begins at 5,222,971,392, leaving 1,536 subsequent bytes uninspected. No claim
   about nonstandard concatenated contents there is made. The enumerated archive
   supplies no identified facility-quality feed. Other months were not selected
   as replacements. Unsupported extended/PAX header types are rejected.

4. Reviewed primary documentation and pinned source code. The measurement
   description establishes PSU input watts and nominal 20-second sampling;
   Nagios records describe monitoring/scheduler conditions, not a documented
   physical power-off classification. Accuracy, internal sensor averaging,
   historical collection delay, and validity between samples remain unresolved.
   See SEMANTICS_AUDIT.txt for the exact source boundaries and review corrections.

5. Located and inspected public IPMI publisher code published in 2025, but found
   no established link to the 2020 deployment. It is contextual evidence, not
   authority for historical emission or missing-value semantics. Independent QA
   rejected the delegated report's stronger claims; those claims are not adopted.

What remains untouched or unestablished

The fixed population is still 980 nodes. April was exposed and all May is
calibration. June 1–14 remains a metadata-inspected candidate, not a selected,
complete, or evaluated holdout. No June power values, node dictionaries, state
codes, or outcome statistics were selected or emitted by this audit. Raw cached
footers do contain statistics; the parser traverses and discards unselected
fields. Selected chunks extend beyond June 14, so future full decoding would
require an explicit exposure plan for the other dates too.

No new dataset response bodies were acquired: consumption remains 93,606,385
of 100,000,000 bytes. Source/documentation access is separate. Locally logged
documentation bodies are metered, while model web-fetch traffic is not measured;
the local ledger must not be represented as total web traffic.

The 14-day training plus 14-day holdout design has NOT become feasible. Ruling
out its current June component is sufficient to stop the current plan; this
audit does not certify any complete training panel. Existing May missingness
results were inherited, not recomputed or promoted to performance evidence.

Physical telemetry also does not establish independent economic owners, firm
capacity rights, feasible joint withdrawals, dispatch authority, service costs,
failure liability, or sponsor funding. AI workload identity is unestablished.
No coalition theorem has been transferred to Gaussian reserve production.

Next decision

Do not repeat this gate using more scheduler-status joins or a smaller favorable
subsample. Reopen the application lane when an operator-grade measurement and
rights package satisfies OPERATOR_DATA_SPEC.txt, or after an explicit redesign
around a documented alternative dataset and institution. Increasing the download
cap alone would leave the measurement and economic gaps open.

The mathematical lane can continue independently through specialist review of
the coalition paper and its unrestricted frontier. This gate adds a reproducible
application feasibility result, not a new theorem or an external review. The
manuscript, repository, and review outreach were not changed by this campaign.

Reproduction

tests, exact header inventory and review adjudications are supplied. All results
