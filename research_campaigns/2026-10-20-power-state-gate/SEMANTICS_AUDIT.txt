S1 — What is measured
Source: documentation/plugins/ipmi.md, metric table psX_input_power.
https://gitlab.com/ecs-lab/exadata/-/blob/db994a39cb256da3d623fbfe28257a4531db075f/documentation/plugins/ipmi.md
Supported: each of two PSU input-power channels is reported in watts with
nominal 20-second cadence. Not supplied: accuracy, freshness, internal averaging,
or an off-versus-failed-reading classifier. total_power is a separately defined
channel; equality to the AC PSU sum is not established by these descriptions.

S2 — What state means
Source: documentation/plugins/nagios.md, column and description tables.
https://gitlab.com/ecs-lab/exadata/-/blob/db994a39cb256da3d623fbfe28257a4531db075f/documentation/plugins/nagios.md
Supported: alive::ping concerns reachability; batchs::client::state concerns
scheduler health. state_type distinguishes SOFT/HARD, not host/service checks.
Host and service numeric encodings differ; the description-to-check-type mapping
is not supplied. Sampling is nominally 15 minutes. Neither an administrative
drain nor failed reachability identifies deenergization. No between-record
validity or carry-forward policy was established. This is an evidence gap, not
a theorem that physical states cannot persist or that no further source exists.

S3 — Timestamp transformation
Sources: ipmi_logic.py and nagios_logic.py in parquet_dataset/csv_to_parquet/plugins/;
Scientific Data DOI 10.1038/s41597-023-02174-3, Data Processing.
https://cris.unibo.it/retrieve/d318f3a4-89f2-48f2-84cb-4cb824ebc6a3/versione_editoriale.pdf
Published converters truncate timestamps to seconds and use UTC-aware output
types. Exact timestamp equality therefore is not proof of simultaneous physical
conversion. Conversion code alone cannot identify upstream averaging or lag.
The article's account of monitoring gaps reinforces keeping missingness explicit;
it supplies no validated zero/off substitution or duration guarantee.

S5 — What the located collector does, and why it is not historical proof
https://github.com/ExamonHPC/ipmi_pub/blob/50d542ff64e803deb4046040707c1f2ef6c6e1ce/ipmi_pub.py
The inspected implementation stamps before running the sensor command, parses
returned readings, and has no explicit time-averaging accumulator in that loop.
Unparsable sensors are skipped. A nonzero command return does NOT force empty
output: run_cmd logs and returns whatever stdout was obtained. Thus even for
this implementation the blanket claim that every read failure emits nothing
is too strong. Its March 2025 public commit history and TS=10 example do not
identify the May/June 2020 deployment. Firmware behavior also remains unknown.
No point-sample/instantaneous-power contract is inferred for the historical data.

S6 — Search scope and provenance
The Exadata IPMI documentation history contains a February 2023 initial commit.
The located publisher has three public March 2025 commits. A complete current
Examon tree (af6886b4dda88192365998324612cb47fb7844c4, 1,468 entries, not
truncated) was inspected for ipmi/nagios/publisher paths; no historical collector
match was established. This is a finite search, not proof of global nonexistence
or that code first originated at its public publication date. We did not inspect
every historical branch or private CINECA system.

S8 — Structural format
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apache/parquet-format/apache-parquet-format-2.9.0/src/main/thrift/parquet.thrift
ColumnMetaData field 13 contains page-type/encoding counts; data page types are
0 and 3. ColumnChunk fields 4–7 advertise the standard offset/column indexes.
Statistics field 12 is unselected. The parser is a bounded audit projection,
not a general certified Parquet implementation. It relies on accurate footer
declarations; actual page headers were not inspected. The independent audit
and synthetic fixtures test selected field IDs, page-count distinctions,
skipping, corruption refusal, and equality with inherited structural metadata.

Adjudication

The delegated semantics report is REJECTED AS WRITTEN, with useful source leads
retained after verification. Opposite-family Codex QA agreed that its historical
collector, sampling, persistence and negative-search claims required revision.
Our final statements keep E07/E08/P01 unresolved. An example's exact-time join
does not disprove persistence; dropping a textual field does not prove loss of
nonredundant information; a present row does not prove an immediate BMC response.
The delegate's isolated workspace lacked inherited docs by design; the parent
has retained and hash-verified the pertinent source copies. Neither model's
literature opinion establishes physical truth or exhaustive absence.
