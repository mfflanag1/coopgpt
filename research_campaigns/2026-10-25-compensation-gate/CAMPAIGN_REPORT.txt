BOUNDED COMPENSATION ATTEMPT — DECISION
25 October 2026

Outcome: STOP the irreversible compensation candidate. No general upper or
lower bound improved, and no new paper or field-level advance is claimed.

What was attempted:
Select efficient production prices, retain their coalition-covering charges,
and add the least nonnegative reserve that remembers every own-charge loss
along all monotone report paths. This specifies a direct report-dependent rule
at the individual-incentive gate. If finite it covers coalitions and preserves
individual payments under restoration. It is not asserted coalition-truthful.

What failed:
With just two fixed pure owners and F(a,b)=min(a,b), increasingly long alternating
restoration paths fit inside the same [0,2]^2 report box. Unique efficient prices
switch their entire charge from one owner to the other. Every switch forces
compensation. If individual reserves can never be released, the exact best
funding factor on an L-step finite chain is 1+3L/4, hence no finite factor works
on the whole box. The attempted envelope is infinite at (2,2).

Why this does not lower-bound the original problem:
On that same whole box, y=(F,0) attains the optimal unrestricted fullOUT factor 1.
With efficient-price domination, y=(F,F) attains the optimal factor 2 by releasing
and reassigning reserves. Both are elementary benchmark rules. The impossible
extra restriction was retaining each owner's reserve through all restorations.

Additional necessary condition:
Any finite-budget individually truthful rule with nonnegative reserves above
efficient prices must release at least max(0,W_L-(K-1)F_L) cumulative reserve
along the witness. This release occurs on owners passive during the individual
restoration. It becomes arbitrarily large although output and total resource
movement remain bounded. Payments themselves need not be discontinuous.
