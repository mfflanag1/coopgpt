IRREVERSIBLE COMPENSATION FAILS ON A TWO-OWNER MINIMUM GAME
25 October 2026 — conventional proof; computational replay is supplementary.
No novelty, general frontier improvement, or specialist approval is claimed.

1. Frozen model and the candidate

Two actual owners have fixed endowments bar b_1=(2,0), bar b_2=(0,2).
Their full original report box is (a,b) in [0,2]^2; reports are vectors (a,0),
(0,b). One activity consumes one of each resource, earns one, and has value
F(a,b)=min(a,b). This is arity two and thus within the arity-at-most-three class.

For every profile choose any efficient nonnegative feasible dual price p:
p_1+p_2 >= 1 and a p_1+b p_2=F(a,b). Let u=(a p_1,b p_2).
An efficient-price-floor rule has y_i=u_i+rho_i, rho_i>=0.
It covers all coalitions: u(S)=p dot b_S >= F(b_S), by weak duality.
Funding factor K means y_1+y_2 <= K F at every profile, equivalently
R=rho_1+rho_2 <= (K-1)F. This factor counts TOTAL payments, not just reserve.
Individual incentives mean y_i cannot fall when only owner i restores resources.
FullOUT additionally requires y_C(h)-y_C(l)>=F(B_h-B_l) for every legal C;
our negative result already follows from the weaker individual condition.

The attempted irreversible rule keeps each owner's reserve nondecreasing when
ANY owner restores resources. This is an extra design restriction, NOT part of
the research problem. In particular it forbids releasing a passive owner's
reserve to compensate the owner whose efficient charge just fell.

A precise candidate is the path envelope
  R_i^*(b) = sup over finite coordinatewise increasing single-owner paths
             from 0 to b of sum over own-i edges (u_i(low)-u_i(high))_+.
Every edge may change any subset of the restoring owner's coordinates.
If finite, R_i^* is nonnegative and nondecreasing in every report coordinate.
Appending an own-i edge yields
  R_i^*(h)-R_i^*(l) >= (u_i(l)-u_i(h))_+,
so u_i+R_i^* is individually truthful. It is the least nonnegative reserve
satisfying both these conditions: along any such path every eligible reserve
must accumulate every displayed positive own-charge loss. Conversely the
supremum satisfies the difference inequalities when finite.
Coalition truthfulness has NOT been proved for this formula. It fails the
necessary finite-budget gate first: R_i^*(2,2)=infinity for both owners.
Prices at zero and equal-resource profiles can be chosen arbitrarily; the
witness below only uses strictly positive off-diagonal profiles.

2. Exact staircase witness

For any integer L>=1 put delta=1/(L+2). Define L+1 profiles q_0,...,q_L:
  q_(2j)   = (1+2j delta,     1+(2j+1)delta),
  q_(2j+1) = (1+(2j+2)delta, 1+(2j+1)delta).
Only indices at most L are used. Every coordinate lies strictly between 0 and 2.
The step q_(k-1)->q_k restores owner 1 if k is odd and owner 2 if k is even.
It restores exactly 2 delta of that owner's resource; all outsiders are fixed.
Both owners and their endowment boxes are the same for every L.

Write F_k=1+k delta. At q_k the smaller resource is unique. Efficient prices
are consequently unique: (1,0) if a<b and (0,1) if b<a. The restoring owner
has charge F_(k-1) before the step and zero afterwards. Individual incentives
therefore FORCE
  rho_i(q_k)-rho_i(q_(k-1)) >= F_(k-1)                 (1)
for the restoring owner i. Under irreversibility, the other reserve cannot fall.
Summing (1) gives
  R(q_L) >= R(q_0)+W_L >= W_L,
  W_L = sum_(k=1)^L F_(k-1) = L + delta L(L-1)/2.
The funding bound then implies
  K >= 1 + W_L/F_L.
For delta=1/(L+2), this quantity is
  1 + 3L(L+1)/[4(L+1)] = 1 + 3L/4.
Thus no finite K works on the single original [0,2]^2 box. This is not an
owner-count lower bound: both the technology and the TWO owners stay fixed.
There are arbitrarily many legal report changes inside that one box.

The claimed simplification uses W_L=3L(L+1)/(2(L+2)) and
F_L=2(L+1)/(L+2). Check these identities exactly in the replay.

This also proves divergence of the attempted path envelope. Start at zero,
reach q_0 using two single-owner steps, follow the staircase, then restore to
(2,2). Initial and final path costs are nonnegative. Each owner incurs at least
floor(L/2) units of its own positive losses. Taking L unbounded gives infinity.
This is a supremum defining a static payment rule, not an assumption that
owners actually traverse these reports over time.

3. Finite-chain bound is attained within the restricted class

On Q_L={q_0,...,q_L}, set rho(q_0)=0. At step k add F_(k-1) to the restoring
owner's reserve and change no other reserve. Set y=u+rho. All reserves are
nonnegative and nondecreasing along Q_L. On an adjacent step the restoring
owner's y is unchanged; the passive owner's y increases from rho_j to
rho_j+F_k. Nonadjacent pairs change both owners. All single-owner comparisons
on Q_L are thus adjacent, and whole-pair fullOUT holds because
  y_N(q_k)-y_N(q_j) = F_k-F_j + W_k-W_j >= F(q_k-q_j).
Here F_k-F_j>=F(q_k-q_j) follows directly from superadditivity of min.
Singleton coalition values are zero; grand coverage holds.
For prefixes W_k=sum_(t=0)^(k-1)F_t, the ratios 1+W_k/F_k increase with k:
  W_(k+1)F_k-W_k F_(k+1)=F_k^2-delta W_k>0,
since W_k<=k F_k and F_k-k delta=1. Therefore the exact minimum funding factor
under the irreversible reserve restriction on this finite profile system is
  K_L=1+3L/4.
This finite attainment is NOT an extension theorem to the full report box.

4. Quantitative reserve release is unavoidable

Now DROP irreversibility. Assume only rho>=0, efficient prices, individual
truthfulness and some finite budget factor K. On the same staircase define
  V^+ = sum_(k,i) max(rho_i(q_k)-rho_i(q_(k-1)),0),
  V^- = sum_(k,i) max(rho_i(q_(k-1))-rho_i(q_k),0).
Equation (1) still holds, so V^+>=W_L. The telescoping identity
V^+-V^-=R(q_L)-R(q_0), together with R(q_L)<=(K-1)F_L and R(q_0)>=0,
gives
  V^- >= max(0, W_L-(K-1)F_L).                       (2)
All negative increments must be on the passive owner, because (1) forces a
positive increment on the restoring owner. Hence bounded funding requires
arbitrarily large cumulative RELEASE of passive-owner reserves along these
monotone paths, even though F stays below 2 and total coordinate change is below 2.
No uniform finite bound on this negative variation, or bound of the form
V^-<=M times total output growth, is possible for the whole box.
This does not require discontinuous payments. The instability is in the
selected efficient charges and their offsetting reserve, as the next example shows.

5. The game itself is easy: exact benchmark comparisons

(a) Without a price floor, y=(F,0) has factor 1 on the ENTIRE [0,2]^2 box.
Singleton coalition production is zero. Grand coverage is equality. Restoring
owner 1 cannot lower F; restoring owner 2 leaves its payment zero. For the pair,
F(h)-F(l)>=F(h-l) by superadditivity. Thus fullOUT holds for every allowable
coalition, including a passive member. Grand coverage also proves factor 1 optimal.

(b) With the efficient-price floor but allowing reserve releases, y=(F,F)
has factor 2, full coverage and fullOUT on the entire box. Each efficient charge
lies in [0,F], so rho_i=F-u_i>=0. For a singleton, the withheld-resource output
is zero and own payment F is monotone. For the pair, 2(F(h)-F(l))>=F(h-l).
At each strict staircase step the restoring owner's reserve rises from zero to
F_k while the passive owner's reserve falls from F_(k-1) to zero. Total reserve
is just F. Payments are continuous although reserves switch as prices switch.
For this rule V^-=W_L exactly; replay verifies the formula.

Factor 2 is optimal under the efficient-price floor and individual incentives,
without assuming continuity. At (1,1), owner 1's payment must be >=y_1(t,1)>=t
for every t<1 (the latter price is uniquely (1,0)); hence y_1(1,1)>=1.
Similarly y_2(1,1)>=1. Budget at (1,1) gives K>=2, irrespective of the
price choice at the tie. This is an elementary calibration, not a novelty claim.

(c) With the further irreversible-reserve restriction, no finite K exists.
The exact hierarchy on this fixed game is therefore 1, 2, infinity.
It separates restrictions on proof/design architectures, not general mechanisms.

6. Decision

The concrete carry-forward formula is blocked at its individual-incentive/budget
gate. It never reaches the harder crossing-coalition gate. The existing CEA rule
for one minimum technology is separately replayed on that four-owner fixture;
that is prior positive calibration, not a new general construction.

A remaining direct rule would need reserve releases and the full inequalities
  Delta rho_C >= -Delta u_C + F(Delta B)
for EVERY eligible C, along with a bounded TOTAL reserve. Controlling only
positive deficits, changed-owner terms, or total variation cannot do that.
These are necessary design lessons, not a constructed solution or a theorem
improving sqrt(s/6) <= K_3(s) <= K_3^OUT(s) <= 3s^2.

Stop this candidate. Do not launch another unrestricted campaign merely because
a reserve-recycling inequality can be restated. A next mathematical gate needs
an explicit simultaneous release/compensation formula with a new proved bound,
or a different direct-payment architecture. Specialist review of the existing
paper remains the higher-confidence next program action.
