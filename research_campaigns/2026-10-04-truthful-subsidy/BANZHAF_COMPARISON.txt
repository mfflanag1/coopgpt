Source ingredient, paraphrased: for a monotone normalized continuous game f on
[0,1]^k, the absolute continuous Banzhaf index of coordinate j is the integral
over other coordinates of the difference obtained by fixing coordinate j to
one and to zero. This is an established functional, not our proposed invention.

COORDINATOR'S EXACT IDENTIFICATION
For T=min_j A_j>0, define f_A(z)=min_j(A_j z_j)/T on [0,1]^k. It is monotone,
continuous, has f_A(0)=0 and f_A(1)=1, and takes values in [0,1]. In this game
the k coordinates are POOLS, not the individual owners in the original game.
The zero-endpoint term for every coordinate is zero. The one-endpoint term is
min(A_j, min_{l != j} A_l z_l)/T. Integrating over independent uniform z_l gives

 T*BZI_j(f_A)
 = E[min(A_j, min_{l != j} A_l U_l)]
 = integral_0^T product_{l != j}(1-t/A_l) dt
 = A_j*p_j(A).

The second equality is the tail-integral identity for a bounded nonnegative
random variable; the upper endpoint is T since some factor becomes zero there,
or the fixed bound A_j=T truncates the integral. For k=1 the empty-coordinate
integral gives BZI_1=1 and A_1*p_1=T. At T=0 use the separate zero-payment rule.
Thus the total payment to pool j in Theorem 1 is precisely T times its absolute
continuous Banzhaf index, subsequently divided among its owners in proportion
to capacity. The Banzhaf vector is not normalized to make its entries sum to 1.
Such normalization would change the rule and may destroy coalition coverage.

PRIORITY IMPLICATION
Withdraw any impression that the integral functional itself is new. The proposed
contribution, whose priority remains unconfirmed, is its use with proportional
within-pool division to obtain own-capacity monotonicity (truthfulness under the
stated underreport-only/no-cost model), coalition inequalities without relaxation,
H/k floors for the original entire efficient core, and the (k-1)/2 subsidy bound.
For two pools it attains the class optimum for alpha<=1/2; larger alpha uses the
auxiliary rule, while optimality rests on a separate lower-bound argument. Identification with an established functional neither proves these
mechanism theorems were previously known nor establishes their novelty.
A dedicated search of continuous-value and resource-sharing literature remains.

The identification is profile-by-profile: changing A changes f_A. It is not a
claim that a single fixed continuous game models all strategic report profiles.
