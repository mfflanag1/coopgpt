1. Huang, Mei, Yang, Cao, Mechanism Design in Max-Flows (2025).
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1
Coordinator directly inspected Sections 2.1-2.2, Example 1, Proposition 1 and
mechanism discussion. Delegate inspected further MC sections; see its raw ledger.
Same one-edge-owner cardinal flow model, underreports only, no use cost.
Their mechanism budget is exactly max flow. Example 1 already rules out truthful
core selection on a 2x2 two-stage network. Proposition 1 establishes a truthful
Shapley mechanism; it does not assert core coverage. The subsidy parameter and
original-core coordinate floors here are separate requirements. Formal capacities
start strictly positive, although deviations/examples admit zero; our boundary
proofs are separate. Do not sell the qualitative impossibility as a new result.

2. Resnick, Bachrach, Meir, Rosenschein, The Cost of Stability in Network Flow
Games (2009). First author is Resnick, correcting the delegate's attribution.
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2009/01/CoS-NFG-MFCS.pdf
Coordinator read title/abstract, threshold definitions, Theorems 5-6 and the
serial-network discussion in Section 5.4. These are binary threshold flow games
with fixed capacities. Subsidized coalition stability is an established idea.
Their bounds do not supply our cardinal-flow, strategic-capacity, H-floor result.
