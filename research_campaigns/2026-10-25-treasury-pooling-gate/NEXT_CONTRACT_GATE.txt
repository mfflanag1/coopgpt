ONE INSTITUTION BEFORE ANOTHER EMPIRICAL CAMPAIGN
Research discovery checklist; no outreach sent or authorized by this artifact.

1. Who is the accountable sponsor/decision-maker? Which fixed internal units own their mandates? Can the partition be changed strategically? What precise decision could an allocation alter?
2. What exposures, position changes and realized P&L can be observed at a common timestamp? Include valuation basis, hedges, options, carry and transaction costs if material. Yield-curve changes alone are not actual unit P&L.
3. What is the present internal risk charge and why is it used? Is the candidate criterion dollar savings, return on allocated capital, loss protection, a liquidity charge, or a risk limit? Avoid interchanging them.
4. Are negative charges/hedging credits permitted? Who pays them, at what shadow price, under which budget, and with what appeal/withdrawal rights? Are coalition appeals actually institutional rights or only a modeling convention?
5. What minimum decision-relevant improvement is worth changing practice? Freeze that threshold and test sample before selecting an allocation or risk model. Obtain an untouched/prospective sample for confirmation.

Success: documented, permissioned decision and compatible records sufficient to compare the current rule, Euler, and a precisely justified alternative on the SAME fixed units, risk scenarios and constraints. A conditional model can remain useful, but cannot establish actual pooling gains.
Stop: no relevant decision owner, credits prohibited with irreconcilable coalition guarantees, no compatible records, no economic reason for absolute-dollar savings egalitarianism, or no independent evaluation sample. Record the gap and park the application rather than inventing institutions.

The current evidence already suggests studying the interaction between risk measurement and transfer permissions, if a real contract makes that question consequential. It does not justify optimizing a synthetic book to make selector separation large.
