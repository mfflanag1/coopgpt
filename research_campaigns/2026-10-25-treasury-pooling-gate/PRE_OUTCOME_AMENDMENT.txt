Written after independent design review and before loading selected yield outcomes. PROTOCOL_V1.txt is preserved and remains operative except for these clarifications.
1. Rolling covariance is the demeaned sample covariance with ddof=1, matching the static estimator; the conditional mean forecast remains fixed0. The already-written implementation uses exactly this choice.
2. Aggregate exposure(20,0) is a positive multiple of unit1(100,0). Their standardized loss series are identical. The calibration screen covers six distinct unit series, with aggregate displayed as a duplicate for budget interpretation, not a seventh independent test. Units4/5 expose the opposite tails of the two outright yield factors. No independence or family-wise inference is claimed.
3. The calendar-block stratification at2022 is informed by the publicly known tightening episode, not a genuinely blind regime division. All dates and thresholds were fixed before outcome calculation in this run.
4. One-day risk is interpreted as one observed trading interval; weekend/holiday calendar gaps are retained under the declared rule. This is not a calendar-time volatility model or an exact cash-bond holding-period return.
