MACRO / FIXED-INCOME APPLICATIONS OF COOPGPT — SCOUT DRAFT,24 OCTOBER2026
This is a research design assessment, not a new theorem, executed experiment,
novelty confirmation, regulatory opinion or investment recommendation.

Working recommendation
One ambitious theory lane: incentives for sharing collateral and netting benefits.
One grounded application lane: debt supported by complementary AI/power assets,
with an auditable ownership-and-contract map before any spread regression.
A particularly clean short mathematical calibration is risk-capital allocation:
first recover a one-factor min-production game, then ask what two factors change.

What transfers from our work
Core allocation asks whether any subgroup would prefer its standalone option.
Our funding work adds consistency across actual resource contributions, allowing
owners to withdraw portions or coordinate, sometimes requiring external funding.
These are useful for financial institutions because gains from cooperation must
be split among parties controlling cash, collateral, positions or contract rights.
The present theorems do not model private valuations, strategic default, arbitrary
reassignment of liabilities, bankruptcy priorities, intertemporal commitment,
endogenous prices, or financial equilibrium. Quantities paid are total rewards,
not automatically regulatory capital, taxpayer subsidy or social resource costs.

Ranked directions
1. Participation incentives in collateral allocation/netting: strongest direct
   match to the current mechanism mathematics; real transactions usually private.
2. Complementary infrastructure and enforceable debt cash flows: strongest match
   to user's AI/energy interests plus macro-credit relevance; needs new contract
   model and verified rights, not just a min formula.
3. Stable capital allocation with few yield-curve factors: cleanest first proof
   and public-data calibration; broad representation and fairness conflict are old.
4. Margin liquidity insurance: important dynamic frontier; pooling arithmetic
   alone is classical and common shocks may eliminate the benefit.
5. Fragmented clearing and scarce intermediation capacity: macro extension of1,
   with explicit private fees versus social benefits; common clearing is mandated
   for covered trades, so legal choices must concern eligible access/routing or
   optional resources, not evasion of the mandate.
6. Monetary transmission through complementary project completion: macro extension
   of2; structural counterfactual first, causal identification remains difficult.
7. International reserve pooling/swap networks: long-run possibility, but sovereign
   enforcement, state-contingent exits and moral hazard are too remote for first work.
Park generic sovereign debt holdouts, generic Shapley risk allocation, generic
portfolio compression, or yield forecasting claimed to follow from our theorems.

DESIGN A — Who will contribute the collateral that makes pooling valuable?
Decision: a specified platform allocates rewards/fee rebates for legally available
cash, securities-lending rights and optional intermediation capacity.
Owners: actual banks/funds/legal entities, fixed across all comparisons.
Deviations: reduce an optional, unencumbered capacity commitment before a stated
cutoff. No deletion of contractual liabilities or reuse of segregated collateral.
Start with public haircuts, eligibility, maturity and surplus per completed trade;
one settlement date; no private cost or credit-risk reporting. Distinguish a
resource-allocation pool from netting of already binding contractual obligations.

Prototype F(c)=max{p'x: Ax<=c, x>=0}, with nonnegative A,p.
This is the classical linear-production setting of Owen(1975); static core
nonemptiness from LP duality is a calibration, not our contribution. Each column describes
one legally feasible, fully funded activity, not merely a graph edge chosen for
mathematical convenience. Include every binding capacity and avoid treating a
single platform-owned indispensable input as a competitive multiowner economy.
A factor K can be invoked only after a theorem is matched to the exact class
and incentive contract. The P4 constant is not a constant for general A. Known
project bounds for broader classes have their own parameters and hypotheses.
Funding K F(c) requires a sponsor ledger: if F is monetary transferable revenue,
outside funding need is max(0,sum y-F), at most(K-1)F under the theorem, not a
measured requirement or taxpayer bill. Repo principal/notional is not revenue.

Question: when do core-compatible rewards also resist joint partial withdrawal?
Possible new result: a general bound tied to eligibility/network structure, or an
exact impossibility with fixed actual owners and unrestricted admissible rules.
First gate: reconstruct a real access/settlement contract, verify every theorem
hypothesis, and reproduce a known netting benchmark. Stop if fixed public prices
give an immediate efficient rule, a veto owner trivializes incentives, or the
model needs legally impossible withdrawals. If existing theorem applies routinely,
record an application corollary rather than announce another paper.

Antecedents: Fleming/Keane2021 already measure central-clearing netting gains;
Garratt2019 uses Shapley liquidity cost allocation and voluntary proposal acceptance.
His discussion explicitly notes that Shapley need not lie in the core. His model
uses payment benefits/costs whose observability is discussed separately; it does
not settle our proposed partial-resource-withholding contract by its title alone.
Strategic portfolio compression is also an existing field, e.g.Mayo/Wellman2021.
Public OFR data give rates/volumes by segment, not the full owner-position-rights
network. Synthetic networks can prove or falsify a theory, not measure US savings.
SEC's current implementation page gives eligible cash deadline31Dec2026 and repo
30Jun2027. Do not infer a voluntary clearing choice where the rule requires it.

DESIGN B — The debt value of keeping complementary assets together
Question: how does debt capacity change when a functioning AI campus needs assets
and permissions controlled by several parties, and lenders cannot preserve that
bundle after distress? Resources include firm deliverable power, powered facilities,
compute equipment, cooling/network service and a revenue-producing customer contract.
Convert physical inputs to declared common service units before q=min(capacities).
Neither powerqueue applications nor announced MW establish enforceable rights.

Compare ownership/control architectures holding technology and demand fixed:
separate claims with limited step-in rights; enforceable bundled step-in/cross-default;
and unified ownership. Bundling can preserve complementarities but may expose
unrelated assets to contagion or weaken local incentives: sign not assumed.
CoopGPT contributes coalition deviation accounting and owner-preserving construction;
our macro bottleneck work contributes readiness, robust scenario and measurement
discipline. A dynamic debt/renegotiation model is new work, not supplied by either.

Two-date prototype: time0 finance; time1 readiness/demand shock and explicit
renegotiation or enforcement; time2 operating cash/recovery. Define the control
rights, legal withdrawal options, replacement costs, salvage and lender seniority.
Do not identify nominal promises with enforceable receipts. State-contingent
cash flows must be valued consistently: market-consistent pricing measure if one
is actually specified, or physical expected loss with an explicit risk premium.
Do not multiply existing K by a debt balance to predict spreads.

This is an adjacent applied-theory direction requiring new finance, not a direct
application of our current theorem. Grand-coalition coverage already allocates at
least F(c) to owners; if F is all project cash, a separate creditor cannot also be
paid from it. Creditors/control rights or residual values must be modeled explicitly
and the theorem hypotheses rechecked; see ROOT_CALIBRATION_AND_BOUNDARIES.txt.

First deliverable: one project with a source-verifiable map of asset ownership,
power delivery rights, tenant termination, guarantees, step-in and debt claims;
a two-date model that reproduces classic hold-up/pledgeability benchmarks; and one
comparative-static result or exact failure of an appealing covenant design.
Scientific gate: show a new many-owner/network implication beyond simple asset
specificity or a routine three-input minimum. For applied work, identify observable
contract variation and outcomes before inspecting spreads. If key rights are
undisclosed, provide ranges or stop at data insufficiency.

Important competition: Hart/Moore already relate withdrawal/asset specificity to
debt capacity. Van Nieuwerburgh's2026BPEA draft maps AI financing structures and
execution risks. Gete/Mercader's March2026preliminary paper studies datacenter
mortgages and originates-to-distribute incentives using proprietary CoStar data.
Our candidate must be about enforceable complementary rights and a new result,
not 'AI debt is risky' or 'datacenters use private credit'.

DESIGN C — Who gets the value of a duration hedge?
Fix a common public coherent risk measure rho on losses. C(S)=rho(sum_S L_i).
Stable charges k satisfy sum_i k_i=C(N),sum_S k_i<=C(S). Set
       y_i=C({i})-k_i; v(S)=sum_S C({i})-C(S).
Then y belongs to the ordinary profit core of v, exactly by subtraction.
This maps allocation of diversification savings, not arbitrary fairness selectors
on raw capital charges. Owner-specific shifts change rankings; capital floors or
prohibitions on negative charges are additional restrictions.

Useful calibration derived here: if L_i=a_i Z, rho is fixed and positively
homogeneous, let A+=sum_i max(a_i,0), A-=sum_i max(-a_i,0), k+=rho(Z),k-=rho(-Z).
Then v(N)=(k++k-)min(A+,A-), and the same holds for every coalition. Subadditivity
implies k++k->=0. Thus one-factor diversification is a two-input min game up to a
constant; this is an elementary calibration/corollary, not asserted novel.
For centered symmetric Gaussian factors and a fixed common ES level, k+=k-=kappa, giving2kappa min(A+,A-).

Exact candidate: Z~N(0,I_2), a fixed common Expected Shortfall tail probability
alpha=0.025, portfolios L_i=a_i'Z, actual owners fixed within each instance and
n unbounded across instances. Determine whether the extreme
opposing savings-core selectors/ideal-share failures from unrestricted exact games
survive this restriction, or derive a dimension-sensitive upper bound. Start with
sum_i a_i=0 to calibrate the riskless aggregate case, then relax it. Centered Gaussian two-
factor ES gives C(S)=kappa*sqrt(a_S' Sigma a_S), but general risk-allocation game
representation does not imply realizability in this restricted norm geometry.
Same fixed owners; no changing the distribution/ES level for each coalition.

First gate: reproduce the one-factor identity exactly and determine what is
already implied by existing multi-glove/assignment results. Then either a new
unbounded two-factor family or a genuine factor-sensitive lemma is needed.
Small synthetic instances only locate candidates. Use public Treasury yield curves
for a later calibrated illustration; they do not identify institutions' actual
books, risk capital, default probability or regulatory compliance.

Csoka/Herings/Koczy2009 already establish the broad risk-game/totally-balanced and
riskless-aggregate/exact-game representation. Denault and the risk-allocation
literature already study coherent allocation. Csoka/Pinter and Balog etal already
study incompatible fairness/monotonicity requirements. Need exact axiom comparison;
their 'incentive compatibility' need not mean our resource-underreport incentives.

DESIGN D — Liquidity insurance against margin calls
Fix two dates and eligible portfolios; a member commits enforceable liquidity
before a shock, with dated withdrawal/recall rights. For portfolio cash-call
variables X_i, diversification benefit under a fixed coherent liquidity-risk
functional is sum_i rho(X_i)-rho(sum_i X_i). This is classical subadditivity.
New question: which commitments and reserves keep the insurance viable when
members may reduce uncalled commitments before new information? Once a margin
liability exists, it cannot simply be removed as an 'underreport'. Compare
ordinary static coverage, ex-ante enforceable commitment, and dynamic deviations.
Common shocks and same-direction exposures are the first kill tests. A pool that
works only under independent calls is weak. Statewise liquidity service must be
distinguished from expected or ES capital savings. Public scenario reports can
calibrate stress; participant-level intraday margin/credit-line data may be missing.
Bank of England's gilt-repo work explicitly discusses netting gains alongside
possible greater cash-margin pressure. Candidate output is a time-consistent
commitment theorem, sharp obstruction, or data-insufficiency finding.

MACRO EXTENSIONS, AFTER A MICRO RESULT
Clearing architecture can affect intermediary balance-sheet capacity and the
transmission of monetary policy. That requires a separately specified model of
dealer constraints, equilibrium prices and participation: the present funding
factor is not a Treasury liquidity premium or an aggregate GDP multiplier.
Similarly, debt maturities crossing uncertain joint completion dates may affect
whether financing shocks delay projects; the empirical question is whether
measured rights/completion mismatch explains outcomes beyond issuer risk, tenor,
power-region, construction stage, guarantees, and demand. Monetary shocks may
also change these fundamentals, so a convenient event study is not identification.

Preferred bounded first commitments (proposal only)
A: 90min institutional/model mapping;90min original-source comparison;3h explicit
mechanism/counterexample;1h independent audit. Stop on invalid rights or routine
reduction. B: first test public contract access for one project, then compare two
clearly defined enforcement structures; no broad spread dataset until coverage
and underwriting definitions are credible. C: one-factor calibration then a
two-factor scope/priority gate; stop if it is already a known risk-allocation result.
No campaign, physical Mac benchmark, purchase, data subscription or outreach is
started by this brainstorm.
