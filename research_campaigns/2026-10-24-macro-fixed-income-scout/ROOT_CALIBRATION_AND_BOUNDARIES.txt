One-factor identity (elementary calibration; no novelty assertion)
Assume a common finite coherent loss-risk functional rho, positive homogeneity,
and positions L_i=a_i Z on one fixed integrable factor. Put a+_S=sum_S max(a_i,0)
and a-_S=sum_S max(-a_i,0); k+=rho(Z), k-=rho(-Z).
Sum of standalone risks is k+ a+_S + k- a-_S.
If a+_S>=a-_S, pooled risk is k+(a+_S-a-_S), so savings equal
(k++k-)a-_S. In the opposite case, pooled risk is k-(a-_S-a+_S), so
savings equal(k++k-)a+_S. Subadditivity gives0=rho(0)<=k++k-.
Therefore for every coalition S the savings game is a common nonnegative
multiple of min(a+_S,a-_S). No Gaussian assumption is necessary. If that multiple
or total savings is zero, normalized selector distances are undefined and must
be excluded. This proof is static; it does not establish an incentive theorem
for hiding positions or ignoring their changing standalone risk.

Cost-core transformation
For C(S)=rho(sum_S L_i), set v(S)=sum_S C({i})-C(S),y_i=C({i})-k_i.
Then y(N)=v(N) iff k(N)=C(N), and y(S)>=v(S) iff k(S)<=C(S).
Thus the two core feasible sets are affine images. All savings-core coordinates
are nonnegative from singleton inequalities; raw k_i can be negative. A chosen
leximin/leximax on charges does NOT in general correspond to the same named
selector on savings after owner-specific subtraction. Transfer project results
only when the selection objective is explicitly defined on normalized savings.
Extra charge floors truncate the domain and may break existing representations.

Important debt-budget obstruction to a naive application
Our current coalition coverage includes the grand coalition, so y(N)>=F(c).
If F is all available project cash flow and every y_i is treated as an operating
owner's cash receipt, adding a separate creditor payment D>=0 under y(N)+D<=F(c)
forces D=0. One cannot derive positive debt capacity by reserving debt service
and then applying the unchanged full-coverage theorem to the same gross F.

A valid finance model must instead specify creditors' capital/control rights as
part of the economic institution, use genuine legally constrained outside options,
or explicitly define a residual value after prior claims and recheck its properties.
Subtracting fixed debt service need not preserve homogeneity/nonnegativity or the
original legal withdrawal domain. No such repair is asserted automatic here.
This is a decisive initial gate for the AI debt direction, not a solved finance
theorem. It reinforces why the contribution must be a new enforcement/rights model.

Netting versus production
F=max p'x over Ax<=c,A,p>=0 represents optional feasible positive activities.
It does not by itself represent a signed existing obligation network, default
waterfall, CCP risk measure, or customer collateral segregation. Cash and collateral
may support a positive allocation problem under explicit public prices and rights;
the exact regulatory/settlement mapping remains the first gate. Repo notional is
neither transferable net surplus nor an amount that can fund incentive payments.

Free data
Rates/volumes do not identify a counterparty network; public yield curves do not
identify bank books; generation queues do not establish delivered power at a
particular data center. The brainstorm uses these as possible inputs with explicit
missing links, not as a measured application or a causal estimate.
