FROZEN MATHEMATICAL CONTRACT
Finite nonnegative packing production, profitable activity support at most
three; fixed actual owners, possibly holding several resource rows; the same
coordinatewise real underreport boxes throughout. Owner and resource counts
may grow. Nonnegative payments cover every coalition at every profile and
have total at most K F(B). A protected coalition has at most s actual owners,
including unchanged passive members. For a legal restoration l->h of C,
  y_C(h)-y_C(l) >= F(B_h-B_l)
is full OUT. The weaker TCWM right side is zero. Neither splitting owners nor
dropping the restorer's term from a protected coalition is allowed.

The finite dual uses coverage weights lambda, one shared whole-coalition
comparison weight mu, and profile budget weights nu. Its owner/profile
coefficient is g=lambda-incidence+incoming-mu-incidence-outgoing-mu-incidence.
Feasibility is g<=nu, with all multipliers nonnegative. Put sigma=nu-g>=0,
L=sum lambda F(b_C)+sum mu F(delta), D=sum nu F(B). The aim is to prove
L<=K(s)D uniformly and then use the inherited classical compactness lift.

ACCEPTED POSITIVE STRUCTURE

A. Balanced two-terminal contraction.
Take the positive-mu comparison graph. Suppose a component has a unique source
a, a unique sink z, and zero COMPARISON-ONLY divergence at every intermediate
owner/profile. Branching and merging are allowed. Then its comparisons can
be replaced by direct a->z comparisons, preserving every owner's divergence,
lambda,nu,sigma,D and increasing or preserving L.

Proof mechanism: cut at an intermediate value of a changed owner's coordinate.
Every crossing comparison contains that owner. Flow conservation shows that
each endpoint-changing owner has the same maximum flow M. Every positive
crossing coalition must therefore contain ALL endpoint-changing owners, so
there are at most s of them. Copy those whole coalitions onto a->z. Resource
telescoping and superadditivity give
  sum_e mu_e F(delta_e) <= M F(B_z-B_a).
The inherited rank-r one-layer argument then bounds this certificate by rD.
"One layer" means disjoint sets of ALL tails and ALL heads, not merely an
acyclic graph with sources and sinks.

Consequence: balanced two-terminal regions are removable at no budget cost.
This does not bound the cost of handling several source/sink incidences or
intermediate nonzero comparison divergence in a general certificate.
Proof: certificate_route/layer_followup.txt.

B. Frozen-background bound.
If only k owners vary over a finite profile component, let J be the constant
owners, f_J their production, and h_J fixed rank charges for them. Paying
F(B)-f_J to each varying owner and h_j to each frozen owner covers all
coalitions and satisfies full OUT. Its exact total yields
  L <= k D + (H_J-k f_J) sum nu <= max(k,r) D.
Thus a ratio above 3 at arity 3 needs a component with at least four genuinely
varying owners. Arbitrarily many valuable frozen passives do not suffice.
The number k is not bounded by s in general. This is not a new uniform upper.
Proof: certificate_route/signed_followup.txt.

ACCEPTED BOUNDARIES OF THE METHODS

C. One essential comparison in an optimal finite dual.
An explicit four-owner, arity-three technology has an exact optimum 285/278
on a five-profile restriction and on its original complete 256-profile lattice.
On the five profiles, an efficient covering test vector satisfies 15 of 16
comparison rows. Its only shortfall is 7/12 on e*=M->Q,{1,3}. Therefore EVERY
feasible dual on that fixed profile set satisfies
  L-D <= (7/12)mu_e*.
For a normalized optimal dual, mu_e*>=6/139. Efficient prices at EVERY profile
force that edge's defect to be -1/3. Consequently the minimum negative-defect
mass across ALL normalized optimal duals and all such price selections is
exactly 2/139. The displayed dual attains it.

This rules out objective-preserving fixed-Q normalization to globally efficient
prices with nonnegative defects. It does not rule out new profiles, inefficient
prices at zero-budget profiles, or a different general mechanism. The exact
one-edge proof is stronger and simpler than the initial sign-region diagnostic.
Proof and exact witnesses: price_selection_diagnostic/RESULT.txt and
price_selection_diagnostic/verify_exact.py.

D. Several terminals require additional freedom.
Even a unique sink with zero internal comparison divergence need not admit a
sum of one-layer duals preserving the coverage and budget arrays. A three-owner
example with F=min(A,B)+epsilon*K, s=2, and positive production from every
owner forces coverage at a profile that differs from the only budget profile
in three owners. No legal direct pair comparison can connect them.
The obstruction permits free reassignment of nonnegativity slacks and new
zero-budget profiles. A specific escape adds a budget at an intermediate
profile, preserves L, and costs at most 3/2 of the original D. That escape is
only for this fixture. It demonstrates why budget relocation must be allowed;
it does not bound its cost in general.
Proof: certificate_route/single_terminal_followup.txt.

E. Other checked stops and calibrations.
- Literal positive-part price charging can grow logarithmically relative to
  budget in a harmonic family. Its signed objective stays small. Zero-report
  label erasure and legitimate whole-coalition recombination can remove that
  family, so it is not a general normalization barrier.
- Preserving divergence separately for each coalition label has unbounded
  layer cost even at fixed s=3. Preserving aggregate actual-owner divergence
  permits an exact repair. The distinction is essential.
- There is no uniform scalar multiplication converting every valid rule into
  an efficient-price-dominating rule, already for two owners and min-r.
- On pure-owner min-r, unrestricted full-OUT optimum 1 versus price-floor
  optimum r is exact on the whole real box. The sharp TCWM correction cost is
  r for an appropriate efficient input rule; requiring only the corrected
  sum to satisfy full OUT gives r-1 for that input. The CEA upper and basic
  efficient veto-owner rule are established ingredients. At r=3 this is a
  constant architecture gap, not a growing-s exponent improvement.
- A global-output-preserving resource cap fails to repair fractional-owner
  smoothing by ANY correction depending only on whole-coalition worths. A
  productive independent resource leaves all those worths unchanged while
  a protected pair loses 1/18. Direction-sensitive corrections remain open.

FINAL PRICE-RELAXATION CHECK
The signed identity only needs price efficiency at profiles with positive nu;
prices at zero-budget profiles may be feasible but inefficient. This is a
certificate-level freedom, not an efficient primal price-base mechanism.
All 56 stored supports have exact rational witnesses under this relaxed condition,
with 262 supported comparison defects nonnegative and the full signed identity
verified. The five-profile obstruction escapes explicitly through a feasible
but inefficient price at its zero-budget profile M. This does not prove the
relaxed property for arbitrary optimal duals. It is false for arbitrary FEASIBLE
duals: adding budget weight 1/1000 at M in the seed-15 certificate keeps it
feasible with ratio 57000/55739>1 but forces efficiency at both endpoints of
the essential negative-defect edge. That deliberately nonoptimal modification
does not obstruct selecting or normalizing an optimal dual. See
root/BUDGET_SUPPORT_QUANTIFIER_GUARD.txt.

A GENERAL sufficient lemma was also derived. Locally erase zero-report owners
from coverage labels and unchanged-zero owners from comparison labels. Positive-
report coordinates keep their original balances; zero-report coordinates are
left with only negative outgoing terms. Replace each remaining zero-low-bundle
comparison by coverage of its high coalition. Full-OUT L,D and feasibility are
preserved. If every positive-budget profile has no outgoing comparison, choose
efficient prices there and sufficiently large feasible prices backwards along
the other profiles. All retained defects are nonnegative and the budget-weighted
price excess vanishes. This preserves actual owners and shared coalitions.

The general proof is in
root/LOCAL_NULL_ERASURE_AND_TERMINAL_BUDGET_PRICES.txt.
Exact normalization checks pass on all 56 stored certificates; 38 satisfy the
terminal-budget hypothesis and admit the stated backward construction. Of those,
21 have L/D>1, including the exact 285/278 obstruction and the 15/11 lower
certificate. Their 113 supported comparisons pass the exact price check. An
additional explicit fixture exercises positive-OUT replacement, which was
absent from the stored 56. These checks supplement the general proof.

A final source audit confirms that the manuscript's existing growing projective
lower certificates also have terminal budget support: after expanding symmetry,
budgets occur only at profiles of report mass 2M+1, while every comparison tail
has mass at most 2M. Each restored bundle has zero production. Thus the lemma
applies without changing their growing Omega(sqrt(s)) objective. This is an
application to an established project certificate, independently checked in this
campaign; it neither improves that lower bound nor supplies an upper bound.

There is an exact quantitative limit: even with two owners, r=3,s=1 and a
terminal budget, nonnegative defects can force the minimum price-cost ratio
at a zero-budget profile to be (1+t)/(2t), unbounded as t decreases to zero.
This is a feasible ratio-1/2 certificate, not an optimal-certificate lower.
It shows why the relaxed price lemma does not itself construct a bounded-price
mechanism or control the remaining signed accounting. See
budget_price_relaxation/RESULT.txt, including the exact escape and limitation.
