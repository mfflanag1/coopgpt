HARMONIC MULTILAYER OBSTRUCTION TO GROSS ENDPOINT-SURPLUS CHARGING
21 October 2026 UTC. Independent bounded certificate route.
Evidence: conventional self-contained proof plus exact rational finite checks.
Scope: a rejected global certificate shortcut; NOT an improved unrestricted
funding bound, an architecture impossibility, or a lower bound on K_3(s).

CLAIM
There is no finite c(2) such that every feasible actual-owner finite dual for
arity-three production and protected coalitions of size at most two satisfies

  sum_e mu_e zeta_high(e)(C_e) <= c(2) sum_p nu_p F(B_p).               (A)

Here zeta is precisely the nonnegative endpoint rank surplus in
PRICE_CHARGE_FRONTIER.txt. Failure persists if one reroutes/subdivides/shortcuts
the comparison multipliers while preserving their signed incidence at every
actual owner/profile coordinate. Thus the proof does not use the number of
subdivided arcs as its complexity measure. Its invariant is the total positive
net flow demand of distinct actual passive owners.

This rejects controlling the gross term in equation (6) of that source, even
after such flow normalization. It leaves the signed identity (5), reserve
constructions that retain outgoing cancellations, and the O(s) target open.

1. ONE FIXED INSTITUTION FOR EACH INTEGER m>=2
Resources are A,B,K. The sole activity consumes one of each and has profit
one. Hence F(a,b,k)=min(a,b,k), and activity arity is exactly three.
There are the following fixed actual owners:
  * restorer R;
  * cap owner Z;
  * one distinct passive owner P_ij for every 0<=i<j<=m-1.
All owners may report any vector in the common real endowment box [0,4]^3.
Thus n=2+m(m-1)/2, and no row is made into a separate strategic player.

At selected profile p_k, put t_k=1+k/(m-1) and set
  b_R(p_k)=(t_k,2t_k,0),
  b_Z(p_k)=(0,0,3),
  b_Pij(p_k)=(0,0,0) for every passive owner.
The restorer genuinely owns two resource rows. All reports are legal.
F(B_k)=t_k. The unique optimal production is x=t_k and the unique optimal
nonnegative production price is w=(1,0,0), since B and K are strictly slack.
Consequently
  u_R=t_k, h_R=2t_k, zeta_R=t_k;
  u_Z=0,   h_Z=t_k,  zeta_Z=t_k;
  u_P=h_P=zeta_P=0.

For every i<j use the actual comparison
  e_ij=(p_i,p_j,C_ij), C_ij={R,P_ij}, mu_ij=1/(j-i).
Only R changes reports, and the zero-report P_ij remains a protected passive
member. Coalition size is exactly two. The restored bundle is
  delta_ij=(t_j-t_i,2(t_j-t_i),0),
so F(delta_ij)=0. These are legal comparisons for BOTH TCWM and full OUT.
In particular, zero outside worth is not mistaken for an absent comparison.

2. EXACT ACTUAL-OWNER DUAL, WITH ONE SHARED MULTIPLIER PER COALITION
Write H_0=0 and H_k=sum_(d=1)^k 1/d. Give the single coverage row at p_0 for
S={R,Z} multiplier one. All other coverage multipliers are zero. Put
  d_k=H_k-H_(m-1-k),
  nu_k=max(1,d_k).
The exact signed balances g=lambda+incoming-outgoing are
  g_(k,R)=d_k+1_{k=0};
  g_(k,Z)=1_{k=0};
  g_(k,P_ij)=(1/(j-i)) [1_{k=j}-1_{k=i}].
Every coordinate is <=nu_k. For R at k=0 this uses 1-H_(m-1)<=1;
for all passives it uses 1/(j-i)<=1. Thus sigma=nu-g>=0 gives a valid
finite dual with all actual owners retained and no separately weighted
member flows. Its objective is L=F(b_R(p_0)+b_Z(p_0))=1.
Its budget denominator is D=sum_k nu_k t_k.

For a simple uniform bound, nu_k<=1+(d_k)_+. Since d_k has the sign of
2k-(m-1), the sum of its positive parts is the flow across the middle cut:
  sum_k (d_k)_+ = sum_(i in lower half, j in upper half) 1/(j-i).
For each distance d there are at most d pairs crossing any fixed cut, so
this is at most m-1. Therefore
  sum_k nu_k<=2m-1,        D<=4m-2.                              (1)
The exact objective ratio L/D is small. It is NOT a funding lower bound.

3. THE GROSS SURPLUS IS LOGARITHMICALLY TOO LARGE
Since zeta_(p_j)(C_ij)=t_j>=1,
  G_in := sum_(i<j) mu_ij zeta_(p_j)(C_ij)
       = sum_(j=1)^(m-1) t_j H_j
       >= sum_(i<j) 1/(j-i)
       = m H_(m-1)-(m-1).
Combining with (1),
  G_in / D >= [m H_(m-1)-(m-1)]/(4m-2) -> infinity.              (2)
This is a symbolic growing family at fixed r=3 and s=2, not extrapolation
from finite optimization.

4. WHY SAME-COALITION COMPRESSION AND SUBDIVISION DO NOT REMOVE (2)
All selected distinct-profile comparisons change R. Therefore any legal
comparison between these profiles must protect R. At size at most two, a
comparison containing a passive owner P can contain only R and P; it cannot
carry a second passive owner's demand simultaneously.

Consider ANY alternative nonnegative comparison multipliers on these same
profiles with exactly the same signed comparison incidence at every actual
owner/profile coordinate. In particular P_ij still has net incoming demand
mu_ij=1/(j-i) at p_j and net outgoing demand mu_ij at p_i, with zero net
incidence elsewhere. The total weight of comparisons containing P_ij is
therefore at least mu_ij: the incoming weight at p_j alone is at least that
amount. Each such comparison has zeta_high({R,P_ij})=t_high>=1.
Since no comparison can contain two of these passive owners, summing gives
  alternative G_in >= sum_(i<j) mu_ij
                    = m H_(m-1)-(m-1).                         (3)
Singleton-R comparisons or comparisons using Z can only add nonnegative
surplus. Thus (2) is invariant under exact flow replacement at these owner
coordinates, including subdivisions, same-coalition path compression and
rerouting. No arc count is used in this argument. The growing parameter is
the number and prescribed divergence of distinct actual passive owners.

The same argument applies if intermediate profiles are added on these
monotone intervals, since t stays in [1,2] and only R can change there, provided
the original owner divergences are preserved and the new vertices have zero
net divergence. This is not a claim about arbitrary transformations that also
change lambda, nu, sigma, introduce different profiles, or change demands.
Such transformations might be precisely what a successful proof needs.

5. GENUINELY MULTILAYER SUPPORT; A SEPARATE CAUTION ABOUT LAYER COUNT
The displayed support is the complete increasing DAG on m distinct profiles.
Every interior profile both receives and emits comparisons, with different
actual passive-owner labels. Each label appears on exactly one original edge.
It is therefore outside the retained one-global-source/sink-layer theorem.

For an edge-preserving cover by k global source/sink layers, the m vertices
need distinct source-membership binary signatures: if i<j has its edge in a
layer, i is in that layer's source set and j in its disjoint sink set. Equal
source signatures would make this impossible. Hence m<=2^k and k>=ceil(log2 m).
Conversely binary labels for the increasing indices give a cover by that many
layers, assigning each pair to its highest differing bit (0 at its source,
1 at its target). The edge-preserving minimum is exactly ceil(log2 m).

This support-only number IS NOT an intrinsic invariant under arbitrary edge
subdivision. It is recorded only to show why applying the one-layer theorem
once does not handle the displayed certificate. The obstruction (3), not this
layer count, survives flow subdivision. No funding claim is based on counting
layers, since splitting a certificate into layer supports does not itself
produce separately feasible duals with controlled budget denominators.

6. THE REQUIRED CANCELLATION IS EXPLICIT, AND STILL AVAILABLE
The actual edge price defect is NEGATIVE:
  u_(p_i)(C_ij)-u_(p_j)(C_ij)+F(delta_ij)=t_i-t_j.
Thus
  sum_(i<j) mu_ij [actual price defect] = -m/2,
because mu_ij(t_j-t_i)=1/(m-1) for every pair.
The outgoing surplus satisfies
  G_out=sum_(i<j) mu_ij t_i,
  G_in-G_out=m/2.
Both G_in and G_out have order m log m; their difference has order m.
The exact price slack identity also checks:
  sum sigma_(p,i) u_(p,i)=D-m/2-1,
  L-D=-m/2-[D-m/2-1]=1-D.
This shows precisely why dropping outgoing terms is the wrong step. The
technology even has ONE common optimal price at all budget profiles, so the
known fixed-price rule already establishes factor one for this finite system.
In fact y_R=t and all other payments zero is efficient on these selected
profiles, covers every coalition, and satisfies every selected comparison.
The example deliberately makes the gap between gross local domination and
global signed accounting exact; it does not refute the target O(s) mechanism.

7. CONSEQUENCE AND GATE DISPOSITION
STOP the unqualified bound (A), including versions justified only by bounded
arity, bounded protected-coalition size, shared edge weights, actual-coordinate
balances, or same-coalition flow normalization. All those conditions hold here.
Also reject the implication that an edge partition into one-layer pieces
immediately decomposes a feasible dual into feasible layer duals.

This route DOES NOT pass the requested upper-bound feasibility gate. It yields
a scoped, all-size no-go theorem for gross endpoint-surplus charging. A possible
positive route must control the signed defect, subtract outgoing surplus/slacks
before estimation, or use a more substantial certificate transformation than
exact comparison-incidence normalization. No O(s), o(s^2), or general
price-plus-reserve impossibility has been established here.
