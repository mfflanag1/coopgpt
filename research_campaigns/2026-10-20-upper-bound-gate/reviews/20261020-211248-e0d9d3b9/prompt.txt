7: 1. CONTRACT AND THE TWO PRESERVATION CLASSES
8: Fix a finite nonnegative packing technology, finite on the legal capacities,
9: one fixed roster of actual owners, and their original real report boxes. In a
10: legal comparison e=(l,h,C), every outsider report is unchanged, every changed
11: owner lies in C, every report increases coordinatewise, and |C|<=s. A single
12: nonnegative multiplier mu_e is shared by the entire coalition C. For OUT,
13: d_e=F(B_h-B_l). No owner is split and no protected passive member is omitted.
14: 
15: For each owner i and profile p put
16:  gamma_(p,i)=incoming comparison incidence-outgoing comparison incidence,
17:  lambdaInc_(p,i)=sum_(S containing i)lambda_(p,S),
18:  g_(p,i)=lambdaInc_(p,i)+gamma_(p,i),
19:  sigma_(p,i)=nu_p-g_(p,i)>=0.
20: The objective and budget are
21:  L=sum lambda_(p,S)F(b_S(p))+sum mu_e F(delta_e),
22:  D=sum nu_p F(B_p).
23: Comparison-only divergence gamma, rather than g, is zero at the internal
24: profiles considered below. A global one-layer family has disjoint sets of
25: source and sink profiles; it is not merely a separate forest for every C.
26: 
27: A decomposition means a sum of feasible one-layer duals. In the first class,
28: aggregate lambda, nu AND sigma must equal their original arrays, so aggregate
29: gamma is also fixed. In the second class only aggregate lambda and nu are
30: fixed; sigma may be reassigned freely, subject to nonnegativity. New profiles
31: may be added with zero aggregate lambda and nu (and zero sigma in the first
32: class). All multipliers remain nonnegative. The examples rule out even sums
33: of layers in their stated class, hence also a single one-layer replacement.

35: 2. UNIQUE SINK: PRESERVING LAMBDA AND NU ALREADY FAILS
36: Take three actual owners, each owning its corresponding resource row. A profile
37: (x,y,k) means reports (x,0,0), (0,y,0), (0,0,k). All report boxes can be [0,2]^3.
38: Let epsilon>0 and
39:  F(A,B,K)=min(A,B)+epsilon K.
40: This is exactly the packing value of two activities: one consumes A and B
41: and yields one; the other consumes K and yields epsilon. Maximum column arity
42: is two, within the arity-three contract. Every owner is productive: removing
43: owner 1 or 2 at a reduces value by one, and removing owner 3 reduces it by
44: epsilon. Thus the third row is not an unused coordinate encoding device.
45: 
46: Use four distinct profiles:
47:  a=(1,1,1), b=(2,2,0), p=(2,2,1), z=(2,2,2).
48: At s=2 the supported comparisons, with whole-coalition shared weights, are
49:  a -> p, C={1,2}, mu=1;
50:  b -> p, C={3},   mu=2;
51:  p -> z, C={1,3}, mu=1;
52:  p -> z, C={2,3}, mu=1.
53: In the last two comparisons owner 3 changes while owner 1 or 2 is protected
54: passively. Each comparison is legal. The graph has two sources a,b and a
55: unique sink z. Its sole internal profile p is balanced for every actual owner:
56:  gamma_a=(-1,-1,0), gamma_b=(0,0,-2),
57:  gamma_p=(0,0,0),   gamma_z=(1,1,2).
58: 
59: Put lambda_(a,{1,2})=1, nu_z=2, and all other lambda and nu equal to zero.
60: Then g_a=g_p=0, g_b=(0,0,-2), g_z=(1,1,2), and
61:  sigma_a=sigma_p=0, sigma_b=(0,0,2), sigma_z=(1,1,0).
62: All finite Farkas inequalities hold. The exact values are
63:  L_OUT=2+4epsilon, L_TCWM=1, D=4+4epsilon.
64: Indeed the coverage value is one and the comparison values, counting weights,
65: are 1, 2epsilon, epsilon, epsilon. The resource check is
66:  sum nu B=(4,4,4), sum mu delta=(1,1,4),
67:  sum nu B-sum mu delta=(3,3,0)
68:    =lambda b_{1,2}(a)+sum sigma b.
69: No normalization of D is needed; all coefficients can be scaled together.

71: CLAIM. There is no decomposition into global one-layer feasible duals retaining
72: aggregate lambda and nu, even if sigma changes and new zero-budget profiles
73: are allowed. In particular no such replacement can preserve the objective.
74: 
75: PROOF. In any feasible one-layer piece, a sink receiving positive comparison
76: weight must have positive nu: at a protected owner it has positive incoming
77: incidence, no outgoing incidence, and nonnegative coverage incidence. Since
78: aggregate nu is supported only at z and all piece budgets are nonnegative,
79: every supported sink of every piece must therefore be z. This also excludes
80: all added zero-budget profiles as sinks.
81: Some piece has positive coverage weight on (a,{1,2}). Because nu_a=0, its
82: balance for owner 1 (and owner 2) forces positive outgoing comparison incidence
83: at a. Thus that piece contains a supported comparison from a to its only
84: possible sink z. But a->z changes ALL THREE actual owners, so every protecting
85: coalition must have size at least three, contradicting s=2. This proof does not
86: fix sigma, comparison incidence, individual coalition labels, or their weights.
87: It uses only fixed nonnegative coverage and budget coefficients. QED.
88: 
89: The mechanism is different from the earlier harmonic charge obstruction:
90: changing or removing labels cannot create an admissible a->z comparison.
91: It also shows exactly where the two-terminal cut proof stops: multiple sources
92: allow owner's incidence to originate at different report vectors. They need
93: not admit a common coalition transport to the single sink.

95: 3. A BOUNDED BUDGET ESCAPE FOR THIS SAME FIXTURE
96: The obstruction is to PRESERVING THE BUDGET ARRAY, not to every bounded-cost
97: normalization. Keep lambda_(a,{1,2})=1 and nu_z=2, add nu_p=1, and use only

10: def verify(bs, edges, coverage, nu, F):
11:     n=len(bs); owners=len(bs[0])
12:     gamma=[[Q(0)]*owners for _ in bs]
13:     lam=[[Q(0)]*owners for _ in bs]
14:     rhs=Q(0); Delta=[Q(0)]*3
15:     for l,h,C,mu in edges:
16:         mu=Q(mu)
17:         assert mu>0 and len(C)<=2
18:         assert all(bs[h][i][r]>=bs[l][i][r] for i in range(owners) for r in range(3))
19:         changed={i for i in range(owners) if bs[l][i]!=bs[h][i]}
20:         assert changed<=C
21:         delta=tuple(sum(bs[h][i][r]-bs[l][i][r] for i in C) for r in range(3))
22:         rhs+=mu*F(delta)
23:         Delta=[a+mu*b for a,b in zip(Delta,delta)]
24:         for i in C: gamma[l][i]-=mu; gamma[h][i]+=mu
25:     cov_value=Q(0)
26:     for p,S,weight in coverage:
27:         weight=Q(weight)
