136: A precise unresolved useful repair theorem would be: for every arity-r
137: institution, every s, and every all-profile coverage/full-OUT rule Y, there
138: exist Z>=0 and efficient nonnegative production prices p such that
139:   sum_i Z_i(b)<=A_r F(B) for every profile,
140:   p(b).b_i<=Y_i(b)+Z_i(b) for every owner and profile,
141:   (Y+Z)_C(h)-(Y+Z)_C(l)>=F(delta_C)
142: for every protected comparison, with A_r=O(r) independent of owner/resource
143: counts, the report boxes and s. One may instead ask for a quantified A_{r,s}.
144: It suffices, but is stronger than necessary, to require Z itself to be TCWM.
145: The example neither proves nor disproves this additive repair statement.
146: If such a theorem held, the optimized price-reserve frontier would be at most
147: the unrestricted frontier plus A_r. Even that would not itself improve the
148: known unrestricted bound 3s^2 at r=3; a new mechanism or a new global estimate
149: would still be required.
