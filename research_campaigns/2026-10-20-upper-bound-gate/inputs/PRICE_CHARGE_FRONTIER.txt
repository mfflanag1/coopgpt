ENDPOINT PRICE CHARGING: VALID LOCAL BOUND AND EXACT REPAIR OBSTRUCTION
Independent derivation, 18 October 2026. No unrestricted upper improvement.
The basic arity/deletion-loss estimate is an established ingredient already
used elsewhere in the campaign. The contribution here is its explicit
interaction with actual dual balances and a failed compensation architecture.

1. A NONNEGATIVE PRIMAL--DUAL SURPLUS AT EACH PROFILE
At a profile b, let B=sum_i b_i, V=F(B), x an optimal nonnegative production,
and p an optimal nonnegative production dual. For each actual owner define
 u_i=p dot b_i,
 h_i=sum_j pi_j x_j sum_(a in supp A_j) b_ia/B_a,
 zeta_i=h_i-u_i.                                      (1)
Set a ratio with B_a=0 to zero; an activity using such a resource cannot
have positive x_j. These are resource shares inside one owner's payment,
not separate strategic owners or independently transported dual flows.

For support arity at most r,
 sum_i u_i=V,
 sum_i h_i=sum_j pi_j x_j |supp A_j|<=rV,
 zeta_i>=0,  sum_i zeta_i<= (r-1)V.                    (2)
To check the nontrivial nonnegativity, complementary slackness gives
 p_a B_a=p_a sum_j A_aj x_j.
For x_j>0, pi_j=sum_a p_a A_aj>=p_a A_aj. Thus
 sum_(j:a in supp A_j) pi_j x_j >=p_a B_a.
Multiply by b_ia/B_a and sum in a to get h_i>=u_i.

For any coalition C, writing t_a=b_Ca/B_a and reducing activity j by the
fraction max_(a in supp A_j) t_a gives feasible production under B-b_C.
Its lost profit is at most h(C), whence
 F(B)-F(B-b_C)<=h(C).                                 (3)
In particular h covers every coalition by superadditivity. Equations (2)--(3)
are static rank bounds, not a monotonicity theorem for the profile-dependent h.

2. VALID LOCAL PRICE-DEFECT BOUND FOR TCWM AND FULL OUT
For a comparison e=(l,h,C), the outsiders' bundle B_out is the same at both
ends. Let d_e=0 for TCWM and F(delta_e) for full OUT. Optimal prices imply
 u_l(C)<=F(B_l)-F(B_out).
Superadditivity gives F(B_l)+d_e<=F(B_h). Therefore
 u_l(C)-u_h(C)+d_e
 <=F(B_h)-F(B_out)-u_h(C)
 <=h_h(C)-u_h(C)=zeta_h(C).                           (4)
Every term uses the SAME actual C. Equation (4) holds for arbitrary optimal
primal/dual choices at the two profiles and requires no differentiability.

3. EXACT INTERACTION WITH A FINITE CROSS-PROFILE CERTIFICATE
Use the actual dual lambda,mu,nu and sigma=nu-g>=0 from
reviews/cross_profile_duality/FINITE_AND_ALL_PROFILE_DUAL.txt. Let
 L=sum lambda F(b_C)+sum mu d_e, D=sum nu F(B_p).
With k_(p,C)=u_p(C)-F(b_C)>=0, exact summation gives
 L-D =sum_e mu_e[u_l(C)-u_h(C)+d_e]
       -sum_(p,C)lambda_(p,C) k_(p,C)
       -sum_(p,i)sigma_(p,i) u_(p,i).                 (5)
There is one shared mu_e for every member at both endpoints. Combining with
(4) yields the VALID but not yet useful general upper bound
 L<=D+sum_e mu_e zeta_h(C).                           (6)

Since zeta>=0, owner-coordinate balances imply the further valid identity
 sum_e mu_e zeta_h(C)
 =sum_p nu_p sum_i zeta_pi +sum_e mu_e zeta_l(C)
   -sum_(p,C)lambda_(p,C)zeta_p(C)-sum_(p,i)sigma_pi zeta_pi.
Hence (6) alone does NOT give rD: outgoing terms remain. Dropping them is an
invalid compression. A bound on these coupled outgoing/incoming terms, with
coverage and nonnegative-payment slacks retained, is the exact missing step.
The endpoint incidence cone constrains each whole edge block; it does not
permit assigning the passive owner's flow coefficient to the restorer alone.

4. NO CONSTANT REPAIR BY THE RANK SURPLUS
A tempting candidate is y_i=u_i+beta zeta_i, beta>=0. It is nonnegative,
covers every coalition, and has funding at most 1+(r-1)beta. Choosing beta
proportional to s would give linear funding if incentives held. They do not.

Use four resources K,A,B,C and two unit-coefficient activities:
 old: consumes K,A,B, profit 1;
 new: consumes K,C, profit 1+epsilon, where 0<epsilon<1.
There are TWO fixed actual owners, both allowed the full unit cube. Along the
displayed reports owner0 holds A=B=1,C=t,K=0, and owner1 holds K=1 only, with
0<t<1. Owner0 restores only C. The optimum is uniquely
 x_old=1-t, x_new=t,
and its dual is uniquely p_K=1,p_C=epsilon,p_A=p_B=0.
Uniqueness follows from the strictly larger new profit, the binding K and C
constraints, and slack A and B constraints. Thus tie-breaking cannot help.

For owner0, the exact payments in (1) are
 u_0=epsilon t,
 h_0=2(1-t)+(1+epsilon)t=2-(1-epsilon)t,
 zeta_0=2-t.
Consequently y_0=2beta+(epsilon-beta)t. For every beta>0 choose
 epsilon=min(beta/2,1/2). Restoring t strictly decreases y_0, violating
singleton TCWM and therefore every protected-coalition family containing
singletons. This is also a full-OUT failure (restored C alone has value zero).
More generally A0*u+B0*zeta fails whenever A0>=1, B0>0: choose
epsilon=min(B0/(2A0),1/2), giving derivative A0*epsilon-B0<0.

At beta=0, optimal-price payments themselves fail. For min(A,B), use owner0
(a,0), owner1(0,1/2), and restore a from1/4 to3/4 within the fixed unit cubes.
The unique low price is (1,0), the unique high price (0,1); owner0's payment
drops from1/4 to0. A positive fixed scaling of optimal prices also fails.

For an exact rational instance of the first fixture, take epsilon=1/2 and
t from1/4 to3/4. Then
 u_0: 1/8 ->3/8; h_0:15/8 ->13/8; zeta_0:7/4 ->5/4.
The beta=1 payment loses1/4 and beta=2 loses3/4. All coalition coverage and
the displayed funding bounds still hold. The violated row is the actual
singleton comparison +y_0(high)-y_0(low)>=0, with one shared coefficient.

This rejects fixed nonnegative surplus compensation for arbitrary choices
of optimal x,p, because this fixture makes both choices unique. It does not
reject an adaptive compensation rule, a different potential, general smooth
rules, or a genuinely global dual inequality.
Indeed the fixture has only two owners, so the crude rule y_i=F(total) already
gives full OUT at factor two. Its purpose is architecture rejection, not a
new unrestricted lower bound.

5. WHY CHANGING-OWNER-ONLY CHARGING FAILS, EVEN WITH EXACT DUAL BALANCES
Take F(A,B,C)=min(A,B,C), three fixed fully eligible owners, 0<epsilon<1/2:
 low b0=(1,0,0), b1=(0,0,0), b2=(0,1+epsilon,2);
 high changes only b1 to(2epsilon,0,0).
Endowments can be the common box [0,2]^3. Protect C={0,1}, so the changed
owner set is D_changed={1}, and owner0 is genuinely unchanged and protected.
The unique optimal prices are p_low=(1,0,0), p_high=(0,1,0). The edge's price
defect is one, and its outside-production value is zero. At the high profile
 zeta_1=h_1=(1+epsilon)*2epsilon/(1+2epsilon),
which tends to zero. Thus no finite c(s), even at s=2 and arity exactly three,
can bound the price defect by c(s)*zeta_high(D_changed). Whole-coalition
zeta_high(C)=1+epsilon remains large, as required by (4).

This example is consistent with a FULLY FEASIBLE actual-coordinate dual:
give low grand coverage weight1, the one comparison weight1, and budgets at
both profiles weight1. Then
 g_low=(0,0,1), g_high=(1,1,0),
 sigma_low=(1,1,0), sigma_high=(0,0,1),
 L=1, D_budget=2+epsilon.
Adding the explicit nonnegative-payment rows cancels every payment coordinate.
The endpoint-incidence cone has M=1,w=(1,1,0); its changed coordinate equals M
and its passive capacity is exactly s-1=1. No owner-wise split of mu or dummy
zero assumption occurs. The ratio is BELOW one; this is an obstruction to a
particular local charging inequality, not a new funding lower certificate.
