VALUE-PRESERVING CAP DOES NOT REPAIR FRACTIONAL PRICE SMOOTHING
21 October 2026 UTC. Root derivation. Scoped architecture diagnostic only.

QUESTION
Can the known individually monotone, covering fractional-owner marginal rule
be capped at economically usable resource levels and then repaired by an
O(s)-scaled binomial marginal (or another correction using only coalition
worths)? The underlying uncapped fractional-rule failure is already in OLD
constructive/ROUTE_LEDGER.txt, R1. The contribution of this check is only that
a natural value-preserving cap and arbitrary worth-only compensation do not
remove that failure. No novelty, new general lower, or gate pass is claimed.

NATURAL CAP AND ITS VALID STATIC PROPERTY
For a finite packing technology, discard zero-profit activities. Put
  Gamma_a = max_e A_ae/pi_e
over the remaining activities (zero if no retained activity uses row a),
and V=F(sum_i b_i). Define capped reports
  btilde_ia=min(b_ia,Gamma_a V).
These preserve EVERY coalition worth at the given profile. Indeed any
optimal coalition production of value W<=V uses at most Gamma_a W units
of row a. Also sum_(i in C) min(b_ia,Gamma_a V) is at least
min(sum_(i in C)b_ia,Gamma_a V), hence at least that plan's row usage.
Thus the original coalition optimum remains feasible; the reverse inequality
follows because capped capacities are smaller. At V=0 all coalition values
are zero and the same conclusion holds. This is a static preservation fact,
not a cross-profile incentive or useful budget theorem.

THE CANDIDATE FAMILY BEING REJECTED
At capped reports define
  phi_i(btilde)=E[F(btilde_i+sum_(j!=i) U_j btilde_j)
                 -F(sum_(j!=i) U_j btilde_j)],
where U_j are independent uniform[0,1]. Consider payments
  y_i(b)=phi_i(btilde(b))+H_i(v_b),
where v_b is the FULL vector (F(b_C):C subseteq N), and H is any deterministic
function of that vector, with the fixed public institution also available.
No sign, linearity, budget bound, or monotonicity is assumed for H. This
includes fixed or worth-adaptive combinations of whole-owner marginal rules.
It does not include corrections using report directions beyond v_b.

EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE, WITH ALL ACTIVITIES ACTIVE
Use four fixed pure owners P,X,Y,Z, resources A,B,D, and
  F(A,B,D)=min(A,B)+D.
Activity1 uses A+B and has profit1; activity2 uses D and has profit1.
Maximum activity arity is two, so this lies in the arity-at-most-three class.
The legal endowment boxes are P: [0,1] in A; X,Y: [0,2] in B;
Z: [0,1] in D. All other owner coordinates are fixed zero.

At the lower profile P=1, X=1, Y=1, Z=1. At the upper profile restore
only X's B report to3/2. The protected coalition is C={X,Y}, including
unchanged Y, and s=2 suffices. Production is two at both endpoints.
Gamma_A=Gamma_B=Gamma_D=1, so the cap is two in every row and leaves
all displayed owner reports unchanged at both endpoints.

Every whole-owner coalition worth is unchanged: the min part is one iff
the coalition contains P and at least one leaf, and zero otherwise; the D
part is one iff it contains Z. Hence H(v_high)=H(v_low) for ANY H above.

For leaf reports x,y>=1, the independent D activity cancels from a leaf's
marginal. Conditional on the sampled P amount u in[0,1], X's full report
already saturates it. Therefore
  phi_X = E[(U_P-y U_Y)_+]
         = integral_0^1 u^2/(2y) du =1/(6y),
  phi_Y =1/(6x).
Thus the protected sum changes from1/3 to1/6+1/9=5/18, a LOSS of1/18.
The correction sum does not change. This violates pair TCWM and full OUT;
the restored B alone has outside production zero.

DISPOSITION
An independent productive anchor makes the global-output cap too loose to
remove a locally redundant report. All whole-owner worths remain unchanged,
so no correction depending only on those worths can repair this base rule.
The result also applies to any positive fixed multiple of phi plus such H.
It leaves direction-sensitive corrections, other caps, and other bases open.
It does not rule out an O(s) mechanism; it rejects this explicitly defined
attempt to obtain one from fractional smoothing plus worth-only reserves.
