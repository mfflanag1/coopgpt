even when the certificate ratio exceeds one. It may still be useful for a
selected optimal dual, after a substantive normalization, or under the proved
terminal-budget hypothesis. Do not replace these quantifiers silently.

changing budget support, or other legal normalization. Its purpose is to rule
out an unnecessarily universal formulation of the price-selection conjecture.
