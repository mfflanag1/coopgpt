1. Perea, Puerto and Fernandez (2012), Avoiding unfairness of Owen allocations
in linear production processes, European Journal of Operational Research
220, 125–131. DOI 10.1016/j.ejor.2012.01.013.
Author-hosted original, inspected sections 2 and 4:
https://grupo.us.es/gpb97/articles/Avoiding%20unfairness%20of%20Owen%20allocations%20in%20linear%20production%20processes.pdf

The production technology and resource-player matrix are directly relevant.
The paper recalls that efficient dual-price allocations belong to the static
core and studies allocations for necessary suppliers whose shadow-price
payments can vanish. Its IM property is set inclusion for specified
surplus-preserving changes of the resource matrix. It is not the present
all-report coalition-sum withholding inequality. EOwen also need not lie in
the original game's core. Thus neither the static core fact nor the generic
concern about zero payments to useful suppliers is a proposed contribution
here. This source does not, in the inspected statements, settle the present
quantitative full-OUT subsidy bound. That is a scope comparison only.
