NO FINITE SCALAR LIFT FROM COALITION COVERS TO EFFICIENT PRODUCTION PRICES
20 October 2026. Exact scoped obstruction; no unrestricted funding lower bound.

1. QUESTION AND COMPLETE ANSWER

Throughout, F is the finite nonnegative packing model in inputs/core.tex:
A,pi>=0 and every positive-profit column has nonempty resource support.
At one profile let b_i be the bundles of the fixed actual owners, B=sum_i b_i,
and let y>=0 satisfy sum_{i in C}y_i>=F(b_C) for every owner coalition C.
Ask for a nonnegative feasible production dual p satisfying both
  p.B=F(B)                         (efficiency),
  p.b_i <= c_r y_i for every i.   (coordinatewise scalar domination)
Here c_r may depend only on the maximum activity arity r.

The best universal constant is c_1=1 and c_r=+infinity for every r>=2.
The negative result holds with exactly two owners, exactly r resources,
one activity, strictly positive bundles for both owners, and a budget-efficient
static cover. It therefore applies to actual multirow owners without splitting.

For r=1, discard zero-profit activities and set q_a to the largest
profit/input ratio among activities using only row a (zero if there is none).
Then F(c)=sum_a q_a c_a. The price q is nonnegative, feasible, and efficient
at every profile. Singleton coverage gives y_i>=F(b_i)=q.b_i, proving c_1=1.
One positive owner paid exactly its worth shows that a universal c_1<1 fails.

2. STRICTLY POSITIVE STATIC COUNTEREXAMPLE

For any integer r>=2 use one unit-profit activity consuming one unit of each
of r resources. Thus
  F(c)=min_{1<=a<=r} c_a,
and its production dual is p>=0, sum_a p_a>=1.

For 0<t<1 take the TWO ACTUAL owners' reports
  b_1(t)=(1,t,...,t),
  b_2(t)=(t,2,...,2).
Both owners supply every resource row. Their total is
  B(t)=(1+t,2+t,...,2+t),   F(B(t))=1+t,
and each owner's standalone worth is t.

The static payment vector y(t)=(t,1) is nonnegative and covers all coalitions:
the singleton requirements are t and t, the grand requirement is 1+t, and the
empty requirement is zero. Its total is exactly F(B(t)).

For every nonnegative feasible price write S=sum_a p_a. Its objective is
  p.B(t)=(1+t)S+sum_{a>=2}p_a.
Since S>=1, its minimum is 1+t. Equality forces S=1 and p_a=0 for a>=2.
Consequently the efficient price is UNIQUE:
  p=e_1,     (p.b_1,p.b_2)=(1,t).
The exact least scalar domination factor for the displayed cover is
  max{1/t,t}=1/t.
Taking t=1/m, integer m>=2, gives an exact growing-ratio family at fixed r,
fixed owner count and fixed resource count. The cover is efficient throughout.
At the allowed endpoint t=0, no finite scalar works even at that one profile.

In particular, a static core allocation need not be an Owen/production-price
allocation, nor does a fixed dilation of every core allocation contain one.
This does NOT create a static optimum-cost gap: minimizing over all static
covers still has optimum factor one, attained by the efficient price allocation.

3. ONE FIXED INSTITUTION AND AN ALL-REAL, FULL-OUT RULE

Fix the preceding technology and fixed endowment boxes
  bar b_1=(1,1,...,1),   bar b_2=(1,2,...,2).
Every b_i in [0,bar b_i] is allowed. All the preceding b(t), including t=0,
are profiles of this SAME institution. Define at EVERY real profile
  Y_1(b)=F(b_1),
  Y_2(b)=F(b_1+b_2).

This rule is nonnegative. It covers owner 1 exactly; covers owner 2 because
F(B)>=F(b_2); and covers the grand coalition because Y_1+Y_2>=F(B).
It satisfies Y_1+Y_2<=2F(B), since F(b_1)<=F(B). If F(B)=0, monotonicity
forces F(b_1)=0 and both payments vanish. Its global factor is exactly two:
take b_2=0 and a productive b_1.

For completeness, verify full OUT including passive members and simultaneous
mixed restorations. Let l be a lower profile and h_i=l_i+delta_i, with
delta_i>=0 for i in the protected coalition C and delta_i=0 outside C.
Only monotonicity and superadditivity, F(x+d)>=F(x)+F(d), are used.

  C={1}: Y_1(h)-Y_1(l)=F(l_1+delta_1)-F(l_1)>=F(delta_1).

  C={2}: Y_2(h)-Y_2(l)=F(B_l+delta_2)-F(B_l)>=F(delta_2).

  C={1,2}: the sum of payment changes equals
    [F(l_1+delta_1)-F(l_1)]
       +[F(B_l+delta_1+delta_2)-F(B_l)]
    >= 0+F(delta_1+delta_2).

The formulas allow either delta_i=0, arbitrary simultaneous changes to every
resource coordinate, and F(B_l)=0. The empty coalition condition is trivial.
Thus Y is full OUT for every coalition, for every integer s>=1 under the
size-s convention, and on the entire real report box. No boundary regularity,
discretization, symmetry, dummy-zero assumption, or owner splitting is used.

At the strictly positive profiles b(t),
  (Y_1,Y_2)=(t,1+t),  (p.b_1,p.b_2)=(1,t).
The exact least scalar domination factor is again 1/t. Hence this single
all-profile full-OUT factor-two rule has NO finite uniform scalar lift, even
if the scalar is permitted to depend on the entire fixed institution or on s.
At t=0 the first payment is zero but its unique efficient-price charge is one.

4. WHAT A VALID SCALAR LIFT WOULD HAVE IMPLIED

If a number c>=1 and efficient prices p(b) existed with p(b).b_i<=cY_i(b)
at every profile, define
  u_i(b)=p(b).b_i,    rho_i(b)=cY_i(b)-u_i(b)>=0.
For every protected comparison (l,h,C), full OUT of Y would give
  rho_C(h)-rho_C(l)
    =c[Y_C(h)-Y_C(l)]+u_C(l)-u_C(h)
    >=F(delta_C)+u_C(l)-u_C(h).
Thus independent selection of prices would indeed give the required global
reserve inequality. Scaling by c>=1 preserves nonnegativity, coalition
coverage and full OUT, and multiplies the funding factor by c. If a selection
convention is desired, choose the minimum-Euclidean-norm price in the closed
convex nonempty feasible set with efficiency and the owner caps; this minimizer
exists and is unique. No report continuity is required by the model.

The failed premise is pointwise scalar domination; the construction above
does not challenge the algebra of the reserve transformation. For r>=2,
price-plus-nonnegative-reserve is NOT without loss by uniformly scaling an
arbitrary valid rule. The finite dynamic optimum-cost gap mentioned in the
parent campaign is a different statement: it compares optimized feasible
mechanisms, whereas this result excludes a specified conversion of every
mechanism. No bound on the ratio of those optimized frontiers follows here.

5. ADDITIVE REPAIR REMAINS A SEPARATE QUESTION

This counterexample has a cheap additive repair. Set
  Y'_1(b)=Y'_2(b)=F(B),
  Z_1(b)=F(B)-F(b_1)>=0,  Z_2(b)=0.
Then Y'=Y+Z, sum_i Z_i<=F(B), and Y' has factor two. It dominates every
efficient nonnegative production-price allocation because each p.b_i<=p.B.
For any nonempty protected C, its payment change is
  |C|[F(B_h)-F(B_l)] >= |C| F(delta_C) >=F(delta_C),
so Y' is full OUT. This makes explicit why failure of scalar conversion gives
no cost separation for the best price-plus-reserve mechanisms.

A precise unresolved useful repair theorem would be: for every arity-r
institution, every s, and every all-profile coverage/full-OUT rule Y, there
exist Z>=0 and efficient nonnegative production prices p such that
  sum_i Z_i(b)<=A_r F(B) for every profile,
  p(b).b_i<=Y_i(b)+Z_i(b) for every owner and profile,
  (Y+Z)_C(h)-(Y+Z)_C(l)>=F(delta_C)
for every protected comparison, with A_r=O(r) independent of owner/resource
counts, the report boxes and s. One may instead ask for a quantified A_{r,s}.
It suffices, but is stronger than necessary, to require Z itself to be TCWM.
The example neither proves nor disproves this additive repair statement.
If such a theorem held, the optimized price-reserve frontier would be at most
the unrestricted frontier plus A_r. Even that would not itself improve the
known unrestricted bound 3s^2 at r=3; a new mechanism or a new global estimate
would still be required.

6. VERIFICATION AND REVIEW SCOPE

check_price_lift.py uses exact Fraction arithmetic to verify the specified
static witnesses and the coefficient identity used in the written uniqueness
proof; it does not solve an LP or independently establish uniqueness. It checks
the dynamic construction and its additive repair on a small rational grid.
The universal all-real OUT proof is the displayed monotonicity/superadditivity
argument, not the finite enumeration. Independent review is to be coordinated
