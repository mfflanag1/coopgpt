21 October 2026 UTC. Screening plus three exact finite dual certificates.
Disposition: no upper-gate pass, no high-ratio or unavoidable cancellation
obstruction. This is a finite diagnostic, not a universal upper or novelty claim.

SCOPE AND PRIOR SEARCH
Read the OLD branching-pilot lattice infrastructure/route ledger and exact
cross-profile dual checker. Its solved binary unit-worth pure-owner family
was not rerun. Targeted searches found prior small mixed-owner selector
failures and two-owner checks, but no matching four-owner complete two-coordinate
lattice sweep. The new code uses the same standard scipy.optimize.linprog /
sparse-row approach; it does not import or modify OLD source files.

Each of 56 deterministic cases (seeds10,...,65) has FOUR fixed actual owners.
Each owner controls two distinct resource coordinates with independent binary
levels zero/endowment, giving four owner states and a COMPLETE product lattice
of 4^4=256 profiles. Owners can therefore restore either coordinate separately
or both together; this is not a zero-or-one-fixed-bundle owner model. There
are4--6 resources,4--8 activities with exactly three positive input rows,
input coefficients1or2, and profits in{1,3/2,2,5/2}. Activities share resources.
Every resource is eligible for at least one owner. Eligibility and endowments
remain fixed throughout each case.

Each unrestricted LP contains:
  1,024 nonnegative owner/profile payments plus funding factor K;
  all3,840 nonempty-coalition coverage rows (all15coalitions per profile);
  all7,520 NONTRIVIAL legal size-one/two full-OUT endpoint comparisons;
  all256 budget rows, including zero-output profiles.
Reflexive comparisons have identically zero sides and are omitted. Pair rows
with one genuinely passive member are included with the SAME coalition on
both endpoints. All legal pairwise simultaneous restorations are included.
The price-floor LP adds nonnegative prices at every profile, all production
dual constraints, exact efficiency equalities, and ownerwise domination rows.

PRICE RESELECTION REMOVES THE MOST INTERESTING CANCELLATION
Negative defects depend on the efficient-price choices. For case35, a separate
small LP minimized their weighted sum over efficient prices at its20support
profiles. Its optimum was zero. The returned rational prices satisfy every
production-dual constraint and exact efficiency equality, and all24positive-
multiplier comparison defects are nonnegative under exact Fraction arithmetic.
Their weighted positive sum is47/416, and negative sum is zero.
check_reselection.py independently replays these identities without scipy.
Hence this example's signed cancellation survives ghost erasure but NOT
efficient-price reselection. It is not evidence of an intrinsic obstruction
to a global price choice, nor does the price choice itself supply incentives.

Among all selected duals, only4of56cases had a comparison component with four
varying owners (seeds25,27,34,35). Only seed35 among those had negative initial
price defects. The top overall factors instead have two or three varying
owners and lie inside the already bounded frozen-background regime. This is
a statement about the duals returned by this solver, not all optimal duals.
