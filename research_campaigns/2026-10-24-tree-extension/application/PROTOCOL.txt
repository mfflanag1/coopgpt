Arrival-driver qualification — premeasurement protocol,24 October2026
UNAPPROVED: no timing collection until root reviews the exact freeze and sends go.

Question and scope
Can a process-isolated absolute-time scheduler meet the fixed arrival criterion
under idle and controlled Python CPU/GIL-contending consumer load, compared with
the old Event.wait producer mechanism in a consumer-process thread? This separate
study uses synthetic request IDs, no corpus, no retrieval, no Metal/MPS, no energy
sampler and no owner/pooling model. It neither replaces nor rehabilitates the
invalid sealed retrieval calibration. Passing does not authorize a serving run.

Treatments and common workload
Modes: thread and spawn-process. Both execute the same producer function, use
threading.Event.wait for the remaining absolute planned interval,
and send the same framed JSON offer through multiprocessing.Connection pipes.
Both add the same conservative early-return guard to the old Event.wait algorithm:
after EVERY return, recheck the unchanged absolute planned time and wait again
if it has not arrived. Every repeated wait remains cancellation-aware. Do not
recenter the schedule or emit early. wake_ns records the final accepted return,
not an intermediate early return. This is a premeasurement robustness repair,
not evidence for a particular host clock implementation or the old failure cause.
Each scheduler has the same otherwise-blocked cancellation-relay thread; its
multiprocessing Event is only for shutdown, not the offer timer. This preserves
the old threading.Event timer implementation in both placements.
The same consumer loop receives and logs messages in both modes. No result cache,
batching or response service exists. Startup finishes before the50ms case lead;
startup/cleanup still consume the shared phase budget.
Loads: idle(no extra consumer-process worker) and python_cpu(one continuously
runnable pure-Python integer-recurrence thread,2048 updates per stop check).
Load begins before the case schedule is sent and ends after all offers/results
or abort. Record iteration count/checksum to verify it ran; this is a synthetic
contention condition, not a calibrated percentage CPU or a model of MPS service.
If its bounded join is incomplete, report final accounting unavailable and an
operational failure, not that the load never ran. The cleanup cap is unchanged.
Do not change sys.setswitchinterval, priority, affinity, sleep policy or thresholds.
Record consumer and producer switch intervals and process IDs; the epoch field
runner_adjusted_switch_interval:false describes only this runner's lack of tuning,
not an observed history of every host/interpreter setting. Hold declared
host/power/desktop conditions unchanged; record interruptions without replacements.

Cases fixed before any new timing
Rates20,50,100,200 offers/sec. Three repetitions with distinct schedule seeds
2410242000+10*rep+rate_index(rep0..2,index0..3), disjoint from the old study.
Each case has exactly50 offered IDs0..49, positive exponential interarrival gaps
from its frozen PRNG. Do not discard, clip or resample an inconvenient gap.
Both modes AND both load levels share the same intended offsets for each rate/rep.
There are4rates*3reps*2loads*2modes=48cases and2,400 planned requests. Mode order
alternates by rep/rate/load; rate/load group order is shuffled with independent
fixed seeds2410244000+rep. All cases and offsets are in the unsigned freeze.
No schedule is chosen using timing outcomes. Repetitions use independent PRNG
streams; they are sequential runs on one host, not independent machines.

Duration and stopping
The generated sum of planned case horizons plus50ms leads must be<70seconds.
A shared80-second phase deadline includes startup, case execution and cleanup;
startup is capped at2seconds and case draining at1second after its last offer.
Bounded cleanup can follow expiry(up to approximately1.5seconds), so the proposed
collection is below90seconds. Slow host/startup may leave cases unrun; that is an
incomplete failed qualification, not permission to extend or retry. No data-driven
early success stop. Timing-criterion failures do NOT prune later frozen cases;
process/protocol/identity/timeout failures abort the phase and preserve all plans.

Clock boundary and primary qualification endpoint
Write EPOCH.json with absolute phase_start_monotonic_ns, wall_epoch_ns and UTC
before launching the first case. Keep monotonic integer nanoseconds as the
interval clock; wall UTC is provenance only. After ready, prewrite every case
planned_ns=case_start_monotonic_ns+offset_ns, with case_start=now+50ms, then send
that exact schedule to the producer. Never recenter delayed offers or wait for a
previous offer/receipt to schedule the next. Late requests stay late and count.
Record for every request:
 wake_ns: immediately after the timed wait returns;
 emitted_ns: immediately before preparing/sending the framed offer;
 admitted_ns: immediately after send_bytes returns;
 received_ns: consumer clock immediately after recv_bytes returns, before parsing.
The PRIMARY arrival jitter is admitted_ns-planned_ns. This includes producer
wakeup, interpreter reacquisition, serialization and any pipe backpressure.
Post-send admitted_ns is a conservative userspace upper bound on completed pipe
acceptance, not an exact kernel timestamp. The consumer can finish reading before
the producer regains its GIL and records admitted_ns; received<admitted is legal.
Wake/pre-send jitter and receipt delay are separately reported diagnostics.
A timely wake/pre-send alone NEVER qualifies the driver. Timely admission also
does not prove timely consumer processing; receipt delay is disclosed separately.
This endpoint is stricter than the old pre-queue.put timestamp and does not alter
any old result. Both new modes use the same endpoint and transport.

Fixed gates and comparisons
For EACH case, exact nearest-rank p99 admission jitter<=2ms and maximum<=10ms;
no omissions, duplicates, reordered IDs, early offers or missing timestamp/data.
Missing admission/receipt is failure; missing admission enters percentile as
infinity(JSON null), never leaves the denominator. With50 requests, nearest-rank
p99 is the maximum; this intentionally stringent finite-sample check is not a
population99th-percentile guarantee. The unchanged10ms maximum is still reported.
A mode/load group qualifies only if the WHOLE48-case phase is complete without
operational failures and all its12 cases meet the gates. A scheduler suitable
for both declared load levels must qualify in both groups. No pooled average or
favorable subset overrides a failed case. Report all case distributions/counts.
Descriptive comparison: pair thread/process within identical rate/load/rep and
report their p99 admission differences, alongside both absolute gates. Do not
claim a statistical confidence interval or a general performance improvement.

Identified question and exclusions
The controlled contrast tests changing scheduler placement under these two
synthetic consumer conditions on this host. Common transport and identical
schedule control obvious confounds; fixed alternating/shuffled order reduces
but does not eliminate time-varying host effects. It is not a pure GIL experiment:
separate interpreter, process placement and OS scheduling all change together.
It does not identify why every old offer was late, whole-Mac causal effects,
retrieval throughput/deadline performance, energy, scaling, or owner pooling.

Accounting and freeze
Immutable phase PLAN includes all48 cases and2,400 requests before execution.
Per-case PLAN fixes absolute timestamps before offering. RAW.jsonl retains every
offer received and final producer record; RESULT lists every planned ID, even
on partial failure. SUMMARY explicitly lists unrun cases. EPOCH fixes the shared
phase start and SUMMARY records its end. A hard kill may leave no SUMMARY; that
is an incomplete study, never success. Sender/receiver partial records survive
where available; missing final post-send times make qualification fail.
Preparation permits only static old-data inspection and deterministic fake-clock
unit tests. --prepare writes authorized:false and hashes code/tests/protocol.
Root must review, obtain cross-model QA, declare host conditions and explicitly
approve the exact freeze before --authorization can run any physical timing.
No approved file is created by preparation, and no automatic real collection,
retry, tuning, threshold change or subsequent serving experiment is authorized.
