the left vertices A,C have capacities A,C; right vertices B,D have capacities
aB,aD; the edges are AB,AD,CD. The nonredundant cut bounds are
  V=min(A+C,a(B+D),A+aD).
Fixing a left-use w_C leaves w_A=V-w_C. Necessity gives
  max(0,V-A)<=w_C<=min(C,aD,V).
Conversely, for any point in this interval route w_C to D, then route
min(w_A,aB) of A to B and the remainder to D. The latter remainder fits
because V<=a(B+D). This proves both necessity and sufficiency of the interval.
The same argument after swapping A<->D and B<->C gives V' and its interval.
Thus the selected clamped vectors really are maximum flows, including ties
and zero capacities; the interval is not merely a necessary relaxation.

For the equivalence of formulas, set T=aD and z=bC in the first component.
If T<=z, then C>=T. Its value is min(A+aD,a(B+D)), and the clamped w_C=T;
therefore w_A=min(A,aB), as in the source's positive-part formula.
If T>z, distinguish the active value:

4. SELF-CONTAINED TIE AND ZERO-BOUNDARY INTEGRATION (LINES 145--162)

The use vector w is continuous and piecewise linear, with finitely many
polyhedral cells. On the positive orthant its prices and coalition payment
map are locally Lipschitz. For fixed outsider aggregate o>=0, let
  H(o,s)=sum_v s_v p_v(o+s).
As any aggregate coordinate tends to zero, its term is between zero and s_v
and tends to zero. The remaining terms are continuous. Hence H is jointly
continuous on all nonnegative (o,s), and H(o,0)=0.

Here is a direct integration proof, without assuming a nonsmooth theorem.
Fix 0<=s'<=s and d=s-s'. Perturb outsiders to o+epsilon z, where z is a
strictly positive generic vector. Choose z and arbitrarily small positive
epsilon so that the segment c(t)=o+epsilon z+s'+td is not contained in any
proper hyperplane bounding the finitely many cells. This is possible by
avoiding finitely many linear equalities; values that cause accidental
containment can be omitted. Every c(t) is strictly positive. The segment
then has finitely many breakpoints, and its coalition payment is continuous
and piecewise continuously differentiable. At other points its derivative
is g dot d.

The checked inequalities g_v>=0 and g_u+g_v>=1 for every edge imply
g dot d>=F(d) directly: for any feasible edge packing z_e at d,
  sum_e z_e <=sum_e(g_u+g_v)z_e
             =sum_v g_v incidence_v(z)<=sum_v g_v d_v.
Taking the packing supremum and integrating gives
  H(o+epsilon z,s)-H(o+epsilon z,s')>=F(d).
Joint continuity lets epsilon tend to zero. This treats segments lying on
cell ties, zero coordinates, equal capacities, the all-zero profile, and
coalitions equal to the entire owner set. Perturbing the formal outsider
aggregate is only an analytic approximation of H; it does not reassign or
add actual owners to the claimed mechanism.

Each coalition's actual payment is H(o,s), irrespective of how its resources
are divided among its fixed members. Passive members contribute to s and
remain in the payment sum. Thus the result is full coalition OUT for mixed
owners at all real reports. Setting s'=0 gives coverage of its own production
because H(o,0)=0. Nonnegative owner payments follow directly from p_v>=0.

5. EXACT BLOW-UP VALUE AND DISJOINT-UNION TRANSFER (LINES 164--178)

Collapsing any feasible packing on a complete path blow-up gives a feasible
four-group packing, so the original value is at most the aggregate value.
For the reverse direction take any four-group packing f_XY. At a group X of
total capacity C_X>0 allocate to its vertex v and incident pair XY the amount
  r_(v,XY)=c_v f_XY/C_X.
Its total allocated use is at most c_v. If C_X=0 all incident f_XY are zero.
For an adjacent pair with f_XY>0, place on each complete bipartite edge vw
the quantity r_(v,XY) r_(w,XY)/f_XY. Its vertex marginals equal the assigned
amounts. Zero-flow pairs receive zero. The resulting packing realizes every
aggregate edge amount with exactly its original objective value. This proves
the value identity even with zero capacities and unequal group sizes.

Applying the group price to each resource exactly preserves each actual
owner's aggregate payment and every withheld bundle's production value.
It therefore transfers OUT, coverage, and the funding bound without changing
actual owners. For disjoint unions both available and withheld production
are additive, and summing component payments proves the same bound, including
owners with resources in many components. Isolated resources contribute zero.
