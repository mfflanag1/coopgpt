SHARP RESOURCE-DIMENSION BOUND FOR GENERAL COALITION PAYMENT MONOTONICITY
17 October2026. Internally accepted in ROOT_ADJUDICATION_12; root has also
rederived the upper argument directly in the final audit. Historical novelty
and external specialist review remain unconfirmed.

Let F:R_+^d ->R_+ be finite, coordinatewise nondecreasing, concave and
positively homogeneous of degree one. Fix arbitrary finitely many actual
owners with nonnegative bundles b_i and binding coordinatewise underreports.
At c=sum_i b_i, put J={j:c_j>0}, k=|J|. For j in J define
 A_j(c)=integral_[0,1]^(J\{j}) F(c_j e_j+sum_(l!=j)u_l c_l e_l) du,
 p_j(c)=A_j(c)/c_j, and pay y_i(b)=sum_(j in J)b_ij p_j(c).
Absent-coordinate terms are zero. The same formula works on every profile.

THEOREM. These nonnegative payments cover every coalition, are monotone in
TOTAL coalition payment under arbitrary simultaneous coordinatewise report
increases by that coalition, and satisfy
 sum_i y_i <= (k+1)F(c)/2 <= (d+1)F(c)/2.
The factor(d+1)/2 is optimal uniformly over all finite actual-owner populations
and all such d-resource technologies: the separately proved min_d TCWM lower
matches it (d=1 is efficient). This is a stronger incentive contract than
individual IC and does not improve the existing sqrt(d) individual-IC bound.
No polynomial-time evaluation claim is made for arbitrary F.

FINITE INCENTIVE PROOF.
For a coalition with aggregate a and outsider bundle o, its coordinate-j
payment is [a_j/(a_j+o_j)] A_j(a+o). Both nonnegative factors are nondecreasing
under ANY coordinatewise increase of a: the share increases in a_j, and every
integrand defining A_j increases with every coordinate of a by monotonicity
of F. Thus every coordinate contribution is nondecreasing. At an initially
absent coordinate its old contribution was zero; contributions on existing
coordinates still increase by the same argument. Integration over an added
coordinate introduces a uniform factor but does not lower any old integrand:
its former coordinate was identically zero. This proves cross-face comparisons
as well as positive-profile TCWM, without differentiating prices.

COALITION COVERAGE.
Work on the k-dimensional active face and draw independent uniforms U_j.
At almost every interior point x=U*c, F is differentiable on that face;
its nonnegative gradient g(x) is a supporting price and g(x).z>=F(z) for all
nonnegative z supported on that face, by concavity and homogeneity. Boundary
z is allowed: apply the support inequality along the interior segment to z,
and use concavity, without assuming continuity across different faces.
For fixed other coordinates, monotonicity and one-dimensional concavity give
 integral_0^1 partial_j F(u_j c_j,U_-j c_-j) du_j
 <= F(c_j,U_-j c_-j)/c_j.
This follows by integrating first from epsilon to1 and letting epsilon decrease
to0. The nonnegative endpoint limit can be dropped; equality with the value
at zero is unnecessary. Tonelli applies to the nonnegative derivatives.
Thus E g_j(U*c)<=p_j(c), and each derivative expectation is finite. Averaging
the supporting inequality gives p(c).z>=F(z). In particular take each actual
coalition aggregate z<=c. Its payment is p(c).z, proving coverage. The empty
active face has zero production and payments.

FUNDING.
Jensen first on each face integral gives
 A_j(c)<=F(c_j e_j+(1/2)sum_(l!=j)c_l e_l).
Jensen again over j gives
 sum_j A_j(c)<=k F(((k+1)/(2k))c)=(k+1)F(c)/2.
Both uses involve only the active face; nonnegative finite concavity suffices.
For k=1, the formula directly pays F(c), and k=0 is trivial.

STATUS AND CONNECTIONS.
For F=min_j c_j, this formula is exactly the independently audited uniform
integral multi-glove rule p_j=c_j^-1 integral_0^T product_(l!=j)(1-t/c_l)dt.
The extension is therefore a general face-average mechanism, not a new proof
that resource proportional sharing is monotone. Integration of supporting
prices, Jensen and the min_d sharp lower are separate ingredients. Historical
priority is unconfirmed. The formula has boundary payment jumps when a new
resource activates and F already had positive value on the previous face;
this does not violate binding-underreport incentives and does not supply the
global continuity assumed by a separate differential full-OUT certificate.

FULL OUT NOT CLAIMED.
On min(A,B), the formula has p_long=t/2, p_short=1-t/2. Its worst local balanced
withdrawal expression is 1-t(1-t)/2<1 for0<t<1, so it does not protect full
outside production. Any claim about full OUT needs an additional argument.
