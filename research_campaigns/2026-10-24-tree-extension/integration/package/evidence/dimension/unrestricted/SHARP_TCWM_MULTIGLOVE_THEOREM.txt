SHARP COALITION-WITHHOLDING FUNDING FOR MIXED-OWNER MULTI-GLOVE PRODUCTION
17 October 2026. Candidate theorem. Independent audit/priority review required.
Supersedes the11/6 rank-three lower in TCWM_ARITY_LOWER_FAMILY.txt.

THEOREM
Fix r>=2 and production F(c)=min_{1<=j<=r}c_j. Each actual owner may hold
an arbitrary nonnegative bundle in all r resources; eligibility sets and owner
identities are fixed before reports. Let B_TCWM(r) be the supremum over finite
owner counts of the infimum worst-case total-funding factor among rules that:
 (i) pay every owner nonnegatively;
 (ii) cover every actual coalition's standalone production; and
 (iii) make every actual coalition's TOTAL payment nondecreasing whenever
       one member increases any owned resource coordinate, all else fixed.
The third requirement is called transferable coalition-withholding monotonicity
(TCWM). Utilities consist only of these payments; there is no return from
using withheld resources outside the mechanism.
Then
                       B_TCWM(r)=(r+1)/2.                    (1)
In particular B_TCWM(3)=2, whereas the corresponding graph theorem gives3/2
for all unit-profit pairwise-resource production graphs.

The infimum allows arbitrary nonlinear, asymmetric, owner-aware rules. No
aggregate-price, smoothness, continuity, scale-invariance, or partition-
consistency axiom is imposed in the lower. The sharp factor is a supremum
over fixed finite owner models, not necessarily attained by any finite one.

UPPER
Use the already established integral prices extended to mixed owners:
 p_j(c)=(1/c_j) int_0^T product_{l!=j}(1-t/c_l)dt, T=min_j c_j>0,
 y_i=sum_j b_ij p_j; pay zero if T=0.
MIXED_MULTIGLOVE_TCWM.txt gives the full all-real-profile proof. Prices sum1,
so coalition coverage follows. A bottleneck resource contributes at most T
and every other resource at most T/2, giving (r+1)T/2. For j!=e, p_j increases
with c_e. The coalition's resource-e contribution is
 [x/(x+a)] int_0^{x+a} product_{l!=e}(1-t/c_l)_+ dt,
where x is its own resource-e total and a the fixed outside total. Both
factors increase with x. This proves TCWM, including bottleneck switches and
zero-capacity boundaries. Thus the upper is valid for arbitrary mixed ownership.

FINITE LOWER STATEMENT
For every integer s>=2, set n=(r-1)s+1. There are n fixed actual fully eligible
owners, and binary report profiles alone force every eligible rule to have
funding factor at least
  B(r,s)=1+(s-1)((r-1)s-1)/[2s^2+(r-3)s-1].                 (2)
For fixed r, letting s->infinity gives (r+1)/2. This proves the lower in(1).

PROOF OF(2)
Let kappa be a finite worst-case factor, and beta=kappa-1>=0. Average a
purported rule over the finite group of all permutations of the n owner labels
and the r resource labels. The fixed fully eligible domain, F, nonnegativity,
coalition coverage, every TCWM inequality and budget factor are preserved.
We can therefore prove the lower for the invariant averaged rule. This averaging
is mathematical symmetrization; no strategic clones or ownership changes occur.
All n owners below are present, with the same eligibility sets, at every profile.

PROFILE P0
There are s pure unit owners in each of resource groups1,...,r-2, one pure
unit owner in group r-1, one pure unit owner in group r, and s-1 zero owners.
The total number is (r-2)s+2+s-1=(r-1)s+1=n.
Each owner in the first r-2 groups receives a common payment a; the two scarce
resource owners each receive b; every zero owner receives w>=0. When r=2,
there are no first groups and every term involving (r-2)a is interpreted as0.
Grand production is1. A coalition selecting one owner from each resource group
also produces1. Therefore
 (r-2)s*a+2b+(s-1)w <= kappa,
 (r-2)a+2b >=1.
Subtracting gives (r-2)(s-1)a+(s-1)w<=beta, hence
               2b >= 1-beta/(s-1).                         (3)

PROFILE P1
Increase each of the s-1 zero owners' reports to the pure unit resource-(r-1)
report. There are now s owners in each of resources1,...,r-1 and one owner in
resource r. TCWM for the coalition consisting of the original resource-(r-1)
owner and the previously zero owners gives its new total payment at least
 b+(s-1)w>=b. At P1 the first r-1 resource groups are symmetric: permutations
of those resource labels together with owner labels preserve the profile.
Consequently, the coalition C of ALL (r-1)s owners in these groups receives
                     y_C(P1)>=(r-1)b.                      (4)

PROFILE P2
For every owner in C, increase its report to the vector that is1 in every
resource1,...,r-1 and0 in resource r. These are coordinatewise increases from
their former pure unit reports. Leave the unique resource-r owner unchanged.
TCWM for the SAME actual coalition C yields y_C(P2)>=y_C(P1).
All m=(r-1)s owners in C now report the same vector; let p be their common
payment and q the unchanged owner's payment. Grand production is1, and a
coalition consisting of one C member and the resource-r owner also produces1.
Thus
                 m*p+q<=kappa,  p+q>=1,
so
                 y_C(P2)=m*p<=[m/(m-1)]beta.              (5)
Combining(3)--(5) gives
   (r-1)/2 * [1-beta/(s-1)] <= [m/(m-1)]beta.
All denominators are positive. Rearranging,
   beta >= [(r-1)/2] / [(r-1)/(2(s-1)) + m/(m-1)]
        = (s-1)((r-1)s-1)/[2s^2+(r-3)s-1],
which is(2). The argument remains valid if the bracket in(3) is negative,
since it is an inequality used before rearrangement. QED.

CONSEQUENCES AND SCOPE
For r=3, B(3,s)=1+(s-1)(2s-1)/(2s^2-1), with n=2s+1 owners.
This approaches2. Thus no TCWM factor below2 works uniformly in the number of
fixed owners even for a single triple-input activity. The integral rule's upper2
is sharp in exactly this sense.
For unbounded r, required TCWM funding grows linearly in resource dimension
(and in the number r of minimum-defining forms) on this elementary technology.
This sharply separates the stronger TCWM requirement from the original individual
truthfulness requirement, for which the manuscript supplies sublinear general
bounds and a logarithmic-over-logarithmic form-count bound. Exact comparison
with the sharp weak-incentive multi-glove theorem should cite the manuscript's
precise current statement before promotion.
The result does not close the unrestricted weak-incentive n/d gap and does not
establish a general rank-r packing upper of(r+1)/2. Only the MINIMUM subclass
has the matching upper here. General rank-three packing may require more.
