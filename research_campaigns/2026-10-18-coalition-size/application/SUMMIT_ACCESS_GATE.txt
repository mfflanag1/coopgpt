DECLARED METADATA (NOT OBSERVED SCHEMA)
The persistent sample is a_fullperiod_10sec_100hosts.tar, advertised as 4.2GB,
127,007,113 rows and 30 variables, with a common 100-node list and day files
across Jan/Aug2020, Feb/Aug2021 and Jan2022. It contains ten-second means.
The two PSU input fields are ps0_input_power and ps1_input_power, in watts;
do not add CPU/GPU component DC readings to them. Non-powered-on source rows
were filtered before release. Consequently missing timestamps do not mean zero.

description names state, and primary-dataset lists name node_state. Neither
name can be assumed to exist in the persistent sample without its actual schema.
CC BY4.0 is documented in the author README. Timestamp timezone and averaging
label convention were not established from these documents.
