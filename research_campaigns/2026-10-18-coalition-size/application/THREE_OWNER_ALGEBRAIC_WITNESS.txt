 b1=(1,(0,1)), b2=(2,(1,-1)), b3=(3/2,(1,0)).
Owner1's full owned bundle is b1+D, with
 D=(1/2,(3/10,2/5)).
Compare its retained report b1 with restoration of the owned unit D. D and b1
are in the cone, as are all full/retained owner bundles; F(D)=0.

p=X/||X|| gives owner1 payment1. Here p is the norm subgradient;
the full production gradient is (1,-p). After restoration,
 X'=(23/10,2/5), ||X'||=sqrt545/10,

because625>545. Thus this efficient coalition-covering allocation fails individual
payment monotonicity on this feasible algebraic partial withdrawal. Coalition
coverage itself holds at both profiles.

Every pair of other owners can produce positive reserve:
 F(b1+b2)=2;
 F(b1+b3)=5/2-sqrt2 >0;

Hence there is no indispensable owner in this profile, and the third owner
has positive standalone reserve1/2. The example does not manufacture a strategic
power veto owner. It nevertheless remains a stipulated ownership model, not
evidence that any real tenant owns D or can exercise this split at a facility.
