NODE LIST, SYNTHETIC OWNERS AND OBSERVATION MASK
Use all nodes in the declared persistent-100-host list. Freeze that list using
metadata alone; hash it. If the list cannot be recovered without selecting
successful days/nodes by outcomes, stop. For a synthetic three-portfolio test
only, sort stable IDs lexicographically and assign successive nodes cyclically
to A/B/C (indices modulo3). This partition is declared synthetic and does not
pass the independent-interest gate. No regrouping to increase measured gains.

At each exact node/time key require concurrent finite, nonnegative ps0 and ps1.
Node input power is their sum. Keep measured zeros as observations unless a
documented state contradicts their validity; never create zero from no row.
Collapse only byte/logically identical duplicates. Conflicting duplicates,
null time/ID, negative power, invalid state or missing PSU are flagged and not
imputed. Do not winsorize peaks. Both within-file and cross-file checks apply.
Do not join near timestamps, round to a convenient grid, or use nearest-neighbor
matching until a separately justified clock policy is frozen.

The full pool uses exact timestamps observed for EVERY predeclared node.
Maintain the full expected calendar grid, per-node masks, full-panel mask and
counts of every exclusion. Report the distribution and maximum duration of
missing stretches. Fitting complete cases is a conditional exercise: if missing
times preferentially contain failures, it does not estimate unconditional risk.
Summit excludes powered-off source rows, so a release-complete-case panel alone
cannot establish unconditional availability. Obtain state logs or retain that
failure state, even if the numerical fit looks good.

FACTOR FIT AND CAPACITY
Proposed fit: training-only empirical mean for each predeclared unit, and full
sample covariance of the exact simultaneous training panel. Use its symmetric
positive-semidefinite square root for a common factor representation. Retain
all positive eigenmodes; clip only negative eigenvalues within a fixed numerical
tolerance 1e-10 times the covariance trace. Larger negative modes indicate a
coding/data failure, not permission to repair the model. No factor dimension
is selected by holdout performance. Freeze unit ordering and linear algebra
library/version for reproduction.

Proposed diagnostic alpha=0.05 at the published mean interval, with a common
one-sided Gaussian z_alpha. This is a transparent diagnostic level, not a
claimed industry service target. An actual reserve contract's alpha would
override it before data evaluation and requires adequate independent evidence.

Primary real-capacity analysis would use executed C_j entitlements. If unavailable,
a separate synthetic replay may size each atomic unit C_j=m_j+z_alpha*sd_j
using training alone. Then every atomic bundle is on the cone boundary; sums
of certified units are in the cone. This is counterfactual sizing, not a measured
capacity right. It can create portfolio slack S_slack; report it, not only G_div.
Verify aggregate synthetic entitlements against real delivery constraints if
any physical interpretation is attempted. Without those constraints, stop at
the algebraic replay. Keep mean, factors, capacity and alpha fixed in holdout.

CURTAILMENT AND PHYSICAL SCORING
For the synthetic unconstrained one-point diagnostic, offer constant reserve
q=z_alpha*F(B) and retain load capacity K=sum C_j-q. Headroom at time h is
sum C_j-sum L_jh. Reserve fails when headroom<q. With nonnegative observed loads,
the proposed diagnostic sheds excess E=max(0,sum L_jh-K) proportionally across
owners' positive loads; each owner's served demand is L_ih-E*L_ih/sum L_jh
(zero if total demand is zero, with the obvious no-shed convention).
This algebraic rule assumes instantly divisible interruptible loads. Actual
owner control, ramp, duration, latency and curtailment permission are hard gates.
If K<0 or any computed service is negative, the setup fails rather than being
clipped into success. A realistic dispatcher may require a new preregistration.

Report aggregate and each owner's failure frequency under the SAME rule.
Calculate uncertainty for the family of four frequencies (pool plus A/B/C),
not only a favorable aggregate. Report physical capacity/delivery constraints
separately. Mean-bin power cannot validate sub-bin peaks or power quality.

DEPENDENCE, MISSINGNESS AND TAIL EVIDENCE
Proposed diagnostic uncertainty: 5,000 synchronized 24-hour moving-block
bootstrap replicates across all nodes, seed20261018; retain cross-node common
shocks. Report 6-hour and48-hour block sensitivities. Explain that fourteen
holdout days give very few day-scale effective blocks and percentile intervals
are approximate under stationarity/mixing assumptions. Do not use an iid
binomial interval for the 10-second rows without an independently defended
independence model. No significance claim is rescued by choosing a shorter block.
Use a one-sided98.75% bootstrap upper quantile for each of the four primary
failure frequencies as a nominal Bonferroni95% family upper bound. The nominal
family adjustment does not make bootstrap coverage exact with fourteen blocks.
Apply the same synchronized resamples to the worst-case missingness version.
If dependable uncertainty cannot be justified, the reliability conclusion is
data-insufficient, regardless of a small point estimate.

Count observed violations V, observed eligible grid cells O, and missing grid
cells M. The full-calendar realized violation fraction lies in
[V/(O+M), (V+M)/(O+M)] before dependence uncertainty. Do not replace this with
V/O alone. The missingness bound is partial identification, not a probabilistic
confidence interval. A narrow complete-case interval does not close it.

For perspective, even zero events over14 independent whole-day blocks gives a
one-sided95% upper bound 1-0.05^(1/14), about19.3%, for the probability of a day
having an event. This is a DIFFERENT estimand from per-interval failure; it
shows why nominal 10-second row counts are not automatically independent tail
evidence. Day independence itself is not established. If precision at the
actual contract target is inadequate, report DATA-INSUFFICIENT and collect a
longer preregistered interval; do not weaken the target after inspection.

FALSIFICATION AND ROBUSTNESS
Preserve common-shock days, temporal bursts and largest loads. Compare the
Gaussian predicted quantile with the training-calibrated empirical aggregate
quantile under identical capacity/alpha; evaluate both once on the holdout.
Report skew/tail/dependence diagnostics and misspecified owner reliabilities.
Common-shock stress is a predeclared multiplication of the training largest
covariance eigenvalue by1.5 and2, retaining its direction; it is a sensitivity
scenario rather than a new fitted factual model. No favorable stress result
repairs failed observed service promises.

PROMOTION
Only after source identity, access, exact schema, timezone, predetermined node
list, windows, alpha, fit, capacity, masks, curtailment, deviations, payment
rules, uncertainty and valuation status are resolved may the COMPLETE protocol
be hashed and declared frozen. Then a separate bounded execution may inspect
holdout once. Outcomes permitted: survives the declared conditional audit;
rejected; unidentified/institution-unverified; or data-insufficient. None of
these makes a public HPC trace an AI workload or a causal welfare experiment.

For conditional diagnostic support, every owner and aggregate worst-case
missingness upper bound must be below the frozen target, physical/delivery
tests must pass, and the dependence assumptions must be defensible. A credible
violation above the target rejects the promise; uncertainty spanning it is
inconclusive. No fit statistic can override missing rights, value or liability.
An actual service-level certification requires a separately justified inference
design and longer evidence if this draft's short window is inadequate.
