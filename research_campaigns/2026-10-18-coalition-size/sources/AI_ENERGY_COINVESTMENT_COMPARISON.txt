ORIGINAL-SOURCE COMPARISON: SHARED DATA-CENTER ENERGY
Read 18 October 2026; application evidence remains sealed and conditional.

Yu, Araldo, Chahed and Patanè, From Individual to Shared Ownership:
A Coalitional Game Approach to Sustainable Co-investment,
arXiv:2607.26725v1 (29 July2026).
Free original: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26725
Inspected model Sections3–4 and case study Section5.

This paper models operators jointly choosing renewable capacity using demand
profiles, time-varying supply, procurement/export prices, capital costs and a
utilization subsidy. It formulates the coalition surplus as a linear-production
game and applies dual prices to construct a core allocation. A three-data-center
PV case uses benchmark parameters and modeled load profiles. Thus shared
energy investment, heterogeneous demand and dual-price core allocation cannot
be claimed as new merely by applying them to data centers.

The displayed formal results concern coalition exit at fixed inputs. They do
not establish the all-report partial-withholding contract of this campaign.
The paper is also not compatible simultaneous measured power evidence for our
proposed institution. Our potential contribution would require verified rights,
a specified withdrawal/sponsor contract, and an evaluated reliable-headroom
benefit; the present metadata-only gate establishes none of those outcomes.

This is a close application comparison, not a correctness or novelty judgment
on that paper. No outreach or external data collection was triggered.
