DYADIC GRID COMPLETENESS FOR ONE FIXED FINITE INSTITUTION
Root candidate18October2026. Routine compactness tool, not a new funding
bound or a claim that any finite experiment verifies all real reports.
Independently audited; effectivity and attainment wording clarified.
Opposite-model review completed; accepted with the stated effectivity scope.
See reviews/REGULARIZATION_AND_PRICE_ROOT_DISPOSITION.txt.

Fix finite actual owners, finite resources and activities, nonnegative fixed
A,pi, no resource-free profitable activity, and finite fixed coordinatewise
endowments e_i. Eligibility is encoded by zero coordinates of e_i. Production
F is the finite fractional packing value. Fix the protected coalition family
(e.g. all sets of size<=s), impose nonnegative payments and coverage of EVERY
coalition. Use either TCWM or full OUT, always with fixed owners and arbitrary
coordinatewise restorations by members of a protected coalition.

Let D_m be the finite report domain whose every owner/resource coordinate is
an integer multiple of2^(-m) in[0,e_ij]. Let K_m be the optimal funding factor
for ALL coverage and legal endpoint comparisons on D_m. At a zero-production
institution define the optimum as zero; otherwise use the usual nonnegative
minimum K. Let K_* be the unrestricted all-real optimum in the same institution.
Then
                       K_* = sup_m K_m = lim_m K_m.       (1)
The finite-grid optima are nondecreasing. Each finite LP minimum and the
all-real optimum are attained as funding factors; this does NOT say the
supremum is reached at any finite grid. The all-real mechanism need not
be constructive or measurable.
For every m, y_i(b)=F(sum b) gives a feasible factor n even for full OUT, because
F(B+delta)-F(B)>=F(delta), and a nonempty coalition has at least one member.
Thus all factors are finite and at most n.

PRODUCTION CONTINUITY
A feasible nonnegative dual cover exists: a sufficiently large equal price
covers every positive-profit column. The dual feasible set is a pointed
polyhedron with finitely many vertices, so F(B) is the minimum of finitely
many finite linear forms p·B. This gives continuity on the entire nonnegative
orthant, including zero faces. Zero-profit resource-free columns do not affect
F. Equivalently use the standard finite-LP piecewise-linear value theorem.

PROOF OF (1)
Restriction proves K_m<=K_* and nested dyadic grids prove monotonicity. Put
L=sup_m K_m. The finite LP is feasible and has an optimal payment vector;
nonnegative payments are bounded by nF(sum e), so an attained optimum follows
from a closed bounded feasible slice with K<=n. Choose one optimal rule y^m
on each D_m. For an arbitrary real report b let R_m b be its coordinatewise
dyadic floor. Then R_m b<=e, R_m b -> b, outsiders equal at two endpoints
remain exactly equal after rounding, and coordinatewise comparisons are
preserved. The original actual owners are never split or changed.

Take ONE nonprincipal ultrafilter U on the natural numbers, and for EVERY
profile and owner simultaneously define
             y_i(b)=lim_U y_i^m(R_m b).
Each sequence is uniformly bounded in[0,nF(sum e)], so the ultralimit exists.
The same U is used for all profiles, owners and inequalities. Finite sums and
ordinary convergent scalar limits commute with this limit. Nonnegativity and
coverage pass because F(sum_C R_m b_i)->F(sum_C b_i). Funding passes with
K_m<=L and F(sum_i R_m b_i)->F(sum_i b_i).

For b<=b' restoring only a protected C, the rounded endpoints are a legal
comparison in the SAME finite mechanism y^m. TCWM passes directly. Full OUT
passes because sum_C(R_m b_i'-R_m b_i) -> sum_C(b_i'-b_i), so continuity of F
converts its rounded outside-production RHS to the required exact one.
Thus y is an all-real L-funded rule and K_*<=L. This proves (1), including
attainment. An equivalent proof uses compactness and a subnet of whole rule
fields; no countable subsequence convergence at all uncountably many profiles
is asserted. The ultrafilter construction uses the usual choice/compactness
principle already underlying all-profile LP compactness.

EXACT COMPUTATIONAL CONSEQUENCE
For rational technology data AND rational finite endowments (so the legal
grids are effectively enumerable), each finite-grid coalition worth and comparison
RHS is a rational LP optimum, so the finite payment LP has a rational optimum
and an exact rational primal/dual certificate. Given any rational k<K_*, (1)
guarantees that SOME finite dyadic grid has K_m>k. Enumerating m and solving
these finite rational LPs is therefore a complete semidecision procedure for
REFUTING a proposed factor strictly below the fixed institution's optimum.
No effective convergence rate, uniform grid size over technologies, efficient
running time, or finite stopping test for K_*<=k follows. A passed finite grid
is only a lower approximation and cannot verify an all-real upper mechanism.
No limit is taken over owner sets or public technologies in this statement.

This lemma reuses standard LP continuity, finite duality and compactness. Its
role is to justify a countable exact search domain without weakening owners,
coverage, incentive coalitions or full OUT. It is supporting methodology, not
a further research-success gate or a novelty claim.
