EXACT HOMOGENIZATION AND THE LIMIT OF POINTWISE JACOBIAN SEARCH
Root derivation, 18 October 2026. Internally audited; scope clarified19October.
Supporting structure only; no improvement to the funding interval is claimed.

Throughout, funding means the REPORT-INDEXED axiom
  0<=y_i(b),  sum_i y_i(b)<=K*F(sum_i b_i)
at every legal report, including zero-output reports. A uniform bound by
K*F(total endowment), used in the earlier smoothing proof to bound an
integrand on a finite box, is only a consequence of this axiom. Replacing
report-indexed funding by that weaker endowment bound would invalidate the
homogenization below. The finite nonnegative packing technology has no
positive-profit resource-free activity and the actual owners stay fixed.

1. ENDOWMENT MAGNITUDES DO NOT CHANGE THE OPTIMUM
Fix the actual owners, public packing technology, and eligible coordinates.
Compare two finite coordinate boxes e and f with exactly the same positive
coordinates. There is a common scalar c>0 such that c*e<=f. Given an admissible
K-funded rule y on box f, define z(b)=y(c*b)/c on box e. All actual owners,
outsider equalities, protected coalitions, and restoration directions remain
the same. Packing homogeneity gives F(c*b_C)=c*F(b_C), including restored
outside production. Thus z preserves nonnegativity, every coverage condition,
TCWM or full OUT, and the EXACT factor K. Reversing e and f proves equality
of their optimal factors. This does not change eligibility or allow overreports.
Positive endowment magnitudes matter empirically, but not to this worst-profile
homogeneous contract when every binding underreport down to zero is admissible.

2. A TRANSLATION-INVARIANT MEAN AT NEGATIVE INFINITY
There is one positive normalized linear functional M on ALL bounded real
functions on R with
 M[f(.+a)]=M[f] for every real a,
 M[f]=0 whenever f(t)=0 for all sufficiently negative t.             (1)
No measurability of f or countable additivity is asserted.

For completeness, use the already proved positive translation-invariant
extension I of ordinary integration on bounded compact-support functions on R
(BANACH_REGULARIZATION_CANDIDATE.txt, Section1, instantiated in dimension1).
Its agreement with ordinary integration gives I[1_[-T,0]]=T. For T>0 set
 M_T[f]=(1/T)*I[f*1_[-T,0]].
These are positive normalized linear functionals with |M_T[f]|<=||f||_infinity.
The product of these bounded coordinate intervals is compact. Take a cluster
point as T tends to infinity, using a cofinal subnet. Translation invariance
of I and the two intervals' symmetric-difference length give
 |M_T[f(.+a)]-M_T[f]| <= 2|a|*||f||_infinity/T.
Consequently the cluster point M is invariant under every fixed translation.
If f vanishes on a negative half-line, its contribution to [-T,0] has bounded
length and tends to zero after division by T; this proves the second part
of (1). All these properties pass simultaneously in the product topology.
This is a classical invariant-mean/compactness construction, not a novel
functional-analysis claim or a computable averaging algorithm.

3. EXACT-FACTOR HOMOGENEOUS RULE ON THE ELIGIBLE ORTHANT
Suppose y is any finite-K admissible rule on one finite positive endowment box.
For an arbitrary finite nonnegative report b supported on the SAME eligible
coordinates, define the bounded function
 g_(i,b)(t)=exp(-t)*y_i(exp(t)*b) if exp(t)*b lies in the original box,
             0 otherwise.
It obeys 0<=g_(i,b)<=K*F(sum b), by homogeneity and funding on its domain.
Every fixed finite list of reports lies in the original box after multiplying
by exp(t) for all sufficiently negative t. Define a single rule
                      Y_i(b)=M[g_(i,b)].                         (2)
The SAME M is used for every owner and every report.

For any coalition coverage inequality, it holds for the g-functions on a
negative half-line, with RHS F(b_C). Applying positive normalized M and (1)
passes it to Y. Funding and nonnegativity hold for all t and pass directly.
For any legal fixed-owner endpoint comparison, both rescaled profiles are in
the box on a negative half-line; TCWM passes the same way. In the OUT case,
exp(-t)*F(exp(t)*sum_C d_i)=F(sum_C d_i), so the exact outside RHS passes too.
Explicitly, if a bounded inequality residual h is nonnegative for t<-R,
write h=h*1_(t<-R)+h*1_(t>=-R). Positivity gives a nonnegative mean to the
first summand, while (1) kills the second. Subtract a constant right-hand
side first and use M[1]=1. Each constraint has finitely many endpoints and
can use its own R; no uniform half-line for all comparisons is required.
No zero-owner payment is assumed zero.

For every lambda>0 the domain condition in g transforms in exactly the same
way as its argument, so
  g_(i,lambda*b)(t)=lambda*g_(i,b)(t+log(lambda))
for ALL t, including those where the definition is zero. Translation
invariance and linearity give Y(lambda*b)=lambda*Y(b). Thus (2) is positively
homogeneous, exact-K funded, and satisfies the original incentive contract on
the entire eligible orthant. Restrict it to any fixed finite owner box when
stating an admissible institution. No owner count or eligibility changed.

In particular an attained optimal factor on one box can be attained by a
homogeneous rule (possibly nonmeasurable) there and extended to all boxes with
the same eligibility. The existence is nonconstructive. Homogenization alone
does not ensure Borel regularity or continuity across zero faces.

4. HOMOGENEOUS SMOOTH RULES HAVE THE SAME INFIMUM
Apply the previously checked coordinatewise multiplicative smoothing to Y.
Its fixed diagonal contractions commute with a common scalar dilation, and
linearity preserves Y(lambda*b)=lambda*Y(b). The result remains homogeneous,
is smooth within each positive-coordinate face, and has factor K/alpha for
any 0<alpha<1, retaining coverage and the same TCWM or OUT contract. Hence
the unrestricted infimum equals the homogeneous facewise-smooth infimum.
Exact-factor smooth attainment and continuity across zero faces still do
not follow. At positive-face interior points, Euler's identity is now valid:
  Y_i(b)=sum_((j,a) in J) b_(j,a)*JAC[i;j,a].              (3)
The local separator and directed admission remain the correct conditions.

5. A POINTWISE JACOBIAN PROGRAM CAN ALWAYS PRETEND THAT K=1
Fix ANY profile b and ANY optimal nonnegative production dual p at B=sum b.
Assign the following payment values and candidate Jacobian at this one point:
  u_i=p*b_i,
  H[i;j,a]=p_a if i=j, and0 otherwise.                    (4)
The values are nonnegative, cover every coalition by dual feasibility, and
sum to F(B). Euler's identity (3) holds with H. Every local TCWM inequality
holds since H has no negative entries. For every coalition and nonnegative
restoration direction, its local payment rate is p*sum_C d_i>=F(sum_C d_i),
so ALL local full-OUT inequalities hold as well, for every s.

Thus independent feasibility at each point of the local incentive cone,
Euler identity, static coverage and funding can NEVER refute factor1 for
any packing technology. This is an exact general obstruction to using those
pointwise constraints alone as an upper or lower proof. The campaign's
growing lower bound shows that a globally feasible payment rule need not exist
despite this pointwise feasibility.

The missing requirements include consistency with nearby binding inequalities
and the requirement that H be the derivative of ONE payment rule. For example,
with one owner, F=min(A,B), b=(1,1) strictly inside its positive endowment
box (for example [0,2]^2), and exact K1 funding, nonnegativity plus
coverage/funding force y=F. A C1 jet cannot realize this kink: the two positive
coordinate directions imply H_A<=0 and H_B<=0 from funding, while Euler gives
H_A+H_B=1. Thus a richer pointwise program including binding-budget derivatives
can exclude an EXACT smooth K1 jet. This is an attainment/regularity issue;
it is not a positive funding gap, since the infimum may still be1.

A stronger local limitation holds for EVERY factor kappa>1 at productive
profiles. Fix b with F(B)>0 and a nonnegative optimal dual p at B. Continuity
of F gives a neighborhood U of b, relative to the eligible orthant, on which
F(B')>0 and p*B'/F(B')<kappa. The actual affine rule z_i(b')=p*b_i'
on U is smooth, homogeneous as a formula, nonnegative, covers all coalitions,
and satisfies full OUT for all legal endpoint comparisons staying in U.
It is kappa-funded on U. These are genuinely consistent local payment rules,
not just candidate jets. The neighborhood need not be a whole downward report
box; zero-worth and cross-face boundaries are deliberately not covered by
this local statement. Consequently local differential tests at productive
profiles alone cannot force any factor bounded away from1.

In (4), assigning different optimal prices at different points ignores the
price derivatives and jumps. Locally valid formulas need not agree on their
intersections or integrate to one globally valid rule. The local separator
remains useful for checking a supplied integrable formula or coupled
piecewise-affine synthesis. It does not remove the need for consistency
across profiles, all boundary inequalities, and zero-face admission.
