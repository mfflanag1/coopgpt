INDEPENDENT CONSTRUCTION QUESTION, OPENING GATE
Read CAMPAIGN_CONTRACT and the baseline first. The hard target is a bound for
arbitrary arity-three packing technologies with growing owner/resource counts.
Fixed owner identities, real multirow binding reports, every coalition covered,
and payment-sum monotonicity for coalitions of size<=s, including unchanged
members. A TCWM improvement is useful even if full OUT is not proved; distinguish.

Current general bounds remain Omega(s^(1/4)) and3s^2. No new upper survives yet.
Whole-owner fixed positive semivalue weights are quadratically blocked. Merely
changing the subset law or correlating it stays inside that known class.

Opening failures, all exact and fixed-owner:
* z_i=F(B+(s-1)b_i)-F(B-b_i) has coverage and(r+s-1)F budget but fails even
  pair TCWM on the min2 star (see supplied root note).
* Uniform fractional-owner marginal averaging still fails on a star with
  two surplus leaves; fractionalization alone does not handle unchanged members.
* Minimizing unweighted squared payments over the coalition-cover polyhedron,
  with any convex nondecreasing penalty on total payments, fails size-three
  TCWM on a two-branch common-cap technology (supplied exact proof).
* Choosing one profile-adaptive GLOBAL p(b),beta(b) in beta*m_i(p;b) appears
  still quadratically blocked: hard min3 component forces beta*p^2>=1/3,
  additive anchor bounds beta byK, and a long star leaf-restoration chain forces
  p<=about1/s. This new architecture proof is being checked separately.
* An ownerwise coverage-normalization of binomial marginals fails a pair test;
  a separately weighted-QP candidate is also rejected. Do not reuse without
  an explicit term that compensates unchanged coalition members.

Independent routes already active elsewhere: interacting-family lower LPs,
and profile-adaptive normalized-surplus/potential-deletion candidates. You may
choose a genuinely different route: e.g. a general report-transition extension
lemma with packing-specific dual charging, or an owner-aware selection principle
with an explicit local coalition derivative inequality. A scalar serial-flow
analogy alone is insufficient: preserve arbitrary multirow owner underreports.

Deliver at most one serious candidate: exact formula or lemma, how it yields a
subquadratic bound if established, and a checked proof or a precise unresolved
inequality. Test obvious stars, the two-branch shared cap, mixed owners, zero
reports, and unchanged coalition members. Reject your own candidate if these
fail. A conjectured lemma with no demonstrated general consequence is not success.
Do not assert a theorem, novelty, or efficient algorithm without proof. A clean
failed attempt is preferable to an overgeneralized analogy.
