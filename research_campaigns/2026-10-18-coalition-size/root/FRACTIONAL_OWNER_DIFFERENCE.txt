FRACTIONAL-OWNER DIFFERENCE: LINEAR BUDGET, FAILED COALITION INCENTIVE
Derived in the opening gate, 18 October 2026. Exact rejection of a candidate
architecture, not a lower bound for arbitrary mechanisms.

MODEL AND CANDIDATE
Let F be a finite, monotone, homogeneous concave packing value function with
activity arity at most r. Actual owners report bundles b_i and B=sum_i b_i.
For s>=1 define
  z_i(b)=F(B+(s-1)b_i)-F(B-b_i).
These are well-defined evaluations of the public technology, not new owners or
feasible alternative strategic profiles. Final payments are nonnegative.

COVERAGE
Write U_i=F(B)-F(B-b_i). Monotonicity gives z_i>=U_i. A nonnegative dual optimum
p at B satisfies F(c)<=p*c and p*B=F(B). Thus U_i>=p*b_i, and for any coalition C,
sum_C z_i>=p*sum_C b_i>=F(sum_C b_i).

BUDGET
The same dual optimum gives
  F(B+(s-1)b_i)-F(B)<=(s-1)p*b_i.
Hence sum_i z_i <= sum_i U_i+(s-1)F(B) <= (r+s-1)F(B),
using the established arity bound on the sum of grand-coalition marginals.
This arithmetic is useful but does not establish an admissible mechanism.

EXACT INCENTIVE FAILURE
Take r=s=2, F(x)=min(x_1,x_2), and three fixed owners
  b_1=(1,0), b_2=(1,0), b_3=(0,3).
Protect C={1,2}. Restore d_1=(delta,0), d_2=0, with 0<delta<1.
Before restoration the payment vector is (2,2,2).
After restoration it is (2,2-delta,2+delta).
Therefore the protected sum falls from 4 to 4-delta. This violates TCWM.
The restored bundle has F(d_1+d_2)=0, so it also violates full OUT.
The unchanged member 2 is essential. Owners and eligibility never change.

DISPOSITION
Reject this candidate as a general coalition mechanism. Do not promote its
linear budget estimate into an upper bound. It lies outside the previously
blocked fixed positive whole-owner sampling class, so that older obstruction
alone would not have rejected it; the concrete restoration check does.
