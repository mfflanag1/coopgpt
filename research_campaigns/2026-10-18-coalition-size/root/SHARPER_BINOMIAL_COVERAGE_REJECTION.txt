EXACT REJECTION OF A TEMPTING BINOMIAL COVERAGE CONSTANT
18 October2026. A rule-specific counterexample, not a general mechanism lower.

Candidate: for arity r, sum_(i in C)m_i(p) might always be at least
min(1,r*p^(r-1))*F(b_C). This would improve the existing sampled-core coverage
constant. The conjecture is FALSE, including at arity3 and p=1/2.

Use six resource rows A,B,C,u,v,w and four fixed actual owners. Owner0 holds
one unit each of A,B,C; the other three hold two units of u,v,w respectively.
Every report is a binding coordinatewise underreport of these fixed bundles.
There are four activities, each with unit resource coefficients and profit1/3:
  ABC; Auv; Buw; Cvw.
Every activity has arity exactly3. At the displayed full reports, coalition
worth is0 without owner0,1/3 for every proper coalition containing owner0,
and1 for the grand coalition.

Proof of the values: with fewer than two private owners only ABC is possible.
With exactly two private owners, only the corresponding branch can accompany
ABC, and their sum of activity levels is at most1 on its shared cap row.
Thus their total profit is at most1/3, attained by ABC. At the grand profile,
all three branches at level1 are feasible (each private row supplies two),
giving value1. Pricing A,B,C each1/3 and the private rows0 is a feasible
full-technology dual of cost1, proving optimality. Without owner0 all activity
cap rows are zero.

For independent p-thinning of all four owners, the expected worth is exactly
  f(p)=p/3+(2/3)p^4.
Hence total binomial marginals equal f'(p)=1/3+(8/3)p^3. At p=1/2 this is
2/3, below the conjectured3/4 of the grand value. More explicitly owner0
gets5/12 and each private owner1/12. No floating optimizer is needed.

Discovery provenance: a random arity3 packing scout of1200 instances and34947
coalition comparisons found no counterexample. An abstract totally-balanced
MPH3 LP then produced this value table at four owners. Root constructed the
actual sparse packing realization above. This is a concrete reminder that
passing a substantial finite random scout does not prove the proposed bound.

The abstract relaxation also reports failures at p=1/3 and1/4 for larger
owner sets; those have NOT been realized as sparse packing technologies or
exactly certified here, so they are not additional contract counterexamples.
The exact result already rejects the universal claimed constant. It does not
rule out a different improved constant or a theorem with extra restrictions.
