MIN-PRODUCTION COMMON-PRICE TCWM CALIBRATION
Existing fixed-resource calibration, independently derived for checking code.
This is not an arity-three theorem with unbounded resource dimension, and not
a full-OUT theorem. The literature/previous-project attribution is still to be
attached by the source audit before manuscript use.

Let F(x)=min_{1<=v<=r}x_v. At strictly positive aggregate x=sum_i b_i put
m=min_v x_v and D=sum_v 1/x_v. Define a common nonnegative price vector:
  if mD>=2: p_v=(1/x_v)/D;
  if mD<2: choose the unique minimizing coordinate k, put
      p_v=m/(2x_v) for v!=k,
      p_k=1-sum_{v!=k}m/(2x_v).
For r=1 the second formula gives p_1=1. Pay y_i=p*b_i.
If any aggregate coordinate is zero, pay every owner zero.

COVERAGE AND BUDGET
Prices are nonnegative and sum to one, so sum_C y_i>=min_v sum_C b_iv.
In the first region total payment is r/D <= (r/2)m.
In the second it is ((r+1)/2)m-(m^2/2)sum_{v!=k}1/x_v
  <=((r+1)/2)m.
Thus the global factor is (r+1)/2; for r=3 it is at most 2.

ALL-COALITION TCWM
Fix the protected coalition's aggregate c with 0<=c<=x. Restoring one of its
resources j increases both c_j and x_j at unit rate.
In the first region write N=sum_v c_v/x_v. Coalition payment Q=N/D has derivative
  ((x_j-c_j)D+N)/(x_j^2 D^2) >= 0.
In the second region
  Q=c_k+(m/2)sum_{v!=k}(c_v-c_k)/x_v.
For j!=k its derivative is m(x_j-c_j+c_k)/(2x_j^2)>=0.
For j=k its derivative is
  1+(1/2)sum_{v!=k}(c_v-c_k-m)/x_v
  >=1-sum_{v!=k}m/x_v >0,
using c_v>=0 and c_k<=m, and sum_{v!=k}m/x_v=mD-1<1 in this region.
At mD=2 the two price formulas agree; ties for the
minimum cannot occur strictly in the second region. At zero-production
boundaries the budget bound forces every payment to tend to zero.
Along one coordinate segment the minimum has finitely many switches and each
regional formula is rational in the changing coordinate. The switching equation
mD=2 consequently has finitely many roots unless it holds on a whole interval,
where the formulas agree. The zero-coordinate portions are constant zero.
Thus payments are continuous and absolutely continuous on each finite segment;
integration proves TCWM for every coalition, of any size.

WARNINGS
This calibration separates arbitrary TCWM payments from fixed positive
whole-owner marginal sampling. It does not satisfy a demonstrated full-OUT
contract. It does not improve the general arity-three bound: its one activity
has arity r, and the constant grows with r.
