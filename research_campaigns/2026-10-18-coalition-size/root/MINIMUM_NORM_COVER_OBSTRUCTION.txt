MINIMUM-QUADRATIC COVER SELECTION AND TOTAL-PAYMENT PENALTIES FAIL TCWM
18 October 2026. Opening-gate architecture obstruction, independently checked
by root from constructive-lane calculations; further independent review pending.
This is not an unrestricted funding lower bound and does not pass the campaign
gate requiring an improvement of the general arity-three frontier.

1. THE TEMPTING CONSTRUCTION
At each report profile minimize
  J(y)= (1/2)sum_i y_i^2 + psi(sum_i y_i)
over y_i>=0 and sum_{i in C}y_i>=F(b_C) for every coalition C.
Here psi is any finite convex nondecreasing function on nonnegative totals.
It may even be selected separately at each profile. The minimizer is unique
by strict convexity. Coverage and nonnegativity are automatic.

In particular psi(T)=lambda*T^2/2, lambda>0, gives a simple general budget
estimate. Compare with an efficient nonnegative LP-dual core allocation z:
sum z_i=F(B), sum z_i^2<=F(B)^2. Optimality and J(y)>=lambda*(sum y)^2/2 give
  sum y_i <= sqrt((1+lambda)/lambda)*F(B).
Choosing lambda about s^(-2) suggests O(s) funding. The following exact
counterexample invalidates the required coalition incentive, regardless of
the nondecreasing penalty. The budget calculation itself is correct.

2. FIXED TECHNOLOGY, OWNERS, AND REPORTS
Five resource rows: P,X1,Y1,X2,Y2. Two unit-profit activities use respectively
(P,X1,Y1) and (P,X2,Y2), so arity is three and
  F(P,X1,Y1,X2,Y2)=min(P, min(X1,Y1)+min(X2,Y2)).
Seven fixed pure-resource owners:
  three power owners each report P=1/3;
  A1 and B1 each report one unit of X1 and Y1 respectively;
  A2 reports X2=0 initially and restores it to 1;
  B2 reports Y2=1 throughout.
Each owner's eligibility and full report bound stay fixed. Only A2 changes.
Protect C={A1,B1,A2}, of size three. Two members are unchanged.

3. ALL COALITION CONSTRAINTS
A coalition with k power owners and at least one complete active private pair
has worth k/3, since 0<=k/3<=1. Without a complete pair it has worth zero.
At the low profile only pair1 is complete. At the high profile either pair is
complete. These observations describe EVERY coalition's production, including
mixed pairs, absent resources, and zero-report owners.

4. LOW PROFILE: EXACT UNIQUE MINIMIZER
The five essential owners (three power plus A1,B1) form a worth-one coalition.
Thus their payments sum to at least one; Cauchy-Schwarz bounds their squared
sum below by 1/5. Equality is attained by giving each 1/5 and giving A2,B2 zero.
For a coalition with k power owners and pair1, payment=(k+2)/5>=k/3 for k<=3.
So this vector is feasible. It simultaneously minimizes the quadratic term and
total payment (=1); any nondecreasing psi preserves its unique optimality.
The protected coalition receives 2/5.

5. HIGH PROFILE: EVERY SUCH PENALTY GIVES A LOSS
By uniqueness and the symmetries of the profile, all three power owners receive
t and all four private owners receive u. Feasibility requires 3t+2u>=1.
At an optimum equality holds: otherwise scale both positive values down until
equality, decreasing the quadratic term and not increasing psi. This scaling
preserves all coalition inequalities when t,u are symmetric: with equality,
  k*t+2u-k/3 = 2u*(1-k/3)>=0, 0<=k<=3.
Consequently t=(1-2u)/3, 0<=u<=1/2, and total payment is T=1+2u.
The quadratic term becomes
  Q(u)=(1/2)[(1-2u)^2/3+4u^2].
It is strictly minimized at u=1/8 and is strictly increasing for u>1/8.
Because psi(1+2u) is nondecreasing, a minimizer of Q(u)+psi(1+2u) cannot
have u>1/8. Therefore the protected coalition's new total is at most 3/8.
It falls by at least 2/5-3/8=1/40. For protected size s>=3 this violates TCWM,
and hence full OUT. This particular coalition does not refute the s=2 rule.
The restored bundle contains only X2, so its outside production is zero.

For the unpenalized rule psi=0, the exact high vector is three copies of1/4
and four copies of1/8; the loss is exactly1/40.
For psi(T)=lambda*T^2/2 with lambda>=0, explicit differentiation gives
  u=max{0,(1-3lambda)/(8+6lambda)}, t=(1-2u)/3.
This confirms the same loss for every nonnegative lambda. Thus allowing a
different nonnegative lambda at each profile does not evade this obstruction.

6. PAIR-SIZE FAILURE OF EVERY FIXED POSITIVE QUADRATIC PENALTY
For any fixed lambda>0, use R>=1/lambda power owners each reporting1/R, with
the same two private pairs. At low, each power owner and pair1 owner gets1/(R+2).
At high the penalized optimum pays every power owner1/R and all private owners0.
A direct KKT certificate uses the two full-power-plus-pair coverage constraints,
with multiplier mu=(1/R+lambda)/2 each. The power gradient is1/R+lambda=2mu.
At every zero private payment the gradient minus its constraint multiplier is
lambda-mu=(lambda-1/R)/2>=0. Primal feasibility and complementarity hold;
strict convexity proves unique optimality. All k-power constraints also hold.
The pair {A1,A2} loses1/(R+2) when only A2 restores its X2 resource.
This rejects any lambda(s)>0 chosen independently of owner count even for s=2.
The independent audit gives the sharper exact pair threshold. Before private
payments hit zero, the pair change is
 [1-R(R+3)lambda]/[(R+2)(1+R+Rlambda)].
It is negative exactly when lambda>1/[R(R+3)]; after private payments vanish
it stays negative. Thus lambda=s^(-2) already fails with R=s power owners.

7. RELATED UNWEIGHTED Lp SELECTION
For min sum_i y_i^p over the same cover polyhedron, 1<p<=2, let a=1/(p-1)>=1.
The low seven-owner solution is still1/5 on essential owners. At high,
the first-order equations give t=2^a*u, u=1/(3*2^a+2)<=1/8. The same size-three
coalition therefore loses at least1/40.
For any fixed p>2 there is already an unbounded budget on an arity-two star:
one unit center owner and M unit leaf owners, F=min(P,sum leaves). Symmetry and
the edge coverage constraints give t+u=1 and t=M^a*u, a=1/(p-1)<1. Total
payment=(M^a+M)/(1+M^a), which grows as M^(1-a). For1<p<=2, fixed positive
rescaling cannot repair the exhibited negative incentive increment. Forp>2,
fixed positive rescaling preserves the unbounded funding ratio; the displayed
size-three increment need not itself be negative in that range.
The p=1 selector is not uniquely defined; it is not analyzed by these formulas.

8. SCOPE AND PROVENANCE
For s>=3 this rejects unweighted quadratic cover selection, nondecreasing
convex total penalties, and fixed unweighted Lp objectives in the stated ranges.
For s=2 the proved incentive obstruction applies to fixed positive quadratic
total penalties; unpenalized quadratic selection is not refuted here at s=2.
For p>2 the owner-independent funding bound fails regardless of s. It says
nothing against owner/report-dependent weighting of the individual quadratic
terms, a different feasible-selection objective, or arbitrary mechanisms.
