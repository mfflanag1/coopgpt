1. EVERY COALITION'S WORTH
For arbitrary nonnegative capacities, the primal has x1,x2>=0,
x1+x2<=P, x1<=X1,Y1 and x2<=X2,Y2. Its optimum is
 min(P,min(X1,Y1)+min(X2,Y2)).
With R equal power owners, a coalition containing k of them has P=k/R<=1.
Each private pair contributes either0 or1, except that low A2 contributes0.
Thus any coalition containing a complete active pair has worth k/R, and all
other coalitions have worth0. This includes coalitions with BOTH pairs and
incomplete mixed pairs. The root check's (k,4-bit-mask) reduction is valid;
binomial multiplicities do not matter for checking each inequality.

2. LOW MINIMIZER, INCLUDING ANY PROFILE-SPECIFIC PENALTY
Let E be the R power owners plus A1,B1. Coverage gives sum_E y>=1. For
nonnegative payments,
  sum_i y_i^2 >= sum_E y_i^2 >= (sum_E y_i)^2/(R+2) >=1/(R+2).
The proposed y=1/(R+2) on E and0 elsewhere attains every bound. It covers
all coalitions because (k+2)/(R+2)>=k/R iff k<=R. Its total is1, the minimum
allowed by coverage. Therefore a nondecreasing psi cannot improve on it;
equality of the quadratic bound forces this unique vector. This argument
does not assume the low profile's feasible set permutes power/private owners.

3. EXISTENCE, UNIQUENESS AND HIGH SYMMETRY
The quadratic term is strictly convex and coercive. A finite convex
nondecreasing psi is bounded below by psi(0), and is continuous at every
positive total. For F(B)>0, the feasible totals stay in that interior domain;
a minimizer exists and is unique. For F(B)=0, y=0 uniquely minimizes directly
by nonnegativity, monotonicity of psi and the positive quadratic term.

At the HIGH profile, arbitrary power permutations and private-pair exchanges
(including within-pair swaps) preserve the coalition-value function. They are
transitive on the three power owners and on all four private owners separately.
Uniqueness forces payments(t,t,t,u,u,u,u). This is an invariance of payments
WITHIN THE FIXED PROFILE. Selecting a different scalar psi for another profile,
or selecting psi with knowledge of owner names, does not invalidate it: at a
given profile psi depends only on the candidate payment total.

For general R, all symmetric coverage constraints are equivalent to
  t,u>=0, R*t+2*u>=1.
Sufficiency is the exact identity
  k*t+2*u = (k/R)*(R*t+2*u)+(1-k/R)*2*u >= k/R.
Any slack in the full-power constraint permits common scaling down to equality,
strictly lowering the quadratic objective and weakly lowering psi. This holds
also when one coordinate is zero. Hence t=(1-2u)/R, 0<=u<=1/2, T=1+2u.

4. ARBITRARY CONVEX NONDECREASING TOTAL PENALTIES
For R=3,
  Q(u)=1/2[(1-2u)^2/3+4u^2], Q'(u)=(-2+16u)/3.
If u>1/8, replacing it by1/8 strictly lowers Q and weakly lowers psi(1+2u).
Thus every optimum has u<=1/8, without assuming psi differentiable. At low,
the protected fixed coalition{A1,B1,A2} receives2/5; at high it receives3u,
so the change is<=-1/40. A2 alone restores X2 and the other two protected
members are unchanged, exactly as allowed by the campaign's TCWM definition.
The restored bundle has F=0. Failure is TCWM and therefore also full OUT.

5. QUADRATIC PENALTY AND PAIR KKT
For psi(T)=lambda*T^2/2, differentiation on the equality face yields
  u=max(0,(1-R*lambda)/(2+2R+2R*lambda)), t=(1-2u)/R.
This matches the stated R=3 formula. At R*lambda>=1, u=0,t=1/R,T=1.
Put multiplier mu=(1/R+lambda)/2 on each of the two full-power-plus-pair
coverage constraints, and zero on the other coalition constraints. The power
stationarity equation is1/R+lambda=2mu. Each zero private payment has
nonnegativity multiplier lambda-mu=(lambda-1/R)/2>=0. Complementarity holds.
Other coalition constraints may also be active; zero multipliers on them are
valid. Strict convexity gives unique global optimality, not merely stationarity.
The fixed pair{A1,A2} then changes from1/(R+2) to0. R is an integer satisfying
R>=1/lambda. This proves the claimed pair obstruction for each fixed lambda>0.

OPTIONAL SHARPENING (SAME ARCHITECTURE, NOT A NEW GENERAL LOWER)
The boundary regime u=0 is unnecessary. In the interior R*lambda<1,
  Delta_pair=2u-1/(R+2)
            =[1-R(R+3)lambda]/[(R+2)(1+R+Rlambda)].
In the boundary regime Delta_pair=-1/(R+2). Combining the regimes gives the
EXACT threshold for this pair in this family:
  Delta_pair<0 iff lambda>1/[R(R+3)].
At equality the pair is unchanged. For integer s>=2 and lambda=s^(-2), R=s
already gives a negative protected-pair change with s+4 owners. The construction still has five resource
rows. It remains a rejection of this selector, entirely consistent with other
efficient/bounded mechanisms on fixed-row technologies. The independent checker
verifies this formula in all its rational cases; for R=10,lambda=1/100 it gives
Delta_pair=-1/444 despite R*lambda=1/10<1.

6. BUDGET ESTIMATE
For a finite feasible packing value with no positive-profit resource-free
columns, a nonnegative optimal dual vector w induces z_i=w*b_i. It is an
efficient nonnegative all-coalition cover: sum z=F(B), sum z_i^2<=F(B)^2.
For the specific nonnegative quadratic total penalty, optimality gives
  lambda*T(y)^2 <= (1+lambda)*F(B)^2.
The stated square-root budget bound is therefore correct for lambda>0.
No such inequality is claimed for an arbitrary possibly negative psi.
The failure is the incentive property, not this budget calculation.

7. FIXED UNWEIGHTED Lp EXTENSION
For p>1, strict convexity gives uniqueness. The low solution is still equal
on the R+2 essential owners, by the power-mean inequality. At high R=3,
the active-face derivative of3*t^p+4*u^p gives t^(p-1)=2*u^(p-1).
With a=1/(p-1), t=2^a*u and u=1/(3*2^a+2). Thus1<p<=2 implies a>=1,
u<=1/8 and the same protected size-three loss at least1/40.

For fixed p>2, the separate arity-two star has full value1, one center payment
t and M equal leaf payments u. Every nonzero coalition includes the center
and a leaf, so coverage is exactly t+u>=1. Scaling gives equality. Minimizing
t^p+M*u^p gives t=M^a*u and total (M^a+M)/(1+M^a), asymptotic M^(1-a).
Since a<1, funding is unbounded with owner count. For p=3 and M=N^2 the
total is EXACTLY N; the checker verifies N=2,4,16,64.

SCOPE CLARIFICATIONS
(a) This family does not show an unpenalized size-two TCWM failure: at lambda0,
the pair change is +1/[(R+1)(R+2)]. The unpenalized quadratic rejection shown
here is size-three; the positive-penalty rejection is size-two. The root's
explicit claims respect this distinction; preserve it when summarizing.
(b) For p>2 the demonstrated failure is BUDGET, not necessarily the same
negative incentive increment. For example at p=3 the seven-owner protected
size-three payment is3/(3sqrt2+2)>2/5, so that particular increment is positive.
The last rescaling sentence should be read narrowly or clarified as:
'Fixed positive rescaling preserves the exhibited negative increments and
also leaves the star budget unbounded.' No p>2 incentive theorem is needed.
(c) p=1 selection is nonunique and correctly excluded. Owner/report-dependent
individual quadratic weights, profile-dependent p, different objectives and
arbitrary mechanisms are outside this rejection. Scalar psi may vary by profile
in the size-three result, as proved above.
