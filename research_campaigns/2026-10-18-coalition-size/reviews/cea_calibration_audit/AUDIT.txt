1. DEFINITION AND ACTUAL-OWNER CONTRACT
For each pool, the estate E=min_a T_a lies between0 and its total claims.
The capped sum is continuous and nondecreasing in the water level, so a
solution exists. Equal total capped awards at two ordered levels force each
award equal. Thus the payments are unambiguous even at a nonunique level.
The specified smallest level is0 when E=0 and max(claims) when E equals total
claims; this includes the all-zero case. The rule uses the original actual
owner labels and reports throughout, with no split owners or additional
payment floor. Zero endowments and arbitrary fixed coordinate boxes cause
no difficulty because the rule is first defined on the whole orthant.

2. FUNDING AND ALL-COALITION COVERAGE
Each pool allocates estate E exactly, so total payment is exactly rE,
including0 at zero output. In any bottleneck pool h, the estate equals total
claims; every owner therefore receives its entire h claim from that pool.
For every actual coalition C, its payment includes sum_C b_(i,h), which
dominates min_a sum_C b_(i,a). This proves coverage for every coalition,
independently of the protected size bound. No dummy-zero premise is used.

3. FINITE COALITION MONOTONICITY AND FULL OUT
The one-pool lemma is correct under its explicit fixed-coalition assumptions.
If the water level weakly rises, each coalition member's award weakly rises.
If it falls, every outsider's unchanged claim receives weakly less; since the
estate weakly rises, subtracting outsiders from total awards proves that the
coalition's total weakly rises. Unchanged members of C remain included. The
lemma never assumes that the level itself is monotone, which would be false.

Along any actual coalition restoration, all total resource reports and their
minimum estate are nondecreasing. Thus the one-pool lemma applies separately
between every pair of times to every pool's aggregate C-award. The finitely
many affine resource totals have finitely many crossing times. On each closed
interval between them a single bottleneck h can be chosen throughout,
including endpoint ties and coincident lines. Its full-claim awards give
exactly the coalition gain (v-u)D_h; every other pool contributes a nonnegative
gain. Summing interval comparisons telescopes to full OUT, since D_h is at
least min_a D_a. This is a finite endpoint argument, not an unverified
derivative or admission argument. Zero output, tied paths, upper boundaries,
simultaneous report restorations and inactive coalition members are included.

The same argument actually gives the scoped stronger inequality
  Y_C(high)-Y_C(low) >= E(high)-E(low),
because the selected bottleneck increments telescope to the estate increment.
This implies the stated OUT bound by superadditivity of the minimum. It is
a consequence within this calibration and does not extend the technology
class or yield a new unrestricted frontier.

4. FINITE RATIONAL PL COVER, TIES AND ZERO COORDINATES
For every non-bottleneck pool, a proper saturated subset leaves q>=1 owners
at the common level. The formula L=(T_h-sum_S claims)/q and the listed closed
inequalities ensure min(claim_i,L) is exactly the stated affine award and
that their sum equals T_h. Conversely, selecting the claims strictly below
the smallest water level gives a proper set whenever E<total claims; at
equality, leaving maximum claims outside the set works. When all claims
vanish the outside set can be all owners and L=0. Thus every report is covered.

Every formula on every overlap computes the same unique CEA awards. Finite
closed-cover pasting therefore proves continuity on any fixed box. This
includes zero faces, so there is no missing admission condition. Rational
endowments give rational region inequalities and linear formulas; restricting
to positive-endowment coordinates handles degenerate boxes. The complete
arrangement refinement argument gives a full-dimensional partition: off its
finitely many hyperplanes, membership in each original region is constant,
and at least one region covers the chamber. Finite closure then covers all
remaining boundary points. No arbitrary-mechanism PL density claim follows.
Scaling reports and the levels proves homogeneity. The count
r(2^n-1)^(r-1) correctly counts the proposed regions, allowing duplicates,
empties and lower-dimensional regions before refinement. The r=1 case also
works: y_i=b_(i,1), with a single region and factor1.

5. PRODUCTION-CHAMBER BARRIER CALIBRATION

1/2+min(t,1/2). At t=1/5,1/2,4/5 its values are7/10,1,1, so the middle

why it is outside the earlier one-affine-formula-per-production-chamber class.
No mechanism for arbitrary shared-resource packing is supplied or implied.
