1. OWNED DOMAIN AND COORDINATE MAP
The linear map (t,x)->(a,b)=(t+x,t-x) bijects the cone t>=|x| with
the nonnegative quadrant, and satisfies t-|x|=min(a,b). Its inverse is
(a,b)->((a+b)/2,(a-b)/2). The two owned units L and R map respectively
to(1,0) and(0,1), and L+R maps to(1,1). Thus each of THREE fixed owners has
four possible retained bundles, giving4^3=64 profiles. There is no splitting
of one owner into separate L/R players.

Every retained bundle and its withdrawn complement lies in the cone. The
restoration used in the proof is0->L for one owner, with another coalition
member unchanged. It is a permitted owned-unit restoration. The result depends
on exactly this TCWM convention: unchanged members still enter coalition
payment changes. It would not automatically hold under a different convention
that required every member to restore strictly positive resources.

At full reports each owner has value1 and any pair has value2. Therefore no
single owner is indispensable for all nonzero production over this domain.
Pivotal owners can arise at particular underreports without becoming a fixed

4. INTERPRETATION AND BOUNDARIES
The expensive original-rule profile lies in the SIX-ELEMENT orbit including
both owner permutations AND global L/R exchange. For precision, the text's
'may be a permuted one' can say 'may be another profile in this symmetry orbit.'
There is no guarantee that the named(L,R,L) profile is expensive for every
nonanonymous rule.

All inequalities in the certificate involve positive-production profiles.
No budget bound at a zero-production profile is used, and the zero-report
owner may receive a nonzero payment. Nonnegativity is used exactly where
declared. The result does not add bargaining floors or require a marginal
contribution payment.

At full reports production is3 and pointwise efficient coverage payments
(1,1,1) exist. The uniform lower does not locate a deficit there or at any
measured real profile; it forces expense somewhere in the allowed domain.
It does not determine the sharp uniform constant or provide an individual-OUT
upper bound on the same institution. Hence no optimal guarantee-cost separation
follows from this corollary alone.

Interpreting25% as external cash requires the stated financing assumption:
one unit of production revenue finances the baseline, normalized utility
payments convert to cash through the same public value, and there are no other
unmodeled funds/costs. It is a conditional premium at some report, not measured
or expected subsidy, sponsor net benefit, or a universal gain-only guarantee.

Real applicability still requires three independently controlled owners, actual
detachable capacity-and-load units, the stipulated opposite public factor
exposures, joint outside rights, deliverability, curtailment/failure liability,
common reliability and public value. Neither the algebra nor the existing
Summit/M100 metadata establishes these. Treating a physical-node partition as
these owners would not validate the institution.
