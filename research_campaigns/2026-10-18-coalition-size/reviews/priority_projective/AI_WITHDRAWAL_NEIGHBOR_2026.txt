Root checked18October2026. No novelty conclusion and no audit of its proofs.

Di Feng, Chenhao Zhang, Zhanzhan Zhao, Incentivizing User Data Contributions
for LLM Improvement under Withdrawal Rights, arXiv:2605.07419v1,8May2026.
Free primary original: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07419v1
Read Sections2.1 and3.1, equations1-3, and Proposition3.

This paper models a threshold public good, quadratic private contribution
costs, a posted per-unit subsidy, and assigned data contributions that users
may partly withdraw before training. Section3.1 explicitly assumes disclosed
costs are verifiable or auditable; Proposition3 concerns whether to disclose,
not unrestricted truthful reports of unauditable costs. This is useful context
for any later AI-data institution. It is different from our homogeneous
fractional packing, all-coalition coverage, and worst-profile coalition-size
funding question. Neither the threshold technology nor its equilibrium
analysis establishes our TCWM/OUT theorem. Conversely our theorem does not
solve its private-cost, assignment, or public-good provision problem.

Keep this as a related-work lead. It supplies neither simultaneous compute
power traces nor ownership/withdrawal rights for the current reserve pilot.
No new application lane or empirical validation follows from this source.
