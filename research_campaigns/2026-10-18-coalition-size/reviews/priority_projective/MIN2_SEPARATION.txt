FULL OUT DOES NOT IMPLY INCUMBENT POPULATION MONOTONICITY
Independent derivation, 2026-10-18. This is a scope-separation lemma, not a
new research-gate result or a priority claim.

Technology and actual owners
----------------------------
F(A,B)=min(A,B), a single nonnegative packing activity requiring both resource
rows. Fix ANY finite roster of owners, each reporting an arbitrary nonnegative
two-row bundle (a_i,b_i). Let A=sum_i a_i, B=sum_i b_i. Define

  y_i = min(A,B) [ a_i/A + b_i/B ],

with a zero-denominator fraction set to zero. These fractions have zero
numerator whenever their denominator is zero. The formula is defined at every
real profile; it does not split owners or presuppose a grid.

Budget, nonnegativity, and every-coalition coverage
-------------------------------------------------
y_i>=0 and sum_i y_i=2F, including F=0. For coalition C put U=sum_C a_i,
V=sum_C b_i. If A<=B with B>0 then y_C=U+AV/B>=U>=min(U,V). The case B<=A
is symmetric; if a total is zero, coalition production is also zero. Thus
every coalition is covered at every profile.

Full outside-production withholding protection for every coalition size
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix the outsiders' aggregate (A0,B0)>=0. Coalition payment as a function of its
own aggregate is

  H(U,V)=min(A0+U,B0+V) [U/(A0+U)+V/(B0+V)].

In the region A=A0+U<=B=B0+V, with positive denominators,

  H=U+AV/B,   H_U=1+V/B>=1,   H_V=A B0/B^2>=0.

In the other region,

  H=V+BU/A,   H_V=1+U/A>=1,   H_U=B A0/A^2>=0.

Restore any aggregate bundle (d,e)>=0 along
(U_t,V_t)=(U-d+t d,V-e+t e), t in [0,1]. In the first region dH/dt>=d;
in the second dH/dt>=e. Hence dH/dt>=min(d,e) wherever differentiable.
The path crosses A=B at most once (or lies in it); H is continuous and
piecewise differentiable along the path. Zero-total endpoints follow by
continuity; a path on A=B has the same compatible directional derivative.
Integration yields

  y_C(high)-y_C(low) >= min(d,e)=F(withdrawn coalition bundle).

Only C changes. This proves the full OUT contract, and therefore TCWM, for
ALL real profiles, partial multirow reports, and ALL actual coalitions.

Failure of incumbent/population monotonicity
-------------------------------------------
At reports ((1,0),(0,1),(0,0)) payments are (1,1,0). Restore owner 3 to (1,0).
Payments become (1/2,1,1/2). Owner 1's unchanged resource bundle receives less,
although coalition {1,3}'s sum stays 1. Thus the rule is not population
monotonic on binary participation subprofiles, while it satisfies even full
OUT with K=2 on the entire real multirow domain.

Relevance to the closest quantitative original
-----------------------------------------------
Immorlica--Mahdian--Mirrokni's two-leg max-flow lower-bound example has the
same min(A,B) technology (one unit of flow pairs one edge from each leg).
Their profit cross-monotonicity requires EVERY incumbent's payment to rise
when other players enter. That hypothesis fails here. Consequently their
linear funding lower bound for that stronger class cannot be read as a
linear lower bound under TCWM or full OUT. This is a concrete logical
separation, not just a change in names.

Independent finite diagnostic
-----------------------------
min2_population_separation.py checks the formula with exact rational
arithmetic on the three-owner grid {0,1/2,1}^6, all coalitions, and every
coordinatewise-lower coalition report. Actual executed counts are in its
JSON output. The all-real proof above, not the finite test, establishes the
stated scope.
