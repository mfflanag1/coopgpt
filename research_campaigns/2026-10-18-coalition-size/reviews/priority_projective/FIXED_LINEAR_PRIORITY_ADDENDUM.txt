FIXED LINEAR COALITION-WORTH RULES: PRECISE PRIOR-LITERATURE COMPARISON
2026-10-18. This is a scope/priority audit, not a proof validation.

TARGET
The candidate in constructive/FIXED_LINEAR_WORTH_RULE_QUADRATIC_BARRIER.txt
fixes one min_r technology, the same full [0,1]^r bundle domain for every
actual owner, and y_i(b)=sum_S alpha_(i,S) F(b_S), with signed coefficients
independent of the report profile. Coefficients may depend on the technology,
roster and box. Under nonnegativity, all-coalition coverage, TCWM up to s and
uniform funding kappa_n, it claims liminf_n kappa_n >= s^(r-1)/r.
For r=3 this is the stated quadratic architecture barrier. This is NOT the
arbitrary-rule projective lower and does not establish a matching general
mechanism lower. No proof-status improvement is assigned by this audit.

THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS
1. Linearity in F versus Additivity as an axiom on a whole domain.
   If a single fixed coefficient array is applied to F,G and F+G, the formula
   is formally additive in their coalition values. But the target allows the
   array to depend on F, and requires the guarantees only for one min_r.
   Formal linear extension of its formula to other production functions need
   not be nonnegative, balanced, or satisfy the other axioms there. Thus it
   does not supply a Moulin--Sprumont or Moulin--Vohra additive method on all
   monotone functions. Conversely, those methods need not use one report-
   independent coefficient array at every profile of this fixed technology.

2. The broad flow representation does NOT require Dummy.
   Moulin--Vohra (April 2003), Theorems 1--2, printed/PDF pp3--4,
   https://www.ruf.rice.edu/~econ/papers/2003papers/07Moulin.pdf
   characterize nonnegative, exactly balanced additive shares over ALL
   monotone integer-demand cost functions, at each fixed demand vector x.
   Labeled-chain mixtures and unit flows allow any agent to receive an edge's
   cost increment. The path/flow can vary with x, but is independent of C at
   that x. Dummy is an extra restriction in Corollary 3, p5, which assigns an
   increment to the agent whose coordinate increased. Therefore dismissing
   their general flow theorem solely because the target lacks Dummy would be
   incorrect. The universal-function, exact-budget and domain requirements,
   plus the different profile-dependence, are the actual obstructions to
   immediate application. This source has no TCWM-size or funding-rate result.

3. The older strong-monotonicity impossibility is qualitative and conditional.
   Moulin--Sprumont 2004, Proposition 2(ii), p12 (S1 in SOURCE_THEOREM_MAP),
   assumes Additivity and Separability, with nonnegative exact budget balance
   built into the model. Lemma 1, p10, gives their Dummy-compatible flow form.
   Their n>=3 incompatibility does not establish the target's s-dependent
   lower bound on a single technology with subsidized payments and no
   Separability assumption. The target's proposed fixed-technology coefficient
   isolator and vanishing padded-owner remainder must be proved directly;
   neither is a consequence of silently imposing Dummy.

4. A relevant published bundle extension exists; do not overstate scalar scope.
   Sprumont (2005), On the Discrete Version of the Aumann--Shapley Cost-Sharing
   Method, Econometrica 73(5), 1693--1712, DOI 10.1111/j.1468-0262.2005.00633.x:
   https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2005.00633.x
   The original publisher abstract advertises an extension to owners consuming
   bundles. Its full published bundle theorem was NOT retrieved here and is
   an unresolved precise lead. The accessible June 2004 author version,
   https://cireqmontreal.com/wp-content/uploads/cahiers/11-2004-cah.pdf,
   proves scalar characterizations via Additivity, Dummy and No Merging or
   Splitting (printed p6/PDF9), or No Reshuffling plus other axioms (p8/PDF11).
   It does not contain that published bundle extension. No Merging/Splitting
   varies identities; No Reshuffling restricts aggregate-demand-equivalent
   reallocations. These are not the target TCWM condition. The earlier version
   must not be passed off as verification of the later extension's scope.

5. Cross-monotonic profit schemes impose a different incentive property.
   Immorlica--Mahdian--Mirrokni, Definition 3.3 and Theorems 3.6--3.7, pp11--13,
   https://immorlica.com/pubs/costshare.pdf, already provide funding lower bounds
   on flow/matching profit games under incumbent-by-incumbent population
   monotonicity. Those lower bounds are not premised on fixed linear formulas.
   They therefore do not explain a separation between linear and adaptive
   rules under TCWM. MIN2_SEPARATION.txt proves the incentive distinction even
   with full OUT and all-real two-row owner reports.

ASSESSMENT
The fixed-linear target is situated near additive cost/production sharing,
not outside that literature. None of the inspected statements supplies this
exact asymptotic rate with its single-technology and actual-bundle quantifiers.
That checked mismatch is NOT priority clearance. Any eventual claim should
state the architecture restriction in its title/abstract, credit additive
flow/chain representations and strong group monotonicity, and complete the
published bundle-extension and later citation checks before asserting novelty.

The source map and intake ledger contain exact locators, short quotations,
original hashes and access outcomes. No source's theorem is being extended
from scalar agents to arbitrary owner bundles by relabeling coordinates.
