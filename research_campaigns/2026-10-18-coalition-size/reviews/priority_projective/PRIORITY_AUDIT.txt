EXACT CLAIM BEING COMPARED
-------------------------
For a fixed finite nonnegative packing technology,
  F(c)=max{p.x : Ax<=c, x>=0},
each positive-profit column has at most r nonzero resource rows. Actual owner
i reports a whole resource bundle b_i; all coordinates can be partially
withheld. The projective witness fixes a common full real unit cube as every
owner's domain, and fixes the technology and roster throughout all reports.
An arbitrary payment rule y, without a formula, additivity, anonymity, or
regularity restriction, must satisfy at every profile:
  y_i>=0;
  y_C(b)>=F(sum_(i in C) b_i) for EVERY coalition C;
  y_C(b)>=y_C(b') whenever |C|<=s, only C changes, and b'_i<=b_i;
  sum_i y_i(b)<=K F(sum_i b_i).
Full OUT strengthens the third right side by F(sum_C(b_i-b'_i)), so a lower
bound using TCWM alone also applies to full OUT.

The audited target texts claim K_3(s)=Omega(sqrt(s)); the general-arity
candidate claims, for fixed r>=3,
  K_r(s)=Omega_r(s^((r-2)/(r-1))).
This audit compares their stated scope, not the mathematical proof's status.
In particular, the general-arity text itself labels its independent proof
review pending. No optimality or matching upper bound is implied here.
The bounded-s tail refinement and its small exact example are quantitative
corollaries of the same construction, not a separate priority category.

WHY THE DIFFERENCES MATTER MATHEMATICALLY
-----------------------------------------
1. Incumbent monotonicity is not a harmless reformulation of TCWM. The
   independently checked min2 rule satisfies even full OUT with K=2 for any
   finite roster and all real two-row bundles, but an unchanged owner's
   payment can fall when another owner restores a report. Thus adding that
   incumbent condition can change the funding problem fundamentally.

2. Static core nonemptiness does not settle a cross-profile selection
   problem. At a given packing profile, any optimal dual price a>=0 gives
   y_i=a.b_i, total F(sum_i b_i), and y_C>=F(sum_C b_i). This proves efficient
   coverage at each separate profile. It does not prove the existence of
   one selection satisfying TCWM across all those profiles. Conversely a
   static projective core obstruction cannot simply be imported into this
   fractional packing setting, where pointwise efficient coverage exists.

3. A scalar demand/input is not one resource coordinate of a multirow owner.
   Splitting an owner into resource agents changes the allowed coalitions,
   side payments, and the size parameter s. A scalar-domain flow theorem
   therefore needs a genuine domain-transfer argument, not relabeling. This
   is not a claim that all related literature is scalar: the published 2005
   Sprumont article advertises a bundle extension whose exact theorem remains
   unverified in this audit (S15).

4. A partial-withdrawal outcome with prior-contract arbitration is not an
   unconditional allocation rule on the reported resource profile. Mapping
   one to the other requires proving that all retained payments can be
   represented by the SAME y(b') independently of the original outcome and
   deviation. The inspected OCF theorem does not assert this compatibility.

5. Size parameters cannot be interchanged: arity r counts resources used by
   an activity; s counts protected actual owners; n may count total or active
   owners; a static blocking-family cap removes different inequalities.
   The target keeps r fixed while increasing s and the witness dimension.

READINESS OF A NARROW RESEARCH CLAIM
----------------------------------
The potential contribution, if the proof and later priority work hold, is
the quantitative cross-report obstruction under this exact arbitrary-rule
contract, with arity controlling the growth exponent. Do not frame the
contribution as discovering coalition resource withholding, strong group
monotonicity, the need for subsidies under incentives, projective geometry,
or square-root lower bounds in cooperative games.

No inspected theorem invalidates the stated target solely by prior
subsumption. This is weaker than establishing novelty. The unresolved leads
and inaccessible originals are in SEARCH_AND_ACCESS_LOG.txt. Citation chaining
beyond the inspected works and checks of later published versions remain
appropriate before an external priority assertion.
