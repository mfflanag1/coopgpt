1. GENERAL-k EXACT WEIGHTS, INCLUDING THE RESTORER PAYMENT
Assume the parent binary-circuit geometry and legitimate finite group average.
Fix k=r-1>=2, s>=2, M>=1, and set
  a_m=min(s-1,k(m-1)), m=2,...,M,
  P=product_(m=2)^M a_m/(a_m+1),
  B=1+[k(M-1)/(k+1)]P.
Let q_m be the averaged payment of an active private owner at C_m; z is the
terminal root payment. Let eta_m be the averaged payment of the SAME actual
restoring owner l_m at C_(m-1), where its current report is zero. It need not
be paid zero. If additional owners are present at C_M, let o be their total
averaged payment; otherwise o=0 and its terms/row can be omitted.

Use these exact inequalities, with the displayed positive weights:

  Row                                              Weight
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  K-z-kM q_M-o >=0                                  1
  z+k q_M >=1                                       1
  (k+1)q_1 >=1                                     lambda
  (a_m+1)q_m-a_m q_(m-1)-eta_m >=0                  w_m
  eta_m >=0                                        w_m
  o >=0                                            1

Here
  w_m = [k(M-1)/(a_m+1)]
        product_(j=m+1)^M a_j/(a_j+1),
  lambda = [k(M-1)/(k+1)]P.
For M>1 every listed seed/transition weight is strictly positive. For M=1
the seed weight is zero and there are no transitions, so omit those rows.
Only the terminal coverage and budget (plus optional o nonnegativity) remain.

Cancellation proof. Terminal coverage and budget leave coefficient
-k(M-1) at q_M. It cancels w_M(a_M+1). For 1<m<M, the q_m coefficient is
  w_m(a_m+1)-w_(m+1)a_(m+1)=0.
At q_1 it is lambda(k+1)-w_2 a_2=0. The z coefficients cancel; each -w_m
eta_m cancels the explicitly weighted nonnegative row; -o cancels o>=0.
The K coefficient is one and the positive right side is 1+lambda=B. Thus
the exact weighted sum is K-B>=0, with no omitted payment term.

If the nonnegative rows are left out, the sum is instead
  K-B-o-sum_m w_m eta_m >=0.
This still implies the bound by payment nonnegativity, but it is not exact
cancellation unless those rows are included. Setting eta_m=0 is unjustified.

The transition protects a_m chosen OLD active actual owners plus l_m. It has
a_m+1<=s members and changes only l_m's report. Every chosen old owner has
payment q_(m-1) before and q_m after averaging. Whole branches are unnecessary.
The inactive high owners are neither silently deleted nor inserted into this
coalition. Coverage has no coalition-size restriction, so the seed/terminal
branch of k+1 owners is valid even when k+1>s.

2. COMPRESSED ORBIT VARIABLES VERSUS ORIGINAL PAYMENTS
The q,z,eta notation is shorthand for linear averages of original payment
variables, not an assumption that the original rule is anonymous or symmetric.
For G=GL(V) times the fixed-owner permutation group, write an action as (g,pi),
where pi maps an old owner label to a new label. Let (g,pi)b put g(b_i) at
owner pi(i). Here g PERMUTES RESOURCE INDICES labelled by binary vectors; it
does not mix real capacity coordinates using binary arithmetic. Define
  bar y_i(b)=(1/|G|)sum_(g,pi) y_(pi(i))((g,pi)b).
All reports remain in the same actual owners' full real unit cubes.

Apply every base coverage, comparison, budget and nonnegative row to EVERY
action, using its base weight divided by |G|. Each row is a legal inequality
in the ORIGINAL payment variables. Stabilizers establish the compressed orbit
identities in the general proof. Equal transformed rows are not independent
assumptions: they are legitimate repeated summands with their weights added.

The general scalar checker validates the above coefficient identity for 2,520
parameter triples: 2<=k<=8, 1<=M<=24, 2<=s<=16. This finite algebra does not
validate all general-k geometry by itself. The explicit lift below proves one
complete actual-variable certificate without relying on quotient identities.
