3. VERIFIED FINDING: SELECTED MEMBER IS NOT TIED TO PARSED TAR ENTRY

The runner validates headers['entries'] against actual tar bytes, but uses
headers['selected_members'] to locate footers without checking that each
selected(name,offset,size,type) equals one of those validated entries.

Concrete isolated-copy test: change the first selected member's name and the
corresponding footer member's name to an audit-only name absent from every
parsed tar entry. Leave all range bodies, request logs, offsets, sizes and
hashes untouched. The runner returns PASS. Its input-hash inventory changes,
but the runner itself does not compare report hashes to an external manifest.
An external authenticated bundle manifest could reject the changed reports;
that is separate from the runner's advertised placement validation.

This is a meaningful gap in associating declared footer names with archive
members. It does not refute the body hash, byte count or numeric-offset checks.

Recommended repair: before footer checking, require unique selected names and
require every selected(name,offset,size,type) to match exactly a tar entry
already validated from saved bytes. Then require every footer member to resolve
to one such selected entry. Re-run the sealed copy and this mismatch fixture.
Alternatively explicitly narrow “archive placement” / “hashes/placement” to
“range hashes and report-declared offsets; selected-member identity linkage
is trusted”. The exact linkage check is preferable and requires no new data.
