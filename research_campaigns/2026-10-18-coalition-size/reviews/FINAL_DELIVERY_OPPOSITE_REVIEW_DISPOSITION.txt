ROOT ADJUDICATION
1. The runner checks parent sys.flags.optimize and strips PYTHONOPTIMIZE from
   every child's inherited environment. Children use sys.executable without
   -O/-OO arguments. The report field assertions_enabled is consequently an
   enforced launch-policy statement, not an embedded measurement from each
   checker. A separate fresh child-mode probe under the same environment
   transformation observed optimize=0 and __debug__=true. It is recorded as
   a separate probe, not retroactively as every checker's measurement.
   Earlier actual -O/-OO/environment and corrupted-lift tests remain the
   evidence for the repaired failure mode, including the parent -E case.

2. The environment is intentionally not hermetic. Existing scientific-library
   paths are retained; no claim is made against malicious sitecustomize, a
   replaced interpreter or altered trusted dependencies. The ordinary probe's
   retained PYTHONPATH is null. The extended invocation records its explicitly
   selected existing NumPy/SciPy paths separately. No packages are installed.

3. Source-to-copy hash equality proves copy fidelity, not trust in a checker.
   The runner records checker hashes; a separate selected-file/archive
   manifest supplies the delivered snapshot identity. A deliberately replaced
   checker that prints PASS is outside that trust boundary, not a verified
   mathematical check. Scientific proofs and independent checker inspection
   remain necessary. The reproduction guide already makes this distinction.

4. The generated_files collection includes copied fixture inputs and captured
   stdout/stderr as well as newly produced scientific outputs. Interpret it
   as files present in the fresh per-checker directory; the source/fixture
   hashes are separately recorded. It is not evidence that every listed file
   was newly derived by the checker.

5. --out must name a nonexistent directory, but may name a new subdirectory
   beneath the source tree. That does not overwrite a sealed result. For the
   delivered root checks, output was placed in fresh /private/tmp directories,
   outside the campaign. No general path-sandbox promise is made.

6. Malformed JSON or missing fields can produce a nonzero exception and leave
   an incomplete report marked RUNNING instead of a polished FAILED result.
   This is diagnostic quality debt, not acceptance: successful evidence needs
   a zero process exit AND the explicit PASS report. A RUNNING report after
   process termination is incomplete and must never be counted as success.

7. The metadata verifier checks the present ledgers, archive-size binding,
   range identity and saved-body consistency. Its lookup key is not a general
   authenticated source identifier. It does not prove remote authenticity or
   defend against a coordinated replacement of ledgers and bytes. The guide
   accurately limits its claim to internal byte/placement consistency.
