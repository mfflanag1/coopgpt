REASONS FOR REJECTING THE DELIVERABLE AS A READY RESEARCH DIRECTION
1. The proposed scalar-agent flow parametrization was promoted too quickly
   into a class for arbitrary multirow owner underreports. Treating resource
   rows as agents changes coalition eligibility; normalizing each reported
   bundle changes the induced function across reports. No valid transfer was
   supplied. Additivity is not the only missing scope hypothesis.
2. Finite flow-LP feasibility does not give an all-real-profile mechanism.
   An exact-balance flow also needs an explicit scaled/enlarged formulation
   before a variable subsidy factor or funding dual has the claimed meaning.
3. Weak existential group monotonicity does not establish the sum requirement,
   especially with unchanged protected members and a bounded coalition cap.
   Root obtained the original strong-sum definition, which already includes
   unchanged members; see sources/MOULIN_SPRUMONT_PRIMARY_CHECK.txt.
4. Profilewise dual prices show coverage+budget alone cannot force K>1. This
   does not rule out balanced combinations WITH cross-profile TCWM constraints.
   The delegate's broad closure of balanced-family lower routes was unwarranted.
5. 'Ezra et al.' is valid citation practice, not a mathematical or bibliographic
   defect. A complete bibliography is useful but should not be presented as a
   verified error merely because a source has five authors or an unexpected title.
