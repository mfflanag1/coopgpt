FULL-FIBER PROJECTION BARRIER: INDEPENDENT SCOPED AUDIT
18 October 2026. Only reviews/full_fiber_audit/ is edited by this reviewer.
Primary note: lower/branching_pilot/FULL_FIBER_PROJECTION_BARRIER.txt.
Dependency audited: UNIT_WORTH_LINEAR_DOMAIN_THEOREM.txt in the same directory.

DISPOSITION
Scoped pass. No invalidating gap found in the projection identity, upper rule,
actual-coalition TCWM/full OUT, or matching growing-instance lower. The theorem
is about complete binary fibers over a fixed W with quotient worth at most
one. The upper does not extend to arbitrary partial-fiber report profiles or
the all-real report box. Partial selections of UNCHANGED fiber members inside
a protected coalition ARE covered and are essential to the proof.

Two scope clarifications are adopted in this disposition, without changing
the sealed source note:
 (i) Matching B(s/R) is a worst-case statement over sufficiently large quotient
     dimensions, not the optimum of every single fixed small quotient.
 (ii) The uniform two-regime upper is 1 for s<R and B(s/R) for s>=R. Thus the
      dimension-uniform shorthand is O(sqrt(max(1,s/R))). The bare
      O(sqrt(s/R)) expression applies in the s>=R regime.

1. GENERAL PROJECTION IDENTITY
Choose a direct-sum identification V=W plus Q solely for the proof. The
actual resource/owner labels remain the nonzero vectors of V. All W\{0}
reports are zero, and each selected nonzero q has R actual unit-report rows.

A supported actual triple {a,b,a+b} cannot contain a W row. Its three quotient
images are therefore nonzero; if two coincided, the third would lie in W,
contradicting support. Consequently it projects to a genuine quotient triple.

For the upper bound, take a nonnegative globally feasible quotient dual and
copy each quotient price to its actual fiber; price every nonzero W row at1.
Every actual triple whose quotient is a quotient triple is covered. Every
other actual triple includes a W row and is covered by that price1. Inactive
rows have zero cost. Total dual cost is R F_Q(S), giving F_V(lift S)<=R F_Q(S).
Equivalently one can set price1 on all missing actual rows and keep prices
only on the selected fibers. The latter description is valid because any
triple containing a missing row is already covered.

For the lower bound, fix an order of two endpoints of each quotient triple.
For each quotient activity of intensity x, enumerate its R^2 actual lifts,
giving each intensity x/R. These are distinct actual triples, since their
first two quotient fibers are fixed and distinct. A particular actual row
in any of the three fibers belongs to exactly R lifts of that quotient edge.
Its load is x; after summing quotient activities it is at most one. The lifted
objective is R x per quotient activity. This proves equality for EVERY binary
S, not only the quotient-unit-worth ideal. It uses real fractional production,
as required by the campaign contract, and does not split an actual owner.

2. WHY THE QUOTIENT IDEAL HAS STAR GEOMETRY
A productive unit-capacity quotient profile has value at least1. If it has
value at most1, an optimal nonnegative cover has total cost1. Every supported
edge must contain all positive-price rows, since its price sum is at least1
and the total price sum is1. Hence the supported edges have a common row.
Two distinct projective triples meet in at most one row, so for two or more
edges this cap is unique and all private endpoint pairs are disjoint. Other
positive quotient rows may be isolated. At one edge the upper's three payments
are all1/3, so the apparent choice of cap is irrelevant.

3. ALL-ACTUAL-COALITION COVERAGE, NOT JUST QUOTIENT COVERAGE
At an m-edge quotient star, each actual cap-fiber row receives1-2q_m and each
actual private-fiber row q_m. Thus each supported ACTUAL triple has price sum1.
Set global row prices to these payments on active rows and to1 on missing rows,
including W. This is a globally feasible actual production dual. For ANY
coalition of individual actual owners, including partial pieces of fibers,
its dual cost is exactly the sum of their payments. Hence every coalition is
covered. No erroneous formula identifies a partial-fiber coalition's worth
with R times a quotient worth.

The total payment at a productive profile is
 R[1+2(m-1)q_m], while actual production is R. Thus the expression in the
source note is a funding RATIO. Zero payments to inactive and isolated owners
are a permitted feature of this explicit upper rule, never an axiom imposed
on arbitrary lower-bound payment variables.

4. THE R-MEMBER RESTORATION COST IS REAL
For two complete-fiber endpoints, activating one quotient row changes reports
of exactly R fixed actual owners. Every protected coalition must include all
of them. It has at most s-R remaining slots. This remains true even if it
includes only portions of other unchanged fibers.

Start from a productive quotient profile and add one whole fiber. The final
star's cap lies in an old edge, so it is already present. Every new quotient
edge contains both that cap and the new quotient row. Projective linearity
permits at most one such edge. Its other private endpoint was formerly an
isolated active quotient row. If no edge is created, all payments are unchanged.
If the branch count rises m-1 to m, the restoring R owners gain R q_m; the
formerly isolated partner fiber and cap gain nonnegative amounts. Exactly
2R(m-1) old private owners can lose, each by q_(m-1)-q_m. Therefore the worst
protected coalition's payment increment is at least
 R q_m-min(s-R,2R(m-1))[q_(m-1)-q_m]=0.
This is the displayed recurrence with L_m=min(s-R,2R(m-1)).

For a simultaneous activation, add its whole fibers in any order. Every
intermediate profile lies in the ideal by monotonicity, and the SAME original
actual coalition contains all restoring owners at each step. The same bound
therefore telescopes. This proves every restricted TCWM comparison, not only
the chosen star ladder. It covers arbitrary branching among certificate nodes.
When s<R there is no nontrivial protected comparison: even one changed fiber
requires R members. A profilewise efficient actual core gives the factor1
upper (the all-zero-production case of course has optimum zero).

5. FULL OUT IS CHECKED AT THE ENDPOINTS
If the low profile is unproductive, its rule payments are all zero. High
coalition coverage dominates production from the restored actual rows alone.
If the low profile is productive, the final quotient star cap belongs to an
old edge and is absent from the newly restored quotient set. No quotient edge
is supported on that set. The restored set consists of complete fibers, so
the projection identity gives actual outside production zero. Thus TCWM gives
full OUT in this case. There is no false summation of individual positive
OUT values or assumption that partial-fiber restorations remain in the domain.

6. PEAK AND ASYMPTOTIC
Set t=s/R>=1. The recurrence ratio is
 q_m/q_(m-1)=min(t-1,2m-2)/(1+min(t-1,2m-2)).
For nonconstant funding terms at m and m+1, the ratio is
 2m^2/[(m-1)(2m+1)]>1 in the unsaturated region,
 m(t-1)/[t(m-1)] in the tail.
The latter is greater than, equal to, or less than1 according as m<t, m=t,
or m>t. Therefore noninteger t>1 has its sole peak at ceil(t); integer t>1
has the two equal peaks t,t+1. At t=1 every funding ratio is1. Monotonicity
in t gives B(t)<=B_ceil(t).

For a precise real-t expression, h=floor((t+1)/2) is the final unsaturated
index, q_h=Q_h/(3h), and q_m=q_h[(t-1)/t]^(m-h) for m>=h.
Since h~t/2 and ceil(t)~t, the usual Q_h~sqrt(pi h)/2 yields
 B(t)-1~sqrt(2*pi)/(3sqrt(e))*sqrt(t).
The rounding does not change the leading constant. These observations support
the stated scaled square-root barrier, with the two-regime qualification above.

7. MATCHING LOWER: FIXED OWNERS AND LEGAL SYMMETRIES
Fix R and an integer M large enough to contain a peak stage, and choose the
quotient cap v and highs e1,...,eM as a BASIS of Q=F2^(M+1). At stage m the
active quotient rows are v, all highs, and v+e_j for j<=m. Basis independence
excludes any extra supported quotient edge; the supported edges are exactly
{v,e_j,v+e_j} for j<=m. Thus all displayed profiles have actual value R.

The W-preserving linear group preserves the entire complete-fiber ideal.
Jointly permuting the actual resource rows and their fixed pure owners maps
each legal report profile and comparison to another legal one of the SAME
institution. Averaging a rule over this finite group preserves all constraints.
No arbitrary owner permutation or division of a fiber into a new strategic
agent is asserted.

Important distinction: the full group preserves the DOMAIN, not each chosen
profile. The profile's stabilizer supplies its payment equalities. In direct-
sum coordinates, maps (w,q)->(w+Tq,Aq), with A invertible and T linear, preserve
W. All shears T fix each complete-fiber profile setwise, and for each nonzero
q their values Tq range over all W. They are transitive inside each fiber.
These are independent choices on a quotient BASIS, not arbitrary independent
permutations of all fibers, which would not preserve triples.

At the seed, the quotient swaps v<->e1 and v<->v+e1 fix the other highs and
preserve the active set. Together with the shears they make all3R productive
owners one orbit. Coverage of ONE lifted actual triple forces their common
payment at least1/3. At stage m, branch permutations and independent flips
e_j<->e_j+v for j<=m preserve the active set. Together with shears they make
the2mR private owners one orbit, and the cap fiber another orbit.

For the next restoration choose all R actual new-fiber owners and ANY L_m
old active private owners. The latter may be partial pieces of fibers.
They have common old and new payments, so TCWM yields
 (R+L_m)q_m-L_m q_(m-1) >= sum_(restoring actual i) y_i(old) >=0.
No dormant owner's old payment is set to zero. A terminal actual triple gives
z+2q_M>=1. Nonnegativity of other payments and the actual budget give
 K R >= R z+2MR q_M, hence K>=1+2(M-1)q_M.
Taking a peak M proves matching B(t) over growing quotient dimensions. This
does not claim every fixed small Q contains that ladder. The lower uses only
TCWM, while the upper has full OUT; consequently both restricted worst-case
optima coincide. It is the same scaled square-root obstruction, not a new
unrestricted lower exponent.

8. SOURCE CHECKER AND INDEPENDENT REPLAY
The original check_full_fiber.py and FULL_FIBER_EXACT.json were read. The code
transports quotient primal/dual witnesses, lifts to actual triples, and tests
the worst coalition by compulsory restoring-owner deltas plus the most
negative eligible unchanged-owner deltas. That minimization is exact for all
actual coalitions of size at most s.

Two replay-hardening points, neither an invalidation of the saved mathematics:
 - The original code did not explicitly assert nonnegativity of imported
   quotient primal intensities and dual prices. An independent JSON scan
   found all60 stored oracles nonnegative:420 primal and420 dual entries,
   both with minimum zero. The owned hardened copy adds both assertions.
 - Its actual loops are s=R,...,32 for each R in1,2,4. Its old flat s list
   must not be interpreted as every R/s combination. The hardened copy
   reports the exact ranges by R. The skipped s<R regime is trivial by the
   proof and is also included in the independent replay below.

Default python could not execute the original script because NumPy was not
installed in that interpreter. No installation was attempted. On root's
request, hardened_check_full_fiber.py is an owned copy pointing explicitly to
the original sealed ternary inputs. It uses the existing supplied NumPy
runtime; the original checker, inputs and JSON remain untouched.
The hardened replay completed successfully and exactly reproduced the saved
384 lifted pairs,11,040 funding checks,47,740 supported-triple coverage checks,
and135,472 full-OUT endpoint checks. Its output is
HARDENED_FULL_FIBER_REPLAY.json; the per-R s metadata is now accurate.

The independent_fiber_check.py replay is separately written stdlib/Fraction
code, importing NO source-pilot code or data. It constructs all Fano primal/
dual witnesses directly and checks them, uses an involutive linear shear to
make W non-coordinate for R>1, and verifies R=1,2,4,8. Saved results:
 -512 lifted primal/dual pairs, covering all128 quotient profiles per R;
 -5,880 funding checks and141,680 actual supported-triple cover checks;
 -36,820 all-coalition full-OUT endpoint checks,30,940 nontrivial;
 -8,190 actual stabilizer/shear actions checking the seed/private/cap orbits;
 -5,880 exact scaled lower coefficient cancellations, including explicit
  nonnegative old-restorer payment rows, and245 peak checks.
These computations support the general proof; their Fano quotient does not
replace the basis argument for arbitrarily large quotient dimension.

FINAL SCOPE
Complete binary fibers with ONE fixed W and quotient worth at most one:
accepted scoped barrier. Arbitrary coalitions at such profiles: covered.
Partial-fiber REPORT profiles, varying W across a certificate, quotient worth
greater than one, and arbitrary real reports: no upper mechanism follows here.
The projection identity itself applies to binary S of higher quotient worth,
but the star payment rule and matching B(t) conclusion do not.
