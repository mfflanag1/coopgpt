2. PRODUCTION-CHAMBER REPRESENTATION BARRIER
Accepted: root/PRODUCTION_CHAMBER_PARTITION_BARRIER.txt. For a single minimum
of r>=2 pool totals and n owners ALL eligible for EVERY pool with positive
endowments, nonnegative continuous payments affine on only the r production
bottleneck chambers and finitely funded must be y_i=c_i F. Coalition coverage
forces c_i>=1, so the restricted optimum is exactly n. The lower does not
need incentives; y_i=F supplies the matching full-OUT upper. This is a
representation obstruction, not a general impossibility or a lower on the
number of cells in finer owner-sensitive partitions.
Native PRODUCTION_CHAMBER_BARRIER_AUDIT.txt and the completed opposite review
both checked zero-output slices, independent owner distributions at tied
totals, the n1 case and the necessity of r>=2. No gap found.

3. CEA CALIBRATION ESCAPES THAT COARSE PARTITION
Accepted: constructive/capped_awards_calibration/CEA_MINIMUM_FULL_OUT.txt.
For ONE minimum technology F(T)=min_a T_a, apply classical constrained equal
awards to the common estate F(T) in each pool and sum each actual owner's
awards. Total funding is exactly rF; coverage and full OUT hold for EVERY
coalition, arbitrary mixed ownership, all real reports, ties and zeros.
The proof uses an exact coalition-award monotonicity lemma and finite
bottleneck-interval telescoping. Saturated-owner sets give a finite rational
homogeneous PL cover; formulas agree on all overlaps. It need not be affine
on a whole production chamber.
