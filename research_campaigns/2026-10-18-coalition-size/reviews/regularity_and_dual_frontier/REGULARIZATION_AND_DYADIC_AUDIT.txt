18 October 2026. Owned review lane only; no root theorem files edited.

BOREL MULTIPLICATIVE REGULARIZATION
The main proof in root/MULTIPLICATIVE_REGULARIZATION_TOOL.txt is valid for a
finite packing institution with bounded coordinate boxes and a Borel rule.
Finite production at total endowment gives a uniform bound on every payment.
The same multiplier array at both comparison endpoints preserves outsiders,
unchanged members of the protected coalition, and the restored owner's whole
resource vector. Monotone homogeneity gives the exact alpha factor for both
coverage and outside production; positivity and linearity preserve the stated
inequalities. No dummy-zero restriction appears.

In log coordinates a compact smooth kernel supported strictly inside the
negative orthant smooths on each positive coordinate face. Kernel derivatives
are controlled on a common compact set against a bounded measurable integrand.
The strict support margin permits extension across upper box boundaries, but
does not establish continuity across a zero-coordinate face. Finite face
stratification ensures the smoothed rule is Borel. Funding is K/alpha; the
infimum consequence does not establish attainment at K.

DYADIC COMPLETENESS
The mathematical assertion in root/DYADIC_GRID_COMPLETENESS.txt is valid:
  K_* = sup_m K_m = lim_m K_m
for one fixed finite actual-owner institution. Coordinatewise dyadic floors
preserve eligibility, all unchanged outsider reports, and coordinatewise
restorations. Every coordinate converges to its original value, including at
non-dyadic upper endowments. A single common nonprincipal ultrafilter, used for
all profiles and payment coordinates, preserves finite sums and inequalities.
Continuity of the finite packing value handles every coalition worth and the
full-OUT value of rounded restoration differences. No continuity or measurable
selection of the original finite-grid solutions is needed.

The finite dual cover polyhedron is nonempty and pointed. A bounded minimum
of a linear functional occurs at a vertex; there are finitely many vertices.
Thus the packing value is a finite minimum of linear forms on the nonnegative
orthant, including its boundary. This justifies the continuity used above.
Zero-profit resource-free activities can be removed without changing value.

Each finite payment LP attains its minimum: restrict 0<=K<=n and use the
budget's uniform payment bounds. Grids with zero production have optimum zero.
The all-real optimum is attained by the ultrafilter construction at the
supremum L. None of this asserts that some finite index m attains sup_m K_m.

EFFECTIVITY CORRECTION REPORTED AND REPAIRED BY ROOT
Rational technology alone is insufficient to make the grid search effective
if endowments are arbitrary, potentially noncomputable real numbers. Require
rational technology AND rational endowments, or explicitly given effectively
constructible legal inner grids that converge coordinatewise and preserve
comparisons. Under rational input the finite LPs and their strict refutations
have exact rational certificates. For every rational k<K_*, some finite grid
has K_m>k, so enumerating/solving the grids semidecides strict refutation.
There is no finite upper verification, convergence rate, or finite-grid
attainment claim. Root agreed to both effectivity and attainment clarifications.
