FROZEN COMPARISON TARGET

A. MOULIN--SPRUMONT 2004: SAME AGGREGATE AXIOM, DIFFERENT THEOREM CLASS
[Direct source checks]
The model has scalar integer demands, nonnegative shares summing exactly to
cost, and methods additive across cost functions (printed/PDF pp4-5).
Strong Group Monotonicity (p6) protects the SUM of shares of any S containing
the one changing agent; passive S members are included. Successive changes
give its simultaneous version. Proposition1 (p6) derives it under additional
axioms. Proposition2(ii) (p12) excludes additive, separable methods satisfying
it when n>=3; its proof is present. Proposition7 (p19, proof pp38-39) instead
uses additivity, strong ranking and weak separability. Theorems1 and2 (pp8,15)
are restricted method characterizations, not coalition-size funding bounds.

[Mapping assessment]
The aggregate incentive inequality itself matches TCWM on the scalar grid
with s=n; calling it per-member monotonicity would be wrong. The obstruction
does NOT concern arbitrary payment rules with total funding K(s)F. Additivity
and separability are extra hypotheses, and normalizing a funded cover to exact
efficiency need not preserve TCWM. Moreover, the proof's cost C1 has
C1(e1)=C1(e2+e3)=C1(e1+e2+e3)=1, contradicting the superadditivity required
of additive-bundle packing production. Its direct witness therefore does not
map to our technology. No quantitative dependence on protected size s is supplied.

B. IMMORLICA--MAHDIAN--MIRROKNI: CLOSEST PROFIT LOWER, STRONGER INCENTIVES
[Direct source checks]
Definition3.3 (printed/PDF p11) gives the matching profit-budget orientation
v(S)<=sum_S xi<=alpha*v(S), its coalition-core condition, and individual
incumbent monotonicity xi(i,S)<=xi(i,T) for i in S subset T. Theorems3.6 and3.7
(pp12-13) derive linear population-size lower bounds for flow/matching.
Their shortened headlines omit cross-monotonicity, but section3.5 and the
proofs explicitly impose/use it. Theorems4.1-4.3 (pp16-17) address service-bid
group-strategyproof mechanisms; conversion to cross-monotonicity needs further
conditions, not merely an aggregate payment inequality.

[Mapping assessment]
The two-leg flow witness maps to min(A,B), already an arity-two packing
technology with fixed edge owners. Binary participation and arity therefore
do NOT explain the nonimplication. The decisive p12 step lower-bounds EACH
incumbent's enlarged-set payment by its old payment. TCWM after entrant j
joins protects only Delta y_i+Delta y_j>=0 when {i,j} is protected; it does
not imply Delta y_i>=0. Full OUT does not repair that implication. The retained
all-real min2 factor2 rule explicitly separates these conditions. Also,
population size n is not a protected-coalition parameter s. Replacing n by s
cannot remove the stronger axiom. The source additionally zeros nonparticipants;
our contract does not require that at positive-output profiles.

C. CHECK AGAINST THE AVAILABLE IMMORLICA--POUNTOURAKIS ORIGINAL
[Direct source checks]
The model uses private service valuations, a selected served set, voluntary
participation and group deviations judged by each member's utility (printed
pp3-4; PDF pp4-5). Theorem3 (printed p7/PDF p8) supplies the edge-cover
construction; Theorem5 and section4.2 (printed pp15-18/PDF pp16-19) give the
set-cover impossibility and constant18/19 cost-recovery barrier.

[Mapping assessment]
These statements do not supply a growing protected-size lower under binding
resource reports. Their valuation/allocation interface and utility criterion
are not supplied by TCWM or full OUT. Replacing a cost-recovery fraction by
its reciprocal only changes budget normalization; it does not establish the
missing mechanism/model reduction. This check does not audit that paper's
entire proof or settle alternative reductions.

EXACT MAPPING CHECKLIST
 - The Moulin--Sprumont aggregate axiom is a genuine antecedent; do not
   manufacture a distinction by changing it to individual monotonicity.
 - Their efficient additive cost-sharing impossibilities do not automatically
   extend to arbitrary overfunded coalition covers on sparse packing games.
 - The closest IMM profit lower has compatible budget signs and a sparse
   witness. Its individual population-monotonicity hypothesis is load-bearing.
 - Restriction to binary profiles can prove an all-real lower, so discreteness
   alone is not a valid dismissal of either comparison.
 - Splitting resource rows into new owners, dropping entrant-payment terms,
   or introducing a fixed auxiliary baseline is not an established transfer.
