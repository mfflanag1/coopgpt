19 October2026. Audit only: no acquisition, SQL execution, raw footer/statistic

DISPOSITION
The retained code and saved reports support the operational claim that no new
power/state observations or power/state value extrema were selected/displayed.
No evidence of outcome leakage was found. Two wording clarifications are useful:
whole footer bytes were acquired, and a June listing exists in the shared record.
Neither is evidence of a measured power/state observation being inspected.
The exact pre-clarification source hashes are preserved in SOURCE_MANIFEST.json.

1. ACQUISITION IS NOT THE SAME AS QUERY OUTPUT
application/m100_may_metadata_gate.py:48-79 fetches exact requested ranges;
95-119 walks tar headers and skips member bodies. Its saved report/ledger
record325 header requests and166,400 bytes. No GNU long-name entry occurred;
the existing independent placement audit already bound these to headers.

application/m100_may_footer_gate.py:79-93 obtains each selected member's4-byte
magic,8-byte tail and WHOLE footer. Nine requests add301,855 bytes. The sparse
wrappers contain those real footer bytes and zero holes where data pages would
be. The acquired footers were not stripped of any encoded value statistics.
They may therefore encode power/state summaries; this audit deliberately did
not inspect which summaries they contain. The saved bytes were handed to
DuckDB's metadata reader. Neither SQL projection nor the saved report proves
that DuckDB never parsed/materialized an unselected statistic internally.

Thus “metadata only” accurately describes the byte categories acquired and
the selected query outputs. It must not mean “no bytes encoding data-derived
power/state summaries were acquired” or “no footer statistics were decoded”.
The latter is false for timestamp statistics and unestablished for unselected
engine internals. This distinction does not manufacture an outcome leak.
