1. THE COMMON FULL REAL CUBE IS EXACTLY WHAT LICENSES THE AVERAGING
Put V=F2^(M+1). A resource label is a NONZERO VECTOR of V, but its reported
capacity is an ordinary real number in[0,1]. An element g of GL(V) acts on
capacity vectors by a PERMUTATION OF RESOURCE INDICES:
  (P_g b)_(g(a))=b_a.
It does not take binary linear combinations of real capacities. Consequently
P_g maps the full real cube bijectively to itself and preserves coordinatewise
order, nonnegativity, and the all-ones actual endowment.

Because g(a+b)=g(a)+g(b), the activity {a,b,a+b} maps to another activity,
with identical profit and unit consumption. Thus F(P_g c)=F(c) for every
nonnegative REAL capacity vector, not just the binary witness profiles.
Each owner has the SAME full cube and the same endowment, so permutations of
owner labels preserve the entire institution as well.

Formally, for owner permutation sigma define
  [T_(sigma,g) b]_i=P_g b_(sigma^{-1}(i)).
Given any rule y, its transformed rule at original label i is
  y_i^(sigma,g)(b)=y_(sigma(i))(T_(sigma,g)b).
For each coalition S, the relevant coalition in the original rule is sigma(S),
whose cardinality is unchanged. Its report and withdrawal sums are transformed
by P_g. Therefore each transformed rule preserves nonnegativity, coverage,
the same uniform funding bound, and every TCWM/full-OUT inequality.
The average over finite S_n x GL(V) preserves these linear inequalities and
is equivariant. There is no measurability or continuity premise.

This construction would FAIL with privately designated pure-resource eligibility
that was not invariant under these maps. The actual proof avoids precisely that
problem by declaring the full cube for EVERY owner before choosing the rule.
The symbols p,h_j,l_j label particular reports, not different eligibility sets.
All zero reports and all restored unit reports belong to this fixed cube.
