ASSESSMENT
The retained frontier is Omega(sqrt(s)) <= K3(s) <= K3_OUT(s) <= 3s^2.
No surviving general constructor yet justifies a long upper-bound campaign.
The most concrete next bounded upper target is an owner-specific reserve over
an efficient production-price allocation, subject to an early growing-family
restriction test. The authorized small diagnostic already detects a strict
finite cost, so I do NOT recommend a long commitment to that architecture.
The best lower alternative is a coupled, nonbinary, multiple-cap-chart
certificate, with a genuinely parameterized recurrence as its continuation
gate. Both choices target an identified missing step rather than another
finite example. These are research judgments, not proved ranking claims.

1. UPPER TARGET: OWNER-SPECIFIC RESERVES THAT COMPENSATE PRICE CHANGES

Fix ANY finite nonnegative A,pi with every profitable column using one to
three positive inputs, ANY fixed actual owners and coordinate endowment boxes,
and integer 1<=s<=n. Put B(b)=sum_i b_i and V(b)=F(B(b)). Seek a full-profile
selection p(b)>=0 satisfying A^T p(b)>=pi and p(b).B(b)=V(b), and reserves
r_i(b)>=0. Set
             u_i(b)=p(b).b_i,             y_i(b)=u_i(b)+r_i(b).
The PRIMARY target is an absolute constant c, independent of the institution,
with
             sum_i r_i(b)<=c*s*V(b)                         (U1)
at every real report, and for EVERY legal restoration l to h protecting the
SAME C, |C|<=s (including unchanged members),
             r_h(C)-r_l(C)>=u_l(C)-u_h(C).                  (U2)
This would give TCWM with factor 1+c*s. Replacing the right side of (U2) by
u_l(C)-u_h(C)+F(delta) is the separately STRONGER full-OUT target. Proving
only (U2) must not be described as a full-OUT upper. A proved uniform
o(s^2) reserve bound would also be substantive progress.

The implication is elementary: a feasible dual price already gives
u(C)>=F(b_C) for EVERY coalition, regardless of size; nonnegative reserves
preserve that coverage. Optimality gives sum u=V. The two endpoint inequalities
give incentives. At V=0 both u and r vanish, so no additive baseline or
zero-output exception is introduced. Full report dependence is permitted.

IMPORTANT RESTRICTION. Requiring y_i>=p(b).b_i is an extra architecture,
NOT without loss for arbitrary covers or arbitrary mechanisms. In a pure-owner
cap star with at least two branches, paying the cap owner zero and each private
pair a total of one covers all coalitions; it does not dominate the efficient
price allocation, which pays the cap owner one. This example does not itself
disprove the existence of a good price-plus-reserve mechanism. It shows that
its feasibility and quality need proof, not a change-of-variables assertion.

Why this architecture, cautiously: the audited single-minimum CEA rule has
exactly such a decomposition. Choose a bottleneck input h, so u_i=b_(i,h);
the other pools' CEA awards are nonnegative reserves totaling (r-1)V. This is
a meaningful all-real calibration with arbitrary mixed owners. General packing
allows substitution between competing activities, which that proof does not
handle. The accepted local price-defect inequality bounds one transition by
the whole protected coalition's high-profile rank surplus. It supplies a
concrete quantity to compensate, but fixed multiples of that surplus fail.

The missing constructive work is an EXPLICIT report-dependent reserve rule
or global charging potential which handles substitutions and pays for repeated
price changes without a factor depending on owner count, profile count or
number of production-price faces. Merely writing (U1)-(U2), solving their
finite restriction, or renaming the known global dual inequality is not a
new intermediate result. In particular, outgoing low-profile charges cannot
be dropped; passive protected owners cannot be charged to restorers alone.

EARLY GO/NO-GO GATE FOR A NEW, BOUNDED BLOCK
First test the architecture itself on growing fixed-owner families. At each
selected exact profile introduce variables y_i and p, impose p>=0,
A^T p>=pi, p.B=V, y_i>=p.b_i, all coalition coverages and all legal protected
endpoint comparisons. This is a genuine finite LP with prices free to choose
among optima. Use growing common-cap/substitution families and the retained
passive-member fixtures; do not fix a favorable tie-breaking price. Seek an
exact divergence obstruction at fixed s, or a quadratic-order obstruction as
s grows, before committing to construction. A finite adverse value alone is
not a universal rejection, and finite passes are not an all-real upper.

AUTHORIZED SMALL RISK CHECK: the existing sealed twelve-profile, three-owner
min3 fixture has exact finite optimum 5/4 without this restriction and 3/2
with it. Profile-dependent prices are free to choose among all efficient
nonnegative duals. Both optima have exact primal and complete dual witnesses;
all84 static coalition rows and108 legal size-at-most-two comparisons were
checked, including24 nontrivial comparisons with unchanged protected members.
Thus the restriction demonstrably sacrifices finite performance. This does
not rule out its O(s) potential, but raises the burden for the growing test.
No family was enlarged. See PRICE_BASE_RISK_CHECK.txt and its Fraction checker.

Continue beyond that bounded gate only if there is an explicit reserve formula
or charging operation, an all-real proof through at least the shared-resource
substitution and passive-member obstructions, and a stated global composition
argument with an s-only budget. Stop this architecture if a growing exact
obstruction is proved, or if the only deliverable remains (U1)-(U2)/generic LP
feasibility. This is a falsifiable candidate, not a reason to presume O(s).

The finite diagnostic downgrades this recommendation to a bounded falsifiable
target, not a favored conjecture that efficient-price domination is harmless.

2. LOWER ALTERNATIVE: COUPLE NONBINARY PRODUCTIVE LEVELS ACROSS CAP CHARTS

Do not enlarge a single homogeneous dormant-to-productive orbit, repeat the
binary unit-worth ideal, or replace rows by complete fixed fibers. Their
retained ceilings apply even when those computations are made much larger.

One precise live family is the existing partial-fiber template in the FULL
projective triple technology on nonzero vectors of F2^(M+2). Give each row
one fixed pure owner with report interval [0,2]. For a basis w,v,e_1,...,e_M,
a local chart has rows
 P_a=v+a*w, H_(j,b)=e_j+b*w, L_(j,c)=v+e_j+c*w, a,b,c in F2,
and supported branches {P_a,H_(j,b),L_(j,a+b)}. Use nonuniform levels in
{0,1,2}, including partial pairs such as (0,1) and (1,2), and take full
owner/resource GL orbits of all selected profiles. The full technology and
actual owner roster remain fixed; charts do not define new public institutions.
Each chart's production is at most its two root capacities, hence at most4.

This family can leave the binary unit-worth and complete-fixed-fiber report
domains. That is only an escape from their hypotheses, not evidence of a
stronger lower. Uniformly doubling old profiles, independent copies, or
restricting the PUBLIC technology to one marked root pair do not supply the
needed interaction. The last restriction has a constant finite-grid bound
in the retained template and destroys the intended cross-chart symmetry.

EXPLICIT PROOF OBLIGATION
For all sufficiently large s, construct a finite actual-variable TCWM dual
on such profiles, with nonnegative lambda,mu,nu and
 g_(p,i)=sum_(S containing i)lambda_(p,S)
          +sum_(e:h=p,i in C_e)mu_e
          -sum_(e:l=p,i in C_e)mu_e <= nu_p,
where each comparison has fixed outsiders and |C_e|<=s. Normalize
D=sum_p nu_p F(B_p)=1. The desired improvement is
                  L=sum_(p,S)lambda_(p,S)F(b_S)
                       >=c*s^(1/2+epsilon)
for some fixed c,epsilon>0. Expanding the symmetry orbit must retain all
actual payment variables and nonnegative-payment slacks. This is a lower for
arbitrary all-real rules because their restrictions obey these finite rows.

A concrete sufficient continuation target is a weighted paid total T_m built
from a bounded-budget average of the selected cap charts, with
 T_(m0)>=c0>0 independent of K,
 T_m >=[1+(1/2+epsilon)/m] T_(m-1), and T_m<=4K,
for m0<=m<=a*s, with fixed positive a,c0,epsilon. Each displayed comparison
must arise from exact nonnegative combinations of legitimate coverage/TCWM
rows; all protected-owner counts must be listed. Such a recurrence would give
the stated exponent by multiplication. This is a SPECULATIVE proof obligation,
not a derived recurrence. Additional payment orbits/productive dormant groups
must do real work: the established one-orbit activation ceiling forbids simply
asserting the larger coefficient.

GO/NO-GO GATE
The first bounded continuation must produce a parameterized cross-chart or
cross-level row combination with a K-independent seed and an improvement
that survives symbolic m, or another explicit asymptotic dual mechanism with
the same consequence. The earlier seed q>=1-K/2 is vacuous once K>=2 and
does not pass. A finite value above B_s can diagnose genuine interaction but
does not meet the asymptotic continuation gate. Stop if the argument reduces
to a scalar dilation, a fixed complete fiber, the one-orbit recurrence, or
an unexplained closure of unrelated owner flows. No new search was run here.

3. WHAT OWNER-SENSITIVE FINITE PL ACTUALLY ADDS

It is presently a rigorous finite certificate AND fixed-partition synthesis
language. CEA makes it a promising practical tool, rather than an empty
representation: owner saturation cells really escape the production-chamber
barrier and admit all-real proofs, including zero faces. It does not tell us
which refinements solve general packing, how many are needed, or whether
continuous PL mechanisms approximate the unrestricted optimum.

Use it to certify an explicit upper candidate, return exact failures, and
study a specified reserve formula on ownership-sensitive cells. Do not infer
a general upper from many grid passes, a few feasible cells, or affine formulas
on production chambers alone. Coverage still requires ALL coalitions and joint
report/production LPs; local Jacobian separation is not a global coverage
oracle. The new upper target needs a mathematical rule/composition lemma,
not simply a larger partition search.
