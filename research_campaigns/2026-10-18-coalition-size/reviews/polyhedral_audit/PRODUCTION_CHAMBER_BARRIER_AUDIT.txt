INDEPENDENT AUDIT: PRODUCTION-CHAMBER PARTITION BARRIER
2026-10-18 23:51 UTC. Auditor: /root/arity3_lower.
Source: root/PRODUCTION_CHAMBER_PARTITION_BARRIER.txt.
Disposition: PASS as a restricted representation theorem, for all n>=1,r>=2.
No source edit required. This is not an unrestricted funding lower bound.

The proof correctly uses the fixed actual owner roster, independent coordinate
boxes, and strictly positive endowments in every owner-resource coordinate.
On the closed chamber P_a, all reports with row-a total0 have production0.
Nonnegativity and finite funding force each owner's payment to be0 there.
Because the other coordinates independently range over a full positive-sided
box, an affine formula that vanishes there has zero intercept and zero
coefficients on every resource except a. Thus its remaining form is
              y_i=sum_j l_(i,j)^a b_(j,a).

The source's simplex-variation proof for equating these coefficients is valid.
An alternative finite algebra argument makes the same step transparent:
for resources a!=c and any owners j,k, take a common sufficiently small t>0;
put row-a report t on owner j, row-c report t on owner k, and for every other
resource place total2t on any fixed owner. All reports are legal if t is below
one half of the minimum positive endowment. This profile lies in P_a intersect
P_c, with the other rows strictly above the tied minimum. Formula agreement
then says t*l_(i,j)^a=t*l_(i,k)^c. Thus all cross-resource owner coefficients
are equal. Since r>=2, comparison through another resource also equates any
two coefficients within the same resource. For n=1 the same argument still
equates the resource coefficients. Hence y_i=c_i F on every chamber, with one
constant c_i. Nonnegativity at a productive report gives c_i>=0.

A legal profile where owner i alone supplies a small equal positive quantity
in every resource has its standalone production equal to total production.
Singleton coverage therefore gives c_i>=1. At any productive profile funding
requires K>=sum_i c_i>=n. These arguments need no incentive inequalities and
allow arbitrary affine intercepts initially; the hypotheses force them away.

Conversely y_i=F has factor n, covers every actual coalition by monotonicity,
and satisfies full OUT for all simultaneous restorations by superadditivity.
Thus n is the exact optimum in this architecture. The independent rational
synthesis checks in independent_synthesis.py calibrate r2, n2 andn3, yielding
the exact factors2 and3 for these two-chamber mixed-owner instances. They are
supporting checks; the preceding argument covers all n,r and all positive
rational or real endowment bounds.

Scope is essential: one affine formula per ORIGINAL production chamber,
continuity, nonnegative payments, finite ordinary funding, and fully eligible
mixed owners. Refinements, non-affine formulas, discontinuity, and eligibility
restrictions are not ruled out. The example says production-price chambers
alone cannot yield a bound independent of owner count. It does not prescribe
a necessary number of refinement cells or improve the unrestricted frontier.
