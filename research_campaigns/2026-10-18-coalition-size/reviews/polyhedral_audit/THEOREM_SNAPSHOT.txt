FINITE ALL-REAL CERTIFICATES FOR CONTINUOUS PIECEWISE-AFFINE PAYMENTS
2026-10-18. Constructive-method result on a SUPPLIED finite rational partition.
No completeness claim for arbitrary mechanisms, polynomial global-coverage
claim, funding improvement, or historical priority claim is made.

DEPENDENCY
root/LOCAL_INCENTIVE_SEPARATION.txt, independently audited in
reviews/local_incentive_audit/AUDIT.txt. The local sorting and input-assignment
equivalences are reused. Continuity replaces separate face admission within

1. FIXED DATA AND REPRESENTATION
There are n fixed actual owners and d resource rows. Owner i has a fixed
nonnegative rational endowment bar_b_i. Remove coordinates with zero endowment
from the variable list, but DO NOT remove their owners from coalition tests.
Let m be the number of remaining report coordinates, indexed by (i,a), and
D=[0,bar_b] the m-dimensional report box. For coalition C, S_C is the fixed
d-by-m aggregation matrix, so S_C b=sum_(i in C)b_i. Actual owners never change.

Production is F(c)=max{pi^T x:A x<=c,x>=0}, with finite rational A,pi>=0.
Remove zero-profit columns; every remaining activity uses a nonempty set of
positive input rows. Hence F is finite on finite bundles, continuous,
positively homogeneous, monotone, concave, and F(0)=0. Its maximum arity is r.
The case with no remaining activities is allowed and has F=0.

Supply finitely many bounded rational full-dimensional closed polytopes
 P_t={b:H_t b<=g_t} subset D, t=1,...,T, whose union is D.
A face-to-face partition suffices; a finite closed cover also suffices when
formulas agree on overlaps. Every boundary point belongs to at least one
such full-dimensional cell. Geometry, including covering, is part of the
certificate data and must be verified; sample points do not verify it.
The m=0 case is trivial: F=0 and all nonnegative finite-funded payments are0.

On cell t specify
                  y_i(b)=a_(t,i)^T b+h_(t,i).             (1)
Write a_(t,C)=sum_(i in C)a_(t,i), h_(t,C)=sum_(i in C)h_(t,i).
The funding factor kappa is finite and nonnegative. Rational coefficients and
rational witnesses allow exact certificate checking.

2. SHARED-BOUNDARY CONSISTENCY AND NONNEGATIVITY
For each cell pair t,u, owner i, and vertex v of P_t intersect P_u, impose
 (a_(t,i)-a_(u,i))^T v+h_(t,i)-h_(u,i)=0.                 (2)
Empty intersections impose nothing. Affine functions agree throughout a
bounded polytope exactly when they agree at its vertices. Thus these finite
equalities make (1) a single continuous function on D. Lower-dimensional
intersections, zero-coordinate faces and corners are included. A verified
adjacency list can reduce this work, but testing every pair is sufficient.

For every cell vertex v and every owner i impose
                       a_(t,i)^T v+h_(t,i)>=0.           (3)
This is exactly nonnegativity throughout D. Zero-report owners retain their
payment variables and coalition eligibility; no extra zero-payment condition
on individual zero reports is assumed.

3. ALL-REAL, ALL-COALITION COVERAGE IS A FAMILY OF LINEAR PROGRAMS
For EACH actual coalition C, of ANY size, and each cell t, form
 max_{b,x} pi^T x-a_(t,C)^T b-h_(t,C)
 subject to H_t b<=g_t, A x<=S_C b, x>=0.               (4)
The b variables are free here; their box restrictions are included in H_t.
The optimum is finite and attained. It is at most0 exactly when
Y_C(b)>=F(S_C b) throughout P_t. This searches all simultaneous mixed reports.

An equivalent finite LP-dual certificate is u_(t,C)>=0, p_(t,C)>=0 with
 H_t^T u_(t,C)-S_C^T p_(t,C)=-a_(t,C),
 A^T p_(t,C)>=pi,
                      g_t^T u_(t,C)<=h_(t,C).           (5)
All constraints are LINEAR jointly in payment coefficients and witnesses.
Indeed a_C^T b=p^T S_C b-u^T H_t b, so for any feasible production x,
 a_C^T b+h_C>=p^T S_C b-u^T g_t+h_C>=p^T S_C b>=pi^T x.
Necessity follows from finite LP strong duality for (4).

Coverage cannot generally be checked only at cell vertices: F is concave,
so F-minus-affine can peak between them. On the triangle b1,b2>=0,
b1+b2<=1 with F=min(b1,b2), zero payments cover all three vertices but fail
at (1/2,1/2), with shortfall1/2. The checker includes this exact test.
There can be 2^n coverage coalitions. The local incentive oracle does NOT
supply a polynomial all-coalition coverage oracle.

4. FUNDING HAS AN EXACT VERTEX TEST
For every vertex v of every cell compute f_v=F(S_N v), and impose
                       a_(t,N)^T v+h_(t,N)<=kappa f_v.  (6)
This is equivalent to funding on D. For b=sum_l lambda_l v_l in one cell,
 Y_N(b)=sum_l lambda_l Y_N(v_l)
       <=kappa sum_l lambda_l F(S_N v_l)
       <=kappa F(S_N b),
using concavity and kappa>=0. Exact primal and dual optimal solutions certify
each rational f_v. These values depend only on the fixed geometry/technology,
so (6) is linear even when kappa is a synthesis variable.

Alternatively use a COMPLETE finite representation F(c)=min_h p_h^T c by
the needed extreme dual covers of Q={p>=0:A^T p>=pi}, and impose
Y_N(v)<=kappa p_h^T S_N v for every v,h. A merely selected feasible-dual list
is insufficient: its minimum can exceed F, giving the wrong funding bound.

Conditions (3) and (6) force all payments to vanish at EVERY zero-output
profile, even away from cell vertices. This follows from proved global
funding/nonnegativity and is not a sample-based inference.

5. EXACT LOCAL INCENTIVES ON EACH CONSTANT JACOBIAN
Fix s>=1, replacing s by min(s,n). On cell t let
 J_t[i;j,a]=(a_(t,i))_(j,a).
Every globally eligible coordinate (bar_b_(j,a)>0) is an interior coordinate
at interior points of each full-dimensional cell. For each such (j,a), set
z_i=J_t[i;j,a]. TCWM is equivalent locally to
 z_j+Neg_(s-1)(z;{j})>=0,                                (7)
where Neg_k sums the up to k most negative entries outside the excluded set.
The sort includes passive and zero-report actual owners.

For FULL OUT additionally test every positive-profit activity e and every
assignment f of each input row a of e to an eligible actual owner f(a).
Let D_f be its image; skip assignments with |D_f|>s. Define
 z_i(e,f)=sum_(a in supp(e)) A_(a,e) J_t[i;f(a),a].
Impose
 sum_(i in D_f)z_i(e,f)+Neg_(s-|D_f|)(z(e,f);D_f)>=pi_e.  (8)
An activity with a globally unavailable input row has no assignment and cannot
produce from any feasible restoration. Condition (7) is still required.

The audited local theorem says (7), respectively (7)+(8), is equivalent to
the appropriate derivative inequality for EVERY protected actual C and EVERY
nonnegative direction d supported on C:
 sum_(i in C)a_(t,i)^T d>=0, or >=F(S_N d).
Whole-input assignments are a separating device, not a change in ownership;
production duality then controls arbitrary distributed restorations.

6. GLOBAL FINITE INCENTIVE COMPARISONS, INCLUDING ALL BOUNDARIES
Take any feasible b to b+d in D, d>=0 supported on one fixed protected C.
A segment meets finitely many closed cells. Subdivide at the endpoints of
their intersection intervals. On each resulting open interval at least one
full-dimensional cell contains the whole interval, so Y_C has that cell's
affine formula there. This also holds when the segment lies in a common face.
Agreement makes the directional derivative independent of the chosen formula.

Apply the derivative bound from (7), or (7)+(8), and sum interval lengths.
Continuity cancels all endpoint terms and gives zero-face admission as equality.
Therefore
             Y_C(b+d)-Y_C(b)>=0, or >=F(S_N d).          (9)
No face Jacobians or generic-position assumptions are needed. Conversely,
global TCWM/OUT of a rule of form (1) implies the local tests: choose a cell
interior point, scale a tested direction enough to stay inside, and divide
the finite inequality by that scale. Such points have every eligible report
strictly between0 and its endowment. Thus these tests are also necessary
within the stated continuous piecewise-affine class.

7. FINITE SYNTHESIS LP AND CERTIFICATE FORMAT
For a fixed verified partition, use variables a_(t,i), h_(t,i), kappa>=0 and
u_(t,C),p_(t,C) for each cell and nonempty actual coalition. Impose (2),(3),
(5),(6), and the linear expansions of (7), optionally (8); minimize kappa.
A negative-entry sum is a minimum of finitely many linear coalition sums.
Its lower bound is therefore a finite family of linear inequalities, separable
by the audited sorting/assignment oracle without materializing every one.
Consequently feasibility and the best finite kappa WITHIN THIS FIXED-PARTITION
CLASS are finite rational LP questions. If feasible, minimizing kappa has a
finite attained rational optimum: the objective is bounded below by0 and
the constraint system is a finite rational polyhedron. This restricted
attainment statement does not concern the unrestricted mechanism infimum.

For fixed r, local separation is polynomial in the explicit technology and
owner count at ONE cell Jacobian. The full representation can be enormous:
cells, vertices, intersections, coverage coalitions and geometry verification
all count. Dual variables in (5) are proof witnesses, not payments/new owners.
Actual affine formulas and continuity impose integrability automatically.

A certificate contains fixed owners/endowments and rational technology;
verified cell geometry; affine formulas and kappa; boundary/nonnegativity
vertex checks; coverage duals (5); production LP certificates at funding
vertices; and exact local incentive checks. A failing coverage LP returns
an actual rational report and feasible production. A failing local test
returns actual owners/direction and becomes a finite violation after small
scaling at an interior point. Continuity failure rejects this representation
class; by itself it need not show every possible discontinuous choice fails.

For homogeneous PL payments, use a finite rational conic partition intersected
with D and set every h_(t,i)=0. The same conditions then certify homogeneity
along every feasible ray, in addition to the original mechanism properties.

8. GEOMETRIC VERIFICATION AND SCOPE
Containment in D can be imposed directly by including its bounds in each H_t.
Cell nonemptiness/full dimension and all vertex/intersection computations are
finite rational polyhedral questions. Covering a box by an arbitrary supplied
finite list is also a finite question, but may be expensive. For example,
a point outside every closed cell chooses a violated inequality from each
cell. Enumerate these choices and test whether some b in D and epsilon>0
satisfy h_(t,chosen)^T b>=g_(t,chosen)+epsilon for all t. One can maximize
epsilon with 0<=epsilon<=1 by an exact LP. Covering holds exactly when no such
choice has a positive optimum (or feasible point with positive epsilon).
This is a correctness procedure, NOT a polynomial complexity assertion.
A complete hyperplane-arrangement subdivision has covering by construction.

The universal rule y_i(b)=F(S_N b) is representable on a partition refining
the finite production dual-price regions. It has funding n and all coverage
by monotonicity; full OUT follows from packing superadditivity:
F(B+d)-F(B)>=F(d), and a nonempty coalition sums at least one such increment.
It is a calibration witness, not a new funding improvement.

A supplied partition can be too coarse. With two pure owners, F=min(b1,b2),
and the whole square as ONE cell, finite funding and nonnegativity force every
affine payment to vanish on both axes and hence everywhere, violating grand
coverage. Fixed-partition infeasibility says nothing about finer partitions.

Continuity is an explicit additional restriction. The facewise-smooth/
admission theorem allows certain boundary discontinuities. No result here
says continuous PL rules attain or approximate the unrestricted infimum.
Approximate gridding/interpolation can spoil coverage, incentives, or funding
near zero-output profiles. This is a finite language for certifying supplied
all-real upper candidates, not a completeness theorem or better K3 bound.
