1. EXACT TCWM ORDER STATISTIC
For an active report coordinate (j,a), a direction supported only there changes
the coalition payment at rate sum_(i in C) JAC[i;j,a]. Exactly the coalitions
containing actual owner j can use that direction. The minimum over |C|<=s is
the j entry plus up to s-1 strictly negative remaining entries; omitting a
nonnegative entry is allowed by the at-most convention. Inactive owners remain
eligible passive coalition members and therefore must remain in this sort.
Every nonnegative direction is a nonnegative sum of coordinate directions.
Thus (2) is necessary and sufficient, including all simultaneous restorations.

2. FULL OUT: ACTIVITY INPUT ASSIGNMENTS ARE SUFFICIENT
Necessity is valid despite finite owner boxes: at a relative interior point,
any finite assigned-input direction is feasible after a sufficiently small
positive scaling. Homogeneity cancels that scale. A whole unit of activity e
may be replicated in the derivative test even when the unscaled direction
would leave the report box. For its actual restoring-owner set D, minimizing
over all protected supersets gives precisely the displayed sorted expression.

For sufficiency fix an actual coalition C. TCWM ensures every relevant rate
ell_(j,a) = sum_(i in C) JAC[i;j,a] is nonnegative. For each row supplied by C,
take the minimum of these rates over its eligible active actual owners. Each
activity can choose a minimizing owner independently for every input row;
the resulting D is contained in C and has size at most s. The sorted test is
the minimum over all eligible supersets of D, so its lower bound also holds
for C. Consequently these row minima cover every usable activity's profit.
Any activity with an unavailable positive input has zero feasible production
in the chosen direction and is correctly omitted. Nonnegative row prices
then give the production dual upper bound for every distributed restoration.

No owner is split or relabeled in this argument. Assignment tests are a
separation device; the resulting dual handles owners supplying fractional
pieces of many rows and mixtures of many activities. Separate TCWM tests are
necessary to ensure nonnegative prices and to cover nonproductive directions.

3. POLYNOMIAL CLAIM HAS THE CORRECT SCOPE
At one supplied Jacobian, each coordinate needs a sort over n owners. For
each activity there are at most n^r input-to-owner assignments; each score
uses at most r*n multiply-adds and one sort. For fixed r this is polynomial
in the explicit input size (technology, eligible coordinates and Jacobian),
with no enumeration of coalitions. For rational input, exact rational sums,
comparisons and sorting also have polynomial bit cost. For arbitrary real
derivatives the note appropriately makes only an arithmetic-complexity claim.
No coverage separation, report-continuum test, integrability construction,
or fixed-profile test of all boundary-admission comparisons is supplied.

4. DIRECTED ADMISSION COMPLETES THE GLOBAL EQUIVALENCE
For b+t*d, t>0, the positive-coordinate set is constant. The same protected
actual coalition C is used throughout. The local inequalities imply g'(t)>=0
for TCWM and g'(t)>=F(sum d) for OUT, where g(t)=Y_C(b+t*d). Smoothness on
that face justifies integration from epsilon to1. Monotonicity gives the
right-hand limit at0; nonnegativity and finite endpoint payments already
bound it, and the stated funding assumption is also sufficient. Admission
is exactly the inequality needed to compare this limit with Y_C(b). Taking
epsilon down to0 proves the global finite comparison. Conversely global
comparisons imply both the face derivatives and admission by limits.

The explicit upper-boundary convention is important and correctly stated:
the equivalence at a fixed point is first proved in the relative interior;
derivative tests at endowment boundaries are inherited by smooth limits from
below. Local feasible directions at an isolated upper corner alone can be
vacuous and should not be read as determining all derivatives there. This is
an optional wording emphasis, not a defect in the existing note.

The two-owner counterexample is exact. At b1>0, y=(b1,b2), so all within-face
OUT rates equal the additive production rates. At b1=0,b2>0, the only active
coordinate is owner2's; its rate vector is (1,1), so every eligible coalition
containing owner2 has rate at least1. The other faces are immediate. All
coalitions are covered and total payment is at most2(b1+b2). Yet owner1 has
payment1 at(0,1), right limit0 along its restoration, and payment epsilon
at(epsilon,1). Thus admission, TCWM and OUT fail on this actual singleton
comparison, for either s=1 or s=2. This proves coverage/funding do not repair
the missing boundary condition.

5. INDEPENDENT EXACT COMPUTATIONAL CHECKS
check_local_separation.py constructs technology and Jacobians independently
of any root implementation. It compares the sorting/assignment oracle with
all actual coalitions' normalized direct direction problems:

 max pi*x - sum_(j,a in C) ell_(j,a)d_(j,a)
 s.t. A*x <= sum_j d_j, sum_(j,a)d_(j,a)<=1, x,d>=0.

The optimum is positive exactly when that coalition has a local OUT
violation. This LP searches arbitrary distributed directions and simultaneous
activity mixtures. Every solver result is reconstructed using rational
numbers and checked for primal feasibility, dual feasibility, nonnegativity,
and exact equality of objective values. No floating tolerance decides PASS.
