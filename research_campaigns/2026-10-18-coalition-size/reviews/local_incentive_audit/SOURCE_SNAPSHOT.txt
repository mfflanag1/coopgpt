LOCAL INCENTIVE SEPARATION FOR FIXED ARITY
Root derivation, 18 October 2026. Candidate pending independent audit.
This is a differential reformulation, not a general funding-bound improvement.
Its ingredients are elementary calculus, packing duality and order statistics;
no historical priority claim is made.

FIXED CONTRACT AND REGULARITY
Keep the campaign's actual owners, finite coordinate boxes, nonnegative finite
packing technology F(c)=max{pi*x:Ax<=c,x>=0}, and protected coalitions of size
at most s. Let y be smooth on each positive-coordinate face, including a smooth
extension at its endowment upper boundaries. Payments to owners with zero
reports remain variables. Coverage and funding are still imposed separately.

On a face J, the positive coordinates are (j,a) in J. Write
  JAC[i;j,a] = partial y_i / partial b_(j,a).
At a relative interior point of this face, consider nonnegative directions d
supported in J and on a protected coalition C. Put Y_C=sum_(i in C)y_i.
The differential conditions are
  TCWM: sum_(j,a) d_(j,a) partial_(j,a)Y_C >=0;
  OUT:  sum_(j,a) d_(j,a) partial_(j,a)Y_C >=F(sum_j d_j). (1)
Only locally feasible directions are used; upper boundary values follow by
the stated smooth extension and limits from below.

1. EXACT TCWM SEPARATION BY SORTING
For every active report coordinate (j,a), define z_i=JAC[i;j,a]. Let
  Neg_k(z;D) = the sum of the at most k most negative entries z_i with i notin D,
where positive entries are never added and k is capped at n-|D|.
Then the TCWM part of (1) for EVERY eligible C is equivalent to
  z_j + Neg_(s-1)(z;{j}) >=0                            (2)
for every (j,a) in J. Indeed a restoring owner j must belong to C, and the
remaining at most s-1 members minimize its coalition derivative by taking
exactly those negative entries. Nonnegative linear combinations handle all d.
Passive owners, including zero-report owners, are included in the sorting.

2. EXACT FULL-OUT SEPARATION USING AT MOST r RESTORING OWNERS
Assume (2). For every positive-profit activity e with support R_e and arity
|R_e|<=r, consider each assignment f:R_e -> actual owners for which
(f(a),a) is in J. Let D be the image of f. Skip assignments with |D|>s.
For this assignment define the owner score
  z_i(e,f) = sum_(a in R_e) A_(a,e) JAC[i;f(a),a].
Then the full OUT part of (1) for every C,d is equivalent to (2) and
  sum_(i in D) z_i(e,f) + Neg_(s-|D|)(z(e,f);D) >= pi_e   (3)
for every such activity and assignment.

Necessity. For each C containing D take d_(f(a),a)=A_(a,e), all other
coordinates zero. One unit of activity e is feasible, hence F(sum d)>=pi_e.
Minimizing the resulting sum_(i in C)z_i over D subset C, |C|<=s gives (3).
Condition (2) is necessary since the nonnegative OUT right side implies TCWM.

Sufficiency. Fix C and write ell_(j,a)=sum_(i in C)JAC[i;j,a] for j in C.
By (2), every eligible ell_(j,a) is nonnegative. For a resource a eligible
to some owner in C, set q_a=min_(j in C:(j,a) in J)ell_(j,a).
For each activity whose every input row is eligible, choose a minimizing
owner separately for every row. Its image D is contained in C. Inequality
(3), and the fact that C cannot have a lower score than its displayed
minimum, give sum_a A_(a,e)q_a>=pi_e. Activities with an ineligible input row
cannot be used at aggregate direction sum d and may be omitted. Thus q is a
nonnegative dual cover for the remaining production problem, and
  sum_(j,a)ell_(j,a)d_(j,a) >= sum_a q_a sum_j d_(j,a)
                                >= F(sum_j d_j).
This proves (1). The minimizers and restoring owners are never split into
fractional agents; f assigns each entire input requirement to one actual owner
only for the separating test. It is enough to test these directions because
dual feasibility then controls arbitrary distributed restorations.

3. WHAT IS AND IS NOT POLYNOMIAL
At one fixed profile, with a supplied Jacobian, (2) uses one sort per eligible
coordinate. For fixed maximum arity r, (3) uses at most n^r assignments per
activity, each requiring one sum and sort over n owners. Hence all local
coalition incentive inequalities can be separated in polynomial time in the
explicit technology and owner count, independently of the number of eligible
coalitions. Arithmetic complexity is meant; exact bit complexity requires
exactly represented derivative data. This does not separate all-coalition
coverage, test a continuum of profiles, or construct an integrable Jacobian.

4. ZERO-FACE ADMISSION IS ESSENTIAL FOR GLOBAL INCENTIVES
Local inequalities alone do not control the jump when a zero coordinate first
becomes positive. In addition impose the following directed admission condition
at every b and nonnegative feasible direction d supported on a protected C:
  Y_C(b) <= lim_(t downarrow0) Y_C(b+t d).                (4)
The limit exists whenever (2) holds, since on t>0 the face is fixed and Y_C
is nondecreasing in t. It is finite if the funding bound is imposed. When the
ray stays in the starting face, (4) follows from continuity; only face entry
needs an additional check. The actual coalition C is the same at both ends.

With facewise smoothness, TCWM is exactly (2)+(4); full OUT is exactly
(2)+(3)+(4). Necessity follows by dividing the finite incentive inequality
between b and b+epsilon*d by epsilon inside a face, using F(epsilon*d)=
epsilon*F(d), and by taking a limit for admission. For sufficiency integrate
the local derivative along t in (epsilon,1). TCWM gives Y_C(b+d)>=Y_C(b+
epsilon*d). Full OUT gives Y_C(b+d)-Y_C(b+epsilon*d)>=(1-epsilon)F(sum d).
Let epsilon decrease to zero and apply (4). This covers simultaneous report
changes and all positive and zero-coordinate endpoints. There is no assumed
continuity across zero faces.

Exact necessity example for admission. Take two actual scalar owners on [0,1]
and the one-resource additive technology F(c)=c. Define y_2(b)=b_2, and
y_1(b)=b_1 for b_1>0 but y_1(0,b_2)=b_2. This rule is nonnegative, covers
every coalition, and is funded by 2F. On every positive-coordinate face it is
smooth and obeys all within-face full-OUT derivative tests, for s=1 or2.
Nevertheless at (0,1) owner1 receives1, and at (epsilon,1) receives epsilon.
The singleton restoration violates even TCWM. Its missing condition is exactly
admission (4). Consequently coverage and a finite funding bound cannot be used
to silently infer the omitted boundary condition.

5. CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITATION
Combining with the independently checked multiplicative regularization theorem,
the unrestricted infimum funding can be expressed over facewise-smooth rules
satisfying these exact local conditions, directed admission, coverage and
funding. No measurability restriction is silently imposed on the original
class; regularization preserves the infimum but need not attain its value.
This yields a polynomial separation oracle for the local incentive part of
a candidate Jacobian at a fixed profile. It does NOT yield a subquadratic
funding mechanism: global coverage, the report continuum, Jacobian
integrability, and face admission remain genuine obligations. In particular,
discarding (4) or treating a feasible Jacobian independently at each point as
an already defined payment rule would invalidate the conclusion.
