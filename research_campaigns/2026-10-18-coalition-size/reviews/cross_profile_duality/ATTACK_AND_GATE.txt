1. EXACT PACKING-SET FORM OF THE MISSING INEQUALITY
Discard columns with no resource consumption and zero profit; retain a matrix
A whose every column consumes some positive resource. Define the compact,
convex, downward-closed packing polytope
  P_A(c)={x>=0:Ax<=c}.
For any finite actual-profile dual lambda,mu,nu with g_(p,i)<=nu_p, define
the Minkowski sums
  S(lambda)=sum_(p,C) lambda_(p,C) P_A(b_C(p)),
  T(nu)=sum_p nu_p P_A(c_p).
Scalar multiplication and sums here act on ACTIVITY AMOUNTS, not owner labels.

The exact universal TCWM upper assertion with factor K(s) is equivalent to
  S(lambda) subset K(s) T(nu)                           (PD)
for EVERY arity-at-most3 matrix A, finite legal report set, fixed owner model,
and nonnegative dual multipliers satisfying the actual coupled balances.
For full OUT the corresponding assertion has the larger left side
  S(lambda)+sum_e mu_e P_A(delta_e) subset K(s) T(nu).  (PD-OUT)
The balance matrix is unchanged; only the right sides of comparison rows differ.

Why this equivalence is exact: for a nonnegative profit vector pi, the support
function of P_A(c) is F_pi(c). Support functions add under Minkowski sums.
Thus (PD) is exactly
  sum lambda F_pi(b_C)<=K(s) sum nu F_pi(c_p)
for every pi>=0, which is the finite dual upper inequality. The sets are
downward-closed in the nonnegative orthant, so a failed inclusion has a separating
profit vector that can be chosen nonnegative: negative coefficients can be
replaced by0 without changing the support function of a downward-closed set.
The all-profile statement then follows from the companion's compactness proof.
For a PARTICULAR fixed profit vector, its scalar inequality alone is needed;
the set inclusion represents the universal-over-public-profits assertion.

Consequently, a proof of (PD) with K(s)<=C*s, or K(s)=o(s^2), would give the
requested upper. The accepted scaled-binomial theorem plus the same support-
function argument already implies (PD-OUT) with K(s)=3s^2. Merely rewriting
this as a polytope containment is not progress on its exponent.

Equivalently, (PD) asks whether every weighted collection of actual coalition
packing plans can be decomposed into weighted plans at the budget profiles,
with at most K(s) resource expansion. Feasibility of the multipliers connects
those profiles through actual protected comparisons. This preserves arbitrary
multirow owners and does not assume any scalar-unit-agent representation.

2. WHAT ARITY THREE DOES GIVE, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

hypergraph support argument in inputs/energy/BOUNDED_COALITION_SPARSE_PRODUCTION.
It is a decomposition of productive usage, not a replacement of one owner by
several strategic agents, and not a decomposition of the whole mechanism.

The unresolved step is simultaneous packing compatibility at the budget profiles.
Different supplier pieces may travel through different owner/profile balances,

4. DISAGGREGATING THE COALITION MULTIPLIER IS AN EXACTLY REJECTED SHORTCUT
A TCWM row for protected pair{i,k}, when only k restores, contains BOTH
  y_i(h)-y_i(l) and y_k(h)-y_k(l)
with the SAME multiplier. Removing the second term invents monotonicity of
owner i's payment in somebody else's report. It is not a consequence of TCWM.

The error can be quantified on min2. Use N fixed fully eligible owners and
the legal reports0,A=(1,0),B=(0,1). For every i!=j let P_ij have only i at A
and j at B. Let H_j have j at B and everyone else at A; let H'_i have i at A
and everyone else at B. All these profiles have production1.

Low coverage gives y_i(P_ij)+y_j(P_ij)>=1. The FALSE disaggregated rows can
transport y_i from P_ij to H_j while other owners restore A one at a time,
and y_j from P_ij to H'_i while other owners restore B. Each individual step
would come from a legal protected pair consisting of the passive owner and
the restoring owner, but the latter owner's payment terms have been omitted.
Summing the false rows and all N(N-1) coverage rows gives
  sum_(2N high profiles) total payment >= N(N-1),
and would therefore claim K>=(N-1)/2 for pair TCWM on min2.
This contradicts the accepted all-coalition min2 factor3/2 theorem as N grows.

The independent checker constructs the entire fake certificate at N=8. It has
672 owner-only comparison rows,16 budget profiles and false lower7/2. When
the omitted restoring-owner terms are restored with exactly the SAME weights,
432 actual owner/profile balances violate g<=nu. Thus it is explicitly NOT
a certificate for the real model. This diagnostic catches the precise flow
uncoupling error, rather than merely citing a known upper as an objection.

Similarly, treating one multirow owner holding(1,1,1) as three pure unit agents
changes the size of a complete withdrawal from1 protected owner to3 agents.
At s=1 those are different incentive contracts. A valid resource-copy argument
must keep every copy under its original actual owner and count coalitions by
actual labels. No external scalar-agent flow theorem is used here as a substitute.

7. STOPPING DECISION
No derivation of (PD) at O(s) or o(s^2) survived this bounded attack. The exact
remaining inequality is (PD), or its stronger OUT variant, with the ACTUAL
coupled comparison matrix and activity packing structure retained. The pooled-
resource relaxation and independent-owner-flow relaxation are explicitly ruled
out as ways to obtain that bound. Pointwise arity support alone is insufficient.

The universal packing inclusion is equivalent in strength to the requested
upper theorem; it is not a newly proved intermediate lemma. This route should
not be reopened merely by restating it. A continuation needs an additional
packing/coupling inequality that quantitatively controls budget-profile resource
congestion while preserving the shared coalition multipliers. None is supplied
here. The classical duality/compactness result alone does not meet the research
success gate. Existing projective lower progress remains intact and independent.
