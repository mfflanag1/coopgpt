FINITE-PROFILE DUAL: VALID CYCLE REDUCTIONS AND A COUPLING OBSTRUCTION
Independent derivation, 18 October 2026. Exact computational consequences,
but no O(s), o(s^2), or other unrestricted funding improvement.

SETUP
Use the actual finite dual in reviews/cross_profile_duality/
FINITE_AND_ALL_PROFILE_DUAL.txt. For profiles Q, a comparison (l,h,C) has
mu_(l,h,C)>=0, contributes +mu to every (h,i), i in C, and -mu to every
(l,i), i in C. Coverage and budget multipliers are lambda and nu. TCWM has
comparison objective zero; full OUT has mu F(delta_(l,h)). Each actual owner
has its full resource vector throughout. The finite LP includes ALL legal
endpoint comparisons among Q. This closure matters for shortcut edges.

1. THERE ARE NO NONTRIVIAL DIRECTED PROFILE CYCLES
For distinct comparable profiles, the sum of all reported resource coordinates
strictly increases along a restoration. Therefore the directed comparison graph
is already acyclic. Equal-endpoint comparisons have a zero row and RHS and can
be dropped. Merely invoking standard directed-cycle cancellation cannot improve
this dual: there are no such cycles to eliminate.

2. EXACT PER-COALITION SOURCE--SINK FOREST NORMAL FORM
Lemma. Fix a feasible finite dual. It can be replaced by one with the SAME
lambda,nu and the SAME owner/profile balances, with the same TCWM objective
or a weakly larger full-OUT objective, such that for each protected C:
 (a) no profile has both a positive incoming and positive outgoing C-edge;
 (b) the undirected support of its C-edges is a forest;
 (c) there are at most |Q|-1 positive C-edges.
Consequently, whenever the finite LP has an optimal dual, it has one with
at most (|Q|-1) sum_(j=1)^min(s,n) binom(n,j) positive comparison variables.
The statements apply to arity three, and in fact to every finite nonnegative
packing technology. No new arity-dependent estimate is hidden in this bound.

Proof. For a fixed C, view mu as a nonnegative scalar flow on its acyclic
profile graph, with divergence d_p=incoming-outgoing. Standard finite flow
decomposition with these prescribed supplies/sinks expresses it as weighted
paths from d_p<0 to d_p>0. Replace each such path by its direct endpoint edge.
All intermediate changes involve only C and are coordinatewise restorations,
so that endpoint comparison is legal, still protects exactly C, and is in Q.
The scalar divergence is unchanged. Its contribution to every owner balance
is d_p times the SAME indicator 1_(i in C), so all coupled balances are unchanged.
For OUT, delta_path is the sum of its successive restored resource bundles.
Superadditivity gives F(delta_path)>=sum_path F(delta_edge); multiplying by
the nonnegative path weight shows that the objective cannot decrease. TCWM's
objective does not involve mu. The resulting graph is bipartite from its
negative-divergence sources to positive-divergence sinks, proving (a).

If this bipartite support contains an undirected cycle, it has even length.
Alternately add/subtract a scalar theta along its edges. This preserves every
scalar divergence. Choose the sign whose linear OUT objective change is
nonnegative (either sign works for TCWM), then increase theta until some edge
weight reaches zero. No new support edge is introduced. Iteration terminates
with a forest; a forest on at most |Q| nonisolated vertices has at most |Q|-1
edges. Repeating independently for each C proves the lemma. It cannot increase
the optimum value because the resulting dual remains feasible with unchanged
budget denominator. This also explains the optimal-dual assertion.

Concrete consequence: in a finite certificate search, same-coalition comparison
depth is unnecessary, and a certificate can be normalized to the above explicit
support bound. For OUT, shortcutting can strengthen a candidate certificate.
For example, one owner restoring A then B+C in min(A,B,C) has two zero OUT
increments but the direct A+B+C restoration has outside value one. Endpoint
coefficients agree exactly. This is a useful reduction, not a funding theorem.

3. WHY THE COALITION LABEL CANNOT BE ERASED: EXACT ARITY-THREE EXAMPLE
There are three fixed actual owners with unit-cube reports and the single
unit-profit activity F(A,B,C)=min(A,B,C), of arity exactly three. Use bundles
  L=(1,0,1), R=(0,1,1), 0=(0,0,0).
At all these profiles and coalitions the third total is A+B, so coalition
production equals min(number of L, number of R). The witness uses the six
permutations of (0,L,R) as low profiles, and the six profiles of types LLR/RRL
as high profiles. Every high profile has total production one.

At each low profile restore its zero owner to L, protecting that owner together
with the unchanged L owner; also restore it to R, protecting it with the
unchanged R owner. These are 12 legitimate pair-TCWM edges. Assign:
 - weight 1/4 to each such comparison;
 - weight 1/4 to grand-coalition coverage at each low profile;
 - weight 1/2 to each unlike-pair coverage at each high profile (12 rows);
 - budget multiplier one at each of the six high profiles.

At each high profile both majority owners receive coefficient one after adding
the two incoming comparison contributions and the unlike-pair coverages; the
minority owner also receives coefficient one. At each low profile the L and R
owner coefficients are zero and the zero-owner coefficient is -1/4. Thus g<=nu
exactly. The nonnegative-payment slack at that low zero owner is 1/4; no
dummy-zero assumption is imposed. Coverage numerator is 6/4+12/2=15/2,
budget denominator is 6, and the valid lower ratio is 5/4.

Every edge goes directly from a low to a high profile. For each fixed protected
pair, the support is already two disjoint two-edge stars, hence a forest in
the normal form of Section 2. The UNLABELED profile graph is a 12-cycle. But
the 12 ACTUAL comparison columns are linearly independent: each edge has a
unique low-profile/incumbent-owner coordinate. That row appears in no other
comparison column. Therefore no nonzero signed change of the 12 comparison
weights can preserve all owner/profile balances. The alternating unlabelled-
cycle perturbation preserves aggregate profile flow but changes owner balances.

Consequences: even an arity-three, pair-protected, per-coalition forest dual
can obstruct K=1. Unlabelled cycle elimination is invalid unless one also
proves how to change coverage/budget multipliers while controlling the ratio.
This example rejects the naive cancellation, not every conceivable theorem
using cycles and not the existence of some other certificate representation.

4. AN EXACT COMPARISON-BLOCK COMPRESSION THAT RETAINS THE COUPLING
For a fixed nontrivial endpoint pair l<h, let D be the set of owners whose
reports change, d=|D|. Legal coalitions are exactly D subset C, |C|<=s; if d>s
there is no such comparison. Suppose d<=s and define
  M=sum_C mu_C,   w_i=sum_(C containing i) mu_C.
The EXACT cone of allowable owner-incidence vectors is
  M>=0;
  w_i=M for i in D;
  0<=w_i<=M for i outside D;
  sum_(i outside D) w_i <=(s-d)M.                       (10)

Necessity is immediate. For sufficiency, if M=0 all weights vanish. Otherwise
x_i=w_i/M on the unchanged owners lies in the uniform-matroid independent-set
polytope {0<=x_i<=1, sum x_i<=s-d}. Every vertex is integral: with two fractional
coordinates one can perturb them oppositely if the sum constraint binds; with
slack, a fractional coordinate can be perturbed alone; with exactly one
fractional coordinate and a binding integer sum it cannot be fractional.
Thus x is a convex combination of indicators of subsets J of size <=s-d.
Multiplying those convex weights by M and setting C=D union J gives valid
comparison multipliers realizing w. Caratheodory needs at most n-d+1 sets.

Replace the whole coalition family for that endpoint pair by M,w satisfying
(10), with +w at h and -w at l in the owner balances. For OUT its objective
is M F(delta), because the restored bundle depends only on the endpoints,
not on which unchanged owners join C. For TCWM it is zero. This is an exact
extended formulation of the comparison block, not independent owner flows.

Explicit consequence: for q=|Q| profiles the comparison part has O(n q^2)
variables and inequalities rather than q^2 times an exponential coalition
family. Separation in primal payments for one pair is equally explicit:
take sum_(i in D) Delta y_i, and add the up to s-d most negative unchanged-
owner increments. A negative result is exactly a violated protected-coalition
constraint. This needs O(n log n) time by sorting. The coverage block still
contains all coalitions, and this does not make the full all-profile problem
polynomial or prove any bound depending only on s.

In particular the restoring owner cannot disappear from (10): every changed
owner receives coefficient M, and every passive coefficient is at most M.
The previously rejected owner-only-flow relaxation violates this condition.

5. ZERO-PRODUCTION PROFILE PRUNING
One more exact simplification: profiles with f_p=0 can be omitted without
changing the finite optimum K_Q. Their payment vectors are forced to zero.
An outgoing comparison from such l to h has nonnegative high coalition payment
under TCWM. Under OUT, coverage at h gives y_C(h)>=F(b_C(h))>=F(delta), so it
is also redundant. A comparison entering a zero-output profile must start at
zero output by monotonicity. This eliminates these vertices without inspecting
the mechanism, but does not eliminate individual zero-report owners at positive
profiles, such as the low profiles in Section 3.

STATUS AND PRECISE MISSING STEP
The valid lemmas provide certificate normalization, stronger OUT shortcut rows,
and an exact polynomial-size comparison block for a chosen finite profile set.
They use actual owners and preserve their coalition coupling. The arity-three
witness rules out the simple cross-coalition cycle cancellation. Nothing here
bounds the interaction of different protected coalitions and different optimal
production-price faces by O(s) or o(s^2). A funding improvement would require
an additional inequality controlling that interaction while preserving (10)
and all coalition coverage. The normal-form support bound still depends on the
profile/owner counts, and is not a research-success-gate result.
