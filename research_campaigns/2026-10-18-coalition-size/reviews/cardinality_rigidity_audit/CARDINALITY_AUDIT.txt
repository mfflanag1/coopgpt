INDEPENDENT AUDIT: GLOBALLY SHARED ADAPTIVE CARDINALITY WEIGHTS
18 October 2026. Started 22:35:36 UTC. Own audit directory only.

FINDING
The stated extension is valid under the parent's uniform-model contract and
the explicit convention that sampling is over the full fixed actual-owner
roster, including owners whose current report is zero. No additional symmetry,
continuity, or consistency across populations is needed. D must bound the
number of distinct other local owners, not merely the polynomial degree;
the extension explicitly imposes this stronger bound. This audit independently
derives the lemma and limit below. Its conclusion concerns the architecture,
not arbitrary mechanisms, and supplies no new unrestricted funding bound.

1. EXACT FIXED-ROSTER COUPLING LEMMA
Let m=n-1 and take d<=m distinct local owners other than the focal owner.
Draw a uniform k-subset of all m other ACTUAL owner labels, where 0<=k<=m.
For any particular local inclusion pattern with t included labels,

  H_(m,k,d)(pattern)=binom(m-d,k-t)/binom(m,k),             (1)

with out-of-range binomial coefficients zero. Equivalently its d bits are
d ordered draws without replacement from m positions containing k ones.
Draw those d positions with replacement instead. The resulting bits are iid
Bernoulli(p), p=k/m. On the event that the drawn positions are distinct, the
ordered position sequence is uniform without replacement. Couple a uniform
without-replacement sequence to this one on that event, and use an independent
uniform distinct sequence on the complement. Its marginal law is correct.
The event of any repeated position has probability at most binom(d,2)/m.
Consequently

  TV(H_(m,k,d), Bernoulli(k/m)^d) <= binom(d,2)/m.        (2)

The bound holds uniformly in k, including 0 and m. It is harmless if the
right side exceeds one. For a function g taking values in [0,W],

  |E_H g-E_B g| <= W binom(d,2)/m.                      (3)

There is no factor two in (3): the oscillation of g, rather than its absolute
supremum in a signed [-W,W] interval, is W. This proof applies to arbitrary
local functions of the inclusion labels. No independence of different owners'
samples or different components' samples is assumed or needed.

For common nonnegative finite cardinality weights lambda_k(b), put
  mu_b=sum_k lambda_k(b) delta_(k/(n-1)),
  beta_b=sum_k lambda_k(b).
If all relevant local functions have d<=D and W<=1, their payments differ
from their Bernoulli-polynomial integrals by at most
  e_(n,b)=beta_b binom(D,2)/(n-1),                       (4)
and therefore by the extension's more conservative beta_b D^2/(n-1).
For a sentinel with size L, divide its payment by L before applying (4).

2. WHAT CANCELS, AND WHICH OWNER LABELS COUNT
For a disjoint production sum F=sum_a F_a, if owner i reports only in
component a, then for EVERY selected subset R,
  F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)=F_a(b_i+b_(R intersect owners(a)))-F_a(b_(R intersect owners(a))).
All other components cancel pointwise. Correlations between component inclusion
counts under fixed-size sampling therefore do not enter an individual marginal.
Owners can instead hold identically coordinated unit copies of that component;
the number d counts distinct OTHER OWNERS, not resource copies or rows.

Zero-report owners remain in the population of m labels. Their own marginal
payment is identically zero in this formula, but their inclusion can occupy a
sample slot. Removing them changes the rule. For instance, let a focal owner
hold one A unit, one other owner hold one B unit for min(A,B), and two more
actual owners report zero. At k=1 the focal marginal is 1/3, not 1. An
active-only implementation would sample from a different population and is
not covered by this extension. A currently zero focal test owner is likewise
still a fixed label, distinct from all unchanged protected members.

The SAME lambda_k(b) must apply to anchor, sentinels, test owners and hard owners
at that profile. This is exactly why one anchor bounds the mass used in every
error estimate. Anonymous, owner-specific or component-specific weights need
not have this property. The proof does not derive it from anonymity.

3. REPLICATION WITH EXPLICIT THREE-PARAMETER ORDER
Fix first a FINITE nonempty list W of threshold comparisons, T=|W|. Choose
D>=r-1 and at least the number of distinct other local owners needed for every
test endpoint. Sentinel k has k+2 owners in total, so k<=D-1 also meets d<=D.
Only after fixing W,T,D let the repetition count N tend to infinity. Set
  L=N^2 T,   A=N(DL+NT+1).
An additive anchor of value A has cardinality marginal exactly A for EVERY
k, so its payment is A beta_b. Total production on the sequence is
  A+DL+NT+1=A(1+1/N).
Funding and nonnegativity imply beta_b<=B_N=K(1+1/N), even when lambda changes
arbitrarily at every endpoint. The test owner's local marginal lies in [0,1],
and hence its actual payment lies in [0,B_N].

Write E_N=B_N D^2/(n-1). At a restoration compare the ACTUAL coalition
consisting of the focal owner and an unchanged designated sentinel owner.
The two normalized sentinel errors have absolute value at most E_N each.
Pair TCWM therefore implies
  Delta integral f_j dmu >= -B_N/L-2E_N.                (5)
The anchor moment beta obeys Delta beta>=-B_N/A, which is stronger. Every
moment is in [0,B_N]. Summing its positive and negative variation over NT
transitions, then averaging over N cycles, gives one cycle whose summed
absolute variation over the D+1 basis moments is at most
  eps_N=(D+1)[B_N/N+2T(B_N/L+2E_N)].                    (6)

Owner count is at least 3NT because each threshold component has at least
three distinct owners; more explicitly
  n=C_0+C_W N,
  C_0=1+r+D(D+3)/2,
  C_W=sum_(w in W) (number of local owners in test w)>=3T,
before optional extra zero-report owners or a connector owner. Thus
  eps_N <= (D+1)B_N[1/N+2/N^2+4T D^2/(3NT-1)] ->0.    (7)
This only asserts convergence with D,T fixed. It does not assert that total
sampling error over the entire NT-transition sequence tends to zero.
The latter may stay bounded. The selected cycle, not the whole sequence,
is the argument's required object.

4. LIMIT INEQUALITIES WITH ACTUAL COALITION ERRORS
Let nu_N be the measure at the selected cycle's start. For a fixed polynomial
P of degree <=D, expand P in the fixed basis {1,p(1-p)^j:0<=j<D}.
At any endpoint in that cycle,
  |integral P d(mu_b-nu_N)| <= ||P||_(basis,1) eps_N.
For a protected q-owner test, q<=s, the sum of its actual marginals differs
from the corresponding polynomial integral by at most q E_N. Therefore its
TCWM inequality yields
  integral (P_high-P_low) dnu_N
    >= -2q E_N-(||P_high||+||P_low||) eps_N.            (8)
There is one common measure at each endpoint, but the low and high measures
need not agree. Equation (8) is what makes a common limiting measure valid.

For the unchanged hard min_r coalition, each owner's Bernoulli polynomial is
p^(r-1). Its cardinality marginal is instead (k)_(r-1)/(n-1)_(r-1), not
exactly p^(r-1). Coverage and (4) give
  integral p^(r-1) dnu_N >= 1/r-E_N.                   (9)
The r-owner coalition need not have size <=s: coverage includes ALL coalitions.

Positive measures on [0,1] with mass bounded by B_N have a weakly convergent
subsequence. Equations (7)--(9) imply one limit nu, mass <=K, satisfying every
test in the fixed finite list and integral p^(r-1)dnu>=1/r. Compactness with
the finite-intersection property THEN ranges over finite test lists. These are
two separate limits. No uniform bound on the basis coefficients as W varies
is required, and no infinite actual-owner game is asserted.

5. COMPLETING THE PARENT SUPPORT ARGUMENT INDEPENDENTLY
The threshold integrands are (1/k)(1-p)^(q-2)(1-qp) T_(k,t)(p), for q=2,s.
For q=2,t=k-1, T=p^k. Dominated convergence gives -nu({1})>=0, removing the
atom at 1. If nu((1/s,1))>0, choose a nonatom a>1/s with nu((a,1))>0.
The tests t=floor(ka) have tails tending to 1_(a,1) away from a. Dominated
convergence in the q=s test gives a nonnegative integral of a function strictly
negative on (a,1), a contradiction. Thus support(nu) is contained in [0,1/s].
Combining (9) in the limit with its mass bound gives
  1/r <= integral p^(r-1)dnu <= K/s^(r-1),
and hence K>=s^(r-1)/r, as claimed.

6. OWNER-COUNT-DEPENDENT FUNDING AND SCOPE
If a subsequence n_j tends to infinity with uniform-over-institutions funding
kappa_(n_j)<=K<s^(r-1)/r, fix W first and choose
  N_j=floor((n_j-C_0)/C_W).
It tends to infinity. Pad the unused roster positions with zero reports.
The cardinality distribution changes with n_j, but (1)--(4) use that exact
n_j and remain uniform in k. The anchor still bounds beta by K(1+1/N_j).
Hence the same contradiction proves liminf_n kappa_n>=s^(r-1)/r.
There is no requirement that the finite-model mechanisms agree across n_j.
It IS necessary that kappa_n bound every selected technology with n owners;
a bound for one fixed institution alone cannot support this replication claim.

All paired sentinel comparisons and q-member threshold comparisons use actual
fixed owner identities. Unchanged protected members are allowed by the contract.
The report-adaptive map need not be measurable. Nonnegative weights and common
weights are essential assumptions. The result does not cover arbitrary rules,
owner-specific weights, subset weights sensitive to reported bundles, or a
single fixed min_r technology across all replication models.
