PRODUCTIVE DORMANT GROUPS: A COVER/LINK SYMMETRY OBSTRUCTION
Created 2026-10-18 22:23 UTC. Method-specific lemma, not a mechanism upper.

LEMMA
Let H be a finite 3-uniform hypergraph and W a vertex cover of H. For w in W,
let L_w be its link graph: {a,b} is an edge of L_w iff {w,a,b} is an edge of H.
If the matching number of L_w exceeds |W|, every automorphism g of H sends
w into W.

Proof. Suppose g(w) is outside W. Since W covers every image edge
{g(w),g(a),g(b)}, at least one of g(a),g(b) lies in W for every link edge
{a,b}. Thus g^{-1}(W) is a vertex cover of L_w with |W| vertices. Every
matching in L_w has size at most |W|, contradiction. No linear geometry,
regularity, or vertex-transitivity assumption is needed.

COROLLARY FOR A CANDIDATE EXPONENT-3/4 ACTIVATION
Take V over F2 with independent generators u,v,e_1,...,e_M. The three cap
rows are W={u,v,u+v}. Private block j is the affine quartet e_j+span(u,v).
Use the full binary projective triple technology, one unit-report owner per
listed positive row, and all other reports zero. At least one private block
is full. Each of N other dormant blocks has exactly three of its four rows
positive; restoring its missing row would enlarge a productive private
orbit from3 to4 with only one changed owner.

All supported triples either are W itself, or contain one cap and two rows
from the same private block. This follows by comparing the independent e_j
coordinates. Therefore W is a vertex cover and total production is at most3
(put dual price1 on W and every zero-capacity row,0 on other positive rows).

For each w in W, a three-point subset of an affine quartet contains exactly
one pair with difference w. Different private blocks provide disjoint pairs.
Consequently the link matching of EACH cap has size at least N. If N>=4,
the lemma implies every supported-profile automorphism preserves W setwise.
In particular no cap can be exchanged with a private owner by a profile
stabilizer. The seven owners of a full Fano seed cannot be one payment orbit
once four productive dormant triples are present.

WHY THIS MATTERS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
A tempting recurrence uses four new symmetric private owners but only one
restorer, producing apparent exponent3/4. Its constant private seed was to
come from Fano seed transitivity (seven equal payments at least1/3). The
lemma proves that transitivity is impossible at the proposed starting
profile with unboundedly many productive dormant groups. Starting before
those groups are productive requires a separate preparation argument;
payments may then transfer to the newly reported dormant owners.

This rejects the proposed symmetry seed for this route. It does NOT prove
an O(sqrt(s)) payment upper bound, does not rule out multi-orbit seed
inequalities, and does not address changing cap covers between profiles.
It applies to any profile automorphism, so allowing more automorphisms than
the linear maps does not repair the failed seed.

CAUTION ABOUT A RELATED FINITE-PROFILE ESCAPE
Uniformly splitting a constant private total P over N positive private
owners satisfies the TCWM inequalities for one-owner restorations along
a path where that uniformity is available: each old owner loses
P/[N(N+1)], and the restorer gains P/(N+1). This verifies only those path
inequalities. It is NOT a full mechanism on the projective institution;
additional profile automorphisms can equate caps with private owners at
small intermediate profiles. In particular no globally equivariant upper
rule or all-profile feasibility claim is made from that calculation.
