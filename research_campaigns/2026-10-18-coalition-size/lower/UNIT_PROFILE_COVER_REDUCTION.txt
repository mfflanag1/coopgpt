BOUNDED-WORTH MONOTONE UNIT PROFILES HAVE ONE SMALL FIXED COVER
2026-10-18 22:31:40 UTC. Structural reduction for certificate searches.

Work with an r-uniform unit-profit packing technology and unit capacities
on a subset S of rows, zero elsewhere. Let H_S be its supported hypergraph.
All statements also hold for edge size at most r. Distinct owner labels and
all-zero owners do not affect these production facts.

1. VALUE1 FORCES A COMMON ROW.
If H_S is nonempty and its fractional matching value equals1, LP duality
gives a nonnegative fractional vertex cover y on S with total cost1.
Every edge e must satisfy sum_(x in e)y_x>=1=sum_(x in S)y_x. Thus every
positive-weight row belongs to every supported edge. Since some weight is
positive, all supported activities have a common row.
Conversely a common row caps production at1, and any edge produces1.

2. WITHOUT A COMMON ROW THERE IS A GAP.
If a nonempty supported hypergraph has empty edge intersection, choose an
edge e0 and, for each x in e0, an edge excluding x. After removing duplicate
choices this is a family of m<=r+1 edges with empty intersection and m>=2.
No vertex belongs to all m. Giving each selected activity intensity1/(m-1)
is feasible at every unit-capacity row. Therefore
  F(S)>=m/(m-1)>=(r+1)/r.
For arity3, any nonempty value strictly below4/3 already forces a common row.

3. A MONOTONE BOUNDED-VALUE CHAIN HAS A FIXED SMALL COVER.
Let S_0 subset ... subset S_L be any finite increasing unit-capacity support
chain and suppose F(S_L)<=B. A maximal disjoint matching in H_(S_L) has at
most floor(B) edges, because that many activities run simultaneously at1.
The union W of its rows is a vertex cover, by maximality, and
  |W|<=r floor(B).
Every earlier supported activity is also supported at S_L, so the SAME W
covers every profile of the chain. For B=1, Part1 gives a single common row
for the entire productive part of the chain.

SEARCH CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITATION
A monotone binary report ladder of bounded production cannot force an
unbounded sequence of unrelated cap covers: a fixed O(B) cover exists for
the whole ladder, even if different convenient descriptions use different
caps. At value1 one can retrospectively choose the final common cap for
all earlier profiles. Genuine unbounded cover interaction at fixed worth
must exploit incomparable profiles, branching inequalities, or reports
outside this unit-support model. Constant-size interacting covers remain
possible. None of these production facts bounds arbitrary mechanism cost
or rules out a stronger lower bound from a finite fixed cover.
