1. GENERAL SHARED-TECHNOLOGY TERNARY GADGET
The stage/bridge construction keeps original resources shared across activities.
Every middle stage consumes one original and two private link rows; endpoints
and bridges consume at most two. All coefficients/profits remain nonnegative.
For any feasible stages, maximizing bridge variables gives the claimed adjacent-
maximum expression. Choosing a minimum stage and orienting path edges toward
it proves sum z-sum adjacent max<=min z, including arbitrary real/zero stages.
Rounding every stage of an activity down to its minimum weakly reduces usage
of EACH shared original row. The rounded activity respects M when r>=2.
Conversely, equal stages and bridges M-x explicitly attain B+F_M. Activities
of original arity1 have no links/baseline/cap and remain unchanged.

Both owner maps preserve the original actual-owner roster. In the linear map,
T is fixed, nonnegative and sends b+d<=endowment into the fixed mapped box.
In the constant map, link supplies stay at owner-specific constants along
image comparisons; originals change only for the same actual restoring owners.
No claim of surjectivity onto the gadget's full box is needed. The mechanism
being pulled back must nevertheless satisfy its promised conditions on that
full fixed gadget box, not merely its image.

For EVERY coalition, the aggregate link cap is the average of normalized
reports, or a larger average of endowments. It is at least each original
activity's feasible amount. This remains true with arbitrary overlapping
activities and mixed owner bundles. Thus caps are redundant coalition by
coalition, not merely for the grand coalition.

3. OWNER-LOCAL MULTIPLICATIVE OBSTRUCTION
The deletion-loss inequality is valid with arbitrary nonnegative owner-block
bundles summing to at most total stocks, including a fixed auxiliary remainder.
Resource-wise fractions sum to at most1; the activity-wise maximum is bounded
by the sum over at most q rows. Zero-capacity unused rows cause no division
problem. Replacing owner j's report by Phi_j(0) can only increase stocks above
c-Phi_j(1), so no monotonicity of Phi itself is needed for the impossibility.
All constant-offset and additive-error algebra is correct. The coalition-
affine seed bound (q-1)B>=(r-q)alpha is distinct from the weaker constant-offset
bound qB>=(r-q)alpha; the notes keep them separate.

Minor wording clarification was sent to the author and has now been applied
and verified in the source: if the qualitative witness claim
permits an unallocated auxiliary stock, a witness of at most q actual owners
