PRECISE PARTIAL-FIBER CAP-SWITCH TEMPLATE AND ITS CURRENT GATE FAILURE
No additional growing lower theorem is claimed.

Let V have basis w,v,e_1,...,e_M and use all projective triples. There is one
fixed pure owner per nonzero vector, with report interval[0,2]. In the proposed
profile family only these rows report positively:
 P_0=v, P_1=v+w;
 H_(j,0)=e_j, H_(j,1)=e_j+w;
 L_(j,0)=v+e_j, L_(j,1)=v+e_j+w.
Every other actual owner, including w, reports zero; no owner is removed.
The supported activities in branch j are precisely
             {P_a,H_(j,b),L_(j,a+b)}, a,b in F2.
There are no supported interbranch activities because w,v,e_1,...,e_M are
linearly independent. The profile class must include its full GL symmetry
images if those images are used to constrain an arbitrary mechanism.

EXACT CURRENT OBSTRUCTION TO PROMOTING THE TEMPLATE
The nonbinary seed only gives q>=1-K/2 (old low private payment q). This
becomes vacuous once K>=2. It cannot supply a positive starting payment in
a proof intended to force K growing with s. The old binary/full-fiber seed
has a K-independent payment lower bound, but every complete-fiber activation
is already bounded by B(s/R). Making one new owner activate an almost full
four-point branch creates productive dormant groups; the former seed's
private/root symmetry is then lost. No replacement symmetry or simultaneous
inequality has yet restored a K-independent seed in that regime.

A tempting simplification is invalid for a growing lower: do not replace the
full projective technology and its orbit closure by a fixed, publicly marked
root-pair technology and assume the old seed equalities remain. In the latter
institution there is an explicit constant upper on the entire integer grid.
More generally, in a unit-profit, unit-coefficient hypergraph packing LP,
if every activity meets a fixed row cover W and one pure owner per row reports
an integer
in{0,...,L}, and k=|W|, set
 y_i(c)=c_i*1_{F(c)>0} for i in W, and0 otherwise.
Every coalition is covered by row price1 on W; if productive, the global
worth is at least1 and total payment<=kL. Thus funding is at most kL.
For full OUT: if the low profile has zero output, high coverage suffices.
Otherwise each covered owner gains exactly its own restoration; production
of the restored coalition bundle is at most the sum of its W restorations.
No owner's payment drops. This is a valid factor kL full-OUT rule for every
coalition size on this INTEGER grid. The proposed fixed root-pair simplification
has k=L=2 and therefore factor<=4, regardless of M or s.

This grid upper does NOT apply to the full projective instance, where the
public technology lacks that fixed two-row cover, or to all GL images of the
restricted profiles: different images carry different cap pairs. It also
does not assert an arbitrary-real upper because productive output can be
arbitrarily small there. Its role is to isolate why preserving the symmetry
orbit closure, rather than merely drawing many Pasch blocks, is essential.
