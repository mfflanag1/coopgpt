1. INSTITUTION AND RESTRICTED PROFILE CLASS
Let V be a finite vector space over F2 and W a subspace of size R=2^a.
Use the full projective triple technology on nonzero vectors of V, with
one fixed pure binary unit owner per row. All nonzero rows inside W report
zero throughout this restricted class. For each nonzero quotient vector
q in Q=V/W, its fiber pi^{-1}(q) has R actual owners. Profiles are unions
of complete such fibers. Owners inside W and all inactive fibers remain
actual owners with unrestricted nonnegative payment variables in any lower
system. A feasible upper assignment may choose their payments to be zero.
Write lift(S) for the complete-fiber profile associated to S subset Q\{0}.
The quotient has the same binary projective triple technology.

For every S,
                 F_V(lift(S)) = R F_Q(S).                 (1)
Every supported triple projects to three distinct nonzero quotient rows,
since a repeated quotient row would require its third row to be in W.
Lift any quotient dual by giving each row in a fiber its quotient price;
give missing rows price1. This proves the <= direction of (1). Conversely,
fix the first two quotient points of each quotient activity. There are R^2
choices of their actual lifts a,b, with the third lift a+b. If quotient
activity intensity is x, give each lifted activity intensity x/R. Each
actual row in each of its three fibers is used exactly R times, hence has
load x; total activity is R x. This proves the >= direction.

Now restrict further to quotient profiles with F_Q(S)<=1. Thus the original
positive profiles all have worth R, rather than worth1. The allowed reports
remain a restricted binary domain, and only whole-fiber restorations compare
profiles in this domain. Coalitions themselves may contain arbitrary individual
actual owners; they need NOT be unions of fibers.

2. A FEASIBLE RULE AGAINST EVERY ACTUAL PROTECTED COALITION
For s>=R put t=s/R. At a quotient star with m branches define
 q_1=1/3,
 L_m=min{s-R,2R(m-1)},
 q_m=[L_m/(R+L_m)]q_(m-1), m>=2.                         (2)
Give every row of each private fiber q_m, every row of the cap fiber
1-2q_m, and all other owners zero. At the one-edge profile this gives all
3R productive owners1/3. At a quotient unproductive profile pay all zero.
Every actual supported triple receives exactly1, hence ALL actual coalitions
are covered by the packing dual argument. Funding at a productive profile is
                 1+2(m-1)q_m.                            (3)

For one whole private fiber restored, the R restoring actual owners gain
R q_m. At most2R(m-1) old private owners lose q_(m-1)-q_m each. A protected
coalition has only s-R unchanged slots, so its total change is at least
          R q_m-L_m[q_(m-1)-q_m]=0.
The common-cap geometry and at-most-one-new-quotient-edge argument are exactly
those in UNIT_WORTH_LINEAR_DOMAIN_THEOREM.txt. Simultaneous whole-fiber
restorations telescope using the same ACTUAL coalition, including arbitrary
partial selections of unchanged fibers. Thus all restricted TCWM comparisons
hold. Full OUT follows directly: if the low profile is unproductive use high
coverage; otherwise the restored quotient set has no supported edge, so (1)
gives outside production zero. There is no assumption that inactive individual
owners must be paid zero by an arbitrary mechanism.

If s<R no nontrivial whole-fiber restoration is protected. One can use any
profilewise efficient core and attain factor1 on this restricted domain.

3. EXACT PEAK AND GENERAL CONSEQUENCE
Let B(t)=max_m[1+2(m-1)q_m] with (2). For integer t this is exactly B_t in
UNIT_WORTH_LINEAR_DOMAIN_THEOREM.txt; for noninteger t the tail ratio of the
nonconstant funding terms is m(t-1)/[t(m-1)], so the peak is m=ceil(t).
For integer t>1 there are two tied peaks m=t,t+1. At t=1 all values are1.
Monotonicity of (2) in t gives
                 B(t)<=B_ceil(t)=O(sqrt(ceil(s/R))).       (4)
More precisely B(t)-1~sqrt(2*pi)/(3sqrt(e))*sqrt(t) as t grows.
Consequently NO certificate using only all-coalition coverage, funding and
protected endpoint comparisons on this FULL-FIBER ideal can force growth
beyond O(sqrt(s/R)). This holds for arbitrary branching and incomparable
intermediate profiles. It is a constructive upper assignment on the entire
specified restricted domain, not merely a single activation path.

For the matching lower within these projective full-fiber ideals, use an
quotient star with sufficiently many branches, choosing its cap v and highs
e_1,...,e_M to be a BASIS of the quotient Q=F2^(M+1). The linear
automorphisms preserving W induce the quotient GL action; independent shears
from quotient basis vectors into W act transitively inside each relevant
fiber. They stabilize the entire specified profile, including the absent
W rows and inactive fibers. Finite averaging therefore gives one seed payment
on all3R productive owners, at least1/3 by coverage of a lifted actual triple.
At stage m the2mR private owners share a payment. The coalition of all R

old payments of restoring owners are retained and dropped only by nonnegativity.
One actual triple gives cap payment+2q_m>=1. Funding then gives (3), and choosing
a peak stage proves the restricted worst optimum B(t). This paragraph is an
