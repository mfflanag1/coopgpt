LINEAR FIXED-CAP SEQUENTIAL SYMMETRY REQUIRES MANY ROWS
2026-10-18 22:31:40 UTC. Stronger than the pointwise-flip STS barrier in one
specific linear model; still not a universal polynomial-row impossibility.

MODEL
Let W be a finite binary vector space, H={h_1,...,h_M} a set of distinct
nonzero vectors spanning W, and v independent of W. Profiles C_A have
positive unit rows
  S_A={v} union H union {v+h:h in A},       A subset H.
Assume their supported projective triples are exactly
  {v,h,v+h}, h in A.
The remaining positive rows H\A are isolated in the supported hypergraph.
All rows of W direct-sum span(v) are available in the technology, with its
usual projective triples. Domains and owner averaging are as in the main
full-cube construction; zero owners remain explicit.

CLAIM1: A NECESSARY LINEAR-SEPARATION CONDITION
Suppose |A|>=2 and the active private rows A union(v+A) are one orbit under
linear maps preserving S_A. Then every h in A lies outside span(H\A).

Proof. By transitivity there is a profile stabilizer g sending h to h+v.
The cap v is the unique supported vertex of degree |A|>=2; every other
nonisolated vertex has degree1. Hence g(v)=v. A dormant high is isolated,
so g permutes H\A setwise. On W decompose the linear map as
  g(x)=T(x)+phi(x)v,
where T maps W to W and phi:W->F2 is linear. The isolated-row permutation
implies phi(x)=0 for every x in H\A. But g(h)=h+v implies phi(h)=1.
Therefore h cannot be in span(H\A).

CLAIM2: SINGLE ACTIVATION AFTER A SMALL SEED
Suppose A_0 subset A_1 subset ... subset A_L=H, each step adding one high,
|A_0|>=2, and active private transitivity holds at every new profile A_j.
Then H\A_0 is linearly independent.

Proof. If those initially dormant highs contained a nonempty dependency,
consider its first member h to activate. All other members remain dormant
at that new profile. Thus h lies in their span, contradicting Claim1.

Consequently dim(W)>=M-|A_0|. The full projective technology therefore has
at least
  2^(M-|A_0|+1)-1
rows. A bounded-size seed cannot give polynomial row count in M for this
single-activation homogeneous ladder, even allowing arbitrary permutations
of highs and coupled active-pair flips. In an affine-coset compression with
rank(H)=O(log M), such a ladder must already begin with at least M-O(log M)
active branches.

SCOPE
This does not assume each high can flip independently while all other highs
are fixed; that stronger hypothesis was used in STEINER_ROW_COUNT_BARRIER.
It does require full active-private transitivity, the displayed star support,
linear projective symmetries, and one new low row at each step. Large batch
activation, multiple private payment orbits, a changing cap, or a different
technology may evade it. The proof does not furnish an upper mechanism.
The single-circuit high construction starts with two active branches and
uses rank M-1; it is compatible with this lower dimension bound.
