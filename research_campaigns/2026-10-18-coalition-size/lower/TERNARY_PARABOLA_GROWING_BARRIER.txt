TERNARY PARABOLA COMPRESSION NEEDS A LARGE ACTIVE SEED
2026-10-18 22:36:42 UTC. Analytic extension of the exact q9 scout; no
mechanism upper and no general impossibility of polynomial-row lower bounds.

CONSTRUCTION
Let q=3^k, k>=2, and identify the additive space F_q^2 with F3^(2k).
Rows are all q² affine points; activities are affine lines of three distinct
points, equivalently triples of distinct vectors with sum0. Put cap0 and
  H={(x,x²):x in F_q nonzero},       M=q-1.
For A subset H let the positive unit rows be
  S_A={0} union H union(-A).
Only triples {0,h,-h}, h in A, are supported. Indeed three highs summing to0
have parameters x+y+z=0 and x²+y²+z²=0, forcing (x-y)²=0 and then x=y=z,
which is not a three-distinct-point activity. With two highs and one negative
high, the equations force xy=0, excluded. The other signs are identical.
Thus grand worth is1 whenever A is nonempty.

THEOREM
If |A|>=2 and an automorphism of the affine triple technology preserves S_A
and sends any active high to any active low, then
  |A|>=q/9.
In particular, active-private transitivity requires at least q/9 branches.
This rules out a bounded-size fully symmetric seed and the direct sequential
homogeneous ladder in this family as q grows, although rows are only q².

REDUCTION TO A LOW-DEGREE POLYNOMIAL
The cap0 is the unique vertex of degree |A| in the supported star, so the
stabilizer fixes0. An automorphism of the full affine triple technology
fixing0 is F3-linear: lines {0,x,-x} give T(-x)=-T(x); a triple {x,y,-x-y}
with x!=y then gives additivity, and the x=y case follows from T(-x)=-T(x).
Hence the entire automorphism group, not just a chosen subgroup, is covered.

Write
  T(x,x²)=(X(x),Y(x))
with X,Y:F_q->F_q. In any fixed F3 basis, every coordinate of x² has degree2
in the k scalar coordinates of x. Since T is F3-linear, X and Y have
coordinate degree at most2, and every coordinate of
  Q(x)=Y(x)-X(x)²
has degree at most4. Reducing powers by x_i³=x_i never increases total
degree and gives a canonical polynomial of degree at most2 in each variable.

Dormant highs are exactly the isolated positive rows and must be permuted
among themselves. Thus Q(x)=0 whenever (x,x²) is dormant, and Q(0)=0.
If T sends (x,x²) to -(y,y²), y!=0, then
  Q(x)=-y²-(-y)²=-2y²=y²!=0
in characteristic3. Some scalar coordinate f of Q is therefore a nonzero
polynomial function of degree at most4, supported only on the active A.

ELEMENTARY SUPPORT BOUND
A nonzero polynomial function f:F3^k->F3 of total canonical degree at most d
has support at least3^(k-d/2). Prove this by induction on k. Regard f as a
polynomial in the last variable with highest exponent a in{0,1,2}, and let
its nonzero leading coefficient g have degree at most d-a. For every input
where g is nonzero, the last-variable polynomial has at least3-a nonzero
values. Hence support(f)>=(3-a) support(g), and the induction gives
  support(f)>=(3-a)3^(k-1-(d-a)/2)>=3^(k-d/2),
because (3-a)3^(a/2-1)>=1 for a=0,1,2. The k0 base is immediate. The usual
uniqueness of polynomial representation with each variable degree<3 follows
by the same one-variable root argument.

Apply d=4 to the scalar coordinate f: |A|>=support(f)>=3^(k-2)=q/9.

SCOPE AND SEARCH CONSEQUENCE
This does not rule out multi-orbit inequalities while fewer than q/9 branches
are active, or a large initial activation batch. Those approaches require a
new seed/transition argument and cannot inherit the projective independent-
high recurrence by assuming high/low equality. It strengthens the bounded
q9 observation to an unbounded-family obstruction. It does not claim any
upper bound on arbitrary payments, nor exclude other polynomial geometries.
