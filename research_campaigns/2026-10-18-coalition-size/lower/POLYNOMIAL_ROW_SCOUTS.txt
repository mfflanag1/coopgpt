POLYNOMIAL-ROW COMPRESSION SCOUTS
18 October 2026. Exact failures of specific representations, not a general
polynomial-dimension impossibility theorem. The accepted exponential-row
square-root construction is unaffected.

1. BINARY AFFINE COSET
Use rows in F2^(h+2), cap v, and high rows E={e0+x:x in F2^h}, with low mates
v+E. There are M=2^h branches and4M-1 rows. The selected support has only cap
branches. However, v<->e0 sends other high e0+x to v+x, leaving the reported
set. The seed cap/high exchange from the independent-basis construction is
invalid. Linear shears that flip private pair orientations have affine-half
supports on E, so they cannot flip one early active pair while preserving all
inactive high reports. The homogeneous small-prefix ladder is not justified.
This obstruction was independently identified by root and lower lane.

2. COMPLETE-GRAPH TRIANGLE TECHNOLOGY
Rows are edges of a complete graph; activities are its triangles. Take cap01,
high rows0j, and low rows1j. Support is the desired book of triangles, and row
count is quadratic. At an intermediate profile C_m the positive graph has
degree M+1 at vertex0 and m+1 at vertex1. With dormant high spokes present,
the endpoint-exchange symmetry is absent; active high and low owners need not
have equal payments. Independent swaps of individual spoke pairs are not
vertex permutations of the complete graph. No growing certificate obtained.

3. TERNARY PARABOLA
Use all affine points of F_(3^h)^2 as rows and all affine F3-lines as arity3
activities. Let cap0 and H={(x,x^2):x!=0}, with low mates -H. There are
M=3^h-1 branches and(M+1)^2 rows. The only supported activities on
{0} union H union -H are the root pairs. A three-high zero-sum relation forces
all parameters equal in characteristic3; a two-high/one-low relation forces
one high parameter0. Neither gives a distinct supported triple.

The affine map (x,y)->(a-x,y-2ax+a^2) swaps0 and(a,a^2), fixes its negative,
and permutes the other high parabola points. Thus seed cap/high equality does
give a constant private seed of at least1/2. The remaining obstacle is active
high/low transitivity at intermediate profiles.

Exact bounded test: ternary_parabola_probe.py exhausts affine stabilizers in
AG(4,3), the q9 case. It constructs an affine basis from the reported points,
enumerates all possible target bases and permitted images of the cap, and
tests every candidate on the entire profile. Output TERNARY_PARABOLA_SCOUT.json
verifies the full supported star. At the seed the cap and high seed owner can
interchange, while the low is a separate orbit. For every two-branch extension
from the fixed first branch, active private owners split into at least two
orbits. Field multiplication makes fixing the first branch without loss for
this bounded test. Full activation has one private orbit, but that alone does
not provide a growing ladder.

The exact result rejects transfer of the SAME orbit-equality argument at q9;
it does not prove that all mechanisms on this family have bounded funding,
that another weighted/distributional certificate cannot work, or that all
polynomial-row realizations fail. No such general no-go claim is made.
