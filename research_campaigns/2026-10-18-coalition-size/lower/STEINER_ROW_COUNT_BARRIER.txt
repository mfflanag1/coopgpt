EXPONENTIAL ROW COUNT IS FORCED BY THE EXACT STEINER SYMMETRIES
18 October 2026. Structural lemma for a specified representation class.
This is not a general lower bound on the resource count needed for any
square-root TCWM obstruction.

THEOREM
Let a finite arity3 technology have row set X and an activity on every pair's
unique completion: for any two distinct rows a,b, there is exactly one row
c with {a,b,c} an activity. Thus the activity supports form a Steiner triple
system. Suppose there are distinct rows x_0,...,x_M such that for every j an
automorphism fixes all x_i with i!=j pointwise and moves x_j. Then
  |X| >= 2^(M+1)-1.

This hypothesis is satisfied by the exact symmetries of the projective proof:
  x_0=v, x_j=e_j;
  for j>=1, the private flip e_j -> e_j+v fixes every other basis row;
  for j=0, the seed transvection v -> v+e_1 fixes all e_j.
Therefore the projective construction attains the MINIMUM possible row count
within all Steiner triple representations retaining these exact pointwise
generator symmetries. Replacing the binary geometry by a different Steiner
triple system cannot provide polynomial rows while preserving this argument.

PROOF OF THE GENERAL STATEMENT
For distinct a,b write a*b for the unique third row of their activity. Call
W closed if a*b belongs to W whenever distinct a,b belong to W. The closure
cl(A) is obtained by repeatedly adjoining such unique completions. Any
automorphism fixing A pointwise fixes cl(A) pointwise, by induction over this
closure process. Hence x_j cannot lie in cl({x_i:i!=j}); its stated witness
automorphism would otherwise fix it. In particular, in any order each x_j
lies outside the closure of its preceding generators.

Now let W be closed and let x lie outside W. For each w in W, the point x*w
is different from x and lies outside W. If x*w were in W, closedness applied
to w and x*w would force x into W, a contradiction. The points x*w for
different w are distinct: equality would make the pair {x,x*w} belong to two
different activities, contradicting unique completion. Thus cl(W union{x})
contains W, x, and |W| distinct additional points, so
  |cl(W union{x})| >= 2|W|+1.

Start with the empty closed set and adjoin x_0,...,x_M in order. Every step
is proper, as proved above. The displayed inequality yields successive lower
bounds1,3,7,...,2^(M+1)-1, proving the theorem.

EXACT LIMITS AND CONSEQUENCE
The lemma uses complete pair coverage and UNIQUE third rows. It also uses
automorphisms fixing the other distinguished generators pointwise. It says
nothing about incomplete/overlapping 3-uniform technologies, non-pointwise
permutations of dormant rows, multi-orbit or distributional certificates, or
different growing lower-bound families. A polynomial-row realization must
leave at least one of these hypotheses. The ternary affine-line and quadratic
triangle scouts illustrate failures of the current symmetry route; they do
not establish a broader impossibility.

The primary square-root proof's exact checker already verifies the required
pointwise generator maps. In the binary example, closure is precisely the
set of nonzero vectors in the linear span, whose sizes are2^j-1, so equality
holds at every doubling step. The proof above is self-contained and does not
depend on a numerical search or a historical-priority assertion.
