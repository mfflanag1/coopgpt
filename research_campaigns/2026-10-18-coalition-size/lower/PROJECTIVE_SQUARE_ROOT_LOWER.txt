EXACT STATEMENT
For each integer M>=1 there is a finite, unit-profit, arity3 packing technology
on d=2^(M+1)-1 resource rows and n=2M+1 fixed fully eligible owners such that
every nonnegative coalition-covering payment rule satisfying TCWM for all
coalitions of size at most s0=2M-1 has worst-case funding factor at least

    (2/3) Q_M,       Q_M = product_(m=2)^M 2m/(2m-1)
                         = 4^M/[2 binom(2M,M)].                 (A)

Retaining terminal coalition coverage gives the stronger finite bound

    kappa >= 1 + [2(M-1)/(3M)] Q_M.                           (B)

Coverage is imposed on every coalition at every feasible real report.
All owners have the same fixed actual all-ones endowment and full unit-cube
report domain. Every strategic comparison keeps the actual owners, eligibility,
and technology fixed. No marginal floor, formula, continuity, linearity,
budget balance, or zero-owner payment axiom is assumed.

Explicitly, write F(c) for the packing LP value at aggregate capacity c,
b=(b_i)_i for a report profile, and y_i(b)>=0 for payments. The contract is:
  Coverage: sum_(i in C)y_i(b) >= F(sum_(i in C)b_i) for EVERY C and b.
  Funding:  sum_i y_i(b) <= kappa F(sum_i b_i) for every b.
  TCWM:     sum_(i in C)y_i(b) >= sum_(i in C)y_i(b'_C,b_(-C))
            whenever |C|<=s0 and 0<=b'_i<=b_i coordinatewise for i in C.
Unchanged members are permitted inside C. All these assertions range over
the full real report cubes; the proof uses a finite subset of their consequences.

Consequently, for every integer s>=1, with M=floor((s+1)/2),

    K3(s) >= (2/3)Q_M >= sqrt(3M+1)/3 >= sqrt(s/6).           (C)

Along s=2M-1, the leading coefficient of (A) relative to sqrt(s) tends to
sqrt(pi/2)/3. The resource count grows exponentially in M; it is finite and
the stated campaign places no bound on its growth. Owner count is n=s0+2.
This applies to arbitrary mechanisms under the supplied TCWM contract, and
therefore also to full OUT wherever OUT implies TCWM.

1. PUBLIC TECHNOLOGY AND SYMMETRIZATION
Let V=F_2^(M+1), with basis v,e_1,...,e_M. Rows are all nonzero vectors of V.
There is one activity for every unordered triple {a,b,a+b}, for distinct
nonzero a,b. Each activity has profit1 and consumes one unit of each of its
three distinct rows. Duplicate triples are included only once. Every column
has arity exactly3; there are no resource-free profitable activities.

There are n=2M+1 actual owners, with fixed labels p,h_1,l_1,...,h_M,l_M.
Each has actual endowment the all-ones vector and can report any vector of
[0,1]^d. Thus all profiles below belong to one fixed ownership/eligibility
institution; owner labels never restrict reporting to a designated row.

Suppose any kappa-funded rule satisfies the contract. Average it over the
finite product of the owner permutation group and GL(V), transforming inputs
and relabeling output payments back to the original owners. Resource maps
permute activities and preserve profit, consumption, report domains, coalition
values, and coordinatewise underreport order. Owner permutations preserve the
common endowment domains and coalition sizes. Each transformed mechanism
satisfies every contract inequality; their finite average does too. Hence
assume the averaged rule invariant under these symmetries. Symmetry is a
proof reduction, not an assumption on the original rule. No actual owner is
strategically replaced or relabeled in any comparison below.
For an original nonsymmetric rule, the resulting expensive report can be some
profile in the finite symmetry orbit of C_M; it need not be the displayed C_M
itself. The theorem asserts a worst-case factor, so this distinction is harmless.

2. PROFILE FAMILY AND EXACT GRAND PRODUCTION
For m=1,...,M define C_m by the reports:
  p reports one unit of v;
  every h_j reports one unit of e_j;
  l_j reports one unit of e_j+v for j<=m;
  l_j reports the zero vector for j>m.
All unmentioned coordinates are zero. Every report is feasible in the same
fixed full cube. Set S={v} union {e_j,e_j+v:1<=j<=M}.

Every activity entirely contained in S is exactly {v,e_j,e_j+v} for some j.
Indeed, summing private rows from distinct indices produces two nonzero
e-coordinates and leaves S. Summing the two private rows with one index gives
v, and adding v to a private row gives its mate. Thus the only supported
activities at C_m are the m branches with j<=m. Each consumes row v, whose
capacity is1, so production is at most1. Any one active branch gives a primal
solution of value1; therefore F(C_m)=1 for all m.

A globally feasible full-LP dual prices v at1, every zero-capacity row at1,
and every other positive-capacity row at0. A supported activity includes v;
an unsupported activity includes a zero-capacity row. Every column is covered
and the dual objective is1. This certificate uses the full technology.
At the displayed profiles p is a veto owner for production, but the fixed
institution has no globally designated veto owner: each fully eligible owner
could report the all-ones vector and produce alone at other feasible profiles.

3. THE SYMMETRIC SEED: COVERAGE FORCES A CONSTANT PRIVATE TOTAL
At C_1, the three active owners p,h_1,l_1 report v,e_1,v+e_1 respectively.
The linear map swapping v and e_1 and fixing each e_j for j>1 fixes v+e_1.
Combined with swapping the labels p,h_1, it preserves the entire profile,
including every other high owner and every zero low owner. The transvection
v -> v+e_1, e_j -> e_j for all j, combined with swapping p,l_1, also preserves
the profile. Both maps are invertible. Hence all three active owners receive
one common payment a.

The coalition {p,h_1,l_1} can produce one unit, so coalition coverage gives
3a>=1. This coalition need not be protected by TCWM: coverage is required for
ALL coalitions. The total payment to the first active private pair is

                    T_1 = 2a >= 2/3.                       (D)

This seed is the improvement over a pure common-cap technology: the full
projective technology permits cap/private row exchange at C_1 while fixing
the dormant high rows. Positive payments to zero-report owners are allowed
and are never set to zero by the argument.

4. EXACT FIXED-OWNER TCWM LADDER
At C_m all 2m active private owners h_j,l_j, j<=m, receive one common payment
q_m. To see this, use permutations of the basis e_j among active indices and
independent flips e_j -> e_j+v for active j, always fixing v and inactive
e_j. These invertible maps fix the entire multiset of reported rows and act
transitively on the active private rows. Combine them with the corresponding
owner permutations; all zero owners remain zero. Thus T_m=2m q_m.

For m>=2 the transition C_(m-1) -> C_m restores only the actual owner l_m
from zero to one unit of e_m+v. Protect the fixed actual coalition

       A_m = {h_j,l_j: j<m} union {l_m}.

It has exactly 2m-1 members, at most s0. Every old active owner in A_m stays
at the identical report; l_m alone changes. At C_(m-1), its total payment is
T_(m-1)+y_(l_m)(C_(m-1)) >= T_(m-1) by nonnegativity. At C_m every member
of A_m is active private, so its total payment is (2m-1)q_m. Applying TCWM
in the full-to-underreport direction (equivalently, restoration cannot lower
coalition payment) gives

       (2m-1)q_m >= 2(m-1)q_(m-1),
       T_m >= [2m/(2m-1)] T_(m-1).                         (E)

Neither p nor the unchanged new high owner h_m is included in A_m.
No coalition membership or actual ownership changes inside a comparison.

Iterating (E) from (D) gives T_M >= (2/3)Q_M. Nonnegative payments and the
budget at C_M, whose production is1, give kappa>=T_M, proving (A).

For (B), let z be the root owner's payment at C_M. Coalition {p,h_1,l_1}
has value1, so z+2q_M>=1. Budget and nonnegativity imply
  kappa >= z+2M q_M >= 1+2(M-1)q_M
        >= 1+[2(M-1)/(3M)]Q_M.
This also gives kappa>=1 for M=1 without a separate boundary argument.

5. EXACT NONNEGATIVE MULTIPLIER CANCELLATION
Write the relevant inequalities as:
  Seed:           3q_1 >= 1.
  Ladder(m):      (2m-1)q_m - 2(m-1)q_(m-1) >= 0, m=2,...,M.
  Final budget:   kappa-2M q_M >= 0.
All are valid consequences already justified above. Give final budget weight1,
give Ladder(m) weight product_(j=m)^M [2j/(2j-1)], and give Seed weight
2Q_M/3. All weights are nonnegative rational numbers. Every q_m coefficient
cancels exactly. The result is kappa >= 2Q_M/3. For M=1 the same statement
uses only final budget and seed, with weights1 and2/3.

For (B), add final coverage z+2q_M>=1 to the actual final budget
kappa-z-2M q_M>=0, both weight1. Scale all ladder and seed weights above
by (M-1)/M. The q and z coefficients cancel, leaving (B). At M=1 this
reduces directly to final coverage plus final budget; zero weights are valid.

6. UNIFORM RATE AND EXACT-CHECK SCOPE
The central binomial identity in (A) follows by separating even and odd
factors. The elementary lower bound Q_M >= sqrt(3M+1)/2 follows by induction:
the squared comparison between consecutive M reduces to
  4(M+1)^2(3M+1) - (2M+1)^2(3M+4) = M >= 0.
The base M=1 is equality. Since floor((s+1)/2)>=s/2 for integer s, (C) follows.
The standard central-binomial asymptotic yields the stated optional leading
constant. It is not needed for the exponent or explicit all-s bound.
