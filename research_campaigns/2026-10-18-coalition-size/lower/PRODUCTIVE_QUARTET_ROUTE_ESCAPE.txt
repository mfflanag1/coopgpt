INSTITUTION AND PROFILES

Block j has four designated owners, one for each row of e_j+span(u,v).
At each profile a block is singleton (only e_j present), partial (three
points, omitting e_j+u+v), or full (all four points). Remaining owners report
zero; they retain their identities throughout. Let f,t denote numbers of
full and partial blocks and B=f+t; M-B blocks are singletons.

All supported activities are the cap triple and triples consisting of one
cap and a private pair in a single block. In particular every coalition's
worth is covered if nonnegative row payments satisfy the corresponding
three-row edge inequalities. This follows from the packing LP dual, placing
price1 on any unavailable row and the indicated payment on each present row.

The elementary profile symmetries give one orbit each of caps, full private

UNIFORM PROTECTED-SIZE ESCAPE, EVEN WITH ARBITRARILY MANY GROUPS

B>B0, replace the recurrence by
