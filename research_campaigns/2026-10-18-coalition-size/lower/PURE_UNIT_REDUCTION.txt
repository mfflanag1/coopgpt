PURE-UNIT FINITE TRANSITION REDUCTION — 18 October 2026
Conventional lemma for bounded lower discovery; not a general funding theorem.

Let H be a finite unit-profit hypergraph, with unit consumption on every edge.
Every edge has at most3 resource rows. Fix n fully eligible actual owners, each
with the same unit-cube report domain. Average any admissible mechanism over
the finite group of owner permutations. This preserves all guarantees and
fixed-owner report comparisons. It does not impose an axiom on the original rule.

Restrict to profiles where each owner reports zero or one pure resource unit.
Write a_j for the number reporting row j, and z=n-sum a_j. By label symmetry
there is a payment q_j(a) to each row-j unit owner and q_0(a) to each zero owner.
ALL these variables are arbitrary and nonnegative, including q_0.

COVERAGE REDUCTION. For each supported edge e (a_j>=1 for j in e), coalition
coverage implies sum_(j in e) q_j(a)>=1: select one unit owner from each row.
Conversely these inequalities imply coverage of every coalition at this profile.
For a coalition's pure-row counts c<=a, restrict to edges supported by c. The
nonnegative q_j form a dual feasible fractional vertex cover of that support.
Therefore F(c)<=sum_j c_j q_j. Adding any zero owners adds nonnegative payment.
This exact equivalence uses unit consumption and pure UNIT reports. It is not
asserted for arbitrary heterogeneous real reports or arbitrary packing matrices.

ONE-OWNER TRANSITION. Choose a particular zero owner i and restore e_j to that
same owner; a becomes a+e_j. Every unchanged row-k owner loses
ell_k=q_k(a)-q_k(a+e_j), with multiplicity a_k. Each remaining zero owner loses
ell_0=q_0(a)-q_0(a+e_j), with multiplicity z-1. The changed owner's gain is
g=q_j(a+e_j)-q_0(a). TCWM for every protected coalition containing i is exactly

 g >= sum of the largest at most s-1 POSITIVE entries in this loss multiset.

Necessity chooses the actual owners with those losses. Sufficiency follows
because any protected coalition uses at most s-1 unchanged owners and its
payment change is g minus their selected losses. Empty selection enforces g>=0.
The changed owner and all unchanged coalition members have fixed identities.

An arbitrary multicoordinate restoration by a protected coalition can be
decomposed into single-owner coordinate increases. Thus imposing the full
real-domain one-owner inequalities is equivalent to TCWM. The probe only
imposes selected pure-unit grid transitions and therefore obtains NECESSARY
constraints, sufficient for lower certificates but never a continuous upper.

BUDGET. sum_j a_j q_j(a)+z q_0(a)<=kappa F(a). A nonnegative combination of
coverage, TCWM and budget rows, leaving nonnegative payment coefficients and
a negative kappa coefficient, yields an unrestricted lower for this fixed
owner institution. Numerical optimizer output is only discovery until exact
nonnegative multiplier cancellation and production primal/dual witnesses pass.

GENERAL CONSEQUENCE. Any all-real arity3 mechanism must pass every finite
system of these inequalities, so a growing family of exact certificates here
would immediately give a lower for K3(s). No such growing certificate is yet
claimed. This reduction alone does not improve the quarter-power frontier.
