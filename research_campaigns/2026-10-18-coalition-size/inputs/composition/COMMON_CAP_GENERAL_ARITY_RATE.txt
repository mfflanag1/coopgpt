THE COMMON-CAP ARITY LAW: SHARP GROWTH EXPONENT FOR TCWM FUNDING
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate for independent audit. Novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL AND RESULT
Fix integers k>=1 and M>=1. There are kM+1 resource rows: one common resource P,
and k private rows x_j1,...,x_jk for each branch j. One unit-profit activity per
branch consumes one unit of P and of all its k private rows. Hence production is
 F(P,x)=min(P,sum_{j=1}^M min_{1<=l<=k} x_jl).                 (1)
Each column has consumption arity k+1. Actual owners, including mixed owners,
are fixed, and may restore any coordinates of their own reports. TCWM requires
that every such fixed coalition's total payment never decreases when its reports
increase and all outsiders stay fixed. Payments are nonnegative and cover every
coalition's production. No outside-production incentive upper is asserted.

For each FIXED k>=2, the least funding factor achievable uniformly over all
finite actual-owner models for(1) grows as
 THETA_k(M^((k-1)/(2k))).                                    (2)
The constants are explicit below. The lower uses pure owners and no regularity
or price restriction; the upper is a price rule for arbitrary mixed owners on
all nonnegative real profiles. This unifies, rather than creates separate papers
from, the quarter-power common-cap result k=2. For k=1 the exponent is zero:
our construction gives a constant bound2, while the already-established sharp
unit-graph3/2 theorem improves that special case. We claim no new k=1 constant.

1. THE FINITE-OWNER LOWER BOUND
For any integer R>=1, take R pure P owners with report bound1/R, and kM pure
private-row owners X_jl with report bound1. There are n=kM+R fixed actual owners.
Every finite-factor mechanism on this owner model satisfies
 kappa >= Q_M,k R/(R^2+Q_M,k),
 Q_M,k=product_{m=2}^M [km/(k(m-1)+1)], Q_1,k=1.              (3)

Proof. Symmetrize over power-owner permutations, branch permutations and private
row/owner permutations within each branch. These are automorphisms of the same
technology, eligibility and report-bound domain. Finite averaging preserves all
linear incentive, funding, coverage and nonnegativity constraints. The original
mechanism need not be anonymous.
All P owners report1/R, so P=1. At D the first branch has every private row at
epsilon=1/R; other branches have the vector(0,1,...,1). Its worth is epsilon.
Let Y_P be power-owner total payment. Funding bounds Y_P<=kappa epsilon; symmetry
makes each power owner's payment Y_P/R. The coalition of all k first-branch
owners and one power owner has worth epsilon, forcing their private-row payment
at least epsilon(1-kappa epsilon). Restore those k private-row owners to1. At the
resulting profile C_1 their total T_1 is no smaller, by TCWM.

At C_m, first m branches have all private rows1; the other branches remain
(0,1,...,1), and power reports stay fixed. Worth is1. Its km active private-row
owners have a common payment q_m=T_m/(km), by profile-preserving symmetry.
Only X_m1 changes from C_(m-1) to C_m, increasing0 to1. Apply TCWM to the fixed
coalition of all k(m-1) old active owners plus X_m1. Every outsider remains fixed.
Its old payment is at least T_(m-1), including a possibly positive payment to
zero-report X_m1. Its new payment is [k(m-1)+1]q_m. Therefore
 [1-(k-1)/(km)]T_m >= T_(m-1).                              (4)
Iterating gives T_M>=Q_M,k epsilon(1-kappa epsilon), while funding gives T_M<=kappa.
Rearrangement proves(3), even if the bootstrap lower expression is negative.
No optimal-factor attainment, null-owner rule, price representation or continuity
is used. All owners and eligibility remain fixed in every comparison.

The product has the exact gamma-function identity
 Q_M,k=Gamma(M+1) Gamma(1+1/k)/Gamma(M+1/k).                   (5)
For fixed k it is asymptotic to Gamma(1+1/k) M^((k-1)/k).
Choose integer R nearest sqrt(Q_M,k); then R/sqrt(Q_M,k) tends to1 for k>=2,
and(3) is asymptotic to
 [sqrt(Gamma(1+1/k))/2] M^((k-1)/(2k)).                       (6)
This choice uses n=kM+O_k(M^((k-1)/(2k))) owners. Formula(3), not its asymptotic
notation, is the exact finite-instance statement.

2. THE EXPLICIT UPPER RULE
Write
 A_k=k/(k+1)^(1+1/k),
 C=max{1,sqrt(k A_k) M^((k-1)/(2k))}.                        (7)
More generally any fixed public C>=1 works with the bound in(16). Define
 g_j=min(P/C,x_j1,...,x_jk), G=sum_j g_j, r=P/G,
 h(r)=min(1,C/r), f(r)=min(1,A_k(r/C)^(1/k)).                  (8)
For P,G>0 use prices
 p_P=h(r), p_jl=f(r)g_j/x_jl.                                (9)
Pay each actual owner its reported bundle dotted with these common prices.
Zero-coordinate contributions are zero; at P=0 or G=0 all payments are zero.

We have G<=MP/C, r>=C/M and
 F>=min(P,G).                                                (10)
Equality in the last comparison is not assumed. Total payment is exactly
 Ph+kGf.                                                    (11)

3. COVERAGE
When r<=C, p_P=1 and P stock alone covers every coalition's production.
When r>C, G<P/C, so no branch is at its cap P/C. Thus g_j=min_l x_jl, and every
supported branch has sum_l g_j/x_jl>=1. Also h+f>=1: the maximum of
 (1-1/z)/z^(1/k), z>1,
is A_k, attained at z=k+1 (differentiate z^(-1/k)-z^(-1-1/k)). Truncating f at1
preserves that inequality. Every feasible activity has price sum at least1;
this is a dual cover on the current support and proves coalition coverage.
A branch with an absent row cannot produce for any coalition.

4. TCWM FOR ARBITRARY FIXED MIXED OWNERS
For a coalition aggregate s<=c, with outsiders fixed, its payment is
 Y=s_P h+f Z,
 Z=sum_j g_j sum_l s_jl/x_jl, 0<=Z<=kG.                      (12)
At regular points of one-coordinate restorations,
 h'<=0, f'>=0, h+r h'>=0, f-k r f'>=0.                       (13)
The last expression is0 on the power branch and1 on the constant branch.
If x_jl increases, then G'=g_j' is0 or1, and
 Z'=g_j' sum_t s_jt/x_jt + g_j(x_jl-s_jl)/x_jl^2.
When g_j'=1, g_j=x_jl and Z'=1+sum_(t!=l) s_jt/x_jt>=1;
otherwise Z'>=0. Thus Z'>=G'. Since r'=-rG'/G,
 Y'=s_P h'r'+f'r'Z+fZ'>=(f-k r f')G'>=0.                    (14)
If P increases, let t count branches strictly capped at P/C. Then G'=t/C,
Z'>=0, G>=Pt/C, r'=(G-Pt/C)/G^2>=0, and P r'<=r. Consequently
 Y'=h+s_P h'r'+f'Zr'+fZ'>=h+P h'r'>=h+r h'>=0.              (15)
These are derivatives of coalition payments with actual changing ownership
shares, not only derivatives of aggregate prices.
Each coordinate path admits a finite partition into smooth formulas: minima
of finitely many affine functions, ratios and fixed power/clipping thresholds.
The owner payments are continuous at all switches. Terms g_j s_jl/x_jl are
bounded by g_j<=x_jl, giving their continuous zero-row extension. As G tends to0
with fixed positive P, f=1 eventually and total payment=(C+k)G tends to0.
As P tends to0, the funding bound below and F<=P force every nonnegative payment
to0. Therefore the derivative inequalities extend to finite coordinate paths,
including support changes. Restore a coalition's whole bundle sequentially,
holding actual owner identities and all outsiders fixed. This proves all-profile
TCWM for every nonnegative real report.

5. FUNDING
For r<=1, F=P and h=1, so
 total/F=1+kf/r <=1+k A_k C^(-1/k)r^(-(k-1)/k)
                    <=1+k A_k M^((k-1)/k)/C.
For r>1, F>=G, so total/F<=rh+kf<=C+k. Thus the factor is at most
 max{C+k,1+k A_k M^((k-1)/k)/C}.                             (16)
The public choice(7) makes the second entry at most1+C<=k+C. Therefore
 kappa_upper <= k+max{1,sqrt(k A_k) M^((k-1)/(2k))}.           (17)
For fixed k>=2 its leading constant is
 sqrt(k A_k)=k/(k+1)^((1+1/k)/2).
Together with(6), this proves the growth-rate claim(2), with no claim that the
leading constants match. For k=1, (17) gives2, only a constant-order statement.
When k varies with M, retain the explicit formulas rather than interpreting
fixed-k asymptotics uniformly.

6. IMPLEMENTATION, STATUS AND SCOPE
For rational reports an exact symbolic implementation may choose the smallest
integer C>=1 satisfying
 (k+1)^(k+1) C^(2k) >= k^(2k) M^(k-1).
The funding factor is C+k. The only radical has kth power
 f^k = k^k P / [(k+1)^(k+1) C G]
when f<1, so each payment is a rational constant plus a rational multiple of one
positive kth root. This specifies exact algebraic output, not rounded truthful
payments. The accompanying checker uses exact rational arithmetic and certified
root intervals; unresolved equality cases raise rather than silently pass.

This result is for the common-cap hyperstar family, not all technologies of the
same consumption arity. It sharpens the quantitative witness behind the boundary:
all two-sparse technologies have bounded TCWM funding, but already arity3 admits
unbounded funding, and this family now has an exact growth exponent at every
fixed higher arity. Individual truthfulness, full coalition outside production,
private costs, integral schedules and measured infrastructure welfare are separate
questions. The family has a literal shared-power interpretation, but no empirical
claim or historical novelty follows from that interpretation.
