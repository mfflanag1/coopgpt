HOW TO READ FUNDING CLAIMS
A bound sum_i y_i<=kappa F(B) concerns TOTAL production. A bound
sum_i y_i<=S+K G concerns only the incremental gain after returning standalone
values. A constant kappa need not give any constant K. The reserve application
makes this distinction economically consequential.

TCWM uses a condition whose scalar-demand counterpart is the established
Strong Group Monotonicity of Moulin–Sprumont2004 (source comparison14).
Every coalition's total payment cannot fall when its own members
restore withheld feasible resources, with outside reports fixed. Full OUT
requires that increase also cover what the restored bundle could produce on
its own. Individual OUT imposes this only for one actual owner at a time. A protected
coalition member may restore zero while another member changes its report.
All fixed-owner statements hold with the same owner identities throughout
report comparisons. Symmetrization in a lower proof is not strategic cloning.
