1. EXACT MODEL AND CONTRACT
For public fixed Gaussian load factors L_i=m_i+<x_i,Z>, Z~N(0,I), choose common
z_alpha>0. With firm capacity C_i define t_i=(C_i-m_i)/z_alpha and admit
b_i=(t_i,x_i) with t_i>=||x_i||2. At one fungible delivery point, headroom is
H_Q=z_alpha[sum_Q t_i-||sum_Q x_i||]. Public linear value r converts to money.
Actual owner identities are fixed. Feasible withholding consists of auditable
owned subportfolios D_i for which BOTH D_i and b_i-D_i satisfy the cone.
The theorem applies to any physically realizable subset of these splits.
Capacity-only withholding can be feasible: D=(d,0), d>0, is in the cone.
The contract does not make private factor estimates or opportunity costs truthful.
It does not cover alternate reliability targets, unmodeled external grid service,
network bottlenecks, ramp/duration limits or temporal shifting by assumption.
Those must be tested, modeled separately, or contractually excluded.

2. WHY SUBSIDY NEEDS A SEPARATE JUSTIFICATION
F(B)=S+G, S=sum_i(t_i-||x_i||), G=sum_i||x_i||-||sum_i x_i||.
The sharp full-OUT theorem gives total payments <=(1+c)F, c=.543063389825... .
Its particular L1 construction pays (1+c)f_i even to an isolated owner.
Thus subsidy/G is not uniformly bounded. A sponsor may value existing reserve,
incremental reserve, or both; that valuation must be documented, not presumed.
Payments exceeding headroom require outside money or genuinely additional
externally supplied capacity. They cannot create physical capacity inside the pool.

Ordinary coalition coverage needs no subsidy: take a norm subgradient p at X
and pay y_i=t_i-<p,x_i>. Sum_i y_i=F and every coalition is covered by duality.
The extra mathematical guarantee purchased by subsidy is robust withholding
protection, including partial pooled withdrawals. If feasible choices are only
complete join/exit with zero payment to absent owners, coverage already protects
those choices. A general partial-withholding theorem may then be unnecessary.

3. EXACT EXAMPLE SHOWING THE INCENTIVE ISSUE CAN BE REAL IN THIS MODEL
Two fixed owners have b1=(1,(0,1)), b2=(2,(1,-1)). Aggregate X=(1,0).
Efficient gradient payment to owner1 is1. Restore to owner1 the cone-boundary
bundle D=(1/2,(3/10,4/10)); its own outside production is zero.
New aggregate X'=(13/10,4/10), norm sqrt(185)/10. Owner1's payment becomes
3/2-19/(2sqrt(185)), strictly less than1 since185<19^2.
The exact lost payment is (19/sqrt(185)-1)/2>0. All retained and restored
bundles are feasible; owner identities and eligibility do not change.
Therefore this efficient stable allocation is not even individually TCWM.
This proves a possible mechanism issue, not that real tenants exploit it,
that the sharp rule is economically optimal, or that subsidy equals social benefit.

4. BOUNDED VALIDATION DESIGN BEFORE ANY PERFORMANCE CLAIM
Choose one measured multivariate trace, physical interval and ownership mapping.
A synthetic owner partition is labeled synthetic; it cannot validate ownership.
Before inspecting holdout, freeze: training/holdout dates; common target alpha;
factor fitting/dimension rule; capacity-entitlement or training-only sizing rule;
curtailment allocation; admissible owned withdrawal units; delivered-capacity
constraints; sponsor valuation and benefits; empirical-tail comparison method.
Report the sampling interval and enough tail observations to assess the target.
Use dependence-aware uncertainty for serial traces; an iid binomial interval is
valid only under an explicitly defended independence model. Stress covariance
and tail dependence, and retain common shocks and aggregate peaks.

Evaluate aggregate AND owner-level reliability under the fixed curtailment rule,
physical deliverability, S/G decomposition, modeled payments, efficient-baseline
withdrawal profitability over the declared deviation set, and sponsor net benefit.
No universal arbitrary gain-share cutoff is asserted. A useful economic gate
requires measured sponsor benefit to justify the required external funding and
requires the relevant withholding opportunities to be physically/contractually real.
Fail the direct application if its reliability or delivery promises fail, if the
actual outside options differ materially, or if the sponsor case is unsupported.
A replay remains a model-compatibility test, not causal evidence of welfare gain.
