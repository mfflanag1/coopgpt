A square-root funding lower for bounded coalitions

A compact packet for checking one mathematical result against its exact contract and the inherited upper bound. The remaining gap and historical priority are unresolved.

The current bounds

√(s/6) ≤ K₃(s) ≤ K₃OUT(s) ≤ 3s²,   s ≥ 1.

K₃(s) and K₃OUT(s) are worst-institution uniform total-funding factors under TCWM and full OUT. The square-root lower is the campaign result under review. The full-OUT upper is inherited and unchanged.

The optional tail sharpens the same lower certificate. For integer s ≥ 2, let h = floor((s+1)/2) and Qₕ = 4ʰ / [2 binom(2h,h)]. Then

K₃(s) ≥ Bₛ = 1 + [2(s−1)Qₕ/(3h)] [(s−1)/s]s−h.

Bₛ/√s → √(2π)/(3√e). The ladder peaks at M = s and M = s+1; this does not optimize over all mechanisms or all certificates. At s=6, B₆=493/243.

The contract matters

InstitutionA fixed finite nonnegative packing technology, fixed actual owners, eligibility and endowments. Profitable resource-free activities are excluded. Activity arity is at most three.
ReportsEvery real binding coordinate underreport in the fixed report boxes. The lower constrains an arbitrary rule defined on that entire domain, using a finite set of necessary inequalities.
PaymentsNonnegative payments cover the standalone reported production of every coalition. Total funding is bounded relative to production at each reported aggregate.
IncentivesOnly incentive protection is restricted to coalitions of size at most s. TCWM requires their payment sum not to fall under restoration; full OUT additionally covers production from restored resources. Unchanged protected members remain included.
No added axiomsNo anonymity, continuity, additivity, marginal payment floor, or zero-payment axiom for zero reporters. Symmetry is a proof device, not a restriction on candidate rules.

The fully eligible lower uses n=2M+1 actual owners and d=2M+1−1 resource rows, each owner with the same full unit report cube. At M=s the sharper witness has n=2s+1 and d=2s+1−1. Resource count is exponential. The original asymmetric rule is expensive somewhere in a finite symmetry orbit, not necessarily at the displayed representative.

Suggested reading order

  1. Read the review brief and the principal lower proof. Check the seed stabilizers, dormant high reports, globally feasible production dual, actual restoring coalition and terminal coverage.
  2. Compare the inherited upper with the common-contract check. The upper works for every eligible institution and full OUT; the lower supplies a worst-case institution and uses TCWM alone.
  3. Read the optional tail, then the included acceptance and independent contract audits. Consult source-comparison metadata only after the mathematical contract is clear.

Three questions for a specialist

  1. Validity. Does the full owner/resource symmetrization preserve the contract and force the stated seed and private-owner orbits, including dormant reports and zero-report payments?
  2. Fixed-owner recurrence. Does each actual restoration yield the claimed inequality with unchanged protected members, and does terminal coalition coverage justify the total-funding bound and endpoint cases?
  3. Antecedents and consequence. Does an existing theorem imply this quantitative arbitrary-payment lower under the same hypotheses? Please identify its version and map owners, report domain, coverage, incentives, budget and arity. A suggestive analogy is distinct from such an implication.
