PRECISE STATEMENT TO ASSESS
For every M>=1, use n=2M+1 fixed actual owners, each with the full report cube
[0,1]^d, d=2^(M+1)-1. Public production is the unit-profit packing LP whose
resource rows are nonzero vectors of F2^(M+1), with one activity for each
distinct triple {a,b,a+b}. Every activity consumes one unit of three resources.

For EVERY nonnegative payment function on the FULL real report domain,
require every coalition's payment to cover its standalone reported production.
For every fixed coalition C of size<=2M-1, restoring any coordinates of its
members while outsiders remain unchanged must not decrease its payment SUM.
Unchanged members inside C are protected. No additivity, anonymity, continuity,
individual marginal floor or zero-payment condition for zero reporters is assumed.
The claim is that any uniform total-funding factor kappa must satisfy
 kappa >= 1+[2(M-1)/(3M)] Q_M,  Q_M=4^M/[2*binom(2M,M)].
Consequently K3(s)>=sqrt(s/6) for every integer s>=1. This is a TCWM lower;
it therefore also constrains full OUT. Resource count is exponential.
