2. WHAT THE QUANTIFIERS MEAN

Each institution fixes its actual owners, each owner's eligible resource
coordinates and finite endowment box, and one public fractional packing
technology F(c)=max{pi*x:A*x<=c,x>=0}. A and pi are nonnegative; there is no
profitable activity with no input. Every positive-profit activity uses at most
three resources in the primary frontier.

Every real coordinatewise binding underreport is admissible. A single payment
rule must work at every such profile. Every coalition is covered: its total
payment is at least what its reported resources can produce on their own.
This coverage requirement is NOT restricted to coalitions of size at most s.

For incentives, every subset C of at most s actual owners is protected. Other
owners' reports remain unchanged. C may restore any nonnegative portions of
its resources; protected members whose reports do not change are still counted.
TCWM requires C's payment sum not to fall. Full OUT additionally requires its
increase to cover production from the restored resources alone. A TCWM lower
therefore also constrains full OUT; a TCWM upper need not satisfy full OUT.

Payments are nonnegative and total funding is at most K(s)*F(total report).
This is a TOTAL-output budget, not extra subsidy alone and not a gain-only
budget. An individual owner reporting zero may receive money when total
production is positive. The proof does not impose a dummy-zero axiom, extra
payment floor, anonymity, continuity or a prescribed formula.

Owner and resource counts grow across the witness sequence, but owners never
change during a comparison within one institution. The new fully eligible
witness has O(s) owners and exponentially many resource rows. The pure-owner
realization has exponentially many owners as well. Neither is a polynomial-
resource square-root construction.

3. THE LOWER-BOUND MECHANISM IN PLAIN LANGUAGE AND FORMULAS

Use the nonzero vectors of F2^(M+1) as resource rows. A unit-profit activity
uses the three rows {a,b,a+b}. Fix a basis v,e1,...,eM. At stage m the supported
productive activities are exactly the m branches {v,e_j,v+e_j}, j<=m; the
other high rows e_j are present but cannot produce. One shared row v limits
total production to1, and a branch attains1.

The finite symmetry group preserves the public technology and the full legal
report domain. Averaging a hypothetical rule over that group preserves all
constraints. It makes the productive seed's three payments equal, so coverage
forces their common payment to be at least1/3. At stage m the2m private branch
owners have one common payment q_m.

Restore ONE fixed low owner and protect it together with the2m-2 old private
owners. The unchanged protected members matter. TCWM and nonnegativity of the
restorer's old payment give

                  (2m-1) q_m >= (2m-2) q_(m-1).

Thus private payments cannot fall as fast as simple equal sharing would like.
Writing Q_M=4^M/[2*binom(2M,M)], one gets q_M>=Q_M/(3M). Terminal branch
coverage, together with all private payments, then gives

                  K >= 1 + 2(M-1) Q_M/(3M).

Since Q_M grows as sqrt(pi*M)/2, the funding ratio grows as sqrt(M). The
bounded-s tail uses only min(s-1,2m-2) old protected owners at later stages;
its exact optimized certificate yields B_s and the s6 example above.

The averaging is a proof device, not an assumed property of mechanisms. For
an asymmetric rule, the expensive report occurs somewhere in the finite
symmetry orbit; the proof need not identify the named representative as the
expensive one. No dormant payment is set to zero. The explicit finite dual
lift retains its nonnegativity row and cancels actual owner/profile variables.

For activity arity r>=3, binary minimal circuits give the same construction
with k=r-1 private inputs per branch. Its recurrence is

       [k(m-1)+1] q_m >= k(m-1) q_(m-1),

which yields K_r(s)=Omega_r(s^((r-2)/(r-1))). This is a higher-arity corollary
of the same idea. At-most-count versions retain the logarithmic dependence
on the available resource dimension. Exact statements and ownership counts:
lower/GENERAL_ARITY_CIRCUIT_LOWER.txt;
root/GENERAL_ARITY_TAIL_AND_DIMENSION_COROLLARY.txt;
root/PROJECTIVE_PURE_OWNER_REALIZATION.txt.

4. WHAT STOPS THE OBVIOUS NEXT LOWER-BOUND ATTEMPT

On the entire binary unit-worth downward domain of a finite linear 3-uniform
technology with unit profits and input coefficients and one pure binary unit
owner per resource row, the same B_s is the exact worst-case optimum, for both
TCWM and full OUT. A productive profile in that restricted domain is a star;
an explicit payment recurrence attains the lower. Therefore no better exponent
can be obtained using ONLY those profiles and that domain. This is not an
upper for all real reports or all sparse production technologies.

Complete fibers over one fixed binary subspace do not amplify the exponent.
If a fiber contains R actual owners, restoring it costs R protected slots.
The restricted frontier is1 when s<R and B(s/R) otherwise, with matching lower
over sufficiently large quotient dimension. Coalitions may contain partial
pieces of unchanged fibers, but the report domain contains complete fibers.
Partial-fiber reports, changing subspaces and higher production values escape
that theorem. Its uniform order is O(sqrt(max(1,s/R))).

Other recorded lower routes include exact finite binary/ternary projective
checks, productive-dormant seed limitations, a homogeneous activation ceiling,
and exponential-row barriers under specific sequential symmetry hypotheses.
These are scoped diagnostic results. Finite optima and restricted obstructions
are not impossibility theorems for every future lower-bound method.

Read reviews/UNIT_WORTH_IDEAL_ROOT_DISPOSITION.txt and
reviews/FULL_FIBER_ROOT_DISPOSITION.txt before reusing those routes.

5. WHAT THE UPPER-BOUND WORK RULED OUT

The fixed-weight marginal obstruction has been strengthened, but remains an
architecture result. Fixed signed report-independent linear combinations of
coalition worths also require quadratic order in s at arity three under the
stated all-profile contract. A single globally shared positive binomial or
cardinality mixture does not escape merely by adapting its coefficients to
the report. Owner-specific or active-component-specific adaptation remains
outside that obstruction.

Several natural coverage selectors fail incentives: minimum-norm/total-penalty
rules, selectors restricted to the three statistics (V,f_i,g_i), and worth-only
Pareto-minimal selection. Their hypotheses differ. In particular the
unpenalized quadratic pair case is not rejected by the size-three counterexample.
A bounded worth-only mechanism must sometimes deliberately retain removable
slack in a coalition cover; choosing a smallest-looking cover is insufficient.

Common prices depending only on total resources are also too restrictive for
the general arity-three problem with funding independent of the number of owners. A local optimal-price
defect can be bounded by a surplus charge, but outgoing low-profile charges
cannot simply be deleted when the dual inequalities are summed. Fixed linear
price/surplus repairs have exact counterexamples.

Positive affine arity reduction is possible with the SAME owners, including
arbitrary shared resources. The obstruction is its additive baseline B:
ordinary gadget funding transfers only to (kappa-1)B+kappa F. On some images
F=0 while B>0, and on the projective witnesses B grows exponentially. A new
gain-only guarantee B+K F would be needed. Neither an ordinary factor theorem
nor reweighting the specified caps supplies it. A proposed shared-resource
extension of the optimal single-min gadget was explicitly refuted.

These results narrow useful designs; they do not prove the quadratic upper
optimal. Their precise hypotheses and dispositions are indexed in
reviews/consolidation_ledger/CLAIM_LEDGER.json, with later root dispositions
governing entries whose original headers retain pending status.

6. GENERAL TOOLS FOR THE NEXT CONSTRUCTION

Finite report-transition LPs now have explicit actual-variable dual certificates.
The projective lift checks that a large finite sum cancels every payment variable,
including dormant-owner terms. For one fixed finite institution, nested dyadic
grid optima have supremum equal to the all-real optimum. This is a compactness
theorem and a strict-lower-factor semidecision under rational data, not a finite
upper verification or an efficient approximation scheme.

Multiplicative smoothing removes measurability as an obstruction at the level
of funding infima. Arbitrary, Borel and facewise-smooth rules have the same
infimum. Exact homogenization preserves any permitted factor on the same
eligible owner coordinates. These nonconstructive tools do not imply exact-
factor smooth attainment, zero-face continuity or preservation of a restricted
mechanism architecture. Their invariant-mean ingredients are classical.

For a supplied smooth rule, local coalition incentives reduce to sorting the
most negative Jacobian entries, plus finitely many activity-input assignments
for full OUT. Fixed arity makes this LOCAL separation polynomial in the explicit
data. Global coverage, integrability and zero-face admission remain essential.
Indeed every productive point has a locally valid affine price rule at any
factor above1; separately feasible local formulas need not form one global rule.

For continuous piecewise-affine payments on a supplied verified rational
partition, all-real coverage, funding, nonnegativity, boundary agreement and
incentives admit finite LP certificates. Coverage needs a joint report/production
LP; checking vertices alone is wrong. Funding does have a vertex test by
concavity. There is no claimed completeness or approximation theorem saying
this representation attains the unrestricted optimum.

The production LP's own bottleneck chambers can be too coarse. For r>=2, with n
owners each having positive endowments in all inputs of a single min_r
technology, continuity and finite
funding force every payment affine on just those r chambers to equal c_i F.
Singleton coverage forces c_i>=1, hence factor n. Ownership-sensitive refinement
or non-affine formulas are required to escape this particular barrier.

The classical constrained-equal-awards primitive supplies a concrete owner-
sensitive PL calibration for ONE minimum technology. Allocate the common estate
F=min_a sum_i b_ia in each pool with claims b_ia; sum each owner's awards across
pools. The rule has exact factor-r total funding, all-coalition coverage and
full OUT for arbitrary mixed owners and every real report. The proof splits a
restoration path into finite bottleneck intervals; saturated-owner sets define
the extra cells. Independent exact region/endpoint checks and opposite-model
proof review passed. This is not a general arity-r packing upper or a novelty
claim. See reviews/POLYHEDRAL_AND_CEA_ROOT_DISPOSITION.txt.

A bounded diagnostic also tests a tempting next architecture: payments that
must dominate SOME efficient nonnegative production-price allocation at every
profile. On the existing twelve-profile, three-owner min3 fixture, its exact
finite optimum is3/2, versus5/4 without that extra condition. Exact expanded
primal/dual witnesses and all legal finite comparisons are supplied. This
is a finite architecture cost, not a growing obstruction, an all-real optimum,
or a rejection of every O(s) reserve rule. Its independent review status is
recorded separately. It prevents treating efficient-price domination as a
harmless change of variables. The proposed next upper direction is therefore
only a bounded falsifiable test, not a justified long commitment.

The final retained-route check supplies two further stopping boundaries. On
the ENTIRE binary unit-worth ideal of a linear triple technology with unit
profits and input coefficients and pure unit owners, efficient-price
domination has exact worst factor A1=1 and
A_s=1+2s q_s for s>=2. Equivalently A_s=1+[s/(s-1)](B_s-1), so it has the
same square-root leading constant as B_s. Cap-price forcing changes the
restricted optimum by only2q_s. This rules out that exact domain as a source
of a worse growing exponent for the price-base architecture; it is not an
all-real mechanism. The actual s2 peak is onlym2; for s>=3 it is m=s-1,s.

For any finite selected profile system with one GLOBAL disjoint source/sink
partition, pay efficient prices at sources and rank charges at sinks. This
satisfies full OUT, every-coalition coverage and price domination with factor
r. Every nonzero restoration is included, even when its outside worth is zero.
The per-coalition forest normal form does not supply this global partition:
one profile can receive a C1 comparison and emit a C2 comparison. Its outgoing
reserve then remains uncontrolled. The next global construction must handle
that term; one-layer stars cannot produce a stronger architecture obstruction.
Both lemmas passed independent exact/proof audit and completed opposite-model
review with two verified wording clarifications. They are supporting route
boundaries, not new unrestricted bounds or separate papers. Read
reviews/PRICE_RESERVE_GROWING_ROOT_DISPOSITION.txt for the precise scope.
