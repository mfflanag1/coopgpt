WORTH-ONLY PARETO-MINIMAL COVER SELECTION CANNOT HAVE UNIFORM PAIR-TCWM FUNDING
2026-10-18 22:39 UTC. Scoped structural route filter; root and opposite-model
review accepted the argument. See reviews/PARETO_COVER_ROOT_DISPOSITION.txt.

CLAIM AND SCOPE
For each integer M>=1, use the arity3 packing technology
 F(P,X,Y)=min(P,sum_(j=1)^M min(X_j,Y_j))
and n=M+2 fixed actual owners, ALL fully eligible for ALL rows, with the same
unit report/endowment box. At every profile define the coalition game
 v_b(S)=F(sum_(i in S)b_i).
Suppose a mechanism's payments depend on reports ONLY through this entire
coalition-worth vector v_b; dependence on the fixed public technology, labels,
owner count, and common report box is allowed. Assume:
 (a) nonnegative payments and all-coalition coverage;
 (b) TCWM for every coalition of size<=2, including one unchanged member;
 (c) at every profile its payment vector is PARETO-MINIMAL in the nonnegative
     coverage polyhedron P(v)={y>=0:y(S)>=v(S) for all S}.
Pareto-minimal means no different z in P(v) satisfies0<=z<=y coordinatewise.
Then any all-profile funding factor on this institution is at least
                              n/3=(M+2)/3.               (1)
Hence no such mechanism family has finite funding uniform over owner/resource
counts. This covers nonseparable and report-adaptive selections as long as they
are worth-only and always choose a Pareto-minimal cover. It does NOT cover rules
using bundle directions beyond v_b, or rules deliberately selecting a dominated
cover with excess payments. It is not a general mechanism lower bound.

THE SAME FIXED-OWNER BRANCH CHAIN
Choose distinguished owner slots P,A,B_1,...,B_M. P reports P=1; leaf B_j reports
Y_j=1. At profile m, A reports X_j=1 for j<=m and0 otherwise. All other entries
are0. Only A restores between successive profiles. Production is1 throughout.
The game v_m has value1 exactly for coalitions containing BOTH P,A and at least
one of B_1,...,B_m; all other coalition values are0. Thus its coverage polyhedron
is given by
                y_P+y_A+y_(B_j)>=1, j<=m; y>=0.          (2)

PARETO-MINIMAL SET ON THIS FAMILY IS CONVEX
Every Pareto-minimal cover of v_m has inactive-owner payments0. Put h=y_P+y_A.
If h>1, reduce a positive common-owner payment while retaining h>=1, contradicting
minimality. If0<=h<=1, each active leaf must equal1-h: a larger leaf payment can
be reduced without violating(2). Conversely every vector of the form
 y_P=p>=0, y_A=a>=0, p+a<=1,
 y_(B_j)=q=1-p-a for j<=m, inactive payments0              (3)
is Pareto-minimal: reducing a positive common payment violates every tight branch
constraint, and reducing an active leaf violates its tight branch constraint.
For q=0, reducing either positive common payment likewise violates(2).
The set(3) is an affine image of a triangle, hence convex.

WHY LABEL AVERAGING IS LEGITIMATE HERE
Average the mechanism over all owner-label permutations, permuting reports and
payments together. The SAME public technology and full common report box are
preserved, so nonnegativity, coverage, pair TCWM, and the funding factor survive.
The averaged rule remains worth-only and is equivariant under owner labels.
Averaging does not in general preserve Pareto minimality; we DO NOT assert that.
But at each of the particular games v_m, every permutation-back payment vector
is a Pareto-minimal cover of v_m, because the original mechanism has property(c).
Convexity of the special set(3) proves that its average remains Pareto-minimal
on EVERY profile in this chain. That is all the proof needs.

GAME SYMMETRY AND PAIR PROPAGATION
In v_m, swapping P,A is a game automorphism. A worth-only label-equivariant rule
therefore pays them equally: p=a=t_m. This does not follow merely from public
technology symmetry; worth-only dependence is essential. Equation(3) gives
                 2t_m+q_m=1,
                 total Y_m=2t_m+m q_m=1+(m-1)q_m.        (4)
At m=1 the three productive owners P,A,B_1 are all symmetric in the game v_1.
Consequently each receives1/3, so q_1=1/3.
Protect the fixed pair C={A,B_1} at each activation. Only A changes. Its payment
at profile m is t_m+q_m=(1+q_m)/2. Pair TCWM therefore forces q_m>=q_(m-1).
Thus q_M>=1/3, and(4) gives Y_M>=1+(M-1)/3=n/3, proving(1).

CONSTRUCTIVE CONSEQUENCE
A bounded-funding upper construction based only on coalition worths must leave
the Pareto-minimal cover frontier somewhere. Minimizing any coordinatewise
strictly increasing objective over coverage, whether separable or nonseparable,
necessarily selects a Pareto-minimal point, so such a worth-only optimization
architecture cannot suffice. Total-payment caps do not help this conclusion:
if z<=y is a covered improvement, it remains under the same upper cap.
A bundle-direction-sensitive objective is outside the theorem and remains a
live option; so does a selector with deliberate reserve payments above every
minimal cover. No funding lower improvement is claimed for those alternatives.

EVIDENCE
The exact game values and covers on this chain through M=8 were checked by
check_scalar_importance_chain.py/.json (14,344 subset/profile comparisons).
That script predates this statement and does not establish label averaging or
Pareto-minimality; those are the explicit analytic arguments above. The scalar
convex-family note is frozen for its separate audit and was not edited here.

QUANTIFIED RESERVE CONSEQUENCE WITHOUT PARETO MINIMALITY
Drop assumption(c), keeping a worth-only pair-TCWM mechanism with funding<=K
uniformly on the fully eligible institution above. Label average as before.
At profile1, the three productive owners have equal payment u>=1/3 by their
coalition coverage, regardless of any payments to inactive owners. At profileM,
game symmetry gives common-owner payments P=A=t and each leaf payment q.
Restore A directly from profile1 to profileM and protect{A,B_1}. Then
                         t+q>=2u>=2/3.                  (5)
Funding gives2t+M q<=K, hence q<=K/M. Every active branch coalition{P,A,B_j},
whose worth is1, therefore receives
                 2t+q>=4/3-q>=4/3-K/M.                  (6)
Thus its coverage slack is at least1/3-K/M. If M>K and M>3K, q<1 and this is
positive. Holding all leaves fixed, reduce the total common-owner payment from
2t to1-q, sharing the reduction between P,A. This remains nonnegative and
satisfies every cover constraint, because each branch then gets exactly1.
The total removable payment is
                         2t+q-1>=1/3-K/M.               (7)
Consequently, for any bounded-funding worth-only pair-TCWM mechanism, its
symmetrized rule must select, for arbitrarily large M, a vector with at least
(1/3-o(1))F of coordinatewise removable reserve payment. This is a necessary structural feature, not a
construction or a general unrestricted lower improvement. The conclusion is
about the symmetrized rule; if all original selections had zero removable
reserve, the earlier convex-minimal-set argument would transfer that property
and contradict(7). No assertion that every original asymmetric rule has the
same exact reserve bound follows merely from averaging.
