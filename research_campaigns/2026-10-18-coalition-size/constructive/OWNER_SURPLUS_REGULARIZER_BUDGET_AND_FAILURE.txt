OWNER-SENSITIVE SURPLUS COVER REGULARIZATION: O(s) BUDGET, EXACT TCWM FAILURE
2026-10-18 22:12 UTC. Constructive route; no surviving mechanism claimed.

GENERAL BUDGET LEMMA
Let F be any finite monotone homogeneous packing value, B=sum_i b_i, V=F(B)>0.
Define standalone floors and owner surplus potentials
 f_i=F(b_i),
 g_i=max_{0<=a<=B-b_i}[F(a+b_i)-F(a)]-f_i >=0.             (1)
Take any positive weights w_i<=a0/g_i for g_i>0, with a0>0. Require y_i=f_i
when g_i=0. For lambda>0, minimize
 J(y)=sum_(g_i>0) w_i(y_i-f_i)^2 +(lambda/V)(sum_i y_i)^2  (2)
over y>=f and all-coalition coverage y(C)>=F(b_C).
A minimizer exists and is unique on the free coordinates because weights are
positive; the feasible set is closed, nonempty, and the objective is coercive.
Then its total payment Y satisfies the explicit bound
 Y <=V sqrt[1+(a0/lambda)(1-sum_i f_i/V)]
   <=V sqrt(1+a0/lambda).                                (3)

Proof. Let p be an optimal nonnegative production dual at B. The payment vector
y_i*=p dot b_i is a covered allocation with sum_i y_i*=V and y_i*>=f_i, since
p is feasible for every coalition/standalone report. Also
 F(B-b_i)<=p dot(B-b_i)=V-y_i*,
so y_i*<=F(B)-F(B-b_i). Choosing a=B-b_i in(1) gives
 0<=z_i*:=y_i*-f_i<=g_i.
Thus g_i=0 implies z_i*=0, so y* satisfies the imposed zero-surplus restriction.
For g_i>0, w_i(z_i*)^2<=a0 z_i*, and consequently
 J(y*)<=a0(V-sum_i f_i)+lambda V.
The minimizing y has (lambda/V)Y^2<=J(y)<=J(y*), proving(3).
If V=0, define all payments0; monotonicity makes every coalition value0.
This lemma uses an actual feasible production dual and does not presume that
all covered vectors are themselves common linear-price allocations.

SPECIFIC OWNER-SENSITIVE CANDIDATE
Set a0=s and
             w_i=min(V/g_i^2,s/g_i), lambda=1/s.          (4)
This is outside fixed/global-mixture marginal architectures. It preserves
standalone floors and weights each owner using a profile-dependent surplus
potential. Formula(3) gives funding at most sqrt(1+s^2)V, and every coalition
is covered by construction. TCWM is the remaining condition; it FAILS.

EXACT THREE-OWNER MIN2 COUNTEREXAMPLE AT s=2
Technology F(X,Y)=min(X,Y), fixed owners with low bundles
 b0=(0,1), b1=(2,0), b2=(2,2).
Only owner2 restores, to b2'=(4,2). The coalition C={1,2} is protected, with
owner1 unchanged. V=3 at both profiles. For min2 the potential before floor
subtraction is min(V,max(X_i,Y_i)); hence
 low f=(0,0,2), g=(1,2,0), w=(2,3/4),
 high f=(0,0,2), g=(1,2,1), w=(2,3/4,2).

Low: z2=y2-f2=0. Coverage reduces to z0+z1>=1; every other constraint is
redundant. The positive objective makes equality optimal, so total Y=3 and
the total penalty is constant. Minimize2 z0^2+(3/4)(1-z0)^2 to obtain
                 y_low=(3/11,8/11,2).                    (5)

High: coverage reduces to z0+z1>=1 and z0+z2>=1. For fixed z0 in[0,1], both
other surpluses take their minimum1-z0. If z0>=1, the minimum has z1=z2=0 and
the objective increases with z0, so the optimum lies in[0,1]. Write
 z0=1-q, z1=z2=q, 0<=q<=1.
The objective, omitting a common factor, becomes
 2(1-q)^2+(11/4)q^2+(lambda/3)(3+q)^2.
At lambda=1/2 its unique stationary point is q=18/59. Thus
                 y_high=(41/59,18/59,136/59).            (6)
Both formulas satisfy every coalition cover constraint exactly or with slack.
The protected coalition payment changes by
 (18/59+136/59)-(8/11+2)=154/59-30/11=-76/649<0.          (7)
This is an exact pair-TCWM failure with fixed owners and a real restoration;
all final payments remain nonnegative. No outside-production condition is used.

WHAT SURVIVES
The general budget lemma is valid independently of the rejected choice(4). It
provides a way to impose an O(s) budget on an owner-sensitive coverage selection
while retaining explicit feasible comparison points. A successful new selection
still needs a substantive coalition-restoration theorem; a quadratic objective,
standalone floors, and maximum marginal importance do not supply that theorem.
