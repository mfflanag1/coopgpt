BROADER FINITE-POPULATION CLASS
For n fixed actual owners, replace the binomial-mixture formula by
 y_i(b)=sum_{k=0}^{n-1} lambda_k(b) E_[R uniform among k-subsets of N\{i}]
                                 [F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)],
where all lambda_k(b)>=0 and the SAME vector lambda(b) is shared by every owner.
It may depend arbitrarily on reports, technology, labels and n. The conclusion
K>=s^(r-1)/r still follows by the main replication construction and a finite
sampling error. This does NOT cover owner-specific or report-sensitive subset
weights; common cardinality coefficients are essential.

COUPLING LEMMA
Fix a local component with d other local owners, embedded in n actual owners.
Conditional on global subset size k, the d inclusion bits are sampling d colors
WITHOUT replacement from a population of n-1 bits containing k ones. If instead
we sample WITH replacement, those bits are independent Bernoulli(p), p=k/(n-1).
Couple the two samples until the with-replacement draw repeats an earlier index.
The probability of any repeat is at most d(d-1)/[2(n-1)] by the union bound.
Hence total variation between the inclusion patterns is at most that quantity.
For any local marginal payoff in[0,W], expectation error is at most W times
this probability. All other components cancel pointwise before this comparison.
A conservative bound W D^2/(n-1) holds whenever d<=D.

APPLICATION TO THE REPLICATION MODEL
For this extension choose D also at least the number of other local owners
in every test component; increasing D in the main construction is harmless.
At each profile define the finite positive measure
             mu_b=sum_k lambda_k(b) delta_(k/(n-1)).
The additive anchor marginal is A even under fixed-cardinality sampling, so
its payment is exactly A beta_b, beta_b=sum_k lambda_k=mu_b([0,1]). Thus the
same bound beta_b<=B_N=K(1+1/N) holds.
For every normalized sentinel marginal f_j, its actual payment divided by L
is within
                 E_N=B_N D^2/(n-1)
of int f_j dmu_b. A focal test owner's actual payment is still in[0,B_N].
Pair TCWM therefore implies at every restoration
 Delta int f_j dmu >=-B_N/L-2E_N.
The mass moment is exact and obeys the stronger anchor bound as before.
Each threshold component has at least3 owners, so the constructed population
satisfies n>=3NT, where T is the fixed number of tests and N is the number of
fresh cycles. The analogue of the selected-cycle variation bound is
 epsilon_N <=(D+1)[B_N/N+2T(B_N/L+2E_N)]
            =(D+1)B_N[1/N+2T/L+4T D^2/(n-1)] ->0.
Here L=N^2T; D,T are fixed while N grows. Although total sampling error across
all transitions need not vanish, its AVERAGE PER CYCLE does vanish, which is
all the proof uses.

The hard min_r owners' actual marginals are within E_N of int p^(r-1)dmu, so
coverage gives int p^(r-1)dmu>=1/r-E_N. Each protected test-coalition payment
has at most s owners and differs from its binomial polynomial integral by at
most s E_N. All these errors vanish along the selected-cycle limit. The same
finite-list compactness argument yields a measure satisfying exact hard
coverage and every root64 threshold test. The main support argument applies,
and K>=s^(r-1)/r follows.

SCOPE CAUTION
An anonymous report-adaptive rule need NOT have common cardinality coefficients:
its subset weights may depend on the actual reported bundles in the subset.
Averaging labels does not remove that dependence. No claim about arbitrary
adaptive positive marginal-weight rules follows from this appendix.
