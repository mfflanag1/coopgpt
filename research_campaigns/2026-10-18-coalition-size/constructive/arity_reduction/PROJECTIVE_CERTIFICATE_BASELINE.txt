PROJECTIVE LOWER CERTIFICATE: THE TERNARY AFFINE BASELINE IS EXPONENTIAL
2026-10-18. Exact calculation for the existing circuit construction and the
stage/bridge gadget. This closes this transfer route; it is not a new lower
frontier, nor an obstruction to every possible certificate-specific reduction.

INPUTS
Use lower/GENERAL_ARITY_CIRCUIT_LOWER.txt with fixed r=k+1>=3 and M>=1.
The binary vector space has dimension D=1+(r-2)M, its nonzero rows number
R=2^D-1, and the fixed actual-owner roster has N=1+(r-1)M members, each with
the full unit-cube endowment on these R rows. Activities are all minimal
binary circuits of cardinality r; coefficients and profits are all1.
At ladder profile C_m, exactly D+m distinct rows have unit capacity; all
others have capacity0. Its production is1. In particular, C_M has N unit
rows and F(C_M)=1. The proof uses a terminal budget at C_M, after averaging
over the fixed institution's GL and owner-permutation symmetries.

1. EXACT NUMBER OF ACTIVITIES AND ROW DEGREE
An ordered independent (r-1)-tuple determines a circuit by adjoining its
sum. Conversely, each r-circuit is obtained r! times: choose its omitted
element in r ways and order the others in (r-1)! ways. Consequently
 |E| = [product_(j=0)^(r-2) (2^D-2^j)] / r!.
Row transitivity and incidence counting give the common degree
 d_r(D) = r|E|/R
        = [product_(j=1)^(r-2) (2^D-2^j)] / (r-1)!.
Here D>=r-1, so all factors are positive. For fixed r,
 d_r(D)=Theta_r(2^((r-2)D)).
For example, since each 2^j<=2^(D-1),
 2^((r-2)(D-1))/(r-1)! <= d_r(D)
                          <=2^((r-2)D)/(r-1)!.

2. THE TWO BASELINES AT THE ACTUAL BUDGET PROFILE
The arithmetic-average linear map from the general ternary gadget has
 M_(i,e)(b_i)=(1/r)sum_(v in e)b_(i,v),
 B(b)=(r-1)sum_e sum_i M_(i,e)(b_i)
     =[(r-1)/r]d_r(D)sum_v b_(N,v).
Therefore
 B_linear(C_m)=[(r-1)/r]d_r(D)(D+m),
 B_linear(C_M)=[(r-1)/r]d_r(D)N.                    (1)
The fixed-endowment map has M_(i,e)=1 for EVERY owner and activity, because
every original owner has the full unit-cube endowment. Hence
 B_constant=N(r-1)|E|=R B_linear(C_M).               (2)
Both are already enormous at the terminal budget profile, whose original
worth is exactly1. They are not merely bad at irrelevant zero-worth profiles.
For fixed r and M tending to infinity,
 B_linear=Theta_r(M 2^((r-2)[1+(r-2)M])),
 B_constant=Theta_r(M 2^((r-1)[1+(r-2)M])).
Every GL/owner permutation of C_M has the same baseline under these maps.

3. EXACT DILUTION OF THE EXISTING LOWER CERTIFICATE
Write
 Q=product_(m=2)^M [(r-1)m/((r-1)(m-1)+1)],
 L=1+[(r-1)(M-1)/(rM)]Q.
The accepted original certificate forces terminal total payment at least L.
Its growth for fixed r is polynomial, L=Theta_r(M^((r-2)/(r-1))).
For completeness, with alpha=(r-2)/(r-1), the identity
log Q=sum_(m=2)^M -log(1-alpha/m)=alpha log M+O_r(1)
follows by the convergent quadratic remainder, proving this stated rate.

For the CONSTANT map, a TCWM gadget rule z with factor kappa gives original
y_i=z_i-B_i with all coverage and TCWM. Its terminal budget is only
 sum_i y_i <= kappa(B+1)-B.
Combining with the lower certificate therefore yields exactly
              kappa >= (B+L)/(B+1)
                     =1+(L-1)/(B+1).               (3)
With B from (2), the excess over1 is exponentially small in M. This is
weaker than the existing arity3 lower bound, not an improvement.

For the LINEAR map, the same calculation with B from (1) is available only
when z satisfies FULL OUT (so baseline subtraction preserves TCWM and indeed
full OUT). Mere TCWM of z does not justify that subtraction. Even this
stronger premise yields only the exponentially diluted expression (3).
The respective coalition-size limits and actual owner roster remain unchanged.

4. REWEIGHTING THE SAME FIXED POSITIVE LINEAR CAP MAP CANNOT CURE THE LOSS
This paragraph concerns only the SAME stage/bridge construction, while
allowing arbitrary owner-specific nonnegative linear cap functionals
 M_(i,e)(b_i)=sum_v a_(i,e,v)b_(i,v), a_(i,e,v)>=0.
Suppose its claimed all-report, all-coalition identity G=B+F really holds.
Give owner i alone the indicator bundle 1_e of a circuit e. The ONLY
original activity supported by these r rows is e, because all activities
have cardinality r. Original worth is1. By the gadget's exact capped identity,
the cap on e must therefore be at least1:
                   sum_(v in e)a_(i,e,v)>=1.        (4)
In particular sum_v a_(i,e,v)>=1.

At C_M every owner reports one nonzero row. Averaging its GL orbit makes
each individual owner's row uniform over the R nonzero rows; no independence
assumption is needed. Thus the orbit-average baseline satisfies
 average B =[(r-1)/R]sum_i sum_e sum_v a_(i,e,v)
           >=[(r-1)/R]N|E|=B_linear(C_M).           (5)
The arithmetic-average map attains equality. A non-equivariant choice can
move baseline cost among orbit profiles but cannot reduce this average.
The symmetrized certificate has the same budget calculation with average B;
its resulting lower bound 1+(L-1)/(average B+1) is no larger than that from
the arithmetic-average map. This observation concerns the bound produced by
this transfer calculation; (5) alone is not a lower bound on kappa.

For arbitrary fixed CONSTANT owner caps in this gadget, the same singleton
test forces M_(i,e)>=1. Hence B>=N(r-1)|E|, and (2) is already minimal in
that class. No extra owner or altered incentive-size convention is used.

DISPOSITION
The general positive affine gadget does not transfer the existing general-r
polynomial lower bound into a useful arity3 funding lower bound, even when
one checks baseline only at its terminal certificate budget profiles.
Fixed positive linear reweighting and fixed owner caps within this gadget
cannot remove the exponential loss. Different gadgets or new baseline-payment
control remain outside this statement.
