AN EXACT POSITIVE PATH GADGET: INCENTIVES TRANSFER, ORDINARY FUNDING DOES NOT
2026-10-18. Explicit affine reduction of one minimum technology, not a reduction
of arbitrary overlapping higher-arity packing technologies. No novelty claim.

1. THE FIXED POSITIVE GADGET
Let r>=2 and F(x)=min_(j=1)^r x_j for x>=0. Let H be the unit-profit path on
2r-2 resource vertices. Its capacities are the fixed linear row-copy map
  T x=(x_1,x_2,x_2,x_3,x_3,...,x_(r-1),x_(r-1),x_r).
Put B(x)=sum_(j=2)^(r-1) x_j, with empty sum0 when r=2. The fractional matching
packing value of this arity-two graph satisfies, on EVERY real capacity vector,
                         G(Tx)=B(x)+F(x).               (1)
All coefficients and profits are nonnegative; there is no signed flow equation.

Primal certificate. Let m=min_j x_j. Number path edges1,...,2r-3. Put flow m
on every odd edge, and x_(j+1)-m on even edge2j for j=1,...,r-2. All flows
are nonnegative. Every interior duplicated resource vertex is exactly saturated;
the two end vertices use m<=x_1,x_r. Total production is B(x)+m.

Dual certificate. For each k=1,...,r take the vertex cover
  C_k={2,4,...,2k-2} union {2k-1,2k+1,...,2r-3},
with empty ranges omitted. It meets every path edge and its total capacity is
B(x)+x_k. Choose a minimizing k. This proves the reverse inequality and (1).

2. ACTUAL OWNERS, COALITIONS AND RESTORATIONS
Each fixed actual original owner i can hold an arbitrary mixed bundle b_i in
R_+^r. Give that SAME owner all row copies T b_i. Thus
  sum_i T b_i=T(sum_i b_i),  T(b_i+d_i)=T b_i+T d_i,
and binding underreports map to binding underreports with no extra owners.
For every coalition C, the exact identity is
       G(sum_(i in C)T b_i)=sum_(i in C)B(b_i)+F(b_C).    (2)
The map remains fixed at zeros and through arbitrary report changes.

Suppose z_i is a nonnegative all-coalition-covering, full-OUT graph mechanism.
Define an original payment by subtracting the owner's linear baseline:
                         y_i(b)=z_i(Tb)-B(b_i).          (3)
This preserves:
 - Nonnegativity: singleton coverage gives z_i>=G(Tb_i)>=B(b_i).
 - ALL-coalition coverage: subtract sum_C B(b_i) from (2)'s graph coverage.
 - Full OUT for each protected original coalition C: graph full OUT gives
     Delta z_C>=G(T d_C)=B(d_C)+F(d_C),
   whereas the subtracted baseline increases by exactly B(d_C). Hence
     Delta y_C>=F(d_C).
TCWM alone for z does NOT suffice for this subtraction; its zero lower bound
would become -B(d_C). The transfer as stated concerns full OUT, and does not
transfer arbitrary TCWM mechanisms or automatically strengthen a TCWM lower.

3. THE FATAL ORDINARY-BUDGET TERM
If the graph mechanism has ordinary funding sum_i z_i<=kappa G(Tb_N), (3) gives
           sum_i y_i<=(kappa-1)B(b_N)+kappa F(b_N).      (4)
The ratio B/F is unbounded and may have F=0<B. Thus (4) gives no finite
multiplicative original funding factor. The previously accepted graph
utilization rule has EXACT total2G, so its transferred total is exactly
                          B+2F.                         (5)
For example, r=4 and x=(0,1,1,1) give F=0,B=2,G=2; transferred payments are
nonnegative but total2, contradicting every bound K F. The failure is actual,
not just a loose estimate in (4).

Subtracting twice the baseline instead would repair total2F for that rule,
but destroys nonnegativity (and is not justified by its outside-production
increment). Exact example: r=3, x=(1,10,10), with each original coordinate
held by its own actual owner. The path capacities are(1,10,10,10). Its Huber
optimizer has prices(29/40,11/40,29/40,11/40), and utilization prices
  q=(1,11/20,1,11/20).
The KKT maximum matching has edge flows(1,9/2,11/2), value11, with vertex uses
(1,11/2,10,11/2)=c*h'(p). All graph cover constraints are tight. The middle
original owner holds both interior rows and receives31/2. Its baseline is10,
so subtracting2B_i would yield -9/2.

4. WHAT EXTRA THEOREM WOULD BE NEEDED
A graph mechanism satisfying the STRONGER gain-only bound on this image domain
  sum_i z_i(Tb)<=B(b_N)+K F(b_N)                          (6)
together with singleton coverage and full OUT would, by (3), yield an original
full-OUT mechanism with factor K. The accepted total-budget graph theorem2G
does not prove (6). Establishing (6) is a separate mechanism problem, not an
algebraic consequence of arity reduction. A fixed additive baseline cannot be
silently reclassified as free sponsor funding under this campaign's contract.

5. LIMIT OF THE GADGET
Equation(1) concerns a single r-way minimum, or disjoint sums where the same
identity is proved separately. Replacing each overlapping higher-arity activity
by a private copy of this path would duplicate shared stocks and generally
change coalition production. Identifying path vertices can create new mixed
paths and also needs a new value theorem. Neither operation has been proved
to reduce the full binary-circuit lower-bound technology.

The gadget is therefore a precise failed transfer, with two separate barriers:
ordinary graph funding does not survive the baseline subtraction, and the
single-minimum value identity is not a whole-technology embedding. It neither
improves the arity3 exponent nor refutes more specialized certificate gadgets.
