CONSTRUCTION ON THIS REPORT SLICE
Put H=1/2, total measure mass B=2.
For m=1 take nu_1=H delta_1+(B-H)delta_0.
For integer m>=2 set
 p_m=1/m,
 a_m=(1-1/m)^(m-1),
 c_m=H/a_m,
 d_m=H-c_m/m^2,
 nu_m=c_m delta_(1/m)+d_m delta_1+(B-c_m-d_m)delta_0.       (1)
All masses are nonnegative. Indeed a_m>=1/e>1/3 and m^2 a_m>=2 for m>=2,
so d_m>=H/2>0. Also c_m+d_m<=H(1+e)<2=B.
The total mass and second moment are exactly
 int 1 dnu_m=B,          int p^2 dnu_m=H.                 (2)
Thus the additive anchor always receives2L and every hard owner receives

STAR PAYMENTS

Every inactive leaf receives0. The center receives
 z_m=d_m+c_m[1-(1-1/m)^m]
    =H[1/m+(1-1/m^2)/a_m].                              (4)
In particular z_2=1, and z_m increases toHe=e/2.

PROOF OF THE MONOTONICITY USED IN(4)
For real x in(0,1/2], let
 f(x)=x+(1+x)(1-x)^(2-1/x),
 g(x)=log(1+x)+(2-1/x)log(1-x).
Then z_m=H f(1/m). The convergent power series for the derivative is
 g'(x)=-1/2 +sum_{k>=1}[(-1)^k-1-1/(k+2)]x^k
       <=-1/2-(7/3)x <0.
Thus exp(g(x)) decreases in x, from e at0 to3/2 at1/2, and equals2 at1/3.
For0<x<=1/3, exp(g)>=2 and g'<-1/2, so f'=1+exp(g)g'<0.
For1/3<=x<=1/2, exp(g)>=3/2 and g'<=-1/2-7/9=-23/18,
again f'<0. Consequently z_m strictly increases for integers m>=2 and is
bounded above byHe. The first step z_1=H<z_2=1 also increases.

INTERPRETATION / LIMITS
This rule is specified only on the binary leaf-activation slice with center,
hard owners and anchor fixed. It is NOT specified on arbitrary real reports,
and it proves no upper bound for the campaign model. Its purpose is to mark
the exact limitation of the single-rate obstruction: allowing a report-dependent
nonnegative mixing measure changes its exponential-decay argument. Constant
moment constraints can prevent spillovers to unrelated anchor/hard owners while
moving probability mass toward1/m as the number of active substitutes grows.
Even one global mixing measure can therefore pass the entire obstruction chain.
Zero-gated bridges to make the public incidence graph connected remain inactive
and do not alter this calculation. The owner-specific/general mechanisms remain
open, and this calibration is not a novelty assertion.
