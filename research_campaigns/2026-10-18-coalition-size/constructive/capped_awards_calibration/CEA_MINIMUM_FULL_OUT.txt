CONSTRAINED EQUAL AWARDS CALIBRATION FOR A SINGLE MINIMUM TECHNOLOGY
2026-10-19. All-real fixed-owner theorem and finite rational PL construction.
This is a calibration application, not a new general packing funding bound.
Historical novelty of the application is unconfirmed.

PRIOR INGREDIENT
The allocation primitive is the classical constrained equal awards rule (CEA),
not a newly introduced rationing rule. Root verified its definition in William
Thomson, "Lorenz rankings of rules for the adjudication of conflicting claims,"
RCER Working Paper538, December21,2007, Section3.1, printedp6/PDFpage9:
https://www.sas.rochester.edu/eco/rcer/papers/rcer_538.pdf
Only the primitive's prior definition is attributed here. The argument below
is the campaign's independently checked application to the specified mechanism.

1. INSTITUTION AND RULE
There are n>=1 fixed actual owners and r>=1 resource pools. Owner i reports
b_i=(b_(i,1),...,b_(i,r)) in one fixed coordinate box [0,bar_b_i]. Endowments
can be arbitrary, including zero coordinates; ownership may be mixed. Let
 T_a=sum_i b_(i,a),  E=F(T)=min_a T_a.
For each pool a independently, allocate the SAME estate E among the actual
owners, using their pool-a reports as claims:
 t_i^a(b)=min(b_(i,a),lambda_a),
 sum_i t_i^a(b)=E.                                      (1)
Define
                         y_i(b)=sum_(a=1)^r t_i^a(b).   (2)
The rule is defined on the entire nonnegative orthant and can be restricted
to any one fixed actual-owner report box. There is no extra agent or payment
floor and no reallocation of reports between actual owners.

For precision choose lambda_a as the smallest nonnegative solution of
sum_i min(b_(i,a),lambda_a)=E. A solution exists because 0<=E<=T_a. When
E=T_a, lambda_a=max_i b_(i,a), and every award is its full claim; when E=0,
choose lambda_a=0, including when all claims vanish. Even if the water level
is not uniquely specified, the awards are unique: raising a level weakly
raises every award, so equal totals force every award unchanged.

THEOREM
The rule (2) is nonnegative, continuous, homogeneous and piecewise linear.
It covers EVERY actual coalition at EVERY real feasible report, has total
funding exactly rF(T), and satisfies FULL OUT for every actual coalition
of every size. Hence it also satisfies TCWM for every size. These conclusions
include mixed ownership, simultaneous restorations, unchanged protected
members, ties, zeros and upper endowment boundaries.

2. FUNDING AND ALL-COALITION COVERAGE
Nonnegativity is immediate. Summing (1) over a gives
                         sum_i y_i=rE.                 (3)
At E=0 every award is0, so the rule satisfies the ordinary multiplicative
funding requirement even at zero-output profiles.

Choose any bottleneck pool h with T_h=E. Since its estate equals its total
claims, t_i^h=b_(i,h) for every owner. For every actual coalition C,
 sum_(i in C)y_i >=sum_(i in C)t_i^h
                 =sum_(i in C)b_(i,h)
                 >=min_a sum_(i in C)b_(i,a)
                 =F(sum_(i in C)b_i).                  (4)
No restriction is imposed on C's size or ownership pattern.

3. A FINITE COALITION-MONOTONICITY LEMMA FOR ONE CEA PROBLEM
Let claim vectors c,c'>=0 and estates E,E' satisfy E' >= E, E<=sum c,
E'<=sum c'. Fix the SAME actual coalition C at both profiles. Suppose
c'_i>=c_i for i in C and c'_j=c_j for j outside C. Then
               sum_(i in C)CEA_i(c',E')>=sum_(i in C)CEA_i(c,E). (5)

Proof. Choose water levels lambda,lambda'. If lambda'>=lambda, each C
member's claim and water level both weakly rise, so its capped award weakly
rises. If lambda'<lambda, each outsider's claim is unchanged and its award
weakly falls. Since total awards rise from E to E', subtracting outsiders'
awards proves (5). The proof includes equality, zero claims/estates, and
members of C whose claims do not change. It does not assume water levels
themselves are monotone in own claims and estate.

4. FULL OUT BY FINITE BOTTLENECK SEGMENTS
Consider ANY feasible simultaneous restoration b to b+d with d>=0 supported
on a fixed actual C. Set D_a=sum_(i in C)d_(i,a). Along b(t)=b+t d,
  T_a(t)=T_a(0)+tD_a, E(t)=min_a T_a(t), 0<=t<=1.
Every T_a and therefore E are nondecreasing. Lemma(5) implies that EACH
pool's aggregate C-award is nondecreasing between ANY two times.

The lower envelope of these finitely many affine totals has finitely many
bottleneck intervals. Split [0,1] at all pairwise crossing times in its
interior (identical lines require no split). On each resulting closed
interval [u,v], choose a pool h that is a bottleneck throughout; ties at
endpoints or throughout the interval cause no problem. Then
 t_i^h(b(t))=b_(i,h)+t d_(i,h) for all t in [u,v].
Its C-award increment is exactly (v-u)D_h. Other pools' C increments are
nonnegative by Lemma(5). Consequently
 Y_C(b(v))-Y_C(b(u)) >=(v-u)D_h
                    >=(v-u)min_a D_a.
Summing over all intervals telescopes and gives
             Y_C(b+d)-Y_C(b)>=min_a D_a
                               =F(sum_(i in C)d_i).     (6)
This is precisely full OUT for the original, unaltered coalition. It does
not rely on differentiability, arbitrary choices at ties, or unproved
boundary admission. The same proof includes a path beginning at zero output
or lying along a tie face. The empty coalition has the trivial0>=0 comparison.

5. EXPLICIT FINITE RATIONAL POLYHEDRAL COVER
For rational fixed endowments, the following gives a finite rational
polyhedral description on the entire report box. Choose a bottleneck h.
For each other pool a!=h, choose a PROPER saturated subset S_a subsetneq N;
put q_a=n-|S_a|>=1 and
             L_a(b)=[T_h-sum_(j in S_a)b_(j,a)]/q_a.    (7)
Use the closed cell defined by the box and
 T_h<=T_c for every c;
 L_a>=0;
 b_(i,a)<=L_a for i in S_a;
 b_(i,a)>=L_a for i outside S_a,  for every a!=h.        (8)
On this cell use
 y_i=b_(i,h)+sum_(a!=h)
       [b_(i,a) if i in S_a, and L_a otherwise].         (9)
These are homogeneous linear formulas with rational coefficients. There
are at most r(2^n-1)^(r-1) listed regions before discarding empties/redundancies.
Some can have only lower dimension; a complete arrangement refinement of
their defining hyperplanes supplies a usual full-dimensional partition
relative to the coordinates with positive endowments.

Every profile is covered. Choose h attaining the minimum; in each other
pool take S_a={i:b_(i,a)<lambda_a}. Its complement is nonempty when E<T_a;
when E=T_a choose all maximum-claim owners outside S_a (including every
owner if all claims vanish). Then (7)-(8) hold. Conversely, (7)-(8) imply
that the capped awards sum to T_h and satisfy the CEA equations, so formula
(9) computes exactly (2). On every overlap both formulas therefore agree,
including all tie and zero faces.

The finite closed-cover pasting property now proves continuity. Scaling
reports scales E, each water level and every award, proving homogeneity.
Thus an ordinary finite rational PL partition exists by construction; this
does not claim that arbitrary mechanisms admit such a representation.

6. WHY OWNER-SENSITIVE CELLS MATTER
The construction uses saturated-owner sets as well as the production
bottleneck. It need not be affine on a whole production chamber. For example,
take two owners/two pools, with
 b_1=(1/2,t), b_2=(1/2,2-t), 1/5<=t<=4/5.
The totals stay (1,2), strictly inside the SAME bottleneck chamber, yet
 y_1=1/2+min(t,1/2).
At t=1/5,1/2,4/5 this gives7/10,1,1; the middle payment differs from the
average of the two endpoint payments. All reports lie in the same fixed
box with positive endowments. This explains the role of the extra ownership
cells in the production-chamber representation obstruction.

SCOPE
For r2 the construction attains the already known factor2 full-OUT
calibration. For one min_r technology its factor is r, independent of n
and protected coalition size. This does not handle arbitrary shared-resource
packing technologies, establish optimality, improve the general K3 bounds,
prove PL completeness, or certify historical novelty. CEA is classical.
