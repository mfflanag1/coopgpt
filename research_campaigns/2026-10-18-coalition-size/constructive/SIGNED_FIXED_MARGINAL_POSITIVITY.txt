SIGNED FIXED MARGINALS: INDIVIDUAL TCWM FORCES COEFFICIENTWISE POSITIVITY
2026-10-18 22:19 UTC. Exact structural lemma; no novelty claim.

STATEMENT
Fix any finite set N of actual owners, all eligible for three rows, and the
single technology F(x,y,z)=min(x,y,z). Suppose a payment rule has the form
 y_i(b)=sum_(T subset N\{i}) w_(i,T)[F(b_i+b_T)-F(b_T)],     (1)
where every w_(i,T) is a fixed real number, independent of reports. Weights may
depend arbitrarily on owner labels, population size, this public technology,
and a common fixed report/endowment box. Every isolator below lies in the SAME
unit box; coefficients are held fixed across all targets and restorations.
If the rule is individually withholding-monotone at every nonnegative real
profile, then every coefficient w_(i,T) is nonnegative. This conclusion uses
no coalition coverage, funding bound, payment nonnegativity, or symmetry.
It remains true if reports are restricted to the unit cube [0,1]^3. This is
a claim about this min3 institution, not arbitrary technologies with potentially
redundant signed marginal representations.

EXACT SUBSET-ISOLATION PROOF
Fix owner i and target subset T*. Index the other m=|N|-1 owners by j=0,...,m-1.
Give owner j a positive scalar a_j=2^j, so every subset has a distinct integer
sum W_T=sum_(j in T) a_j. Put W*=W_(T*) and choose 0<delta<epsilon<1.
At the low profile set
 b_j=(2a_j,a_j,0) for j!=i,
 b_i=(epsilon,W*,2W*+epsilon).
At high only owner i restores its second row by delta. Owners and eligibility
are fixed throughout. For any T subset N\{i},
 F(b_T)=0,
 F(b_i+b_T)=min(epsilon+2W_T, W*+W_T, 2W*+epsilon).          (2)
If W_T<W*, integer separation implies W_T<=W*-1. The first coordinate is the
strict minimum before and after restoration, since epsilon<1. If W_T>W*, the
third coordinate is the strict minimum before and after restoration. If W_T=W*,
the second coordinate is the unique minimum, and rises by delta<epsilon.
Unique subset sums therefore give the exact identity
 F(b_i'+b_T)-F(b_i+b_T)=delta * 1{T=T*}.                   (3)
As F(b_T) never changes, the owner's payment increment is
                    y_i(b')-y_i(b)=delta w_(i,T*).        (4)
Individual TCWM makes(4) nonnegative. Since i,T* were arbitrary, all weights
are nonnegative. Empty and full target subsets are included without exception;
for T*=empty, the isolated minimum at low is0 and rises to delta.

BOUNDED REPORTS
Let W_all=2^m-1 and M=2W_all+1. Take epsilon=1/4 and delta=1/8, and divide
EVERY low and high report by M. All coordinates then belong to[0,1]; at m=0,
M=1 and the same construction works. Homogeneity replaces delta in(3)-(4)
by delta/M, preserving the sign. No report comparison changes the population.

HIGHER ARITY
For min_r with r>=3, give owner i sufficiently large fixed supplies in the
remaining r-3 rows, with all other owners zero there. These rows remain strictly
above the values in(2) at both profiles. A common scaling again puts all reports
in[0,1]^r. Hence the same coefficientwise positivity holds on every min_r,
r>=3. This argument does not prove the corresponding statement for min2.

CONSEQUENCE USING THE ACCEPTED ROOT64 THEOREM
The accepted input ROOT_ADJUDICATION_64_POSITIVE_MARGINAL_BARRIER.txt proves:
on the SAME single fully eligible min_r technology, a fixed NONNEGATIVE
whole-owner marginal rule satisfying all-coalition coverage, TCWM for |C|<=s,
and funding kappa_n F must have liminf_(n->infinity) kappa_n>=s^(r-1)/r.
For r>=3, the positivity lemma removes the need to assume nonnegative weights:
individual TCWM, already a required part of TCWM<=s, forces them. Therefore
arbitrary SIGNED report-independent whole-owner marginal rules have the same
quadratic arity3 obstruction liminf kappa_n>=s^2/3.
This is an architecture extension, not an unrestricted lower-bound improvement.
It does not exclude report-dependent signed weights, fractional-resource rules,
nonlinear payments, or general linear value formulas with independent terms
not expressible in the marginal form(1). Source priority is unassessed.

SLIGHTLY BROADER DIAGONAL FACT
For any fixed linear rule y_i=sum_(S subset N) alpha_(i,S)F(b_S), the same
restoration proves alpha_(i,T* union{i})>=0 for every T*. Coefficients on sets
not containing i are invisible to this own-restoration test and remain free.
Without additional structure, this does NOT recover the marginal form(1).
