REPORT-ADAPTIVE GLOBAL BINOMIAL PARAMETERS STILL REQUIRE QUADRATIC FUNDING
2026-10-18 21:29 UTC. Constructive-lane derivation; root audit requested.
Architecture obstruction only. No unrestricted lower improvement or novelty claim.

MODEL / CLASS
Fix integer s>=2. For each finite fixed actual-owner model, allow an arbitrary
report-adaptive common probability p(b) in[0,1] and a common finite nonnegative
multiplier beta(b). The rule is
 y_i(b)=beta(b) m_i(p(b);b),
 m_i(p;b)=E_R[F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)],
where R independently includes each OTHER actual owner with probability p.
The functions beta,p may depend on the entire report profile, technology,
actual-owner labels and total owner count. No continuity, anonymity, derivative,
fixed coefficient or report-independent parameter assumption is made.
Require all-coalition coverage, TCWM for |C|<=s, and total funding <=K F(B).

CLAIM
Uniformly over arity-at-most3 technologies and growing owner counts, this class
requires asymptotic funding at least s^2/3. More precisely the finite construction
below forces inequality(6); consequently liminf_M K_M >=s^2/[3(1+(epsilon+1)/L)]
for fixed positive L,epsilon, and letting L grow gives the s^2/3 architecture
lower. This extends the fixed-parameter limitation to globally adaptive scalar
parameters, but NOT to owner-specific adaptive marginal weights or general rules.

ONE FIXED TECHNOLOGY FOR EACH M
Take the disjoint sum of three production components:
 F(c)=a+min(x1,x2,x3)+min(P,sum_{j=1}^M z_j).                 (1)
There is one additive activity, one three-input activity, and M two-input star
activities. All unit-profit columns have arity<=3. Actual owners are:
 one anchor owner A holding a=L;
 three hard-complement owners H_l, each holding x_l=epsilon;
 one star-center owner O holding P=1;
 M distinct star-leaf owners Z_j, each eligible for its own row z_j in[0,1].
Thus n=M+5 actual owners. Technology, eligibility and identities are fixed.
At profile b^(m), m leaves report1, the rest0; all other reports are fixed.
Total production is L+epsilon+1 for every m>=1.

Because production is additive across these disjoint components, each owner's
binomial marginal depends only on its own component. Contributions from other
components cancel pointwise inside every marginal difference. Therefore:
 m_A=L;
 m_H_l=epsilon p^2;
 m_Z_j=p(1-p)^(m-1) for any active leaf j at profile b^(m);
 m_Z_j=0 for a zero-report leaf.                            (2)
Write beta_m=beta(b^(m)), p_m=p(b^(m)) and
 q_m=beta_m p_m(1-p_m)^(m-1), the payment to an active leaf.
All active leaves have the same payment because the common scalar parameters
are shared even if the parameter functions themselves inspect owner labels.

POINTWISE COVERAGE AND FUNDING BOUNDS
Coverage of the coalition of the three H owners implies
                 3 beta_m p_m^2 >=1.                     (3)
Funding, just through the anchor's payment beta_m L, implies
 beta_m <=B_M:=K_M (L+epsilon+1)/L.
Thus p_m>=delta_M:=1/sqrt(3 B_M), and beta_m p_m>=1/3.
In particular q_1>=1/3. Coverage rules out p_m=0 and beta_m=0.
If a parameter choice p_m=1 with m>=2 would make q_m=0, the transition bound
below rules it out automatically. No limiting endpoint convention is needed.

THE FIXED-OWNER TRANSITION CHAIN
Restore leaf Z_m from0 to1. For 2<=m<=s choose C to contain the new leaf and
all m-1 previously active leaves; its size m is permitted. For m>s choose C
containing the new leaf and any s-1 previously active leaves. The previously
active members make NO report change; all outsiders remain fixed.
The old new-leaf payment is exactly0 by the marginal formula. TCWM implies
 m q_m >=(m-1) q_(m-1)          (2<=m<=s),
 s q_m >=(s-1) q_(m-1)          (m>s).
For M>=s, iteration yields
 q_M >= (1/(3s)) (1-1/s)^(M-s).                            (4)
This proof retains all inactive actual owners throughout every comparison.

On the other hand, beta_M<=B_M, p_M<=1 and p_M>=delta_M give
 q_M <= B_M (1-1/sqrt(3 B_M))^(M-1).                       (5)
Combining(4)-(5) gives the exact finite necessary condition
 (1/(3s))(1-1/s)^(M-s)
 <= B_M (1-1/sqrt(3 B_M))^(M-1).                           (6)
If B_M<1/3 then (3) already contradicts p_m<=1, so the square root expression
in any feasible instance is nonnegative.

ASYMPTOTIC CONSEQUENCE
Suppose along an unbounded sequence of M one had B_M<=B0<s^2/3. The function
B -> B(1-1/sqrt(3B))^(M-1) is increasing for B>=1/3; hence(6) also holds with
B0 in place of B_M. But 1-1/sqrt(3B0)<1-1/s, so its right side decays strictly
faster in M than the left side, a contradiction. Therefore
 liminf_M B_M>=s^2/3,
 liminf_M K_M >= s^2/[3(1+(epsilon+1)/L)].                 (7)
For a factor claimed uniformly over all finite arity3 models, choose L as large
as desired while epsilon remains positive. This gives K(s)>=s^2/3 in this class.
If one requires all report bounds<=1, replace the single anchor by L separate
unit additive owners for integer L; their TOTAL payment is beta L and the same
proof works, with n=M+L+4. Growing finite owner counts remain allowed.

SCOPE / WHY THIS DOES NOT SOLVE THE CAMPAIGN
The lower is caused by forcing one adaptive scalar pair(beta,p) to serve a hard
complement component, an additive budget anchor and a repeated star transition
simultaneously. General mechanisms can localize across independent technologies;
owner-specific adaptive weights can as well. This is an architecture exclusion,
not a stronger unrestricted exponent than the recorded1/4. It rules out a
natural report-adaptive escape route and identifies owner/component localization
as necessary for this family of binomial methods. Full OUT is not used.

FINITE QUADRATIC OBSTRUCTION WITH O(s log s) OWNERS
For every integer s>=2, set epsilon=1,L=12 and test claimed K0=s^2/4.
Then B0=7s^2/24. Choose the rational q=16/(15s)>1/s; it satisfies
 3 B0 q^2=224/225<1,
so hard coverage plus beta<=B0 forces p>q. Put
 a=(s-1)/s, b=1-q=(15s-16)/(15s).
A contradiction follows once
 (a/b)^(M-s)>3s B0 b^(s-1).                               (8)
Since a/b=1+1/(15s-16) and log(1+x)>=x/(1+x),
 log(a/b)>=1/(15s-15)>1/(15s).
Also 3sB0 b^(s-1)<(7/8)s^3<s^3. Consequently the explicit choice
 M=s+ceil(45s log s)
satisfies(8). Thus this finite n=M+5 owner family already excludes factor
s^2/4 for every s>=2 in the adaptive-global-scalar class. This is a growing
architecture obstruction rather than just a fixed-s compactness argument.
The exact rational checker finds smaller M by direct integer-fraction arithmetic;
its conclusions do not depend on floating point logarithms.

CONNECTED TECHNOLOGY VARIANT
Decomposing only by public connected components does not evade this example.
Add a new gate resource q with its own fixed actual owner reporting0 at every
comparison. Add positive-profit arity3 activities {a,x1,q} and {x1,P,q}.
The technology's resource/activity incidence graph becomes connected, but these
bridge activities are infeasible at every calibration report because q=0.
Therefore all worths and marginal formulas above remain EXACT. This uses allowed
zero reports, not changing ownership or eligibility. It excludes a common beta,p
per PUBLIC connected component as well. Report-adaptive active-support/component
splitting and owner-specific weights are not excluded. No dummy-zero axiom is
added to the unrestricted model; the gate owner has zero payment only because
this particular marginal formula assigns it zero.

GENERAL FIXED ARITY r>=2
Replace the hard min3 by a hard min_r. Hard coverage is
 r beta p^(r-1)>=1,
so delta=(rB)^(-1/(r-1)); also beta p>=1/r. The same star transition chain gives
 (1/(r s))(1-1/s)^(M-s)<=B(1-(rB)^(-1/(r-1)))^(M-1).
Hence liminf funding in the same adaptive-global-scalar class is at least
 s^(r-1)/r after the anchor limit. For r=2, star and hard component both have
arity2; for r>=3 the bridge variant remains within arity r. This generalization
is the same proof and remains an architecture result only.
