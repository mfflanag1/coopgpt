CLASS AND CLAIM
For each report profile b, let mu_b be ANY finite nonnegative measure on[0,1],
common to all actual owners, and define
 y_i(b)=int m_i(p;b) dmu_b(p),
 m_i(p;b)=E_R[F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)],
with each other actual owner included independently with probability p.
The measure may depend arbitrarily on all reports, labels, technology, owner
count and s. It may have any support, including atoms at0 and1. No regularity,
continuity, anonymity or fixed coefficients are assumed.

If this rule covers every coalition and is TCWM for every coalition of size
at most integer s>=1, then a uniform funding factor over finite arity2 models
must satisfy
                         K(s)>=H_s/2,                    (1)
where H_s=sum_{m=1}^s1/m. The construction has O(s^2) fixed actual owners.
The finite inequality is K>=H_s/[2(1+s/L)] for anchor size L>0.

ONE FIXED TECHNOLOGY AND FIXED OWNERS
Take an additive anchor and one disjoint unit star for each m=1,...,s:
 F(c)=a+sum_{m=1}^s min(P_m,sum_{j=1}^m z_(m,j)).          (2)
There is one additive activity and sum_m m two-input star activities, all with
unit profit. Every column has arity at most2. The fixed actual owners are:
 - one anchor owner with a=L;
 - s center owners, center m holding P_m=1;
 - sum_m m distinct leaf owners, each holding one z_(m,j) in[0,1].
Thus n=1+s+s(s+1)/2. At the common final profile b*, all leaves report1.
For each m, define b^(m) by changing only leaves j=2,...,m of star m to0;
all other star leaves remain1, and all centers/anchor remain fixed. Every
comparison retains the exact same actual-owner set and eligibility. All
profiles have total production L+s because every star has at least one leaf.

LOW-PROFILE COVERAGE
At b^(m), the center and sole active leaf of star m each have binomial marginal
exactly p. Other components cancel pointwise from this marginal difference.
The coalition of these two owners has production1, so all-coalition coverage
requires
              2 int p dmu_(b^(m))(p)>=1.                 (3)
This coverage condition applies even at s=1 because coverage is for ALL
coalitions, whereas only TCWM coalition sizes are bounded.
Inactive leaf owners have zero marginal and zero payment in this architecture.

A DIRECT TCWM RESTORATION FOR EACH m
Let C_m contain all m leaf owners of star m, including the first unchanged
active leaf. Move from b^(m) to b* by restoring its other m-1 leaves from0 to1.
Every changing owner lies in C_m and every outsider remains fixed. Since m<=s,
TCWM applies. At b^(m), the coalition payment is int p dmu_(b^(m))>=1/2.
At b*, each leaf of star m has marginal
                    p(1-p)^(m-1),                       (4)
because the center must be included and all m-1 other leaves excluded.
Consequently, writing mu*=mu_(b*),
 q_m:=int p(1-p)^(m-1) dmu*(p) >=1/(2m)                 (5)
for EVERY m=1,...,s at the SAME final report profile and SAME measure mu*.
For m=1 this follows directly from coverage; no nontrivial restoration is needed.
This direct comparison avoids any need to assume continuity along a chain.

EXACT HARMONIC MASS BOUND
Sum(5) over m and use the finite geometric identity, valid also at endpoints:
 sum_{m=1}^s p(1-p)^(m-1)=1-(1-p)^s <=1.
Nonnegativity of mu* therefore gives
 mu*([0,1]) >=int[1-(1-p)^s]dmu*(p)
             =sum_m q_m >=H_s/2.                        (6)
The anchor's marginal is L at every p, including p=0. Its payment is exactly
L mu*([0,1]). Funding at b* bounds this by K(L+s), proving
                 K>=H_s/[2(1+s/L)].                     (7)
For a factor uniform over finite models, L can be arbitrarily large, yielding(1).
Alternatively L=s gives the completely finite bound K>=H_s/4. If individual
report upper bounds must be at most1, replace an integer-size anchor L by L
unit additive owners; their total payment is still L times the mixing mass.

WHY THIS IS AN ARCHITECTURE SEPARATION, NOT A GENERAL LOWER
The original disjoint-star family admits an explicit factor2 mechanism for ALL
nonnegative real reports and arbitrary mixed ownership, with TCWM for ALL
coalition sizes. For star m write P=sum_i P_i, Z=sum_i Z_i, F_m=min(P,Z), and
pay owner i
                 F_m(P_i/P+Z_i/Z),                      (8)
with the whole expression defined as0 when PZ=0. Additive anchor value is paid
to its actual contributors. Summing these payments gives at most2F.
Coverage: if P<=Z, the star coalition payment is P_C+P Z_C/Z>=P_C>=min(P_C,Z_C);
the other regime is symmetric. TCWM: when C restores its P supply, in P<=Z the
coalition sum is P_C+P Z_C/Z and increases; in P>=Z it is Z P_C/P+Z_C and its
P_C derivative is Z(P-P_C)/P^2>=0. Restoring Z is symmetric. Boundary values
are continuous. Summing over components preserves coverage and TCWM, including
owners contributing to multiple rows/components. Thus no unrestricted growing
factor is forced by this family; the logarithmic growth belongs to the GLOBAL
mixture architecture. Known sharper graph mechanisms are not needed for this
separation argument.

CONNECTED PUBLIC TECHNOLOGY VARIANT
Add one gate row q, held by a fixed owner reporting0 throughout these comparisons.
Add arity2 positive-profit activities {a,q} and {P_m,q} for every m. The public
resource/activity incidence graph is now connected. All bridges remain infeasible
at every compared profile, so every worth and marginal calculation above is
unchanged. A different measure per PUBLIC connected component therefore does not
evade the bound. Report-adaptive splitting by active support, owner-specific
weights or component-local measures are not excluded.

WHAT THE RESULT RULES OUT
Even allowing arbitrarily supported, fully report-dependent nonnegative global
mixtures cannot yield a constant funding factor as s grows. The one-star factor2
calibration in ADAPTIVE_MIXTURE_ESCAPE_CALIBRATION.txt is consistent: simultaneous
stars at every active-count scale force one measure to satisfy all constraints(5).
The theorem does not exclude O(s), O(s log s), or any other subquadratic global
mixture rule. It does not establish full OUT, which was not used. It does not
improve the campaign's unrestricted arity3 bound.
