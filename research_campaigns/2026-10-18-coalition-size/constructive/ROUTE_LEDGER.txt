R1. INDEPENDENT FRACTIONAL-OWNER MARGINALS: REJECTED AS UNCOMPENSATED RULE
Define m_i(b)=E[F(b_i+sum_{j!=i} U_j b_j)-F(sum_{j!=i} U_j b_j)],
with report-independent independent U_j uniform[0,1]. This leaves root64's
whole-owner class, is nonnegative and individually monotone. Via integration
in the i coordinate it is the common-price rule
 m_i=b_i dot E grad F(sum_j U_j b_j),
so it covers every coalition. This equality is valid at a.e. interior points
and can be obtained for packing F by finite polyhedral integration.

But TCWM fails exactly at arity2. Fixed owners 0,1,2 have reports
 b_0=(1,0), b_1=(0,x), b_2=(0,y); F(P,L)=min(P,L).
For x,y>=1, direct integration gives
 m_1=1/(6y), m_2=1/(6x).
Indeed for fixed P=u_0, m_1=E[(u_0-y U_2)_+]=u_0^2/(2y),
and E u_0^2=1/3. Thus restoring x from1 to1+delta holds m_1 fixed
but strictly reduces m_2. Coalition {1,2} (one unchanged member) loses
 delta/[6(1+delta)]. Fixed owners, same eligibility, all real reports.
Any positive fixed multiplier retains this violation.

The failure extends to independent fractional laws with an interval of
positive mass near0: an already individually saturated supplier gets no own
increment, while its unchanged competitor loses some marginal value.
This extension needs exact law hypotheses if promoted beyond this diagnosis.

R2. COMMON-PRICE GRADIENT SMOOTHING: EXACT DIFFERENTIAL DIAGNOSTIC
For smooth F, B(t)=sum_j t_j b_j, y_j=b_j dot E grad F(B(t)),
