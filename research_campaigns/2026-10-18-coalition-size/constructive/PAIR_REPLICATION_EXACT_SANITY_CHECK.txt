EXACT FINITE PAIR-REPLICATION SANITY CHECK FOR GLOBAL ADAPTIVE MIXTURES
2026-10-18 21:58 UTC. Architecture result; no unrestricted claim.

This small argument independently checks the replication idea without sentinels
or measure compactness. It applies for every s>=2, at arity3.

Use a fixed unit hard min3 component, N disjoint two-leaf unit stars, and an
additive anchor of value A. In each star a center and one leaf report1; a second
leaf initially reports0. Restore the second leaves one at a time. All N stars
have production1 at all profiles, so total production stays A+N+1.
Let mu_t be the shared nonnegative mixing measure after t restorations. Write
 a_t=int p dmu_t, b_t=int p^2 dmu_t, h_t=int p(1-p)dmu_t=a_t-b_t.
Hard min3 coverage gives b_t>=1/3 at every profile.

For the freshly activated two-leaf star, coalition C contains its unchanged
first leaf and restoring second leaf. Its low payment is a_(t-1), and its high
payment is2h_t. Therefore pair TCWM gives
 2h_t>=a_(t-1)=h_(t-1)+b_(t-1)>=h_(t-1)+1/3.
Starting from h_0>=0, induction yields
               h_N>=(1/3)(1-2^(-N)).
Since p(1-p)<=1/4, beta_N=mu_N([0,1])>=4h_N. The anchor's actual payment A beta_N
is at most K(A+N+1), proving the exact finite necessary inequality
 K >= [(4/3)(1-2^(-N))]/[1+(N+1)/A].
Letting N and then A/(N+1) grow yields K>=4/3, the r3,s2 target s^2/3.
Every comparison retains the same3N+4 actual owners. The hard component and
old leaves are unchanged, and the zero-report restoring leaves are actual
owners throughout. No requirement on report-to-measure continuity is used.

This does not prove the s-dependent quadratic theorem alone. It checks that
adaptive parameters can be constrained by sequential activations of fresh
components at fixed total production, and it gives an exact finite certificate
independent of the sentinel/compactness argument.
