The script enumerates exact vertices and verifies a dual normal-cone witness

and assignment tests are run for every s=1,...,n, including passive owners.

PARTITION EXHAUSTIVENESS
Each candidate uses ALL full-dimensional chambers of a supplied finite
hyperplane arrangement in the unit box. Sign patterns are exhaustively
enumerated; infeasible and lower-dimensional pieces are discarded using exact
vertex enumeration and affine rank. The remaining closures cover the box:
away from finitely many hyperplanes, an interior point belongs to a unique
full-dimensional chamber; such points are dense in the box; by finiteness,
every limit point lies in at least one chamber closure. This proves all-report
coverage of the partition. It is separate from production coalition coverage.

A. VALID UNIVERSAL RULE, WITH GENUINELY MIXED OWNERS
Technology: one unit-profit activity requiring one unit of each of two rows.
Owners1 and2 each may report either resource independently in [0,1].
Report order is (b11,b12,b21,b22); F(total)=min(b11+b21,b12+b22).
Partition: the two half-boxes separated by
 b11+b21=b12+b22.
Both owners receive F(total). Exact result:
 2 cells,22 cell-vertex occurrences,6 all-coalition coverage LPs,
 22 production LPs for funding vertices.
Nonnegativity, shared-boundary agreement, all-coalition coverage, factor2
funding, and local/full global OUT all pass. The local checks include s1
and s2, and directions where one mixed owner supplies both inputs.

The general calibration proof is independent of computation:
Y_C=|C|F(B)>=F(B_C), total=nF(B), and
Delta Y_C=|C|[F(B+d)-F(B)]>=|C|F(d)>=F(d)
for every nonempty coalition, by packing superadditivity. The result is a
known universal mechanism calibration, not an improved bound.

B. CONTINUOUS SWITCHING CORE SELECTOR: LOCAL INCENTIVES FAIL
Now keep two pure actual owners: owner1 supplies row1, owner2 row2, each in[0,1].
Set
 y=(b1,0)                         if b1<=b2;
 y=(2b2-b1,b1-b2)                 if b2<=b1<=2b2;
 y=(0,b2)                         if b1>=2b2.
The formulas agree at both interfaces, are nonnegative, and sum exactly to
F=min(b1,b2). Every singleton has standalone worth0, so all-coalition coverage
and factor1 funding hold for all real reports.
In the middle cell partial y1/partial b1=-1. Its singleton restoration violates
TCWM. Exactly:
 (b1,b2)=(3/4,1/2) -> (1,1/2),
 y1=1/4 ->0.
The checker confirms continuity, nonnegativity, all coverage and funding, but
rejects local TCWM and hence full OUT. This is a continuous homogeneous PL
candidate whose failure cannot be blamed on boundary admission.

C. DISCONTINUOUS SWITCHING CORE SELECTOR: INTERNAL BOUNDARY FAILS
On the same pure-owner institution define y=(b1,0) if b1<=b2, and y=(0,b2)
otherwise. Nonnegativity, all coverage and factor1 funding again hold.
Each OPEN cell passes every local TCWM and OUT test: within a cell the paid
owner's derivative is1 and the other owner's payment is locally0. Yet at
(t,t), t>0, the two formulas have limits (t,0) and (0,t), so shared-boundary
consistency fails. An actual finite singleton restoration is
 (1/4,1/2) -> (3/4,1/2), with y1=1/4 ->0.
The checker labels the within-cell incentive fields LOCAL; the FULL certificate
is false because continuity fails. A local pass is never reported as global
OUT for this rule.
