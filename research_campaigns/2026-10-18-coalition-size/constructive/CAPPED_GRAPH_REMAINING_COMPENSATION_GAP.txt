CAPPED GRAPH ROUTE: PRECISE REMAINING COMPENSATION GAP
2026-10-18. Bounded route sealed without a surviving subquadratic candidate.

AVAILABLE INGREDIENT, ALREADY IMPLICIT IN THE EARLIER GRAPH PROOF
For unit-profit graph production G, utilization payments Z_i satisfy
 sum_i Z_i=2G, all-coalition coverage, and in fact
   Z_C(b')-Z_C(b)>=G(sum b'_i)-G(sum b_i)                  (1)
whenever only members of C restore reports. This is a direct strengthening of
OLD/root/GRAPH_OUTSIDE_TWO_CANDIDATE.txt, not a newly discovered construction.
On each fixed Huber pattern the old derivative dominates z.d, where z is the
rounded optimal production dual. Here z.d is exactly the derivative of G.
Integrate across the same finitely many seams. At zero profiles the old positive-
capacity regularization bounds the change in G(c+epsilon*1), whose endpoint
values converge to G(c). This proves (1) with the same boundary argument.

A COVERING BASE RULE AND THE EXACT DEFICIT
For F(P,c)=min(P,G(c)), arbitrary mixed actual owners, define
  a_i=P_i min(1,G/P)+min(1,P/G) Z_i,                     (2)
with the zero conventions making payments vanish when F=0. This is nonnegative,
covers every coalition, and sums to3F. Coverage when P<=G follows from the
cap shares P_i alone; when P>=G it follows from graph payments Z_i alone.
Its cap-restoration comparisons are TCWM. In the uncapped regime, its graph-
restoration comparisons are TCWM. The only unresolved increments lie in P<G.
There, for a graph-resource restoration belonging to an owner j in C,
  d a_C=(P/G)[dZ_C-(Z_C/G)dG].                           (3)
The first term has dZ_C>=dG by (1), but Z_C/G may approach2. Thus (3) can be
negative even for a singleton coalition. This is exactly the old mixed-owner
capped-utilization obstruction, not a new failure.

SUFFICIENT COMPENSATION LEMMA THAT WOULD GIVE A SPECIAL-CLASS O(s) UPPER
One would need nonnegative owner reserves R_i on all real profiles such that:
 (i) sum_i R_i<=c*s*F for an absolute c;
 (ii) the sums R_C do not decrease under cap restorations, and do not destroy
      TCWM of (2) on uncapped graph restorations;
 (iii) on each capped graph coordinate path changed by j in C, |C|<=s,
      dR_C >= (P/G)[(Z_C/G)dG-dZ_C];                     (4)
 (iv) the combined payments a_i+R_i have valid seams/zero-profile limits.
Then integrating (4) coordinatewise would give a (3+c*s) TCWM mechanism for
this common-cap graph family. All-coalition coverage would be inherited from
(2). Full OUT would require another argument. No reduction from arbitrary
arity-three production to this class has been proved.

WHY THIS IS STILL A GAP, NOT A CANDIDATE RESULT
The reserves in (4) have no established formula or budget proof. Writing down
(4) alone does not count as progress on the original existence question. Simple
shared scalar multipliers are blocked by the old capped-utilization theorem.
An attempted owner-diversification term based on distributions of Z_i among
graph components also fails when one unchanged protected owner owns both ends
of many passive components and the changing owner activates one endpoint of a
new component. The restorer's initial gain cannot cover the normalization loss
of that large passive portfolio. No claim of a universal impossibility for
owner-dependent reserves follows from these failed formulas.

DISPOSITION
Stop the unrestricted constructive claim here: no O(s), no o(s²), and no new
general upper is established in this lane. CAPPED_GRAPH_MARGINAL_NEAR_MISS.txt
is a fully specified surviving TCWM construction, but has an exact Θ(s²) budget
obstruction and is only for the common-cap graph family. Its exact checker and
all preceding architecture barriers are preserved. Reopen the compensation
branch only with a concrete reserve formula or a nontrivial inequality implying
(i)--(iv); do not relabel the obligations themselves as a structural theorem.
