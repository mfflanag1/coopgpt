Hiroshi Hirai, On uncrossing games for skew-supermodular functions, Journal of the Operations Research Society of Japan 59(2), 218–223 (2016).
Original full PDF read, especially section 3:
https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jorsj/59/2/59_218/_pdf
The result concerns skew-supermodular cut-covering LPs. Its uncrossing operation relies on a value inequality and the cut-incidence system. Our owner-incidence dual is different; no theorem from that paper is imported as a guarantee. At q in the new example, v(AB)+v(AD)=3 exceeds v(A)+v(ABD)=2 and v(B)+v(D)=1. Thus even the analogous value-side inequality fails. Hirai uses a bipartition crossing convention; our theorem explicitly uses ordinary set laminarity (disjoint or nested), not an unstated identification of the two conventions.
