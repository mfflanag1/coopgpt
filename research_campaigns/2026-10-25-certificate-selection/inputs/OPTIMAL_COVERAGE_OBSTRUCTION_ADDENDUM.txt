CLAIM
The obstruction can be witnessed by an OPTIMAL full-OUT dual certificate with
L=D=6m and sigma identically zero. It still forces every replacement retaining

The same conclusion has a variant in which every positive coverage row is
a singleton row. Every original owner remains productive and owns three rows.

CONSTRUCTION
Keep all original comparison rows and nu_z=1. Retain the original coverage
lambda_(a_g,{u_g})=1/2. Add
    lambda_(b_g,{v_g})=1/2 for every g;
    lambda_(c_g,{w_g})=1   for every g;

All these are valid coverage rows; coverage coalitions have no protection-size

FEASIBILITY AND ZERO SLACK
In the base certificate, the only nonzero full coefficients away from z were
g_(b_g,v_g)=-1/2 and g_(c_g,w_g)=-1. The first two added coverage families
cancel those coefficients exactly. Thus g_(p,i)=0 for every owner at every
nonterminal selected profile.

At z the old coefficients on each owner triple were (1/2,1/2,1).
The last added row contributes (1/2,1/2,0), giving g_(z,i)=1 for every
actual owner. Since nu_z=1 and all other budgets are zero, every balance
has equality and sigma_(p,i)=nu_p-g_(p,i)=0 everywhere.
This verifies feasibility without inserting any new budget or comparison.

OBJECTIVE AND OPTIMALITY
The b_g singleton is worth one, the c_g singleton is worth one, and
F(b_(U union V)(z))=4m. The additional objective is therefore
    (1/2)m + m + (1/2)(4m) = (7/2)m.
Adding this to the old L=(5/2)m gives L=6m=D.
Equivalently, coverage now contributes 4m and comparisons contribute 2m.

Section 2 of the base proof supplies an admissible all-profile payment rule
with factor one on every original coordinatewise real box. Weak duality
therefore bounds every feasible finite dual ratio by one. The augmented
certificate attains that ratio, so it is optimal on its finite profile set
and attains the full-domain optimal factor. Dividing all its coefficients by
6m produces a normalized D=1, L=1 optimum if desired.

For an additional conservation check, each actual owner has total coverage
incidence one: u_g has one half at a_g and one half at z; v_g has one half
at b_g and one half at z; w_g has one at c_g. The resource residual in the
base proof is now represented entirely by coverage, with no slack term.

ALL-SINGLETON VARIANT

replace the single z coverage row by

Thus feasibility, zero slack, L=D, and optimality all remain valid. This
variant has only singleton positive coverage rows and still has the same

REPAIR LOWER BOUND AND ITS LIMIT
The original marked rows at a_g remain present with coefficient one half.
The disjoint-neighborhood lemma from the base proof uses only those rows.
Consequently every allowed one-layer sum retaining those marked rows costs
    D'>=(m/2)(6m-3),
even if all other augmented coverage rows are removed, altered, or added.
The displayed ratio follows from D=6m. In particular, restricting a
coverage-preserving normalization to optimal or zero-slack input certificates
does not rescue a bound depending only on s.

This statement concerns a chosen optimal certificate. It does not rule out
selecting a DIFFERENT optimal certificate before choosing which coverage to
retain. Indeed grand-coalition coverage at z with coefficient one and nu_z=1,
with no comparisons, is already an optimal one-layer certificate in this
same institution. Therefore no unrestricted funding lower bound, no lower
bound for every possible choice of optimal certificate, and no failure of
the underlying K=1 payment rule is asserted.
