ONE RESTORATION LAYER CANNOT FORCE LARGE PRICE-BASE FUNDING
19 October 2026. Structural consequence of retained primal-dual charging.
No unrestricted all-real upper, new rank inequality, or novelty claim.

STATEMENT
Fix any finite nonnegative packing technology of arity at most r, fixed
actual owners and report boxes, with finite production values as in the
campaign contract. Let Q be ANY finite set of legal real profiles.
Here a nontrivial comparison means a nonzero restored bundle delta (distinct
report profiles), even when F(delta)=0. Zero-restoration comparisons are
self-loops and impose only equality with themselves. Suppose ALL included
nontrivial legal protected comparisons admit a GLOBAL partition
Q=L disjointunion H disjointunion I with every comparison going from L to H;
I is isolated. Equivalently, no profile is both a source and a sink of
nontrivial comparisons. All unchanged protected members remain included.

On this selected profile system there are nonnegative payments which cover
EVERY coalition, satisfy full OUT on EVERY included comparison, dominate some
efficient nonnegative price allocation at every profile, and cost at most
r F(total) at every profile. Thus every lower certificate confined to such
a system has ratio at most r, even for the extra price-base architecture.
Owner, resource, activity and profile counts are arbitrary; s is arbitrary.

This is a finite-profile obstruction to a proposed proof architecture, not a
rule on every report in the original box. At arity3 its bound is3. It says
that one-layer price-collapse stars cannot establish fixed-s divergence or
quadratic-order lower bounds. Multiple successive layers remain open.

PROOF
At each profile choose an optimal nonnegative primal x and dual p. Let B be
aggregate resources, V=F(B), and define for every ACTUAL owner i
 u_i=p.b_i,
 h_i=sum_e pi_e x_e sum_(a in supp e) b_(i,a)/B_a,
with0/0 set to0. The accepted charging lemma proves
 0<=u_i<=h_i, sum_i u_i=V, sum_i h_i<=rV,                 (1)
 h(C)>=V-F(B-b_C) for every coalition C.                 (2)
For completeness, complementary slackness gives
 p_a B_a=p_a sum_e A_(a,e)x_e <=sum_(e using a)pi_e x_e;
multiply by b_(i,a)/B_a and sum to obtain h_i>=u_i.
To prove(2), reduce each x_e by the largest resource fraction b_Ca/B_a on
that activity; the remainder is feasible outside C. Bounding the maximum
fraction by its sum yields the loss bound h(C). Zero-capacity rows cannot
be used by positive production, so these formulas include zeros.

Pay y=u on L and I, and y=h on H. Nonnegativity, price domination and funding
follow from(1). A feasible dual covers every coalition, u(C)>=F(b_C), so
both assignments cover every coalition, without a coalition-size truncation.

For any legal comparison l to h protecting C, let O be the common outsider
bundle and delta the restored bundle. Dual feasibility and optimality give
 u_l(C)=F(B_l)-u_l(outside)<=F(B_l)-F(O).
Packing superadditivity gives F(B_h)>=F(B_l)+F(delta). Hence
 u_l(C)+F(delta)<=F(B_h)-F(O)<=h_h(C),
where the last step is(2) at h. These are exactly the finite full-OUT
inequalities for the selected payments. No individual member's comparison
has been removed or given a separate flow multiplier. At V=0 all u,h vanish.

LIMIT OF THE CONSEQUENCE
The accepted PER-COALITION source-sink forest normal form does not satisfy
the GLOBAL hypothesis above: a profile can receive C1 comparisons and emit
C2 comparisons. Paying that profile h for incoming obligations can create
outgoing reserve obligations; the proof only controls outgoing u. This is
the unresolved global term, not a permissible cancellation. It is precisely
why this structural lemma does not upgrade the general bound to r.
