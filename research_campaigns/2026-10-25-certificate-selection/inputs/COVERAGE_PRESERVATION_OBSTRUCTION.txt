COVERAGE-PRESERVING ONE-LAYER REPAIR HAS UNBOUNDED COST AT s=2
25 October 2026. Conventional proof; base result received opposite-model audit and root verification. See reviews/OBSTRUCTION_DISPOSITION.txt.
No novelty claim, no unrestricted funding lower bound, and no O(s) upper bound.

RESULT
For every integer m>=1 there is one fixed institution with 3m actual owners,
an exactly arity-three packing technology, full original real report boxes,
and a feasible full-OUT finite certificate with D=6m and L=5m/2, such that
EVERY finite sum of feasible global one-layer certificates at the SAME protection
limit s=2 retaining its
coverage array has aggregate budget
    D' >= (m/2)(6m-3),
    D'/D >= m/2-1/4 >= m/4.
The bound remains true if the replacement merely retains the original coverage
as a coefficientwise subarray and adds other nonnegative coverage rows.
All original comparison coefficients, COMPARISON coalition labels, budget coefficients,
nonnegativity slacks, and profile support may change. New legal profiles and
arbitrarily many pieces are allowed. The lower bound does not even require
the replacement objective to be at least L; hence it also applies with that
requirement. Budget coefficients at a common profile in different pieces are
all counted.

The institution itself has all-profile funding factor exactly ONE. Every
actual owner is strictly productive at every selected profile. Thus the result
rejects preservation of coverage as a universally bounded layer-repair
architecture; it is not a lower bound for the underlying funding problem.

1. A GENERAL DISJOINT-NEIGHBORHOOD CHARGING LEMMA

Here feasible means the homogeneous cone lambda,mu,nu>=0 and g<=nu, without
a D<=1 normalization. Within EACH global one-layer piece, the sets of tails
and heads of positive nontrivial comparisons are disjoint. Every replacement
comparison obeys the SAME protection limit s and original report boxes.
Coverage labels and coefficients of the retained rows are preserved as stated;
comparison labels may be recombined.

Keep one fixed actual-owner institution and protection limit s. For a legal
profile a let H_s(a) contain all DISTINCT legal heads h of admissible protected
comparisons a->h with |C|<=s, and put A_s(a)={a} union H_s(a).
Suppose marked coverage rows (a_j,S_j) have prescribed positive coefficients
eta_j, choose an actual owner i_j in each nonempty S_j, and suppose the sets
A_s(a_j) are pairwise disjoint.

Then any finite sum of feasible global one-layer dual certificates whose
aggregate coverage coefficient on each marked row is at least eta_j has
    D' >= sum_j eta_j f_(a_j).                         (1)

Proof. Consider one piece and abbreviate its coefficients by lambda,mu,nu.
At a_j its balance for i_j implies
    lambda_(a_j,S_j) <= nu_(a_j) + outgoing_(a_j,i_j),
because all other coverage and incoming terms are nonnegative.
For each positive comparison from a_j to h, global one-layer support makes h
a head with NO outgoing comparison. Hence
    nu_h >= incoming_(h,i_j)
           >= sum_(e:a_j->h, i_j in C_e) mu_e.
This remains true with other incoming rows, other coverage, and freely chosen
slacks. Summing over such heads proves
    lambda_(a_j,S_j) <= sum_(q in A_s(a_j)) nu_q.
Every q in this set is coordinatewise at least a_j, so monotonicity gives
f_q>=f_(a_j). Consequently
    lambda_(a_j,S_j) f_(a_j)
       <= sum_(q in A_s(a_j)) nu_q f_q.
The pairwise disjoint sets ensure no budget is charged to two marked rows
within a piece. Sum over j and over pieces. All sums actually used are finite,
even though the legal profile domain is a continuum. Nonnegative coefficients
and aggregate retention of marked rows yield (1). Trivial self-comparisons
have zero incidence and objective and may be deleted. QED.

A marked source paid locally is covered by nu_(a_j) in this argument; it need
not emit any comparison. The argument neither treats owner coefficients as
independent coalition weights nor overlooks multiple incoming rows.

2. EXACTLY ARITY-THREE, MULTIROW-OWNER INSTITUTION

There are 3m ACTUAL owners, partitioned into triples G_g=(u_g,v_g,w_g),
1<=g<=m. Owner i owns three private resource rows (i,1),(i,2),(i,3).
Its original legal report box is [0,2]^3 on those rows and zero on all other
owners' rows. This is fixed eligibility, not replacement of one owner by three
strategic row-owners.

Activity i consumes one unit of EACH of its owner's three private rows and
earns one unit. Thus every activity has arity exactly three, and
    F(c)=sum_i min(c_(i,1),c_(i,2),c_(i,3)).
For a vector x in [0,2]^(3m), let p(x) be the selected diagonal profile where
owner i reports (x_i,x_i,x_i) on its three rows. Then
    f_(p(x))=sum_i x_i,
    F(b_S(p(x)))=sum_(i in S) x_i.
All other coordinatewise real reports in the original boxes remain legal.

On the ENTIRE report domain, pay
    y_i(p)=min_r b_(i,r)(p).
Coverage holds with equality for every coalition, and total payment is F(B_p).
For a restoration l->h,
    min_r b_(i,r)(h)-min_r b_(i,r)(l)
       >= min_r [b_(i,r)(h)-b_(i,r)(l)].
Summing this over the protected coalition, including unchanged members, gives
full OUT because F(delta) is the sum of those private-row minima. Thus K=1
works at all profiles. Grand-coalition coverage at positive-output profiles
requires K>=1, so the true all-profile factor is exactly one.

3. THE ORIGINAL FINITE CERTIFICATE

Let z be the profile with x_i=2 for every owner. For each group g define:
  a_g: x_(u_g)=x_(v_g)=x_(w_g)=1, all other x_i=2;
  b_g: x_(u_g)=2, x_(v_g)=x_(w_g)=1, all other x_i=2;
  c_g: x_(u_g)=x_(v_g)=2, x_(w_g)=1, all other x_i=2.
All these are complete profiles of the same actual owners. The only coverage
rows are
    lambda_(a_g,{u_g})=1/2.
The only budget is nu_z=1. For every g use precisely these comparison rows:
    a_g -> b_g, C={u_g},       mu=1/2;
    b_g -> c_g, C={u_g,v_g},   mu=1/2;
    c_g -> z,   C={u_g,w_g},   mu=1/2;
    c_g -> z,   C={v_g,w_g},   mu=1/2.
Thus s=2. Every row restores exactly one actual owner's three coordinates
from (1,1,1) to (2,2,2), so its OUT right side is one. Protected passive
members are retained explicitly, and each row has one shared whole-coalition
multiplier.

The full coefficients g=lambda-incidence+incoming-outgoing are:
  at a_g: zero for every owner;
  at b_g: -1/2 for v_g, zero for every other owner;
  at c_g: -1 for w_g, zero for every other owner;
  at z: (1/2,1/2,1) on each group (u_g,v_g,w_g).
Consequently g<=nu everywhere. The internal negative coefficients are legal
nonnegativity slacks. No positive budget is hidden at an internal profile.

Each covered singleton has value one. Therefore
    L_cov=m/2, L_OUT-comparisons=2m, L=5m/2,
    D=F(B_z)=6m, L/D=5/12.
Every actual owner contributes either one or two to production at every
selected profile; deleting that owner strictly reduces grand-coalition value.
Every private resource row is used by its arity-three activity.

The resource identity can also be read groupwise, in each of an owner's
three resource rows. Budget capacity is (2,2,2), weighted restored capacity
is (1/2,1/2,1), and the residual is (3/2,3/2,1).
Coverage supplies (1/2,0,0); internal slacks supply (0,1/2,1);
the final-profile slacks supply (1,1,0). These add to the same residual.
The triples in this paragraph index owners u_g,v_g,w_g; each entry repeats
on that owner's three resource rows.

4. ALL LEGAL ONE-STEP NEIGHBORHOODS ARE DISJOINT

For distinct groups g,h, the coordinatewise join of the complete profiles
a_g and a_h is z: each owner's three coordinates are already two in at least
one source. Every legal profile is coordinatewise at most z, by the original
boxes. Therefore any common coordinatewise upper profile of a_g,a_h must be z.

But a_g->z changes ALL THREE actual owners in G_g. It is not admissible at
s=2, irrespective of coalition labels or passive membership. Nor is a_g=z.
It follows that
    A_2(a_g) intersection A_2(a_h) is empty for g!=h.
This proof applies to every coordinatewise real profile in the legal boxes,
not just to the selected diagonal profiles or a finite grid.

Apply (1) with marked rows (a_g,{u_g}), eta_g=1/2. Since
f_(a_g)=6m-3, every coverage-retaining layer sum costs
    D'>=(m/2)(6m-3).
Dividing by the original D=6m proves D'/D>=m/2-1/4. The bound holds before
asking for L'>=L. In particular no finite function depending only on s can
bound this repair cost, even at fixed s=2 and fixed arity three.

5. OPTIONAL FINITE UPPER WITNESS FOR THIS RESTRICTED REPAIR PROBLEM

This is not an impossibility of all coverage-retaining replacements. Put each
original singleton coverage row into its own zero-comparison piece with
nu_(a_g)=1/2. These pieces cost exactly (m/2)(6m-3) and have total objective m/2.

To restore the missing objective 2m, add a zero-coverage one-layer piece:
take the all-zero legal profile o, let h have one chosen actual owner report
(2,2,2) and every other owner report zero, and use o->h protected by that
singleton with weight m. Give h budget weight m. This feasible piece has
objective 2m and budget 2m, with no new owner or altered report box.
The aggregate coverage array is EXACTLY the original array and L'=L. Hence
if J_lambda(L) is the restricted repair infimum for this example,
    m/2-1/4 <= J_lambda(L)/D <= m/2+1/12.
This establishes linear growth in the number of groups within a bounded
additive error; no exact optimizer is claimed.

6. SCOPE AND VERIFICATION

The general replacement guard allowing coverage coefficients to CHANGE is
unaffected: the present institution has true K=1, and no unrestricted lower
bound follows. The obstruction is to preserving even just the original
positive coverage rows during reduction to feasible global one-layer pieces.
Allowing coverage to be weakened, removed, or recombined is a necessary
freedom for a universal cost bound using this architecture. Whether an
architecture with that freedom gives a subquadratic factor remains unresolved.

The construction already allows budget relocation, free nonnegative slacks,
coalition-label recombination, new legal profiles, arbitrary finite layer
sums, and multirow actual owners. It uses growing productive owner/resource
counts as allowed by the original contract. Its obstruction is not raw arc
mass, per-label divergence, zero-valued coverage, zero-report original owners,
or a bounded fixture.

Worker exact Fraction checks at m=1,2,3,7,20 verified all shared-owner balances,
legality, positivity, L,D, the resource identity, joins, and both repair-ratio
formulas. Those numerical checks support bookkeeping; sections 1 and 4 are the
symbolic proofs covering arbitrary m and all real legal report profiles.
The base proof was independently reviewed by Fable5.1 and checked by the root.
The review found no invalidating error and requested explicit one-layer and
same-protection-limit definitions, now added. The later optimal-certificate
strengthening is separate from this reviewed base proof.
