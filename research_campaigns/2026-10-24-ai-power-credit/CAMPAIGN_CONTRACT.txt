Question
When do lender rights over complementary power and compute assets increase
financeable cash flow, and when does bundling those rights spread distress?

Evidence classes
1. Descriptive contract mapping from freely accessible originals.
2. A conditional theoretical model, with classical calibration and exact proofs.
3. A priority comparison. No causal or return-prediction claim in this campaign.

Initial object
One historical CoreWeave financing, preferably the July 28, 2025 DDTL3.0 credit
agreement, related guarantee and public amendments. Freeze the actual contract
version in the map; do not call historical clauses current terms. Financial
reporting as of December31,2025 is context, not a complete site-level rights map.

Gate1: first90minutes maximum
Find borrower/guarantor/creditor identities, collateral, cash control, assignment,
physical access, power and customer-contract continuity, termination and remedies.
Record unknowns and redactions. If the operating-rights chain cannot be verified,
stop empirical/issuer-specific conclusions. A conditional abstract model may
continue only if it has a distinct mathematical target and is labeled as such.

Gate2: next90minutes maximum
Read closest primary theory: incomplete contracts/pledgeability, complementary
assets and ownership, multiple creditors/coordination, project finance and legal
partitioning. State a difference beyond labels or number of owners. Stop a route
if a known theorem or elementary reformulation already settles it.

Gate3: three-hour mathematical attempt maximum
Minimal financing model: date0investment, date1shock/control or renegotiation,
date2operating cash or salvage. Explicit utility, participation, budget, actual
owners, legal deviations, information, timing and enforcement. Prove either a
general implication, a new structural lemma with consequence, or an exact
obstruction. Calibrations and bounded examples are useful but not promotion gates.

Gate4: final one-to-two-hour independent review maximum
Check proofs, counterexamples and source comparisons; retain failed suggestions.
No finding is promoted from model opinion alone. Outcome may be source limitation,
classical reduction, corrected theorem, or rigorously isolated unresolved problem.

Hard no-go ledger at launch
- Our old production coverage y(N)>=F cannot fund separate creditor D>0 from the
  same F under y(N)+D<=F. Creditors/rights/residuals need an actual new model.
- LP-dual core allocations, minimum-production bottlenecks and two-party hold-up
  are established calibrations, not novelty.
- Contractual inability to revoke a right is not voluntary resource underreporting.
- Parent recourse is not site operation; security over receivables is not power.
- More feasible control options with identical costs trivially cannot lower an
  optimizing lender's value. Any bundling harm needs an explicit lost option,
  liability, cost or equilibrium incentive channel.
- No company credit valuation from missing rights, synthetic recoveries or spreads.

Deliverables
Contract/source manifest; exact known/unknown map; literature comparison;
model/proof note; reproducible arithmetic where meaningful; review disposition;
browser-readable report with a dated continue/stop judgment and next decisive gate.
