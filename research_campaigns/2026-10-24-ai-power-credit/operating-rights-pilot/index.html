What this run established

Keep AI/power credit as the application lane. Do not promote the present model to a new paper yet. The public documents reveal how control, operating rights and cash support are packaged. They do not yet verify one complete transition for the cash-flow strategy being tested, or a distinct general financing result.

This follows the first research gate. We obtained the full closest paper, traced an actual tenant assignment into its equipment financing, and checked a supplier guarantee against a companion lease that the first delegated extraction had missed.

The productive question is now precise: which party can choose a replacement, what continuing services follow that choice, and what cash must be available before the choice can produce creditor receipts? A list of rights, an obligation to deliver an agreement, and an executable transition are different evidence.

There is also a necessary distinction between cash flows. Preserving landlord rent or premises resale value does not require preserving all of the original tenant’s AI-service contracts. Each proposed operating bundle must be evaluated against its own revenue strategy.

The closest paper is no longer an access gap

We obtained Alexander F. Wagner’s Leverage Beyond Debt, revised October 24, 2026. The 72-page PDF’s financing model and appendix were inspected, along with its discussion of default remedies. This supersedes the earlier abstract-only status for those sections; the database and stress calculations were not reproduced.

The model already connects supplier support to the right to recover a redeployment premium. With its stated constraints, the vendor’s optimal financing payoff is:

Π* = (μ − 1)t* + qΔ − Γ. q = probability of failure Δ = vendor’s asset-use premium over market value Γ = financing gap after customer incentive and participation constraints t* = optimal upfront receipt; μ = vendor’s value of upfront funds d = investment less customer equity and market liquidation value

Financing feasibility and vendor willingness are separate: the model requires Γ ≤ d for a feasible contract, then Π* ≥ 0 for acceptance. Its codebook already records assumption, purchase, sublease and sale rights. Crucially, the paper acknowledges that remedies are untested in insolvency and assigns them no recovery credit in its stress test. Our document gaps do not refute that treatment. B · A real amendment changes several rights together

The May 2025 Building 3 lease allocates infrastructure maintenance to the landlord and allows a utility-agreement assignment only with utility agreement. Transfer, non-disturbance and default provisions impose additional conditions; several terms and schedules are redacted or unavailable.

The March 30, 2026 assignment transfers the tenant position to CoreWeave Compute Acquisition Co. VIII, LLC. Section 6 deletes the utility-assignment provision and the permitted-transfer category while adding collateral assignment to a financial institution. Sections 3 and 15 contain apparently competing retention/release language; this record does not resolve it. The issuer’s filing describes the original tenant as released.

The springing guaranty identifies the same borrower and March 30 equipment financing. It links support for tenant obligations to specified events and includes requested assumption after lease rejection, subject to law. This supports tenant obligations to the landlord; it is not an automatic transfer of every operating input.

The matched DDTL4.0 agreement has operating-expense priority, power reserves and hedging provisions (§§2.20, 5.25). It references an MSA Direct Agreement, constrains enforcement notices by that agreement, and distinguishes initial collateral-access delivery from later reasonable-efforts requirements (§§4.01(g), 4.02(q), 5.19). Important costs and site labels are redacted. The full direct/access agreements were not established here.

Interpretation: the package cannot be ordered as simply “more rights” or “fewer rights.” Different permissions changed alongside support and financing terms. The data do not identify their separate effect on credit pricing.

C · The companion lease improves the evidence

NVIDIA reports entering agreements; the public exhibit is a form. Execution reported by an issuer is evidence of a transaction, while individual executed documents remain a separate evidentiary requirement.

The guaranty form makes assumption one of several elections. For either insolvency or monetary default, §12(a)(iii) also requires the landlord’s right and actual intent to terminate. Cure protection spans the lease, PPA and transmission agreement; verification involves redacted payments. Assumption procedures are redacted. The form therefore establishes conditional choices, not a quantified transition.

The PORTS lease form adds material links: §35(kk) recognizes conditional guarantor assumption; §4(c) covers landlord/affiliate electricity arrangements; §7 separates an operating-services agreement from lease duties. A DOE recognition provision is bracketed. Parties, alternatives and attachments remain unresolved. Direct ownership of a PPA is not the only possible route to continued power service.

Interpretation: lack of a demonstrated transfer of tenant-owned GPUs or downstream customer contracts does not establish failure of a premises-sale or reletting remedy. It limits the stronger claim that the same compute business can continue unchanged.

The simple extension collapses to the existing model

Suppose all permissions succeed with exogenous probability ρ conditional on failure, and usable surplus is reduced by a known amount c. Keep customer payoffs, creditor receipts and every financing constraint unchanged; impose no additional interim cash constraint. Then:

Δ_eff = ρ(Δ − c), assuming 0 ≤ c ≤ Δ. Π* = (μ − 1)t* + qΔ_eff − Γ.

This follows by taking the conditional expectation and substituting into the original objective. It is an elementary reduction, not a new theorem. Physical asset value and expected usable value remain distinct. A cost that must be funded before recovery, or a permission affected by contract choices, requires a different derivation.

There is a second elementary check. Let Eⱼ contain the operator/time pairs compatible with permission j. A common transition requires:

E_equipment ∩ E_premises ∩ E_services ∩ E_customer ≠ ∅, using only the permissions required by the chosen cash-flow strategy.

Every set can be nonempty while their intersection is empty. Conversely, a common pair establishes neither sufficient cash nor selection by the actual controller. These are diagnostic facts, not novelty claims or descriptions of the sampled issuers.

The complete note states the proof boundary. 144 exact arithmetic checks and three synthetic compatibility examples supplement it; no numerical inputs were fitted to issuers.

The next target that could change the conclusion

Test transition liquidity jointly with control. Can the entity that has permission to continue operation obtain the money for the intervening power, maintenance and cure payments—and can the contract induce the relevant parties to supply it?

This is a candidate research direction, not an established open problem. The documents give it institutional motivation; they do not establish priority. A known fixed cost that can simply be subtracted from recovery is insufficient. More rights fields alone are also insufficient.

  1. Freeze one cash-flow layer. For the strongest dated case, use CCAC VIII service revenues, rather than conflating them with Applied Digital rent or NVIDIA premises support. Keep the actual borrower and counterparties fixed.
  2. Require the missing documents or remain conditional. Match the MSA Direct Agreement, collateral-access instrument, lease/service terms and available cash to the same transition. Preserve redactions and do not infer compliance merely from a covenant.
  3. Apply a short theory gate. Compare the resulting liquidity-and-control problem with established financial contracting and the exact Wagner model. Continue only if an endogenous constraint survives reduction and produces a distinct bound or structural implication. Otherwise retain the contract case study and return mathematical effort to the existing coalition frontiers.

The current pilot therefore passes a useful institutional evidence gate, but does not pass the frozen promotion gate requiring a complete operating chain plus a distinct financing implication. No longer proof campaign has been silently launched.
