OPERATING-RIGHTS PILOT: MODEL COMPARISON AND STOPPING BOUNDARY
24 October 2026. Research comparison; no novelty or issuer-recovery claim.

SOURCE AND SCOPE
Alexander F. Wagner, Leverage Beyond Debt, SSRN 7627018, revised 24 October 2026, 72 PDF pages. Inspected model section 3.1-3.2, Appendix A.1-A.2 and empirical sections 5.1-5.2; key PDF page 12 visually checked. This is not a reproduction of the database or stress test. Prior abstract-only status is superseded for this narrow model comparison.

WHAT IS ALREADY IN THE SOURCE
The vendor can fund the customer through upfront cash, failure coverage and a success claim. Binary ex-ante asset access lets it realize a redeployment premium Delta. Access enters the objective, not the reduced financing constraints. The paper's Proposition 1 establishes optimal access for Delta>0 and vendor participation conditional on financing feasibility. Its empirical discussion explicitly treats default remedies as untested in insolvency, and its stress test gives no recovery credit to these rights. Our incomplete-document result therefore does not refute an empirical recovery assumption in that stress test.

Exact reduced program (paper equation 2):
max mu*t + p*w + q*(a*Delta-f)
s.t. t+p*w <= q*f-Gamma;
     P_M+f <= I_0-A+t;
     t <= mbar; 0 <= w <= R-k; f >= 0; a in {0,1}.
Here q=1-p, k=B/(p-p_L), Gamma=G_0+max(A+u-p*k,0),
G_0=I_0-A-p*(R-k)-q*P_M>0, d=I_0-A-P_M.
Maintain the paper's assumptions including 0<=p_L<p<1, B,A>0, P_M,Delta,mbar,u>=0, mu>=1, p*R+q*P_V>I_0, p_L*R+B+(1-p_L)*P_V<I_0, and P_V=P_M+Delta. The customer receives zero in failure; financier failure receipts cannot exceed the advance. Financing feasibility is Gamma<=d. This is conditional feasibility, distinct from the vendor accepting the transaction.

ELEMENTARY PARAMETER-SUBSTITUTION BOUNDARY (OUR DERIVATION; NOT A NEW THEOREM)
Let Z indicate simultaneous permission success conditional on project failure. Suppose Z is exogenous to every contract and effort choice, is risk-neutral valued, has Pr(Z=1|failure)=rho, and leaves every original constraint, financier failure receipt and customer payoff unchanged. Let c be a nonnegative reduction in the vendor's realized use surplus, netted only when access succeeds; it is not a separate cash outlay requiring bridge finance. Assume no vendor interim-liquidity or additional limited-liability constraint. Assume Delta>=c and access is optional. Replace the access payoff a*Delta with a*Z*(Delta-c).
Then E[a*Z*(Delta-c)|failure]=a*rho*(Delta-c). Set Delta_eff=rho*(Delta-c). The entire reduced optimization problem is identical to the original equation (2) with Delta replaced by Delta_eff. The feasible set remains unchanged and compact: f>=0 and the shortfall constraint imply t>=-d, while t<=mbar and p*(f+w)<=d-Gamma bound the remaining variables; its binding-constraint proof applies. Thus maximum vendor payoff is (mu-1)*t_star+q*Delta_eff-Gamma, with t_star maximizing t on the binding face when mu>1, or any feasible binding t when mu=1. Acceptance still requires this payoff >=0 and Gamma<=d.
Keep physical P_V=P_M+Delta. Delta_eff is an expected usable premium, not a redefinition of physical P_V. Do not assert that primitives obtained by mechanically replacing P_V with P_M+Delta_eff would satisfy the paper's original social-efficiency assumptions; this is an equivalence of the stated reduced programs. Costs paid upfront, before success, by other parties, or affecting budget/IC constraints need a separate derivation. Contract-dependent rho, endogenous refusal, multiple operators and private information are expressly outside this reduction. Their mere presence does not establish novelty.

JOINT RIGHTS: A LOGICAL CHECK, NOT A FINANCING THEOREM
For fixed public rights and no subsequent renegotiation, let E_j be the set of (operator, transition-time) pairs compatible with permission j. Necessary joint compatibility is intersection_j E_j nonempty. If these sets encode every relevant condition, joint compatibility is equivalent to that intersection being nonempty. Physical operation, working capital and enforceability remain additional conditions unless included explicitly. Separate nonempty E_j do not imply a nonempty intersection.
Synthetic identity example: equipment permits only Alpha; premises only Beta; each right exists but no one operator holds both. Synthetic timing example: the same operator can use equipment only at times 0-1 and premises only at times 2-3. These demonstrate elementary logical nonimplication; they describe none of the sampled issuers.
Even a nonempty intersection is only an existential statement. It does not show that a customer-controlled choice will select the creditor's preferred operator, or that all permitted choices support a promised cash floor.

RESEARCH VERDICT
A scalar access haircut or fixed known cure cost has not earned a new model. A per-contract rights list is also already part of Wagner's database. A source-linked, dated, cross-contract compatibility certificate would be a distinct measurement task, but priority remains unchecked. Promotion requires a complete chain and a nonseparable financing implication. No examined case is assigned a numerical rights score or recovery value.
