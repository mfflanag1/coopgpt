The bounded audit is complete

Park the quantitative case until its operating and cash-access inputs exist. We established neither a funding shortfall nor adequate successor funding. Unknown cash is not zero cash.

The specified revenue strategy is continued compute service at Building 3 under a hypothetical successor. Landlord rent protection and reletting the premises are different outcomes. No actual default, transition appointment or distressed event is asserted.

The predecessor transition-liquidity gate separated eventual value from the ability to pay on time. This audit checked whether that distinction can be measured in one transaction. It found important funding provisions and borrower-level disclosure, but not the full operator-specific event stream.

Three funding channels must stay separate

The March 30 credit agreement identifies CCAC VIII as borrower. Its reserve measure includes eligible account cash and undrawn reserve letters of credit. Section 6.12 permits specified L/C draws, including during a continuing Event of Default; proceeds enter the Liquidity Account. Directed mandatory draws are specified for nonrenewal and issuer-downgrade cases. An issued instrument and timely successor access remain unverified.

Cash already deposited → authorized account use Issued reserve L/C → qualifying draw → account deposit → authorized use New term borrowing → satisfied or waived conditions → permitted uses

Sections 2.20(e)/(g) provide reserve uses for specified shortfalls and costs. Sections 4.02 and 7.03 govern fresh loans separately. A general claim that support vanishes on default would therefore be unsupported.

The liquidity cure in §6.12 uses 15 Business Days. Section 7.03 separately states 30th/15th-day cure periods without that qualifier and suspends lenders’ obligation to extend new credit during its specified period. No actual deadline is computed. Section 7.04 has a distinct post-remedy scope.

Monthly payments use the fifteenth Business Day. The later liquidity requirement sums separate component maxima; it is not a maximum of their combined sum. Neither formula identifies actual dollar needs.

Two support instruments that answer different questions

The actual limited guarantee, Schedule 1(b), excludes specified physical-damage or waste liability arising from inadequate project funds or denied use of available funds, and does not require equity funding to prevent that damage. It supplies no general working-capital promise for this exercise. This narrow reading does not establish that other sponsor support is absent.

The Applied Digital subsequent-events disclosure separately describes Building 2’s $50m parent-lease L/C requirement and Building 3’s assignment to CCAC VIII with a springing guaranty. The $50m cannot be assigned to the Building 3 operating case. A requirement to issue is not an inspected issued instrument.

Creditors’ control rights, an existing parent service arrangement and an obligation to deliver executed documents do not themselves identify a successor’s authority. An agent’s power to direct payments also differs from the successor’s right to receive or spend those payments.

The independent document search timed out without a result. A focused root fallback retrieved no executed successor instrument; this is not evidence that none exists. No independent search corroboration is claimed. See the delivery and fallback record.

For a fixed implementable plan, list payments and usable receipts at their actual settlement deadlines, then check every cumulative balance. Count an L/C only when its proceeds become usable. Record reimbursement or financing repayment separately. Do not count the same reserve as both initial cash and new funding.

Balance at t = accessible initial cash + usable receipts through t + executable funding received through t − mandatory payments through t

Feasibility requires a nonnegative balance at every event. This accounting criterion is inherited from the previous gate; it is not a new theorem or an all-profile incentive mechanism. The reported deficiency remains unidentified, not zero.
