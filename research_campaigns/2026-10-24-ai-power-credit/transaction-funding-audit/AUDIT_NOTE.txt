RESERVE CHANNEL
CA 1.01: Funded Liquidity Reserve Amount sums eligible cash in the Liquidity
Account and the undrawn amount of any Liquidity Reserve L/C.
Definition Liquidity Reserve L/C: irrevocable standby, beneficiary Collateral
Agent for Secured Parties; non-recourse to Borrower and not secured on its
property or otherwise Borrower Indebtedness. This does NOT prove no other party
has reimbursement obligations or any L/C was actually issued.
CA 6.12(b)(i)(B) authorizes drawing for an uncured reserve deficit after the
Liquidity Cure Deadline, nonrenewal, continuing Event of Default, or specified
issuer downgrade/replacement failure. Mandatory directed drawing language is
specifically attached to (2) nonrenewal and (4) downgrade; do not call every
trigger an automatic/mandatory draw. Proceeds go to Liquidity Account.
CA 2.20(e)(ii),(iii) provides liquidity-account shortfall and remaining operating/
capital expense uses, subject to ordering and actual deposited funds.
CA 2.20(g)(ii) provides a route for excess power costs. No balance is disclosed.
Conclusion: a cash-only or 'all support disappears on default' characterization
would be unsupported. Authorized reserve support is not the same as proof of an
issued, adequately sized and timely drawable instrument for a successor.

TWO CURE REGIMES, NOT ONE
CA 6.12(b)(ii): Liquidity Cure Right; 15 Business Days after non-compliance;
written notice; cash equity into Liquidity Account; conditional default/remedy
treatment for this specific covenant. This must not automatically be assigned
the separate rule in 7.03(b).
CA 7.03: Cash Shortfall/DSCR equity cure. Respectively 30 days after Monthly
Payment Date or 15 days after financial-statement delivery due date. During the
specified period, 7.03(b) withholds obligation to make new credit extensions.
CA 4.02 opening: conditions can be satisfied OR waived as specified.
4.02(i) addresses Default/Event of Default/Cash Trap Event; 4.02(k) requires
reserves after the credit event. No conclusion that fresh loans are absolutely
legally impossible, or that a reserve L/C equals a term-loan Credit Extension.

TIMING
CA 1.01 Monthly Payment Date: 15th Business Day of following calendar month,
with the specified initial-funding commencement convention. Not 15th calendar day.
CA 2.20(b)(i): operating-expense route includes due or expected within 60 days,
with Management Agreement exclusion/reimbursement carveout.
CA 2.08: amortization timing depends on site/commitment/top-up events and schedule.
CA 1.01 Liquidity Requirement: before commitment termination, scheduled/expected
next-three-month interest, principal, ordinary swaps and operating costs;
afterwards sum of separate component maxima over three-month periods.
Do not silently replace sum-of-maxima with maximum-of-sum.
Base Case Model, scheduled amounts and applicable sites/timing are not supplied.
Monthly Date/Payment Date formulas alone cannot produce dollar cash requirements.

CONTROL/REMEDY SCOPE
CA 1.01 Management Agreement names Borrower and Parent (Services Access and
Services Agreement). It does not name an executed successor.
4.01(g) requires delivery of executed agreements, not publication of their contents.
CA 7.04's post-remedy waterfall applies within its stated scope to amounts on
Obligations; it does not establish that every operating account is confiscated.
An independent bounded search for actual successor/direct/access instruments is
pending. Until checked, no successor operating rights or cash-account powers are
