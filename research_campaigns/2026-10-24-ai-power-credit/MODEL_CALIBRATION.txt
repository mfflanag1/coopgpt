MODEL CALIBRATION AND NO-GO LEMMAS — DRAFT FOR INDEPENDENT REVIEW
24October2026. All examples synthetic. None are estimates or proposed novelty.

Common timing and bookkeeping
Date0competitive risk-neutral lenders advance I>=0. Date1state w is observed and
contractually permitted control/continuation decisions are made. Date2cash is
distributed. Exogenous gross required return is1+r. State probabilities are fixed.
The state space is finite. Pledgeable receipts must cover I(1+r) in expectation;
the nonnegative faceD is fixed throughout comparisons, and receipts are
capped atD. Operating transfers, salvage and guarantees are counted once.
This is a simplified benchmark. Default priorities, rights and decision-makers
must be inputs rather than consequences attributed to a physical min technology.

L1 — Additional optional control cannot harm an optimizing creditor
For each state let a finite nonempty A_K(w) be the permitted action set under
rights K. Let R(a,w)>=0 be net cash available to this lender under an unchanged
waterfall and unchanged liabilities/costs. Define P_K(w)=max_{a in A_K(w)}R(a,w)
and B(K)=E[min(D,P_K)]/(1+r), for1+r>0. B is the maximum advance supported by
this fixed-face contract, not optimized debt capacity across all contract designs.
If A_K subset A_H in every state and all
payoffs/selection authority remain unchanged, then P_K<=P_H pointwise and
B(K)<=B(H). Proof: maximize the same function over nested finite sets, then apply
the increasing cap and expectation. No independence assumptions needed. This
compares one creditor's receipts; other parties' receipts can have different signs.
Consequence: harm to this optimizing creditor must explicitly violate a hypothesis:
mandatory exercise, additional liabilities/cure costs, different controller,
changed incentives, renegotiation or changed feasible actions. Mere complementarity
and a larger option set cannot generate a negative sign.

L2 — Static multiowner participation reduces to a familiar cover program
Fix one state and m>=1 actual noncreditor owners. V>=0 is total cash available after
exogenous operating costs. b(S)>=0 is a public coalition participation requirement
for every nonempty S subset N; no private reports. Choose x_i>=0 and creditor
receipt q>=0 with x(N)+q=V and x(S)>=b(S). These are ONLY the declared static
owner-participation inequalities, not a full bargaining equilibrium or constraints
for coalitions containing the creditor. Let
 tau(b)=min sum_i x_i subject to x_i>=0,x(S)>=b(S).
Finite b makes this LP feasible and bounded below; an optimum exists. A feasible
allocation with q>=0 exists iff V>=tau(b). When feasible, max q=V-tau(b).
Single-state financing of I additionally requires the attainable receipt to cover
I(1+r). Under a debt capD,
max receipt is min(D,V-tau(b)), allowing surplus to be paid to owners.
The dual is
 tau(b)=max sum_S lambda_S b(S), lambda_S>=0,
                    sum_{S containing i}lambda_S<=1 for every i.
For additive b(S)=sum_{i in S}b_i, tau=sum_i b_i. More owners alone add no new
coordination effect. If b has a nonempty ordinary profit core with grand value
b(N), tau=b(N): grand participation is a lower bound and a core allocation attains
it. Nonnegative singleton values guarantee nonnegative coordinates.
These are classical LP/core facts, not a new debt theorem. Our prior production
payments cannot simply be stacked on top of creditor service from the sameV.

Calibration witness for genuinely incompatible participation constraints
m=3, b(singletons)=0, b(pairs)=1, b(N)=1. Primal x=(1/2,1/2,1/2) has total3/2;
dual lambda=1/2 on each pair has objective3/2 and each owner's load1. Therefore
tau=3/2. A budgetV=5/2 leaves q=1, not the naiveV-b(N)=3/2. This b has an empty
ordinary core and is not asserted to arise from the actual financing or a
superadditive production technology. It is a verifier calibration only.

L3 — Controller selection differs from creditor optimization
Same two feasible operating choices, cash R and nontransferable controller benefitU:
 a: R=12,U=1; b: R=8,U=8. FaceD=10, no costs omitted, risk-free one-state benchmark.
Creditor control yields10 viaa. A controller maximizingU uniquely choosesb, paying8.
TotalR+U is13 fora and16 forb. The comparison is a classical incomplete-contract
calibration: lender receipts and total surplus need not have the same ordering.
Giving the creditor actual selection authority would change this comparison;
an undefined veto alone does not guarantee that result. Simply renaming a
customer option a lender option would change the model.

L4 — Missing operating consent prevents point identification in a public skeleton
Abstract disclosure contains debtD=8, gross operating cash10, liquidation cash2,
and a statement that any operating step-in follows a separate consent agreement.
Consent is costless once granted; there is no guarantee or other recovery source.
Two completions keep those disclosures/technology fixed: one permits continuation
and pays8; the other permits only liquidation and pays2. Hence this skeleton
does not point-identify recovery or the advance supported by that fixed-face debt.
With continuation allowed with probabilityp and no other risk, that advance is
(2+6p)/(1+r). Optimizing faceD would be a different target; withD>=10 it becomes
(2+8p)/(1+r) in this toy model. Probabilityp is not identified by the skeleton.
These are expressly hypothetical completions of this abstract skeleton, not a
claim that both satisfy all actual CoreWeave contracts. Actual identified bounds
would require constructing all legally admissible completions, including recourse.

Current live research distinction (not yet an open-problem claim)
Quantify when noncreditor control of a substitute operator can be made compatible
with a pledged cash-flow floor under fixed actual ownership, limited liability
and coalition participation across states. Must compare to established contingent-
control theory before selecting it. No incentive theorem follows from L1–L4.

Promotion gate
Any continuation needs a substantive general implication beyond L1–L4 or a
specific real contract gap for which a new mechanism can be stated. Otherwise
close the scope as a useful institutional map and classical-reduction result.
