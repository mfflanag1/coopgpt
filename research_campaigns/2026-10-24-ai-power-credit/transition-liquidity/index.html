What changed

Keep the AI/power credit application; stop the generic timing-plus-control theorem branch. Adding a payment deadline changes feasibility, but the mechanism is established in liquidity and debt-overhang theory. The remaining application must be supported by an executable operating plan and accessible cash.

The previous pilot showed that an exogenous permission probability or a fixed netted cost merely modifies an existing model’s objective. This run tested the stronger possibility: expenses are due before recovery revenues, and cash cannot automatically move between owners or through existing creditor claims.

The result is useful discipline. Positive terminal surplus, control rights, and the ability to pay on time are separate conditions. None establishes the others. We have not identified an actual issuer funding shortfall, priced a security, or confirmed a novel contribution.

Two exact, deliberately narrow results

1 · A fixed continuation plan has a peak cash requirement

Order every mandatory payment and usable receipt by its actual availability time. Let at be cash due, bt be cash accessible to this operator, s initial cash, and B an executable upfront facility. Let g be an additional nonrepayable contribution.

H = max(0, max over t of Σ[j ≤ t](aⱼ − bⱼ)) g* = max(0, H − s − B)

Necessity follows from a nonnegative balance at every deadline. Sufficiency follows by drawing B and contributing g*. This formula assumes a fixed operating plan and a facility whose repayment and fees are already covered by separate later pledged proceeds. Those proceeds are excluded from the interim receipts. The formula does not prove that such a facility exists.

Both examples set initial cash and borrowing to zero. These are mathematical examples, not measured infrastructure costs.

There is no bound on peak cash from terminal net surplus and party count alone: pay M, later receive M + 1, and the surplus stays 1 while the initial need is M. This obstruction assumes no accessible cash or credit. It says nothing against a feasible facility that funds the interval.

2 · Creditor consent helps only within pledgeable value

A separate two-date model has an immediate cost K, pledgeable terminal cash P, an old senior face claim D, and liquidation payoff L ≤ D. All operating rights are already granted. No private information, uncertainty, initial cash, or alternative financing is present.

Unchanged senior debt, junior new money: funding exists ⇔ K ≤ max(0, P − D) Voluntary restructuring with the stated outside options: funding exists ⇔ P ≥ K + L

For P = 12, K = 6, D = 10 and L = 4, junior capacity is only 2. A consented split of (6, 4, 2) funds continuation. If total value stays 12 but only 8 can satisfy any financial claim, consent alone cannot cover 6 + 4.

If junior financing already works, the old creditor is paid D, rather than being asked to accept L. Otherwise liquidation is the specified outside option. The construction is an existence result with transferable financial payoffs; it does not establish actual consent or legal enforceability.

The public-contract finding

The CCAC VIII agreement, §7.03(b), separates specified cure-period enforcement restraint from fresh credit availability. A standstill therefore does not establish an operating cash facility. Clause qualifications and post-remedy waterfall limits are recorded in the model note.

For Building 3, the public record inspected does not quantify an executable successor operation. The relevant cash flow is compute-service continuation, which must not be conflated with landlord rent.

What transfers from CoopGPT—and what still needs a model

The useful inheritance is the discipline of fixed owners, explicit deviations and an identified budget. The coalition-withholding results protect payments across resource reports, with an additional outside-production benchmark under the stronger contract. They do not supply a peak-cash bound, price private opportunity costs, or resolve creditor renegotiation.

A real bridge needs a report domain, actual withdrawal rights, induced cash-flow timing, and a distinction between reversible advances and consumed subsidies. Our fixed-plan cash identity has none of those incentive claims. Retaining the same party names does not make it an all-profile mechanism.

Recommended next commitment

One transaction-level continuation-funding audit, conditional on a complete enough record. Choose one operator, remedy and revenue strategy; populate the four evidence blocks above. Calculate a dated cash requirement only if the inputs support it.

If the public record remains incomplete, finish with a document-based research note on disclosed continuation funding and its limits. Do not manufacture a recovery estimate or continue a generic theory campaign merely by adding more financing parameters. A new mathematical campaign should introduce a verified incentive or control interaction and demonstrate a consequence beyond these classical reductions.

This gate does not close the broader macro/fixed-income agenda. It narrows this particular AI/power branch to its defensible application.
