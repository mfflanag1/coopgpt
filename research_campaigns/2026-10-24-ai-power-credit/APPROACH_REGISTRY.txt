Status: DATA INSUFFICIENT. Public contract language alone does not reveal the

2. Show that more optional lender rights reduce the same lender's optimized value.
Status: REJECTED under unchanged feasible payoffs, costs, liabilities and authority.
L1 proves nested-action monotonicity. A new model must identify the changed term.
This does not deny other-party losses or endogenous-incentive effects.

3. Add creditors to our completed coalition production theorem.
Status: DIRECT TRANSFER FAILS BOOKKEEPING. Paying owners at least the entire
production value leaves no positive creditor payment from that same value.
An external sponsor, a different baseline or ex ante rights contract must be explicit.
Existing fixed-owner partial-withholding contracts do not describe illegal breach
or an owner's attempt to reclaim a right already irrevocably pledged.

4. Public, separable coalition participation with customer operator selection.
Status: ELEMENTARY REDUCTION. L2 and OPERATOR_MENU_BOUNDARY give the exact static
LP and min-residual floor. No cross-profile incentive or dynamic finance claim.

5. Complementarity plus fragmented foreclosure; bundling plus contagion.
Status: INGREDIENTS ESTABLISHED, exact extensions unresolved. Bolton-Scharfstein1996
already studies complementary secured assets. Banal-Estanol et al.2013 already
studies financial pooling contamination. A new contract must be mapped to the
closest model; a shared assumption alone is not a proof of equivalence.

6. Customer-directed replacement with private/nonverifiable quality or investment.
Status: POSSIBLE DISTINCTION, NOT YET AN ISOLATED OPEN PROBLEM. Specify actual
decision-maker, quality technology, transfers, observable outcomes, default,
renegotiation and ex ante investment before searching. Compare contingent control,
supplier finance and current AI rights models. Do not label new without originals.

7. Public dataset of AI infrastructure guarantees and asset rights.
Status: BROAD VERSION ALREADY HAS A CLOSE PRECEDENT. Wagner2026 supplies a
contract database and rights fields. Its current codebook includes assumption,
purchase, sublease and sale, plus source evidence and unknown-data handling.
Do not rebuild and relabel this as new. Full-paper theory comparison remains open.

Recommended next bounded commitment (prepared, not launched)
Obtain the free complete Wagner working paper and reconcile its theory and
rights coding with this exact question: can one named qualified replacement
operator obtain all equipment, premises, power and customer rights at the same
time, and how do those permissions interact with the cash waterfall?

Use the existing database as a cited starting point. A potential three-case pilot
would freeze selection BEFORE reading recoveries or market prices: the audited
DDTL3.0 case; one separately matched data-center lease financing; one supplier
guarantee whose executed form is public. These are selection categories, not a
claim that any unverified projects have matching contracts. Avoid conflating a
site, an SPV, a common parent and an independent resource owner.

Evidence fields for each case: controlling party; permitted operator; activation
trigger; cure/standstill dates; assignment consent; premises access; equipment
access; power/cooling access; customer continuity; replacement-servicer duties;
cash priority; additional cure obligations; governing interparty precedence;
document/version/effective date; execution evidence; redacted/not found status.
No scalar 'rights score' until a model justifies how fields combine.
