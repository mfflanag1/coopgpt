INDEPENDENT-THRESHOLD CLASS OBSTRUCTION -- CANDIDATE 2026-10-04
Derived by independent Fable task; coordinator checked and simplified constant.
Pending independent QA and exact finite checks.

CLASS
Fix k, a finite owner partition, and for each pool a finite-real-valued measurable
statistic Phi_j on nonnegative owner-coordinate vectors, coordinatewise
nondecreasing. These functions may depend on fixed labels, ownership and k, but
NOT separately on reports, d or T: their only varying inputs are Z_j=(d_i U_i).
Let U_i be mutually independent uniform [0,1], d_i=min(c_i,T), and choose the pool
W minimizing Phi_j(Z_j), with a fixed total priority order for ties. Pay
b_i=d_i P(W=j) in pool j; optionally max with M_i/k as in the preceding campaign.
At T=0 pay zero. Deterministic expectations, not realized lottery payments.

GUARANTEES
Coverage: the winning probabilities sum to one; capping preserves every coalition
value, so sum b_i over S is a convex combination of the capped pool totals and
is at least v(S).
Own monotonicity: fix an owner report x, other own-pool total a, and least other
pool total B. If B=0 all payments vanish. Otherwise T(x)=min(x+a,B), d_i=min(x,B).
Other own-pool capped coordinates are constant in x, as proved in the preceding
SCALING_THEOREM.txt. Every other pool coordinate is nondecreasing in x.
Conditional on U_-i, the set E_x of z>=0 for which pool j wins is a down-set
because Phi_j is nondecreasing in that coordinate. As x grows, E_x expands
because the other pool statistics do not decrease and the fixed tie order stays.
Conditional b_i equals Lebesgue length of E_x intersect [0,d_i(x)]. This is
nondecreasing. Endpoints have measure zero; d_i=0 gives zero. Taking expectations
proves monotonicity. The M/k top-up preserves it, covers H/k, and adds at most T.

CLASS-WIDE LOWER BOUND
Suppose r>=2 and every pool has at least r fixed owner slots. If
 k-1 >= 2 r^r,
then for EVERY family of statistics and priority order in this class there is
a profile whose base subsidy is at least (r-1)/4.
Use r designated owners per pool; set extra owners to zero. For each j, let
F_j be the distribution of Phi_j on the r-dimensional small box [0,1/r]^r,
with all extra coordinates zero. Choose a finite median a_j, so both
P(Phi_j<=a_j)>=1/2 and P(Phi_j>=a_j)>=1/2.
Choose s with maximum median, taking the lowest tie priority among maximizers.
Pool s has capacity 1/r on each designated owner (total T=1); all other pools
have capacity 1 on each designated owner (capped total r).
With probability >=1/2, Phi_s(Z_s)>=a_s. Independently each other pool has a
chance >=p=1/(2 r^r) of both falling in the small box and having Phi_j<=a_j.
If either such pool event occurs and Phi_s>=a_s, pool s loses: a_j<a_s gives
strict comparison; equal medians use the chosen priority, including atoms.
Thus P(W!=s)>= [1-(1-p)^(k-1)]/2 >=1/4.
The last inequality follows from (1-p)^n <=1/(1+n p), n=k-1 and n p>=1.
The normalized base total is 1+(r-1)P(W!=s), giving the claimed lower bound.
The stronger original constant (1-exp(-1))/2 also follows, but is unnecessary.

ASYMPTOTIC CONSEQUENCE
For every fixed epsilon>0 and sufficiently large k, take
r=floor((1-epsilon)log k/log log k). Then 2r^r<=k-1.
The class needs Omega(log k/log log k) subsidy over unbounded finite owner counts.
The previous sum-statistic rule achieves O(log k/log log k) including H/k.
Hence its growth ORDER is optimal within this full fixed-statistic independent-
threshold class. The leading constant is not matched by this class lower bound.
No report-dependent statistic, correlated threshold mechanism, nonlinear owner
payment rule, or arbitrary mechanism is ruled out. The witness is not an
unrestricted lower bound: the marginal vector M costs only T there.
Novelty remains unconfirmed; continuous Banzhaf and minimum-sum case are established
functional ingredients, while this class obstruction is a proposed contribution.
