LOWER CERTIFICATES
For each k,m, construct the original positive-capacity profiles X and Y. Coalition
rows use actual owner subsets; values are computed by minimum pool total. Payment
roles are derived from capacity multisets and owner values, justified by the written
symmetrization proof. The one-owner increase is applied to Y and checked to match
X up to pool/owner permutations. Positive integer combinations cancel every payment
variable and leave a positive beta coefficient with the advertised rational bound.
53 instantiated cases include m=2 and k=3 boundaries and the 19/21 diagonal witness.
Large diagonal identities are arithmetic only, with no implied profile enumeration.
16 negative controls include a coefficient-preserving loss of coalition worth,
wrong owner/report, hidden floor, absent budget, bad multiplier and altered claim.
