UNRESTRICTED LOWER BOUND APPROACHING 100% -- 2026-10-04
Conventional proof; independent proof review and exact dual checks completed.

MODEL
Use CONTRACT.txt. No fairness floors are needed. k>=3 pools, each with m>=2
fixed original owners. Put q=m-2 and t=2m-2=2q+2. Two pool types are
 L=(1,1,2,...,2) with q copies of 2, total t;
 H=(1,2,...,2,t) with q copies of 2, total 2t-1.
For m=2 these are (1,1) and (1,2); formulas with q=0 omit the 2-owner role.
All profiles in the proof are strictly positive. No cloning or owner removal.

THEOREM
For every k>=3 and m>=2, on k fixed pools of exactly m owners each,
every nonnegative, coalition-covering, own-report-monotone rule with budget
sum y<=(1+beta)T on all real profiles satisfies
 beta >= (k-1)(t-1) / [t(k-1+2t)].
Therefore any subsidy constant uniform in both stages AND owner counts is >=1.
This does not prove divergence or existence of a constant. For m=3, t=4,
 beta >=3(k-1)/(4k+28), tending to 3/4 and exceeding 1/2 when k>17.

SYMMETRIZATION
Average an arbitrary rule over all pool permutations and all within-pool owner
permutations. Each preserves the fixed partition, values, budgets and own-report
order, so the averaged rule has the same guarantees. It suffices to prove the
bound for that invariant rule. Labels and all original owners remain fixed;
permutations are only a proof device, not a permitted strategic report.

PROFILE X: one L pool, k-1 H pools; T=t.
Payments in L: a to each capacity-1 owner, b to each capacity-2 owner.
Payments in H: u to its capacity-1 owner, v to each of its q capacity-2 owners,
and w to the capacity-t owner. P=2a+q b, Q=u+q v+w.
Budget: P+(k-1)Q <=t(1+beta).
The following actual coalitions give:
 C1: a+(k-1)u >=1.
 C2: 2a+(k-1)v >=2.                     [used only if q>0]
 C3: a+q b+(k-1)(u+q v) >=t-1.
 C4: P+(k-1)w >=t.
C1 selects one unit owner in L and the unit owner in each H.
C2 selects both unit owners in L and one 2-owner in each H.
C3 selects one unit and all 2-owners in every pool.
C4 selects all L owners and the t-owner in every H.
Take C1 + q*C2 + (t-1)*C3 + C4. Since 2q=t-2, its left side is
 t[P+(k-1)Q] -(t-1)(k-1)w,
and its right side is t^2. Thus
 (t-1)(k-1)w <=t^2 beta.                 (X)

PROFILE Y: two L pools, k-2 H pools; T=t.
Payments in L: A to each unit owner, B to each 2-owner.
Payments in H: U,V,W for the corresponding three roles.
Budget: 2(2A+q B)+(k-2)(U+q V+W) <=t(1+beta).
Coalitions give:
 D1: 2A+(k-2)U >=1,
 D2: 4A+(k-2)V >=2,                     [used only if q>0]
 D4: 2(2A+q B)+(k-2)W >=t.
D1 uses one unit owner in each L; D2 uses both units in each L; D4 uses all
owners in both L. Each H supplies respectively its unit, one 2-owner, or t-owner.
Adding D1+q*D2+D4, the left side is the budget sum plus 2(t-1)A,
and the right side is 2t-1. Consequently
 2(t-1)A >=t-1-t beta.                  (Y)

ONE-OWNER REPORT COMPARISON
Raise one unit owner in one L pool of Y from 1 to t. That pool becomes H;
the resulting profile is X up to relabeling. Own monotonicity gives w>=A.
Combine (X), (Y), and w>=A:
 (k-1)(t-1-t beta) <=2(t-1)(k-1)A
 <=2(t-1)(k-1)w <=2t^2 beta.
Rearrange to the stated bound. No sign assumption on t-1-t beta is necessary.

QUANTIFIERS AND LIMITS
For every fixed even t>=2, taking k to infinity gives beta_uniform>=1-1/t.
Then take even t to infinity to obtain beta_uniform>=1. Equivalently a diagonal
choice t->infinity with t/k->0 makes the displayed exact lower bound tend to 1.
All witnesses have k*m finite original owners and positive integer capacities.
This is a lower bound on the UNRESTRICTED rule class, unlike threshold or ratio
obstructions. It sharpens the preceding 1/2 necessary constant to 1; the existence
of any constant remains unresolved. Source priority remains unconfirmed.

EXPLICIT DIAGONAL
For every integer n>=1, take m=n+1, t=2n and k=8n^2+1. The exact bound is
 (2n-1)/(2n+1).
At n=10 this is 19/21 (>90%), with 801 stages and 11 owners per stage.
Letting n grow proves the 100% necessary uniform constant; no finite instance in
this family attains 100%. This statement requires no numerical limiting guess.
