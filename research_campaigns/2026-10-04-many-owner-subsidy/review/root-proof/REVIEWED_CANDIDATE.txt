COORDINATOR CANDIDATE: SHARP1/2 SHAPLEY COALITION FACTOR -- 2026-10-04
Not yet independently reviewed. Proposed proof on arbitrary finite two-pool games.

Setup: L,R finite disjoint, capacities c>=0. v(S)=min(c(S intersect L),c(S intersect R)).
phi denotes ordinary individual Shapley value. Original core ideals H_i.

1. UNIVERSAL LOWER FACTOR
For positive-capacity i in L and j in R, write a=c_i,b=c_j. The independent
uniform-arrival representation of Shapley gives, conditional on own arrival t,
Bernoulli(t) predecessor indicators for all other owners. Excluding i,j let
D=right predecessor capacity minus left predecessor capacity, and f_a(z)=min(a,max(z,0)).
Then, at this t, expected marginal of i is
 E[(1-t)f_a(D)+t f_a(D+b)],
and expected marginal of j is
 E[(1-t)f_b(-D)+t f_b(a-D)].
The distributions of the OTHER owners are the same in both expressions.
For every D, f_a(D+b)/a+f_b(a-D)/b>=1: if D>=a-b the first term is1,
and if D<=a-b the second is1. Non-t terms are nonnegative. Integrating gives
 phi_i/a+phi_j/b>=integral_0^1 t dt=1/2.

For any coalition S with V=v(S)>0, choose nonnegative bilateral quantities q_ij
between its left and right owners with row sums<=c_i, column sums<=c_j and
sum q=V. Such a fractional matching exists by filling the two resource pools
until the smaller is exhausted. Zero-capacity owners carry no flow.
Summing q_ij times the pair-price inequality gives
 (1/2)V <= sum q_ij(phi_i/c_i+phi_j/c_j) <= sum_{i in S}phi_i.
This allocation of quantities is a proof device; owners are not cloned.
Thus phi(S)>=v(S)/2 for ALL coalitions and owner counts. T=0 is immediate.

2. UNIVERSAL INDIVIDUAL FLOOR
Fix i capacity a, total same-pool capacity of others A, opposite total B, D=B-A.
For predecessor subset U, let z=opposite capacity in U minus same capacity in U.
Its marginal is f_a(z). The complementary predecessor subset has marginal f_a(D-z).
For every real z,D, f_a(z)+f_a(D-z)>=f_a(D): trivial when D<=0; when D>0,
if either argument>=a the result holds, otherwise their positive parts sum>=D
until the cap a is reached. Complementary subsets have equal Shapley weights.
Averaging yields phi_i>=f_a(D)/2=[T-v(N\{i})]/2>=H_i/2.
Nonempty original core follows from a bottleneck-pool capacity vector.

3. SHARPNESS FAMILY (capacity profile, not a cloning equivalence)
For integer q>=2 put m=q^4, r=q^4-q^3. There are m left owners of capacity1,
r right owners of capacity1, and one additional right owner b of capacity m.
Let S be all left owners plus b. Then v(S)=T=m. Put delta=m-r=q^3 and
D0=m-1-r=q^3-1>0.

A given left unit earns1 if b arrives before it (probability1/2). If b arrives
later, it earns1 exactly when strictly more right units than other left units
have preceded it. Conditional on its arrival t, the counts X,Y are independent
Binomial(m-1,t), Binomial(r,t). Therefore phi_left=1/2+eta, where
 eta=integral_0^1 (1-t) Pr(Y>X)dt.
The variable Y-X has mean -D0*t and variance (m-1+r)t(1-t)<=m/2.
Chebyshev gives Pr(Y>X)<=min(1,m/(2 D0^2 t^2)) for t>0. With
h=sqrt(m/2)/D0, integration yields eta<=2h=sqrt(2m)/D0 (also valid if h>=1).

At b's arrival t, let X~Binomial(m,t),Y~Binomial(r,t). Its marginal is(X-Y)_+,
whose mean is at most delta*t+sqrt(m/2) by Cauchy-Schwarz applied to centered
fluctuations. Thus phi_b<=delta/2+sqrt(m/2).
Combining,
 phi(S)/m=1/2+eta+phi_b/m
 <=1/2+sqrt(2)*q^2/(q^3-1)+1/(2q)+1/(sqrt(2)*q^2) ->1/2.
Together with1, the optimal universal coalition factor is exactly1/2 (infimum).

CONSEQUENCE
Every constant scalar multiple lambda*phi that covers every coalition for all
owner counts requires lambda>=2, and lambda=2 works. The rule2phi is truthful
