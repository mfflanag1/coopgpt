The verifier writes EXACT_CHECKS.json with its own SHA256. It checks4,923 integer
profiles: capacities{0,1,2}, every pool split and every owner count2 through6.
It also checks150 seeded rational profiles with2 through8 owners. The5073 profiles
give273,691 nonempty-coalition inequalities,28,609 removal-marginal floors and
14,670 positive-capacity cross-pair price inequalities. There are716 strict report
reductions by halving a positive capacity; the required payment response is WEAK
monotonicity. Eleven zero-capacity halvings are skipped.

Two five-owner witnesses are independently checked against all120 permutations,
and the old2+2 calibration against all24. Coalition bit masks use zero-based owner
indices, with the left pool first. These3 extra witness profiles are not included
in the5073 stress-profile count.

The symmetric-count calculation is cross-checked against the individual-subset
formula on16 families(m=1..4,r=0..3). It then computes exact Shapley values for
q=2,3,5 in the explicit family m=q^4,r=q^4-q^3. The q=2,3 rational upper bounds
exceed1 and are vacuous as upper-bound tests; the q=5 bound is below1. All three
cases additionally check the individual fluctuation estimates by exact squared
inequalities. No floating-point number decides a PASS result; decimal ratios are
for display only. The q=5 exact ratio is also checked to be below2/3.

The q=13 rational proof bound162883/247416<2/3 is checked arithmetically. The
54,926-owner game itself is NOT enumerated. The conventional proof, not these
finite computations, establishes the universal half factor and sharp infimum.
Fairness tests use M_i=T-v(N without i), with H_i<=M_i proved separately; they
are not LP computations of original-core ideals.

use different implementations; neither establishes novelty.

instead of6. The theorem's original calibration is the small left owner plus one

comparisons; q=5 and exact intermediate bounds were added, factorial/binomial
