WHY THE OLD GLOBAL CAP DOES NOT EXTEND TO NESTED NETWORKS
6 October 2026. Obstruction to one specific rule, not to truthful mechanisms.

Consider the directed TTSP network with flow value
 F(x,a,b,d_1,...,d_n)=min(x+min(a,b),sum_j d_j).
There are n+3 fixed edge owners, three structural minimal cuts, and paths of
length at most3. The old rule globally caps each edge at grand flow T and
applies the absolute continuous Banzhaf functional. The theorem in THEOREM.txt
uses a different effective cap and remains truthful on this network.

For every integer n>=4 set epsilon=1/10, a=epsilon, b=n+epsilon,
d_1=...=d_n=1. Compare x0=n-2epsilon with x1=n-epsilon, holding every
other original owner's report fixed. Then T0=n-epsilon and T1=n.
After GLOBAL capping, the x, a and d capacities are unchanged, but b is T.
Let S_n=sum of n independent uniform[0,1] variables and let
 A_T=min(epsilon U,T V), independently of S_n.
The payment of owner x under the old rule is
 z(x,T)=E[min(x,(S_n-A_T)_+)].                                      (1)
This follows either by integrating the minimum-cut indicator conditional on
all uniforms except the one for x, or by subtracting flow at the two x endpoints.

Write delta=n-T in {epsilon,0} and t=epsilon+delta, so x=n-t.
Here 0<=A_T<=epsilon<=t<=2epsilon<1. The simplex-tail identity
 E[(u-S_n)_+]=u^(n+1)/(n+1)! for 0<=u<=1
follows by integrating over {s_i>=0: sum s_i<=u}, which lies in the unit cube.
Also n-S_n has the same distribution as S_n. Thus (1) equals
 z(x,T)=n/2-E[A_T]
       +(E[A_T^(n+1)]-E[(t-A_T)^(n+1)])/(n+1)!.                    (2)
This uses (S-A)_+=S-A+(A-S)_+ and min(x,y_+)=y_+-(y-x)_+.

Since P(A_T>u)=(1-u/epsilon)(1-u/T) for 0<=u<=epsilon,
 E[A_T^r]=epsilon^r/(r+1)
          -r*epsilon^(r+1)/((r+1)(r+2)T), for each integer r>=1.    (3)
In particular E[A_T1]-E[A_T0]=epsilon^3/(6n(n-epsilon)).
Discarding the negative terms in the difference of the two remainders in (2)
and bounding each remaining nonnegative term by (2epsilon)^(n+1) gives
 z(x1,T1)-z(x0,T0)
 <=-epsilon^3/(6n(n-epsilon))+2(2epsilon)^(n+1)/(n+1)! <0.           (4)
For the last strict inequality, replace n(n-epsilon) by n^2. The ratio of the
positive bound to epsilon^3/(6n^2) is
 r_n=6n^2*2^(n+2)*epsilon^(n-2)/(n+1)!.
At n=4, r_4=64/125<1; thereafter
 r_(n+1)/r_n=2epsilon*(n+1)^2/(n^2(n+2))<1.
Thus (4) holds for every n>=4, with owners growing and every comparison on a
fixed owner set. This does not supply a growing lower bound on optimal subsidies:
it only rejects the old global-cap formula, even when K=3 is fixed.

EXACT SEVEN-OWNER INSTANCE
Scale the n=4 pair by10. Profiles in edge order (x,a,b,d_1,...,d_4) are
 low =(38,1,41,10,10,10,10), high=(39,1,41,10,10,10,10).
Old global-cap payments of owner x are respectively
 737261209/37800000 and 3932039999/201600000.
The payment DROP on increasing x is 59347/604800000>0.
With the new effective cap, the large detour edge b is capped at a=1 in both
profiles; payments become 1548749/78750 and 99119999/5040000, increasing
by exactly1/80000.
verification/verify.py reconstructs the old values independently using (2)-(3)
and exact convolution/CDF arithmetic, and checks the new payments as well.

INTERPRETATION
The global cap lets x's report change the normalized capacity of b even though
x and b share a minimal cut. Effective capping restores the co-cut independence
needed for the conditional incentive proof. This is an unbounded family of
failures of the naive extension, repaired by the general theorem; it is not an
impossibility theorem for all mechanisms or for general directed networks.
