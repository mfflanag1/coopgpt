EFFECTIVE-CAPACITY BANZHAF PAYMENTS ON SERIES-PARALLEL NETWORKS
Conventional proof, 6 October 2026. Independent Fable audit found no invalid step;
coordinator verified and supplied three requested justifications. Exact finite
checks support the implementation. Not formal verification or human review.

MODEL AND STATEMENT
G is a finite directed two-terminal series-parallel (TTSP) network. Construct it
from single directed edges by binary series and parallel composition; components
have disjoint edge sets and disjoint internal vertices before their terminals
are identified. These directed networks are acyclic, by induction on the
composition tree. Thus feasible flows decompose into s-t paths without cycles.
Distinct edge leaves are distinct fixed original owners.
The graph and decomposition tree do not depend on reports. Edge reports c>=0
are arbitrary real capacities. For S subset E, v_c(S) is the maximum s-t flow
using edges in S only; T=F(c)=v_c(E). Payments are nonnegative deterministic
functions of c, cover every coalition, and are nondecreasing in an owner's
own capacity holding all other original reports fixed. This is binding
underreport truthfulness with payment utility, without use costs.

Let C(G) be the family of inclusion-minimal s-t edge cutsets of the graph,
independent of capacities, and K=|C(G)|. Equivalently these are the minimal
edge sets meeting every s-t path. The cut recursion is:
 single edge e: {{e}};
 series(A,B): C(A) disjoint-union C(B);
 parallel(A,B): {CA union CB : CA in C(A), CB in C(B)}.
Consequently K_leaf=1, K_series=K_A+K_B, K_parallel=K_A*K_B.
Every cut is nonempty; all edges are on s-t paths. Zero capacities do not change
this structural family. Min-cut/max-flow gives F(c)=min_C sum_(e in C)c_e.

For K>=2, define R_K>e by R_K log(R_K/e)=log K and U(K)=R_K+1/log R_K.
Claim: a deterministic nonnegative coalition-covering own-report-monotone rule
has total payment <=U(K)T for every profile, regardless of owner count.
For K=1 the construction is efficient. Moreover the worst optimum over all
TTSP graphs with at most K minimal cuts has leading asymptotic
 (1+o(1))*log K/log log K.
The matching lower bound is INHERITED from the existing weighted multi-glove
report-transition theorem. The proposed extension is the new cap/monotonicity
argument and upper bound on the larger TTSP class, not a new lower construction.

1. EFFECTIVE CAPACITIES AND EVERY-COALITION PRESERVATION
Write F_H(c) for the maximum terminal flow in a component H. It obeys
F_leaf=c_e, F_series=min(F_A,F_B), F_parallel=F_A+F_B.
For each edge e define
 L_e(c_-e)=min{F_B(c): B is the sibling of the child containing e at a
                         SERIES ancestor of e in the fixed tree},
with empty minimum infinity, and D_e=min(c_e,L_e).
Thus D_e is nondecreasing in every report and, in its own report,
D_e=min(c_e,constant). All capacities are finite even when L_e=infinity.

Claim: D_e is the maximum feasible amount of s-t flow through e, allowing any
total flow value (not restricting to maximum-value flows). Proof by the tree.
Inside a leaf the possible edge flow interval is [0,c_e]. At a parallel parent,
route a desired amount through the chosen child and zero through the other.
At a series parent, the same total must cross its sibling; any amount at most
F_sibling can be routed there by scaling a maximum flow. Proceed from the leaf
to the root. This constructs an s-t flow of total exactly D_e through e.
Conversely every path decomposition of a feasible flow using e must traverse
each series sibling, whose total flow capacity is at most F_sibling(c).
Hence its edge-e flow cannot exceed any of the displayed bounds.

In particular D_e<=T. Every feasible coalition flow under c has every edge flow
at most D_e(c), because it is also a feasible grand-network flow. Conversely
D<=c. Therefore the entire feasible flow set of EVERY coalition is unchanged
when capacities c are replaced by D(c):
 v_c(S)=v_D(S) for every S, and F(D)=T.
This is simultaneous capping with fixed original edges, not owner cloning.
It also proves D=0 whenever T=0.

2. CO-CUT INDEPENDENCE
If two distinct edges e,h belong to a common C in C(G), their lowest common
ancestor in the composition tree is PARALLEL. At a series node a minimal cut
is wholly in just one child, so it cannot contain one edge in each child.
Now D_h depends only on c_h and the report vectors in siblings at SERIES
ancestors of h. Such a sibling contains e exactly when e and h first diverge
at a series node. This is impossible for edges in a common minimal cut.
Consequently D_h is independent of c_e whenever e and h occur in a common cut.
All other D_h remain nondecreasing as c_e increases.
This lemma supplies the additional structure needed for the incentive proof;
no counterpart is asserted here for unrestricted directed graphs.

3. RULE AND COVERAGE
Use independent U_e uniform[0,1] as an integration device. Select a minimizer
 C_*(U,c) in argmin_(C in C(G)) sum_(e in C)D_e(c)U_e,
breaking ties by a fixed order of the finite structural cut family. Define
 z_e(c)=D_e(c)*P(e in C_*).
These are deterministic expectations, not realized random payments. At T=0
set z=0, which agrees with D=0. For each realized selected cut and every S,
 sum_(e in S intersect C_*)D_e >= min_C sum_(e in S intersect C)D_e
 =v_D(S)=v_c(S).
To justify the restricted-cut identity explicitly: for any coalition S, give
all edges outside S capacity0 in the fixed full graph. Every structural cut C
restricts to a cut of S. Conversely a cut Cprime of S, together with E without S,
cuts the full graph and contains an inclusion-minimal structural cut C. Hence
C intersect S is contained in Cprime. Nonnegative capacities and mincut/maxflow
prove the equality, including disconnected S, whose value and minimum are0.
Taking expectation proves coverage, including singleton and grand coalitions.
Nonnegativity is immediate. The finite piecewise-linear comparisons are measurable.

4. ALL-PROFILE OWN MONOTONICITY
Fix e, all reports c_-e, and U_-e. Write x=c_e and d(x)=D_e(c). Let
 A=min_(C contains e) sum_(h in C without e)D_h(c)U_h,
 B(x)=min_(C does not contain e) sum_(h in C)D_h(c)U_h,
with B=+infinity if every cut contains e. The containing-cut family is nonempty.
By co-cut independence A is independent of x. B is nondecreasing in x because
every D_h is. The smallest containing-cut score is d(x)U_e+A.
If d(x)>0 and B is finite, equality to B occurs at at most one U_e, hence with
probability0 under its uniform law, irrespective of ties within either family.
Conditional integration gives
 D_e P(e in C_* | U_-e)=min(d(x),(B(x)-A)_+).
For B=+infinity this equals d(x). For d=0 both sides equal0. Every expression
is nondecreasing in x. Integrating U_-e proves own-report monotonicity for all
nonnegative real reports, including changing bottlenecks, zero reports and ties.
No payment of an unchanged owner is transported or assumed monotone in this step.

5. BUDGET DEPENDING ONLY ON NUMBER OF MINIMAL CUTS
For T>0 set a_e=D_e/T in[0,1], rho_C=sum_(e in C)a_e. A minimum cut for D has
rho_C0=1; all rho_C>=1. Put W=C_* and X_C=sum_(e in C)a_eU_e. Then
 z(E)/T=E[rho_W], and X_W<=X_C0<=1 for every U.
For any C and t>0, independence within C and concavity of q->q^a for a in[0,1]
give
 E exp(-t X_C) <= product_(e in C) (E exp(-t U_e))^a_e
 <=t^(-rho_C).
Thus P(X_C<=1)<=exp(t)t^(-rho_C); put t=rho_C. The logarithm of
(e/rho)^rho has derivative -log rho, so it decreases for rho>1. For s>=e, a union bound over
the K cuts gives
 P(rho_W>s)<=min(1,K*(e/s)^s).
The cuts MAY OVERLAP: no independence between their scores is used.
At s=R_K the second term is1; its logarithmic derivative is -log s, at most
-log R_K thereafter. Tail integration gives
 E rho_W <= R_K+integral_(R_K)^infinity exp(-(s-R_K)log R_K)ds
          =R_K+1/log R_K.
For K=1 the sole cut has rho=1 and the rule is efficient. The standard
asymptotic inversion gives U(K)~log K/log log K.

6. SHARPNESS OF THE CLASS MINIMAX
Define B_SP(K)=sup_(fixed finite TTSP G with |C(G)|<=K) inf_(eligible
all-profile rules on its fixed edge owners) sup_(c:T>0) z(E,c)/T.
Upper: the preceding construction gives B_SP(K)<=U(K), since the tail union
bound can use K even if a graph has fewer cuts. Lower: realize k=K multi-glove
pools as K serial layers of parallel original edges, one distinct owner per
edge. The structural minimal cuts are exactly those K layers; every coalition
value is precisely its minimum selected pool sum. Owner identities, capacity
reports and unilateral comparisons coincide with the original model.
Therefore B_SP(K)>=B_K from the prior paper, whose proved asymptotic leading
constant is1 by weighted report-transition certificates. The two bounds imply
B_SP(K)~log K/log log K. No consistency across different graphs is required.

7. CALIBRATION AND LIMITS
In a K-layer parallel-edge chain, the series-sibling bounds give
D_e=min(c_e,min(other pool totals))=min(c_e,T). Thus both caps and payments
recover the prior mechanism exactly. In a pure parallel network K=1, D=c and
z=c, an efficient rule. Nested example: F=min(a+min(b,c),d+e) has the three
minimal cuts {a,b},{a,c},{d,e}; its overlapping-cut structure is outside a
partition into disjoint complementary pools. The class allows arbitrarily
large such nesting and arbitrarily many original owners.

K is a structural cut COUNT, not edge connectivity, longest path or treewidth.
It can be exponential in graph size, so the bound is not a sublogarithmic bound
in the number of owners. No polynomial-time exact integration is asserted.
General directed networks, Wheatstone interactions, multiple edges per strategic
owner, costs, overreports, group reports and split/merge remain outside the claim.
The core exists by the classical mincut allocation, but these payments use
external subsidy; B is total-payment factor, subsidy factor is B-1.
The continuous Banzhaf functional, min-cut theorem, composition recursions and
small-ball tail bound are established/previous ingredients. Novelty of this
effective-capacity co-cut argument and its application remains to be checked.


8. ABSTRACT SUFFICIENT PRINCIPLE
The proof applies to any finite cut representation v_c(S)=min_C c(S intersect C)
with a fixed family C, provided a normalization D(c) (i) preserves every coalition
value, (ii) obeys 0<=D_e<=v_c(E), (iii) is nondecreasing in every report, and (iv)
has D_h independent of c_e whenever distinct e,h share a cut. Require each owner
to appear in a cut. The conditional formula, coverage and tail bound above then
apply verbatim. The TTSP argument supplies these hypotheses for a substantive
unbounded class. The sufficient principle is not asserted necessary, and failure
of co-cut independence is not a proof that all truthful mechanisms fail.

9. ENDPOINT FORMULA USED BY THE EXACT VERIFIER
For fixed c and U_-e, put A and B as in section4. The difference between flow
with normalized edge-e capacity D_e and with that capacity0 is
 min(A+D_e,B)-min(A,B)=min(D_e,(B-A)_+).
This includes B=infinity by the evident limit and D_e=0. Hence the rule equals
the absolute continuous Banzhaf endpoint integral
 z_e=E_[U_-e][F(D_-e U_-e,D_e)-F(D_-e U_-e,0)].
The verifier evaluates these expectations by exact rational CDF arithmetic.
At a parallel composition independent component flows add; at a series
composition their minimum is taken. Disjoint leaves guarantee independence.
