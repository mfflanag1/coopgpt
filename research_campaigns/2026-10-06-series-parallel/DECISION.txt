RESEARCH GATE: MET FOR THE STATED SERIES-PARALLEL CLASS

Result: all-profile truthful coalition-covering payments for every finite TTSP
network with a fixed original owner on every edge; U(K) upper and sharp class
minimax asymptotic (1+o(1))*log K/log log K, with K the minimal-cut count.
The weighted report-transition lower transfers exactly from the layered subclass.
The new work is the effective-capacity/co-cut lemma and larger-class upper.
No new lower bound requiring non-layered interactions is claimed.

This is an unbounded class with unrestricted nonnegative real reports. Its
proof does not freeze the number of owners, the graph size or the number of
altered reports. The old global-cap rule fails even at three structural cuts;
the new normalization provably repairs the incentive issue.

Validation: two completed independent high-effort static model audits, direct
coordinator rederivations, and exact finite implementation checks. The separate
independent theorem-search delegate timed out and contributed no claim.
Formal validation, historical novelty and human specialist review remain open.

Recommended next mathematical gate: test the effective-capacity method on
unbounded networks containing interacting Wheatstone bridges. An exact failure
of co-cut independence would only block this sufficient proof; seek either an
alternative incentive argument giving a general bound, or a concrete violated
own-report comparison before declaring the construction itself obstructed.
Do not confuse failure of this rule with impossibility for all mechanisms.
This is a proposed next target, not a newly started goal or outreach action.
