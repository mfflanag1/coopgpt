SERIES-PARALLEL EXTENSION: RESEARCH CONTRACT
6 October 2026

Authorized objective: extend weighted report-transition methods beyond weighted
multi-glove games, proving a general subsidy bound or structural obstruction
with fixed owners and all-profile incentives. No fixed owner or network-size cap.

Model: finite directed two-terminal series-parallel networks constructed from
distinct directed single-edge leaves by series and parallel composition. One
fixed original owner per edge, all nonnegative real capacity reports, deterministic
nonnegative payments, all coalition max-flow inequalities, own-report monotonicity
under binding underreports and payment utility. No costs or strategic split/merge.
T=grand maxflow; total-payment factor B bounds sum payments/T for T>0; pay0 at T0.
Structural parameter K=number of inclusion-minimal s-t edge cutsets in the fixed
network, not number of vertices, number of paths, or edge connectivity.

Candidate route: local effective-capacity normalization D_e=max feasible s-t
flow through edge e, followed by continuous-Banzhaf/mincut expected payments.
Prove the co-cut independence lemma, value preservation on EVERY coalition,
all-profile own monotonicity including zero capacities, and U(K) upper bound.
Combine with existing weighted report-transition lower on the layered subclass
only if its exact quantifiers match. Do not advertise the inherited lower as new.

Independent route: detached cross-model search for a structural obstruction or
lower bound beyond merely embedding the existing layered witness.

Calibration and exclusions: original K-pool network must recover D_e=min(c_e,T)
and the current capped-Banzhaf rule; pure parallel network has factor1. The
known core/marginal arguments imply the baseline y_e=T-v(N without e) is monotone,
covers all coalitions and costs at most L*T where L=max simple s-t path length
(use a cycle-free maximum flow). Thus fixed-depth divergent unrestricted bounds
are impossible. This elementary baseline is not itself the desired contribution.
Do not infer own monotonicity from a pointwise Banzhaf identification.

Gate: a conventional all-parameter proof with independent audit and reproducible
exact small checks, or a precise proved obstruction. A finite-family mechanism,
more fixed-profile LPs, and a renamed weak-duality statement do not meet the gate.
No claim of priority, human review, formal verification or field breakthrough.
No manuscript edits until a claim survives; keep the existing editor open.
