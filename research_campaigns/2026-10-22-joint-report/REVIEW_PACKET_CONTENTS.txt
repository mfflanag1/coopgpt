FIXED-CAP ENVELOPE REVIEW PACKET

Purpose: a self-contained first-party packet for an eventual specialist
correctness and priority review. This campaign prepares the packet only;
it does not send it or claim external review. No third-party PDFs, browser
captures, raw model traces, private credentials, or canonical manuscript
are included. The source ledger links to freely available originals.

Reading order:
1. THEOREM_REVIEW_BRIEF.txt -- contract, theorem, proof outline, review questions.
2. theory/selection_cap_two_analysis.txt -- full new quadratic certificate.
3. theory/selection_power_regularizer_extension.txt -- every fixed r>1.
4. inputs/previous_full_envelope_proof.txt -- inherited row distribution,
   all-cut transport proof and simultaneous fixed-owner realization.
5. theory/selection_efficient_core_envelope_barrier.txt -- cap-one and
   tie-selected r=1 endpoints.
6. theory/composition_cap_audit.txt, reviews/FINAL_POWER_AND_RESUME_AUDIT.txt,
   and reviews/ROOT_VERIFICATION.txt -- actual internal review scope.
7. sources/root_primary_comparisons.txt and theory/dual_priority_addendum.txt
   -- established ingredients and remaining priority questions.

Three exact scalar replays are included. From the extracted packet root:

row family or replace its written proof. Their saved diagnostic JSONs are

The packet includes an internal SHA256 manifest of its exact first-party
files. Its file hashes and full campaign provenance are recorded separately
