ONE COHERENT STORY.
At a single reported profile an efficient coalition-covering allocation
exists. Selecting such allocations independently across reports can destroy
incentives. Taking each owner's largest selected payment obtainable by
underreporting repairs payment monotonicity and preserves coverage, but can
require much more funding. The new contribution describes how severely this
repair fails across several natural selection prescriptions.
