REOPENING THE UNRESTRICTED PROBLEM: EXACT EVIDENCE GATES
22 October2026. Planning clarification, not a new rate theorem.

The frozen all-profile payment-only contract remains in force. The next
campaign must alter an unrestricted bound, or establish an explicit new
lemma that implies such an alteration. Do not spend another full campaign
changing fixed random weights, own-local weights, or the cap/power of a
seed before taking its own-report envelope.

A. A GENERAL TRANSPORT-CHARGING INEQUALITY.
For each finite set P of actual profiles of one fixed technology, let
lambda_pS>=0 be coalition weights and mu_p>=0 be profile prices. Set
ell_ip=sum_{S containing i}lambda_pS. There must be nonnegative transports
tau_i(p,q), restricted to the SAME owner and same-others report fiber,
with p_i<=q_i, satisfying
 sum_q tau_i(p,q)=ell_ip,   sum_p tau_i(p,q)<=mu_q.
A useful new inequality would be
 sum_p,S lambda_pS*v_p(S) <= g(n,d)*sum_p mu_p*T_p,
uniformly over EVERY such finite P, technology and feasible load system,
where g demonstrably improves the existing owner or dimension rate.
Keeping each source profile and owner fiber is essential. Replacing all
source production by f(sum_p mu_p*aggregate_p) loses the denominator and
is exactly the saved obstruction, not a proof.

B. A SCALABLE ARBITRARY-MECHANISM LOWER.
Construct a parameterized family of those legal finite transport systems
whose ratio exceeds the recorded logarithmic/log-logarithmic lower rate.
Count actual owners and resource coordinates, exhibit one fixed production
function per instance, and prove that every comparison is a legal unilateral
change with all other owners fixed. The source-specific dual loads must be
exactly feasible. An additional finite optimum or an architecture-specific
failure does not meet this gate.

C. A GENUINELY CONTEXTUAL SELECTOR.
Supply an explicit invariant linking changes in incident coalition demands
to changes in contextual weights or payments. Prove eligibility on all real
profiles and a funding reduction with a stated general consequence. Check
analytically against the petal, multi-pool, and q-ary families already saved.
A rule excluded only by the stronger cover-uniform weight-comparison lemma
may still work when production couples the changes, but that coupling must
be proved, not assumed. A report-dependent cap has no positive bound here.

STANDARD COMPACTNESS BRIDGE: WHY A UNIFORM FINITE DUAL UPPER WOULD SUFFICE.
This is a classical finite-intersection argument, not an original theorem.
It clarifies the exact quantifier needed in route A.

Fix n,d and one admissible finite-valued technology f. Let beta(P) be the
optimal finite-profile LP factor for every finite P of actual real profiles,
including every comparable same-others pair within P. Define
                     B*=sup_{finite P} beta(P),
and let G* be the infimum of all-profile eligible funding factors.
Then G*=B*, and a global rule attaining this factor exists.
This claim is about deterministic existence only; it supplies neither an
algorithm nor a measurable selection, neither of which is in the contract.

Proof. Restricting any global rule to P gives B*<=G*. Also
0<=B*<=n: paying T_p to each owner is a global eligible n-factor rule.
If f is not identically zero, some profile has positive output and B*>=1;
the identically zero technology has B*=0 and the zero rule directly.
For every profile p and owner i take the compact interval [0,B* T_p], and
form their product over all profiles and owners. The finite-valued hypothesis
makes each interval compact; when T_p=0 it is the singleton {0}. Each budget,
coalition-cover and unilateral-monotonicity constraint is a closed cylinder
in this compact product. Any finite set of constraints mentions only a
finite profile set P. The finite LP attains its optimum: restrict beta to
[0,n] and x_ip to [0,n T_p], a finite compact feasible set. Since beta(P)<=B*,
its solution satisfies all constraints on P with factor B*; other profile
coordinates can be set to zero to obtain a point satisfying the selected
finite constraints. Therefore the closed cylinders have the finite
intersection property. Compactness gives a global point satisfying them
all with factor B*. Thus G*<=B*, establishing equality and attainment.

Finite LP strong duality and the saved ownerwise transport formulation now
show that a bound in route A for ALL finite profile sets implies an all-real
rule. A bound verified on one grid, finitely many grids, a restricted profile
family, or only one technology does not establish the general theorem.
This bridge changes no bound and cannot itself pass the continuation gate.

DECISION.
No route A, B, or C met its rate gate during this campaign. Preserve the new
sharp-in-owner-order architecture obstruction in the existing paper track;
seek specialist correctness, priority and relevance assessment separately.
An ambitious new campaign should begin only with a concrete proposed lemma
or certificate meeting one of A--C, rather than a broad mandate to vary
already-blocked selectors.
