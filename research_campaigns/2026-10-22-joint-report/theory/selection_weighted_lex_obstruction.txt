FIXED WEIGHT MIXTURES HAVE AN OMEGA(n/log n) FUNDING OBSTRUCTION
22 October 2026. Independent exact audit and fixed-law extension.
The coordinator/scout supplied the iid petal obstruction. This note verifies
its formula and extends it to arbitrary fixed joint weight distributions.
It supersedes the conjectural rate target in selection_weighted_lex_route.txt.

THEOREM. For r>=2, k=ceil(log_2(4r)), and n=rk+1, there is one fixed monotone,
concave, homogeneous production technology on d=n resources such that every
report-independent probability distribution on strictly positive, finite
owner weights has a unit-basis report profile at which its expected weighted
lexicographic cover costs at least (r+1)/8, while grand production and the
minimum total cover both equal 1. The distribution may depend on the fixed
technology and may be asymmetric, correlated, or discrete.

1. FIXED TECHNOLOGY AND FULL COALITION POLYHEDRON.
Index resources by a root 0 and r disjoint petals P_l, each of size k. Set
 f(X)=min{X_0, sum_{l=1}^r min_{j in P_l} X_j}.
Minimums of nonnegative linear functions are concave; sums of concave
functions are concave, and the minimum of two concave functions is concave
(its hypograph is their hypograph intersection). Thus f has every stated
production property and is finite everywhere.
Assign the n actual owners bijectively to these resources, giving each owner
the corresponding unit basis vector. A coalition produces 1 exactly when it
contains the root owner and every owner of at least one petal; otherwise it
produces 0. Consequently its entire nonnegative coalition-cover polyhedron is
 x_root + sum_{i in P_l} x_i >= 1, l=1,...,r; x>=0.
Grand production is 1. Paying the root 1 is an efficient cover.

2. EXACT WEIGHTED LEXICOGRAPHIC SOLUTION.
For deterministic positive owner weights, write w_0 for the root weight,
A_l=sum_{i in P_l}w_i, and m=min_l A_l. The first minimax level is
 delta=1/(w_0+m).
It freezes the root and all minimum-weight petals. The root payment is
w_0/(w_0+m). Every other petal must cover residual m/(w_0+m); after the root
is frozen these petal constraints are disjoint, so lexicographic minimization
gives exactly that total to each petal. Therefore
 sum_i L_w(v)_i=(w_0+r*m)/(w_0+m).
In particular m>=w_0 implies total >=(r+1)/2.

3. RANDOM ROLE ASSIGNMENT, WITH WEIGHTS HELD FIXED.
Take a uniformly random bijection from owner labels to the fixed resource
roles. Rank the deterministic weights increasingly, breaking ties by owner
label. Conditional on root rank t<=floor(n/2), a given petal consists entirely
of lower-ranked owners with probability
 binom(t-1,k)/binom(n-1,k) <= ((t-1)/(n-1))^k <= 2^(-k).
The bound remains valid when t-1<k, interpreting the numerator as zero.
If a petal has at least one owner ranked above the root, its weight sum is at
least w_0. By a union bound, with conditional probability at least
1-r*2^(-k)>=3/4, every petal has that property. Hence
 P_role(m>=w_0)>=3*floor(n/2)/(4n),
 E_role sum_i L_w(v_role)_i
   >=3*(r+1)*floor(n/2)/(8n)>=(r+1)/8.
The last step uses n>=3 and floor(n/2)/n>=1/3.

4. ARBITRARY FIXED MIXTURES AND QUANTIFIERS.
The preceding inequality holds pointwise for every positive vector w.
Integrate with respect to any report-independent joint distribution D of w,
then interchange the finite average over role assignments and expectation.
There is a single role assignment pi satisfying
 E_{W~D} sum_i L_W(v_pi)_i >=(r+1)/8.
The production function is the same for every assignment in this averaging
argument. We use different full report profiles only to select one profile
at which the universal budget guarantee fails. No underreport comparison
changes owners, endowments, or their ownership roles.

Since n=r*ceil(log_2(4r))+1, the lower bound is Omega(n/log n) on this infinite
sequence. It excludes every uniform O(sqrt n) funding guarantee within the
fixed-weight-mixture architecture, and therefore refutes the proposed
power-law O(log n) cost lemma, including laws tuned to n.

5. EXACT SCOPE.
This is an architecture lower bound, not an arbitrary-mechanism lower bound.
For the displayed unit ownership profile, paying the root is efficient.
Report-dependent weights or a different joint selector can escape this
argument. They need a separate all-profile own-monotonicity proof. We make
no assertion here about zero/infinite weights or unproved limiting rules.
