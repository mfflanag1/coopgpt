CAMPAIGN EXAMPLE, NOT A SOURCE ATTRIBUTION.
The coordinator's k-pool example is internally consistent: first pool(1,1),
remaining pools(4,1), f=min pool totals, 2k actual pure-coordinate owners.
At the full profile, low owners outside pool1 are forced to zero because
removing them leaves grand output2. Coalitions containing one pool1 owner
and every outside low owner then force both pool1 owners to receive1; the
efficient core is unique. After a selected outside high owner reports4->1,
the two small pools have a symmetric minimum-norm core allocation paying
each of their four owners1/2. Hence the actual own-report envelope totals
at least 2+(k-1)/2, a factor at least(k+3)/4 at output2. This is an envelope
architecture obstruction, not an arbitrary eligible-payment lower bound.
