EXACT COVER-UNIFORM CHARACTERIZATION OF ADMISSIBLE WEIGHT CHANGES
22 October 2026. Structural theorem; no arbitrary-mechanism lower bound.

THEOREM. Fix n>=2, an owner i, and strictly positive finite vectors w,w'.
The following statements are equivalent:
(A) For EVERY finite nonnegative coalition cover game v,
       L_{w'}(v)_i >= L_w(v)_i.
(B) There is c>0 such that w'_j=c w_j for all j!=i, and w'_i>=c w_i.
Here L is weighted lexicographic minimax. Zero-valued nonlisted rows may be
included; the hard examples below are monotone games with efficient covers.

SUFFICIENCY. Multiplying every weight by the same positive number changes
no selected payment. Divide w' by c and apply own-weight monotonicity from
dual_report_dependent_weights.txt.

NECESSITY, OFF-WEIGHT RATIOS. Suppose w'_-i is not proportional to w_-i.
Then n>=3 and there exist j,k!=i, after swapping their names if necessary,
  r=w_j/(w_j+w_k) < r'=w'_j/(w'_j+w'_k).
Choose explicitly
  a=r+(1/2) min{r'-r, w_i/(w_j+w_k)}.
Thus r<a<r'<1 and a<(w_i+w_j)/(w_j+w_k). Use only the two constraints
  x_i+x_j>=a,       x_j+x_k>=1.
Under w the second row has strictly higher first pinning ratio. It freezes
x_j=r and x_k=1-r, after which i pays a-r>0. Under w' the second row again
has strictly higher first ratio, since
  a<r'<(w'_i+w'_j)/(w'_j+w'_k).
It freezes x_j=r'>a, so i pays 0. This contradicts (A), regardless of how
large w'_i is. All remaining owners pay 0.

The full monotone game can be defined as the maximum demand among contained
displayed rows, or 0 if neither is contained. It has grand value 1 and
efficient cover e_j, and is realized at a unit basis profile by the fixed
monotone concave homogeneous technology
  f(z_i,y_j,z_k)=min{y_j, a z_i+z_k}.
For example the full three-owner coalition has value 1, and its coverage row
is already implied by x_j+x_k>=1. Thus the example is not using an arbitrary
cover system excluded from the production model.

NECESSITY, OWN RELATIVE WEIGHT. Once w'_-i=c w_-i, if w'_i<c w_i, the single
unit row {i,j} yields x_i=w_i/(w_i+w_j) and x'_i=w'_i/(w'_i+w'_j)<x_i.
This proves the theorem, including n=2 (where off proportionality is
automatic). With n=1 every selected payment is independent of weights.

COROLLARY: BLACK-BOX ADAPTIVITY IS PROJECTIVELY OWN-ONLY WHEN n>=3.
Let W(b)>0 be a weight map on the whole product report domain. Suppose that
for every owner-i increase b->b', its weight comparison satisfies (A) for
every fixed cover game v. Then there are positive componentwise
nondecreasing own-only functions g_i(b_i) and a positive common factor C(b)
such that
  W_i(b)=C(b) W_i(0) g_i(b_i),       g_i(0)=1.
Thus the resulting selector is identical to one using own-only weights.

Proof. By the theorem every own-i change preserves all off-i weight ratios.
Hence W_j/W_k depends only on b_j,b_k: alter all other reports from zero one
at a time. Define g_i(b_i) by the change in W_i/W_j at the profile with only
i's report nonzero. This definition does not depend on j!=i because the
ratios between any two off-i weights are preserved. To evaluate W_i/W_j at
a general profile, choose k distinct from i,j and write it as
(W_i/W_k)/(W_j/W_k) at the profile with only i,j nonzero. The first ratio is
independent of b_j and the second of b_i. Therefore
  W_i(b)/W_j(b)=[W_i(0)/W_j(0)] g_i(b_i)/g_j(b_j).
This gives the common factor C. The theorem's own-relative inequality gives
the componentwise monotonicity of each g_i. The converse follows from the
own-only sufficient condition. The n>=3 hypothesis is essential for this
factorization argument; no such statement is asserted for n=2.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMIT. Increasing i's relative weight against every
other individual is NOT enough: changing the relative weights of two other
owners can cause one to cover i's incident demand and drive i's payment to
zero. A report-dependent law that genuinely changes off-owner ratios needs
a production-specific coupling argument relating those changes to the
incident RHS increases. A cover-uniform argument that ignores this coupling
cannot establish its eligibility. This theorem does NOT say that every
technology-dependent adaptive weight map is impossible: its failure game
need not coincide with the technology at the profile pair generating the
weight change. Nor does the factorization by itself yield a funding bound.
