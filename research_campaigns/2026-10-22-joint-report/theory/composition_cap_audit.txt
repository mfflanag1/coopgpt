1. EXACT INPUT NEEDED FROM THE OLD CONSTRUCTION

Fix an integer Q>=3 and let
 d=16jQ(Q-1)(Q+2), h=8jQ(Q-1), n=Q^d, j>=1.
Write r=(Q-1)d and m=n-r-1. The old full row family is the complements
of ALL independent s-sets in the Q-ary axis-line hypergraph, with
 s=(Q-2)d+h+K,
 K=floor(beta0*m), beta0=((Q-2)+h/d)/(Q-1)+1/[4Q(Q-1)].
Its fixed-cardinality distribution, translated to a focal vertex i, gives
a finite rational distribution alpha over retained incident rows, with
coordinate marginals

  i: 1; each of its r neighbors: a0=(d-h)/r;
  each of its m outside vertices: b=1-K/m.

Every supported incident row contains EXACTLY d-h neighbors. Every row
not containing i contains AT LEAST d neighbors: its complementary
independent set contains i and must omit another point on every axis line
through i. All off-i rows are retained. Unsupported incident rows are the
only rows deleted. The old exact scalar inequality is

  h(a0-b) > 1-b > 0.                                  (G)

Adjoining one common veto u to every generator preserves these counts.
The generator game and full coalition-cover polyhedron agree for
nonnegative payments. The old direct finite min-linear realization uses
the SAME actual owners, bundles, and technology for every translated
private-owner control underreport. It makes each displayed deletion game
exactly the induced game at that actual unilateral report. Both full and
selected profiles have production one because there is a retained unit
generator. No unretained demand-one constraint is silently used below.

2. FEASIBILITY AT THE NATURAL CAP

Define D=d+h and

  p=h/D=1/(2Q+5), z=1/D,
  C_Q=(r+2h)/D=(2Q^2+2Q-2)/(2Q+5).

At the selected focal underreport let q_u=q_i=p, every neighbor receive z,
and every outside private owner receive zero. The total is exactly C_Q.
Every retained supported own row receives

  q_u+q_i+(d-h)z=2p+(d-h)z=1.

Every off-i row receives at least q_u+d z=1. Deleted rows have RHS zero
and nonnegative payment. Thus ALL generator inequalities hold and hence
all coalition-cover inequalities hold, including empty and singleton
coalitions. In particular no check of only the support rows is substituted
for the full off-owner feasibility requirement.

3. INDEPENDENT OPTIMALITY CERTIFICATE

Use the objective (1/2)||q||^2. Set

  L=(p-z)/(1-a0), eta=L-p.

Here h>1, so L>0. Assign multiplier L alpha_R to the retained supported
rows, zero to all other generator constraints, and eta to the upper-budget
constraint. On the two positive focal/veto coordinates, stationarity is
L-eta=p; on each positive neighbor it is L a0-eta=z.

For outside zero coordinates choose nonnegativity multiplier

  nu=eta-Lb=L(1-b)-p.

It is strictly positive, because

  nu>0
  iff (h-1)(1-b)>h(1-a0)
  iff h(a0-b)>1-b.

This is precisely (G), with no strengthened distribution assumption.
It also implies eta>0 since Lb>0. All positive row multipliers are on
tight retained rows, the budget is tight, and the outside coordinates
are zero. Therefore every KKT sign and complementarity condition holds.

There is also a direct quadratic certificate avoiding any constraint-
qualification issue. Let a be the sum of the row incidence vectors with
weights L alpha_R, including the veto coordinate; let nu denote the
nonnegative vector supported on outside coordinates. Then

  q=a-eta*1+nu.

For any feasible competitor y, row feasibility, the upper budget, and
y>=0 imply

  q.y >= L-eta C_Q = ||q||^2.

The equality on the right follows either from tightness or by substituting
2p^2+r z^2. Consequently

  ||y||^2-||q||^2
    =||y-q||^2+2q.(y-q) >= ||y-q||^2.

Thus q is the unique minimum-norm capped cover. The outside multiplier
sign is important: stationarity is q=a-eta*1+nu, not q=a-eta*1-nu.

For Q=3 the cap is exactly two and p=1/11. Translating the selected
underreport to each of the n private owners proves true envelope funding
at least n/11 at the ONE full profile with N=n+1 and T=1. Taking a
supremum over additional real underreports can only increase this lower
bound; it need not preserve the selected support or the candidate vector.

4. WHY THE OLD TRANSPORT REMAINS VALID WHEN d GROWS

Fix Q and vary the positive integer j. Then h/d, a0, mu_old, delta and
beta0 are constant, while h delta=2j>=2. The local repair construction
only requires integral h and 0<h<d, both valid. The far-color counts and
all-cut concavity reduction are unchanged formulas in d. The old proof's
uniform estimate

  Q^(d-1)>100 Q^4 d^2

continues to hold for all these d, and supplies the same three sufficient
transport corner inequalities, positivity of all far classes, m>h, and
m>1/delta. Flooring gives beta=K/m>=beta0-1/m, so

  h(a0-b)-(1-b)=h(beta-mu_old)-beta
    >=h delta-h/m-beta0 > 2-1-1=0.

The strict last inequality uses h/m<1 and beta0<1; equality in h delta>=2
causes no problem. Thus the finite rational distribution exists and its
needed strict scalar gap holds for every j. This is a proof for an
unbounded dimension sequence, not extrapolation from finitely many
numerical cases. Alphabet Q need not be prime.

5. ARBITRARY FIXED CAP C>1: FORCED ADDITIVE OWNER

Since C_Q grows unboundedly, choose ONE fixed Q with C_Q>C. (For example,
Q=ceil(C)+2 suffices.) Put

  A=(C_Q-C)/(C-1)>0.

Adjoin one new resource Y and one new actual owner, with full bundle A
units of Y and no old resources. Old owners have zero Y. The new technology
is F_new=F_old+Y, a finite minimum of nonnegative linear forms with Y
coefficient one in every form. All old owners retain their fixed old
bundles. The new actual report space is still all nonnegative real bundle
vectors; only these specified profiles are used for the lower bound.

At the full profile and every selected OLD private-owner underreport the
induced coalition game is an additive direct sum of the old game and an
owner of worth A. Its cover constraints factor EXACTLY into:
 (i) the old coordinates cover every old coalition;
 (ii) the new coordinate is at least A.
These two conditions also imply every mixed coalition constraint.

At a minimum-norm solution the new coordinate equals A: reducing any
excess preserves every cover, relaxes the upper budget, and reduces the
objective. Hence the remaining old-coordinate budget is exactly

  C(1+A)-A=C_Q.

The remaining objective differs from the old squared norm by the constant
A^2. It follows that the old optimizer is precisely the vector certified
in Section 3, not merely a feasible vector. Equivalently, the additive
singleton constraint has multiplier A+eta in the half-squared-norm KKT
system; all mixed-coalition multipliers may be zero.

The true envelope therefore has, at the one full profile,

  (sum_i M_i)/(1+A) >= n*p/(1+A)
      = [(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)] n.                         (L)

Here N=n+2, and Q,A are fixed before j tends to infinity. For every N0,
some finite j gives N>=N0. Formula (L) is Omega_C(N) along this unbounded
sequence of actual owner counts; it does not assert every prescribed N.
No limiting interchange or growing-alphabet assumption is needed.

The architecture is well defined on ALL real profiles: any active
nonnegative supporting form gives an efficient coalition cover, hence a
feasible point for cap C>=1. Strict convexity gives a unique seed. At a
zero-output profile the cap forces the zero seed. Its true envelope is
finite because each seed coordinate at an underreport is at most C times
that profile's output, which is at most C times full output. Thus the
linear lower bound is consistent with the trivial O_C(N) envelope upper.

6. CAP ONE AND SCOPE

At C=1, the separate seed barrier already applies. Its proof needs only
covering and total seed payment <=T, not a particular objective. Taking
m=2 in selection_seed_barrier_below_two.txt gives, for k pools and N=2k
owners, a full profile with normalized envelope funding at least
1+(k-1)/4. The paired own-report edges, coalition averages, and seed-budget
subtraction in that proof are valid, so C=1 also has linear obstruction.

The present statement addresses every FIXED finite C>=1. It does not
prove the behavior for a cap selected as a function of N, and it imposes
no claim on a different joint selection rule. The base and padded examples
admit the stated efficient competing rule on their fixed-bundle downward
report domain: pay old output to the old common veto, and additive output
to the new owner. This local competing rule is not asserted to work when
unrelated owners report arbitrary veto-resource coordinates elsewhere in
the global report space. The counterexample concerns the specified seed's
true envelope at the designated full profiles, not every base profile.

The previous many-block cap-two estimate is compatible with this result:
it uses B of order k^2/n, whereas this construction uses ONE block. Its
clipping argument x_i<=1-u and unchanged-block total lower bounds are
valid when their stated denominator is positive. They cannot bound the
one-block example, where that denominator condition fails.
