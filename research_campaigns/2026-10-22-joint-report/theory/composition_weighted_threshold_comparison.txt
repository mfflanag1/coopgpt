WEIGHTED THRESHOLDS, AND WHY THE RATE ALSO FOLLOWS FROM GRAND MARGINALS
22 October 2026. Addendum; composition_threshold_mechanism.txt remains frozen.

1. Weighted-row statement

Let A be a fixed finite nonempty family of nonnegative coefficient vectors,
and F(B)=min_{a in A} a.B. If A contains the zero vector, F is identically
zero and the zero payment rule settles the problem. Otherwise discard
resource coordinates r with w_r=max_{a in A} a_r=0, and let d_eff denote
the number remaining. Define

  kappa = min_{a in A} sum_r a_r/w_r,
  beta = min{d_eff, 1+d_eff-kappa},
  tau(B) = min_{a in A} max_r a_r B_r,
  X_i^tau = sum_r min{w_r b_ir, tau(B)b_ir/B_r},
  Y_i = sum_r min{w_r b_ir, F(B)b_ir/B_r}.

Terms with B_r=0 are zero. Every formula uses the same fixed actual
owners, and accepts arbitrary nonnegative real vector reports. Both X^tau
and Y are nonnegative, cover all owner coalitions, are own-monotone, and
have total payment at most beta F(B). Moreover X_i^tau<=Y_i pointwise.

The parameter depends on the chosen finite representation. Removing
redundant coefficient rows before evaluating it can improve the guarantee.
No assertion that kappa is a technology invariant is made. A common
positive resourcewise rescaling leaves the coefficient ratios unchanged.

2. Proof for X^tau and Y

Both tau and F are nonnegative and coordinatewise nondecreasing. With all
other reports fixed, b_ir/B_r is nondecreasing in the own coordinate; its
0/0 value is zero. Hence the product with either threshold is nondecreasing
in the entire own vector. Taking a minimum with w_r b_ir and then a sum
preserves own monotonicity.

For X^tau choose a bottleneck-minimizing row a. For every coordinate r,
a_r B_r<=tau and a_r<=w_r. Consequently

  min{w_r b_ir,tau b_ir/B_r} >= a_r b_ir.

Summing over an arbitrary owner coalition S proves coverage, since the
right side is a.(sum_{i in S} b_i)>=F(sum_{i in S} b_i). Coordinates with
B_r=0 have b_ir=0 and satisfy the same inequality. For Y choose a sum-
minimizing row a: a_r B_r<=a.B=F(B), so the same argument applies.

Since tau<=max_r a_r B_r<=a.B=F(B) for a sum-minimizing row, X^tau<=Y.
Total Y payment equals sum_r min{w_r B_r,F(B)}. Set c_r=a_r/w_r in [0,1].
The elementary inequality min{z,T}<=c z+(1-c)T gives

  sum_i Y_i <= a.B + (d_eff-sum_r a_r/w_r)F(B)
             <= (1+d_eff-kappa)F(B).

The separate bound sum_i Y_i<=d_eff F(B) gives the stated minimum. At zero
output all thresholds and payments vanish. Both rules are homogeneous.

3. This same bound applies to the EXISTING grand-marginal rule

Let U_i=F(B)-F(B-b_i), the rule already described under "Baseline, selection
and remaining gap" in inputs/manuscript.tex. It is nonnegative and own-
monotone, since its second term is independent of the owner's report.
For completeness, a minimizing full-profile coefficient row a gives

  F(B-b_i)<=a.(B-b_i)=F(B)-a.b_i,

so U_i>=a.b_i and the payments cover every owner coalition. This is an
all-profile statement, including all zero and boundary reports.

In fact the following pointwise comparison holds:

  U_i <= Y_i.

To prove it, remove owner i's coordinates one at a time. Immediately
before removing coordinate r, call the remaining full resource vector C.
Its rth coordinate is still B_r, and C<=B. If B_r>0 put s=b_ir/B_r in [0,1].
Concavity, applied to the two vectors C and C-B_r e_r, yields

  F(C)-F(C-b_ir e_r)
    <= s [F(C)-F(C-B_r e_r)]
    <= s min{w_r B_r,F(B)}.

For the second line, monotonicity and nonnegativity give a bound F(C)<=F(B).
Also F is coordinatewise w_r-Lipschitz: a row attaining the minimum at
C-B_r e_r proves F(C)<=F(C-B_r e_r)+w_r B_r. The case B_r=0 contributes
zero. Summing the successive losses telescopes to U_i and proves U_i<=Y_i.
Therefore the same beta bound holds for the manuscript's existing rule U.

No relation X^tau<=U or U<=X^tau is asserted. For example with rows
{1,2} and {3,4}, totals (1,1,0,3/2), and one owner per coordinate,
X^tau=(1,1,0,1) while U=(1/2,1/2,0,3/2); neither dominates the other.

This comparison means the structural factor is an analysis refinement of
an existing mechanism, not evidence that a new mechanism architecture is
needed or that the unrestricted campaign gate has been passed.

4. Unit forms and the order-statistic example

For unit forms, all nonzero w_r equal one and kappa is the minimum row
cardinality among the retained resources. The factor reduces to
d_eff-k_min+1. In particular, sum of the k smallest among d totals has
factor d-k+1 under U, Y, and X^tau, on ALL real vector-report profiles.

The contract's generic recorded factor is

  min{d, n sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2 sqrt(d)},

and the manuscript additionally has a finite-form-count guarantee. The
deficiency factor can be numerically smaller for a special technology;
one selects an entire rule in advance using the public technology and n,d.
Pointwise minima of different complete payment vectors are not claimed
eligible. There is no unrestricted asymptotic improvement because kappa
can be arbitrarily small and d-k_min can be of order d.

The constant-codimension example is not simply a linear resource-dimension
reduction. For 1<=k<d, the k-subset coefficient vectors span R^d: differences
of two subsets exchanging r,s span the zero-sum subspace, and any subset
vector has nonzero coordinate sum. Each is uniquely active at some strictly
positive vector (put totals 1 on that subset and 2 outside). If the function
factored as G(LB) for a linear map L:R^d->R^m, then every differentiable
gradient would lie in the row span of L. Equivalently, it is locally
constant along ker L at those strictly positive points. Thus rank L>=d.
One cannot explain the bound by a literal lower-dimensional linear
description of the same technology. This does NOT rule out other known
structural arguments or establish literature priority.

5. Scope of the useful obstruction

Mixed bundles do preserve a strict joint-report gap (see the separate
composition_mixed_resources.txt). But for any fixed c, technologies that
sum the d-c smallest totals admit the constant c+1 guarantee regardless
of n,d and regardless of overlapping actual ownership. Thus that family
cannot support a growing arbitrary-mechanism lower bound at fixed c.
To use growing codimension as a hardness parameter, a lower construction
must still respect the generic square-root upper and the complete joint-
report constraints; the present derivation supplies no such construction.

6. Verification and provenance

The theorem uses only elementary finite min-linear geometry and concavity.
The existing grand-marginal mechanism and its eligibility are explicitly
present in the supplied inputs/manuscript.tex; no claim that the displayed
refined bound is absent from prior literature is made. The companion
composition_weighted_threshold_check.py checks all small rational bundle
profiles in its listed fixtures, all coalitions and legal own-coordinate
edges, and U<=Y, X^tau<=Y, and the two budget inequalities. Its complete
finite diagnostics supplement, rather than establish, the all-real proof.
