EXTRA GROUP PROPERTY OF THE RESOURCE THRESHOLD RULES
22 October 2026. This property is beyond, and does not change, the contract.

Use the weighted setup of composition_weighted_threshold_comparison.txt.
Fix a coalition S of actual owners and keep all outside reports fixed.
Let A_r=sum_{i in S}b_ir and C_r=sum_{i notin S}b_ir. For either threshold
q(B)=tau(B) or q(B)=F(B), the coalition's total payment is exactly

  sum_{i in S}X_i^q
    = sum_r min{w_r A_r, q(A+C) A_r/(A_r+C_r)}.

This follows by factoring the common resource price
min{w_r,q(B)/B_r} out of the sum over coalition members. Terms with
A_r+C_r=0 are zero, consistently on both sides.

For fixed C, q(A+C) and A_r/(A_r+C_r) are coordinatewise nondecreasing
in A, using the same zero convention as in the individual proof. The
displayed total is therefore coordinatewise nondecreasing in the aggregate
coalition report A. In particular simultaneous coordinatewise underreports
by any coalition cannot increase that coalition's TOTAL payment. The
claim does not say that every individual member's payment is monotone
when other members change their reports.

The total depends only on A and C, not on how A is distributed among
coalition members. If the same formula is applied to different owner
partitions, splitting or merging the coalition's aggregate bundle leaves
its total payment unchanged. This is an algebraic property of the formula,
not permission to reassign owners in a lower-bound certificate. The
campaign's actual owners and production technology remain fixed.

This extra property distinguishes the threshold rule from grand marginals,
although both have the deficiency budget bound. For F(B)=min{B_1,B_2},
start with two actual reports b_1=(2,0), b_2=(0,1). The grand marginals
are (1,1). If owner 2 increases its report to (1,1), they become (0,1),
so the grand coalition's total falls from 2 to 1 at unchanged output 1.
The threshold rule's total is 2 at both profiles. Own monotonicity alone
does not forbid the grand-marginal decline because owner 1's report did
not change. The separate exact checker verifies this example.

Scope: these observations furnish no new arbitrary-mechanism lower bound.
One cannot impose group-total monotonicity or partition invariance on
competing rules when proving a lower bound for the original contract.
Likewise no literature priority or general asymptotic improvement is
claimed. Their potential use is a stronger positive guarantee for the
same dense-unit-form or small-weighted-deficiency technologies.
