A JOINT THRESHOLD RULE FOR UNIT-COEFFICIENT RESOURCE FORMS
22 October 2026. Independent structural derivation; no priority claim.

1. Statement and scope

Let R be any finite nonempty family of nonempty subsets of [d], and let

  F(B) = min_{D in R} sum_{r in D} B_r,
  k_min = min_{D in R} |D|.

There are n fixed actual owners. Owner i reports an arbitrary nonnegative
resource bundle b_i in R_+^d; ownership may overlap in every resource.
The following ONE deterministic rule is nonnegative, covers every owner
coalition, is monotone under every real coordinatewise own-report increase,
and has budget factor d-k_min+1 at EVERY profile. It is homogeneous.
There is no scalar-ray, endpoint, generic-position, or matroid assumption.

Write B_r=sum_i b_ir and define

  tau(B) = min_{D in R} max_{r in D} B_r,
  x_i(b) = sum_r min{b_ir, tau(B) b_ir/B_r},

where a summand with B_r=0 is defined to be zero. Equivalently, the total
payment assigned to resource r is min{B_r,tau}, shared proportionally
among its actual contributors. One need not choose or report a particular
minimizing row to define this rule; ties cause no ambiguity.

2. All-real own-report monotonicity

Fix the reports of all owners other than i and put C_r=sum_{j!=i} b_jr.
The function tau is coordinatewise nondecreasing because every max in its
definition is coordinatewise nondecreasing and the family is fixed.
The ratio z/(z+C_r) is nondecreasing in z>=0. When C_r=0, define its value
at z=0 to be zero; it jumps to one at z>0 and is still nondecreasing.
Thus both b_ir and tau(B)b_ir/(b_ir+C_r) are nonnegative nondecreasing
functions of the ENTIRE own-report vector b_i, coordinatewise. Their
minimum is nondecreasing, and so is their sum. This proves the required
inequality for arbitrary real vector underreports, including zero entries.

3. Every owner coalition is covered

At the given full profile choose a row D attaining tau. For every r in D,
B_r<=tau, so resource r pays each contribution b_ir in full. Therefore for
every owner coalition S (including the empty coalition),

  sum_{i in S} x_i >= sum_{r in D} sum_{i in S} b_ir
                   >= F(sum_{i in S} b_i).

The last inequality uses D as one candidate in the minimum defining F.
This proof does not split or reassign owners, and it uses the same full-
profile threshold for every coalition.

4. Budget and zero output

Summing proportional shares gives the exact identity

  sum_i x_i = sum_r min{B_r,tau}.

Choose a row E minimizing the SUM of full resource totals. This row may
differ from the row D minimizing the MAX used for coalition coverage.
Since all coefficients are nonnegative and E is nonempty,

  tau <= max_{r in E} B_r <= sum_{r in E} B_r = F(B).

Consequently

  sum_i x_i <= sum_{r in E} B_r + (d-|E|)tau
             <= F(B)+(d-k_min)F(B)
              = (d-k_min+1)F(B).

If F(B)=0, some row has all its totals zero, so tau=0 and all payments
vanish. Conversely tau=0 implies F(B)=0. This addresses profiles with zero
output and arbitrary positive reports outside the zero row. An empty
generator row, excluded in the theorem, would make F identically zero;
that separate technology simply admits the zero rule.

5. Sum of the k smallest resource totals

For the family of ALL k-subsets, tau is the kth order statistic and

  sum_i x_i = F(B)+(d-k)tau <= (d-k+1)F(B).

Thus fixed codimension d-k gives a constant all-profile guarantee even
under arbitrary mixed ownership and for any number of owners. At k=d
the rule is exactly efficient. At k=d-1 it has factor two. This rules out
using this particular fixed-codimension class to obtain an arbitrary-
mechanism funding lower bound that diverges with n.

The factor is sharp for THIS RULE: take k-1 totals zero and d-k+1 totals
one. Then F=tau=1 and the rule's total payment is d-k+1. Positive totals
approaching this profile give the same limiting ratio. This is NOT a
lower bound on all mechanisms; competing rules could do better.

6. Comparison and limitations

The campaign contract records the general upper
min{d, n sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2 sqrt(d)}. The displayed mechanism improves the
d term to d-k_min+1 for this class, and improves the recorded numerical
guarantee whenever that quantity is smaller than its other terms. It has
no favorable general-rate consequence when k_min is small. For a fixed
technology one may choose, in advance, the best available complete rule;
no profile-dependent switching of payment vectors is claimed safe.

For the recursive four-block technology f_h there are d=n=4^h physical
resources and each unit form has 2^h resources. This theorem only gives
n-sqrt(n)+1, weaker than the recorded general upper. Therefore it does
not bypass the joint-report continuation gate or establish a new
unrestricted rate. Exact equivalence with a known mechanism or prior
literature is not asserted; the proof above is self-contained.

The assertion is about literal unit-coefficient forms after any fixed
resourcewise rescaling common to ALL rows. Arbitrary row-dependent
coefficients require a separate construction; replacing each coefficient
by one in this proof is invalid.

7. Verification

composition_threshold_check.py independently evaluates rational payments
on complete small bundle grids, checks every coalition and every adjacent
legal own-coordinate edge, checks the exact total-payment identity and
the claimed budget, and includes zero profiles and ties. It also checks
that the bottleneck and sum minimizing rows need not agree. These finite
tests diagnose implementation errors; Sections 2-4 establish the all-real
theorem and do not depend on a solver or grid extrapolation.
