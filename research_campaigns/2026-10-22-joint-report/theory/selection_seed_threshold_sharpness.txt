THE FINITE PAIR-POOL TEST HAS AN EXACT 3/2 SEED THRESHOLD
22 October 2026. A finite-family diagnostic, not a mechanism construction.

For k>=3, consider only the F_j,L_ij profiles in
selection_low_subsidy_seed_barrier.txt. At F_j choose its efficient allocation
(1,1) on the unique small pool j. At each L_ij choose any third pool ell
outside {i,j}, and pay its high owner 2 and its low owner 1, paying everyone
else zero. This is a nonnegative coalition cover: any coalition's production
is at most 2 and at most its capacity from pool ell. A coalition containing
the high owner receives at least 2; one containing only the low owner from
that pool receives 1, which bounds its production; one containing neither
has zero production. Its total cost is 3=(3/2)T.

The selected payments to both high owners i and j at L_ij are zero. Thus all
the pair charges q_hi(L_ij)+q_hj(L_ij) driving the previous lower bound vanish
at B=3/2. At each F_j the only lower-profile comparisons included in this
finite test therefore impose no added high-owner payment beyond its own
efficient allocation. The linear seed-envelope inequality for B<3/2 is
sharp as a statement derived from these particular finite pair comparisons.

This assignment is NOT asserted to extend to an eligible all-profile rule.
Other reports, other own-report comparisons, or larger profile families can
still obstruct it. In particular this is not a funding upper bound for the
original problem or for any complete envelope mechanism.
