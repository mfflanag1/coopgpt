ANY SEED FUNDED BELOW 3/2 CAN HAVE LINEAR OWN-ENVELOPE INFLATION
22 October 2026. Coordinator's tradeoff, independently verified.

Use the same fixed k-pool technology and the finite profiles F_j,L_ij from
selection_efficient_core_envelope_barrier.txt. There are n=2k actual owners,
two per pool, and grand production is 2 at every displayed profile.
Let q be ANY nonnegative covering allocation chosen at every profile, with
total at most 2(1+epsilon) on these finite profiles, where 0<=epsilon<1/2.
No norm objective, continuity, symmetry, or efficient-core membership is
assumed. If q is randomized, use its expected allocation and assume the same
expected total bound. Define its actual own-report envelope
 E_i(b)=sup_{0<=a_i<=b_i}q_i(a_i,b_-i).

At either F_j or L_ij let L be the total payment to low owners in the LARGE
pools, meaning those with capacities (4,1). The coalition including all
small-pool owners and all large-pool high owners has worth 2. Coverage and
the total budget imply L<=2epsilon.

At F_j, either one small-pool owner together with all large-pool low owners
forms a coalition of worth 1. Thus each small-pool payment is at least
1-L>=1-2epsilon, and their combined payment is at least 2-4epsilon.

At L_ij, the coalition containing the high owner from EACH small pool and
all large-pool low owners has worth 1. Consequently
 q_{h_i}(L_ij)+q_{h_j}(L_ij)>=1-L>=1-2epsilon.
As L_ij is a genuine own underreport for h_i from F_j, and for h_j from F_i,
the same pair-averaging argument gives
 (1/k)sum_j sum_owner E_owner(F_j)
   >= (2-4epsilon)+(k-1)(1-2epsilon)/2
   = (1-2epsilon)(k+3)/2.
Some full profile F_j therefore has envelope funding factor at least
 (1-2epsilon)(k+3)/4=(3-2B)(n+6)/8,
where B=1+epsilon is the seed funding factor.

This is linear in n for every fixed B<3/2. The epsilon=0 case recovers the
efficient-core-selector theorem. It also shows that imposing a seed budget
cap below 3/2 cannot repair the unconstrained minimum-norm-cover envelope.
No conclusion is asserted at B=3/2 or above. This remains an architecture
lower bound; arbitrary eligible mechanisms are not required to be envelopes
of low-subsidy covering seeds.
