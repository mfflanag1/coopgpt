SURVIVING CANDIDATE: A 2-BUDGET MINIMUM-NORM COVER SEED
22 October 2026. Candidate only; NO general funding theorem established.
The initial 3/2-cap candidate was refuted by the subsequent multi-owner
extension in selection_seed_barrier_below_two.txt. This note now records
the surviving cap-2 version and retains its exact pool calibration.

For a profile b with T>0, choose the unique minimizer q(b) of ||x||_2^2 over
 x>=0, x(S)>=v_b(S) for all S, sum_i x_i <= 2T.
The feasible set is nonempty because an efficient core allocation exists.
At T=0 choose zero. Take the actual own-report envelope of q. This envelope
is nonnegative, covering, and own-monotone by its definition; its budget is
the unresolved question. The preceding efficient-core proof does not apply
because q is not required to dominate an efficient core, and the earlier
unconstrained cover-projection obstruction does not directly accommodate
the additional total-payment cap.

The exact seed lower bound in selection_seed_barrier_below_two.txt rules

The multi-owner pair-profile tests approach threshold2. This motivates the

EXACT CALIBRATION ON ALL {1,4} POOL STATES.
Use k pools of two owners each, with s>=1 small pools of capacities (1,1)
and m=k-s large pools of capacities (4,1). Grand production is 2.
Symmetry forces the unique minimum-norm cover to pay a to every small-pool
owner, h to every large high owner, and l to every large low owner. Put
 D=2m+5s. The solution is
 a=5/D, h=4/D, l=2/D.
Every positive coalition has value either 1 or 2. A worth-1 coalition
contains an owner from each small pool and an owner from each large pool;
its minimum possible selected payment is sa+ml=1, since h>=l. A worth-2
coalition includes both owners from every small pool and a high owner from
every large pool, whose minimum selected payment is 2sa+mh=2.
The displayed vector therefore covers all coalitions.

For optimality, any symmetric cover obeys sa+ml>=1 and 2sa+mh>=2. Its squared
norm is 2s*a^2+m*h^2+m*l^2. The positive KKT multipliers for these two rows
are respectively 4/D and 8/D: 4a=4/D+2*(8/D), 2l=4/D, 2h=8/D.
Thus it minimizes even this relaxed symmetric program, and so the full
cover problem. The total is
 2sa+m(h+l)=3-sa<=3=(3/2)T.
Consequently the additional budget cap does not alter the minimizer on any
of these states. For m=0 the formula gives a=1/s and total2 directly.
This avoids the LINEAR pair-profile efficient-core pathology; it does not
bound the true envelope over all real underreports.

In particular one must handle changing grand production and preserve the
