EVERY FIXED POWER r>1 AND FIXED FUNDING CAP C>=1 HAS LINEAR ENVELOPE COST
22 October 2026. Extension of the frozen quadratic certificate.
Coordinator supplied the power multiplier calculation; verified below.
This is a selection-plus-envelope architecture obstruction only.

THEOREM.
Fix any real power r>1 and any finite constant cap C>=1. At each real
profile, select the unique nonnegative coalition cover minimizing sum_i x_i^r
subject to sum_i x_i<=C*T, and take its actual own-report envelope. There
are arbitrarily large owner counts, along a sequence, and fixed finite
min-linear production instances at which this envelope has funding factor
Omega_C(N). The constant in the lower bound can be chosen independently
of r; the dimension needed to begin the sequence can depend on r. Nothing
is claimed for a cap or power varying with N, or for arbitrary mechanisms.

1. INHERITED FINITE PRODUCTION INPUT.
Use the same q-ary family, ONE common-veto block, row deletion witnesses,
and exact direct production realization as in selection_cap_two_analysis.txt.
Its inherited source is the previous campaign's
proofs/FINAL_ENVELOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt, Parts I, II, and III.B.
Fix Q>=3 and choose a positive integer j. Put
 d=16jQ(Q-1)(Q+2), h=8jQ(Q-1), n=Q^d,
 m=n-(Q-1)d-1, delta=1/[4Q(Q-1)].
The supported incident-row mixture alpha has focal marginal1, every
neighbor marginal a0=(d-h)/[(Q-1)d], and every outside marginal b=1-beta.
The construction uses beta=K/m with K=floor(beta0*m), where
 beta0=mu_old+delta and a0=1-mu_old.
Thus
 a0-b=beta-mu_old>=delta-1/m.
The inherited estimates give m>h>=8Q(Q-1)=2/delta. Consequently
                  a0-b>delta/2.                         (A)
Also 0<b<1. The finite rational row mixture exists for EVERY positive j;
its transport proof is unchanged along these multiples of d0.

Every supported own row has exactly d-h neighbors, and every off-focal
row has at least d neighbors. Retained own support rows and all off rows
have demand1; other own rows are deleted to0. Every translated witness

                  h^(r-1)>=2/delta.                     (B)

3. PRIMAL VECTOR AND POWER CERTIFICATE.
Put D=d+h, p=h/D, z=1/D, and C_Q=[(Q-1)d+2h]/D.
Use the SAME primal vector as in the quadratic proof:
 veto=p, focal=p, all (Q-1)d neighbors=z, all outside coordinates0.
The total is C_Q. Every retained own row receives
2p+(d-h)z=1; every off row receives at least p+d*z=1. Deleted rows have
demand0. This proves feasibility for the entire coalition cover.

Use the objective Phi(x)=(1/r)sum_i x_i^r, which has the same minimizer as
the stated objective. Let
 lambda=[p^(r-1)-z^(r-1)]/(1-a0),
 tau=lambda-p^(r-1).
Assign row multipliers lambda*alpha_R to the retained support, zero to
other rows, and tau to the budget constraint sum x<=C_Q.
Since h>1, lambda>0. The outside stationarity slack is
 nu=tau-lambda*b.
Its sign is determined EXACTLY by
 nu>0
 iff [p^(r-1)-z^(r-1)]*(1-b)>p^(r-1)*(1-a0)
 iff p^(r-1)*(a0-b)>z^(r-1)*(1-b)
 iff h^(r-1)*(a0-b)>1-b.
This is(C). In particular tau>0, since lambda*b>0.
Stationarity at the positive coordinates is
 lambda-tau=p^(r-1)                     (veto and focal),
 lambda*a0-tau=z^(r-1)                 (every neighbor).
At outside zero coordinates, the derivative of Phi is zero because r>1,
and the nonnegativity multiplier is nu>0. Complementarity holds on every
row and on the budget. Thus the full KKT certificate is valid.

A direct convexity proof avoids any constraint-qualification issue. Let a
be the generator-incidence mean with weights lambda*alpha_R, including
the veto. Let nu_vec have value nu on outside coordinates and zero elsewhere.
Then grad Phi(q)=a-tau*1+nu_vec. For ANY feasible competitor y,
 grad Phi(q).y >= lambda-tau*C_Q = grad Phi(q).q,
by row coverage, the upper budget, and nonnegativity. Convexity gives
Phi(y)>=Phi(q)+grad Phi(q).(y-q)>=Phi(q). Strict convexity for r>1 makes
q the UNIQUE optimizer. Its focal payment is
                    p=1/(2Q+5),
independent of j and of r. This also checks the direction of the outside
nonnegativity multiplier: it enters grad Phi as +nu_vec.

4. REDUCTION TO AN ARBITRARY FIXED CAP C>1.
The natural cap is C_Q=Q-1-(Q-3)/(2Q+5), unbounded as Q grows. Given C>1,
choose ONE fixed Q with C_Q>C and put A=(C_Q-C)/(C-1)>0.
Append one additive resource Y and one actual owner holding A units of Y;
extend the technology by F_new=F_old+Y. This is the exact same production
padding as in the quadratic proof, with binary coefficients preserved.
At each selected old-owner comparison, coalition covers factor into an
old-game cover and an additive coordinate >=A. At the optimum the latter
equals A: lowering excess preserves coverage and the cap and strictly
decreases its r-power objective. The remaining old budget is
 C(1+A)-A=C_Q,
and the remaining objective differs from Phi_old by the constant A^r/r.
Hence the old coordinates are exactly the optimizer certified above.

Translate the actual focal underreport to all n private owners. At the
one full profile with total1+A and N=n+2 actual owners, the true envelope
satisfies
 sum_i E_i/(1+A) >= n*p/(1+A)
                   = [(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)]*n.
Fix Q,A,r and let j tend to infinity subject to(B). This gives the claimed
linear lower bound along arbitrarily large owner-count instances. No
exchange of varying-power or varying-alphabet limits is used.

At C=1, every feasible cover is an efficient-core allocation, independently
of the strictly convex objective. The separate all-efficient-core-selector
barrier supplies the linear envelope obstruction directly.

5. SCOPE.
The same fixed owners, bundles, and finite production function realize
every selected unilateral comparison within each instance. Taking the
supremum over additional real underreports can only strengthen the lower
bound. These examples still admit the simple efficient competing rule on
their fixed-bundle underreport domain: pay old output to the old veto and
additive output to the additive owner. Thus no lower bound for arbitrary
eligible mechanisms is claimed. This extension does not pass the original
research gate requiring a new improved general funding bound.
