WEIGHTED LEXICOGRAPHIC COVER SELECTION: VALIDITY AND A CLOSED RATE ROUTE
22 October2026. Valid selection proof; proposed rate conjecture refuted.
No efficiency constraint or fairness floor is imposed.

1. DEFINITION.
For a finite nonnegative coalition game v with v(empty)=0, let
 C(v)={x>=0: x(S)>=v(S) for every nonempty S}.
Fix strictly positive weights w, independently of reports. Select the unique
cover L_w(v) whose nonincreasingly sorted ratios x_i/w_i are lexicographically
minimal. This can be constructed without an efficiency constraint:
Initially F=empty and R=N. With frozen coordinates x_F, compute
 delta=max(0,max_{S:S intersect R nonempty}
             [v(S)-x(S intersect F)]/w(S intersect R)).
If delta=0, set every remaining coordinate0 and stop. Otherwise freeze at
x_i=delta*w_i every i in the union of the residual parts S intersect R of
maximizing rows. Recurse on the remaining owners.
All rows with no remaining owners are already satisfied. Uniform residual
ratios at the preceding level witness feasibility; the new level cannot
increase. The process freezes at least one coordinate each time. In fact,
the next positive level is strictly smaller: every previous maximizing row
has no residual owner, while every remaining row had strictly positive slack
at the preceding uniform cap. Subtracting frozen contributions preserves
that strict slack at the same cap.

At any step delta is the smallest possible maximum ratio among unfrozen
coordinates. Every maximizing row forces all its remaining coordinates to
have ratio delta in any cover attaining that maximum. Conversely, every
coordinate outside the maximizing union can be lowered slightly from the
uniform residual ratio delta, because all its incident residual constraints
are strictly slack. More explicitly, lower ALL coordinates outside the union
simultaneously to ratio delta-epsilon, with epsilon positive and no larger
than the minimum, over rows meeting that complement, of row slack divided
by the total weight of those complement coordinates. There are finitely many
such rows, all with positive slack; also choose epsilon<=delta. Thus the
simultaneous reduction is feasible. This is exactly weighted lexicographic minimax,
and it determines a unique vector.

2. OWN-INCIDENCE MONOTONICITY LEMMA.
If v' agrees with v on every coalition omitting i, and v'(S)>=v(S) for
coalitions containing i, then L_w(v')_i>=L_w(v)_i.
Proof: compare the two algorithms before i is frozen. If all preceding
levels and pinning sets agree, the remaining systems differ only in rows
containing i. The new level delta' cannot be below delta. If delta'>delta,
a maximizing new row must contain i, since off-i rows are unchanged; hence
i is frozen at delta'w_i, at least its old eventual payment. If delta'=delta,
all old maximizing rows remain maximizing. Any newly maximizing row contains
i. If such a row appears, i is frozen at this level, again at least its old
eventual payment. Otherwise the frozen sets and coordinates coincide, so
recurse. If i was already frozen in the common steps, its payment is equal.
This proves the claim, including ties and zero terminal levels.

Consequently b -> L_w(v_b) is a globally eligible payment rule for EVERY
monotone production function: increasing owner i's report increases only its
incident coalition values. Coverage and nonnegativity hold by construction.
For the present concave homogeneous production model there is always an
efficient nonnegative cover c with sum c=T, but L_w need not be efficient.
Every positive selected coordinate belongs to a tight coalition, so x_i<=T.
The selector is piecewise rational/Borel as a function of positive weights.
Any report-independent probability mixture over weights gives a deterministic
expected payment rule, finite, covering, and own-monotone at ALL real profiles.
This construction is not a supporting-price mask/endpoint rule and does not
take the own-report envelope of separately selected cover coordinates.

3. THE ORIGINAL RATE TARGET (REFUTED).
For iid W_i=U_i^(1/alpha), U_i uniform(0,1), investigate a bound of the form
 E sum_i L_W(v)_i <=C*(1+1/alpha)*n^alpha *min_{c in C(v)}sum_i c_i.
Taking alpha≈1/log n would give an all-profile O(log n) funding mechanism.
This inequality is FALSE. The exact petal construction in
selection_weighted_lex_obstruction.txt gives an Omega(n/log n) obstruction
for every report-independent positive weight distribution, even correlated,
asymmetric distributions chosen using the fixed technology. The expression
above is retained solely to identify the abandoned conjecture.

4. EXACT CALIBRATION FAMILIES AND OBSTRUCTIONS.
(a) Equal weights can cost n/2 on the unit star rows{veto,j}, while paying
only the veto1 is an efficient cover. Thus deterministic egalitarianization
alone is insufficient.
(b) All k-subset unit rows: order weights W_(1)<=...<=W_(n), put S_k=sum of
the smallest k weights. The selector pays W_(j)/S_k to the first k owners
and W_(k)/S_k to every later owner. Total is
 1+(n-k)*W_(k)/S_k,
while the minimum total cover is n/k. These constraints are the full cover
polyhedron of the realizable game v(S)=0 for|S|<k, v(S)=|S|/k otherwise.
The formula remains valid with tied weights. All owners tied at W_(k) lie
in the initial maximizing union and receive W_(k)/S_k. Every larger-weight
owner is forced to pay at least that amount by its row together with the
first k-1 owners; the stated vector satisfies all rows and is lex minimal.
Pure random priority, the alpha->0 limit at fixed n, has funding ratio
k*(n-k+1)/n, which is Theta(n) when both k and n-k are Theta(n).
For alpha fixed and k/n bounded away from0, order-statistic heuristics give
ratio O(1/alpha); this is heuristic until an exact expectation bound is shown.
(c) Complete-product rows: partition owners into groups G_j and take every
row selecting one owner per group. Set Z_j=min_{i in G_j}W_i. The selector
pays EVERY owner of group j the common amount Z_j/(sum_l Z_l). Hence total
is sum_j |G_j|Z_j/(sum_j Z_j), whereas the minimum cover cost is min_j|G_j|.
For one singleton veto and k groups of size m, iid exponential weights have
Z_0~Exp(1), Z_j~Exp(m), independently. With k=m->infinity, total/efficient
cost is Θ(m), while n=m^2+1. Thus ordinary exponential weights have a genuine
square-root obstruction. They are not the proposed weight law.

5. AN EXACT BUT INSUFFICIENT CHARGING INEQUALITY.
Let x=L_w(v), let c be any feasible comparator, and put lambda_i=x_i/w_i.
Every positive coordinate has a tight witness coalition S_i all of whose
members have ratios at least lambda_i. Thus
 x_i<=c_i+sum_{j:lambda_j>=lambda_i}(c_j-x_j)_+
     <=c_i+sum_j(c_j-lambda_i*w_j)_+.
The first inequality retains actual deficits and is potentially useful.
The second loses the pinning dependence: on a star with comparator paying
the veto only, it allows leaf payment W_i/(W_i+W_v), whose expectation is1/2
for every iid continuous weight law. Summing that bound gives n/2, so an
O(log n) proof must preserve the coupling through shared frozen coordinates.
