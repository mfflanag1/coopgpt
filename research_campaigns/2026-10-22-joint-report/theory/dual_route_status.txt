DUAL/STRUCTURAL ROUTE STATUS, 22 OCTOBER 2026
Scope: independent bounded route in the joint-report campaign; only dual_*
files written. Parent owns integration and cross-model review.

PROVED STRUCTURAL PROGRESS.
1. Exact ownerwise upward-transport dual, with equivalent upper-set capacity
inequalities. Standard LP/flow formulation; no novelty or rate claim.
2. Matroid-basis min-linear technologies under fixed disjoint resource-owner
supports have an efficient own-monotone marginal rule. This is an exclusion
using classical rank/convex-game ingredients, not an unrestricted result.
3. Every report-independent mixture of positive weighted-lex selectors has
an Omega(N/log N) worst-profile funding ratio on one explicit technology
f=min{y,sum_l min_j z_lj}. This includes technology-dependent asymmetric laws
because the all-bundle domain contains every basis-assignment profile.
4. Exact characterization of weight comparisons that preserve owner i's
payment for EVERY cover game: off-i weights must change by one common factor
and i's relative weight must increase. For n>=3, maps satisfying this at
every own increase factor projectively into monotone own-only weights.
5. Such own-only increasing weights do preserve eligibility, but positive
linear/unbounded private-ray choices fail by saturation on the same petal
technology. A raw marginal-loss adaptive alternative fails already on four
owners with positive regularization (exact rational counterexample).

WHAT IS NOT PROVED.
No improved universal funding upper bound, and no arbitrary eligible-rule
lower bound beyond the manuscript's known rates. Items 3--5 concern a broad
selector architecture, not all possible mechanisms. The bad fixed profile
has a static efficient cover; the pure-coordinate subdomain has a veto-only
efficient rule, but that rule is not automatically valid on all bundle
profiles. No historical novelty claim has been checked for these lemmas.

EXACT REMAINING DUAL BOTTLENECK.
For a useful universal upper bound, ownerwise transport loads must be charged
against sum_p mu_p T_p while retaining their source-specific coalition and
same-others fiber. Static balancedness controls loads at one profile, and
monotonicity permits upward movement, but neither controls their joint
congestion. Collapsing source prices into one concavity bound gives
f(sum_p mu_p aggregate_p), which can far exceed sum_p mu_p T_p. No replacement
charging inequality with an improved dependence on n or d was obtained.

For a stronger lower bound, the known multi-pool hierarchy requires too many
independent pools/actual owners. Compressing its exponentially many rows into
overlapping cuts on polynomially many owners is the live direction, but one
owner's increase then changes many row counts simultaneously. No transport
certificate preserving the hierarchy identity under that overlap was found.
Uniform-subset and matroid-basis pure-coordinate compressions are excluded
by item 2. Arbitrary Boolean arrays still require the already known locality,
monotonicity, and total-balancedness realization conditions; using generic
realization alone is not a fresh lower construction.

GATE JUDGMENT.
The weighted-lex candidate is decisively rejected as a general rate route
under any report-independent law. A continuation based only on adjusting its
iid distribution, using norms/positive linear own weights, or taking raw
marginal-loss weights is not justified. A new joint selector must exploit
production-specific coupling between changes in other owners' weights and
incident RHS changes, or leave weighted lex entirely. The dual route itself
has not met the campaign's improved-general-rate continuation gate.
