INDEPENDENT ASSESSMENT: CLIPPED-PROPORTIONAL UNIT-FORM RULE
22 October 2026. Bounded source/proof assessment. No priority clearance.

VERDICT

The mechanism in composition_threshold_mechanism.txt is correct on its
stated all-real, arbitrary mixed-owner domain. Fixed corank c=d-k_min gives
a genuine c+1 structural upper bound for an unbounded class: both owner
count and resource dimension may grow, and there is no bound on the number
of forms. It can improve the recorded general numerical upper bounds.

However, the rate itself is an elementary clipping corollary: it already
follows with threshold T=F(B), without the new bottleneck threshold tau.
Tau improves the resulting payments pointwise. The close repository and
literature antecedents below contain the component primitives, not the
exact full theorem located in this bounded read. Therefore neither
"already the same published theorem" nor "historically new" is justified.
Recommended classification: useful explicit structural corollary and
excluded lower-bound class; not by itself a nonroutine solution of the
unrestricted n/d continuation gate.

1. EXACT HYPOTHESES AND DIRECT AUDIT

Fix a finite nonempty family R of nonempty subsets of [d]. Define
 F(B)=min_{D in R} sum_{r in D} B_r,
 tau(B)=min_{D in R} max_{r in D} B_r,
 k_min=min_{D in R}|D|.
There are any finite number of fixed actual owners, each reporting an
arbitrary vector b_i>=0, with B=sum_i b_i. Pay
 x_i=sum_r b_ir min(1,tau(B)/B_r),
using zero for a summand with B_r=0.

For own monotonicity, hold C_r=sum_{j!=i}b_jr fixed. Both z and
tau(z+C) z/(z+C_r) are coordinatewise nondecreasing functions of the whole
own vector z. Their minimum is therefore nondecreasing. At z=C_r=0 the
summand is zero; the convention for the ratio causes no violation.

For coverage choose D attaining tau. Every r in D has B_r<=tau, so the
rule pays every contribution on D in full. Thus for every owner coalition
S, with a=sum_{i in S}b_i,
 x(S)>=sum_{r in D}a_r>=F(a).
This argument uses the actual owners, not a refined ownership instance.

The exact total is sum_r min(B_r,tau). Choose E attaining F(B)=T. Then
 tau<=max_{r in E}B_r<=T,
 sum_i x_i<=T+(d-|E|)tau<=(d-k_min+1)T.
If T=0 some row is zero, hence tau=0 and all payments are zero. Ties need
no selection convention in the mechanism. Homogeneity is immediate.

The same proof gives a slightly stronger profilewise bound: among all
sum-minimizing rows choose one of maximum cardinality, and use
 T+(d-max{|E|:E minimizes the sum})tau.

Literal unit coefficients are load-bearing. A fixed positive multiplier
a_r common to every appearance of resource r is allowed by replacing
b_ir with a_r b_ir. Arbitrary row-dependent coefficients are not covered.
Unused coordinates can be discarded in advance; this improves d.

2. TWO ELEMENTARY STRENGTHENINGS / CLASSIFICATION FACTS

(a) The bottleneck is unnecessary for the c+1 theorem. Set instead
 y_i=sum_r b_ir min(1,T/B_r).
T is nondecreasing, so the same own-monotonicity proof applies. Every
coordinate of a sum-minimizing row E is <=T, so E is paid in full and
coverage holds. The same budget proof gives c+1. Since tau<=T, x_i<=y_i
at every profile. This shows precisely what the bottleneck refinement adds.

(b) Both rules satisfy coalition-total withholding monotonicity. Fix a
coalition S and outsider aggregate C. Write a=sum_{i in S}b_i. Its total
payment is
 sum_r min(a_r,theta(a+C) a_r/(a_r+C_r)), theta=tau or T.
The preceding monotonicity proof applies to a. Therefore simultaneous
coordinatewise increases by S cannot reduce its total payment. This is a
direct consequence of aggregate proportional sharing, not a separate
incentive assumption needed for the original theorem.

For all k-subsets, tau is the kth order statistic. Taking k-1 resource
totals zero and the other d-k+1 totals one attains ratio d-k+1 for this
particular rule. This is not an arbitrary-mechanism lower bound. At fixed
c=d-k, K=binomial(d,c) may diverge, so the constant guarantee is a real
class statement, rather than a bounded-form or finite-example result.
It does not improve the unrestricted worst case: small k_min leaves a
linear bound, and the recursive four-block family still gets only
d-sqrt(d)+1 from this construction.

4. FREE ORIGINAL LITERATURE READ (TWO CLOSE PRIMITIVE ANTECEDENTS)

A. Robert J. Aumann and Michael Maschler (1985), Game theoretic analysis
of a bankruptcy problem from the Talmud, Journal of Economic Theory36,
195-213, DOI10.1016/0022-0531(85)90102-4.
Free original PDF: https://gwern.net/doc/statistics/decision/1985-aumann.pdf
Read scope: Section4, printedpp202-203, especially definition and (4.1).
It explicitly defines constrained equal awards as min(claim,common level).
The resource totals min(B_r,tau) have this classical form, with endogenous
estate sum_r min(B_r,tau), then proportional division within each resource.
That observation identifies the primitive only: a bankruptcy estate is
fixed externally, whereas here the rule must finance production coalition
constraints across owner reports. The source does not supply this mixed-
owner production mechanism or its fixed-corank guarantee in the read scope.

B. Jean-Luc Marichal, Weighted lattice polynomials (2007 preprint,
January2008 version), https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.0570.
Read scope: Section2, Definition1, Proposition2, and Remark4, pp2-3.
Minima of maxima of coordinates are ordinary lattice polynomial functions;
their coordinatewise monotonicity is classical. Thus tau's functional
form and its monotonicity should not receive a novelty claim. This paper
does not discuss the production payments or subsidy bound in that scope.

These are component antecedents, not evidence that the exact theorem has
or has not appeared before. No absence-based novelty conclusion, broad
literature clearance, or nonroutine research claim is warranted.

5. RECOMMENDED PRESENTATION

State an elementary fixed-corank unit-form proposition, with arbitrary
mixed owners and all real profiles explicitly quantified. Present the
T-clipped proof first if emphasizing the rate, then the tau refinement as
a pointwise cheaper eligible rule. Credit classical cap/proportional and
min-max primitives, and label exact priority unassessed. The meaningful
consequence is that fixed-corank unit forms cannot witness an unbounded
funding lower bound, even under coalition-total monotonicity. This result
is useful independently of whether it meets the campaign continuation gate.
