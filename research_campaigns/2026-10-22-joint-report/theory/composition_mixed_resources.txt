MIXED RESOURCE OWNERSHIP: A STRICT LOWER AND AN EXACT COMPRESSION BARRIER
22 October2026. Structural investigation, fixed actual owner identities.
Physical technology: sum of the k smallest among d resource totals.
Each owner i reports t_i times its fixed nonnegative bundle ray A_.i.
Thus resource totals are A*t, with A a d-by-n nonnegative matrix.

RESULTS AND SCOPE
1. For k1, mixed bundle ownership already represents every finite
   nonnegative min-linear scalar technology exactly, using one physical
   resource for each linear form. Disjoint-ownership efficiency therefore
   cannot extend to arbitrary overlapping bundle matrices.
2. An explicit parametric joint-report dual remains strictly greater than1
   when EVERY owner bundle has positive coordinates. For every k>=1 and
   d=k+1 there is a four-owner positive-bundle example with lower>33/32.
   This is a strict gap, not a growing lower rate or a new hierarchy.
3. The recursive technology f_h from composition_obstructions.txt cannot
   be exactly compressed into this order-statistic class with few resources.
   If it is represented on ALL positive scalar ray profiles, then
     binomial(d,2) >= c_h^2/4,  c_h=2^(2^h-1).
   Since n=4^h, this requires d=Omega(2^(sqrt(n))). Arbitrary overlaps,
   arbitrary k and arbitrary nonnegative real matrix entries do not evade it.
   This last theorem does not rule out matching only a finite certificate's
   values, or constructing a different hard technology with fewer edge
   directions. Those remain separate proof obligations.

1. EXACT UNIVERSALITY WHEN k=1
Given any finite nonnegative min-linear scalar technology
  G(t)=min_{r<=d} sum_i A_ri*t_i,
take physical technology f(X)=min_r X_r and owner i's fixed bundle ray
A_.i. Then f(sum_i t_i A_.i)=G(t) identically. Scalar own-report changes
remain legal underreports by the same actual owner. No other owner's
report changes. Thus arbitrary overlap removes the special disjoint-ray
structure entirely. This observation does not compress the number of forms:
its physical dimension is the number d of specified linear forms.

2. POSITIVE OVERLAP PRESERVES THE TWO-POOL JOINT LOWER
Keep the physical technology f(X)=min(X1,X2). Give owners0,1 the bundle
ray(1,epsilon), and owners2,3 the ray(epsilon,1), with0<epsilon<1. The
induced scalar technology is
  G_epsilon(t)=epsilon*sum_i t_i
                +(1-epsilon)*min(t0+t1,t2+t3).
Every owner's bundle is strictly positive in both resources.

Use P0=(1,1,1,1), PA=(R,1,1,1), PB=(1,1,1,R), R>=2. The SAME actual
owner0 and actual owner3 comparisons used in the base certificate remain
valid. Sum the same seven coalition inequalities and two monotonicity
inequalities as in Section1 of composition_obstructions.txt. The right
side is now
  [1+epsilon] + 2[4+(R+2)epsilon]
    =9+(2R+5)epsilon.
The left side is2X_A+2X_B. Both target outputs are2+(R+1)epsilon, so
  beta >= [9+(2R+5)epsilon]/[8+4(R+1)epsilon].                (1)
This exceeds1 whenever epsilon<1/(2R-1). At R2 the bound is
  9(1+epsilon)/(8+12epsilon)>1 for epsilon<1/3.
No optimization output enters this lower; it is one explicit nonnegative
combination of the original, legal mechanism constraints. It is not a
claim that(1) is the exact all-real optimum.

3. STRICTLY POSITIVE BUNDLES FOR EVERY k
Set d=k+1. Keep the two resource rows above and add k-1 identical rows
  (delta,delta,delta,delta), with0<delta<=epsilon.
Every entry of every owner bundle is positive. For every nonnegative t,
each added resource total delta*sum t_i is at most BOTH original totals.
Therefore summing the k smallest resources gives exactly
  G(t)=c*sum t_i+(1-epsilon)*min(t0+t1,t2+t3),
  c=epsilon+(k-1)delta.
With R2, the base joint combination gives
  beta >= [9+9epsilon+18(k-1)delta]
             /[8+12epsilon+20(k-1)delta].                    (2)
Choose epsilon=1/8 and delta=1/(8k). The difference between the numerator
times32 and the denominator times33 is21/(2k)>0. Thus(2)>33/32 for every
finite k, and its right side tends33/32. This shows that strict positive
overlap defeats an efficiency claim throughout this order-statistic family.
It supplies no growing-factor improvement: the extra resources are always
cheapest and add an additive term rather than independent difficult layers.

4. COEFFICIENT POLYTOPES OF THE ORDER-STATISTIC TECHNOLOGY
Let a_r be resource row r of A, regarded as a vector in R^n. The induced
technology is
  G(t)=min_{S subset[d], |S|=k} (sum_{r in S}a_r) dot t.
Its coefficient polytope is
  Q=conv{sum_{r in S}a_r: |S|=k}=A^T Delta(d,k),
where Delta(d,k)=conv{1_S:|S|=k} is the hypersimplex.

LEMMA (edge-direction bound).
Every nondegenerate edge of Q is parallel to a_r-a_s for some r!=s.
Consequently Q has at most binomial(d,2) distinct unoriented edge directions.

Proof. Every hypersimplex edge changes just one selected index: its
direction is e_r-e_s. To see why larger changes are not edges, if two
k-sets differ in at least two exchanges, swap one of them to obtain two
different k-set vertices having the same midpoint. They are outside the
original segment, contradicting that segment being an exposed edge.
The single-exchange segments are faces obtained by fixing all other
coordinates at0 or1.

Now take an exposed edge E of Q. Pull its exposing functional back through
A^T. The resulting face H of Delta(d,k) maps ONTO E. Two vertices of H
have distinct images. Along an edge path connecting them in H, some edge
has a nonzero image. All these images lie in E, so that image direction
is parallel to E. Edges of H are hypersimplex edges, establishing the claim.
This argument allows projections to collapse many dimensions and does not
assume the image map is injective. Degenerate differences only reduce the
number of available directions. QED.

5. EXACT REPRESENTATION RECOVERS THE RECURSIVE CUT POLYTOPE AS A FACE
Let P_h be the convex hull of all indicator cuts in the min-linear expansion
of f_h. Every cut has cardinality L=2^h. Suppose G(t)=f_h(t) for every
strictly positive scalar profile t. At t=1, both minimum values equal L.
Let Q0={q in Q:1 dot q=L}, the exposed minimizing face of Q.

For any direction v in R^n, choose a sufficiently small positive epsilon
so1+epsilon*v remains strictly positive. Since Q has finitely many vertices,
for sufficiently small epsilon its minimizing vertices at this perturbed
point all belong to Q0: every other vertex has a positive gap at1. Hence
  min_Q (1+epsilon*v) dot q = L+epsilon*min_Q0 v dot q.
For P_h all coordinate sums already equal L, so
  min_Ph (1+epsilon*v) dot p = L+epsilon*min_Ph v dot p.
Equality of the two technologies forces min_Q0 v dot q=min_Ph v dot p
for EVERY v. Compact convex polytopes are determined by these support
values, so Q0=P_h. In particular every edge of P_h is an edge of Q and
must obey the direction bound from Section4.

This face argument is needed: equality on nonnegative reports alone need
not make the entire coefficient polytopes equal; extra dominated forms
may be present. Constant cardinality and the interior point1 isolate the
entire recursive polytope as one exposed face, which is sufficient.

6. EXPONENTIALLY MANY DISTINCT CROSS-BRANCH DIRECTIONS
Write c_h for the number of recursive cut indicators. They are distinct,
and satisfy c0=1 and c_h=2*c_(h-1)^2, hence
  c_h=2^(2^h-1).
Every cut is a vertex: give its leaves cost1 and all other leaves cost2.
Because all cuts have equal cardinality, that cut uniquely minimizes cost.

Exactly c_h/2 cuts lie in the left top pair of blocks, and the other half
in the right pair. For ANY left cut C and right cut D, assign cost1 to
C union D and cost2 elsewhere. A left cut other than C contains a leaf
outside C, and similarly on the right. Therefore exactly C,D minimize,
and their segment is an exposed edge of P_h. There are(c_h/2)^2 such edges.

Orient each difference as1_C-1_D. It has+1 entries on the left, -1 on the
right, and zeros elsewhere. Two such differences can be parallel only
with scalar factor1 (the orientation excludes-1 and all nonzero magnitudes
are1). Equality of differences then determines C and D uniquely. Thus all
(c_h/2)^2 directions are distinct. Section4 now forces
  binomial(d,2) >= c_h^2/4.                                  (3)

For h=1,2,3,4 this gives respectively d>=2,7,92,23171, while direct k1
encoding uses c_h=2,8,128,32768 resources. Asymptotically d>=c_h/sqrt(2)
up to the harmless integer correction. In terms of n=4^h owners, exact
order-statistic compression therefore needs d=Omega(2^(sqrt(n))). This
holds for ANY k and ANY nonnegative real matrix of overlapping bundle rays.

7. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE
The simple ownership-safe case does not survive positive overlaps, as the
explicit legal joint lower(1)--(2) shows. Conversely, exponentially many
k-subset forms do not automatically provide exponential compression of
the proposed recursive hierarchy: its edge geometry forces almost as many
resources as its direct min-linear encoding.

The theorem concerns identity of technologies on all positive scalar ray
profiles. A finite lower certificate could conceivably be matched on just
its profiles and coalition aggregates with fewer resources; Section5 would
then lack its arbitrary infinitesimal directions. That restricted matching
problem remains open here and needs an explicit exact map, not the count
binomial(d,k) alone. Another hierarchy with many coefficient vertices but
few edge directions is also not excluded. No stronger arbitrary-mechanism
asymptotic lower rate has been proved by this investigation.
