OWN-WEIGHT MONOTONICITY AND A SATURATION OBSTRUCTION
22 October 2026. Exact structural lemmas; no improved general funding bound.

1. OWN-WEIGHT MONOTONICITY.
Fix finitely many nonnegative covering constraints x(S)>=v(S), x>=0, and
strictly positive weights w_-i. If t=w_i>0 varies while all other weights
remain fixed, then the weighted lexicographic minimax payment L_w(v)_i is a
continuous nondecreasing function of t.

Proof. Run the freezing algorithm while i is still unfrozen. At a fixed
off-i freezing prefix F with fixed payments x_F, the largest off-i ratio is
  delta=max_{S not containing i, S\F nonempty}
                [v(S)-x(S intersect F)]/w(S\F),
with 0 included in the maximum. It is independent of t. The largest incident
ratio is
  A(t)=max_{S containing i} c_S/(t+b_S),
where c_S=v(S)-x(S intersect F), b_S=w(S\(F union {i}))>=0.
Negative residuals can be replaced by 0 in this expression. Until i freezes,
only off-i maximizing rows freeze, so their levels, unions, and frozen
payments are independent of t. If A(t)>delta, then i freezes immediately and
  L_w(v)_i=t A(t)=max_S c_S t/(t+b_S).
Each displayed positive-residual function is nondecreasing in t. If
A(t)<delta, the same off-i maximizing union freezes for every such t and the
argument repeats. Since A(t) decreases in t, increasing t can only extend
this single off-i freezing sequence before i freezes; it cannot switch to a
different off-i prefix.

At a boundary A(t)=delta, i's payment is t delta. Freezing the tied off-i
union first does not change this value: subtracting delta times the weights
of newly frozen members from an incident row preserves the equality of its
residual ratio to delta. Other residual ratios remain <=delta. Thus the
neighboring formulas meet at t delta, including simultaneous ties. There are
only finitely many freezing prefixes. Hence the piecewise nondecreasing
formulas join continuously, proving the lemma. If all incident residuals
vanish the payment is 0, which gives the same conclusion. Singleton incident
rows have b_S=0 and contribute constant functions c_S.

2. A VALID REPORT-DEPENDENT ESCAPE CLASS.
Let a common random seed omega be sampled independently of reports. For
each owner i choose a strictly positive finite function W_i(b_i,omega) that
depends on only i's own bundle (and fixed technology/data) and is
componentwise nondecreasing in that bundle, for each seed. The expected rule
  x_i(b)=E_omega L_{W(b,omega)}(v_b)_i
is eligible at all real profiles. To compare an own increase, first increase
the incident demands while holding weights fixed, using the own-incidence
monotonicity lemma; then increase just W_i using part 1. Coverage survives
expectation. Payments are bounded by T at each seed because every positive
coordinate lies in a tight coalition and every coalition value is <=T, so
there is no integrability issue. Appropriate measurability is assumed (and
holds for the usual Borel weight functions).

This construction is outside report-independent mixtures and hence is not
excluded by the N/log N theorem. It gives no budget guarantee by itself.

3. SATURATION OBSTRUCTS POSITIVE LINEAR/UNBOUNDED OWN-RAY WEIGHTS.
Use the fixed technology and owner basis assignment from the companion note:
  f(y,z)=min{y, sum_{l=1}^r min_{j<=k}z_lj}.
Consider the continuum of profiles b_u=e_y and b_lj=L e_lj, L>=1. Every one
has exactly the same unit coalition game and grand output 1. Assume a
report-dependent weighting law has, almost surely,
  0<W_u(e_y,omega)<infinity,
  min_l sum_{j<=k}W_lj(L e_lj,omega) -> infinity as L->infinity.
The exact petal formula gives
  sum_i L_{W(b,omega)}(v_b)_i
    =[W_u+r m(L)]/[W_u+m(L)] -> r.
It always lies between 1 and r, so dominated convergence gives the same
limit in expectation. Thus for every epsilon>0 some finite real L has
expected funding at least r-epsilon at unit output.

In particular this applies to own-only affine weights
  W_i(b_i,omega)=epsilon_i(omega)+a_i(omega) dot b_i
with epsilon_i>0 finite, a_i>=0 finite, and at least one strictly positive
coefficient on a reported coordinate in EACH petal almost surely. It applies
in particular when all coefficients are strictly positive. Random positive
linear weights, after a positive offset or another definition at zero, have
the same obstruction. Zero coefficients deliberately placed on entire
petals do not meet the hypothesis and are NOT ruled out by this argument.

SCOPE AND REMAINING GATE. Own-only monotone weights preserve incentives,
but weights that grow on redundant private input rays overcharge after the
production output has saturated. Report-dependent weights with deliberate
saturation or zero directions, and dependence on other owners' reports,
remain live. The latter requires a new monotonicity argument: part 2 does
not apply when an own report changes other owners' weights. No arbitrary
eligible-mechanism lower bound or improved rate is established here.
