2. A profile box with scalar report range ratio R has a feasible monotone

that the unrestricted optimal factor for f_h is bounded as h grows.

1. TECHNOLOGY AND THE BASE CERTIFICATE
Let f0(t)=t and, on four disjoint blocks,
 f_h=min(f_(h-1)(B1)+f_(h-1)(B2),
         f_(h-1)(B3)+f_(h-1)(B4)).
There are n=4^h actual owners, one fixed scalar coordinate each. This is
a finite nonnegative min-linear, monotone, concave homogeneous technology.
Every linear form in its recursive expansion is the indicator of a cut
with2^h leaves. Indeed a level-h cut chooses one top pair of blocks and
one level-(h-1) cut in each chosen block. At the all-ones profile f_h=2^h.

Write f1=min(x1+x2,x3+x4), and use zero-based owner indices below. The
9/8 base lower needs just three profiles:
 P0=(1,1,1,1), PA=(4,1,1,1), PB=(1,1,1,4).
Let X_A,X_B be their total payments. At P0 coalition{0,3} requires
x0(P0)+x3(P0)>=1. Legal own-monotonicity gives
x0(PA)+x3(PB)>=1. At PA, sum coverage for coalitions
{1,2},{1,3},{0,2,3} to obtain2X_A-x0(PA)>=4. At PB, sum coverage
for{0,2},{1,2},{0,1,3} to obtain2X_B-x3(PB)>=4. Hence
  2X_A+2X_B>=9.
Both profiles have output2, so a common budget factor beta satisfies
8beta>=9. The supplied full16-profile primal is independently checked
by composition_check.py, establishing exact optimum9/8 on{1,4}^4.

2. PERFECT VALUE FACTORIZATION DOES NOT TENSORIZE MONOTONICITY
For positive vectors a,c in R_+^4, consider the16-leaf profile b=a tensor c,
whose block j is a_j*c. Homogeneity gives
  f2(a tensor c)=f1(a)f1(c).
For a rectangular coalition S times T, likewise
  f2((a tensor c)_(S times T))=f1(a_S)f1(c_T).
The analogous h-fold identity follows by induction. Thus neither value
factorization nor coalition value factorization is the missing step.

LEMMA (no single-coordinate edges in positive tensors).
Let m,k>=2. Two positive rank-one m-by-k arrays agreeing everywhere except
possibly entry(i,j) agree there as well. Choose i'!=i,j'!=j. Rank one gives
 b_ij=b_ij' b_i'j/b_i'j', and the three right-hand entries are unchanged.
For h>=2, flatten a positive h-fold tensor into a4-by-4^(h-1) array.
The lemma proves that distinct such leaf profiles never differ in exactly
one actual owner's report.

COROLLARY (exact finite-grid optimum1).
Any finite family of these positive pure tensor profiles has no nontrivial
own-monotonicity constraints. At each profile independently choose one
active linear form a of f_h and pay x_i=a_i*b_i. This covers every coalition,
because f_h(b_S)<=a dot b_S, and the total is exactly f_h(b). Thus beta1
is feasible. The grand-coalition constraint at a positive-output profile
forces beta>=1. The finite joint-report optimum is exactly1.

In particular the9 products of P0,PA,PB have optimum1, as do the255 distinct
products of the full16-profile base grid with itself. Changing an outer
factor changes an entire leaf block; changing an inner factor changes one
leaf in every block. Neither is an allowed single-owner comparison.
The tensor product of the base dual therefore does not provide a valid
lower certificate. A valid repair must introduce non-product hybrid profiles.
At those profiles the factorization above no longer supplies all values.
Strict positivity matters: zero-supported degenerate tensors can have
single-coordinate edges. The calibration uses strictly positive levels.

3. A DYNAMIC-RANGE OBSTRUCTION AT EVERY RECURSION DEPTH
LEMMA (fixed supporting-form allocation).
For any finite nonnegative min-linear technology f, fix a positive lower
profile r and choose an active form a there. The allocation x_i(t)=a_i*t_i
is nonnegative, own-monotone on all scalar reports, and covers every
coalition at every profile. On the whole box r_i<=t_i<=R*r_i,
 sum x_i(t)<=R a dot r=R f(r)<=R f(t).
Thus every finite joint-report LP using only profiles from that box has
optimum at most R. The mechanism's factor R is claimed on the box only;
its global monotonicity and coverage are valid, but its global budget
factor may be unbounded. This distinction is sufficient for a finite-LP
upper certificate and is not a claimed all-real factor-R mechanism.

The same argument applies to a general finite monotone concave homogeneous
scalar technology via a nonnegative supporting supergradient at positive r.
Homogeneity gives a dot r=f(r), and the supporting inequality gives
f(t)<=a dot t. More generally fixed owner bundle rays reduce to scalar
coordinates t_i through f(sum t_i*r_i), without changing actual ownership.

For f_h, one fixed cut has2^h leaves. Its capacity payments give factor4
on the ENTIRE box[1,4]^(4^h). Therefore the full{1,4} leaf grid cannot prove
(9/8)^h indefinitely: already(9/8)^12>4. Any proposed growing lower must
explicitly grow report dynamic range. The box bound does not obstruct a
construction with levels extending to4^h, but Section2 still obstructs
pure tensor profiles in that larger box.

4. EXACT LOCALITY REQUIREMENT FOR PAYMENT AGGREGATION
Consider arbitrary nonnegative functions x_i(b) nondecreasing in their
OWN coordinate with others held fixed. Fix c_i>=0 and two distinct profiles
p,q with p coordinatewise>=q. Then the implication
  sum c_i*x_i(p)>=sum c_i*x_i(q)
holds for EVERY such collection of functions if and only if, for each
i with c_i>0, p_-i=q_-i. In particular p,q must differ in just one owner,
and every nonzero c_i must belong to that owner.

Sufficiency is the stated individual monotonicity. For necessity, if
p_-i!=q_-i, choose x_i(b)=1_{b_-i=q_-i} and every other function0. This
function is independent of its own report, hence own-monotone, while its
weighted contribution drops from c_i to0. No continuity hypothesis is
part of the contract. The next example establishes failure even for a
homogeneous, globally covering payment rule with a finite budget bound.

For any technology F on n scalar owners, choose distinct i,r and define
  x_k(b)=F(b) for k!=r,
  x_r(b)=F(b)+F(b with coordinate i set to0)*b_r/(b_i+b_r),
where the ratio is0 when its denominator is0. The rule is nonnegative and
covers all coalitions: a nonempty coalition receives at least F(b)>=F(b_S).
It has total at most(n+1)F(b). Every payment is own-monotone. For recipient
r, both factors in the bonus are nonnegative and nondecreasing in b_r;
the other payments are just the monotone function F. The rule is also
homogeneous of degree one.

Apply this to F=f2,n16,i0,r1 and profiles
 block1=(t,1,4,4), block2=(1,1,1,1),
 blocks3,4=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2), with t=1 or4.
Block1's output is t+1, increasing2->5. The full output and the output
after deleting i are both2. Its aggregate payment is8+2/(t+1), falling
9->42/5. The controlling owner's own payment remains2. Thus even changing
only ONE actual leaf can raise an internal output while reducing the
block's total payment. Aggregation preserves coverage for unions of
whole blocks, but it cannot produce a truthful abstract-owner mechanism.

5. FROZEN-HELPER TRANSITION GADGETS ALSO LOSE THE LOWER BOUND
Suppose an abstract scalar y_j is implemented by a path group P_j of actual
owners, one controlling leaf with variable report y_j, all other path
leaves fixed at R, and all reports outside the chosen path0. Restrict
0<=y_j<=R. A recursively series-parallel f_h can realize such a chosen
path: its value on a coalition is y_j if the coalition contains the whole
path and0 otherwise. This is proved by taking one parallel branch at each
choice and both factors at each series operation.

Choose a fixed helper anchor r_j in each path (path length at least2).
At each profile choose any efficient covering allocation z_j for the
abstract outer technology. Pay z_j only to r_j and pay all other actual
owners0. For a leaf coalition S, let J(S) be the groups whose complete paths
are present. Its production equals the outer technology on those groups.
Its payments include sum_{j in J(S)}z_j, so it is covered. Total payments
equal total abstract output. The only owners whose reports vary receive0;
each paid anchor's report is fixed throughout the profile family. Thus
every same-others own-report inequality in that family holds. Its joint
optimum is1, for any dynamic range and any number of control profiles.

This is another finite-profile obstruction, not an all-real efficient
mechanism claim. It shows that replacing macro owners by controllable
output paths does not repair tensorization: fixed helper owners can absorb
all payments. A successful transition gadget must prevent that leakage,
for example through many alternative helper coalitions and an explicit
charging argument, while preserving legal single-owner comparison edges.

6. REMAINING AMPLIFICATION OBLIGATION
None of these results settles the optimal all-real factor of f_h. A valid
growing certificate must simultaneously supply:
 - a profile family of growing report dynamic range;
 - legal comparisons changing one fixed actual owner at a time;
 - sufficient non-product hybrid profiles with their true coalition values;
 - a bound on payments escaping to unchanged helper owners;
 - an exact nonnegative dual combination yielding a growing ratio after
   counting every new owner's payment and every new profile's budget.
Simply reinterpreting a block as one owner, reusing factored values at
hybrids, or assuming helper payments vanish fails the contract. These are
specific missing inequalities, not evidence that multiplication is valid.
