MAIN FINDING

Putting any FIXED funding cap into the minimum-norm optimization does not
repair its own-report envelope. For every fixed C>=1 there are arbitrarily
large fixed-owner instances on which that envelope needs funding linear
in the number of owners. At C=2 the explicit lower bound is (N-1)/11 times
output. This is a substantial strengthening of the previous envelope-route
obstruction, and belongs with that theorem rather than in a separate paper
for every cap.

The linear owner order is sharp for each fixed cap: any seed bounded by
C*T has each envelope coordinate at most C*T, hence total at most N*C*T.
The matching lower is along an unbounded sequence of owner counts, with
growing resource dimension; it is not a lower for each prescribed N or d.

The unrestricted best-mechanism bound was NOT improved. All-profile
positive results were obtained for dense resource-form classes, including
mixed ownership and coalition-total incentives, but their funding rates
follow from elementary refinements of existing mechanisms. No finding
justified the conditional six-hour extension under the frozen gate; that
extension was not run. This report distinguishes a useful theorem from a solved frontier.

WHAT THE PROBLEM ACTUALLY REQUIRES

There are n fixed actual owners, each reporting a nonnegative resource
vector. One fixed monotone concave homogeneous technology f determines
v_b(S)=f(sum_{i in S}b_i). A single payment rule must work at ALL real
profiles, cover every coalition, pay nonnegative amounts, and never reduce
an owner's payment when that owner reports more while others stay fixed.
The benchmark is total output T=f(sum_i b_i), with total payment<=beta*T.
These are payment-only individual incentives. There is no efficiency
equality, fairness floor, private cost, outside-option contract, or assumed
coalition-proofness. A finite profile test is not an all-profile mechanism.

The recorded general upper remains
 min{d, n*sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2*sqrt(d)}.
The recorded unrestricted lower rates remain logarithmic/log-logarithmic
in the relevant growing parameters. The new linear results below apply
to particular selection architectures and do not contradict that upper.

1. THE STRONGEST NEW THEOREM: EVERY FIXED QUADRATIC SEED CAP FAILS

Let q^C(b) minimize the squared Euclidean norm among nonnegative coalition
covers with total at most C*T, for a fixed C>=1. Define the ACTUAL envelope
 E_i(b)=sup_{0<=a_i<=b_i} q_i^C(a_i,b_-i).
For the finite min-linear instances used here, the seed is well defined
at all real profiles: an active supporting form provides an efficient
cover, and strict convexity gives a unique minimizer.
The envelope is own-monotone and coalition-covering, but its funding is
Omega_C(N) along a growing sequence of actual owner counts.

For C=2, use the prior ternary row construction with ONE common-veto block,
N=3^d+1 owners and d a positive multiple of480. At each private owner's
selected actual control underreport, the capped optimizer pays1/11 to that
owner and1/11 to the veto,1/(d+h) to each of its2d neighbors, and zero to
the remaining private owners, where h=d/10. Its total is exactly2.
Every retained incident row and every off-owner row is covered. An exact
KKT certificate proves this is the optimizer, not just a feasible vector.
Translating the witness across owners gives sum E_i>=(N-1)/11 at one full
profile with T=1. The true supremum includes every real underreport; using
one legal witness per owner is sufficient for a lower bound.

For general C>1, the Q-ary version has natural cap
 C_Q=Q-1-(Q-3)/(2Q+5), with focal payment1/(2Q+5).
Choose fixed Q with C_Q>C and add one owner supplying an additive resource
of value A=(C_Q-C)/(C-1). The singleton constraint forces a payment of at
least A, and norm minimization makes it exactly A; the remaining budget
is C(1+A)-A=C_Q. Thus
 sum E_i/T >= [(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)]*(N-2).
Q and A are fixed before N grows. The C=1 endpoint follows from the
independently proved efficient-core-selector theorem described next.

The same obstruction now holds for EVERY fixed power objective sum_i x_i^r,
r>1, with the same lower coefficient. Only the starting dimension depends
on r. The row mixture has a uniform margin a0-b>delta/2; choosing h large
enough that h^(r-1)>=2/delta makes the power-gradient certificate positive.
Thus changing the fixed power does not repair this route, even arbitrarily
close to r=1. At r=1, minimizing total payment selects an efficient core
for every cap C>=1; the separate all-core-selector theorem below handles
any deterministic tie-breaking. The power-gradient proof does not cover
that nonunique endpoint, variable powers, or other objectives.

These results do NOT address a cap growing with N or arbitrary joint
payment selection. The base construction admits efficient competing
payments on its fixed-bundle downward report domain. The instances are
astronomically large but finite; no practical enumeration is claimed.

2. TWO MORE GENERAL SEED-ENVELOPE BARRIERS

Every efficient-core selector, not just a quadratic selector, has linear
envelope inflation on a simple growing multi-pool family. With k pools
and two owners per pool, some profile requires factor(k+3)/4=(N+6)/8.
The proof uses a pair of small pools at an underreport, exact core
descriptions, and averaging over genuine single-owner report comparisons.
It needs no symmetry, continuity, or norm objective.

More generally, for every fixed seed funding factor B<2, ANY covering
seed has linear envelope inflation on an appropriate m-owners-per-pool
family. Put gamma=1-(B-1)m/(m-1)>0. Some profile has envelope factor at
least gamma*[1+(k-1)/(2m)], with N=km. Randomization does not escape the
argument when guarantees are in expectation. Substituting a mechanism
for its own envelope also gives a constant arbitrary-mechanism lower
approaching2, but no growing unrestricted-rate improvement.

Proofs: theory/selection_efficient_core_envelope_barrier.txt and
theory/selection_seed_barrier_below_two.txt.

3. WEIGHTED LEXICOGRAPHIC SELECTION: VALID BUT BLOCKED

The descending normalized-level lexicographic minimizer of a nonnegative
coalition cover is monotone when demands incident to one owner increase.
Increasing only that owner's positive weight also cannot lower its
payment. Therefore positive nondecreasing own-local report weights give
eligible all-profile rules. The eligibility ingredient has close and now
verified classical antecedents; it should not be advertised as new.

The proposed power-law random-weight funding guarantee was REFUTED.
A common-veto/disjoint-petal family forces Omega(n/log n) EXPECTED funding for
EVERY positive report-independent weight law, including correlated and
technology-dependent asymmetric laws. The bad full profile may depend
on the law; actual owners do not change within a report comparison.

Even arbitrary owner-specific own-local weights and their fixed mixtures
face Omega(sqrt(n)) funding (in expectation for mixtures). This matches the order of the existing owner
upper; it is not a resource-dimension lower, because the construction has
many more resource coordinates. A universal weight-change comparison
forces relative weights of unchanged owners to remain proportional;
globally safe maps under that stronger comparison factor into own-local
weights up to a common scale. Contextual weights must therefore exploit
the actual changing coalition demands, rather than a generic order lemma.
Marginal-contribution and Shapley-based contextual choices have exact own-
monotonicity counterexamples in the saved notes.

4. A USEFUL POSITIVE STRUCTURAL BOUND, WITHOUT A GENERAL RATE CHANGE

For a fixed finite min-linear representation F(B)=min_a a.B, put
 w_r=max_a a_r, discard unused resources, and let
 kappa=min_a sum_r a_r/w_r.
Resource payments capped at total output and shared proportionally among
actual contributors give factor min{d_eff,1+d_eff-kappa}. A bottleneck
threshold tau=min_a max_r a_r B_r gives pointwise smaller payments with
the same guarantee. Both rules work for arbitrary overlapping bundles
and all nonnegative real reports. They also satisfy coalition-TOTAL
monotonicity, a stronger property than required by the frozen contract.

For unit-coefficient forms of minimum support size k, the factor becomes
d-k+1. Hence summing the d-c smallest resource totals admits factor c+1
for every number of owners and resources, even under mixed ownership.
This class cannot witness a divergent lower bound at fixed c.

The same funding bound also applies to the manuscript's EXISTING grand-
marginal rule. It is therefore an analysis refinement and a useful class
exclusion, not an unrestricted breakthrough. Adding a large common linear
baseline and subtracting it from threshold payments can destroy own
monotonicity; an exact parameterized example blocks that shortcut.

5. WHY THE LOWER-BOUND AMPLIFICATION ROUTES STOPPED

Pure tensor report profiles can lose ALL unilateral comparison edges.
Finite fixed-range scalar boxes admit a range-factor rule, so bounded
report ranges cannot support indefinite multiplicative amplification.
Payments to a block do not inherit a hypothetical macro-owner incentive.
If every child technology has one fixed actual veto owner, substitution
preserves the outer mechanism's funding factor by paying only that veto.
Adding helper owners in the tested gadget approaches the original9/8
finite optimum instead of multiplying it.

Mixed ownership preserves a strict finite lower gap, but an exact linear
resource compression of the recursive four-block technology into a sum
of k smallest totals needs exponentially many resources in sqrt(n).
The proof counts exposed edge directions of the coefficient polytope;
equality on positive profiles identifies the required EXPOSED FACE, not
the entire projected polytope. This excludes one exact compression route;
finite-certificate interpolation and different technologies remain open.

The joint-profile LP and its ownerwise upward-transport dual were written
down precisely. They are standard tools, not a new theorem about rates.
Exact finite calibrations9/8,7/6 and17/15 were independently replayed;
none was promoted into an asymptotic or all-real upper result.

Proofs and checks: theory/composition_*.txt/.py and
theory/dual_flow_formulation.txt; computations/verify_joint.py.
