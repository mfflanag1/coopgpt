REPRODUCING THE JOINT-REPORT CAMPAIGN

All mathematical conclusions are stated in CAMPAIGN_REPORT.txt with links
to plain-text proofs. The frozen contract is CONTRACT.txt. No paper or
canonical repository files must be edited to reproduce these checks.

From this campaign directory, use Python3 (standard library only) for:
 python3 theory/selection_cap_two_check.py
 python3 root/power_certificate_check.py
 python3 theory/selection_seed_barrier_check.py
 python3 theory/selection_weighted_lex_check.py
 python3 theory/selection_contextual_check.py
 python3 theory/dual_exchangeable_lex_check.py
 python3 theory/dual_marginal_weight_check.py
 python3 theory/composition_check.py
 python3 theory/composition_veto_check.py
 python3 theory/composition_mixed_check.py
 python3 theory/composition_threshold_check.py
 python3 theory/composition_weighted_threshold_check.py

Independent finite-LP certificate replay, also standard library only:
 python3 computations/verify_joint.py computations/two_pools.json
 python3 computations/verify_joint.py computations/three_pools.json
 python3 computations/verify_joint.py computations/triangle.json
 python3 computations/verify_joint.py computations/veto.json
 python3 computations/verify_joint.py computations/clutter_best.json

Each finite-LP replay rebuilds actual coalition values and all comparable
same-others unilateral inequalities from the semantic technology, then
checks rational primal and dual optimality. A replayed finite optimum is
not an upper bound valid on the full continuous report domain.

The search scripts joint_lp.py/clutter_scan.py need the versions in
computations/requirements.txt; the temporary tested runtime is recorded in
computations/RUNTIME.txt. Discovery used HiGHS dual simplex with presolve
disabled because an earlier campaign exposed a presolve reliability issue.
Do not mistake floating search logs for independent proof certificates.

The fixed-cap KKT checker verifies exact row counts and marginal identities
for huge finite integers. It does not enumerate Q^d owners, every generator,
or the transport mixture. Existence of that mixture and off-owner coverage
use the proof in inputs/previous_full_envelope_proof.txt. The all-parameter
proof, native independent derivation, and cross-model scope are separately
documented; finite arithmetic alone cannot establish their quantifiers.

Check scripts can refresh their companion result JSONs in this directory.
FINAL_MANIFEST.json records source hashes at campaign close; ROOT_CHECKS.json
records actual root executions during this campaign, source/certificate hashes,
and saved diagnostic-result hashes. Some PASS outputs were observed in the
terminal without separate stdout capture; those are identified explicitly.
Creating the index did not rerun already-passed checks.
