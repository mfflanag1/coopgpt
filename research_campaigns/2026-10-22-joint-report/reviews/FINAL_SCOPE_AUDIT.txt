1. EXACT CAP-ONE ENDPOINT

At C=1, nonnegative coalition coverage includes q(N)>=T, while the seed
cap says q(N)<=T. Thus the feasible set is exactly the efficient core.
In particular the unique quadratic minimizer q^1 is an efficient-core
selector at every real profile. The earlier selector theorem applies to
every such selector, with no symmetry, continuity, or norm assumption.

For k pools and N=2k owners, at F_j only pool j is (1,1); the others are
(4,1). The unique efficient core at F_j pays the two small-pool owners1
each. At L_ij, obtained by a genuine unilateral 4-to-1 underreport, the
core is the interval assigning (lambda,lambda) to pool i and
(1-lambda,1-lambda) to pool j. All other payments vanish. Hence if
a_ij=q_{h_i}(L_ij), then a_ij+a_ji=1.

The true envelope at F_j costs at least2+sum_{i!=j}a_ij. Averaging over j
gives at least2+(k-1)/2 at some F_j, and T=2 throughout. Therefore

 sum_i E_i/T >= (k+3)/4 = (N+6)/8.

For the quadratic selector specifically, the interval optimizer is
lambda=1/2, so every displayed F_j already attains this lower estimate.
The more general seed theorem independently confirms the same constant:
substitute B=1 and m=2 to obtain gamma=1 and1+(k-1)/4=(k+3)/4.
The scalar-pool profiles are legal profiles inside the full mixed-bundle
domain of one fixed min-linear technology; they do not shrink the scope
required of the selector. Efficient core existence at arbitrary profiles
is supplied by an active pool form. There is no missing endpoint limit.
