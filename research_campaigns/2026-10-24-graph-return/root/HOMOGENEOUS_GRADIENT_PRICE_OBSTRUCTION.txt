HOMOGENEOUS GRADIENT-PRICE OBSTRUCTION — candidate, 24 October 2026
Research branch diagnostic; historical priority not checked. Does not improve the
unrestricted graph constant. This stops one natural construction architecture.

Proposition. On F(A,B)=min(A,B), there is no continuously differentiable potential
Phi on the positive quadrant, homogeneous of degree one, whose gradient prices
p=(Phi_A,Phi_B) implement nonnegative aggregate-linear owner payments, individual
payment monotonicity under binding capacity underreports, coalition coverage, and
any finite uniform budget K F. It suffices that the contract hold for the two fixed
pure owners with holdings (A,0),(0,B) over all A,B>0.

Proof. Euler gives Phi(A,B)=A p_A(A,B)+B p_B(A,B). Nonnegative payments and budget
imply 0<=Phi(A,B)<=K min(A,B). Fix B>0. Underreports by the first pure owner imply
m(A)=A p_A(A,B) is nondecreasing on (0,infinity). If m(A0)=eta>0, then for T>A0,
  Phi(T,B)-Phi(A0,B)=integral_{A0}^{T} p_A(u,B)du
                  >= eta log(T/A0).
But both potential values lie between0 andKB, contradiction asT tends to infinity.
Thus m(A)=0 for allA>0. The identical argument with roles reversed gives
B p_B(A,B)=0 everywhere. Total payment is zero, contradicting coverage since
min(A,B)>0. QED.

Assumptions and precise consequence.
- Homogeneity and gradient integrability are substantive extra restrictions.
- C1 is enough; no Hessian, OUT term, graph asymmetry or growing-owner argument used.
- The all-profile report domain must allow arbitrarily large finite true capacities;
  a uniform mechanism over it is the target. No owner is added during a deviation.
- This excludes homogeneous capacity-potential gradient prices even for weaker
  individual payment monotonicity, hence also for full OUT.
- It does NOT exclude Huber optimizer prices, arbitrary variational definitions,
  nonintegrable aggregate fields, owner-specific mechanisms, or finite-domain rules.
- No general lower bound follows. The demonstrated consequence is branch stopping:
  do not pursue a homogeneous gradient-potential smoothing of min for this contract.
