FULL OUT FROM SINGLE-ACTIVITY RESTORATIONS
2026-10-17. Elementary exact reduction; parent verification pending.

Let F(d)=max{w.x:Ax<=d,x>=0} be finite nonnegative linear production. Fix
actual owners and a coalition. Full coalition OUT is equivalent to the following
restricted finite comparisons on ALL reports and all coalitions:

 (i) restoring any amount of one resource to members of the coalition cannot
     lower the coalition's total payment;
 (ii) restoring any multiple t A_e of one production activity, distributed in
      any feasible way among coalition members, increases coalition payment
      by at least t w_e.

Necessity follows immediately: the one-resource bundle has nonnegative outside
worth, and t A_e can produce t w_e. For sufficiency, take any withheld aggregate
d. Choose an optimal production plan x using it. Starting from the low report,
restore the activity bundles A_e x_e one at a time, then restore unused resource
coordinates. Since Ax<=d, no coordinate ever exceeds its final high report.
Each resource's restoration can be assigned among its actual coalition owners
without exceeding their withheld quantities. The coalition remains the same;
there is no owner splitting, identity change, or removal. Sum the payment gains:
they telescope to at least sum_e w_e x_e=F(d), exactly the required OUT inequality.

The statement is a property of arbitrary payment mechanisms. Aggregate-price
form is not required. The restricted comparisons still cover arbitrary
coalitions and all report profiles; this is not a claim that checking one owner
or a few selected profiles suffices.

INTEGER GRID COROLLARY FOR BIPARTITE GRAPHS

For a unit-profit bipartite resource graph, integral nonnegative capacities
admit an integral maximum matching flow. On a full integer box {0,...,M}^V,
all aggregate-price OUT inequalities are therefore equivalent to only:
  - one-unit additions to a single resource, with required gain0;
  - one-unit additions to both endpoints of a graph edge, with required gain1.

Each intermediate aggregate remains in the same integer box. For each local
transition, the coalition's high holdings range over the box between the added
bundle and the high aggregate. The payment difference is affine in these
holdings, so checking its corners verifies every feasible real coalition bundle.
The same fixed coalition and outside bundle are retained throughout telescoping.

This replaces all pairs of high/low profiles by O((|V|+|E|)M^|V|) local profile
comparisons, each with at most2^|V| holding corners. It is an exact reduction of
the finite necessary price LP, not a weaker numerical test. Coverage on an
integer profile follows from the edge-addition comparison with only the added
endpoints held by the coalition; budget and nonnegativity remain separate.

For nonbipartite graphs, unit edge steps alone are not equivalent on integer
capacities: a triangle with one unit at each vertex has fractional worth3/2
but no integral plan exceeding1. One can use half-unit edge steps on a refined
grid or add the half-integral odd-cycle activity patterns. No such extension
is silently assumed in the path scout.

scout_local_graph_outside.py uses the exact bipartite reduction, homogeneity,
and reflection symmetrization for paths. Its solver output is floating-point
discovery only; a lower-bound theorem needs an exact dual or analytic proof.
