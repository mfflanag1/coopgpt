MODEL
Finite simple unit-profit undirected resource graph. F(c) is the maximum
fractional edge packing with vertex capacities c. Fixed actual owners i hold
arbitrary nonnegative mixed resource bundles b_i; all coordinatewise binding
underreports are permitted. The same owner set is used at every comparison.
Every coalition S is eligible, including unchanged members during a deviation.
Payments are nonnegative at every profile. For all coalitions and all profiles:
  sum_{i in S} y_i(b) >= F(sum_{i in S} b_i).
For any simultaneous underreport by S, with total withheld bundle d:
  sum_{i in S} [y_i(high)-y_i(low)] >= F(d).
Budget: sum_i y_i(b) <= K F(sum_i b_i).
There is no imposed marginal-allocation floor. Owners and resources may grow
across instances; any uniform bound must be independent of both counts.
