ANALYTIC EXTRACTION: THE R>=1 CYCLIC DIAGRAM'S 4/3 CERTIFICATE
24 October 2026. Explains the exact discovery outcome; no novelty claim.

F(x)=min(x0+x2,x1+x3,x1+x2). For any rational or real R>=1, take these three
profiles inside the cyclic full-bundle box recorded in BRANCH_DECISION.txt:

          A       B        C        D

All three aggregate outputs are1. Write s_i for payment at P, l_i at L,
and h_i at H. No zero payment to a zero reporter is assumed.

At L the three coalitions AB,BC,BD each have value1. Consequently
  (l_A+l_B)+(l_B+l_C)+(l_B+l_D)=Ltotal+2l_B>=3.
The legal comparison P->L by coalition ACD restores only resource3, so its

  Ltotal-l_B >=s_A+s_C+s_D.
Multiplying the latter inequality by2 and adding yields
  3Ltotal>=3+2s_A+2s_C+2s_D.

At H the three coalitions AB,AC,AD each have value1. Similarly,
  Htotal+2h_A>=3.
The legal comparison P->H by coalition BCD restores R e2+e0, which has
outside-production0 because resources0 and2 are nonadjacent. Thus
  Htotal-h_A >=s_B+s_C+s_D,
and
  3Htotal>=3+2s_B+2s_C+2s_D.

Coverage at P gives s_A+s_B>=1; all payments are nonnegative. Summing gives
  3(Ltotal+Htotal)>=6+2(s_A+s_B)+4(s_C+s_D)>=8.
The two budget inequalities Ltotal,Htotal<=K now imply K>=4/3.

WHY NAIVE REPLICATION DOES NOT CROSS THE FULL-OUT FRONTIER
The same elementary pattern with m additional owners has m+1 unit-valued
star-pair cover constraints at each terminal. Take P to have A=e1,B=e2 and
the m additional owners zero. At L let each additional owner report e3;
at H let each report e0. These reports are legal from fixed full bundles
e0+e3, and the unchanged A or B joins the restoring coalition.

Let E denote the additional owners and s_E their baseline payment sum.
At L, pair coverage gives Ltotal+m*l_B>=m+1; OUT by A union E gives
Ltotal-l_B>=s_A+s_E. Hence
  (m+1)Ltotal>=m+1+m(s_A+s_E).
At H the analogous inequality has s_B+s_E. Therefore
  K >= [3m+2]/[2m+2] = 3/2 - 1/[2(m+1)].

not an upper bound on all finite diagrams with these owners. It tends to
