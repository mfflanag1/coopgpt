NONLINEAR PIVOTAL-HEIGHT RULE: EXACT COVERAGE FAILURE
2026-10-05 -- independent construction attempt, not a valid mechanism

Write C_j=sum_{i in N_j}c_i, T=min C_j, a_i=C_j-c_i,
q_j=C_j-max_{i in N_j}c_i, Q(h)=number of pools with q_j<=h. The candidate pays
  y_i=integral from a_i to T of 1/Q(h) dh if a_i<T, otherwise zero.
Along owner i's coordinate a_i is fixed; its own pool always contributes one
to Q(h) on the integration interval, while other q values do not change.
Hence its integrand is fixed and its upper limit is nondecreasing: own
monotonicity holds. This does not establish coalition coverage.

EXACT FAILURE
Pool 1 capacities (1/2,1/2), pool 2 capacities (1,1/4,1/4), T=1.
Both q values are 1/2. Each pool-1 owner and the unit pool-2 owner receives
integral_(1/2)^1 1/2 dh=1/4. The two small pool-2 owners get zero.
The coalition consisting of one pool-1 owner and the two small pool-2 owners
is worth 1/2, but receives only 1/4. verify_beyond.py recomputes the integrals
from exact breakpoints and checks this deficit.

The raw delegate also claimed a constant-budget rule must pay non-pivotal
owners. That was not proved: in its follow-up profile, pivotal pool-1 owners
can cover the displayed coalitions at constant cost. That assertion, an
underspecified tightness example, and three unchecked exact-family claims are
excluded. No global mechanism is obtained by this construction.
