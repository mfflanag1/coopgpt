MULTISTEP OBSTRUCTION FOR ALL WITHIN-POOL PROPORTIONAL RULES
2026-10-05 -- coordinator proof; independent reading review completed

MODEL AND EXACT SCOPE
Use CONTRACT.txt. Write d_i=min(c_i,T). Restrict only the form of final payments:
y_i(c)=d_i p_j(c) for every owner i in pool j. Prices p_j are arbitrary
nonnegative functions of the entire labeled capacity profile and fixed ownership.
They need not sum to one, be continuous, local, anonymous, or generated by random
thresholds. Require every-coalition coverage and own-report monotonicity.
The budget factor is B, meaning total payment <=B*T at every profile.
The theorem also applies to raw-capacity-proportional rules y_i=c_i p_j(c),
because every witness profile below has all c_i<=T. Fairness is not needed.
An arbitrary owner-dependent top-up need not remain in this class. A lower bound
on an already covering and monotone proportional base is inherited by any
nonnegative top-up, but a nonmonotone/noncovering base repaired by a top-up is
not covered by this statement merely because its base is proportional.

THEOREM 1 (FINITE LINKED-PROFILE LOWER BOUND)
For every k>=2 and integer r>=2, on k pools with exactly r fixed owners each,
any rule in this class with a uniform budget factor B satisfies

  B >= k*r / [k+(r-1)*r**r].                                      (1)

In particular, no constant B works over all k and owner counts. At r=3,k=216,
B>=12/5, or subsidy beta>=7/5. All capacities in the witness are strictly positive.

PROOF
Let H have capacity 1 at each of its k*r owners. Then T(H)=r, and its budget
constraint gives sum_j p_j(H)<=B.

For each j, let L_j have all r owners in pool j report 1/r, while all other
owners report 1. Then T(L_j)=1. A coalition taking all r owners of pool j and
one owner from each other pool has value 1. Coverage therefore gives
  p_j(L_j)+sum_{l!=j}p_l(L_j)>=1.
The budget at the same profile gives
  p_j(L_j)+r*sum_{l!=j}p_l(L_j)<=B.
Subtracting the latter from r times the former gives
  B+(r-1)*p_j(L_j)>=r.                                         (2)
This remains valid even when B>=r; no positivity of r-B is assumed.

Join L_j to H by raising the r owners in pool j, one at a time, from 1/r to 1.
Call the successive profiles P_{j,0}=L_j,...,P_{j,r}=H. At step h the changing
owner is the only report changed. Every profile has T>=1 and every capacity
<=1, so no capping changes the payment formula. Its own-report monotonicity is
  p_j(P_{j,h+1}) >= (1/r)*p_j(P_{j,h}).
Multiplying along the r steps proves
  r**r * p_j(H) >= p_j(L_j).                                    (3)
Here proportionality allows consecutive comparisons to transport the SAME pool
price even though different owners change. Unrestricted payments have no such
identity; replacing different owners' payments by a common price is essential.

Equations (2)-(3) imply B+(r-1)*r**r*p_j(H)>=r. Sum over j, and then use
sum_j p_j(H)<=B, to obtain [k+(r-1)*r**r]*B>=k*r. This is (1).

EXPLICIT NONNEGATIVE CERTIFICATE
Write every row as left side >= right side. For each j use:
  r times the L_j coalition-coverage row (right side 1),
  once the L_j budget row B-p_j-r*sum_{l!=j}p_l>=0,
  for h=0,...,r-1 use (r-1)*r**(h+1) times the actual changing-owner
     monotonicity row p_j(P_{j,h+1})-p_j(P_{j,h})/r>=0.
Finally, ONCE IN TOTAL, add (r-1)*r**r times the normalized H budget row
B-sum_j p_j(H)>=0. All price variables cancel, leaving
[k+(r-1)*r**r]*B>=k*r. This is a finite exact rational certificate for the
restricted class, not a dual certificate against all mechanisms.

COROLLARY 2 (SHARP FIRST-ORDER FLOOR-FREE PROPORTIONAL SCALING)
Let A_k be the infimum of achievable worst-case budget factors, among families
of floor-free CAPPED-proportional rules defined for every finite fixed ownership with
k pools. Owner counts are unbounded. Then
  A_k = (1+o(1))*log(k)/log(log(k)).
For the lower bound fix epsilon>0, put L=log(k) and
r=floor((1-epsilon)*L/log(L)). For sufficiently large k, r>=2 and
log((r-1)*r**r) = log(r-1)+r*log(r) <= (1-epsilon)*L+o(L).
Thus (r-1)*r**r/k tends to zero, and (1) gives B>=(1-o(1))*r.
Take the liminf and then epsilon down to zero.
For the upper bound use the BASE rule of SCALING_THEOREM.txt in
../2026-10-04-stage-scaling/: y_i=d_i*Pr[sum_{h in N_j}d_h U_h is minimal],
with independent Uniform[0,1] U_h. Its coverage and own-monotonicity were proved
there, and its factor is <=R_k+1/log(R_k), where
R_k*log(R_k/e)=log(k), hence the asserted upper asymptotic.
This corollary omits the original-core floor. The prior max top-up restores that
floor at additive budget cost <=1, but the final rule need not be proportional.
We do NOT claim a matching upper bound within the class of final proportional
rules that satisfy the floor, nor a minimax result for unrestricted mechanisms.

IMPLICATION FOR THE ACTIVE TARGET
Any universally bounded mechanism must have genuinely nonproportional payments
inside some pool at some profile. Allowing an arbitrary profile-dependent pool
price or correlated threshold selection alone cannot solve the target if final
payments remain proportional. This extends the obstruction to arbitrary profile-dependent pool prices.
The earlier fixed-statistic independent-threshold class has exactly the form
d_i*p_j(c), so it is contained here; its quantitative bounds and the separate
ratio-only price bounds are not claimed to be pointwise weaker than (1). It does not rule out nonlinear owner payments,
and it does not establish an unrestricted diverging lower bound.
