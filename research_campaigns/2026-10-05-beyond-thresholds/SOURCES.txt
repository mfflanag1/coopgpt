3. Kurz (2013), Measuring voting power in convex policy spaces.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.5936
Original Definition 6.11 inspected again: absolute continuous Banzhaf is the
integrated endpoint marginal. The earlier project upper rule is this established
functional applied to a normalized capped game. The new result is a proposed
lower bound for every covering own-monotone capped-proportional payment rule,
plus an asymptotic minimax consequence using the existing upper analysis; no
claim to have invented the functional, integration method or probability tools.
