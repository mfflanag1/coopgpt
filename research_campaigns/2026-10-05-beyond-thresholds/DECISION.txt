WHAT CHANGED
The new linked-owner proof excludes EVERY within-pool capped-proportional rule,
even with arbitrary report-dependent prices. For floor-free capped-proportional
rules the exact first-order minimax is now (1+o(1))*log k/log log k. Thus further
optimization of common per-unit pool prices cannot reach a universal constant.
A second obstruction excludes all fixed report-independent random-order values
with bounded rescaling, including arbitrarily correlated arrival orders.

A nonlinear assignment escapes the first obstruction on its entire labeled
witness family: budget <=2T without floors, <=3T with the original floor, while
covering every coalition and respecting every retained report edge. This is a
precise reason the new proportional proof does not lift to unrestricted payments.
It is not a mechanism on all reports. The independent nonlinear all-profile
candidate failed on a two-pool five-owner coalition, which is retained exactly.

The independent unrestricted-lower attempt produced a sound barrier for one
single-sink/single-exporter certificate architecture, but included a false
weighted source equality. That error was caught, refuted exactly, and excluded.
Both raw delegate packages required corrections; routing adjudications record
that fact rather than labeling their unedited output accepted.

NEXT MATHEMATICAL TARGET

finite-family assignment to profiles with TWO simultaneously nonunit pools and
unequal positive capacities, while maintaining a uniform bound (preferably 2T
before fairness). In parallel as a mathematical alternative, construct a profile
network with several exporters and intermediate profiles that both receive and
send own-monotonicity constraints. It must survive the explicit bounded payment
assignment on the old chains and avoid the proved star architecture.

First gate: an exact contradiction or an explicit valid payment assignment on a
small closed two-pool-changing family. Only then enlarge the parameter. A claim
of divergence must produce a symbolic family of nonnegative inequality weights,
fixed original ownership and unbounded factor; a numerical grid is insufficient.
A positive route must prove all-report coverage and own-monotonicity separately
before its budget estimate is used. Do not expand common-price or fixed-order
searches, and do not assume earlier owners retain payments after others change.
