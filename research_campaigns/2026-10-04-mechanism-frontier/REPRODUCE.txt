EXPONENTIAL ENCLOSURES
For rational q>=0, let P_N=sum_{k=0}^N q^k/k!, a=q^(N+1)/(N+1)! and
r=q/(N+2)<1. All terms of exp(q) are positive and each successive tail ratio is
at most r, so P_N<=exp(q)<=P_N+a/(1-r). Invert positive endpoints to obtain
 1/[P_N+a/(1-r)] <= exp(-q) <= 1/P_N.
The verifier takes N=max(80,ceil(q)+64). Payment inequalities are finite rational
linear combinations of exp(-q). Identical exponents are combined EXACTLY first;
coefficient signs select the appropriate interval endpoints. Exact symbolic zero
or nonnegative coefficients are immediate certificates. Otherwise the lower bound
must be nonnegative. An unresolved interval raises an error; it never becomes PASS.
