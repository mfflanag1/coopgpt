EXISTING 1/8 LOWER BOUND IN THREE PROFILES -- rechecked 2026-10-04
This is the previously obtained obstruction, not a new improvement.
Four fixed original owners, pools {0,1}|{2,3}. No fairness assumption needed.
Suppose y is nonnegative, covers every coalition, is own-report nondecreasing,
and has total payment <=(1+beta)T at every profile.

Base profile c=(1,1|1,1). The coalition {0,3} implies y_0(c)+y_3(c)>=1.

Profile a=(1,1|1,2). The coalitions {0,2},{1,2},{0,1,3} respectively give
 y_0(a)+y_2(a)>=1,
 y_1(a)+y_2(a)>=1,
 y_0(a)+y_1(a)+y_3(a)>=2.
Adding gives 2 sum_i y_i(a)-y_3(a)>=4. Since T(a)=2, the budget bound implies
 y_3(a)<=4beta. Monotonicity along c_3:1 to 2 implies y_3(c)<=y_3(a)<=4beta.

Profile b=(2,1|1,1). The coalitions {1,2},{1,3},{0,2,3} respectively give
 y_1(b)+y_2(b)>=1,
 y_1(b)+y_3(b)>=1,
 y_0(b)+y_2(b)+y_3(b)>=2.
Adding gives 2 sum_i y_i(b)-y_0(b)>=4. Here T(b)=2, so y_0(b)<=4beta.
Monotonicity along c_0:1 to 2 implies y_0(c)<=y_0(b)<=4beta.

Therefore 1<=y_0(c)+y_3(c)<=8beta, proving beta>=1/8. Every capacity is positive.
This applies to unrestricted mechanisms with the required H/3 floors because it
already applies when those floors are omitted. It uses no anonymity, continuity,
homogeneity, owner cloning or numerical LP conclusion.

The multipliers 1/8 on coalition/monotonicity rows and 1/4 on the two budget rows
produce the 11-row certificate in lp-2x2-012.json. verify_frontier.py reconstructs
those rows without importing the discovery code and checks their exact sum.
