Graph: s->u has two parallel arc players1,2; u->t has two players3,4.
Every coalition has value min(total available source capacity,total sink capacity).
A payment rule y(c) may use an external subsidy. It is nonnegative, covers every
coalition at every capacity profile, and is nondecreasing in each owner's own
capacity holding all other reports fixed (the under-report-only no-cost model).
Define Delta(c)=sum_i y_i(c)-T(c)>=0.

Claim. Any such rule, on a domain containing c0=(1,1,1,1) and the four profiles
obtained by raising one coordinate to2, has some profile with Delta/T>=1/8.
Any additional original-core fairness floor only restricts the class further.

Proof. At c0, grand-coalition coverage gives sum y_i(c0)>=2, so one player i
receives at least1/2. Suppose i=1 by interchanging sides and within-side labels.
At c1=(2,1,1,1), own-capacity monotonicity gives y_1(c1)>=1/2 and T(c1)=2.
Write x=y(c1). Coalitions{2,3}, {2,4}, {1,3,4} have worths1,1,2. Adding their
three rationality constraints yields
  x_1+2*x_2+2*x_3+2*x_4 >=4,
that is 2*sum x - x_1 >=4. Hence
  Delta(c1)=sum x-2 >= x_1/2 >=1/4,
and Delta(c1)/T(c1)>=1/8. The other choices of i are symmetric. QED.
No enumeration, LP or integer-payoff assumption is used.

The four-player qualitative obstruction is established in Huang-Mei-Yang-Cao,
Mechanism Design in Max-Flows2025, Example1. The displayed three-coalition
summation is our calibration calculation, not a certified novel lower bound.
It establishes no upper bound for the full capacity domain;1/8 may be weak there.
Under truthful efficient core selection Delta=0 the contradiction is the already
known qualitative impossibility. Our12-player example concerns a different,
static source-ideal compatibility property and is not needed for this lemma.

FINITE-DOMAIN CALIBRATION (not a full-domain mechanism)
The five profiles alone have exact optimum1/8. At c0 pay every player1/2.
At a profile with raised player i, pay i1/2, its same-side partner1/4, and
each opposite-side player3/4. These are report-dependent deterministic payments.
The only pairs of distinct profiles with identical other-player capacities are
c0 and the profile where that same player is raised. Its payment stays1/2.
For a raised source, coalitions with neither source have value0; those with
the unraised source only and k sink players have value min(1,k) and payments
1/4+3k/4; those with the raised source only have value k and payments1/2+3k/4,
where k=0,1,2. Those with both sources have value k and payments3/4+3k/4.
Each inequality holds. Side reversal covers raised sinks. Total payment9/4
gives subsidy1/4 and ratio1/8; the baseline total is2.
The original core ideals are (1,1,1,1) at c0 and (0,0,1,1) at a raised source
(up to labels): the lower-bound inequality with Delta=0 forces the raised
source's payoff0, its two source-sink pair constraints force both sinks to1,
and efficiency forces the other source to0. Thus all H/3 floors also hold.
