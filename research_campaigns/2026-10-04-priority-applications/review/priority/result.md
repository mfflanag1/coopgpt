## New full-text evidence

**Sotomayor, "Labor time shared in the assignment game generating new cooperative and competitive structures"**, FEA-USP Working Paper 2013-02 (title page dated April 2012).
- URL: http://www.repec.eae.fea.usp.br/documentos/Marilda02WP.pdf (RePEc record: https://ideas.repec.org/p/spa/wpaper/2013wpecon2.html)
- I read printed pp. 2–20 and 27–34 as page images. Pages 21–26 and the appendix proofs are unread.

What it establishes:
- **Model match (pp. 8–10, Def. 2.1.1).** Real quotas r(p), s(q), x_pq ≥ 0 with no per-pair cap, and per-unit gain a_pq. This is the unrestricted transportation model; the target game is its complete a ≡ 1 case (my inference from the LP on p. 29, not stated in the paper).
- **Sotomayor 2002 is the same game's core (p. 12 and p. 20).** The "one-dimensional payoffs" version "is studied in Sotomayor (2002)", and corewise-stability is the coalitional inequalities plus efficiency "as it is shown in Sotomayor (2002)". This confirms second-hand that Sotomayor 2002 is the right comparison paper; the 2002 paper itself remains unread.
- **Provenance (p. 3, footnote 1).** A draft dates from 1997, and a 2002 version was titled "The multiple partners game with a general quota".
- **Integer version covered (p. 3 fn. 5; p. 33).** Results hold for integer quotas where "a buyer is allowed to purchase more than one item from the same seller"; multiple-partners is the pair-cap-1 restriction.
- **Core results present.**
  - Prop. 2.2.1–2.2.2 (pp. 13–14): a sufficient condition for corewise-stability, and individual rationality.
  - Example 2.2.1 (pp. 15–16): the core is strictly bigger than the setwise-stable set, on a 2×2 market with r = (2,2), s = (1,2).
  - Example 2.2.2 (p. 16): setwise-stable but not strongly stable.
  - Theorem 5.4 (p. 30): non-emptiness via dual allocations.
- **What is absent in the pages read.** There is no theorem, example or open-problem sentence on a P-optimal or Q-optimal point of the entire core, and no lattice statement for the core. Section 6 (p. 34) defers "the algebraic structure of the five solution sets" to an unnamed "companion paper", which I did not locate. That companion paper is the most specific new unread lead.

## Secondary item read (not the target model)

**Sotomayor, ICERM 2020 slides on "Competition and cooperation in a two-sided matching market with replication"** (JET 183, 2019, pp. 1030–1056): https://app.icerm.brown.edu/posters/event_289_posterId_10.pdf
- I read slides 1–18 of 33.
- The model is the pair-cap-1 multiple-partners game, replicated. Slide 18 notes that in replicated markets a buyer can trade with several copies of the same seller, "as if she was trading more than once with the same seller".
- It concerns stability and competitive sets and the core not shrinking under replication. Nothing in the slides read addresses whole-core side-optimal existence.
- Its reference list cites a 2013 working paper, "The time-sharing assignment models, lending new insights to the theory of two-sided matching markets"; whether this is the same paper as the FEA-USP one is unverified.
