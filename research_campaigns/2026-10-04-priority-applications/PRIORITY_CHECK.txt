EXACT MODEL MATCH (coordinator derivation)
In unrestricted transportation, a coalition with left owners S and right owners R
can choose nonnegative amounts z_ij, with row sums <=a_i and column sums <=b_j.
Set every unit return to1 and allow every pair. Its value is
 max sum z_ij = min(sum_{i in S} a_i, sum_{j in R} b_j).
The two sums bound the objective. Greedily pairing residual supply and demand
attains their minimum; for integer capacities it also gives integer z. This is
exactly the two-hop arc-player characteristic function. Players remain owners,
not cloned units, and monetary core payoffs remain real-valued. It is not the
pair-capacity-one multiple-partners model. The entire core is compared, not only
competitive, strongly stable, or optimal-dual payoff subsets.
The existing exact proof gives H_A=6, H_B=38, but x_A+x_B<=43 throughout C.
Thus no whole-core point simultaneously maximizes every left owner's payoff.
The proof and witnesses are in ../2026-10-04-flow-compatibility/ENDPOINT_COUNTEREXAMPLE.txt.
