PROPOSED TITLE
Unbounded Funding Inflation of Own-Report Minimum-Norm Envelopes

ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY
Consider nonnegative coalition-cover payments for a fixed-owner production
game, and choose the Euclidean minimum-norm cover without imposing total
efficiency. Taking each owner's supremum over its own feasible underreports
makes that coordinate monotone while retaining coalition coverage. We give
a finite min-linear production family for which this monotone envelope has
no uniform C sqrt(N) total-funding bound, for any fixed C. The construction
uses N fixed owners, two resources per owner, binary supporting-form
coefficients, and the full nonnegative real report domain. It yields an
explicit sequence with funding greater than
(1/40)sqrt(N)(log N/log log N)^(1/3) times output. The proof combines a
q-ary line-hypergraph marginal construction, finite rational transport,
exact projection certificates and simultaneous realization of all selected
own-report comparisons. The examples have a common veto and admit an
efficient competing rule; the result is specifically an obstruction to
the projection-envelope architecture, not to truthful mechanisms generally.

EXACT OBJECT TO REVIEW
q(v)=argmin{sum_i x_i^2:x>=0,x(S)>=v(S) for every S}.
M_i(b)=sup_{0<=a_i<=b_i} q_i(v_(a_i,b_-i)).
All owners, bundles and the ONE production function are fixed across these
comparisons. There is no efficiency equality in q, no private-cost report,
no coalition-proofness claim, and no endpoint restriction in M. The proof
uses endpoints only to obtain lower bounds on the full supremum.
