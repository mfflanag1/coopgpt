The `v`-branch is `min{v(S),t} = t·v(S)` since `v ∈ {0,1}`. So `F = t·v` and `M_u = R/(1+R)` stand. Verification: enumerate all `A, j` for a 2–3 owner instance with one fractional `a^j_R`.
