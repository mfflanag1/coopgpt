### 2. The chosen target, stated precisely

**Observation (checkable in 10 minutes, the whole basis of the target).** In the manuscript's fixed-arity proof, arity enters the ladder arithmetic **only** through the *activation number* $k$ = number of private owners that become active per single restored owner. Lines 1491–1503: the protected coalition is restorer + old actives, giving $(a_m+1)q_m\ge a_mq_{m-1}$, $T_m=kmq_m$, $T_M\ge\frac{k}{k+1}Q_{k,M}$, $Q_{k,M}=\prod_{m=2}^{M}\frac{km}{k(m-1)+1}$. Writing $A_m$ for the active count and $\Delta$ for activation, the step factor is exactly
$$T_m/T_{m-1}\;\ge\;\frac{A_{m-1}+\Delta}{A_{m-1}+1},\qquad\text{so } T_M\sim A_M^{(\Delta-1)/\Delta}.$$
The manuscript sets $\Delta=k=r-1$ *because its technology is the binary-circuit (i.e. $\mathbb F_2$) one*, where any two rows determine the activity (lines 1294–1296, 1341–1349). The $(r-2)/(r-1)$ law is therefore an **artifact of linearity of the design, not of arity**.

**Proposed Lemma (A$_\lambda$).** For every fixed integer $\lambda\ge3$ there exist, for all $M\ge1$, finite **arity-three** packing institutions ($F(c)=\max\{\pi x:Ax\le c,x\ge0\}$, $A,\pi\ge0$, $\le3$ positive entries per profitable column) with fixed actual fully eligible owners, full real report boxes $[0,1]^d$, a profile ladder $C_1,\dots,C_M$ in which (i) exactly one actual owner raises its report at each step, (ii) $F(b_N(C_m))$ is constant in $m$, (iii) the stabilizer of $C_m$ in the technology's automorphism group acts transitively on the $A_m=\lambda m$ active private owners, (iv) the $C_1$-stabilizer acts transitively on the three owners of a supported activity, and (v) $A_m=A_{m-1}+\lambda$.

**Consequence (already proved; drop-in with $k:=\lambda$).** For every integer $s\ge2\lambda$,
$$K_3(s)\;\ge\;K^{\rm ladder}_{3,\lambda}(s)\;\ge\;\tfrac{\lambda}{3}\big(s/2\lambda\big)^{(\lambda-1)/\lambda},$$
by the certificate of lines 1459–1522 with the arity-3 seed $3q_1\ge1$ (lines 1080–1082). At $\lambda=3$: $K_3(s)=\Omega(s^{2/3})$, which **supersedes $\sqrt{s/6}$ and refutes $K_3(s)=\Theta(\sqrt s)$**; $\lambda$ growing gives $s^{1-o(1)}$, i.e. it would show the campaign's $O(s)$ upper-bound target is exponent-optimal.

### 3. Concrete route (fresh leverage)

1. **Leave $\mathbb F_2$.** Use a *non-linear* symmetric arity-3 design: collinear point-triples of $\mathrm{PG}(m,q)$ with $q\ge3$. Two points lie on a unique line of $q+1$ points, hence in exactly $q-1$ collinear triples (standard incidence geometry — stated here as standard, **not** fetched in this scout), while $\mathrm{PGL}_{m+1}(q)$ is 2-transitive on points, supplying hypothesis (iv). $q=2$ degenerates to the manuscript's linear case with pair-multiplicity 1 — this is exactly where the $\Delta=r-1$ ceiling comes from.
2. **Alternative, purely LP-side:** repeated columns $\{v,u,y_1\},\dots,\{v,u,y_\lambda\}$ (legal: $A$ arbitrary nonnegative) give $\Delta=\lambda$ with the ceiling enforced by $v$'s single unit. Its *known failure mode must be repaired first*: if any row is group-theoretically identifiable as "the cap", the rule paying everything to the cap owner satisfies coverage (private pairs produce 0) with $\kappa=1$. So hypothesis (iv) is load-bearing, and the attack must verify it before anything else.
3. **Exact check order:** (a) smallest instance, $\le 20$ rows, two consecutive rungs, exact rational LP for $F$ and for the symmetric dual; (b) verify the profile stabilizer's orbits by explicit matrices; (c) only then extend to the $\lambda$-indexed, $M$-growing family and recompute the truncated product $P_{\lambda,M,s}$, $a_m=\min\{s-1,\lambda(m-1)\}$.

**Falsifier (and the dual branch, also a deliverable).** Prove an **activation–production inequality**: for every arity-$r$ packing institution and every single-owner restoration,
$$\frac{A_{m-1}+\Delta_m}{A_{m-1}+t_m}\cdot\frac{F_{m-1}}{F_m}\;\le\;\frac{A_{m-1}+(r-1)t_m}{A_{m-1}+t_m}.$$
Quantitative hint that this may be true: for a **vertex-transitive** unit-profit 3-uniform technology the symmetric fractional matching $x_e=1/\deg$ is optimal, so $F=A/3$ exactly and $T/F\le 3q\le$ const — the gap exists only because the *profile-restricted* configuration (a pencil) is far from transitive while the *technology* is transitive. If every extra awakened owner necessarily adds matching capacity at rate $\ge1/3$, the ladder is capped and $s^{(r-2)/(r-1)}$ is optimal for the whole architecture — the first lower-side obstruction, the mirror of `positive_marginal_barrier.txt`, and it would redirect all remaining effort to the upper bound.

### 4. Why this is not routine and not already excluded

- It does **not** touch the fixed nonnegative whole-owner marginal-weight barrier (`positive_marginal_barrier.txt:4–18`): that theorem constrains *mechanisms* (upper bounds); this target constrains *arbitrary nonnegative rules* from below. No overlap.
- It is not the inherited recommendation (upper bound) and does not reuse the failed dual-certificate route of `upper_gate_report.txt`.
- It is not a bounded-family claim: the deliverable is a $\lambda$-indexed family with $M\to\infty$ plugged into an already-proved growing certificate; a single finite instance would be a diagnostic only.
- Antecedent scan gave no exponent $>1/2$ for a comparable coalition-sum quantity. The nearest primary leverage is Immorlica–Mahdian–Mirrokni's $\Omega(n)$ (set cover) vs $\Omega(n^{1/3})$ (vertex cover) budget-balance lower bounds for cross-monotonic schemes — an *arity-sensitive* pattern that motivates candidate 4 — but their hypothesis is per-incumbent cross-monotonicity, strictly stronger than coalition-sum TCWM (`priority_matrix.txt:50–61`), so no verbatim transfer.

### 5. Stopping criterion

- **t+60 min:** a verified two-rung arity-3 instance with $\Delta\ge3$, constant $F$, and transitive active orbit, or switch branch.
- **t+120 min:** either the $\lambda$-family + recomputed $B_{\lambda,s}$, or a proof of the activation–production inequality for single-ceiling ladders.
- **t+150 min:** if neither, report the exact tension (awakening vs. matching capacity) as an unresolved obstruction with the smallest failing configuration. Explicit stop rules: do not claim a new $K_3$ bound without a growing family and a nonnegative rational certificate in original owner/profile variables; do not promote a finite witness to a general theorem.

### Limitations (honest)

15-minute scout, not a novelty or correctness check. I did **not** run any LP, did not construct the geometry, and did not verify Lemma (A$_\lambda$) is satisfiable — it may well be false, in which case the dual branch is the output. The Immorlica et al. primary PDF would not text-extract through the fetch tool, so its exact theorem numbers/bounds here rest on a secondary survey statement, not on the primary text. The $\mathrm{PG}(m,q)$ pair-multiplicity $q-1$ and 2-transitivity of $\mathrm{PGL}$ are standard facts asserted from knowledge, not fetched. The step-factor identity $T_m/T_{m-1}\ge(A_{m-1}+\Delta)/(A_{m-1}+t)$ is my own derivation from manuscript lines 1459–1503 (inference, matching their $Q_{k,M}$ exactly at $\Delta=k$, $t=1$) and should be re-derived independently before being relied on. The "all payment to the cap owner" failure mode is my reasoning, not a cited result. Others are scouting independently; this is one ranked opinion.
