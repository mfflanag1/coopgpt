P4 ASYMMETRIC CONE: HOMOGENEOUS COMMON-PRICE TCWM LOWER 7/4
21 October 2026. Scout derivation; no independent audit or novelty claim.
This is an architecture obstruction, NOT an unrestricted P4 mechanism lower.

Let K={(t,x,y): t>=max(|x|,|y|)} and F=t-max(|x|,y).
A common price P(C) pays an owner B the amount P(C).B. Assume homogeneous
prices, coalition coverage, nonnegative payments, and own-report monotonicity
for arbitrary cone restorations. All examples fit two fixed actual owners with
cone upper bounds (4,0,0). Symmetry averaging under x->-x preserves these
properties and yields P_x=0 at aggregates with x=0. Only this symmetry is used.

At every interior aggregate C, write P=(a,b,c). Singleton coverage using an
owner eta(1,0,0) forces a>=1. Nonnegative payments using a small ray owner
force a+b+c>=0. The latter will be used only at the axis R below.

Choose 0<s<1 and 2s/(1+s)<=r<1. Write e=1-s, d=1-r, lambda=d/e.
Use five aggregate shapes:
 O=(1,0,0), S=(1,s,s), T=(1,0,r), R=(1,r,0), N=(1,r,-r).
Their prices carry subscripts. At O and T, b=0 by reflection symmetry.
Put q_S=a_S+b_S+c_S, q_T=a_T+c_T,
q_R=a_R+b_R, q_N=a_N+b_N-c_N.
All of S,T,R,N have production respectively e,d,d,d.
Budget at T,R,N implies
 q_T<=d(kappa-a_T)/r, q_R<=d(kappa-a_R)/r,
 q_N<=d(kappa-a_N)/r.                                 (B)

FOUR FINITE FIXED-OWNER RESTORATIONS
1. Low aggregate e O: owner1 holds e(1,1,1)/2, owner2 holds e(1,-1,-1)/2.
Restore owner1 by s(1,1,1), obtaining aggregate S. Homogeneity and TCWM give
 (1+s)q_S >= e(a_O+c_O).                              (I)

2. Low aggregate d O: owner1 holds d(1,1,-1)/2, owner2 holds d(1,-1,1)/2.
Restore owner1 by r(1,1,-1), obtaining aggregate N. Thus
 (1+r)q_N >= d(a_O-c_O).                              (II)

3. Low aggregate lambda S: owner1 holds
 lambda((1+s)/2,(3s-1)/2,(1+s)/2),
and owner2 holds d(1,1,-1)/2. Restore owner1 by
 D_T=(1-lambda,-lambda s,1-lambda), obtaining aggregate T.
The restored vector is in K because lambda s<=1-lambda, equivalent to the
assumption r>=2s/(1+s). Both low holdings are in K; |3s-1|<=1+s.
The high owner1 bundle is ((1+r)/2,-d/2,(1+r)/2). Therefore
 (1+r)q_T >= lambda[(1+s)q_S-2e b_S].                (III)

4. Low aggregate lambda S: owner1 holds
 lambda((1+s)/2,(1+s)/2,(3s-1)/2),
and owner2 holds d(1,-1,1)/2. Restore owner1 by
 D_R=(1-lambda,1-lambda,-lambda s), obtaining aggregate R.
The high owner1 bundle is ((1+r)/2,(1+r)/2,-d/2). Thus
 (1+r)q_R-d c_R >=lambda[(1+s)q_S-2e c_S].           (IV)

All four restorations have F(D)=0, so they are TCWM comparisons and remain
necessary for full OUT. No owner is split, merged, added or removed.

ELIMINATION
Since q_R+c_R>=0, adding (III)+(IV) and using (B) and a_T,a_R>=1 yields
 (3+r)(kappa-1)/r >= 4s q_S/e+2a_S
                         >=4s(a_O+c_O)/(1+s)+2.       (V)
From (II),(B), and a_N>=1,
 a_O-c_O <=(1+r)(kappa-1)/r.
Since a_O>=1, a_O+c_O>=2-(1+r)(kappa-1)/r. Substitution into (V) gives

 kappa >= 1+r(2+10s)/(3+r+7s+5rs).                   (L)

Take s=1-e and r=1-e^2 with e down to0. The restoration condition holds,
and (L) tends to7/4. This proves the stated homogeneous-price-class lower.
At s=9/10,r=97/100, the bound is exactly5061/2927=1.72907413734....
The independent rational dual reconstruction in graph_cone_exact_small_certificate.json
matches that value; the analytic elimination above is the main evidence.

LIMITS
This does not prove factor2 and does not exclude compressed common-price
rules with funding strictly between7/4 and2. The finite larger LP scouts give
numerical values near1.8344 but no exact all-parameter implication is asserted.
Homogeneity is an explicit hypothesis; no unproved cone homogeneity reduction
is used. A lower against compressed common cone prices does not transfer to
all physical orthant prices or arbitrary owner-aware mechanisms.

APPENDIX: A CANDIDATE REMOVAL OF HOMOGENEITY WITHIN THE COMMON-PRICE CLASS
This extension was checked by the scout but has not received independent QA.
It does NOT assert that arbitrary owner-aware mechanisms reduce to cone prices.

Suppose P is any common price field for TWO actual owners with public bound
E=(4,0,0) each, on every real report in [0,E]_K. No regularity or homogeneity
is assumed. Restrict attention to rational interior aggregates C with C_t<2.
Every bundle B in [0,C]_K has B_t<2 and hence E-B in K, so all such ownership
decompositions are allowed by the fixed public box. Nonnegative singleton
payments for small ray owners imply P(C).R_j>=0 for the four extreme rays.
Budget implies C.P(C)<=kappa F(C). Since C is interior, it has a decomposition
C=sum_j alpha_j R_j with all alpha_j>0. Thus each ray price is bounded above
by kappa F(C)/alpha_j. The rays span R^3, giving a compact coordinate box
for P(C), depending on C but uniform over all feasible fields.

Let D be the countable rational interior domain C_t<2. In the product of
these compact coordinate boxes, take the closed convex set of fields obeying
all rational two-owner coverage, budget, nonnegativity and TCWM constraints
whose aggregates lie in D. It is nonempty by restriction of the purported
rule. For a positive rational s<=1 define (T_s P)(C)=P(sC). This maps the set
into itself: scale every owner's low/high report and restored bundle by s,
apply the original finite inequality, and divide it by s. Every scaled report
stays in the SAME fixed public box. Homogeneity of F is used, not of P.

The commuting contractions T_(1/p), p prime, have a common fixed point:
average T_s P over s=product_(j<=L) p_j^(-e_j), 0<=e_j<L, for the first L
primes. For any fixed prime p and coordinate C, shifting its exponent changes
the average by at most twice the uniform C-coordinate bound divided by L.
A diagonal compactness subsequence therefore has a limit fixed by every
T_(1/p), hence by every rational contraction. Because all defining finite
constraints are closed, the limiting field retains the same contract and K.
Rational dilation invariance whenever both endpoints belong to D follows
by applying the inverse contraction. This gives precisely the homogeneity
needed by the rational five-profile proof and by rational finite LP rows.

If this compactness argument is accepted, both the7/4 limit and the exact
finite full-OUT certificate in graph_cone_out_refined_exact.json lower-bound
ALL common cone price fields on this fixed two-owner model, not merely the
homogeneous subfamily. The prices of an arbitrary owner-aware mechanism
cannot be inserted into this argument: common aggregate-linearity remains
the indispensable architecture hypothesis. No boundary price continuity,
measurability, or pointwise convergence as C approaches zero is invoked.
