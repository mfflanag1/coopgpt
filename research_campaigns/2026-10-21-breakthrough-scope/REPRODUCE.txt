REPRODUCTION — FINAL OWN-REPORT ENVELOPE OBSTRUCTION
21 October2026. Python3 standard library only; no solver, network or GPU.

From this directory run:
    python3 replay_essentials.py

It runs five exact diagnostic programs, saves their output in replay-logs/,
and records script hashes, return codes and timings in REPLAY_RESULTS.json.
Expected result: five PASS lines and a PASS summary. The q-ary independent
audit includes large-integer arithmetic through Q16 and may take longer
than the other checks. A timeout or nonzero return is a failed replay,
not an approval. No canonical CoopGPT files are edited by this replay.

WHAT EACH CHECK ESTABLISHES
1. activation_qary_cube_check.py: small exhaustive local repairs for
   Q3,4,5 and exact construction inequalities for Q3 through10.
2. mass_search_qary_cube_audit.py: independently written repair/marginal
   enumeration (2,014 states, including composite Q4 and Q6); exact
   parameter and KKT conditions through Q16; dimension-extension cases.
3. mass_geometry_qary_verify.py: independent125 exact finite assertions,
   including the finite quantitative amplification inequalities.
4. activation_qary_direct_check.py: exact direct common-veto funding and
   diagonal certificates at selected Q and dimension multiples.
5. mass_search_true_envelope_upper.py: supplementary all-real report
   transfer and common-veto cap checks, including empty/singleton coalitions
   and negative residual clipping. This supports the stronger squeeze
   results; the final lower theorem needs only its explicit selected reports.

WHAT THE CHECKS DO NOT ESTABLISH
They do not prove the theorem for every Q by enumeration, solve the
astronomically large transport network, enumerate every coalition in the
final production game, or establish novelty. The all-Q proof, all-cut
transport argument, fixed-owner realization and quantifiers are in
proofs/FINAL_ENVELOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt. Independent audits reproduce those
arguments. This is conventional mathematical verification, not formal
proof-assistant verification.
