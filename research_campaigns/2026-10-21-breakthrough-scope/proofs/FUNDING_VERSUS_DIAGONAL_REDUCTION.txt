FUNDING AND SQUARED-DIAGONAL AGGREGATES ARE DIFFERENT TARGETS
21 October2026. Exact asymptotic consequence of the recorded union/veto KKT
construction. This note concerns certified witness lower bounds, not a
universal upper theorem. It requires independent audit before promotion.

BASE CONTRACT.
Let A have finitely many NONEMPTY0/1 rows on n coordinates, all with demand1.
Let z be its minimum-norm cover and rho=||z||^2>0. For each owner i choose
an incident-only downward RHS relaxation, its projection q^i, and particular
nonnegative KKT multipliers lambda^i with mass L_i>0. Define
 p_i=q^i_i, theta_i=p_i/L_i,
 H=rho*sum_i theta_i^2,
 J=sqrt(rho/n)*sum_i theta_i.
Zero-coordinate witnesses may be replaced by full witnesses if needed to
ensure positive L_i. Always L_i<=rho and0<=theta_i<=1. Cauchy gives J^2<=H;
equality holds when the chosen theta_i are all equal. Thus H>4 alone does
NOT imply J>2 in a nonuniform family.

UNION PRODUCT.
For integer t>=1 take t disjoint copies of the base and every row formed by
choosing one base row in each copy and taking their union; all demands1.
Its full projection is z/t in each copy, with squared norm rho_t=rho/t.
The explicit incident-row witnesses from
 inputs/UNIFORM_VETO_AMPLIFICATION_REDUCTION.txt
have, for a focal owner of type i,
 p_i(t)=rho*p_i/[rho+(t-1)L_i]
       =rho*theta_i/[t+rho/L_i-1],
 L_i(t)=rho*L_i/[rho+(t-1)L_i]
       =rho/[t+rho/L_i-1].
All own RHS are in[0,1], all off-owner RHS remain1, and their KKT certificate
is the product of the selected focal multipliers with the other full ones,
scaled exactly as in the cited proof. In particular, as t tends to infinity,
 t*p_i(t)->rho*theta_i and t*L_i(t)->rho for each fixed base owner.

VETO PRODUCT AND LIMITS.
Fix c>0. Choose B_t=max(1,floor(c*t/rho)) independent union-product blocks,
and add one common veto coordinate to every row. There are
 N_t=B_t*t*n+1 owners, with minimum total cover T=1. Its full veto coordinate
is u_t=(B_t*rho/t)/(1+B_t*rho/t). The certified private witness of type i is
 a_i(t)=p_i(t)/[1+(B_t-1)*rho/t+L_i(t)].
This follows from the same exact common-veto KKT proof, including the
veto stationarity identity. Every selected own-report comparison has the
same actual owners and can be simultaneously realized by the explicit
2N_t-resource direct production function in CONSOLIDATED_THEOREM.txt.

Let S_t=u_t+B_t*t*sum_i a_i(t) and
D_t=u_t^2+B_t*t*sum_i a_i(t)^2. These are LOWER bounds on the true envelope's
total and squared total at its designated full bundles. Then
 B_t/t->c/rho,  N_t/t^2->cn/rho,
 t*a_i(t)->rho*theta_i/(1+c),  u_t->c/(1+c).
Consequently
 lim S_t/sqrt(N_t)=sqrt(c)/(1+c)*J,
 lim D_t=(c^2+cH)/(1+c)^2.
These are exact limits of the selected certificates; other real reports
could make the true envelope larger. No upper bound is inferred here.

CONSEQUENCES AND CORRECT NEXT GATES.
The funding expression is maximized over c>0 at c=1, giving J/2. Hence
one finite exact unit-demand base with J>2 would, for a sufficiently large
finite t, produce a genuine fixed-owner production instance with true
funding STRICTLY GREATER than sqrt(N_t)*T. This is a sufficient architecture
counterexample criterion. Conversely a universal sqrt(N) funding theorem
for this rule would imply J<=2 for every such base/witness collection.
Proving J<=2 alone would only block this amplification route; sufficiency
for arbitrary production is not established.

For H>2 the squared expression has maximum H^2/[4(H-1)] at c=H/(H-2).
Thus H>2 refutes the old diagonal constant1, while H>4 would produce a
selected squared diagonal above4/3. H<=2 gives supremum1 as c->infinity.
The new hypercube construction has equal theta_i, with H->4 and J->2;
its separate class upper theorem upgrades the corresponding witness limits
to true-envelope limits. That upgrade is not available for an arbitrary base.
