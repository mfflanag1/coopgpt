2. LOCAL AVAILABLE INDEPENDENT SETS: NO HIDDEN LINE CONFLICT.
Color x by the sum of its coordinates modulo3. Each axis triple contains
all three colors. Start by excluding a uniformly selected color c.
If c=0, keep a uniform (d+h)-subset of the 2d neighbors, and all initially
available outside vertices. This only removes vertices, so independence
persists and o is absent.
If c=1, start with colors0,2 and remove o. Select a uniform h-subset S of
coordinates and add e_j for j in S. For each ordered pair(j,l), j in S,
l!=j, independently delete e_j+2e_l with probability r=49/100, otherwise
delete e_j+e_l. Take the union of deletions. No axis triple contains two
added e_j, because distinct e_j differ in two coordinates. A new triple
through e_j in direction j is broken by deleting o. A new triple in any
other direction l is broken by the specified deletion. Every deletion has
weight2, so no neighbor is accidentally deleted. The available set has
exactly d+h neighbors and excludes o. For c=2 use the image under x->-x.

For a fixed neighbor, the conditional probabilities in these three phases
are3/5,1/5,1, in an order depending on its sign. Its unconditional marginal
is therefore3/5. Subsequent thinning never changes the neighbor set.

3. EXCEPTIONAL VERTICES AND EXACT CAPACITY MARGINS.
The exceptional outside vertices have Hamming weight2. Their number is

A mixed-sign vertex e_j+2e_l, j!=l, occurs in the two phases containing its

Its availability probability is

A same-sign vertex has one phase in which it is available without repairs,

 Psame=2/3-[2x(1-r)-y(1-r)^2]/3

4. FAR VERTICES: ALL CUTS, NOT ONLY MARGINAL CAPACITY.
Let F_j be the vertices of weight>=3 and color j. Their sizes are
 f0=n/3-1-d(d-1),

They sum to m-e. No repair touches them. In phase c, precisely the two classes
with colors other than c are available. For every state omega after the
exceptional thinning, choose Q_omega=K-P_omega far vertices. This will make
the final independent set have exactly d+h+K elements. Its desired far
marginals are beta. The expected total is correct:
 E Q=K-e beta=beta(f0+f1+f2).

For completeness, the exact finite transportation criterion is
 beta|U| <= sum_omega p_omega min(Q_omega,|U intersect A_omega|)
for every U subset F, together with equality for total expected size. Here
A_omega is the available far set. Construct a network with source-state
capacity p_omega Q_omega, state-vertex capacity p_omega on available pairs,
and vertex-sink capacity beta. Optimizing each state's placement in a cut
gives exactly the displayed inequality. Rational max flow then gives, in
each state, conditional marginals in[0,1] summing to integer Q_omega.
Any such rational vector is a finite rational mixture of Q_omega-subsets:
if it is not integral, shift mass between two fractional coordinates until
one becomes integral in each of the two opposite directions, express the
original as the convex combination of those endpoints, and recurse.
Thus the transport proof actually constructs a finite rational distribution.

To verify EVERY cut, put u_j=|U intersect F_j|. Its right side minus left side
is a concave function on the box 0<=u_j<=f_j: each term is the minimum of a
constant and an affine function, minus the affine demand. A concave function
is at least the convex interpolation of its values at the eight box corners.
Thus the eight unions of whole color classes suffice, even though Q depends
on the detailed state. The availability of far vertices depends only on c.

The following strict bounds hold:
 K-e>=max_j f_j,
 K<=min_{r!=s}(f_r+f_s),
 K-e>=(3beta-1)(f_r+f_s) for all distinct r,s,

An elementary proof avoids enormous integer arithmetic. We have n>=100d^2,

The first bound follows from (803/3000)n>3d^2. The next two follow from

For one whole class r, Q>=f_r means its expected available capacity is
(2/3)f_r>=beta f_r. For two classes r,s and third color c, the cut capacity
is exactly (f_r+f_s)/3+E[Q 1_{phase c}], at least
(f_r+f_s+K-e)/3, sufficient by the third bound. For all three classes,

5. FULL PROJECTION AND EVERY OFF-OWNER CONSTRAINT.
Translations of V preserve all axis triples and all independent s-sets.
