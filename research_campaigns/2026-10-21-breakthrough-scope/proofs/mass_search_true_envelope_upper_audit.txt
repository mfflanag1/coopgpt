INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF THE COMMON-VETO TRUE-ENVELOPE UPPER BOUND
21 October 2026. Reviewed frozen mass_geometry_final_upper_attempt.txt.

VERDICT
The caps, heterogeneous aggregation, real-report transfer, and hypercube
sharpness limits are valid under the intended hypothesis that EVERY base
generator is nonempty. The phrase "nonempty generator families" should
explicitly say this, rather than merely requiring each family to contain
some rows. The proof needs a finite feasible unit-demand base and u<1.
An empty base row has no feasible unit-demand cover; after adjoining the
veto it would force u>=1. This is a hypothesis clarification, not a valid
counterexample to the intended finite-rho theorem.

I independently derived the conclusions below; I did not rely on the
frozen proof's claimed numerical checks. The only frozen-file correction
recommended is the explicit nonempty-row hypothesis. This audit does not
edit that file or extend the theorem to arbitrary production functions.

1. MASS INEQUALITY WITHOUT IDENTIFYING MASS WITH NORM
Use the multiplier normalization q=A^T lambda. Nonnegative-coordinate
constraints have zero multipliers here: at a positive coordinate their
multiplier vanishes by complementarity; at a zero coordinate stationarity
is0=(A^T lambda)_j+nu_j with both terms nonnegative, hence both vanish.

For a unit full RHS and any downward RHS b in[0,1], full projection z remains
feasible for the relaxed system. For ANY chosen nonnegative KKT multipliers,
  L=sum lambda <=sum lambda*z(R)=z dot q
    <=||z||*||q||<=||z||^2=rho.
Also q_i is a subset sum of lambda, so q_i<=L. Complementarity separately
gives ||q||^2=sum lambda*b<=L. This establishes all mass claims, even when
the relaxed RHS is fractional and L differs from ||q||^2. At full RHS,
L=||z||^2=rho. The frozen proof's alternative quadratic-dual calculation
agrees with these inequalities and its normalization is consistent.

2. COMMON-VETO CAP: INDEPENDENT DERIVATION
Let rho_c denote each full base squared norm, R=sum rho_c. At a fixed
veto value u in[0,1], the full objective is
  u^2+(1-u)^2 R.
Its minimum is at u0=R/(1+R), with full squared norm also u0. Values u>1
cannot improve on the feasible point paying the veto1. At any full-system
RHS relaxation the total multiplier mass is the veto coordinate, since
every row contains that coordinate. Applying the preceding mass inequality
to the FULL augmented system gives u<=u0<1. Full demand attains u0, proving
the abstract veto envelope exactly.

For a private owner i in block c, only its incident rows in c can change.
Write h=1-u>0. Other blocks have unique conditional projections h*z^d and
multiplier masses h*rho_d. In the focal block set q=x^c/h and
  b'_r=max(0,(a'_r-u)/h).
The off-i residual demands equal1 and every residual lies in[0,1].

The clipping step is sound. If an original focal-row multiplier mu_r is
positive, stationarity gives x_j>=mu_r for every base coordinate j in r.
Since r is nonempty, x(r)>0. Complementarity then gives a'_r-u=x(r)>0.
Hence a nonpositive residual always has multiplier zero. Dividing all
focal multipliers by h gives KKT for the clipped base system, with mass L
and owner coordinate p. Veto stationarity now reads
  u=h*(R-rho_c+L),
and therefore
  x_i=p/[1+R-rho_c+L].
Since0<=p<=L<=rho_c and1+R-rho_c>0, monotonicity of s/(1+R-rho_c+s) yields
  x_i<=rho_c/(1+R).
No endpoint restriction, dual uniqueness, equal block size, uniform full
coordinate, or base own-coordinate monotonicity enters this proof.

3. HETEROGENEOUS AGGREGATION
The caps imply
  sum_i M_i^2 <=[R^2+sum_c n_c*rho_c^2]/(1+R)^2,
  sum_i M_i <=[sum_c(n_c+1)rho_c]/(1+R).
If n_c*rho_c<=4, the first numerator is at most R^2+4R; subtracting from
4/3 gives (R-2)^2/[3(1+R)^2]>=0.

If(n_c+1)^2*rho_c<=4n_c, then
  (n_c+1)rho_c<=2sqrt(n_c*rho_c).
Summing and applying Cauchy-Schwarz gives at most2sqrt(mR)/(1+R)<=sqrt(m),
where m=sum n_c=N-1. This includes the veto coordinate. The same arguments
permit different rho_c and different n_c; block symmetry is unnecessary.

For uniform row size k with the uniform row vector in the row convex hull,
rho=n/k^2. The squared-diagonal condition is k>=n/2, while the funding
condition is exactly k>=(n+1)/2, which follows from the stated integer
condition k>n/2. The hypercube bases satisfy this condition.

4. REAL PRIVATE REPORTS IN THE PARTICULAR PRODUCTION FUNCTION
Let Phi_g(X)=min_{A nonempty} h^(g,A) dot X for the DIRECT forms specified
in the frozen proof. Consider a coalition S containing private owner j.
At the designated full bundles, after j alone reports X_j=t in[0,1], every
other present base coordinate is1. I obtain
  Phi_g(X_S)=min(g(S),g(S\{j})+t).                              (D1)

To prove this, any non-j element of S outside A contributes1, which cannot
improve g(S)<=1. If all such elements are inside A, either j is also inside
and the value is at least g(S), or j is outside and the value is at least
g(S\{j})+t. Choosing A=S or A=S\{j} attains these cases whenever needed.
The apparently missing empty A causes no problem: when S={j}, or more
generally S omits u, g(S)=0 and the nonempty choice A=S already attains0.
At S=empty every form evaluates to0.

At a unilateral report(t,z), all other present control coordinates stay1.
An alternative k-family with k present therefore costs at least1; with
k absent it agrees with the baseline by locality, on both S and S\{j}.
The j-family contributes
  min(w^j(S),w^j(S\{j})+t)+z.
Because w^j(S\{j})=v(S\{j}), its second term is redundant against the
baseline. The exact value is consequently
  min(v(S),v(S\{j})+t,w^j(S)+z).                              (D2)

If a contained generator avoids j, this is1. If no generator is contained,
it is0. Otherwise all contained generators contain j, and it equals
  min(1,t,max_{R subset S} a^j_R+z)
   =max_{R subset S} min(1,t,a^j_R+z).
The maximum here is over a finite nonempty family. Thus the entire real
report game is EXACTLY represented by the original generator family with
off-j RHS1 and incident RHS min(1,t,a^j_R+z). All are in[0,1]. Coalitions
omitting j are unchanged. This proves inclusion of EVERY actual unilateral
real underreport in the abstract envelope domain used above.

5. VETO, EMPTY AND SINGLETON COALITIONS
For a veto report X_u=t, a coalition containing u has all other present
base coordinates1. Its direct-form minimum is
  Phi_g(X_S)=t*g(S).
An omitted nonveto coordinate costs1>=t*g(S), an omitted veto costs t,
and A=S attains t*g(S). This includes singleton S={u}; nonempty base rows
actually make g({u})=0, and A={u} gives0. Coalitions omitting u have value0.
Empty coalition values are0 for every form and control branch.

The selected veto game is w^u=v. Its own branch is t*v(S)+z and cannot
improve the baseline. Every other branch is either protected by its unit
control or agrees with v off that branch's owner. Hence the actual game is
t*v, independent of z, and its projection scales by t. The veto's true
envelope is exactly the full u0. Thus the theorem covers the veto owner's
entire real report square, not just its selected Boolean report.

An exact two-owner countercheck to that finding is V={u,1}, with the sole

  (0,1), (1,0), (1/2,0).
At X=(1/2,1) their values are1,1/2,1/4. The minimum is1/4=t*g(S), whereas
the review's proposed min(g(S),t) is1/2. The original identity is correct;
its suggested repair should not be adopted. The independent checker also
tests the identity on320 fractional-game/coalition/report combinations,
separately from the control branches. The review's distinct nonempty-row
clarification is valid and is recorded in the verdict above.

6. PRECISE SCOPE AND A VERIFIED BOUNDARY EXAMPLE
The production function is valid on all R_+^(2N), but the proved caps and
real-report transfer are evaluated at the DESIGNATED full bundles
b_i=e_i+e_(N+i), allowing one owner to lower its bundle arbitrarily. They
do not assert the same caps at every other base profile. In (D1) the
other present base coordinates must be1, and in (D2) the other present
controls must be1. These hypotheses cannot be silently discarded.

In fact original-generator preservation fails at a simultaneous report
even within the fixed bundle box. Take V={u,1,2}, generators{u,1},{u,2},
w^1 deleting the first row and w^2 deleting the second, with w^u=v.
Set all X coordinates to1 and drop BOTH private controls to0. Direct
evaluation gives values
  g({u,1})=0, g({u,2})=0, g(V)=1.
No RHS assignment to those original two generators can represent this
game: their coalition values force both RHS to0, which would force g(V)=0.
This does NOT contradict the theorem, whose envelope comparisons keep all
other owners at the designated full reports. It demonstrates why that
scope restriction is substantive. Likewise, arbitrarily scaling unrelated
full bundles cannot preserve the theorem's fixed absolute numerical caps.

The separate resource bottleneck F(X,Z)<=X_u remains globally valid for
these direct forms. It implies a veto OWNER throughout the fixed bundles'
underreport box, since only that owner can supply X_u. It does not imply
the original generator representation or these sharper projection caps at
all those base profiles, nor a fixed veto owner at unrelated bundle profiles.

7. SHARPNESS OF TRUE ENVELOPES
For the hypercube family, p/rho->1, ell/rho->1, n*rho->4 and rho->0.
Choose J with J*rho->r>0. Each private true envelope is sandwiched between
  p/[1+(J-1)rho+ell] and rho/(1+J*rho).
The ratio tends1 uniformly over all private owners because both bounds
are identical across those owners; joint symmetries of the entire F are
not additionally needed. The veto envelope is exactly J*rho/(1+J*rho).
Squaring/summing, or summing and dividing by sqrt(Jn+1), gives by squeezing
  sum_i M_i^2 ->(r^2+4r)/(1+r)^2,
  (sum_i M_i)/sqrt(N) ->2sqrt(r)/(1+r).
At r=2 the first limit is4/3; at r=1 the second is1 and the first is5/4.
These are genuine true-envelope limits at the designated full bundles.
They are stronger than witness-only lower limits and remain restricted to
this production class. No unrestricted sqrt(N) upper bound follows.
