realization. The row-family proof is activation_qary_cube_proof.txt.

1. DIMENSION EXTENSION.
For fixed Q>=3 let d run positive multiples of
 d0=16Q(Q-1)(Q+2), and h=d/[2(Q+2)].
Keep x=h/d, mu, delta, beta0, and all other definitions from that proof.
The proof is unchanged: d>=480 and M>100Q^4d^2 continue to hold. The only
place using the exact initial dimension was h delta=2; now h delta>=2,
which only increases the off-row potential gap. In particular every such
d gives a valid finite transitive family with theta_i=1 for all n=Q^d
owners and J>Q-1.

2. DIRECT AMPLIFICATION.
Let rho=n/k^2 be the full cover squared norm, ell=1/t the local witness
mass, and take B=floor(1/rho)=floor(k^2/n) disjoint private copies with one
common veto owner. Total owner count is N=Bn+1. For each private owner,
the standard common-veto KKT construction gives selected own-report price
 p=ell/[1+(B-1)rho+ell].
The veto's full-profile price is p_v=B rho/(1+B rho). These witnesses are
all evaluated as coordinates of the true own-report envelope at the same
full profile. Their production realization has full output T=1.
Therefore the actual funding is at least
 S=B n p+p_v,
and the actual diagonal is at least
 D=B n p^2+p_v^2.
There is no union-product step in this corollary.

3. FIXED Q, ARBITRARILY LARGE OWNER COUNTS.
Write b0=1-beta0. As d tends to infinity along the above multiples,
 beta->beta0, k/n->b0, t/n->b0^2,
 rho~1/(b0^2 n), ell/rho->1, B rho->1.
Consequently N~b0^2 n^2 and
 (B n p)/sqrt(N) ->1/(2b0)>(Q-1)/2,
 B n p^2+p_v^2 ->(1+b0^(-2))/4.
The single veto contributes o(1) to S/sqrt(N). Since the true envelopes
are at least these selected witnesses, these are lower-limit statements
for the actual mechanism's funding and diagonal; no upper equality is
needed or asserted here.

For every finite C, choose Q so that (Q-1)/2>C. The displayed convergence
then gives arbitrarily large N with actual funding>C sqrt(N)T. Thus this
true-envelope architecture has NO universal constant-times-sqrt(N) funding
bound. This is a statement about that architecture, not every mechanism.
The construction gives an infinite subsequence, not a claim at every N.

4. OPTIONAL EXPLICIT GROWTH RATE ALONG ONE SEQUENCE.
Take each integer Q>=3 at its initial dimension d=d0. Then
 b0=(4Q^2+5Q-2)/[4Q(Q-1)(Q+2)]~1/Q.
Since n=Q^d grows faster than any polynomial in Q, the same approximations
hold jointly as Q->infinity: k/n~b0, t/n~b0^2, ell/rho->1 and B rho->1.
Hence the selected private funding divided by sqrt(N) is asymptotic toQ/2,
and selected diagonal is asymptotic toQ^2/4. Furthermore
 log N~2d log Q~32Q^3 log Q,
 log log N~3 log Q,
so Q~[(3/32)*(log N/log log N)]^(1/3).
Along this explicit subsequence the selected funding is asymptotic to
 (1/2)*(3/32)^(1/3)*sqrt(N)*(log N/log log N)^(1/3),
and the selected diagonal is asymptotic to
 (1/4)*(3/32)^(2/3)*(log N/log log N)^(2/3).
These asymptotics are optional refinements; the fixed-Q argument above
already rules out every constant-times-sqrt(N) bound.

5. BINARY DEMANDS SUFFICE AFTER ADDING THE COMMON VETO.
The base witness already retains its supported own rows at RHS1 and deletes
all other own rows to RHS0. Let R=(B-1)rho+ell and eta=1/(1+R), u=R/(1+R).
The common-veto certificate uses veto price u, changed private block eta
q_local, and unchanged private blocks eta z. Every retained supported row
has total price u+eta=1. Other retained rows are feasible because their
private base cover value is at least1. Deleted rows keep RHS0, so feasibility
is automatic and their dual multipliers are0. Scale each base multiplier
by eta; the sum across all blocks is eta R=u, exactly veto stationarity.
Thus all selected common-veto witness demand vectors are binary. There is
no need to give deleted rows the intermediate RHS u. This agrees with a
binary Boolean-profile production realization, whose chosen generator
values are0 or1, subject to the separately audited realization lemma.
