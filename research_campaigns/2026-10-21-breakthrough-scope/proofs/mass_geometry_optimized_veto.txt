EXACT VETO OPTIMIZATION FOR THE MOBIUS-LADDER WITNESS FAMILY
2026-10-21. Independent algebra and exact one-dimensional arithmetic.
This note optimizes the selected witness certificate, not the unknown actual
envelope. The newer hypercube family supersedes its numerical strength.

1. GENERAL FORMULA.
Suppose a symmetric n-owner base has full squared norm rho, and for each
owner an own-relaxation witness with focal coordinate p and total dual mass
ell. Let E=n*p^2, R=E/rho, delta=rho-ell and c=J*rho. Add one common veto
owner to J disjoint base blocks. Its full-profile coordinate is c/(1+c),
and each selected nonveto coordinate is p/(1+(J-1)*rho+ell). Thus the
selected squared diagonal is exactly
 D_delta(c)=c^2/(1+c)^2 + R*c/(1+c-delta)^2.
This is a LOWER bound on the true envelope squared diagonal.
If delta>=0, a conservative lower bound is
 D_0(c)=(c^2+R*c)/(1+c)^2.
For R>2,
 D_0'(c)=[R+(2-R)c]/(1+c)^3,
 c_opt=R/(R-2),  max D_0=R^2/[4(R-1)].
For R<=2 the conservative expression increases to1. In particular R->9/4
has optimum c->9 and maximum81/80, not9/8.

For the exact expression put a=1-delta. Its derivative is
 2c/(1+c)^3 + R*(a-c)/(a+c)^3.
It is positive for c<=a. For c>a, its sign changes when
 R=Psi(c)=2c*(a+c)^3/[(c-a)*(1+c)^3].
The sign of the logarithmic derivative of Psi is the sign of
 (3-4a)c^2-2a(2-a)c-a^2.
For 3/4<=a<=1 this is negative. Hence Psi decreases from infinity to2;
for R>2 there is exactly one maximizer. The integer-J maximum is attained
at one of the two integers bracketing this critical point. Exact signs
and binary search suffice; no numerical optimization is needed.

2. MOBIUS PARAMETERS AND AN EXACT CERTIFICATE CEILING.
For even L>=4 put D=2L^2+9L-18. The closed-form ladder construction gives
 n=2L, rho=2L/(L+3)^2, p=3(L-2)/D, ell=4(L-2)/D,
 R=[3(L-2)(L+3)/D]^2,
 delta=2(L-6)^2/[(L+3)^2*D].
Here 0<=delta<1/4. Write r=sqrt(R)=p*(L+3). The inequality
 r < (3/2)*(1-delta)
is equivalent to (7L-6)*(L+3)^2>2(L-6)^2, which holds for every L>=4
(expanding the difference gives 7L^3+34L^2+51L-126>0).
Since 1+c-delta >= (1-delta)*(1+c), it follows that for every finite L,J,
 D_delta(c) < [c^2+(9/4)c]/(1+c)^2 <=81/80.
Conversely choosing integer J with J*rho->9 as L->infinity attains limit
81/80. This is therefore the exact supremum of this selected-certificate
architecture within the ladder family. It is NOT an upper bound on the
actual envelope, which could have other stronger own reports.

The selected nonveto funding sum divided by sqrt(J*n) is
 r*sqrt(c)/(1+c-delta).
Its maximum over c>0 is r/[2sqrt(1-delta)]<3/4, attained at c=1-delta.
Consequently the selected total including veto is below
 1+(3/4)*sqrt(J*n).
As L->infinity the leading coefficient is (3/2)*sqrt(c)/(1+c), with maximum
3/4 at c=1. At the squared-diagonal-optimal c=9 it equals9/20. Asymptotic
squared-diagonal violation requires c>4, so the supremum of the funding
coefficient along such ladder choices is3/5. These are limitations of the
selected ladder witnesses only, not of the true envelope or of general
constructions. The hypercube family changes R's limiting value to4.
