INDEPENDENT Q-ARY AUDIT AND FINITE TRUE-ENVELOPE FUNDING LOWER BOUND
2026-10-21. Conventional proof plus independent exact scalar replay.
Originating construction: proofs/activation_qary_cube_proof.txt.
Cross-model review and novelty checking are separate; no claim about all
mechanisms follows from this result about a particular envelope rule.

THEOREM.
For each integer Q>=3, the construction below gives one finite monotone,
concave, homogeneous production function F on 2N nonnegative real resources,
with N fixed owners each owning its two specified coordinates at full levels1,
full output F(b)=1, and full minimum-l1 cover value T=1, such that the TRUE
own-report envelope E of the minimum-Euclidean-norm cover satisfies
 sum_i E_i > ((Q-1)/8)*sqrt(N).
Consequently no universal constant C can bound these true envelopes by
C sqrt(N) T. Moreover, along this explicit sequence of N,
 sum_i E_i > (1/40)*sqrt(N)*(log N/log log N)^(1/3).
All unilateral real underreports in the fixed boxes remain admissible; the
lower bound uses particular endpoint underreports. No report-dependent
change in ownership or production function is used.

A. PARAMETERS AND THE ALL-Q LARGE-N BOUNDS.
Fix Q>=3, not necessarily prime. Put
 d=16Q(Q-1)(Q+2), h=8Q(Q-1), x=h/d=1/[2(Q+2)],
 n=Q^d, M=Q^(d-1), m=n-(Q-1)d-1,
 mu=(Q-2+x)/(Q-1), delta=1/[4Q(Q-1)], beta0=mu+delta,
 K=floor(beta0*m), beta=K/m, b0=1-beta0, b=1-beta,
 a=1-mu, s=(Q-2)d+h+K, k=n-s.
Here M denotes a color-class size; the number of amplification blocks below
is B, a different symbol.

Every D>=20 satisfies3^(D-5)>100D^2: it holds at D20 and the ratio at the
next integer is3D^2/(D+1)^2>1. Since d>=480,
 M=Q^4 Q^(d-5)>=Q^4 3^(d-5)>100Q^4d^2.                 (1)
In particular m>M>h and m>1/delta. To see m>M explicitly, use
m-M=(Q-1)(M-d)-1>0. Flooring therefore gives beta>mu.

Let B_exc=(Q-1)/Q-2x/[Q(Q-1)]. Direct algebra gives
 B_exc-mu=2delta,
 gamma=(Q-1)/Q-beta0=delta+2x/[Q(Q-1)]>1/(4Q^2).
Thus beta<=beta0<(Q-1)/Q<1, and b>=b0>1/Q. Also
 b0<1/(Q-1)<=3/(2Q), so Q beta0-Q+2=2-Qb0>1/2.
These strict signs are needed in the cut estimates, not merely convenient
asymptotic approximations.

B. INDEPENDENT-SET MIXTURE, INCLUDING COMPOSITE ALPHABETS.
Vertices are (Z/QZ)^d. A hyperedge is a complete axis-parallel line of Q
points. Independence means containing no complete line. Color a vertex by
its coordinate sum modulo Q. Every axis line contains every color exactly
once, also when Q is composite.

At focal0 the neighbors are c e_j for1<=c<=Q-1 and1<=j<=d. Choose an excluded
color uniformly. If it is0, start with all other colors and keep a uniform
((Q-2)d+h)-subset of the neighbors. If the excluded color is c!=0, start
with all other colors, delete0, choose a uniform h-subset S of coordinate
indices, and add c e_j for j in S. For each added c e_j and each l!=j,
independently delete c e_j+A e_l for uniform A in{1,...,Q-1}.
No line contains two added c e_j: two of them differ in two coordinates.
The line in direction j is broken by deleting0, and each other line through
an added vertex is broken by its repair. Repairs have weight2 and delete
no neighbor. Thus each available set is independent, excludes0, and has
exactly (Q-2)d+h neighbors. A fixed neighbor's phase-dependent marginals are
mu,x,1,...,1, averaging to mu because (mu+x+Q-2)/Q=mu.

There are e=(Q-1)^2 binomial(d,2) weight2 exceptional vertices. A vertex
u=a1 e_j+a2 e_l can be deleted only in excluded-color phases a1 and a2.
Those phases initially contain u because neither a1 nor a2 equals a1+a2
modulo Q (both are nonzero). If a1!=a2, the deletion events occupy distinct
phases, giving availability exactly B_exc. If a1=a2, union probability is
at most the sum, hence availability at least B_exc; the duplicate correction
is nonnegative. This also handles a1+a2=0 in composite alphabets.
Every availability P_u is rational and exceeds beta. Conditional on being
available, retain u independently with probability beta/P_u. Every exceptional
marginal is then exactly beta. Let P be the retained number;0<=P<=e and
E P=beta e. Thinning preserves independence and all neighbor counts.

C. FAR-VERTEX TRANSPORT: EXHAUSTIVE CUT ARGUMENT.
For far vertices (weight>=3), let F_c be color c. Their sizes are
 f0=M-1-(Q-1)binomial(d,2),
 fc=M-d-(Q-2)binomial(d,2), c!=0.
For each coordinate pair there are Q-1 nonzero coefficient pairs summing
to0 and Q-2 summing to a specified nonzero color. Hence these formulas are
valid for every integer Q, and sum f_c=m-e. Repairs never touch far vertices.
In excluded phase c, precisely all far classes except F_c are available.

For each finite rational state omega after exceptional thinning, choose
T_omega=K-P_omega available far vertices. We need their common marginal beta.
The expected size is correct: E T=K-beta e=beta sum f_c.
The exact finite transport condition is, for every U in the far set,
 beta|U|<=sum_omega p_omega min(T_omega,|U intersect A_omega|).   (2)
This is the maxflow/mincut criterion for source-state capacities pT,
state-available-vertex capacities p, and vertex-sink capacities beta.
A saturating rational flow yields conditional vectors in[0,1] of integer
sum T. Each such vector is a rational mixture of T-subset indicators by
successively shifting mass between two fractional coordinates to boundary
points. Therefore(2) yields actual finite rational subset distributions.

The cut deficit is concave in u_c=|U intersect F_c| on0<=u_c<=f_c. Its terms
are minima of a constant and an affine function. Every point of the box is
a convex combination of corners, so checking all corners proves every cut.
A corner is a union of whole color classes. We verify them via these bounds:
 (i) K-e >=(Q-2)M;
 (ii) K <=sum_{c!=a}f_c for every a;
 (iii) K-e >=(Q beta-Q+2)sum_{c!=a}f_c for every a.            (3)

Here is a uniform proof of(3), without finite enumeration in Q. From(1),
 M-Qd^2<=f_c<=M and f_c>0, while e+Qd+2<=Q^2d^2.
Flooring gives K-e>=Q beta0 M-Q^2d^2 and K<=Q beta0 M.
For(i) subtract(Q-2)M; the difference is at least
 (Q beta0-Q+2)M-Q^2d^2>M/2-Q^2d^2>0.
For(ii), use sum_{c!=a}f_c>=(Q-1)(M-Qd^2); its excess above K is at least
 Q gamma M-Q(Q-1)d^2>M/(4Q)-Q^2d^2>0.
For(iii), Q beta-Q+2>0 since beta>mu and Qmu-Q+2>1/2. Its product with a
Q-1-class union is at most(Q beta0-Q+2)(Q-1)M. Subtraction leaves at least
 (Q-2)Q gamma M-Q^2d^2>M/12-Q^2d^2>0.
All final inequalities follow from(1).

For a corner with r<=Q-2 classes, T>=K-e>=(Q-2)M exceeds every available
portion, so the cut's RHS is exactly(Q-1)/Q times its size, at least beta
times its size. For r=Q-1 with missing class a, the RHS is exactly
 ((Q-2)/Q)*sum_{c!=a}f_c + E[T 1_{phase a}],
which is at least [ (Q-2)sum_{c!=a}f_c+K-e ]/Q, sufficient by(iii).
For all Q classes,(ii) makes the RHS E T, equal to the total demand. The
empty cut is zero. Thus every cut(2) holds.

Consequently there exists a finite rational distribution of independent
s-sets with focal absent, exactly(Q-2)d+h neighbors of common marginal mu,
and exactly K outside vertices of common marginal beta. This establishes
nonemptiness of the row universe as well as the mixture; it is not a
marginal-only argument that ignores fixed set sizes or transport cuts.

D. FULL ROW UNIVERSE AND EXACT DELETION KKT.
Let the rows be complements of ALL independent s-sets. Translations preserve
the full row family and act transitively on vertices. Every row has size k.
Averaging translates of any row gives (k/n)1. Cauchy-Schwarz on the row hull
makes that its unique minimum-norm point. Equivalently, the full cover is
z=(1/k)1, with rho=n/k^2 and a full unit-demand KKT certificate obtained by
multiplying a uniform translate mixture by rho.

The constructed complement mixture w has focal1, neighbor entries a, and
outside entries b. Each support row has d-h neighbors and m-K outside
vertices, hence potential
 t=1+(d-h)a+(m-K)b=1+(Q-1)d a^2+m b^2=||w||^2.             (4)
Every off-focal row complements an independent set containing0. Each of
the d axis lines through0 must omit at least one neighbor, so the row has
at least d neighbors. Since a>b, all such row potentials are at least
 d a+(k-d)b=t+h(beta-mu)-beta.
The last gap is positive: h delta=2, beta>=beta0-1/m, m>h, and beta<=beta0<1
imply h(beta-mu)-beta>=2-h/m-beta0>0. This handles every unsupported off-row.

An own row with r neighbors has potential1+r a+(k-1-r)b, increasing in r.
Keep rows with r>=d-h at demand1 and delete those with r<d-h. No off row is
deleted, because its r>=d. Every support row remains tight at potential t.
With lambda_R=alpha_R/t on the support, q=w/t=A^T lambda is feasible and
satisfies complementary slackness. It is therefore exactly the unique
minimum-norm cover of this incident-only deletion system. Its multiplier
mass ell=1/t equals its focal coordinate. Translations supply this witness
for every owner, with theta_i=1.

For density, flooring gives
 k<=b0 n+d/(4Q)+2<n/(Q-1),                                 (5)
since1/(Q-1)-b0=x/(Q-1)+delta>delta and(1) implies
delta n>d/(4Q)+2. Positivity follows from independence (each parallel-line
partition permits at most Q-1 points per line). In fact the exact identity
 k-nb=beta+(Q-1)d(beta-mu)>0                               (6)
shows k>nb>n/Q. Therefore H=n^2/k^2>(Q-1)^2 and J=n/k>Q-1.

E. FINITE BALANCED-BLOCK AMPLIFICATION: NO ASYMPTOTIC LEAP.
By(4), t<=nb^2+Qd: subtract nb^2 and bound the remaining at most
1+(Q-1)d<=Qd. Since b>1/Q and(1) gives n>Q^3d, nb^2>Qd. By(6),
 ell/rho=k^2/(nt)>=nb^2/(nb^2+Qd)>1/2.                     (7)
Cauchy-Schwarz on w also gives ell<=rho. Moreover rho<Q^2/n<1/2.
Choose the integer number of base blocks
 B=floor(1/rho)=floor(k^2/n), c=B rho.
Then B>=1 and1/2<c<=1. Add one common veto u to every row in all B copies,
so N=Bn+1. The full minimum-l1 cover is1: the veto alone is feasible, and
any unit row forces total cover at least1.

For each private owner delete its translated bad rows in its own block.
Scale the focal local projection q and all B-1 other full projections z by
1/[1+(B-1)rho+ell], and put the veto coordinate at
[(B-1)rho+ell]/[1+(B-1)rho+ell]. Scaling the base multipliers proves stationarity
at the veto and every private coordinate. Every positive row is unit and
tight; deleted rows impose no constraint. This exactly certifies the augmented
projection. The selected private coordinate is
 ell/[1+(B-1)rho+ell].
Thus the sum over PRIVATE actual envelopes is at least
 P=Bn ell/[1+(B-1)rho+ell]>=Bn ell/(1+B rho)>=Bn ell/2.
Since N=Bn+1<=2Bn,
 P/sqrt(N)>=ell sqrt(Bn)/(2sqrt(2))
          =(ell/rho)*sqrt(c n rho)/(2sqrt(2))
          >sqrt(n rho)/8>(Q-1)/8.                          (8)
This is one finite instance per Q, with no limit interchange. It suffices
to rule out every universal constant times sqrt(N) funding bound for this
own-envelope rule once the production transfer in SectionG is applied.
The veto payment would only strengthen the lower bound and is not needed.

F. RATE IN N WITH AN EXPLICIT CONSTANT.
The chosen block count satisfies B< n/(Q-1)^2<=n/4 by(5), hence N<=n^2.
Also N>=n since B>=1. The exact identity
 Q(Q-1)(Q+2)<= (9/8)Q^3
is equivalent to(Q-4)^2>=0. Thus d<=18Q^3 and
 log N<=2d log Q<=36Q^3 log Q.
Because log N>=d log Q>Q, we have log log N>log Q>0. Therefore
 (log N/log log N)^(1/3)<=36^(1/3)Q.
For Q>=3, Q-1>=2Q/3, so(8) gives
 P/sqrt(N)>Q/12
 >=[1/(12*36^(1/3))]*(log N/log log N)^(1/3)
 >(1/40)*(log N/log log N)^(1/3).
The final numerical comparison is exact because36<(10/3)^3.
As Q grows, N>=Q^d grows without bound. This is an along-sequence lower bound,
not a claim that every possible integer N has the stated rate.

G. ACTUAL PRODUCTION, FIXED OWNERS, AND THE REAL REPORT DOMAIN.
For the N-owner common-veto generator system let v(S) be1 exactly when S
contains a full generator, else0. For each private j, let w^j(S) be1 exactly
when S contains a generator retained in j's selected deletion system, else0.
Set w^u=v. These games vanish without the veto, are monotone, and satisfy
w^j(S)=v(S) whenever j is absent from S.

Use 2N resources X_1,...,X_N,Z_1,...,Z_N. Owner j's fixed full bundle has
X_j=Z_j=1 and every other coordinate0. For each nonempty A subset of owners
and any game g among v,w^1,...,w^N, define a nonnegative form h^(g,A) with
coefficient1 on coordinates outside A, coefficient g(A) on the veto if it
belongs to A, and coefficient0 on all other coordinates inside A. Define
 F(X,Z)=min{ h^(v,A) dot X,
             h^(w^j,A) dot X+Z_j : A nonempty, j an owner }.
This is a finite minimum of nonnegative linear forms, hence monotone,
concave, homogeneous and defined on ALL nonnegative real resource profiles.

For a Boolean coalition S, min_A h^(g,A)(S)=g(S): A=S attains it for nonempty
S; if S is not contained in A, an outside coordinate costs1>=g(S), while
if S is contained in A monotonicity supplies g(A)>=g(S). If the veto is
absent then g(S)=0, and the same conclusion holds. Empty S costs0 directly.
At full bundles the baseline gives v; each present-owner branch has penalty1,
and every absent-owner branch equals v on that coalition. Thus the full
coalition game is v and F(full)=1.

For private j, keep X_j=1 and underreport Z_j from1 to0. The j-branch now
induces w^j. Other present-owner branches cost at least1, and absent-owner
branches equal v on the coalition. Since w^j<=v, the entire induced game
is w^j. This is an admissible endpoint in j's fixed real report box[0,1]^2;
all other real reports remain admissible. Nonnegative cover inequalities
for a max-of-contained-generators game are exactly its generator inequalities.
Therefore the actual minimum-norm response at this own report is precisely
the projection in SectionE. All these witnesses occur in the SAME F, with
fixed ownership, one selected game per owner, and one common full profile.
The true envelope, a supremum over every real own underreport, dominates
each witness, proving(8) and SectionF for actual envelopes.

SCOPE AND REVIEW STATUS.
This refutes a universal C sqrt(N) T bound for the coordinatewise own-report
envelope of the minimum-Euclidean-norm cover rule, even at full output1,
with fixed two-coordinate owner bundles and an all-real production domain.
It does not refute existence of a different incentive-compatible mechanism,
and it is not an all-mechanism lower bound. The normalized H,J assertions
are exact row-system statements; their production consequence requires and
has used SectionG. No unproved preservation of integrality, uniformity under
own reports, or endpoint reduction is invoked. This family happens to use
endpoint deletions; no assertion about arbitrary families follows.
