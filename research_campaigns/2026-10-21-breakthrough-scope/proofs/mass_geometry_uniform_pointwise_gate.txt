1. A COMPACT THIRTEEN-OWNER CERTIFICATE.
Let the owners be a focal i, a set A of5 other owners, and a disjoint set B
of7 owners. Use precisely the following unit-demand rows:
 G={i} union A;
 all6-subsets of B;
 all sets {i,a} union D with a in A and D a4-subset of B.
There are1+7+5*35=183 rows. EVERY row has cardinality6. The row family is
explicit, finite, and not claimed vertex transitive.

The full minimum-norm cover is z=(1/6)*1 on all13 owners. An exact full KKT
certificate assigns multiplier1/6 to G, multiplier1/36 to each of the seven
B-rows, and zero to all175 mixed rows. Stationarity holds: i and every A
coordinate get1/6 from G, and each B coordinate lies in six B-rows, hence
gets6/36=1/6. Every row is tight. Thus
 rho=||z||^2=13/36,
 v=z/rho=(6/13)*1,
and v is the full minimum-norm point of the convex hull of row incidence
vectors. In particular the proposed uniformity hypothesis holds with p=6/13.
One can also see v directly as the mixture giving G mass6/13 and the seven
B-rows total mass7/13, uniformly distributed. All rows have size6, so uniform
v has the least possible squared norm among any vectors with that sum.

Delete ONLY G by lowering its RHS from1 to0. It is incident to i. Every
off-i row is a B-row and retains RHS1. Keep all175 mixed rows at RHS1.
The exact new minimum-norm cover is

Its KKT multipliers are1/630 on each mixed row,1/756 on each B-row, and0

The deleted row has payment5/18+5/18=5/9>=0. Thus feasibility,
complementary slackness, and nonnegative stationarity all hold, proving
that q is the unique quadratic projection of this legitimate own relaxation.

The total dual mass is

2. AN UNBOUNDED UNIFORM-FULL FAMILY.
Let s>=2 be an integer. Put k=s^2 and n=s^3. Partition the n owners into
one focal i, A of size k-1, and B of size(s-1)k. Use unit-demand rows:
 G={i} union A;
 all k-subsets of B;
 all {i} union C union D with |C|=s-1, C subset A,
                            |D|=k-s, D subset B.
All rows have size k. The full projection is z=(1/k)*1: give G multiplier
1/k and the uniform B-row distribution total mass |B|/k^2. Every B coordinate
then receives1/k, every G coordinate receives1/k, and all rows are tight.
Thus rho=n/k^2=1/s and the full normalized hull point is v=(1/s)*1.

Again delete ONLY G. Set
 q_i=(s+1)/(2s^2),
 q_A=1/(2s^2),
 q_B=1/s^2.
Give the mixed-row class total multiplier
 lambda_mix=(s+1)/(2s^2),
and the B-row class total multiplier
 lambda_B=(s-1)^2/(2s^3),
uniformly within each class. Every multiplier is positive. A fixed A owner
has mixed-row inclusion probability(s-1)/(s^2-1)=1/(s+1), hence receives
1/(2s^2). A fixed B owner has mixed-row inclusion probability
(s^2-s)/[(s-1)s^2]=1/s, and B-row inclusion probability1/(s-1). Its total is
 (s+1)/(2s^3)+(s-1)/(2s^3)=1/s^2.
The focal coordinate receives lambda_mix as required.

Every mixed row is tight since
 (s+1)/(2s^2)+(s-1)/(2s^2)+(s^2-s)/s^2=1.
Every B-row is tight. The deleted row has payment(s+1)/(2s), between0 and1.
Therefore this is an exact KKT witness for every integer s>=2, without any
asymptotic or computational existence assumption.
Its mass is
 L=(2s^2-s+1)/(2s^3)=||q||^2,
 theta_i=s(s+1)/(2s^2-s+1),
 theta_i/p=s^2(s+1)/(2s^2-s+1) ~s/2.
The ratio is unbounded, although the full row-hull point is perfectly uniform
and every row has exactly k entries. Already s=3 gives n27,k9,p1/3 and
theta_i=3/4>2/3. For all s>=3 the violation follows from
 s^3-3s^2+2s-2>0
(the value at3 is4 and the expression increases thereafter).
The raw coordinate ratio is also unbounded:
 q_i/z_i=(s+1)/2.
Thus a constant pointwise comparison of raw coordinates fails even for
unit-demand bases with uniform full projection and constant row cardinality.

3. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE.
The counterexample refutes both the factor2 pointwise gate and every fixed
constant replacement theta_i<=C*p under the same uniform-full hypotheses.
It does not establish H=rho*sum_j(theta_j)^2>4, because it certifies the large
normalized increase for only ONE focal owner. The other focal roles cannot
be inferred by relabeling: the constructed row family distinguishes i,A,B.
A new relabeling or symmetrization would add off-owner rows and would require
a fresh KKT proof. No transitivity is claimed.

For the unbounded family, choose this changed witness for i and the full
witness for every other owner. The resulting aggregate is exactly
 H=(1/s)*[theta_i^2+(s^3-1)/s^2]
  =1-1/s^3+theta_i^2/s,
which tends to1, not4. This explicitly illustrates the failure of pointwise
inflation to supply an aggregate obstruction.
The previously proved common-veto upper theorem is unaffected: its sufficient
condition n*rho<=4 fails for this growing family, since n*rho=s^2. The13-owner
certificate has n*rho=169/36>4 as well. Uniformity by itself did not impose
the density/norm restriction needed by that theorem.

An exact remaining bottleneck is aggregate coupling across distinct focal
owners. The gate H<=4 for every uniform-full base remains open after this
pointwise refutation. No new universal diagonal or total-funding conclusion
is drawn from it. The standalone verifier

checks all183 rows and both KKT certificates using exact Fraction arithmetic,
and separately replays the parameter identities over a finite sample. The
all-s proof above, not the sample, establishes the unbounded family.
