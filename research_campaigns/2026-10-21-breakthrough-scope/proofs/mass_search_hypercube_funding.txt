HYPERCUBE FUNDING COROLLARY: ASYMPTOTIC COEFFICIENT ONE
21 October 2026. Independent derivation and exact finite bounds.

THE QUANTIFIED RESULT
For every epsilon in(0,1) and every owner-count threshold N0 there exist
an integer N>=N0, one finite nonnegative min-linear production function F
on2N resources, and fixed actual bundles b_i=e_i+e_(N+i), such that

  F(sum_i b_i)=1,
  min{sum_i x_i: x>=0 covers every full-profile coalition}=1,
  sum_i M_i^F(b) >= (1-epsilon)*sqrt(N).                           (1)

Here M_i^F is the TRUE own-report envelope: the supremum of owner i's
minimum-Euclidean-norm covering payment over all real coordinatewise
underreports0<=a_i<=b_i, with all other actual owners fixed. The function F
is concave, monotone, continuous and positively homogeneous on the entire
nonnegative real resource domain. The theorem follows from finitely many
explicit underreport witnesses; extra real reports can only increase M.

This is an architecture-specific lower bound. It is not an impossibility
theorem for arbitrary mechanisms, does not prove a sqrt(N) upper bound,
and does not exceed its proposed leading coefficient1. It concerns
arbitrarily large N along an explicit sequence, not every integer N.
No padding or literature-priority claim is made.

BASE AND INDEPENDENT CERTIFICATE AUDIT
Use the finite distribution in proofs/activation_hypercube_proof.txt.
Its parameters, for any integer d>=4, are

  n=2^d, a=n/2-1, b=n/2-d, m=n-d-1,
  K=floor[a(d-2)/d], c=n-K-1,
  theta=d/(d+1), gamma=(m-K)/m,
  tau=d^2/(d+1)+(m-K)^2/m,
  rho=n/c^2, ell=1/tau, p=theta/tau.

The rows are complements of ALL independent(K+1)-sets of Q_d, hence have
size c. For a focal vertex o, its closed neighborhood C has d+1 vertices.
The distribution chooses one star vertex uniformly and a K-element
background outside C. It mixes the even and odd parity backgrounds with
probabilities a/m and b/m.

The odd background is a uniform K-subset of the b odd non-neighbors; it is
compatible with every selected star vertex. For the even background put
D=binomial(d,2), R=a-D, f=K/a and mu=Df. Choose P as the two neighboring
integers of mu with mean mu. If the star vertex is o, choose P weight2
vertices from all D; if it is e_j, choose them only from the
D'=(d-1)(d-2)/2 pairs avoiding j. Choose the remaining K-P background
vertices among the R even vertices of weight at least4. All choices are
uniform conditional on these counts.

I independently checked the construction's possible failure points:
R>0 follows from2^d-2>d(d-1), valid at4 and preserved by induction;
K<=b; and mu belongs to[max(0,K-R),min(K,D')], whose endpoints are integers,
so both rounded choices are feasible. Every output is an independent set
of exactly K+1 vertices with exactly one member of C. At any weight2 vertex,
the marginal within the even branch is
  [mu/D+(d-2)mu/D']/(d+1)=f.
At higher even weights it is(K-mu)/R=f. After phase mixing, every outside
vertex has independent-set marginal K/m; every star vertex has1/(d+1).
Thus the complementary-row mixture w equals theta on C and gamma outside.

The support and every off-o row contain exactly d star vertices. Their
w-potential is d*theta+(c-d)*gamma=tau=||w||^2. Put q=w/tau and divide the
distribution weights by tau for the KKT multipliers. Keep demand1 on all
supported and off-o rows and demand0 on other own-o rows. This gives exact
primal feasibility, stationarity and complementarity, mass ell and payment p.
XOR translations transport the witness to every owner. Full KKT follows
from the XOR orbit of one row with multiplier1/c^2 on each translated row,
with repeated rows combined. Thus the full point is1/c and its norm is rho.
Here ||q||^2=ell because all positive multipliers have relaxed RHS1; the
full point is feasible for the relaxation, so0<ell<=rho. Alternatively
this inequality follows for any unit-demand base from
ell<=z dot q<=||z||*||q||<=rho, without assuming binary relaxed RHS values.

These arguments prove the certificate for every d>=4. The independent
checker also enumerates the entire explicit support for d=4,5 (71 and2480
entries), and checks all independent4-sets of Q_4 as an additional off-row
test. It imports no generator, solver, or activation checker.

NORMALIZED COEFFICIENT AND A USEFUL VARIANCE IDENTITY
Define
  C_d=theta*sqrt(n*rho)/2=n*theta/(2c),
  H_d=4C_d^2.
Since K=floor[(n/2-1)(1-2/d)],
  n/2 <= c <= n/2+n/d+1.
Consequently 0<C_d<=1 and
  1-C_d <=3/d+2/n <=4/d,       C_d>=1-4/d.                         (2)
Here n>=2d for d>=4. In particular H_d tends to4, from below for this family.

The mean of w is c/n, with d+1 coordinates theta and m coordinates gamma.
The exact two-value variance formula gives

  tau-c^2/n = [(d+1)m/n]*(theta-gamma)^2 <= d+1.                  (3)

Both entries lie in[0,1], so the displayed bound follows. Thus
  1<=rho*tau<=1+4(d+1)/n,
using c>=n/2. For d>=9,2^d>=4d(d+1): it holds at9 and the induction step
uses2d>=d+2. Hence

  rho*tau<=1+1/d,
  c*p/2=C_d/(rho*tau)>=(1-4/d)/(1+1/d)>=1-5/d.                 (4)

DIRECT VETO: AN EXPLICIT FINITE BOUND
For d>=9 take
  B=floor(1/rho)=floor(c^2/n), N=Bn+1.
There are B disjoint base blocks and one common veto owner; each generator
is u+x^b(S)>=1. Set u=1 to see the minimum total cover is1; any row forces
total payment at least1. The exact veto lifting gives every private owner
a witness payment
  p/[1+(B-1)rho+ell] >= p/(1+B*rho),
because ell<=rho. Therefore the envelope sum is at least
  W=B*n*p/(1+B*rho).
The full veto owner's nonnegative payment may be omitted in this bound.

Put z=B*rho. Then1-rho<z<=1 and B>=1. An exact rearrangement gives

  W/sqrt(N)=(c*p/2)*[2sqrt(z)/(1+z)]
                         *[1+1/(Bn)]^(-1/2).                   (5)

The second factor is at least sqrt(z)>=z>=1-rho; the third is at least
1-1/(2Bn)>=1-1/(2n), by the tangent bound for(1+x)^(-1/2). All lower factors
are nonnegative. Combining them with(4) and rho<=4/n yields

  W/sqrt(N) >=1-5/d-rho-1/(2n)
             >=1-5/d-9/(2n)
             >=1-6/d.                                           (6)

The last step follows already from n>=4d(d+1), d>=9. Thus the direct finite
construction, without a union product, proves a quantitative coefficient
arbitrarily close to1.

EXPLICIT QUANTIFIERS AND PRODUCTION TRANSFER
Given epsilon in(0,1) and N0, choose ANY integer d such that
  d>=9, d>=6/epsilon, and2^d>=N0.
Define n,K,c,B,N by the preceding formulas. They are all finite integers;
B>=1 and N>=n>=N0. Formula(6) gives the right side of(1).

To obtain one genuine production function, use the direct realization
audited in proofs/mass_search_direct_realization_audit.txt. Each generator
contains the common veto. For its full and selected relaxed demand arrays
a define g_a(S)=max({a_R:R subset S} union{0}). This preserves every cover
polyhedron. The full game v and each local game w^i obey0<=w^i<=v<=1 and
w^i(S)=v(S) off i. For every nonempty A, take the supporting vector with
all outside-A coefficients1, the veto coefficient g_a(A) if the veto lies
in A, and all other inside-A coefficients0. These forms majorize g_a and
attain it at A. The single production function is

  F(X,Z)=min{h^(v,A) dot X,
             h^(w^i,A) dot X+Z_i: A nonempty, i an owner}.

At b_i=e_i+e_(N+i) it realizes v; when only i drops its control resource,
it realizes w^i. It is a finite nonnegative min of linear forms on2N real
resources, so it is valid on the entire nonnegative real domain. Every
selected report lies in the fixed owner's genuine underreport box. Since
the base relaxations are deletions, final relaxed generator demands are
only1 or the selected veto payment u_i; thus coefficients are in
{0,1} union{u_i}. Symmetry makes all private u_i equal. Empty coalition
values are zero. F at the full aggregate equals1.

No compatibility assumption between distinct owners' selected relaxed
profiles is missing: the control-coordinate realization constructs them
simultaneously. No assertion that an owner changes identity or bundle
between witness profiles is made. Other real reports do not weaken a
supremum lower bound. All generators, supports and forms may be numerous;
this is an existence theorem, not an efficient representation claim.

DIRECT ASYMPTOTIC SEQUENCE
Along all integers d tending to infinity, c/n->1/2, tau/n->1/4,
rho*n->4, p*n->4 and ell/rho->1. Also B/n->1/4, so N/n^2->1/4 and N grows
without bound. The explicit conservative lower bound in(5) tends to1.
The actual envelope sum consequently has normalized liminf at least1.
This is not an assertion that the actual envelope's normalized limit is1.

FIXED-BASE UNION/VETO ALTERNATIVE
For completeness the earlier union theorem supplies a second quantitative
route. Fix a dimension d>=4 and take a union product of t copies. Its
rho_t=rho/t, p_t=rho*p/[rho+(t-1)ell], and
ell_t=rho*ell/[rho+(t-1)ell]. Choose B_t=ceil(t/rho), N_t=B_t*n*t+1.
The finite bound in proofs/mass_search_funding_corollary.txt is

  W_t/sqrt(N_t) >= C_d * [t/(t+c0)]
                         /sqrt(1+rho/t+rho/(n*t^2)),
  c0=rho/ell-1>=0.

Because every coordinate of w lies in[0,1], tau<=n. Hence c0<=3 and
rho<=4/n<=1/4. The product of the last two factors is at least
  1-[c0+(rho/2)(1+1/n)]/t >=1-4/t.
For d,t>=4, (2) therefore gives
  W_t/sqrt(N_t) >=1-4/d-4/t.                                    (7)

Given epsilon,N0 choose d,t>=8/epsilon and2^d>=N0. Then N_t>=N0 and(7)
proves(1). This explicitly resolves the two limiting parameters; no illicit
interchange of a fixed-base limit and a growing-base limit is needed.
The direct route(6) is simpler and retains the three-value coefficient
description, so it is the preferred construction.

REPRODUCIBILITY AND INTERPRETATION
Run python3 checks/mass_search_hypercube_funding.py. All checks use integers
and Fraction; square-root inequalities are checked after valid squaring.
It reconstructs the d=4,5 distributions, independently checks graph edges,
marginals, KKT and XOR symmetry, verifies the complete d=4 off-row universe,
and checks the exact formulas and finite funding bounds for many dimensions.
The universal statements follow from the proof, not from finite tests.

The coefficient improves the old interval construction's2/3 and the
Möbius-ladder construction's3/4. It rules out every architecture-specific
universal leading upper coefficient strictly below1. It does not rule out
a sqrt(N) upper bound with coefficient1, and it says nothing comparable
about arbitrary mechanisms. These veto instances admit efficient covers.
