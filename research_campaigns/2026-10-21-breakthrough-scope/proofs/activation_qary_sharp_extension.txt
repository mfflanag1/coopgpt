SHARP FIXED-Q FUNDING COEFFICIENT Q/2
Independent extension audit, 21 October2026. New file; frozen earlier proofs
are unchanged. Uses the local construction and finite transport lemma from
activation_qary_cube_proof.txt and the all-real-report common-veto cap in
mass_geometry_final_upper_attempt.txt.

VERDICT. The proposed parameters are valid. They approach the sharp leading
funding coefficient Q/2 for the common-veto architecture built from transitive
Q-uniform hypergraph independent-set complements. Both the construction and
the matching upper bound concern this class, not arbitrary production rules.

1. NEW PARAMETERS, SAME FINITE CONSTRUCTION.
Fix any integer Q>=3, not necessarily prime. Put
 h=8Q(Q-1), d0=16Q(Q-1)(Q+3), and d=r*d0 for integer r>=1.
Set n=Q^d, M=Q^(d-1), m=n-(Q-1)d-1, x=h/d,
 mu=(Q-2+x)/(Q-1), beta0=(Q-1)/Q-24/d,
 K=floor(beta0*m), beta=K/m, b=1-beta, a=1-mu,
 s=(Q-2)d+h+K, k=n-s.
Use all complements of independent s-sets for the complete axis-line
Q-uniform hypergraph on(Z/QZ)^d. The existing local construction applies
without change: in phase0 retain exactly(Q-2)d+h neighbors; in nonzero
phase c add exactly h excluded-color neighbors c e_j, repair each new
line by deleting one uniformly chosen non-neighbor point, and subsequently
thin weight2 vertices and fill far vertices by the finite rational flow.

The neighbor marginals are mu, every support has exactly(Q-2)d+h selected
neighbors, and the worst exceptional availability is
 B_avail=(Q-1)/Q-2x/[Q(Q-1)]=(Q-1)/Q-16/d.
Consequently B_avail-beta0=8/d>0, so all exceptional thinning probabilities
are in(0,1). Also
 beta0-mu=1/[Q(Q-1)]-8(Q+3)/d >=1/[2Q(Q-1)].
Since m>h=8Q(Q-1), beta>=beta0-1/m>mu. Thus a>b, as required by the
all-off-row potential comparison.

2. ALL HALL CUTS, WITH A VANISHING AVAILABILITY MARGIN.
The far-color class counts remain
 f0=M-1-(Q-1)*binomial(d,2),
 fc=M-d-(Q-2)*binomial(d,2) for c!=0.
The exceptional count is e=(Q-1)^2*binomial(d,2). As before,
 M-Qd^2<=f_c<=M, e+Qd+2<=Q^2d^2,
 K-e>=Q beta0 M-Q^2d^2, K<=Q beta0 M.
All these inequalities hold at the current d>=576.

For every integer D>=20, 3^(D-5)>100D^3. At D20 this is
14348907>800000; induction uses3(D/(D+1))^3>1. Therefore
 M=Q^4*Q^(d-5)>=Q^4*3^(d-5)>100Q^4d^3.
This dominates the smaller vanishing cut margins here. Specifically,
 Q beta0-Q+2=1-24Q/d>=7/8,
 Q-1-Q beta0=24Q/d.
Hence the three sufficient Hall inequalities are:
 (i) K-e-(Q-2)M >=(7/8)M-Q^2d^2>0;
 (ii) sum_{c!=j}f_c-K >=24QM/d-Q(Q-1)d^2>0;
 (iii) K-e-(Q beta-Q+2)sum_{c!=j}f_c
       >=24Q(Q-2)M/d-Q^2d^2>0.
For(iii), the coefficient Q beta-Q+2 is positive since beta>mu and
Qmu-Q+2=(Q-2+Qx)/(Q-1)>0. Its upper bound follows by replacing beta with
beta0 and the class sum with(Q-1)M. The displayed exponential bound makes
each final expression positive.

These are exactly the three conditions used by the earlier transport proof.

3. EXACT NORMALIZED KKT WITNESSES.
The complement row mixture w has focal1, all(Q-1)d neighbors a, and every
outside coordinate b. Every supported row contains focal0, exactly d-h
neighbors and m-K outside vertices, so its potential is
 t=1+(d-h)a+(m-K)b=1+(Q-1)d*a^2+m*b^2=||w||^2.
Every off-focal row has at least one covered neighbor in each of d focal
axis lines. Because a>b, its potential is at least d*a+(k-d)b. The gap is
 h(beta-mu)-beta >=4-h/m-beta0>2>0.
Thus all off rows are feasible and every supported own row is tight.
The unchanged binary deletion construction gives q_local=w/t and total
dual mass ell=1/t, with theta0=1. Translation supplies such a witness for
every actual base owner. Uniform row size and transitivity give full
cover z=(1/k)1 and rho=n/k^2, exactly as in the frozen proof.

4. ACTUAL ENVELOPE LIMIT Q/2.
Hold Q fixed and let r tend to infinity. Then x->0 and beta0->(Q-1)/Q.
Flooring contributes at most1/m, while d/n->0. Hence
 k/n->1/Q, t/n->1/Q^2, rho~Q^2/n, ell/rho->1.
For each base choose B=floor(1/rho), and add the common veto owner, so
N=Bn+1, B rho->1 and N~n^2/Q^2. The selected private own-report coordinate is
 p=ell/[1+(B-1)rho+ell].
For the explicit direct production realization, the all-real-report cap is
 E_i<=rho/(1+B rho) for every private owner, and the true veto envelope is
 E_u=B rho/(1+B rho).
The lower and upper private coordinate bounds are asymptotic to rho/2,
uniformly across all Bn private owners. Squeezing therefore proves for the
ACTUAL true own-report envelope, at full output T=1,
  (sum_i E_i)/sqrt(N) -> Q/2,
  sum_i E_i^2 ->(Q^2+1)/4.
This is stronger than a selected-witness lower limit and uses the explicit
production class for which every real own report preserves generators.
It makes no claim about arbitrary min-linear realizations.

5. MATCHING CLASS UPPER BOUND.
Let H be a finite nonempty vertex-transitive Q-uniform hypergraph, and take
a transitive family of complement rows of fixed-cardinality independent
sets. Vertex transitivity makes every vertex have the same positive degree
Delta. Every independent set I omits at least one vertex from every edge.
Double-counting omissions gives
 Delta*|V\I| =sum_edges |edge\I| >=|edges|=n*Delta/Q.
Thus its complement row size k>=n/Q. Its full uniform cover has
rho=n/k^2 and therefore n*rho<=Q^2.

For arbitrary heterogeneous common-veto blocks satisfying n_c*rho_c<=Q^2,
put R=sum rho_c and m=sum n_c=N-1. The same true-envelope cap gives
 sum_private E_i <=[sum_c n_c*rho_c]/(1+R)
 <=Q*[sum_c sqrt(n_c*rho_c)]/(1+R)
 <=Q*sqrt(mR)/(1+R) <=(Q/2)*sqrt(m).
The veto contributes R/(1+R)<=1. Therefore
 sum_all E_i <=(Q/2)*sqrt(N-1)+1,
so every sequence in this class with N->infinity has leading funding
coefficient at most Q/2. Part4 attains it. The upper bound requires the
same direct production class (or the abstract incident-relaxation envelope)
for which the generator cap holds. The hypergraph must be nonempty; a
vertex-transitive hypergraph with no edges gives no positive-degree count.

PORTABILITY AND SCOPE.
The Q/2 statement is an exact supremum of asymptotic leading coefficients
in this stated hypergraph/common-veto class, attained as a limit. It is not
an exact finite-N inequality with no additive1, nor an all-N construction.
All selected demands and final production-form coefficients remain binary.
Actual owners stay fixed, with two resources each, and total dimension grows.
