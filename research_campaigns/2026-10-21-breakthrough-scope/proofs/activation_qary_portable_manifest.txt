NO DEPENDENCY ON EARLIER SEARCH HISTORY
The Möbius, hypercube, pointwise-counterexample, optimized ternary, and
numerical search artifacts are not needed for the q-ary theorem. The q-ary
proof repeats the transport lemma and all needed local combinatorics.
The arbitrary-game balanced-closure theorem is unnecessary because the
common-veto generator games have a direct efficient-cover realization.
The Boolean-profile realization input is useful background, but item3's
direct formula is sufficient for these veto games.

OWNER/RESOURCE SCOPE
At the amplified instance N=B n+1 is the number of actual owners. There
are2N resource coordinates (X_i,Z_i). Actual owner i has the fixed bundle
b_i=e_i+e_(N+i); its selected lower report is e_i. Thus each owner controls
two resources, while total owners and total resource dimension both grow.
No owner splitting, owner relabeling across compared profiles, or replacement
by artificial subowners occurs. A single production function simultaneously
realizes all selected own underreports. The veto's optional control resource
may be redundant; omitting it leaves2N-1 coordinates and does not affect the
unbounded-dimensional conclusion. This is not a minimal-dimension claim.

The combinatorial axis-line size Q is not a claim about bounded arity of
production recipes. The full production function is a finite minimum of
nonnegative linear forms; these forms can involve many resource coordinates.
The row support size k and number of forms are huge and grow with parameters.
No fixed owner-count, fixed resource-dimension, fixed recipe-arity, or compact
representation bound is asserted.

BINARY PRODUCTION FORMS
All full generator demands and every selected local generator demand are
in{0,1}. Therefore g(S)=max({a_R:R subset S} union{0}) is binary. In the
direct realization, the supporting form for A has coefficient1 outside A,
coefficient g(A) at the veto if it is in A, and0 at other members of A.
Every such coefficient is0 or1. A selected owner-family merely adds its
unit control coefficient Z_i, so the final finite min-linear F has only
binary coefficients. Its definition on the entire nonnegative orthant is
finite, monotone, continuous, concave, and positively homogeneous.

The full profile and selected own-report profiles have output T=1. At all
admissible real underreports of the fixed actual bundles, the veto remains
a resource bottleneck. That gives an instance-specific efficient pay-veto
cover, so these lower bounds refute the true own-report envelope architecture,
not every possible allocation/payment mechanism. Additional real reports
can only increase its true envelope relative to the selected witnesses.

ASYMPTOTIC QUANTIFIERS
For every finite C, choose Q with(Q-1)/2>C, hold Q fixed, let d grow through
multiples of d0, and choose B=floor(1/rho) anew for each growing base. This
gives arbitrarily large N with funding>C√N*T. It does not say that increasing
B alone at a fixed base has the claimed limit, nor that every sufficiently
large integer N belongs to the construction.
