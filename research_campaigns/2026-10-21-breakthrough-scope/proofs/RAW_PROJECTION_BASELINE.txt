STATIC RAW PROJECTION BENCHMARK AND THE OPEN AGGREGATE ROUTE
21October2026. Elementary consequence of standard projection geometry;
no publication-novelty claim for this baseline.

Let K be a nonnegative coalition-cover polyhedron with an efficient cover
x*>=0 of total T. Let q be its Euclidean minimum-norm point on N owners.
Then
                 sum_i q_i <= (1+sqrt(N))*T/2.                (1)
For finite nonnegative min-linear production, an active supporting linear
form at the grand bundle gives such an efficient cover at every profile.

Proof. Projection optimality gives ||q||^2<=q dot x*<=T*m, where m=max q_i.
Thus0<=m<=T. For N>=2, put S=sum q_i. Cauchy over the remaining coordinates
implies m^2+(S-m)^2/(N-1)<=T*m. Consequently
 S<=m+sqrt((N-1)*m*(T-m))
   <=T/2+(T/2)*sqrt(N),
where the last inequality is Cauchy applied to
 (m-T/2, sqrt((T/2)^2-(m-T/2)^2)) and(1,sqrt(N-1)).
The zero-output and N=1 cases are immediate. This uses no unit-demand or
uniform-coordinate assumption.

The constant is attained along square owner counts. For integer k>=1, take
one veto and k+2 disjoint groups of k private owners. Impose veto+group>=1
for every group. Then N=k(k+2)+1=(k+1)^2, T=1, and exact KKT gives
 q_veto=(k+2)/(2k+2), q_private=1/(2k+2).
Their total is(k+2)/2=(1+sqrt(N))/2. The game has the fixed-owner production
 F(c)=min(c_veto, sum_groups min_(j in group)c_j),
with one resource per actual owner, at the all-ones profile. This family and
its own-coordinate monotonicity were already present in the inherited notes.
The static calculation is context, not a claimed new campaign breakthrough.

WHAT THE NEW HYPERCUBE RESULT ADDS.

If proved, (1) would give funding at most(1+sqrt(N))*T for every min-linear

This would be a genuine general-bound advance even without the exact
