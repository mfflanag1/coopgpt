INDEPENDENT AUDIT: DIRECT COMMON-VETO PRODUCTION REALIZATION
21 October 2026. Verdict: valid, with the scope distinctions below.

1. FINITE VETO GENERATORS AND THEIR MONOTONE GAME
Let V be a nonempty finite owner set, u its designated veto owner, and let
every generator R contain u. Assign each generator a finite demand
0<=a_R<=1. For every S subset V define

  g_a(S)=max({a_R: R subset S} union {0}).

This game is monotone, nonnegative, at most1, and normalized at the empty
coalition. It vanishes whenever u is absent. There are no empty generators
because every generator contains u. Duplicated generators cause no problem.

For x>=0 the generator inequalities x(R)>=a_R are equivalent to all
coalition inequalities x(S)>=g_a(S). In the forward direction use
x(S)>=x(R) for any R subset S, and take a maximum. In the reverse direction
use S=R and g_a(R)>=a_R. Thus this direct monotone extension preserves the
entire nonnegative covering polyhedron, including its unique minimum-norm
point. The same argument applies within every subcoalition A. In fact its
minimum total cover is exactly g_a(A): all required mass can be paid to u
when u belongs to A, and otherwise all demands are zero. Hence no general
balanced-closure construction is needed in this veto setting.

2. EXPLICIT SUPPORTING FORMS
For every nonempty A subset V define a nonnegative vector h^(g,A) by

  h_i=1                        if i is outside A;
  h_u=g(A)                     if u belongs to A;
  h_i=0                        for all other i in A.

For every S, h^(g,A)(S)>=g(S). If S is not a subset of A, an outside-A
coordinate contributes1. If S is a subset of A and contains u, the form
equals g(A)>=g(S). If S is a subset of A and omits u, both g(S) and the
form are zero. Equality holds at S=A. It follows that

  g(S)=min_{nonempty A subset V} h^(g,A)(S).                       (1)

At S=empty every form is zero, so (1) still holds even though A is required
nonempty. The argument also works for a single-owner set. The evaluation
point in (1) must be S: writing min_A h^(g,A)(A) would be incorrect.

3. LOCAL RELAXATIONS
Let v be the full generator game and let w^j be the game obtained by
reducing only generator demands incident to owner j. Then w^j<=v. For any
coalition S omitting j, every generator R subset S omits j and is unchanged,
so w^j(S)=v(S). This remains true when j=u, since then such coalitions all
have value zero. If no special veto-owner witness is needed, take w^u=v.
Each game retains the direct representation in (1).

4. ONE PRODUCTION FUNCTION, 2N RESOURCES, FIXED OWNERS
Write N=|V| and let X=(X_i) and Z=(Z_i) be two nonnegative N-resource vectors.
Define the finite min-linear function

  F(X,Z)=min({h^(v,A) dot X: A nonempty}
          union {h^(w^j,A) dot X+Z_j: j in V, A nonempty}).         (2)

There are at most (N+1)(2^N-1) displayed forms, with repetitions allowed.
Give actual owner i the fixed bundle b_i=e_i+e_(N+i). Its full report is
b_i and its selected lower report is e_i. All owners remain the same across
all profiles. The one function F is finite, nonnegative, monotone, continuous,
concave, and positively homogeneous on ALL of R_+^(2N). These facts follow
directly from its finitely many nonnegative linear forms, not only from
checking the Boolean witness profiles.

At the full profile and a coalition S, the baseline forms minimize to v(S).
The j-family minimizes to w^j(S)+1_{j in S}. If j is outside S, this equals
v(S); if j is in S, it is at least1>=v(S). Thus the coalition value is v(S).

Now only owner j drops its Z_j resource. For the coalition S, the j-family
minimizes to w^j(S). Every other k-family is either w^k(S)+1>=v(S), if k
belongs to S, or w^k(S)=v(S), if k is outside S. The baseline value is v(S).
Since w^j<=v, the minimum is w^j(S). This also covers j outside S because
then w^j(S)=v(S). Empty-coalition evaluation is identically zero at every
profile. These identities prove simultaneous realization without assigning
independent functions or changing bundles between owner witnesses.

Therefore the true own-report envelope at the full bundles includes every
displayed minimum-norm witness: the selected report e_i is an admissible
coordinatewise underreport of e_i+e_(N+i). Extra intermediate real reports
may increase the true envelope; they cannot invalidate these lower bounds.
At the full profile, if some full generator has demand1, F(sum_i b_i)=1 and
the minimum total cover is1, so the cover normalization is exactly preserved.

5. COEFFICIENT AUDIT
For arbitrary demands a_R in[0,1], each h coefficient lies in
{0,1} union {a_R}; the coefficient g(A) is a maximum of finitely many such
demands. Thus an assertion that ALL arbitrary systems use only coefficients
{0,1,u_j} would require an additional demand restriction.

That restriction holds for the direct-veto witnesses in this campaign.
Their base witnesses are deletions with demands in{0,1}; after the veto lift,
the focal incident generator demands are u_j or1, and all other generators
retain demand1. Hence v takes values in{0,1}, while w^j takes values in
{0,u_j,1}. The j-family in (2) has coefficients in{0,u_j,1}, including its
unit control coefficient. Across all families the allowed set is
{0,1} union {u_j:j in V}. In the symmetric constructions all u_j for private
owners agree, so the whole function uses just three possible coefficients.
All are rational for the exact campaign witnesses. A preceding union product
can introduce another relaxed demand value; its realization remains valid,
but that value should not automatically be called the veto payment u_j.

6. ALL REAL REPORTS AND THE PRECISE VETO SCOPE
There are two distinct claims.

(a) Global production validity: (2) defines a valid production function for
every nonnegative real aggregate (X,Z), with no restriction to the unit box
and no requirement that a Boolean coalition game formula persist there.

(b) Fixed-bundle report validity: at the actual bundles above every admissible
underreport has the form X_i e_i+Z_i e_(N+i) with0<=X_i,Z_i<=1. For N>=2,
the baseline form A=V\{u} is exactly X_u, since u is outside A and all other
coefficients vanish. Consequently

                         0<=F(X,Z)<=X_u                           (3)

globally. Thus every coalition omitting owner u has zero production at EVERY
admissible real underreport profile of these fixed bundles: no other owner
can supply resource X_u. This is stronger than merely having a veto at the
selected Boolean profiles. For N=1 the sole owner is trivially a veto, and
the baseline form gives F<=v({u})X_u<=X_u.

It would nevertheless be incorrect to assert that owner u is a veto at all
unrelated owner-bundle profiles permitted by the general production model.
Another owner could hold resource X_u at such a profile. For example, giving
one other owner the entire full aggregate bundle yields positive singleton
production even though the designated owner u is absent. The globally valid
statement is the resource bottleneck (3); the identity of its supplying owner
is fixed only in the specified actual bundles and their underreport box.

At intermediate underreports the resulting coalition game need not equal
the original max-of-contained-generators formula with any preassigned demand
array. No such equality is needed. Within the fixed box it still has the veto
property and monotonicity, and paying all grand-coalition output to u is an
efficient coalition cover. This reinforces the interpretation that the large
funding result is a failure of the chosen envelope architecture, not an
impossibility for every mechanism on this instance. No general global-domain
mechanism theorem follows from this instance-specific efficient payment.

7. AUDIT EVIDENCE AND CONCLUSION
The preceding proof is general and does not rely on finite tests. As a
separate exact Fraction check, a four-owner toy instance with generators
{u,1},{u,2},{u,3} and local reductions1/3,1/2,3/5 passed80 full/one-lowered
coalition-value checks, all256 Boolean aggregate evaluations,1024 coordinate
monotonicity comparisons and768 homogeneity checks. Its75 forms had exactly
the coefficient set{0,1,1/3,1/2,3/5}. All aggregates lacking X_u had output0.
The toy checker was executed in memory and did not alter campaign files.

The direct construction makes the production realization shorter, explicit,
and easier to interpret. It removes any need to invoke a general balanced
closure or market-game representation theorem for these veto constructions.
It preserves exact KKT witness geometry and the fixed-owner/all-real-domain
requirements. It does not claim a small representation, arbitrary-game
realizability, an arbitrary-mechanism funding lower bound, or a universal
owner-veto property outside the fixed actual bundles' underreport domain.
