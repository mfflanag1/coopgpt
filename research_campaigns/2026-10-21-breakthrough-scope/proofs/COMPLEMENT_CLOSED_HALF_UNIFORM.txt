Complement-closed half-uniform cover systems: no normalized amplification
Parent derivation, 21 October 2026; independently checked by native analytic scout.
Not yet a novelty claim. All original row demands are exactly one.

Let A be a nonempty family of k-subsets of a 2k-element owner set, closed
under taking complements. Put C=conv{1_S:S in A}. Its minimum-norm point is
v=(1/2)1: every row has coordinate sum k, and each complement pair averages
to v. Thus ||v||^2=k/2, full cover q=(1/k)1, and rho=||q||^2=2/k.

Fix owner i. A normalized local witness consists of alpha>=0 of total mass
one, w=A^T alpha, and t>0. Every off-i row S satisfies w(S)>=t; a used off-i
row has equality; a used incident row satisfies w(S)<=t. Write theta=w_i,
which equals the alpha-mass of incident rows. Since sum_j w_j=k,
  ||w||^2 >= k/2.
Every used row has potential at most t, so ||w||^2<=t. The complement of
an incident row is off-i; hence every incident row has potential at most
k-t. Splitting the alpha-average into incident and off-i rows gives
  ||w||^2 <= theta*(k-t)+(1-theta)*t
            = k/2+(1-2theta)*(t-k/2).
If theta>1/2, t>=k/2 forces the right side<=k/2. Equality in the elementary
sum-of-squares bound then forces w=(1/2)1, contradicting theta>1/2.
Therefore theta<=1/2. The full-profile witness attains equality.

Consequently H=rho*sum_i theta_i^2=1 for the coordinatewise suprema. Also
L<=rho for every local witness, so q_i=L*theta<=rho/2=1/k. Thus the actual
minimum-norm own-report envelope at this uniform-demand base profile is
exactly the full-profile vector. This is stronger than a search nonhit.

Scope: this says nothing about arbitrary nonuniform base demands, every
profile of an associated production model, or optimal unrestricted funding.
It rules out a broad symmetric family as a seed for the H>2 amplification.
