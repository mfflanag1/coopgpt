0. EXACT CONTRACT AND RESULTS
Fix N actual owners, a resource dimension D, and one production function
F:R_+^D -> R_+ that is a finite minimum of nonnegative linear forms. At a
profile b=(b_1,...,b_N), set v_b(S)=F(sum_{i in S}b_i) and T=F(sum_i b_i).
Let q(v) be the unique minimizer of sum_i x_i^2 subject to x>=0 and
x(S)>=v(S) for every coalition S. There is no efficiency equality in this
definition. Define the true own-report envelope

 M_i^F(b)=sup{q_i(v_(a_i,b_-i)):0<=a_i<=b_i coordinatewise}.

This rule covers every coalition because it dominates q(v_b) coordinatewise.
It is nondecreasing in each owner's own bundle because its supremum domain
expands with that bundle. These are payment-only individual incentives;
no coalition incentive or private opportunity-cost claim is made. At every
profile an active supporting form of F gives a nonnegative efficient cover,
so every q coordinate at an underreport is at most T. The supremum is finite.

THEOREM A. The proposed bound sum_i M_i^F(b)^2<=T^2 is false. There are
fixed-owner min-linear examples with T=1 whose actual squared-envelope
values tend to4/3. One finite example has N=2,048,001 and a certified
squared-envelope value greater than1.01. An earlier independent construction
has only98,041 owners and a smaller strict rational gap.

THEOREM B. For every epsilon>0 and every N0, there is a finite N>=N0 and
such a fixed-owner production example with T=1 and

                    sum_i M_i^F(b)>=(1-epsilon)*sqrt(N).

Consequently no universal upper bound c*sqrt(N)+O(1), c<1, can hold for this
particular rule. This does NOT disprove the coefficient-one sqrt(N) upper
bound, prove a universal upper bound, or give a lower bound for arbitrary mechanisms.
Every example uses2N resource coordinates and retains the same owners and
their bundles in every comparison. The representation is finite and explicit
but may have exponentially many forms. Polynomial-time computation is not
asserted. Publication novelty and specialist review remain separate questions.

THEOREM C. A matching upper theorem holds for an unbounded explicit class.
Take disjoint blocks c with n_c owners, each with a finite nonempty family of
NONEMPTY 0/1 unit-demand generators, full minimum-norm cover z^c, and
rho_c=||z^c||^2. Adjoin a common veto owner to every generator, put
R=sum_c rho_c and N=1+sum_c n_c, and realize any selected incident demand
arrays by the direct production function in Section4. At its stated full
bundles, including ALL real unilateral underreports, the true envelope has

 M_u=R/(1+R),      M_i<=rho_c/(1+R) for i in block c.

If every n_c*rho_c<=4, then sum M_i^2<=4/3. If every
(n_c+1)^2*rho_c<=4n_c, then sum M_i<=sqrt(N-1). The hypercube families satisfy

This theorem is about those designated profiles and all their real own

1. GENERAL GRAPH-TO-PROJECTION LEMMA
Let G be a vertex-transitive d-regular graph on n vertices. Fix a focal
vertex o, let C=N[o], and take all complements of independent a-sets as rows;
their common size is k=n-a. Suppose there is a finite rational distribution
on independent a-sets that meets C exactly once, selects every vertex of C
with probability1/(d+1), and selects every outside vertex with probability
(a-1)/(n-d-1). Assume n>d+1 so the latter denominator is positive.

Write theta=d/(d+1), gamma=(n-d-a)/(n-d-1), and let w be the mean of the
complementary row vectors. Then w equals theta on C and gamma elsewhere.
Every supported row has exactly d vertices of C. Any row omitting o also has
exactly d vertices of C, since its independent complement contains o and
cannot contain any neighbor of o. Thus supported and off-owner rows all have
the same potential

 t=d*theta+(k-d)*gamma=(d+1)*theta^2+(n-d-1)*gamma^2=||w||^2.

The equality with ||w||^2 also follows by averaging the supported row
potentials against their distribution alpha. Keep demand1 on off-owner and
supported rows, set all remaining own-owner row demands to0. Then q=w/t,
lambda_R=alpha_R/t satisfy feasibility, nonnegative stationarity
q=sum_R lambda_R*1_R, and complementary slackness. These conditions prove
q is the unique minimum-norm cover. Its focal coordinate is p=theta/t and
total multiplier mass is ell=1/t=||q||^2.

The full minimum-norm cover is z=1/k on each coordinate, with rho=n/k^2:
average the automorphism orbit of any row to obtain a uniform convex mean,
or average the covering constraints and apply Cauchy-Schwarz. Translating
the local distribution provides one legitimate own-row witness per owner.
Its normalized mass is H=rho*n*theta^2. All off-owner rows, including those
outside the distribution's support, have been handled analytically above.

2. AN EXPLICIT HYPERCUBE DISTRIBUTION
Use G=Q_d for integer d>=4. Its vertices are binary d-vectors, with edges
between vectors differing in one coordinate. Set

 n=2^d, a0=n/2-1, b0=n/2-d, m=n-d-1=a0+b0,
 P2=binomial(d,2), R=a0-P2,
 K=floor(a0*(d-2)/d), f=K/a0, k=n-K-1.

Here the independent sets will have size a=K+1. Let E' be the even-parity
vertices except0, and O' the odd-parity vertices except the d unit vectors.
Their sizes are a0,b0. The closed star C consists of0 and those unit vectors.
For d>=4, 2^d-2>d(d-1), by induction from d=4; hence R>0 and K<=b0.

Choose a star vertex s uniformly. Independently choose an even background
with probability a0/m and an odd background with probability b0/m.
In the odd branch, take a uniform K-subset of O'. It is compatible with s:
if s=0 all its neighbors have been removed; if s is a unit vector both it
and O' have odd parity.

For the even branch choose an integer P in{floor(P2*f),ceil(P2*f)} with mean
P2*f. Choose P allowed weight-two vertices and K-P even vertices of weight
at least four, uniformly in each category. If s=0 all P2 weight-two vertices
are allowed. If s=e_j, the allowed pairs avoid j and number
P2'=P2-(d-1)=P2*(d-2)/d. Higher even weights never neighbor e_j.
The subset sizes are feasible: the mean P2*f lies in the integer-bounded
interval[max(0,K-R),min(K,P2')], so its floor and ceiling do too.
The union of the chosen background and{s} is independent, has exactly K+1
vertices, and meets C exactly once. Every choice has rational probability.

Every higher-weight even vertex has conditional even-branch marginal f.
For a fixed weight-two vertex, averaging over the selected star vertex gives

 [f + (d-2)*(d/(d-2))*f]/(d+1)=f:

the two unit-vector neighbors forbid it; the other d-2 unit vectors and0
permit it with the displayed conditional probabilities. Thus every outside
even vertex has total marginal(a0/m)*f=K/m. Every outside odd vertex has
total marginal(b0/m)*(K/b0)=K/m. The graph lemma applies, with

 theta=d/(d+1), gamma=(m-K)/m,
 t=d^2/(d+1)+(m-K)^2/m,
 rho=n/k^2, ell=1/t, p=theta/t.

XOR translation is vertex transitive and preserves the full row universe.
The displayed distribution is a finite exact construction, not a claim
deduced from numerical feasibility. Explicit support enumeration is only a
supplementary check at small dimensions.

As d grows, K/n->1/2, k/n->1/2, theta->1 and t/n->1/4. Therefore

 H=rho*n*theta^2 ->4,
 E=n*p^2,  R0=E/rho=(k*theta/t)^2 ->4,
 rho->0, ell/rho->1.

Also ell<=rho. In this deletion construction ell=||q||^2, and the relaxed
feasible set contains the full one. More generally any nonnegative KKT
representation of a unit-demand relaxation satisfies
ell<=z.q<=||z||||q||<=rho because z(R)>=1 on every full row.

4. ONE EXPLICIT PRODUCTION FUNCTION ON ALL REAL RESOURCES
All amplified generators contain u. For any demand array a_R in[0,1], set
g_a(S)=max({a_R:R subset S} union{0}). This is monotone, takes values in[0,1],
and vanishes without u. Its nonnegative coalition-cover polyhedron equals
the generator cover: a generator inequality bounds every larger coalition,
and conversely the coalition inequality for R bounds its generator demand.
Thus no projection changes when all coalition rows are included.

Let v be the full game and w^j each selected relaxed game, with w^u=v.
They satisfy w^j<=v and w^j(S)=v(S) whenever j is absent from S. For every
such game g and every nonempty coalition A define a nonnegative vector h:
outside A its entries are1; inside A only u may be nonzero, with entry g(A).
Then h(S)>=g(S): an outside-A member contributes1, or S is contained in A
and monotonicity plus the veto property applies. Equality holds at A.
Hence g(S)=min_A h^(g,A)(S), also at the empty coalition.

Use N base resources X and N control resources Z. Define the ONE function

 F(X,Z)=min{h^(v,A).X; h^(w^j,A).X+Z_j: all nonempty A, all owners j}.

It is a finite nonnegative minimum of nonnegative linear forms, so is
monotone, continuous, concave, and positively homogeneous on all R_+^(2N).
Give owner j the fixed full bundle e_j+e_(N+j). At full reports the coalition
game is v: a j-family with j present is penalized by1, and one with j absent
equals v on that coalition. If only j hides its control resource, the j-family
becomes w^j; every other family remains at least v or equals v. Thus the
coalition game is exactly w^j. This simultaneously realizes every selected
own-report witness in one institution. Additional admissible real reports
can only enlarge the envelope. At the full aggregate F=v(N)=1.

This direct construction has at most(N+1)(2^N-1) displayed forms. In these
symmetric deletion-based examples their coefficients are drawn from three
rational values:0,1,u_i. There is no claim of efficient enumeration.
For N>=2 the baseline form A=owners\{u} is X_u, so F<=X_u globally.
Within the specified fixed bundles' underreport domain, u is consequently
a veto at every real profile. Paying all grand output to u is an efficient,
own-report-monotone coalition cover on that domain. This demonstrates why
the large envelope payments are specific to this rule; it does not assert
a global-domain mechanism theorem under arbitrary ownership reassignments.

5. SHARP UPPER BOUNDS FOR THE TRUE ENVELOPE
Every base row is nonempty throughout this section.

5.1 UNIFORM DUAL MASS: THE ONLY BASE INPUT.
For a unit-demand base let z be its full projection and rho=||z||^2. After
ANY downward RHS relaxation b' in[0,1], let q be the new projection and
lambda>=0 its row multipliers. Nonnegativity constraints need no additional
stationarity term: q=A^T lambda>=0; at a zero coordinate every incident
positive multiplier must vanish. Put L=sum lambda. Complementarity gives
 ||q||^2=sum lambda_r b'_r<=L.
The same multipliers are feasible for the full quadratic dual, whose value
under the objective ||x||^2 is2L-||q||^2. Hence
 L<=2L-||q||^2<=rho.                                           (1)
Every coordinate of q is a sum of a subset of these multipliers, so
                         q_i<=L<=rho.                        (2)
These statements hold for any particular chosen KKT multipliers, even when
the dual multipliers are not unique. At the full unit RHS, L=rho.

5.2 EXACT COMMON-VETO ENVELOPE CAPS.
At full demand, fixing the veto coordinate u in[0,1] leaves scaled independent
base systems. The objective is u^2+(1-u)^2R. It is minimized at
 u_full=R/(1+R), x^c=z^c/(1+R), full squared norm=R/(1+R).
At any downward relaxation of any full veto rows, all rows still contain u.
If mu are its KKT multipliers, then u=sum mu and ||x||^2<=sum mu=u. Their
full-dual objective2u-||x||^2 is at most the full squared norm. Therefore
 u<=R/(1+R). The full profile attains equality, proving the stated veto cap.
In particular u<1 at every witness.

Now fix a private owner i in block c and ANY of its allowed row relaxations.
Conditional on its veto coordinate u, all other blocks remain full and
have unique projections(1-u)z^d. Their total row-multiplier mass is
(1-u)rho_d, as follows from their scaled unit RHS complementary slackness.
Divide the focal block coordinates by1-u. They form the projection q of
its base system with residual demands
 b'_r=max{0,(a'_r-u)/(1-u)} in[0,1].
Off-i rows have residual1. This is an allowed base own relaxation.
Let L be the total focal multiplier mass after division by1-u, and p=q_i.
The veto stationarity identity becomes
 u=(1-u)(R-rho_c+L).
Thus its private coordinate is exactly
 x_i=p/[1+R-rho_c+L].                                        (3)
Negative residual demands cause no gap: a row with positive multiplier has
positive coordinate sum by nonnegative stationarity, so its original
residual must be positive. Clipping nonpositive residuals preserves KKT.
By(2), p<=L<=rho_c. The function L/(1+R-rho_c+L) is nondecreasing, because
1+R-rho_c>0. Therefore(3)<=rho_c/(1+R). This proves the stated caps for every witness
and hence its supremum. Identical-block symmetry is not used.

5.3 AGGREGATION AND SHARPNESS.
From the caps,
 sum_i M_i^2 <= [R^2+sum_c n_c*rho_c^2]/(1+R)^2.
If n_c*rho_c<=4, the sum in the numerator is at most4R. The identity
 4/3-(R^2+4R)/(1+R)^2=(R-2)^2/[3(1+R)^2]
proves the diagonal assertion, with its scalar maximum atR=2.

For total funding, the coordinate caps give
 sum_i M_i <= [sum_c(n_c+1)rho_c]/(1+R).
Under the funding hypothesis,
 (n_c+1)rho_c<=2sqrt(n_c*rho_c).
Cauchy-Schwarz gives sum_c sqrt(n_c*rho_c)<=sqrt(mR), and
2sqrt(R)/(1+R)<=1. Therefore sum_i M_i<=sqrt(m), proving the funding assertion.
The stronger bound sqrt(N-1) includes the veto payment; it is not merely
a nonveto bound. It holds for arbitrary numbers of heterogeneous blocks.

Hypercube sharpness uses the audited bases with n=2^d, row size k>n/2,
rho=n/k^2, focal selected p=theta/t and dual mass ell=1/t. They satisfy
 n*rho->4, p/rho->1, ell/rho->1, rho->0.
For any fixed c>0 choose integer J with J*rho->c. The exact selected private
coordinate is p/[1+(J-1)rho+ell], whereasthe cap bounds the TRUE private envelope
by rho/(1+Jrho). Their ratio tends1, uniformly over private owners by the
hypercube symmetries. The veto envelope is exactly Jrho/(1+Jrho).
Thus, after the exact production transfer below, the ACTUAL envelopes obey
 sum_i M_i^2 -> [c^2+4c]/(1+c)^2,
 (sum_i M_i)/sqrt(N) ->2sqrt(c)/(1+c).                         (4)
These now are exact asymptotic limits by squeezing, not merely lower bounds.
At c=2 the squared diagonal tends4/3. At c=1 the funding coefficient tends1
and the squared diagonal tends5/4. These are different choices of instances.

5.4 AN EXPLICIT PRODUCTION CLASS PRESERVES THE GENERATORS FOR EVERY OWN REPORT.
This subsection is essential: an arbitrary production realization or an
arbitrary efficient-core choice would not automatically preserve generators.
Use the following particular direct forms.

Let V be the N owners, with veto u. Let G be all block generators after
adjoining u. Set the full generator demand1. For each owner j select any
array a^j_R in[0,1] that differs from1 only on R containing j. For veto u
select a^u_R=1. Define
 v(S)=max({1:R in G,R subset S} union{0}),
 w^j(S)=max({a^j_R:R in G,R subset S} union{0}).
These games lie in[0,1], vanish without u, and satisfy w^j=v off j.
For each nonempty A subset V and each such game g define
 h_i^(g,A)=1 if i is outside A;
 h_u^(g,A)=g(A) if u is in A;
 h_i^(g,A)=0 for every other i inside A.
For Boolean S, min_A h^(g,A)(S)=g(S), including S=empty.
Define one finite min-linear production function
 F(X,Z)=min{h^(v,A) dot X, h^(w^j,A) dot X+Z_j:
             nonempty A subset V, j in V}.
Owner j's fixed full bundle is e_j in X plus e_j in Z. Every permissible
unilateral underreport at these full bundles is X_j=t,Z_j=z in[0,1], with
all other owners' full reports unchanged. The forms are nonnegative, so F
is defined, monotone, concave and homogeneous on ALL R_+^(2N).

Private owner j!=u.
For S containing j, write X_S for its coalition aggregate with j's base
coordinate t. For any g among the displayed games,
 min_A h^(g,A) dot X_S=min{g(S),g(S\{j})+t}.                   (5)
If a non-j element of S is outside A, its contribution is1, at least g(S).
Otherwise A contains S\{j}. If it also contains j, the minimum is attained
by A=S and equals g(S). If not, the contribution is t plus g(A), minimized
by A=S\{j}. In this latter relevant positive-value case S contains u!=j,
so S\{j} is NONEMPTY and is an available A. If S omits u, g(S)=0 and A=S
already attains0; this handles singleton S={j}. Empty S has all forms0.
Thus omission of A=empty in the definition does not create a boundary gap.

For another family k!=j, if k belongs to S its control remains1, so it
cannot beat the baseline, which is at most1. If k is absent from S, then
w^k equals v on BOTH S and S\{j}, so(5) gives the same baseline. The j-family
therefore yields the exact coalition value
 F(X_S,Z_S)=min{v(S),v(S\{j})+t,w^j(S)+z}.                    (6)
For S omitting j the value remains v(S).

If S contains an off-j generator, v(S\{j})=w^j(S)=1, so(6)=1. If S contains
no generator, (6)=0. In the remaining case all its contained generators
are incident to j, and(6) equals
 min{1,t,max_(R subset S)a^j_R+z}
 =max_(R subset S) min{1,t,a^j_R+z}.
The equality uses monotonicity of the truncation on a finite nonempty set.
Consequently the WHOLE game after the real report(t,z) is exactly the
max-of-contained-generators game for the demand array
 a_R(t,z)=1                           when j is absent from R,
 a_R(t,z)=min{1,t,a^j_R+z}             when j belongs to R.       (7)
For nonnegative payments, the max-generator game and generator inequalities
have exactly the same covering polyhedron. Hence every actual private
report is covered by the fixed-generator coordinate cap.

Veto owner u.
For a coalition containing u, take X_u=t and all other present coordinates1.
For any game g,
 min_A h^(g,A) dot X_S=t*g(S).
A nonveto element outside A costs1>=t*g(S); if all are inside A but u is
outside A the cost is t>=t*g(S); if all S is inside A the minimum is attained
at A=S. Coalitions without u have value0. Since w^u=v, its own branch is
t*v(S)+z. A different family k present in S has its full control1 and cannot
improve the baseline; if k is absent, w^k(S)=v(S). Thus the actual game is
exactly t*v, independent of z. Its minimum-norm cover scales by t, and the
veto's true envelope is its full coordinate R/(1+R).

This proves the caps and both aggregate conclusions hold for the TRUE all-real own-report envelope of
this explicit F at its fixed full bundles. The class admits arbitrary
chosen incident witness arrays a^j, and in particular all campaign hypercube
and ladder arrays. No additional restrictions are imposed on intermediate
real underreports; formula(7) computes them exactly.
