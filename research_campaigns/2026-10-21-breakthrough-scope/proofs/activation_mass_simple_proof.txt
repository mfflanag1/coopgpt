THE GRAPH AND ROW FAMILY
Let L>=4 be even. Set n=2L and take the graph on Z/(2L)Z with edges {i,i+1} and {i,i+L}. Write T_j=j, B_j=L+j, j=0,...,L-1. There are vertical rung edges T_j--B_j, ordinary path edges T_j--T_(j+1), B_j--B_(j+1), and twisted wrap edges T_(L-1)--B_0 and B_(L-1)--T_0. Let C={T_0,T_1,B_0,B_(L-1)} be the closed neighborhood of focal vertex0.
Let the covering rows be complements of all independent sets of cardinality L-3. Every row has cardinality k=L+3. Cyclic translation acts transitively on vertices and preserves the row family.

FOUR EXPLICIT INDEPENDENT SETS
For every even h in {2,4,...,L-2}, define four independent sets. A hole is an empty rung; at every other rung choose precisely one vertex.

A_h: holes {h-1,h,h+1}. For j<h choose T_j when j is even and B_j when j is odd. For j>h choose T_j when j is odd and B_j when j is even.
B_h: holes {1,h,L-1}. For j<h choose T_j when j is odd and B_j when j is even. For j>h choose T_j when j is even and B_j when j is odd.
C_h: holes {0,h,h+1}. At occupied rungs choose T_j for odd j and B_j for even j.
D_h: holes {0,h-1,h}. At occupied rungs choose T_j for even j and B_j for odd j.

Each set has exactly3 distinct holes and cardinality L-3. Choices alternate along every occupied path segment, so no interior path edge is selected twice, and every rung has at most one vertex. For A_h, T_0 is selected and B_(L-1) is absent, so the twisted wrap is safe. For B_h rung L-1 is empty; for C_h,D_h rung0 is empty. Hence all sets are independent.
Their intersections with C are respectively {T_0}, {B_0}, {T_1}, {B_(L-1)}. Give every set the same weight 1/[2(L-2)]. There are exactly2(L-2) sets, counted with multiplicity; duplicates, if any, may be combined. Thus the weights sum to1 and every vertex of C has marginal1/4.

EXACT MARGINAL COUNT

At rung L-1, B_(L-1) appears M times and T_(L-1) appears2M-2 times: T_(L-1) occurs in A_h and C_h except h=L-2.

At any interior rung j=2,...,L-2, the combined A/B families select each layer exactly M-1 times, and the combined C/D families also select each layer exactly M-1 times. For completeness:
* j=2r even: A has top M-r, bottom r-1; B has top r-1, bottom M-r. C chooses bottom M-1 times, D top M-1 times.
* j=2r+1 odd: A has top r-1, bottom M-r-1; B has top M-r, bottom r. C chooses top M-1 times, D bottom M-1 times.
