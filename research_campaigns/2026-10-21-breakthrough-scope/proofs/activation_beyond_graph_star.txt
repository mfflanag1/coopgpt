BEYOND GRAPH-STAR WITNESSES: CEILING AND A POINTWISE COUNTEREXAMPLE
21 October2026. Exact deductions; no aggregate H>4 claim.

1. THE GRAPH-STAR CEILING
Let a nonempty vertex-transitive simple graph have n vertices and degree D>0. If I is independent, all D|I| edges leaving I terminate outside I, whose total degree is D(n-|I|). Hence |I|<=n/2. A nonempty vertex-transitive family of complements of fixed-cardinality independent sets therefore has row cardinality k>=n/2. Its full minimum-norm convex-hull point is uniform k/n, so the associated cover has rho=n/k^2.
A uniform closed-star witness has theta=D/(D+1). Thus
 H=rho*n*theta^2=(n*theta/k)^2 <=4theta^2<4.
The hypercube family asymptotically attains the ceiling4.

A broader version blocks defective independence. Suppose a D-regular vertex-transitive graph supplies closed stars, and the complemented sets obey |I intersect N[i]|<=s whenever i belongs to I, with1<=s<=D. Each vertex of I then has at most s-1 neighbors in I. Counting edges to the complement gives
 (D-s+1)|I|<=D(n-|I|),
 so |I|/n<=D/(2D-s+1), and k/n>= (D-s+1)/(2D-s+1).
If a uniform star mixture has exactlys independent vertices in each star, its row marginal is theta=(D+1-s)/(D+1). Consequently
 n*theta/k <= (2D-s+1)/(D+1)<2,
 and H<4 again. The same argument works for directed local neighborhoods when both in-degree and out-degree are D: count outgoing internal arcs and incoming capacity of the complement. Thus replacing graph independence by a regular local cardinality cap does not escape the ceiling.

2. SELECTED-WITNESS FUNDING CONSTANT UNDER UNION/VETO
Let M be the private coordinate count after a union product, r its full squared cover norm, and ell<=r the selected local dual mass. In a symmetric witness family, the selected focal coordinate is ell*theta. Its exact value after B common-veto copies is
 ell*theta/[1+(B-1)r+ell].
The function ell/(1+(B-1)r+ell) increases with ell, so this is at most r*theta/(1+Br). The private selected-witness total divided by sqrt(BM) is therefore at most
 sqrt(Mr)*theta *sqrt(Br)/(1+Br)
 <=sqrt(H)/2<=1.
Here Mr is the unchanged base n*rho and theta is unchanged by union product, so H=Mr*theta^2. This bound applies to the exact selected witnesses, not merely their conservative lower estimates. The full-report veto coordinate is at most1 and contributes o(1) after division by sqrt(BM+1) along growing owner counts. Therefore this graph-star architecture cannot produce an asymptotic selected-witness funding coefficient above1 by union/veto amplification.
This is not an upper bound on the TRUE envelope, since additional unexamined reports may give larger coordinates.

3. UNIVERSAL POINTWISE DOUBLING IS FALSE
Proposed broader statement: for ANY0/1 row polytope, full minimum-norm point v and normalized own-row witness w satisfy w_i<=2v_i.
The following exact binary construction refutes that statement, even for a binary own-row deletion. The ratio is5.

Partition1443 coordinates into six groups F,A,B,C,D,E of sizes
 11,448,652,24,148,160.
There are four row classes numbered0,1,2,3. The number of coordinates selected from each group is given by the following table; EVERY subset of the indicated cardinality is allowed independently in each group:

 group size | class0 class1 class2 class3
 F      11  |    11     11      0      0
 A     448  |   112    448      0    112
 B     652  |   163    652    163      0
 C      24  |    12     24      0     24
 D     148  |   111      0    111    148
 E     160  |   120      0    160    120

All rows are nonempty, with respective sizes529,1135,434,404. The row family is finite, although enumerating all within-class subset choices is unnecessary. In each class, the uniform distribution over its rows has marginal equal to the displayed count divided by group size. Any vector constant on each group gives the same row potential to every row of a class.

Choose a full row mixture with class probabilities
 beta=(0,1/10,9/20,9/20),
and uniform within each used class. Its marginal vector v is constant by group with values
 (1/10,17/80,17/80,11/20,63/80,63/80).
Exact calculation gives
 ||v||^2=4961/20,
 v(R)=4961/20 for every row in ALL FOUR classes.
Thus v is the unique minimum-norm point of the full row convex hull: for every x in that hull, v.x=||v||^2, so ||x||^2>=||v||^2, with equality only at v. Equivalently, the full cover projection is z=v/(4961/20), with rho=20/4961 and full multipliers obtained by scaling beta by rho.

Fix any focal coordinate i in group F. Precisely the rows in classes0 and1 contain i. Delete class1 by setting its RHS to0; keep RHS1 in classes0,2,3. This changes only own-i rows.
Choose the retained-row mixture with class probabilities
 alpha=(1/2,0,1/4,1/4),
uniform within each used class. Its marginal vector w has group values
 (1/2,3/16,3/16,1/2,13/16,13/16).
Exact calculation gives
 t=||w||^2=1003/4,
 w(R)=1003/4 on classes0,2,3,
 w(R)=895/4 on deleted class1.
Hence q=w/t is the relaxed cover projection, with nonnegative multipliers alpha/t. Every off-i row (classes2,3) is retained and tight. All used rows are tight; stationarity and complementary slackness hold exactly. The selected dual mass is ell=4/1003 and focal cover coordinate p=2/1003, so the normalized focal coordinate is p/ell=1/2.
For every one of the11 actual focal coordinates,
 v_i=1/10, w_i=1/2, and w_i/v_i=5>2.
The full and relaxed convex-hull minimizers are unique despite possible nonuniqueness of row representations.

4. WHAT THE COUNTEREXAMPLE DOES NOT ESTABLISH
The normalized aggregate is H=rho*sum_i(theta_i)^2 and requires one witness for EVERY actual coordinate. A ratio5 on11 coordinates does not imply H>4: those11 selected contributions total only
 rho*11*(1/2)^2=55/4961.
If all other coordinates merely use their full witness theta_j=v_j, the aggregate is exactly
 H=1+rho*11*((1/2)^2-(1/10)^2)
  =1+264/24805,
 far below4. The large outside groups are essential actual coordinates, not ignorable resources. The construction is not vertex transitive and its row sizes differ. Any symmetrization or gluing proposal must account for all coordinates and its changed full norm; simple relabeling cannot turn the ratio5 into an aggregate bound violation.

5. DISCOVERY AND VERIFICATION
The counterexample arose from a four-route block model, followed by an exact0/1 lift using all fixed-cardinality subsets in each block. A linear feasibility solve supplied block sizes; all stated quantities were then recomputed with rational arithmetic. The companion activation_beyond_graph_star_check.py uses only the Python standard library and verifies the incidence counts, both convex mixtures, all class potentials, both norms, full/relaxed KKT, and the precise aggregate contribution. The proof itself does not rely on numerical optimization.

6. ANALYTIC LIMITS OF SIMPLE GLUING
The pointwise obstruction does not automatically amplify into aggregate H>4.

Direct sums do not increase H. Suppose separate row systems have full cover norms r_a and chosen normalized witness values theta_(a,i), with selected local dual masses L_(a,i)<=r_a. Put their rows on disjoint coordinate blocks without taking unions, and write R=sum_a r_a. The combined full projection is the concatenation, with squared norm R. A witness in block a keeps full projections in all other blocks, so its normalized focal value is
 theta'_(a,i)=theta_(a,i)*L_(a,i)/(R-r_a+L_(a,i)).
Since L<=r_a,
 L/(R-r_a+L)<=r_a/R.
Consequently
 H_direct <=sum_a (r_a/R)*H_a <=max_a H_a.
This argument accommodates heterogeneous blocks and arbitrary chosen dual masses.

Equal union products preserve H exactly for the transported witnesses. The union construction changes full rho to rho/t and produces t copies of each coordinate, while the focal payment and chosen dual mass are multiplied by the same factor. Hence theta is unchanged and H is unchanged. Relabeling also leaves H invariant. Thus disjoint copies, relabeling, their direct sums, and equal union products cannot promote the certified H=2279/2255 into H>4.

A common-incidence class cannot supply the missing aggregate mass by itself. Let F contain g coordinates that belong to exactly the same rows. Every full normalized convex-hull coordinate in F equals a, and every selected normalized witness coordinate in F equals theta. Write nu=||v||^2=1/rho. Let t be the witness threshold, so off-owner rows have w-potential>=t, used own rows have potential<=t, and ||w||^2<=t. Because an own row contains all of F, its w-potential is at least g*theta; hence t>=g*theta when theta>0. A full row representation puts total probability a on own rows and1-a on off rows. Thus
 v.w >=(1-a)t+a*g*theta.
By Cauchy-Schwarz and ||w||^2<=t,
 nu >=[(1-a)t+a*g*theta]^2/t.
If a<=1/2, the right side is increasing over t>=g*theta and is minimized at t=g*theta. Therefore
 nu>=g*theta.
If only F uses its improved witness and every other coordinate uses its full witness, its aggregate satisfies
 H=1+g*(theta^2-a^2)/nu <=1+theta-a^2/theta <=2.
If theta<=a the aggregate is already<=1. If a>1/2, the simpler nu>=g*a^2 yields H<=1/a^2<4. Therefore no single common-incidence class, with all other coordinates kept at their full witnesses, can violate H<=4; in particular the low-marginal focal class in the ratio5 construction cannot do so even after retuning its multiplicity.

A genuine aggregate counterexample must therefore coordinate improvements in several distinct incidence classes, or use a shared-coordinate gluing operation whose new off-owner constraints are explicitly verified. Sharing outside blocks between copies is not innocuous: another copy's formerly relaxable rows become off-owner rows for the focal copy. In the displayed counterexample, deleted class1 has w-potential895/4 below the required1003/4, so naive sharing immediately violates those newly fixed constraints. A theorem H<=4 is not proved by these observations. They isolate the unresolved aggregate problem and eliminate the straightforward uses of the pointwise ratio5 obstruction.
