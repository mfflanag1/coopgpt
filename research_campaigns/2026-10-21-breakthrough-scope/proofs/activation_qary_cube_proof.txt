Q-ARY AXIS-LINE FAMILY: UNBOUNDED NORMALIZED MASS AND L1 PARAMETER
Candidate for independent review; 21 October2026. Canonical repo untouched.
This extends the frozen ternary construction but does not modify it.

THEOREM CLAIM (subject to independent verification).
For every integer Q>=3 there is a finite transitive 0/1 row family with
full minimum-norm unit-demand cover squared norm rho, and an incident-only
relaxation witness for every owner with normalized coordinate theta_i=1,
such that J=sqrt(rho/n)*sum_i theta_i>Q-1 and H=rho*sum_i theta_i^2>(Q-1)^2.
In particular both normalized aggregate parameters are unbounded.

1. PARAMETERS AND ROW UNIVERSE.
Fix integer Q>=3 (no primality assumption). Set
 d=16Q(Q-1)(Q+2), h=8Q(Q-1), x=h/d=1/[2(Q+2)],
 n=Q^d, M=Q^(d-1), m=n-(Q-1)d-1,
 mu=(Q-2+x)/(Q-1), delta=1/[4Q(Q-1)], beta0=mu+delta,
 K=floor(beta0*m), beta=K/m, s=(Q-2)d+h+K, k=n-s.
Vertices are (Z/QZ)^d. Hyperedges are complete axis-parallel Q-point lines.
Independent sets contain no entire line. The FULL row family consists of
complements of ALL independent s-sets. Translation acts transitively on
vertices and preserves this family. We construct one such set distribution,
proving in particular that the row family is nonempty.

At focal0 the neighbors are a e_j, 1<=a<=Q-1, 1<=j<=d. We construct
independent s-sets with focal absent, exactly (Q-2)d+h selected neighbors,
each neighbor marginal mu, exactly K selected outside vertices, and each
outside vertex marginal beta. Their complement mixture w has focal1,
neighbor entries a0=1-mu=(1-x)/(Q-1), outside b=1-beta.

2. AVAILABLE SETS AND LOCAL REPAIRS.
Color a vertex by its coordinate sum modulo Q; every axis line has all Q
colors. Choose excluded color c uniformly from0,...,Q-1.
If c=0, start with all nonzero colors and retain a uniformly selected
((Q-2)d+h)-subset of the (Q-1)d neighbors. Keep all other available vertices.
If c!=0, start with all colors except c, delete focal0, choose a uniform
h-subset S of coordinate indices, and add c e_j for j in S. For each added
c e_j and every l!=j independently choose A uniformly from{1,...,Q-1} and
delete c e_j+A e_l. Take the union of all these deletions.

Each resulting available set is independent. The original Q-1 colors
contain no full line. No axis line can contain two added vertices c e_j.
The axis line through c e_j in direction j loses0; in direction l!=j the
construction deletes one of its other Q-1 vertices. Thus every possible
newly completed line is broken. Each set excludes0 and contains exactly
(Q-2)d+h neighbors; no repair deletion is a neighbor.
For a fixed neighbor a e_j, conditional inclusion probabilities are mu
in phase0, x in phasea, and1 in each of the other Q-2 phases. Their mean
(mu+x+Q-2)/Q equals mu by the definition of mu.

3. EXCEPTIONAL MARGINALS.
Let E be all Hamming-weight2 vertices, e=(Q-1)^2*binomial(d,2).
For u=a e_j+b e_l (j!=l,a,b!=0), the original color-phase availability is
(Q-1)/Q. It can be deleted only through an added a e_j in phasea or an
added b e_l in phaseb. If a!=b, these are distinct phases, giving exact
availability
 B=(Q-1)/Q-2x/[Q(Q-1)].
If a=b, their union deletion probability is at most the sum of the two
probabilities, so availability is at least B (the duplicate correction
is positive). This argument also covers composite Q and a+b=0: neither
a nor b equals a+b, since both coefficients are nonzero.
A direct computation gives
 B-mu=1/[2Q(Q-1)]=2delta,
so every exceptional vertex has availability P_u>=B=beta0+delta>beta.
Each P_u is rational because the construction is finite rational.
Conditionally on availability, retain u with probability beta/P_u, using
independent thinning coins. Then every exceptional vertex has marginal
exactly beta. Independence and all neighbor counts are preserved. Let P
be the number retained, so0<=P<=e and E[P]=beta e.

4. FAR CLASSES AND FINITE TRANSPORT.
Far vertices have Hamming weight>=3. Let F_c be their color classes. Their
sizes are
 f0=M-1-(Q-1)*binomial(d,2),
 fc=M-d-(Q-2)*binomial(d,2) for c!=0.
Indeed there are Q-1 nonzero ordered pairs summing to0 and Q-2 summing to
a fixed nonzero color, for each chosen coordinate pair. Thus sum f_c=m-e.
In phase c every far vertex of color !=c is available and none of color c
is. A state omega records all local and exceptional-thinning choices;
its probability p_omega is rational. We need retain exactly
 T_omega=K-P_omega
far vertices, with each far vertex's unconditional marginal beta.
E T=K-beta e=beta*sum f_c.

We use the following elementary finite rational transport lemma. For
states with probabilities p_omega, available sets A_omega, integer target
sizes T_omega, and common desired marginal beta, such a selection exists
if expected target size=beta|F| and every U subset F satisfies
 beta|U| <= sum_omega p_omega min(T_omega,|U intersect A_omega|).
Proof: source->state capacities p_omega T_omega, state->available-vertex
capacities p_omega, vertex->sink capacities beta. Maxflow/mincut gives
precisely these inequalities (optimize each state's side of a fixed cut).
A rational flow saturates all source and sink arcs. Divide state-vertex
flows by p_omega to get conditional marginals in[0,1] of integer sum T.
Any such rational vector is a rational mixture of fixed-cardinality
indicators: shift between two fractional coordinates in both directions
until a coordinate hits0 or1, express the original as a convex mixture,
and recurse. Each endpoint has fewer fractional coordinates. This gives
a finite rational distribution of T-subsets of A_omega.

For our states the cut deficit depends only on u_c=|U intersect F_c|.
It is concave on the box0<=u_c<=f_c: every summand is the minimum of a
constant and a linear function, minus a linear desired demand. Therefore
it suffices to check box corners, i.e. unions of entire color classes.
We will prove below these three uniform bounds:
 (i) K-e >=(Q-2)M;
 (ii) K <= sum_{c!=a} f_c for every a;
 (iii) K-e >=(Q beta-Q+2)*sum_{c!=a} f_c for every a.
For a corner containing r<=Q-2 classes, (i) implies T is at least every
available count in that union, so its RHS is exactly (Q-1)/Q times its
size, >=beta times its size. For r=Q-1, let a be the omitted color and
write f_R for the union size. In each phase c in R the available portion
has at most(Q-2)M vertices, so (i) makes the minimum its available count.
In phasea, (ii) makes the minimum T. Thus the cut RHS is
 ((Q-2)/Q)*f_R + E[T*1_{phase=a}]
 >=((Q-2)*f_R+K-e)/Q >=beta*f_R by(iii).
For all Q classes, (ii) gives RHS=E T=beta sum f_c, equality. The empty
cut is trivial. All cuts hold, so the transport lemma completes the
fixed-size independent-set distribution.

5. UNIFORM SCALAR BOUNDS VALID FOR ALL Q>=3.
First d>=480. For every integer D>=20, 3^(D-5)>100D^2: check D20 and use
3(D/(D+1))^2>1 for induction. Hence
 M=Q^4*Q^(d-5)>=Q^4*3^(d-5)>100Q^4 d^2.
Every far-class size is positive and in[M-Qd^2,M]. Also
 e+Qd+2 <=Q^2d^2,
so flooring yields
 K-e >=beta0*Q*M-Q^2d^2,       K<=beta0*Q*M.
Put gamma=(Q-1)/Q-beta0. Since B=beta0+delta,
 gamma=delta+2x/[Q(Q-1)]>delta>=1/(4Q^2).
Moreover b0=1-beta0<1/(Q-1)<=3/(2Q), hence
 Q beta0-Q+2=2-Q b0>1/2.
Now(i) follows from
 K-e-(Q-2)M >=(Q beta0-Q+2)M-Q^2d^2>0.
For(ii), sum_{c!=a}f_c>=(Q-1)(M-Qd^2), so its excess over K is at least
 Q gamma M-Q(Q-1)d^2 > M/(4Q)-Q^2d^2>0.
For(iii), the coefficient Q beta-Q+2 is positive; beta>=beta0-1/m>mu
(as verified below), and Qmu-Q+2>0. Its product with a Q-1-class union is
at most (Q beta0-Q+2)(Q-1)M. Thus the excess of K-e is at least
 (Q-2)(Q-1-Q beta0)M-Q^2d^2
 =(Q-2)Q gamma M-Q^2d^2 >=M/12-Q^2d^2>0.
(The weak1/12 bound uses(Q-2)/(4Q)>=1/12.) These prove all cuts.

6. OFF-OWNER KKT AND FULL PROJECTION.
The enormous M bound gives m>h and m>1/delta. Therefore beta>=beta0-1/m
>mu, so a0>b. Every supported row contains focal0, exactly d-h neighbors,
and k-1-d+h=m-K outside vertices. Its potential is the common value
 t=1+(d-h)a0+(m-K)b
  =1+(Q-1)d*a0^2+m*b^2=||w||^2>0.
Every off-focal row complements an independent set containing0. Each of
the d axis lines through0 must omit at least one of its Q-1 neighbors, so
such a row has at least d neighbors. Its potential is at least
 d*a0+(k-d)*b.
This minus t is h(a0-b)-(1-b)=h(beta-mu)-beta. Because h delta=2,
 h(beta-mu)-beta >=2-h/m-beta0>0
using m>h and beta0<1. Thus ALL off rows are feasible at threshold t.

Set lambda=alpha/t and q_local=w/t, where alpha is the finite row-mixture
constructed above. Keep every off-row RHS1. Set own-row RHS1 on support
of alpha and0 on other own rows. Primal feasibility, stationarity
q_local=A^T lambda, and complementary slackness hold exactly. This is the
unique minimum-norm cover for an incident-only relaxation. Total dual
mass ell=1/t and focal coordinate q_local,0=1/t give theta0=1.

Since the full row universe is nonempty, all its rows have size k, and
translation acts transitively, the uniform point v=(k/n)1 lies in its
convex hull. Every point in that hull has coordinate sum k, so Cauchy-
Schwarz proves v is the unique minnorm point. Therefore the full cover
is z=(1/k)1 and rho=n/k^2. Translate the focal witness to each owner.

7. UNBOUNDED AGGREGATES.
The flooring bound and mu(Q-1)d=(Q-2)d+h give
 k<=b0*n+delta*(Q-1)d+beta0+1
   <b0*n+d/(4Q)+2.
But1/(Q-1)-b0=x/(Q-1)+delta>delta, and the M bound ensures
 delta*n>d/(4Q)+2.
Therefore0<k<n/(Q-1), so
 J=sqrt(rho/n)*n=n/k>Q-1,
 H=rho*n=(n/k)^2>(Q-1)^2.
Positivity of k follows because beta<1 and the same bounds give s<n;
alternatively every constructed independent set omits at least one vertex
per axis line and hence has size<=n(Q-1)/Q<n.

SCOPE. This is a finite exact family of normalized incident-relaxation
witnesses, with actual translation symmetry on the full row universe.
Funding consequences require the separately audited production realization
and amplification theorem. If those apply, for each fixed Q the amplified
funding coefficient approaches J/2>(Q-1)/2 along arbitrarily large owner
counts. Varying Q then rules out any universal constant times sqrt(N)
funding bound for this particular true-envelope architecture. It does not
prove a corresponding lower bound for arbitrary mechanisms.
