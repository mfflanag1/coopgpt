INDEPENDENT TERNARY-CUBE AUDIT
21 October 2026. Source: activation_ternary_cube_candidate.txt and its scalar
completion. This audit does not import the author's checker or certificates.

VERDICT. The local construction and the finite transport existence proof
are valid. The independent exact checker establishes all required scalar
inequalities at d=10000. Consequently this is a finite rational normalized
mass example with theta_i=1 for every owner, J>5/2 and H>25/4. Claims about
funding additionally invoke the separately audited common-veto realization.

1. LOCAL INDEPENDENCE, INCLUDING MULTIPLE ADDITIONS.
Every axis line meets every sum-color exactly once. Thus omitting one color
starts with an independent set. In phase1, two distinct added vertices e_j
and e_l differ in two coordinates. They cannot lie on the same axis line.
Any line newly completed by additions therefore contains precisely one
added e_j. In direction j this line contains0, which was removed. In each
direction l!=j the other points are e_j+e_l and e_j+2e_l; the repair removes
one. All repair targets are originally available weight2 points. Repeated
deletions are harmless. Negation proves the phase2 assertion. All later
steps only delete available vertices, so no repaired line is restored.

Every phase excludes0 and keeps precisely d+h neighbors. For a fixed
neighbor the three inclusion probabilities are3/5,1/5,1 in some order,
so the unconditional marginal is3/5. Repairs never affect neighbors.

2. EXCEPTIONAL AVAILABILITY AND CORRELATION.
For a fixed mixed weight2 point e_j+2e_l, only the repair indexed(j,l)
can remove it in phase1. This has probability (h/d)r. Negation exchanges
j,l in phase2 and gives the same probability. The point is absent in
phase0. Hence Pmix=(2/3)(1-(h/d)r).

A same-sign point e_j+e_l is present in phase0, absent in one other phase,
and exposed to the two repairs(j,l),(l,j) in the remaining phase. Their
joint index-selection probability is h(h-1)/(d(d-1)), not (h/d)^2;
their repair coins are independent. Inclusion-exclusion therefore gives
Psame=2/3-[2(h/d)(1-r)-h(h-1)(1-r)^2/(d(d-1))]/3.
Both exceed601/1000 at d10000. Independent conditional thinning by
beta/Pmix or beta/Psame is a valid finite rational procedure and yields
every exceptional marginal beta. The retained count P can correlate
arbitrarily with the phase and with neighbor selection; nothing below
assumes otherwise. Its only required properties are0<=P<=e and EP=e beta.

3. FAR-SET TRANSPORT, ALL CUTS AND PRESERVED STATE PROBABILITIES.
Weight>=3 points never occur in a repair or exceptional thinning. In a
state of phase c their available set is exactly the two entire far color
classes other than c. Class sizes are
f0=3^(d-1)-1-d(d-1), f1=f2=3^(d-1)-d-d(d-1)/2.
They sum to m-e. With Q=K-P, EQ=beta(m-e). Exact scalar inequalities give
Q>=K-e>=max f_j>0 and Q<=K<=every pair sum.

Here is an independent derivation of the transport criterion. Give the
source-to-state arc capacity p_omega Q_omega, every available state-to-point
arc capacity p_omega, and each point-to-sink arc capacity beta. Let U be
the points on the sink side of a cut. For each state the smaller possible
contribution is p_omega min(Q_omega,|U intersect A_omega|). The remaining
point arcs cost beta(|F|-|U|). Thus every cut has capacity at least
beta|F| exactly when the displayed all-subsets inequalities hold. Total
source and sink capacities equal beta|F|; rational maxflow therefore
saturates both. States of probability0 can be discarded.

Divide each state-to-point flow by its positive p_omega. The resulting
conditional vector has coordinates in[0,1], integer sum Q, and zero entries
outside the available set. A vector with integer sum cannot have exactly
one fractional coordinate. Given two, shift mass between them in both
directions until an endpoint hits0 or1. The original vector is a rational
convex combination of the two endpoints, each with fewer fractional
coordinates. Finite recursion gives a rational mixture of Q-subsets.
Crucially, this does not reweight the states: their probabilities remain
p_omega. It changes only which available far points are retained.

For a subset U let u_j count its points in far color j. The Hall deficit
sum p_omega min(Q_omega,sum_{j!=phase}u_j)-beta sum u_j is concave on the
box0<=u_j<=f_j. Each point of this box is a convex combination of its eight
corners; concavity therefore makes the corner inequalities sufficient.
Empty and full corners give equality. A single-class corner gives
2f_j/3>=beta f_j. For two classes r,s, the exact contribution is
(f_r+f_s)/3+E[Q 1_{phase=third}]. The latter expectation is at least
(K-e)/3 pointwise, regardless of phase dependence. Therefore
K-e >= (3beta-1)(f_r+f_s) proves every two-class corner. The checker verifies
these inequalities exactly. This establishes every cut, not only vertex
availability. No exponentially large transport is numerically executed:
the finite rational maxflow theorem plus these inequalities proves it exists.

4. KKT AND FULL PROJECTION.
The constructed sets all exclude0 and contain d+h neighbors and K outside
points. Each is independent and has s=d+h+K elements. Their complement
mixture w has coordinates1,2/5,1-beta on the focal, neighbor, outside types.
Every used row has potential
t=1+(d-h)(2/5)+(m-K)(1-beta)=1+2d(2/5)^2+m(1-beta)^2=||w||^2.
Every off-focal row complements an independent set containing0 and hence
contains at least one member of each neighbor pair. Since2/5>1-beta, its
potential is at least d(2/5)+(k-d)(1-beta); the excess over t is exactly
h(beta-3/5)-beta>0. This uses the complete row universe, not a restricted
collection of checked rows.

If alpha is the finite rational complement distribution, q=w/t and
lambda=alpha/t satisfy stationarity. Give every supported own row RHS1,
every other own row RHS0, and every off row RHS1. Feasibility and
complementarity now hold exactly; q is the unique minimum-norm cover.
The multiplier mass and focal coordinate are both1/t, so theta0=1.
Translation preserves the full independent-set universe and transfers
the witness to every owner. Since all rows have size k and any one row
has a uniform translation average, their hull's minimum norm is k²/n.
Equivalently the full unit cover is(1/k)1 with rho=n/k². The checker gives
k<2n/5, so J=n/k>5/2 and H=n²/k²>25/4.
