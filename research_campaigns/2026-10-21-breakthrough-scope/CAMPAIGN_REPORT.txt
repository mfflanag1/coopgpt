MAIN OUTCOME
The own-report monotone envelope of the minimum-Euclidean-norm coalition
cover cannot have a universal C sqrt(N) funding guarantee, for ANY fixed
constant C. We constructed a finite, fixed-owner, all-real-report production
family proving this. The result closes a specific candidate route to a
better general payment rule. It does not prove an impossibility for all
mechanisms or establish a field-level breakthrough.

The sharper explicit consequence is, along a sequence of finite owner counts,

    sum_i M_i(b) > (1/40) sqrt(N) (log N/log log N)^(1/3) T.

The squared-envelope aggregate is also unbounded relative to T^2, and
envelope funding can exceed the raw full-profile projection's total by
an arbitrarily large factor. These are the final frontiers for this run.
The earlier diagonal counterexamples and hypercube coefficient1 result
remain correct but are superseded as universal obstructions.

THE ECONOMIC AND MATHEMATICAL CONTRACT
There are N actual owners, each with a fixed two-coordinate resource bundle.
The production function is one finite minimum of nonnegative linear forms,
defined on all nonnegative real resource vectors. Its coefficients can all
be0 or1. The induced coalition value is production from that coalition's
reported resources. Full output in the examples is T=1.

The raw payment q minimizes sum_i x_i^2 over nonnegative vectors covering
every coalition. There is no efficiency equality. The proposed monotone
rule sets M_i to the supremum of q_i over all feasible underreports by i,
holding every other owner fixed. It covers coalitions and is own-report
monotone by construction. That incentive statement is payment-only and
individual. It is not coalition-proofness, full outside-option protection,
private-cost truthfulness, or a different ownership assignment.

The proof uses particular endpoint reports to lower-bound the TRUE supremum.
It does not replace the report domain by endpoints. Every other real
underreport remains admissible and can only increase the lower bound.

HOW THE FINAL CONSTRUCTION WORKS
1. Use the vertices of a q-ary cube and hyperedges consisting of complete
   axis-parallel lines. Cover rows are complements of independent sets
   of one fixed size. Translation gives a uniform full projection.
2. For each focal owner, construct a rational distribution of independent
   sets that avoids the focal owner while controlling neighbor and outside
   marginals. Local repairs break every newly completed line.
3. Thin the exceptional weight-two points. A finite rational transport
   argument selects the far points at an exact set size. Concavity reduces
   ALL transport cuts to unions of color classes; the proof checks every
   such case uniformly for every integer alphabet size Q>=3.
4. The resulting row mixture gives exact KKT multipliers for deleting only
   some focal-incident rows. Every off-owner row remains covered, including
   those outside the distribution's support. The focal coordinate equals
   the total multiplier mass. Symmetry supplies a witness for every owner.
5. Add a common veto to balanced disjoint copies. One explicit min-linear
   function with owner-specific control resources realizes all selected
   unilateral comparisons simultaneously, with fixed owners and bundles.
6. A finite inequality gives funding/sqrt(N)>(Q-1)/8. The owner-count
   growth converts this into the displayed logarithmic refinement.

The proof and all quantifiers are consolidated in
proofs/FINAL_ENVELOPE_OBSTRUCTION.txt. It does not need the earlier graph,
ternary, or union-product constructions as dependencies.

FINAL SHARP REFINEMENT WITHIN THE CONSTRUCTION CLASS

we also established a sharp asymptotic funding coefficient Q/2. For the
specified common-veto direct-production class at its designated full bundles,

    sum_i M_i <= [1+sqrt(1+Q^2(N-1))]/2,
    sum_i M_i^2 <= Q^4/[4(Q^2-1)]                 (T=1).
