EXACT FINITE MILP FOR THE CONTINUOUS LOCAL-RELAXATION ENVELOPE
2026-10-17. Mathematical formulation; numerical implementation is discovery only.

Given nonempty 0/1 coalition rows A_j and demands b_j>=0, let
 P={q>=0:Aq>=b}, T=min_{q in P}sum_i q_i>0.
Fix owner i. Retain every row with A_ji=0. For every row containing i, allow
its right-hand side to be any value in[0,b_j]. We seek the maximum i-coordinate
of the minimum-Euclidean-norm projection of zero onto any resulting polyhedron.

A vector q is such a projection iff there are lambda_j>=0 satisfying
 q=A^T lambda,
 A_j q>=b_j for off-i rows,
 lambda_j>0 => A_j q=b_j for off-i rows,
 lambda_j>0 => A_j q<=b_j for own-i rows.
Necessity is the projection's KKT system. For sufficiency, set every active
own-i row's relaxed RHS to A_j q and every inactive own-i row's RHS to0.
Then q is feasible and q=A^T lambda with complementary slackness. The strictly
convex objective gives the unique projection. Nonnegativity causes no missing
normal term: q=A^T lambda>=0; at a zero coordinate any nonnegativity multiplier
must be zero because A and lambda are nonnegative.

Every such projection has norm at most T, since any full L1-minimizer is still
feasible and has Euclidean norm at most T. Hence q_i<=T, and every lambda_j<=T
because a nonempty row has a member whose q-coordinate is at least lambda_j.
Also b_j<=T. With binary z_j, the equivalent MILP is
 maximize q_i,
 q=A^T lambda, lambda>=0, lambda_j<=T z_j,
 A_j q>=b_j for off-i rows,
 A_j q <= b_j+(|A_j|T-b_j)(1-z_j) for every row,
 z_j in{0,1}.
The displayed big-M is sufficient because A_j q<=|A_j|T. Eliminating q gives
the implementation in projection_envelope_milp.py. The binary flag may equal1
when lambda=0; this only adds a harmless restriction and does not exclude any
true optimum because flags can instead be zero.

The balanced-closure and simultaneous-realization lemmas make this MILP's
coordinate maxima attainable in the true own-report envelope of a fixed-owner
production instance. Each coordinate may use a different relaxed game, all
jointly embedded by the star realization. Thus a rational KKT violation of
sum_i M_i^2<=T^2 or sum_i M_i<=sqrt(n)T would be a genuine architecture
counterexample, not merely a relaxation artifact.
