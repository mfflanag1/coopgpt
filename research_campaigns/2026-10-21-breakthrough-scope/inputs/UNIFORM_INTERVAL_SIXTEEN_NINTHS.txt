It does not violate the required threshold2 and does not refute the original

For any integer k>=3, put n=2k-1 and index owners and rows modulo n.
Row r is the k-owner cyclic interval S_r={r,...,r+k-1}, with demand1.
The full quadratic projection is z_i=1/k, since every column has k incidences
and the constant multipliers lambda_r=1/k^2 give stationarity. Thus
 rho=||z||^2=n/k^2.

Take focal owner0 and D=3k+2. Define a relaxed witness q by

Give rows r=0,1,...,k demand1. Each contains exactly two of the distinguished
three owners, and hence has q(S_r)=1. Give rows r=k+1,...,2k-2 demand(3k+1)/D;
each contains exactly owner0 among the distinguished three. All those changed
rows contain owner0. In particular all rows EXCLUDING owner0 stay full.

The exact nonnegative KKT multipliers are

Equivalently the relaxed rows may be deleted completely: they all have zero

above simply makes every row active or slack with an explicit exact value.
The witness total mass is L=6/D and its focal value is p=4/D. Rotate this
construction for every owner.
