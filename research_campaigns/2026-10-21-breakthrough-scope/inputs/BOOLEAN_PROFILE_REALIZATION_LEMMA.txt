EXACT REALIZATION OF LOCAL MONOTONE TOTALLY BALANCED REPORT ARRAYS
2026-10-17. Structural lemma for original individual underreport incentives.
Status: proposed proof; priority/novelty not established. The ordinary
single-game totally-balanced/market-game representation is established material.
The contribution proposed here is the simultaneous fixed-owner report embedding.

STATEMENT.
Let N be a fixed n-owner set. For every U subset N, let v^U:2^N -> R_+ be a
monotone normalized coalition game. Assume:
 (L) Locality: v^U(S)=v^(U intersect S)(S).
 (M) Report monotonicity: U subset V implies v^U(S)<=v^V(S).
 (B) Every subgame of every v^U has a nonnegative efficient core.
Then there is ONE finite nonnegative min-linear production function F on 2n
resources, and two nested reports for each SAME actual owner i,
 b_i^low=e_i, b_i^high=e_i+e_(n+i),
such that for every profile U and coalition S,
 F(sum_{i in S} b_i^(1_{i in U}))=v^U(S).
There are at most 2^n(2^n-1) supporting forms. Rational input games admit
rational forms. All-profile production and all-profile incentive requirements
remain those of the original model; the finite array only specifies profiles
used in a lower-bound certificate.

Conversely, every finite two-report snapshot with nested reports in the
original production model satisfies (L),(M),(B). Thus these three conditions
characterize exactly the coalition-value arrays possible on Boolean report
domains, without an additional hidden realization restriction.

PROOF.
Let H=max_U v^U(N). H=0 is trivial. For every U and nonempty A subset N choose
a nonnegative efficient core z^(U,A) of the subgame on A. Define a vector p^(U,A)
on the n base resources by p_i=z_i^(U,A) for i in A, and p_i=H for i outside A.
For every coalition S, p^(U,A)(S)>=v^U(S): when S subset A this is its core
inequality, and otherwise one outside-A coordinate alone contributes H.
Moreover p^(U,A)(A)=v^U(A). Thus
 v^U(S)=min_{nonempty A} p^(U,A)(S), including S=empty with value 0.
This is the standard finite supporting-form representation of a totally
balanced nonnegative monotone game.

On base coordinates x and control coordinates z define
 F(x,z)=min_{U,A nonempty} [p^(U,A) dot x + H sum_{i notin U} z_i].
At a reported coalition (V,S), the form indexed by (U,A) evaluates to
 p^(U,A)(S)+H |(V intersect S) minus U|.
If (V intersect S) is not a subset of U, this is at least H>=v^V(S).
Otherwise it is at least v^U(S)>=v^(V intersect S)(S)=v^V(S), by (M),(L).
Choosing U=V and A=S attains equality for nonempty S. Empty S is automatic.
All coefficients are finite and nonnegative, so F is monotone, concave,
nonnegative and positively homogeneous on all R_+^(2n), not only the grid.
The owner identities and their nested reports are fixed throughout.

Necessity of (L),(M) is immediate. For (B), fix a subgame A with aggregate
bundle c=sum_{i in A}b_i. If c=0 its value and every subcoalition value are0.
Otherwise c lies in the relative interior of the coordinate face determined
by its positive coordinates. The restriction of the finite concave function F
to this face has a finite supergradient p at c. Monotonicity gives p>=0 on the
face, and homogeneity, applied to positive scalar multiples of c, gives
p dot c=F(c). Its supporting inequality extends to boundary sub-bundles z on
the same face: apply it to z+epsilon c in the relative interior, use
F(z)<=F(z+epsilon c), and let epsilon decrease to0. Thus F(z)<=p dot z.
Every bundle of a subcoalition of A lies on that face, so z_i=p dot b_i is a
nonnegative efficient core on A. For finite min-linear F one can simply pick
a minimizing form at c. The constructed F has no boundary issue.
Rational cores exist because the finitely many defining inequalities and
objective equality have rational coefficients and right-hand sides.

STAR-PROFILE COROLLARY (USEFUL FOR THE TRUE MINIMUM-NORM ENVELOPE).
Suppose only a full game v and one relaxed game w^i per owner are given, each
totally balanced and monotone, with w^i<=v and w^i(S)=v(S) whenever i notin S.
No compatibility among different w^i is required. Represent v and all w^i by
base forms as above. Keep the v forms unchanged; add H z_i to every w^i form.
At the all-high profile the game is v. When ONLY owner i drops z_i, the game
is exactly w^i. For j!=i, a w^j form is at least H if j lies in the coalition,
and otherwise already majorizes v on that coalition. This proves the claim.
Consequently any such star of abstract minimum-norm core-cover relaxations
can be achieved by actual own underreports in one fixed production problem.
In particular, to refute a universal bound on the true own-report envelope,
it suffices to find a totally balanced full game v and totally balanced local
relaxations w^i whose projection diagonal already violates that bound. The
remaining realization question is solved by this corollary; arbitrary
row-deletions can be repaired by balanced closure without changing the cover
polyhedron; see BALANCED_CLOSURE_AND_ROW_RELAXATION.txt for the precise lemma.

LOWER-BOUND CONSEQUENCE.
Any exact finite report-transition LP dual on an array satisfying (L),(M),(B)
is automatically an original-domain fixed-owner production lower bound, using
n owners and dimension 2n. All its nonnegative payment, coverage, individual
monotonicity, and budget rows are preserved exactly. Additional profiles in
the full real domain cannot weaken the lower bound. This does not itself
produce an improved numerical bound, but gives a complete and finite model
for attacking the unrestricted owner/dimension gap.

EXTENSION TO FINITE CHAINS.
For owner i with levels 0,...,L_i, use one base resource plus L_i threshold
controls, and let report level a_i activate controls 1,...,a_i. For every
index profile u, add H times the sum of controls above u_i to its base forms.
The same proof applies to any local coordinatewise monotone totally balanced
array. Dimension is n+sum_i L_i; owner count remains exactly n. This extension
is recorded as a direct corollary, not as a separate conceptual result.

SCOPE.
The lemma preserves INDIVIDUAL coordinatewise underreport comparisons. It does
not convert a stronger TCWM lower bound into an individual-IC lower bound.
It also does not make arbitrary monotone game arrays admissible: efficient
nonnegative cores in every subgame are essential for the base representation.
No priority claim is made pending comparison to original market-game and
parametric realization sources.
