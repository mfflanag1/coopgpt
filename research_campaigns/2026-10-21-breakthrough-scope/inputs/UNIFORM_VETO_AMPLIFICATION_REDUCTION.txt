A FINITE AMPLIFICATION TEST FOR THE GENERAL DIAGONAL CONJECTURE
2026-10-17. Exact structural reduction; it neither proves nor refutes the conjecture.

BASE SYSTEM.
Let A have finitely many nonempty 0/1 rows on m coordinates, all with demand1.
Let z be its minimum-norm cover and rho=||z||^2>0. For each owner i choose
ANY incident-row downward relaxation 0<=b^i<=1, keeping off-i rows exactly1.
Let q^i be its minimum-norm cover, with nonnegative KKT multipliers lambda^i,
and let p_i=q^i_i, L_i=sum lambda^i. Put E=sum_i p_i^2.
No rank-two or own-monotonicity hypothesis is imposed.

UNIFORM DUAL-MASS LEMMA.
For every such relaxation, ||q^i||^2=sum lambda^i_S b^i_S<=L_i.
The same multipliers are feasible for the full quadratic dual, whose value
is 2L_i-||q^i||^2. Therefore
                  0<=L_i<=2L_i-||q^i||^2<=rho.             (1)
This holds for any full-row relaxation, not only own-row relaxation.

THE REDUCTION.
If E>2rho, then there is an explicit finite fixed-generator cover system
with minimum l1 value T=1 whose actual own-row envelope M violates
                         sum_j M_j^2<=T^2.                 (2)
It uses K copies of the m base coordinates plus one common veto coordinate,
where ANY integer K satisfying K(E-2rho)>1 is sufficient. Conversely, the
universal diagonal conjecture implies, on every uniform-demand base system,
                         sum_i M_i(base)^2<=2rho.           (3)
The constant2 in (3) is a NECESSARY bound obtained by this reduction; no
claim is made that it is sharp or sufficient for the full conjecture.

CONSTRUCTION AND EXACT KKT PROOF.
Create disjoint coordinate blocks x^1,...,x^K and a common coordinate u.
For every original row S in every block k impose
                         u+x^k(S)>=1.
The full minimum l1 value is1: setting u=1 is feasible, and any one row
requires total payment at least1. The full quadratic projection has
 u_full=K*rho/(1+K*rho), x^k=z/(1+K*rho).
It follows in particular that the veto's own envelope is at least u_full.

For owner i in block k define
 H_i=(K-1)rho+L_i,  alpha_i=1/(1+H_i), u_i=1-alpha_i.
In block k replace each incident row demand by
                         u_i+alpha_i*b^i_S;
all other row demands remain1. These demands lie in[0,1], and off-i rows
are exactly1. The proposed projection is
 u=u_i,  x^k=alpha_i*q^i,  x^ell=alpha_i*z for ell!=k.
Its multipliers are alpha_i*lambda^i in block k, and alpha_i*lambda(full)
in each other block. Base-coordinate stationarity is immediate. Veto
stationarity is
 alpha_i*((K-1)rho+L_i)=u_i.
Feasibility and complementary slackness follow by scaling the corresponding
base systems. Thus this is the unique minimum-norm projection. In particular,
                    M_(k,i)>=p_i/[1+(K-1)rho+L_i].
Using (1), all denominators are at most1+K*rho. Therefore
 sum_j M_j^2 >= [K^2*rho^2+K*E]/(1+K*rho)^2
              =1+[K*(E-2rho)-1]/(1+K*rho)^2.               (4)
The stated condition on K proves a strict counterexample. If only suprema
rather than attaining witnesses are available in the base, choose finite
approximating witnesses first. The finitely many coordinates allow E>2rho
whenever the base supremum violates (3).

FIXED-OWNER PRODUCTION TRANSFER AND SCOPE.
The construction has exactly Km+1 fixed owners throughout its witness star.
Balanced-close each coalition demand array, which preserves every cover
polyhedron and all off-owner games, then apply the simultaneous Boolean
realization in BALANCED_CLOSURE_AND_ROW_RELAXATION.txt and
BOOLEAN_PROFILE_REALIZATION_LEMMA.txt. This produces one monotone concave
homogeneous production function with fixed owner bundles and own control
underreports realizing all the displayed projection witnesses. It does not
change owners between report profiles. Additional coalition rows produced
by the closure have exactly the same polyhedra at those profiles.

This gives a smaller, normalized falsification target for the still-open
original envelope conjecture: find a uniform unit-demand base whose own-row
envelope has squared norm greater than twice its raw squared norm. It avoids
endpoint assumptions and the already false efficient-core support and
max-coordinate inequalities. It does NOT show that such a base exists.
The common-veto final system also admits an efficient mechanism; a failure
would reject this projection-envelope architecture, not prove an unrestricted
incentive lower bound.

A STRONGER NECESSARY TEST VIA A UNION-OF-ROWS PRODUCT.
Retain the same base witnesses and choose ones with L_i>0 (a zero witness
can simply be replaced by the full witness). Form K disjoint base blocks,
but this time put one generator for EACH union S_1 union ... union S_K,
where S_k is a base row in block k; all demands are1. Call this the union
product. Its full projection is z/K in every block, and rho_K=rho/K.
A full KKT multiplier on a row tuple is
       (product_k lambda_(S_k))/(K*rho^(K-1)).

For focal owner i in block k put
 alpha_i=rho/[rho+(K-1)L_i],  beta_i=L_i/[rho+(K-1)L_i].
Use alpha_i*q^i in its block, beta_i*z in every other block. Give a row tuple
right side alpha_i*b^i_(S_k)+(K-1)*beta_i. These are at most1, and rows not
containing the focal owner keep demand1. KKT multipliers are
 (alpha_i/rho^(K-1))*lambda^i_(S_k)*product_(ell!=k)lambda_(S_ell).
Summing over other blocks gives the asserted focal stationarity; summing
for another block gives beta_i*z. Complementarity and feasibility follow
from the base systems. Hence this is an exact own-row projection witness.
Its diagonal payment is alpha_i*p_i, and its total dual mass is alpha_i*L_i.

The squared diagonal witnesses divided by the full squared norm satisfy
 E_K/rho_K = K^2*rho*sum_i p_i^2/[rho+(K-1)L_i]^2
            increases to rho*sum_i (p_i/L_i)^2 as K tends to infinity.
Monotonicity uses L_i<=rho from(1). Consequently, if
                         rho*sum_i (p_i/L_i)^2>2,           (5)
then some FINITE union product has E_K>2rho_K, and the common-veto construction
above produces an exact counterexample to the original diagonal conjecture.
The universal conjecture therefore implies (5) with <=2 for EVERY chosen
collection of uniform-base KKT witnesses. This is a stronger necessary test
than(3). The ratio p_i/L_i is the fraction of witness dual mass placed on
rows containing i. Nonunique multipliers cause no issue: the construction
uses the particular chosen nonnegative KKT multipliers explicitly.

This reduction is valid for all finite base hypergraphs, with arbitrary
arity and owner counts, and requires no transitivity or equal row lengths.
The generated number of rows can grow exponentially with K; no efficiency
claim is made. It remains a structural falsification reduction, not a found
counterexample and not a new general funding bound.
