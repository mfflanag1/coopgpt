A PROVED AGGREGATE DIAGONAL BOUND UNDER ONE UNIFORM VETO EXTENSION
2026-10-17. Structural progress on the minimum-norm own-envelope conjecture.

STATEMENT AND SCOPE.
Let A be a finite nonnegative0/1 matrix with nonempty rows on base owners
1,...,m. Its base full cover system is A*z>=1, z>=0. Assume its raw minimum-
norm projection is own-row monotone: lowering any subset of demands on rows
containing i, while fixing rows excluding i, cannot increase coordinate i.
The hypothesis quantifies over EVERY arbitrary nonnegative downward RHS
perturbation on incident rows, not only report-realizable perturbations.
It suffices that this holds below the uniform demand vector; homogeneity
then gives the scaled cases used below. All fixed graph/singleton generator
systems satisfy the hypothesis by RANK_TWO_PROJECTION_MONOTONICITY.txt
(the full arbitrary-RHS statement and its active-component proof). Its exact
finite derivative verifier is verify_rank_two_projection_derivatives.py. Add a common veto owner0 to EVERY row, giving
 x_0+sum_(i in S)x_i>=1 for each generator S.
Let M_i be the supremum of coordinate i of the minimum-norm projection after
arbitrary downward relaxations of rows containing i, with all off-i rows
fixed. Then
                     sum_(i=0)^m M_i^2 <=1.                 (V)
The full veto cover system has minimum l1 value T=1. Thus(V) proves the
diagonal inequality for this growing class of FIXED GENERATING systems.
It is not a characterization of every production-report relaxation of the
same full-profile game. The conclusion is about the stated generator envelope. If additional coalition
rows are independently allowed to relax or remain binding, the assumption
must be checked for that actual system; do not silently discard such rows.
In particular, the theorem directly covers real report domains for which
the indicated fixed generator description remains valid at every profile.
A veto already admits an efficient mechanism, so this is a geometric
structural lemma, NOT an improved unrestricted funding theorem.

WRITE rho=||z||^2 FOR THE BASE FULL PROJECTION.
The empty-row-family case is zero and trivial. Otherwise rho>0. Choose
nonnegative base KKT row multipliers lambda with z=A^T lambda, supported
on tight rows. Uniform unit demands give
 rho=sum_S lambda_S, and 0<=z_i<=min{1,rho}.
The upper bound1 follows because any positive z_i has a positive incident
multiplier, whose tight row has total exactly1.

LEMMA1: A UNIFORM DELETION LOSS IS BOUNDED BY ONE COORDINATE.
Let rho_-i be the minimum squared norm after deleting all rows containing i.
Define w_i=sum_(S contains i) lambda_S*1_S. Then0<=w_i<=z coordinatewise,
and z.w_i=sum_(S contains i)lambda_S*z(S)=z_i. Remove these incident dual
multipliers from the base dual2*sum lambda-||A^T lambda||^2. Its value is
 rho-||w_i||^2>=rho-z_i. Therefore
                         rho_-i>=rho-z_i.                 (D)
This is the cross-owner coupling used below: all those off-i rows remain
full at every witness for i. A cruder bound ignoring them loses the result.

LEMMA2: THE VETO'S OWN ENVELOPE IS EXACTLY t=rho/(1+rho).
At the full profile, fixing x_0=u leaves the scaled base problem, with
minimum objective u^2+(1-u)^2*rho for0<=u<=1. Hence the full solution is
 x_0=t=rho/(1+rho), x_base=z/(1+rho), and full squared norm is t.
At ANY relaxation of the veto rows, let mu be its projection KKT multipliers.
Every row contains0, so x_0=sum mu. All relaxed right sides are at most1,
so ||x||^2=sum mu*b'_S<=sum mu=x_0. Use the SAME mu in the full dual:
 2*sum mu-||x||^2>=x_0.
The full dual optimum equals t. Hence x_0<=t under every relaxation, with
equality at the full profile. This proves M_0=t.

LEMMA3: EACH BASE OWNER HAS A COUPLED ENVELOPE CAP.
Take any own-row relaxation witness for base owner i and write its veto
coordinate as u. All off-i rows remain full. Conditional on u, the base
projection is an own-row relaxation of the original base demand vector
scaled by1-u. (Negative residual demands can be replaced by0.) The assumed
own monotonicity therefore gives
 x_i <=(1-u)*z_i.
Also the base coordinates satisfy all off-i rows at residual demand1-u, so
 ||x_base||^2 >=(1-u)^2*rho_-i.
The unit-demand dual calculation above gives||x_base||^2<=u(1-u). Thus
 u>=rho_-i/(1+rho_-i), and consequently
 x_i<=z_i/(1+rho_-i)<=z_i/(1+rho-z_i).
Because every row containing i also contains0, KKT gives x_i<=x_0<=t.
Combining and taking suprema proves
             M_i<=min{t, z_i/(1+rho-z_i)}.                (C)
This includes zero coordinates; 1+rho-z_i>=1. The case u=1 is trivial,
but actually u<=t<1 whenever rho is finite.

AGGREGATION: THE GOLDEN-RATIO SPLIT IS AN EXACT ALGEBRAIC THRESHOLD.
Let phi=(1+sqrt5)/2. For0<=z_i<=min{1,rho}, the ratio M_i/z_i is bounded
by the smaller of t/z_i and1/(1+rho-z_i). They cross at
 z_star=rho*(1+rho)/(1+2rho).
When rho<=phi, this crossing lies in the allowed interval. Therefore
 sum_i M_i^2 <= rho*(1+2rho)^2/(1+rho)^4.
Adding M_0^2=rho^2/(1+rho)^2 gives at most1 because the numerator of the
remaining gap is
 (1+rho)^4-rho^2*(1+rho)^2-rho*(1+2rho)^2
   =-(2rho+1)*(rho^2-rho-1) >=0.
When rho>=phi, z_star>=1 and1/(1+rho-z_i)<=1/rho. Consequently
 sum_i M_i^2<=1/rho.
Then M_0^2+sum_i M_i^2<=rho^2/(1+rho)^2+1/rho<=1, since
 rho^2-rho-1>=0. This completes(V).

WHAT ADVANCED / WHAT REMAINS OPEN.
The conclusion does not assume own monotonicity of the veto-extended
projection itself. The proof combines exact uniform dual mass, off-owner
deletion, and an aggregate scalar inequality; it does not use the false
efficient-core support or T*max-coordinate inequalities. No example of raw
incentive failure in this precise uniform graph subclass is asserted here. General nonuniform demands and
base systems whose own projection is not monotone remain unresolved.
No historical novelty claim is made for KKT duality or the individual base
lemmas; the assembled envelope theorem is a proposed structural result.
