Monotonicity in Quadratically Regularized Linear Programs, April16 2025,
https://www.math.columbia.edu/~mnutz/docs/GNR24.pdf (arXiv2408.07871v2).
Their Theorem3.2 characterizes face invariance as a regularization parameter
changes for a fixed polytope. Our operation changes incident RHS while
retaining off-owner rows. Their theorem does not directly imply any envelope
or dual-mass inequality here. Convex projection and KKT facts are classical.
The source warns that numerical monotonicity nonhits can miss structural
counterexamples, but that is motivation, not transferred mathematical proof.
Earlier campaign amplification/realization statements are local dependencies,
not externally confirmed novel results. No novelty clearance is asserted.
