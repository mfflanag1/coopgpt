FROZEN FRONTIER
Source: paper5/truthful_cooperation/manuscript.tex, repository 075e392.
- General base upper U(k)=R_k+1/log R_k, R_k>e and R_k log(R_k/e)=log k.
  Hence B_k<=(1+o(1)) log k/log log k, independently of all owner counts.
- Unrestricted lower: for k>=3, equal r>=2 and t=2r-2,
  B_k>=1+(k-1)(t-1)/[t(k-1+2t)]. Its supremum tends to 2 as k,r grow.
  No universal constant below 2 can work; factor 2 feasibility is not proved.
- Capped-proportional final payments d_i*pi_j(c), d_i=min(c_i,T), have sharp
  floor-free first-order minimax (1+o(1)) log k/log log k, even with arbitrary
  whole-profile pool prices. Fixed report-independent random-order marginal
  rules with bounded rescaling are also blocked.
- Bounded pool count and bounded maximum owner count cannot prove divergence.
- The complete linked binary witness family and its two-altered-pool extension
  admit nonlinear base factor 2. Real reports in [1/r,1] with at most m altered
  equal-size pools admit base 2m+3. Any fixed m is insufficient for divergence
  in that family. This continuous formula itself costs 3+4k/3 at r=3, all c=1/3.
