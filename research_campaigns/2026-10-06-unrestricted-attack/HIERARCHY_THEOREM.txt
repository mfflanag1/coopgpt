UNRESTRICTED TRUTHFUL COALITION COVERAGE REQUIRES GROWING BUDGETS
6 October 2026 — mathematical result after independent model audit and
coordinator verification. Historical novelty and specialist review unresolved.

MODEL AND QUANTIFIERS
A finite fixed partition N=N_1 disjoint-union ... N_k, k>=2, has arbitrary
nonempty pools. Reports c range over ALL nonnegative real vectors. Define
C_j=sum_(i in N_j)c_i, T=min_j C_j, and v_c(S)=min_j sum_(i in S intersect N_j)c_i.
A rule assigns nonnegative deterministic payments, covers every coalition,
and y_i(t,c_-i) is nondecreasing in t. Under binding underreports and payment
utility without usage costs this is precisely the incentive requirement.
No proportionality, symmetry, population, split/merge or group-report axiom
is imposed. At T=0 payments are zero. Its factor is sup_(c:T>0)y(c)(N)/T.
Let B_k be the supremum over finite k-pool ownerships of the infimum of that
factor over such rules. The argument below applies to EVERY eligible rule
on its explicitly constructed ownership, including nonanonymous rules.

MAIN RESULT
No constant independent of both k and owner counts can be a valid factor.
More precisely, with all logarithms natural,
 (1/2-o(1)) log k/log log k <= B_k <= (1+o(1)) log k/log log k.
The upper is the pre-existing all-profile capped-Banzhaf theorem in
paper5/truthful_cooperation/manuscript.tex, Theorem labelled thm:upper,
uniform over arbitrary unequal finite owner counts. The new lower below
closes the constant-versus-divergence question and establishes its order.
The leading coefficient remains between1/2 and1; this is NOT a sharp leading
constant theorem. The optional H_i/k floor increases the upper by at most1
and leaves this order unchanged; the lower omits the floor entirely.

COMPLETE LOWER PROOF
Fix integers d>=1, m>d, H=m. There are k pools, m fixed owners per pool.
Every report is 1 or m. Type h has h high owners and m-h low owners,
for h=0,...,d. Select counts n_0>=2, n_h>=1, sum n_h=k.
For any unrestricted mechanism with worst-case factor B on this ownership,
average over pool and within-pool owner permutations. This preserves every
coalition constraint, nonnegative payments, budgets and own-report comparisons.
At any histogram let x_h be a low owner's payment, z_h a high owner's payment.
This is a proof by symmetrization; no anonymity assumption or strategic cloning.

Base profile S has counts n_0,...,n_d. Its T=m because n_0>=2.
For each h<d, increase ONE low original owner in a type-h pool from1 to m.
The destination S_h has counts n_h-1, n_(h+1)+1 (others unchanged).
It still has an all-low pool, hence T=m. At S_h, symmetry and its budget give
 (h+1)(n_(h+1)+1) z_(h+1)(S_h) <= Bm.
Own monotonicity therefore bounds the BASE low payment by
 x_h(S) <= Bm / [(h+1)(n_(h+1)+1)].                         (1)
This bounds low payments in ALL earlier types at the SAME base profile.
No other owner's payment is transported between profiles.

Two actual base coalitions:
 C0 chooses one low owner from every pool (possible since m>d):
       sum_(h=0)^d n_h x_h >=1.                            (2)
 C1 chooses m-d+1 low owners from every type h<d, and one high owner
 from every type-d pool. Its worth is m-d+1 (all former pools supply that,
 latter pools supply m):
       (m-d+1) sum_(h<d)n_h x_h + n_d z_d >= m-d+1.         (3)
Let A=(m-d)+d(m-d+1)=(d+1)m-d^2.
Multiply (2) by m-d, (3) by d. The type-d payment coefficients are exactly
its full budget coefficients, yielding
 A <= A sum_(h<d)n_h x_h + n_d[(m-d)x_d+d z_d]
   <= A sum_(h<d)n_h x_h + Bm
   <= Bm [1 + A sum_(h<d) n_h/((h+1)(n_(h+1)+1))].          (4)
Thus the explicit general lower bound is
 B >= A / {m [1+A sum_(h<d) n_h/((h+1)(n_(h+1)+1))]}.       (5)
Every used coefficient is nonnegative; dropping earlier-type budget costs
only weakens the conclusion. Equation(4) uses no unproved cross-profile
retention, report-dependent normalization or proportionality.

Elementary divergence family: choose m=2d, A=d(d+2), L=2Ad,
n_h=2 L^h (h=0,...,d), k_d=2 sum_(h=0)^d L^h.
Since n_h/(n_(h+1)+1)<1/L,
 A sum n_h/((h+1)(n_(h+1)+1)) < A H_d/L <= A d/L=1/2.
Therefore B > (d+2)/3. Both pool and original-owner counts grow.
For any k>=k_d, add k-k_d pools of type d. The same proof applies;
the final ratio only decreases, and all earlier ratios are unchanged.
This is an explicit padded construction, not assumed monotonicity of B_k.

Sharper asymptotics: for d>=2 choose
 q_d=ceil(log(d+1)), m=d q_d,
 A=(d+1)m-d^2,
 L=ceil[A(1+log d)^2], n_h=2 L^h.
Then the bracket in(5) is <=1+1/(1+log d), because H_d<=1+log d.
Moreover A/m=d+1-d/q_d=(1-o(1))d,
 log L=(2+o(1))log d, log k_d=(2+o(1))d log d.
For any fixed epsilon>0 and sufficiently large k, take
 d=floor((1/2-epsilon) log k/log log k), so k_d<=k.
Pad type-d pools as above. Then B_k >=(1/2-epsilon-o(1))log k/log log k.
Since epsilon is arbitrary, B_k >=(1/2-o(1))log k/log log k.
Together with the existing manuscript upper this gives the claimed Theta order.

How this escapes the old star barrier: a single base profile has d distinct
exporting owner types, with different donor destinations each containing many
owners of the corresponding new high type. The old barrier restricts a single
exporting owner/star architecture. Here the same base low-payment mass is
bounded simultaneously for every h<d and the final type retains both low and
high coalition demands. The prior STAR_BARRIER.txt explicitly excludes multi-exporter sources and
cascades. This comparison is explanatory; the proof stands independently.
