CLAIM TO REVIEW
For weighted multi-glove games, the least worst-case total-payment factor among
nonnegative own-report-monotone coalition-covering rules satisfies
 (1/2-o(1)) log k/log log k <= B_k <= (1+o(1)) log k/log log k,
where k counts pools and B_k allows every finite ownership. Thus no universal
constant works when both pool counts and owner counts grow. The factor-two gap
in leading coefficients remains. Historical novelty is unconfirmed.

PROOF IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Have n_h pools with h high owners (report m), and m-h low owners (report1),
for h=0,...,d<m. Keep at least2 all-low pools so every one-owner increase still
has total worthm. After a low owner of typeh raises its report, symmetry and
the destination budget bound its payment by Bm/[(h+1)(n_(h+1)+1)]. Own-report
monotonicity therefore makes every earlier type's low payment small at the
base profile when successive type populations grow rapidly. Two actual base
coalitions then force both low and high payments in type d, costing roughly
d*m. An explicit nonnegative inequality combination quantifies the tradeoff.
Choosing m about d log d and successive population ratio about d^2 log^3 d
gives log k about2d log d and required factor aboutd. All symmetrization is
finite relabel averaging, with no strategic owner splitting.

SCOPE AND ATTRIBUTION
Moretti–Norde define the game. Huang et al. supply the closest capacity-report
incentive model and truthful-core-selection impossibility. Kurz defines the
continuous Banzhaf functional. Immorlica et al. prove a different max-flow
profit-sharing impossibility using population cross-monotonicity. Branzei,
Solymosi and Tijs study efficient type-monotonic schemes with changing unit-owner
populations; this close comparator also requires credit. Our lower
also embeds in a serial chain of parallel-edge layers; it therefore obstructs
constant factors for mechanisms intended for all max-flow networks. The matching
upper asserted here is only for the multi-glove/layered subclass, not all flow
networks. No new power index, game model, general-network upper, computational
polynomial-time implementation or recognized-major-open-problem claim is made.
