CONTRIBUTION AND PRIORITY DECISION
24 October 2026 — local consolidation of the existing envelope paper candidate

DECISION
Keep one focused working-paper candidate: the funding cost of making a baseline
coalition-covering allocation incentive compatible without reducing any baseline
payment at any report. The strongest result is the linear owner-count lower order
for every fixed finite cap and fixed power objective. The earlier uncapped
quadratic obstruction is a companion theorem. Pool-selector obstructions and
sharp common-veto refinements are supporting results, not separate papers.

The mathematical contribution is now stated more precisely. Historical novelty
is NOT certified. Close originals already contain the selection criteria,
monotonicity conflict, and convex optimality ingredients. No checked theorem was
identified as supplying our exact quantitative funding statements, but remaining
source/version gaps are listed below rather than called resolved.

WHAT THE PAPER ASKS
Owners have nonnegative bundles and may report coordinatewise smaller bundles.
One fixed technology F(y)=min_l w_l.y, with w_l>=0, determines every coalition's
output at every report. Utility here is payment only. A baseline q covers all
coalitions. A repair M must be own-report monotone and satisfy M_i(b)>=q_i(b)
for every owner and every profile. The cheapest repair coordinate by coordinate is
    E_i[q](b)=sup_{0<=a_i<=b_i} q_i(a_i,b_-i).
This is a routine order fact, proved in the draft, not a novelty claim.

Why this matters: if an allocation schedule is a commitment that may only be
topped up, its funding consequences apply to every repair, not just to a specific
envelope implementation. This supplies a defensible conditional design principle.
It is not evidence of an actual institution or a claim that unrestricted mechanism
design requires such a commitment. Preserving one observed allocation is weaker
than preserving the whole report-contingent schedule and is not what is proved.

Total funding is sum_i M_i. External subsidy is total funding minus production.
The theorem ratios use total funding/production, not net subsidy/production.

CONTRIBUTION LEDGER
1. Efficient quadratic seed = classical least-squares core selection.
   AKV 2008 Definition 1 is an exact match at C=1,r=2. Attribute, do not claim.
   Strictly concave efficient-core objectives also appear in Hougaard–Peleg–
   Osterdal; fixed powers at C=1 are not a new selection principle.
2. Static minimum-norm cover/KKT identities = established ingredients.
   Hof et al.; Abdi–Cornuejols–Lee; AKV's multiplier lemma. A cap adds a standard
   constraint. No new convex-duality theorem is claimed.
3. Own-report envelope = elementary least monotone majorant.
   Classical monotonicization principle. The new interpretation is only the
   explicit baseline-preservation contract, with its proof and scope.
4. Simultaneous fixed-owner realization = useful construction, not a new general
   representation theorem. Kalai–Zemel supplies the classical static context.
   Control coordinates implement the required comparisons; present the reduction
   transparently and do not treat its absence from one source as novelty evidence.
5. Efficient-selector lower: every efficient-core seed has envelope factor at
   least (N+6)/8 along N=2g. Proposed quantitative result; proof is elementary.
   Qualitative core/monotonicity incompatibility is already classical. Arin's
   incident-coalition comparisons are a particularly close antecedent.
6. Every B<2 seed: choose m>1/(2-B), gamma=1-(B-1)m/(m-1)>0. For N=gm the factor
   is at least gamma[1+(g-1)/(2m)]. A supporting extension, no norm assumption.
   Taking q=M for an already own-monotone rule gives E[M]=M, so a uniform
   unrestricted factor B<2 is impossible on a sufficiently large constructed
   technology. This immediate constant threshold does not yield a growing
   lower bound for unrestricted mechanisms with larger funding factors. It is
   recorded for scope accuracy, not presented as a new campaign contribution.
7. Main fixed-cap theorem: for each fixed C>=1 and r>1, minimize sum x_i^r
   over nonnegative covers with sum x_i<=C T. The true envelope has a linear
   lower sequence. At C=2, factor >=(N-1)/11. For C>1, choose fixed Q with
   C_Q=Q-1-(Q-3)/(2Q+5)>C; the factor is at least
       [(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)](N-2).
   The NC upper makes the owner order sharp along this sequence. Constants
   are not matched; dimension grows; C and r cannot vary with N in this claim.
   This is the principal proposed contribution requiring priority assessment.
8. Uncapped quadratic companion: the envelope exceeds every A sqrt(N) with
   fixed A. A sequence has factor > (1/40)sqrt(N)(log N/log log N)^(1/3).
   It is not implied by the fixed-cap theorem. No unrestricted-mechanism lower
   bound follows. The sharp fixed-Q common-veto supplement is class-restricted.
9. Weighted egalitarian-claims eligibility = removed from novelty story.
   The previous campaign obtained Dietzenbacher's unadjusted-claims antecedent;
   weighting via cloning/continuity is a routine extension. It is not presented
   here as an additional theorem contribution.

NAMED PRIORITY QUESTIONS: DISPOSITION
- AKV minimum norm: RESOLVED as an exact classical ingredient, freshly checked.
- Kultti–Salonen minimum norm: RESOLVED at the inspected displayed formulations;
  projection of a game/coalition-error objective differs from our payment norm.
  Prior working-version inspection is retained; no final-version equivalence claim.
- Kalai–Zemel: RESOLVED as static representation precedent. Treat the extra
  report-control construction conservatively as a reduction, not historical novelty.
- Projection sensitivity: fixed-polytope regularization-path results (Gonzalez-
  Sanz–Nutz–Riveros Valdevenito) and efficient-core reallocation (Laplace Mermoud)
  use different changing objects. The inspected theorems do not imply our bound.
- Incident-coalition core monotonicity: CLOSER PRECEDENT FOUND in Arin's free
  TCER E-24. It uses exactly the game-level direction generated by an owner's
  resource increase. Its finite incompatibility proof has no growing funding
  rate. Our contribution must be quantitative, not qualitative. Final 2013
  version has not been compared with this working paper.
- Convex-game positive results: Hougaard–Peleg–Osterdal's efficient-core
  generalized Lorenz selector is coalitionally monotone on convex games. The
  pool counterexample is demonstrably nonconvex. No claimed contradiction.
- Population monotonicity: the final 2025 Dietzenbacher–Dogan original was
  obtained and checked, including Theorems 3.1–3.2. Changing participation/subgames
  differs from fixed-owner partial withholding, while providing related precedents.
- Static cost of stability: RESOLVED distinction. Our games have nonempty
  efficient cores already; the cost arises from the cross-report baseline floor.
- Uhan 2014: INCOMPLETE. Original introduction and section excerpts distinguish
  valuation bids/service prices from resource reports/production payments.
  Full original retrieval failed; no theorem-level clearance is claimed. Do not
  say that no free copy exists, only that the checked routes did not deliver one.

REVIEW QUESTIONS THAT REMAIN MATERIAL
1. Does prior work already give the quantitative fixed-owner, fixed-technology
   lower order for all fixed caps/powers, perhaps as an implication of a general
   theorem on monotonicity repair or subsidized core selection?
2. Is the unconditional efficient-selector lower best viewed as an elementary
   amplification of known core-monotonicity examples? If so, keep it as exposition,
   not the paper's claimed central innovation.
3. Is the cap/power theorem and uncapped companion sufficient for a standalone
   theory paper under the explicit allocation-preservation commitment? The present
   mathematical result does not establish a real-world policy or application.
4. Are the all-cut row-mixture existence argument and all-real fixed-owner
   realization sound under the draft's precise quantifiers?

The Uhan full text, Arin final-version delta, and broader endowment-withholding
literature remain bounded source tasks. They must not be represented as closed
through an absence of search hits. New long mathematical campaigns are not the
remedy for these publication questions. Next action is correctness/priority
assessment of this exact draft, with any missing original obtained freely or
through access already available to the author. No purchase or outreach occurred.
