## 2. Exact definitions/theorems located and compared to the proposed rule

Proposed rule (from `THEOREM_REVIEW_BRIEF.txt` lines 17–26): seed `q^C(b) = argmin Σ x_i^r` over `{x ≥ 0, x(S) ≥ v_b(S) ∀S, x(N) ≤ C·v_b(N)}`, `v_b(S) = F(Σ_{i∈S} b_i)`, F a finite min of nonnegative linear forms; envelope `E_i(b) = sup_{0≤a_i≤b_i} q_i^C(a_i, b_{-i})`.

**AKV 2008, Definition 1 (p. 569):** "For a balanced game v, the least squares solution LS(v) is defined as the unique allocation x in C(v) for which ‖x‖ ≤ ‖y‖ for all y ∈ C(v)", with C(v) the *efficient* core (p. 567: x(N)=v(N), x(S) ≥ v(S)). Noted there as a Lorenz concept and as the point of C(v) closest to equal shares.
- *Comparison:* at **C = 1** with nonempty core, the seed's feasible set forces x(N)=v(N) and nonnegativity is implied by singleton constraints (v_b({i}) ≥ 0), so **q^1(b) = LS(v_b) exactly**. The C=1 seed is therefore a *published, axiomatized* solution concept, not a new object.
- **AKV 2008, Lemma 5 (p. 578):** LS(v) = Σ_{T∈𝒯} λ_T e_T with λ_T ≥ 0 for tight T ≠ N, via KKT "2LS(v) + Σ μ_S e_S = 0". This is the same tight-coalition multiplier certificate structure the brief uses (plus one cap multiplier η). Established ingredient.
- **AKV 2008, Theorem 4 (p. 579):** LS is the only core concept satisfying IIC, ETP and CADD (conditional additivity, p. 575). **Theorem 2 (p. 573):** every continuous core concept with IIC and ETP is egalitarian. **Theorem 3 (p. 576):** projection of an additive solution onto the core satisfies CADD.
- *Not in AKV 2008:* any monotonicity, resource/report domain, manipulation, production/LP games, budget cap, or subsidy. Report domain, incentive notion, and envelope operation are absent; the reference list (p. 580) contains no monotonicity paper.

**AKV 2003, Theorem 6 (p. 334):** on convex games, Dutta–Ray is the only core solution with IIC, SYM, CON; its proof states DR = unique core allocation minimizing ‖·‖₂ on convex games. Section 4 (p. 335) uses AMON (monotonicity in v(N)) for Lmax on large-core games; p. 328 defines large core via the *upper core* (non-efficient allocations satisfying core inequalities). Aggregate monotonicity only; no resource reports.

**Hougaard–Peleg–Østerdal 2003, p. 4:** generalized Lorenz solution `φ^GL(v) = argmax{Σ u_i(x_i) | x ∈ C(v)}`, u_i strictly concave differentiable, C(v) efficient core. Coalitional monotonicity: `[v(S) ≤ w(S), v(T)=w(T), S≠T] ⇒ φ_i(v) ≤ φ_i(w)` for i∈S. **Theorem 1 (p. 7):** φ^GL is coalitionally monotonic on convex games. **Theorem 2 (p. 11):** φ^GL is population monotonic on convex games. Hypotheses: convexity, efficiency, no cap.
- *Comparison:* with u_i(x) = −x^r this is exactly the brief's fixed-power efficient-core selector (C=1, r>1). So a **positive** monotonicity result for the C=1 power seed is published **on convex games**, one coalition at a time. An own-report decrease changes all coalitions containing i simultaneously; iterating Theorem 1 needs the intermediate games to stay convex, which is not guaranteed. *Inference (not evidence):* the C=1 envelope obstruction must live on non-convex totally balanced games; the manuscript should state this explicitly and verify the k-pool/2k-owner family is non-convex.

**Dietzenbacher–Doğan 2025:** definitions p. 4–5 (core, egalitarian core EC, strong egalitarian core, efficiency, population monotonicity `f_i(T, v_T) ≤ f_i(N, v)`, core/EC selection), Dutta–Ray on exact partition games p. 5; **Theorem 3.1 (p. 8), Corollary 3.1 (p. 9), Theorem 3.2 (p. 10), Corollary 3.2 (p. 11), Lemmas 4.1–4.2 (p. 13), Theorem 4.1 (p. 14).** Population monotonicity quantifies over *other* players' payoffs when a player is removed entirely; the envelope quantifies over the *withholding* player's own payoff under partial withholding. No LP/production games, resource reports, subsidy, or cap anywhere in the paper.

**Calleja–Llerena–Sudhölter:** aggregate monotonicity and bounded richness defined p. 5–6; **Theorem 1 (p. 8):** CS + AM + BR characterize DR on convex games; **Theorem 2 (p. 9):** CS + SBR. Aggregate monotonicity varies only v(N). Distinct.

**Uhan 2014 (abstract only):** NP-hardness of the "most cost-efficient subset problem" for LP games, hence NP-hardness of computing "the prices output by an egalitarian mechanism" and of an equal split-off allocation. Cost-game, computational-complexity content; nothing about reports, withholding, monotonicity, or subsidy is visible. **Theorem-level comparison not performed** — mark unverified; do not cite its definitions from the abstract.

## 4. Classification

- **Published (established ingredient):** the C=1, r=2 seed (= AKV LS solution, Def. 1, with axiomatization Thm 4); the KKT tight-coalition multiplier representation (AKV Lemma 5; also Hof et al. and Abdi et al. per ledger); the general strictly-concave efficient-core selector (HPØ p. 4) covering the power seed at C=1; coalitional/population monotonicity of that selector on **convex** games (HPØ Thms 1–2).
- **Routine extrapolation of a known principle:** the C>1 seed (adding a budget-cap row to the nonnegative cover projection of Hof et al.) — a combination of two published objects; no source applying the cap was found.
- **Only the proposal (no antecedent found):** the sup-over-underreports envelope of a core/cover selector; the fixed-owner cross-report funding ratio; both lower bounds (efficient-core universal envelope lower; all-fixed-cap power lower). **Not falsified, not confirmed novel.** The only published result bearing on them is HPØ's positive monotonicity on convex games, which constrains scope rather than contradicting the claims.
- **Subsumed:** nothing in the proposal is subsumed by the read sources.
- **Distinct:** Uhan 2014 (complexity; unverified beyond abstract), AKV 2003 §4 AMON, CLS aggregate monotonicity, DD population monotonicity.
