1. BASELINE FLOOR AND THE UNRESTRICTED PROBLEM

The model keeps a fixed finite technology, fixed actual owners, and an
all-real vector report domain. It correctly separates covering at one
profile from own monotonicity across profiles. The efficient-core witness
from an aggregate-minimizing nonnegative form proves nonemptiness on the
whole domain, including boundary and zero-output profiles.

The least-majorant lemma is valid: any own-monotone M dominating q at
EVERY profile has M_i(b)>=q_i(a_i,b_-i) for each admissible a_i, and hence
dominates its supremum. The envelope's domain expands under own-report
increases; it dominates the original seed and therefore covers. Generic
seeds may have infinite envelopes, as the definition expressly permits.
The later bounded seeds have finite envelopes by their separate bounds.

The baseline-payment floor is stated as an ADDITIONAL contract on the
entire report-contingent schedule. It is not silently imposed on arbitrary
own-monotone covering mechanisms. The introduction explicitly distinguishes
it from preserving one observed allocation, and treats institutional use
as a possible conditional design motivation rather than an observed fact.
The resulting interpretation is mathematically justified, not merely a
change of terminology for the envelope.

The bounds E_i<=CT and sum_i E_i<=NCT follow directly from the seed budget
and monotone production. For the uncapped quadratic seed, a feasible
efficient cover c has ||c||_2<=T; optimality gives q_i<=||q||_2<=T.
This proves the separate C=1 coordinate/envelope bounds without incorrectly
asserting that the uncapped seed itself is efficient.

2. EFFICIENT-CORE AND B<2 QUANTIFIERS

At the one-small-pool profile F_j the efficient core is the singleton
paying its two owners one each. At the two-small-pool profile L_ij the
four cross-pool inequalities and total two force
(lambda,lambda;1-lambda,1-lambda), with all other payments zero. Conversely
these allocations cover every coalition. Thus a_ij+a_ji=1 and averaging
the genuine high-owner underreports yields normalized envelope funding
(g+3)/4=(N+6)/8. The draft correctly lets the bad full-profile index depend
on the selector, while using one technology for all comparisons. It does
not require symmetry, continuity, or a particular objective.

The stated supermodularity counterexample is correct: the two coalitions
containing the same one small-pool owner have values 1,1, their union has
value 1, and their intersection has value zero. Thus the pool example is
outside the convex-game domain mentioned in the introduction.

For 1<=B<2, the condition m>1/(2-B) is exactly what gives
gamma=1-(B-1)m/(m-1)>0. The large-pool low-owner total L_* is bounded by
(B-1)m. Averaging low-owner choices gives a small-pool payment lower bound
m gamma at F_j, and a pair charge at least gamma at L_ij. Summing the
envelope inequalities over j yields
gamma[1+(g-1)/(2m)] after dividing by the common output m.

B and then m are fixed before g grows; this is a linear lower bound along
N=gm. The finite list includes both designated full and pair profiles, so
the hypothesis constrains every seed payment used in the proof. Empty
collections of large pools, including g=2 at a pair profile, cause no
exception. The global B-funded-seed consequence follows, but the theorem
does not assert the existence of an unrestricted eligible B-funded rule.

The r=1 endpoint correctly reduces to total-payment minimization, whose
minimizers are the efficient core. At C=1 every feasible seed is in the
efficient core regardless of objective. These endpoints do not depend on
the strict-convexity argument for r>1.

3. FIXED CAP AND ARBITRARY FIXED REAL POWER

The renaming from local dimension d in the source to L in the draft is
consistent. Production-resource dimension D is kept separate. The stated
q-ary lemma supplies the same retained-own and complete off-owner counts,
uniform marginals, and positive margins as the frozen input. In particular
k>=n/Q ensures nonempty base rows.

Write p=h/(L+h), z=1/(L+h), a for the neighbor marginal, and b_* for the
outside marginal. The proposed vector pays p at both veto and focal owner,
z at each of (Q-1)L neighbors, and zero outside. Retained supported rows
receive 2p+(L-h)z=1; ALL off-owner rows receive at least p+Lz=1. Its total
is C_Q. This verifies the full generator system, not just active rows.

For Phi=sum x_i^r/r, the displayed multiplier is
lambda=(p^(r-1)-z^(r-1))/(1-a), with eta=lambda-p^(r-1).
Its outside nonnegativity correction has the correct sign:

  eta-lambda b_* >=0
    iff p^(r-1)(a-b_*) >= z^(r-1)(1-b_*)
    iff h^(r-1)(a-b_*) >=1-b_*.

The derivative at zero is zero for every real r>1. Since b_*>0 and
lambda>0, the displayed outside condition also gives eta>=0. The direct
gradient inequality follows from nonnegative coordinates, active row
constraints and the upper budget; strict convexity proves uniqueness.
There is no hidden differentiability issue at zero or missing constraint-
qualification assumption.

The input gives a-b_*>delta/2 uniformly along the dimension multiples.
Thus h^(r-1)>=2/delta suffices, and is eventually true for each FIXED
r>1. The coefficient p=1/(2Q+5) is independent of r; only the sequence's
starting point depends on r. For r=2 the sharper inherited gap works
for every allowed local dimension. The draft does not interchange a
varying-r limit with this argument.

For arbitrary fixed C>1, the additive owner is fixed at
A=(C_Q-C)/(C-1)>0 after choosing fixed Q with C_Q>C. At every selected
profile, coalition covers factor into the old cover and x_new>=A.
Reducing x_new to A preserves every coalition and relaxes the budget, so
the power minimizer has exactly that value. The remaining budget is
C(1+A)-A=C_Q, and its objective differs by the constant A^r/r. Hence the
old optimum is unchanged, not merely feasible.

The exact translation identities are

  C_Q=(2Q^2+2Q-2)/(2Q+5), p=1/(2Q+5),
  np/(1+A)=[(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)](N-2).

The resource count is 2(n+1)+1=2N-1. At C=2, Q=3 requires no added owner:
N=n+1, D=2N, and the certified lower is (N-1)/11. Real A causes no issue
with the binary-coefficient claim because A is in the fixed bundle and
the added resource's coefficient in every technology form is one.

The introduction and theorem both fix C and r before owner count grows.
They claim neither a bound at every prescribed N, a starting dimension
uniform in r, fixed D, nor matching funding constants. Linear owner order
is therefore accurately described as sharp in the stated sequence sense.

4. UNCAPPED QUADRATIC TRANSLATION AND ASYMPTOTICS

The full base projection z=(1/k)1 follows from the translated row mixture
with total multiplier rho=n/k^2. At the focal deletion the supported row
potential t equals ||w||^2; every off row has additional potential at least
h(a-b_*)-(1-b_*)>0. The focal projection w/t has squared norm and multiplier
mass ell=1/t. Since it relaxes the full unit cover, ell<=rho.

The J-block common-veto formulas retain the correct denominator:
full veto J rho/(1+J rho), and selected private payment
p_J=ell/[1+(J-1)rho+ell]. The latter comes from a separate unilateral
deletion in one block with the other blocks unchanged. The simultaneous
realization lemma is explicitly invoked before aggregating across owners.

For j=1 and J=floor(1/rho), the draft's estimates give ell/rho>1/2,
1/2<J rho<=1, and n/k>Q-1. Consequently
Jn p_J/sqrt(N)>(Q-1)/8. Also

  18Q^3-L=2Q(Q-4)^2>=0,
  n<=N<=k^2+1<=n^2,
  log N<=36Q^3 log Q, log log N>log Q.

Using (Q-1)/8>=Q/12 and 12*36^(1/3)<40 proves the stated strict 1/40
lower bound. Increasing Q also gives both arbitrary owner-count minimum
N0 and arbitrarily large square-root coefficient; no fixed-cap theorem
is being used at an N-dependent inactive cap.

For the separate fixed-Q, j-growing sequence, k/n->b0 and t/n->b0^2 give
J rho->1 and N~b0^2 n^2. The certified quantities converge to
(1+b0^(-2))/4 for the squared-payment lower bound and 1/b0 for the ratio
of certified private funding to full raw seed funding. The draft calls
these LOWER bounds on the corresponding actual envelope quantities; it
does not incorrectly claim these selected-report limits are exact limits
of the full real-report envelope. Letting Q then grow proves unboundedness.

5. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE ASSESSMENT

No mathematical overstatement was found in the abstract or introduction.
They clearly state:
  - the floor is global and additional;
  - the result concerns repairs of specified baseline schedules;
  - the cap and power are fixed before the sequence;
  - the uncapped result is separate from the fixed-cap result;
  - all within-instance comparisons preserve technology and actual owners;
  - the construction is finite but not exhaustively enumerated or claimed
    computationally efficient;
  - neither unrestricted mechanism lower bounds nor historical priority
    are established.

The appendix's competing efficient veto rule is explicitly confined to
the fixed full bundles' downward box. It is not asserted to work at
arbitrary redistributions of resource types among owners. That distinction
is important and is consistent with the earlier audit.

I did not independently reopen all external bibliographic sources during
this bounded translation audit. The source-comparison passages are treated
as the separate provenance review's responsibility. This verdict therefore
does not clear priority, verify institutional relevance, or certify every
historical assertion. It also does not re-audit the entire old all-cut
construction; it checks the main draft's use of its previously audited
interface and the scope of the resulting conclusions.
