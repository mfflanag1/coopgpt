**Verdict: revise.** The candidate contains useful distinctions, but its central retrieval-stop claim is contradicted by available free-source leads. It also omits a closer antecedent to its incentive notion. Neither proposed lower bound is subsumed by the evidence I checked; historical priority remains unresolved.

1. **Arin is freely available as a working paper and materially closer than the candidate reports.** I obtained and read the relevant original sections of [TCER Working Paper E-24](https://www.tcer.or.jp/wp/pdf/e24.pdf). Printed p. 4 defines monotonicity by increasing every coalition containing player \(i\), holding every coalition excluding \(i\) unchanged, and requiring \(i\)’s allocation not to decrease. This is exactly the **game-level direction induced by increasing an owner’s resource report** under the proposed technology.

   Printed p. 5, Theorem 3, proves impossibility of strong core monotonicity on balanced games; pp. 6–7 introduce compatibility across lists of player–game pairs. These are relevant antecedents to simultaneous cross-profile core-selection obstructions.

   **Classification: closely related, unresolved—not subsumed.** The paper does not supply the fixed-technology, all-real resource domain, envelope operation, growing subsidy ratio, or fixed-cap power quantifiers. This working-paper version also needs comparison with the final 2013 article; they should not silently be treated as identical.

2. **Uhan’s “no free copy” conclusion is unsupported.** A [CiteSeerX-hosted original PDF](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=10d212c65f6a40ac19ed521e277e47f4ce10dcd0&repid=rep1&type=pdf) is indexed with substantial original text. Direct fetching failed during my review, so I cannot claim a completed full-paper reading.

   Nevertheless, indexed original p. 6 exposes **Algorithm 3.1**: input is a cost game and scalar valuation bids; output is served players and charged prices. It iteratively selects a coalition minimizing average marginal cost, assigns that price when bids suffice, and otherwise removes insufficient bidders. Indexed p. 11 gives **Corollary 3.4**, NP-hardness of computing those prices.

   **Classification: distinct at the inspected definition level; full comparison unverified.** This is service procurement with valuation bids, rather than resource withholding with production payments. Its budget balance concerns recovering service cost, rather than funding coordinatewise maxima from separate reports. The missing comparison should include its antecedent, **Bleischwitz–Monien–Schoppmann, “To be or not to be (served): cost sharing without indifferences.”**

3. **The published-seed identification is sound, with an essential power qualification.** [AKV 2008](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s00182-008-0133-6.pdf), Definition 1, printed p. 569, defines Euclidean-norm minimization over the efficient core. Thus \(q^{1,2}=LS\) when the core is nonempty and singleton values are nonnegative. This does **not** identify arbitrary-power \(q^{1,r}\) with LS on general balanced games.

   Lemma 5, p. 578, permits nonnegative proper-coalition coefficients and an **unrestricted real grand-coalition coefficient**. Preserve that distinction when comparing it with the cap multiplier. Theorem 4, p. 579, supplies the stated axiomatization.

   **Classification: the quadratic efficient seed and KKT ingredient are subsumed; the envelope lower bounds are unresolved.** “Nothing is subsumed” should therefore explicitly mean nothing among the claimed new lower-bound contributions.

4. **The envelope operation itself needs more restrained attribution.** Independently of historical priority,
   \[
   E_i(b_i,b_{-i})=\sup_{a_i\le b_i}q_i(a_i,b_{-i})
   \]
   is the least own-report-monotone majorant of \(q_i\): its indexing sets are nested, and every monotone majorant dominates every indexed seed value. This elementary observation is not evidence of publication, but it supports describing monotonicization as a routine construction. The potentially substantive contribution is its **quantified funding obstruction on the specified production domain**, not merely taking a supremum.

Likewise, “only two items remain” conflicts with the candidate’s own unread pages and withholding-literature gaps. The supplied co-investment comparison and Dietzenbacher claims antecedent also deserve explicit reconciliation, even if ultimately distinct.
