CONTRACT
There are N fixed actual owners and one nonnegative finite-minlinear technology
F. At report b, coalition S can produce v_b(S)=F(sum_{i in S}b_i). Reports range
over all nonnegative real bundles; owner i may underreport a_i<=b_i. Utility is
payment alone. No extra outside value or private cost is included. Baselines
cover every coalition. A repair must be own-monotone and dominate every baseline
payment at every profile. The least repair is E_i(b)=sup_{a_i<=b_i}q_i(a_i,b_-i).
Funding ratios below are total payments/output, not net subsidy/output.

MAIN STATEMENTS
A. Every efficient-core selection has E(N)/T >=(N+6)/8 at some designated profile
   in the N=2g pool family. No symmetry, continuity or objective is required.
B. Every fixed B<2 budget-bounded baseline has a linear lower sequence, with
   explicit coefficient from m>1/(2-B) and gamma=1-(B-1)m/(m-1).
C. For each fixed finite C>=1 and fixed r>1, the seed minimizing sum x_i^r over
   nonnegative covers with sum x_i<=CT has true-envelope funding Omega_C(N).
   At C=2 the coefficient is 1/11 up to one owner. A fixed Q with C_Q>C gives
   factor [(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)](N-2) for C>1. The NC upper establishes owner order
   along the lower sequence, not matching constants or fixed-dimension sharpness.
D. For the uncapped quadratic cover selector, no universal A sqrt(N) envelope
   upper exists; the explicit sequence exceeds
   (1/40)sqrt(N)(log N/log log N)^(1/3). C does not tend to infinity in C's theorem;
   the uncapped statement has a separate proof. Its fixed-Q sharp refinement is
   restricted to the specified common-veto realization.
