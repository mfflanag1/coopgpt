HOUGAARD–PELEG–OSTERDAL: CONVEX-GAME PRECEDENT VERIFIED
https://www.economics.ku.dk/research/publications/wp/2003/0329.pdf
Direct download succeeded. Because the embedded text font is corrupt, root
rendered and visually inspected printed p5 (generalized Lorenz definition) and
p7 (Theorem1 statement and proof start). It maximizes a sum of strictly concave
differentiable utilities over an efficient core; the power selector is included
on nonnegative core allocations by u_i=-|x_i|^r. Theorem1's positive coalitional-
monotonicity result requires convex games. Our F_j example is nonconvex: two
coalitions sharing h_j but using respectively all other high and low owners each
have value1, their union value1 and intersection0. No claim about simultaneous
multi-coalition changes inside convex games was added.
