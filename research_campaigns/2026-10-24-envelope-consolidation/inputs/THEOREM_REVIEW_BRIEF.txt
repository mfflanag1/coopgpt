SPECIALIST REVIEW BRIEF: FIXED-CAP MINIMUM-NORM ENVELOPES
Internal preparation only,22 October2026. Nothing has been sent.
Read with the frozen contract and proof; historical priority remains open.

THE QUESTION IN PLAIN TERMS

A producer pools resources owned by different participants. Payments must
cover what every group could produce on its own. A proposed way to discourage
withholding is to first choose a balanced-looking covering payment vector,
then give each owner the largest payment it could obtain from that choice
by reporting less. This last operation guarantees payment monotonicity but
can raise the total cost. Does imposing a budget cap on the initial choice
control that inflation? For squared-norm minimization, no fixed cap does.
The same result now covers every fixed power objective sum_i x_i^r, r>1.

EXACT CONTRACT

Fixed actual owners i=1,...,N report b_i>=0. One fixed finite minimum of
nonnegative linear forms F determines v_b(S)=F(sum_{i in S}b_i). The seed
q^C(b) uniquely minimizes sum_i x_i^2 subject to
 x_i>=0, x(S)>=v_b(S) for every S, and sum_i x_i<=C*v_b(N),
where C>=1 is a fixed constant. The envelope is
 E_i(b)=sup_{0<=a_i<=b_i}q_i^C(a_i,b_-i).
All nonnegative real underreports are admitted. The seed cap C and the
envelope's final funding ratio are different quantities. No private-cost,
full-outside-option or coalition-strategyproof contract is claimed.

PROPOSED THEOREM

For every fixed finite C>=1 there are arbitrarily large actual owner counts
N and one fixed production technology and full profile for each instance
such that sum_i E_i(b)/v_b(N)=Omega_C(N).
At C=2, a concrete family gives (N-1)/11.
For C>1 choose a fixed integer Q>=3 with
 C_Q=Q-1-(Q-3)/(2Q+5)>C.
Then the normalized lower is
 [(C-1)/(2Q^2-7)]*(N-2)
along a specified unbounded sequence. The claim is not for every prescribed
N and does not cover caps chosen as functions of N.

The linear owner order is sharp for fixed C. At any underreport,
 q_i^C(a_i,b_-i)<=C*T(a_i,b_-i)<=C*T(b),
so E_i(b)<=C*T(b) and sum_i E_i(b)<=N*C*T(b). Zero-output profiles have
zero envelope. This upper uses only the seed cap and monotone production;
the substantive contribution is the matching linear lower along a sequence.
It does not fix the resource dimension or yield a pointwise lower for every N.

The underlying forms can have binary coefficients. Owner identities and
resource bundles are not changed during any incentive comparison. The
technology is the same for all selected unilateral witnesses. The simple
efficient competing rule on the fixed-bundle downward domain emphasizes
that this is a failure of the proposed architecture, not a lower bound
for arbitrary eligible mechanisms.

LOAD-BEARING CERTIFICATE

The previous q-ary construction supplies a transitive row family with
n=Q^d private owners, one common veto, h=d/[2(Q+2)], and d any positive
multiple of16Q(Q-1)(Q+2). At each focal private owner its retained incident
rows have exactly d-h neighbors. Every off-owner row has at least d
neighbors. A rational mixture of retained rows has focal marginal1,
neighbor marginal a0=(d-h)/[(Q-1)d], and outside marginal b, satisfying
 h(a0-b)>1-b>0.
Its binary incident-row deletion is realized by a genuine underreport of
that owner's control resource; all other owners' reports remain unchanged.

Put D=d+h, p=h/D and z=1/D. The candidate seed pays p to the focal owner
and veto, z to all(Q-1)d neighbors, zero to all outside private owners.
Its cost is C_Q. Retained own rows receive2p+(d-h)z=1; off rows receive
at least p+dz=1. With the half-squared-norm objective, use
 lambda=(p-z)/(1-a0), eta=lambda-p.
Give retained rows their mixture weights times lambda, the upper budget
multiplier eta, and each outside zero coordinate nonnegativity multiplier
 eta-lambda*b>0. The displayed strict inequality is exactly the condition
for that last sign. All other stationarity and complementarity identities
hold. This is a complete optimum certificate, including ALL off-owner
rows, not just the rows supporting the multipliers.

To obtain any smaller fixed cap C>1, add an owner with additive worth
A=(C_Q-C)/(C-1). Covering factors into the old constraints and that owner's
singleton lower bound A. At a norm minimizer its payment is exactly A;
the residual old budget is C(1+A)-A=C_Q. The old optimizer is unchanged.
Translation gives the focal payment p to each of the n owners at its own
selected underreport. Summing those lower bounds proves the theorem.

FIXED-POWER EXTENSION.
For every fixed real r>1, minimize sum_i x_i^r with the same cap. The
identical candidate is optimal once d is sufficiently large. With the
objective normalized by1/r, replace the multipliers by
 lambda=(p^(r-1)-z^(r-1))/(1-a0), eta=lambda-p^(r-1).
The outside nonnegativity sign is equivalent to
 h^(r-1)*(a0-b)>1-b.
Part I of the inherited source defines delta=1/[4Q(Q-1)] and
beta0=mu+delta; its construction gives a0-b>delta/2 on the chosen sequence.
Choose h^(r-1)>=2/delta. This proves the sign for every fixed r>1, with
no numerical approximation to a real power. Strict convexity certifies
uniqueness, and the additive-owner reduction is unchanged. The coefficient
in the theorem is independent of r; its starting dimension need not be.
The strict-convexity argument does not cover r=1 or a power varying with N.
At r=1 separately, minimizing total payment selects an efficient core for
any C>=1; the all-core-selector obstruction applies to any deterministic
tie-breaking. Thus r=1 is not an escape from the architecture obstruction.

The C=1 endpoint needs no q-ary proof: every efficient-core selector has
linear envelope inflation on the separate k-pool,2k-owner family.

WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, AND WHAT IS BEING PROPOSED

Established ingredients include static coalition covers, Euclidean
projection/KKT optimality, efficient-core least-squares selection,
minimum-of-additive representations, and finite flow/transport arguments.
An existing egalitarian-claims proof also underlies the lexicographic
monotonicity lemma used elsewhere in the campaign; it is not the theorem
being proposed here.

The candidate contribution is the quantitative fixed-owner, cross-report
funding obstruction, the exact capped certificate, and the additive-owner
reduction that treats every fixed cap. The q-ary row-distribution and
simultaneous production-realization lemmas came from the previous campaign
and are substantive dependencies, not newly proved in this extension.

REVIEW QUESTIONS

1. Does the q-ary distribution/all-cut argument establish the required
uniform marginal and off-owner conditions for every stated parameter?
2. Does the fixed production realization preserve every off-owner coalition
value while implementing all translated witnesses with the same owners?
3. Are the cap multiplier, zero-coordinate signs, and additive factorization
correct? Is any unlisted full-coalition constraint relevant?
4. Is the C=1 versus C>1 separation and the fixed-cap quantifier precise?
   Does the power extension correctly separate fixed r>1 from varying r?
5. Is this funding phenomenon already known under another regularization,
aspiration, monotone-selection or resource-withholding formulation?
6. What economic use, if any, justifies this exact seed-and-envelope rule?
The paper should not claim broad institutional relevance without an answer.
