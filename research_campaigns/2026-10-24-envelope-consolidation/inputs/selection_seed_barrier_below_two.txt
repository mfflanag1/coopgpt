EVERY SEED CAP B<2 HAS A LINEAR OWN-ENVELOPE OBSTRUCTION
22 October 2026. Coordinator's multi-owner extension, independently checked.

THEOREM. Fix B with 1<=B<2. Choose any integer m>=2 satisfying
 m>1/(2-B),
and write epsilon=B-1 and gamma=1-epsilon*m/(m-1)>0. For every k>=2 there
is one fixed concave homogeneous technology with n=km actual owners and
finite profiles F_j,L_ij such that every nonnegative covering seed q with
total at most B*T at those profiles has an actual own-report envelope E
satisfying, at at least one F_j,
 (sum_owner E_owner(F_j))/T >= gamma*[1+(k-1)/(2m)].
For fixed B and m this is Omega(n). No norm, symmetry, efficiency, or
regularity condition is placed on q. The bad full profile may depend on q.

TECHNOLOGY AND PROFILES.
There are k disjoint pools, each with m actual scalar-capacity owners: one
designated high owner h_j and m-1 designated low owners. Production is the
minimum of the k pool capacity sums. Choose a fixed R>=m.
A small pool has all m capacities equal to 1. A large pool has high-owner
capacity R and each low-owner capacity 1. At F_j only pool j is small; at
L_ij exactly pools i,j are small. Every displayed profile has T=m, and
L_ij is obtained from F_j by the single owner h_i underreporting R to 1;
the reverse role comparison from F_i changes only h_j.

LOW-PAYMENT BOUND.
At either profile, take the coalition consisting of all small-pool owners
and the high owner of every large pool. It has worth m. All omitted owners
are exactly the large-pool low owners. If L is their total seed payment,
coverage and the seed budget give L<=epsilon*m.

SMALL-POOL CHARGE AT F_j.
Fix one owner a in the small pool. For each large pool independently choose
one of its m-1 low owners, and form the coalition with a and those choices.
It has worth 1. Averaging its covering inequalities over the finite uniform
choices gives
 q_a(F_j)+L/(m-1)>=1.
Summing this inequality over the m small-pool owners gives
 q(pool_j;F_j)>=m-m*L/(m-1)>=m*gamma.

PAIR CHARGE AT L_ij.
Take h_i,h_j and one low owner from each large pool, again averaging
uniformly over the low choices. Each such coalition has worth 1, so
 q_{h_i}(L_ij)+q_{h_j}(L_ij)>=1-L/(m-1)>=gamma.
For k=2 there are no large pools and the empty choice average has L=0,
which only strengthens the bound.

ENVELOPE AVERAGING.
At F_j, E dominates q on all small-pool coordinates and dominates
q_{h_i}(L_ij) on each i!=j by the displayed genuine own underreport. Thus
 sum_owner E_owner(F_j)>=m*gamma+sum_{i!=j}q_{h_i}(L_ij).
Summing over all j, using the pair charge once per unordered pair, and
dividing by k*m yields the asserted funding lower bound.
The conclusion also applies to any expected seed allocation, so arbitrary
randomization before enveloping does not repair it.

EXACT THRESHOLD OF THIS FINITE TEST.
The charge becomes zero at B=2-1/m. For k>=3 this threshold is sharp for
these particular comparisons: use the efficient small-pool allocation at
F_j; at L_ij select any third, large pool ell and pay its high owner m and
each of its m-1 low owners 1. This covers every coalition, since production
is at most m and at most capacity in pool ell. Its cost is 2m-1, and it
pays neither h_i nor h_j. This is a finite-profile assignment only, not an
eligible all-profile mechanism. Taking m arbitrarily large closes every
fixed seed cap B<2, but the proof makes no claim at B=2 or above.

SCOPE AND CONSTANT ARBITRARY-MECHANISM COROLLARY.
The LINEAR conclusion is a lower bound for own-report envelopes of uniformly
low-subsidy covering seeds, not a growing lower bound for arbitrary eligible
mechanisms. There is a weaker direct consequence for those mechanisms: an
own-monotone mechanism equals its own envelope. Substituting its factor beta
for B above gives, with c=1+(k-1)/(2m),
 beta >= c*(2m-1)/(m-1+c*m).
This tends to2-1/m as k grows, and then approaches2 as m grows. It is a
constant obstruction, dominated asymptotically by the recorded unrestricted
log/loglog lower rate; it does not pass the continuation gate. The algebraic
inequality remains valid when its intermediate gamma is nonpositive.
One technology and one fixed owner set are used throughout each instance.
All relevant production levels are the same; variable-T effects play no role.
