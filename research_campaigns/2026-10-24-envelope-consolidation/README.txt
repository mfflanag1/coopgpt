WHAT IS CHECKED
- efficient/below-two pool inequalities on finite instances;
- 12 rational capped KKT parameter cases and 6 additive-padding reductions;
- 32 scaled power-gradient cases, including exponents near one;
- q-ary local repair, marginal and parameter checks, including composite Q;
- uncapped amplification arithmetic, selected real-report cap checks;
- sharp fixed-Q refinement's scalar inequalities and limits.
These are finite diagnostics. They do not enumerate the huge final production
games, establish all-real incentives by testing, solve the all-cut transport
network explicitly, or verify the proof in a proof assistant. The written proofs
do that conventional mathematical work; review records state their own scope.
