The measurement boundary matters

  • Reported does not mean validated. Differences can reflect nonlinear valuation, approximations, conventions or source problems. Paired metrics alone do not distinguish those explanations.
  • Direction and units need checking. A positive sensitivity is not automatically a gain on an upward rate move. Do not flip signs or infer dollar precision from padded decimal zeros.
  • Nodes are not a complete stress surface. Summing absolute residuals summarizes a vector. It does not reconstruct a simultaneous nonlinear yield-curve shock.
  • Ownership remains real. Aggregating observations for a research summary does not authorize transfers between funds. Portfolio mark-to-market changes are not automatically derivative margin calls.

The earlier exact identification example remains relevant: public aggregate and individual risk summaries need not recover coalition risk. This disclosure audit does not overcome that missing information.

This is an audit opportunity, not a novelty confirmation

The SEC’s 2026 proposal already explains the convexity motivation for the paired metrics and discusses currency-reporting inconsistencies. We cite the proposal for those observations, without assuming its changes became law.

Choi, Kim and Randall’s Hidden Duration studies fixed-income funds’ derivative exposure. We inspected relevant sections of its December 2024 draft and the newer March 2026 abstract; comparison against the latest full manuscript remains incomplete. A follow-up check of both authors’ research pages found SSRN links, not an independently inspected newer full draft.

The IMF’s October 2025 stress exercise, Annex 1.4, combines N-PORT with Lipper and models derivative-specific shocks. It already keeps gross forced sales separate from purchases elsewhere. Replacing its private inputs would create an adapted replication, not a full public replication or a new aggregation insight.

A worthwhile extension must show a material, validated measurement consequence and explain its incremental contribution. Simply observing that duration is imperfect or that gross and net differ is insufficient.
