The [six-hour campaign report](research_campaigns/2026-10-07-six-hour-campaign/REPORT.txt) now establishes a **sharp general payment bound**. For every finite nonnegative min-linear production technology with at most K supporting forms, fixed owners can report whole resource bundles while a deterministic rule covers every coalition and prevents profitable binding underreports. Worst-case total payments are **(1+o(1)) T log K / log log K**. The matching lower holds even on **simple directed acyclic networks**, with all auxiliary owners strategic. [Proof and verification index](research_campaigns/2026-10-07-six-hour-campaign/INDEX.txt).

The same work gives an **O(n/log n)** owner-count upper for every finite-valued monotone concave homogeneous production function, plus a finite-sample rule with an exact all-profile certification procedure. Certification can require exponential preprocessing; subsequent evaluation uses polynomially many LP calls. Conventional proofs, independent model reviews and exact checks are recorded. Novelty, human specialist validation and field-level significance remain unconfirmed. These results and the remaining accepted extensions are now incorporated into the [truthful-production manuscript](paper5/truthful_cooperation/manuscript.tex) and the [sharp-protection manuscript](paper5/sharp_subsidy/manuscript.tex). The [7 October consolidation record](review_packages/2026-10-07/consolidation/README.md) maps proofs, exact checks and internal reviews. Existing PDF exports are historical; current sources are authoritative. The authorized six-hour campaign completed on **7 October 2026**. The [continuous-report extension](research_campaigns/2026-10-05-continuous-pools/INDEX.txt) now gives an explicit rule for **every real report in [1/r,1]**, with at most two pools altered from all-unit: **total payment ≤7T**, or **≤8T with the original fairness floor**, uniformly in pool count, owner count and report-grid refinement. It revises the earlier binary payments. The formula is own-report monotone and covers all coalitions; a complete proof, independent model reviews and exact labeled checks are recorded. The [two-altered-pool extension](research_campaigns/2026-10-05-two-altered-pools/INDEX.txt) now preserves the nonlinear construction at **total payment ≤2T**, or **≤3T with the original fairness floor**, when two pools simultaneously contain mixtures of reports **1/r and 1**. It covers all coalitions, respects every retained own-report change, and agrees exactly with the earlier one-pool boundary. The proof covers all k,r; exhaustive labeled checks and independent model reviews support it.
