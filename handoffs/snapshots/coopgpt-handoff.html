01 / The new positive result

A public participation floor, with an exact funding price.

One owner A controls local energy allowances across pods. A second owner C controls shared grid capacity. Each hardware owner Hi contributes hardware at one pod. Output needs hardware and either that pod’s local energy or shared grid power.

The contract guarantees the local-energy owner a fixed public share θ of its marginal contribution. A truthful, coalition-covering mechanism achieves the optimal uniform funding factor:

Cθ = 1 + θ/4,   0 ≤ θ ≤ 1.

At a 100% contribution-share floor, total funding is at most 125% of production value. Some admissible instances require that much. This is a payment guarantee, not a measured energy saving.

The result holds on every real nonnegative report profile and over arbitrarily many pods. Uniform sharpness does not mean every individual instance requires the worst-case subsidy.

Model, assumptions and explicit payment formula

Reports are binding resource commitments. Owners are fixed and may withhold their own resources coordinatewise; utility is payment only. The theorem does not establish incentives for private values, private opportunity costs, overreports, collusion or false names.

ℓᵢ = min(hᵢ, Lᵢ)       rᵢ = hᵢ − ℓᵢ
L = Σ ℓᵢ                Q = Σ rᵢ
T = L + min(P, Q)       G = min(P, L + Q)
M = T − G              Required floor: y_A ≥ θM

At θ = 1 and L > 0:
xᵢ = ℓᵢ · clip((P − Q + rᵢ) / L, 0, 1)
X = Σ xᵢ
y_A = M,   y_Hᵢ = xᵢ,   y_C = G − max(X − L/4, 0).

At L = 0: pay C = G and every other owner 0.
For other θ: mix this rule with (0, min(P,Q), (ℓᵢ)ᵢ).
Total funding = T + θ · min(X, L/4) ≤ (1 + θ/4)T.

The unit-value mechanism uses O(m) arithmetic operations. The lower-bound family has even m, with hi = Li = 1 and P = m/2; all owners and resources are active. For a single hardware pod, an efficient truthful floor rule exists, so the universal worst-case statement must not be read as sharpness for m = 1.

Public heterogeneous values: what extends, and what does not

Each pod may have an arbitrary fixed public value vi ≥ 0, provided that value is the same whether it is served by local or shared power. Sort values with a fixed tie order and write the coalition value as a weighted sum of prefix games:

Fv(S) = Σk (vk − vk+1) Tk(S).

A greedy fractional-knapsack allocation simultaneously realizes these prefix outputs. Each prefix retains the same actual owners. Combining its payments with fixed nonnegative weights preserves truthfulness, coalition coverage and the marginal-contribution floor, with the same uniform factor 1 + θ/4.

The direct implementation is O(m²) after sorting. Route-specific values, private values, heterogeneous input coefficients and arbitrary mixed ownership are outside this theorem. The decomposition is an established ingredient; priority of the combined guarantee remains unconfirmed.

A separate incentive premium is still open

Let Bθ(b) be the cheapest coalition-covering, floor-respecting payment at one fixed profile, without cross-report incentive constraints. For a single all-profile mechanism, define C as its worst total-funding / production ratio and K as its worst total-funding / Bθ ratio.

C + K ≥ 2 + θ/2   (m ≥ 2).
The constructed rule attains C = K = 1 + θ/4.

This is a tradeoff for the same mechanism, not a statement adding two separately optimized infima. Minimizing K alone is unresolved. At θ = 1, the optimal K lies between 6/5 and 5/4. For m = 1, an efficient rule has C = K = 1; that exception was incorporated during the audit.

02 / Consolidated manuscript

General bounds are now in the paper.

For finite-valued monotone, concave, homogeneous production with n fixed owners and d resource dimensions, the manuscript now gives complete proofs of:

Σi yi(b) ≤ min { d,   n√(2/(n+1)),   1 + 2√d } · T.

What is consolidated

The general owner/dimension bounds are in the main manuscript. The supplement contains square-root-order barriers for specified fixed-mask/common-support architectures, including maximum-marginal caps. Boundary discontinuities are covered.

The earlier sharp leading-constant-one asymptotic theorem, (1 + o(1)) log K / log log K for at most K supporting forms or structural cuts, remains unchanged.

What remains separate

The new energy participation theorem and its public-values extension are research supplements. The architecture barriers are not unrestricted mechanism lower bounds, and the unrestricted owner/resource-dimension gap remains open.

All three current LaTeX sources compile. The specialist source/check packet is ready. Existing 14 October PDF exports are historical and are not presented as current versions here.

03 / The unresolved general target

Arbitrary mixed ownership still needs a new argument.

Allow fixed owners to hold arbitrary bundles of hardware, local energy and grid capacity, with both owner and resource counts unbounded. A uniform truthful funding factor below 2 for this depth-two class remains unproved.

Residual compositionCoalition coverage and ≈1.77591 budget factorAn exact three-owner example violates own-report incentives.
Nested two-pool ruleCoalition coverage and ≈1.40171 budget factorAn exact four-owner example violates own-report incentives.
Monotone-envelope analysisA valid response-sign and ½-Lipschitz lemmaExact examples defeat the proposed influence-charge estimates.
These failures do not prove that the actual monotone envelope needs factor 2. The rejected estimates can overcount envelope gains. The next proof must couple an owner’s own payment loss with its effects on other accounts, or construct an obstruction for the actual envelope.

The bounded campaign’s positive-progress gate is unmet for this general target. A larger finite grid, another bounded family or another failed loose inequality would not resolve it.
