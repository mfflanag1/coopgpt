The results worth carrying forward

1. Sharp funding in technology complexity

B(K) = (1 + o(1)) log K / log log K

For production given by the minimum of at most K fixed nonnegative linear forms, there is a single deterministic rule with individual own-report monotonicity and coalition coverage at every real nonnegative profile. The matching worst-case lower bound already holds on simple directed acyclic networks with one fixed strategic owner per edge.

The leading constant is one. K may be exponential in the encoded instance; this is not a polynomial preprocessing result. The general owner/resource bounds are min{d, n√(2/(n+1)), 1+2√d}. Their unrestricted optimal rates remain open.

2. Mixed ownership in the actual envelope

F = min(H, P + Σⱼ min(Hⱼ,Lⱼ)),   total payment ≤ 1.615 F

The prior ownership campaign established the 323/200 bound for the actual own-report monotone envelope, with arbitrary fixed mixed bundles across roles and branches and unrestricted finite owner/branch counts. 3. Sharp coalition contracts for general concave production

BTCWM(d) = (d+1)/2Boutside(d) = 1 + c(d−1),   c ≈ 0.5430633898

These are sharp worst-case total-output funding factors over finite-valued nonnegative, monotone, concave, degree-one technologies on d nonnegative resources. Lower examples allow growing finite owner populations; they do not say every technology needs the worst factor.

Weighted two-input resource graphs have sharp factor 3/2 for coalition payment monotonicity, even with mixed owners. For full outside production, arbitrary graphs have upper bound 2; complete-multipartite unit-profit components attain 1+c. The floor-free general-graph gap is still [1+c,2].

Σ payments ≤ S + KₙG,   (n−4)/22 ≤ Kₙ ≤ n/2

Here S is standalone production and G is additional cooperative gain. In two-input min production, with n≥5 fixed owners each eligible for both inputs and the full report domain, singleton/pair full-outside protection has Θ(n) funding cost in this benchmark.

This distinguishes funding against total pooled output from funding solely against gains. The leading constant and the payment-only pair-TCWM analogue remain open.

Keep the contracts separate

Owners, ownership rights and technology remain fixed as reports change. Let yC be the coalition's total payment and δ its feasible withheld resources. Coverage means yC(b)≥F(Σi∈Cbᵢ). The incentive conditions differ:

Contract | Required comparison | Interpretation | 

Individual monotonicity | yᵢ(b)≥yᵢ(b−δᵢ) | One owner cannot improve its payment by underreporting. | 

Coalition monotonicity (TCWM) | yC(b)≥yC(b−δ) | Coordinating owners cannot increase their combined payment by withholding. | 

Full outside production | yC(b)≥yC(b−δ)+F(Σi∈Cδᵢ) | The comparison also compensates what withheld resources could jointly produce outside. | 

A protected coalition member can restore zero resources while another restores a positive amount. For reserve cones, feasible removals and remainders must belong to the cone. A total-payment factor B means subsidy at most (B−1)F. Static core existence does not imply any of these report-linked guarantees.

The scalar-demand counterpart of TCWM is established Strong Group Monotonicity. The proposed contributions concern quantitative funding bounds and resource-report domains, not inventing the incentive concept.
