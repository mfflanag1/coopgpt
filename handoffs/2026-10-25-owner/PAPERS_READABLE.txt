P1 — Acyclicity and simultaneous protection of individual core maxima

EXACT CLAIM
For a fixed connected allowed interaction graph G on K>=2 equal-sized sectors, the worst leximin ideal fraction c(G) and the worst best-achievable simultaneous fraction R(G) both equal 1/K for trees and 0 for cyclic G. The triangle family has exact best simultaneous fraction 1/(2q+3).

ESSENTIAL LIMITS
- Nonnegative real pair matrices, productive full teams only, integral matching worth, positive grand worth and a nonempty entire core are essential. Zero pair matrices are allowed.
- Cyclic zero is an infimum over growing finite markets; every finite admissible market still has positive best simultaneous protection.
- Does not cover arbitrary productive partial teams; integral matching does not mean integer-valued input weights.

P2 — Sharp asymptotic separation of opposing egalitarian core selections

EXACT CLAIM
For singleton-normalized exact TU games with n players and positive total T, s_n=sup ||Lmin-Lmax||_1/T satisfies 2-s_n=2/n+O(log(n)/n^2), hence n(2-s_n)->2. Lmax minimizes descending sorted payoffs. A universal compact-convex overlap inequality and whole-core Fibonacci segments establish the asymptotic comparison.

ESSENTIAL LIMITS
- Not an exact finite-n formula, a unique-extremizer classification or an aggregate welfare-loss claim. Half the normalized L1 distance is the fraction of surplus changing recipients.
- The construction must identify the entire core; an arbitrary feasible segment inside the core is insufficient.

P3 — Fair core selections: a sharp boundary between pairs and triples

EXACT CLAIM
For unit-capacity nonnegative graph matching games with positive total T and nonempty entire core, the sharp normalized distance rho=||Lmin-Lmax||_1/T is floor(n/2)/(floor(n/2)+1) for n>=4 (and zero for n=2,3). P4 — Balanced transport at the egalitarian core: a complete boundary phase diagram

EXACT CLAIM
At a strict Lmin payoff boundary in a singleton-normalized exact game, let m be the number of poorer players and h the number of richer players. Slack of the richer suffix is possible iff m>=m_min(h), where m_min(1)=4, m_min(2)=m_min(3)=3, and m_min(h)=2 for h>=4. ESSENTIAL LIMITS

P7 — Sharp subsidies for protecting core maxima in three-sided assignment markets

EXACT CLAIM
Freeze H_i at each owner's individual maximum over the original entire efficient core, and minimize s(v)=sum_i y_i-T subject to all coalition constraints and y_i>=H_i/3. P8 — Sharp subsidies for truthful production and network cooperation

EXACT CLAIM
For f(c)=min_k w^k.c with at most K fixed nonnegative forms, the optimal worst-case all-profile total-payment factor under individual own-bundle monotonicity and all-coalition coverage is B_K=(1+o(1)) log(K)/log(log(K)). A capped hybrid proves the upper, and weighted report transitions give a matching leading-constant lower on simple DAGs with strategic auxiliary owners. For finite-valued monotone concave homogeneous production, including support-boundary discontinuities, the current general factor is min{d,n*sqrt(2/(n+1)),1+2*sqrt(d)}.

ESSENTIAL LIMITS
- Incentives concern binding coordinatewise underreports and payment-only individual utility, not collusion, outside production, private costs, overreports or identity splitting.
- K can be exponential in a compact network description; polynomial-time evaluation or certification is not guaranteed.
- Restricted fixed-mask/common-support square-root barriers do not settle unrestricted owner/dimension rates. Choosing among general upper rules uses fixed public parameters before reports, not a pointwise minimum of payment vectors.

P9 — The cost of coalition withholding: resource dimension and coalition size

EXACT CLAIM
Sharp universal coalition factors D_d=(d+1)/2 and D_d^OUT=1+c(d-1), where c*(2-exp(-1/c))=1. For sparse packing, K_r(s)<=K_r^OUT(s)<=r*s^(r-1); arbitrary-rule arity-three lower K_3(s)>=sqrt(s/6), with sharper B_s asymptotic to sqrt(2*pi*s)/(3*sqrt(e)) and B_6=493/243. The graph section gives full-OUT factor 1+1/sqrt(2) on unit-profit P4, complete blow-ups and disjoint unions; a separate P6 example blocks the defined fixed-leaf-reserve layerwise recursion.

ESSENTIAL LIMITS
