COOPGPT: SIX-TO-EIGHT-HOUR RESEARCH SCOPING CAMPAIGN
Prepared 3 October 2026. This is a proposed goal; the campaign has not started.

OBJECTIVE
Determine whether the audited core-maximum protection results support a research
target with credible significance beyond the current assignment-market class.
Select one precise next target only if there is evidence for it. The intended
outcome is a defensible research decision, not a promised field-level breakthrough.

Seek either (A) a transferable compatibility theorem with a substantive second
application, or (B) a currently unresolved, explicitly sourced problem whose
missing step is plausibly addressed by our techniques. Returning NO-GO with a
proved obstruction or an exact unresolved bottleneck is successful scoping.

TIME AND OPERATING LIMITS
Aim to decide within six hours; eight hours is the hard ceiling after launch.
Record start time and account for wall time, including delegate and solver waits;
parallelism does not enlarge the budget. Stop early if the evidence is decisive.
At hour six, extend only to verify a specific candidate lemma/counterexample or
complete the decision and review, not to open another research direction.
Use free primary sources, existing local software and at most two simultaneous
research workers, with one solver process. Computational work is discovery or
bounded certification, never evidence of all-instance validity by itself.
No paid access, outreach, submission, cloud compute, scheduled continuation or
automatic launch of a subsequent long campaign. Preserve the two manuscripts.

For the profit games studied here, use the ORIGINAL ENTIRE core
  C={x>=0: sum_i x_i=T and x(Q)>=v(Q) for all coalitions Q}, T=v(N)>0,
and H_i=max_{x in C} x_i, with C nonempty. Freeze H before intervention.
For a fixed alpha define

with its own object and acceptance criteria, not an equivalent formulation.

QUESTIONS AND FIXED TEST CASES

1. Mechanism extraction.
Identify the weakest independently meaningful hypotheses behind the same-sector
range inequality, aggregation of comparisons between separate attaining core
points, and weighted residual witness budget. Distinguish consequences of generic
LP duality from additional compatibility forced by the market. An abstract theorem
that assumes the desired two bounds and averages them is not a new mechanism.

2. Within-class stress test, not the main significance claim.
Use K=4 equal-size finite sectors, nonnegative pair-additive productive full teams,
integral matching worth, positive T and nonempty original entire core; alpha=1/4.
Study the four-cycle sector graph and the complete four-sector graph. Re-read the
existing graph theorem: trees at alpha=1/K have zero subsidy and are calibration
only. The four-cycle is a complete-graph case with two zero pair matrices, so an
inherited upper bound is not a second application. Identify embeddings/padding
explicitly and do not count them as new structural breadth. Work symbolically before trying numerical instances.

A prior planning calculation, NOT an audited theorem, suggests for V_K=K W+S:
  V_K <= (3K-4)T-(K-2) beta-E/(K-1),
  V_K <= K T+beta-C2/k,
where W is fractional full-team worth, q are residual player loads,
S=sum q_i H_i, k is common sector residual mass, beta=sum q_i u_i,
C2=sum q_i^2 u_i, and E=sum_(a,j)(sum_(b!=a) q_(b,j))(d_j-H_(a,j)).
Here d_j is optimal diagonal-team worth and u_(a,j) its incident diagonal pair
budget. First rederive or refute this calculation, including repeated labels,
real fractional loads, k=0, and attaining-witness orientation. Unequal beta
coefficients can be canceled by unequal positive weights; they are not alone an
obstruction. At K=4, weights 1/3 and 2/3 suggest V_4<=16T/3 and a subsidy bound
T/3 after the dual translation; verify the premises and translation. A routine
K-extension, even if valid, is a baseline rather than a reason by itself to claim
high impact. The substantive within-class questions are sharpness and a family
whose obstruction adds something beyond padding the three-sector construction.

3. Transfer test with a genuinely different coalition model.
Default: finite directed acyclic max-flow profit games, with one player per arc,
nonnegative rational capacities and designated source s and sink t. A coalition
owns its arcs and has the maximum s-t flow achievable using only those arcs.
Require T>0 and every directed s-t path to have at most THREE arcs. Set alpha=1/3
before any search. Allow unequal capacities and arc sharing across paths; do not
restrict calculations to dual/Owen-set payoffs. The predeclared conjecture to
prove or refute is s_(1/3)<=T/3 for every game in this class. This is speculative,
not an established bound or sharpness claim. Seek an independently meaningful
compatibility lemma, an exact counterexample, or the precise missing hypothesis.
This bounded class is a scoping testbed, not a claim that its solution alone
would be a field-level breakthrough. Explain any intended extension beyond it.

Calibrate the restriction: a single path of m unit-capacity arcs is a unanimity
game with T=1, H_i=1, and s_alpha=max(0,m alpha-1). Thus unrestricted path length
rules out a size-independent subsidy bound at fixed positive alpha. Reproduce
this directly; do not spend the campaign rediscovering it. The generic construction
y=max(x,alpha H) for any x in C gives s_alpha<=alpha(sum H-T), for 0<=alpha<=1.
For any fixed acyclic maximum flow f, removing all path flow through e and applying
core coverage to N\{e} gives H_e<=T-v(N\{e})<=f_e. Hence sum H<=sum f<=3T and
s_(1/3)<=2T/3 in this test class. Recheck these preparation arguments in
review/COORDINATOR_CHECKS.txt; they are baselines, excluded from the GO gate.
The proposed T/3 target must improve on this generic 2T/3 bound.

Probe nontriviality early: cap this probe at 45 minutes by hour three and reserve
at most the first three of the twelve total instances for it. Seek an exact
positive-subsidy instance, a proof of identically zero subsidy, or report UNKNOWN.
Finite zero examples do not prove universal zero; UNKNOWN is not a counterexample
to the transfer. A new nontrivial zero-subsidy theorem could itself be substantive.
A positive example or a checked, nontrivial universal-zero argument is required
before an unconditional GO on this transfer route; otherwise its status remains
conditional or inconclusive, even if the proposed upper bound looks plausible.

If the flow test is already settled by a checked source/reduction, or a checked
elementary argument makes it routine, permit ONE predeclared fallback: nonnegative
rational 3-uniform hypergraph matching games, players=vertices, coalition worth=maximum
integral weight of disjoint hyperedges inside the coalition, T>0, C nonempty,
alpha=1/3. No tripartition or pair-additivity is assumed. Document how it differs
from the arbitrary-triple archives and whether known bounded-rank arguments
already settle it. A failed flow proof attempt alone does not justify a pivot.

4. Published-problem alternative.
Cap this scout at 45 minutes total. Inspect ONE primary lead: the geometry of
equitable assignment-core points in Vazirani (arXiv:2402.11437v6, conclusion).
Only if that lead is already resolved or demonstrably mismatched to our tools,
use the remaining scout budget on combinatorial algorithms for equitable Owen-set
allocations in MST/minimum-branching games in Gangam et al. (arXiv:2403.06037v3,
introduction). Do not attempt to solve both.
Record the exact question, source location/version, later developments, known
barriers, and a concrete lemma connecting our tools to the missing step. Authored
open does not mean still open, important, or accessible to our method. An unfinished
status search is a named gap, not permission to assume the problem remains open.

PHASES AND GATES
Hours 0-2: establish the source baseline and pre-register candidate statements.
Use existing source audits to select only claim-relevant original passages about
payoff-vector cost of stability, half-optimal cores, graph-width/packing-covering
bounds, and fair-core/Owen-set results. Do not repeat the whole portfolio audit.
Record what was actually read, free-access failures and unresolved Quint/Lucas/
Stuart comparisons; paid access is not a gate. The 45-minute published-problem
scout may run in parallel through hour four, but its total allowance stays fixed.
Freeze at most three candidate claims before testing: one K=4 claim, one transfer
claim, and at most one published-problem bridge. State which hypotheses are new
and what would falsify each claim. Start the bounded flow nontriviality probe.

Hours 2-4: rederive the K=4 baseline and isolate a candidate compatibility lemma.
Give a proof, an explicit counterexample, or an exact missing hypothesis. Cap
routine K-extension proof work at 45 minutes, with at most another 45 minutes for
sharpness or a genuinely different lower-bound mechanism. This is a ceiling, not
a quota: prioritize the transfer, including the early nontriviality probe. Stop
a route that merely restates the target as its own missing lemma.

Hours 4-5.5: stress-test the transfer and check the strongest competing explanation
from the literature. If computation is needed, pre-register at most twelve small
instances across all tests, state the instance limits and provenance, use exact
certificate checks, and cap each solve at five minutes with one retry overall.
For small flow instances enumerate ALL coalitions when checking the entire core;
do not treat path inequalities or a dual-optimal-payoff restriction as equivalent
without proof. Unknown, timeout and unrecovered floating-point output remain
unresolved. No new general-purpose solver framework or expanding size census.

Hours 5.5-6: write the decision and submit the strongest claim and source comparison
to an opposite-model reviewer, then independently adjudicate its findings.
Start review earlier when the candidate is stable. A review opinion is neither
execution nor proof. If needed, use hours 6-8 only for that bounded verification
and final synthesis. An unfinished review remains explicit and cannot support
an unconditional GO verdict.

GO / NO-GO CRITERIA
Recommend a longer mathematical campaign only if at least one route has:
- a precise target and dated primary-source comparison separating known results;
- a checked nontrivial bridge lemma, counterexample identifying the exact repair,
  or structural certificate that changes the plausible attack;
- a specific remaining obstruction and at least one mechanism for attacking it;
- for a breadth route, an explicit connection to the different game class with
  the same frozen benchmark, beyond a generic LP reformulation or dummy padding;
- for a published-problem route, a current-status search and concrete relevance
  to the source's stated question, with significance argued separately;
- independent review with coordinator-verified responses and all residual gaps
  named. Unresolved material priority or review questions imply CONDITIONAL-GO
  at most, with the condition stated, not a novelty or breakthrough claim.

Do not count extra manuscript pages, tighter finite numerical values, routine
interpolation in alpha, a straightforward fourth-sector upper bound, or a solver
encoding alone as passing the significance gate. A new class-specific lemma is
allowed if it provides substantive insight; the goal is not to ban new ideas.
NO-GO is a legitimate scientific outcome. Distinguish a refuted conjecture from
an unproductive method and an inconclusive time-limited attempt.

DELIVERABLES AT STOP
Create a dated campaign folder with: (i) concise decision report; (ii) exact
candidate/obstruction statements and proof-state ledger; (iii) claim-specific
source table with read scope and open gaps; (iv) any reproducible exact checks
and negative controls; (v) independent review and adjudication; (vi) ONE copyable
follow-on goal if justified, otherwise the evidence-based reason to stop or redirect.
Rank alternatives qualitatively by significance, tool fit, novelty exposure,
verification strength and tractability; do not invent probabilities of success.
Label all evidence accurately. Do not describe the scoping result as a field-level
breakthrough merely because a pass criterion was met.
