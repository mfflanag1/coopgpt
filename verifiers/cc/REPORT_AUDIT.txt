Line 1616 reads "In every one of the $1041$ recorded failures exactly one
suffix was slack, so $H^*=\widehat H$ there and the choice of end in
Definition~\ref{mono:def:hstar} was invisible".  This is false.

verifiers/monotonicity/summary.json, statistics:
      "with_two_or_more_slack_boundaries": 494
      "multi_slack_exact_violations": 20
and its refinement_conjecture entry: "20/20 exact-domain violations over base
games with two slack boundaries, but only 5 distinct base games".
verifiers/monotonicity/summary2.json, sweep_statistics: "multi_viol": 10.

So at least 20 of the 1041 recorded failures sat on multi-slack bases, where
$H^*\ne\widehat H$ and the choice of end was NOT invisible.  The continuation
"multi-slack bases are rare under the harvest generators, which is why so much
sampling never met one" is wrong the same way: the harvest met 494 multi-slack
bases and produced 20 violations on them.

The true mechanism is different and worth stating correctly, because it is a
better cautionary tale: multi-slack violations DID occur, and in every one of
them $W$ still lay inside $H^*$ -- indeed the retired conjecture C-A' ("$W$ is
the innermost slack suffix") was tested on exactly those 20 and held 20/20.
What had never been sampled is not a multi-slack base but a multi-slack
violation whose loser ESCAPES the innermost suffix.  Suggested replacement:
"Multi-slack bases were sampled -- 494 of them, carrying 20 of the 1041
failures -- but in every one the loser set still lay inside $H^*$, so the two
readings of Definition~\ref{mono:def:hstar} were never separated by the data."

Line 1280 carries the same error in the definition's discussion ("the regime,
it will turn out, of every recorded failure but one").  It is additionally
inconsistent INTERNALLY with the paper's own line 1691, which reports 1,815
failures of the left inclusion in the n=6 corpus -- every one of which
necessarily has $H^*\ne\widehat H$.  "Every recorded failure but one" is off by
more than three orders of magnitude against the paper's own later count.

S2  HIGH -- WRONG COUNT, twice: paper4 lines 1641-1642 and line 1750
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Line 1641-1642: "the $31$ strict/tie patterns of $\phi$ with the choice of
slack cut give $32$ shapes".

At $n=5$ there are 4 adjacent pairs in the sorted payoff vector, so there are
$2^4=16$ strict/tie patterns and $15$ with at least one strict inequality
(a cut is needed for $H^*$/$\widehat H$ to exist).  The number is 15, not 31.
31 is $2^5-1$, the count of nonempty player subsets -- a different object.

The derived "32 shapes" is CORRECT but does not follow from 31: the shape count
is $\sum_{r=1}^{4} r\binom{4}{r} = 4+12+12+4 = 32$, i.e. each of the 15 gap
patterns paired with each of its own cuts.  Driver d2_lab.py enumerates exactly
this (`for r in range(1,n): for gaps in combinations(range(1,n), r): for cstar
in gaps`), and lab5b_right.txt/lab5_leftmin.txt each record 32 rows.

Line 1750 repeats the error in thm:expart's proof: "all $31$ stratum patterns
return infeasible".  The exact-partition run has NO cut choice (the group
constrains every suffix at once), so it ranges over the 15 gap patterns, and
lab output d23_expart.py records exactly 15 rows: {"sat": 0, "unsat": 15,
"unknown": 0}.  The number is 15, not 31.

S4  MODERATE -- PROOF-SCOPE GAP at k=1 (thm:expart, and hence cor:allfail)
    paper4 lines 1745-1760 (proof), 1770ff (corollary)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
thm:expart quantifies over exact-partition $\phi$.  A CONSTANT $\phi$ -- one
payoff stratum, $k=1$ -- has no richer suffixes at all, so it satisfies "every
richer suffix is tight" VACUOUSLY, and it has no slack richer suffix.  The
decision procedure did not cover it: it enumerates only NONEMPTY gap patterns,
i.e. $k\ge2$ (that is exactly why the count is 15 and not 16).  So as recorded,
neither thm:expart nor cor:allfail covers $k=1$, and cor:allfail ("every
five-player exact-domain monotonicity failure has a slack richer suffix") would
be FALSE if a failure existed there.

I closed the gap rather than only reporting it (driver d28_k1.py): the same
complete encoding with all five payoffs tied, exactness of $v$ and $v'$,
superadditivity, both core memberships, Lemma L for $\phi'$, and a strict loser
inside $S$ asserted, returns UNSAT.  So the conclusion stands; the proof needs
one added sentence, e.g. "the constant-$\phi$ case, vacuously exact-partition
and outside the gap-pattern enumeration, is infeasible by the same encoding
with all payoffs tied."
