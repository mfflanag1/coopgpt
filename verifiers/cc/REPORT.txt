Not a knife edge (driver d12_window.py): over the admissible exact window

Mechanism.  The base game has TWO slack richer suffixes.  In both paper4
examples, in the whole BT family and in the recorded 1041, the top stratum is
phi-tight and exactly ONE suffix is slack, so innermost and outermost slack
suffix coincide and mono:def:hstar's choice of end is invisible.  Here nothing
above the poorest stratum is tight, Proposition D shields nothing, and the
middle stratum {4} -- inside the outer slack suffix {4,5}, outside the inner
one {5} -- pays.  This is the multi-slack class summary2 records as rare
(2.1% of the untuned generator, 17.7% after tuning), and the only earlier
evidence bearing on the innermost/outermost distinction was C-A''s 20
violations from 5 base games.

Consequence for the two retired laws: this witness SATISFIES C-A (W = {4,5} is
a slack richer suffix -- the outer one), while mono:ex:notsuffix satisfies C-C
left and refutes C-A.  So C-A and C-C-left are incomparable and both false.

5.  NEW PROVED LEMMAS (all in PROOF.txt Part I, all with controls)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEMMA L  (leximin optimality certificate) -- necessary AND sufficient.  x in
  core(u) is leximin-maximal iff at every cut there are lambda_T >= 0 supported
  on proper x-tight coalitions and nu with cover = nu on the richer suffix and
  cover <= nu - 1 on the block.  Necessity by Farkas on the tangent cone;
  sufficiency by a sorted-vector argument.  Verified 31/31 + 2/2 positive,
  6/6 negative (d3_lemmaL.py).  This is what makes the lab a DECISION procedure
  and not merely a source of necessary conditions.
LEMMA D+  Prop D holds for EVERY proper phi-tight T, including T = S (where
  Delta(S) >= alpha > 0).  The exclusion of S in mono:prop:shield is not needed.
LEMMA C+  Lemma C holds for EVERY proper phi'-tight T =/= S, not only those
  with S not a subset of T: v' agrees with v at every coalition except S, so
  phi'-tight supersets of S also satisfy Delta(T) <= 0.  This strictly enlarges
  the usable certificate family.
LEMMA M   The two master inequalities: for a Lemma-L cover c of phi,
  sum_i c_i Delta_i >= 0; for a cover c' of phi', sum_i c'_i Delta_i <=
  lambda'_S * rho.  Their difference is the engine any proof of the right
  inclusion will have to run; the j = 1 instance of the first is the weighted
  form of the known descent lemma.
LEMMA WIN The admissible exact-bump window is the CLOSED INTERVAL
  (s_phi(S), alpha_ex(S)] with
      alpha_ex(S) = min_{T =/= S proper} max{ x(S) : x in core(v), x(T)=v(T) } - v(S),
  because min_{core(v^{S,alpha})} x(T) is nondecreasing in alpha.  Controlled in
  both directions on both paper4 games (exact at alpha_ex, inexact just above;
  reproduces the recorded windows (1/2,1] and d_max = 4).  This replaces
  grid-guessing of alpha by an exact endpoint and makes every sampled bump
  exact by construction.

REMAINING BLOCKER for a human proof of the right inclusion.  Suppose p in H*\S.
Lemma D+ gives Delta(T) >= 0 at all phi-tight T, Lemma C+ gives Delta(T) <= 0
at all phi'-tight T =/= S, and Delta(S) >= rho > 0.  What is missing is a
choice of Lemma-L certificate -- for phi at the cut j*, or for phi' at the
level of a loser -- whose cover vector makes (MA) and (MB) contradict each
other.  Because S = H* never occurs and the only proved link between S and the
suffix chain is Theorem B(ii) (phi_j >= alpha - s_phi(S) for outsiders), the
argument must be genuinely two-sided; a one-sided slack-budget count does not
close it.
