R2  MODERATE -- CORPUS CONFLATION in the new inversion paragraph
    paper4, the paragraph added at bb6b86c ("What the statement asserts also
    deserves a sharper reading")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The paragraph reads: "On certified exact bumps of exact-partition bases ($200$
at $n=5$, and $2{,}224$ at $n=6$ from a designed generator together with the
random search), the bump ALWAYS moves phi, ALWAYS produces strict losers, and
the loser set is ALWAYS slack without being a richer suffix ... with an
individual stratum redistributing internally in about three quarters of the
cases".

Those four properties were measured by ONE driver, d27_epredist.py, whose
corpora are 200 exact bumps at n=5 (epredist_5_7.json) and 80 at n=6
(epredist_6_9.json).  Its recorded keys are exactly delta_zero, any_loser,
loser_inside_S, internal_redistribution, loser_set_is_suffix,
loser_set_slack_not_suffix, top_stratum_moves.

The 2,224 figure is a different corpus: d24_ep.py (528) plus d25_epfam.py
(1,696).  Neither of those drivers records ANY of the four properties -- their
stat dictionaries carry only games/expart/(prefilter)/windows/bumps/
exact_bumps/viol.  They establish "0 failures", nothing else.

So the paragraph attributes to 2,224 bumps a set of measurements taken on 80.
The claims are true on what was measured; the attribution is not.  Suggested:
"(200 at n=5 and 80 at n=6)" for the four properties, keeping 2,224 where it
belongs -- as the corpus on which no failure occurred.  The "about three
quarters" figure is 144/200 and 64/80, i.e. 74% of 280, and should be attached
to the same 280.
