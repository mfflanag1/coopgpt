--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Full statements and proofs: PROOF.txt sections L1-L4.  All are consequences of
A3 (paper5 thm:n5closed) plus core membership of Lmin and Lmax; none needs a
computation.

 L1  the coalition attaining nu has >= 2 players and lies in the Lmax TOP
     stratum;  hence L1': |Lmax top stratum| >= 2.
 L2  the coalition attaining lambda lies in the Lmin BOTTOM stratum P and
     satisfies Lmax(S) <= mu|S|;  hence L2(c): mu >= min_{i in P} Lmax_i.
     This sharpens paper5 cor:floorceiling (min over ALL players) and is what
     gives L2' above.
 L3  2M + 3mu <= T, universally.
 L4  ||Lmin - Lmax||_1 <= 2|Abar|(M - mu), Abar = {i : Lmax_i > Lmin_i}.
     Tight at CFG: 2*2*(1/4 - 1/8) = 1/2.

      and L2 alone.  A hand proof plausibly exists for a large part of the

 (R3) NOT CHECKED HERE: n >= 6.  Pipeline 2 is fully dimension-independent

      configuration universe explodes and s(6) is a different number, so this
      is a new project rather than an experiment.  Worth doing; not attempted.
      Note the facet-description caveat that blocked n >= 6 elsewhere in the
      program does not apply to pipeline 2.
 (R4) The sharper statements of paper5 cor:cfg (the PINNED PAIR, not just the
      bound) were not re-examined; L2' removes its first extra hypothesis

8.  THE INDEPENDENT RE-DERIVATION, AND WHY T0 IS UNCONDITIONAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mid-run the lead supplied two theorems from task #23.  THEOREM A: v is exact iff
every coalition has an attaining core point -- exactness is existential and
linear, with no catalogue and no completeness assumption.  THEOREM B: Lmin is
characterized by a quantifier-free Farkas identity per (stage, player), with the
tight-support condition as a Boolean disjunction.  The leximax mirror needed
here was described only as "signs flipped"; it is RE-DERIVED FROM SCRATCH in the
docstring of farkas.py (tangent cone at psi, polar cone, membership of -e_i) and
comes out as: sigma enters with a PLUS sign and the right-hand side is -[j=i].

This yields pipeline 2 (closecfg3.py), which shares NO machinery with pipeline 1:

   pipeline 1 (closecfg/closecfg2)      pipeline 2 (closecfg3)
   exactness: 280 facet inequalities    exactness: Theorem A, 30 attaining
              as lazy separating cuts              core vectors, all linear
   Lmin/Lmax: pinning ladders, LP-      Lmin/Lmax: Theorem B + mirror, one
              dualized, dual polyhedron            Farkas identity per (stage,
              VERTICES enumerated,                 player), no reduced game, no
              reduced-game caps as                 preimage minima, no vertex
              minima over preimages                enumeration

     5250 floor certificates, 0 failures;  5250 ceiling certificates, 0 failures.
   Negative controls, on all 261 games where Lmin != Lmax: offering Lmin as a
