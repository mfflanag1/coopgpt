VERDICT UP FRONT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Conjecture 10 of verifiers/zp/PROOF.txt -- "PART(p,q) is realizable exactly
when p,q >= 1 and max(p,q) >= 3" -- is FALSE.  PART(2,2) IS realizable at
m = 4.  The threshold is on m, not on max(p,q):

        PART(p,q) is realizable  <==>  p,q >= 1  and  p + q >= 4.

BOTH halves are proved, with no residue.  Necessity: PART(1,1) by the n <= 4
rigidity, PART(1,2) and PART(2,1) by a decisive finite check.  Sufficiency:
three explicit games at m = 4, lifted to every larger m by a padding lemma
(adjoin a null player and put it on either side of the partition).

The ZP residue is therefore closed -- in the POSITIVE direction.  Suspending a
null player over the PART(2,2) witness (Lemma 4 of verifiers/zp/PROOF.txt)
produces an explicit seven-player exact game carrying a ZP(2,2) configuration
at m = 5.  The n = 7 resisting type B is REALIZABLE, not impossible.

        Lmin-tight proper coalitions:
          ad, abd, ade, acde, adf, acdf, cef, def, acdef
        S1 = cef, S2 = abd: tight, disjoint, covering, traces {c} and {d}

Adjoining a null player g (same rows with a seventh coordinate 0) gives the

Why the archive missed it: the census reported "PART(2,2) NEVER" over 8,431
random plus 11,396 targeted m=4 boundaries.  The present harvest hit it once in

rare configuration, not an absent one -- the same sampling artefact that
paper4 already warns about for split packets on five players.


HOW THE TWO BLOCKERS OF verifiers/zp/REPORT.txt WERE REMOVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The previous attempt stalled because exactness needed the min-semi-balanced
catalogue (converse unvalidated at n = 6) and because no relaxation captured
the leximin condition -- all three tried relaxations returned models that were
not realized at their own Lmin.  Both are now exact.

(1) EXACTNESS IS EXISTENTIAL.  v is exact iff every coalition S has a core
    point z^S attaining v(S).  One extra allocation vector per coalition; all
    linear; no catalogue, no vertex enumeration, no completeness assumption, at
    any n.  The n = 6 caveat in the brief simply does not arise: the n = 6 and
    n = 7 results here are unconditional.

(2) LEXIMIN IS A FARKAS CERTIFICATE.  Fix the stratification.  Stage t pins
    player i iff max{x_i} over the core, with earlier strata pinned and the
    free players held at or above the stage level, equals phi_i.  At phi the
    active constraints are exactly efficiency, the phi-TIGHT coalitions, the
    earlier pins, and the level bound on B_t only (it is slack on B_>t).  So
    the condition is "d_i <= 0 on the tangent cone", and Farkas turns it into
    an EXISTENTIAL certificate: nonnegative multipliers y supported on tight
    coalitions, satisfying one vector identity.  Complementary slackness kills
    the bilinear terms y_A v(A) that would otherwise appear, leaving the
    support requirement as a DISJUNCTION "y_A = 0 or phi(A) = v(A)".  Linear
    arithmetic plus Boolean structure -- decided exactly.

Both directions of both statements are proved in PROOF.txt (Theorems A and B),
so the encoding is an EQUIVALENCE.  UNSAT over every stratification is a proof
of impossibility; SAT is a candidate, and every SAT model here was audited end
to end.

ROUTE A (structural): WHAT IT GAVE AND WHERE IT STOPPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROVED, and new as far as this campaign records:

  BLOCK RIGIDITY.  In a PART configuration S1 and S2 are tight at EVERY core
  point, not just at phi -- because v(S1) + v(S2) = v(N) and two core
  inequalities summing to an equality must both be equalities.  The core is
  confined to the product of the two block faces.  This is the exact sense in
  which a PART configuration is "close to a direct sum", and it is the engine
  of the hand lemmas below.

  RICH-PAIR MERGE.  Contracting H = {f,g} to a single player r and
  renormalizing yields an exact singleton-normalized game w' whose core
  contains the image psi = (phi|_L, s) of phi.  Exactness of the contraction
  (M1) holds for ANY coalition of ANY exact game.

REFUTED, and recorded so it is not retried:

  The merge does NOT transport Lmin.  The two links needed to import the h = 1
  phase bound m_min(1) = 4 both fail at genuine PART configurations:
  (T1) s > max_L phi fails 5 times in 7 tested configurations -- at the
  harvested PART(1,3) witness s = 1/2 while max_L phi = 5/4, so r is not even
  the richest player of the merged game; (T2) psi = Lmin(w') fails 3 times in
  7, including at the PART(2,2) witness itself.  The negative control behaves
  correctly: at the (m,h) = (3,2) member of paper4's three-poorer escape family
  -- a slack h = 2 boundary with no PART pair, living at m = 3 -- (T1) fails, as
  it must.

  So the coincidence "PART threshold = m_min(1) = 4" is real but UNEXPLAINED.
  That is the sharpest remaining structural question, and it is a better
  question than the one the task started from, because the answer is now known
  to be 4 rather than conjectured to be 3.

ALSO PROVED BY HAND, confining the m = 3 case (but not closing it):
  * protectors: phi_i < phi_j gives a tight T with j in T, i not in T;
  * with |P| = 1, no tight set contains H and omits the single member a of P;
  * there is a tight T0 = R u {f} with R a nonempty subset of Q, and
    s_phi(R) <= phi_a;
  * null-player descent: a zero payoff in L drops a PART(p,q) at m to a
    PART(p-1,q) or PART(p,q-1) at m-1, so in a minimal PART configuration
    every poorer payoff is positive and no singleton is tight.


WHAT IS LEFT OPEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  A HUMAN PROOF that no PART configuration exists at m = 3.  The finite check
    settles it (48 UNSAT, two independent methods), and that is the same status
    class paper4 already uses for Theorems 'mthr' and 'h3'.  The hand lemmas
    above reduce the case to the interaction of the tight-set constraints with
    the leximin pinning conditions across 24 stratifications; the CEGAR shows
    the true case tree is small (25 to 155 nodes per stratification), so a
    human proof looks reachable but was not obtained.

2.  A STRUCTURAL REASON for the threshold 4.  The merge route is closed
    (Theorem 8).  What is wanted is a transfer that survives the fact that
    core(v) is strictly inside the product of the two block faces.

3.  DOWNSTREAM.  verifiers/zp/PROOF.txt Conjecture 10 and the paragraph "WHY
    THE THRESHOLD 3 IS PLAUSIBLE" are wrong and should be replaced; the n = 7
    census line "resisting type (2,2), OPEN" becomes "realized, explicit
    witness"; REPORT.txt's claim that the finite-feasibility route stops should
    be replaced by Theorems A and B, which are what unstick it.  (No repo file
    was edited by this task.)
