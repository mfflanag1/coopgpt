   NEW at n=6 - the RICH-OVERLAP lemma (RO).  For an exact singleton-
   normalized game, a core point phi and phi-tight S1, S2,
        sum_{i not in S1 u S2} phi_i  >=  s_phi(S1 ^ S2).
   Proof: exactness gives z in core with z(S1^S2) = v(S1^S2); modularity of
   z gives z(S1 u S2) = z(S1)+z(S2)-z(S1^S2) >= phi(S1 u S2)+s_phi(S1^S2);
   subtract from z(N) = phi(N) and use z >= 0.
   Consequence: if two lifts meet in a single RICH player j and exactly one
   POORER player i lies outside their union, then phi_i >= phi_j, which
   contradicts the strict boundary.  Every (P) shape of rows 24, 51, 52 has
   that configuration, so those three rows are unrealizable.
   (RO) tested exactly on 279 tight pairs from the
   coverage witnesses and on 367,291 tight pairs over 30,214 further random
   games: 0 violations.
   Re-checked shape by shape rather than class by class: rows 24, 51, 52
   have 18, 6, 18 enumerated (P) shapes respectively and EVERY one carries
   the killing configuration; no shape of any realized row does.

   WHAT RESISTED (targeted constructions, section 3 of the brief).
   Before the lemma was found, rows 24 and 52 were attacked with (i) the
   paper's three families plus 400 perturbations each, (ii) 83,332 hill-
   climbing evaluations over integer generator matrices scored by how many
   pair-traces of H are tight, (iii) 197,186 constructions in which phi
   itself is a generator, so that tightness of the required lifts is a
   linear condition on the difference vectors.  All failed, which is now
   explained: they are impossible.  The hill climb plateaued exactly at the
   configuration (RO) forbids.

(c2) THE STAR FAMILY, AND THE ONE EXCEPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

For a boundary H with poorer side L and nonempty proper A of L, the
full-trace STAR is
     1_{H u A} + sum_{i in L\A} 1_{H u {i}} - (|L|-|A|) 1_H = 1_N,
i.e. the (P) packet with the trivial collection {H}, lift H u A, spokes
over L\A, b = 1, delta = |L|-|A|.  At n=6 there are ten stars, all
catalogued:
   h=1: |A|=1,2,3,4 -> rows 48, 44, 35, 30
   h=2: |A|=1,2,3   -> rows 39, 33, 28
   h=3: |A|=1,2     -> rows 32, 27
   h=4: |A|=1       -> row 26

Every one of the ten is realized, and at h=1, 2, 4 the realized set is

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(c4) THE W1 FAMILY (split packets at h=2)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Provenance: W1 = family2(2,4) plus one extra generator = the family's own
phi row (2,2,4,4,4,4) with one unit moved from p1 to the last K player,
giving (1,2,4,4,4,5).  W2 = the same base plus the u row with one unit
moved from a K player to p2.  A sweep of every single transfer
row + t(e_j - e_i), t <= 4, of every base row produces W1's type from 27
distinct perturbations and W2's from 2, so neither is a fluke.

W1's set system generalises.  With H = {x,y} the top pair, L the poorer
side and b in L distinguished:

   1_{(L\b) u {x}} + 1_{(L\b) u {y}} + 2*1_{H u {b}} - 1*1_H = 2*1_N

(traces {x},{y} with weights 1,1; lifts (L\b)u{x} and (L\b)u{y}; r = 2 on
L\b, r_b = 0, b_max = 2, u_b = 2, delta = 1).  It is a valid semi-balanced
system for every m >= 2 - in particular it EXISTS at n=5 - but at n=5 it is
never realized.  The m-staircase is therefore a statement about which
games can realise an existing shape, not about which shapes exist.

(d) THE n=6 SELECTION-THEOREM CANDIDATE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A catalogued type arises as an Lmin one-packet obstruction at n=6 if and
  only if it is
     (i)   phase-admissible : m = 6-|T| >= m_min(|T|)         [kills 15]
     (ii)  shape-compatible : it is a system (P) - one lift per trace of a
           minimal balanced collection on T plus spokes, no rich-singleton
           lift                                                [kills 19]
     (iii) PB-admissible    : some (P) shape has u != 0        [kills 6]
     (iv)  RO-admissible    : some (P) shape avoids the rich-overlap
           configuration                                       [kills 2 more]
  leaving exactly
     {26, 27, 28, 30, 32, 33, 35, 39, 44, 48, 53}   (11 of 53),
  which is exactly the realized set, and equals the ten full-trace stars
  plus row 53.  Plus, as a new phenomenon at n=6, a
  family of NON-catalogued certificates: minimal-but-decomposable systems,
  all of them split-trace, occurring at (m,h) = (4,2), (3,3) and (2,4).

  Comparison with n=5 (16 rows, realized {10,11,13,14,16}):
    - n=5 had three exclusion mechanisms (cardinality, slack budget,
      lift-sparsity); n=6 needs a FOURTH, the rich-overlap lemma.
    - n=5 had no decomposable certificates and no split packets at all;
      n=6 has 16 decomposable types and split packets at h=2,3,4.
    - the realized fraction falls from 5/16 to 11/53.
