--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  WHAT IS VALID AT n = 6, AND WHAT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A3 IS FALSE ON THE M-SIDE FROM n = 6 (paper5 obs:Mside), so two things from the
n = 5 toolkit are DROPPED and appear nowhere in this recon:
  * the anchor conditions (lem:anchors (a),(b) as disjunctions over attaining
    coalitions) -- they presuppose nu = M and lambda = mu;
  * L3 (2M + 3mu <= T), whose proof runs through the ceiling anchor.
EVERYTHING ELSE SURVIVES UNCHANGED, because each proof is a perturbation inside
the core and never mentions the number of players:
  L4, L5, L6, L7, L7', L1'', L11, L12, L13, L14-L17,
together with both halves of pipeline 2 -- Theorem A (exactness is existential
and linear) and the staged Farkas certificates with their mirror.
Nothing in nsix.py assumes A3.

12 cores (triage32.py, sharded, -> triage32.json).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.  THE HARVEST  (calibration)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2,400 draws over two seeds, 374 games with positive ratio, exact rationals.
Games are lower envelopes of common-total nonnegative allocations, which is
exactly the exact class -- so exactness needs no test, and no core-vertex
enumeration is performed anywhere at n = 6.

        MAXIMUM RATIO = 2/3, EXACTLY.  NOTHING ABOVE.

No refutation of two-generator extremality; consistent with s(6) = 2/3 = s_2(6).
2/3 is attained in exactly two configurations:
        Lmin (5,1) x Lmax (2,4)   tbl [[2,3],[0,1]]
        Lmin (4,2) x Lmax (2,4)   tbl [[2,2],[0,2]]
Next values down: 3/5, 4/7, then 1/2 (36 games).  74 distinct configurations
carry positive ratio at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.  PIPELINE-2 COST  (the problem)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  sample 1  8 configurations, 120s cap : mean 42.1s, TWO returned UNKNOWN
  sample 2  like-for-like, 300s cap, 4 completed : mean 2.8s, zero unknown
The median is a few seconds; the tail is heavy; the unknown rate lies somewhere
in 0-25% on samples far too small to pin down.  THIS IS THE CRUX.  At 3,350
survivors a 10% unknown rate leaves ~335 configurations the encoding cannot
close, and s(6) does not close with it.

AN OPTIMIZATION PROPOSED, TESTED, AND WITHDRAWN.  I expected the Boolean cost to
come from re-deciding the support condition "y_A = 0 or phi(A) = v(A)" inside
every Farkas bundle (744 independent choices at n = 6), and built a variant
hoisting tightness into 124 shared markers tx[A]/tz[A].  It is logically
equivalent and STRICTLY WORSE: it returned unknown at 300s on a configuration
the original solved in 3.1s.  In hindsight the biconditional
tx[S] <-> (x(S) = v(S)) forces z3 to decide tightness for all 62 coalitions
eagerly, while the original disjunction forces it only where y is nonzero.
Recorded in opt32.py so it is not reinvented.  DO NOT ADOPT.

A METHODOLOGICAL ERROR OF MY OWN, CORRECTED.  My first comparison of the two
encodings used tables(r,c)[0] instead of the sampled table, so it compared
different configurations and appeared to show the optimization fixing the
unknowns.  The numbers above are from the corrected like-for-like run
(time32b.py), which reverses that conclusion.

6.  THE STAGED PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE A -- DONE.  Triage complete; 3,350 survivors archived in triage32.json.

STAGE B -- RUN ON YOUR GO.  ~74 configurations, hours not days.  Pipeline 2 at
target 2/3 on every configuration the harvest populates (all 74 survive the
net; stageB.json lists them with observed maxima), plus the two n = 6 controls
the discipline requires:
   drop-the-hypothesis -- deleting the certificates must restore feasibility
       wherever the configuration is possible;
   completeness-with-teeth -- the 2/3 games must come back SAT at a lower
       target and audit end-to-end as genuine exact n = 6 games, exactness
       verified by Theorem-A LPs (62 per game), never by vertex enumeration.
This decides whether anything OBSERVED can exceed 2/3 and validates the n = 6
encoding end to end.  It is the highest information per CPU-hour available.

STAGE C -- GATED, NOT LAUNCHED BLIND.  Before committing to the other 3,276:
   measure the unknown rate on a random 60-configuration sample at a 600s cap
   (~1 CPU-hour).  If it is ~0, the full run is roughly 5-15 CPU-hours sharded
   twelve ways, with checkpointing -- launch it.  If it is materially positive,
   the honest deliverable is a PARTIAL theorem, "ratio <= 2/3 for all but a
   named list of N configurations", reported as such rather than chased.
