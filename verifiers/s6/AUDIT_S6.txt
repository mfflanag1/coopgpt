** LOW DEFECT -- INCONSISTENT MEDIAN CONVENTION IN mkverdict.py. **
VERDICT32_S456 Sec.4.3 prints "decided solve times: min 4.4s median 20.2s
max 383.1s".  The true median of the 32 decided gate times is 18.85s.  20.2s is
median_HIGH (the 17th of 32 sorted; the 16th is 17.5s).  Cause, line 127:
      out(f"... median {ts[len(ts)//2]}s ...")
which is the upper order statistic, while line 190 uses statistics.median for
the paired ratios.  The same [len//2] idiom recurs at lines 260 and 314.
So one machine-generated document uses two different meanings of "median".
Neither number is fabricated and no verdict depends on either; min and max are
correct.  Fix: use statistics.median throughout, or state the convention.
This is the only number in either document I could not reproduce.
