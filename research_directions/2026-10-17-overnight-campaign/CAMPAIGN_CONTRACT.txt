LANE A -- COMPOSITION
Unit-production resource forests: vertices are resource rows, each activity
edge consumes one unit of both endpoints and earns one unit. Nonnegative row
capacities sum fixed actual owner reports. Owners can hold arbitrary fixed
mixed bundles across all levels. Feasible deviations are whole-bundle
coordinatewise binding underreports, utilities payments only. Seek one kappa<2
independent of depth, resource count and owner count, preserving coalition
coverage at every real profile. First gate: compensated separator invariant.
F=A+min(H,P+M) is exact but M may decrease when internal substitutes grow.
Accept a recursive invariant with a proved consequence or an exact obstruction
to a clearly stated invariant. A method obstruction is not an unrestricted lower.

LANE B -- ENERGY CONTRACT
Start from the public-participation-floor AI-power contract and allow different
public net values/costs for local and grid service. Freeze who owns what, who
pays the subsidy, feasible deviations and value definition before proof search.
Test whether a classical reduction disposes of the question early. Public
per-pod values common to both modes are already handled. Do not add private
costs, deadlines, indivisibility and batteries simultaneously. Seek a uniform
theorem, a meaningful impossibility or a precise model/institution boundary.
No Mac benchmark or synthetic trace will be described as measured cluster gain.

LANE C -- UNRESTRICTED LIMITS
Inspect the gap between established logarithmic-over-logarithmic lower bounds
and square-root owner/dimension upper bounds for monotone concave homogeneous
production. Seek an improved general rate or a cross-profile fixed-owner
structural lemma with consequence. Restricted-mask architecture obstructions
and bigger grids in existing 9/8-limited single-pool families do not pass.
