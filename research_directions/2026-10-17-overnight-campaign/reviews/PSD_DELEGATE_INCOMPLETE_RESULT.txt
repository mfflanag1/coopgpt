For eligible j, w_j is a unit vector in K_B, so under Lemma 1 the payout is min{(w_j·ξ)², G} with (w_j·ξ)² ~ Beta(1/2,(d−1)/2). By Lemma 2 with t = G and d ≤ m: P((w_j·ξ)² < G) ≤ √(2mG/π) ≤ √(2/(50π)) = 0.1128. Hence E_Q[payout to j] ≥ 0.887·G, and

  E_Q[closed payout at B] ≥ 0.887·G·(|B^c|−1)^+ ≥ 0.887·G·(|B^c|−1).

Take E over B (independent of Q) and over q ~ μ: E_Q C(μ; QW_ε) ≥ 0.887·G·(m q̄ − 1). Since this is an average over Q, some rotation Q* attains it: the profile {Q*U_iQ*^T} satisfies Theorem A. (Existence is rigorous; Q* is not given explicitly — see Limitations.) ∎

Sanity check m=2: masks {1},{2} have 1-dimensional kernels forcing the selector, and the single closed owner is paid exactly G; mask ∅ pays min{v_1²,G}+min{v_2²,G} ≈ 2G for generic v. Consistent.

## 2. Result B — the wrapper is Θ(√m) for every mask law and every selector

**Junta profiles J_J (2 ≤ J ≤ m).** Owners 1..J−1: U_i = e_ie_i^T; owner J: U_J = I − Σ_{i<J} e_ie_i^T (PSD, λ_min=0). U = I, G = 1. λ_min(U_B) = 1 iff B = [J], else 0, so every retained owner's marginal is 1[B=[J]]: open payout = J·(1−q)^J in expectation; closed payouts ≥ 0. (J = m is the orthonormal "grand marginals" profile.)

**Theorem B.** For every fixed μ and selector Φ, sup over profiles of (total payment)/G ≥ 0.12√m − 0.9 (m ≥ 16). For the uniform law on [0,a]: ≥ 0.44√m − 0.9 for every a.
*Proof.* Let q̄ = ∫q dμ.
- q̄ > 1/4: Theorem A gives ≥ 0.887(m/4 − 1).
- q̄ < 1/(2m): J_m gives open ≥ m·(1/2)(1−2q̄)^m ≥ (m/2)(1−1/m)^m ≥ m/8 (Markov: μ(q ≤ 2q̄) ≥ 1/2).
- 1/(2m) ≤ q̄ ≤ 1/4: J := ⌈1/(2q̄)⌉ ∈ [2,m]. Markov plus (1−x)^{1/x} ≥ e^{−1/(1−x)} with x = 2q̄ ≤ 1/2 gives ∫(1−q)^J dμ ≥ (1/2)(1−2q̄)^{1/(2q̄)+1} ≥ e^{−2}/4, so open_J ≥ 0.0338·J ≥ 0.0169/q̄. Combined with Theorem A, max{0.0169/q̄, 0.887(mq̄−1)} ≥ 0.1225√m − 0.887.
Uniform [0,a] (q̄ = a/2): a ≤ 1/√m: J_m gives (m/(m+1))(1−(1−a)^{m+1})/a ≥ (m/(m+1))√m(1−e^{−√m}); a ≥ 1/√m: Theorem A gives ≥ 0.887(√m/2 − 1). ∎

Upper bound from the file is 1+2√m; so the exponent 1/2 is exact for this wrapper. The uniform-in-q averaging is also necessary: at a point mass q* = ln m/m, J_J with J = m/ln m pays open ≈ (J)(1−q*)^J ≈ 0.37·m/ln m ≫ ln m.
