Overnight research handoff design,17 October2026.
Audience: researcher returning after an eight-hour campaign; specialist deciding
which theorem to inspect. Job: understand accepted progress and its exact scope.
Palette: white#ffffff, paper-blue#f3f6fc, ink#182746, violet#5544a6,
teal#17665e, muted#64718b. Georgia display/formulas; system sans body/navigation.
Layout: compact left reading-map rail; wide main column with a contract comparison
followed by thematic results. Wide tables have horizontal overflow on mobile.
No animated counters, decorative scorecards or artificial breakthrough score.
One distinctive device: a three-column contract map aligns the same mathematical
quantity under individual, coalition and outside-production incentives.

Plan critique: a grid of identical result cards would obscure dependencies.
Replace it with a grouped reading list and expandable proof/evidence annotations.
A large theorem count would encourage counting corollaries as discoveries.
Replace that with four major conclusions and an explicitly unresolved frontier.
Use native details and a small search/filter control; no external scripts/fonts.
Every accepted item links to its root adjudication; superseded constructions are
identified rather than removed. No human review, empirical validation or novelty
is implied by internal acceptance. Header remains interim until campaign close.
