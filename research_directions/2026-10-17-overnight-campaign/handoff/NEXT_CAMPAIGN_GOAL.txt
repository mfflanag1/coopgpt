PROPOSED NEXT GOAL — NOT STARTED OR SCHEDULED
17 October2026. A self-contained mandate for a later authorized session.

OBJECTIVE
Improve the dependence of truthful cooperative funding on the largest protected
coalition size in three-resource-per-activity production. Work on unrestricted
mechanisms with growing resource and owner counts. The current interval is
 Omega(s^(1/4)) <= K_3(s) <= K_3^OUT(s) <= 3s^2.
Target a general O(s) upper; any proved O(s^(2-epsilon)), epsilon>0, or a proved
unrestricted lower exponent above1/4 meets the primary research gate. A weaker
but genuinely general improvement must be explicitly quantified and independently
reviewed before deciding whether it warrants another campaign.

MODEL THAT MUST STAY FIXED
For each finite public packing technology,
 F(c)=max{pi*x : x>=0, A*x<=c},
A and pi are nonnegative, every positive-profit activity uses at most three
owned resource rows, and no positive-profit activity is resource-free. Owners
form a fixed finite set, have fixed eligibility and binding divisible-resource
underreports, and may each hold multiple rows. Technology, actual owner identities
and eligibility remain unchanged throughout every report comparison. Both row
and owner counts may grow across instances. No extra marginal-payment floor is
part of this question.

At every feasible real report profile b, payments y_i(b) must be nonnegative
and cover EVERY coalition: sum_(i in C)y_i(b)>=F(sum_(i in C)b_i), for all C.
For each protected C with |C|<=s and feasible restorations d_i>=0, zero outside C,
TCWM requires sum_(i in C)[y_i(b+d)-y_i(b)]>=0. Some protected members may have
d_i=0. Full OUT replaces zero by F(sum_(i in C)d_i). Funding is TOTAL output,
 sum_i y_i(b)<=K F(sum_i b_i),
not the distinct gain-only S+KG contract. A full-OUT upper also gives a TCWM
upper; a TCWM lower also applies to full OUT. State which has been proved.

  fixed-owner theorem and references are in energy/BOUNDED_COALITION_SPARSE_PRODUCTION.txt.
- Common-cap three-input branches give the unrestricted Omega(s^(1/4)) lower;
  see root adjudications20 and23 and their proof dependencies.
- Root64 proves that EVERY report-independent nonnegative weighting of
  whole-owner marginal contributions requires Omega_r(s^(r-1)) funding. The
  optimal order within that class is Theta_r(s^(r-1)). Owner-specific, asymmetric
  and population-dependent fixed weights are included.
  Changing the fixed sampling distribution cannot improve the exponent.
- Root65 supplies an exact16-owner, size4 factor5 certificate on min3. General
  mechanisms on that same min3 technology have factor2. This diagnoses the
  architecture, not a hard unrestricted family.
- Root66 shows that report-adaptive positive marginal weights escape the
  fixed-weight barrier on min2, via a sharp game-null-zero corollary. Uniform
  min3 dummy-zero funding remains open. Do not add dummy-zero as an axiom of
  the unrestricted target; it is only a restriction of that constructive class.
- Arbitrary coalition TCWM already admits the general dimension upper(d+1)/2.
  A useful lower growing with s therefore needs growing resource dimension;
  a fixed-three-row min game cannot supply that unrestricted lower.

FIRST ATTACKS
1. Construct a profile-adaptive or fractional-resource mechanism with an explicit
   compensation term. Prove the actual protected coalition's total-payment
   increment in original reports. Do not infer incentives from coefficient
   positivity, an optimizer's envelope theorem, or a finite sampling grid.
2. For a lower, seek a growing sequence of interacting fixed-owner report
   transitions whose weighted cancellation leaves a positive funding deficit.
   The variables must be arbitrary payments, not a prescribed price or marginal
   formula. Every row needs an exact feasible economic interpretation.
3. Use the min3 finite certificate, mixed owners holding several resource rows,
   null reports, zero-production profiles and unchanged protected members as
   immediate adversarial checks. Passing these is necessary evidence, not a
   general proof. Test the general lemma on the common-cap lower family too.
