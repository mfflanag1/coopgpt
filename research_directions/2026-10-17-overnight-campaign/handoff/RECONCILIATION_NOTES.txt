READING THE ARCHIVE WITHOUT REVIVING SUPERSEDED CLAIMS
17 October2026. Final-status companion; original exploratory records preserved.

The authoritative headline is the latest numbered ROOT_ADJUDICATION for a
claim, together with the actual proof and listed later audits. A PASS in a
scout or an old candidate title does not establish a current theorem.

1. The original individual owner/resource gap remains open. Universal sharp
coalition factors answer a stronger contract, and do not close that question.
The existing manuscript's sharp cut-count rate predates this campaign.

2. The old open paragraph in energy/GAIN_ONLY_MASK_AND_DIMENSION_THEOREM.txt
calling a dimension-based square-root gain bound open is superseded by
root/LOW_RANK_CAPPED_SAMPLING_LEMMA.txt and
root/REFINED_GAIN_SAMPLING_DENSITY.txt (root30). Likewise the cone/PSD base
notes' older constants are valid but superseded by the refined density.
Use public D=d+1 for general cones and D=m for PSD matrix order, never an
observed report rank to change the mechanism. These are individual OUT rules
that cover every coalition, not coalition-report incentive guarantees.

3. Earlier golden-ratio Lorentz/radial bounds remain statements about their
specified architecture. They are superseded as general L1-embeddable reserve
bounds by the sharp3/2TCWM and1+c fullOUT signed transfer (root42). Every
planar norm is a corollary of its L1 embedding, not a separate mechanism paper.

4. General square-cone prices do not preserve the sharp reserve constant:
root57 proves an exact common-aggregate-price2 versus ownership-aware1
separation on the same two-owner model. The latter efficient rule is classical.
The architecture lower is not a lower for unrestricted mechanisms.

5. The one-common/private-DR theorem (root58) protects payment-only TCWM,
not full outside production. The exact logarithmic block candidate fails by
more than1/600 on a three-activity polymatroid (root60). Uniform three-block
averaging fails with two heterogeneous common resources (root59). These
failures do not invalidate the accepted one-common theorem or prove an
all-mechanism impossibility for either extension.

6. Pairwise fullOUT gain-only funding has linear owner order (root40), on the
full unbounded report domain with both-resource eligibility. Its19row finite
certificate is used AFTER a translation reduction. It cannot be advertised
as an unprocessed bounded-cube certificate. Pair payment-only TCWM remains
open. The partial-OUT threshold27/28 is sufficient for that lower, not sharp.

7. M100 metadata-only notes are superseded by an actual observed-panel gate
(root61). The raw April files contain simultaneous power readings, but sparse
release coverage and different meter definitions prevent a reliability or
pooling conclusion. Physical nodes are not proven independent owners.

8. General nonsmooth private-DR existence uses all-profile compactness and
has no evaluation algorithm claim. Its public-polymatroid subclass is more
explicit. A principal-partition oracle-evaluation corollary was accepted in root62
after independent review; the established submodular algorithm is credited rather
than counted as a new mathematical invention. An exhaustive test oracle
remains exponential even when the reduction uses polynomially many calls.

9. Strong Group Monotonicity is an established scalar-demand counterpart of
TCWM. Static fuzzy-core/Euler allocation, norm embeddings, submodular
uncrossing and principal partitions are established. Proposed quantitative
claims require specialist priority review. Moulin1999's original remains
unavailable in this free-only search; abstracts do not resolve the comparison.

No source note, internal reviewer opinion, computational PASS or number of
files is a substitute for the precise theorem and its complete proof.

10. The weighted-coverage public-polymatroid rule now has an accepted exact
max-flow backend (root63). Arbitrary rank oracles do not automatically have
that representation. This fixed-fleet-mix subclass already embeds into the
earlier bipartite resource-graph theorem; implementation is not a new domain
extension or measured AI service result.

11. The final positive-marginal barrier applies to arbitrary nonnegative fixed
whole-owner marginal weights, including asymmetric population-dependent ones
(root64). It is not limited to a common Bernoulli mixture. Only the weak-limit
measure has support in[0,1/s]; no finite-n support claim follows. The exact
16-owner certificate(root65) needs no weak limit and gives factor5 versus the
general min3 mechanism's2. Neither result is an unrestricted funding lower.
Normalized semivalues are classical; arbitrary scaled positive rules here need
not have unit mass. Report-adaptive, signed, resource-level and nonlinear rules
remain outside the obstruction.
