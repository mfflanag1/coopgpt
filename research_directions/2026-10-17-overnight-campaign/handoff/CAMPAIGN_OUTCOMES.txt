WHAT THE EIGHT-HOUR CAMPAIGN CHANGED
17 October2026. Outcome synthesis; final gate review is separately recorded.

THE ORIGINAL QUESTION AND THE NEW CONTRACT
The original individual-incentive owner/resource gap remains open. Before this
run, the manuscript already had the sharp structural-cut-count rate and its
network lower bound. This campaign has preserved that manuscript. Its strongest
new results concern coalition withholding: unchanged coalition members remain
protected, and members may transfer receipts among themselves. That is a stronger
contract than an individual owner's truthfulness, not a solution to its gap.
The original open rates concern owner count and resource dimension under
individual incentives. A linear-in-d coalition bound does not improve the
already available order-sqrt(d) individual bound.

1. A COHERENT GENERAL COALITION THEOREM
For d nonnegative resources and finite monotone concave homogeneous production,
the internally audited worst-case total-funding factors are(d+1)/2 for coalition
payment monotonicity and1+c(d-1) for pooled outside-production protection, where
c(2-exp(-1/c))=1. The upper and lower bounds match across the class and growing
finite owner populations. A partial-outside/floor frontier refines the endpoints.
This is the primary proposed paper package. Strong Group Monotonicity and other
ingredients have prior literature; historical novelty still needs specialist
assessment. It is not a claim of a major open-problem resolution.

2. TECHNOLOGY AND COALITION SIZE GIVE A USEFUL NEXT FRONTIER
For activities using at most two owned resource rows, the coalition-monotone
factor is3/2. For three rows, even the common-cap technology
 F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_j min(a_j,b_j))
needs a worst-case factor of order M^(1/4) when all coalitions are protected.
Its resource-activity incidence graph is a tree: one common resource connects
M activities, each with two private resource leaves. Thus this particular
notion of shallow forest structure cannot ensure a constant under the stronger
coalition contract. It does not establish such an impossibility for individual
incentives; the arity-three grand-marginal rule still gives an individual-OUT
upper3 with all-coalition coverage.

If only groups of at most s owners receive incentive protection, while EVERY
coalition is still covered, the arity-three frontier is presently
 Omega(s^(1/4)) <= K_3(s) <= K_3^OUT(s) <= 3s^2.
The distinction between unrestricted mechanisms and failures of a proposed
formula is essential. The final gate proves an Omega_r(s^(r-1)) funding lower for EVERY eligible fixed
nonnegative whole-owner marginal-weight rule, even with asymmetric and population-
dependent weights. A matching-order construction makes the best achievable
architecture factor Theta_r(s^(r-1)). The lower already holds on one fixed min-r
technology. At r=3 this architecture cannot improve the quadratic exponent.
An exact16-owner certificate requires factor5 for protected groups up to4;
general mechanisms on the same min3 technology have factor2. These accepted
root64/65 obstructions identify a class to leave behind; they do not improve
the unrestricted lower or establish that the general exponent is quadratic.

The final scope corollary shows that report-adaptive positive marginal weights
already escape this barrier on min2: sharp3/2 and1+c remain achievable while
paying all game-null owners zero. The min3 owner-uniform dummy-zero question
remains open; aggregate-only prices cannot preserve that extra axiom. This is
a corollary and method boundary, not another unrestricted rate improvement.

An independent structural theorem gives sharp3/2 for one common resource and
an unbounded private diminishing-returns block. Public polymatroids have an
exact principal-partition implementation using established SFM machinery;
explicit coverage ranks now have a checked rational max-flow backend. This
is computational progress, not evidence for a measured service/energy benefit.

3. A MATHEMATICALLY CLEAN, EMPIRICALLY CONDITIONAL ENERGY BRIDGE
The starting public mode-dependent-value question yielded a routine route-value
extension (root02) and a sharp4/3 source-price theorem (root04) for its stated
same-side owner roles with full energy-owner grand-marginal floors. The public
reimbursement ledger makes payment-only net-surplus utility exact under fixed
linear cost/outside-value assumptions. Mixed ownership, nonlinear retained
self-production and negotiated floors are distinct contracts, not automatically
covered by that economic interpretation. These initial results motivated the
later reserve and outside-production work; they are not measured cluster gains.

The signed-L1 transfer gives sharp3/2 and1+c, uniformly over finite owner
populations, for L1-embeddable norm reserve pooling, including Euclidean reserves. Its exact fixed-owner compensation is the proposed theorem;
norm embeddings, static core allocations and risk diversification are classical.
It is the second potential paper package. The application needs real ownership,
withdrawal rights, delivery and curtailment rules, reliability targets and a
sponsor whose benefit justifies external funding. A total-output funding bound
does not imply bounded subsidy per incremental cooperation gain.

Public M100 data now provide verified simultaneous PSU-input readings across
980physical nodes, but the retrieved April release covers only parts of four
days, has a51-hour timestamp gap, and exposes a material meter mismatch. These
are data-intake findings. They establish neither independent ownership nor
reliability, energy savings, service latency or pooling gains. Another synthetic
partition or Mac throughput test cannot supply the missing institution.
