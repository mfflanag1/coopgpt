WHAT WE WOULD LIKE CHECKED
The two proposed main contributions are quantitative fixed-owner withholding
contracts: universal concave production has sharp dimension-dependent total-
funding factors, and a signed compensation construction transfers sharp binary
constants to L1-embeddable norm reserve cones. Internal audits and executable
certificates are supplied. We seek both correctness and historical-priority
assessment; neither is represented as already established by external review.

PACKET A: SHARP COALITION CONTRACTS
1. Are the same-owner, simultaneous-restoration inequalities exactly the relevant
   version of Strong Group Monotonicity? Every coalition is covered, owners may
   hold multiple resource rows, and unchanged members of a restoring coalition
   remain protected. Does a prior cost-sharing or production result already give
   these quantitative factors after a valid domain/payment transformation?
2. Do the universal upper proofs handle zero faces and finite concave homogeneous
   technologies without silently assuming boundary continuity? The TCWM rule
   averages face VALUES; it is not an unqualified average of derivatives at a
   discontinuous boundary.
3. Does the unrestricted lower reduction preserve all-profile compatibility,
   actual fixed owners, and funding bounds? Symmetrization and finite-population
   limits are analytic proof devices, not permitted strategic splitting.
4. Are the matching worst-case constants(d+1)/2 and1+c(d-1),
   c(2-exp(-1/c))=1, already implied by established results? The sharpness statement
   ranges over finite owner models; it does not claim every fixed population or
   every technology attains the lower.

PACKET B: SIGNED RESERVE TRANSFER
1. Does the signed compensation proof cover every cone-feasible withdrawal,
   including features whose signs change? Positive/negative feature coordinates
   are not separate owners. Both retained and removed bundles lie in the cone.
2. Do classical norm embeddings, coherent capital allocation or existing
   entry-monotone risk pools already imply this SAME partial-withdrawal contract
   and these quantitative subsidy constants? Static core coverage alone does
   not establish recomputed payment monotonicity.
3. Are the general L1 embedding and lower comparison legitimate for arbitrary
   finite owner populations? The continuum representation is an existence rule;
   no finite exact feature implementation is asserted without its own proof.
4. Is the proposed Gaussian reserve institution economically meaningful with
   identifiable ownership, delivery, feasible withdrawals and a paying sponsor?
   This is a conditional application, not a measured AI-energy benefit.

PRIORITY QUESTIONS STILL OPEN
The existing notes credit Strong Group Monotonicity, norm embeddings, static
risk allocation, sampling, principal partitions and classical efficient-core
incompatibility. The sharp quantitative combination is the candidate contribution.
Moulin1999's full original remains a priority. Young1985's exact five-player
antecedent has now been checked in the original; Csoka-Pinter's fixed-owner
realization matches every coalition value. The Balog2017 author presentation
clarifies definitions but does not replace the unavailable final article. Different names
for a property do not establish novelty. A direct theorem implication is more
valuable than a broad topical similarity.
