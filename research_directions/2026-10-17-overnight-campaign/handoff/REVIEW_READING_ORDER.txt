COMMON CONTRACT — READ BEFORE EITHER PACKET

Owners are a fixed finite set throughout every report comparison. Each owner may
hold several resources. Payments are nonnegative and cover every actual coalition's
production. TCWM means that a fixed coalition's total payment cannot decrease when
its members restore feasible resources; some coalition members may restore zero.
Full coalition OUT requires the increase also to cover production from the pooled
restored bundle. Individual OUT, pair OUT, and unrestricted coalition OUT are
different requirements. The total-production benchmark kappa F is different from
the gain-only benchmark S+K G, where S is total standalone production and G=F-S.
Sharp constants here are uniform worst cases over growing finite owner models,
not claims of attainment or optimality at every fixed owner count.
