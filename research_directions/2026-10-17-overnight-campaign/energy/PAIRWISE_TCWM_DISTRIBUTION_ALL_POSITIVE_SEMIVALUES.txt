ALL FINITE POSITIVE BERNOULLI-SEMIVALUE MIXTURES FAIL, EVEN WITH POPULATION-DEPENDENT MASS
17 October 2026. Analytic completion of the semivalue barrier; audit pending.

Fix any public n>=4. Let mu_n be ANY finite nonnegative measure on[0,1], with
arbitrary total mass and arbitrary dependence on n, but independent of reports.
At pure residual min2 profiles set u_i=integral B_i^p dmu_n(p), where B_i^p is
the usual independent-p expected coalition marginal, and pay y_i=f_i+u_i(z).
Owners are fixed and mixed-eligible on the full real nonnegative report domain.

THEOREM. No such rule satisfies all-coalition coverage, pair-TCWM and any finite
gain coefficient. This closes the population-dependent unnormalized-law gap
left open in the preceding normalized-mixture note. No limit in n is used.

Ignore the subscript n while proving the claim. Null owners are irrelevant
to every expected marginal, so the following two-, three-, and four-active
profiles are valid at this SAME fixed n. Atoms at p=0 also have no effect on
pure residual min2 payments.

1. TWO-ACTIVE EFFICIENCY
For one unit A owner and one unit B owner, each expected marginal is p.
The two-active efficiency lemma gives
 integral 2p dmu=1.                                     (1)

2. THREE-ACTIVE LOWER PAIR SLOPE
For A1=1,A2=epsilon,B=1, the protected A-pair pays
 integral[p+epsilon*p(1-2p)]dmu.
Restoring A2 from0 to any epsilon in(0,1] gives
 I:=integral p(1-2p)dmu>=0.                              (2)
This is an exact finite comparison, not a differential assumption.

3. FOUR-ACTIVE UPPER PAIR SLOPE
Use a single A owner of capacity x in[0,1] and three unit B owners. Protect
the A owner and one of the B owners. Direct subset conditioning gives
 B_A^p=x[1-(1-p)^3],
 B_B^p=x*p(1-p)^2,
so their total is x C(p), where
 C(p)=1+(2p-1)(1-p)^2=4p-5p^2+2p^3.
The upper-Lipschitz half of the exact residual characterization forces
 integral C(p)dmu<=1.                                   (3)
Equivalently, use the concrete comparison x=1/2 versus residual x=1/4:
restore1/4 of B to the A owner, increasing its standalone by1/4 and reducing
its residual A by1/4. The protected pair's payment increment is
 (1/4)[1-integral C(p)dmu], which pair-TCWM makes nonnegative.

4. A NONNEGATIVE POLYNOMIAL PINS THE ENTIRE LAW
Subtract(1) from(3) and factor exactly:

 0 >= integral[C(p)-2p]dmu
    = integral p(1-2p)(2-p)dmu
    = (3/2) I + (1/2) integral p(1-2p)^2 dmu.            (4)

Both terms on the last line are nonnegative by(2) and positivity of mu.
They must vanish. Hence mu is supported on{0,1/2}. Equation(1) fixes its
mass at1/2 to be exactly1; its arbitrary mass at0 is irrelevant. The rule
is therefore precisely raw Banzhaf on every pure residual profile.

5. COVERAGE CONTRADICTION
For signed profile(2,-1,-1,1), raw Banzhaf pays the first three owners15/8
although their coalition produces2. This contradicts required coverage.
The same witness persists after padding by n-4 zero-report owners.

This theorem excludes all finite positive mixtures of report-independent
sampling probabilities, including measures chosen separately for each fixed
population. It does NOT exclude report-dependent sampling laws, signed
combinations, nonlinear corrections, or arbitrary mechanisms. It also does
not relax the full mixed-owner report domain: the key upper comparison restores
an opposite resource to one actual owner.
