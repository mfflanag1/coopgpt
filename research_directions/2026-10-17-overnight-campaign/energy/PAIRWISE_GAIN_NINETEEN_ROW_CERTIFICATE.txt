THEOREM
For n>=5 fixed owners eligible for both coordinates, F(A,B)=min(A,B), full
nonnegative real report domain, nonnegative payments, coverage of EVERY
coalition, and singleton/pair beta-outside-production protection (0<=beta<=1),
any gain-only funding bound Y<=S+K G satisfies

 K >= (n-4) max{0,28 beta-27}/22.                         (1)

In particular full outside production forces K>=(n-4)/22. Together with the
universal gain-only pairwise rule y_i=f_i+G/2, this proves sharp linear order
for every fixed beta>27/28. The cutoff and finite coefficient are not claimed
optimal. Pairwise TCWM(beta=0) remains unresolved by this certificate.

This proof uses nineteen explicit finite inequalities. Its only preliminary
reductions are compact translation averaging, finite owner/resource symmetry,
and a two-active-owner efficiency lemma. All coefficients are integers and
all payment variables for active owners cancel exactly.

1. PRELIMINARY LEMMAS
As proved in PAIRWISE_PARTIAL_OUTSIDE_GAIN_ROBUSTNESS.txt, translation averaging
preserves the beta-OUT contract and gain funding, without any starting
regularity premise. Therefore y_i=b_i+h_i(z), z_i=a_i-b_i. Owner/resource
symmetrization is also without loss.

For a signed transfer inside a pair, replacing(x,y) by(u,v) with x+y=u+v, choose
the owner matching with smallest transferred magnitude

 D=min{|u-x|,|u-y|}.

The minimal nonnegative restorations have withheld totals(D,D), and the finite
beta-OUT inequalities imply

 |H_pair(new)-H_pair(old)|<=(1-beta)D.                    (2)

Let p_X(t) be the actual payment to an owner whose pure signed report is t at
profile X. Positive t means(t,0), negative t means(0,-t); null t=0 means(0,0).
Since h=p+min(t,0), one direction of(2) is exactly

 p_X(x)+p_X(y)-p_Y(u)-p_Y(v)+D beta
 <=D+min(u,0)+min(v,0)-min(x,0)-min(y,0).                 (3)

Repeated types denote distinct actual owners, with equal payments by anonymity.
Each comparison can be realized among the same fixed owners; only reports
change, and all unlisted/padded owners stay null.

Protected coalitions include a member withdrawing the zero bundle, as in the
original weak-underreport contract and PAIRWISE_GAIN_TRANSLATION_REDUCTION.txt.
This convention is used in the following two-active-owner efficiency argument.
A profile with just two nonzero owners has its ACTIVE pair paid efficiently:
for opposite pure types(a,0),(0,b), restore the missing input of the scarce
owner until it can produce min(a,b) alone. Total production stays constant and
G becomes0. Pairwise monotonicity plus the gain cap and coverage then sandwich
the original pair total at min(a,b). Same-sign profiles have G=T=0.
At the balanced profile(2,-2), symmetry therefore pays each active owner1;
own monotonicity on restoring2 to4 proves p_A(4)>=1 for profile A below.

4. EXACT SUM AND FUNDING CONSEQUENCE
Multiplying and summing the nineteen rows gives

 28 beta +u_A-2u_D-3u_C-8u_E-6u_F-6u_Z <=27.              (5)

All active payment coefficients cancel. Insert u_Z=0, discard nonnegative u_A,
and use(4):

 28 beta-27
 <= K[2/(n-2)+8/(n-3)+12/(n-4)]
 <= 22K/(n-4).                                           (6)

Together with K>=0, this proves(1). The first inequality in(6) supplies a
slightly stronger exact n-dependent bound if desired; no coefficient tuning
is needed for the linear-order conclusion.
