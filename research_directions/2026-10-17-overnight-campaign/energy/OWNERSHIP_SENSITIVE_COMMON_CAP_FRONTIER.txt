OWNERSHIP-SENSITIVE COMMON-CAP FUNDING: A SHARP-RATE COROLLARY
17 October 2026. Routine synthesis of the composition lane's common-cap
theorems and this lane's owner-cover/compression lemmas. Constituent proofs
have separate review records. Root + Fable032355 accepted the mathematical
claims; requested dependency clarifications are applied here.
No new leading constant, exact finite optimum, or historical novelty claimed.

MODEL
F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_(j<=M)min(a_j,b_j)), M>=1. The class contains all fixed
finite actual-owner institutions with at most R>=1 owners eligible for P,
arbitrary fixed mixed ownership of private rows, and binding underreports.
All coalitions are covered and all have full outside-production protection.
Optionally impose y_i>=theta [F(C)-F(C-b_i)] on every owner,0<=theta<=1.
The worst optimal factor, uniform over institutions in this class, has order

  Theta( min{R,M^(1/4)} + theta sqrt(M) ).                 (1)

The constants implicit in Theta are absolute, uniform in M,R,theta. The
floor-free order also applies to TCWM. The floor lower applies to TCWM and
therefore full OUT; the displayed upper uses full OUT. Fixed actual owners
are never strategically split or merged. For R=1,theta=0, the exact factor1
is achieved, though (1) asserts only an order more generally.

LOWER AND WHY EXTRA ELIGIBLE OWNERS DO NOT WEAKEN IT
For any integer r<=R, use r pure power owners and two pure owners per private
branch. The active-owner ladder gives, for Q=A_M=4^M/[2 binom(2M,M)],

  K >= Qr/(r^2+Q).                                      (3)

The stronger finite expression from FINITE_ARITY_THREE_LADDER_CERTIFICATE is
(r^2+D r)/(r^2+D), D=(1-1/M)Q. Either suffices for the rate. If the desired
institution has exactly R eligible power owners, append R-r owners with power
eligibility and hold their reports at zero in every lower-bound comparison.
Discard their nonnegative payments when restricting to the old owners. Old
coalition coverage, incentive inequalities, floors and the upper budget all
survive. No null-payment axiom is needed; positive transfers to appended
owners cannot invalidate the lower. Their identities and eligibility remain
fixed, and one restricts reports rather than deleting strategic players.

Choose r of order min(R,sqrt(Q)), using r=1 and coverage K>=1 for the small
endpoint cases. Since Q is of order sqrt(M), (3) gives
K=Omega(min(R,M^(1/4))). An explicit elementary bound is

  sqrt(3M+1)/2 <= Q <= sqrt(M).

The lower follows by induction from binom(2M,M)/4^M<=1/sqrt(3M+1);
the upper follows because Q/sqrt(M) decreases from1. These bounds also show
that integer rounding in the choice of r changes only an absolute constant.

The concentrated-power floor witness uses just ONE power owner, so it belongs
to every class R>=1. Its active seed gives K>=2theta Q (composition's
COMMON_CAP_PARTICIPATION_FLOOR_FRONTIER.txt §2, equation(5), with k=2),
in addition to coverage
K>=1, and thus K=Omega(theta sqrt(M)). The maximum of the two lower bounds
is at least one half of their sum, proving the lower order in (1).

ONLY t SPLIT BRANCHES: REPLACE M BY t+1
Suppose all but at most t branches have both inputs wholly controlled by one
fixed provider, and all such bundled branches may grow in number. Aggregate
their local min capacities by ACTUAL owner:

  h_i=sum_(j bundled and owned by i)min(a_j,b_j).

Keep every exceptional branch's two rows and all original owners. Insert one
additional virtual branch with two equal rows h,h, assigning h_i units of
BOTH rows to that same actual owner i. Then

  min(P,min(h,h)+sum_(j split)min(a_j,b_j))
  =min(P,h+sum_(j split)min(a_j,b_j))

is exactly the original value for EVERY coalition and report profile. There
are at most t+1 branches. This is duplication of a resource description held
by the same owner, not creation of a new strategic owner or independent stock.
The owner transform is monotone and superadditive; each of the two copied
losses dominates the original withdrawn bundled capacity. Consequently the
compression lemma transfers full OUT and every full-owner marginal floor.
Exactly the same fixed power owners remain eligible, so the upper in (2)
uses M=t+1 independently of the number of bundled branches.

For t>=1 the lower class contains the hard t-split-branch institution, with
any extra bundled branches fixed at zero in the comparison. Since t and t+1
differ by at most a factor2, this gives the matching order. For t=0, coverage
gives a lower1 and the M=1 full-floor rule above gives K1=3, including theta>0;
mixing as in(2) gives a constant upper. Alternatively the sharp two-pool
full-OUT/floor theorem applies. The claimed
order is still Theta(min{R,1}+theta)=Theta(1). Thus uniformly

  Theta( min{R,(t+1)^(1/4)} + theta sqrt(t+1) ).            (4)

These are worst-case class results, not a claim that every institution with
those counts has matching difficulty. An additional fixed owner cover may
give a smaller factor. With floors, concentrated power does not by itself
remove the growing term; that contrast is the point of the separate floor note.
