PAIRWISE-TCWM GAIN-ONLY CONTRACT: BOUNDED SUCCESSOR GATE
17 October2026. The beta=0 problem remains open after this gate.

QUESTION
Can min2 with arbitrarily many fixed mixed-eligible owners admit all-coalition
coverage, pairwise total-payment monotonicity and Y<=S+K G for a constant K
independent of n? Pairwise full outside production requires Theta(n); the exact
19-row certificate extends this to every fixed beta>27/28. That argument does
not answer beta=0.

CONSEQUENTIAL POSITIVE/NEGATIVE FINDINGS
1. Any gain-funded pairwise-TCWM mechanism pays a TWO-active-owner pure pair
exactly its production. Constant-production completion to G=0 proves this,
regardless of what null owners receive. This is a genuine all-profile lemma.

2. A residual aggregate-price architecture cannot solve the problem, even
when its prices depend on public n. Suppose after netting f_i=min(a_i,b_i),
residual pure positive/negative capacities receive scalar prices p_A(P,N),
p_B(P,N) that depend only on aggregate residual capacities. Null owners get
zero residual payment. For every P,N, a two-active-owner profile has the same
prices, so the preceding lemma forces

  P p_A+N p_B=min(P,N).                                    (1)

Fix P>N>0 and use three active owners with residual bundles(P-N,0),(N,0),(0,N).
The coalition formed by the last two owners has worth N. Its coverage gives
p_A+p_B>=1. Together with(1), this implies p_A=0,p_B=1. At P=N, resource symmetry
forces p_A=p_B=1/2. Therefore at a two-active-owner profile(N,0),(0,N), restoring
the first owner's capacity to P>N lowers that owner's payment from N/2 to0,
violating even individual monotonicity. Extra actual owners may remain null.
Finite owner/resource symmetrization preserves the aggregate-price form, so
assuming symmetry loses no feasible rule in this architecture.

Thus a constructive beta=0 rule must use the distribution among actual owners
(or otherwise leave this netted aggregate-price architecture), not merely tune
one pair of residual resource prices. This is an architecture obstruction, not
an unrestricted impossibility.

3. The residual Huber individual-gain rule fails pairwise TCWM on just two
nonzero owners(10,0),(0,1): restoring a first-resource amount1/100 to the second
owner changes total payment from29/20 to289199/200000, a decline801/200000,
while total production stays1. Exact check recorded separately. Importing this
individual-only rule does not supply a positive answer.

4. One bounded finite feasibility diagnostic was informative but inconclusive.
scout_pairwise_tcwm_null.py imposed the translated signed minimal-restoration
constraints, all-coalition coverage, null-zero, resource symmetry, the gain
budget and exact two-active efficiency on all126 anonymous signed profiles
from{-2,-1,0,1,2} with five owners. The141-variable/15667-constraint LP was
feasible with optimal finite-grid gain coefficient1.25. This does NOT establish
an all-real mechanism, uniformity over owners, or an upper bound for the open
problem. It shows only that this particular finite domain cannot yield the
sought contradiction. The scout and JSON output are retained as discovery data.

STOPPING DECISION
The bounded gate produced the robust linear theorem above27/28, a useful exact
two-active efficiency lemma, and a clear barrier to aggregate residual pricing.
It did not settle pairwise TCWM or determine the critical outside-protection
fraction. Repeating adjacent finite grids is not justified. A successor should
supply a new owner-sensitive mechanism, a finite/all-profile lower that applies
at beta0, or a structural threshold argument with an unbounded consequence.
