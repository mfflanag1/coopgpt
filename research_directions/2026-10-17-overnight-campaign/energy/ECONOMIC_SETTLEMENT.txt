A SETTLEMENT MODEL THAT MAKES THE PAYMENT UTILITY EXACT
17 October2026. Economic interpretation, not an empirical assertion.

Each actual owner j holds true energy quantities bar_e_{jr} in fixed public cost
or outside-value tiers c_{jr}>=0. Hardware is committed/prepaid for the service
window and has zero additional private operating/opportunity cost in this model.
A hardware tier's public v is revenue from one completed divisible service unit.
The owner may bindingly report any e_{jr}<=bar_e_{jr}; admitted hardware reports
are also binding reductions of a fixed true bundle. Resource ownership, public
prices, access and service values do not change with these reports.

For a reported profile, the operator chooses an efficient feasible service
allocation, uses u_{jr}<=e_{jr} energy from tier jr, and earns gross service
revenue R=sum v_i z_ij. It reimburses the energy owner exactly c_{jr}u_{jr} for
used energy and separately pays actual owner j the nonnegative surplus amount
y_j specified by the mechanism. Every retained/unused energy unit, including a
unit withheld from this window, retains fixed public value c_{jr}. This may be
an accounting outside-value numeraire; no specific resale market is asserted.
The actual owner's resulting utility is therefore
 sum_r c_{jr}(bar_e_{jr}-u_{jr}) + sum_r c_{jr}u_{jr} + y_j
 = sum_r c_{jr}bar_e_{jr} + y_j.
The baseline term is fixed by the true endowment and is independent of reports
and dispatch. Maximizing utility is consequently EXACTLY maximizing y_j.
This also works for a mixed hardware/energy owner whose hardware baseline is
fixed. Thus own-report monotonicity of y implements truthful full capacity
revelation against the stated feasible underreports in this settlement model.

The operator's external funding requirement is
 [sum c_{jr}u_{jr}+sum_j y_j]-R = sum_j y_j-F,
where F=R-sum c_{jr}u_{jr} is optimized NET production. Thus a funding bound
sum y<=kappa F means the sponsor covers at most(kappa-1)F beyond gross service
receipts after energy reimbursement. A value c is deducted once from welfare
and reimbursed once: it is not also an extra surplus floor. Any fixed availability
retainer must be separately specified and separately funded.

If c is instead a physical running expense, the same cancellation follows from
reimbursement minus actual expense, with zero retained-unit outside value and
zero energy baseline. These are alternative interpretations; they must not be
added together. The fixed-outside-value version above permits retained units to
keep their public opportunity value. Neither interpretation accommodates an
unobserved, privately changing or allocation-dependent cost without new analysis.

CONTRACTS MUST IDENTIFY WHAT IS REMOVED IN THEIR COUNTERFACTUAL
A full owner marginal M_j=F-F_without_owner removes ALL of a mixed owner's
hardware and energy. Merely calling that an energy floor does not remove its
hardware bargaining contribution. Its sharp uniform factor becomes2.
An energy-service marginal U_j=F-F_without_own_energy retains that owner's
hardware. This is a resource-specific negotiated surplus benchmark and preserves
the sharp4/3 guarantee, subject to the separate proof. It is not a coalition's
outside option and not implied by ordinary individual rationality. Both contracts
are compatible with the same public reimbursement ledger but have different
funding costs. At no point is either one claimed to be an existing campus norm.

RETAINED SELF-PRODUCTION IS A DISTINCT DEVIATION
The linear baseline argument does not account for an owner withholding BOTH
hardware and energy, then jointly producing services outside the committed
operator window. If retained bundles have nonlinear stand-alone worth, utility
would include that additional production term, and monotonicity of y alone is
insufficient. The present settlement therefore assumes no such additional
outside-service opportunity in the commitment window (or a fixed public linear
baseline already fully accounted for). Prepayment of hardware by itself does
NOT prove its opportunity value is zero. This is a substantive model boundary,
particularly under mixed ownership, and must be stated wherever the economic
interpretation is used. Absence-proofness with retained coalition production is
a stronger and separate requirement; the present theorem does not establish it.
