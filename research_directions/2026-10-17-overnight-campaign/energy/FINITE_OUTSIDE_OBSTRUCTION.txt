EXPLICIT FINITE-OWNER FULL-OUT OBSTRUCTION ABOVE 3/2
2026-10-17. Candidate exact certificate; internal parent review pending.

This is a compressed analytic certificate, not an enumerated LP certificate.
It makes the earlier compactness lower bound effective at an enormous finite
owner count. Its modest numerical constant is not the sharp asymptotic result.

THEOREM. Put L=2000 and N=12000*1110^(L+3). For the two-input production
function F(A,B)=min(A,B), some binary report profile on N pure owners of each
input forces funding at least

             1588934000/1058001797 = 1.5018254264836566... .

This holds for every nonnegative coalition-covering mechanism satisfying full
coalition OUT on all binary profiles. Owners and their resource types are fixed.
An owner reports either zero or one unit. OUT allows a coalition to withhold
units, then produce min(withheld A,withheld B) outside the mechanism. No
regularity, anonymity, resource-price, or scale-invariance assumption is made.

1. NECESSARY ACTIVE-PAYMENT ARRAY

Average any candidate rule within the two resource types, and average with its
resource-swapped version. This preserves all linear requirements. At a profile
with m active A owners and n active B owners write x_mn,y_mn for their respective
per-owner payments. Inactive owners may receive positive payments. Dropping
their nonnegative payments gives the following necessary inequalities:

 m x_mn+n y_mn <= k min(m,n);
 r x_mn+s y_mn >= min(r,s) for r<=m,s<=n;
 r x_mn+s y_mn >= (r-u)x_(m-u,n-v)+(s-v)y_(m-u,n-v)+min(u,v)
       for 0<=u<=r<=m, 0<=v<=s<=n.

All are actual fixed-owner comparisons. The last inequality drops any payments
to the newly inactive coalition members. The array is nonnegative and obeys
x_mn=y_nm. The funding factor is denoted k.

2. FINITE DILATION AVERAGE WITH CONTROLLED ERROR

Let D range uniformly over 2^e2 3^e3 5^e5 37^e37, with each exponent in
{0,...,L-1}. Define xbar_mn=average_D x_(Dm,Dn), and likewise ybar.
Scaling all counts in any necessary inequality and dividing by D proves the
same inequality for xbar,ybar. This operation averages different actual
profiles; it does not change owners during a strategic comparison.

For a positive integer h with prime factors only in {2,3,5,37}, let Omega(h)
count those factors with multiplicity. A finite-box shift and telescoping give

 |xbar_(hm,hn)-xbar_mn| <= Omega(h)*k min(m,n)/(m L),
 |ybar_(hm,hn)-ybar_mn| <= Omega(h)*k min(m,n)/(n L).

To see the one-prime step, all but one exponent layer cancel; the difference
is an average of last-layer minus first-layer payments, each in [0,M], hence
has magnitude at most M/L. Iterate for each prime factor. The chosen N is a
loose upper bound covering every profile and every shifted box below.

Set
 p0=xbar_(1000,1), q0=ybar_(1000,1), C=1000 p0;
 p1=xbar_(5,2), q1=ybar_(5,2);
 p2=xbar_(5,4), q2=ybar_(5,4);
 a=xbar_(1,1)=ybar_(1,1).

Budget and coverage give exactly

       k >= C+q0,   q0 >= 1-C/1000,
       k >= 1+(999/1000)C.                         (1)

Coalitions consisting only of A owners imply
5p1 <= 2000 xbar_(2000,2),
5p2 <= 4000 xbar_(4000,4), and a<=C.
Applying the h=2 and h=4 shift bounds yields

       p1 <= (2/5)C +(2/5)k/L,
       p2 <= (4/5)C +(8/5)k/L.                    (2)

An all-B coalition gives 4q2<=5 ybar_(5,5). Using h=5 and a<=C,

       (4/5)q2 <= C+k/L.                           (3)

3. TWO BALANCED WITHHOLDING COMPARISONS

At (1665,666), let a coalition with holdings (665,666) withhold (665,665).
The low aggregate is (1000,1), and its retained holding is (0,1). Therefore

 665 xbar_(1665,666)+666 ybar_(1665,666) >= q0+665.

The high profile is 333*(5,2), and Omega(333)=3. The shift errors contribute
665*(6/5)k/L+666*3k/L=2796k/L. Thus

       665p1+666q1 >= q0+665-2796k/L.

Using (1),(2),

       q1 >= 1-(266001/666000)C-(1531/333)k/L.       (4)

At (15,12), let a coalition (10,12) withhold (10,10), leaving (5,2) and a
retained coalition holding (0,2). The h=3 shift contributes 20k/L:

       10p2+12q2 >= 2q1+10-20k/L.                  (5)

By (2),(3), the left side of (5) is at most 23C+31k/L. Substituting (4)
therefore gives

       (2641667/111000)C >= 12-(20045/333)k/L.       (6)

4. EXACT ELIMINATION

Put A=2641667/111000, B=20045/333, d=999/1000. From (1), C<=(k-1)/d.
Combining with (6),

       k >= (12+A/d)/(A/d+B/L).

At L=2000 this is exactly 1588934000/1058001797 > 3/2, with difference
3862609/2116003594. Every comparison uses integer owner counts. The astronomical
N is intentional: this certificate establishes finite realization, not a
practical adversarial instance. The sharper asymptotic constant 1.543063... is
proved separately in UNRESTRICTED_OUTSIDE_SHARP_CANDIDATE.txt.

Verification: check_finite_outside_obstruction.py uses exact Fraction arithmetic
to check all coefficients, the four dilation factors, and the final bound. It
does not enumerate all N-owner profiles or substitute for the proof above.
