RECENT FREE PRIMARY COMPARATOR FOR THE OUT CONTRACT
17 October 2026. Limited source check, not a novelty confirmation.

Fujinaka and Wakayama, Endowment manipulations involving population variations
in object exchange problems, UTMD-095, dated26 October2025:
https://www.mdc.e.u-tokyo.ac.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/UTMD-095.pdf

Read abstract, introduction, model, withdrawal definition and Theorem1
(printed pages1--8), plus relevant references. Agents each own one indivisible
object and have ordinal preferences. Withdrawal removes an agent and object;
the withdrawing agent can subsequently exchange with a participant. Theorem1
rules out individual rationality plus withdrawal-proofness on the specified
rich domains; weakened variants support TTC characterizations. The paper
connects Doğan's absence-proofness to coalitional withdrawal and motivates
the issue with internal matching by hospitals.

Comparison: our contribution candidate concerns divisible production resources,
fixed actual owners, monetary transfers, partial resource withholding, and
explicit worst-case external funding. The two-input and r-input constants,
aggregate-price reduction, and subsidy frontier are not supplied by the stated
object-exchange results. This is a conceptual predecessor and terminology
guide, not evidence that withdrawal protection itself is new. Its references
identify Postlewaite1979 on endowment withholding/hiding and Thomson2014 on
variable-population manipulation as important earlier comparisons.
