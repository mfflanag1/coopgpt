appropriate one-constraint knapsack. Conversely each candidate supplies a
feasible dual. Therefore equation(1), F=min(P+Q,K_P,K_Q), is valid.

profits strictly positive, forces every cap to be filled and hence b.c<=Q;

zero even at exact capacity equality. This proves the stated tie lemma.
