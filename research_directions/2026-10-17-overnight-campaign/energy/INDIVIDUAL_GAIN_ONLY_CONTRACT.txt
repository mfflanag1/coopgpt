INDIVIDUAL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION INCENTIVES WITH GAIN-ONLY FUNDING
17 October 2026. Candidate theorem; root co-derived, independent audit pending.
Historical priority unconfirmed. The grand-marginal ingredient is established;
the economic comparison and residual two-pool construction require comparison.

CONTRACT AND DENOMINATOR
Fix actual owners with binding nonnegative resource underreports. F is finite,
nonnegative, monotone, concave and degree-one homogeneous on the nonnegative
orthant, with F(0)=0. Coalition value is F(sum of its reported bundles).
At a profile write C=sum_i b_i, f_i=F(b_i), S=sum_i f_i, and G=F(C)-S>=0.
Coverage is required for EVERY coalition. Individual full outside-production
protection means, for every owner i and every 0<=d<=b_i,

  y_i(b_i,b_-i)-y_i(b_i-d,b_-i)>=F(d).                  (IC-OUT)

Withdrawn resources may be used by that owner alone. This does not protect a
coalition's joint production, transfers, or simultaneous strategic changes.

1. ESTABLISHED GRAND MARGINALS GIVE A UNIVERSAL GAIN-ONLY CONTRACT
Set m_i=F(C)-F(C-b_i). These payments satisfy coverage and (IC-OUT), and

  sum_i m_i <= S+nG.                                    (1)

For a finite collection of owners, the concave homogeneous production game
has a nonnegative efficient core allocation: use a supporting linear majorant
at C, restricted to the active resource face, or the standard balancedness
argument. Let z_i be such an allocation. Complement coverage implies
z_i<=F(C)-F(C-b_i)=m_i, so m covers every coalition. This is the standard
grand-marginal coverage argument. At zero total production all quantities
are zero. Boundary faces can be treated directly in the induced game.

When only owner i restores d, the aggregate of the OTHER owners is fixed.
Hence its payment increment is exactly F(C)-F(C-d)>=F(d), by superadditivity.
This proves (IC-OUT) directly, including arbitrary mixed bundles and faces.
Finally F(C-b_i)>=sum_{h!=i}F(b_h)=S-f_i, so m_i<=f_i+G. Summing gives (1).
This last inequality uses only superadditivity; the coverage claim uses the
concave homogeneous production class stated above.

Thus the mechanism is efficient whenever G=0, even with positive standalone
output. Unlike the strong coalition contract in ZERO_COOPERATION_GAIN_RESERVE_
OBSTRUCTION.txt, individual incentives permit genuine gain-only funding.

2. MIN-r IMPROVES n TO r FOR THE SAME GRAND-MARGINAL RULE
For F(c)=min_{1<=j<=r}c_j, let f_i=min_j b_ij, residual u_ij=b_ij-f_i, and
R_j=sum_i u_ij. Then G=min_j R_j and

  m_i=f_i+G-min_j(R_j-u_ij)
     =f_i+max_j [u_ij-(R_j-G)].                           (2)

Each residual marginal is nonnegative. Using positive parts only increases
the sum, and for nonnegative numbers summing to R_j>=G,
sum_i [u_ij-(R_j-G)]_+<=G. Indeed, if k terms are positive, their sum is at
most R_j-k(R_j-G)<=G for k>=1; if none is positive the sum is zero.
Therefore

  sum_i m_i <= S+rG.                                     (3)

The coefficient r is sharp FOR GRAND MARGINALS: with r pure unit owners,
one for each resource, S=0,G=1, and every m_i=1. This is not an unrestricted
individual-incentive lower bound. The residual transformation above is used
only for accounting; it is not coordinatewise monotone and is NOT the proof
of (IC-OUT). That proof was already supplied in original resources in §1.

3. TWO POOLS: A TRANSPARENT RESIDUAL HUBER CONSTRUCTION WITH FACTOR 3/2
For owner i with bundle (a_i,b_i), define
 f_i=min(a_i,b_i), x_i=(a_i-b_i)_+, z_i=(b_i-a_i)_+.
Write A=sum x_i, B=sum z_i, T=min(A,B)=G.
Apply the existing two-pool Huber/integral prices to these pure residuals:

 A<=B, B>0: p_A=1-A/(2B), p_B=A/(2B);
 A>=B, A>0: p_A=B/(2A),   p_B=1-B/(2A).

At A=B=0 all residual payments are zero. If one total is zero the positive
resource receives price zero; the absent resource's numerical price is
irrelevant. Pay

  y_i=f_i+x_i p_A+z_i p_B.                               (4)

Then (4) covers every coalition, satisfies INDIVIDUAL full (IC-OUT), and

  sum_i y_i <= S+(3/2)G.                                 (5)

Coverage. For every coalition Q, subtracting its owners' f_i from both
resource totals gives the exact identity
 F(b(Q))=sum_{i in Q} f_i+min(sum_{i in Q}x_i,sum_{i in Q}z_i).
Residual prices are nonnegative and sum to one whenever T>0, so the second
term is covered. If T=0 every coalition's residual production is zero.
Funding follows from the accepted two-pool Huber bound 3T/2, or directly:
if A<=B, total residual payment=3A/2-A^2/(2B)<=3A/2.

Own-coordinate monotonicity. Hold others' residual reports fixed. An owner's
pure residual payment u(q), as a function of its one residual capacity q>=0,
is nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz. Let C=q+o be the total of its residual type
and D the fixed opposite total. In the scarce region C<=D,

  u'(q)=1-(C+q)/(2D),                       0<=u'<=1;

in the long region C>=D,

  u'(q)=D(C-q)/(2C^2),                      0<=u'<=1/2.

The values and derivatives agree at C=D; u(0)=0; the case D=0 is identically
zero. Thus the bounds hold across all ties and zero boundaries. When an owner
increases an abundant coordinate, f_i stays fixed and u increases. When it
increases its deficient coordinate before equality, f_i increases one-for-one
while q decreases one-for-one, so the loss in u is at most the increase in f_i.
The two cases join continuously where its own coordinates become equal.
Hence (4) is nondecreasing in either own coordinate on the full domain.

Full individual outside production. Starting from b_i-d, first restore
delta=min(d_a,d_b) to BOTH coordinates. This increases f_i by delta and
leaves all residuals unchanged, so payment increases by exactly delta.
Restore the remaining amount in the one remaining coordinate; own monotonicity
makes this increment nonnegative. Total increase is at least delta=F(d),
proving (IC-OUT) without differentiating a minimum or assuming strict reports.

The factor 3/2 is a simple UPPER bound, and is NOT our best available bound.
The residual rule itself approaches 3/2 as its two residual aggregate totals
become highly imbalanced. Section 4 records an existing stronger construction.

4. EXISTING EXPONENTIAL RULE ALREADY IMPROVES THE GAIN FACTOR TO 1+1/(2e)
Root's earlier OUTSIDE_PRODUCTION_BOUNDS.txt §1 proves the same ownerwise
netting construction using the established capped exponential pure-side rule.
For residual T=min(A,B)>0, set

  rho=sum_long min(z_i,T)/T >=1,   q=exp(1-rho)/2.

Each short-side owner receives (1-q)z_i and each long-side owner receives
q min(z_i,T). Its own scalar payment is nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz:
on the long side the derivative is q(1-z_i/T) below the cap and zero above;
on the short side it is 1-q-z_i q'(T), between zero and one because
q'(T)<=q rho/T, z_i<=T and q(1+rho)<=1 for rho>=1. Values join at cap and
side-change boundaries. The identical netting and diagonal-restoration proof
from §3 therefore yields individual full OUT and coalition coverage, with

  sum_i y_i <= S+[1+1/(2e)]G.                            (6)

Indeed residual total is [1+q(rho-1)]T and the scalar maximum occurs at
rho=2. This is a ROUTINE gain-normalized restatement of the earlier theorem,
whose proof already gives sum standalone plus K times residual production.
It must not be presented as new improvement over that prior campaign result.
Historical novelty and the optimal unrestricted individual gain factor remain
open. The Huber example is retained only for a rational transparent mechanism
and the exact comparison below.

5. EXACT LIMIT: THE HUBER RESIDUAL RULE FAILS COALITION MONOTONICITY
Take three fixed owners (1,0),(1,0),(0,1). S=0,G=1 and payments are
(1/4,1/4,3/4), total5/4. Restore only the third owner's first coordinate,
obtaining (1,0),(1,0),(1,1). Now S=1,G=0 and payments are (0,0,1), total1.
The grand coalition loses1/4 upon a feasible restoration, violating TCWM.
This is consistent with the separate zero-gain reserve theorem. Individual
payment rises from3/4 to1, exactly as the weaker contract permits.

6. THE NATURAL min-r RESIDUAL WRAPPER ALREADY FAILS AT r=3
Replacing the two-pool residual rule in (4) by the existing min-r integral
prices is NOT automatically individual monotone. An exact rational deviation
is recorded by check_individual_gain_contract.py and the companion obstruction
note. The issue is a one-unit own-coordinate rise in f_i forcing a simultaneous
decrease of r-1 residual coordinates; the residual payment can fall faster
than one. Thus (5) has been proved here only for two resource types.

ECONOMIC SCOPE
S is actual independently feasible output from each fixed owner's resource
bundle, and G is added output from pooling those bundles. The individual
contract makes payments efficient when pooling adds nothing. It permits
profitable coordination by multiple owners and therefore changes the
incentive promise relative to full OUT. It does not model private costs,
energy dispatch, nonfungible GPU memory, request latency, or measured gains.
These remain separate modeling and empirical gates.
