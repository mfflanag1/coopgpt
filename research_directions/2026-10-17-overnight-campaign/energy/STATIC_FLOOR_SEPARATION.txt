PROOF OF THEOREM A: STATIC UPPER
First take the unit game T=min(E,H). For E<=H, pay each energy owner its e_j
and hardware owners zero. This is efficient, covers all coalitions, and meets
all full marginal floors, so it also meets fractional floors.

For H<=E, normalize H=T=1. Put d_j=min(e_j,1), rho=sum_j d_j,
m_j=(1-E+e_j)_+, and w=max_j m_j/d_j, interpreting a zero numerator and
zero denominator as zero. Every m_j<=d_j, so 0<=w<=1. Pay energy owner j
w d_j, and hardware owner i (1-w)h_i. These payments meet the full floors.
For any coalition, h(S)<=H and sum_{j in S}d_j>=min(e(S),H), so the convex
combination covers min(h(S),e(S)). Total payment is 1+w(rho-1).

If two energy owners have quantities >=1, all m_j are zero and the total is1.
If exactly one energy owner b has e_b>=1, its marginal is (1-t)_+ where
t=sum_{j!=b}e_j; all other marginals vanish. If t>=1, w=0. If t<1, all
other quantities are <1, rho=1+t and w=1-t, so excess is t(1-t)<=1/4.
If all energy quantities are <=1, rho=E=1+delta. For delta>=1, w=0.
For 0<=delta<1, let e_max=max_j e_j<=1. Then
 w=(1-delta/e_max)_+,
so excess delta w <= delta(1-delta)<=1/4.
This proves the full-floor upper 5/4. The fixed convex mixture with weight
theta of this static allocation and weight 1-theta of an efficient core
allocation gives 1+theta/4 and every theta floor. At T=0 all payments arezero.

For public source costs/service values, integrate this pointwise allocation over
the exact public price decomposition F(S)=integral min(E_t(S),H_t(S))dt from
the companion theorem. Marginals telescope. Thus the same static upper holds
for every profile, including multiple public tiers per actual same-side owner.
This step makes no claim that the static allocation is own-report monotone.

STATIC LOWER
Take energy owners A,C with quantities1 and1/2 and cost0, and hardware owners
H1,H2 with quantities1/2 each and value1. Grand worth F=1, A's marginal is1/2.
Write a,c,x1,x2 for any feasible nonnegative static payments. The coalitions
A+H1+H2, C+H1 and C+H2 and A's floor give
 a+x1+x2 >=1,
 c+x1 >=1/2,
 c+x2 >=1/2,
 a >=theta/2.
Multiply the first inequality by1/2, the middle two by1/2 each, and the last
by1/2. Their sum is a+c+x1+x2 >=1+theta/4, proving sharpness.
The remaining energy floor is zero and does not invalidate the example.

PROOF OF THEOREM B
Always B_1>=F by grand-coalition coverage. The internally audited all-profile
rule pays <=4F/3, hence <=4B_1/3, establishing K_1<=4/3.
For the reverse inequality use the companion theorem's same two profiles:
energy owners A,C each quantity1, one hardware owner quantity h=1 or h=2.
At h=1, both energy marginals are zero. Paying hardware1 and energy0 is a
static feasible core allocation, so B_1=F=1.
At h=2, paying both energy owners1 and hardware0 meets all coalitions/floors,
so B_1=F=2. Thus a factor K relative to B_1 on these two profiles satisfies
the identical inequalities x>=2-K and x'<=2K-2. Hardware own-monotonicity
forces x<=x', hence K>=4/3. This lower is independent of payment architecture.
