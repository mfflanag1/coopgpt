NO COMMON SCALAR DEGREE CORRECTION CAN SATISFY PAIR-TCWM
17 October 2026. Exact four-owner architecture obstruction; audit pending.

For a pure residual min2 profile z, define the interaction degree
 d_i(z)=sum_{j:z_i z_j<0} min(|z_i|,|z_j|).
Consider ANY rule u_i(z)=gamma(z)d_i(z), with an arbitrary finite nonnegative
common scale gamma(z), and original payment y_i=f_i+u_i(a-b). No continuity,
optimality, homogeneity, anonymity or budget-minimality of gamma is assumed.

THEOREM. On the unrestricted fixed mixed-owner report domain, no such rule
can simultaneously cover all coalitions and satisfy pair-TCWM, already with
four actual owners. The theorem includes choosing the smallest covering
scale gamma=max_Q v(Q)/sum_{i in Q}d_i and every larger common-scale repair.

1. LOW PROFILE FORCES THE SCALE
Take signed residual capacities
 z_L=(1,1/4,-1,0), d_L=(1,1/4,5/4,0).
The protected pair {0,2} can produce1. If owner2's negative capacity is zero,
there is no production and all interaction degrees vanish. The residual
pair upper-Lipschitz property therefore bounds their total at z_L by1.
Coverage gives the reverse inequality, so
 gamma_L*(1+5/4)=1,
 gamma_L=4/9, and u_0(z_L)=4/9.                         (1)

2. HIGH PROFILE FORCES TOO MUCH PAYMENT TO ANOTHER PAIR
Restore1/4 of the negative resource to owner3:
 z_H=(1,1/4,-1,-1/4), d_H=(5/4,1/2,5/4,1/2).
The coalition {0,2} still can produce1, so its coverage requires
 gamma_H*(5/4+5/4)>=1, hence gamma_H>=2/5.               (2)
The protected pair {0,3} must then have payment
 gamma_H*(5/4+1/2)>=(2/5)*(7/4)=7/10.                  (3)
But the upper-Lipschitz condition for increasing owner3's residual magnitude
by1/4 from z_L to z_H requires this total to be at most
 u_0(z_L)+1/4=4/9+1/4=25/36.                            (4)
Since 7/10-25/36=1/180>0, (3)--(4) contradict one another.

3. DIRECT ORIGINAL-REPORT INCENTIVE COMPARISON
Equivalently, start with the pure reports at z_H and restore1/4 of the positive
resource to owner3. It becomes balanced with standalone1/4 and residual zero;
the resulting residual profile is z_L. Pair {0,3} then receives25/36, whereas
its previous payment was at least7/10. Restoring resources lowers its payment
by at least1/180. The unchanged protected owner0 is allowed by the campaign's
weak-underreport definition. All identities and eligibility remain fixed.

4. SYMBOLIC PARAMETER RANGE
Replace1/4 by t in(0,1). The same argument gives low scale1/(2+t), high scale
at least1/[2(1+t)], and a contradiction whenever
 1/[2(1+t)] > (1+t)^2/[(2+t)(1+3t)],
equivalently 0<t<(sqrt(17)-3)/4.
The rational t=1/4 witness avoids any numerical boundary issue.

CONSEQUENCE
The natural singular p->0 scaled-semivalue endpoint, even with arbitrary
report-dependent common scaling, cannot repair the finite-positive-mixture
obstruction. This closes the common-degree route as a whole, not merely one
choice of scalar. Arbitrary owner-specific degree factors are not excluded;
since d_i>0 for productive residual owners, allowing completely arbitrary
such factors would simply reparameterize a general allocation and does not
by itself supply a constructive mechanism class.

The unrestricted uniform-K pair-TCWM gain-only problem remains open. This note
does not claim an unbounded lower or impossibility for all nonlinear rules.
Historical novelty unconfirmed; independent review requested.
