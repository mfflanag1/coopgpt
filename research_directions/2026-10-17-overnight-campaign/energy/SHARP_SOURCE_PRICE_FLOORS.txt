1. THEOREM AND PRECISE DOMAIN
Fix disjoint finite sets of actual energy owners and hardware owners. Energy
owner j has nonnegative reported quantity e_j and PUBLIC settled unit cost c_j;
hardware owner i has nonnegative reported quantity h_i and PUBLIC service value
v_i. Costs and values are nonnegative and fixed independently of all reports.
Every energy unit can serve every admitted hardware unit in this committed
window. Coalition production is
 F(S)=max Σ_ij (v_i-c_j) z_ij,
 subject to Σ_i z_ij<=e_j(S), Σ_j z_ij<=h_i(S), z_ij>=0.
Unprofitable matches may remain unused; absent owners have zero capacities.
Actual identities and resource roles remain fixed. Deviations are arbitrary
coordinatewise binding underreports, and utility is the owner's net-surplus
payment after the public operating costs have been settled once by the operator.

Require nonnegative coalition-covering payments, all-profile own-bundle
monotonicity, and EVERY energy owner's public floor
 y_j >= M_j := F(N)-F(N\{j}).
Then the optimal universal total-funding factor is EXACTLY4/3, uniformly over
both owner counts and all public costs/values. There is an explicit continuous
rule; subsidy above net production is at most F/3. The sharpness statement is
for the class, not every fixed valuation/cost vector or owner arrangement.
Multiple fixed public cost/value tiers per actual owner are permitted, provided
an actual owner remains wholly on one side. No mixed energy/hardware ownership,
private-cost truthfulness, scheduling/latency, integer tasks or deployment result
is implied. A floor for only one selected energy owner is also respected by the
upper, but the matching4/3 lower REQUIRES the all-energy-owner floor contract.

2. UNIT GAME RULE
First consider unit value/cost-zero production T=min(E,H), where E=Σe_j,H=Σh_i.
At T=0 all payments are zero.

If E<=H (energy is short), put d_i=min(h_i,E), ρ=Σ_i d_i/E>=1,
 q_H(ρ)=exp(1-ρ)/3.
Pay energy owner j exactly e_j and hardware owner i exactly q_H(ρ)d_i.

If H<=E (hardware is short), put d_j=min(e_j,H), ρ=Σ_j d_j/H>=1 and
 q_E(ρ) = 1-ρ/3                    for1<=ρ<=2,
          exp(2-ρ)/3              forρ>=2.
Pay hardware owner i (1-q_E)h_i, and energy owner j
 max{q_E d_j, m_j}, where m_j=(H-E+e_j)_+.
The formulas agree at E=H: every energy owner receives e_j and every hardware
owner h_i/3. They also agree at ρ=2 and at all individual cap thresholds.

3. COVERAGE AND THE FLOORS
For E<=H, each energy owner gets e_j=M_j, where globally
M_j=min(E,H)-min(H,E-e_j). A coalition's worth is no greater
than its total energy quantity, which its energy payments alone cover.
For H<=E, before raising energy payments to m_j, a coalition receives
 (1-q_E)h(S)+q_E Σ_{j∈S}min(e_j,H).
Here h(S)<=H, and the capped energy sum is >=min(e(S),H); both displayed
quantities are >=min(h(S),e(S)), the coalition's worth. Thus this convex
combination covers, and raising energy payments preserves coverage. The stated
m_j equals T-min(H,E-e_j), exactly its unit-game grand marginal. All payments
are nonnegative. Zero own quantities give zero own payment.

4. ALL-PROFILE INCENTIVES
For a long-side owner its capped own quantity d∈[0,T] has payment q(ρ)d with
ρ=(D+d)/T and other-owner D fixed. Its derivative is q+(d/T)q'. It is
nonnegative whenever q+q'>=0. For q_H, q_H'=-q_H. For q_E's linear branch,
q_E'=-1/3 and q_E>=1/3; on its exponential branch q_E'=-q_E. Thus both long-side
payments are own-monotone. A long energy owner's marginal m_j is itself
own-monotone because T-min(H,E-e_j) subtracts a term independent of its report;
therefore the maximum of its two own-monotone payments is own-monotone.

A short energy owner receives exactly its own quantity, hence is own-monotone.
For a short hardware owner, its own report x changes H=T at unit rate while
energy quantities remain fixed. On a cap cell let U=Σ_{e_j<T}e_j, so
 dρ/dT=-U/T², with0<=U/T<=ρ. Differentiating its payment (1-q_E(ρ))x gives
 1-q_E + x q_E' U/T² >= 1-q_E+ρ q_E',
because q_E'<=0 and x<=T. The final expression is zero on1<=ρ<=2.
Onρ>=2 it is1-q_E(1+ρ)>=0: it equals zero atρ2, and
exp(2-ρ)(1+ρ)/3 is nonincreasing forρ>=2. Thus short hardware payments are
own-monotone too. Continuity handles cap cells and the total-side switch.
At T=0 the same nonnegative formulas limit to zero. These arguments apply to
whole bundles after their retained same-side capacities are aggregated within
the actual owner; there is no virtual-owner splitting.

5. FUNDING: ENERGY-SHORT REGIME
Energy payments total T and hardware payments total q_H ρ T. Since
ρ exp(1-ρ)<=1 forρ>=1, total<=T+T/3=4T/3.

6. FUNDING: HARDWARE-SHORT REGIME
Normalize H=T=1. Put d_j=min(e_j,1), ρ=Σd_j>=1 and q=q_E(ρ).
The unraised payments total1+q(ρ-1). This is <=4/3: for1<=ρ<=2,
q(ρ-1)=(1-ρ/3)(ρ-1) is increasing to1/3 atρ2; forρ>=2,
q(ρ-1)=exp(2-ρ)(ρ-1)/3 is nonincreasing from1/3.
It remains to bound any active marginal-floor raises.

If some e_b>=1, all other energy marginals are zero. If two owners have e>=1,
all marginals are zero, so no raise is needed. With a unique large owner b,
put t=Σ_{j≠b}e_j. An active floor requires t<1; then every other e_j<1,
ρ=1+t, q=(2-t)/3 and m_b=1-t. Total is
 max{1+qt, 1+(1-t)(1-q)}.
The first is the already-bounded base total; the second equals
1+(1-t²)/3<=4/3.

Otherwise all e_j<=1. Put E=ρ=1+δ. Ifδ>=1 all marginals are zero. For0<=δ<1,
q=(2-δ)/3. Let B={j:(1-q)e_j>δ}, k=|B|, e_B=Σ_B e_j. Exactly these owners
have active floor raises. Total is
 1+qδ+(1-q)e_B-kδ.                                            (4)
For k0 use the base bound. For k1, e_B<=1, and excess above1 is at most
 (1-q)(1-δ)=(1-δ²)/3<=1/3.
For k>=2, e_B<=1+δ, and excess is at most
 1-q-(k-1)δ=(1+δ)/3-(k-1)δ<=1/3.
This proves the4/3 bound for every real profile.

7. MATCHING LOWER AND ACTIVE GROWING-COUNT FAMILY
Take two actual energy owners A,C, each quantity1 and cost0, and one hardware
owner with value1. Compare hardware report h=1 to h=2, holding all others fixed.
Let a,c,x be payments at h1 and let κ be a uniform funding factor. Coalitions
A+H and C+H force a+x>=1 and c+x>=1. Budget a+c+x<=κ gives x>=2-κ.
At h2, each energy owner's grand marginal is1, so their floors plus budget
force hardware payment x'<=2κ-2. Own-H monotonicity gives x<=x', hence
2-κ<=2κ-2, or κ>=4/3. This lower applies to ANY rule satisfying the contract,
without symmetry, continuity, separability or a concentration-price architecture.

To realize the same limiting obstruction with both counts diverging and strictly
positive capacities, for each k>=2 take k energy owners: two have quantity1 and
k-2 have quantityε. Take k hardware owners: one reports1 or2, and k-1 each have
quantityε, with0<ε<=1; all costs0 and values1. At the low profile H_low=1+(k-1)ε and the two
big-energy-plus-main-hardware coalitions again force the main hardware payment
at least2-κ H_low. At the high profile E=2+(k-2)ε < H_high=2+(k-1)ε, so EVERY
energy owner's marginal floor equals its own positive quantity. Therefore the
main hardware payment is at most(κ-1)E. Monotonicity yields
 κ >= [4+(k-2)ε]/[3+(2k-3)ε].
With ε=1/k² this tends4/3. Every owner has strictly positive capacity and access
to productive matches; no null owner, zero padding or virtual identity is used.
This is a concentrated active asymptotic family, not a diffuse-owner theorem.

8. EXACT PUBLIC SOURCE-PRICE DECOMPOSITION
For each real threshold t>=0 retain energy tiers with c_j<=t and hardware tiers
with v_i>=t. Let E_t(S),H_t(S) be their coalition totals. Then coalitionwise
 F(S)=∫_0^∞ min(E_t(S),H_t(S))dt.                             (5)
Proof: order energy units by increasing public cost c(x) and hardware units by
decreasing service value v(x). For any fixed served quantity the cheapest energy
and highest-value hardware are optimal; select the initial range with v(x)>=c(x).
Its net surplus is ∫(v(x)-c(x))_+dx. Integrating the nonnegative indicator
c(x)<=t<=v(x) in the opposite order, the x-length at t is exactly
min(E_t,H_t), proving(5). These step functions have finite support and finite
quantities, so interchanging the integrals is elementary. This proof uses one
feasible allocation, not unrelated threshold optima.

Run sections2-6's unit rule in every component and integrate payments using fixed
nonnegative interval lengths. Retained sets depend only on public costs/values,
so actual-owner underreports remain componentwise allowed. Equation(5) gives
coalition coverage and total<=4F/3. For each energy owner j, integrated marginal
floors equal
 ∫[T_t(N)-T_t(N\{j})]dt=F(N)-F(N\{j}),
which proves the contractual floor. Fixed costs0 and values1 embed section7,
so the full public-price class has the same sharp universal factor4/3.
At F0 every nonnegative component value vanishes, hence so do payments.

A fixed energy owner may hold several public cost tiers, and a hardware owner
several public service-value tiers. At each threshold FIRST aggregate retained
capacity within each actual owner; apply the same rule to those aggregate actual
owners. Joint own-tier underreports decrease that owner's pooled component
quantity, and removing it removes all its retained tiers. Thus incentives and
floors persist. An owner may not span the two roles in this theorem.

9. FRACTIONAL FLOORS AND ACCOUNTING

symmetric exponential floor-free primitive gives all energy floors≥theta M_j

c_j is a PUBLIC settled cost used once in NET production. A marginal floor is
