SIX-PROFILE OBSTRUCTION TO FIXED POSITIVE UNIT-SUBGRAPH DECOMPOSITION
17 October 2026. Exact analytic method obstruction, with independent rational
primal/dual replay. It is NOT an unrestricted mechanism impossibility, an
optimal funding lower bound, or a claim of historical novelty.

STATEMENT
In the shared-local domain, fixed owners are A,C,H_1,H_2,H_3. Let a=(3,0,2),
b=(3,4,5) be the public local/grid margins. There are no nonnegative constants
w_G, indexed by unit-value subgraphs G of the same bipartite graph {A,C}×{H_i},
such that, for EVERY real capacity profile r,
 F_{a,b}(r)=Σ_G w_G F_G(r).
Each F_G retains the SAME reports L,P,h_i and fixed owner identities and permits
exactly its selected edges, with unit profit on selected edges. All64 graphs,
including empty, are allowed. Coefficients may depend on the public matrix but
not on capacity reports. This forbids both finite and measure-valued mixtures,
since there are only64 distinct component functions. It does not rule out
rescaled/auxiliary resources, signed or report-dependent decompositions, a new
payment rule, or a bound below2 by another method.

SIX PROFILES
Profile order is (L,P,h1,h2,h3). Let the linear functional be
 D(f)=f(r1)+f(r3)+f(r4)+f(r6)-f(r2)-f(r5), where
 r1=(0,1,2,2,0),  F(r1)=4;
 r2=(0,2,1,1,0),  F(r2)=7;
 r3=(1,0,2,0,2),  F(r3)=3;
 r4=(1,1,0,2,1),  F(r4)=6;
 r5=(2,1,1,0,2),  F(r5)=10;
 r6=(2,2,1,0,0),  F(r6)=3.
The values follow directly by allocating to the best routes; exact duals also
certify no fractional improvement. Thus D(F)=4+3+6+3-7-10=-1.

UNIVERSAL UNIT-GRAPH INEQUALITY
Write the graph's six edge indicators as A,B,C on the local row and D,E,F on
the grid row, each in{0,1}. (These letters are Boolean labels in this proof,
not owner names or production functions.) Direct feasible allocations give
 u1=max(D,E), u2=D+E, u3=max(A,C), u6=max(A,D),
 u4=max(B+E,B+F,C+E,C,F),
 u5=max(min(2,A+2C), D+2C, F+A+C).
These formulas also follow from the unit transportation LP; shared hardware3
has capacity1 at r4 and capacity2 at r5, explaining their different terms.
Since max(D,E)-(D+E)=-DE and u4>=max(C+E,F), it suffices to prove
 max(min(2,A+2C),D+2C,F+A+C)
 <=max(A,C)+max(A,D)+max(C+E,F)-DE=:K.                        (*)

If C=1, then K=2+E+max(A,D)-DE. The three terms on the left of(*) are
2, D+2, and F+A+1. The first is <=K since E-DE>=0. For the second,
K-(D+2)=(E-DE)+(max(A,D)-D)>=0. For the third,
K-(F+A+1)=(1-F)+(E-DE)+(max(A,D)-A)>=0.

If C=0, then K=A+max(A,D)+max(E,F)-DE and the left terms reduce to A,D,F+A.
For A, max(A,D)>=D>=DE ensures K>=A. For D, max(A,D)>=D and max(E,F)>=E>=DE
ensure K>=D. For F+A, if D=0 use max(E,F)>=F; if D=1 then max(A,D)=1 and
K-(F+A)=1-E+max(E,F)-F>=0. Thus(*) holds in every Boolean case.
Consequently D(F_G)>=0 for every unit subgraph G. Any fixed nonnegative sum
would also have D>=0, contradicting the weighted function's D=-1. QED.

WHY THIS IS A STRUCTURAL GATE
The per-pod-local theorem ROUTE_VALUES_THEOREM.txt succeeds by an exact positive
sum of fixed unit games. Sharing the local budget across pods removes that
independent baseline. This certificate proves the broadest same-row unit-
subgraph version of that reduction already fails in the shared-budget class.
Because any claimed all-size reduction must also handle these fixed five owners,
it blocks a universal reduction on the unbounded class. It is not presented as
an active-count asymptotic lower family. The first continuation needs a genuinely
new weighted mechanism, a more expressive positive decomposition, or the
separable source-price restriction proved in SHARED_SOURCE_PRICE_THEOREM.txt.
