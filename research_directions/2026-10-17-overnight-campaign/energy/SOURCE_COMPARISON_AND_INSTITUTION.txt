PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON AND ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
Checked 17 October 2026. Free sources only. No purchase or outreach performed.
This is a targeted comparison, NOT historical novelty confirmation. It records
what was read rather than inferring nonexistence from search results.

1. THE CLOSEST VERIFIED CAPACITY-REPORT PRECURSOR
Yingqian Zhang and Mathijs de Weerdt (2007), VCG-based truthful mechanisms for
social task allocation, EUMAS proceedings, accepted original manuscript:
https://research.tue.nl/files/117653881/eumas07.pdf
Read model pp1-6, section3.2 pp8-9, the paper's original pagination.
Their resource-owning contractors report quantities/types; resources are prepaid
or their usage cost negligible. Public tasks have fixed benefits and resource
requirements, with network restrictions and indivisibility. The exact mechanism
makes total utility W-W_without_i and explicitly explains why underreporting
resources cannot increase it. Thus grand-marginal utility and its capacity
underreport monotonicity are PRIOR INGREDIENTS, not contributions here.
Our proposed contribution is the specific subsidy/floor/coalition-coverage
tradeoff for continuous shared-source production. Their mechanism-design
objective and allocation-dependent utility accounting differ. Their displayed
payment-budget discussion should not be imported algebraically without checking
its separate intrinsic-utility and transfer ledgers.
Access: free institutional accepted original; not just an abstract.

2. VCG / MARGINAL CONTRIBUTION INTERPRETATION
David Delacretaz, Simon Loertscher and Claudio Mezzetti (2021 working version),
When Walras Meets Vickrey:
https://simonloertscher.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/DLM_2021_04_17.pdf
Read introduction and section3 pp6-9. Their equation for VCG PAYOFF is welfare
minus welfare without the agent and its endowment. They study private valuation
reports in asset markets, characterize VCG deficits through Walrasian prices,
and discuss efficient individually rational dominant-strategy mechanisms.
This supplies a useful interpretation of M_j as a marginal-contribution/VCG-
utility benchmark under a suitable matching normalization. It does NOT make
our capacity-only payment rule truthful for private opportunity costs or values.
Call our condition a one-sided marginal-surplus contract; a VCG benchmark is an
interpretation, not an implemented VCG mechanism or evidence of a real contract.
Access: free author original, 54 pages; cited comparison concerns sections read.

3. ELECTRICITY CORE-SELECTION PRECURSOR
Orcun Karaca and Maryam Kamgarpour, Core-Selecting Mechanisms in Electricity
Markets, IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid11(3),2604-2614(2020), free original:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09646
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.09646
Read framework section2, main claims and Lemma2. They study electricity reverse
auctions with an operator and cost bids, relate core selection to competitive
equilibrium, and quantify profitable unilateral bid deviations by the gap to
VCG utility. Core selection generally approximates rather than guarantees
truthfulness for their richer bid domain. Their exchange-market budget balance
is not our externally subsidized production-payoff budget.
Essential comparison: our all-profile incentives are exact only for binding
capacity reductions with public costs already settled. Do not describe our
result as solving truthful electricity markets, replacing LMP, or obtaining
cost-truthful core selection.
Access: free original arXiv manuscript with journal metadata.

4. DIRECT COMPARISON WITH MARINA'S BID-DIRECTION PAPER
Marina Nunez and Francisco Robles (2024), Overbidding and underbidding in package
allocation problems, Social Choice and Welfare62,739-759:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00355-024-01506-5
Read full-text abstract, introduction and model. One seller owns indivisible
objects; buyers report valuations for packages. The article studies directional
valuation misreports and characterizes the Vickrey rule using efficiency,
individual rationality and over/underbidding-proofness. Its underbidding means
lower package VALUES, not withholding a physical capacity. This makes it a
valuable model-comparison citation and specialist-review entry, not a direct
contradiction of our capacity-only theorem.
Access: open publisher original.

5. RECENT ASSIGNMENT/TRANSPORTATION STATIC THEORY
Saadia El Obadi, Silvia Miquel and Marina Nunez (2026), Assignment markets with
capacitated middlemen: core and competitive equilibria, IJGT55,39:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00182-026-01007-y
Read abstract and introduction. Three disjoint sides, unit buyers/sellers and
capacitated middlemen, with pair values independent of middleman identity.
They characterize nonempty core and competitive-equilibrium/dual relations.
The introduction also explicitly distinguishes transportation-game cores from
competitive payoff and dual sets. This confirms that static LP/assignment core
existence is established background and should never be sold as our novelty.
Our dynamic contract needs capacity-report inequalities linking distinct
profiles; static dual feasibility alone does not supply them.
Access: open publisher original, published25August2026.

6. CLOUD-PRICING COOPERATIVE-GAME PRECURSOR
Gideon Blocq, Yoram Bachrach and Peter Key, The Shared Assignment Game and
Applications to Pricing in Cloud Computing, free author original:
https://peterbkey.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/fp507-blocq.pdf
Read abstract, framework and Shapley discussion/section2.1. Sellers have resource
constraints; goods/tasks are indivisible and heterogeneous. The paper proposes
Shapley revenue sharing and discusses manipulation and practical payment issues.
This precludes claiming cooperative-game cloud pricing or resource-capacitated
cloud assignment as new. Our proposed difference is a continuous net-surplus
model with coalition coverage, capacity-underreport incentives, and a sharp
uniform subsidy under explicit energy-owner marginal floors.
Access: free author PDF original; bibliographic date should be imported from
verified journal/conference metadata rather than inferred from URL date.

7. RECENT VCG/CORE WORK WITH JOINT FEASIBILITY CONSTRAINTS
Lawrence M. Ausubel and Oleg Baranov (2026), The VCG Mechanism, the Core, and
Assignment Stages in Auctions, free author version dated2March2026:
https://www.obaranov.com/docs/Ausubel-Baranov-Assignment-Stage.pdf
Publisher record: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022053126000554
Read abstract and section6 plus linked main propositions. Joint feasibility can
create problematic VCG outcomes even with substitutable individual values;
they develop bidder complementarity for spectrum assignment stages. Section6
sets out marginal-contribution inequalities connecting bidder substitution and
VCG/core conditions. This is relevant conceptual context for why a physical
feasibility model matters. It is not our continuous capacity-report/floor
funding theorem. Publisher full page failed, so comparison uses author original.

10. WHAT CAN BE CLAIMED NOW
Established inputs: fractional transportation/sorted supply-demand allocation,
static LP/core theory, grand-marginal utility and its capacity monotonicity,
public fixed price-threshold integration, and our accepted earlier cap-price
primitives. Routine local-route reduction is expressly labelled routine.
Proposed and internally checked here: asymmetric full-floor mechanism with
sharp4/3, its static5/4 versus dynamic4/3 distinction, the shared-row positive-
layering obstruction, and the candidate improved partial-floor upper/boundary.
Historical priority for those precise combinations remains unconfirmed.

The economic application is a model of net revenue from completed divisible
service within a committed window. PUBLIC verified operating energy costs are
settled once, before surplus allocation. The external sponsor/operator covers
any promised net-surplus subsidy. Capacity reports bind; no concealed units are
resold inside this model. Hardware/energy ownership, admission, topology, costs
and service values are fixed. Latency, integer jobs, private costs, energy
storage dynamics and chance constraints require separate models and evidence.
