COMPLETION: NO IDENTICAL C1 STRICTLY CONVEX SEPARABLE PROJECTION WORKS
17 October 2026. Companion strengthening; independent review pending.

The preceding six-owner theorem assumed phi'(0)=0. This companion removes
that restriction. Take any C1 strictly convex phi:[0,infinity)->R, used
identically and independently of reports in the residual coalition-cover
projection argmin sum_i phi(x_i).

THEOREM. If phi'(0)>=0, the projection fails pair-TCWM. If phi'(0)<0, it
already fails the finite gain-budget contract at the zero production profile
(or fails to attain an optimum). Thus no objective in this entire stated
separable class yields the desired gain-only pair-TCWM mechanism.

Write g0=phi'(0) and L=sup_{x>=0}phi'(x), possibly infinity. Strict convexity
and C1 imply the derivative is continuous and strictly increasing.

CASE 1: g0>=0 and L>2g0.
Choose v>0 sufficiently small that phi'(2v)+phi'(v)<L, which is possible since
the left side tends to 2g0. The construction in
PAIRWISE_TCWM_DISTRIBUTION_SEPARABLE_OBSTRUCTION.txt applies unchanged:
there is s>2 with phi'(sv)=phi'(2v)+phi'(v), and its six-owner low/high KKT
certificates remain nonnegative. The proof did not otherwise use g0=0;
all displayed payment coordinates are positive. The protected pair loses vh.

CASE 2: g0>=0 and L<=2g0.
This case necessarily has g0>0. Use THREE fixed pure owners with capacities
 (A1,A2,B)=(1,t,1), where 1/2<t<1. The claimed unique cover projection is
 (x_A1,x_A2,x_B)=(1-t,0,t).                              (1)
Its two binding productive pairs are {A1,B}, worth1, and {A2,B}, wortht.
All other covers hold: grand total1 covers grand worth1 and same-type or
singleton coalitions have worth0.

Set lambda_1=phi'(1-t)>0 and
lambda_2=phi'(t)-phi'(1-t)>0 on those two productive pair constraints.
At the zero A2 coordinate use nonnegativity multiplier
 eta=g0-lambda_2>=0,
because lambda_2<=L-g0<=g0. These multipliers yield exact stationarity:
 gradient A1=lambda_1;
 gradient B=lambda_1+lambda_2=phi'(t);
 gradient A2=lambda_2+eta=g0.
Feasibility, complementary slackness and convexity prove global optimality;
strict convexity proves uniqueness, including the boundary coordinate.

Now increase the A2 capacity from t=3/4 to t'=7/8. Owner A1 changes zero.
The protected pair {A1,A2} drops from1/4 to1/8. This violates pair-TCWM.
The changed owner's own payment stays zero, so the result again concerns
pairwise payment externalities rather than individual own monotonicity.

CASE 3: g0<0.
At the all-zero capacity profile every nonnegative payment vector is feasible
for the cover constraints, whereas a finite gain budget forces x=0. Since
phi'(0)<0, a sufficiently small positive payment in any coordinate strictly
reduces the objective. Thus zero cannot be a minimizer. If the unconstrained
separable minimum is attained, its selected payment violates the zero gain
budget; if it is not attained, the supposed rule is not defined there.

The theorem is an architecture obstruction. It does not exclude different
owner objectives, report-dependent weights, nonseparable objectives, or a
general non-projection allocation. The earlier exact rational certificates
remain valid. This strengthening adds the analytic three-owner certificate
and elementary derivative-range dichotomy, not a numerical search.
Historical novelty unconfirmed; no external specialist review.
