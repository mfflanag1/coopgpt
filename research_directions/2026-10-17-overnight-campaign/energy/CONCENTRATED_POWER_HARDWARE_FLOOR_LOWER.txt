ONE POWER OWNER: HARDWARE PARTICIPATION FLOORS RESTORE A GROWING OBSTRUCTION
17 October 2026. Candidate corollary of the active-owner ladder; conventional
finite proof and exact checker. Parent audit requested. Novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL AND CLAIM
Take F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_(j=1)^M min(a_j,b_j)). There is ONE fixed actual owner
of P, plus separate fixed actual owners A_j and B_j of the branch inputs a_j
and b_j. All capacities have public upper bound1 and binding underreports.
Require nonnegative coalition-covering payments at every profile, and for
EVERY hardware owner i the public full-owner marginal floor

  y_i >= theta [F(C)-F(C-b_i)],  0<=theta<=1.                 (1)

There need not be any floor for the power owner. Every coalition must be
covered; incentive coalitions may be unrestricted or have size at most s>=2.
The cost of satisfying TCWM already obeys

  kappa >= 1+2 theta D_(M,s),                              (2)
  D_(M,s)=(M-1) product_(m=2)^M k_m/(k_m+1),
  k_m=min(s-1,2m-2).

For unrestricted TCWM, or s>=2M-1, this becomes

  kappa >=1+2 theta (1-1/M) A_M,
  A_M=4^M/[2 binom(2M,M)],                               (3)

so every fixed theta>0 forces Omega(theta sqrt(M)) funding with only
n=2M+1 actual owners and a SINGLE common-power owner. Full OUT implies TCWM,
so the same lower bound applies to full outside-production protection.
This is a lower bound, not an exact optimum or a matching upper theorem.

COMPANION SHARP-RATE UPPER (CONDITIONAL ON ITS SEPARATE AUDIT)

The mixture uses fixed contract weights, not profile-dependent selection.

WHY THIS DOES NOT CONTRADICT THE EFFICIENT VETO-OWNER RULE
Without floor (1), paying F(C) to the power owner and zero to everybody else
is efficient and full OUT. That rule violates (1) whenever a hardware owner
has positive marginal contribution. Thus a negotiated surplus floor changes
the mathematical difficulty. The floor is a public bargaining requirement,
not private cost elicitation, ordinary stand-alone individual rationality,
or a claim about an existing GPU market contract.

PROOF
Symmetrize any proposed all-profile mechanism over branch permutations and
within-branch swaps of the two hardware owners. These are automorphisms of
this FIXED ownership institution and its report domain. Averaging preserves
nonnegativity, funding, coverage, TCWM for the same coalition-size bound, and
the full set of floors (1). It does not merge or strategically relabel owners.
The power owner remains fixed under every automorphism.

At C_m set P=1, a_j=b_j=1 for j<=m, and (a_j,b_j)=(0,1) for j>m. Then F=1.
All 2m active hardware owners have the same payment q_m. Let T_m=2m q_m.
At C_1 each of the two active hardware owners has marginal contribution1:
deleting either destroys the only productive branch. Thus q_1>=theta and

  T_1>=2theta.                                          (4)

For m>=2, C_m is obtained from C_(m-1) by restoring a_m from0 to1. Use the
coalition consisting of A_m and ANY k_m old active hardware owners. Its size
is k_m+1<=s. Its high payment is (k_m+1)q_m. Its low payment is
k_m q_(m-1) plus A_m's nonnegative low-profile payment. TCWM therefore gives

  q_m >= [k_m/(k_m+1)] q_(m-1).                          (5)

No zero-payment convention is used. Inactive owners' positive reserve
payments only strengthen this inequality. Iterating (5) from q_1>=theta gives
T_M>=2M theta product k_m/(k_m+1).

At C_M the coalition containing the power owner and one active pair produces
one. If its power payment is Y_P, coverage yields Y_P+T_M/M>=1. Since ALL
hardware owners are active, the grand payment is Y_P+T_M, so

  kappa >= Y_P+T_M >=1+(1-1/M)T_M.

Substitution proves (2). The central-binomial identity proves (3). Every
profile, owner, deviation and coalition used here is finite and explicit.

CONSEQUENCES
With unrestricted TCWM, Stirling's formula in (3) gives

  liminf_(M->infinity) kappa/sqrt(M) >=theta sqrt(pi),
  liminf_(n->infinity) kappa/sqrt(n) >=theta sqrt(pi/2)

along n=2M+1; zero-eligibility padding supplies even n if required. These
are lower bounds for this FLOOR-CONSTRAINED class, not the floor-free class.

For fixed incentive size s, the recurrence ratio shows D_(M,s) is maximized
at M=s or M=s+1, with equal values. If m0=floor((s+1)/2),

  D_(s,s)=((s-1)/m0) A_m0 ((s-1)/s)^(s-m0)
         ~sqrt(pi/(2e)) sqrt(s).

Consequently the best factors for all such institutions satisfy

  liminf_(s->infinity) kappa/sqrt(s) >=theta sqrt(2pi/e).

Only n=2s+1 owners and d=2s+1 resources are required for this bounded-collusion
lower. Coverage still applies to unrestricted coalitions. This square-root
growth is stronger than the floor-free quarter-power lower, because the
additional public floor supplies a direct nonvanishing seed at C_1.

For example theta=1 and M=4 give A_4=64/35 and unrestricted lower
1+2(3/4)(64/35)=131/35>3.74 with9 fixed actual owners. At the same production
and ownership institution, theta=0 permits efficient full OUT. This finite
contrast is about contract choice, not measured welfare losses.
