THREE OWNERS, TWO RESOURCES: SHARP PAIRWISE OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION GAIN FACTOR 5/4
17 October 2026. Exact candidate theorem, independent audit requested.
Banzhaf allocation and pair-merger cancellation are established ingredients.
The gain-only coverage/optimality synthesis is proposed here, novelty open.

CONTRACT
Exactly three fixed actual owners may report arbitrary nonnegative bundles
in two resource coordinates. F is finite, nonnegative, monotone, concave and
degree-one homogeneous, with F(0)=0. Cover EVERY coalition. Protect full
outside production under withdrawals by any ONE or TWO actual owners:

  y(Q;b)-y(Q;b-d_Q)>=F(sum_{i in Q}d_i), |Q|<=2.          (1)

Write f_i=F(b_i), S=sum_i f_i, T=F(C), G=T-S. Then there is a mechanism with

  T<=sum_i y_i<=S+(5/4)G.                               (2)

For the min2 subclass the coefficient5/4 is SHARP among arbitrary mechanisms
with this contract, even allowing nonzero payments to null-report owners.
It is efficient at G=0. By contrast, allowing all three owners to coordinate
precludes every finite gain-only coefficient on the fully eligible min2 domain.

1. THE CLASSICAL RAW BANZHAF RULE
For each i, independently include each of the other two owners with probability
1/2 and let

  y_i=E_R[F(b_R+b_i)-F(b_R)].                            (3)

This is the unnormalized/probabilistic Banzhaf value; no efficiency normalization
or profile-dependent multiplier is applied. Nonnegativity and singleton coverage
y_i>=F(b_i) follow from superadditivity.

2. ALL SINGLETON AND PAIR WITHDRAWALS ARE PROTECTED
Each marginal in (3) increases by at least F(d_i) when i restores d_i, proving
individual full outside protection. For the pair{i,j} and remaining owner k,
direct cancellation gives

  y_i+y_j=[F(b_i+b_j)+F(C)-F(b_k)]/2.                   (4)

When the pair restores bundles of total d, BOTH changing production terms
increase by at least F(d). Their half-weighted sum therefore increases by at
least F(d), proving(1). Formula(4) also gives pair coverage since
F(C)>=F(b_i+b_j)+F(b_k). These statements hold for arbitrary profiles and
partial withdrawals, including zero resources and boundary discontinuities.

More generally, the same pair cancellation works for ANY actual owner count:
condition on an independent half-sample R of the other owners to get
y_i+y_j=E_R[F(b_R+b_i+b_j)-F(b_R)]. Thus raw Banzhaf universally has pairwise
full OUT. What is special in the present theorem is ALL-coalition coverage.
This pair-neutrality ingredient is established in the Banzhaf literature.

3. TWO-RESOURCE GEOMETRY SUPPLIES GRAND-COALITION COVERAGE
Summing(3) yields

  Y=[3T+sum_i F(C-b_i)-S]/4.                             (5)

The complement-deficit lemma in GAIN_ONLY_MASK_AND_DIMENSION_THEOREM.txt
applied in resource dimension2 gives grand-marginal total at most S+2G,
or equivalently

  sum_i F(C-b_i)>=T+S.                                 (6)

Substitution into(5) gives Y>=T. Together with singleton and pair coverage,
this covers every coalition of the three-owner institution.

Conversely grand marginals dominate an efficient supporting-price core, so
their total is at least T. Hence sum_i F(C-b_i)<=2T, and(5) gives

  Y<=(5T-S)/4=S+(5/4)G.

The same reasoning applies whenever all admissible profiles satisfy the
Hlawka-type inequality(6), for example fixed positive sums/integrals of
two-resource concave homogeneous production functions after nonnegative
linear substitutions. It is NOT enough to observe rank<=2 at just one profile
while allowing unrelated higher-rank deviations elsewhere. The full two-resource
domain stated above automatically satisfies the requirement at every profile.

4. EXACT LOWER BOUND FOR THREE OWNERS AND F=min2
Symmetrize an arbitrary eligible mechanism over the three fixed owner labels
and interchange of the two resource labels. All contract constraints and the
gain budget are preserved by this finite averaging; owners are not strategically
renamed or split.

At P0=((1,0),(0,1),(0,0)), the active owners each receive b>=1/2 by their pair
coverage; the null owner receives w>=0. Restore the null owner to(1,0), giving
P1 with two identical pure-A owners and one pure-B owner. Write x for each
pure-A payment and q for the pure-B payment. Pairwise monotonicity for the
original A owner together with the restored owner implies

  2x>=b+w>=1/2.

Coverage of one A owner and the B owner gives x+q>=1. Therefore

  Y(P1)=2x+q>=1+x>=5/4.

At P1 all standalone values are zero and G=T=1, so any gain coefficient is
at least5/4. Only binary reports and pairwise TCWM were used; the stronger
pairwise OUT contract cannot improve this lower. Combined with the upper,
the min2 optimum is exactly5/4.

6. NEXT STRUCTURAL GATE
The unresolved target is a coverage-preserving modification with pairwise
full OUT and gain-only funding for UNBOUNDED owner counts. Multiplying just
the Banzhaf excess by a fixed number>1 is not a valid repair: two pure
complementary owners have total scaled payment greater than T, while a
constant-value restoration to G=0 forces total T, violating their pairwise
monotonicity. New profile-dependent redistribution or a new obstruction is
needed; this note does not assert that the missing mechanism exists.
