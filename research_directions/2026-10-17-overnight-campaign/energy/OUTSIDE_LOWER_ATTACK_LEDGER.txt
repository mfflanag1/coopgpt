1. MIXED-BUNDLE REPLICATION DOES NOT STRENGTHEN THE EXISTING3/2 CHAIN
Symmetrize any candidate rule over the fixed owner labels and exchange of the
two resources, as in the accepted root TCWM lower. Take m+1 actual owners,
two initial bundles u=(a,b), v=(b,a), with a>=b>=0 and a>0, and m-1 null reports.
The coalition S of the u owner plus all initially null owners gets at least
(a+b)/2 at this baseline: the two active owners have equal payments and the
null-owner payments are nonnegative.

Raise every null S owner to u. The withheld increment when returning to the
baseline is(m-1)u and can produce(m-1)b outside. Full OUT therefore implies
 m x >= (a+b)/2+(m-1)b,
where x is the common payment of each of the m identical u owners at the new
profile. The opposing v owner gets y; each u+v pair produces a+b, so x+y>=a+b.
Grand production is a+mb. Thus this chain proves only
 kappa >=1+(m-1)(a-b)/[2m(a+mb)]
        <=1+(m-1)/(2m)=3/2-1/(2m).
The maximum occurs at b=0, exactly the old TCWM construction. Giving the
replicated owners both resources adds outside production, but adds enough to
the denominator that this family yields no stronger constant.

This is a limit of THIS proof template, not an upper bound on the best lower
bound obtainable from all mixed reports. It is stated explicitly to avoid
recycling the same replication mechanism as a new unrestricted obstruction.

2. WHY THE AGGREGATE-PRICE LOWER CANNOT SIMPLY BE PROMOTED
The logarithmic theorem's lower assumes each owner's payment is linear in its
bundle with prices depending only on the aggregate capacity ratio. A general
mechanism may depend on the complete fixed-owner report distribution. Finite
owner permutation symmetrization supplies equality of payments for identical
reports, but does not erase dependence on the number or sizes of other reports.
Likewise, zeroing reports changes the active population while retaining actual
identities; it does not justify splitting one owner into many new owners.

The root's independently run finite pure-binary anonymous OUT LPs return the
known3/2-1/(2m) lower. This is evidence about those finite domains only. Neither
that computation nor a diffuse heuristic proves an aggregate-price reduction,
continuity, homogeneity in owner counts, or the unrestricted1.543 lower.

3. CURRENT NEXT REQUIREMENT
A stronger lower needs a genuinely new cross-profile comparison that controls
how a general rule responds to changing owner distributions, or a proved
regularization/symmetrization theorem retaining full OUT and the funding bound.
An owner-dependent upper below1.543 would instead separate the architecture
from the unrestricted problem. Either route must preserve the same fixed actual
owner set throughout each strategic comparison.
