REPORT-DEPENDENT FINITE POSITIVE SAMPLING MIXTURES ALSO FAIL
17 October 2026. Four-owner analytic obstruction; independently reviewed.
Superseded in scope by PAIRWISE_TCWM_DISTRIBUTION_CLOSED_SAMPLING_CONE.txt.

Consider the same standalone-plus-residual architecture y_i=f_i+u_i(z), but
now allow an ARBITRARY finite nonnegative measure mu_z on[0,1] at EVERY pure
residual profile z. The law can depend on every report and on public owner
count, with arbitrary mass. It must be common to all owners at that profile:

 u_i(z)=integral B_i^p(z) dmu_z(p).

No continuity, fixed-law assumption, or normalization of mu_z is imposed.
Here this initial statement permits dependence on residual z. Dependence on
all raw reports is handled by the direct-restoration corollary in the later
closed-sampling-cone note; it is not silently assumed in the Lipschitz proof.
As before all actual owners are fixed and eligible for both resource types.

THEOREM. For any n>=4, no such mechanism satisfies all-coalition coverage,
pair-TCWM and finite gain funding. In fact the contradiction uses coverage
and the residual pair upper-Lipschitz property; gain funding is needed only
to justify the broader target contract/reduction, not any numerical bound.

1. A THREE-ACTIVE PROFILE
Let z_L=(2,1,-1,0), padded by null owners if needed. Both positive owners have
the identical expected marginal p(1-p), since either alone can use all of the
unique negative owner's capacity. Write their common payment as
 a=integral p(1-p)dmu_L.
The unique negative owner is paid
 b=integral(2p-p^2)dmu_L.
Null residual owners receive zero for every semivalue and every such mixture.

The protected pair consisting of the capacity2 positive owner and the unique
negative owner has worth1, so a+b>=1. Starting with that negative owner's
capacity zero, all production and all expected marginals are zero. The upper
half of the residual pair-Lipschitz condition bounds the protected pair total
after increasing this negative capacity to1 by1. Therefore a+b=1, or

 3a+integral p^2 dmu_L=1.                              (1)

The last integral is STRICTLY positive: otherwise the finite nonnegative
measure is supported on p0, which would give a+b=0 rather than1. Thus a<1/3.

2. A FOUR-ACTIVE PROFILE
Now restore one unit of the negative resource to the fourth owner:
 z_H=(2,1,-1,-1).
At this profile the capacity2 positive owner and EACH negative owner have
the same expected marginal polynomial 2p-2p^2+p^3. Hence they all receive the
same payment t, even though mu_H may differ arbitrarily from mu_L.
These three owners together can produce2, so coverage forces
 3t>=2, hence t>=2/3.                                  (2)

Protect the capacity2 positive owner and the fourth owner. Between z_L and
z_H, their pure-residual total rises from a to2t while the fourth owner's
capacity rises by1. The upper-Lipschitz condition requires
 2t-a<=1.                                             (3)
But (1)--(2) imply 2t-a>4/3-1/3=1, contradiction.

EQUIVALENT ORIGINAL-REPORT DEVIATION
Start at z_H's pure reports. Restore one unit of the POSITIVE resource to the
fourth owner, whose negative capacity is1. That owner's standalone becomes1
and its residual becomes0, so the protected pair's new payment is1+a. Its
old payment is2t>1+a. This is a genuine binding-report restoration by a fixed
actual owner, and violates pair-TCWM. The other protected member changes zero.

SCOPE BOUNDARY
The finite-positive-measure condition matters. A formal p->0 limit of B_i^p/p
is a pair-interaction allocation and is not representable as an integral of
raw B_i^p against a finite measure. If such singular scaled endpoint kernels
are admitted, the strict inequality a<1/3 can become equality and this proof
alone does not close that larger class. Owner-specific sampling measures or
nonlinear corrections are also outside the theorem. No unrestricted pair-TCWM
impossibility is claimed.

This directly excludes adaptive choices of a positive p(z), including the
candidate B_i^{p(z)}/[2p(z)] whenever p(z)>0 at the decisive low profile. A
targeted numerical scout had found a different violation of that particular
candidate; the present analytic four-owner argument supersedes the scout as
the relevant method boundary. It is not a growing-grid observation.
