SHARP FULL-OUT MARGINAL-FLOOR FRONTIER FOR ANY NUMBER OF COMPLEMENTARY INPUTS
2026-10-17. Routine corollary of the internally audited sharp all-r theorem;
corollary proof recorded here for parent verification. Priority remains open.

MODEL. F(C)=min_(1<=j<=r) C_j, r>=2, fixed actual mixed-resource owners,
nonnegative payments, coalition coverage, and full coalition OUT. In addition
every owner is promised y_i>=theta M_i, with public theta in[0,1] and
M_i=F(C)-F(C-b_i), the full-owner deletion marginal.

THEOREM. The optimal funding factor uniformly over finite owner populations is

                    K_r(theta)=1+(r-1)max(c,theta),

where c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1. The lower only requires one designated protected pure
owner in each of r-1 resource roles; it does NOT follow from one protected owner
overall when r>2. The upper meets the full-owner floor for every mixed owner.

1. THE LOGARITHMIC RULE ALREADY HAS THE c FLOOR

At positive aggregate C and T=min_j C_j, the logarithmic rule has
p_j>=cT/C_j for every j, including the shortest coordinate because q>=c.
For an owner holding b<=C,

       (T/C_j)b_j -(T-C_j+b_j)
           =(C_j-T)(1-b_j/C_j)>=0.

Moreover
 M= T-min_j(C_j-b_j)=max_j(T-C_j+b_j)>=0.

Thus y=b.p>=c(T/C_j)b_j>=c(T-C_j+b_j) for every j, hence y>=cM.
The argument also covers zero production by continuity or directly M=0.

2. THE PROPORTIONAL r RULE HAS THE FULL FLOOR AND FULL OUT

Set p_j^prop=T/C_j when T>0, and zero payments otherwise. Total payment is rT.
The same inequality proves y_i^prop>=M_i. On a region with unique shortest
coordinate s, writing t_j=T/C_j and alpha_j=a_j/C_j for coalition holdings,
the derivatives of coalition payment under additions to itself are

       g_j=t_j(1-alpha_j)>=0 for j!=s,
       g_s=1+sum_(j!=s)alpha_j>=1.

Therefore g.d>=min_j d_j for every nonnegative addition d. Prices agree at
shortest-coordinate ties, and payments converge to zero at zero production.
Piecewise integration proves full OUT on all profiles.

For theta<=c use the logarithmic rule. For theta>=c mix the proportional rule
with weight lambda=(theta-c)/(1-c) and the logarithmic rule with weight1-lambda.
All coverage and OUT inequalities are linear in payments. The floor becomes
[(1-lambda)c+lambda]M=theta M, and funding becomes

 (1-lambda)[1+c(r-1)]+lambda r=1+theta(r-1).

3. MATCHING LOWER WITHOUT MECHANISM REGULARITY ASSUMPTIONS

The sharp floor-free all-r theorem gives K>=1+c(r-1). To show K>=1+theta(r-1),
use m pure owners in each of r-1 long resource roles and one pure owner of the
remaining role. At the final profile every owner reports one unit. Production
is one. Let X_j be total payment to the m owners of long role j, and z the
payment to the single short-role owner.

For each long role j separately, compare with the profile where only its
designated protected owner reports one and its other m-1 owners report zero;
all OTHER roles remain at their final reports. The designated owner's full
deletion marginal is one, so the role-j coalition receives at least theta.
Activating the other owners of that same role produces no outside value on
the withheld bundle and invokes TCWM, implied by OUT. Hence X_j>=theta at the
common final profile. No anonymity or zero inactive-owner payment assumption
is used.

Any coalition consisting of the short owner and one owner from each long role
has worth one. Average these m^(r-1) coverage inequalities to get

                  z+(1/m)sum_j X_j>=1.

Consequently total payment is at least

                  z+sum_j X_j
                  >=1+(1-1/m)sum_j X_j
                  >=1+(1-1/m)(r-1)theta.

Let m tend to infinity. Combining with the floor-free lower proves the theorem.
Every finite comparison preserves actual owners and eligibility; only the
universal worst-case theorem ranges over different finite owner populations.

INTERPRETATION. Protecting a fraction up to c of each owner's grand marginal
requires no additional worst-case funding beyond full OUT. Above that point,
the additional cost grows at slope r-1. The full floor has sharp factor r.
For normalized four-input GPU/memory/bandwidth/energy complementarity, this
is a contract calculation, not evidence of feasible cluster pooling or gains:
the floor-free funding factor is about2.62919 and full marginal floors require4.
Private values/costs, indivisibility, scheduling, and actual outside production
feasibility remain separate modeling questions.
