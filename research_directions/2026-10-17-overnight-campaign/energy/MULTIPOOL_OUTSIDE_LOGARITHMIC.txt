THEOREM. Let r>=2 be arbitrary and let F(C)=min_j C_j. There is a single
nonnegative aggregate-price mechanism for arbitrary fixed mixed-resource owners
which covers every coalition, satisfies full coalition OUT on all real reports,
and uses at most [1+c(r-1)]F(C), where c is the root

               c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1,  c=0.5430633898250876... .

Thus the leading coefficient improves from 1 to c. Compared with the accepted
TCWM optimum (r+1)/2, the additional full-OUT cost is (c-1/2)(r-1). The matching
unrestricted full-OUT lower proves this construction sharp for every r>=2.

MODEL. Owners i hold fixed bundles x_i in R_+^r and may bindingly underreport
any coordinates. For a coalition S let d be its aggregate withheld bundle.
Full OUT means

      sum_(i in S) y_i(x) >= sum_(i in S) y_i(x-d_S)+min_j d_j.

The retained and withheld bundles use disjoint resources. This is stronger
than payment-only coalition underreport monotonicity. No private costs, false
identities, or overreports are introduced.

1. THE RULE

Put t0=1-exp(-1/c), so c(1+t0)=1, and define

 q(t)=1-c-c(1-t)log(1-t)/t,       0<t<=t0;
 q(t)=c/t,                       t0<=t<=1;
 q(0)=1.

At positive aggregate C, choose a coordinate s attaining the smallest amount
T, let S be the second-smallest amount (counting multiplicity), and t=T/S.
Set

      p_s(C)=q(t),
      p_j(C)=c T/C_j for j!=s,
      y_i(x)=sum_j p_j(C)x_ij.

If the minimum is attained at least twice, t=1 and q(1)=c. Every minimum
coordinate then has price c, independent of the choice of s. Ties for second
smallest also create no ambiguity. At T=0 set all payments to zero.

The previously proved one-variable identities are

 q'<0, q+tq'>=0, q+ct>=1,
 q+ct+t(1-t)q'>=1,
 c<=q<=1.

For t<=t0, q+tq'=1+c log(1-t)>=0 and the fourth expression equals 1.
For t>=t0, q+tq'=0 and the fourth expression is c(1+t)>=1.
The function q and its first derivative agree at t0. These identities verify
all transcendental parts of the construction analytically.

2. BUDGET AND COVERAGE

Every non-minimum coordinate contributes exactly cT to total payment, so

      sum_i y_i = T[q(t)+c(r-1)] <= [1+c(r-1)]F(C).

The bound is a supremum approached when the unique minimum stays fixed and
all other coordinates grow. For every coalition with aggregate a,

      p(C).a >= min_j a_j * sum_j p_j(C).

The sum of prices is at least q(t)+ct>=1, using any second-smallest coordinate.
Hence coalition coverage holds. At F(C)=0 every coalition also has zero value.

3. DERIVATIVE TEST FOR FULL OUT

On a region with unique shortest s and unique second-shortest l, write
t_j=T/C_j for j!=s and alpha_j=a_j/C_j for a coalition's holdings a<=C.
Only t_l=t enters q. As the coalition adds a unit of coordinate k to itself,
let g_k denote the derivative of its total mechanism payment. Direct
differentiation gives

 g_j=c t_j(1-alpha_j)                    for j not in {s,l};
 g_l=c t(1-alpha_l)-alpha_s t^2 q';
 g_s=q+c sum_(j!=s) alpha_j+alpha_s t q'.

Since q'<=0, every g_j for j!=s is nonnegative. For g_s the minimum on the
holding-fraction cube is q+tq'>=0. Summing,

 sum_j g_j = q+c sum_(j!=s)t_j
             +c sum_(j!=s)alpha_j(1-t_j)+alpha_s t(1-t)q'
           >= q+ct+t(1-t)q' + c sum_(j not in {s,l})t_j
           >=1.

Consequently g.d>=min_j d_j for every nonnegative addition d: subtract
min_j d_j from each coordinate and use g>=0, sum g>=1.

For a finite coalition underreport compare the low report with the high
report by restoring the withheld bundle along a straight line. Along this
line the outsiders are fixed and coalition fractions stay in [0,1]. Integrate
the displayed derivative inequality to obtain full OUT exactly.

4. TIES, ZERO COORDINATES, AND UNBOUNDED DIMENSION

Prices are continuous on the positive orthant. At a shortest-coordinate tie
both branches assign c to every tied coordinate, and at a second-shortest tie
the same ratio enters q. Along a line there are finitely many ordering changes;
when a line remains in a tie hyperplane, either coincident formula has the
same restricted derivative. The switch q at t0 is C1. Thus the piecewise
integration is valid without a generic-position assumption.

At a zero-production endpoint all payments vanish, and nonnegativity plus
the budget bound makes their total converge to zero as T decreases to zero.
If the restoration path stays on a zero-production face, both outside worth
and mechanism payments are zero. Otherwise integrate over its positive
portion and take the endpoint limit. This proves every boundary case.

The number r and the number of fixed owners are unrestricted finite integers.
The formulas and proof are uniform in both. Owners may hold several resource
types; the proof uses only their actual coalition aggregates, and never splits
owners strategically. The rule is homogeneous, anonymous, and resource-
symmetric as a construction, but no lower-bound claim relies on those choices.

5. SHARPNESS AND VERIFICATION

The difference between c and1/2 is now a proved separation between full OUT and
payment-only TCWM, not an open gap for this technology. The companion lower
removes all architecture assumptions and proves that coupling several nearby
bottlenecks cannot improve this universal full-OUT constant. Arbitrary graph
technologies and the original individual-only incentive problem remain separate
frontiers.

check_multipool_outside.py runs floating diagnostics over varying dimensions,

Those checks do not replace this analytic proof or certify the transcendental
