EXACT THREE-POOL FAILURE OF THE NATURAL STANDALONE-NETTING WRAPPER
17 October 2026. Exact rational rule-specific obstruction, co-derived with
root. This does not preclude a different gain-only individual mechanism.

The accepted min-r integral rule uses prices

  p_j(C)=C_j^(-1) integral_0^min(C) product_{l!=j}(1-t/C_l) dt.

It is TCWM in ORIGINAL bundles. A tempting gain-only wrapper subtracts
f_i=min_j b_ij from every coordinate of each owner, applies these prices to
the residuals u_ij=b_ij-f_i, then returns f_i+u_i dot p(sum u).
Coalition coverage and a funding bound S+(r+1)G/2 transfer algebraically,
but own-report monotonicity does not. Residual subtraction is not monotone.

EXACT FIXED-OWNER DEVIATION
There are two actual owners and three resource types:

  owner 1: (1/10, 10, 0),   owner 2: (9/10, 0, 100).

Both standalone outputs are zero, and residual totals are C=(1,10,100).
Restore only owner1's third coordinate from0 to1/100. Its standalone output
becomes1/100, its first two residual amounts decrease to9/100 and999/100,
and the residual totals become (99/100,999/100,100). Owner2 is unchanged.

For totals a<=b<=c, polynomial integration gives

  p_a=1-a/(2b)-a/(2c)+a^2/(3bc),
  p_b=a/(2b)-a^2/(6bc),
  p_c=a/(2c)-a^2/(6bc).

Substituting exactly yields owner1 payments

  low report:   8893/15000,
  high report:  10887083/18500000,
  loss:         242851/55500000 >0.

The restoration increases a single OWN resource coordinate with actual
identities and all opponents fixed. Its withheld standalone production is
zero, so this violates even ordinary own-report monotonicity, and consequently
also individual full OUT. No asymptotic, floating-point, or tie convention is
needed. The low profile's grand value and the high profile's grand value both
equal1; this is a strategic issue, not a production loss.

DIFFERENTIAL EXPLANATION
At totals (1,10,100), change owner1's first two residual quantities equally.
The resulting derivative of its residual payment is

  p_a+p_b+(1/10)D p_a+10 D p_b,
  D p_a=-1481/30000,    D p_b=2681/60000,

which is greater than1 (the exact checker records the finite deviation).
Thus restoring the original third coordinate increases its standalone term
at rate1 but decreases its residual payment faster than1. The two-pool proof
used a one-coordinate 1-Lipschitz property; in three pools the simultaneous
change of two residual coordinates defeats that argument.

SCOPE
This closes the specific ownerwise residual wrapper around the accepted
uniform-face/integral min-r prices. It is not an unrestricted lower bound
on individual incentives or gain-only subsidy. Grand marginals still give
S+rG, and the two-pool exponential rule gives S+[1+1/(2e)]G. A better general-r
gain-only rule would need a different residual response bound or a direct
mechanism in original owner bundles.
