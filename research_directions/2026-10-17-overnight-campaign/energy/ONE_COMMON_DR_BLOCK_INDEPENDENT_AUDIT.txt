VERDICT
No substantive mathematical gap found. The stated continuous orthant class
admits one all-profile aggregate-price field, preserving arbitrary fixed mixed
owners, coalition coverage and payment-only TCWM at factor3/2. The explicit
polymatroid construction and exact ratio integration are sound. The proof does
not establish full coalition outside-production protection, an efficient
general evaluation algorithm, or a two-common-resource theorem.

1. SMOOTH BLOCK RULE AND FUNDING
For q=E grad F(tP,s c), t,s independent uniform, all gradient coordinates are
nonnegative. Tonelli and the one-dimensional fundamental theorem on truncated
intervals yield P q_P=integral F(P,s c)ds=H_P and c.q_c=integral F(tP,c)dt=H_c.
The zero-axis hypotheses and continuity justify the endpoint limits. Thus
every charged coordinate has finite price despite possibly unbounded
derivatives near an axis. Jensen followed by homogeneity gives
H_P+H_c<=F(P,c/2)+F(P/2,c)<=3F(P,c)/2.

Every sampled gradient majorizes F on its active face by concavity and Euler
homogeneity. A coalition's bundle belongs to the aggregate's active face, so
averaging proves coverage. No majorization of bundles using absent resources
is required; assigning zero prices to absent coordinates causes no gap.

2. THE ONE-COMMON IDENTITY AND FINITE MIXED-OWNER INCENTIVES
Private DR implies every private Hessian entry F_jk<=0, including the diagonal.
Differentiating Euler gives P F_Pj=-sum_k c_k F_kj>=0. This is precisely the
place where having ONE common coordinate matters. Hence private prices are
nondecreasing with P and antitone in private capacities on each fixed face.

Across activation of private rows, finite DR difference quotients still order
prices on all OLD positive coordinates. A newly activated coordinate has no
old outsider holdings. Thus the antitonicity used by the incentive proof is
valid even though absent-coordinate prices are conventionally set to zero.
One should not separately quote global antitonicity of a newly activated
coordinate's price: that convention can give0 before and a positive price
after. Sections3--4 already make the correct old-coordinate distinction.

For an actual coalition with aggregate holdings(a_P,a_c), outside holdings
(o_P,o_c), a_c.q_c=H_c-o_c.q_c increases under private restoration. Its common
payment (a_P/P)H_P also increases. Under common restoration, private prices
increase and the common payment is the product of the increasing share
a_P/(a_P+o_P) and increasing H_P. Zero P or a zero private block starts with
zero payments. Finite composition of these two steps gives arbitrary mixed
restorations. This uses original holdings of the same actual owners, not
virtual owners, cloning, differentiable owner shares or separate pure-owner
incentive assumptions.

3. LOG REGULARIZATION AND COMMON PRICE-FIELD COMPACTNESS
F_epsilon(x)=E F(diag(exp(epsilon z))x) preserves all required properties.
In particular positive diagonal scaling preserves finite private DR, and
both zero-axis identities remain exact. Convolution in logarithmic active
coordinates makes the function smooth on every positive relative face; the
inactive-coordinate marginal of a smooth compactly supported density is again
smooth. This does not require differentiability of the original F.

Monotonicity and homogeneity imply the same relative bounds on ALL faces:
exp(-epsilon)F<=F_epsilon<=exp(epsilon)F. For0<epsilon<=1 all charged prices
therefore lie in the fixed compact intervals
[0,(3e/2)F(C)/C_j]. Absent prices are0. I initially requested that the interval
be made epsilon-independent; composition/root have now explicitly patched it.

The product over all profiles and coordinates is compact. Intersecting the
nested nonempty closed epsilon-tail closures yields one field, equivalently
one subnet with epsilon tending to0. This avoids incompatible profilewise
subsequences. Each coverage, funding or finite TCWM inequality contains only
finitely many prices and convergent F_epsilon values, and is closed. Every
such comparison therefore survives simultaneously. Arbitrarily many finite
owner populations introduce more closed inequalities, not a new compactness
assumption. No uniqueness, measurability, continuity or computational claim
for the final limiting field is needed or asserted.

4. POLYMATROID MAXIMAL-MINIMIZER ARGUMENT
For phi_c(S)=P r(S)-c(S), minimizers form a union/intersection lattice. A
maximal minimizer exists because E is finite. For c<=d, the uncrossing gap

phi_c(A)+phi_d(B)-phi_c(A intersect B)-phi_d(A union B)

equals P times the nonnegative submodularity gap plus
sum_{j in A\B}(d_j-c_j). Optimality gives the reverse inequality, so equality
holds and A union B minimizes phi_d. Maximality forces A subset B. Therefore
g_j=1{j notin S^+(c)} is an antitone active supporting gradient, establishing
full private DR by coordinate integration.

For P<=P', the second uncrossing gap is
P[r(A)+r(B)-r(A union B)-r(A intersect B)]
 +(P'-P)[r(B)-r(A intersect B)]>=0.
Monotonicity of r is essential and explicitly assumed. Optimality and
maximality of A imply B subset A. Thus the private gradient increases with
P, and the direct finite proof replaces regularization in this subclass.
Rank degeneracy and zero capacities do not invalidate maximal-minimizer
existence or these weak inequalities.

At(tP,s c), the active set depends on z=t/s. The CDF is z/2 below1 and
1-1/(2z) above1. Integrating the finite line-envelope intervals therefore
gives the claimed exact price formula. Persistent ties have equal directional
derivative; taking their union gives the maximal minimizer. As z increases,
these sets decrease by inclusion, so at most |E|+1 different gradients have
positive interval weight. Rational breakpoints and weights are exact.
Direct enumeration remains exponential; no polynomial oracle claim follows.

5. HOLDING-BOX CHECKER: EXACT MEANING
For an aggregate-price field, fix a coordinate transition C'=C+delta e_j.
Every old coalition aggregate a can range over0<=a<=C under unrestricted
fixed mixed ownership. Its payment change is

delta q_j(C') + a.[q(C')-q(C)].

The minimum over that box is EXACTLY

delta q_j(C')-sum_k C_k max(q_k(C)-q_k(C'),0).

Consequently the checker's inequality is necessary and sufficient for TCWM
over EVERY holding split on THAT tested transition. A two-owner realization
(a,C-a), with the restoration assigned to the first owner, verifies necessity
in the class of arbitrary fixed mixed-owner models. Coordinate transitions
compose for general restorations when the criterion holds everywhere.

The finite checker tests only selected C,delta,j and technologies. Its exact
box minimization does not make those tests exhaustive over all reports. The
theorem's analytic proof supplies those missing quantifiers. Its coverage
tests likewise sample bundles; average support proves universal coverage.

6. SCOPE AND PROMOTION
The sharp3/2 lower is correctly delegated to the already audited one-edge
min(P,c1) lower. One common RESOURCE ROW is not necessarily one actual owner:
the worst case permits fragmented ownership and arbitrary mixed holdings.
This result should not be described as requiring subsidy when one actual
veto owner holds the entire common resource.

The earlier two-common aligned-private theorem fixes both common reports;
it does not imply the present mixed-owner proof with two common rows. The
single-common Euler identity only yields a sum of cross effects in that
case, exactly as the final scope section states. No new empirical or
application claims are justified by this audit.
