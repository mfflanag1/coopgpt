PUBLIC POLYMATROID RULE: PRINCIPAL-CHAIN ORACLE SOURCE GATE
17 October 2026. Free original sources read; implementation application only.

RESULT: the chain-recovery step is an established decomposition algorithm.
An original conference paper gives the exact recursion, maximal-minimizer tie
rule, and at most 2m-1 minimizations. It can replace subset enumeration in the
accepted one-common polymatroid rule, subject to exact oracle assumptions.
The algorithm itself, its nested-chain structure, and polynomial solvability
of submodular minimization must not be claimed as our contributions.

1. Directly read original source for the recursion

Kiyohito Nagano and Yoshinobu Kawahara, Structured Convex Optimization under
Submodular Constraints, Proceedings of UAI 2013, Section 5.1--5.2, PDF pp6--7.
Free original proceedings PDF:
https://auai.org/uai2013/prints/papers/151.pdf

The setting is normalized, nondecreasing submodular f and strictly positive
modular weights b. The parameterized objectives are f-alpha b. Section5.1
uses their unique maximal minimizers. Section5.2 presents Algorithm DA(T,T'):
compute alpha=(f(T')-f(T))/b(T'\T), then the unique maximal minimizer M;
if M=T', the two endpoint sets are consecutive; otherwise recursively treat
(T,M) and (M,T'). The paper states at most 2m-1 submodular minimizations.
It credits this framework to Fujishige's 2005 book and Nagano--Aihara2012,
not as a new contribution of the 2013 application. Section3.1 also explains
maximal-cut recovery by complementing nodes that can reach the sink in the
residual network; a source-reachable minimum cut is generally the other
extremal choice. These are exact ties, not a numerical epsilon convention.

2. Direct original support for an exact maximal-minimizer oracle

Satoru Iwata and James B. Orlin, A Simple Combinatorial Algorithm for
Submodular Function Minimization, June2008 preprint, Sections3 and6.
https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/preprint/file/RIMS1634.pdf

Algorithm SFM and Theorem3.1 (PDF pp5--6) explicitly return the unique maximal
minimizer for integer-valued input. Section6's Algorithm SPM (PDF pp10--11)
handles real-valued submodular functions on ring families and again returns
the unique maximal minimizer. The stated arithmetic/value-oracle running
time is O((m^5 EO+m^6) log m), with EO the function-evaluation cost. This is
a sufficient historical polynomial oracle bound, not a claim about the
current fastest implementation. Their algorithm supplies exactly the tie
interface needed here. Approximate minimization or an arbitrary minimizer
cannot silently replace it. A full-power-set instance is a special ring
family, and subtracting a modular function preserves submodularity.

Satoru Fujishige, Theory of Principal Partitions Revisited, author manuscript,

https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~fujishig/Preprint010208.pdf

Theorem14 gives the box-truncation min-max formula; Remark8 describes the

O(m SFM) weighted lexicographically optimal-base algorithm to Fujishige1980.
This confirms established provenance. The original 1980 article and
Groenevelt1991 full texts were not obtained in this gate; the inspected
2013 original supplies the explicit recursion instead. No first-invention
priority is inferred from this limited retrieval.

4. Translation to our rule, and boundary precautions (our analysis)

At a fixed positive report P, put f(S)=P r(S), b_j=c_j. For z>0,

  argmin_S [z P r(S)+c(E\S)]
       = argmin_S [P r(S)-(1/z)c(S)].

Thus alpha=1/z reverses the established increasing principal chain. If
successive chain members are A subsetneq B, their positive breakpoint is

  alpha=[P(r(B)-r(A))]/c(B\A),
  z=c(B\A)/[P(r(B)-r(A))].

At a tie choose the maximal minimizer, matching the accepted gradient
(r(S),1_{E\S}). Isolated ratio breakpoints have zero integration weight;
the choice still matters for the algorithm and must remain consistent.

Handle zero cases before applying a positive-weight theorem:
- P=0 or all c=0 gives zero production/payments under the accepted rule.
- Restrict to J={j:c_j>0}. For monotone r, deleting zero-capacity elements
  from S cannot increase the objective, so the minimum and charged
  active-face gradients can be computed on r restricted to J.
- A loop j with r({j})=0 never changes r(S) when adjoined: submodularity
  bounds its marginal by zero and monotonicity bounds it below by zero.
  Every maximal minimizer contains all loops, whose private prices are zero.
  Remove them, reconstruct them in every selected S, and start the remaining
  chain at empty. Equivalently start with the maximal zero-rank set. Do not
  assume the empty set is the maximal alpha=0 minimizer when loops remain.
- If no nonloop active element remains, F=0 and the rule is identically zero.

The active-face restriction is analysis/evaluation at the current report,
not a change of actual owners or eligibility. It evaluates the same fixed
all-profile field already proved incentive compatible.

5. What complexity conclusion is justified

Let T_maxSFM(m) be the cost of exact maximal minimization for f-alpha c and
its restrictions/contractions, with exact rank evaluation available. The
standard recursion uses O(m) such calls, plus polynomial bookkeeping and
rank evaluations. Each positive interval contributes its dual gradient with
weight H(z_next)-H(z_prev), where the already-proved ratio CDF is z/2 below1
and 1-1/(2z) above1. Hence rational exact inputs/oracle outputs yield rational
breakpoints, weights, and prices without numerical quadrature.

An exact rational value oracle for r together with a cited exact SFM routine
supports a polynomial oracle/arithmetic implementation. This does not mean
one rank evaluation is one minimization, and it does not certify bit-cost
complexity for an unspecified oracle, floating rank estimates, or arbitrary
black-box real values. State the rational output encoding and arithmetic
model before a bit-complexity theorem. Graph-specific max-flow shortcuts
require an explicit graph representation; arbitrary public polymatroids
cannot simply be assumed to have one. No practical runtime was measured.
