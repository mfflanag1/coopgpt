FINITE ARITY-THREE LADDER: SMALL CERTIFICATES AND BOUNDED-COALITION GROWTH
17 October 2026. Candidate refinement awaiting independent review.
The unrestricted active-owner recurrence was found by composition; root
strengthened the final coverage constraint. Energy supplies the finite pure-
power realization, exact small certificates and bounded-coalition optimization.
Historical novelty remains open.

THEOREM

Fix integers M>=2, R>=2 and s>=2. Use the common-cap production technology

 F(P,a,b)=min(P, sum_{j=1}^M min(a_j,b_j)).                  (1)

There are R actual owners eligible only for P and two actual pure-resource
owners per branch a_j,b_j. Thus n=2M+R and d=2M+1, and every activity has
resource arity3. All identities and eligibility sets are fixed at all profiles.
For each m=2,...,M define

 k_m=min(s-1,2m-2),
 D_(M,s)=(M-1) product_{m=2}^M k_m/(k_m+1).                (2)

Any nonnegative rule covering ALL actual coalitions and satisfying TCWM for
every incentive coalition of size at most s has worst-case funding factor

           kappa >= (R^2+D_(M,s)*R)/(R^2+D_(M,s)).         (3)

This is a rational finite inequality for arbitrary nonlinear mechanisms on
all real report profiles, with no aggregate-price or null-payment assumption.
Full outside-production protection for coalitions of size at most s implies
these TCWM inequalities, so(3) also applies to that stronger contract.

PROOF

Symmetrize over power-owner permutations and the fixed technology's branch
permutations and within-branch owner/resource swaps. These finite domain
automorphisms preserve all assumptions, including the incentive size limit.
They are mathematical averaging of a purported rule, not strategic identities.

Every power owner reports 1/R at every profile, giving total P=1. At the
bootstrap profile only branch1 is active, with a_1=b_1=1/R, while each other
branch has a_j=0,b_j=1. Grand production is1/R. If Y_P is total power-owner
payment, budget gives Y_P<=kappa/R. Symmetry gives each power owner Y_P/R.
The coalition of one power owner and the two active branch owners has worth
1/R. Therefore the active pair's total payment is at least

                        1/R-kappa/R^2.                   (4)

Restore this pair to a_1=b_1=1. Applying TCWM to the SAME pair (size2<=s)
shows that its total T_1 at the new profile C_1 obeys(4). This remains a valid
inequality when its right side is negative; no positivity of that lower bound
is assumed in the subsequent elimination.

For m=1,...,M, let C_m have active branches j<=m with(a_j,b_j)=(1,1), and
inactive branches j>m with(0,1). All have grand production1. Symmetry implies
that all 2m active branch owners receive one common payment q_m; write
T_m=2m*q_m. For the transition C_(m-1) to C_m, only a_m increases from0 to1.
Choose the actual coalition consisting of a_m and ANY k_m old active owners.
Its size k_m+1 is at most s. Its low-profile payment is k_m*q_(m-1)+w,
where w is a_m's old nonnegative payment; its new payment is(k_m+1)q_m.
TCWM therefore gives

                     q_m >= [k_m/(k_m+1)]q_(m-1).         (5)

No inactive b-owner is in this incentive coalition. In particular, positive
reserve payments to inactive resources cannot invalidate(5); the old zero-
report a_m payment w only strengthens it. Iteration yields

 T_M >= M product_{m=2}^M[k_m/(k_m+1)]*(1/R-kappa/R^2).    (6)

At C_M take the coalition of ALL R power owners and ONE active branch pair.
It has worth1. Thus Y_P+T_M/M>=1, and the grand budget gives

 kappa >=Y_P+T_M >=1+(1-1/M)T_M.                         (7)

Combining(6)--(7) proves kappa>=1+D/R-D*kappa/R^2, equivalent to(3).
The final coalition can exceed s: it invokes COVERAGE only, which is required
for every coalition. All actual incentive coalitions have size at most s.

UNRESTRICTED-COALITION SPECIALIZATION AND A 172-OWNER CERTIFICATE

If s>=2M-1, then k_m=2m-2 and

 A_M=product_{m=2}^M 2m/(2m-1)=4^M/[2*binom(2M,M)],
 D_(M,s)=(1-1/M)A_M.

Take R=4,M=84,s=167. Exact rational calculation gives D_(84,167)>8 and

 kappa >=181672767161629977921046690736847227014054680515183 /

       >2.0031.                                          (8)

This uses 172 fixed actual owners, 169 resource rows and84 activities. It
substantially supersedes the enormous earlier finite factor-two certificate.
Within this particular R=4 full-ladder certificate, M=84 is the first M with
D>8; no minimal-owner claim for unrestricted mechanisms is made.

OPTIMIZING THE LADDER FOR A FIXED COLLUSION LIMIT

For fixed s, equation(2) shows

 D_(M+1,s)/D_(M,s)=[M/(M-1)]*[min(s-1,2M)/(min(s-1,2M)+1)].

Before saturation this is >1. Once min(s-1,2M)=s-1, it is >1 for M<s,
equals1 for M=s, and is <1 for M>s. Hence the exact maximum of this ladder
occurs at M=s or M=s+1. The two values are equal.

For M=s, put m0=floor((s+1)/2). The initial full-coalition product followed
by the constant-ratio part yields

 D_(s,s)=[(s-1)/m0] A_m0 [(s-1)/s]^(s-m0).

The central-binomial asymptotic A_m~sqrt(pi*m)/2 gives

                    D_(s,s)~sqrt(pi/(2e))*sqrt(s).        (9)

For fixed D>0, the right side of(3), as a function of real R>0, is maximized
at R=1+sqrt(1+D), where its value is(1+sqrt(1+D))/2. Choosing the closest
integer R>=2 preserves this asymptotic. Consequently, for the unrestricted-
mechanism quantities defined in BOUNDED_COLLUSION_GROWTH.txt,

 liminf_{s->infinity} K_3(s)/s^(1/4)
 >= (1/2)*(pi/(2e))^(1/4), approximately0.436.              (10)

The same lower applies to K_3^OUT(s). Only n=2s+O(s^(1/4)) actual owners are
needed, with d=2s+1 resources. Every integer s is treated directly, so no
subsequence interpolation is hidden in(10). Together with the binomial upper,
the currently proved/candidate interval becomes order s^(1/4) versus3s^2.
The power1/4 is a lower rate; it is not asserted optimal.

A SMALLER COLLUSION LIMIT FOR A FACTOR-TWO CERTIFICATE

Taking s=M=113 and R=4 gives D_(113,113)>8 and the exact rational bound(3)

OWNER-COUNT CONSEQUENCE

In the unrestricted-coalition specialization choose R nearest the real
optimizer for D_(M,2M-1). Then n=2M+O(M^(1/4)), and

 liminf_{n->infinity} K_TCWM,3(n)/n^(1/4) >= pi^(1/4)/2^(7/4).

For all n, pad by owners with empty fixed eligibility, or use a zero-report
face and discard their nonnegative payments. Consecutive model sizes have
ratio tending1. This lower concerns activity arity3; the already known general
owner lower of order sqrt(n), which allows growing arity, remains stronger in
its broader class. This result separates the bounded-arity subproblem.
