MIXED OWNERSHIP WITH AN ENERGY-SERVICE FLOOR: SHARP4/3
17 October2026. Explicit owner-merging corollary of the sharp price rule.
Candidate proof; independent audit outstanding. Historical novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL AND CONTRACT
Permit a fixed actual owner j to hold any finite bundle of energy cost tiers
and hardware service-value tiers in the shared complete-access source-price
model. Reports are arbitrary binding coordinatewise reductions of that actual
bundle, with utility equal to net-surplus payment (or the equivalent settlement
in ECONOMIC_SETTLEMENT.txt). Define the public contractual energy-service floor
 U_j(b)=F(b)-F(b with ONLY j's energy tiers set to zero).
All its hardware tiers remain in that counterfactual. Require y_j>=U_j, for
every owner, including the trivial zero condition for hardware-only owners.

THEOREM
There is an all-profile own-bundle monotone, nonnegative coalition-covering
rule with total at most4F/3 and every stated floor. This is the sharp universal
factor, since pure disjoint ownership is a subclass and the accepted two-report
lower4/3 embeds. The same owner-merging proof preserves the candidate partial-
floor bound in PARTIAL_FLOOR_FRONTIER.txt for y_j>=theta U_j, theta in[4/5,1].
This corollary does NOT claim the full-owner-deletion floor4/3 under mixed roles;
that different contract has sharp factor2 by MIXED_FLOOR_BOUNDARY.txt.

UNIT CONSTRUCTION
At a unit threshold let e_j,h_j be the energy and hardware totals belonging to
ACTUAL owner j. Write E=sum e_j,H=sum h_j. Use the sharp full-floor rule with
one labelled energy slot and one labelled hardware slot for each fixed actual
owner; add its two slot payments to produce its actual payment y_j.
These slots are only formula coordinates for the owner's two resource bundles.
No strategic identities are split, no coalition gains separate access to a slot,
and all deviations change the actual owner's whole reported bundle.

For E<=H: d_j=min(h_j,E),rho=sum_j d_j/E, r=exp(1-rho)/3.
Energy coordinate payment is e_j; hardware coordinate payment is r d_j.
For H<=E: d_j=min(e_j,H),rho=sum_j d_j/H, q=q_E(rho) from the sharp theorem.
Hardware coordinate payment is(1-q)h_j; energy coordinate payment is
 max{q d_j,(H-E+e_j)_+}.
At zero grand value set all paymentszero. Add the two coordinates per owner.

FIXED-ACTUAL-OWNER INCENTIVES: POSITIVE CROSS EFFECTS
Pure-coordinate own-report effects were proved in SHARP_SOURCE_PRICE_FLOORS.txt.
The only additional issue is whether changing one of an owner's roles harms
its OTHER role's payment. It does not:

In E<=H, increasing own energy increases E. For every fixed hardware quantity,
min(h/E,1) is nonincreasing in E, hence rho is nonincreasing. Thus r is
nondecreasing, and min(h_j,E) also nondecreasing: the owner's hardware payment
cannot fall. Increasing own hardware leaves its energy payment e_j unchanged.

In H<=E, increasing own energy with fixed H weakly increases rho, so q weakly
decreases. Its hardware payment(1-q)h_j therefore weakly increases. Increasing
own hardware raises H; rho=sum min(e_j/H,1) is nonincreasing, so q is
nondecreasing, and min(e_j,H) is nondecreasing. Both q d_j and its energy
marginal(H-E+e_j)_+ are nondecreasing. Thus its energy payment cannot fall.

Together with the own-coordinate effects, these cross effects show that each
actual payment is coordinatewise nondecreasing inside each total-side regime.
At E=H both formulas give y_j=e_j+h_j/3; cap thresholds and q's breakpoint
are continuous. Hence crossing a tie cannot produce a downward jump. At T0
all paymentszero and their nonnegative formulas approachzero. This proves all
nonnegative real profiles, not merely a grid. Any whole-bundle underreport is
a sequence of single-coordinate reductions, so the entire fixed owner's
payment cannot rise. No independence between its energy/hardware reports is
assumed by the conclusion.

COVERAGE, FLOORS AND BUDGET
For any coalition of actual owners S, select both resource coordinates for
every j in S. The pure-rule coverage inequality for that coordinate set gives
sum_{j in S}y_j>=min(E_S,H_S), its actual coalition worth. The unit energy
coordinate receives at least min(E,H)-min(E-e_j,H)=U_j. Adding the nonnegative
hardware coordinate preserves that floor. Adding coordinates does not change
total payment, so total<=4 min(E,H)/3.

PUBLIC PRICE TIERS
At each fixed public threshold t, first aggregate every actual owner's retained
energy tiers and retained hardware tiers separately, then use this unit rule.
An arbitrary own-tier reduction decreases its aggregated coordinate at each
threshold. Integrate fixed nonnegative price-interval weights. Coalitionwise
F(S)=integral min(E_t(S),H_t(S))dt remains valid with mixed ownership because
the production identity concerns retained resources, not strategic identities.
The resource-deletion floor telescopes to exactly U_j. Thus coverage, whole-
bundle incentives and4/3 all persist for arbitrary owner/tier counts.

PARTIAL FLOORS
The same cross-effect signs hold for the partial rule, with one extra case:
when E<=H, energy payment is max{theta,1-r(rho)}e_j. Increasing own hardware
increases rho, decreases r, and hence weakly INCREASES this coefficient. The
other three cross effects are unchanged. Own-coordinate monotonicity and
continuity are supplied by the partial-rule proof. Therefore its complete
funding theorem extends to mixed ownership with theta U_j floors as stated.

SIGNIFICANCE AND STOPPING GATE
This is a routine extension once positive cross effects are recognized, not a
new unrestricted mechanism breakthrough. Its useful contribution to the model
is a precise contract distinction: full actual-owner marginal protection costs
factor2 under vertical integration; protecting only the energy-service marginal
retains4/3. General access restrictions require a new UNIT-GRAPH mechanism with floors.
The initially suspected failure of the public-price identity was incorrect:
ACCESS_PRICE_COAREA.txt proves that identity even with arbitrary fixed access.

OUTSIDE-OPTION SCOPE
Payment monotonicity concerns the committed window and the linear settlement
baseline. It does not allow an owner to add nonlinear independent service value
from its withheld hardware+energy. A model permitting that alternative must add
it to deviation utility and recheck incentives; the4/3 theorem is not a result
about absence-proofness or arbitrary retained-resource self-production.
