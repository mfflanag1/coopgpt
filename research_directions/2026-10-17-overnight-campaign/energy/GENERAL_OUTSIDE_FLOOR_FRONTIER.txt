UNIVERSAL FULL-OUT MARGINAL-FLOOR FRONTIER
17 October 2026. Internally audited corollary: root and Fable review 015659
found no substantive defect; the main theorem passed review 015023.
External specialist review and historical priority remain open.

MODEL AND CLAIM.
Use the model of GENERAL_LOGARITHMIC_FACE_OUTSIDE.txt: a finite monotone,
concave, degree-one homogeneous technology F on d resource coordinates;
arbitrary fixed actual mixed-resource owners; binding coordinatewise reports;
nonnegative payments; and full coalition OUT including production with the
coalition's aggregate withheld bundle. At a profile with aggregate C, define
each actual owner's full deletion marginal

             M_i=F(C)-F(C-b_i).

Impose the public contract y_i>=theta M_i for a fixed 0<=theta<=1 at every
report profile. The sharp universal funding factor is

             K_d(theta)=1+(d-1)max(c,theta),

where c=0.5430633898250876... solves c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1. For d=1 this is 1.
The analogous profile-dependent upper uses the number of active coordinates.
The lower is uniform over all technologies and finite owner populations; it
does not say every individual technology requires this factor.

1. THE LOGARITHMIC RULE DOMINATES c TIMES EACH FULL OWNER MARGINAL

For every face vector v_j(u)=C_j e_j+u C_-j, monotonicity and homogeneity give
F(v_j(u))>=u F(C). Consequently H_j(C)>=c F(C). Writing alpha_ij=b_ij/C_j
on active coordinates, the logarithmic rule pays

     y_i >= c F(C) sum_j alpha_ij.

Let rho_i=max_j alpha_ij. Since C-b_i>=(1-rho_i) C,

     M_i<=rho_i F(C)<=F(C) sum_j alpha_ij.

Thus y_i>=c M_i. This argument is finite and valid on every face, including
rho_i=1 and technologies with downward boundary jumps.

2. THE PROPORTIONAL d-FACTOR RULE IS FULL OUT AND DOMINATES M_i

Set p_j^P(C)=F(C)/C_j on positive coordinates and pay actual owners
y_i^P=b_i.p^P. Total funding is k F(C), where k is the active coordinate
count. The preceding share argument immediately gives y_i^P>=M_i.

Finite TCWM holds because each coalition coordinate contribution is
[a_j/(a_j+o_j)] F(a+o), a product of nonnegative coordinatewise nondecreasing
functions. For smooth F on an active face, let alpha_j=a_j/C_j, A=sum alpha_j,
and d>=0. The derivative of total coalition payment under restoration is

  D Y_S^P[d]
    = A grad F(C).d + F(C) sum_j (1-alpha_j)d_j/C_j.

If A>=1, the first term is at least F(d), and the second is nonnegative.
If A<=1, then alpha_j<=A for every j. Also monotonicity and homogeneity give

  F(d)<=F(C) max_j(d_j/C_j)<=F(C) sum_j d_j/C_j.

Therefore the two derivative terms together are at least
A F(d)+(1-A)F(d)=F(d). Integrating coalition restorations and using finite
TCWM at face entries proves full OUT. The diagonal logarithmic smoothing
from the companion theorem removes differentiability with all-face relative
convergence. Thus no boundary continuity assumption is needed.

3. INTERPOLATION AND SHARPNESS

For theta<=c use the logarithmic rule. For theta>=c mix the logarithmic and
proportional rules with weight lambda=(theta-c)/(1-c) on the latter. Payment
nonnegativity, full OUT, coalition coverage, and floors are linear
inequalities, so the mixture preserves them and satisfies y_i>=theta M_i.
Its funding factor is

  (1-lambda)[1+c(k-1)]+lambda k=1+theta(k-1).

The matching lower already holds for F=min_j C_j. Its floor-free unrestricted
OUT lower is 1+c(d-1). For the additional floor lower, fix m actual pure
owners in each of d-1 long roles and one actual pure owner in the final role.
At the final profile all have capacity one. For each long role j, consider
the report profile with only one designated protected owner of that role
active, and all owners of the other roles at their final reports. That
protected owner's deletion marginal is one, hence its payment is at least
theta. By nonnegativity, its entire role coalition receives at least theta.
Restore the other owners of this role, holding every outsider fixed. TCWM,
which follows from full OUT, gives final role total X_j>=theta.

There is one protected owner in EACH of the d-1 roles. The preceding
comparisons are separate valid profiles in the SAME fixed owner population.
Let z be the short-role owner's final payment. Average coalition coverage
over coalitions formed by that owner and one uniformly selected owner from
each long role. Every such coalition can produce one, so

                   z+(1/m)sum_j X_j>=1.

Total funding at this value-one profile is therefore at least
1+(1-1/m)sum_j X_j>=1+(1-1/m)(d-1)theta. Letting m grow gives
1+(d-1)theta. Combining the two lower bounds proves the frontier.

4. PUBLIC AI-INFRASTRUCTURE AND ENERGY INTERPRETATION

A finite public divisible-activity model fits this theorem when

             F(C)=max { v.x : A x<=C, x>=0 },

where A is a nonnegative public resource-use matrix and v contains public
net operating values. Assume F finite for every finite C; discard activities
with negative net value. Resource rows may represent GPU service, memory,
bandwidth, and energy allowances; actual owners can own mixed bundles and
different owners can supply the same row. The theorem allows arbitrary
numbers of owners and public activities, and arbitrary heterogeneous
coefficients. It does not establish that a chosen divisible model accurately
captures real request latency, discrete hardware configuration, or deployment
performance. Such questions require separate measurements and validation.

For public linear opportunity costs, the earlier reimbursement settlement
reimburses used resources at the public cost while unused capacity retains
its baseline value. The owner's net utility is its baseline plus the surplus
payment analyzed here. If retained resources can instead be pooled to carry
out the same public activities, full OUT protects the coalition's additional
net value F(withheld). This is the relevant distinction from payment-only
underreport monotonicity. Private opportunity costs, strategic job values,
overreports, false ownership, and nonreplicable outside resources are outside
the present theorem.

The floor refers to the full actual owner's deletion marginal. Replacing it
by an energy-only-removal marginal changes the contract and should be stated
separately. The funding factor is total surplus payment divided by F(C);
the required external subsidy is at most (K_d(theta)-1)F(C), in addition to
the agreed public operating-cost reimbursement. These are worst-case contract
bounds, not measured gains from pooling an actual cluster.
