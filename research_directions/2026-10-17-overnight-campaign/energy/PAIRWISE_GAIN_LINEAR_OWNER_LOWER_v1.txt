PAIRWISE OUTSIDE PRODUCTION: SHARP LINEAR ORDER IN GAIN-ONLY OWNER FUNDING
17 October2026. Frozen candidate quantitative theorem; independent audit pending.

THEOREM
Let K_n be the infimum gain coefficient over all mechanisms for n fixed owners,
each eligible for both coordinates, on the full nonnegative real report domain,
with production F(A,B)=min(A,B). Require nonnegative payments, coverage of EVERY
coalition, and full outside-production protection for coalitions of size1 or2
(including zero withdrawals by a member). For n>103,

  K_n >= c_0 (n-103),
  c_0 = 1/[1800((3/2)^101-1)] >0.                           (1)

For every n>=2, K_n<=n/2. Thus K_n=Theta(n) as n grows. The displayed lower
constant is intentionally crude; this is an asymptotic structural separation,
not a practically calibrated subsidy lower bound for a small consortium.
The exact n=3 value is5/4 and n=4 lower9/7 from separate audited results.

More generally the upper n/2 holds for ANY nonnegative superadditive production
function F with F(0)=0. For the lower, concavity and degree-one homogeneity are
available because the witness is min2, but the proof never assumes a particular
architecture or regularity for the original mechanism.

NOTATION
f_i=F(b_i), S=sum_i f_i, T=F(sum_i b_i), G=T-S.
The gain-only contract is sum_i y_i<=S+K G. External budget excess over total
production is at most(K-1)G. Owner identities remain fixed across all profiles.

1. UNIVERSAL UPPER: HALF THE COOPERATION GAIN TO EACH OWNER
Define

  y_i=f_i+G/2.                                             (2)

For a singleton, its payment is one half f_i plus one half(T-S_outside).
Upon restoring its own bundle d_i, both terms increase by at least F(d_i),
by superadditivity. Thus singleton full outside-production protection holds.
For a pair Q, its total payment is exactly T-S_outsideQ; when that pair restores
d_i,d_j this increases by at least F(d_i+d_j). Hence pair full protection holds.

Every singleton is covered. For any coalition Q of size at least2,

  y(Q)>=sum_Q f_i+G>=F(b(Q)),

since superadditivity gives T>=F(b(Q))+sum_outsideQ f_i. Total funding is exactly
S+(n/2)G. At G=0 every owner receives its standalone value. This construction
is deliberately simple; it is not claimed sharp for each fixed n.

2. REGULARIZE ONE FIXED-n MECHANISM WITHOUT CHANGING ITS BUDGET
Suppose a finite K works at this n. Use the independently audited translation
reduction in PAIRWISE_GAIN_TRANSLATION_REDUCTION.txt, then finite owner and
resource symmetrization. We may assume

  y_i=b_i+h_i(z), z_i=a_i-b_i;

pair h-sums are invariant under changing their own signed split while fixing
their sum, and each h_i is1-Lipschitz in each coordinate. No homogeneity or
measurability premise is required. The translation argument uses the full
orthant and mixed eligibility, so this lower does not apply automatically to
owners permanently restricted to one resource type or to fixed report cubes.

For a pure signed profile with r active owners, each null owner has the same
payment. All active standalone values are zero, so

  0<=y_null<=K G/(n-r).                                    (3)

3. A TWO-OWNER POTENTIAL AT TOTAL IMBALANCE2
Hold n fixed throughout. On a profile with only two active signed coordinates,
define

  q(x)=h_1(x,2-x,0,...,0).

Pair neutrality compares this pair with the profile(2,0,0,...,0), whose gain
is zero. Consequently, even if null owners have positive payments at other
profiles,

  q(x)+q(2-x)=0, q(x)=0 for0<=x<=2.                        (4)

The function q is2-Lipschitz, by the coordinatewise bound on h.
At the balanced profile(1,-1,0,...), pair neutrality makes active-pair payments
sum to1. Owner/resource symmetry makes them equal. Restoring the positive
owner's coordinate from1 to3, leaving the negative owner at-1, and using own
monotonicity gives

  q(3)>=1/2.                                               (5)

4. AN APPROXIMATE MERGER REPRESENTATION
Let M=103 and assume n>M. Consider all pure signed profiles with total2, at most
M active coordinates, and total negative mass at most2. Then G<=2, and every
null payment occurring in this class is at most

  epsilon=2K/(n-M).                                        (6)

Merging two signed coordinates, by replacing(z_i,z_j) with(z_i+z_j,0), stays in
this class: total imbalance is preserved and cancellation cannot increase the
negative mass. This is a report comparison among the SAME actual owners.

For any such profile with r active coordinates, form the potential marginal

  B_i^q(z)=2^{-(r-1)} sum_{R subset [r]\{i}}
               [q(z(R)+z_i)-q(z(R))].                     (7)

Zero coordinates may be omitted. From(4), the representation h_i=B_i^q is exact
for r=1,2. The standard Banzhaf merger identity gives

  B_i^q+B_j^q=B_merged^q.

Actual pair neutrality gives h_i+h_j=h_merged+h_new-null. The new-null payment
lies in[0,epsilon] by(6). If all individual errors for at most r-1 active
coordinates are bounded by E_(r-1), every pair-sum error at r is therefore at
most E_(r-1)+epsilon in absolute value. For any three distinct active i,j,k,

  2(h_i-B_i^q)=(pairerror_ij)+(pairerror_ik)-(pairerror_jk).

Thus the following valid, deliberately loose recursion holds:

  E_2=0, E_r=(3/2)(E_(r-1)+epsilon)
      =3 epsilon[(3/2)^(r-2)-1].                          (8)

If a merge removes two active coordinates, use the smaller previous error,
which is at most E_(r-1). No assumption about outsiders' payments staying
unchanged under a merge is made or needed.

5. ONE FIXED FINITE WITNESS VIOLATES COVERAGE UNLESS K IS LINEAR
Use a three-owner coalition with signed reports(2,-1,-1), and100 outside owners,
each with positive signed report1/50. There are M=103 active owners, total
imbalance2, and negative mass2. The coalition's worth is2.

In the potential representation(7), the included outside sum is

  W=(1/50) Binomial(100,1/2).

Conditional on W=t, the coalition's total h-payment is

  L(t)=[-q(t+2)+2q(t+1)-2q(t-1)+q(t-2)]/4.                 (9)

Equations(4),(5) give L(1)=-q(3)/2<=-1/4. The2-Lipschitz property of q makes L
3-Lipschitz. The exact elementary binomial identity gives

  E|W-1|=binom(100,50)/2^100 <2/25.

Hence

  E L(W)<-1/4+3(2/25)=-1/100.                             (10)

The actual coalition h-total is nonnegative by coverage, because its signed
sum is zero. Its difference from the potential h-total is at most3E_103. Thus

  1/100 < 3E_103
        =9 epsilon[(3/2)^101-1]
        =18K[(3/2)^101-1]/(n-103).

This proves(1), even with a weak non-strict inequality. For n>=206 it implies
K_n>=(c_0/2)n, and the upper in section1 proves sharp linear asymptotic order.

6. WHY THIS DOES NOT CONTRADICT PREVIOUS CONSTANT THEOREMS
- Individual-only full outside production has constant gain-only funding for
  min2. This lower uses pairwise full outside production essentially.
- The min2 constant1+c full-coalition OUT theorem bounds funding by TOTAL T,
  allowing subsidy when cooperation gain G is zero. The present gain-only
  normalization forbids that reserve and changes the problem.
- Fixed pure-type eligibility makes every owner's standalone value zero;
  the translation reduction needs eligibility for both resource types. Thus
  this lower is not a claim about a permanently pure-type owner institution.
- The lower constant is extremely small. The theorem identifies the order as
  owner count grows, not a large finite subsidy for ordinary market sizes.
- The upper and lower retain coverage for ALL coalitions, even though only
  singleton and pair deviations receive outside-production protection.

merging, and an all-real fixed-owner coverage contradiction giving linear order.
