EXACT PUBLIC-PRICE COAREA FOR ARBITRARY BIPARTITE ACCESS
17 October2026. Candidate conventional proof, independent audit outstanding.
A structural transfer lemma; historical priority remains unconfirmed. The
identity is closely related to classical LP duality and threshold vertex covers.
Do not advertise either of those ingredients as new.

MODEL
Fix any finite bipartite access graph G=(A union B,E). An energy resource a has
PUBLIC source cost c_a; a hardware/service resource b has PUBLIC service value
v_b. For the economic interpretation take c_a,v_b>=0. Reported capacities
x_a,h_b>=0 may be sums of fixed actual-owner bundles, with arbitrary mixed
ownership. Profitable access activity ab has net unit value v_b-c_a and consumes
one unit at each endpoint. Nonpositive-profit service may be omitted. Thus
 F_G(x,h)=max sum_{ab in E}(v_b-c_a)z_ab
 subject to z>=0, sum_b z_ab<=x_a, sum_a z_ab<=h_b.
The fixed-access restriction does NOT require a complete graph.

At each threshold t, retain source vertices with c_a<=t and hardware vertices
with v_b>t, retaining their full reported capacities and the original access
edges between them. Let T_t(x,h) be the UNIT-PROFIT fractional matching value
of this retained graph. The strict/non-strict convention only controls finitely
many endpoints; it is fixed independently of reports.

THEOREM: EXACT COALITIONWISE IDENTITY
 F_G(x,h)=integral over real t of T_t(x,h) dt.                (1)
For nonnegative costs/values, T_t=0 for t<0 and for t>=max_b v_b, so the usual
integral from0 to infinity is identical. There are finitely many fixed public
cost/value breakpoints. The identity applies to every coalition by substituting
its own aggregate capacities. It holds for all nonnegative REAL capacities.

PROOF, PRIMAL INEQUALITY
Choose any profitable feasible allocation z. At a threshold t, retain just
its activity quantities on edges satisfying c_a<=t<v_b. This remains feasible
for the retained unit-profit problem, so its total is <=T_t. Integrating each
edge's indicator gives (v_b-c_a)z_ab. Summing yields F_G<=integral T_t.
Nonpositive-profit edges can be assignedzero in an optimum, so they introduce
no negative-interval issue.

PROOF, DUAL INEQUALITY
The nonnegative LP dual is
 min sum_a x_a p_a +sum_b h_b q_b,
 p_a+q_b>=v_b-c_a for every ab in E, p_a,q_b>=0.
It is feasible and attains the finite primal optimum. For any feasible dual put
 lambda_a=c_a+p_a, mu_b=v_b-q_b.
Then lambda_a>=mu_b on every access edge.
At each t define a binary vertex cover of the RETAINED graph by choosing
 energy vertex a iff c_a<=t<lambda_a;
 hardware vertex b iff mu_b<=t<v_b.
Indeed if retained edge ab has unchosen energy a, then t>=lambda_a>=mu_b,
so its retained hardware b is chosen. This reasoning also covers equality
lambda_a=mu_b=t using the stated endpoint convention. Therefore
 T_t<=sum_a x_a 1[c_a<=t<lambda_a]
       +sum_b h_b 1[mu_b<=t<v_b].
Integrate over ALL REAL t. The two interval lengths are exactly p_a and q_b,
even if some mu_b is negative. Thus integral T_t <= sum x p+sum h q.
Using an optimal dual gives integral T_t<=F_G and proves(1).
No selection of a different favorable matching at each threshold is assumed to
be jointly feasible; weak duality supplies the missing reverse inequality.
No truncation or positivity assumption on the shifted dual mu is needed.

TRANSFER COROLLARY
Suppose a mechanism on every retained UNIT-PROFIT bipartite access graph has
nonnegative payments, coalition coverage, fixed-actual-owner whole-bundle
underreport monotonicity, and a uniform funding factor K. Integrate its owner
payments with the fixed nonnegative public interval lengths. Then(1) gives the
same K for the weighted source-price access class. Actual identities stay fixed;
retained capacities are aggregated within those identities at every threshold.
If the component mechanisms additionally satisfy a deletion floor, the same
floor telescopes whenever the same actual-owner/resource deletion is applied
on both sides of(1). This includes full-owner deletion and energy-only removal;
it does NOT assert that a particular unit-graph rule satisfies those floors.

INSTANT CONSEQUENCE USING THE INTERNALLY AUDITED GRAPH RULE
The composition lane's Huber unit-profit graph mechanism gives K=3/2 on arbitrary
finite graphs, even with fixed mixed actual-owner bundles. Applying it at every
public threshold proves a3/2 funding bound for the present arbitrary-access
weighted source-price graphs, with all-profile whole-bundle incentives.
Its stronger coalition TOTAL-payment monotonicity and identity aggregation
properties also integrate. This statement is FLOOR-FREE: the Huber rule does
not itself meet the energy grand-marginal or energy-service floors. The full
floor4/3 theorem remains established for COMPLETE access, and its extension to
arbitrary access remains open here.

The proposed contribution is this precise transfer in combination with the

The parent/composition lane owns weighted-forest and additive-graph corollaries.

RELATION TO EARLIER OBSTRUCTION
This does not contradict SHARED_LOCAL_DECOMPOSITION_OBSTRUCTION.txt. That file
uses an arbitrary route-profit matrix that has no consistent source/service
potentials: among overlapping source routes its profit differences vary by
hardware. Such a matrix need not admit representation(1). Arbitrary access is
allowed here, but arbitrary independent edge profits are not.

ECONOMIC AND DEVIATION LIMITS
Use ECONOMIC_SETTLEMENT.txt for the public-cost ledger. This identity and its
mechanism transfer do not supply private-cost truthfulness, nonlinear retained-
resource self-production incentives, latency feasibility, storage dynamics or
real AI-cluster measurements. All fixed public compatibility restrictions may be
included without assuming that resources can reach inaccessible tasks.
