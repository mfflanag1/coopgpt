Exact comparison that remains necessary

Our contract is fixed-owner resource contribution, not a private-demand
mechanism by definition. At every report profile, payments y_i are nonnegative,
all coalitions S satisfy sum_{i in S} y_i >= F(sum_{i in S} b_i), and funding
has an explicit upper bound. For a fixed protected coalition A, restoring
feasible bundles D_i to its same actual owners (including members with D_i=0)
requires

  sum_{i in A} [y_i(b+D)-y_i(b)] >= 0                       (TCWM),
  sum_{i in A} [y_i(b+D)-y_i(b)] >= F(sum_{i in A} D_i)      (full OUT).

The production function is public; binding underreports remove actual usable
resources. Full OUT includes independent production using the coalition's
pooled withheld bundle. In the total-output contract, funding is sum_i y_i <=
kappa F(sum_i b_i); this must not be confused with the separate gain-only
contract sum_i y_i <= sum_i F(b_i) + K[F(sum_i b_i)-sum_i F(b_i)].

The original must be checked on each of the following before an implication,
separation, or priority claim is made:

- Does coalition improvement mean every member weakly gains and one strictly
  gains, every member strictly gains, or a gain in transferable aggregate cash?
  What role do indifferent and zero-change coalition members have?
- Are reports scalar integer demands, preferences, or physical resource
  endowments? Are deviations restricted downward and binding as resources?
- Which precise own-marginal and cross-marginal cost signs are required in
  each theorem, and are ties or strictness assumptions used?
- Does the conclusion concern the demand game, a selected strong equilibrium,
  or every input profile of the underlying cost-sharing rule?
- Is budget balance an exact identity for collected service costs, and what
  additional argument would produce all-coalition lower covers and an external
  subsidy bound on our public concave production domain?
- Is any retained-resource outside-production term included? If not, can it
  be added by an explicit reduction while preserving fixed owners and all
  profiles? No such reduction is established by this search.

The previous abstract description is retained only as bibliographic context
in RESERVE_CLASSICAL_SOURCE_COMPARISON.txt, not as answers to these questions.
In particular, no statement about the original's exact coalition definition,
budget hypothesis, or tie treatment has been inferred here from its abstract.
