contracts; preserves actual owner identities; separates total from gain-only

remaining corrections below should be applied before treating the handoff as
a specialist packet. No claim of a field-level breakthrough is justified.

C. ECONOMIC GATE: TOTAL-OUTPUT SUBSIDY IS CONDITIONAL, NOT YET JUSTIFIED

The reserve theorem is mathematically applicable once public load factors,
firm capacity, common reliability, delivery point, physical subportfolio
withdrawals and common linear outside valuation are fixed. The handoff's
empirical cautions are appropriate. Existing Mac/request-trace results provide
no measured multiowner reserve, power, reliability or sponsor-benefit evidence.

C1. Naming an external sponsor does not supply its economic objective. With
cash unit r*z_alpha, the allocation base is r*z_alpha F and the mechanism's
additional cost is sigma=sum payments-r*z_alpha F. The theorem bounds sigma
by c*r*z_alpha F; it does not prove sponsor benefit at leastsigma. Choose and
document whether the sponsor purchases existing reserve S, new reserve G,
avoided withholding losses, a reliability service, or an explicit public
externality. Those are different counterfactuals and valuations. Paying for S
can be ordinary procurement; it must not be counted as newly created G.

C2. If the same public per-unit value r is already used to value all pooled
headroom F, its sale proceeds alone cannot finance payments above r*z_alpha F.
A separate procurement margin, avoided loss, external value or subsidy budget
must be identified. No measured magnitude is currently available. This does
not refute the mechanism; it leaves the participation/sponsor objective open.

C3. The two-owner Euler failure demonstrates a defect of THAT efficient rule,
not an unavoidable subsidy for two owners. The accepted two-party fixed-share
gain rule y_i=f_i+theta_i G is efficient and full OUT. Therefore neither the
existing Euler example nor its stronger proportional version in the Denault
comparison justifies extra money on its own. Suggested sentence near the
application: "A vulnerable efficient baseline is not itself evidence that
every truthful settlement requires subsidy for the observed ownership model."

C4. Conversely, a constant gain-only guarantee can truly fail under the full
coalition contract: ZERO_COOPERATION_GAIN_RESERVE_OBSTRUCTION gives the exact
three-owner min2 obstruction. The line-cone embedding in root42 transfers it
to norm reserves. This is a useful conditional economic warning, not evidence
that a particular measured instance needs the worst-case subsidy. Keep the
interactive c(S+G) display explicitly an UPPER-CAP illustration, as it is now.
At build_handoff.py line37, "the required subsidy" is better written "the
chosen mechanism's subsidy" to avoid treating the cap as a per-profile lower.

D. PRIORITY AND REVIEW STATUS

The novelty caveats in all three documents are sound. Euler allocation, fuzzy
core/subgradients, L1 embeddings and risk diversification are established.
The proposed contribution is the exact withholding/funding contract and its
construction. Our mechanism does not preserve every Denault axiom: allowing
subsidy changes full allocation, and ordinary owner-coalition coverage is not
the same as his at-a-profile fractional-portfolio core condition.
