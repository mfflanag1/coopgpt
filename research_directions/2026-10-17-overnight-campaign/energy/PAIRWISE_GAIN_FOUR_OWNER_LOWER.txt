FOUR-OWNER PAIRWISE OUTSIDE PROTECTION NEEDS GAIN FACTOR AT LEAST 9/7
17 October 2026. Exact finite inequality proof, discovered by a bounded LP and
then independently replayed with rational arithmetic. Root audit requested.
This is an unrestricted-mechanism LOWER bound, not a finite-grid upper theorem.

CONTRACT
Four fixed actual owners report arbitrary nonnegative bundles for F(a,b)=min(a,b).
Payments are nonnegative, cover every coalition, and protect full outside
production for deviations by at most TWO actual owners. Suppose everywhere

  total payment <= S+K G,
  S=sum_i min(b_i), G=min(sum_i b_i)-S.                  (1)

Then K>=9/7. Only reports in{0,1,2}^2 are needed. This strictly exceeds the
sharp three-owner coefficient5/4, so that guarantee cannot extend unchanged
to four or more fully eligible owners.

SYMMETRY AND NOTATION
Average over the finite group of owner permutations and interchange of the
two resource labels. The domain, every contract inequality, and S,G are
invariant. Thus it suffices to treat an anonymous resource-symmetric rule.
Permutations identify values in the proof; every actual deviation keeps its
participants and all other reports fixed. Null payments need not vanish.

Use the following profiles, listed as multisets of FOUR owner bundles. Equal
bundles receive equal payments, and swapped profiles have swapped payments.

 A: (1,0),(1,1),(1,2),(1,2).       Payments a,b,c,c.       S=3,G=1.
 B: (0,0),(1,0),(1,2),(1,2).       Payments w,e,f,f.       S=2,G=1.
 C: (0,2),(1,0),(1,1),(1,1).       Payments g,h,l,l.
 D: (0,1),(0,2),(1,0),(1,0).       Payments p,q,r,r.
 E: (0,1),(1,0),(1,1),(1,1).       Pure-owner payments u,u.
 I: (0,0),(0,0),(1,1),(1,1).       Null-owner payments z,z.
 J: (0,1),(0,1),(1,0),(1,0).       All four payments t.
 H: (0,1),(1,0),(2,0),(2,2).       Payments alpha,beta,gamma,delta. S=2,G=1.

All of w,z are nonnegative. Their possible positivity strengthens the lower.

STEP 1: THE A/B/C/E CHAIN
In A, the first two owners withdraw to(0,0),(1,0), producing profile B.
Their jointly withdrawn bundle is(1,1), so pairwise full OUT gives

  a+b>=1+w+e.

The two(1,2) owners in A can instead withdraw to(0,2),(1,1), producing C.
Again their total withheld bundle is(1,1), hence

  2c>=1+g+l.

The budget a+b+2c<=3+K consequently gives

  K>=-1+w+e+g+l.                                       (2)

In C, the two balanced owners can withdraw to(0,1),(1,0), producing D,
with withheld bundle(1,1). Hence2l>=1+p+r.
The resource-swapped profile C has owners(2,0),(0,1),(1,1),(1,1).
Its(2,0) owner can withdraw one unit to(1,0), producing E. Individual
monotonicity, a consequence of pairwise OUT, yields g>=u.
In E the two pure owners can both withdraw to zero, producing I, with jointly
withheld bundle(1,1). Therefore2u>=1+2z.
Substituting these inequalities into(2) gives

  K>=w+e+z+(p+r)/2.                                    (3)

At B, coverage of its(1,0) owner and either(1,2) owner gives e+f>=2.
Its budget w+e+2f<=2+K therefore implies e>=w+2-K. Combining with(3),

  4K>=4+4w+2z+p+r.                                     (4)

STEP 2: THE H/D/J CHAIN
At H, its first two owners can produce1, so alpha+beta>=1.
The pair consisting of the(2,0) and(2,2) owners can leave(2,0) unchanged
and reduce(2,2) to(0,1). Their withheld bundle is(2,1), worth1. The low
profile is the resource swap of D. The participating owners then receive
q and r, so gamma+delta>=1+q+r. The budget of H gives

  K>=q+r.                                              (5)

Coverage at D of its(0,2) owner and both(1,0) owners yields

  q+2r>=2.                                             (6)

At D, the pair of B-only owners can leave(0,1) unchanged and reduce(0,2)
to(0,1), producing J. Their withheld bundle has zero standalone production,
so pairwise monotonicity gives p+q>=2t. At J, one owner of each type produces1,
so2t>=1. Thus

  p+q>=1.                                              (7)

From(5),(6), r>=2-K and q<=K-r. Equation(7) then gives

  p+r>=1-q+r>=1-K+2r>=5-3K.                            (8)

Finally(4),(8) imply7K>=9+4w+2z>=9, proving K>=9/7.

SCOPE AND CERTIFICATION
Every deviation above involves at most two of the SAME four actual owners.
All resource bundles are nonnegative; no owner count or physical technology
changes between compared profiles. The symmetrization is a proof reduction,
not a strategic identity manipulation. No anonymous or linear architecture
is imposed on the original mechanism.

The discovery LP minimized K on the complete ternary report grid and returned
9/7. That numerical optimum is NOT an all-real upper guarantee. The lower
proved here is exact and independent of numerical optimization: the supplied
stdlib verifier sums14 valid rational inequalities, obtaining coefficient-1
on K, right-hand side-9/7, and only nonnegative null-payment terms4w/7+2z/7.
The elementary argument above is a human-readable elimination of that same
certificate. Additional null-eligible owners may be fixed at zero when using
this as a lower bound for a larger-owner class; their nonnegative payments
can be discarded without weakening the old-owner contract.

The unbounded-owner frontier remains unresolved: this note neither proves a
uniform pairwise gain-only upper nor rules one out. It does show that the
three-owner5/4 construction is not the final constant in that problem.
