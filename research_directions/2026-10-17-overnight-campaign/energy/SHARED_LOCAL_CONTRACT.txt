SUCCESSOR CONTRACT: ONE SHARED LOCAL ENERGY BUDGET
17 October 2026. Activated because route-specific per-pod values reduce exactly
to the accepted theorem; they do not require a new mechanism.

Owners A,C,H_1,...,H_m fixed; m arbitrary. A owns common LOCAL energy allowance
L, C owns common GRID allowance P, H_i owns h_i. Local energy is physically
deliverable to every admitted pod in one committed short window. This is an
assumption about an authorized campus network, not inferred from rooftop/battery
ownership. Public nonnegative net values a_i,b_i are route dependent as before.

Coalition worth F(S)=max Σ(a_i q_i+b_i g_i)
subject to q_i+g_i<=h_i(S), Σq_i<=L(S), Σg_i<=P(S), q,g>=0.
All-profile coordinatewise binding underreports, payment utility, fixed identities.
Optional floor y_A>=theta(F-F_without_A), theta∈[0,1]; all costs/retainers outside
net-value ledger exactly as in CONTRACT.txt. No private costs/latency additions.

FIRST TARGET. Determine whether this class admits a uniform truthful coalition-
covering funding factor kappa<2 independent of m and all public route values,
with theta1 floor if feasible. Alternatively isolate an exact obstruction to a
specific candidate, or an all-mechanism lower bound. Do not treat a method failure
as unrestricted impossibility.

CALIBRATION. Equal values a_i=b_i=1 collapse to F=min(Σh_i,L+P), a two-input
multi-glove game with two energy owners, so an efficient all-profile mechanism
cannot simply be presumed. The old append-local-output proof is invalid: local
baseline outside a coalition competes for the same L. Naive weighted threshold-
edge decompositions of assignment values also fail (explicit witness pending).

TRIVIAL UPPER. Let F_A=maxΣa_iq_i with Σq_i<=L,q_i<=h_i, and analogously F_C.
Pay A=F_A,C=F_C,hardware0. This is own-monotone, null-own-zero, covers every
coalition since each feasible allocation's route portions are bounded by F_A,F_C,
and total<=2F. The A floor is met since F-F_C<=F_A. This is baseline only.
