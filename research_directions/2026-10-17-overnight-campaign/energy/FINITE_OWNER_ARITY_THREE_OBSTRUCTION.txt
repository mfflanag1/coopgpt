FINITE ACTUAL-OWNER RATE FOR THE COMMON-CAP THREE-RESOURCE OBSTRUCTION
17 October 2026. Companion to the composition lane's common-cap theorem.
Status: internally accepted after root rederivation and independent Fable
review023151, with exact arithmetic replay. Historical novelty unconfirmed.

1. CLAIM AND SCOPE

For integer B>=1 and integers M>K>=2, consider

 F(P,a,b) = min(P, sum_{j=1}^M min(a_j,b_j)).                 (1)

This is a finite nonnegative monotone concave homogeneous production function.
It is the optimum of the unit-profit packing LP

 max sum_j x_j,   sum_j x_j<=P,   x_j<=a_j, x_j<=b_j, x_j>=0.

Every activity consumes exactly THREE resources, although both resource count
and actual owner count grow. There are L=2BK actual power owners, each eligible
only for resource P; and two actual branch owners per branch, eligible only for
a_j and b_j respectively. Their identities and eligibility are fixed across
every report profile. Thus n=2M+2BK and d=2M+1. No strategic cloning, splitting,
identity creation, aggregate-price hypothesis, or infinite-population limit is
used in the finite inequality below.

Assume nonnegative payments, coverage of every actual coalition, and TCWM:
the total payment to every actual coalition is nondecreasing when its members
increase their resource reports coordinatewise while all outsiders stay fixed.
Suppose total payments are at most B times grand production at every profile.
Define

 k_m=floor(K/m),   x_m=m*k_m/K,
 A_K=sum_{m=2}^K [1-(1-x_m)^2]/m,
 S_(M,K)=sum_{m=2}^K 1/(M-m+1)=H_(M-1)-H_(M-K).

Then necessarily

 B^2 >= A_K/[8(1+S_(M,K))]
     >= [H_K-1-delta_K]/[8(1+S_(M,K))],                    (2)

 where delta_K=[K(K+1)/2-1]/K^2.

In particular, if K_TCWM,3(n) denotes the worst necessary factor among such
three-resource-per-activity models with n fixed actual owners, then

 liminf_{n->infinity} K_TCWM,3(n)/sqrt(log n) >= 1/sqrt(8).  (3)

The logarithm is natural. This is a quantitative bounded-arity obstruction;
it does NOT improve the already established Omega(sqrt(n)) lower when activity
arity is allowed to grow, and does not concern only individual truthfulness.
Full outside-production incentives imply TCWM, so the same lower also applies
to that stronger contract.
The model class defining K_TCWM,3(n) permits additional fixed owners with empty
eligibility sets (or reports restricted to zero). This convention makes the
padding step in Section5 explicit; it never changes an owner's identity within
a report domain.

2. FIXED-OWNER SYMMETRIZATION

Average a hypothetical rule over permutations of power-owner labels, branch
permutations with the corresponding branch-owner/resource labels, and swaps
of the two owner/resource roles within any branch. These are automorphisms of
the SAME fixed ownership eligibility model and of (1). Nonnegativity, every
coverage inequality, every TCWM inequality and the factor B all survive this
finite averaging. We therefore use the resulting invariant rule below.

All L power owners report 1/L at all profiles, so aggregate P=1. For m=1,...,K,
profile C_m has (a_j,b_j)=(1,1) for j<=m and (0,1) for j>m. Its production is1.
At C_m, all 2m active branch owners receive the same payment q_m. All M-m
inactive b-owners receive the same payment z_m. Let L_m be the TOTAL payment
to all 2M branch owners, including zero-report inactive a-owners. Thus

 L_m<=B,   z_m<=B/(M-m).                                  (4)

The second expression is used only for m<K<=M-1. We never assume zero payments
to zero-report owners. Swaps among active branch roles preserve C_m even though
inactive branches have the asymmetric report (0,1).

3. EXACT RATIONAL BOOTSTRAP

For fixed m<=K, put k=k_m>=1 and epsilon=k/L. Change the m active branch pairs
to (epsilon,epsilon), leaving all power and inactive branch reports unchanged.
Production is m*epsilon<=1/(2B). Let Y_P be total payment to all power owners
at this bootstrap profile. Budget and symmetry imply

 Y_P<=B*m*epsilon,  each power owner receives Y_P/L.

For any active branch j, take the coalition consisting of its two actual branch
owners and ANY k actual power owners. This coalition has power k/L=epsilon and
paired branch capacity epsilon, hence worth epsilon. Coverage gives

 y_(a_j)+y_(b_j) >= epsilon-(k/L)Y_P
                  >= epsilon(1-B*m*epsilon).

Summing over m active pairs, then restoring all these same owners' reports to1
and applying TCWM to their actual coalition, yields

 2m*q_m >= m*epsilon(1-B*m*epsilon)
          = [1-(1-x_m)^2]/(4B).

Consequently

 q_m >= [1-(1-x_m)^2]/(8Bm).                              (5)

Keeping the exact factor 1-B*m*epsilon is essential to the leading constant.
Replacing it immediately by1/2 and rounding k loses an unnecessary factor2.

4. LINKED RESTORATIONS

Transition C_(m-1) to C_m, for m=2,...,K, by increasing only a_m from0 to1.
Take the coalition of ALL branch owners except b_m. It contains the changing
owner a_m and no power owner. TCWM for this same coalition gives exactly

 L_m-q_m >= L_(m-1)-z_(m-1).

Sum these inequalities. Since L_1>=0 and L_K<=B, equations(4)--(5) imply

 B*(1+S_(M,K)) >= sum_{m=2}^K q_m >= A_K/(8B).

This is the first inequality in(2), with no unmodeled owner participation.
Since 0<=1-x_m<m/K,

 A_K >= H_K-1 - (1/K^2)sum_{m=2}^K m = H_K-1-delta_K,

which proves the second inequality. QED for the finite result.

There is also a direct rational lower without presupposing an integer budget.
Keep the INSTANCE parameter B, L=2BK and epsilon_m=floor(K/m)/L fixed, but let
kappa be any candidate mechanism's factor. The same bootstrap gives
q_m>=epsilon_m(1-kappa*m*epsilon_m)/2 (valid even if its right side is negative).
Thus, with the finite rational quantities

 R=sum_{m=2}^K epsilon_m/2,  Q=sum_{m=2}^K m*epsilon_m^2/2,

every mechanism necessarily satisfies

                 kappa >= R/(1+S_(M,K)+Q).                (7)

In particular R-BQ=A_K/(8B). Any strict contradiction at kappa=B gives an
explicit strict rational gap in(7), so no attainment/closure assumption is
hidden in statements that the optimum exceeds B.

5. QUANTITATIVE OWNER-COUNT CONSEQUENCE

Convexity of 1/x gives the trapezoid bound

 H_K >= log K + (1+1/K)/2.

It follows that H_K-1-delta_K >= log K-1+1/K^2 > log K-1.
For integer B>=2 take M=BK. Then n=4BK and

 S_(BK,K) <= (K-1)/(BK-K+1) < 1/(B-1).

Thus a factor-B rule would require

 B^2 > [(B-1)/(8B)]*(log K-1)                             (6)

whenever log K>1. Set

 K_B=ceil(exp(8B^3/(B-1)+1)),  N_B=4B*K_B.

Equation(6) is impossible at K=K_B. Therefore K_TCWM,3(N_B)>B.
Here log N_B=8B^2+O(B+log B), and log N_(B+1)/log N_B tends1.
For any additional actual owners, give them empty eligibility sets, or restrict
them to a zero-report face and discard their nonnegative payments. The original
owners' coverage and TCWM inequalities remain valid, with no greater budget.
Hence the worst necessary factor is nondecreasing in n. Padding between the
successive N_B proves(3), not merely a subsequence statement.

6. A FINITE EXACT CERTIFICATE ABOVE FACTOR TWO

The asymptotic statement is not needed to exhibit a fixed finite obstruction.
Take B=2, K=2^49, M=35K, L=4K, and n=74K=41658296553177088 owners.
All profiles in the proof have rational capacities. Since S_(M,K)<1/34 and

 log2 > 6931/10000,
 log K-1 > 49*(6931/10000)-1 > 8*4*(35/34),

equation(2) contradicts funding factor2. The elementary logarithm certificate
uses log2=2*sum_{r>=0} (1/3)^(2r+1)/(2r+1); six positive terms already exceed
6931/10000, and their geometric tail bound gives log2<6932/10000.
No enumeration of this enormous owner population is necessary. More precisely,

 R-2Q-2(1+S) > 3523/2720000,
 1+S+Q < 35/34 + 49*(6932/10000)/32 = 2843589/1360000.

Here Q<=(H_K-1)/(8B^2)<=log K/(8B^2). Inserting these inequalities into(7)
gives the explicit fixed-owner bound

 kappa > 2 + 3523/5687178 = 11377879/5687178 > 2.0006.       (8)

This certificate establishes existence and preserves all fixed-owner details;
it is not a practical deployment scale or a claim that this owner count is
close to minimal. Sharper finite examples and the true owner-rate remain open.

7. VERIFICATION AND PROVENANCE

check_finite_owner_arity_three.py uses Fraction arithmetic to check the exact
bootstrap identity, floor-error estimate, harmonic ladder and explicit finite
factor-two certificate. These checks supplement the analytic proof rather than
replacing its all-profile conclusion.

The common-cap production function and linked C_m transition idea are due to
the composition lane of this campaign. That lane independently found the pure
power-owner finite realization. This companion retains the quadratic bootstrap
rounding loss, allows M to vary independently of K, and derives the explicit
owner-count rate and compact exact certificate. No historical priority claim
is made here.
