1. THE MATHEMATICAL RESULT
Read handoff/CAMPAIGN_OUTCOMES.txt, section 3, followed by
root/RESERVE_APPLICATION_CORRECTED_GATE.txt.

The internally accepted signed-L1 transfer gives sharp funding factors 3/2
for coalition payment monotonicity and 1+c for pooled outside-production
protection, where c(2-exp(-1/c))=1 and c=0.543063389825... . These bounds are
uniform over finite owner populations for L1-embeddable norm reserves,
including Euclidean reserves. Historical novelty is unconfirmed.

The Gaussian application fixes public factors, a common reliability target,
actual owner identities and one fungible delivery point. In normalized units,
F=sum_i t_i-||sum_i x_i||, with t_i>=||x_i||. An admissible owned withdrawal
and its retained remainder must both satisfy this cone. Physical and
contractual feasibility further restrict the permitted withdrawals.

The theorem protects a specified coalition-withholding contract; it does not
establish truthful private load estimates or private opportunity costs.
Ordinary coalition coverage already has an efficient norm-subgradient
allocation. The extra funding purchases stronger withholding protection. Two actual owners
already admit an efficient full-OUT rule; subsidy is not necessary in every
particular ownership arrangement.
Writing F=S+G separates existing slack from incremental diversification gain.
A total-funding bound does not bound subsidy/G, establish sponsor value, or
create physical capacity. Delivery, temporal constraints, owner reliability
and outside options still require validation.
