Positive restricted chart, explicitly not a global mechanism:

PAIRWISE_TCWM_DISTRIBUTION_STAR_BOUNDARY.txt supplies a continuous covering
selection when only one residual owner has the opposite resource, with pair
0..1 slopes on paths staying in this chart and funding <=
1+1/(2 sqrt(e)) times residual production. It works for arbitrary many owners
on the other side. The cap/equal-total branches are treated separately.
Exact harness: 780 profiles, 21,096 checks of coalitions containing the unique
opposite owner (the others are trivial), 10,365 slope checks. The exponential
branch has an analytic proof. Root and Fable053200 accepted after clarifying
the protected-pair convention and deriving witness payments in the checker.

Its extension consequence is the architecture-free deletion inequality in
that same note. For a dominant owner i and m opposite owners, any extension
must obey (m-1)u_i(z)<=sum_j[u_i(z^{-j})+u_j(z^{-j})]. With null-zero this is
a subadditivity restriction across opposite-owner deletion. Under a finite
anonymous n-owner gain bound, the residual null allowance is explicit.
The natural proportional extension violates this at (2,1/2,-1,-1/2) by5/48.

This closes the prescribed nonconic conceptual gate: chart values alone do
not solve the problem; an extension must redistribute across both owner
distributions while satisfying all finite pair-Lipschitz inequalities and
all coalition covers simultaneously. More positive sampling mixtures,
separable projections or proportional short-side continuations would revisit
closed mechanisms. A genuinely new continuous-selection theorem, or an
analytic report-cycle contradiction beyond these classes, is required.
