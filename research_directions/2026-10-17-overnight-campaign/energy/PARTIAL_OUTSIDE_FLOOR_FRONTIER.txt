SHARP PARTIAL-MARGINAL-FLOOR FRONTIER UNDER FULL COALITION OUTSIDE PRODUCTION
17 October 2026. Internally audited sharp corollary: root and independent Fable
review 011328 found no correctness defects; the unrestricted lower was separately
audited in review 010239. External specialist review and priority remain open.

MODEL AND CONTRACT
Use F=min(E,H), fixed actual owners with arbitrary mixed resource bundles, and
full coalition outside-production protection(OUT). Let theta be public in[0,1].
The strongest floor considered here requires every owner's payment to satisfy
          y_i>=theta M_i,
where M_i=F(x)-F(x without ALL of actual owner i's resources).
We also consider weaker contracts protecting only designated energy owners,
or removing only the protected owner's energy service rather than its full
bundle. The upper below meets the strongest floor and therefore all of these
weaker versions. For the universal matching lower, the class need only include
one designated pure energy owner who receives the theta floor; other owners
need not receive any floor at all.

THEOREM CLAIM
The optimal universal funding factor, with full OUT, is
          K(theta)=1+max(c,theta),
where c=0.5430633898250876... solves c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1.
Counts of owners are unrestricted. The same frontier holds for complete-access
public source-price production by the exact threshold identity. It is a worst-
case theorem over the stated class, not a claim about every fixed owner model.

1. THE LOGARITHMIC RULE ALREADY GIVES FRACTION c OF EVERY FULL MARGINAL
Assume E>=H>0 and t=H/E. The logarithmic rule pays
          y_i=c t e_i+q(t)h_i, with q(t)>=c.
The full owner-deletion marginal is
          M_i=max(h_i,H-E+e_i).
First y_i>=c h_i. Second,
 t e_i-(H-E+e_i)=(E-H)(1-e_i/E)>=0,
so y_i>=c t e_i>=c(H-E+e_i). Taking the maximum proves y_i>=c M_i.
The argument is symmetric when H>=E, and zero production is immediate.
Thus this floor applies to arbitrary fixed mixed owners, not just pure roles.
An energy-service-only deletion removes a subset of the full owner bundle,
so its marginal is at most M_i and is covered as well.

2. UPPER FOR ALL theta
For0<=theta<=c use the logarithmic rule unchanged: it has full OUT, coverage,
the required floors and total funding at most(1+c)F.
For c<=theta<=1, put lambda=(theta-c)/(1-c) and take the convex combination
 (1-lambda)*(logarithmic rule)+lambda*(proportional2 rule).
Both rules satisfy full OUT. Their convex combination does too, since the
outside-worth term appears with coefficients summing to1. Coverage and
nonnegativity also persist. The proportional rule dominates the full marginal,
so the combination meets floor[(1-lambda)c+lambda]M_i=theta M_i. Its budget is
 [(1-lambda)(1+c)+2lambda]F=(1+theta)F.

For public source-price tiers, integrate the same component rules using the
fixed public threshold lengths. Both full-owner deletion marginals and outside
production telescope exactly. The same theta frontier therefore holds for
that weighted complete-access class, with arbitrary fixed mixed actual owners.

3. NEW FLOOR LOWER: ONE PROTECTED OWNER SUFFICES
Fix m actual pure energy owners and one actual pure hardware owner, all with
unit capacity bounds. Designate one energy owner as protected by the theta
marginal floor. No other owner's floor is used in the argument.

At the baseline profile, only the protected energy owner reports1, the other
m-1 energy owners report0, and the hardware owner reports1. The protected
owner's full marginal AND energy-service marginal both equal1. Nonnegativity
therefore makes the TOTAL payment of the fixed coalition of all energy owners
at least theta, including any payments to its inactive members.

Increase the m-1 inactive energy reports to1. Full OUT implies payment-only
coalition monotonicity for this one-sided increase, because the withheld bundle
contains energy alone and has outside production0. Thus the new total energy
payment X is still at least theta. Write x_j for its individual payments and y
for the hardware payment. Each energy owner together with the hardware owner
can produce1, so coalition coverage gives x_j+y>=1 for every j. Averaging the
m inequalities gives y>=1-X/m. Therefore total funding at value1 obeys
          X+y>=1+(1-1/m)X>=1+theta(1-1/m).
Let m tend to infinity to obtain K>=1+theta. This proof makes no anonymity,
continuity, homogeneity, aggregate-price or owner-splitting assumption. Every
comparison uses the same fixed actual owners and their binding underreports.

The internally audited floor-free unrestricted OUT lower in
UNRESTRICTED_OUTSIDE_SHARP_CANDIDATE.txt also gives K>=1+c because this contract
only adds restrictions. The maximum of the two bounds matches Section2.

4. ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION AND DISTINCTIONS
For a public fractional marginal-surplus promise up to theta=c, the logarithmic
outside-production rule meets that promise without extra worst-case funding.
Above c, the sharp worst-case factor increases one-for-one with theta. At the
full floor theta=1 it equals2, already necessary with pure separated roles and
only one protected energy owner. Mixed-role static floor examples are useful
but are not needed for this stronger incentive-based lower.

In particular the earlier sharp4/3 full-energy-floor theorem protects individual
payment incentives, whereas full coalition OUT plus that floor has sharp factor2.
These are different incentive contracts. Changing the name of the floor or
retaining the same local mechanism does not remove the additional requirement.
No private-cost truthfulness, arbitrary access, latency, indivisible serving,
or measured economic gain is asserted. Costs and outside production must be
accounted for using the public net-value ledger of the stated model.
