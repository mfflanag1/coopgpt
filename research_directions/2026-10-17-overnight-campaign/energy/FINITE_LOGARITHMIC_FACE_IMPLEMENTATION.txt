FINITE RATIONAL FACE AVERAGES WITH EXACT FULL-OUT INCENTIVES
17 October 2026. Internally audited constructive corollary: root and Fable
review 015659 found no substantive defect. External review and priority remain.
This is an exact mechanism, not approximate incentive compatibility obtained
by applying an arbitrary numerical quadrature to a payment formula.

1. A PURELY RATIONAL SUFFICIENT CONDITION

Choose a public rational endpoint 0<tau<1 and a public rational partition
0=u_0<u_1<...<u_n=tau. Define

  gamma=1/(1+tau),
  w_l=gamma (u_l-u_(l-1))/(1-u_l),
  W=sum_l w_l,
  M=sum_l w_l u_l=W-gamma tau=gamma+(W-1).

Suppose the exact rational inequality W>=1 holds. For any finite nonnegative
monotone concave degree-one homogeneous F, define

  H_j^D(C)=sum_l w_l F(C_j e_j+u_l C_-j),
  p_j^D(C)=H_j^D(C)/C_j  if C_j>0,
  y_i^D=b_i.p^D,

with absent-coordinate contributions zero. Then these payments satisfy
nonnegativity, coalition coverage and full coalition OUT on every real
report profile of arbitrary fixed mixed owners. With k active coordinates,

        sum_i y_i^D <= [W+M(k-1)] F(C).

All constants and weights can be rational. In a rational linear-programming
technology, rational report profiles therefore have exactly rational
payments, computable by finitely many rational LP evaluations.

2. PROOF

Finite TCWM is immediate from the share-times-monotone-face expression, as
in GENERAL_LOGARITHMIC_FACE_OUTSIDE.txt. Weighted Jensen gives

  sum_j H_j^D
   <= W sum_j F(C_j e_j+(M/W)C_-j)
   <= [W+M(k-1)]F(C).

The derivative proof from the continuous theorem still reduces to holding
corners J. Every own-coordinate derivative term B_j is at least M F(d),
and M>=gamma>1/2. Thus corners with |J|>=2 are covered.

For the empty corner, use the same ray domination and shortest/second
shortest ratio t. The two relevant prices are q_D(t)=sum_l w_l min(1,u_l/t)
and Mt. If t>=tau, q_D(t)=M/t, so q_D(t)+Mt>=2M>1. If t<=tau, the function

                   (t-u)_+/(1-u)

is nonincreasing on [0,tau]. Its right-endpoint Riemann sum is at most its
integral, giving

  W-q_D(t)
    =(gamma/t)sum_l (u_l-u_(l-1))(t-u_l)_+/(1-u_l)
    <=gamma[1+(1-t)log(1-t)/t].

Consequently

  q_D(t)+Mt
    >=W+gamma(1-t)[-1-log(1-t)/t]>=W>=1.

For singleton J={j}, use the continuous proof's normalization G(1)=1,
min_(l!=j)x_l=1, t=x_j, V=G(x). The base B_j contributes at least M>=gamma,
and the outside coordinates contribute at least M V>=gamma V. If V>=tau
these already total at least gamma(1+tau)=1. Otherwise 0<t<=V<tau.

Let H(u)=G(t,u x_-j). By Euler, the own-coordinate gradient is

          h(u)=partial_j G(t,u x_-j)=[H(u)-u H'(u)]/t.

This function is nondecreasing in u. For smooth H, its derivative is
-u H''(u)/t>=0. General smooth positive-face F gives precisely this case;
nonsmooth F is handled below. Thus the discrete extra derivative satisfies

  sum_l w_l(1-u_l)h(u_l)
    =gamma sum_l (u_l-u_(l-1))h(u_l)
    >=gamma integral_0^tau h(u)du
    >=gamma(tau-V),

where the scalar concave lemma from the continuous proof is valid with any
endpoint tau. Combining all terms proves the singleton bound 1. Hence every
coalition derivative in every nonnegative direction d is at least F(d).

Finite deviations, zero coordinates, and possible boundary jumps follow by
the same monotone-restoration and diagonal-log-smoothing argument as in the
continuous theorem. All u_l are strictly positive, so each discrete face
evaluation stays in the original active relative face. Smoothing convergence
passes through the finite sums directly. The proof establishes exact OUT;
it does not rely on numerical approximation of a derivative or an inequality.

3. ARBITRARILY NEAR-SHARP VALUE-ORACLE COMPLEXITY

Let c and t0 be the sharp continuous constants, and let 0<epsilon<=1. Choose
a rational tau with

       t0+epsilon/48<=tau<=t0+epsilon/24,  tau<=9/10,

and use the uniform grid with n=ceil(24/epsilon). Such a rational endpoint
can be certified by bisection on

              -log(1-tau)-(1+tau),

whose root is t0. A fully rational certificate uses
-log(1-tau)=sum_(m>=1) tau^m/m, with the positive omitted tail at most
tau^(N+1)/[(N+1)(1-tau)]. On the fixed interval below 9/10, O(log(1/epsilon))
terms suffice to bracket the function to the needed accuracy. Explicitly,
first bracket t0 by rational endpoints [l,h] of width at most epsilon/192,
then choose a rational tau in [h+epsilon/48,l+epsilon/24]. This interval is
nonempty and places tau in the displayed range. The enforced distance from
the root is at least epsilon/48, so its positive sign has an order-epsilon
margin; it is not necessary to distinguish an arbitrarily near-zero sign.
The same series bounds certify this sign with O(log(1/epsilon)) terms.

Put W_cont=gamma[-log(1-tau)]. Then W_cont>=1. A right-sum estimate gives

  0<=W-W_cont
    <=gamma (tau/n)[1/(1-tau)-1]
    <=(81/10)/n.

The derivative of [-log(1-tau)]/(1+tau) is at most
1/(1-tau^2)<=100/19<6, so

  0<=W_cont-1<=6(tau-t0)<=epsilon/4.

It follows that W>=1 and W-1<epsilon. Since gamma<=c,

  W+M(k-1)
    =1+gamma(k-1)+k(W-1)
    <=1+c(k-1)+epsilon k.

The rational endpoint has O(log(1/epsilon)) bits, each grid node and weight
has polynomial bit length, and summing n rational weights uses at most the
sum of their denominator bit lengths. Therefore the exact W>=1 check and
all auxiliary arithmetic have polynomial bit complexity in 1/epsilon.
The rule obtains the sharp universal factor to additive epsilon k
using O(k/epsilon) exact value-oracle calls at each profile, independent of
the number of owners except for aggregation and payment arithmetic. For a
rational explicit production LP, this is a polynomial-time near-optimal
mechanism in the usual rational LP and 1/epsilon model. It has exact full OUT
for its defined rational rule, not merely epsilon-truthfulness.

The qualification 'exact oracle calls' matters. Uncertified floating output
from a solver can violate exact economic inequalities. Numerical deployment
requires verified values, interval bounds with an incentive-preserving
implementation, or an explicitly approximate economic contract. This theorem
does not silently convert a floating solver into an exact mechanism.

4. REPRODUCIBLE RATIONAL EXAMPLES AND SCOPE

For tau=84141/100000 and n=1000, exact rational arithmetic gives W>=1 and
approximately

       W=1.0012259025039554,   M=0.5442880125174776.

Thus the all-profile rule has factor W+M(d-1). The decimals are only a
readable display of an exactly specified rational certificate. A smaller
working example tau=421/500,n=100 gives W about1.0140209026 and M about0.5569090676.

The finite construction supplies an implementable approximation to the
universal theorem. It does not prove optimality within fixed n-node schemes,
and it does not improve the exact sharp constant c. Its significance is that
the near-optimal computation preserves the full incentive contract for all
profiles instead of hoping that numerical integration preserves it.
