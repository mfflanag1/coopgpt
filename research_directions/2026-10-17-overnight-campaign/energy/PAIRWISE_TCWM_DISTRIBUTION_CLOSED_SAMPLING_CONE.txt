PAIR-LIPSCHITZ NECESSITY
For the standalone-plus-residual architecture(A), pair-TCWM implies that each
protected pair's residual payment is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz in the pure
capacity magnitude of either member. The upper inequality follows by starting
at the larger pure capacity and restoring the opposite resource: standalone
payment rises by exactly the capacity difference while residual magnitude
falls by the same amount. The other protected member may change zero. See
PAIRWISE_TCWM_DISTRIBUTION_LIPSCHITZ_REDUCTION.txt for the complete equivalence.
Only this upper inequality is used below.

2. HIGH PROFILE

the large positive owner can alone use both negative owners' total capacity.
Exact enumeration gives

The following exact identity now has incompatible signs unless both moments
