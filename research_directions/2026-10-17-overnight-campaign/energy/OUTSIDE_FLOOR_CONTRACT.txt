FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION PROTECTION WITH FULL-OWNER MARGINAL FLOORS
17 October 2026. Routine contract closure from the root's proportional OUT rule
and the energy lane's exact static mixed-role floor obstruction.
Not a new unrestricted lower-bound technique; independent review pending.

MODEL
Use the complete-access two-resource model, fixed actual mixed owners, binding
underreports, and coalition outside-production condition(OUT) from
OUTSIDE_LOGARITHMIC_PRICE_THEOREM.txt. Require additionally each selected energy-
holding actual owner's FULL OWNER-DELETION marginal floor
 M_i=F(x)-F(x without ALL of owner i's energy AND hardware).
It is essential that the floor removes the full actual bundle. This is different
from the energy-service floor obtained by removing energy alone.

THEOREM
The optimal universal funding factor is exactly2 when every energy-holding
owner receives this floor and arbitrary fixed mixed ownership is permitted.
The same factor2 suffices if the floor is required for EVERY actual owner.
The upper satisfies full coalition OUT; the lower is already a static floor
obstruction, with no incentive argument needed.

PROOF OF UPPER
Write E=sum e_i, H=sum h_i, T=min(E,H), and pay
 y_i=T(e_i/E+h_i/H) when T>0, otherwise0.
The root's OUTSIDE_PRODUCTION_BOUNDS.txt proves coalition coverage, full OUT
and total funding exactly2T. The floor verification is elementary. If E<=H,
 M_i=E-min(E-e_i,H-h_i)=max(e_i,E-H+h_i).
The proportional payment y_i=e_i+E h_i/H is at least e_i. It is also at least
E-H+h_i because
 y_i-(E-H+h_i)=e_i+(H-E)(1-h_i/H)>=0.
When H<=E the symmetric argument applies. Thus every owner gets its full
deletion marginal, even if only energy-holding owners were promised that floor.
Zero production gives zero marginal and zero payment.

For public source-cost and hardware-value tiers with complete access, apply the
same proportional rule at each fixed public threshold and integrate payments.
The exact production identity gives the same2 funding bound and full OUT.
Deleting the actual owner's entire bundle commutes with every threshold, so
the integrated component marginal is exactly its weighted full-owner marginal.
Hence arbitrary fixed mixed owners and public net route values are covered.

PROOF OF LOWER
Take actual owner A with(e,h)=(1,0), actual owner B with(e,h)=(epsilon,1),
where0<epsilon<1. Both owners hold energy and are therefore protected by the
stated contract. Production is1. Deleting A leaves epsilon, so M_A=1-epsilon;
deleting B leaves0, so M_B=1. Required payments total at least2-epsilon.
Let epsilon tend to0. This lower already follows from the floors at ONE
profile, and therefore remains valid under the additional full OUT constraint.
If every owner, including pure hardware owners, must receive its full marginal,
the exact two-owner profile(1,0),(0,1) already forces total2 at value1.

ECONOMIC CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITS
Under this full-bundle floor contract, protecting coalitions against separate
production with withheld resources requires no additional worst-case funding
above the static optimum2. The payment-only contract was already that expensive.
This does not say outside-production protection is free under other contracts:
the energy-service-only4/3 theorem uses a different deletion operation, and the
floor-free full-OUT logarithmic theorem has constant1.543063... .

The mixed-role static lower above does not apply to purely separated roles.
The later PARTIAL_OUTSIDE_FLOOR_FRONTIER.txt supplies a DIFFERENT incentive
lower proving2 is necessary there too: even one protected energy owner plus
coalition withholding monotonicity forces2 as owner counts grow. That new
argument also gives the partial-floor lower1+theta without symmetry assumptions.
These results do not supply private-cost truthfulness, arbitrary access, or
