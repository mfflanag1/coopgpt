FINITE-FORM GAIN-ONLY FUNDING: THE ESTABLISHED SHARP ORDER SURVIVES
17 October 2026. Routine transfer of the canonical manuscript's upper/lower
rates. No new lower-bound method or historical priority claim.

Let a gain coefficient L>=1 mean that a mechanism covers all coalitions,
satisfies individual full outside-production protection, and has

  Y<=S+L G,   S=sum_i F(b_i), G=F(C)-S.

UPPER. The modified-cap mask theorem proves exactly the manuscript's finite
K expression C_j(K) for this stronger budget and incentive contract whenever
F is the minimum of at most K fixed nonnegative linear forms. In particular
the leading upper is (1+o(1))log K/log log K as K grows, with owner counts
unbounded. The finite-K coefficient proof is a fixed-profile residual-array
identity; incentive validity is separately proved in original owner bundles.

LOWER. Any such mechanism also has ordinary own-report monotonicity and

  Y<=S+L(T-S)=LT-(L-1)S<=LT.

Therefore EVERY established lower bound for ordinary individual incentives
and total funding applies immediately. No assumption that S=0 is needed for
this implication. The canonical manuscript proves its asymptotic lower for
the worst class with at most K forms, using a sequence of simple-DAG examples
and an explicit at-most-K parameter choice. Using that exact previously
established statement yields the same sharp leading order for gain-only
funding. This note does not upgrade a bare subsequence result by assertion.

For the concrete series-network witnesses, each actual owner holds only one
edge, and there are at least two modules in series. Thus an owner cannot
produce source-to-sink flow alone, even at positive edge reports: S=0 and
G=T on every witness profile. In that example the two funding denominators
also coincide directly. This is an interpretation, not a required new premise.

MINIMUM OF r POOLS. Any min-linear technology with at most r forms embeds
into F_r(z)=min_{j<=r}z_j by the fixed nonnegative linear map

  b_i -> (w_1 dot b_i,...,w_K dot b_i),

duplicating a row if K<r. All coalition values are exactly preserved. Every
original owner's coordinatewise restoration maps to a coordinatewise
restoration by the SAME actual owner; unused reports outside the image need
not be used in a lower-bound restriction. Standalone values and gains are
also preserved. Thus a min-r gain-only mechanism would induce a mechanism
on every original at-most-r-form example. The existing at-most-K lower gives
the same sharp asymptotic order/leading constant for min-r with unbounded
actual owner counts. The upper uses the r coordinate forms directly.

SCOPE
This concerns INDIVIDUAL full outside-production incentives. The separately
proved TCWM min-r constant is (r+1)/2 and full coalition-OUT constant is
1+c(r-1), both far larger as r grows. Full coalition incentives also prohibit
any finite gain-only coefficient on fully eligible min2 domains, whereas the
individual contract above remains feasible. These are different contracts,
not contradictory numerical claims.

The asymptotic lower source is the current canonical manuscript's general
sharp-order theorem and simple-network proof; this note adds no novelty
confirmation and no computational-efficiency claim.
