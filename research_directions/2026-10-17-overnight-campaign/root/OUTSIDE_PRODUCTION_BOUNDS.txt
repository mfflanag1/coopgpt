WITHHELD RESOURCES CAN PRODUCE: TWO GENERAL POSITIVE BOUNDS AND A SCOPE OBSTRUCTION
17 October2026. Root candidate, independent review pending.

MODEL
Fixed actual owners report bundles b_i=(a_i,b_i) in R_+^2; use x_i=(a_i,b_i)
below to avoid overloading the vector. F(x)=min(A,B), A=sum a_i,B=sum b_i.
A coalition S may withhold d_i<=x_i, receive its mechanism payments at the
reduced reports, and independently produce F(sum_S d_i) using withheld inputs.
The strong condition is
 sum_S y_i(x) >= sum_S y_i(x-d_S)+F(sum_S d_i).           (OUT)
Individual OUT restricts S to singletons. Full OUT includes all coalitions.
All identities, eligibility, technology, and opponents' reports stay fixed.
No double use of a resource is permitted: withheld inputs are excluded from
the mechanism's production and used only outside.

1. INDIVIDUAL OUT WITH FACTOR K=1+1/(2 exp(1))
Begin with the pure-side exponential rule, an established project ingredient:
T=min(P,N), each short-side owner z gets (1-q)z, each long-side owner z gets
q min(z,T), where rho=sum_long min(z,T)/T and q=exp(1-rho)/2. Zero production
gives zero payments. It covers coalitions and has total<=K T.

Its payment to a pure owner is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz in that owner's
scalar report, including zero and side-switch points. Long-side derivative
below its cap is q(1-z/T) in[0,1/2], and above the cap is0. On the short side,
g(z)=(1-q(T))z, T=z+fixed short opponents. Here q'(T)>=0 and
  0 <= 1-q-z q'(T) <=1.
The lower bound follows from z<=T, q'(T)<=q rho/T and
q(1+rho)<=1 for rho>=1. All cap/seam formulas join continuously. This proves
the global scalar Lipschitz assertion without numerical approximation.

For each actual mixed owner define standalone production s_i=min(a_i,b_i)
and residual r_i=a_i-b_i. Run the pure rule on positive r_i on one side and
negative r_i on the other, keeping actual labels (a zero residual gets0).
Pay y_i=s_i+g_i(r). This is the established ownerwise-netting construction;
the new question here is its stronger outside-production interpretation.

Production decomposes EXACTLY, coalition by coalition:
 F(sum_S x_i)=sum_S s_i+min(sum_S(r_i)_+,sum_S(-r_i)_+).
Therefore coverage and funding follow, since
 sum_i y_i <=sum_i s_i+K min(P,N)<=K F(sum_i x_i).

Own-coordinate monotonicity follows from the0..1 Lipschitz bound. On a fixed
residual-sign cell, raising the abundant coordinate increases g; raising the
scarce coordinate increases s by the same amount by which it decreases the
absolute residual, so the loss of g is at most that gain. Across r_i=0 both
rules meet at y_i=s_i; g tends0 by the Lipschitz property. Hence y_i is
coordinatewise nondecreasing for arbitrary real own reports.

More strongly, adding (t,t) to this actual owner's bundle changes only s_i,
raising its payment EXACTLY by t. For an arbitrary withheld bundle d_i, put
t=min(d_i). Moving from x_i-d_i to x_i first add(t,t), then add the remaining
nonnegative one-sided bundle. Payment increases by at least t=F(d_i).
This proves individual OUT at every profile, with unbounded owner counts.

This also transfers to complete-access public source-price production:
at each fixed public threshold first aggregate each actual owner's energy
and hardware amounts, net within it, and apply the rule. Integrating both
mechanism inequality and standalone withheld worth gives individual OUT for
arbitrary public tiers, because F(d)=integral min(E_t(d),H_t(d))dt.
This particular transfer assumes complete access; an arbitrary-access threshold
component is a graph game, not necessarily a scalar two-pool minimum.

2. FULL COALITION OUT WITH FACTOR TWO
For T>0 define
 y_i=T(a_i/A+b_i/B); set all payments0 when T0.
The total is exactly2T, and coverage is immediate: when A>=B, each coalition
gets at least its B amount, and the other orientation is symmetric.

Fix S and grow its reports on the straight line from x-d_S to x. Let
a=sum_S a_i,b=sum_S b_i, with aggregates A,B. When A>B, its total is
Y_S=B a/A+b. Its derivative for an increase to any own A-coordinate is
g_A=(B/A)(1-a/A)>=0; for an increase to any own B-coordinate it is
g_B=1+a/A>=1. For A<B the roles swap. Along the line, total changes in the
two resource sums are Delta_A,Delta_B>=0, so at every differentiable point
dY_S/dt >=min(Delta_A,Delta_B). Integrating over the finite side-switch cells
gives(OUT). At A=B the formulas agree; at zero total, continuity follows
from0<=Y_S<=2T. The line may start at zero; use a positive limiting segment.
This is a full all-profile coalition statement, not merely one deviation.

More generally, for a single r-input minimum T=min_j C_j, the rule
 y_i=T sum_j x_ij/C_j (zero if T0)
has full coalition OUT and funding rT. On a cell with bottleneck coordinate k,
the coalition derivative in k is1+sum_{j!=k} a_j/C_j>=1, while every other
coordinate derivative is(T/C_j)(1-a_j/C_j)>=0. Integrate as above, giving
at least min_j Delta_j. Any tied bottlenecks can be handled by one-sided
derivatives or direct subdivision of the linear path.

No optimality claim for these full coalition OUT bounds is made. The existing
TCWM3/2 lower applies to the two-input subclass because OUT implies TCWM.

3. NO CONSTANT MULTIPLIER REPAIRS HUBER ON ALL UNIT RESOURCE GRAPHS
Consider the four-vertex unit path0--1--2--3. One actual owner holds the two
endpoints with capacity1 each. A second holds the two interior resources with
capacity1 each. At the full profile the unique Huber prices are all1/2; the
interior owner's total payment is1.
That owner withholds delta in(0,1) of EACH interior resource. Its reports are
now1-delta each. The tight outer edges have endpoint prices(1-delta)/2 and
interior prices(1+delta)/2; the middle edge is slack. Positive outer KKT
multipliers1-delta and zero middle multiplier certify the unique optimum.
The interior owner's mechanism payment is1-delta^2, while its withheld
resources can use the middle activity to produce delta.
For ANY fixed multiplier L>0 applied to every Huber payment, the withholding
gain is delta-L delta^2>0 for delta<min(1,1/L). Thus no uniform scalar payment
inflation repairs individual OUT on all unit resource graphs.
This is an architecture obstruction, not an impossibility for other rules.
It shows why a mere constant-factor rescaling of the accepted graph theorem
cannot justify retained-resource self-production in the economic model.

PRIORITY AND STATUS
Ownerwise netting and the pure exponential rule are established project
ingredients. Absence-proofness/withholding with retained resources have older
literature, notably Dogan2013/2016 and Postlewaite1979. This note supplies a
precise model extension and its proofs, not historical novelty confirmation.
General graph OUT bounds and the sharp two-input full-coalition constant remain
open here. No private-cost, latency, energy-measurement or deployment claim.
