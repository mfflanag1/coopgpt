SHARP THREE-HALVES UNDER TRANSFERABLE COALITION-WITHHOLDING INCENTIVES
17 October2026. Candidate theorem extracted from exact finite-grid lower scouts.
Root-authored; independent audit required. This strengthens the architecture lower
but imposes stronger incentives than the project's original individual condition.

DEFINITIONS
There are finitely many fixed actual owners, each with nonnegative resource
reports in a fixed eligibility set. Coalition production uses only its members'
reported resources. A payment rule is nonnegative and coalition covering if
sum_{i in S} y_i(b)>=F(b_S) for every S,b. Funding factor kappa means
sum_i y_i(b)<=kappa F(b) for all b with F(b)>0 (and zero at F0 if required).

TRANSFERABLE COALITION-WITHHOLDING MONOTONICITY (TCWM): for every fixed coalition
S, fixed outside reports, and b'_S<=b_S coordinatewise,
 sum_{i in S}y_i(b'_S,b_-S)<=sum_{i in S}y_i(b_S,b_-S).
This rules out gain in the coalition's SUM of payment-only utilities from jointly
binding underreports, even if its members can transfer utility. It is not the
usual weaker individual monotonicity requirement. No costs or overreports enter.

LOWER THEOREM
For one two-input activity F(A,B)=min(A,B), with n=m+1 fixed owners all eligible
for both resources, any nonnegative coalition-covering TCWM rule has worst-case
funding factor at least 3/2-1/(2m), for every integer m>=1. Consequently, over
unbounded owner counts,3/2 is necessary. No price, anonymity, continuity,
homogeneity or separability restriction is imposed on the original rule.

PROOF BY VALID SYMMETRIZATION
Suppose a rule has factor kappa. Average it over the finite group generated by
permutations of the n owner identities and exchange of resource labels A,B.
For each relabeling g evaluate the original rule at the relabeled reports and
relabel payments back. Eligibility (all owners can hold both resources) and F
are invariant. Nonnegativity, every coverage inequality, every TCWM inequality,
and the factor-kappa budget inequality are linear in payments and are preserved
under each relabeling and the finite average. The resulting rule is anonymous
and A/B symmetric, with no larger worst-case funding factor. It suffices to
lower-bound this symmetric rule. This is a mathematical averaging argument,
not a change of identities within a strategic report comparison.

Baseline profile b0 has owner1 report(1,0), owner2 report(0,1), and m-1 remaining
owners report(0,0). Symmetry gives payments a,a to owners1,2 and z>=0 to each
remaining owner. Grand coverage gives2a+(m-1)z>=1.
Let S be the fixed coalition of every owner except owner2. Its baseline total is
 a+(m-1)z = [2a+(m-1)z]/2 + (m-1)z/2 >=1/2.
Increase every initially zero owner's A report from0 to1, leaving all other
coordinates and owners unchanged. At this new profile b1 the m members of S
all report(1,0), hence each receives the same payment x; owner2 receives y.
TCWM for S implies m x>=1/2. Every coalition consisting of owner2 and one member
of S produces1, so x+y>=1. The grand value at b1 remains1. Thus
 kappa >= m x+y >=1+(m-1)x >=1+(m-1)/(2m)=3/2-1/(2m).
This proves the lower. Baseline null reports are feasible underreports of actual
owners that are all strictly positive and productive at b1; they are not added
identities or irrelevant padding. At m1 the conclusion is the trivial bound1.

UNBOUNDED RESOURCE AND OWNER COUNTS
Take k disconnected unit activity edges with2k resource rows. Each block has
m+1 owners, each eligible for both resources of that block and no other block.
These eligibility sets and identities remain fixed. Production is
 F(c)=sum_{r=1}^k min(A_r,B_r).
Average a candidate rule over block permutations, owner permutations within each
block, and independent resource swaps inside each block, relabeling payments
back. The same preservation argument applies even if the original rule couples
payments between blocks.
Use b0 with one unit-A owner and one unit-B owner per block, remaining m-1
owners zero. All2k active owners have the same payment a and all k(m-1) null
owners the same z. Grand coverage gives2a+(m-1)z>=1 after division byk.
Let S contain all owners except the designated B owner in each block. Its
baseline payment is k[a+(m-1)z]>=k/2. Increase all initially null S owners to
one A unit within their own block. At b1 every A owner has payment x and every
B owner has payment y. TCWM gives k m x>=k/2; each within-block A+B coalition
has worth1 and hence x+y>=1. Grand production is k. Therefore the same ratio
bound3/2-1/(2m) follows. Taking k=m->infinity makes d=2m and
n=m(m+1) both grow, with every owner's final capacity strictly positive.
The bound is not restricted to decomposable payment mechanisms.

MATCHING UPPER AND SCOPE
The independently audited Huber graph rule in composition/HUBER_PRICE_THREE_HALVES_v2.txt
covers all coalitions and has total<=3F/2 on every finite unit-profit fractional
resource graph with arbitrary fixed mixed actual owners. For any coalition S,
replace the owner's low/high capacity sums in its derivative proof by the
coalition's low/high capacity sums. They still lie between0 and aggregate
capacities, so the same inequalities show TCWM. Boundary and finite-seam arguments
are unchanged. Hence3/2 is the exact universal optimum under TCWM on this graph
class, already forced by disconnected single-edge activities as above.

This DOES NOT prove3/2 necessary under individual truthfulness alone. Our earlier
9/8 lower and stronger small-domain owner-aware constructions are compatible.
It DOES improve upon a lower confined to aggregate-only linear prices: arbitrary
mechanisms satisfying the stronger coalition incentive requirement are covered.
The symmetrization is an existential proof reduction and requires the symmetric
eligibility domains used by the lower examples; the upper allows arbitrary ones.
Historical novelty, standard terminology and external significance require review.
