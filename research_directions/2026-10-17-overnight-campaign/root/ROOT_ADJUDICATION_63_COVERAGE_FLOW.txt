RESULT
For explicit rational weighted coverage ranks r(S)=sum_f w_f 1{S meets E_f},
the accepted public-polymatroid3/2 price rule now has an actual dependency-free
exact rational max-flow backend. It avoids subset enumeration and numerical
quadrature. Fixed original resource indices, zero-capacity rows, loops and
exact ties are preserved. The theorem remains coalition coverage plus TCWM,
not full coalition outside production; funding is relative to total output.

CORRECTNESS AND COMPLEXITY
To minimize a r(S)-c(S), source-job arcs carry c_j, job-feature arcs carry
M=sum c+1, and feature-sink arcs carry a w_f. Every minimum cut costs at most
sum c, hence crosses no M arc. Its job set has cut cost c(E\S)+a r(S).
After exact max flow, complementing all vertices that can reach the sink in
the positive residual graph gives the unique maximal source side and thus
maximal minimizing job set. Source reachability alone would pick the wrong
extremal cut in tie cases. Fraction Edmonds--Karp and at most2m-1principal-chain
calls give polynomial arithmetic complexity for the explicit graph; rational
bit costs and rank bookkeeping remain counted separately. No arbitrary public
polymatroid backend or high-performance guarantee is inferred.
