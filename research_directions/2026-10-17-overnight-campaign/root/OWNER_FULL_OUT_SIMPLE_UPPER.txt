A SIMPLE FULL-OUT UPPER IN THE NUMBER OF ACTUAL OWNERS
17 October2026. Elementary candidate corollary, priority unassessed.

For finite nonnegative monotone concave degree-one F, put c=sum_i b_i and
 y_i(b)=F((c+b_i)/2)=(1/2)F(c+b_i).
The same fixed n actual owners remain present at every report profile. Zero
reporters can receive positive payments; the original eligibility definition
allows this. This is not a no-pay-to-inactive rule.

Coalition coverage: for a singleton i, (c+b_i)/2>=b_i. For |S|>=2, every
payment in S is at least F(b_S)/2, so their sum covers F(b_S).

Full outside production: for a coalition withholding bundles d_i and aggregate
d=sum_(i in S)d_i, its i-th high argument exceeds its low argument by
(d+d_i)/2. Concavity and degree-one homogeneity imply superadditivity, so
 y_i(high)-y_i(low)>=F((d+d_i)/2).
For a singleton this is F(d); for |S|>=2 each term is >=F(d)/2. Summing proves
full coalition OUT. This proof is finite and includes all boundary profiles.

Funding: Jensen and homogeneity give
 sum_i F((c+b_i)/2)<=n F(((n+1)/(2n))c)=(n+1)F(c)/2.
Thus the general full-OUT upper can be chosen using public n,d as the smaller
of this owner rule and the separately proposed logarithmic face-average
resource rule1+c0(d-1). Selection must be public before reports, never a
pointwise minimum of payments. No claim that this owner bound is sharp.

For n=2 a better efficient full-OUT rule is the usual two-owner Shapley vector:
 y_1=[F(b_1)+F(c)-F(b_2)]/2 and symmetrically for y_2.
Singleton withdrawal raises each of the two own-dependent terms by at least
F(d_i), by superadditivity; full two-owner withdrawal changes totalF by at
leastF(d). Coverage follows from superadditivity. Hence n=1,2 have factor1.
The simple (n+1)/2 bound is useful only from n>=3, without an optimality claim.

This elementary corollary does not improve the original individual-IC sqrt(n)
upper; it concerns the strictly stronger full-OUT contract. The possible
owner-count gap for that stronger contract remains a separate frontier.
