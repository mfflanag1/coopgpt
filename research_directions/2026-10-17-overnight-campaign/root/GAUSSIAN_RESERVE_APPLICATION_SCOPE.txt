PUBLIC-FACTOR RESERVE POOLING: A PRECISE AI/ENERGY APPLICATION CANDIDATE
17 October2026. Model proposal, not empirical validation or market advice.

OBJECT AND OWNERSHIP
Owner i controls a portfolio of load obligations and firm, deliverable capacity.
Over one specified settlement interval, its net uncertain load is
 Li=mi+<xi,Z>, Z~N(0,I_d), with a fixed public factor model. Correlation is
encoded by the vectors xi; signed coordinates are allowed. The owner controls
firm capacity Ci and has headroom ti=(Ci-mi)/z_alpha, where z_alpha>0 is
the common Gaussian quantile for a declared one-sided reliability target.
An admitted portfolio must satisfy ti>=||xi||2. Aggregating a coalition
releases usable firm capacity
 H(S)=sum_S Ci-sum_S mi-z_alpha||sum_S xi||2
     =z_alpha F(sum_S(ti,xi)).
This identity follows from the Gaussian linear-combination distribution.
The statistical and physical assumptions are the substantive modeling gate.

FEASIBLE DEVIATIONS AND PAYMENT UNITS
An owner may withhold a verifiable subportfolio of obligations together with
specified capacity. Both the withheld subportfolio and the retained portfolio
must meet the same standalone reserve standard. Their mean/capacity/factor
vectors add. This maps exactly to D_i in K and b_i-D_i in K. The mechanism
does not make private covariance estimates, fabricated capacity or hidden
future demand truthful. Ownership and admissible subportfolios must be
auditable; a report need not be an arbitrary economically attainable point
of the mathematical cone for the theorem to remain valid on the subset.
If unused deliverable capacity has one fixed public value r per unit, scale
payments by r*z_alpha. FullOUT then protects a deviating coalition's own
pooled withheld headroom, under that same linear valuation.

WHAT THE SHARP NORM THEOREM WOULD SAY
Under those assumptions, the proposed L1-embedding rule covers every
coalition's standalone pooled value and all feasible joint withholding,
while total payments are at most(1+c) times realized model headroom value,
c=0.543063389825.... The maximal external subsidy is at most c times that
value; funding it requires an explicit sponsor benefit or subsidy budget.
It is NOT an internally budget-balanced sharing rule. It does not establish
a constant subsidy relative only to incremental diversification gains:
the budget benchmark includes any preexisting standalone headroom.

AI/ENERGY INTERPRETATION TO TEST FIRST
The cleanest initial setting is several independently owned flexible compute
portfolios sharing one electrical delivery point, with common settlement and
reserve rules. Firm capacity is an enforceable power entitlement; uncertain
net load includes compute demand and any colocated generation. Releasing
reserve may permit additional contracted service or sale of capacity. This
is a power/headroom contract, not a GPU-throughput or latency theorem.
Network bottlenecks, backup-duration limits, rebound, correlated forecast
errors, non-Gaussian tails and private opportunity costs are absent and must
be tested or explicitly excluded. The Mac serving pilot does not validate
this multiowner power model.

FALSIFIABLE NEXT GATE
Use a compatible public multivariate load/renewable trace with declared units,
sampling interval, and ownership mapping. Split time into model fitting and
held-out evaluation before comparing pooling. Estimate a common covariance
model only on fitting data; quantify out-of-sample aggregate tail exceedance,
owner-level tail exceedance, reserve released and any distribution shift.
Compare fixed Gaussian, a conservative empirical-tail benchmark, and no
pooling at the same measured reliability target. No causal efficiency claim
follows merely from replaying correlated traces. If the common quantile/norm
model fails reliability or capacity cannot be transferred, stop this direct
application and identify the missing constraint before adding incentives.

PRIORITY AND ECONOMIC COMPARISON
Gaussian aggregation, covariance diversification and capital/risk allocation
are established. The proposed contribution is a sharp subsidy/incentive
contract under the stated cone withdrawals, conditional on its mathematical
audit. Existing capital-allocation IC often orders profits state by state;
that differs from cone-feasible subportfolio withholding. Population-entry
monotonicity likewise differs from fixed-owner report changes. These
differences require explicit comparison, not a blanket novelty assertion.
