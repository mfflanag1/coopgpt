TUNABLE-CAP SCALAR PRICES: SHARP ASYMPTOTIC LEADING CONSTANT
17 October2026. Root theorem candidate, independent audit pending.

This extends CAPPED_UTILIZATION_CUBE_ROOT_BARRIER.txt to the parameter-dependent
cap used by the new fourth-root mechanism, and to k complementary branch
inputs. The earlier fixed-cap theorem remains correct but is narrower.

CLASS.
Fix integers M>=2,k>=2 and public L>=1. Technology is
 F(P,x)=min(P,sum_(j=1)^M min_(ell=1)^k x_jell).
Define g_j=min(P/L,x_j1,...,x_jk), G=sum_j g_j, r=P/G.
Use nonnegative common scalar functions h,f and resource prices
 p_P=h(r), p_jell=f(r)g_j/x_jell.
Each fixed actual owner receives its REPORTED bundle dotted with these prices.
Require coalition coverage, all-profile TCWM for arbitrary fixed mixed owners,
and total funding<=kappa F. Two fully eligible owners suffice for the test
profiles: one holds the chosen test coalition bundle, one the remainder.
Assume f is locally absolutely continuous on(r0,infinity), right-continuous
at r0=L/(M-1), and h is right-continuous at L. These explicit regularity
conditions hold for the proposed construction. No arbitrary-mechanism claim.

THEOREM.
Put alpha=(k-1)/k and A_k=k/(k+1)^(1+1/k). Then
             kappa^2 >= k A_k (M-1)^alpha.                  (1)
For fixed k, the construction with capP/C, C=max(1,sqrt(k A_k)M^(alpha/2)),
h=min(1,C/r), f=min(1,A_k(r/C)^(1/k)) has funding<=C+k and therefore matches
the asymptotic lower coefficient sqrt(k A_k) within this scalar class.
For k=2, this says kappa>=(16(M-1)/27)^(1/4), matching the fourth-root
upper's leading coefficient(16/27)^(1/4). This does NOT show that coefficient
is optimal among arbitrary owner-dependent or vector-dependent mechanisms.

1. NECESSARY SCALAR ELASTICITY.
Fix r>r0, P=1. One branch has first input epsilon and all other inputs2.
Distribute the remaining G=1/r-epsilon equally among M-1 branches, giving
each of their k inputs u=(1/r-epsilon)/(M-1)<1/L. This is possible for every
sufficiently small positive epsilon. Let S own all stocks in these M-1
branches and only the first input of the epsilon branch, and no P.
Its payment is f(r)Z with Z=kG-(k-1)epsilon. Restore that first input by a
small amount while outsiders remain fixed. G'=1, Z'=1, r'=-r/G, so TCWM at
every differentiability point of f gives
 f-r f'[k-(k-1)epsilon/G]>=0.
Let epsilon decrease to0 at this fixed r. Thus f-k r f'>=0 almost everywhere,
and absolute continuity gives
       f(r0)>=f(R)(r0/R)^(1/k) for every R>r0.             (2)

2. THE CAP PARAMETER ITSELF COSTS AT LEAST L.
Use one active branch with all k inputs H, take P=1 and let finite H grow
without bound. Then G=1/L and r=L. A coalition holding an equal small amount
delta of P and each branch input has worthdelta. Its total price tends to
h(L), proving h(L)>=1 by coverage.
Next take that branch's first input1/R with R>L, the other inputs sufficiently
large, and every other branch absent. Here g=1/R, F=G=1/R and funding gives
 R h(R)+k f(R)<=kappa.
As R decreases to L, right-continuity of h and nonnegativity imply
                         L<=kappa.                       (3)
The same imbalanced profile, sending the k-1 large inputs to infinity, gives
h(R)+f(R)>=1; hence, for all R>L,
                         f(R)>=1-kappa/R.                (4)

3. CROWDED PROFILE AND OPTIMIZATION.
Take M-1 branches with every input1, P=1 and the remaining branch absent.
Since L>=1, G=(M-1)/L, r=r0, while original production F=1. Funding gives
 kappa>=k(M-1)f(r0)/L.
Use(2)--(4) at R=(k+1)kappa>L, where f(R)>=k/(k+1). Then
 kappa^(1+1/k)>=k A_k [(M-1)/L]^alpha.
Using L<=kappa and 1+1/k+alpha=2 gives(1).

4. MATCHING SUFFICIENT CONSTRUCTION.
For the stated h,f, f-k r f'>=0, h+r h'>=0, h'<=0 and f'>=0. The coalition
proof has Z<=kG and Z'>=G' on an inner-input restoration; on P restoration
G'=t/C and P r'<=r. Thus the previous finite-restoration TCWM proof applies
with k in place of2. If h<1, G<P/C and no branch is capped, so h+f>=1 covers
every supported activity. The scalar maximum of(1-1/x)/x^(1/k) is A_k at
x=k+1. Total payment is Ph+kGf, F>=min(P,G), and r>=C/M. Its funding factor
is at most max(C+k,1+k A_k M^alpha/C). The chosen C proves<=C+k.
The separate composition-lane general theorem supplies the full finite and
zero-boundary construction proof; this note's main new role is necessity.

SCOPE.
No continuity of arbitrary mechanisms is inferred. This is a structural
stopping theorem for this explicitly regular common-scalar price class.
It does not resolve the unrestricted leading-constant gap, and historical
novelty remains unconfirmed.
