17 October2026. Accepted architecture theorem; unrestricted frontier unchanged.

THEOREM
Fix r,s>=2. On the single technology F=min(x1,...,xr), with n actual owners
all eligible for all rows, allow arbitrary nonnegative report-independent
weights w_(n,i,R) on whole-owner marginal contributions
 y_i= sum_(R subset N\{i}) w_(n,i,R)[F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)].
Weights may depend on owner labels, n and public technology. If every coalition
is covered, coalitions of size at most s satisfy TCWM, and funding is bounded
by kappa_n F at all profiles, then
 liminf_(n->infinity) kappa_n >= s^(r-1)/r.
The accepted scaled-binomial rule achieves r*s^(r-1) on all arity-at-most-r
packing technologies, even with full OUT for protected coalitions. Therefore
this fixed positive marginal-weight class has sharp ORDER Theta_r(s^(r-1));
constants differ by r^2. At arity3, the class cannot improve the quadratic
s-exponent. These are scaled marginal rules, not necessarily normalized
semivalues preserving additive payoffs.

WHY THE LOWER WORKS
A fixed positive Bernoulli mixture has an exact threshold-star restoration
increment epsilon(1-p)^(s-2)(1-sp)Pr[Bin(k,p)>=t+1]. It includes unchanged
protected leaf owners. Protected pairs exclude an atom at1; increasing finite
threshold witnesses force measure support inside[0,1/s]. This argument uses
actual report comparisons, not an unsupported coefficient-sign necessity.

For general fixed weights, average over owner-label permutations in the same
fully eligible domain, obtaining nonnegative cardinality weights. A balanced
profile bounds their total mass by kappa_n. On a fixed active profile padded
with zero reports, uniform-cardinality sampling differs from Bernoulli sampling
at the corresponding fraction by at most the usual sampling-collision bound.
A bounded-mass weak limit therefore inherits every finite threshold comparison.
Pure r-owner coverage gives r integral p^(r-1)>=1, which forces the stated mass
and funding lower. Only the LIMIT measure has the support restriction; the
finite-n weights need not be Bernoulli mixtures or have that support.
