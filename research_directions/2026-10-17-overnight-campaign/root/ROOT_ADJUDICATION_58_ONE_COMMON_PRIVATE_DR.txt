RESULT
Let F(P,c) be finite continuous nonnegative monotone concave degree-one on the
nonnegative orthant, zero when P=0 or c=0, and fully DR in the private block c.
There is one aggregate-resource price field giving coalition coverage, TCWM
on all profiles of arbitrary fixed mixed owners, and funding<=3F/2. Private
resource count and owner count are unbounded. Sharpness is the supremum over
growing finite owner populations via the previously audited min2 lower.
No full outside-production guarantee is claimed.

ROOT PROOF CHECK
For smooth F, average grad F(tP,sc) over independent uniform t,s. The exact
block charges are H_P=integral F(P,sc)ds and H_c=integral F(tP,c)dt. Root
verified both integration identities, supporting-price coverage and
H_P+H_c<=F(P,c/2)+F(P/2,c)<=3F(P,c)/2.
Euler differentiation with private DR gives F_{Pc_j}>=0. Averaged private
prices therefore increase in P and decrease in c. With outsiders fixed,
coalition private payment is H_c-o_c.q_c; common payment is(a_P/P)H_P.
Private restorations increase both expressions; common restorations increase
each private price and the common share-times-total. Composition handles
all simultaneous mixed restorations, including newly activated rows.
For continuous nonsmooth F, diagonal log convolution preserves every stated
hypothesis and gives e^-epsilon F<=F_epsilon<=e^epsilon F. For0<epsilon<=1
use the fixed product bounds0<=q_epsilon,j(C)<=1.5eF(C)/C_j on active rows.
One convergent all-profile subnet, not unrelated profilewise subsequences,
preserves each finite coverage/funding/incentive inequality. Zero reports
and faces are handled by zero prices on absent coordinates. Root checked
the limiting quantifiers and finite-dependency argument.

EXPLICIT COMBINATORIAL SUBCLASS
F_r(P,c)=min_S[P r(S)+c(E\S)] for arbitrary public normalized monotone
submodular r. Root checked both uncrossing inequalities: the maximal minimizer
increases with c and decreases with P. Its private0/1 dual coordinates have
the needed signs. The lower envelope of lines zP r(S)+c(E\S), averaged with
CDF z/2 below1 and1-1/(2z) above1, gives exact finite prices. Rational data
give rational prices; enumeration may be exponential. This avoids compactness
for the polymatroid subclass and does not assert an efficient oracle algorithm.

PRIORITY AND STOPPING BOUNDARY
Strong Group Monotonicity is an established scalar-demand counterpart of
TCWM. Radial Aumann–Shapley efficiency/DR antitonicity and polymatroid
truncation/uncrossing are established ingredients. The proposed contribution
is the precise common/private all-profile construction and funding theorem.
For two common rows, Euler controls only the weighted sum of cross effects;
the separate three-block counterexample shows the obvious averaging extension
fails. This theorem does not solve that genuinely heterogeneous two-common gap.
