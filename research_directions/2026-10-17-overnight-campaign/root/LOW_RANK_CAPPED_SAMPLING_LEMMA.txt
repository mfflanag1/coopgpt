LOW-RANK CAPPED SAMPLING VIA A STOCHASTIC-MATRIX TRACE
17 October2026. Root proof candidate, independent audit pending.

LEMMA. Let L be a fixed D-dimensional linear subspace of R^n. For each subset
B of [n], choose a vector a(B) in L intersect R_+^n such that
sum_{j in B}a_j(B)<=G, where G>0 is fixed. No optimality or independence of the
selected vector from B is assumed. Let B retain each coordinate independently
with probability p=1-q, 0<p<1. Then

 E sum_{i notin B}min{a_i(B),G} <= 2D q/p G.             (1)

Proof. The own-bit conditioning identity gives
 E sum_{i notin B}min{a_i(B)/G,1}
 =q/p E sum_{i in B}min{a_i(B\{i})/G,1}.               (2)
For any fixed B and i in B, let v^i=a(B\{i}) restricted to B. Its entries
are nonnegative and the sum of entries other than i is at most G. Thus
 min{v_i^i/G,1}<=2v_i^i/(G+v_i^i)<=2v_i^i/sum_{j in B}v_j^i.
If the last denominator is zero, all its entries are zero and the ratio is
set to zero. Form the |B| by |B| matrix V whose i-th column is v^i divided
by that column sum, or the zero column. It is nonnegative and column-
substochastic. Its spectral radius is at most1. All columns belong to the
restriction of L to B, so rank(V)<=D. The trace is the sum of eigenvalues,
and at most rank(V) eigenvalues are nonzero; therefore
 sum_{i in B}v_i^i/sum_Bv^i=trace(V)<=rank(V)<=D.
Combining with(2) proves(1). This is exact linear algebra; diagonalizability
is not needed. Algebraic multiplicity of zero is at least nullity.

PRODUCTION CONSEQUENCE.
At a fixed profile, fi=F(bi), S=sumfi, G=F(C)-S. For a finite global support
row w(B) selected at C_B, set a_i(B)=w(B) dot bi-fi. Every entry is
nonnegative, training sum is w_game(B)<=G, and all these vectors lie in the
linear span generated by the d resource columns and the standalone vector f.
Thus D<=d+1. The modified-cap mechanism's expected closed excess is at most
2(d+1)q/(1-q)G. Its open-excess endpoint integral is at mostG. Averaging over
q in[0,a] gives total payments at most
 S+[1+2(d+1)(-a-log(1-a))]/a *G,
and choosing a=1/(1+sqrt(d+1)) gives S+[1+2sqrt(d+1)]G.
Combined with the gain-only owner bound and grand-marginal rank lemma, a
public fixed choice of whole rules yields
 S+min{d,n sqrt(2/(n+1)),1+2sqrt(d+1)}G.

GENERAL F AND EXTENDED SUPPORTS.
For finite monotone concave homogeneous F, the mechanism uses supports on
the active face of C_B, extended as infinity outside that face. At this FIXED
profile there are finitely many masks and owner bundles. Replace every
infinite off-face coefficient by one common sufficiently large finite M.
Choose M so every resulting residual a_i(B)>=0 and every outside-face owner
has a_i(B)>=G. Such M exists: an outside-face bundle has a strictly positive
sum of off-face coordinates; inside-face residuals are already nonnegative.
The capped payments match the extended supports EXACTLY, while selected
training sums remain w_game(B), since retained bundles have zero coordinates
outside the active face. All resulting vectors lie in the same augmented
resource/standalone span of dimension<=d+1, so the lemma applies unchanged.
M is an analytical surrogate at a fixed profile, not part of the mechanism
or a report-dependent incentive choice. G=0 is treated separately: the
modified-cap rule pays each fi exactly. Thus boundary downward discontinuities
do not require an unjustified global finite-support approximation.

STATUS.
This proof was derived independently after the earlier reciprocal-coordinate
route stalled. The capped scalar inequality, Bernoulli conditioning, and
stochastic-matrix rank/trace estimate are established elementary ingredients.
Novelty of their combination and the economic consequence is unconfirmed.
The result strengthens the funding denominator to gains from pooling; it does
not improve the original worst-case sqrt-n or sqrt-d total-value orders, and
it protects unilateral withholding rather than coordinated coalition reports.
