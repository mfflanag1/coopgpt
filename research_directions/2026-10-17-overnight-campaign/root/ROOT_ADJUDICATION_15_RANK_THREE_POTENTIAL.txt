ROOT ADJUDICATION15 — RANK-THREE SEPARABLE-POTENTIAL OBSTRUCTION
17 October2026. Internally accepted scoped impossibility, no priority claim.

For any fixed convex h twice continuously differentiable near1/3 with h'>0
and finite h''>0 there, the resource-price rule minimizing sum c_v h(p_v)
over a three-uniform cover system fails individual own-report monotonicity
on some finite instance with two fixed mixed owners. Root checked the exact
six-resource Huber witness and the general kernel family: kerT is one-dimensional,
T has n-1 independent rows, prices move along1/3+theta z, and the IFT derivative
is -[h'/h'']z_j/sum c_v z_v^2. The fixed-owner payment derivative is negative
for a large even level, then sufficiently small positive edge weight, then a
sufficiently small legal report increase. Continuity of the unique multipliers
preserves positivity; convexity plus local strictness proves global uniqueness.

Fable015712 independently rederived all these steps and found no defect. Root
ran the exact Fraction checker: low/high KKT identities and payment gain1/105,
plus four increasing kernel levels, all passed. The analytic local-branch proof
is the evidence for general h; the checker only corroborates specific algebra.

Also accepted: a nonnegative linear resource lift into an ordinary unit graph
cannot approximate min(c1,c2,c3) within ANY finite factor on all profiles. Each
productive lifted edge can be activated using at most two original resources,
contradicting the target's zero values on all two-resource support faces.
This is an architecture boundary. Nonsmooth or graph-dependent potentials,
owner-aware rules, nonlinear lifts and unrestricted rank-three mechanisms are
not excluded. No claim is made about omitted h hypotheses beyond this theorem.
