PARTIAL WITHHOLDING WITH OUTSIDE PRODUCTION IS A STRONGER CONTRACT
17 October 2026. Root exact scope check; independent audit pending.

Current accepted graph theorem assumes payment-only utility. Its coalition-total
withholding monotonicity compares only payments under two binding reports of
the SAME fixed actual owners. It does not value resources withheld from the
mechanism as an additional production opportunity.

An economically stronger condition would require, for every coalition S and
componentwise withheld bundles d_i <= b_i,
  sum_{i in S} y_i(b) >= sum_{i in S} y_i(b-d_S) + F(sum_{i in S} d_i).
This allows S to operate its withheld bundle separately, besides keeping its
mechanism payments. It differs also from allowing arbitrary reallocation of
the mechanism's physical output: here all mechanism compensation is money.

EXACT FAILURE OF THE ACCEPTED HUBER RULE UNDER THIS STRONGER CONTRACT
One unit-profit pair activity F(A,B)=min(A,B), two fixed actual owners.
Other owner's bundle: (2,0). Focal owner's true bundle: (1,2).
Aggregate (3,2) gives Huber prices (1/3,2/3).
Focal full-report payment is 1/3 + 2*(2/3) = 5/3.
The same owner reports (0,1), leaving aggregate (2,1) with prices (1/4,3/4).
Its mechanism payment becomes 3/4. Its withheld bundle (1,1) can produce1.
Combined return becomes7/4, exceeding5/3 by1/12.
All reports are binding and owner identities/eligibility stay fixed.
The payment-only inequality remains valid:5/3 >=3/4.
Thus this is a scope obstruction, not a gap in the accepted graph theorem.

PRIOR-LITERATURE CONNECTION
Emre Dogan, 'Absence-proofness: A new cooperative stability concept',
11 September2013 original working paper, Definition2.1, printed p6:
https://econ.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/seminars/201402/absence-proofness.pdf
For T subset K subset S, its TU definition requires
  sum_{i in K\T} X_i(S\T) + v(T) <= sum_{i in K} X_i(S).
The argument permits a subgroup to remain outside, generate its own worth,
and share proceeds with the manipulating coalition. The paper assumes efficient
allocation schemes and population-varying subgames. Our contract has subsidies,
fixed owner identities, continuous bundle underreports and retained payment
eligibility, so neither theorem can be substituted for the other without proof.
Its zero-outside-worth examples are especially close to our lower-bound setup.
The concept of coalitions paying some members to stay out is established; do
not claim that strategic idea itself as new.

Same author, Essays in Cooperative Stability, Rice dissertation, February2014,
Chapter1, is freely available and locally inspected:
https://repository.rice.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/81bb6a86-77b8-4b22-90d9-679e799ca54c/content
The dissertation is a related original, not an independent second discovery.

POSSIBLE SUCCESSOR TARGET
Bound the extra funding needed for partial withholding plus outside production,
first for unit pair activities and fixed mixed owners. Any result must distinguish
individual from coalition deviations and state whether withheld resources can
be reused simultaneously with resources already dispatched by the mechanism.
No new general bound or impossibility is established in this note.
