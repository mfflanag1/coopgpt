BOUNDED ADVERSARIAL QUESTION: LOW-DIMENSION NONNEGATIVE LINEAR LOSSES
17 October2026. Open question, NOT accepted theorem.

Let A be a fixed finite nonempty set of vectors a in R_+^n, all lying in a
linear subspace of dimension D. Let G=min_{a in A}sum_i a_i>0. Retain each
owner independently with probability p=1-q. Given retained subset B, choose
with fixed report-independent priority a(B) minimizing sum_{i in B}a_i.
Define C(q)=E sum_{i notin B}min(a_i(B),G).

Question: is C(q)<=c D q/(1-q) G for an absolute constant c (especially2)?
Find either a proof or explicit finite rational counterexample. If false for
arbitrary low-rank nonnegative A, determine whether the following geometric
production subclass avoids it: a_i(w)=w dot b_i-F(b_i), with b_i in R_+^d,
F(x)=min_{w in V}w dot x for fixed V subset R_+^d, and D<=d+1. Note every a_i
is nonnegative, and the minimizer is the original supporting row at C_B.

Do NOT replace rank by nonnegative rank. Do not assume the selected minimizer
is independent of sampled coordinates. Root numerical scouts found no violation
for random production profiles, but are not proofs. The selected training total

sum_{i in B}[w(B)-w(B\i)]<=d w(B). It does not itself bound selected slopes.
