VALUE-SENSITIVE CAPPED SAMPLING AND AN IMPROVED DIMENSION CONSTANT
17 October 2026. Root candidate for independent audit; no novelty claim.

SETUP
Use the already proved gain-only fixed-mask construction. At a fixed profile
let w(B)=F(b_B)-sum_(i in B)f_i, G=w(N), S=sum_i f_i.
The residual game w is monotone, 0<=w(B)<=G. Select supports excluding closed
owners exactly as in the cone theorem. Let g(q)=E w(B) with independent
retention p=1-q. Open excess expectation is -p*g'(q). Closed excess C(q)
is a capped selected nonnegative vector, min{a_i(B),G} on i outside B.
For each mask B a(B) lies in a fixed D-dimensional linear subspace, is
nonnegative, and sum_(j in B)a_j(B)=w(B). The finite-face surrogate proves
this exactly for all cone profiles, with D<=d+1. It is used only to analyze
funding; the actual extended-support mechanism remains unchanged.

REFINED LEMMA
For q in(0,1),
                 C(q)<=D*q/p*[G+g(q)].                    (1)
Own-bit conditioning gives C(q)=q/p E_B sum_(i in B)min{a_i(B\i),G}.
Fix B. For each i in B put x_i=a_i(B\i), h_i=sum_(j in B\i)a_j(B\i)
=w(B\i)<=w(B). For any x,h>=0,
                 min{x,G}<=(G+h)*x/(x+h),                  (2)
where the zero denominator has value0. This follows separately for x<=G
and x>=G; when G=0 all capped losses are0 and can be excluded first.
Normalize each nonzero column a(B\i)|_B to sum1. These columns form a
nonnegative column-substochastic matrix V of rank at most D. Its trace is
at most D: spectral radius<=1, number of nonzero eigenvalues<=rank, and
trace<=sum of eigenvalue absolute values. Summing(2) gives
 sum_(i in B)min{x_i,G}<=[G+w(B)]trace(V)<=[G+w(B)]D.
Average to obtain(1). This strengthens the earlier 2D*q/p*G estimate.

PSD SPECIALIZATION
For F(X)=lambda_min(X), residual Ui=Xi-fi I gives w(B)=lambda_min(U_B).
Use the minimum-eigenvector selector of U_(B\i), so h_i=w(B\i).
The diagonal ratio x_i/(x_i+h_i) is at most the spectral leverage
Tr(U_B^(dagger/2) U_i U_B^(dagger/2)). These sum to rank(U_B)<=m.
Thus(1) holds with D=m even though the linear span may have dimension~m².
The actual all-profile mechanism is unchanged, so individual OUT and coverage
continue to hold. Noncommuting and singular matrices are included.

FIXED DENSITY
For a positive integer D define
 h_D(q)=(1-q)^(-D-1)*exp[-D*q/(1-q)], 0<=q<1,
 Z_D=integral_0^1 h_D(q)dq, omega_D=h_D/Z_D.
This density is positive, finite-integral, report independent. Average the
already valid fixed-mask rules against it; the resulting deterministic
finite-mask expectation preserves every incentive and coverage inequality.
No evaluation efficiency is claimed. The only report dependence remains
inside the original mask mechanisms.

Since h'_D/h_D=1/p-D*q/p², we have
                 (p*h_D)'=-D*q/p*h_D.                    (3)
By(1), the total excess R(q)=C(q)-p*g'(q) obeys, after integration by parts,
 integral h_D R <= D G integral(q/p)h_D
                  -[p*h_D*g]_0^1
                  +integral[(p*h_D)'+D*q/p*h_D]g
                =G[1+D integral(q/p)h_D].                (4)
Here g is a finite polynomial, g(0)=G, and p*h_D tends to0 at q=1; all
integrals converge by the exponential decay. Equation(3) cancels the last
integrand exactly. The endpoint at0 contributes G because h_D(0)=1.

Put t=q/p. Then
 Z_D=integral_0^infinity (1+t)^(D-1)*exp(-Dt)dt,
 D integral_0^1(q/p)h_D(q)dq
   =D integral_0^infinity t(1+t)^(D-1)*exp(-Dt)dt=1,
where the last equality is minus the integral of the derivative of
(1+t)^D exp(-Dt), whose endpoints are1 and0. Thus
                 total payments <=S+K_D G,
                 K_D=2/Z_D.                              (5)
For integer D the normalizing constant has the exact rational formula
 Z_D=sum_(k=0)^(D-1) binom(D-1,k)*k!/D^(k+1).
The density and individual mask weights need not be rational, even though
K_D is rational. Do not claim exact rational mechanism evaluation from(5).

ASYMPTOTICS
The change t=s/sqrt(D) gives
 sqrt(D) Z_D=integral_0^infinity
 exp[(D-1)log(1+s/sqrt(D))-s sqrt(D)]ds ->sqrt(pi/2).
For a rigorous domination split t<=1 and t>=1. On t<=1,
log(1+t)<=t-t²/4, so integrand before substitution is at most
exp[-t-(D-1)t²/4]; for D>=2 this gives exp(-s²/8).
On t>=1 use log(1+t)<=t*log2 (the ratio log(1+t)/t decreases), yielding
a tail at most exp[-D(1-log2)]/[D(1-log2)] before multiplication bysqrt(D).
Pointwise Taylor expansion plus dominated convergence proves
                  K_D~sqrt(8D/pi).
No additive O(1) rate is asserted without a further uniform expansion.

CONSEQUENCES
For a closed pointed resource cone of dimension d and n fixed owners,
choose among entire report-independent mechanisms before reports to obtain
 S+min{d,n*sqrt(2/(n+1)),K_(d+1)}G.
For PSD_m with minimum-eigenvalue production replace the dimension terms by
 min{m,K_m}; hence S+min{m,n*sqrt(2/(n+1)),K_m}G.
Both protect individual outside production and cover every coalition on all
profiles. The improved asymptotic dimension coefficient is sqrt(8/pi),
compared with2 in the preceding general sampler. This is not the unresolved
original owner-count leading-constant gap, and no global optimality is claimed.
