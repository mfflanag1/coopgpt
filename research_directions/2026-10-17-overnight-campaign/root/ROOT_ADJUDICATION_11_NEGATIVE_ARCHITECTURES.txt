ROOT ADJUDICATION 11 — SHAPLEY FAILURE AND BINARY-MINIMUM CLASSIFICATION
17 October2026. Internal adjudication, not historical-priority confirmation.

Accepted: square-root Shapley payments fail coalition coverage by an unbounded
factor on an explicit concave homogeneous production family with fixed actual
owners. Root rederived coalition values, beta integral, exact43-owner witness
and growing-family estimate; the exact checker passed. Fable013353 independently
recomputed these steps. This rejects that payment architecture, not unrestricted
truthful mechanisms: the family has a veto player and admits efficient payment.
Motivational own-report monotonicity follows because total value and the owner's
Shapley marginal terms increase when only its report increases; off-owner terms
stay fixed. The undefined projection-envelope remark is ancillary, not used in
the unbounded failure theorem.

Accepted: a unit fractional graph-production function is a finite positive sum
of binary minima of nonnegative linear forms (plus a linear term) exactly when
each connected component is complete multipartite. Root checked the antipodal
matching construction, partial-kink P4/paw witnesses, noncancellation and hereditary
forbidden-subgraph argument; all33867 labeled graphs through six vertices and
four exact hinge witnesses passed the checker. Fable013353 independently checked
the proof. Combining this identity with the separately audited two-input rule
gives sharp1+c OUT for this graph class. Other graph mechanisms remain possible.
No claim of optimality for arbitrary graphs or novelty of this classification.
