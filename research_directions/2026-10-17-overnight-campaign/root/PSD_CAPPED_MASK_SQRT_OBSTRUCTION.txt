A SQUARE-ROOT MATRIX-ORDER BARRIER FOR RETAINED-ONLY CAPPED MASK RULES
17 October2026. Root reconstruction prompted by incomplete Fable result034600.
Candidate for independent audit. Not an unrestricted mechanism lower bound.

EXACT CLASS AND CLAIM
Fix matrix order m>=4 and n=m actual owners. Production is lambda_min on PSD_m.
Consider the existing capped mask mechanism, with a public report-independent
probability law mu on q in[0,1], then independent owner retention with p=1-q.
Its retained owners receive deletion marginals; closed owner i receives
min{Tr(P_B U_i),G} on residual profiles where every lambda_min(U_i)=0 and
G=lambda_min(sum_i U_i). P_B is any BOREL MEASURABLE retained-only choice of
PSD trace-one matrix supported on the minimum eigenspace of U_B. It may use
all retained individual reports, labels and B, but no closed report. This
includes measurable minimum-eigenvector selectors and mixed projectors.
The mechanism may depend on m,n before reports. The same law/selector is used
on all profiles of that fixed problem.

For every such law and selector,
 sup_profiles (expected total payment)/G >= sqrt(7m/80)-7/10.       (1)
Thus the best achievable order WITHIN THIS CLASS is Theta(sqrt(m)), using
the previously proved K_m~sqrt(8m/pi) upper. The lower does not hold separately
for every fixed n independent of m; its witness regime is n=m. Nor is mu an
arbitrary correlated subset law: it is a mixture of iid Bernoulli masks.
Measurability is explicit because the proof averages over rotations. No
claim is made for arbitrary nonmeasurable selectors or all truthful mechanisms.

1. A FULLY SPECIFIED ALMOST-DEPENDENT FRAME
Let epsilon=1/10, w_i=e_i for1<=i<m, and
 w_m=e_1+...+e_(m-1)+epsilon e_m.
Put U_i=w_i w_i^T. All are rank-one PSD and have zero standalone value.
The vectors are independent. On the span of the normalized all-ones vector
in the first m-1 coordinates and e_m, U=sum_i U_i has matrix
 [[m,epsilon sqrt(m-1)],[epsilon sqrt(m-1),epsilon^2]].
Its determinant is epsilon^2 and its larger eigenvalue is at least m.
The other m-2 eigenvalues are1. Therefore
 0<G=lambda_min(U)<=epsilon^2/m=1/(100m).                       (2)
Rotate the entire fixed-owner profile by Q in O(m), chosen Haar-uniformly.
Its G is unchanged. The mechanism does not choose the rotation; the averaging
argument selects an adverse profile from this one family.

2. CONDITIONAL ROTATIONAL GEOMETRY
Fix a proper retained set B, and let l=m-|B|>=1. The retained vectors are
independent, so K_B=ker(U_B) has dimension l. Condition on their ORIENTED
images Qw_i, i in B. This reveals at least as much as the retained PSD reports.
Conditional on these images, Q is Haar on the stabilizer acting orthogonally
on K_B. In particular every fixed projected missing vector has uniform
orientation in this kernel, at its deterministic projection length.
The selector P_B is fixed under this conditioning, since it reads only the
retained reports. Its support lies in K_B.

There are at least(l-1)^+ missing vectors whose projection lengths are >=1/sqrt2:
- If m is missing, each other missing e_i is orthogonal to retained standard
  basis vectors and has projection length1; there are l-1 such vectors.
- If m is retained and l>=2, the l missing standard basis directions and e_m
  form an l+1 dimensional space. The remaining retained constraint is the
  vector (1,...,1,epsilon). For each missing e_i its squared projection is
  1-1/(l+epsilon^2)>=1/2. There are l such eligible vectors.
- For l=1 the claimed count is0 and no estimate is needed.
This handles every B, including the empty mask and one-dimensional kernels.

3. AN ELEMENTARY SMALL-BALL BOUND
For a uniform unit vector V on S^(l-1), and0<=delta<=1/2,
 P(|V_1|<delta)<=2delta sqrt(l).                              (3)
For l=1 it is zero. For l=2 use the arcsine density, bounded on[-1/2,1/2].
For l>=3 the density is maximized at0 and equals
 Gamma(l/2)/(sqrt(pi)Gamma((l-1)/2))<=sqrt((l-1)/(2pi))<sqrt(l).
The gamma-ratio inequality follows from Jensen/Cauchy-Schwarz applied to
sqrt(Y) for a Gamma((l-1)/2,1) variable. Integration over length2delta proves(3).

For any unit selected direction v in K_B and eligible vector of length a>=1/sqrt2,
 delta=sqrt(G)/a<=sqrt(2G)<=1/2. Equations(2),(3) imply
 P(|v dot Qw_i|^2<G)<=2sqrt(2lG)<=2sqrt(1/50)<3/10.
Consequently its conditional expected capped payment is at least(7/10)G.
For a mixed projector P=sum alpha_j v_jv_j^T, concavity of min{x,G} gives
 min{Tr(PU_i),G}>=sum alpha_j min{v_j^T U_i v_j,G}; the same lower follows.
No independence between different missing vectors is required.

Summing and averaging masks gives
 E_Q C(q,Q)>= (7/10)G E_B[(m-|B|-1)^+]
             >=(7/10)G(mq-1).
Hence, with delta_bar=integral q dmu,
 sup_Q total(Q)/G >= (7/10)(m delta_bar-1).                    (4)
All quantities are bounded and measurable, so conditional expectation and
Fubini are valid. The assertion is a supremum bound, not a supplied explicit Q.

4. A SECOND FAMILY CONTROLS VERY SMALL MISSING PROBABILITY
For any integer2<=J<=m, use the SAME n=m owners and set
 U_i=e_i e_i^T for i<J,
 U_J=I-sum_(i<J)e_ie_i^T,
 U_i=0 for J<i<=m.
All standalone values are zero, U=I and G=1. A retained sum has positive
minimum eigenvalue iff all J nonzero owners are retained. In that case each
of those owners has deletion marginal1. Thus open payout alone is
                 J integral(1-q)^J dmu(q).                   (5)
By Jensen and Bernoulli's inequality, this is at least J(1-J delta_bar)
whenever J delta_bar<=1. Null owners are fixed actual owners throughout.

5. MINIMAX OVER THE FIXED MIXING LAW
Write delta=delta_bar.
If delta<=1/(2m), J=m in(5) gives at least m/2, which exceeds(1).
If delta>=1/4, (4) gives7m/40-7/10, at least(1) for m>=4.
Otherwise choose J=floor(1/(2delta)). Then2<=J<=m, Jdelta<=1/2,
and J>=1/(4delta). Equation(5) gives at least1/(8delta).
Together with(4), the worst profile therefore costs at least
 max{1/(8delta),(7/10)m delta-7/10}
 >=sqrt(7m/80)-7/10.
This proves(1). The two adverse profiles may differ; a single fixed rule must
handle both. No choice of q after seeing the profile is permitted.

6. TWO PRECISE SHORTCUT FAILURES
The incomplete delegate asserted closed loss<=(kappa-1)G when U has condition
number kappa. This is false: U1=diag(1,0),U2=diag(0,1), U=I,G=kappa=1 and
empty retained mask with selector e1 gives closed loss1, not0. The immediate
valid bound is kappa*G-lambda_min(U_B).
Replacing closed support payments by retained-relative marginals destroys
fixed-mask coverage: in m3 take Ui=e_ie_i^T and B={1}. Every open deletion
marginal and every closed addition marginal is0, while grand production is1.
An averaged replacement might need a new coverage proof and funding factor;
it is not automatically another valid mask mechanism.

PROVENANCE AND REMAINING GAP
The Fable research run suggested a rotated-frame obstruction and junta minimax,
but its delivered result omitted the frame, lemmas and core proof; opposite-model
QA marked it insufficient. This note supplies an explicit frame, conservative
constants, Borel restriction, same n=m in both families, and a full argument.
The spectral, Haar, spherical, and Jensen ingredients are established. Priority
of this architecture application is unconfirmed. The all-mechanism PSD gap
between the diagonal log(m)/loglog(m) lower and sqrt(m) upper remains OPEN.
A real improvement must leave at least one of the capped retained-only mask
conditions, or exploit a restricted matrix family. Matrix concentration cannot
improve this class's worst-case order, if the present proof survives audit.
