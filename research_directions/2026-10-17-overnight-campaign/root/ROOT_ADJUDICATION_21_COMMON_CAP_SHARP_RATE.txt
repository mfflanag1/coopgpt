CLAIM AND SCOPE.
For F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_j min(a_j,b_j)) with M branches, fixed actual owners,
nonnegative payments, coalition coverage and weak total-coalition report
monotonicity (TCWM), the optimal funding factor uniformly over finite owner
models has order Theta(M^(1/4)). The lower has pure owners; the upper permits
arbitrary fixed mixed owners. This is not an upper bound on all rank-three LPs.
