ACCEPTED CONSEQUENCES
For min2 production with fixed actual owners and binding nonnegative reports,
the sharp total-funding constants3/2 for TCWM and1+c for full OUT remain
achievable while paying every game-null owner zero. Here a game-null owner's
marginal is zero to EVERY predecessor coalition, not only the grand coalition.
Write f_i=min(a_i,b_i), u_i=(a_i-b_i)^+, v_i=(b_i-a_i)^+. The payment is
 kappa*f_i + pi_i(u,v),
where pi is the corresponding accepted binary rule. The coefficient kappa,
not1, compensates residual decreases under an actual owner's restoration.
The accepted signed-L1 theorem proves the same-owner all-profile transfer.
A pure-A nonzero owner is null exactly when every other owner is A-heavy;
then the opposite residual aggregate is zero and its payment is zero.

For finite monotone games, finite nonnegative game-null-zero rules are exactly
the rules representable pointwise by report-dependent nonnegative whole-owner
marginal weights. Choose a positive marginal and divide the intended payment
by it. No normalization, coefficient bound, continuity or efficient evaluation
is obtained. The sharp min2 construction therefore escapes root64's fixed-
weight growing barrier. It does not improve the already accepted min2 bounds.

For any min_r, r>=2, aggregate-only linear resource prices cannot satisfy
coverage, game-null-zero and individual report monotonicity even with TWO
fixed fully eligible owners. At totals(1/2,1,...,1), a dummy decomposition
forces the sum of the long-row prices to zero and the first price at least1.
At totals(2,1,...,1), another dummy decomposition forces the first price0.
The same first owner restoring(1/2,0,...,0) to(2,0,...,0) while its fixed
partner holds(0,1,...,1) then loses payment. This uses singleton monotonicity;
grand-total monotonicity alone does not suffice. Label-specific aggregate
prices are also excluded by finite label averaging. No funding premise or
sign assumption on individual prices is needed. Thus the old aggregate-price
reduction cannot be augmented with this extra dummy axiom.

MIN3 REMAINS OPEN
Simple standalone netting fails to remove all min3 game dummies. The precise
face-value rule is cited and its positive dummy payment is directly derived.
No owner-uniform min3 dummy-zero bound or impossibility has been proved. For
each fixed n, the accepted binomial rule already gives finite funding, so an
unbudgeted fixed-n infeasibility would be false. The unrestricted arity-three
coalition-size interval and original individual owner/dimension gap are unchanged.
