GENERAL ARITY.
For F=min(P,sum_(j<=M)min_(l<=k)x_jl), fixed k>=2, the uniform-over-fixed-owner
optimal funding order for nonnegative coalition coverage and TCWM is
Theta_k(M^((k-1)/(2k))). The exact lower product is
Q=M! Gamma(1+1/k)/Gamma(M+1/k), giving kappa>=QR/(R^2+Q).

independently checked the same analytical derivations without substantive
findings. Fixed-k asymptotics are not a uniform statement for growing k.

FULL OUTSIDE PRODUCTION.
The stronger contract requires, for every fixed actual coalition, fixed
outsiders and coordinatewise restoration d, cash gain at least F(d).
The explicit rule g_j=min(P/C,x_j1,...,x_jk),G=sumg,r=P/G,
h=min(1,C/r),f=min(1,(r/C)^(1/k)) has this property and funding
max(C+k,1+k M^((k-1)/k)/C). Choose C=max(1,sqrt(k)M^((k-1)/(2k))).
Thus full OUT has the same sharp exponent, though its leading constant is open.

Root independently derived the joint-direction proof: for r<C, write
G'=t dP/C+L, Z'>=L,Z<=kG; the private loss cancels since f-k r f_r=0,
leaving coalition derivative>=dP>=F(d). For r>C there is no cap, f=1,
and Z'>=G'>=sum_j min_l d_jl>=F(d), with other derivative terms nonnegative.
Persistent r=C, tied minima, finite switches and zero-support continuity
were checked separately. Coverage follows by restoration from zero.
Root replay passed7000 funding,7000 coverage,8296 fullOUT comparisons and
the exact grand-marginal-floor failure. Fable025851-49d89541 independently
audited the full proof and code, finding no substantive defect. No floor is
inferred from this rule; its one-active-branch counterexample is valid.
