FIVE-FOURTHS LOWER UNDER TRANSFERABLE COALITION-WITHHOLDING INCENTIVES
17 October2026. Simple proof extracted from finite LP discovery. Not reviewed.

Three fixed actual owners, all eligible for both resources A,B; F=min(A,B).
Assume nonnegative payments, all coalition coverage, all-profile factor kappa,
and the following stronger incentive property: every coalition's TOTAL payment
is nondecreasing in any coordinate belonging to one of its members while all
other reports are fixed. This follows from immunity to joint binding capacity
underreports even when coalition members may transfer utility among themselves.
No aggregate-price or linear-payment assumption is imposed.

Take profiles
 b0=((1,0),(0,1),(0,0)),
 bA=((1,0),(0,1),(1,0)),
 bB=((1,0),(0,1),(0,1)).
Each grand production value is1. Write baseline payments x1,x2,x3.
At bA, coalitions{1,2} and{2,3} each have value1. The factor-kappa budget
therefore bounds payment1<=kappa-1 and payment3<=kappa-1. Hence payment of
coalition{1,3} is at most2(kappa-1). Increasing owner3's A report from b0 to bA
and coalition-total monotonicity imply x1+x3<=2(kappa-1).
At bB, symmetric reasoning gives x2+x3<=2(kappa-1).
Baseline grand coverage and x3>=0 give
 1<=x1+x2+x3<=x1+x2+2x3<=4(kappa-1).
Thus kappa>=5/4.

This is a lower bound for the stronger coalition-withholding incentive class,
NOT for all individually truthful mechanisms. The Huber graph rule satisfies
the stronger condition and gives upper3/2; the new class gap is[5/4,3/2].
The simple proof is independent of numerical LP optimality. Historical novelty
and whether a stronger known cooperative incentive result implies it are open.
