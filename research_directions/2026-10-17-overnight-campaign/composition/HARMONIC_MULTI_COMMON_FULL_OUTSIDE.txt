HARMONIC MULTI-COMMON COMPOSITION WITH FULL OUTSIDE PRODUCTION
17 October 2026. Root construction; independent composition proof/checks.
Frozen candidate. Historical novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL AND RESULT
Use TheoremA's technology in HARMONIC_MULTI_COMMON_TCWM.txt:
 F(P,x)=min(min_{v<=t}P_v/a_v, sum_{j<=M}min_{l<=k}x_jl),
with fixed positive common coefficients a_v, distinct private rows, and arbitrary
fixed mixed actual owners. Define
 gamma_t=(3t+1)/(4t), A_t=1/gamma_t=4t/(3t+1), B_t=t A_t.
Then full coalition outside-production protection is possible with funding at most
 B_t C+max(B_t,k),
 C=max{1,sqrt(k/B_t) M^((k-1)/(2k))}.                        (1)
Thus for fixed t,k this has the same O(M^((k-1)/(2k))) rate as the one-common
result. The proof works directly with actual shared-resource ownership; it does
not apply an invalid additive-owner interpretation to the harmonic aggregate.

1. THE RULE
For positive shared capacities let
 Q=(sum_v a_v/P_v)^(-1),
 g_j=min(Q/C,x_j1,...,x_jk), G=sum_jg_j, r=Q/G,
 h=min(1,C/r), f=min(1,(r/C)^(1/k)).                         (2)
Use actual resource prices
 p_v=A_t h Q/P_v, p_jl=f g_j/x_jl.                           (3)
All owner payments are their actual bundles dotted with these prices. Zero
private terms are0; if a required common capacity or G is0, pay everyone0.
For a coalition's holdings s<=c write
 theta_v=s_v/P_v, Z_P=Q sum_v theta_v,
 Z=sum_j g_j sum_l s_jl/x_jl,
 Y=A_t h Z_P+f Z.                                           (4)
No participation-floor guarantee is claimed.

2. EXACT HARMONIC COALITION DERIVATIVE LEMMA
Normalize shared coordinates by fixed linear scaling P'_v=P_v/a_v and
s'_v=s_v/a_v. For a coalition restoration d, put d'_v=d_v/a_v and
w_v=Q/P'_v. Then sum_v w_v=1, theta_v=s'_v/P'_v, and
 Q'=sum_v w_v^2 d'_v,
 Z_P'=Q' sum_v theta_v+sum_v w_v d'_v(1-theta_v).              (5)
Every coefficient of d'_v is nonnegative. Let D=min_v d'_v. Replacing all d'_v
by D in(5) decreases it, and minimizing independently over theta_v in[0,1] gives
 Z_P'>=D sum_v min(w_v,S_2), S_2=sum_v w_v^2.
For every simplex vector w of length t,
 sum_v min(w_v,S_2)>=gamma_t=(3t+1)/(4t).                    (6)

Proof of the exact constant. If no weight exceeds S_2, the sum is1. Otherwise let
m be the number exceeding it,1<=m<t, and let q be their sum. Cauchy--Schwarz gives
S_2>=q^2/m+(1-q)^2/(t-m), hence
 sum min=mS_2+1-q
 >=q^2+m(1-q)^2/(t-m)+1-q
 >=(3t+m)/(4t)>=(3t+1)/(4t),                                (7)
where completing the square places the unconstrained minimum at q=(t+m)/(2t).
Equality in(6) occurs for t>=2 at w_1=(t+1)/(2t), w_2=...=w_t=1/(2t).
For t=1 the value is1. Thus the factor A_t<4/3 is sharp for this harmonic
common-block derivative lemma, not asserted sharp among all mechanisms.
Equations(5),(6) imply
 A_t Z_P'>=D.                                               (8)

3. FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION INEQUALITY
Fix one coalition, its outside reports o, its low report s and restored bundle
d>=0. Follow c(u)=o+s+u d,s(u)=s+u d for0<=u<=1. We prove Y'>=F(d) on every
regular interval. Every private/common ownership share in(4) changes with the
same actual owner's restoration.

If r<C, h=1 and f-k r f_r=0. Assign every branch a minimizing common cap or
private row; persistent ties have equal slopes. Let u_cap count cap assignments
and L be the sum of d_jl for privately assigned minimizers. Then
 G'=u_cap Q'/C+L, Z'>=L,
 r'=Q'(G-u_cap Q/C)/G^2-rL/G.
The first term is nonnegative. Since Z<=kG and f_r>=0,
 Y'=A_t Z_P'+f_r r'Z+fZ'
    >=A_t Z_P'+(f-k r f_r)L
    >=D>=F(d).                                             (9)

If r>C, no branch is capped and f=1. Here the common payment has the particularly
simple form
 hZ_P=C G sum_v theta_v.
Its derivative is nonnegative because G'>=0 and theta'_v=d_v(P_v-s_v)/P_v^2>=0.
For each branch, g_j'=min_{l currently minimizing}d_jl and its private coalition
payment derivative satisfies Z_j'>=g_j'. Therefore
 Y'>=Z'>=sum_j min_all_l d_jl>=F(d).                         (10)
The middle inequality follows by selecting one current minimizer: its own-share
and cap-growth derivative terms add to g_j', with all remaining terms nonnegative.

If r=C persists over an interval, h=f=1 are constant along that interval, Z'>=0,
and Y'=A_t Z_P'+Z'>=D>=F(d). Isolated crossings are treated by continuity.

Along the line, Q is rational on positive common support; each cap/minimum and
fixed threshold has finitely many crossings unless identical, giving a finite
piecewise smooth partition. Terms extend continuously at zero private rows.
The funding bound below forces all nonnegative payments to0 at any absent
required common row, and as G tends to0 the total tends to0. If F(d)>0 then
F(c(u))>=u F(d)>0 for u>0, so only the initial endpoint can be zero-valued.
Apply the mean value theorem to Y(u)-uF(d) on positive pieces and pass through
continuous boundaries; if F(d)=0 the same reasoning gives monotonicity. Thus
 Y(s+d;o)-Y(s;o)>=F(d)                                      (11)
for every real profile, coalition and whole-bundle underreport. Coverage follows
by restoring a coalition from zero holdings with outsiders fixed.

4. FUNDING
Total payment is B_t Qh+kGf. We retain Q<=min_v P_v/a_v, F>=min(Q,G) and r>=C/M.
If r<=1, h=1 and F>=Q, so total/F<=B_t+k M^((k-1)/k)/C.
If r>1, F>=G and total/F<=B_t C+k. Hence every C>=1 gives factor
 max{B_t C+k,B_t+k M^((k-1)/k)/C}.                           (12)
Choosing C in(1) gives its displayed simple bound. The asymptotic leading upper
constant is sqrt(B_t k) for fixed t,k>=2; no optimality claim is made for it.
For t=1, A_t=B_t=1 and this recovers the previous common-cap full-OUT mechanism.
For k=1, exact private-pool aggregation into t+1 pools can give a better constant;
this general construction is primarily for complementary private inputs.

5. INTERACTING TYPES, PUBLIC PROFITS AND RATIO APPROXIMATION
The fixed-positive profit layers and weighted type-congestion proof in
HARMONIC_MULTI_COMMON_TCWM.txt preserve full OUT as well when each type uses
this full-OUT rule: the summed gain is at least sum_tau F_tau(d), which is at
least the true interacting game's F(d). The same D=max_v sum_{tau using v}K_tau
bounds funding by the feasible time-sharing argument. This is a direct inequality
in the true withdrawn capacities, not a nonlinear aggregation shortcut.

Consequently the geometric two-common-ratio approximation in
TWO_COMMON_RATIO_CONDITION_BOUND.txt extends to full OUT for every k. With
T nonempty buckets, rho>1, a=(k-1)/(2k), B_2=16/7, a valid explicit factor is
 rho[sqrt(B_2 k) T^(1-a)M^a+(B_2+max(B_2,k))T].              (13)
It follows from K_tau<=sqrt(B_2 k)M_tau^a+B_2+max(B_2,k) and Jensen's inequality.
For k=1, retain the sharper rho(1+2c)T bound already proved there.
No floor transfer is inferred from the rounded technology comparison.

6. STATUS AND VERIFICATION
harmonic_multi_common_full_outside.py returns exact rational-plus-kth-root prices
using an integer public C with B_t^k C^(2k)>=k^k M^(k-1). The exact checker covers
mixed/common/private joint restorations and separately verifies the sharp gamma_t
simplex inequality and its rational equality witness. It raises on unresolved
algebraic comparisons. The proof is analytical; finite tests corroborate it.
Historical novelty, application fit and arbitrary-ratio bounds independent of
condition number remain separate open questions.
FIXED-t,k RATE LOWER BOUND. For fixed t and k>=2 the displayed upper has
the same M exponent as the one-common lower. Give each power owner identical
holdings on all t normalized common rows P_v/a_v, and restrict its reports
to the same diagonal duplication. The technology on this subdomain is
exactly the one-common technology; every embedded whole-bundle underreport
is feasible, and the value of every coalition and withheld bundle is
unchanged. Restricting any full-domain rule to these fixed owners/reports
therefore preserves coverage, TCWM, full OUT and its funding factor. The
one-common Omega_k(M^((k-1)/(2k))) lower applies. Thus the fixed-t,k rate is
Theta_(t,k)(M^((k-1)/(2k))); this does not remove dependence on demand-type
count or on heterogeneous common-resource ratios.
