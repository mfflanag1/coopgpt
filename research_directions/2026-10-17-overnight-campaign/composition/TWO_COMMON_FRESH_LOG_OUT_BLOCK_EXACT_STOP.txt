PUBLIC TECHNOLOGY

 F(P,c)=max sum_j x_j subject to
        0<=x_j<=c_j, x_j<=P, sum_j x_j<=2P.

Equivalently,
 F(P,c)=min(2P, c1+c2+c3, P+c1+c2, P+c1+c3, P+c2+c3).

This is in the accepted one-common/private-DR class. Whenever c3>=c1+c2,
put B=c1+c2 and H=c3. Then, for EVERY nonnegative scaling a,b,

 F(aP,b c)=min(aP,b B)+min(aP,b H).                     (1)

To verify, because H>=B, the displayed minimum defining F becomes
min(2aP,b(B+H),aP+bB), exactly the sum of the two binary minima.
This identity holds along all integration rays, not only at sampled profiles.

Define the nonincreasing function

For 0<t<tau, direct integration gives
 Q(t)=1-gamma-gamma(1-t)log(1-t)/t.                    (2)

On any profile with H>B>P, equation(1) and integration in s give exactly

Indeed the private gradient for rows1,2 is the indicator UP>sB, whose

rely on differentiating a moving chain or on a numerical LP solution.

WHAT THIS STOPS AND WHAT REMAINS OPEN
The sharp binary logarithmic primitive does not transfer routinely by
replacing one scalar input with an arbitrary DR-submodular private block.
The principal partition depends on the current private profile: rows1,2
share one rank increment along the low/high integration rays, but the
restored bundle can use two units of polymatroid rank. Positive scalar
layer reasoning at the current profile therefore undercounts pooled outside
production. The failure is exact and already occurs in three activities.

The accepted sharp3/2 payment-only TCWM theorem is unaffected. This stop does
not prove that a(1+gamma)-funded full-OUT mechanism is impossible for public
polymatroids, nor does it exclude a different compensation rule. A successor
must control pooled outside rank beyond the current principal partition;
continued tuning of this uncorrected block-face rule does not satisfy the gate.
