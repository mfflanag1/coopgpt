EXACT EVALUATION OF THE POLYMATROID3/2 RULE BY A PRINCIPAL PARTITION
17 October2026. Accepted implementation corollary after independent mathematical/code review.
This applies established parametric submodular minimization, not a new SFM algorithm.
Direct original: Nagano--Kawahara, UAI2013, Section5.2 AlgorithmDA,
https://auai.org/uai2013/prints/papers/151.pdf . The transformation is
f=P r, alpha=1/z. See energy/POLYMATROID_ORACLE_SOURCE_GATE.txt for the
exact original-source mapping, maximal-oracle support and attribution.

CONTRACT AND OBJECTIVE
The accepted theorem in TWO_COMMON_FRESH_ONE_COMMON_DR_BLOCK_THEOREM.txt gives
coverage, TCWM and total funding<=3F/2 for arbitrary fixed mixed owners of
 F(P,c)=min_(S subset E)[P r(S)+c(E\S)],
where r is public, normalized, nonnegative, monotone and submodular. Its exact
prices average(r(S),1_(E\S)) along the lower envelope
 L_S(z)=z P r(S)+c(E\S), z>0,
with the ratio-law CDF G(z)=z/2 for0<=z<=1 and G(z)=1-1/(2z) forz>=1.
This note replaces subset enumeration by an exact maximal-submodular-minimizer
oracle. It changes neither the mechanism nor its incentive proof.

ORACLE MODEL
The public rank oracle evaluates rational r(S) exactly. At parameter z an
exact maximal-minimization oracle returns the UNIQUE inclusion-maximal
minimizer of zP r(S)-c(S). The union of all minimizers is a minimizer by the
submodular minimizer lattice. An ordinary exact SFM value oracle can implement
this via one unconstrained minimum and at most|E| constrained minima: j belongs
to the maximal minimizer iff min_(S containing j)f(S)=min_S f(S). Constrained
minimization is SFM on T -> f(T union{j}). The union of the resulting possible
elements is exactly the maximal minimizer. No numeric epsilon tie-breaking
or approximate minimization is used. Original algorithms can return the
maximal minimizer directly; the separate source comparison confirms this.

ZERO FACES AND ENDPOINTS
If P=0, or every private capacity is0, all payments are0. Otherwise restrict
to active private indices J={j:c_j>0}, using the restriction of r to J.
This preserves F: removing inactive elements from any S cannot increase rank
or alter the private intercept, while every restricted set is still feasible.
Set absent-coordinate prices to0. Owners and their eligible rows do not change.

Zero prices on absent coordinates are the convention of the accepted theorem.
They do not assert a full-space supporting gradient at a boundary. Every owner
has zero holding in an absent row, so changing that row's price changes no
payment at that profile. If P=0, private directional derivatives on the active
face vanish; the literal nonzero common derivative, when defined, multiplies
zero common holdings. If all c=0, the reverse statement holds. Thus the zero
payment returned on either block axis equals the accepted mechanism's payment.
When r(J)=0 the production on the active face is identically0, so all charged
prices are0. Coverage and TCWM are statements about payments, not unused prices.
In a cross-face restoration any newly active row is evaluated at its correct
high-profile price; its old holding was0. No cross-face continuity is assumed.

If r(J)=0, F is identically0 on this active face. Otherwise define the loop set
L={j in J:r({j})=0}. Monotonicity and submodularity imply r(L)=0 and every
zero-rank set is contained in L. For z=0, J is the unique minimizer since all
active c_j>0. For all sufficiently large z, L is the unique minimizer: any
positive-rank set loses to L eventually, and among zero-rank sets L has the
smallest intercept. Thus the envelope has endpoints J and L. With positive
capacities a strict positive-interval nested chain has at most|J|+1sets.
The maximal minimizers shrink as z grows, as proved by the theorem's uncrossing
argument (equivalently divide by zP and vary the modular weight1/(zP)).

RECURSIVE BREAKPOINT ALGORITHM
For two known envelope sets A strictly containing B, with r(A)>r(B), put
 z*=[c(A\B)]/[P(r(A)-r(B))].
This is the intersection of L_A and L_B. Query the maximal minimizer M at z*.

If L_M(z*)=L_A(z*)=L_B(z*), record an adjacent-envelope breakpoint z* between
A and B. Intermediate sets that tie at this single point have zero probability
under the continuous ratio law and need not be retained.

If L_M(z*) is strictly smaller, recurse on(A,M) and(M,B). In this case B is a
strict subset of M and M a strict subset of A; their ranks strictly decrease.
Start at(J,L). Return the ordered sets and all recorded breakpoints.

WHY THIS RECOVERS THE WHOLE ENVELOPE
Maintain anchors alpha<beta at which A and B are optimal, respectively;
initially alpha=0 and beta=infinity. Intersection z* lies between the anchors.
If another line is below the two lines at z*, the oracle finds such a line M.
Parametric nesting puts B subset M subset A, and strict improvement makes
both inclusions and rank inequalities strict. The two recursive intersections
lie between their corresponding anchors; M is optimal at the new shared anchor.

If there is no strict improvement at z*, every competing affine line lies
above A at alpha and at z*, hence throughout[alpha,z*]; it lies above B at
z* and beta, hence throughout[z*,beta]. For beta=infinity use its nonnegative
slope relative to the zero-slope L line. Thus no hidden positive-length
segment was skipped. The same affine argument applies recursively. A set
with equal slope and smaller intercept could not coexist with the claimed
optimal anchor, so division by a zero rank difference is never required.

Let k be the number of retained positive-interval sets. Each strict query
introduces one new chain set, and each terminal query joins two adjacent sets.
A binary recursion with k-2 strict nodes has2k-3queries. Since k<=|J|+1,
there are at most2|J|-1maximal-minimizer calls. A direct induction gives the
stronger2|J\L|-1 bound: a strict split partitions A\B into A\M and M\B.
With cached ranks, at most2|J|+1 external rank evaluations suffice, including
normalization, singleton loops, the full set and strict-query new ranks.
This excludes rank evaluations made internally by the SFM backend. A naive
conversion to ordinary exact SFM uses O(|J|^2)calls; a maximal-returning SFM
algorithm yields O(|J|)calls. These are oracle counts, not wall-clock promises.

EXACT PRICES
Write the ordered chain S_0=J,...,S_(k-1)=L and breakpoints
0=z_0<z_1<...<z_(k-1)<z_k=infinity. For each interval define
 w_l=G(z_(l+1))-G(z_l).
Then
 q_P=sum_l w_l r(S_l),
 q_j=sum_l w_l 1_{j notin S_l}.
All weights are nonnegative and sum to1. Rational rank/capacity data produce
rational breakpoints and prices. Ties at isolated breakpoints have zero mass.
On active faces this is exactly the accepted two-block gradient average, so
all-profile coverage, mixed-owner TCWM and3/2funding follow unchanged. It does
not produce the missing full-OUT guarantee.

COMPLEXITY QUALIFIERS
With exact rational ranks of polynomially bounded encoding length and a
polynomial exact SFM implementation, this gives polynomial oracle evaluation
of the prices. Aggregate owner sums and final dot products are additional
straightforward work. No polynomial guarantee is asserted for an arbitrary
rank representation whose oracle itself is expensive, or for approximate or
floating-point SFM. The small executable verification harness uses exhaustive
SFM as a test oracle; that backend is exponential and is NOT an efficient
implementation. The improvement is the general oracle reduction, not the
performance of the finite test harness.
