FULL COALITION OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION PROTECTION AT THE SAME SHARP GROWTH RATE
17 October 2026. Root construction and independent composition-lane derivation.
Frozen candidate for cross-model audit. Historical novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL AND THEOREM
For integers k>=1,M>=1 consider
 F(P,x)=min(P,sum_{j=1}^M min_{1<=l<=k}x_jl).
Actual owners hold arbitrary fixed mixed bundles. At any fixed coalition's low
aggregate report s, fixed outside reports o, and coordinatewise nonnegative
withheld/restored bundle d, full outside-production protection means
 Y(s+d;o)-Y(s;o)>=F(d),                                      (1)
where Y is the coalition's total payment. Thus the coalition cannot improve its
total payoff by underreporting d and producing independently with d. The owners
and the public technology stay fixed; this is not a false-name or ownership-
change statement. Coalition payoffs are transferable totals.

There is a nonnegative all-profile mechanism satisfying(1) and coalition coverage
with funding factor at most
 k+max{1,sqrt(k) M^((k-1)/(2k))}.                             (2)
Consequently, for each fixed k>=2, the optimal uniform-over-finite-owner-model
full-OUT factor is Theta_k(M^((k-1)/(2k))). The unrestricted TCWM lower in
COMMON_CAP_GENERAL_ARITY_RATE.txt applies because full OUT implies TCWM.
The exponent therefore remains the same under this stronger deviation model.
The upper leading constant sqrt(k) is not claimed sharp. No grand-marginal
participation floors are asserted; an explicit failure is stated below.

1. THE RULE
Fix any public C>=1. Let
 g_j=min(P/C,x_j1,...,x_jk), G=sum_jg_j, r=P/G,
 h(r)=min(1,C/r), f(r)=min(1,(r/C)^(1/k)).                    (3)
Use resource prices p_P=h, p_jl=f g_j/x_jl and pay every actual owner its bundle
dotted with these common prices. A zero-coordinate contribution is zero; if
P=0 or G=0 all payments are zero. A coalition with own holdings s<=c=s+o has
 Y=s_P h+f Z,
 Z=sum_j g_j sum_l s_jl/x_jl, 0<=Z<=kG.                      (4)
This differs from the TCWM-only sharp-rate rule by the amplitude of f: here it
reaches1 exactly where h leaves1, at r=C.

2. ONE DIRECTIONAL PROOF OF THE FULL COALITION INEQUALITY
Follow the line s(t)=s+t d, c(t)=s+o+t d,0<=t<=1, with all d coordinates>=0.
On any open interval where P,G>0 the minimum/cap pattern is constant except at
finitely many switches. Persistent ties have identical affine coordinates and
therefore identical slopes. Choose a minimizing row in every tied branch;
any such choice gives the actual derivative on that interval. Write primes for
derivatives with respect to t and d_P for the common-resource increment.

CASE A: r<C.
Here h=1, f=(r/C)^(1/k), f'>=0 and f-k r f'=0, where f' in this expression is
the derivative with respect to r. Let t_cap count branches assigned a common
cap minimizer P/C, and let
 L=sum_{j assigned a private minimizer l_j} d_j,l_j>=0.
Then G'=t_cap d_P/C+L and G>=t_cap P/C. The derivative of a branch contribution
to Z is
 g_j' sum_l s_jl/x_jl + g_j sum_l d_jl(x_jl-s_jl)/x_jl^2.       (5)
For a privately minimized branch, the chosen minimizer satisfies x_j,l_j=g_j
and g_j'=d_j,l_j; its two terms in(5) sum to d_j,l_j. Every other term is
nonnegative. Cap-minimized branches also contribute nonnegative derivatives.
Thus Z'>=L. Moreover
 r'=(G-t_cap P/C)d_P/G^2-rL/G.
The first term is nonnegative. Using Z<=kG,
 Y'=d_P+f_r r' Z+f Z'
    >=d_P+(f-k r f_r)L=d_P>=F(d).                           (6)
This argument works for arbitrary joint restoration directions, rather than
inferring full OUT from individual-coordinate monotonicity.

CASE B: r>C.
Now G<P/C, so no branch is capped; f=1 and h=CG/P. Put theta=s_P/P in[0,1].
Then
 Y'=C theta G'+h(1-theta)d_P+Z'.                             (7)
For each branch, g_j'=min_{l currently minimizing x_jl}d_jl. Formula(5) again
shows Z_j'>=g_j', by choosing one currently minimizing row. Therefore
 G'=sum_j g_j' >=sum_j min_all_l d_jl >=F(d),
 Z'>=G',
and every term in(7) is nonnegative. Hence Y'>=F(d).

CASE C: r=C identically on an interval.
Here h=f=1 are constant along the line, and every g_j is nondecreasing. Formula(5)
gives Z'>=0. Thus Y'=d_P+Z'>=d_P>=F(d). Isolated crossings are handled by
continuity, not a derivative convention.

3. SUPPORT CHANGES AND FINITE RESTORATIONS
The line is finitely piecewise analytic: each g_j is a minimum of finitely many
affine functions, G is piecewise affine, r a ratio, and the only h/f switch is
r=C. Identical ties admit the choices above. Each owner payment is continuous
at all positive-support switches. The term g_j s_jl/x_jl is bounded by g_j<=x_jl
and tends to0 at a zero row. If G tends to0 with P fixed, eventually f=1 and
h=CG/P, giving total payment(C+k)G. If P tends to0, the funding bound below and
F<=P force all nonnegative payments to0. Thus the zero conventions are continuous.

If F(d)>0, c(t)>=t d implies F(c(t))>=t F(d)>0 for every t>0, so only the initial
endpoint can have zero production. Apply(6),(7) on every positive open interval,
use the mean value theorem to show Y(t)-tF(d) nondecreasing there, pass through
its finitely many continuous boundaries, and take the initial endpoint limit.
If F(d)=0 the same argument proves ordinary monotonicity, including any initial
zero-value interval. In both cases integration/monotonicity yields(1). No uniform
bound on derivatives at a zero endpoint or hidden differentiability assumption
on arbitrary mechanisms is needed.

4. COVERAGE
It follows directly from(1): start the coalition at zero holdings, with the same
outsiders, and restore d=s. Its initial payment is0 under the price rule, so its
new payment is at least F(s). Alternatively h+f>=1, because one of h and f is1
on either side of C, and the support-dual argument from the TCWM proof applies.

5. FUNDING
Always G<=MP/C, r>=C/M and F>=min(P,G). Total payment is exactly Ph+kGf.
If r<=1, then h=1 and F=P, so
 total/F=1+kf/r<=1+k C^(-1/k)r^(-(k-1)/k)
                    <=1+k M^((k-1)/k)/C.
If r>1, F>=G and total/F<=rh+kf<=C+k. Thus the factor is bounded by
 max{C+k,1+k M^((k-1)/k)/C}.                                (8)
Choose C=max{1,sqrt(k)M^((k-1)/(2k))}; the second entry is at most1+C<=C+k,
proving(2). The public C is fixed through every report/deviation comparison.

6. HETEROGENEITY AND A PRECISE FLOOR LIMITATION
The finite fixed-positive-layer construction in HETEROGENEOUS_COMMON_CAP_
COROLLARY.txt transfers(1) to arbitrary public profits and positive consumption
coefficients on a common-resource hyperstar with distinct private rows. The
layer identity also holds for F(d), so summing full-OUT inequalities preserves
the exact outside-production term. This uses the same fixed actual owners.

Full OUT does NOT imply grand-marginal participation floors here. For example,
choose C>1, P=1, a single active branch with all k private capacities1 and all
other branches inactive. Its g=1/C,r=C,h=f=1. An owner supplying just one of
those private rows receives1/C, while removing that owner destroys all production
and has grand marginal1. Thus the stated rule generally violates that floor.
No floor claim should be added without a new construction and proof.
