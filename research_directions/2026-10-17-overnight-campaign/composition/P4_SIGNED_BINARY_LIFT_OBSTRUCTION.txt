P4 EXCLUDES EVEN NONLINEAR SAME-OWNER POSITIVE BINARY LIFTS
17 October 2026. Exact structural result; historical priority unconfirmed.
This strengthens the finite public-linear obstruction in
../energy/GRAPH_BINARY_MINIMUM_DECOMPOSITION.txt. It is NOT an unrestricted
mechanism lower bound. The graph's floor-free full-OUT interval remains
[1+c,2], c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1.

MODEL AND CLAIM
The unit resource path is0--1--2--3, with production
 F(x)=min(x0+x2,x1+x3,x1+x2).
Take FOUR fixed actual owners; owner j reports one unit of resource j and
nothing else. Write v(S)=F(1_S). Thus all singleton values and all nonedge
pair values are0; every edge pair has value1; v(012)=v(123)=1; v(0123)=2.

Consider ANY representation of this single coalition game of the form
 H(S)=sum_(i in S) f_i + integral min(sum_(i in S) a_i(w),
                                    sum_(i in S) b_i(w)) dmu(w),       (1)
where f_i>=0, a_i,b_i are nonnegative measurable functions, and mu is a
positive measure; every displayed coalition integral is finite. Finite positive sums
are included by counting measure and absorption of weights into a,b.
These auxiliary quantities may be arbitrary nonlinear functions of an
owner's report, and may even depend on the WHOLE report profile. No linearity,
continuity, finite number of components, or report-preservation assumption is
needed for the obstruction. They must define the SAME quantities for all
coalitions at the chosen profile; no auxiliary strategic identities are added.

THEOREM A. There is no exact representation H(S)=v(S) of the form(1).
THEOREM B. If v(S)<=H(S)<=rho v(S) for EVERY coalition at this profile,
then rho>=3/2. This bound is attained by one fixed public-linear positive
binary majorant valid for all real nonnegative capacities, hence for every
coalition and every fixed ownership model.

1. A SINGLE-PROFILE COALITION IDENTITY
If H vanishes on all singleton and nonedge pairs of this P4, positivity in(1)
forces f_i=0 and, outside one common null set, the following facts:
 * no owner has BOTH a_i>0 and b_i>0;
 * if a_i>0 and b_j>0, then i--j is an edge of the P4.
There are finitely many zero-value coalitions, so a common null set suffices.
For each w the two positive supports are disjoint and span a complete
bipartite subgraph of P4. Every nonzero such subgraph is contained in one
of its two maximal stars: {1} versus{0,2}, or {2} versus{1,3}.
A component with just one nonempty support has identically zero value.

For a component in the first star, owner3 has zero holdings, so its grand
value equals its012 value and its123 value equals its12 value. For a
component in the second star, owner0 has zero holdings, giving the analogous
identity. Therefore EVERY component, and hence its integral, satisfies

                   H(0123)=H(012)+H(123)-H(12).       (2)

The actual P4 values violate(2):2 is not1+1-1. This proves Theorem A.
For a coalitionwise rho-majorant, zeros of v still force zeros of H, so(2)
still applies. Then

           2<=H(0123)=H(012)+H(123)-H(12)<=2rho-1,

proving rho>=3/2. No incentive assumption and no computational search enter
this lower bound. It excludes the required value identity before a payment
or signed restoration argument can be attempted.

2. AN ALL-CAPACITY MAJORANT ATTAINING3/2
For x=(a,b,c,d)>=0 define

 H(x)=(1/2)[min(b,a+c)+min(c,b+d)+min(a,b)+min(c,d)].  (3)

Every term is a two-input production function of fixed nonnegative public
linear forms. Put
 L=min(a,b), R=min(c,d), U=(b-a)^+, V=(c-d)^+.
Then b=L+U, c=R+V and the exact leaf-first production identity is

                        F=L+R+min(U,V).              (4)

Also min(b,a+c)=L+min(U,R+V), and
min(c,b+d)=R+min(V,L+U). Consequently

 H-F=(1/2)min(U-V,R) if U>=V,
     =(1/2)min(V-U,L) if V>=U.                        (5)

Thus0<=H-F<=F/2 for ALL real capacities, proving F<=H<=3F/2.
At the unit-owner profile, H(0123)=2,H(12)=1,
H(012)=H(123)=3/2, so the lower bound is attained.
Every actual owner keeps each copy of its own resources in(3). Formula(5)
applies to coalition aggregates and pooled withdrawals as well as the grand
aggregate. There is no finite-grid extension assumption.

3. CONSEQUENCE FOR THE SIGNED L1 COMPENSATION ROUTE
The exact signed-transfer construction in
CONE_L1_EMBEDDABLE_SHARP_THEOREM.txt needs a coalitionwise identity consisting
of nonnegative private standalone reserves plus positive two-input layers.
Theorem A prevents ANY such exact lift of P4, even if its maps are nonlinear,
profile-dependent, and use infinitely many layers. This is a stronger scope
statement than the earlier obstruction to finite fixed linear resource maps.
It does not forbid compensation by signed terms, a genuinely coupled payment
rule without such a value decomposition, or another production primitive.
It also does not contradict the sharp L1 theorem: P4 fails its value-identity
hypothesis, despite its useful leaf-first residual formula(4).

The best coalitionwise value distortion for positive binary majorants is
exactly3/2. Therefore a proof using ONLY the separate worst-case scalar
funding factor1+c and a value comparison H<=rho F cannot certify a funding
factor smaller than (3/2)(1+c)>2. This is a limitation of that PRODUCT-BOUND
argument, not a claim that every binary composition mechanism has that cost.

For the explicit optimal majorant(3), applying the sharp logarithmic min2
rule independently to its four terms does in fact exceed2. At
(a,b,c,d)=(0,M,1,1), F=1. As M tends to infinity, the first two scalar
terms have total funding tending to1+c, the third has zero, and the fourth
is balanced with total2c. The composed total tends to

                     (1/2)[2(1+c)+2c]=1+2c>2.

This is an analytic limiting obstruction; finite M already violates2 by
continuity. It is not necessary to infer anything from rounded numerical
constants. Since c>1/2, the limiting excess is strictly positive.

5. INDEPENDENT SCALAR PAYMENT TUNING DOES NOT REPAIR THE NATURAL MAJORANTS
For any fixed t in[0,1], the more general majorant
 H_t=t min(b,a+c)+(1-t)min(c,b+d)
                     +(1-t)min(a,b)+t min(c,d)
also dominates F. Its excess is t min(U-V,R) if U>=V and
(1-t)min(V-U,L) otherwise. Thus its value distortion is
max(1+t,2-t), minimized at t=1/2.

Suppose each of these four binary terms is paid by any symmetric homogeneous
aggregate-price scalar rule, independently of the other terms. The four rules
may DIFFER. Assume each scalar rule covers coalitions, is coalition-report
monotone for arbitrary fixed mixed owners, and has some finite uniform funding
factor. Assume its prices agree at equal positive inputs; continuity there is
sufficient. Then the composed P4 rule cannot have funding factor below2,
even if only TCWM is required, before adding the stronger full-OUT requirement.

Here is the elementary scalar lower input. For long input A and short input
B, let r=B/A and write prices ell(r),s(r). Coalition coverage for a small
balanced coalition requires ell(r)+s(r)>=1. Uniform finite funding implies
ell(r)=O(r), so liminf_(r->0)s(r)>=1. The payment of an owner holding ALL
of the long resource is B ell(r)/r. Restoring that resource increases A and
decreases r, so TCWM makes L(r)=ell(r)/r nonincreasing in r. At r=1,
symmetry and coverage give L(1)=ell(1)>=1/2. Hence

 liminf_(r->0) [ell(r)/r+s(r)]>=3/2,                 (6)

and every balanced scalar pair has total payment at least its value.
These statements hold for each of the four possibly different primitives.

At(a,b,c,d)=(0,M,1,1), the first two binary terms become increasingly
imbalanced with short input1; the third term has value0 and hence payment0
by its finite funding bound; the fourth is a balanced unit pair. Therefore

 liminf total_payment >= (3/2)[t+(1-t)]+t=3/2+t.

At the reflected capacities(1,1,M,0), the same reasoning gives at least
3/2+(1-t). Both original P4 values are1. Thus the composed funding factor is
at least3/2+max(t,1-t)>=2. This excludes tuning the scalar primitives inside
this entire fixed majorant family; it is distinct from pricing separate Huber
tight components, which is the architecture excluded by the root note.

This lower does not cover arbitrary positive binary majorants outside the
stated family, non-symmetric scalar primitives without an additional argument,
report-dependent component weights, or coupled payments between components.
Theorem A and the optimal VALUE-distortion theorem remain unrestricted in
their stated positive-decomposition classes and do not need these regularity
or scalar-mechanism hypotheses.

6. COMPLETE GRAPH-CLASS BOUNDARY FOR NONLINEAR POSITIVE BINARY LIFTS
The earlier complete-multipartite classification extends to the much broader
class in(1): an arbitrary unit resource graph admits such a coalitionwise
positive binary representation universally across ALL fixed finite actual-owner
models and EVERY report profile if and only if each connected component is
complete multipartite. Necessity uses pure owners; it is not asserted for
every separately pre-fixed coowned eligibility model. Components and
owner counts may be arbitrarily large; the representation is allowed to
change with the entire profile and to use a positive integral. Exact public
linear finite representations already prove sufficiency, by the formula
 (1/2) sum_j min(C_j,C_total-C_j)
for part totals C_j, as established in the energy decomposition note.

For necessity, induced P4 subgraphs are excluded by Theorem A. The other
minimal forbidden graph, the paw, is also excluded at ONE unit-owner profile.
Label its triangle A,B,C and its pendant vertex D, joined only to A. Take
one pure unit owner at each vertex. Then
 v(ABCD)=2, v(ABC)=3/2, v(ABD)=v(AB)=1,
and all singleton values and both BD,CD values are0.

For a nonnegative binary component, these zeros again force disjoint positive
supports spanning a complete bipartite subgraph. If D has nonzero holdings
and the component has nonzero value, the opposite support is contained in
{A}; any active B,C lie on D's side. Conditional on A being present, this
is a scalar capped sum min(a_A,b_B+b_C+b_D). Adding more of the same-side
input weakly decreases D's marginal contribution. If D has zero holdings,
both D marginals vanish. Therefore every component obeys

 H(ABCD)-H(ABC) <= H(ABD)-H(AB).                      (7)

Integration preserves(7), but the actual paw values require1/2<=0. Thus
there is no positive binary representation at this unit profile. This argument
uses no public linearity, no continuity in capacities, and no finite-layer
hypothesis. Nonnegative private singleton reserves vanish as before.

Every connected graph that is not complete multipartite contains an induced
P4 or paw; an elementary proof is already recorded in Section4 of the energy
note. Set all other resource capacities to zero and restrict coalitions to
these four actual pure owners. Thus either obstruction applies, completing
the strengthened classification.

Consequently profile-dependent nonlinear positive binary decomposition does
not enlarge the exact graph class at all. A new full-OUT graph mechanism
beyond this class must escape the positive binary value identity itself,
not merely replace public linear copies by nonlinear same-owner copies.
This remains a decomposition statement, not a no-go theorem for arbitrary
incentive mechanisms, approximate representations, or signed compensation.

7. SHARPENING THE NATURAL-MAJORANT PAYMENT BARRIER TO9/4
The preceding Section5 lower2 can be sharpened without any new assumption.
At(a,b,c,d)=(0,M,1,M), F=1 and ALL THREE nonzero binary terms of H_t
are increasingly imbalanced with short input1. Their weights are t,1-t,t.
Equation(6) therefore gives

                 liminf total_payment >=(3/2)(1+t).

At the reflected profile(M,1,M,0), the lower is(3/2)(2-t). Hence ANY four
independent symmetric homogeneous TCWM scalar primitives as in Section5
have composed funding factor at least

             (3/2) max(1+t,2-t) >=9/4.               (8)

This is sharp for this TCWM composition family: apply the unit-edge Huber
TCWM primitive, whose exact scalar bound is3/2, to each term and take t=1/2.
The value bound H<=3F/2 proves total<=9F/4, and these same profiles attain
the factor in the limit. This does not improve the known P4 TCWM bound3/2;
it precisely quantifies the cost of this decomposition architecture.

For the sharp logarithmic full-OUT primitive, the imbalanced scalar cost
actually tends to1+c. Thus the t=1/2 composition has EXACT uniform funding
factor(3/2)(1+c): the general product bound is attained on(0,M,1,M).
It is therefore strictly worse than the available graph factor2. This
stronger witness supersedes the weaker illustrative limit in Section3,
which remains correct. No statement is made that9/4 is the sharp factor
within the full-OUT primitive family, only that full OUT cannot evade its
TCWM lower9/4. The sharp9/4 assertion concerns the stated TCWM family.
