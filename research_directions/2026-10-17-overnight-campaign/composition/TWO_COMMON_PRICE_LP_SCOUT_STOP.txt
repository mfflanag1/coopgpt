BOUNDED PRICE-FIELD SCOUT: TWO SHARED RESOURCES, ONE PRIVATE ROW PER JOB
17 October 2026. Numerical necessary-condition search; no new lower bound.

QUESTION
Can heterogeneous common-resource consumption ratios force TCWM funding above
the sharp equal-ratio three-pool benchmark2, with arbitrary finite fixed owner
populations? A uniform constant independent of ratio range remains open.

RELAXATION
For each chosen capacity profile C, introduce nonnegative prices p(C), with
zero prices on absent rows, and identify price variables on positively
proportional capacity rays. Enforce the budget C.p<=kappa F(C), and every
activity's dual coverage inequality when all of its resources are positive.
The exact rational production value is obtained by enumerating the vertices
of the two-dimensional common-price dual:

 min_{p,q>=0} Pp+Qq+sum_j c_j max(v_j-a_jp-b_jq,0).

For every adjacent grid transition C'=C+delta e_j, impose ALL coalition
holding-box corners through equivalent nonnegative loss variables:

 z_h >= C_h[p_h(C)-p_h(C')], z_h>=0,
 sum_h z_h <= delta p_j(C').

Indeed the worst retained coalition bundle s in [0,C] has payment loss
sum_h C_h[p_h(C)-p_h(C')]_+, and its newly restored coordinate earns
delta p_j(C'). Coordinate transitions compose to all grid-comparable
profiles with the coalition's actual holdings updated at each step.
Homogeneous price necessity is justified by the separately audited
energy/COALITION_PRICE_REDUCTION_CANDIDATE.txt. No original owner's identity
is changed in that theorem. A lower from this finite relaxation would need
an exact certificate plus that existence reduction before being used as an
unrestricted uniform-owner lower; a finite-grid feasible point is NEVER a
full-domain mechanism or a universal upper bound.

STOPPING DECISION
No value above2 was found, so the specified lower-bound promotion gate was
not met. There is no exact lower certificate to promote and no evidence for
an amplifiable ratio-chain obstruction from this scout. The finite programs
remain useful falsification artifacts, not evidence that constant2 holds on
all real profiles. Further grid enlargement without a new analytic lead is
stopped. The proved positive-three-pool linear-lift obstruction is retained
separately; the logarithmic ratio-condition upper is also retained.
