FINITE EXACT UNIT-GRAPH REPRESENTATION OF TWO-SPARSE PRODUCTION
Frozen candidate, 17 October 2026. Internal mathematical review pending.

1. SCOPE AND CLAIM
Let A be a finite nonnegative resource-by-activity matrix, each column having
at most two positive entries, and let w>=0 be public activity profits. Exclude
positive-profit zero-resource columns (these make production infinite). Define
 F(c)=max{w.x : x>=0, Ax<=c}, c>=0.
All activity amounts and resource capacities are real and divisible. Public
coefficients themselves may be arbitrary real numbers. There is a finite
simple unweighted graph H and a fixed nonnegative diagonal-copy linear map B
from original resources to virtual resources such that
 F(c)=gamma.c + F_H(Bc)                 for EVERY c>=0.                 (1)
Here gamma>=0 is public, and F_H is fractional unit-edge production. The graph
may be exponentially large; no efficient enumeration claim is made here.

For fixed actual owners b_i, virtual owner i holds B b_i; ownership never
changes, and there are no free auxiliary resources or new owners. Thus (1)
holds simultaneously for every coalition and every report profile. Feeding
these linked reports to the audited unit-Huber rule and adding gamma.b_i gives
coalition coverage and coalition-total whole-bundle-underreport monotonicity,
with total payment at most (3/2)F(c). The uniform 3/2 is sharp already on the
unit single-edge subclass as actual owner counts grow. It is NOT a lower bound
for individually truthful mechanisms without coalition-total incentives.
The audited unit full-OUT factor-2 rule also transfers, including its full
owner grand-marginal payment floors. These incentive consequences use previously
proved graph mechanisms; the representation proof below is independent.

2. REMOVE UNARY ACTIVITIES EXACTLY
Discard zero-profit columns. For each resource v let gamma_v be the maximum
profit/consumption ratio of unary activities at v, or zero if absent. The dual
of F requires p_v>=gamma_v and, for each binary activity e=uv,
 a_e p_u + b_e p_v >= w_e, with a_e,b_e>0.
Write p=gamma+q. Retain only binary activities with positive residual profit
 r_e=w_e-a_e gamma_u-b_e gamma_v>0; all others impose redundant inequalities
because q>=0. Strong LP duality gives exactly
 F(c)=gamma.c + max{r.x : x>=0, A_binary x<=c}.                       (2)
This is an identity of value functions, not a claim that the optimal original
plan physically executes all baseline and residual activities simultaneously.
If no residual activities remain, H is empty and (1) is already proved.

3. A BOUNDED DUAL POLYTOPE AND FIXED FINITE GRIDS
Now use residual profits r_e>0 and omit the word residual. Put
 M_v=max_{e incident v} w_e/a_{ev}, or zero at an isolated resource.
The dual is min c.p subject to p>=0 and a_e p_u+b_e p_v>=w_e.
Clipping every coordinate to M_v preserves feasibility: if an endpoint of an
edge is clipped, its new contribution alone is at least that edge's profit;
if neither is clipped, nothing changes. Clipping cannot increase c.p.
Consequently the nonempty compact polytope
 P={p: 0<=p_v<=M_v, a_e p_u+b_e p_v>=w_e for every e}
contains an optimal dual vertex for every nonnegative capacity vector c.
It has finitely many vertices, even for arbitrary real public coefficients.

At resource v sort 0, M_v, and ALL coordinates p_v of vertices p of P.
Remove duplicates. Each consecutive interval I=[l_I,h_I] of positive width
becomes one virtual resource (v,I), with capacity (h_I-l_I)c_v. Isolated
resources with M_v=0 have no copies. This grid is fixed by the public technology
and does not depend on capacities, owner counts, or reports.

Connect (u,I) to (v,J) iff at least one original binary activity e=uv satisfies
 a_e l_I+b_e l_J < w_e.                                             (3)
Multiple activities/qualifying reasons create only one unit edge. The resulting
finite H is simple and loop-free; it need not be bipartite.

4. PRIMAL EMBEDDING: F_H(Bc)>=F(c)
Take any feasible original activity plan x. For an activity e=uv, distribute
mass x_e dz along z in [0,w_e] to the pair of heights
 s=z/a_e at u,   t=(w_e-z)/b_e at v.
Both heights lie in their resource grids' ranges, since w_e/a_ev<=M_v.
Outside finitely many bin boundaries (which have Lebesgue measure zero),
let I,J be their unique bins. Then l_I<s and l_J<t, so
 a_e l_I+b_e l_J < a_e s+b_e t=w_e;
therefore (3) includes that unit edge. Add the corresponding mass to its flow.
At a bin I of u, mass contributed by e is at most a_e |I| x_e, because the
preimage of I under z/a_e has length at most a_e |I|. Summing activities at u,
 bin load <= |I| sum_{e incident u} a_{eu} x_e <= |I| c_u.
All lifted flows are feasible, including where different activities share a
lift edge. Total lifted value is sum_e w_e x_e. Maximizing gives the claim.
For exact rational replay the integrals reduce to finitely many intervals cut
at a_e times u-grid boundaries and w_e-b_e times v-grid boundaries.

5. DUAL EMBEDDING: F_H(Bc)<=F(c)
Choose an optimal vertex p of P for c. On a bin I at v place unit cover price
 q_{v,I}=1 if l_I<p_v, and 0 otherwise.
If a lift edge from e were uncovered, l_I>=p_u and l_J>=p_v, contradicting
 a_e l_I+b_e l_J<w_e<=a_e p_u+b_e p_v.
Thus q is a feasible vertex cover, including all ties under convention (3).
Because p_v is a grid boundary, the total width of bins with l_I<p_v is
EXACTLY p_v. Its total cover cost is sum_v c_v p_v=F(c). Weak duality proves
the reverse inequality. Together with section 4 and then (2), this proves (1).

6. FIXED OWNER, COALITION, AND OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION TRANSFER
For every actual owner i and bin I at v set b'_{i,v,I}=|I|b_{iv}. This positive
linear map preserves coordinatewise underreports, coalition sums, and withheld
bundles. The same graph H works on every real report profile, including zeros.
If graph payments satisfy coverage, then for any owner coalition S their sum
plus gamma.b_S is >=F_H(B b_S)+gamma.b_S=F(b_S).
If graph coalition-total payments cannot decrease when members increase reports,
the added linear nonnegative payments preserve this property. Funding obeys
 gamma.c+(3/2)F_H(Bc) <= (3/2)F(c).
For full OUT, every withheld aggregate d satisfies F(d)=gamma.d+F_H(Bd), so
adding linear gamma payments to a full-OUT graph mechanism proves the original
full-OUT inequality exactly. Likewise the full grand-marginal floor transfers:
 F(c)-F(c-b_i)=gamma.b_i+F_H(Bc)-F_H(B(c-b_i)).
No interpretation that withheld resources are destroyed is needed for the full
OUT consequence. Payment-only TCWM by itself does not imply full OUT.

Weighted matching unfolding and its cover proof are established ingredients:
Kao--Lam--Sung--Ting (2001), section 4.1/Lemma 4.1, original freely retrieved
and read by root 17 October 2026. Our earlier antidiagonal proof instantiated
that ingredient. This new finite vertex-aligned interval construction handles
arbitrary heterogeneous two-sparse columns, arbitrary real coefficients, all
capacities, and linked same-owner reports. Its historical priority is NOT
confirmed. LP duality, vertex enumeration, and the graph mechanisms remain
separate established/audited inputs; do not label the whole reduction novel.

Divisibility is essential. This is not a result for integer scheduling,
nonlinear activity costs, private technology, unrestricted overreports, or
consumption support size three. Indeed the known sharp TCWM factor 2 on a
single three-input min technology rules out any exact positive same-owner
unit-graph representation of that technology: such a representation would
transfer the graph factor 3/2 and contradict that lower bound. Separately the
proof's two endpoint height allocation cannot be carried over verbatim to
arity three. Neither statement is an unbounded-subsidy lower bound for
three-sparse technologies. The finite vertex construction
alone gives no polynomial runtime in the original input size.
