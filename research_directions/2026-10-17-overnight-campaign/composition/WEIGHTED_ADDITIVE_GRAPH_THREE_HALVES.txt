THREE-HALVES FOR WEIGHTED FORESTS AND VERTEX-ADDITIVE RESOURCE GRAPHS
17 October 2026. Proposed corollary of the audited unit-profit graph theorem
and the energy lane's independently derived source-price coarea identity.
Root/independent review is required before promotion. No historical novelty
claim is made for either the layer identity or these consequences.

MODEL
A finite undirected simple resource graph G=(V,E) has public nonnegative
activity profits w_uv. An edge activity consumes one unit of each endpoint;
fractional production is allowed. Fixed actual owners report arbitrary mixed
nonnegative resource bundles b_i, aggregate c=sum_i b_i. Feasible deviations
are coordinatewise binding underreports of the whole bundle, utility payment
only. Every actual-owner coalition must receive at least its own production
value. We require the stronger property that a joint underreport cannot
increase the sum of that coalition's payments, even with transfers.

THEOREM
Suppose the positive-profit edges admit public real vertex potentials alpha_v
such that
  w_uv=alpha_u+alpha_v.                               (1)
Such weights are conventionally called node-induced; see the source note
below. Then there is a mechanism satisfying all the above requirements, at every
real profile, with total funding at most(3/2)F_w(c). Counts of owners and
resources are unrestricted. Zero-profit activities can be discarded.

In particular, this holds for:
(a) every weighted resource forest, with completely arbitrary public positive
    profits on its edges;
(b) every weighted graph whose connected components are trees or have exactly
    one cycle of odd length, again with arbitrary positive edge profits;
(c) any other graph satisfying the explicit linear compatibility condition(1).
For this strengthened coalition-incentive requirement, the constant3/2 is
sharp uniformly over owner count, since the class contains the single edge
in root's matching lower-bound theorem.

INGREDIENT 1: EXACT SOURCE-PRICE LAYER IDENTITY
Take any fixed bipartite access graph with left resource costs a_u, right
resource values d_v, and edge profits(d_v-a_u)_+. Capacities are arbitrary
and need not be owned by pure-side owners. For each real threshold t retain
left resources with a_u<=t and right resources with t<d_v, and their access
edges; let F_t be the UNIT-profit production in that retained graph. Then
  F_w(c)=integral_R F_t(c) dt.                        (2)
Only finitely many intervals between public a's and d's contribute.

Proof of(2), included for self-containment. Any feasible weighted edge plan
x remains feasible after restricting to the edges a_u<=t<d_v. Its integrated
unit output is sum_uv(d_v-a_u)_+ x_uv. Hence F_w<=integral F_t.
Conversely take an optimal nonnegative weighted dual p, so p_u+p_v>=d_v-a_u
on profitable access edges. Put lambda_u=a_u+p_u and mu_v=d_v-p_v. For every
t, choose retained left vertices with t<lambda_u and retained right vertices
with mu_v<=t. This is a vertex cover of every retained edge: if neither
endpoint were chosen, lambda_u<=t<mu_v would contradict lambda_u>=mu_v.
The integrated chosen cost at left u is c_u times the interval lengthp_u,
and at right v is c_v times lengthp_v, integrating over all real t. Thus
integral F_t<=sum c_v p_v=F_w. Endpoints and negative dual-shifted mu values
cause no problem because the integral is over R; outside a finite public
range the retained graph has no profitable edge.

INGREDIENT 2: EXACT SAME-OWNER BIPARTITE DOUBLE COVER
Replace each original resource v by two copies L_v,R_v, EACH of capacityc_v/2.
Actual owner i owns b_iv/2 of each copy. No owner is created, removed, merged,
or reassigned. For each original edge uv add activities L_u--R_v and L_v--R_u,
both with original profit w_uv.

This transformation preserves production EXACTLY for every actual coalition,
not merely the grand coalition. Given original edge quantityx_uv, putx_uv/2
on each oriented copy. Each copy resource respects its half-capacity. Given
any feasible doubled edge quantitiesz_uv,z_vu, putx_uv=z_uv+z_vu on the
original edge. Its total use at resourcev is the sum of outgoing and incoming
uses at its two copies, at mostc_v/2+c_v/2=c_v. Profit is unchanged in either
direction. The same proof uses any coalition's aggregate bundle.

Choose a public constant K>=max_v|alpha_v| and set
  a_{L_u}=K-alpha_u,     d_{R_v}=K+alpha_v.
Both lists are nonnegative, and every directed copy's profit is exactly
d_{R_v}-a_{L_u}=alpha_u+alpha_v=w_uv. Thus(2) applies to this fixed doubled
technology. Public potentials with negative coordinates are allowed; K makes
the auxiliary source costs and values nonnegative without changing profits.

MECHANISM AND PROOF
For each of the finitely many public threshold intervals, run the unit-profit
3/2 mechanism from HUBER_PRICE_THREE_HALVES_v2.txt on that interval's retained
doubled resource graph. Each actual owner keeps its two half-bundles; omitted
resource rows are simply inactive in that layer. Pay that layer's payment
times the interval length, summed over all layers.

For every actual coalition T, each layer covers its F_t(b_T). Sum the layer
inequalities and apply(2) plus the exact double-cover transformation to obtain
payment(T)>=F_w(b_T). Each layer is coalition-total underreport monotone for
arbitrary mixed ownership, so their fixed positive linear combination is too.
An original-coordinate underreport reduces BOTH half-copies; this is a
permitted joint coordinate decrease in the layer theorem. Layer truthfulness
holds on a larger report domain than this restricted diagonal image, so it
certainly holds on the original report domain.

Finally sum the layer funding inequalities:
  sum_i y_i <=(3/2)integral F_t(c/2,c/2)dt
             =(3/2)F_w(c).
The layer intervals depend only on public technology, never on reports, so
there is no moving-normalization seam of the kind that invalidated the
tight-component Huber candidate.

WHY ALL WEIGHTED FORESTS AND ODD UNICYCLES QUALIFY
On a tree choose any real alpha at a root and propagate alpha_v=w_uv-alpha_u
along its edges. There is no cycle consistency requirement. For an odd cycle
v0,...,v_{ell-1}, close the recursion by choosing
  alpha_0=(w_0-w_1+w_2-...+w_{ell-1})/2,
then propagate around the cycle and out through attached trees. Odd length
makes this the unique consistent cycle solution. A disconnected graph is
handled componentwise. Thus arbitrary weights work on the stated classes.

For an even cycle, alternating edge profits must sum tozero. For general
graphs the exact condition is the solvability of(1), checked by linear
equations. This is a sufficient structural class, not an assertion that all
weighted graphs satisfy it. For example a four-cycle with weights(1,1,1,2)
fails its alternating-sum condition. That failure only blocks this particular
potential representation; it is NOT an impossibility for other mechanisms.

ESTABLISHED GRAPH-WEIGHT INGREDIENT
Berczi, Csaplar and Yamaguchi, "A Hierarchy of Edge-Weight Symmetries in
Perfect Matchings," arXiv:2604.20009v1,21 April2026, define node-induced weights
in precisely the form(1). Their Theorem1.4 characterizes them by equality of
alternating parity sums on every even CLOSED WALK, including multiplicity;
checking only simple even cycles is insufficient on general nonbipartite
graphs. This representation/characterization is an established ingredient,
not a contribution of this campaign. The proposed contribution here is its
use in the fixed-owner capacity-incentive and subsidy mechanism.

RELATION TO OTHER CAMPAIGN RESULTS
This is a different mechanism from single-scale weighted Huber, whose sharp
funding factor is2. The prior local-knee and tight-component normalizations
also fail incentives. Those obstructions do not apply to a fixed public sum
of unit mechanisms whose production values add EXACTLY as in(2).
The energy lane's4/3 theorem with public participation floors and pure-side
owners remains distinct; this theorem asserts3/2 with arbitrary mixed owners
and does not impose extra grand-marginal participation floors.
Likewise, payment-only coalition-underreport monotonicity does not protect
an owner who can also monetize withheld resources outside the mechanism;
that is a different utility/deviation model. See root/OUTSIDE_PRODUCTION_BOUNDS.txt
for the campaign's separate bounds in that model.
The known unit theorem still covers arbitrary unweighted graphs, even those
with many cycles. The present result adds a weighted structural class; it
does not solve arbitrary weighted activity matrices or general hypergraphs.
