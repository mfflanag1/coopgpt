FIXED RADIAL AVERAGES OF PRIVATE PRICES DO NOT GIVE COALITION INCENTIVES
17 October 2026, version2. Exact architecture obstruction; historical novelty unconfirmed.
Version1 is preserved in TWO_COMMON_FRESH_RADIAL_PRIVATE_OBSTRUCTION.txt.

STATUS AND SCOPE
This is a new bounded-gate result for the heterogeneous two-common-resource,
one-private-resource-per-activity model. It does NOT show an unrestricted
mechanism lower bound or unavoidable dependence on the ratio spread. It rules
out an entire natural fixed-kernel block-radial price construction, even when
common-resource payments are arbitrary and all dual ties may be resolved
adaptively. The earlier positive-three-pool lift obstruction is logically
separate.

MODEL AND CONTRACT
There are three activities with public (P consumption,Q consumption,profit):

  job0=(1,1,3/2), job1=(1,1/4,1), job2=(1/4,1,1).

Private capacity c_j bounds activity x_j. Production is

 F(P,Q,c)=max{(3/2)x0+x1+x2: x>=0, x<=c,
                         x0+x1+x2/4<=P, x0+x1/4+x2<=Q}.

An actual owner's bundle consists of its P,Q and private capacities. Owners
and their eligible rows are fixed throughout each comparison. TCWM means:
for every actual coalition S, restoring any nonnegative bundle to one or
more members of S cannot decrease the sum of S's payments, while all other
owners' reports remain fixed. Coverage means the payment sum of each S is
at least the production available from S's reported resources. Payments are
nonnegative; funding factor kappa means total payment <= kappa F.

FIXED-KERNEL CLASS
Let h:[0,1]->R_+ be any Lebesgue integrable function. It is fixed
independently of reports and may depend on the public technology. No
continuity, boundedness, or pointwise limit at zero is assumed. At P,Q>0 and c>=0 define private prices by

 p_j(P,Q,c)= integral_0^1 h(t) g_j(P,Q,t c) dt,

where g(P,Q,z) is any measurable choice of nonnegative LP private dual
prices in [0,w], supporting the concave piecewise-linear map
z -> F(P,Q,z). At positive c this includes all private supergradients;
allowing arbitrary dual tie choices on boundary faces does not affect the
proof because only positively held private coordinates are charged. A private-only owner
with reported bundle b receives exactly sum_j b_j p_j(P,Q,c). Common-owner
payments are unrestricted. Additional bonuses to private-only owners are
outside this architecture. No resource or owner identity is changed.

THEOREM
If h is nonzero on a set of positive measure, this construction cannot be
both TCWM and finitely funded, already with five fixed pure owners. If
h=0 a.e., no finite funding factor uniform over all finite fixed owner
populations is compatible with coalition coverage. Consequently no kernel
in this class supplies a uniformly funded TCWM covering mechanism.

1. EXACT UNIFORM-KERNEL OBSTRUCTION, INCLUDING EVERY TIE SELECTION
Fix P=Q=1 and base c=(1,1,1). Along the ray z=s c, the full supergradient
sets away from the displayed breakpoints are:

 interval                 F(1,1,s c)       possible g
 0<s<4/9                 7s/2             (3/2,1,1)
 4/9<s<4/5               3/2+s/8         conv{(0,1/8,0),(0,0,1/8)}
 s>4/5                   8/5              (0,0,0).

Thus for h=1, p0=2/3 and p1+p2=14/15, irrespective of tie selection.
At least one j in {1,2} has p_j>=7/15, so the payment to private owners
{0,j} is at least 17/15.

Restore that owner's capacity from 1 to 3/2. By symmetry take j=1, giving
c'=(1,3/2,1). All private gradients are unique off finitely many points:

 interval                 F(1,1,s c')      g
 0<s<4/11                4s                (3/2,1,1)
 4/11<s<4/9              1+5s/4           (1/2,0,3/4)
 4/9<s<4/5               3/2+s/8         (0,0,1/8)
 s>4/5                   8/5              (0,0,0).

Hence p0'=58/99 and p1'=4/11, and the new coalition payment is

 58/99+(3/2)(4/11)=112/99 < 17/15,

with a tie-independent loss of at least 1/495. The reflected restoration of
private owner2 has the same new payment. Use five fixed pure owners, one
for P, one for Q, and one for each private row; the changing coalition is
{private0,private j}. The common owners remain outside it and cannot repair
its loss by any choice of their payments. This is a feasible whole-bundle
restoration with one coordinate changed and all owner identities fixed.

The tables follow from the exact private-price dual

 F(1,1,c)=min_{p,q>=0}[p+q+sum_j c_j(w_j-a_jp-b_jq)_+].

The checker enumerates all intersections of p=0,q=0 and the three lines
 a_j p+b_j q=w_j, then verifies every active affine form on each interval.
At the base middle interval precisely the two displayed forms are active;
the supergradient is their convex hull. Inactive dual cells add no gradients.

2. TCWM AND FINITE FUNDING FORCE A POSITIVE CESARO LIMIT
Suppose, toward a contradiction, that a nonzero h gives a TCWM mechanism
with finite funding factor kappa. Keep the same five actual owners and
P=Q=1, but first put private capacities c=(r,0,0), r>=1. Production is3/2.
The only positively charged private coordinate has gradient3/2 for
0<t<1/r and0 for t>1/r. Its owner's payment is exactly

 (3/2) r H(1/r),   H(x)=integral_0^x h(t)dt.

TCWM for that owner's own restorations implies that A(x)=H(x)/x is
nonincreasing in x on (0,1]. Nonnegative payments and the funding bound
imply A(x)<=kappa. Consequently

 L=lim_{x downarrow0} A(x) exists, and
 0 < H(1) <= L <= kappa < infinity.

For each fixed 0<=a<b<infinity and lambda large enough,

 integral_a^b h(s/lambda)ds
 =lambda[H(b/lambda)-H(a/lambda)] -> L(b-a).

For a=0 the corresponding term is zero. This is interval-integral
convergence, not a claim of pointwise or uniform convergence of h.

3. THE CESARO LIMIT TRANSFERS THE EXACT COALITION LOSS
Return to base c=(1,1,1), scale all three private capacities by lambda,
and compare to the selected restoration lambda c' from Section1. Commons
remain P=Q=1, and the five actual owners remain unchanged. Owner i's
payment is

 c_i integral_0^lambda h(s/lambda) g_i(1,1,s c)ds.

All relevant gradients vanish beyond s=4/5. At the base, g0 and g1+g2
are constant on each interval in the exact table, even though the separate
g1,g2 may depend arbitrarily on the selected dual tie. Section2 therefore
gives, uniformly over such choices,

 base owner0 payment -> (2/3)L,
 base owner1+owner2 payment -> (14/15)L.

For each lambda choose the larger of the two latter payments. The two
possible restored profiles have unique gradients off finitely many points,
so both corresponding new coalition payments converge to112L/99. Thus

 liminf_lambda [old selected coalition pay - new coalition pay]
       >= L(17/15-112/99)=L/495>0.

For a finite lambda one of the two feasible restorations strictly reduces
its changing coalition's total payment, contradicting TCWM. The argument
allows all dual ties to depend on the entire profile and on lambda, and
common-owner payments remain unrestricted.

4. ZERO KERNEL CANNOT GIVE A UNIFORM FUNDING FACTOR
If h=0 a.e., every private-only owner's payment is zero. Fix n pure owners
of P, each with capacity1; n pure owners of Q, each with capacity1; and one
private0 owner with capacity1. Other private capacities are zero. Grand
production is 3/2. Each coalition consisting of one P owner, one Q owner,
and the private0 owner also produces3/2. Writing common payments u_i,v_j,
coverage requires u_i+v_j>=3/2 for all n^2 pairs. Summing yields

 sum_i u_i+sum_j v_j >= (3/2)n = n F.

Thus no funding factor independent of finite owner count exists. Owner
identities and eligibility are fixed in each such (2n+1)-owner instance.

CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITATION
A fixed private-block radial average cannot repair the heterogeneous
interaction by choosing any different nonnegative integrable density.
The uniform averaging rule even assigns the private block exactly F when
commons are fixed (the radial fundamental theorem), but its coalition
subpayments are not monotone. Any successful route must change the private
price architecture: e.g. report-dependent compensation coupled to common
prices, or a non-radial owner-preserving construction. This theorem does not
exclude such repairs or arbitrary mechanisms. The separate
TWO_COMMON_FRESH_RADIAL_MEASURE_COROLLARY.txt also closes every fixed positive
non-density measure; that strengthening is not assumed in this density proof.
The ratio-independent funding question remains open.
