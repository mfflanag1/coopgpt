EXACT UNIT-HUBER PRICES BY DENSITY MIN-CUTS
17 October2026. Independent implementation/audit of the algorithmic proposition
suggested by the Fable delegate. Existing flow/principal-partition machinery,
not a new algorithmic-priority assertion. No strongly-polynomial claim.

INPUT AND OUTPUT
Input: finite undirected simple graph and rational nonnegative capacities.
Output: exact Huber resource prices, exact original-edge KKT multipliers,
exact fractional production and rounded-dual certificates, and density blocks.
Zero-capacity vertices receive price1 as a cover convention and paymentzero.
The positive induced graph is solved; isolated positive vertices have price0.
No numerical optimizer or floating-point comparison is used.

STRUCTURAL LEMMA, INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED
At a positive profile, let p be the unique Huber optimizer and let lambda be
its nonnegative tight-edge KKT flow, with degree(v)=c_v min(2p_v,1). For any
vertex setX, counting each edge endpoint gives
  degree(X)<=degree(N(X))<=c(N(X)).                    (1)
Let tau=min_v p_v. If tau<1/2, then
  rho=2tau=min_{nonempty X} c(N(X))/c(X).               (2)
Indeed(1) and degree(v)>=2tau c_v give the lower bound. For the minimum-price
setX0, every neighbor has price1−tau: feasibility forces price>=1−tau, and
that neighbor's tight edge to some vertex forces the reverse inequality.
All its positive KKT flow therefore goes toX0. Hence c(N(X0))=2tau c(X0).
Any minimizing set in(2) can contain only minimum-price vertices, by equality
in the same chain. ThusX0 is the unique inclusion-maximal minimizing set,
and is independent. Assign p=rho/2 onX0, p=1−rho/2 onN(X0).

All edges fromN(X0) to the remaining vertices are strictly slack, since all
remaining prices exceedtau. Restricting KKT to the remaining induced graph
therefore proves the same optimizer problem remains. RemoveX0 andN(X0) and
repeat. If the minimum ratio is at least1, the remainder has all prices1/2.
The reasoning uses the original graph, so odd cycles cause no exception.

MIN-CUT ORACLE
Scale rational capacities to positive integers on the active induced graph;
write C for their sum. For rationaltheta=a/b in[0,1], build a double-cover
network: source->L_v capacitya c_v; R_v->sink capacityb c_v; L_u->R_v and
L_v->R_u for each original edge, capacity(a+b)C+1. The latter exceeds every
potential finite mincut. A finite cut with left source setX optimally includes
exactlyN(X) on the right, and its cost is
  a C + b[c(N(X))−theta c(X)].                       (3)
Thus a maxflow detects a negative minimum precisely when its value<a C.

At the EXACT critical ratio rho the empty set also minimizes(3). Ordinary
source reachability may return that empty set. The implementation deliberately
takes the MAXIMAL source side: complement of all residual vertices that can
reach the sink. This is the union/maximal side of all mincuts and returns the
maximal minimizingX0 required above. A one-edge positive profile provides a
calibration where this distinction is essential.

EXACT RATIONAL RECOVERY
After integerization, each critical ratio is B/A for capacity sumsA,B with
1<=A<=C. Distinct rationals of denominator<=C differ by at least1/C^2.
Binary-search the sign from(3) until the enclosing interval has width below
1/(4C^2). The closest denominator<=C rational to its midpoint is then uniquely
the desired rho. Python Fraction.limit_denominator implements this exact
continued-fraction operation. A fresh exact mincut at rho verifies zero
reduced cost, nonempty disjointX,N(X), and c(N(X))/c(X)=rho before assigning
any prices. There are at mostn nontrivial peeling blocks.

BIT COMPLEXITY
Each block uses O(log C) maxflows. Clearing input denominators uses their LCM,
whose bit length is at most the sum of input denominator bit lengths.
All bisection and network-capacity bit lengths are polynomial in the input
encoding length. Edmonds-Karp uses O(V E) augmentations and O(V E^2) arithmetic operations,
with exact integer capacities here; bit complexity is polynomial in graph size
and input bit length. This is a weakly polynomial implementation. It does not
assume a strongly-polynomial parametric-flow theorem or an unverified runtime.
KKT certificate construction is one further rational double-cover maxflow
with demands d_v=c_v min(2p_v,1); its bit sizes also remain polynomial.

CERTIFICATES AND REPLAY
The KKT flow is mapped back to ORIGINAL edges by averaging the two oriented
copy flows. The standalone checker verifies nonnegativity, original cover
constraints, tight-edge support, degree stationarity, rounded cover feasibility,
sumlambda=sum c rounded_p, and funding<=3/2 sumlambda. It does not rerun the
mincut optimizer. Exact checks raise RuntimeError rather than use assert.
