UNRESTRICTED COMMON-CAP TCWM FUNDING: A QUARTER-POWER LOWER BOUND
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate for independent review. Novelty unconfirmed.

THEOREM.
Fix integers M>=2 and R>=1. The unit three-sparse technology
 F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_{j=1}^M min(a_j,b_j))                     (1)
with R fixed pure power owners (each report bound1/R) and two fixed pure branch
owners A_j,B_j (report bound1) per branch has the following property. Every
nonnegative, coalition-covering, all-profile TCWM mechanism of finite funding
factor kappa must satisfy
 kappa >= P_M R/(R^2+P_M),
 P_M=product_{m=2}^M 2m/(2m-1)=4^M/[2 binom(2M,M)].           (2)
TCWM says a fixed coalition's total payment cannot decrease when its members
weakly increase their reports coordinatewise (some or all members may leave
their reports unchanged) and all outsiders' reports stay fixed. Funding says total
payment<=kappa F at every report profile. No price form, continuity, homogeneity,
anonymity, null-owner or inactive-branch payment axiom is assumed. Randomized
mechanisms are included through expected payments.

In particular, for integers B>=1, set R=2B. A factor at most B is possible only
if
 B^2 >= P_M/4 = 4^M/[8 binom(2M,M)].                         (3)
Hence unrestricted TCWM funding is Omega(M^(1/4)); the right side of(3) is
asymptotic to sqrt(pi M)/8. The instance has d=2M+1 resources and n=2M+R actual
owners, all pure-role, fixed through every deviation. This strengthens the
previous square-root-logarithmic lower on the same common-cap family.

1. FIXED DOMAIN AND VALID SYMMETRIZATION
There is one unit-profit activity(P,a_j,b_j) per branch, consuming one unit of
its three resource rows. The continuous packing optimum is(1). Every power
owner is eligible only for P, with report in[0,1/R]. A_j and B_j are eligible
only for their respective rows, with reports in[0,1]. All these actual owners
exist at every profile, including when their report is zero.

Average any purported mechanism over the finite group of power-owner
permutations, branch permutations, and independent endpoint/owner swaps within
branches. The technology, actual eligibility model and report bounds are
invariant under these simultaneous relabelings. Apply the original rule to
relabeled reports and undo the payment relabeling. Every coverage, funding,
nonnegativity and TCWM constraint is linear in payments, so the average has
all guarantees with the same kappa. This is a proof reduction, not an anonymity
assumption. The argument below applies to the symmetric average and therefore
to existence of any mechanism on this same fixed model.

2. A SINGLE EXACT BOOTSTRAP
All power owners report1/R, hence P=1. Let epsilon=1/R. At auxiliary profile D,
branch1 reports(epsilon,epsilon); all other branches report(0,1). Thus F(D)=epsilon.
Let Y_P be total power-owner payment. Funding gives Y_P<=kappa epsilon. By
power-owner symmetry every power owner receives Y_P/R. The coalition of A_1,
B_1 and any ONE power owner has worth epsilon, so its pair payment obeys
 y_A1(D)+y_B1(D)>=epsilon-Y_P/R
                 >=epsilon(1-kappa epsilon).                (4)
This remains a valid inequality even if its right side is negative.

Let C_m have branches1,...,m report(1,1), all remaining branches report(0,1),
and exactly the same fixed power reports1/R, for m=1,...,M. Each C_m has worth1.
Write T_m for payment to its2m active branch owners. Branch permutations and
independent swaps of active endpoints preserve C_m, so each active owner is
paid q_m=T_m/(2m). Nonnegative payments to all other owners are unrestricted.
Restoring A_1,B_1 from D to C_1 and using TCWM for that fixed two-owner coalition
gives
 T_1>=epsilon(1-kappa epsilon).                              (5)

3. THE LOAD-BEARING TRANSITION COALITION
For m>=2, the ONLY report changed from C_{m-1} to C_m is A_m:0 to1.
Use the coalition
 S_m={A_j,B_j:1<=j<m} UNION {A_m}.
It contains every changed owner. B_m, all inactive-branch owners and all power
owners are outside; their reports are identical at the two profiles.
The coalition's low payment is T_{m-1}+y_A_m(C_{m-1})>=T_{m-1}, by nonnegativity.
Its high payment is (2m-1)q_m=(1-1/(2m))T_m, by active-owner symmetry. TCWM gives
 (1-1/(2m))T_m >= T_{m-1}.                                  (6)
This is the crucial improvement over the earlier harmonic argument: no inactive
payment is subtracted. Reserves to inactive branches, even to zero reporters,
cannot defeat(6). No inactive-payment axiom is used.

Iterating(6), using(5), and bounding T_M<=kappa because F(C_M)=1 yields
 kappa >= P_M epsilon(1-kappa epsilon).
Rearrange the linear inequality in kappa:
 kappa(1+P_M epsilon^2)>=P_M epsilon.
With epsilon=1/R this is exactly(2). The product identity follows by separating
the even and odd factors in(2M)!. This proves the theorem.

4. CONSEQUENCES AND QUANTIFIERS
For R=2B, formula(2) exceeds B exactly when P_M>4B^2, proving(3). This is an
explicit rational obstruction for a fixed finite model, and makes no assumption
about an optimal mechanism or funding infimum being attained. For any fixed
M one may also choose the integer R nearest sqrt(P_M); then(2) is asymptotic to
sqrt(P_M)/2 ~ pi^(1/4) M^(1/4)/(2 sqrt(2)). This uses n=2M+O(M^(1/4)) actual
owners. All capacities/profiles remain rational.

This is a LOWER bound. Its former quarter-power-versus-cube-root gap has since
been closed: COMMON_CAP_QUARTER_POWER_UPPER.txt gives O(M^(1/4)) funding for
arbitrary fixed mixed owners, matching the exponent here. The unified theorem
COMMON_CAP_GENERAL_ARITY_RATE.txt extends the matched rate to every fixed
private arity; COMMON_CAP_FULL_OUTSIDE_RATE.txt preserves it under full coalition
outside-production protection. Leading constants remain open. The older cube-root
upper and its fixed-cap architecture lower are correct historical intermediates,
not the current frontier.

The result applies a fortiori to full coalition outside-production incentives,
which imply TCWM. It does not apply to individual incentives alone: grand
marginals give factor3 with individual truthfulness and coalition coverage for
all three-sparse nonnegative production LPs. Together with the sharp3/2 TCWM
upper for all two-sparse production, this maintains and quantitatively
strengthens the qualitative consumption-arity boundary established earlier.

CORRECTION OF PREDECESSOR INTERPRETATION.
INACTIVE_BRANCH_RESERVE_BARRIER.txt had a valid but unnecessarily restricted
Theorem1 and a valid reserve inequality using a larger coalition. Its suggestion

No historical priority or field-level significance is asserted by this note.
