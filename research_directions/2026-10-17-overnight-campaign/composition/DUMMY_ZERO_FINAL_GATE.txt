GAME-DUMMY ZERO: A SHARP MIN-TWO ESCAPE AND AN AGGREGATE-PRICE BOUNDARY
17 October 2026. Bounded final gate; no independent novelty claim.
Composition lane independently derived the min-two consequence also found by
the unrestricted lane. This note records exact corollaries and a method boundary,
not a solution of the open min-three dummy-zero problem. Independent review
is requested for these consequences; the underlying signed transfer was audited.

1. CONTRACT AND THE QUESTION THAT IS ACTUALLY OPEN
There are finitely many fixed actual owners with fixed resource eligibility.
Production is min_r(C)=min(C1,...,Cr); reports and feasible restored bundles
are nonnegative. Coverage concerns every actual coalition. TCWM compares the
same coalition before and after some members restore resources; unchanged
members stay in that coalition. Full OUT additionally requires the payment
increase to cover production from its pooled restored bundle.

Owner i is a GAME DUMMY at a report b if
 min_r(b_i+b_R)-min_r(b_R)=0 for EVERY R subset N\{i}.
GAME-DUMMY-ZERO requires y_i(b)=0 in that event. This is a whole-owner game
property, not the claim that the owner holds zero resources or has zero grand
marginal alone. Payments are nonnegative.

For each fixed n, finite funding is already possible: the accepted scaled
binomial rule with p=1/n gives factor r*n^(r-1), full OUT for every coalition,
and coverage for every coalition. It pays every game dummy zero termwise.
The substantive question is a bound uniform in owner count, or its necessary
growth. This gate does not assert an unbudgeted fixed-n impossibility.

2. MIN-TWO HAS THE SHARP UNIFORM CONSTANTS EVEN WITH DUMMY ZERO
For an actual owner's two-resource bundle (a_i,b_i), put
 d_i=min(a_i,b_i), u_i=(a_i-b_i)^+, v_i=(b_i-a_i)^+,
 D=sum_i d_i, U=sum_i u_i, V=sum_i v_i.
Then every actual coalition satisfies the exact identity
 min(sum_S a_i,sum_S b_i)=sum_S d_i+min(sum_S u_i,sum_S v_i).

Let pi_i=u_i p(U,V)+v_i q(U,V) be either scalar rule in Sections3-4 of
composition/CONE_L1_EMBEDDABLE_SHARP_THEOREM.txt. Define
                       y_i=kappa*d_i+pi_i.                    (1)
The coefficient kappa on d_i is essential; replacing it by 1 is not justified
by the signed-compensation proof. For payment-only TCWM use kappa=3/2 and,
on U>=V>0, p=V/(2U), q=1-V/(2U), with the symmetric branch and continuous
zero-value payments. For full OUT use kappa=1+c and the explicit logarithmic
scalar prices in that theorem, where c(2-exp(-1/c))=1.

The accepted signed-transfer theorem applies by the fixed linear owner map
 (a_i,b_i) -> (t_i,x_i)=(a_i+b_i,a_i-b_i).
Its reserve value t-|x| is twice min(a,b). Its standalone term is 2d_i and
its positive/negative features are exactly u_i,v_i. Dividing its payments by
2 gives (1). Both retained and restored original nonnegative bundles map into
the cone; original owner identities and eligibility remain fixed. Therefore
(1) has all-profile coverage and TCWM/full OUT respectively, with total
funding at most kappa*min(sum a,sum b). This is a direct corollary of the
existing audited proof, not an unproved monotonicity assertion about netting.

Here is the complete additional game-dummy argument. A nonzero dummy must
have d_i=0, since its marginal to the empty coalition otherwise is positive.
If it is pure A, a_i>0,b_i=0, then it is a dummy exactly when a_j>=b_j for
every other actual owner j. Necessity follows because any b_j>a_j gives the
positive marginal min(a_i,b_j-a_j) to the singleton {j}. Sufficiency follows
by summing a_j>=b_j over any predecessor coalition. The pure-B case is symmetric.
Thus a pure-A dummy has V=0. All scalar payments pi_i are zero there by scalar
nonnegativity and funding against min(U,V)=0; also d_i=0. The same holds for a
pure-B dummy, and a zero owner has all three derived components zero. Hence
(1) satisfies GAME-DUMMY-ZERO at every report.

The unrestricted min-two lower bounds remain lower bounds after adding the
dummy axiom. Thus the constants 3/2 TCWM and 1+c full OUT are SHARP uniformly
over growing finite owner populations in this dummy-zero class. Exact lower
dependencies are root/COALITION_THREE_HALVES_CANDIDATE.txt, LOWER THEOREM, and
energy/SHARP_MULTIPOOL_OUTSIDE_CANDIDATE.txt at r=2, with its aggregate-price
reduction dependency. The new upper itself is ownership-aware.

3. REPORT-ADAPTIVE POSITIVE MARGINAL FORMULAS: EXACT CLASS EQUIVALENCE
For any fixed monotone production game and finite owner set, a finite
nonnegative payment rule can be represented as
 y_i(b)=sum_(R subset N\{i}) w_(i,R)(b)
                   [F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)],  w_(i,R)(b)>=0,
with finite, report-dependent coefficients, IF AND ONLY IF it is game-dummy-zero.
The forward implication is immediate. For the reverse, fix a subset ordering,
let M_i(b) be the maximum of the finitely many nonnegative marginal values,
and choose the first maximizing predecessor R_i. If M_i>0 set
 w_(i,R_i)(b)=y_i(b)/M_i(b) and all other coefficients zero. If M_i=0 the
owner is a dummy, y_i=0, and all weights are zero. This reproduces the rule at
every report without changing owners. No normalization, coefficient bound,
continuity or polynomial evaluation is asserted.

Consequently (1) is also an explicit existence result for report-adaptive
positive whole-owner marginal weighting with sharp constant funding on min2.
The accepted FIXED-weight lower of order s for r=2 therefore cannot be extended
to arbitrary report-dependent nonnegative weights: adaptation escapes that
architecture barrier on the same technology. This does not resolve min3.

4. AGGREGATE RESOURCE PRICES CANNOT PRESERVE THE DUMMY AXIOM
For every r>=2, with just TWO fixed fully eligible owners, no rule
                    y_i=b_i dot p(sum_j b_j)
can have coalition coverage, GAME-DUMMY-ZERO and even individual report
monotonicity on all nonnegative profiles. The price field may depend on the
fixed population size n=2, but not on how the aggregate is divided between
those owners. No funding premise is needed.

Proof. At C^-=(1/2,1,...,1), use the ownership profile
 b1=(1/2,...,1/2), b2=(0,1/2,...,1/2).
Owner2 is a game dummy: both its empty-predecessor and {1}-predecessor
marginals are zero. Therefore (1/2)sum_(j>1)p_j(C^-)=0. Grand-coalition
coverage gives
 (1/2)p1(C^-)+sum_(j>1)p_j(C^-) >=1/2,
so p1(C^-)>=1. This argument does not require each price to be nonnegative.

At C^+=(2,1,...,1), use b1=(1,0,...,0), b2=(1,...,1).
Owner1 is a game dummy, so p1(C^+)=0.

Now compare reports of those SAME two fully eligible owners:
 low:  b1=(1/2,0,...,0), b2=(0,1,...,1);
 high: b1=(2,0,...,0),   b2=(0,1,...,1).
The first owner restores only 3/2 units of its first resource. Its payment
decreases from at least 1/2 to zero. This violates individual monotonicity,
namely TCWM applied to the singleton coalition {1}. Grand-coalition-total
monotonicity alone would not give this contradiction. All profiles belong to
one fixed domain.

The same conclusion holds for owner-label-specific aggregate price vectors
p_i(C): averaging over the two label permutations preserves every axiom and
produces the forbidden common price field. The result excludes ownership-blind
linear resource pricing, not nonlinear dependence on the actual owner bundles.

Combining this impossibility with Section2 shows a precise limitation of the
existing aggregate-price existence reduction: it CANNOT be extended to preserve
GAME-DUMMY-ZERO. Its original theorem remains valid for its original axioms.
One cannot impose the new dummy condition on its output to infer an unrestricted
dummy-zero lower. This is a concrete method boundary, not merely a failed grid.

5. MIN-THREE STOP AND REMAINING QUESTION
Standalone netting alone does not make min3 residual mechanisms dummy-zero.
For example, take three fixed owners
 (1,0,0), (1,2,0), (1,0,2).
Every standalone minimum is zero. Owner1 is a game dummy: predecessors empty,
{2},{3} all have value zero with or without it; predecessor {2,3} has value2
with or without it. Yet residual grand production is2. The rule in
root/GENERAL_FACE_AVERAGE_TCWM.txt gives first-resource price
(1/3) integral_[0,1]^2 min(3,2u,2v) du dv=2/9 at aggregate(3,2,2),
and so pays this pure first-row dummy positively. Thus the min2
argument does not transfer simply by removing each owner's standalone minimum.

No uniform-owner min3 dummy-zero lower or constant upper was proved in this
gate. No finite LP optimum is promoted to a theorem. The remaining question is
whether ownership-aware dummy-zero rules have uniformly bounded funding on
min3, equivalently whether arbitrary report-adaptive positive whole-owner
marginal formulas do. New inequalities must use the actual ownership profile;
the aggregate-price reduction is unavailable under this extra axiom.
