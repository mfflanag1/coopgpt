AN EXACT POSITIVE-UNIT DECOMPOSITION FAILS ON ONE WEIGHTED SIX-CYCLE
17 October2026. Analytic obstruction to a specified representation class,
with exact finite replay. No claim against unrestricted weighted mechanisms.

QUESTION AND ANSWER
The new source-price identity makes every weighted forest a fixed positive
sum/integral of unit-profit subgraph production functions. Does this extend
to every single weighted even cycle? NO, even for an arbitrarily small public
profit perturbation of one edge of C6. This stops that unbounded-class route.

Fix vertices0,1,2 on the left and3,4,5 on the right, and cycle edges
  a=03, b=13, c=14, d=24, e=25, f=05.
Profits are1 on a,b,c,d,e and1+epsilon onf, for ANY epsilon>0. Suppose its
fractional production function could be written, for every real capacityc,
as a fixed nonnegative combination of unit-profit graph production functions
on these same six resource rows. It suffices to allow only the64 subgraphs
of this cycle: any extra edge on the same rows would yield positive value
on its two-endpoint coalition, where the target value iszero, and nonnegative
coefficients cannot cancel that contribution. Resource-copy lifts and changes
to row consumptions are outside this particular representation claim.

SIX-PROFILE SEPARATING FUNCTIONAL
Let L(F) be the following signed sum; omitted capacities arezero:
  minus F(0,1,1,1,0,1)
  plus  F(0,1,1,1,1,1)
  plus  F(1,0,1,1,0,1)
  minus F(1,0,1,1,1,0)
  minus F(1,1,0,0,1,1)
  plus  F(1,1,1,1,1,0).
These are coalition-deletion profiles of ONE full capacity-one profile with
six fixed actual owners, each owning its corresponding resource. No changing
owner set or virtual strategic identity is involved.

For any unit subgraph, write a,b,c,d,e,f in{0,1} for its six edge indicators.
The three negative terms are b+e, a+d, c+f. The positive terms are maximum
matching cardinalities of two four-edge paths and one three-edge path:
  M1=max(b+d,b+e,c+e),
  M2=max(a+e,f),
  M3=max(a+c,a+d,b+d).
All these graphs are bipartite, so with the binary capacities their fractional
production values equal these matching cardinalities. Thus
  L(F_unit)=M1+M2+M3−(a+b+c+d+e+f).
Now M1+M3>=b+c+d+max(a,e), by choosing respectively(b+d,a+c)
or(c+e,b+d) in those maxima. Also, for binary a,e,f,
  max(a,e)+max(a+e,f)>=a+e+f.
For the second inequality, if a=e=1 its left side is3 and the right is2+f;
otherwise min(a,e)=0 and max(a+e,f)>=f. Consequently L(F_unit)>=0 for EVERY
unit subgraph, and hence for every fixed nonnegative sum or integral of them.

For the weighted cycle, the six values in listed order are
  2, 2, max(2,1+epsilon), 2, 2+epsilon, 2.
Therefore
  L(F_weighted)=max(2,1+epsilon)−2−epsilon
               =−min(epsilon,1)<0.
This contradicts the putative representation, at actual coalition profiles
alone, so there is no exact all-real-profile positive-unit decomposition.

WHAT REMAINS POSSIBLE
This is not a lower bound on the subsidy of all truthful mechanisms. It does
not exclude lifted unit representations with resource copies, nonseparable
price rules, or owner-aware constructions. It also does not conflict with
the exact node-induced theorem: the cycle's alternating parity sums are3 and
3+epsilon, so it is not node-induced when epsilon>0.

There is a useful quantitative bridge to the energy lane's majorant result:
on this single positive cycle the optimal node-induced multiplicative majorant
distortion isrho=(3+epsilon)/3. The associated funding guarantee is3rho/2
=3/2+epsilon/2, strictly below2 for0<epsilon<1. Thus approximate public
majorants can progress beyond the exact positive-decomposition cone even
arbitrarily near the node-induced class. The energy lane owns that general
distortion theorem and its independent review; the present obstruction does
not rely on it.
