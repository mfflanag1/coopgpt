GOLDEN-RATIO COALITION-MONOTONE PAYMENTS ON LORENTZ RESOURCE CONES
17 October 2026. Root candidate, independent composition derivation.
Frozen for audit. Historical novelty unconfirmed.

THEOREM
Let K={(t,x):t>=||x||_2} in R x R^d, where d is any positive integer.
Production is F(t,x)=t-||x||_2. There are n fixed actual owners with reports
b_i=(t_i,x_i) in K. Joint binding underreports subtract D_i in K while
b_i-D_i remains in K. Put T=sum_i t_i, X=sum_i x_i, R=||X||,

 c=(sqrt(5)-1)/2,  b=1-c,  phi=1+c.

For T>0 set

            y_i=t_i - <x_i,X>/(c T+b R).                 (1)

At T=0 set every payment to zero. This one deterministic all-profile rule is
nonnegative, covers every coalition, and is transferable-coalition-report
monotone: whenever a fixed coalition A jointly increases its reports by
cone-feasible increments, sum_(i in A)y_i cannot decrease. Its total satisfies

            sum_i y_i <= phi F(T,X).                    (2)

Both the number of resource coordinates and number of owners are unbounded.
No synthetic owners, profile-dependent ownership, or free resources are used.
This theorem does NOT assert outside-production protection, even individually,
and it is a total-output funding bound, not a bound only on cooperation gain.

1. NONNEGATIVITY, COVERAGE AND FUNDING
For T>0 put s=R/T in[0,1], D=c+b s, and a(s)=s/D.
The price vector v=X/(cT+bR) has norm a(s)<=1, since D-s=c(1-s)>=0.
Thus y_i>=t_i-||x_i||>=0, and for every coalition A,
 sum_A y_i=t_A-v dot x_A>=t_A-||x_A||=F(b_A).
The total is

 T[1-s*a(s)] = F(T,X)*(c+s)/(c+b s),                   (3)

when s<1. The last ratio increases with s and tends to 1+c=phi as s tends
to1. At s=1 all owner vectors are aligned boundary vectors and every payment
is zero, agreeing with F=0. At T=0 every report is zero. Formula (3) supplies
continuity there and proves (2).

2. ALL-COALITION DERIVATIVE CRITERION
Fix a coalition aggregate (t,x) in K and an outside aggregate (u,z) in K.
The coalition's payment is
 Y=t-a(s)<x,e>, e=(x+z)/||x+z||, T=t+u, s=||x+z||/T.
Consider a joint restoration (dt,dx) in K. It suffices to show the gradient
of Y in (t,x) belongs to the Lorentz dual cone, which is the same cone.
For 0<s<1 write
 h=<x,e>/T, w=(x-<x,e>e)/T.
Direct differentiation gives temporal coefficient and spatial negative-gradient

 A=1+s a'(s)h,
 V=[a(s)+a'(s)h]e+[a(s)/s]w.                           (4)

We need A>=||V||. Feasibility ||x||+||X-x||<=T is exactly the prolate ellipse

 h=(s+q)/2,  -1<=q<=1,
 ||w||^2 <= (1-s^2)(1-q^2)/4.                         (5)

This follows by squaring the two-focus ellipse equation, or by the standard
ellipse with foci 0 and s e and major-axis length1. The boundary formula also
follows directly because the two distances are (1+s q)/2 and (1-s q)/2.

3. EXACT NONNEGATIVITY OF THE DERIVATIVE SLACK
For a=s/D, a'=c/D^2, multiply A^2-||V||^2 by D^4, and use the maximum
permitted transverse norm in(5). Calling the resulting numerator N, we have

 N=(D^2+s c h)^2-(sD+c h)^2
                  -D^2(1-s^2)(1-q^2)/4.

The polynomial P(q)=4(1+s)N/(1-s^2) is quadratic with coefficients

 P(q)=A_2 q^2+A_1 q+A_0,
 A_2=(1+s)(D^2-c^2),
 A_1=-4s c bD-2c^2 s(1+s),
 A_0=4cD^2(D+s)-4s^2 c bD-c^2(1+s)s^2-D^2(1+s).

For 0<s<1, A_2>0 and its minimizer is

 q_*=c[2bD+c(1+s)]/[(1+s)b(D+c)].

Using c^2+c=1, numerator minus denominator is exactly

 (1-s)[(7-11c)+(2-3c)s]>=0.                            (6)

Both constants in square brackets are positive. Hence q_*>=1, so P reaches
its minimum on[-1,1] at q=1. There w=0, h=(1+s)/2, and the temporal coefficient
minus the positive longitudinal spatial coefficient equals

 A-[a+a'h] = c(c-1/2)(1-s)^2/D^2 >=0.                  (7)

Also A>0 throughout the ellipse: h>=(s-1)/2, so
 A>=1-s c(1-s)/(2D^2)>0,
because D>=c, s(1-s)<=1/4 and 1/(8c)<1. Equations(6)-(7) prove A^2>=||V||^2
and A>=0, hence the desired derivative inequality for every feasible cone
increment, not just coordinatewise increases or one-resource reports.

At s=0, X=0 and (1) is differentiable in X with derivative x/(cT).
Feasibility gives ||x||<=T/2, so A=1 and ||V||<=1/(2c)<1.
For boundary s=1 use continuity. Along a feasible affine segment, the
nonnegative function T-||X|| is concave; an interior zero forces it to be
identically zero. Thus a segment of feasible reports either stays
on the same boundary ray, with all payments zero, or lies in the interior
apart from a possible endpoint. Integrate the nonnegative directional
derivative on the interior segment and take endpoint limits. T=0 is handled
similarly by homogeneity/continuity. Thus every weak coalition increase is
covered on the whole domain.

4. SHARPNESS WITHIN A REGULAR RADIAL CLASS
For d>=2 consider rules of the restricted form
 y_i=t_i-a(s)<x_i,e>, s=||X||/T, e=X/||X||,
where a is continuous on[0,1], C^1 near1, and the same radial function is used
for all owners and all profiles. Assume all-profile coverage and TCWM, and a
finite uniform total-output funding factor. Coverage on arbitrarily small
boundary bundles in an interior total forces |a(s)|<=1. Finite funding forces
a(1)=1. Write gamma=a'(1)>=0. The funding ratio tends to 1+gamma at s=1.

Take the ellipse-boundary coalitions in(5), with h tending to a fixed h_0
in(0,1) as s tends to1. They are realizable by TWO fixed actual owners,
each holding its indicated boundary Lorentz bundle. The gradient criterion
is necessary because the first owner may restore any cone increment.
Expanding its squared slack gives

 lim_(s->1-) [A^2-||V||^2]/[2(1-s)]
        =(1-h_0)[gamma+(gamma^2-1)h_0].                (8)

If gamma^2+gamma<1, choose h_0 close enough to1 and this limit is negative.
Thus TCWM requires gamma>=c. Every such regular radial rule has worst-case
funding at least phi, attained by(1). This is NOT a lower bound for all
mechanisms: it fixes the reserve coefficient at1 and assumes regularity at
the boundary. More general price fields or owner-dependent mechanisms are
not excluded.

5. EXACT OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION FAILURE AND GAIN-ONLY LIMITATION
Let owner1 hold (1,-1), owner2 hold (3,2), in one exposure dimension.
Restore (1,0) to owner1. Its payment increases by

 1+1/(1+4c)-1/(1+3c) < 1=F(1,0).

Hence even individual full outside-production protection fails. This is
consistent with TCWM because the increase remains nonnegative.
Also a single interior noncentral owner has no cooperation gain but receives
more than F from(1). Do not convert(2) to an S+phi G statement.

6. CONTEXT AND PRIORITY
The Lorentz cone, its self-duality, two-focus ellipse geometry, and the
calculus used above are established ingredients. This note proves its own
needed geometry and does not rely on an unchecked symmetric-cone extension.
The prior composition/CONE_LORENTZ_GAIN_TWO.txt concerns individual full OUT
and funding only the cooperation gain; it is a different guarantee and uses
an established Djokovic norm inequality. The present theorem protects
simultaneous coalition reports, but does not protect outside production.
