DIMENSION-FREE COALITION AND OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION GUARANTEES FOR FOUR-NORM RESERVES
17 October 2026. Composition proof candidate; historical novelty unconfirmed.

THEOREM
Let d,n be arbitrary positive integers. Fixed actual owners report b_i=(t_i,x_i)
in K_4={(t,x) in R x R^d : t>=||x||_4}. Production is

                       F(t,x)=t-||x||_4.

Feasible binding withdrawals require both withdrawn and retained bundles in
K_4, and coalition members may jointly change their entire mixed bundles.
Let T=sum_i t_i, X=sum_i x_i. For T>0 define

       y_i=t_i-sum_j x_ij X_j^3/T^3;                   (1)

set all payments to zero at T=0. Then this deterministic all-profile rule is
nonnegative, covers every coalition, is transferable-coalition-report
monotone, and has total funding at most4F(T,X).
Multiplying (1) by512/431 gives FULL coalition outside-production protection
and total funding at most

                       (2048/431)F(T,X).              (2)

Thus fixed p=4 admits a bound independent of BOTH exposure dimension and
owner count. This is not a gain-only S+constant*G result and does not claim
these constants are optimal among all mechanisms. No auxiliary owners or
profile-dependent ownership transformation are used.

1. COVERAGE AND FUNDING
Let s=||X||_4/T in[0,1]. The vector v=(X_j^3/T^3)_j has dual norm
||v||_(4/3)=s^3<=1. Hence each owner receives at least F(b_i), and each
coalition A receives t_A-v dot x_A>=F(b_A).
Summing (1) gives

 T-||X||_4^4/T^3 = F(T,X)(1+s+s^2+s^3)<=4F(T,X).      (3)

This identity remains valid on the boundary s=1. Nonnegativity and the
bound by4T give continuity at the zero profile. The norm, Holder inequality,
and perspective homogeneity here are established ingredients.

2. SCALAR QUARTIC INEQUALITY
For theta in[0,1], r=1-theta, and ANY real u,v,d, put x=theta u, z=r v,
X=x+z. Then

 d X^3+3x d X^2-3x X^3
 <=[3theta r u^4+3r^2 v^4+(1+3theta)d^4]/4.           (4)

Here d denotes a scalar test coordinate, unrelated to the exposure dimension.
The assertion allows arbitrary signs; it is not a positive-orthant inequality.

A complete sum-of-squares certificate follows. Set
 h=u-v, k=d-X, L=1+3theta, M=1-3theta+3theta^2.
The right side of(4) minus its left side is exactly

 L/4 * k^2(k+2X)^2
 +3theta r^2 M/4 * h^2[h+2(1-2theta)X/M]^2
 +L/2 * X^2[k-3theta r h/L]^2
 +3theta r^2(1-theta+7theta^2-6theta^3)/(2LM)*X^2 h^2.  (5)

Every coefficient is nonnegative: L>=1, M=3(theta-1/2)^2+1/4>0, and
1-theta+7theta^2-6theta^3=(1-theta)+theta^2(7-6theta)>=0.
To verify the identity without guessing, write u=X+r h, v=X-theta h,
d=X+k. Expanding the difference in(4) gives

 L[3X^2k^2/2+Xk^3+k^4/4]
 +3theta r^2[3X^2h^2/2+(1-2theta)Xh^3+M h^4/4]
 -3theta r X^2 h k.

Completing the k and h squares yields(5). The attached checker verifies the
cleared-denominator polynomial identity exactly, as well as rational cases.

3. FROM THE SCALAR INEQUALITY TO ALL COALITION INCENTIVES
Normalize T=1 at an arbitrary positive-total profile. For a fixed coalition,
write its aggregate as(theta,x) and the outside aggregate as(r,z), with
||x||_4<=theta and ||z||_4<=r. Choose u=x/theta and v=z/r when denominators
are positive; at zero use0, since the corresponding exposure is zero.
For ANY vector d with ||d||_4<=1, apply(4) in each coordinate and sum. Its
right side is at most
 [3theta r+3r^2+1+3theta]/4=1.
Therefore

 ||X^3+3x times X^2||_(4/3) <=1+3<x,X^3>.              (6)

Powers and products in(6) are coordinatewise. The dual-norm variational
identity justifies taking the supremum over every signed test vector d.

The fixed coalition's payment Y=t_A-sum_j x_Aj X_j^3/T^3 has derivative,
along aggregate restoration (dt,dx),

 Y'=A dt-V dot dx,
 A=1+3sum_j x_Aj X_j^3/T^4,
 V_j=X_j^3/T^3+3x_Aj X_j^2/T^3.

After rescaling, (6) says A>=||V||_(4/3). Hence every cone-feasible direction
(dt,dx), dt>=||dx||_4, gives Y'>=0 by Holder. The formula is smooth at every
T>0, including X=0 and norm-boundary profiles. Integrate along any straight
joint restoration of the SAME coalition. The only possible T=0 endpoint is
handled by continuity. This proves all-profile coalition monotonicity.

4. FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION SCALING
For X!=0 let u^*_j=X_j^3/||X||_4^3, a dual unit support of the norm at X.
Let h=<x_A,u^*>/T. Cone feasibility implies h>=(s-1)/2: indeed
 h>=-t_A/T and s-h=<X-x_A,u^*>/T<=1-t_A/T; combining gives the bound.
Thus

 A=1+3s^3 h >=1-(3/2)s^3(1-s)>=1-81/512=431/512,      (7)

because s^3(1-s) has its maximum27/256 at s=3/4. At X=0, A=1.
After multiplication by512/431, the temporal derivative is at least1 and
still at least the spatial dual norm. Consequently, for every cone direction,

 Y'_scaled >=dt-||dx||_4=F(dt,dx).

Indeed, writing alpha for the scaled temporal coefficient and beta for the
scaled spatial dual norm, alpha>=1 and alpha>=beta imply
 (alpha-1)dt-(beta-1)||dx||_4
 =(alpha-1)(dt-||dx||_4)+(alpha-beta)||dx||_4>=0. Integrating along a joint coalition
restoration gives exactly F(sum_A D_i), including arbitrary mixed exposure
withdrawals. Equation(3) proves(2).

The temporal minimum431/512 is attained by opposite collinear coalition and
outside exposures with total norm ratio s=3/4. Thus512/431 is the smallest
UNIFORM multiplier of rule(1) that can satisfy full OUT. This does not bound
other mechanisms. The funding ratios4 and2048/431 are suprema as s tends to1.

5. CONSEQUENCE, LIMITS AND NEXT GATE
The Euclidean construction is not isolated: a non-Euclidean smooth norm with
arbitrary dimension also admits bounded coalition-safe funding. The proof
uses a scalar quartic SOS that survives summation over any number of signed
coordinates. This settles the requested p=4 existence gate, while leaving
other p, sharp constants, and economically calibrated uncertainty contracts
open. The absence of a dimension-free bound for the infinity-norm limit
therefore does not by itself rule out fixed finite-p guarantees.
No general p theorem is asserted from numerics. A future extension must
supply the corresponding scalar inequality or another all-profile argument.
