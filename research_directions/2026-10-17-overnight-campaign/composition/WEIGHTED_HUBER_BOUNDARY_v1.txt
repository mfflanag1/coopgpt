WEIGHTED RESOURCE GRAPHS: GLOBAL-SCALE HUBER BOUNDARY
17 October 2026. Candidate successor for independent review.
The unit-profit theorem is in HUBER_PRICE_THREE_HALVES_v2.txt.

QUESTION
Does applying its convex price construction with one public global profit
scale extend the strict-below-two funding theorem to arbitrary weighted edges?
Answer for THIS construction: NO. It retains coalition-total underreport
monotonicity and exact coalition coverage, but its sharp uniform funding
factor becomes two. The obstruction already uses a connected weighted path.
This does not rule out other weighted mechanisms or all weighted price rules.

MODEL AND RULE
Activity e=uv consumes one unit at u and v and has fixed public profit w_e>=0.
Discard zero-profit activities. If none remain, pay zero. Otherwise divide
all w_e by the fixed technology scale W=max_e w_e, so 0<w_e<=1; restore W to
all payments/output at the end. Define h(t)=t^2 below1/2, t-1/4 above1/2.
Choose p minimizing sum_v c_v h(p_v) over p>=0 and p_u+p_v>=w_uv. One may
restrict p<=1 without changing the optimum. Pay i sum_v b_iv p_v.
Zero capacities use the unique prices on the positive induced graph and
p_v=1 on zero vertices, as in the unit-profit construction.
The value1 on a zero vertex is a dual-cover convention, not a claim about
the limiting regularized optimizer. As positive capacities tend to zero,
its limiting price can differ from1; the actual payment on that vertex is
zero in either convention. Positive-coordinate prices and all owner/coalition
payments have the same limit. This distinction also applies in the unit note.

CLAIMS
(A) The positive-coordinate prices are unique; actual-owner payments and sums
    of coalition payments are continuous and own/coalition-underreport monotone.
(B) Every actual-owner coalition is covered, and total payment is at most2F.
(C) The supremum funding factor for this global-scale rule is exactly2,
    even on connected four-vertex resource paths with all capacities positive.

PROOF OF UNIQUE PRICES AND THE ACTIVE-COMPONENT INVARIANT
Existence is compactness. A price above1 can decrease to1. At positive c no
optimal vertex has price1: raise every zero price by epsilon and lower every
price-one vertex by epsilon. Tight zero/one edges have weight1 and stay tight;
other affected constraints have positive slack and remain feasible for small
epsilon. The cost rises only quadratically at zero prices and decreases
linearly at positive-cost price-one vertices. Thus 0<=p_v<1.

Unlike the unit case, p_v=0 can occur at nonisolated positive-capacity vertices
whose incident low-profit constraints are slack. It cannot be assumed away.
KKT nonetheless gives nonnegative edge multipliers lambda with
  degree_lambda(v)=c_v h'(p_v)=c_v min(2p_v,1).          (1)
Indeed at p_v=0, the lower-bound stationarity is -degree_lambda(v)-alpha_v=0,
so both nonnegative quantities vanish. Upper bounds are inactive. Multipliers
are zero on non-tight edges.

Strict convexity below1/2 fixes all coordinates below1/2 across any two
minimizers. At every price p_v>1/2 a tight incident edge must exist, or v's
price can decrease. Its neighbor has p_u=w_uv-p_v<1/2 because w_uv<=1. Thus
the same uniqueness argument as the unit case applies. Positive-coordinate
continuity and zero-capacity payment limits follow by compactness as before.

Every connected tight-edge component is either pinned (an odd cycle or a
zero-price vertex fixes all affine edge equations) or is bipartite with
  p_v=a_v+sigma_v t, sigma_v in {+1,-1},                 (2)
where public offsets a_v come from the tight edge weights. Fix a smooth piece
where tight edges and the signs of p_v-1/2 are unchanged. If any p_v remains
identically1/2 on the piece then t is fixed and all its prices are constant.
Otherwise let L={v:p_v<1/2}, H={v:p_v>1/2}, C_L=sum_L c_v.
No tight edge joins two H vertices, since its price sum would exceed1>=w_e.

For each bipartition sign s, every high-price vertex on side s is saturated
in (1), and all its incident positive flow goes to LOW vertices on side -s.
Consequently
  C_{H,s} <= sum_{v in L,-s} degree_lambda(v)
          <= C_{L,-s},
and therefore
  C_s := sum_{sigma_v=s}c_v <= C_L.                     (3)
For a non-pinned varying component C_L>0. This capacity domination, rather
than a false assertion that one entire bipartition is low, is the key new
weighted incentive invariant.

COALITION-TOTAL INCENTIVES
Summing signed KKT equations over a bipartite tight component gives
  sum_v sigma_v c_v h'(p_v)=0.
Differentiate an increase of one coalition-owned report coordinate at k:
  dt/dc_k = -sigma_k h'(p_k)/(2 C_L).                   (4)
Let B_s be that coalition's total report on sign s and Bsign=B_+-B_-.
Its payment derivative is
  p_k - sigma_k Bsign h'(p_k)/(2 C_L).                 (5)
We have sigma_k Bsign<=C_{sigma_k}<=C_L by(3). If k is low,
(5)>=p_k(1-C_{sigma_k}/C_L)>=0. If k is high,
(5)>=p_k-C_{sigma_k}/(2C_L)>=p_k-1/2>0. On pinned pieces the
derivative is just p_k>=0.

There are finitely many tight-edge, zero-coordinate and low/high patterns.
On each free bipartite pattern, the signed KKT equation expresses t as a
rational function of capacities with denominator2 C_L; pinned patterns have
constant prices. Finite rational candidate intersections and continuity give
the same finite-interval/clopen argument as the unit theorem. Boundaries with
p=0 or p=1/2 are included by continuity. Regularize objective capacities to
c+epsilon*1 to cover zero aggregate resources; coalition holdings still obey
B_s<=C_s. Thus every joint binding underreport cannot increase the coalition's
sum of payments. All actual owner identities and eligibility sets stay fixed.

COALITION COVERAGE AND THE FACTOR TWO
Dual feasibility p_u+p_v>=w_uv directly covers each actual coalition.
Let d_v=h'(p_v). For each positive-lambda edge define
  x_uv=lambda_uv/max(d_u,d_v),                          (6)
and use x=0 on zero-lambda edges. Positive lambda implies p_u,p_v>0 by(1).
For every v, sum_incident x<=degree_lambda(v)/d_v=c_v, so x is a feasible
production plan. Also, by(1), total payment equals
  Y=sum_edges lambda_uv (p_u/d_u+p_v/d_v).             (7)
On a low/low edge, d_v=2p_v, so the coefficient in(7) is1, while its profit
coefficient in sum w_e x_e is (p_u+p_v)/(2max(p_u,p_v))>=1/2.
On a low/high edge, write low p=a<=1/2 and high p=b>=1/2. The respective
coefficients are1/2+b and a+b, and1/2+b<=2(a+b). No high/high edge can carry
positive lambda. Half-price endpoints can be treated in either case.
Thus Y<=2 sum_e w_e x_e<=2F. This proof does not mistakenly treat rounded
prices {0,1/2,1} as a weighted dual cover; low/low positive-profit edges would
invalidate that shortcut.

EXACT CONNECTED-PATH OBSTRUCTION
For an integer m>=1, use path0--1--2--3 with fixed profits
  w01=1/4, w12=1/4, w23=1,
and capacities
  c=(m,1,1/m,1/m).
All resources and profits are positive. The claimed unique price optimum is
  p=(1/[4(m+1)], m/[4(m+1)], 1/2, 1/2).
Its edge01 and edge23 constraints are tight; edge12 is strictly slack.
Exact KKT multipliers are
  lambda01=m/[2(m+1)], lambda12=0, lambda23=1/m.
They satisfy(1), so convexity certifies optimality; uniqueness was proved above.
The true production value is
  F=1/4+1/m:
primal x01=1,x12=0,x23=1/m attains it, and dual prices(0,1/4,0,1)
certify its upper bound. The payment total is
  Y=m/[2(m+1)]+1/m.
Consequently Y/F tends to2 as m tends to infinity. Together with the upper
bound this proves exact rule-specific worst-case factor2.

INTERPRETATION / STOPPING GATE
Global scale covariance avoids the naive fixed-knee failure at profits above1
and does preserve the strengthened incentive property. It does not yield a
strictly-below-two weighted theorem. The connected path shows why: a weak
activity can remain in the wholly quadratic regime, behaving almost like the
factor-two quadratic price rule, while a negligible-capacity high-profit
activity determines the global knee. Inactive connecting edges do not help.
This stops attempts to rescue the theorem by merely replacing the fixed unit
knee with the maximum edge profit. It leaves open multiscale/owner-aware
mechanisms and economically structured route values. No unrestricted weighted
mechanism lower bound, and no historical novelty claim, follows.
