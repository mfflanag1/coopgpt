SUPPORT-RADIAL PRICES NECESSARILY LOSE A LINEAR FACTOR IN p
17 October 2026. Architecture theorem, not an unrestricted lower bound.

CLAIM
Fix p>=2. On the ell_p reserve cone with exposure dimension2, consider

 y_i=t_i-a(s)<x_i,e*(X)>,
 s=||X||_p/T, e*(X)=J_p(X)/||X||_p^(p-1),

with reserve coefficient1, a locally absolutely continuous on(0,1), and
C1 near1. TCWM means that every fixed coalition's TOTAL payment is
nondecreasing on restoring a cone-feasible bundle to any of its members,
with outsiders unchanged. Assume coalition coverage, all-profile TCWM, and finite uniform
total funding factor kappa. Then

                       kappa>=1+(p+1)/8.              (1)

Therefore this regular norm-support-radial architecture cannot improve the
general p upper to O(sqrt(p)). A constant reserve multiplier lambda>=1
does not evade the order lower: normalizing the payments gives the same
TCWM conditions and multiplies the boundary funding ratio by lambda.
The theorem permits arbitrary fixed actual owners and uses only TWO-owner
profiles and feasible cone restorations. It is NOT a lower on all mechanisms.

1. COVERAGE AND FINITE FUNDING AT THE BOUNDARY
Grand-coalition coverage at each interior profile implies a(s)<=1. Total
funding is T[1-s a(s)], whereas F=T(1-s). Finiteness forces a(1)=1.
Writing gamma=a'(1), continuity near1 gives the boundary funding ratio

                 lim_(s->1) [1-s a(s)]/(1-s)=1+gamma. (2)

Hence kappa>=1+gamma. We bound gamma below using two necessary TCWM tests.

2. A COLLINEAR NECESSARY DIFFERENTIAL INEQUALITY
At any ratio0<s<1, take a unit vector e and two retained owner reports

 b_1=((1+s)/2, ((1+s)/2)e),
 b_2=((1-s)/2,-((1-s)/2)e).

Their aggregate is (1,s e). Restore a positive multiple of the cone-boundary
direction(1,e) to owner1. Differentiating its payment gives the necessary
inequality

                    1-a(s)-(1-s^2)a'(s)/2>=0.         (3)

This uses a restoration of a feasible retained report, not a withdrawal from
a boundary report. Set b(s)=1-a(s)>=0. Equation(3) gives
b'(s)>=-2b(s)/(1-s^2). Integrating on[s,u] and sending u to1, using(2), gives

          gamma>=[1-a(s)](1+s)/[2(1-s)]               (4)

for every interior s where a is continuous. The same conclusion follows
under the stated local absolute continuity because the differential bound
holds almost everywhere.

3. A TRANSVERSE TWO-OWNER TEST
Put e=2^(-1/p)(1,1), w=(1-s)/2, and use the retained reports

 b_1=(w, w 2^(-1/p)(1,-1)),
 b_2=(1-w, s e-w 2^(-1/p)(1,-1)).

The first lies on the cone boundary. The second is feasible because
||s e-w 2^(-1/p)(1,-1)||_p<=max(s+w,|s-w|)=s+w=1-w.
The aggregate again is(1,s e). The coalition is owner1 alone. Its support
projection h=<x_1,e*(e)> is0. Therefore the temporal coefficient of its
directional derivative is1, while the spatial coefficient is

 V=a(s)e*(e)+(p-1)a(s)D x_1/s,
 D=diag(|e_j|^(p-2)).

Every cone direction is a feasible restoration of this retained owner. TCWM
therefore forces ||V||_q<=1, q=p/(p-1). Direct calculation gives

 ||V||_q=|a(s)|[(|1+z|^q+|1-z|^q)/2]^(1/q),
 z=(p-1)(1-s)/(2s).

Convexity of |.|^q, applied to z+1 and z-1, bounds the bracket below by z.
Thus the necessary price bound is

                |a(s)|<=2s/[(p-1)(1-s)].             (5)

Unlike a numerical derivative search, this is an exact all-report constraint
from a specified pair of actual owners; the only tested dimension is2.

4. COMBINING THE TESTS
Choose s=(p-1)/(p+3), so z=2. Equation(5) gives a(s)<=1/2. Equation(4)
then yields

 gamma>=(1+s)/[4(1-s)]=(p+1)/8.

Together with(2), this proves(1). For a rule multiplied by fixed lambda>=1,
TCWM is unchanged and its funding ratio is lambda times(2). The normalized
condition a(s)<=1 still follows from nonnegativity for an aligned cone-
boundary owner at that aggregate ratio, even if dividing by lambda weakens
positive-value coverage. Thus the same linear-in-p obstruction remains. No global lower for general reserve-
coefficient functions, nonradial prices, or owner-dependent constructions
is asserted.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE NEXT ATTACK
The earlier p-power mechanism gives O(p), and this note proves that the
entire regular support-radial route cannot reduce that order. An improved
O(sqrt(p)) mechanism, if it exists, must use additional structure, such as
owner-dependent cancellation compensation or a genuinely nonradial price
field. This is separate from the all-mechanism lower
Omega(sqrt(p/log p)) in P_NORM_GROWTH_FINITE_OWNER_LOWER.txt.
Historical priority of this architecture obstruction remains unconfirmed.
