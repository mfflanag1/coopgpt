FULLY POLYNOMIAL EXACT-INCENTIVE APPROXIMATION FOR TWO-SPARSE PRODUCTION
Frozen candidate, 17 October 2026. Independent audit pending.

THEOREM
For any finite nonnegative RATIONAL two-sparse production technology (A,w),
any rational 0<epsilon<=1, and any rational fixed-owner report profile, one
can compute rational payments in time polynomial in the technology/report
binary encoding lengths and 1/epsilon with:
 (i) coalition coverage at EVERY real profile;
 (ii) coalition-total payment monotonicity under simultaneous binding whole-
      bundle underreports, for arbitrary fixed mixed owners;
 (iii) total payments at most (3/2+epsilon)F(c).
This is an FPTAS in funding factor, with exact incentives and exact coverage,
not approximately truthful payments. The same construction using the audited
unit full-OUT mechanism gives exact full OUT with funding at most
(2+epsilon)F(c). Full original marginal floors are not asserted for this
approximate representation; see section 4.

A single finite public graph and a fixed positive linear owner-copy map define
the rule on all REAL report profiles. Polynomial exact evaluation is asserted
for rational reports; the mathematical rule and its incentive guarantees have
all-real-report scope. The exact optimum factor 3/2 is attainable by the finite
vertex-coordinate construction, potentially exponential; no exact polynomial
3/2 algorithm is claimed here.

1. NORMALIZE, BOUND, AND INTEGERIZE
Use section 2 of HETEROGENEOUS_TWO_SPARSE_EXACT_LIFT.txt to extract unary gamma
and retain positive residual binary profits. This exact rational arithmetic
has polynomial bit cost. If no residual activity remains, pay gamma.b_i and
stop. Form the explicit bounded dual polytope
 P={p: 0<=p_v<=M_v, a_e p_u+b_e p_v>=w_e},
 M_v=max_e w_e/a_ev (or zero for isolated v).
Write EVERY inequality in the form C_j p>=d_j and clear denominators in each
row INCLUDING its right-hand side. This yields an integral matrix C and
integral vector d, computed with polynomial bit length. Let
 H=max(1, max absolute entry of C and d),   D=n! H^n,   delta=1/D.
The n-dimensional formula is safe even when P is lower-dimensional: every
vertex still has n linearly independent active inequality normals, by the
standard definition of a polytope vertex. Take such a square active submatrix.
Its nonzero determinant is an integer, of absolute value at most n!H^n=D.
By Cramer's rule every positive vertex coordinate is a ratio of two nonzero
integers with denominator of absolute value at most D. Hence
 p_v>0 at a vertex of P  ==>  p_v>=delta.                            (4)
No enumeration of vertices is needed to compute delta. Since the all-M vector
is a vertex of P (the n upper-bound normals are independent), every positive
M_v itself is >=delta.

2. PUBLIC GEOMETRIC GRIDS
For the 3/2+epsilon result put eta=2epsilon/3; for 2+epsilon put eta=epsilon/2.
For each nonisolated v use the sorted grid
 {0, delta, delta(1+eta), delta(1+eta)^2,... below M_v, M_v}.
Remove repeated endpoints and make interval copies with capacity width times
c_v. Use exactly the edge condition (3) of the exact-lift theorem:
 a_e lower(I)+b_e lower(J)<w_e for some original activity e.
The construction depends only on public technology and epsilon, not reports.
The primal height embedding is unchanged and proves, uniformly for every c,
 F_res(c)<=G(c):=F_H(Bc).                                           (5)

3. MULTIPLICATIVE DUAL ROUNDING, INCLUDING ZERO PRICES
Choose an optimal VERTEX p of P for c. Round each p_v upward to the first grid
point q_v>=p_v, retaining q_v=0 exactly when p_v=0. By (4), every positive p_v
lies at or above delta, so
 q_v<=(1+eta)p_v; in particular no additive error occurs at zero coordinates.
Set a bin's cover price to 1 iff its lower endpoint is less than q_v. If a
lift edge were uncovered, its lower endpoints would be >=q>=p, contradicting
original dual feasibility and the strict edge condition. The cover has exact
cost c.q <=(1+eta)c.p. Therefore
 F_res(c)<=G(c)<=(1+eta)F_res(c)               for EVERY c>=0.        (6)
This includes profiles with F_res(c)=0: both sides force G(c)=0. An arbitrary
fixed small first interval without a vertex-coordinate lower bound would not
justify (6); the Cramer bound is load-bearing.

4. PAYMENTS, INCENTIVES, COVERAGE, FUNDING
Apply the audited unit-Huber rule to H with owner reports B b_i, then add
linear payment gamma.b_i. Linked report maps preserve all admissible coalition
underreports exactly. Graph all-profile coalition monotonicity gives original
all-profile coalition monotonicity. Coalition coverage follows from
 payment(S)>=gamma.b_S+G(b_S)>=gamma.b_S+F_res(b_S)=F(b_S).
Total payment is at most
 gamma.c+(3/2)G(c) <= gamma.c+(3/2)(1+eta)F_res(c)
 <= (3/2+epsilon)F(c).
All inequalities are exact. There is no randomized owner sampling or rounding
of a report, and no assumption that owners control copies separately.

For full OUT, graph full OUT covers withheld production G(d)>=F_res(d), so
linear plus graph payments also satisfy original full OUT, with funding2+epsilon.
CAUTION: approximate equality (6) alone does NOT transfer the full original
owner marginal floor: G(c)-G(c-b_i) need not dominate the corresponding F_res
marginal. Thus the full-OUT FPTAS conclusion in this note DOES NOT assert the
marginal floor unless an independent argument establishes it. The exact lift
DOES transfer full floors. This distinction does not affect TCWM or full OUT.

5. BIT COMPLEXITY AND GRAPH SIZE
Let B be the total binary encoding length of the explicit rational technology
after elementary residualization. Rational M and rowwise denominator clearing
have bit lengths polynomial in B and n; products of denominators have at most
the sum of their bit lengths. Thus log H and log D are polynomial in B,n.
Each grid has at most
 2+ceil(log(M_v/delta)/log(1+eta))
interval endpoints, with log(M_v/delta) polynomial in B,n. Since eta<=1,
log(1+eta)>=eta/2, giving a polynomial number K of bins in B,n,1/epsilon.
Each endpoint is a rational product of at most K factors of the rational
1+eta times delta (or M_v), so its binary encoding length is also polynomial
in B,n,1/epsilon and the encoding length of epsilon.
The graph has at most nK vertices and mK^2 candidate pairs before duplicate
edge removal, with exact rational comparisons to decide each edge. Computing
all virtual owner reports requires a polynomial number of rational products.
The exact unit-Huber density/mincut algorithm is polynomial in graph size and
rational capacity bit length (EXACT_HUBER_ALGORITHM.txt). The full-OUT graph
rule q=min(2p,1) uses the same exact prices. Composing these bounds establishes
fully polynomial running time. This is a bit-complexity theorem, not a claim
that this conservative grid is fast for large practical instances.

6. STATUS / PRIORITY
Correctness rests on the all-capacity bin-lift proof and independently audited
unit graph mechanisms. Rational LP vertex bounds and multiplicative geometric
grids are standard ingredients. Their use for exact linked-owner incentive
transfer on arbitrary heterogeneous two-sparse technology is a proposed
contribution with historical novelty unconfirmed. This stronger scheme would
supersede the earlier exponential-in-1/epsilon separated-weight-block scheme;
that earlier proof remains valid as an independently checked stepping stone.
