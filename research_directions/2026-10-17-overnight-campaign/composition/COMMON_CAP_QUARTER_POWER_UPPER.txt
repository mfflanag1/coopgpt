MATCHING QUARTER-POWER TCWM UPPER FOR THE COMMON-CAP FAMILY
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate for independent audit. Novelty unconfirmed.

THEOREM.
For every finite M>=1 and every fixed actual-owner model with arbitrary mixed
bundles across the2M+1 resources, the technology
 F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_{j=1}^M min(a_j,b_j))
admits nonnegative, coalition-covering, all-profile TCWM payments with total
funding at most
 [2+max{1,(16M/27)^(1/4)}]F.                                 (1)
Combined with UNRESTRICTED_COMMON_CAP_QUARTER_POWER_LOWER.txt, this determines
the uniform-over-owner-model optimal growth rate as THETA(M^(1/4)). The leading
constant remains unclosed. The lower uses fixed pure owners; the upper covers
arbitrary fixed mixed owners and every whole-bundle restoration. No full outside-
production incentive or grand-marginal-floor assertion is made for this rule.

1. A PARAMETER-DEPENDENT BRANCH CAP
Fix a public real C>=1, independent of reports. Set
 g_j=min(P/C,a_j,b_j), G=sum_j g_j, r=P/G,
 h(r)=min(1,C/r), f(r)=min(1,2sqrt(r)/(3sqrt(3C))).              (2)
For P>0,G>0, pay actual owner i its bundle dotted with prices
 p_P=h(r), p_aj=f(r)g_j/a_j, p_bj=f(r)g_j/b_j,                 (3)
where zero-coordinate contributions are zero. If P=0 or G=0, pay everyone zero.
The only change from the preceding cube-root construction is the cap P/C in
place of P. It is a crucial mathematical change, not a retuning of h or f.

For every profile,
 G<=MP/C, r>=C/M, and F>=min(P,G).                            (4)
Usually F is NOT equal to min(P,G), because the smaller cap can lower G. Only
the displayed lower comparison is used in funding. Total payment is exactly
 Ph(r)+2Gf(r).                                               (5)
Each positive endpoint contributes g_j f, and g_j=0 if either endpoint is zero.
Also G=0 iff all branches have zero production (when P>0), so the zero-payment
convention agrees with F=0.

2. COALITION COVERAGE
If r<=C, p_P=1. Every coalition's P stock covers its entire production, so
coverage follows without any requirement on inner prices.
If r>C, then G<P/C. No branch is at its cap P/C; therefore
 g_j=min(a_j,b_j)
for every branch. Each supported branch has g_j/a_j+g_j/b_j>=1. Moreover
 h(r)+f(r)>=1:
write x=r/C>1 and maximize (1-1/x)/sqrt(x), obtaining2/(3sqrt3) at x=3.
Thus 2sqrt(x)/(3sqrt3)>=1-1/x, and truncation at1 preserves this inequality.
Each currently feasible triple(P,a_j,b_j) has total price at least1. These
prices form a feasible production dual on the current support; consequently
any fixed-owner coalition, whose capacities lie within that support, receives
at least its own production value. Absent rows cannot support a coalition
activity. At F=0 coverage is trivial.

3. ALL-MIXED-OWNER TCWM
For any fixed coalition aggregate holdings s, with0<=s<=c and fixed outsiders,
its payment is
 Y=s_P h(r)+f(r)Z,
 Z=sum_j g_j(s_aj/a_j+s_bj/b_j), 0<=Z<=2G.                    (6)
Restoring a coalition coordinate increases both its s-coordinate and the
aggregate coordinate by the same amount. At every regular point,
 h'<=0, f'>=0, h+r h'>=0, f-2r f'>=0.                        (7)
For h, the third expression is1 on the constant branch and0 on C/r. For f,
the fourth is0 on its square-root branch and1 on its constant branch.

Restore a_j, keeping P and every other aggregate row fixed. Write derivatives
along this path. Then G'=g_j' is0 or1 away from ties, and
 Z'=g_j'(s_aj/a_j+s_bj/b_j)+g_j(a_j-s_aj)/a_j^2.
If g_j'=1, g_j=a_j and Z'=1+s_bj/b_j>=1=G'. Otherwise Z'>=0=G'. Since
 r'=-rG'/G,
 Y'=s_P h'r'+f'r'Z+fZ'
    >=(f-2r f')G'>=0.                                      (8)
This proof accounts for actual coalition ownership shares, not only aggregate
prices. The b_j restoration is identical.

Now restore P, keeping every other row fixed. Let t count branches for which
P/C<min(a_j,b_j). Then
 G'=t/C,
 Z'=(1/C)sum_{j:P/C<min(a_j,b_j)}(s_aj/a_j+s_bj/b_j)>=0.
Each counted branch contributes P/C to G, so G>=Pt/C. Therefore
 r'=(G-Pt/C)/G^2>=0, and P r'<=r.
Using s_P'=1 and0<=s_P<=P,
 Y'=h+s_P h'r'+f'Zr'+fZ'
    >=h+P h'r'>=h+r h'>=0.                                 (9)
The parameter C remains fixed through every deviation.

4. SWITCHES, ZERO REPORTS AND FINITE DEVIATIONS
Each one-coordinate path is finitely piecewise smooth: the g_j are minima of
finitely many affine functions; G is piecewise affine; r is a ratio; the fixed
h,f thresholds give finitely many additional pieces, aside from identically
tied intervals which admit a fixed branch expression. Owner payments agree at
all clipping/threshold boundaries. Thus(8),(9) imply monotonicity along the
entire path wherever P,G>0.
The contribution g_j s_aj/a_j is bounded by g_j<=a_j and extends continuously
by0 at a_j=0; likewise for b_j. As G tends to0 with fixed positive P, eventually
f=1 and h=CG/P, so total payment=(C+2)G tends to0. As P tends to0, the funding
bound below and F<=P force every nonnegative owner payment to0. The formula
therefore extends continuously to all zero-value faces. A coordinate restoration
starting at F=0 starts with zero payment, and F is monotone. Sequentially restore
all coalition coordinates, keeping actual identities and outside reports fixed.
This proves every finite whole-bundle TCWM inequality on all real profiles.

5. FUNDING AND PARAMETER CHOICE
If r<=1, then G>=P and F=P; also h=1 because C>=1. Consequently
 (Ph+2Gf)/F =1+2f/r
 <=1+4/(3sqrt(3Cr))
 <=1+4sqrt(M)/(3sqrt(3)C),                                  (10)
using r>=C/M. If r>1, then min(P,G)=G<=F. Thus
 (Ph+2Gf)/F <=rh+2f<=C+2.                                  (11)
The last inequality covers both r<=C (rh=r<=C) and r>C (rh=C).
Hence every public C>=1 gives funding factor at most
 max{C+2,1+4sqrt(M)/(3sqrt(3)C)}.                             (12)
Choose C=max{1,(16M/27)^(1/4)}. If C>1, the second entry is1+C; if C=1,
for integer M this means M=1 and that entry is less than3. Formula(1) follows.
The leading asymptotic upper constant is (16/27)^(1/4), while the current lower
constant from optimized finite-owner replication is pi^(1/4)/(2sqrt(2)).
These constants are not claimed optimal.

6. POSITION RELATIVE TO EARLIER RESULTS
The preceding cube-root upper remains correct but is quantitatively superseded.
The root's matching cube-root architecture barrier assumes the old cap P; the
new report-dependent cap P/C changes the minimum attainable ratio from1/M to
C/M and escapes that specific architecture. It does not contradict that barrier.
The prior inactive-reserve hypothesis was unnecessary: the unrestricted lower
uses only active owners plus the newly increasing owner, so inactive reserves
cannot evade its quarter-power rate. The present upper itself pays inactive
branches zero, showing reserves are not needed to achieve the correct exponent.

The result handles this entire unbounded common-cap family, not arbitrary
three-sparse production LPs. It does not establish a finite arity-only bound;
the matching lower proves none exists. It establishes the sharp growth exponent
within this family under transferable coalition underreports. Individual-IC
factor3, two-sparse TCWM factor3/2, and external production incentives remain
distinct results. Historical priority and specialist significance are unresolved.

7. EXACT SYMBOLIC IMPLEMENTATION
common_cap_quarter_mechanism.py uses the smallest integer C>=1 with27C^4>=16M,
which gives the exact funding bound C+2. The only radicals are the common factor
sqrt(4P/(27CG)); an owner's payment is A+B sqrt(Q) with rational A,B,Q when
reports and C are rational. Payments are never silently rounded into rational
values claimed to satisfy exact incentives. check_common_cap_quarter_mechanism.py
reuses the independently checked rational-interval plus exact-zero radical
comparison routine, checks funding/coverage/finite coalition restorations, and
reports the exact counts in COMMON_CAP_QUARTER_UPPER_CHECKS.json. Finite checks
corroborate the proof and do not replace its all-profile argument.
