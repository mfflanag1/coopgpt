SUBQUADRATIC NORM GEOMETRY BLOCKS SUPPORT-RADIAL COALITION PRICES
17 October 2026. Architecture obstruction, NOT an unrestricted mechanism lower.

MODEL AND CLAIM
Fix 1<p<2 and exposure dimension at least2. Use the same fixed-owner cone
K_p and production F=t-||x||_p as in CONE_FINITE_P_DIMENSION_FREE_THEOREM.txt.
Consider a regular support-radial rule

 y_i=lambda t_i-a(s)<x_i,J_p(X)>/||X||_p^(p-1),
 s=||X||_p/T,

with a continuous on(0,1), fixed finite lambda>0, and any convention at X=0.
If a(s_0)!=0 at any interior s_0, this rule fails even INDIVIDUAL report
monotonicity at a two-owner, two-exposure-coordinate profile. Consequently
no such rule can combine all-profile TCWM and a finite total-output funding
factor: TCWM forces a identically0 on(0,1), while sum_i lambda t_i/F(C)
is unbounded as ||X||_p/T approaches1.

This closes the direct support-radial extension of the p>=2 construction.
It does not exclude a nonradial, owner-aware or otherwise different mechanism.

PROOF
Fix s_0 in(0,1), take aggregate spatial vector X=(1,0), and total reserve
T=1/s_0. Choose a sufficiently small z>0 so that

 z+(1+z^p)^(1/p)<=T.

Such z exists by continuity and T>1. Fix TWO actual owners with reports

 b_1=(z,(0,z)),       b_2=(T-z,(1,-z)).

Both lie in K_p. Initially owner1 receives lambda z because its exposure is
orthogonal to J_p(1,0). Let sigma be the sign of a(s_0), and let owner1
restore the cone-feasible bundle D=(epsilon,(0,sigma epsilon)).
Both D and the original retained bundle are in K_p. The new aggregate is
T_e=T+epsilon and X_e=(1,sigma epsilon), with
R_e=(1+epsilon^p)^(1/p) and s_e=R_e/(T+epsilon)->s_0.
The payment change is exactly

 lambda epsilon
 -a(s_e)(z+sigma epsilon)sigma epsilon^(p-1)/R_e^(p-1).

For small epsilon this equals
 lambda epsilon-|a(s_0)|z epsilon^(p-1)+o(epsilon^(p-1)).
Since p-1<1, it is strictly negative. This is a feasible restoration of
one fixed owner's full cone bundle, so individual monotonicity fails.
No differentiation at a zero aggregate coordinate is assumed.

EXACT p=3/2 CERTIFICATE FOR THE POWER RULE
For the natural p-power candidate a(s)=s^(p-1), lambda=1, take

 b_1=(1,(0,1)), b_2=(2,(1,-1)), D=(3/8,(0,3/8)).

The second owner is feasible because ||(1,-1)||_(3/2)=2^(2/3)<2;
the first owner and D are cone-boundary bundles. Initially y_1=1.
After owner1 restores D, T=27/8 and its second exposure is11/8, while the
aggregate second exposure is3/8. Its payment is therefore

 y'_1=11/8-(11/8)*sqrt((3/8)/(27/8))=11/12<1.

Thus the exact loss is1/12, without a numerical norm or limiting argument.
More generally the same initial reports and any0<epsilon<=1 give payment
change epsilon-(1+epsilon)[epsilon/(3+epsilon)]^(p-1), which is negative
for all sufficiently small epsilon for every1<p<2.

SCOPE AND NEXT QUESTION
The p>=2 proof used quadratic domination of the scalar Bregman divergence.
Below2, the support price instead changes at order epsilon^(p-1) near an
aggregate coordinate with cancellation; a feasible owner can gain only order
epsilon in reserve. This exact mismatch explains failure of the entire
regular support-radial architecture, not just a particular constant choice.
A general finite-p guarantee below2 would require a price field that smooths
or redistributes these cancellation singularities. Existence of such a field,
or a dimension-growing lower bound for ALL mechanisms, remains unresolved.
