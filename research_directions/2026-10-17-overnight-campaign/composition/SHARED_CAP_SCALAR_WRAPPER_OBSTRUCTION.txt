NO UNIFORM SCALAR WRAPPER OF GRAPH HUBER SHARES UNDER A SHARED CAP
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate; independent audit pending.

TECHNOLOGY AND ARCHITECTURE
Let G(a,b,c,d)=min(a,b)+min(c,d), the production value of two disjoint unit
edges. Add one common resource P, consumed once by every activity. Then
 F(P,a,b,c,d)=min(P,G(a,b,c,d)).
This is a unit-profit three-sparse technology with just two activities,
(P,a,b) and (P,c,d). All quantities are continuous, and all actual owner
identities and eligibility sets remain fixed.
Let z_i(a,b,c,d) be the audited unit-graph Huber payment to owner i, using
its reported inner resource bundle. Consider ANY scalar wrapper
 y_i=q_P(P,G)b_iP+q_G(P,G) z_i.                                    (1)
The scalar functions may depend on the fixed public technology, eligibility
sets, and owner count, but on a report profile only through the two aggregates
(P,G). They are finite and nonnegative at positive P,G. No smoothness,
continuity, homogeneity, symmetry, or fixed-constant mixture assumption is made.

THEOREM
No wrapper (1) can satisfy all-profile coalition coverage, TCWM, and any finite
uniform funding factor kappa over all positive capacity profiles. In fact the
incentive step below uses only INDIVIDUAL own-bundle monotonicity. This is a
restriction on the wrapper architecture, not an impossibility for rank-three
mechanisms. The general dimension theorem already provides finite mechanisms
for this five-resource technology outside the wrapper class.

FIXED OWNERS
Three owners suffice, with fixed eligible coordinates:
 A: inner a,b,c;
 B: common P and inner d;
 R: common P.
Zero reports are allowed. No owner is introduced between profiles. The scalars
are the same functions throughout this fixed three-owner domain. The argument
also permits extra fixed owners reporting zero; q may depend publicly on n.
All statements concern the standard all-nonnegative-report domain, not a
separate public finite cap on every owner's reports.

1. INCENTIVES FORCE q_G(P,.) TO BE NONDECREASING
Fix P>0. For any v,b>0 and 0<delta<b use the two profiles
 A_low=(a=v,b=v,c=b-delta),   A_high=(a=v,b=v,c=b),
 B=(P,d=b),                 R=0,
where the symbol b in c and d denotes the numerical parameter; the first inner
edge has both capacities v. These are legitimate one-coordinate reports of A.
The inner values are G_low=v+b-delta and G_high=v+b.
On a balanced edge fully owned by A, the graph Huber payment is exactly v.
On the other edge with A's scarce coordinate x<=b, A receives
 x[1-x/(2b)]. Therefore
 z_A(low)=v+b/2-delta^2/(2b),     z_A(high)=v+b/2.
A owns no P, so individual monotonicity of (1) requires
 q_G(P,v+b)(v+b/2)
 >=q_G(P,v+b-delta)(v+b/2-delta^2/(2b)).                             (2)

For any x>0 set v=b=x/2, with 0<delta<x/2. Writing q(x)=q_G(P,x), (2) becomes
 q(x)>=q(x-delta)[1-4delta^2/(3x^2)].                               (3)
Fix 0<s<t. Divide [s,t] into N equal steps Delta=(t-s)/N, with N large enough
that Delta<s/2 and every bracket below is nonnegative. Apply (3) at each
x_k=s+k Delta and multiply the inequalities (all q are nonnegative):
 q(t)>=q(s) product_{k=1}^N [1-4Delta^2/(3x_k^2)]
      >=q(s)[1-4N Delta^2/(3s^2)].
The final product bound follows by induction from (1-u)(1-v)>=1-u-v for
u,v in [0,1]. Let N tend to infinity. Since q(s) is finite,
 q(t)>=q(s).
This is a finite-comparison proof; no derivative or continuity of q is assumed.
The same three owners and their eligibility sets are used in every comparison,
and outsiders are held fixed within each low/high comparison. Different pairs
may of course use different true profiles, as all-profile truthfulness requires.

2. FINITE FUNDING NOW FORCES q_G TO VANISH
For every g>0 choose a profile with inner grand value G=g. Graph Huber grand
payments satisfy sum_i z_i>=g by coalition coverage. The nonnegative common-
resource payment and a factor-kappa funding bound imply
 q_G(P,g) g <= sum_i y_i <= kappa min(P,g) <= kappa P.
Thus q_G(P,g)<=kappa P/g. Because q_G(P,.) is nonnegative and nondecreasing,
for any fixed g0>0 and all g>=g0,
 0<=q_G(P,g0)<=q_G(P,g)<=kappa P/g.
Let g increase without bound. Hence q_G(P,g0)=0. Since P,g0 were arbitrary,
 q_G(P,G)=0 at every positive aggregate pair.                          (4)

3. COVERAGE THEN FORCES UNBOUNDED COMMON-RESOURCE FUNDING
Fix P>g>0. Let A own the balanced first inner edge (g,g), with c=0. Let B own
P-capacity g and d=0, and let R own P-capacity P-g. This respects the same
fixed eligibility sets. The grand inner value is g and the grand value is g.
Coalition {A,B} has common resource g and inner value g, hence produces g.
By (4), A receives zero, while B receives q_P(P,g)g. Coalition coverage forces
 q_P(P,g)>=1.
Grand payment is therefore q_P(P,g)P>=P. Choosing P/g>kappa contradicts the
supposed funding factor. Only two common-resource owners, with unequal but
permitted reports, are needed; a growing count is unnecessary in this domain.

4. AN EXACT FAILURE OF THE MOST NATURAL WRAPPER/POTENTIAL
The same technology already defeats direct Huber minimization on its rank-three
cover constraints. Set P=b=c=d=1. One owner holds a,c,d; other fixed owners hold
P,b. At reported a=1 the unique prices are p_P=1/2 and all four inner prices1/4,
so that owner's payment is3/4. At true a=2 the prices are
 p_P=4/7, p_a=1/7, p_b=2/7, p_c=p_d=3/14,
so its payment is5/7. Underreporting a from2 to1 gains1/28. KKT multipliers
for the two activities are respectively(1/2,1/2) and(4/7,3/7). This example
illustrates the obstruction; the theorem in sections1--3 is strictly broader
because it excludes ALL scalar wrapper functions, not just this potential.

CONSEQUENCE
A shared peak-power cap on two independently feasible resource pairs is already
outside every bounded scalar rescaling of the graph Huber shares. A successful
extension must change the inner allocation across components, use additional
ownership/report information, or use a mechanism outside (1). This is a precise
composition stopping criterion; it neither proves unbounded rank-three funding
nor rules out owner-aware or nonseparable constructions. Novelty is unconfirmed.
