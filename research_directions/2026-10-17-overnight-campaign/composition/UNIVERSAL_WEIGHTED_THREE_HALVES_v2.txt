UNIVERSAL WEIGHTED RESOURCE GRAPHS: SHARP3/2 FUNDING
Version2, 2026-10-17. Rational explicit mechanism; real-profit existence.
The graph unfolding ingredient is established; mechanism novelty unconfirmed.

STATEMENT AND SCOPE
Let G=(V,E) be any finite simple undirected graph with fixed public
nonnegative rational edge profits w_e. Resources have arbitrary finite
nonnegative real capacities. Production is FRACTIONAL capacitated matching:
 F_w(c)=max {sum_e w_e x_e:x>=0, sum_{e incident v}x_e<=c_v}.
Actual owners are fixed and can hold arbitrary mixed bundles of resources.
Reports are binding coordinatewise underreports; utility here is payment
only. Coalition coverage means sum_{i in S}P_i(b)>=F_w(sum_{i in S}b_i).
Coalition-total monotonicity means a coalition cannot increase its summed
payment by jointly withholding any subset of its whole resource bundles,
with all outsiders' reports fixed; side transfers within it are allowed.

There is an explicit public positive linear resource-copy lift to a finite
unit-profit graph H and scalar q>0 such that
 F_w(c)=(1/q)F_H(Bc)                                (1)
for EVERY real capacity vector, including zeros. Therefore unit-Huber
transfers coalition coverage, coalition-total monotonicity, and total
funding <=(3/2)F_w(c), without any bound on graph or actual-owner counts.
The unit single-edge lower family belongs to this class; root's separately
audited general-mechanism lower theorem makes3/2 the sharp uniform constant
for this payment-only coalition incentive requirement.

INTEGER LIFT
First let all retained edge profits be positive integers, and let
W=max_e w_e. Delete zero-profit edges (they never improve production).
Create W copies (v,a), a=0,...,W-1, of each resource v. Every copy has
capacity c_v. For every original uv of profit w=w_uv, connect copies
(u,a),(v,b) whenever a+b=w-1. These are unit-profit edges.
There are nW resources and sum_e w_e edges. An original owner
holding b_iv receives b_iv on EACH copy (v,a). Copies are a deterministic
public transformation of the same report, not extra actual owners,
independently reportable endowments, or report-independent free capacity.

PRIMAL DIRECTION
Given an original feasible x, for each edge uv of integer profit w put
x_uv on each of the w lifted edges with a+b=w-1, a,b>=0. An original
edge uses any particular copy of either endpoint at most once. Thus
at (v,a), total incident lifted flow is at most the original incident
flow sum, which is <=c_v. Lifted unit production is exactly sum_e w_e x_e.
Hence F_H(Bc)>=F_w(c).

DUAL DIRECTION
The original weighted dual is
 min sum_v c_v p_v subject to p_v>=0, p_u+p_v>=w_uv.
It has an optimum, and clipping all prices at W preserves feasibility
and cannot increase cost, so take 0<=p_v<=W. Assign lifted prices
 r_(v,a)=min(1,max(0,p_v-a)).
For a lifted edge with a+b=w_uv-1, weighted feasibility gives
 (p_u-a)+(p_v-b)>=1.
For ANY two real numbers s,t with s+t>=1,
 clip_[0,1](s)+clip_[0,1](t)>=1:
if either is>=1 it is immediate; otherwise neither can be negative,
and the sum is unchanged. Thus r is a feasible unit vertex cover.
For every p in[0,W], sum_{a=0}^{W-1}clip_[0,1](p-a)=p. Therefore
 sum_(v,a)c_v r_(v,a)=sum_v c_v p_v=F_w(c).
Weak duality gives F_H(Bc)<=F_w(c), completing exact identity.
This argument applies to odd cycles and mixed ownership without changes.

RATIONAL PROFITS
Choose a common positive denominator q so k_e=q w_e are integers.
Use the integer construction for k. Then F_k=q F_w and (1) holds.
All transformations and identities are independent of capacities, owners,
coalitions, and reports. Fixed zero-profit or isolated resources can be
ignored or assigned zero payments without changing any claim.

MECHANISM TRANSFER
At every report profile b, apply B separately to each actual owner's
bundle, run the unit-Huber mechanism on the lifted graph, and divide
payments by q. For any coalition, total lifted resource vector is
B(sum_{i in S}b_i). Equation(1) proves exact coalition production and
coverage. If a coalition increases its reports while outsiders stay fixed,
only that coalition's lifted reports increase coordinatewise because
B>=0; unit-Huber coalition-total monotonicity therefore applies globally,
with no report-specific changing ownership or unrelated price selection.
The global funding bound divides identically by q.

COMPLEXITY AND LIMITATIONS
The lift is finite and explicit. Its size is O(nW+mW), W=max q w_e.
This is polynomial in the INTEGER magnitude W, not polynomial in binary
input length. Large rational denominators can make the graph exponential
in the original encoded input size. Combining it with the exact unit-Huber
solver gives an exact rational algorithm polynomial in the expanded graph
and capacity bit length. No strongly polynomial or polynomial-in-original-
bit-length claim is made. Real irrational profits are handled by the separate all-profile compactness
argument below; that result is existential, not a finite exact algorithm.

This theorem is not an outside-production incentive theorem. If owners
can use withheld resources elsewhere, see root/OUTSIDE_PRODUCTION_BOUNDS.txt.
It also does not establish that the copying reduction itself is historically
new. The proposed research contribution is the audited incentive/funding
transfer combined with the sharp unit theorem; prior-source review remains
necessary before asserting novelty or a field-level breakthrough.


ALL REAL PROFITS: ONE COMMON ALL-PROFILE LIMIT
The rational lift constructs an aggregate price field. At aggregate c, let
p^(k)_(v,a)(B_k c) be the unit-Huber copy price, and define original price
 r^(k)_v(c)=(1/k) sum_a p^(k)_(v,a)(B_k c)
for rational weights w^(k)_e=ceil(k w_e)/k. Zero-profit edges remain deleted.
Assume at least one positive edge; otherwise pay zero everywhere. Let
W0=max_e w_e. Then 0<=r^(k)_v(c)<=W0+1 uniformly in k,c,v, including
zero-capacity coordinates, because each copy price lies in[0,1].
The field is independent of owner identities and allocation of aggregate
capacity across owners. Actual owner payment is b_i.r^(k)(c).

For all c>=0, all 0<=d<=c, and all h>=0, the rational construction gives
 (i)  d.r^(k)(c)>=F_(w^(k))(d),
 (ii) c.r^(k)(c)<=(3/2) F_(w^(k))(c),
 (iii)(d+h).r^(k)(c+h)>=d.r^(k)(c).
Here(i) follows by assigning d and c-d to two actual owners and applying
coverage; (iii) is coalition-total monotonicity when its holdings d rise
by h. The same aggregate rule applies to all such ownership assignments.

Consider the compact product space
 K=product_{c in R_+^V} [0,W0+1]^V
with its product topology. The sequence of entire price fields r^(k) has
a convergent subnet by Tychonoff compactness. Choose ONE such subnet once
for the fixed public graph and profit vector; it converges at EVERY c
simultaneously to a field r. This is not a selection of unrelated limits
for separate reports or separate owner systems. The subnet indices tend
to infinity, so w^(k)->w along it.

For every finite d, production is continuous in edge profits; explicitly
 |F_u(d)-F_w(d)| <= ||u-w||_infinity (sum_v d_v)/2,
because every feasible edge plan uses two resource units per activity.
Each of(i)-(iii) involves only finitely many coordinates of the product
field, so passing to this common subnet gives
 d.r(c)>=F_w(d), c.r(c)<=(3/2)F_w(c),
 (d+h).r(c+h)>=d.r(c).
Define P_i(b)=b_i.r(sum_j b_j). These inequalities prove coverage,
funding, and coalition-total monotonicity for every actual-owner system
simultaneously, regardless of owner count or mixed bundles. This proves
existence for arbitrary nonnegative real profits. It does not assert
measurability, continuity, a computable choice of limiting field, or an
exact finite algorithm for irrational input data. Rational public profits
retain the explicit finite algorithm above and need no compactness.

ESTABLISHED UNFOLDING INGREDIENT AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
Hashemi and Wrzos-Kaminska, Weighted Matching in the Random-Order Streaming
and Robust Communication Models (APPROX/RANDOM2024), Definition2.1,
define the antidiagonal graph unfolding used here and attribute it to
Kao, Lam, Sung and Ting, A decomposition theorem for maximum weight
bipartite matchings, SIAM J. Comput.31(1):18-26(2001). Their definition
uses each vertex's maximum incident weight rather than global W; surplus
isolated copies here can be deleted. This construction is established and
is NOT claimed as a new graph reduction. The self-contained argument above
checks arbitrary real capacities, fractional nonbipartite production,
and the same-owner all-profile incentive transfer required in this project.
