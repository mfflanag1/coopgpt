STRUCTURE OF EXACT POSITIVE LINEAR RESOURCE LIFTS
2026-10-17. Elementary architecture lemma; priority unchecked.

Suppose a weighted simple resource graph G with strictly positive profits
on E admits an exact unit-graph representation F_G(c)=F_H(Bc) for all
c>=0, where B is a fixed entrywise nonnegative matrix. No constant
auxiliary capacities are allowed. Remove rows of B identically zero.

1. ZERO-SUPPORT NECESSITY.
For every unit edge xy of H, the supports S_x={v:B_xv>0} and S_y are
disjoint, and every uv in S_x cross S_y is an edge of G.
If the supports overlap at v, c=e_v activates both endpoints of xy,
contradicting F_G(e_v)=0. If u in S_x and v in S_y are distinct and uv
is not an edge, c=e_u+e_v activates xy but has F_G(c)=0. These arguments
use nonnegativity and an exact identity, not incentive assumptions.
In particular every lift edge is supported on a complete bipartite
subgraph of G. On C6 every such biclique is a star, so at least one
endpoint support is a singleton.

2. PURE-COPY NORMAL FORM.
Replace every lifted vertex x by twins (x,v), v in S_x, of capacities
B_xv c_v. Replace xy by all edges (x,u)(y,v) for u in S_x,v in S_y.
This transformation preserves unit production for all c. Aggregating
new edge flows proves one direction. For the other, given an old flow
z_xy, put z_xy alpha_xu alpha_yv on the corresponding new edge, where
alpha_xu=B_xu c_u/(Bc)_x when (Bc)_x>0. Degrees of (x,u) are alpha_xu
times the old degree, hence at most B_xu c_u. Rows of zero current
capacity have zero incident old flow and may use alpha=0.
Thus any exact positive linear lift can be replaced by one in which
each auxiliary resource is a nonnegative scalar copy of ONE original
resource and every lifted edge joins copies of adjacent original
resources. Ownership is preserved by the same linear copy map.

The conclusion is a graph-homomorphic blowup normal form, not a proof
that such lifts always exist. It excludes free baseline gadgets and
mixed-support tricks as independent sources of expressive power.
The matching-premium construction supplies a nontrivial valid blowup;
the earlier six-profile same-resource cone inequality does not extend
to this whole architecture (its explicit doubled-edge lift violates it).
