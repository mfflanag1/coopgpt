ROUTINE TWO-RESOURCE EXTENSION: FINITE CONCAVE HOMOGENEOUS TECHNOLOGIES
2026-10-17. Elementary positive-min decomposition; historical priority unchecked.

Let F:R_+^2->R_+ be finite, continuous, concave, nondecreasing, homogeneous
of degree1, and piecewise linear with finitely many pieces. This includes
fractional production with any finite menu of activities using two resources,
with arbitrary public nonnegative profits and nonnegative consumption
coefficients, provided no positive-profit activity consumes zero of both.

Put f(t)=F(t,1). Its slopes are nonnegative and nonincreasing. Let beta=f(0),
alpha=lim_(t->infinity)f'(t), positive breakpoints t_j, and downward slope
jumps Delta_j>=0. Then
 f(t)=beta+alpha t+sum_j Delta_j min(t,t_j),
 F(x,y)=alpha x+beta y+sum_j Delta_j min(x,t_j y).          (1)
The first identity follows by matching the value at0 and the slope on every
linear interval; homogeneity gives the second for y>0, and continuity extends
it to y=0. For a strict two-input activity menu F(x,0)=F(0,y)=0, so the linear
terms vanish. This is an elementary concave-function decomposition, not a
claim of a novel representation theorem.

Every summand is a fixed public positive scalar times a rescaled unit
complementarity technology; every actual owner keeps its own rescaled pair.
Run the established two-input mechanism separately on each term and sum.
Identity(1) holds for every coalition and every withheld bundle, so it gives:
- payment-only coalition-total incentives and coverage with funding<=3/2 F;
- full coalition outside-production protection with funding<=K* F,
  K*=1.5430633898..., using energy/OUTSIDE_LOGARITHMIC_PRICE_THEOREM.txt.
Linear terms are paid exactly by linear owner holdings and satisfy full OUT.
The class includes F=min(x,y), so the known lower families make both constants
uniform sharp over this entire two-resource class, with unbounded fixed owners.
For the full-OUT lower see energy/UNRESTRICTED_OUTSIDE_SHARP_CANDIDATE.txt;
for payment-only3/2 see root/ROOT_ADJUDICATION_03_COALITION_OPTIMUM.txt.

SCOPE GATE
This allows arbitrarily many heterogeneous activity ratios but only two
resource types. It is a routine corollary, not the requested unbounded-
resource heterogeneous-network advance. Summing pairwise F_uv across a
network would reuse shared resource capacities and is not authorized by(1).
