EXACT CLAIM AND SCOPE
For F(x)=min(x1,x2,x3), fix 16 actual owners, each eligible for all three
resources, with reports in [0,1]^3. A rule with nonnegative report-independent
whole-owner marginal-contribution weights that covers every coalition and
satisfies payment-only TCWM for groups of at most four needs worst-case total
funding at least 5F. The weights may be asymmetric and unnormalized. This is
an architecture obstruction. It is not a lower against all mechanisms: the
separately established unrestricted min-three TCWM factor is 2.

1. ANALYTICAL OWNER AND DOMAIN OBLIGATIONS
I checked the actual symmetrization argument, rather than assuming anonymity.
Average the rule over all owner-label permutations, transporting both reports
and output labels. The cube and full resource eligibility are invariant under
this operation. Every coalition-coverage, same-coalition restoration, and
funding inequality is linear in payments, so each survives averaging with its
original constant. The resulting coefficient for a marginal depends only on
the predecessor subset cardinality h. Its cardinality mass is
 W_h=binom(15,h)w_h >=0.
No new owners are introduced or deleted. Null reports remain possible reports
of the same fixed owners; null owners have exactly zero marginal contribution.

The balanced profile b_i=(1,1,1) gives every marginal value 1 and F=16. Hence
funding implies sum_h W_h<=kappa. Three pure-resource owners, with the other
thirteen reporting zero, give coverage coefficients
 c_h=3h(h-1)/(15*14), so c.W>=1. Both profiles lie in the stated cube.

2. THE THRESHOLD ROW IS A GENUINE TCWM COMPARISON
For each displayed (q,k,t), there are q protected leaf owners, k outside center
owners, and z=16-q-k null owners. The focal leaf changes t/k to (t+1)/k;
the other q-1 leaves remain at one. Center bundles are (1/k,1/k,0). Every
holding remains in the cube, and every restoration is feasible and nonnegative.
The coalition includes its unchanged members, as required by TCWM.

For the focal owner's marginal change to be 1/k, all other protected leaves
must be absent from the sampled predecessor, and at least t+1 centers present.
For an unchanged protected leaf to lose 1/k, the focal leaf must be present,
the other protected leaves absent, and the same center threshold met. Thus the
increment divided by epsilon=1/k has exact coefficient
 D_h = sum_(j=t+1)^k binom(k,j)
       [binom(z,h-j)-(q-1)binom(z,h-1-j)] / binom(15,h).
TCWM gives D.W>=0. Out-of-range binomials are zero. This derivation includes
t=0, the null focal report, h=0, and h=15 without an endpoint exception.

Optional wording clarification only: the theorem's phrase 'changes coalition
production' here means a sampled coalition's production. The protected all-leaf
coalition itself has value zero at both profiles. This does not affect the
formula, the certificate, or the theorem.

4. THE DUAL IMPLICATION
All 14 multipliers are nonnegative exact rationals. Adding their transition
rows to five times coverage gives precisely the published vector v. Each
component is at most 1; its maximum is 594/625 and minimum slack is 31/625.
Consequently v.W>=5, and nonnegative W gives
