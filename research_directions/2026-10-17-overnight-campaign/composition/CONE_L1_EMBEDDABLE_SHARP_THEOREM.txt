SHARP COALITION-SAFE FUNDING FOR EVERY L1-EMBEDDABLE RESERVE NORM
17 October 2026. Composition proof candidate; historical priority unconfirmed.
This supersedes the Euclidean radial constants as unrestricted upper bounds,
while leaving the earlier, explicitly restricted radial-class results valid.

THEOREM
Let V be a nonzero finite-dimensional real normed space with a FIXED linear
isometric embedding L:V->L1(Omega,mu). Thus

                   ||x||=integral |(Lx)(omega)| dmu.  (1)

Fixed actual owners report arbitrary cone bundles b_i=(t_i,x_i) in
K={(t,x):t>=||x||}; production is F(t,x)=t-||x||. Every withdrawn and
retained owner bundle must belong to K. The number of owners and dim(V)
are arbitrary. There are explicit all-profile, nonnegative, coalition-covering
rules with the following guarantees:

 (i)  transferable-coalition-report monotonicity, total <=(3/2) F(C);
 (ii) FULL coalition outside-production protection, total <=(1+c) F(C),

where c is the unique root in(1/2,1) of c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1,
so1+c=1.543063389825... . The constants are optimal uniformly over all
finite owner populations, already on every nonzero one-dimensional subspace.
The lower-bound inputs are the independently audited two-input min-game
theorems cited in Section7; the reductions to the present domain are explicit.

The result applies directly to every finite weighted sum of absolute linear
functionals, in particular ell1. It applies to Euclidean norms using the
standard Gaussian integral representation. Classical L1-embedding results
also include ell_p for1<p<=2; original-source verification is recorded by
the root lane separately. The theorem itself assumes only the exact identity(1).
There is no claim of a finite arithmetic implementation for an arbitrary
continuous embedding, nor of gain-only S+constant*G funding.

1. ACTUAL-OWNER DECOMPOSITION AND PAYMENT FORMULA
Put u_i(omega)=(Lx_i)(omega), and write
 a_i=u_i^+=max(u_i,0), b_i=u_i^-=max(-u_i,0),
 f_i=t_i-||x_i||>=0,
 A=sum_i a_i, B=sum_i b_i.
Choose a basis of V and measurable representatives of its finitely many
L-images once; define Lx pointwise by their linear combination. All linear
identities for all reports and restoration paths then hold outside one common
null set. Here a_i,b_i are auxiliary quantities used to EVALUATE the rule. They are
not strategic identities or assumed coordinatewise underreports. Their
nonmonotone changes under actual cone withdrawals are handled in the proof.

Let pi_i(a,b) be a two-input unit-production payment rule for value min(A,B),
with total funding at most kappa min(A,B). Define actual-owner payments

                  y_i=kappa f_i+2 integral pi_i dmu.  (2)

The two choices of pi and kappa used below have prices in[0,1], so
0<=pi_i<=a_i+b_i=|u_i|. Thus all integrals are finite and(2) is well-defined.
Each actual owner remains the same owner throughout every report comparison.

For every coalition S, the exact value identity is

 F(sum_S b_i)=sum_S f_i+2 integral min(sum_S a_i,sum_S b_i)dmu.  (3)

This follows from a+b-|a-b|=2min(a,b), applied at each omega. Consequently
coalition coverage for pi and kappa>=1 imply coverage for(2). Its total is
at most kappa sum_i f_i+2kappa integral min(A,B)=kappa F(C).

2. A SIGNED TRANSFER LEMMA
Fix outsiders' scalar holdings a_o,b_o. Let W(a,b) be the payment of a
coalition with scalar holdings(a,b), so totals are A=a+a_o,B=b+b_o.
Suppose W is continuous, locally Lipschitz including the boundary, and its
almost-everywhere partial derivatives alpha=W_a,beta=W_b satisfy

                   0<=alpha,beta<=kappa.              (4)

Then(2) is coalition-report monotone. If, in addition,

                    1<=alpha+beta<=2kappa-1,          (5)

then(2) satisfies FULL coalition outside-production protection.
The inequalities are to hold on each smooth piece; continuity handles seams.
The explicit rules below are globally Lipschitz, so no integrability issue
arises from approaching a zero scalar resource.

PROOF OF TCWM, WITHOUT DIFFERENTIATING THE EMBEDDING.
Restore actual coalition bundles D_i=(dt_i,dx_i) in K, leaving outsiders fixed.
Let da(omega),db(omega) be the finite changes in the coalition's positive and
negative scalar holdings. The identity

 |z^+-w^+|+|z^--w^-|=|z-w|

and the triangle inequality give

 integral (|da|+|db|) <=sum_S ||dx_i||<=sum_S dt_i=:dt. (6)

Meanwhile sum_S df_i=dt-integral(da+db). By(4),
 W_new-W_old>=-kappa[(-da)^++(-db)^+].
Substituting in(2) yields

 Y_new-Y_old>=kappa[dt-integral(|da|+|db|)]>=0.        (7)

This proof allows the canonical positive and negative holdings to decrease;
the increase in the standalone reserve term pays for the worst such loss.

PROOF OF FULL OUT.
Follow the straight restoration b_i(s)=b_i(0)+sD_i,0<=s<=1. For almost every
s and omega, let r=a'_S(omega),v=b'_S(omega). Then

 r-v=(L sum_S dx_i)(omega),
 integral(|r|+|v|)<=sum_S ||dx_i||<=dt.

The coalition's derivative under(2) is
 Y'=kappa dt+integral[2alpha r+2beta v-kappa(r+v)].
For ANY real r,v, conditions(4)-(5) imply

 (kappa-1)(|r|+|v|)+2alpha r+2beta v
                      -kappa(r+v)+|r-v|>=0.          (8)

An explicit sign check proves(8). For r>=v>=0, its left side is
2alpha(r-v)+2(alpha+beta-1)v. For r>=0>=v it is
2alpha r+2(kappa-beta)(-v). For0>=r>=v it is
2(kappa-beta)((-v)-(-r))+2(2kappa-1-alpha-beta)(-r).
The remaining cases follow by interchanging r,v and alpha,beta. All terms
are nonnegative by(4)-(5).
Combining(8) with the preceding integral bound gives

 Y'>=dt-integral |r-v|=F(sum_S D_i).

The right side is constant along the path. Integrating from0 to1 proves
full OUT. To justify differentiation, every scalar u_i(s) is affine, its
positive and negative parts are absolutely continuous with derivative bounded
by |Ldx_i|, and W is globally Lipschitz by(4). These integrable bounds permit
Fubini and dominated differentiation for almost every s. Consequently the
actual coalition payment is absolutely continuous. Scalar zero crossings,
ties A=B, and zero-total scalar components therefore cause no gap. For each
fixed omega the signed owner quantities are piecewise affine along the path;
the finitely many scalar seams are crossed finitely often or followed
tangentially. Along a followed seam either one-sided gradient gives the same
path derivative, by continuity of the explicit scalar formulas.

3. THE SHARP 3/2 TCWM INPUT
For A>=B>0 set r=B/A and

 p_A=r/2, p_B=1-r/2, pi_i=a_i p_A+b_i p_B.

For B>=A use the symmetric formula; at zero totals use the continuous
payments. Prices sum to1, giving min-game coalition coverage. Total funding
is B(3/2-r/2)<=3B/2. For a coalition(a,b) on A>=B,

 W=a r/2+b(1-r/2),
 alpha=(r/2)[1-(a-b)/A],
 beta=1-r/2+(a-b)/(2A).

Since0<=a<=A and0<=b<=B=rA, both derivatives lie in[0,3/2]. Specifically
0<=alpha<=r(1+r)/2<=1 and0<=beta<=3/2-r/2<=3/2.
The other region is symmetric. The payment is continuous at A=B and when
one scalar input vanishes; bounded one-sided partials prove global Lipschitz
continuity on the nonnegative quadrant. Thus(4) applies with kappa=3/2,
proving theorem(i). This is the accepted unit-edge Huber rule, used here
through its explicit derivative bounds as well as its funding bound.

4. THE SHARP 1+c FULL-OUT INPUT
Set t0=1-exp(-1/c)=1/c-1, so c(1+t0)=1 and0<t0<1. Define

 q(r)=1-c-c(1-r)log(1-r)/r,      0<r<=t0;
      c/r,                      t0<=r<=1;
 q(0)=1.

For A>=B>0, r=B/A, set p_A=c r,p_B=q(r), and pi_i=a_i p_A+b_i p_B.
For B>=A use the symmetric formula. Set continuous payments at zero totals.
This is exactly the two-input specialization of the root/energy logarithmic
face-average rule, not an independently claimed new scalar mechanism.

Here0<=q<=1, q'<=0, and c r+q>=1. For r<=t0, the latter follows from
-log(1-r)>=r; for r>=t0, c(r+1/r)>=2c>1. Hence scalar coalition coverage
holds. Total funding is B[c+q(r)]<=(1+c)B; put kappa=1+c.

For a coalition with a/A=u and b/B=v in[0,1], its derivatives are

 alpha=c r(1-u)-v r^2 q'(r),
 beta=q(r)+c u+v r q'(r),
 alpha+beta=c r+q(r)+(1-r)[c u+v r q'(r)].             (9)

If0<r<=t0 then
 q'=c[log(1-r)+r]/r^2,
 q+r q'=1+c log(1-r)>=0,
 c r-r^2 q'=-c log(1-r)<=1,
 c r+q+(1-r)r q'=1.

Therefore0<=alpha<=1,0<=beta<=c+q<=kappa, and
1<=alpha+beta<=c+q<=kappa.
If t0<=r<=1 then q=c/r,q'=-c/r^2. Consequently
0<=alpha<=c(1+r)<=2c<=kappa,
0<=beta<=c+c/r<=kappa,
c(1+r)<=alpha+beta<=c+c/r<=kappa.
The last lower bound is at least1 because r>=t0. Thus throughout the domain

 0<=alpha,beta<=kappa,   1<=alpha+beta<=kappa<=2kappa-1. (10)

The bound c+c/r<=kappa for r>=t0 follows from q(r)<=1, already established;
at r=t0 this also follows directly from q(t0)<=1. The two formulas for q
and q' agree at t0 because log(1-t0)=-1/c and c(1+t0)=1.
At A=B both price assignments agree. Payments are continuous at zero input,
since 0<=pi_i<=a_i+b_i and the vanishing side controls its total value.
The derivative bounds show global Lipschitz continuity for each fixed outsider
bundle. Thus(4)-(5) hold and the signed transfer lemma proves theorem(ii).

5. IMPORTANT SEMANTICS
The rule is evaluated at the original fixed actual owners and protects their
joint cone-feasible deviations. It does NOT assume that the nonlinear maps
x_i -> x_i^+,x_i^- preserve underreports; they generally do not. Equations
(6)-(8) are the compensation argument that makes the original incentive
statement valid. The embedding L is fixed, public and linear. The outside
option in(ii) is F(sum_S D_i), including pooling of all withdrawn resources
by the deviating coalition, not merely the sum of its standalone values.

6. CONCRETE NORM CLASSES
For ell1 on R^d use counting measure and Lx=x. A weighted sum
||x||=sum_j w_j|ell_j(x)| uses the same proof with weights absorbed in mu.
For Euclidean norms take a standard Gaussian vector G and
(Lx)(G)=sqrt(pi/2)<G,x>. Since E|N(0,sigma^2)|=sqrt(2/pi)sigma,
E|Lx|=||x||_2 exactly. No auxiliary owners are created by this integral.
Classical symmetric p-stable projections similarly give (1) for1<p<2;
the original-source comparison is a separate established-ingredient check.
The unrestricted Euclidean TCWM factor is therefore3/2, despite the larger
optimal constant phi in the earlier, much narrower regular-radial class.
Likewise the unrestricted Euclidean full-OUT upper is now1+c. These results
use nonlinear actual-owner standalone reserves and need not be aggregate
linear prices, so the radial lower bounds are not contradicted.

7. SHARPNESS REDUCTION AND REFERENCES TO ACCEPTED LOWER BOUNDS
Fix any unit vector e in V. Map an arbitrary two-resource owner bundle
(a_i,b_i) in R_+^2 to

             (t_i,x_i)=(a_i+b_i,(a_i-b_i)e).

For every coalition and every profile this gives F=2min(sum a_i,sum b_i).
Coordinatewise underreports (da_i,db_i)>=0 map to cone-feasible withdrawals
(da_i+db_i,(da_i-db_i)e), and retained reports also belong to K. Restricting
ANY mechanism on the full cone domain to these profiles and dividing all
payments by2 therefore gives a valid mechanism for the two-input min game
with the SAME funding factor and the SAME incentive contract. Owners remain
fixed, and no ability to choose a report-dependent ownership mapping is used.
