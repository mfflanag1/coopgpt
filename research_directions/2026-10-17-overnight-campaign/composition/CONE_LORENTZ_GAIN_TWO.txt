DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT GAIN FUNDING FOR EUCLIDEAN RESERVE CONES
17 October 2026. Frozen for independent audit.
The norm inequality is established mathematics (Djokovic/Hlawka); the
fixed-owner incentive corollary has not undergone a complete priority check.

THEOREM
For any finite Euclidean dimension d and any finite number n of fixed actual
owners, let reports b_i=(t_i,x_i) lie in the Lorentz cone

 K={(t,x): t>=||x||_2},       F(t,x)=t-||x||_2.

A feasible unilateral withdrawal D_i requires D_i in K and b_i-D_i in K.
Let C=sum_i b_i, f_i=F(b_i), S=sum_i f_i, G=F(C)-S.
The explicit grand-marginal payments

             y_i=F(C)-F(C-b_i)

are nonnegative, cover every coalition, protect each individual owner's full
outside production, and satisfy

             sum_i y_i <= S+2G.                       (1)

The factor does not depend on d or n. Choosing a whole rule publicly in advance
also permits S+min{2,n sqrt(2/(n+1))}G using the general cone mask theorem.
Neither claim implies immunity to simultaneous coalition underreports.
The constant 2 is sharp for the grand-marginal rule, not proved optimal among
all admissible mechanisms.

1. COVERAGE AND INDIVIDUAL OUTSIDE PROTECTION
F is concave, homogeneous, nonnegative on K, and superadditive. At C=(T,X),
choose u=X/||X|| when X!=0 and u=0 otherwise. The linear functional
L(t,x)=t-u dot x majorizes F everywhere, is nonnegative on K, and L(C)=F(C).
Consequently y_i>=L(b_i). Every coalition A therefore receives at least
L(sum_A b_i)>=F(sum_A b_i).
Under a unilateral withdrawal D_i, C-b_i is unchanged, so the owner's payment
loss is F(C)-F(C-D_i)>=F(D_i) by superadditivity. This proof uses original
reports directly; there is no claim that the residualization below preserves
feasible changes of report.

2. ELEMENTARY LORENTZ ORDER PROOF OF THE GAIN BOUND
Write r_i=||x_i||, U_i=(r_i,x_i), U=sum_i U_i=(R,X), R=sum_i r_i.
Since b_i=f_i(1,0)+U_i, F(sum_A b_i)=sum_A f_i+F(sum_A U_i) for every A.
Thus G=F(U), and y_i-f_i=G-F(U-U_i).
If G=0, monotonicity forces every residual marginal to vanish.
Otherwise Delta=R^2-||X||^2>0, and set

             alpha_i=2(R r_i-X dot x_i)/Delta.        (2)

These numbers obey 0<=alpha_i<=1, sum_i alpha_i=2, and U_i<=_K alpha_i U.
For completeness, expanding the squared Lorentz boundary equation gives

 (alpha R-r_i)^2-||alpha X-x_i||^2
       =alpha^2 Delta-2alpha(R r_i-X dot x_i).

At alpha=alpha_i it vanishes; moreover
alpha_i R-r_i=[(R^2+||X||^2)r_i-2R X dot x_i]/Delta>=0
by Cauchy-Schwarz. Hence alpha_i U-U_i lies in K. For r_i>0 the only
positive root is alpha_i, and Lorentz feasibility for alpha>=0 occurs only
at or above that root. Since U-U_i lies in K, alpha_i<=1. For r_i=0,
U_i=0 and alpha_i=0. Summing (2) gives exactly 2.
Therefore U-U_i >=_K (1-alpha_i)U, and

 G-F(U-U_i) <= alpha_i G.

Summing proves (1). The proof holds for all real capacities and all fixed
owner bundles, with no enumeration or finite-profile extension assumption.
The same calculation works in any real Hilbert space for finitely many
reports, though the general closed-cone theorem was stated finite-dimensional.

3. ESTABLISHED NORM INEQUALITY AND EXACT BOUNDARIES
The gain inequality is precisely

 sum_i ||X-x_i|| <= sum_i ||x_i||+(n-2)||X||,
                 X=sum_i x_i.                         (3)

This is Djokovic's inequality. The freely available primary paper
S.-E. Takahasi, Y. Takahashi, S. Wada, An extension of Hlawka's inequality,
Mathematical Inequalities & Applications 3 (2000), 63-67,
https://files.ele-math.com/articles/mia-03-07.pdf
states (3) on page 63 and develops an integral extension. Its original text
was retrieved and read on 17 October 2026. We independently derived (3) using
Lorentz order; we do not claim a new norm inequality. The proposed contribution
is only the incentive/funding interpretation under the specified owner contract.

For two owners U_1=(1,e), U_2=(1,-e), ||e||=1, S=0, G=2, and y_1=y_2=2.
Thus total=2G. This witnesses sharpness of THIS rule only.

The Euclidean geometry is essential to the stated grand-marginal factor.
On the norm cone K_infinity={(t,x):t>=||x||_infinity} in R x R^3, take
 x_1=(1,1,1), x_2=(1,-1,-1), x_3=(-1,1,-1), and t_i=1.
Each standalone value is 0, the pooled value is 2, and each two-owner value
is 0. Thus every grand marginal is 2 and their sum is 3G, contradicting
an attempted factor-2 extension to arbitrary norm cones. This is an
architecture counterexample, not a lower bound for every mechanism.

4. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS
A report can represent guaranteed reserve t together with signed exposure x;
F is the reserve left after a worst-case Euclidean uncertainty deduction.
Both the withdrawn bundle and retained bundle must remain reserve-backed.
An ellipsoidal exposure norm follows by a fixed linear change of coordinates.
No claim is made that this is a calibrated electricity market, monetary
valuation, private-cost model, or verified physical reserve certificate.
The elementary Lorentz proof supplies the desired rank-two example without
asserting an unchecked general Euclidean Jordan algebra theorem.
