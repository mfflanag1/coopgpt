A DIMENSION-INDEPENDENT FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION GUARANTEE ON LORENTZ CONES
17 October 2026. Root/composition corollary; frozen for independent audit.

THEOREM
In the entire fixed-owner Lorentz resource model of
CONE_LORENTZ_PHI_COALITION_THEOREM.txt, multiply every payment of that theorem
by 8/7. The resulting payments cover every coalition, are nonnegative, and
satisfy FULL coalition outside-production protection: if a coalition A jointly
withdraws D_i in K, with both withdrawn and retained bundles in K, then

 sum_(i in A)[y_i(b)-y_i(b-D)] >= F(sum_(i in A)D_i).

Their total is at most

                (8 phi/7) F(C),   phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2,

uniformly in the number of owners and Euclidean uncertainty coordinates.
This is a total-output bound, not a funding-only-the-cooperation-gain theorem.
The coefficient is approximately1.84918. No optimality among all rules is
claimed. The one-exposure-dimensional subdomain is equivalent, under the
fixed linear map (t,x)->(t+x,t-x), to two complementary resources.

PROOF
Use the notation of the phi theorem. A coalition's derivative along a joint
restoration (dt,dx) in K has form A dt-V dot dx, with

 A=1+s a'(s)h,  A>=||V||,
 a(s)=s/[c+(1-c)s],  a'(s)=c/[c+(1-c)s]^2,
 c=(sqrt(5)-1)/2,  h>=(s-1)/2.

The exact identity, for D=c+(1-c)s, is

 D^2-4c s(1-s)=[c-(1+c)s]^2 >=0.

It implies
 A>=1-c s(1-s)/(2D^2)>=7/8.                           (1)
This holds also at the central and boundary limits described in the phi
proof. After scaling, A_new>=1 and A_new>=||V_new||.
For ANY A>=1 with A>=||V|| and ANY dt>=||dx||,

 A dt-V dot dx >= A dt-||V|| ||dx|| >= dt-||dx||.

Indeed, writing alpha=A and beta=||V||, the exact difference is
 (alpha-1)dt-(beta-1)||dx||
 =(alpha-1)(dt-||dx||)+(alpha-beta)||dx||>=0.
Consequently the scaled directional derivative is at least F(dt,dx).
Integrate along the straight joint-restoration segment of the SAME actual
coalition. Its aggregate restoration direction is fixed and belongs to K;
homogeneity makes the integral bound exactly F(sum_A D_i). Interior portions
are differentiable, and the already proved continuity handles boundary and
zero endpoints. Coverage and total funding follow by positive scaling.

SCOPE AND FOLLOW-UP
This supplies a fixed bound on an unbounded family of nonpolyhedral resource
cones, with unrestricted mixed cone-bundle ownership and simultaneous
coalition reports. It does not claim that Euclidean reserve slack is already
a calibrated monetary model for an actual energy market. The classical
Lorentz geometry is an input. Historical priority of this mechanism and its
contract-specific application remains unconfirmed.
A precise further question is whether a different regular radial price curve
can improve8 phi/7 while retaining both the Lorentz gradient inequality and
the temporal lower bound1. The present proof gives no universal lower bound.
