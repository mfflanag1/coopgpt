A SUBLINEAR TCWM UPPER BOUND FOR THE RANK-THREE COMMON-CAP FAMILY
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate; independent audit requested.

TECHNOLOGY AND CLAIM
For arbitrary finite M>=1 let
 F(P,a,b)=min(P,sum_{j=1}^M min(a_j,b_j)).
Actual owners may hold arbitrary fixed mixed bundles across all2M+1 resource
rows; binding reports are nonnegative real quantities. There is an all-profile
nonnegative coalition-covering TCWM mechanism with funding at most
 [2+max{1,(16M/27)^(1/3)}] F.                                  (1)
Thus the same family that has an unrestricted square-root-logarithmic lower
bound also admits an O(M^(1/3)) upper bound, independent of owner count. This
does not match the lower bound. No full-OUT or marginal-floor claim is made.
Historical novelty is unconfirmed.

1. PUBLIC PARAMETER AND PRICE RULE
Choose any fixed public C>=1. Put
 g_j=min(P,a_j,b_j),  G=sum_j g_j,  r=P/G,
 h(r)=min(1,C/r),
 f(r)=min(1,2 sqrt(r)/(3 sqrt(3C))).                            (2)
For P>0,G>0 set resource prices
 p_P=h(r), p_{a_j}=f(r)g_j/a_j, p_{b_j}=f(r)g_j/b_j,             (3)
where a zero-coordinate contribution is defined to be zero. Owners receive
their actual reported bundles dotted with these common aggregate prices.
If P=0 or G=0, all owner payments are zero, as required by F=0. The per-branch
cap at the REPORTED common resource P is essential. The price rule is not a
fixed positive linear resource lift and is not a scalar rescaling of the graph
Huber mechanism ruled out in the earlier obstruction.

For all profiles,
 F=min(P,G), G<=MP, and hence r>=1/M whenever P,G>0.             (4)
Clipping each branch's feasible amount at P does not change the outer capped
sum in F. The total payment under(3) is EXACTLY
 P h(r)+2G f(r).                                               (5)
Every branch contributes g_j f from each positive endpoint; if either endpoint
is zero, g_j=0 and both contributions vanish.

2. COALITION COVERAGE
If r<=C, then p_P=1 and common-resource payment alone covers any coalition,
because its production cannot exceed its reported P stock.
If r>C, then G<P/C<=P. No branch can have g_j=P, so every branch with both
endpoints positive has g_j=min(a_j,b_j) and
 g_j/a_j+g_j/b_j>=1.
Moreover h(r)+f(r)>=1 for r>C. To see this, set x=r/C>1; the largest value of
(1-1/x)/sqrt(x) is2/(3sqrt3), attained at x=3. Therefore
 2sqrt(x)/(3sqrt3)>=1-1/x,
and truncating that left side at1 preserves the inequality. Thus each active
triple(P,a_j,b_j) has total price at least1. Nonnegative resource prices are
a feasible dual cover for the production LP restricted to the current support,
and hence every actual-owner coalition receives at least its production value.
An activity using an absent resource cannot run for any coalition and needs no
cover inequality at that profile. Zero-value profiles satisfy coverage trivially.

3. ALL-MIXED-OWNER TCWM: THE COALITION FORMULA
Fix ANY coalition with aggregate holdings s=(s_P,s_a,s_b), where0<=s<=c, and
hold every outside report fixed. Its payment is
 Y=s_P h(r)+f(r) Z,
 Z=sum_j g_j(s_{a_j}/a_j+s_{b_j}/b_j),  0<=Z<=2G.               (6)
A restoration of one coalition coordinate increases both its s-coordinate and
the corresponding aggregate coordinate by the same amount. It suffices to
prove Y is nondecreasing under each such coordinate restoration. At regular
points of the finitely piecewise-smooth coordinate path,
 h'<=0, f'>=0, h+r h'>=0, and f-2r f'>=0.                     (7)
For h, the penultimate expression is1 on its constant branch and0 on C/r.
For f, the last expression is0 on its square-root branch and1 on its constant
branch. These inequalities also hold on either one-sided branch at a switch.

4. RESTORING ONE INNER RESOURCE
Restore a_j, keeping P and every other aggregate resource fixed. Write a prime
for this one-coordinate derivative. Except at a tie, g_j' is either0 or1,
and G'=g_j'>=0. Differentiating the coalition's branch term gives
 Z'=g_j'(s_{a_j}/a_j+s_{b_j}/b_j)
       +g_j(a_j-s_{a_j})/a_j^2.
If g_j'=1, then g_j=a_j and this equals1+s_{b_j}/b_j>=1=G'.
If g_j'=0, it is nonnegative. Thus in either case Z'>=G'. Since
 r'=-rG'/G,
 Y'=s_P h' r'+f' r' Z+f Z'
    >=[f-2r f']G'>=0,                                       (8)
using s_P>=0,h'<=0,Z<=2G,f'>=0. The b_j argument is identical. This accounts
for the change in coalition OWNERSHIP SHARE s_{a_j}/a_j, not merely aggregate
resource-price monotonicity.

5. RESTORING THE COMMON RESOURCE
Now restore P. Let t be the number of branches where P is strictly the minimum
coordinate. Then G'=t and
 Z'=sum_{j:P<min(a_j,b_j)}(s_{a_j}/a_j+s_{b_j}/b_j)>=0.
Because G>=Pt,
 r'=(G-Pt)/G^2>=0, and P r'<=r.
Here s_P'=1, so
 Y'=h+s_P h'r'+f'Zr'+fZ'
    >=h+P h'r'>=h+r h'>=0,                                  (9)
where the first inequality uses s_P<=P and h'<=0. This proves monotonicity
for common-resource restoration, including arbitrary ownership of inner rows.

6. SWITCHES, ZERO REPORTS AND FINITE DEVIATIONS
Along any single-coordinate restoration with P,G>0, each g_j is a minimum of
finitely many affine functions, G is piecewise affine, and r is a ratio of
such functions. Its crossings of the fixed h,f thresholds give a finite
partition into smooth intervals, except identically tied intervals where one
may directly use the relevant fixed expression. On each interval(8) or(9)
applies. The owner-payment formula is continuous at all clipping and threshold
switches, so it is nondecreasing on the full coordinate path.
A branch term g_j s_{a_j}/a_j is defined to be0 at a_j=0 and is continuous in
actual reports there: its absolute value is at most g_j<=a_j. The other term
has the analogous property. If G tends to0 with P fixed positive, f eventually
is1 and h=CG/P, so total payment is(C+2)G and tends to0. If P tends to0, (4)
and the funding bound proved below force all payments to tend to0. All payments
are nonnegative, so a path starting on the zero-value set starts at0 and can
only enter the positive region with nonnegative payment. The same formulas
prove monotonicity at all support changes. Finally perform any whole-bundle
restoration coordinate by coordinate, keeping the coalition and all outsiders
fixed. This proves the finite TCWM inequality on every real profile.
No derivative or continuity of an arbitrary candidate mechanism is assumed;
these properties are proved directly for the explicit rule(2)--(3).

7. FUNDING
If r<=1 then F=P,h=1 and
 (Ph+2Gf)/F=1+2f/r
 <=1+4/(3sqrt(3C r))
 <=1+4sqrt(M)/(3sqrt(3C)).                                    (10)
If1<r<=C then F=G and the ratio is r+2f<=C+2. If r>C it is C+2f<=C+2.
Thus for every public C>=1 the funding factor is at most
 max{C+2,1+4sqrt(M)/(3sqrt(3C))}.                              (11)
Choose C=max{1,(16M/27)^(1/3)}. When the second entry exceeds1, the second
term in(11) is1+C, so C+2 dominates. When C=1 (for integer M this means M=1),
that term is less than3=C+2. This proves(1). Optimizing(11) more closely can
slightly improve additive constants but does not change the exponent; no such
tuning is needed for the sublinear theorem.

8. SCOPE AND REMAINING GAP
The proof covers arbitrary unbounded M and owner counts, mixed ownership,
all nonnegative real reports, and every fixed-owner coalition restoration.
The lower bound in RANK_THREE_UNBOUNDED_TCWM.txt applies to unrestricted
mechanisms; this explicit upper establishes a nontrivial rate on the SAME
family. It neither extends to arbitrary rank-three hypergraphs nor resolves
the sqrt(log M) versus M^(1/3) gap. The prices are given in elementary real
arithmetic; exact rational output is not claimed because square roots can occur.
No measured AI-system welfare, full coalition OUT, private costs, novelty, or
field-level significance claim is inferred from this proof alone.

9. EXACT SYMBOLIC IMPLEMENTATION AND VERIFICATION
common_cap_mechanism.py implements a rational public choice: the smallest integer
C>=1 with27C^3>=16M. This gives the exact funding bound C+2, at most one larger
than(1). For rational reports the single square-root factor satisfies
 f^2=4P/(27CG) when f<1. Each owner's payment is returned symbolically as
 A+B sqrt(Q), with rational A,B,Q; it is not rounded to a purportedly truthful
rational payment. The demonstration computes G and coalition payments directly.
check_common_cap_mechanism.py uses exact rational intervals plus exact algebraic
zero identities to compare such payments. It passed1743 funding profiles,
1644 coalition-coverage checks and14460 finite TCWM comparisons, including
zero-value faces, shared fractional holdings, simultaneous restorations and
up to12 branches. These finite exact tests corroborate, and do not replace,
the analytic all-profile proof above. The comparison routine has a precision
guard and raises rather than returning an uncertified approximate sign.
