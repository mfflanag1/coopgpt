EXPLICIT INPUT MODEL
Suppose the production function is supplied as K nonnegative rational linear
forms in d resources:
 F(x)=min_{1<=k<=K} a^k.x,  a^k>=0.
Owners submit nonnegative rational bundles b_i and aggregate C=sum_i b_i.
This is an EXPLICIT min-linear representation. If F is instead supplied as a
packing LP with exponentially many dual vertices, this note does not assert
polynomial conversion to the explicit representation. Arbitrary oracle F is
outside this algorithmic claim.

THE UNIVERSAL CONSTANT
Let c be the unique root in (1/2,1) of c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1 and t0=1/c-1.
The exact output format treats c as this named real constant; it is generally
not rational. The formula below also uses logarithms and min with t0. It is
not a rational-payment algorithm and does not rely on exact sign decisions
between arbitrary rational breakpoints and the transcendental constant t0.

POLYNOMIAL EXACT SYMBOLIC CONSTRUCTION
For each active coordinate j, form the K rational affine functions
 alpha_k=a^k_j C_j,   beta_k=sum_{l!=j} a^k_l C_l,
 h_j(u)=min_k(alpha_k+beta_k u), 0<=u<=1.
All alpha,beta are nonnegative. A direct polynomial algorithm is:
1. Enumerate all pairwise line intersections strictly between 0 and 1; include
   0 and 1, remove duplicates, and sort the resulting rational numbers.
2. On each consecutive open interval evaluate all K lines at its rational
   midpoint, choose a minimizing line with a fixed tie convention, and merge
   adjacent intervals whose chosen affine functions agree.
3. Record the concave hinge decomposition
 h_j(u)=alpha+beta u+sum_s Delta_s min(u,s),
 where alpha=h_j(0), beta is the final slope, and Delta_s>=0 is each downward
 slope jump at rational breakpoint s in (0,1). Line intersections and slope
 continuity establish this identity at every u in [0,1].
The naive method takes O(K^3) rational arithmetic operations per face and uses
polynomial bit length; a standard lower-hull algorithm is faster but unnecessary
for the claim. There are at most K distinct active affine pieces, so the final
expression has O(K) hinge terms per face (O(K^2) also suffices as a bound).

Define z=min(s,t0) and
 psi(s)=(1-c)z-c(1-z)log(1-z).
Then psi(s)=E[min(U,s)] for density c/(1-u) on (0,t0). To verify, for s<t0 split
the integral at s and integrate u/(1-u); for s>=t0 it equals E U=c. At s=t0 the
formula equals c using log(1-t0)=-1/c and c(1+t0)=1. Consequently the EXACT face
average and payments are
 H_j(C)=alpha+beta c+sum_s Delta_s psi(s),
 y_i=sum_{j:C_j>0} (b_ij/C_j) H_j(C).
Absent-coordinate terms are exactly zero. The output can be a finite symbolic
expression tree, or the rational hinge coefficient lists together with these
two universal formulas. min(s,t0) may equivalently be written
 (s+t0-|s-t0|)/2 if an absolute-value expression format is preferred.
No transcendental comparison is needed to construct this exact expression.

BIT AND PRECISION GUARANTEES
Products/sums of rational input coefficients and reports have polynomial binary
encoding length. Intersections are ratios of their differences, also with
polynomial encoding length. Sorting and midpoint comparisons are exact rational
operations. Thus the complete symbolic output is constructed in polynomial time
in d,K, owner count, and rational input bit length.
For numerical values to any prescribed absolute precision 2^-P, c can be
computed by interval root finding with elementary exp evaluation; the derivative
of its defining function is bounded below by 1 on (1/2,1). Clamp min is Lipschitz,
and all logarithm arguments satisfy 1-z>=1-t0=exp(-1/c), a positive UNIVERSAL
constant. Therefore interval evaluation to polynomially many extra bits gives
certified absolute precision in time polynomial in input bits and P using
standard arbitrary-precision elementary arithmetic. No difficult rational-vs-t0
separation bound is required. Large rational coefficients only require extra
precision proportional to their encoding length.

Crucial implementation distinction: an approximation of each payment is not
itself an exact truthful/full-OUT mechanism. Independent rounding errors can
reverse a tight incentive or coalition inequality. The theorem guarantees the
EXACT symbolic real-valued rule; this note makes no claim that a finite-decimal
payment settlement retains exact inequalities without a separate robust
rounding theorem. The included numerical checks are only corroboration.
