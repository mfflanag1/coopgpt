17 October 2026. Bounded gate; conventional proof, not novelty confirmation.

VERDICT

I independently derived the threshold-star identity and checked the complete
measure argument in unrestricted/SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_OBSTRUCTION_GATE.txt
Sections1-4. No material gap found. The construction constrains a whole positive
mixture; it does not infer mixture necessity from a single-p failure or from
formal coefficient signs. The root-proposed extension to owner-count-dependent
measures and then all fixed positive marginal-contribution rules also survives
the checks below. Opposite-model review of the new result remains a separate gate.

These are SCALED POSITIVE MARGINAL-CONTRIBUTION RULES: their total coefficient
mass need not be1, so the mixture itself is not necessarily a normalized
semivalue. Each individual Bernoulli m_i is the standard semivalue ingredient.

For every fixed s>=2 and r>=2, the resulting scoped obstruction is:

 For arbitrary fixed-owner all-profile rules of the form
 y_i(b)=sum_(R subset N\i) w_(n,i,R) [F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)],
 with finite nonnegative report-independent coefficients, coverage of every
 coalition and TCWM for all coalitions of size at most s imply

                liminf_(n->infinity) kappa_n >= s^(r-1)/r,             (A)

 already for the single technology F(c)=min(c_1,...,c_r), on fully eligible
 fixed actual-owner domains. Coefficients may depend on n, owner label, public
 technology, and s. The bound applies a fortiori under full coalition OUT.

For arity at most3, (A) is s^2/3. The existing 3s^2 construction in
energy/BOUNDED_COALITION_SPARSE_PRODUCTION.txt is a member of this architecture.
Thus this architecture has quadratic order in s, up to constants, for the
uniform arbitrary-owner problem. On the fixed min_r technology itself, the
already accepted unrestricted TCWM factor(r+1)/2 is independent of s (and the
full-OUT factor1+c(r-1) is also independent of s). Thus the present lower
reveals architecture inefficiency even on a constant-dimension technology;
it does not improve the general bounded-collusion lower. This is NOT a lower
for all mechanisms;
signed coefficients, report-adaptive coefficients and non-marginal rules are
outside the theorem. It does not assert unsafe support is absent at finite n.

1. CHECKED FINITE TRANSITION, WITH ACTUAL OWNERS

First consider one common finite nonnegative measure mu on[0,1] and
y_i=integral m_i(p) dmu, where m_i is the Bernoulli semivalue. Fix k>=1,
0<=t<k. There are k outside owners each supplying center capacity1/k and
s protected leaf owners. One focal leaf restores1/k from baseline t/k;
the other s-1 protected leaves each supply1 and remain unchanged. Production
is min(total center,total leaf). The identical coalition contains all s leaves
before and after. Outsiders, owner identities and eligibility remain fixed.

Write epsilon=1/k and T(p)=Pr[Bin(k,p)>=t+1]. If any nonfocal protected leaf
is sampled, it saturates center supply, whose total never exceeds1. Otherwise
the focal increment raises value by exactly epsilon on the event defining T.
Therefore the focal marginal changes by epsilon(1-p)^(s-1)T(p).

For an unchanged protected leaf j, its positive marginal term includes its
own unit and is saturated both times. Its negative term increases only when
the focal leaf is sampled and all other s-2 protected leaves are absent.
Its marginal change is -epsilon p(1-p)^(s-2)T(p). Summing gives exactly

 Delta sum_S m_i(p)=epsilon(1-p)^(s-2)(1-sp)T(p).                       (B)

This formula is valid at p=0,1 by direct evaluation, with exponent-zero
factors interpreted as1. The centers are outsiders, so there are no omitted
center payments. Unchanged protected members are indispensable: the contract
explicitly allows their zero withdrawals. These are genuine permitted report
comparisons, not manipulations of a free-standing characteristic function.

The same witness lives in fixed min_r: give each center owner1/k of each of
the first r-1 resources and each leaf only the last resource. Every owner is
eligible for all r rows throughout; the profiles merely use particular rows.
No unowned/free auxiliary resource or change of technology is introduced.

2. SUPPORT NECESSITY FOR ONE MEASURE

First use protected pairs, t=k-1. Then T=p^k, so TCWM forces
integral(1-2p)p^k dmu>=0 for every k. Dominated convergence yields
-mu({1})>=0. This removes the endpoint invisible to the s>2 version of(B).

If mu((1/s,1))>0, choose a non-atom a in(1/s,1) with positive mass above a.
Take t=floor(ka). Binomial concentration makes T tend to0 below a and1 above a.
Finite mu and the bounded polynomial factor justify dominated convergence.
The limit of the TCWM inequalities is
integral_(a,1)(1-p)^(s-2)(1-sp) dmu>=0,
but its integrand is strictly negative on a set of positive measure. Thus
support(mu) is contained in[0,1/s]. Atoms at0 and1/s are allowed.

Each k uses a finite owner model. The limit is a mathematical consequence of
requiring the same measure to meet every finite model; no infinite strategic
population is used. Infinite total measure cannot evade finite funding:
at a profile with one owner supplying a balanced positive bundle and everyone
else zero, its Bernoulli marginal is a positive constant for every p.

3. COVERAGE AND FUNDING NORMALIZATION

In min_r, let r owners each supply one different unit resource, with any
remaining owners reporting zero. Each active owner's m_i(p)=p^(r-1), so
coverage of their coalition requires r integral p^(r-1)dmu>=1. Support in
[0,1/s] gives mass(mu)>=s^(r-1)/r. At an all-balanced profile, each owner
supplies(1,...,1): F(b_R)=|R| and every marginal is1. Funding therefore bounds
mass(mu) by kappa. This proves the common-measure lower without using a
loose grand-marginal upper bound as if it were necessary.

4. OWNER-SPECIFIC AND n-DEPENDENT MIXTURES

On the fully eligible n-owner domain, permutation-average the complete rule.
Coverage, funding and all coalition-size-s TCWM constraints are linear and
preserved under this averaging. Actual owners are not replaced within a
comparison. For Bernoulli mixtures, permutation symmetry yields the common
measure mu_bar_n=(1/n)sum_i mu_(n,i), with mass at most kappa_n.

For any subsequence with bounded kappa_n, finite positive measures on compact
[0,1] have a weakly convergent further subsequence. Every fixed threshold
witness can be padded with zero owners once n is large enough. Null owners
have zero marginals, and adding their independent inclusion bits changes no
active owner's semivalue. Thus every fixed-k polynomial inequality passes to
the common weak limit. So does the pure-r-owner coverage moment, and so does
the mass bound (test function1). The limit measure satisfies Section2.

Take a subsequence realizing a finite liminf of kappa_n; if none exists, (A)
is trivial. Sections2-3 now give (A). The measure at a particular finite n
can still put mass above1/s. Only the limit support is restricted here.

5. ALL FIXED NONNEGATIVE MARGINAL COEFFICIENTS

The same permutation average makes w_(n,i,R) depend only on |R|=h. Write
alpha_(n,h)=binom(n-1,h) w_(n,h), and define
mu_n=sum_(h=0)^(n-1) alpha_(n,h) delta_(h/(n-1)).
The all-balanced profile gives sum_h alpha_(n,h)<=kappa_n. These measures
represent subset-size masses; at finite n they need not exactly reproduce
the rule by independent Bernoulli sampling.

Fix any report profile involving A potentially nonzero actual owners, and
pad all others with zeros. For an active focal owner, only a=A-1 other
inclusion bits affect its marginal. A uniform h-subset of n-1 owners induces
sampling without replacement of these a bits from n-1 labels containing h
ones. Bernoulli p=h/(n-1) corresponds to sampling those labels with replacement.

Couple the two procedures sequentially. Until the with-replacement procedure
repeats a label, the sampled distinct labels are identical. At a repetition,
resample uniformly among unused labels for the without-replacement version.
This gives the correct distributions, and their disagreement probability is
at most a(a-1)/[2(n-1)] by the union bound. It is uniform in h, including h=0
and h=n-1. Thus total variation is at most this quantity, truncated at1.

Every relevant nonnegative marginal lies in[0,F(b_N)], a fixed finite interval.
Its expectation error is at most F(b_N) times that variation bound. Averaging
with alpha masses multiplies this bound by at most kappa_n. The same conclusion
applies to each of the finitely many payments in a fixed transition, separately
at its two endpoint profiles. Its error tends to zero uniformly over h.

After selecting a weakly convergent subsequence of mu_n, active payments
therefore converge to the genuine Bernoulli-mixture payments of the limit:
the remaining test functions m_i(p;b) are continuous finite polynomials.
Every padded threshold TCWM and pure-r coverage inequality passes to that
limit. Null owners pay zero exactly, not merely asymptotically. Sections2-3
again give(A). No coherence of the finite-n coefficients across n was assumed.

6. ADVERSARIAL CHECKS, EVIDENCE AND STOP

The fragile points all have explicit resolutions: negative single-p values
may cancel in a mixture, so concentration is needed; p=1 needs the separate
pair witness; n-dependent rules need weak compactness plus dummy padding;
general fixed weights need finite-active hypergeometric approximation, not an
unjustified exact de Finetti representation; and mixed eligibility is handled
by choosing the legitimate fully eligible fixed-owner instance for the lower.

The sibling's exact checker independently enumerates230 finite transition

My additional SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_EXCHANGEABLE_CHECK.py verifies2920 exact
hypergeometric/Bernoulli distributions with up to30 population labels and6
relevant labels. It checks normalization, endpoints and the coupling bound,
including a=1. Its stored CHECKS.json is finite corroboration only; the
coupling, weak-limit and support proofs establish the universal quantifiers.

No further mixture tuning is justified within fixed positive marginal rules.
The previous log-density lead fails in this architecture regardless of a
finite positive truncation/completion intended to work uniformly in n.
The remaining mathematical question is whether a genuinely different rule
can improve O(s^2) for general arity-three production. Any successor must leave
fixed positive whole-owner marginal weights, for example through a justified
resource-level or profile-adaptive construction; merely choosing different
positive cardinality weights cannot pass this gate. This gate does not
settle that unrestricted question or the sharp constant within the excluded
architecture. Semivalues, symmetrization, concentration, weak compactness and
sampling couplings are established ingredients; the particular synthesis and
lower-bound priority have not been independently cleared in the literature.
