TWO INTERACTING COMMON RESOURCES: A LOGARITHMIC DEMAND-RATIO BOUND
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate; novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL
Activity j consumes a_j>0 units of shared resource P, b_j>0 units of shared
resource Q, and beta_jl>0 units of each of k branch-private resources c_jl. Its
public profit is w_j>=0. Private rows are distinct between activities. Fixed
actual owners may hold arbitrary mixed bundles across all rows. The LP is
 F=max sum_j w_j x_j, subject to sum_j a_j x_j<=P,
 sum_j b_j x_j<=Q, beta_jl x_j<=c_jl, x_j>=0.                 (1)
Both common resources may bind and the ratios a_j/b_j may all differ. Coefficients
and profits are public and fixed; only resource capacities are reported.

Let R=max_j(a_j/b_j)/min_j(a_j/b_j)>=1 after discarding zero-profit activities.
For any chosen public rho>1, let T be the number of nonempty geometric buckets
of these ratios, anchored at their public minimum. Then
 T<=1+floor(log_rho R).                                     (2)
All claims below hold for every capacity profile and every fixed owner model;
there is no bounded-grid or probabilistic hypothesis.

THE PARTICULARLY CLEAN CASE k=1
There is a nonnegative coalition-covering TCWM mechanism with funding at most
 2 rho T F.                                                 (3)
There is also a full coalition outside-production-protecting mechanism with
funding at most
 rho(1+2c)T F,                                              (4)
where c is the previously proved universal constant satisfying
 c(2-exp(-1/c))=1, c approximately0.543063.
Here full OUT means a coalition's payment gain on restoring its withheld bundle
d is at least F(d). Bounds(3),(4) are independent of the number of activities and
actual owners, except through the public ratio condition R. For rho=2, (4) is
2(1+2c)T with T<=1+floor(log_2 R). When all ratios coincide, bucketing is unnecessary:
use exact one-type bounds2 and1+2c without rho.

For arbitrary fixed k, the harmonic type-composition theorem yields the explicit
TCWM-only bound in section5 below. Full OUT is not silently inferred from that
nonlinear harmonic aggregation.

1. FIXED LINEAR OWNER RESCALING
Set z_j=b_j x_j, r_j=a_j/b_j, v_j=w_j/b_j, and
 q_j=min_l (b_j/beta_jl)c_jl.
Then(1) is exactly max sum_j v_j z_j subject to
 sum_j r_j z_j<=P, sum_j z_j<=Q,0<=z_j<=q_j.                  (5)
Each private holding of each actual owner is scaled by the same fixed positive
b_j/beta_jl. This is additive across owners and maps every binding underreport
and withheld bundle to its scaled counterpart. No actual owner is split or merged.

2. A ONE-SIDED PUBLIC TECHNOLOGY APPROXIMATION
Let r_min=min_j r_j and define
 rhat_j=r_min rho^m where m is the unique integer with
 r_min rho^m<=r_j<r_min rho^(m+1).
Define F+ by replacing r_j in(5) with rhat_j, leaving all other data unchanged.
For EVERY capacity vector, including zeros,
 F<=F+<=rho F.                                             (6)
The first inequality relaxes a shared constraint. For the second, take any proxy
plan z and scale it to z/rho. Since r_j<rho rhat_j,
 sum_j r_j z_j/rho<=sum_j rhat_j z_j<=P,
 sum_j z_j/rho<=Q, and z_j/rho<=q_j.
It is feasible in the true game and has exactly1/rho of the proxy value. The same
argument holds for each coalition and each withheld bundle. In particular F=0
implies F+=0, so approximation creates no spurious positive value on zero faces.

3. EXACT PRIVATE-POOL REDUCTION INSIDE A TYPE WHEN k=1
A type consists of activities with the same rhat. Its isolated game has common
throughput bound B=min(P/rhat,Q) and private caps q_j. Sorting its public profits
v_j gives the exact finite fractional-knapsack decomposition
 F_type=sum_l (v_(l)-v_(l+1)) min(P/rhat,Q,sum_{j in prefix_l}q_j).
For k=1 each prefix private pool is a FIXED LINEAR sum of original private rows.
Its actual-owner allocation is the corresponding sum of that owner's scaled
holdings, while the P and Q holdings stay with the original owners. Thus each
prefix is exactly a three-pool minimum production game, with fixed additive owner
mapping for reports and withheld resources.

Use the independently proved three-pool TCWM factor2 or three-pool full-OUT factor
1+2c. The former is the d=3 instance of the earlier sharp(d+1)/2 theorem; the
latter is the d=3 instance of energy/GENERAL_LOGARITHMIC_FACE_OUTSIDE.txt. These
are existing ingredients in this campaign, not claims of a new three-pool rule.
A fixed positive sum over public profit prefixes gives the same factor for the
isolated type, preserves coverage, and in the OUT case preserves its exact
outside-production value. At most the number of activities many prefixes are used.

4. INTERACTING TYPE COMPOSITION AND TRANSFER BACK TO THE TRUE GAME
Pay the sum of these mechanisms for the T isolated proxy types, on the same
fixed actual-owner profile. Relaxing competition between types gives
 F+(c)<=sum_tau F_tau(c).
The sum is TCWM. In the full-OUT case its gain on restoring d is at least
sum_tau F_tau(d)>=F+(d)>=F(d), so it protects the actual outside-production option.
Coverage for the true game follows by the same inequality on coalition holdings.

Each type's optimum is individually feasible in the proxy game; time-share those
T plans equally to obtain sum_tau F_tau<=T F+. More generally this is the exact
type-congestion argument in HARMONIC_MULTI_COMMON_TCWM.txt. Total funding is
therefore <=K3 sum_tau F_tau<=K3 T F+<=rho K3 T F,
where K3 is2 or1+2c. This proves(3),(4). The public approximation game is fixed
through every deviation, so exact incentives are not inferred from approximate
payments or from a report-dependent choice of buckets.

5. MORE THAN ONE PRIVATE COMPLEMENTARY ROW
Set alpha=(k-1)/k, a=alpha/2 and A_k=k/(k+1)^(1+1/k). For type tau with M_tau
branches, the harmonic theorem gives a TCWM factor bounded by
 sqrt(2k A_k) M_tau^a + B_k, B_k=2+max(2,k).
This follows from its integer-free C=max(1,sqrt((k A_k/2)M_tau^alpha)) and
max(1,x)<=1+x. All positive-ratio types use both shared rows. Concavity of x^a
(or direct counting if a=0) yields
 sum_tau M_tau^a <=T^(1-a) M^a.
Thus the true game has TCWM funding factor at most
 rho [sqrt(2k A_k) T^(1-a) M^a+B_k T].                       (7)
For k=1 prefer the sharper three-pool bounds above. For k>=2, (7) extends the
common-cap exponent to noncollinear two-resource demands with a controlled
public-ratio loss. It does not settle whether that dependence on R is necessary.

SCOPE, PRIORITY AND REMAINING QUESTION
The geometric bucketing, fractional-knapsack layering and time-sharing comparison
are elementary established approximation ingredients. The contribution proposed
here is their combination with fixed-owner all-profile incentive theorems and the
harmonic shared-resource composition lemma; historical priority is unconfirmed.
The case of arbitrary ratios with a constant independent of R remains open in
this investigation. Zero common coefficients, shared private rows, private profits,
indivisibility and scheduling constraints are outside the displayed theorem.
They must not be silently treated as negligible positive coefficients.
No participation-floor transfer is asserted across(6): value approximation alone
does not order grand-marginal increments.
