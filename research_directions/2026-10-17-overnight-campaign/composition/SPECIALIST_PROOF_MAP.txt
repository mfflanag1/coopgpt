1. EXACT ECONOMIC AND MATHEMATICAL CONTRACT

Technology F:R_+ x R_+^m -> R_+ is finite, continuous, concave, coordinatewise
nondecreasing, and homogeneous of degree one. Write its resources as(P,c).
Assume F(0,c)=F(P,0)=0. At each fixed P, private diminishing returns means

 F(P,x+delta e_j)-F(P,x) >= F(P,z+delta e_j)-F(P,z)

for every x<=z, delta>=0, and private coordinate j. This includes decreasing
own-coordinate marginal returns; for a smooth F, all private Hessian entries
are nonpositive. No differentiability is required in the final theorem.

There are finitely many actual owners, fixed throughout all comparisons.
Owner i reports a nonnegative bundle b_i of its eligible resource rows and
may withhold any componentwise bounded subbundle. Owners may hold both common
and private rows; the common row need not have one owner. Eligibility does not
change, owners are not replaced by virtual identities, and coalition members
who change nothing remain in the coalition's payment total. This contract
does not model overreports, false identities, private costs, or outside inputs.

Put C=sum_i b_i and Y_S=sum_(i in S)y_i. Payments are nonnegative. Coverage is
Y_S>=F(sum_(i in S)b_i). Funding factor kappa means total payments<=kappa F(C),
including production revenue, not only the added subsidy.

For a coalition restoring bundles d_i>=0, with outsiders fixed, put D=sum d_i.
Payment-only TCWM requires Y_S(high)>=Y_S(low). Full coalition OUT instead
requires Y_S(high)>=Y_S(low)+F(D): members can pool ALL restored/withheld
resources for outside production. Checking separate coordinate outside values
does not establish this stronger condition.

The accepted mechanism sets absent-coordinate prices to zero. If P=0 or the
entire private block is zero, all payments are zero. Coverage only concerns
bundles on the current active resource face; zero prices on absent rows are
not asserted to support hypothetical production using those rows.

2. ACCEPTED THEOREM AND PROOF OBLIGATIONS

Claim: one all-profile price field q gives y_i=b_i.q(C), coverage, and TCWM
with total funding<=3F(C)/2 for every fixed finite owner model. The constant
is sharp as a supremum over this technology class and growing finite owner
counts. No claim of a matching optimum for each fixed owner population follows.

The main theorem file contains the following obligations, in reading order.

Sections1-2: smooth construction and coverage. Independently uniform s,t in
(0,1) give q=integral integral grad F(tP,s c) ds dt. Every sampled homogeneous
supporting gradient covers coalition production. Integrating along each block
gives Pq_P=integral F(P,s c)ds and c.q_c=integral F(tP,c)dt. Two applications
of concavity bound their sum by3F(C)/2; these identities also prove that every
charged price is finite, despite possible improper integrals at the axes.

Sections3-4: all-profile fixed-owner incentives. Euler differentiation gives
P F_Pj=-sum_k c_k F_kj>=0 for every private j. Thus private prices decrease
under private increases and increase under common increases. For a fixed
coalition with private holdings a and outside holdings o, its private payment
is H_c-o.q_c, which increases under private restoration. Its common payment
is its common ownership fraction times H_P, also increasing. Common restoration
increases each private receipt and the common receipt. Compose these finite
steps; no assumption that coalition holdings are pure or infinitesimal enters.
At newly activated rows, outsiders have zero holdings, so the proof only needs
antitonicity on old positive rows, not on the zero-price convention itself.

Section5: continuous nonsmooth technologies and simultaneous quantifiers.
Log-diagonal smoothing preserves every hypothesis and gives
exp(-epsilon)F<=F_epsilon<=exp(epsilon)F on ALL faces. For0<epsilon<=1 all
charged prices lie in the same intervals[0,(3e/2)F(C)/C_j]. Compactness of the
product over all profiles yields ONE limiting field through nested epsilon
tails. Every finite coverage, funding, and restoration inequality is closed
and survives simultaneously. This is an existence argument: no uniqueness,
measurability, continuity, or evaluation algorithm for the general limiting
field is asserted. Unrelated pointwise subsequences would not suffice.

Sharpness dependency: root/COALITION_THREE_HALVES_CANDIDATE.txt, LOWER THEOREM
and its single-edge two-profile proof, with adjudication03. The subclass
F(P,c1)=min(P,c1) forces factors approaching3/2 as the finite population grows.
This is a lower for all mechanisms in the stated uniform domain, not merely
for aggregate-price or smooth mechanisms.

3. PUBLIC POLYMATROIDS: EXPLICIT NONSMOOTH SUBCLASS

For any finite E and normalized, nondecreasing, submodular rank function r,

 F_r(P,c)=min_(S subset E) {P r(S)+sum_(j notin S)c_j}.

Its interpretation as maximum total output in a box-truncated polymatroid
is established polymatroid theory. Formula alone defines the technology here.
Sections6-6A of the theorem provide an elementary uncrossing proof: the unique
maximal minimizing set S^+ grows under private increases and shrinks under
common increases. The dual gradient(r(S^+),1_(E\S^+)) consequently has exactly
the monotonicity used by the finite incentive proof. Persistent ties have
equal directional slopes; no full-dimensional differentiability assumption
is applied to a two-dimensional sampling plane.

At a fixed report, the lower envelope of lines zP r(S)+c(E\S) has a nested
chain of at most|E|+1 gradients with positive interval weight. The ratio t/s
has CDF z/2 below1 and1-1/(2z) above1. Integrating those interval masses gives
exact rational prices when reports and rank values are rational. This bypasses
compactness for this subclass. The accepted implementation corollary
composition/POLYMATROID_ORACLE_COROLLARY.txt (root adjudication62) recovers the
chain with at most2m-1 exact maximal submodular minimizations on m active rows.
Its conditional polynomial oracle/bit model assumes rational rank values of
polynomial encoding length and an exact SFM backend. Nagano--Kawahara2013
supplies the established decomposition recursion; Iwata--Orlin2008 supplies
exact maximal SFM. See energy/POLYMATROID_ORACLE_SOURCE_GATE.txt. The supplied
exhaustive backend is a verification oracle, not a practical generic backend.
No novelty is claimed for principal partitions, SFM, or this decomposition.

4. EXACT OUT STOP: WHAT IT PROVES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

Let gamma[2-exp(-1/gamma)]=1, gamma approximately0.543063. The failed candidate
uses the existing logarithmic scalar U, with density gamma/(1-u), to set
q_P=E F(P,Uc)/P and q_j=E integral grad_j F(UP,s c)ds. Its component prices
use different sampling laws; general supporting-price coverage is not
automatic and is not needed for the failure certificate.

Take the uniform rank-two polymatroid on three private activities. Owner A
initially holds(0,3/5,3/5,0); owner B holds(1,0,0,5/4). Owner A restores
D=(1/50,1/50,1/50,0). Its outside production is F(D)=1/25, but its payment
increase is strictly less than1/25-1/600. The note proves this using exact
binary identities along all integration rays and elementary positive Taylor
bounds on exponentials. No numerical optimizer, logarithm approximation, or
limit in owner count establishes this example.

The current principal partition groups the two restored private rows under
one rank increment, whereas their pooled outside bundle can use two units
of rank. This defeats that specific completion. It does NOT establish a
larger necessary funding factor for arbitrary full-OUT mechanisms; the sharp
all-mechanism OUT question for public polymatroids remains open here.
