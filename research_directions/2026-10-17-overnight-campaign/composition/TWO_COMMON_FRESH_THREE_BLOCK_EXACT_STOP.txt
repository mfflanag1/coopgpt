CANDIDATE AND ITS VALID PROPERTIES
For a finite concave monotone degree-one homogeneous two-common/private
technology (in particular the packing LP below), let U,V,S be independent uniforms on
[0,1] and set q(C)=E grad F(UP,VQ,S c), paying each actual owner b_i.q(C).
If F vanishes when either common coordinate or the entire private block is
zero, this always gives supporting-price coalition coverage and funding<=2F:
integrating each coordinate/block gives three face averages; their average
arguments equal (2/3)C, so concavity bounds their sum by3F((2/3)C)=2F(C).
This observation alone does not establish incentives.

STOPPING CONSEQUENCE
The accepted one-common/private-DR theorem uses complementarity of every
private marginal with the single common row. With two commons, homogeneity
only controls their weighted sum. Uniformly averaging both common reports
does not repair this missing sign, even for aligned profits. Further work
must introduce a different common/private compensation rule. This is an
exact architecture stop, not evidence that a ratio-independent mechanism
for the underlying two-common technology is impossible.
