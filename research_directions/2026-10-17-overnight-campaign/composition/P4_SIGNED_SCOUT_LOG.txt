P4 COUPLED-MECHANISM SCOUT: NON-PROMOTED CANDIDATES
17 October 2026. These numerical screens are discovery evidence only.

1. Bipartition face averaging with within-side radial marginal allocation.
For path capacities(a,b,c,d), L=a+c,R=b+d, and the sharp binary short-price
function q, the resulting prices are
 p_a=q(max(a/b,L/R)), p_b=q(min(R/L,b/a)),
 p_c=q(min(L/R,c/d)), p_d=q(max(d/c,R/L)).
This has promising total funding by block Jensen, but on separated outer-edge
regions it reproduces the independent binary logarithmic component prices.
The root's exact GRAPH_COMPONENTWISE_OUT_BARRIER therefore already excludes
it; no new obstruction was claimed. P4_SIGNED_BLOCK_SCOUT.py preserves the
initial finite-difference screen, which also finds violations.

2. Clip resource prices of the four-layer majorant mechanism.
The positive binary proxy H=.5[min(b,a+c)+min(c,b+d)+min(a,b)+min(c,d)]
was paid by the logarithmic scalar rule. Clipping its combined resource prices
to1 lowered some budgets but violated a necessary full-OUT directional cover
in a numerical screen. Example approximate capacities
 (804.348218, .00217600417, 2.53112132, 2.14202073)
had a directional edge margin about-.3202. A second clipping rule
 p_v <- min(p_v,F(c)/c_v)
also failed numerically: a screen found a margin about-.06988 near
 (.00242999182,.0130950715,.00850601990,279.966416).
P4_SIGNED_CLIP_SCOUT.py contains this latter exploratory rule and screen.
No exact counterexample or universal statement is inferred from finite
differences. Neither candidate is included in a theorem or called verified.

3. Promoted outcome and stopping reason.
The exact proofs, rather than these screens, close the gate:
 P4_SIGNED_BINARY_LIFT_OBSTRUCTION.txt excludes even arbitrary nonlinear
 same-owner positive binary integral lifts and gives optimal P4 value distortion.
 P4_SIGNED_HIGH_GIRTH_LIFT_BARRIER.txt proves the distortion can grow without
 bound even for unit two-sparse resource graphs, and gives a matching maximum-
 degree upper. No direct P4 full-OUT improvement below2 was established.
Further blind numerical price tuning was stopped once the structural gate
had a general, exactly proved consequence.
