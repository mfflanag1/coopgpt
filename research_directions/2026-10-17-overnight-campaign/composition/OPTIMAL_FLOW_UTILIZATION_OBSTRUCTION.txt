QUADRATICALLY BALANCED OPTIMAL FLOW DOES NOT RESTORE RANK-THREE INCENTIVES
17 October 2026. Exact architecture obstruction, not an unrestricted lower bound.

Candidate proposed independently by root: choose a maximum unit-profit fractional
production plan x, and pay each resource's owners proportional to utilization
q_v=(Ax)_v/c_v. A maximal plan has a saturated resource in every activity,
so q is a feasible resource-price cover. Total payment is at most rF for rank r.
The missing requirement is a selection of optimal x giving fixed-owner TCWM.

The natural selection minimizing sum_v (Ax)_v^2/c_v among all maximum plans
fails even for two rank-three activities sharing a common resource. Let activities
be (P,a,b) and (P,c,d), all coefficients and profits one. Capacities P=b=c=d=1.
A fixed owner A holds a,c,d; other fixed owners hold P and b. Compare a=1 to a=2.
All maximum plans have x_1+x_2=1; the common resource contributes a constant1 to
the quadratic objective. The varying objective is
 (1/a+1)x_1^2+2(1-x_1)^2.
It is strictly convex and has the feasible unique minimizer
 x_1=2/(3+1/a), x_2=1-x_1.
At a=1, x_1=x_2=1/2; at a=2, x_1=4/7,x_2=3/7.
Owner A is sole supplier of a,c,d, so its utilization payment is x_1+2x_2.
It receives3/2 after underreporting a=1, but only10/7 at true a=2: gain1/14.
This violates individual whole-bundle monotonicity, hence TCWM and full OUT.

The factor-r coverage/funding observation remains correct; this example blocks
the quadratic tie-break only. An owner-aware or otherwise different selection
of optimal flows is not ruled out. The checker verify_shared_cap_wrapper.py
checks the optimizer equation, feasibility and exact1/14 gain. Novelty unconfirmed.
