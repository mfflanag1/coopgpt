BASELINE FOR HETEROGENEOUS TWO-SPARSE ACTIVITY NETWORKS
2026-10-17. Routine row-rescaling criterion, not a new graph-theory claim.

Activity e=uv consumes a_e>0 units at u and b_e>0 at v per activity quantity,
and earns fixed public w_e>=0. Fixed actual owners report capacities c_v.
Suppose positive row scales lambda_v exist with lambda_u/lambda_v=a_e/b_e
for every oriented edge e=uv. Set k_e=a_e/lambda_u=b_e/lambda_v,
normalized capacities c_v/lambda_v and normalized activity y_e=k_e x_e.
The technology becomes ordinary unit-endpoint graph production with profit
w_e/k_e. The transformation is fixed and preserves each actual owner's
normalized bundle. Universal weighted graph results therefore transfer:
3/2 payment-only coalition-total incentives; full-OUT2 by root's graph rule.

The scales exist iff the product of oriented consumption ratios around every
cycle is1 (reverse traversal inverts the ratio). Necessity telescopes lambda;
sufficiency assigns scales along a spanning forest and checks remaining edges.
Every heterogeneous forest qualifies. Inconsistent parallel activities are
already beyond this criterion, but the separate two-resource concave corollary
handles arbitrary two-resource menus. The new unbounded-resource target is
interacting cycles with inconsistent gain products; neither these baselines
nor a sum of isolated pair technologies resolves it.
