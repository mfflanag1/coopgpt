THEOREM
Let F:R_+ x R_+^m -> R_+ be finite, continuous, concave, coordinatewise
nondecreasing and positively homogeneous of degree one. Write resources as
(P,c), where P is one common row and c comprises arbitrarily many private
rows. Assume

 F(0,c)=F(P,0)=0,

and, for every fixed P, F is DR-submodular in c: all coordinate increments
weakly decrease as any private capacities increase. There exists a
nonnegative aggregate-resource price field giving coalition coverage and
TCWM, on all profiles and for arbitrary finite fixed actual owners of
arbitrary mixed bundles, with total funding at most (3/2)F(P,c).

TCWM means every actual coalition's total payment is nondecreasing when
any nonnegative bundle is restored to its members, holding outsiders fixed.
Owner identities/eligible resource rows remain fixed throughout. Funding
means total payments, not only payments in excess of production. This is
payment-only TCWM; no full coalition outside-production guarantee is claimed.

The factor3/2 is sharp as a supremum over this class and growing finite
owner populations, because the class contains F(P,c1)=min(P,c1), whose unrestricted
coalition-TCWM lower approaches3/2. The precise earlier dependency is
../root/COALITION_THREE_HALVES_CANDIDATE.txt, 'LOWER THEOREM' and its two-profile
single-edge proof; see also ../root/ROOT_ADJUDICATION_03_COALITION_OPTIMUM.txt.

1. EXPLICIT RULE WHEN F IS SMOOTH ON EVERY POSITIVE RELATIVE FACE
Use independent uniform s,t in (0,1) and define, for every active coordinate,

 q(P,c)=integral_0^1 integral_0^1 grad F(tP,s c) ds dt,
 y_i=b_i.q(P,c).

The gradient is taken on the active resource face; zero coordinates receive
zero price and have no holdings. If P=0 or c=0 all payments are zero. The
same price field is used for every actual owner population, with no changes
to identities. The integrals can be improper at s=0 or t=0; nonnegativity
and the identities below show every charged coordinate has finite price.
This is a two-block average of supporting gradients, not independent thinning
of all private resources and not a private-only fixed-kernel completion.

2. EXACT BLOCK TOTALS, COVERAGE, AND FUNDING
Let

 H_P(P,c)=integral_0^1 F(P,s c) ds,
 H_c(P,c)=integral_0^1 F(tP,c) dt.

Integrating the derivative along the common coordinate and using F(0,c)=0
shows P q_P=H_P. Integrating along the private ray and using F(P,0)=0 shows
sum_j c_j q_j=H_c. The endpoint identities follow from continuity, concavity,
and monotonicity, first on [epsilon,1] and then by monotone integration.
Thus all payments are finite and

 P q_P+c.q_c=H_P+H_c
 <=F(P,c/2)+F(P/2,c)
 <=2F(3P/4,3c/4)=(3/2)F(P,c).

Every gradient of a concave degree-one homogeneous F is a supporting price:
grad F(z).d>=F(d) for every d>=0 on that face. Its average q has the same
property. In particular a coalition's reported bundle d lies on the current
active face and its payment d.q is at least its stand-alone production F(d).

3. PRIVATE DR IMPLIES COMPLEMENTARITY WITH THE ONE COMMON ROW
First suppose F is twice differentiable in the relative interior. Private
DR and concavity give F_{c_j c_k}<=0 for all private j,k, including j=k.
Differentiating Euler's identity with respect to c_j yields

 P F_{P c_j}+sum_k c_k F_{c_k c_j}=0,

hence F_{P c_j}>=0 whenever P>0. Therefore every private gradient coordinate
is nondecreasing in P. Private DR also makes each such coordinate
nonincreasing in every c_k. Both properties survive the block average:

 q_j(P,c) is nondecreasing in P and antitone in c, for private j.

For a smooth function this argument applies on every relative face. The
across-face private antitonicity follows directly from the finite DR
inequalities by taking coordinate difference quotients in coordinates
positive on the smaller face. New coordinates have nonnegative prices.
Smooth log-regularization below supplies a rigorous construction when the
original F lacks the stated derivatives.

4. FINITE COALITION INCENTIVES, INCLUDING FIXED MIXED OWNERS
Fix any actual coalition. Let a_P,a_c denote its reported aggregate bundle,
o_P,o_c the outsiders' fixed bundle, and P=a_P+o_P, c=a_c+o_c.

First restore private resources, keeping P fixed. The coalition's private
payment equals

 a_c.q_c=H_c(P,c)-o_c.q_c(P,c).

The first term increases with c, and the subtracted term decreases by private
antitonicity. Its common-resource payment is (a_P/P)H_P, also nondecreasing
because H_P increases with c. Thus the coalition total cannot decrease.
This argument includes newly activated private rows: outsider holdings on
those rows are zero, while prices on old outsider rows cannot increase.

Next restore common P, keeping private holdings fixed. Every private price
is nondecreasing in P, so the coalition's private payment cannot decrease.
The common payment is

 [a_P/(a_P+o_P)] H_P(a_P+o_P,c),

a product of two nonnegative nondecreasing functions of a_P. It too cannot
decrease. If P starts at zero, all old payments are zero. Composing these
two finite restoration steps proves TCWM for any mixed bundle and any
coalition of the original fixed owners. No infinitesimal owner assumption
or owner splitting enters this proof.

5. NONSMOOTH CONTINUOUS TECHNOLOGIES: ONE ALL-PROFILE PRICE FIELD
For 0<epsilon<=1 choose a smooth compactly supported probability density on
[-1,1]^(m+1) and define

 F_epsilon(x)=E_z F(diag(exp(epsilon z_j))x).

Fixed positive diagonal maps preserve concavity, monotonicity, homogeneity,
continuity, the zero-axis conditions, and private DR. Logarithmic convolution
makes F_epsilon smooth on every positive relative face. Monotonicity and
homogeneity give, simultaneously on all faces,

 exp(-epsilon)F(x)<=F_epsilon(x)<=exp(epsilon)F(x).

Sections1-4 therefore supply a price field q_epsilon for each epsilon. For
each profile C=(P,c) and each positive coordinate j,

 0<=q_epsilon,j(C)<= (3/2)exp(epsilon)F(C)/C_j.

Set absent-coordinate prices to zero. Put every q_epsilon in the SAME
product of compact intervals [0,(3/2)e F(C)/C_j], indexed by ALL profiles C
and charged coordinates j. This fixed product is compact. Taking an accumulation
point of the nested closures of epsilon-tail fields as epsilon decreases to
zero gives ONE price field q on all profiles. Equivalently use a convergent
subnet with epsilon tending to zero; unrelated profilewise subsequences are
not sufficient and are not used.

Funding, coalition coverage and each finite TCWM comparison are closed
linear inequalities involving only finitely many prices and values. They
pass to this common-field limit, using F_epsilon->F at every profile. All
finite actual owner populations and every feasible mixed-bundle comparison
are preserved simultaneously because the inequalities were true for every
population before the limit. This proves the nonsmooth theorem. It is an
existence transfer; it does not assert a unique limiting selector, an
explicit computational algorithm, or continuity of the final price field.

6. AN UNBOUNDED COMBINATORIAL COROLLARY: PUBLIC POLYMATROIDS
Let E be any finite set of activities and r:2^E->R_+ any normalized,
nondecreasing submodular function. Define

 F_r(P,c)=min_{S subset E} [P r(S)+sum_{j notin S} c_j].       (2)

This is a continuous concave homogeneous monotone technology with zero
common/private axes. The usual box-truncation identity for a polymatroid
interprets it as max sum_j x_j subject to 0<=x<=c and
sum_{j in S} x_j<=P r(S) for every S. That interpretation is established
polymatroid theory; formula(2) alone defines the technology used here and
suffices for the theorem.

Private DR has an elementary exact proof. For fixed P, minimize the
submodular set function phi_c(S)=P r(S)-sum_{j in S}c_j. Its minimizers are
closed under union/intersection, so there is a unique maximal minimizer
S^+(c). If c<=d, put A=S^+(c), B=S^+(d). Submodularity and d>=c give

 phi_c(A)+phi_d(B)>=phi_c(A intersect B)+phi_d(A union B).

Optimality forces equality and A union B is a minimizer for d. Maximality
of B implies A subset B. Hence

 g_j(c)=1{j notin S^+(c)}

is a coordinatewise antitone private supporting price for(2). Integrating
coordinate derivatives proves DR. The theorem therefore gives sharp3/2
TCWM for arbitrary finite public polymatroids, arbitrary private activity
counts, and arbitrary fixed mixed owners. No graph representation, finite
support bound on r, or strategic replication of owners is assumed.


6A. AN EXPLICIT EXACT PRICE FIELD FOR THE POLYMATROID COROLLARY
For(2), the compactness passage is unnecessary. At every point use the
maximal-minimizer dual gradient (r(S^+), 1_{E\S^+}). The previous uncrossing
shows its private part is antitone in c. It is also nondecreasing in P:
for P<=P', let A,B be the maximal minimizers at these two common capacities.
Submodularity and monotonicity of r give

 phi_P(A)+phi_P'(B)>=phi_P(A union B)+phi_P'(A intersect B).

Optimality and maximality of A imply B subset A. Thus every private
coordinate of the gradient increases with P. These finite comparisons
replace the Hessian argument, and Sections2 and4 apply directly. Along each
radial segment the selected gradient is a valid directional derivative
except at finitely many breakpoints; a persistent tie has the same derivative
in the path direction. No differentiability on a full-dimensional set is
being inferred from a two-dimensional sampling plane.

There is a finite exact evaluation formula. At a fixed positive profile,
consider the lower envelope, for z>0, of the 2^|E| lines

 L_S(z)=z P r(S)+sum_{j notin S}c_j.

If S is the maximal minimizer on a ratio interval (a,b), its dual gradient
gets weight H(b)-H(a), where

 H(z)=Pr[t/s<=z]=z/2 for0<=z<=1,
                    1-1/(2z) forz>=1, and H(infinity)=1.

Therefore q is a finite positive weighted sum of the displayed dual
gradients. Rational rank values and capacities give exact rational
breakpoints, weights and prices. Moreover the maximal minimizing sets form
a decreasing inclusion chain as z increases, by the same P-uncrossing
argument. Thus at most |E|+1 distinct gradients have positive interval
weight, even though 2^|E| candidate lines are available. Finding that chain
by direct enumeration may be exponential in |E|; no polynomial
oracle-complexity claim is made here. The independent exact
checker uses this formula and verifies complete coalition holding-box
inequalities on its test transitions, not floating quadrature.

7. RELATION TO THE OPEN TWO-COMMON TARGET
The theorem compresses arbitrarily many substitute private resources into
one block without losing the sharp two-block funding constant. The key
identity uses the single common coordinate: P F_{P c_j} balances only
nonpositive private Hessian terms. With two common coordinates it yields
P F_{P c_j}+Q F_{Q c_j}>=0, which does not control either individual common
restoration. The explicit aligned-profit example in
TWO_COMMON_FRESH_ALIGNED_PRIVATE_SUBMODULARITY.txt demonstrates that failure.
Therefore this theorem advances a general structural class but leaves the
heterogeneous two-common all-profile problem open. No full OUT claim or
historical priority claim is made.
