THREE-UNIFORM PRODUCTION: A FIXED SMOOTH POTENTIAL OBSTRUCTION
17 October 2026. Frozen candidate; independent audit pending.

CONTEXT AND PRECISE LIMIT
For a unit hypergraph, choose nonnegative cover prices p satisfying sum_{v in e}
p_v>=1 and minimize sum_v c_v h(p_v), then pay fixed owner i the dot product
b_i.p. For ordinary graphs the audited Huber potential h(p)=p^2 below 1/2,
p-1/4 above 1/2 gives sharp coalition-total factor 3/2. Its funding calculation
extends to rank r, but its incentives do not. Below is first an exact two-owner
counterexample at rank three, then an unbounded-family obstruction to ANY
fixed smooth convex h of the stated type. This is not an unrestricted lower
bound on truthful mechanisms, nor a claim that arity three needs unbounded
funding. It identifies the loss of the graph tight-component property.

1. EXACT SIX-RESOURCE HUBER COUNTEREXAMPLE
Resources 0,...,5. Unit-profit activities are the five triples, in this order:
 (2,3,5), (2,3,4), (2,4,5), (0,1,2), (0,4,5).
Two fixed actual owners have true bundles
 A=(0,2,0,3,3,4), B=(2,0,5,0,0,0).
Owner A underreports just resource 1, from 2 to 1. Thus the two aggregate
profiles are c_low=(2,1,5,3,3,4), c_high=(2,2,5,3,3,4).

At c_low the unique minimizing Huber price is p_low=(1/3,...,1/3).
All five activity inequalities are tight. Positive KKT multipliers, in activity
order, are (4,2,2,2,2)/3, and their incident sums equal 2 c_low p_low.
At c_high the unique minimizing price is
 p_high=(13,9,13,11,11,11)/35.
All five inequalities are again tight, and positive KKT multipliers are
 (44,22,28,36,16)/35, with incident sums exactly 2 c_high p_high.
All prices lie strictly between 0 and 1/2, so both use the strictly convex
quadratic branch. KKT sufficiency and strict convexity near these positive
profiles make these the unique global minimizers.
A receives 11/3 on its smaller report, but 128/35 on its true report:
 11/3 - 128/35 = 1/105 > 0.
This violates individual payment-only whole-bundle-underreport incentives,
and therefore also violates coalition-total underreport monotonicity.

2. WHY THE FUNDING CALCULATION ALONE DID NOT DETECT THE FAILURE
For Huber at a positive capacity profile, KKT gives
 sum_{e containing v} lambda_e = c_v min(2p_v,1).
For any edge with lambda_e>0 all its endpoint prices are positive, and its
cover constraint is tight. Hence
 sum_v c_v p_v
 =sum_e lambda_e sum_{v in e} max(1/2,p_v)
 <=(r+1)/2 sum_e lambda_e <=(r+1)/2 F(c).
The last inequality uses primal feasibility of lambda: its resource loads
are <=c. Thus this potential has the desired factor 2 funding for rank three;
the issue is exactly the incentive derivative. At zero capacities the same
funding conclusion follows by a limiting payment argument, but no boundary
claim is needed for the strictly positive exact counterexample in section 1.

3. GENERAL FIXED-SMOOTH-POTENTIAL NO-GO THEOREM
Let h:[0,infinity)->R be convex. Assume h is twice continuously differentiable
in a neighborhood of a=1/3, with h'(a)>0 and 0<h''(a)<infinity. Consider on every
finite three-uniform hypergraph the rule selecting a minimizer of
 sum_v c_v h(p_v), subject to p>=0 and each hyperedge sum>=1,
and paying each actual owner b_i.p. Then there is a finite three-uniform
hypergraph, two fixed mixed owners, and a binding one-coordinate underreport
that is strictly profitable. The relevant minimizers are unique, so selection
among minimizers cannot fix this example.
The potential h is fixed independently of the graph. Graph-dependent h,
nonseparable objectives, owner-aware rules, and nonsmooth behavior at 1/3 are
not ruled out by this theorem.

4. EXPLICIT UNBOUNDED FAMILY OF INCIDENCE KERNELS
Start with vertices u,x_01,x_02,x_03 and edges
 (u,x_01,x_02), (u,x_01,x_03), (u,x_02,x_03).
For levels k=1,...,L add three new vertices x_k1,x_k2,x_k3 and the three edges
 (x_ki,x_(k-1)1,x_(k-1)2), i=1,2,3.
All edges contain three distinct resources. There are n=4+3L vertices and
m=3+3L=n-1 edges. Write T for the edge-by-vertex incidence matrix.
The equations Tz=0 force all three level-zero coordinates equal, z_u=-2z_0,
and recursively z_ki=-2z_(k-1)1. Thus ker T is one-dimensional, generated by
 z_u=-2, z_ki=(-2)^k.
In particular T has rank n-1. At an even level L, take j=x_L1 and write
 Z=z_j=2^L>0. The base edge e0=(u,x_01,x_02) has z-values (-2,1,1).

5. CAPACITIES AND LOCAL SOLUTION BRANCH
Give edge e0 positive weight 1 and every other edge weight tau>0. Let
 c=T^T ell be the resulting positive resource degree vector. Then z.c=0.
At p=a*1 all edge constraints are tight and
 c_v h'(a)=(T^T [h'(a)ell])_v.
These positive multipliers certify p as a global optimum. Local strict convexity
and all c_v>0 imply it is the unique global minimizer: a segment to a distinct
minimum would contradict strict convexity near p.

Increase capacity j by t>=0. Prices on the tight affine line have form
 p(t)=a*1+theta(t)z.
The stationarity range condition is
 sum_v (c_v+t 1_{v=j}) z_v h'(a+theta z_v)=0.
At t=theta=0 its theta derivative is h''(a)D>0, where D=sum_v c_v z_v^2.
The implicit function theorem supplies a continuously differentiable theta(t)
for sufficiently small t, with
 theta'(0)=-[h'(a)/h''(a)] z_j/D.
Prices remain positive and in the neighborhood of strict convexity. Since
range(T^T)=z-perp, this stationarity condition gives unique activity multipliers;
they vary continuously and stay strictly positive for small t. Therefore all
these prices are indeed the unique global minimizing cover prices.

6. FIXED MIXED OWNER AND NEGATIVE OWN-REPORT DERIVATIVE
Let owner A own c_v for every v with z_v>0 and zero elsewhere; owner B owns the
remaining c_v. Give A additionally t units of resource j at its true profile.
It may legally withhold exactly those t units. Put S=sum_{z_v>0} c_v z_v.
At t=0 the derivative of A's payment as it increases its report at j is
 a - [h'(a)/h''(a)] Z S/D.                                           (7)
As tau tends to zero, only base edge e0 contributes to c, and
 D -> (-2)^2+1^2+1^2=6,   S -> 1+1=2,   S/D -> 1/3.
Choose even L so that Z>h''(a)/h'(a). Then the limiting expression (7) is
strictly negative. Choose tau sufficiently small but positive to retain that
strict sign. Finally choose t>0 sufficiently small so that the KKT branch
persists and A's payment strictly decreases. The higher report is the true
bundle and the lower report is its profitable binding underreport.
Both resource count and hypergraph depth can grow. Two actual owners suffice;
additional zero or disjoint fixed owner roles can be added, but are unnecessary
for the mechanism obstruction.

7. MATHEMATICAL CONSEQUENCE AND NEXT GATE
For graph incidence constraints each nontrivial bipartite tight-component
kernel has equal absolute coordinate values. Three-uniform incidence kernels
can grow geometrically, making the weighted own-bundle projection derivative
arbitrarily adverse. Thus simply retuning one fixed smooth separable potential
cannot extend the graph proof. Any successful universal rank-three method must
use something outside this class, for example graph-dependent curvature,
nonseparable/owner-aware structure, or a non-potential construction. Whether
rank-three TCWM funding has a universal constant remains unresolved here.
No priority claim is made for the matrix/projection obstruction.

8. SEPARATE POSITIVE-LIFT SUPPORT OBSTRUCTION
For any fixed nonnegative linear map B from three resource capacities to unit-
graph capacities, there is no finite K with
 min(c1,c2,c3)<=F_H(Bc)<=K min(c1,c2,c3)  for all c>=0.
Indeed the target vanishes on every profile supported by at most two original
resources. If a lifted edge xy has two nonzero rows B_x,B_y, pick an original
resource in the positive support of each row. Giving those at most two original
resources positive capacities activates both endpoints and makes F_H positive,
a contradiction. Thus every lifted edge has a zero row at some endpoint, so
F_H is identically zero, contradicting the positive target at (1,1,1).
This also rules out public positive sums of such graph lifts, since nonnegative
summands cannot cancel forbidden production. It does not rule out nonlinear
owner maps or auxiliary resources with separate economic semantics, nor does
it establish any unrestricted funding lower. It is independent of section 3.
