NO TCWM SELECTION OF OPTIMAL RESOURCE-UTILIZATION PAYMENTS
17 October 2026. Exact three-profile architecture impossibility.

MODEL
Five resource rows P,a,b,c,d, two unit-profit unit-consumption activities
 e_1=(P,a,b), e_2=(P,c,d).
Production F=min(P,min(a,b)+min(c,d)). Fixed actual owners:
 A owns a,c; B owns b; C owns P,d.
All five true capacities are one. Binding underreports may set either of A's
resources to zero. There are exactly these three actual owners throughout.

ARCHITECTURE
At every report, choose ANY maximum-production fractional plan x. Pay each
actual owner its total resource utilization in that plan. Equivalently choose
resource price q_v=(Ax)_v/c_v when c_v>0, and pay b_i.q. The rule may depend on
all ownership data and use any deterministic or randomized tie-breaking;
randomized expected payments reduce to the mean fractional plan. The result
also excludes multiplying every utilization payment by the same fixed positive
constant. It does not exclude arbitrary payments or report-dependent changes
of architecture.

THREE-PROFILE PROOF
At the all-one true profile, maximum production is one. Every optimum has
activity quantities (x,1-x), 0<=x<=1. The owner-payment vector is
 (y_A,y_B,y_C)=(1,x,2-x).                                      (1)
A receives x+(1-x); B receives x; C receives the common resource1 and second-
activity use1-x.

First underreport: A hides c and continues reporting a=1. The unique maximum
plan is (1,0), and payments are (1,1,1). Coalition {A,B} can make this report
with B unchanged. TCWM requires its truthful total to be at least its lower-
report total:
 1+x >= 1+1, hence x=1.                                      (2)

Second underreport: A hides a and continues reporting c=1. The unique maximum
plan is (0,1), and payments are (1,0,2). Coalition {A,C} can make this report
with C unchanged. TCWM requires
 1+(2-x) >= 1+2, hence x=0.                                  (3)
Equations(2),(3) contradict one another. Arbitrary ownership-aware selection,
continuity, computational power and randomization cannot repair this.
No cross-profile owner change or outsider report change occurs in either
comparison. The same true profile must satisfy both coalitions' inequalities.
Full coalition outside-production incentives imply TCWM, so they fail as well.

WHY THIS WAS A CREDIBLE CANDIDATE
For every maximum unit-profit rank-r plan, every activity contains a saturated
resource: otherwise that activity could be increased. Consequently utilization
prices form a dual cover. Grand payments are sum_e |e|x_e<=rF, and fixed-owner
coalition coverage follows. This observation is correct. The impossibility
isolates the missing all-profile coalition incentive property.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLOSED
This is stronger than the quadratic tie-break counterexample, which used only
individual incentives and gave an exact1/14 gain. The present theorem excludes
ALL optimal-utilization selectors, but its proof specifically uses TCWM; it
does not prove that every selector violates individual-only truthfulness.
It is not a lower bound against arbitrary rank-three subsidy mechanisms.
The general dimension theorem supplies mechanisms outside this architecture.

RELATED OWNER-SHAPLEY CHECK
At the first lower report, the actual-owner game is unanimity on {A,B,C}, so
Shapley payments are(1/3,1/3,1/3). At the true profile it is unanimity on {A,C},
so Shapley payments are(1/2,0,1/2). Coalition {A,B} decreases from2/3 to1/2.
Thus constant-scaled actual-owner Shapley also fails TCWM already in this unit
rank-three example. This does not say Shapley lacks individual monotonicity,
and no universal Shapley coverage bound is claimed here.
