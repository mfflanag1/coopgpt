AN UNRESTRICTED FUNDING LOWER GROWING WITH THE FINITE NORM EXPONENT
17 October 2026. Composition proof candidate; historical priority unconfirmed.

THEOREM
For every integer m>=2 and every real

                     p>=m^2 ceil(log_2 m),             (1)

consider the m-dimensional ell_p reserve cone K_p and F(t,x)=t-||x||_p.
There are n=m(m-1)+1 FIXED fully eligible actual owners and two report
profiles, linked by one feasible coalition restoration, such that any
nonnegative coalition-covering TCWM rule with uniform funding factor kappa
must satisfy

                         kappa>=2m/9.                 (2)

This is an all-mechanism lower, allowing arbitrary nonlinear asymmetric
owner-aware rules with no regularity or aggregate-price assumption. It also
applies to the stronger full-coalition OUT contract. The same fixed n owners
are present in both profiles; zero reports are permitted actual reports.

Consequently the optimal factor over arbitrary finite exposure dimensions
and owner counts is unbounded as p increases. More precisely, for p>=32,

 kappa_opt(p)>=(1/9)sqrt(p/log_2 p).                   (3)

Together with CONE_FINITE_P_DIMENSION_FREE_THEOREM.txt this gives
Omega(sqrt(p/log p)) <= kappa_TCWM(p) <= p,
and the full-OUT upper p/[1-(1/2)(1-1/p)^p]. This does not determine the
optimal growth rate or exclude a constant bound for any one fixed finite p.
For1<=p<=2, the independently established L1-embedding theorem gives the
sharp constant3/2; the present result concerns the growth toward infinity.

1. A FIXED POSITIVE RESOURCE MAP INTO THE CONE
For a nonnegative vector a in R_+^m, write S=sum_j a_j and define

 L(a)=(t,x),  t=S,  x_j=m^(-1/p)(S-2a_j).             (4)

This is a fixed LINEAR map. Since |S-2a_j|<=S, ||x||_p<=S, so L(a) lies
in K_p. Every coordinatewise nonnegative restoration da maps to a cone
restoration L(da); retained reports remain feasible. Owner identities and
eligibility do not change. A pure unit report e_j maps to a boundary bundle:
all m spatial coordinates have magnitude m^(-1/p), hence norm1=t.

The induced production is

 V(a)=S-[m^(-1) sum_j |S-2a_j|^p]^(1/p).              (5)

For the diagonal a=(1,...,1), V(a)=m-(m-2)=2 exactly, including m=2.
For ANY nonnegative a with min_j a_j=1,

 max_j |S-2a_j|=S-2.

Indeed S-2a_j<=S-2, and 2a_j-S<=S-2 because a_j+1<=S when m>=2.
Therefore

 V(a)<=S-m^(-1/p)(S-2)
      <=2+[1-m^(-1/p)]S.                              (6)

The embedding is equivariant under coordinate permutations. They act as
isometries of the full ell_p cone, preserving the report domain and F.

2. THE TWO GRAND VALUES ARE AT MOST3
Put h=ceil(log_2 m), an integer with2^h>=m. The binomial theorem gives

 (1+1/m^2)^(m^2)>=1+m^2(1/m^2)=2.

Thus for p>=m^2 h,
 (1+1/m^2)^p>=2^h>=m,
 m^(-1/p)>=m^2/(m^2+1),
 1-m^(-1/p)<=1/(m^2+1).                              (7)

Both aggregate report vectors used below have minimum1 and sum less than
m^2. Hence(6)-(7) give grand production strictly less than3. We use the
weak bound3 to keep the finite certificate rational. No numerical norm
evaluation, limiting profile or approximation of mechanism payments is used.

3. FIXED-OWNER SYMMETRIZATION AND TWO PROFILES
Average any purported kappa-funded rule over all n owner permutations and
all m coordinate permutations. The full cone domain, F, all coalition
coverage and TCWM inequalities, nonnegativity and budget are invariant.
The averaged rule has the same guarantees. We may therefore analyze this
invariant rule; this is mathematical averaging, not strategic owner cloning.

In profile P0 there are:
 - m pure owners of each resource1,...,m-2;
 - one pure owner of resource m-1 and one of resource m;
 - m-1 zero-report owners.
Every pure report means the cone bundle L(e_j). The total number is
(m-2)m+2+(m-1)=m(m-1)+1=n. Its aggregate underlying vector has total
S0=(m-2)m+2<m^2 and minimum1, so V0<=3.

By symmetry, each owner in the first m-2 groups is paid a, both scarce
owners are paid b, and each zero owner is paid w>=0. A coalition selecting
one owner from each resource group has the diagonal underlying bundle,
and therefore value2. Budget and coverage imply

 m(m-2)a+2b+(m-1)w<=kappa V0,
             (m-2)a+2b>=2.

Subtracting yields (m-2)(m-1)a+(m-1)w<=kappa V0-2.
Nonnegativity and the coverage inequality then give

                   2b>=2-(kappa V0-2)/(m-1).          (8)

For m=2 there are no first groups, and the same inequality remains valid.

To obtain P1, restore the pure cone bundle L(e_(m-1)) to EACH of the same
m-1 zero owners; leave all other owners unchanged. There are now m owners
in each of groups1,...,m-1 and one owner in group m. The aggregate total is
S1=(m-1)m+1<m^2, so V1<=3.

The coalition consisting of the original group-(m-1) owner and the formerly
zero owners has its total payment increase, by TCWM, from b+(m-1)w to the
new group total. Thus this new group is paid at least b. In P1, simultaneous
owner and coordinate permutations make all first m-1 groups equivalent.
Their total payment is at least(m-1)b. Nonnegativity of the last owner's
payment and the budget yield

                         (m-1)b<=kappa V1.            (9)

Combining(8)-(9) gives the stronger exact finite inequality

                      m<=kappa(V1+V0/2).              (10)

Since V0,V1<=3, equation(2) follows. No payment is assumed to vanish at a
zero report, and every unchanged coalition member is explicitly included.

4. GROWTH IN p
For p>=32 set m=floor(sqrt(p/log_2 p)). Then m>=2,
ceil(log_2 m)<=log_2 p and m^2<=p/log_2 p, so (1) holds.
Also m>=sqrt(p/log_2 p)/2, proving(3). The constants are deliberately
conservative; the point is a nonconstant lower for ALL mechanisms on finite
p norm cones, using only finitely many fixed-owner profiles.

The dependence on dimension here is explicit: the exposure dimension is m,
the owner count is m(m-1)+1, and p can be the finite integer
m^2 ceil(log_2 m). The lower does not claim an obstruction for fixed p as
dimension increases; the earlier finite-p construction already bounds that
case independently of dimension.

5. PROVENANCE AND REMAINING GAP
The finite symmetrization and restoration pattern are adapted from the
accepted mixed multi-glove lower in
../unrestricted/SHARP_TCWM_MULTIGLOVE_THEOREM.txt. The fixed ell_p boundary
embedding and the quantitative finite-p control(6)-(7) are the new bridge
proposed here. The infinity-norm embedding is the same formula without
m^(-1/p), and gives V(a)=2min_j a_j exactly. This makes the endpoint
connection transparent without treating finite-p values as exact minima.

A substantive next question is whether the p upper can be improved toward
sqrt(p), or whether another all-mechanism lower recovers a larger power.
The present proof does not justify treating the logarithm as intrinsic.
