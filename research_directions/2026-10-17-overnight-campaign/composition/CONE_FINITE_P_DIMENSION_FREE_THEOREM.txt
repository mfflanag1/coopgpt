DIMENSION-FREE COALITION-SAFE RESERVE MECHANISMS FOR EVERY FINITE p>=2
17 October 2026. Composition proof candidate; historical priority unconfirmed.
This general theorem subsumes the p=4 existence result. Its elementary proof
does not depend on the longer quartic SOS certificate preserved in that note.

THEOREM
Fix ANY real p>=2. For arbitrary finite dimensions d and owner populations n,
let the resource cone and production function be

 K_p={(t,x) in R x R^d : t>=||x||_p},  F(t,x)=t-||x||_p.

Actual owners are fixed and report arbitrary bundles b_i=(t_i,x_i) in K_p.
Feasible withdrawals require both the withdrawn and retained bundles in K_p;
members of a coalition may jointly change their entire mixed bundles. Write
T=sum_i t_i and X=sum_i x_i. At T>0 define

 y_i=t_i-<x_i,J_p(X)>/T^(p-1),
 (J_p(X))_j=|X_j|^(p-2)X_j.                           (1)

At T=0 set all payments to zero. Then (1) is nonnegative, covers every
coalition, and is transferable-coalition-report monotone on ALL profiles.
Its total funding is at most p F(T,X), independently of BOTH d and n.
Define

 a_p=1-(1/2)(1-1/p)^p,   lambda_p=1/a_p.

Multiplying (1) by lambda_p gives FULL coalition outside-production protection:
for each fixed coalition A and all feasible joint withdrawals D_i,

 sum_(i in A)[lambda_p y_i(b)-lambda_p y_i(b-D)]
                  >= F(sum_(i in A)D_i).              (2)

Total funding is at most (p/a_p)F(T,X). In particular lambda_p is bounded
above by (1-1/(2e))^-1 for all finite p>=2, while the funding factor grows
linearly with p. The guarantee is not S+constant*G gain-only funding.
No optimality among all mechanisms or historical novelty is asserted.

1. COVERAGE AND TOTAL FUNDING
Put q=p/(p-1), R=||X||_p and s=R/T in[0,1]. The price vector
v=J_p(X)/T^(p-1) has dual norm ||v||_q=s^(p-1)<=1. Holder's inequality gives
y_A=t_A-v dot x_A>=t_A-||x_A||_p=F(b_A) for every coalition, including
single owners. Thus payments are nonnegative. Their sum is

 T-R^p/T^(p-1)=T(1-s^p)<=p T(1-s)=p F(T,X).          (3)

The inequality follows from convexity of s^p at s=1, or integration of
p s^(p-1)<=p on[0,1]. Every individual payment lies between0 and2t_i,
so all payments extend continuously to the zero-total profile.

2. AN ELEMENTARY SCALAR INEQUALITY
Let f(z)=|z|^p/p and J_p(z)=f'(z). For all real z,X,

 D_f(z,X):=f(z)-f(X)-f'(X)(z-X)
                    >= (1/2)|X|^(p-2)(z-X)^2.        (4)

For X!=0, multiply the tangent inequality for the convex function u^(p/2)
at u=1, with u=z^2/X^2, by |X|^p/p. This gives
 f(z)>=(1/2)|X|^(p-2)z^2+(1/p-1/2)|X|^p,
which is exactly(4) after rearrangement. At X=0, (4) is immediate for p>2.
For p=2 interpret |X|^0=1 even at X=0; equality then holds everywhere.

Take theta in[0,1], r=1-theta, arbitrary real u,v,d, and set
 X=theta u+r v, x=theta u, h=u-v, k=d-X,
 m=p-1, L=1+m theta.
The following scalar inequality holds for arbitrary signs:

 d J_p(X)+m x d |X|^(p-2)-m x J_p(X)
 <=[m theta r |u|^p+m r^2 |v|^p+L|d|^p]/p.          (5)

To prove it, subtract the left side from the right side. Exact algebra gives

 L D_f(d,X)+m r[theta D_f(u,X)+r D_f(v,X)]
                         -m theta r |X|^(p-2)h k.     (6)

Here u-X=r h and v-X=-theta h. Applying(4) to all three Bregman terms
bounds(6) below by

 |X|^(p-2)/2 * [L k^2+m theta r^2 h^2-2m theta r h k]
 =|X|^(p-2)/2 *
   [L(k-m theta r h/L)^2+(m theta r^2/L)h^2] >=0.     (7)

All coefficients are nonnegative because m>=1, L>=1 and theta,r>=0.
At X=0,p>2, the left side of(5) vanishes and its right side is nonnegative;
the same formulas therefore cover that case without division by X.
Equation(7) is the important point: no dimension-dependent norm comparison
and no assumption that any of the signed coordinates is positive is used.

3. ALL-PROFILE COALITION MONOTONICITY
Normalize a positive-total profile to T=1. For one fixed coalition A write
its bundle (theta,x) and the outsiders' bundle (r,z), where theta+r=1,
||x||_p<=theta, ||z||_p<=r and X=x+z. Choose u=x/theta and v=z/r at
positive denominators, and choose0 at a zero denominator (the exposure is
then zero). For any signed vector d with ||d||_p<=1, sum(5) over coordinates.
Its right side is at most

 [m theta r+m r^2+1+m theta]/p=(m+1)/p=1.

Taking the supremum over all such d proves

 ||J_p(X)+m x times |X|^(p-2)||_q
                          <=1+m<x,J_p(X)>.           (8)

Products and absolute powers here are coordinatewise. For p=2 the zeroth
power is1 in every coordinate; for p>2 it is0 at a zero coordinate.

At a general T>0, the coalition payment Y=t_A-<x_A,J_p(X)>/T^(p-1)
has directional derivative along a joint restoration (dt,dx):

 Y'=A dt-V dot dx,
 A=1+m<x_A,J_p(X)>/T^p,
 V=[J_p(X)+m x_A times |X|^(p-2)]/T^(p-1).

Rescaling(8) gives A>=||V||_q. For every cone direction dt>=||dx||_p,
Holder therefore gives Y'>=0. Formula(1) is continuously differentiable on
T>0 for every p>=2, including coordinates with X_j=0 and boundary profiles.
Integrate along a straight joint restoration of the SAME actual coalition;
the aggregate direction belongs to K_p. Continuity handles the only possible
T=0 endpoint. This proves weak coalition monotonicity for all feasible
underreports, including inactive coalition members whose reports do not change.

4. FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION GUARANTEE
When X!=0, let e*=J_p(X)/R^(p-1), a dual unit support of the norm at X.
Put h=<x_A,e*>/T. If theta=t_A/T then h>=-theta and
s-h=<X-x_A,e*>/T<=1-theta. Adding these two lower bounds gives
h>=(s-1)/2. Consequently

 A=1+m s^(p-1)h
 >=1-(m/2)s^(p-1)(1-s)
 >=1-(1/2)(1-1/p)^p=a_p.                             (9)

The maximum of s^(p-1)(1-s) occurs at s=(p-1)/p. At X=0, A=1.
After multiplying payments by lambda_p, their temporal coefficient alpha
is at least1 and at least their spatial dual norm beta. For dt>=||dx||_p,

 (alpha-1)dt-(beta-1)||dx||_p
 =(alpha-1)(dt-||dx||_p)+(alpha-beta)||dx||_p>=0.

Thus the scaled derivative is at least dt-||dx||_p=F(dt,dx). Integration
along the fixed coalition's joint-restoration segment proves(2). Positive
scaling preserves coverage and (3) gives the stated funding factor.

The temporal minimum a_p is attained by opposite collinear coalition and
outside boundary bundles with total ratio s=(p-1)/p: use coalition exposure
-(1-s)e/2 and reserve(1-s)/2, outsiders +(1+s)e/2 and reserve(1+s)/2,
where ||e||_p=1. These are the RETAINED reports. Restore D=(epsilon,0)
to the coalition owner, leaving the outsiders fixed; both D and the retained
bundle belong to K_p. Full OUT for this feasible restoration, divided by
epsilon and sent to zero, requires the multiplier times a_p to be at least1.
Thus lambda_p is the smallest uniform multiplier of THIS rule giving full OUT. The unscaled
funding ratio approaches p as s increases to1. These are architecture facts,
not lower bounds on unrestricted mechanisms.

5. CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITATIONS
For every fixed finite p>=2, arbitrary many signed exposure coordinates and
arbitrary fixed mixed owners admit constant funding under simultaneous
coalition withdrawal. This is a structural extension of the Euclidean cone
result; p=4 is now a corollary. It is consistent with an unbounded constant
for the infinity-norm limit, since the present funding factor grows with p.
For p=2 the more carefully chosen radial mechanisms in the Lorentz notes
give substantially smaller constants; this theorem prioritizes a uniform
proof across p. It does not establish the case1<p<2, sharp p-dependence,
optimal funding, or a calibrated monetary model for uncertainty reserves.

The convexity of powers, Holder duality, Bregman identity and differentiation
are established mathematical ingredients. Historical priority of the specific
payment rule and contract guarantee remains unconfirmed. The proof is valid
over arbitrary real p; finite exact even-p checks are supplementary validation,
not the argument establishing the continuum of exponents.
