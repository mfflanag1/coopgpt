1. Casajus, Collusion, symmetry, and the Banzhaf value, author original.
https://home.uni-leipzig.de/casajus/texts/colluba.pdf
Read definitions and Theorem3/Remark2, printedpp4–8. The paper relates pair
proxy neutrality, dummy treatment, and Banzhaf characterization on fixed player
sets. Its proof recovers coordinates from pair sums after mergers; Remark2
credits Lehrer1988Remark3 for the induction. This recursion is established
material and must not be presented as our new theorem. Our candidate uses a
restricted signed-sum potential, derives small null payments from a gain-only
budget, and converts approximate merger consistency into a quantitative
coverage contradiction. Those are our mathematical distinctions, not a claim
that no previous paper treats them. Lehrer's1988 original was located only
through its institutional metadata/abstract and was not fully read.
https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/an-axiomatization-of-the-banzhaf-value/

2. Takahasi, Takahashi, Wada2000, An extension of Hlawka's inequality.
https://files.ele-math.com/articles/mia-03-07.pdf
Read originalp63 introduction andp64 Theorem1/Corollary2. The introduction
states the classical Djokovic inequality for finite Hilbert-space vectors;
the paper supplies a weighted/integral extension under its hypotheses.
Our Euclidean grand-marginal gain coefficient2 is an application of this
established norm inequality, not a newly discovered inequality. The later
radial all-coalition incentive mechanisms require a separate proof and are
not established by that citation. No claim is made about all Banach norms.

3. Balog, Batyı, Csoka, Pinter2014 working paper, risk-capital methods.
https://unipub.lib.uni-corvinus.hu/1659/1/cewp_201413.pdf
Read definitions, Theorem2.3 and relevant Euler-method discussion. The paper
compares core compatibility, equal treatment and strong marginal-contribution
monotonicity and cites the incompatibility theorem of Csoka/Pinter. This is
substantial prior cooperative-risk-allocation literature. Its strong
monotonicity compares marginal worths across games; it is not definitionally
our fixed-owner cone report/withdrawal contract. Our subsidy permits total
payments beyond production, another material distinction. These distinctions
prevent a direct contradiction but do not establish novelty. The original
Csoka/Pinter working paper was opened for follow-up, not yet fully audited:
https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/26515/1/MPRA_paper_26515.pdf
