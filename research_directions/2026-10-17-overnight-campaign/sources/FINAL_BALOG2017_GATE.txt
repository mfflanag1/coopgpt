AUTHOR PRESENTATION: WHAT WAS ACTUALLY READ
The Csoka publications page links the paper's author-contributed Kudos page:
https://www.growkudos.com/publications/10.1016%25252Fj.ejor.2016.10.052/reader
Its resource link provides this freely downloadable presentation:
https://www.dropbox.com/s/pubb64fk38k1nx2/PropertiesAndComparisonOfRCAMethods.pdf?dl=1
It is 31 presentation pages, with Csoka as presenter and several related papers
discussed. No final-article status or final-article proof verification is inferred.

Slide 9 defines core compatibility by efficiency and coalition no-undercut:
  sum_i phi_i(X)=rho(sum_i X_i),
  sum_{i in S} phi_i(X)<=rho(sum_{i in S}X_i), for every S.
Slide 13 attributes individual incentive compatibility to Csoka-Pinter 2016:
  X_i>=Y_i, X_j=Y_j for j!=i  =>  phi_i(X)<=phi_i(Y).
Slides 15-17 state the FD+CC+IC impossibility and label it Balog et al. 2017;
slide 14 attributes an earlier CC/IC impossibility to Csoka-Pinter 2016.
Slide 19 defines Strong Monotonicity by comparing all of an individual's
coalition marginal risk contributions. It is not the name of a coalition-total
report monotonicity condition in that presentation.

OUR COMPARISON AND ITS LIMITS
These deductions concern the displayed definitions, not unseen article claims.

1. Under the sign conversion F=-rho and y_i=-phi_i, slide 9 becomes efficient
coalition coverage, and slide 13 becomes individual own-report monotonicity.
The direct finite-state embedding into our nonnegative production domain was
already checked against the full Csoka-Pinter original in comparison12 and
root/CSOKA_PINTER_DIRECT_ANTECEDENT.txt. Basic incompatibility of efficient core
selection and individual withholding incentives must therefore remain credited
as prior work. The newly found presentation reinforces that attribution.

2. Our TCWM requires aggregate payments of every protected coalition to be
nondecreasing when that coalition restores owned resources, including cases
where some protected members are unchanged. The presentation's individual IC
is only its singleton analogue after the sign conversion. Its Strong
Monotonicity compares marginal-contribution vectors; confusing the two uses of
"strong" would be a material error. The slides do not establish their
equivalence. Our full OUT additionally requires the coalition payment increase
to cover production from the withdrawn bundle; this is another distinct term.

3. The accepted campaign universal theorems allow funded coalition covers with
total factors (d+1)/2 for TCWM and 1+c(d-1) for full OUT, where
c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1. They do not assert efficient-core selection under the old
axioms. Thus the displayed impossibility does not by itself refute those
theorems. Conversely, lack of those constants in a presentation does not prove
their absence from the final article or elsewhere.

4. The signed-reserve theorem in composition/CONE_L1_EMBEDDABLE_SHARP_THEOREM.txt
uses actual owners with cone bundles (t_i,x_i), t_i>=||x_i||, and feasible
retained/withdrawn cone bundles. Its compensation for changes in positive and
negative auxiliary quantities is an all-profile proof obligation; it cannot be
replaced by a citation to individual statewise risk IC. The presentation does
not verify that transfer, its full OUT condition, or the sharp funded factors.
This is a precise remaining comparison task, not an assertion of non-overlap.
