Root inspection, 17 October 2026. Not a novelty confirmation.

Huang, Karimireddy, Jordan, Evaluating and Incentivizing Diverse Data
Contributions in Collaborative Learning (2023), arXiv:2306.05592v1.
Original HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05592
Original PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05592
Read sections2–5, especially Definition3.1, Proposition4.2 and5.3.
They study strategic experiment-design contributions, additive Fisher matrices,
linear collection costs, and model/accuracy assignments. Proposition4.2 singles
out D-optimality for efficiency under their standard sharing mechanism;
Proposition5.3 implements their information-maximizing target through accuracy
penalties. Their utility uses local uncertainty criteria and collection costs.
Our proposed matrix theorem instead covers every coalition in scalar
lambda_min output units, using externally funded transfers and binding Loewner
underreports at all profiles. It does not implement their design equilibrium,
private costs, or accuracy-sharing institution. This is substantial adjacent
prior art: neither incentive-aware information pooling nor Fisher-matrix
valuation is a new application idea. The proposed subsidy theorem requires
its own priority review; this comparison does not establish novelty.

Dette and Grigoriev, E-optimal designs for second-order response surface models
(2014), Annals of Statistics42(4),1635–1656.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.3805
The minimum information eigenvalue is an established E-optimal design
criterion. The abstract and bibliographic record were inspected; no claim of
full proof replication. Our use of this criterion must be attributed, and
must not be described as a validated AI accuracy or monetary objective.

MODEL GATE FOR OUR OWN FOLLOW-UP (OUR INFERENCE)
Fixed independent linear observations at a common public parameterization
add PSD information matrices. Withholding a subset gives a PSD difference,
so Loewner underreports include physically feasible subset withdrawals.
General Loewner submatrices may not be obtainable from a fixed dataset;
protecting the larger report cone is mathematically stronger, not proof that
all reports are verifiable. Cash transfers denominated in minimum-eigenvalue
units require a separately specified purchaser value and payment scale.
Changing covariate units changes E-optimality; use a fixed public metric or
reference matrix. Correlation, model misspecification, nonrival reuse,
privacy budgets, and realized prediction gains are not resolved by this
algebraic model. No GPU/energy performance data was created by this theorem.
