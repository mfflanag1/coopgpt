PRIMARY SOURCE COMPARISONS05 -- SEMIVALUES AND MPH INGREDIENTS
17 October2026. Freely accessible originals inspected. No novelty clearance.

Feige,Feldman,Immorlica,Izsak,Lucier,Syrgkanis(2014/2015), A Unifying Hierarchy
of Valuations with Complements and Substitutes, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1211
Read Definition1.1, Definition3.1, Proposition3.1 and its AppendixD proof.
MPH-r is a maximum of positive rank-r hypergraphs; equivalently every
restriction admits such a positive lower envelope. This representation
framework is established. Our sparse-packing owner-support construction is
a direct application; historical priority of that particular application
has not been checked exhaustively. Do not claim the MPH hierarchy itself.

Ezra,Feldman,Neyman,Talgam-Cohen,Weinberg(2018), Complement-Free Couples Must
Communicate: A Hardness Result for Two-Player Combinatorial Auctions,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.09871
Read Observation6.1 and Lemmas6.2,6.4, pp23--25. Lemma6.2 gives exactly
sum_i[v(N)-v(N-i)]<=r v(N) for MPH-r. Lemma6.4 gives the sampling bound when
each subset of size<=r survives with probability at least q. Bernoulli
retention with q=p^r is an immediate case. Both inequalities in our new
bounded-coalition proof must be attributed as established ingredients.

Casajus(2011), Collusion, symmetry, and the Banzhaf value,
https://home.uni-leipzig.de/casajus/texts/colluba.pdf
Read Eq2, amalgamation Eq3, 2-efficiency and proxy-neutrality axioms, and
Theorems1--3. Banzhaf is the p=1/2 binomial marginal; pair payoff sum equals
the merged player's Banzhaf payoff by established2-efficiency (Lehrer1988).
Fixed-player proxy neutrality is attributed to Haller1994. Therefore the
pair-marginal identity and generic collusion connection are not ours. The
proposed synthesis concerns binding divisible reports, coalition coverage,
outside production and a uniform sparse-production funding guarantee.

LIMIT.
These checked originals establish ingredients and warn against overclaiming.
They do not prove that the combined theorem is historically new. Original
general-p binomial-semivalue comparisons and related capacity-withholding
mechanisms remain to be checked before any novelty statement.
