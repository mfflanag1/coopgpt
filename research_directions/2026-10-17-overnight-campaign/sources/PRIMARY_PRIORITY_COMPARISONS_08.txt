SOURCE COMPARISON08 -- SAMPLING AND SENSITIVITY INGREDIENTS
17 October2026. Primary sources inspected online; proposed application priority open.

Gartner and Welzl, A Simple Sampling Lemma: Analysis and Applications in
Geometric Optimization, author-hosted original:
https://people.inf.ethz.ch/gaertner/subdir/texts/own_work/sampling.pdf
Root read the introduction and Lemmas1.1–1.2 with proofs, PDFpages2–3.
Lemma1.2 states the Bernoulli inclusion/deletion counting identity; the proof
is the same conditioning principle used in our capped-loss lemma. Weighted
versions follow by the identical finite summation. This sampling identity is
an established ingredient, not a proposed new contribution. Their later
LP-type bounds use combinatorial bases; no equivalence with our ordinary-
linear-rank capped-loss statement is asserted without a separate comparison.

Padmanabhan, Woodruff and Zhang, Computing Approximate lp Sensitivities,
NeurIPS2023, conference original:
https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/746d2254f6892f3badadb07cc9c0f0da-Paper-Conference.pdf
Root read Definition1.1, section2 and Fact3.3 with the Lewis-weight sum
statement. l1 sensitivities bound each coordinate divided by the l1 norm;
Fact3.3 plus total Lewis weight equal to dimension gives total sensitivity
at most dimension. Consequently our nonnegative normalized-column trace
bound also follows from this established sensitivity bound. The direct
stochastic-matrix proof is an elementary special-case derivation, not evidence
of a new general rank principle. This source concerns sensitivity computation
and embeddings; our checked passages do not establish the cooperative
funding/incentive application. Earlier sources cited by Fact3.3 remain to be
inspected for precise historical attribution of that ingredient.

INTEGRATION JUDGMENT
The candidate contribution is the modified standalone-aware payment cap,
gain-denominated all-profile incentive theorem and its combination with the
rank sampling estimate. Whether that combination already appears elsewhere
remains unconfirmed. No field-level novelty claim follows from these checks.
