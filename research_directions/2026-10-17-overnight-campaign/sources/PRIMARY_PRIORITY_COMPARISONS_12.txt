https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00972v1
Additional inspection: Sections3.2,4.1,4.3,4.4; Theorem9 and Corollary4.
The paper uses coalition-indexed risk-sharing surplus and population-monotonic
core allocations. Its dual-price and elliptical-risk results impose explicit
cross-coalition compatibility conditions. These are relevant established
comparators for the reserve application. Our report transitions retain owner
identities and allow partial owned-bundle withdrawal; their population comparison
changes the coalition. Atomic splitting would require additional consistency
and budget arguments to transfer a theorem. No automatic implication or
non-overlap assertion follows merely from different terminology. The exact
sharp subsidy formulas still require a broader original-source audit.
