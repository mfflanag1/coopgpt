Fable030535 reported reading the26-page original. Opposite-model Codex QA
independently checked indexed numbered statements, not the inaccessible rawPDF.
Root checked the indexed abstract and the displayed proportional formula, and
verified the model comparison against our own common-cap theorem definitions.
The original studies one scalar input with increasing average returns, exact
core efficiency, and existence of an improved core allocation after endowments
rise. Its incentive core protects each raiser and each non-raiser individually.
Our common-cap results concern one fixed all-profile mechanism, several resource
types, binding underreports, coalition-total incentives and external funding.
These differences prevent a direct identification of the statements checked.
The original identifies multidimensional contributions as a further question;
our different contract does not establish resolution of that question.
