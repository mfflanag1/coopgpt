OUR DEDUCTIONS, NOT CLAIMS QUOTED FROM THAT SOURCE
1. Incentive correspondence. Restrict our actual-owner model to scalar integer
reports. TCWM gives precisely the displayed source inequality. For simultaneous
restorations by several members, keep S fixed and telescope one-coordinate
restorations. Conversely, simultaneous TCWM includes each single restoration.
No continuity, symmetry or changing owner identities is needed. Mixed-resource
and cone reports extend the domain, not the historical monotonicity axiom.

2. Why the cited impossibility cannot be imported directly. A concave
degree-one production function satisfies
 F(a+b)=2F((a+b)/2)>=F(a)+F(b).
Consequently it cannot match the three witness values above: the grand value
would have to be at least2. Independently, our upper rules permit subsidy and
do not impose additivity across production technologies. Exact balance, which
turns one coalition's payment increase into its complement's decrease, is
absent. These differences prevent that proof from automatically providing a
quantitative lower or contradicting the accepted upper. They do not prove
novelty of the latter.

3. Coverage is a separate requirement. For F(z1,z2,z3)=z1 with one coordinate
per actual owner and reports(1,1,1), a proportional rule using the same demand
scaling for each owner pays1/3 each. Owner1 produces1 alone and is uncovered.
Thus positive balanced demand-sharing rules of this form do not supply an
efficient covering rule for our production problem.

4. Cross-coordinate versus own-coordinate curvature. Let C(z)=z1^2+z2^2.
All distinct-coordinate mixed differences vanish, satisfying both signs of
cross sub/supermodularity. Separability gives phi_i=x_i^2, so the per-unit
charge phi_i/x_i=x_i increases with its OWN positive demand. Hence a theorem
about distinct-agent externalities alone cannot imply antitonicity of the
entire price vector. Under the stronger assumption of differentiable full DR,
q(x)=integral_0^1 grad V(tx)dt is coordinatewise antitone by integrating the
DR gradient inequality at each t. This last argument is elementary and
independent; comparison14 now distinguishes the continuous-radial source
from Proposition5's related cost-sharing antecedent.
