EVERY EXACT-CORE MAJORANT ARCHITECTURE NEEDS LINEAR FUNDING
2026-10-17. Original individual-IC model; architecture obstruction only.
Status: proposed theorem and exact proof, independent review pending.

THEOREM.
For every integer k>=2, there is one k-resource min-linear production function
with n=2k fixed actual owners such that every own-report-monotone nonnegative
rule y satisfying the following additional property must spend at least k+1
at a profile of grand worth3:
 (CD) At every report profile b, there exists an efficient core allocation
      z(b) of that ACTUAL-OWNER coalition game with y_i(b)>=z_i(b) for every i.
Hence its worst funding factor is at least(k+1)/3=(n+2)/6.
This applies to the own-report envelope of EVERY exact efficient-core selector,
including the minimum-norm efficient-core selector. No chosen tie-breaking,
symmetry, continuity or price representation is assumed in the lower bound.

CONSTRUCTION AND PROOF.
Use k pools, one resource per pool, and
 F(c)=min_{1<=s<=k}c_s.
Each pool s has two distinct actual owners H_s and L_s. At the full profile,
 H_s reports2e_s and L_s reports e_s.
Thus T=3. For each i separately, lower ONLY H_i's report from2e_i to e_i.
All owners and every other report stay fixed. The reduced profile hasT_i=2.

At that reduced profile the efficient core is UNIQUE: H_i and L_i each get1,
and every other owner gets0. To prove this, the actual coalition containing
both owners in pool i and only H_s in every other pool has worth2, equal to
the grand worth. Efficiency/nonnegativity force every outside L_s (s!=i) to0.
The coalition {H_i} union {L_s:s!=i} has worth1, so H_i must get at least1.
The coalition {L_i} union {L_s:s!=i} also has worth1, forcing L_i at least1.
Efficiency then gives exactly the allocation asserted. It is a core allocation:
every coalition with positive production contains at least one pool-i owner;
one such owner gives worth at most1 and both give worth at most2.

Property(CD) at the i-th reduced profile gives y_Hi>=1. Own monotonicity of
that SAME actual owner carries this to the full profile, independently for
all i. Hence sum_i y_Hi(full)>=k.
At the full profile, the coalition of all low owners has worth1. Property(CD)
there implies sum_i y_Li(full)>=1, giving total y(full)>=k+1.
(In fact ordinary coalition coverage at the full profile suffices for this
last step.) Dividing by grand worth3 proves the lower bound.

MINIMUM-NORM EFFICIENT CORE AT THE FULL PROFILE.
Every full efficient core has aggregate high-owner payment2 and low-owner
payment1: the all-high and all-low coalitions have those values, and their
sum is the grand worth3. Cauchy–Schwarz in the two classes gives the unique
minimum-norm efficient core
 z_Hs=2/k, z_Ls=1/k.
It is feasible because its payment to any coalition is the average of that
coalition's k pool capacities, which dominates their minimum.
Nevertheless its own-report envelope has every high-owner payment at least1,
so the envelope is linearly expensive despite this perfectly balanced full
allocation. This closes the proposed efficient-core-envelope route.

GENERAL CONSEQUENCES AND LIMITS.
1. The envelope of any efficient-core selector is own-monotone, covers, and
   majorizes that selector at each profile. Therefore the theorem defeats ALL
   such selectors, not just the ABV equalization selector or bottleneck prices.
2. The class(CD) has worst-case funding Theta(n): the lower is linear, while
   grand-marginal payments U_i=T-v(N\{i}) are own-monotone, at mostT each,
   and dominate EVERY efficient core since z_i=T-z(N\{i})<=U_i.
   No sharp leading coefficient for this architecture class is claimed.
3. Here d=k and the number of min-linear forms is exactlyk. Thus the same
   obstruction is linear in resource dimension and representation size.
   Any general sublinear-funding mechanism must sometimes fail to dominate
   every candidate efficient core coordinatewise: more precisely, at some
   profile its payment box contains NO efficient core allocation.
4. This is NOT an unrestricted subsidy lower bound. A subsidized covering
   vector need not dominate any efficient core. At the reduced profiles an
   unrestricted rule may use its extra budget to pay other owners and keep
   H_i's payment below1. The existing sublinear unrestricted upper bounds
   therefore remain fully compatible with this theorem.
5. Odd owner counts can be padded by a zero-resource dummy, giving
   (floor(n/2)+1)/3 for n>=4. No owner is replaced or created during deviations.
