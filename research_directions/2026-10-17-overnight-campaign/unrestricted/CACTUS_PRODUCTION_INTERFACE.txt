CONTINUOUS PRODUCTION INTERFACE: AN HONEST INSTANTIATION AND A STAR OBSTRUCTION
2026-10-17. Conditional incidence-selector theorem remains separate from the
unrestricted owner/dimension funding problem.

1. A GROWING ALL-PROFILE PRODUCTION INSTANTIATION.
For n>=2 fixed actual owners, give owner i its own two resource coordinates
(p_i,q_i), with arbitrary nonnegative reports and every coordinatewise
underreport feasible. Define the SINGLE production function
                  F(p,q)=sum_i p_i + min_i q_i.
It is nonnegative, monotone, concave and homogeneous. At any profile let
P=sum_i p_i and G=min_i q_i. Every proper coalition S omits at least one
joint-production input and therefore has value sum_(i in S)p_i. The grand
coalition has value P+G. Its ENTIRE upper-cover polyhedron is exactly
        x_i>=p_i for every i,        sum_i x_i>=P+G.
The fixed coalition-generator incidence graph is a tree: one grand row is
joined to every owner, and each owner also has its singleton leaf row.

Thus the efficient selector theorem applies at EVERY continuous report,
with growing owner/resource counts and fixed actual identities. In explicit
form its payment is x_i=max{p_i,t}, where t is chosen so
sum_i max{p_i,t}=P+G (when G=0 simply use x=p). The rule is individually
own-report monotone. Raising q_i cannot lower G or t. Raising p_i by delta
increases the RHS by delta, while the LHS at the old t rises by at most
delta, so the new t cannot be smaller; the focal payment cannot decrease.
This supplies an elementary independent check of the abstract theorem.

More generally, replace sum p_i by sum_i f_i(c_i), where each f_i is a
nonnegative monotone concave homogeneous technology on owner i's disjoint
resource block. Add any nonnegative monotone concave homogeneous H(c) that
vanishes when ANY owner block is zero. Proper coalition values remain additive,
and the same fixed tree generators and efficient monotone selector apply.
Disjoint unions of these components preserve the conclusion.

This is a genuine continuous fixed-owner instantiation, but the underlying
private-plus-unanimity model is a simple established class. It is not claimed
as a new unrestricted subsidy improvement or a field-level breakthrough.
The incentive conclusion is payment-only individual underreport protection;
outside-production protection is a different condition.

2. A PRECISE OBSTRUCTION FOR NATURAL RESOURCE POOLING.
Let owner0 hold the sole resource c0, and let m>=2 other owners each hold its
own resource c_j. Use the natural one-veto/many-substitute production
                      F(c)=min(c0,sum_(j=1)^m c_j).
This is a monotone concave homogeneous production function, representable as
flow through a common bottleneck and m substitutable branches. Allow the full
continuous report box0<=c_j<=1 for ALL m+1 fixed owners.

At the allowed profile c0=1 and c_j=1/m for every leaf, the coalition value
is0 without owner0, and for every nonempty S subset{1,...,m},
                     v({0} union S)=|S|/m.
The upper-cover polyhedron therefore contains the2^m-1 positive inequalities
                 x0+sum_(j in S)x_j >= |S|/m.                (1)
EVERY ONE of these inequalities is an exposed facet and is indispensable
in any exact representation by coalition inequalities and nonnegativity.

Exact proof. Fix a nonempty S of size s, and choose delta=1/(4m). Set
 x0=delta; x_j=1/m-delta/s for j in S; x_j=1/m+delta for j outside S.
All coordinates are strictly positive. Inequality(1) for S holds at equality.
For any other nonempty U its slack is
               delta*(1-|U intersect S|/s)+delta*|U minus S|.
This is strictly positive: if U is a proper subset of S the first term is
positive; if U has an outside member the second term is positive and the
first term is nonnegative. Thus the S row is the only tight inequality at
this point, proving it supports a facet of this full-dimensional polyhedron.
A sufficiently small decrease of x0 violates just this row, proving that
deleting it changes the feasible set. All non-veto coalition rows have zero
RHS and are already enforced by nonnegativity.

Already the mandatory rows{0,1},{0,2},{0,1,2} have two overlapping incidence
cycles. Therefore no adequate cactus generator system exists for this natural
star production model throughout the full continuous report domain. In fact
even this one profile needs exponentially many distinct coalition facets.
The row-arity-two and column-frequency-two alternatives are likewise excluded
for growing m by their mandatory facet incidence patterns.

At the profile c0=c1=...=cm=1, only the m pair rows x0+x_j>=1 are needed.
Those rows DO have forest incidence. Thus checking a convenient high profile
and then extending its sparse generator representation to all reports would
be mathematically invalid: smaller simultaneous substitute reports create
essential larger-coalition constraints.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CAMPAIGN.
The incidence theorem has a correct all-profile interpretation, but it does
not by itself solve the original pooling problem on resource trees, cactus
networks, or mixed ownership. Natural continuous pooling already leaves its
scope in the smallest overlapping-substitute example. This is a rigorous
stopping condition for that tempting transfer, not evidence against the
structural theorem or against other mechanisms for the pooling technology.
