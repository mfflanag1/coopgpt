OWN-ROW WITNESSES AS A PROJECTED POSITIVE FIELD
2026-10-17. General exact KKT identity; diagonal conjecture remains open.

SETUP
Let Ax>=b be a finite nonnegative 0/1 cover system, b>=0. Fix coordinate i.
Let b' lower only rows containing i, and let q=(d,u), d=q_i>0, be its unique
minimum-Euclidean-norm cover. Nonnegativity bounds may be omitted: the
projection's nonnegative KKT representation q=A^T lambda already implies
q>=0. Zero-demand empty rows may be discarded. Let K_-i be the unchanged
cover polyhedron on the remaining coordinates from all rows excluding i.
Put r=projection_(K_-i)(0).

PROJECTED-FIELD IDENTITY
The total multiplier on incident rows is exactly d. Normalize it to a
probability distribution alpha_S=lambda_S/d, S containing i, and write
 p=sum_S alpha_S 1_(S\i),       B=sum_S alpha_S b_S.
Then
                    u=projection_(K_-i)(d*p),               (1)
                    d+p.u <= B.                            (2)
Indeed stationarity on the other coordinates is
u=d*p+A_off^T lambda_off. Every off-row with positive multiplier is tight
at its original demand. These are precisely the shifted-projection KKT
conditions for(1). Multiplying incident complementary slackness by alpha
gives d+p.u=sum alpha_S b'_S<=B, proving(2).

CURVATURE AND SLACK CONSEQUENCES
Firm nonexpansivity of Euclidean projection, applied to centers dp and0,
gives d*p.(u-r)>=||u-r||^2. Combining this with(2) yields

 d^2+||u-r||^2 <= d*sum_S alpha_S[b_S-r(S\i)].                (3)

This conclusion requires no chosen efficient core and no assumptions about
active-row independence. It keeps the response of the off-owner projection
to the incident positive field. Discarding its nonnegative curvature term
gives the simpler general bound

 E_i <= sigma_i:=max{0,max_(S containing i)[b_S-r(S\i)]},      (4)

where E_i is the full supremum over all allowed incident-row relaxations.
The bound also holds when q_i=0, and passes to nonattained suprema.

The vector sigma is itself a covering allocation: each sigma_i dominates
the full raw projection coordinate. It is own-report monotone whenever
own report restoration only raises incident demands, because r depends
only on unchanged off-owner rows. Thus(4) suggests a legitimate alternative
mechanism. The following exact growing example shows why this scalar
shortcut cannot prove the desired diagonal bound or a useful owner bound.

AN EXACT GROWING OBSTRUCTION TO DISCARDING CURVATURE
Take one veto coordinate0 and m disjoint groups of k coordinates. Use rows
 x_0+sum_(j in group g)x_j>=1,       g=1,...,m.
The efficient l1 value is T=1, attained at x_0=1. The full quadratic
projection and TRUE incident-row envelope coincide and equal
 x_0=m/(m+k),         x_j=1/(m+k) on every group coordinate.  (5)
To see own-row monotonicity directly, allow group demands b_g in[0,1].
The projection has veto value u solving
                  u=(1/k)sum_g(b_g-u)_+,
and group-coordinate value (b_g-u)_+/k. The veto value is nondecreasing in
every b_g. A group owner's incident demand is only b_g; on each active
region du/db_g is 0 or 1/(k+a), where a is the number of positive groups.
Hence its own payment is nondecreasing. Continuity handles boundaries.
Consequently the actual squared-envelope norm is m/(m+k)<=1.

For a group coordinate i, deleting its incident row leaves m-1 groups.
Its zero-field off-owner projection has veto value (m-1)/(m+k-1), and all
other coordinates in i's group are zero. Thus
             sigma_0=1,       sigma_i=k/(m+k-1).
Taking m=k=t>=2, with n=t^2+1 actual coordinates, gives
 sum sigma_i=1+t^3/(2t-1)=Theta(n),
 sum sigma_i^2=1+t^4/(2t-1)^2=Theta(n),
while the true squared envelope in(5) is exactly1/2. The scalar slack
relaxation in(4) loses an unbounded factor. This is an aggregate obstruction,
not merely a failure of an extra pointwise bound.

GENUINE FIXED-OWNER PRODUCTION INTERFACE
The same profile is the all-ones profile of the concave homogeneous
technology
 F(c)=min{c_0, sum_g min_(j in group g)c_j},
with each actual owner holding its own single resource coordinate.
A coalition is productive precisely when it contains the veto and a whole
group, in which case its worth is1. All other original coalition rows are
redundant. For a group owner i, the full off-owner cover is exactly the
m-1-group system above. In(4), productive coalitions using a different group
have nonpositive slack; its own whole group gives the displayed maximum.
For the veto, all off-owner coalition values vanish. Thus the sigma values
remain exact with ALL coalition rows present.

Under one group owner's actual coordinate underreport h in[0,1], its group
row has demand h and every other group row remains1. Every multi-group
coalition row is implied by a remaining full group row. Under a veto
underreport h, all group rows have demand h. Therefore(5) also gives the
true own-report envelope at the all-ones base profile, accounting for every
individual underreport from that base in this genuine fixed-owner domain.
No formula at arbitrary heterogeneous base profiles is asserted here.
No Boolean-only transfer or changed owner population is used.

LIVE CONSEQUENCE AND LIMIT
Equation(3) identifies a concrete term any successful proof through deleted
games must retain: the squared motion ||projection_K(dp)-projection_K(0)||^2.
The example proves zero-field scalar slacks alone cannot yield a sublinear
funding factor. No universal cross-owner curvature sum is proved here, and
the main conjecture sum E_i^2<=T^2 remains open. This note does not improve
the existing general mechanism bound or prove an unrestricted lower bound.

PRECISE UNPROVED AGGREGATE COUPLING TARGET; GATE CLOSED
For chosen own witnesses, let a^i=(1,p^i) with coordinates restored to their
original positions and B_i=sum alpha^i_S b_S. Every full cover satisfies
a^i.z>=B_i. A sufficient coupling lemma would assert
 min{sum_j z_j: z>=0, a^i.z>=B_i for all positive d_i}
                      >= sqrt(sum_i d_i^2).                 (U)
Its LP dual would combine the incident-row KKT distributions into a feasible
fractional packing. Equation(3) supplies additional information beyond
B_i>=d_i||a^i||^2, but no proof of(U) from that information was found.
Condition(U) is stronger than the original diagonal conjecture and is not
being asserted as a theorem. No more search under this gate is authorized.

One tempting simplification of(U) is FALSE even with no own relaxations.
Set v=sum_i d_i a^i and C=sum_i d_i B_i, and normalize this one aggregate
dual by max_j v_j. Its value C/max v need not reach sqrt(sum d_i^2).
Take the common-veto family above with m=k^2, and adjoin a completely
disjoint singleton constraint x_*=1. Use the full raw projection as every
witness. Each veto-group row has original multiplier1/[k(k+1)] and k+1
incident owners, so its aggregate incident multiplier is1/k. Consequently
the veto column load is k, every leaf load1/k, the singleton load1, and
C=k+1. This particular normalized dual gives only1+1/k. But
 sum_i d_i^2=1+k/(k+1),
which exceeds (1+1/k)^2 for every k>=4. The full efficient value is2, so
this does not refute the original conjecture. It shows the incident-dual
weights must adapt across components; a single maximum-load normalization
throws away necessary information. The full LP in(U) can rescale these
two components separately and is not refuted by this example.
