BALANCED CLOSURE PRESERVES COVER GEOMETRY AND LEGITIMIZES LOCAL ROW RELAXATIONS
2026-10-17. Proposed structural lemma; standard ingredients, novelty unassessed.

Let a(S)>=0, a(empty)=0 be arbitrary finite coalition demands on N. Write
 P_A(a)={x in R_+^A : x(U)>=a(U) for every U subset A},
and define its balanced closure
 C(a)(A)=min_{x in P_A(a)} sum_{i in A}x_i.
The minimum exists: large equal coordinates cover all finite demands, and a
bounded minimizing sublevel is compact. No balancedness of a is assumed.

1. C(a)>=a, since the row for A itself is one of the constraints.
2. P_A(C(a))=P_A(a) for every A. One inclusion follows from1. For the reverse,
   if x covers a on A, then for every U subset A the restriction x|U covers a
   on U, hence x(U)>=C(a)(U).
3. Every subgame C(a)|A has a nonnegative efficient core: any minimizer for
   P_A(a) covers C(a) by2 and has sum C(a)(A). Thus C(a) is totally balanced.
4. C(a) is monotone in coalitions: restricting a cover for a larger coalition
   to a smaller one cannot increase its total nonnegative payment. It is also
   monotone in the demand array a.
5. C(v)=v for an already totally balanced nonnegative monotone game v: its
   efficient subgame cores attain each required minimum, while1 gives the
   reverse inequality.

LOCAL RELAXATION CONSEQUENCE.
Take a totally balanced full game v, and any a^i with 0<=a^i<=v and
 a^i(S)=v(S) whenever i notin S. Its closure w^i=C(a^i) satisfies
 w^i<=C(v)=v, w^i(S)=v(S) off i, and is totally balanced. Most importantly,
 P_N(w^i)=P_N(a^i), so its minimum-Euclidean-norm covering vector is unchanged
by closure. The star realization corollary in BOOLEAN_PROFILE_REALIZATION_LEMMA
therefore embeds ALL such local row relaxations as actual own underreports
of fixed owners in one production problem with2n resources.

Sparse form: begin with any nonnegative 0/1 coalition-cover system, close it
once to v, and for owner i delete or reduce any constraints containing i while
retaining all constraints not containing i. Closing each relaxed system gives
the same projection polyhedra, and agrees with v on every coalition omitting i,
because no deleted i-row is contained in such a coalition. Hence these sparse
relaxations are equally eligible for the realization theorem.

This supersedes the cautious sentence in BOOLEAN_PROFILE_REALIZATION_LEMMA
that arbitrary row deletion is not directly allowed: deletion alone may fail
balancedness, but balanced closure repairs it with EXACTLY the same cover
polyhedron and preserves the required local agreement. No counterexample from
an abstract covering polyhedron should be discarded solely for that reason.

PROJECTION AND ENVELOPE DEFINITIONS.
For any game w, let q(w) be the unique minimizer of sum_i x_i^2 over P_N(w).
For a fixed production function F and actual-owner report profile b, write
v_b(S)=F(sum_{j in S}b_j). The true own-report envelope at that profile is
 M_i^F(b)=sup_{0<=a_i<=b_i} q_i(v_(a_i,b_-i)),
where the vector inequality is coordinatewise and every other actual owner
keeps exactly the same report. It contains the full-report choice and hence
covers every coalition. The supremum is finite because all the lower-profile
projection norms are bounded by any full-profile efficient core's norm.
Increasing b_i enlarges the supremum's set, so M_i^F is own-report monotone.
Define also the abstract local envelope at a totally balanced game v by
 E_i(v)=sup{q_i(a):0<=a<=v, a(S)=v(S) for every S omitting i}.
Every actual envelope obeys M_i^F(b)<=E_i(v_b). Conversely, for any epsilon>0,
choose one local demand array a^i per owner approaching E_i(v), close it as
above, and apply the star realization. A single fixed-owner production
instance then realizes all these chosen witnesses at one full profile.
The characterization is therefore a WORST-CASE characterization across
production instances. It does not claim that an already fixed F realizes
every abstract relaxation of its current full game.

CONSEQUENCES FOR THE UNRESTRICTED OWNER GAP.
A growing family of such full/relaxed covering polyhedra with projection
coordinates m_i and sum m_i>sqrt(n)T, where T=min_{x in P_N(v)}sum x_i, refutes
the proposed sqrt(n) funding bound for the true own-report monotone envelope.
Conversely, a uniform geometry bound for all these local relaxations proves
it for every production problem, since an actual underreport changes only
rows containing its owner and only decreases their demands. Therefore this
cover-geometry problem is not merely a loose proxy: after balanced closure
and simultaneous realization, it is an exact finite characterization of the
worst possible projection-envelope behavior (up to using arbitrary many
local relaxations to approximate a supremum).

The same construction can transfer star-shaped payment LP lower certificates,
but no new unrestricted funding lower bound is asserted here.
