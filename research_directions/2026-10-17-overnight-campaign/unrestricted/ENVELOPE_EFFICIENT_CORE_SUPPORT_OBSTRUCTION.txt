THE RAW PROJECTION SUPPORT INEQUALITY DOES NOT PASS TO ITS OWN ENVELOPE
2026-10-17. Exact analytical stopping lemma for a proposed proof step.

For a cover polyhedron let q be its least-Euclidean-norm point. Every full
feasible cover a satisfies ||q||^2<=q.a. It is tempting to prove the same
inequality for the own-report envelope M by choosing a favorable efficient
core a. That stronger assertion is false on a growing pure-pool family.
This does NOT refute either live candidate
  ||M||^2<=T^2, or ||M||^2<=T*max_i M_i.
The latter would imply sum M_i<=(1+sqrt(n))*T/2, but needs another proof.

EXACT FAMILY.
There are k>=7 pools and two fixed owners H_s,L_s per pool. Production is
F(c)=min_s c_s. At the full profile H_s has capacity2 and L_s capacity1;
grand worth is T=3. The full minimum-norm cover is
 q_H=2/k, q_L=1/k.
Indeed the all-high coalition has worth2 and the all-low coalition worth1,
so every cover has those class totals at least2 and1. The displayed vector
covers: a positive coalition with a low-only pool has worth at most1;
otherwise it includes every high, giving payment at least2; worth3 requires
every owner. Cauchy--Schwarz on the two class totals proves optimality.
Every EFFICIENT core, symmetric or not, has high total2 and low total1.

Now lower ONLY H_i's capacity2->1. The two focal owners are identical in
the coalition game, so their projection payments both equal s. Write a,b
for a nonfocal high and low respectively. All positive coalition constraints
are equivalent to
 s+(k-1)*min(a,b)>=1,  2s+(k-1)*a>=2.
For 0<=s<=1 the least feasible a,b are
 a=2(1-s)/(k-1), b=(1-s)/(k-1).
The squared objective is2s^2+5(1-s)^2/(k-1), hence
 s=h=5/(2k+3).
The optimal s lies in[0,1]; s>1 cannot improve it. Thus each full-profile
high owner's own envelope is at least h, while each low envelope is at
least its full payment1/k. Owners are never cloned or reassigned.

By symmetry of the full game and admissible report domains, write the TRUE
full own envelope as p on highs and z on lows. Then p>=h and z>=1/k.
For every efficient core a, regardless of its asymmetry,
 M.a=2p+z,
 ||M||^2-M.a=k*p*(p-2/k)+k*z*(z-1/k).
Since k>=7 gives h>2/k, this expression is strictly positive. Therefore
NO efficient core supports M in the raw-projection variational sense.
Even the finite diagonal witness(h,1/k) already has the exact positive gap
 h*(k*h-2)=5*(k-6)/(2k+3)^2.

STRUCTURAL REASON.
Whenever symmetry fixes each orbit's total in every efficient core and the
full projection is uniform on each orbit with those totals, any strict
own-envelope inflation on an orbit destroys this support inequality. The
problem is not choosing the wrong efficient core: all cores have the same
dot product with the orbit-constant envelope. A successful diagonal-norm
proof must use off-owner locality in a different way or exploit the weaker
T*max M bound directly.

SCOPE.
The underlying technology and every report change are genuine monotone
concave homogeneous fixed-owner production. The support obstruction holds
for the full all-profile own envelope, since it dominates the displayed
unilateral witnesses. No general lower bound is obtained: the family has
a separate factor2 mechanism when backup capacities are fixed.
