17 October 2026. Read-only implementation/proof review of the composition lane.

composition/COVERAGE_POLYMATROID_FLOW_CHECK.py, or the accompanying _NOTE.txt.
The backend supplies the exact maximal-minimizer oracle required by the already

1. CUT IDENTITY AND FINITE BIG-M
Write r(S)=sum_f w_f 1{S intersects E_f}. The network has source-job capacity
c_j, job-feature capacity M=1+sum_j c_j, and feature-sink capacity a*w_f.
The cut with only the source on its source side costs sum c. Since all capacities
are nonnegative, every cut crossing even one incidence edge costs at least
M>sum c and therefore cannot be minimum. A source-side job set S must include
all incident features. Including additional features can only increase the cut
cost, or leave it unchanged when a*w_f=0. Thus its least cut cost is exactly
c(J\S)+a*r(S), proving mincut=sum c+min_S(a*r(S)-c(S)).

The chosen M does not need to exceed the feature-sink capacity sum. It remains
valid when weights/a are enormous and capacities are tiny rationals, or when
sum c=0. Empty and zero-weight features can be removed without altering r or
the minimizing job sets. Repeated feature masks are represented by distinct
feature vertices and correctly add their weights.

2. MAXIMAL MINIMIZER, NOT MERELY SOME MINIMUM CUT
At termination, Edmonds--Karp has no positive residual source-sink path.
Let R contain precisely the vertices that have a positive residual path to the
sink. Then U=V\R contains the source, excludes the sink, and has no positive
residual edge to its complement: such an edge would place its tail in R.
Hence U is a minimum-cut source side. Every other minimum-cut source side W
is residual-closed and cannot intersect R, since following a path from that
intersection to the sink would violate closure. Therefore W subset U.

Every minimizing job set has a minimum-cut extension, by the cut identity.
Consequently the jobs in U are exactly the inclusion-maximal minimizing set.
The implementation computes R by traversing the reverse of each positive
residual edge. Both directions are always present as dictionary keys, and
zero-capacity keys are retained, so residual[u][v] is valid in that traversal.
Source reachability alone would select the opposite extremal cut and would be
wrong for the required tie convention; this implementation does not make that
mistake. Its exact cut/flow/objective equality checks are consistent with the
graph-theoretic argument, not substitutes for it.

3. MAX-FLOW IMPLEMENTATION AND BOUNDARIES
The BFS is a standard shortest augmenting-path search. Every augmentation uses
an exact positive Fraction bottleneck, updates forward/reverse residuals, and
increases the recorded flow by that bottleneck. The network has no original
antiparallel layer edges that would complicate its cut accounting. Zero edges
may be omitted safely. Termination is justified by the Edmonds--Karp argument,
not by a numerical tolerance or a capacity-dependent unit augmentation bound.

For a=0, all jobs belong to the maximal minimizer even with zero capacities;
the sink is isolated and the reverse-reachability procedure returns them.
For c=0 and a>0, precisely the public loops can occur in a zero-rank minimizing
set. Positive-capacity loops are included and have zero private payment.
The empty ground set also works. The wrapper's P=0/private-zero/rank-zero
cases use the accepted evaluator's zero-payment convention and no flow calls.

4. ACTIVE AND ORIGINAL INDICES
The wrapper computes active original indices in increasing order, exactly as
the generic evaluator does. The explicit rank restriction maps each original
feature to those local indices, retaining zero/duplicate features harmlessly.
Its callback binds that same local rank and validates capacities plus empty,
full and returned rank values against the generic callback. These checks guard
the known representation interface; the note correctly does not claim that
three rank queries certify an arbitrary black-box rank. Returned prices and
chain masks are mapped back to original resource indices. No strategic owners
are split, merged or created by this bookkeeping.

5. RATIONAL AND OPERATION COMPLEXITY
For the explicit coverage representation the network has m+f+2 vertices and
at most m+f+I positive original edges. The stated O(V E^2) Edmonds--Karp
arithmetic bound and O(V+E) graph storage apply in the usual nontrivial case.
The principal chain invokes at most 2m-1 such flows. Rank evaluation and
incidence construction also have polynomial work in the explicit mask input.

At each call, all capacities are rational expressions in original data and
one exact breakpoint. A common denominator can be chosen as the product of
the finitely many capacity denominators, whose bit length is at most their
combined bit length. Each residual remains between zero and its pair's total
capacity; flow stays at most sum c. Thus exact addition/subtraction/comparison
does not conceal exponentially growing integer encoding. Together with the
previously audited polynomial breakpoint encoding, this supports polynomial
rational implementation, while the note appropriately avoids practical runtime
or arbitrary-real unit-cost promises. Fraction arithmetic uses no floating
epsilon for zero capacities or ties.

6. INDEPENDENT ADVERSARIAL REPLAY
I inspected the author's checker and recorded JSON, including its actual
24/72/160-job flow runs. Its larger cases invoke the real flow backend, not a
dummy or exhaustive SFM callback. The extra z=1 call independently checks the
chain's value through the same backend; it is not an independent flow algorithm.
Small exhaustive comparisons provide a separate oracle reference. I did not
blindly rerun the author's unchanged suite.

Instead I copied the evaluated implementation and generic dependency into
unrestricted/coverage_flow_final_replay and wrote an independent exact checker.
Six deliberately chosen small fixtures compare against direct weighted-feature
objectives: reverse-edge rerouting; tied components with duplicates/empty/zero
features; huge feature weights versus tiny c; coefficient zero; capacity zero;
and the empty ground set. All PASS. In the two-job rerouting fixture, the first
flow uses feature0 for job0; the second must send job1 through feature0 and its
reverse edge to reroute job0 through feature1. The maximal tied job mask is3.

A 137-job case uses32 disjoint, noncontiguous eligibility groups,64 positive
duplicate features, zero-capacity jobs and9 loop jobs. Here the production
and prices have independent additive min formulas, so no 2^137 enumeration
or second use of the generic price algorithm is needed for the reference.
All prices match exactly. The run uses63 actual flow calls,33 principal sets,
7,934 augmentations, and networks up to195 vertices/435 original edges.
Six additional standalone scale/tie checks and64 deliberately permuted rank
restrictions pass. These are implementation evidence, not universal incentive
proofs or a general scalability benchmark.
