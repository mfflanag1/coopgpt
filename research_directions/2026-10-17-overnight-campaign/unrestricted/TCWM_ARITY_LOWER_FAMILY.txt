A SCALABLE STRONG-INCENTIVE LOWER FOR r-WAY COMPLEMENTARITY
17 October 2026. Candidate theorem; independent audit and priority check pending.

THEOREM
Fix integers r>=2 and s>=2. Let n=r+s-1 actual owners all have all r resources
in their fixed eligibility sets. Production is F(c)=min_j c_j. Every rule with
nonnegative payments, coalition coverage, and transferable coalition-withholding
monotonicity (TCWM) has worst-case total-funding factor at least
 B(r,s) = 1 + (s+r-3)/(s+r-2) *
       [ (r-2)/(r-1) + 1/r - (r-2)/(r(r-1)s) ].                 (1)
TCWM requires every coalition's total payment to be nondecreasing whenever
one member increases any resource coordinate, holding all others fixed.
The whole proof uses binary reports and the SAME n actual owners.

Consequently, for fixed r and unbounded finite owner counts,
 sup_n optimal-TCWM-factor >= 2-1/[r(r-1)].                     (2)
For THREE resources this tends11/6. Combined with the existing integral rule's
mixed-owner TCWM extension, the rank-three MINIMUM technology has factor in
[11/6,2] when owner counts are unrestricted. This is an all-mechanism lower
within the stronger TCWM class. It is not a lower for merely individual IC.

PROOF
If the rule's worst-case factor is infinite there is nothing to prove.
Otherwise average the rule over all permutations of actual owner labels and
resource labels. The fixed domain, production, all coalitions, report comparisons,
and budget bound are preserved. This is a finite convex combination of rules,
not a strategic cloning or partition-consistency assumption. We may therefore
use the invariant averaged rule in the following profiles.

P0: one pure unit owner for each of the r resources, and s-1 zero-report owners.
All pure owners receive the same x; zero owners receive the same w>=0.
The coalition consisting of the r pure owners has value1, so r*x>=1.

P1: increase every zero-report owner to the pure unit resource-1 report.
There are now s identical resource-1 owners, each receiving a, and one pure
owner of each other resource, each receiving b. Both types of symmetry follow
from the averaging above. The coalition consisting of the original resource-1
owner and all previously zero owners has the SAME actual members throughout.
TCWM, applied successively to the s-1 increases, gives
   s*a >= x+(s-1)w >=1/r.                                    (3)
A coalition consisting of one resource-1 owner and the other r-1 pure owners
has production1, so
   a+(r-1)b >=1.                                             (4)

P2: leave the unique resource-2 owner unchanged. Increase EVERY other owner's
report to the vector with zero in resource2 and one in every other resource.
Every such change is coordinatewise nondecreasing: the changed owners initially
hold a pure unit resource different from resource2. Let C denote all these
m=s+r-2 actual owners, excluding only the unchanged resource-2 owner.
At P2 all owners in C have identical report vectors; write p for their common
payment and q for the unchanged owner's payment. Production at P2 is1.
One member of C together with the resource-2 owner has worth1. Hence
   p+q>=1,  m*p+q<=kappa,
which imply
   m*p <= m/(m-1)*(kappa-1).                                 (5)
TCWM for C throughout the P1-to-P2 increases gives
   s*a+(r-2)b <= m*p.                                        (6)
By(4),
   s*a+(r-2)b
    >= (r-2)/(r-1) + [s-(r-2)/(r-1)]a
    >= (r-2)/(r-1) + 1/r - (r-2)/(r(r-1)s),                  (7)
where the coefficient on a is positive and(3) was used. Combine(5)--(7)
and rearrange to obtain(1). Letting s tend to infinity gives(2), since
 (r-2)/(r-1)+1/r=1-1/[r(r-1)]. QED.

SPECIAL CASES AND EXACT CHECKS
r=2 gives B=3/2-1/(2s), approaching the graph factor3/2.
r=3 gives B=1+(5s-1)/(6(s+1))=11/6-1/(s+1).
Thus r=3, s=3 (five owners) yields19/12>3/2 already. The separate exact
five-owner LP certificate improves this finite instance to103/64; it is not
needed for the asymptotic family or separation.

No numerical optimization occurs in the proof. verify_tcwm_arity_family.py
checks the symbolic rational rearrangements at hundreds of integer parameters.

SCOPE
This establishes a clean obstruction to extending graph factor3/2 while
retaining TCWM: even a SINGLE triple-input activity requires at least11/6
as the fixed owner set grows. The rule is unrestricted in all other respects:
nonlinear owner-aware rules are covered. The same lower applies to any wider
class containing these technologies. It does not improve the original weaker-
incentive logarithmic-versus-square-root owner/dimension gap. The class of
arbitrary rank-three packing hypergraphs may require more than this single-
activity subclass; its general baseline remains3 unless improved elsewhere.
