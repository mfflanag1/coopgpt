ONE ADJUSTABLE OWNER PER POOL: A GLOBAL FACTOR-TWO STOPPING LEMMA
2026-10-17. Individual own-report incentives, restricted ownership/domain.

Let F(c)=min_s c_s on finitely many pure resource pools. In each pool s,
exactly one owner has an adjustable capacity h_s>=L_s>=0. All other owners
have fixed capacities, totaling B_s. No owner has resources in another pool.
There is a covering own-monotone rule, simultaneously on the entire product
of these half-lines, with funding factor at most2, regardless of the number
of pools, backup owners, report levels, or simultaneously changed owners.

Choose a pool g minimizing T_min=L_s+B_s. Pay its adjustable owner the
current grand worth T=min_s(h_s+B_s). Pay every fixed backup owner in pool g
its fixed capacity, and pay everybody else zero. A coalition containing the
pivot is covered by its T payment alone. A coalition excluding the pivot
produces at most the sum of its capacities in pool g, exactly the backup
payments it receives there. Own-report monotonicity is immediate: only the
pivot's payment varies with its own report, and T is nondecreasing.
Total payment is T+B_g. Since B_g<=T_min<=T, the factor is at most2. More
precisely it is at most1+B_g/T_min when T_min>0; when T_min=0, B_g=0 and
the rule is efficient. Zero-production profiles therefore cause no issue.

This includes every Boolean or multi-level extension of the sharp one-star
lower family in SHARP_GENERAL_STAR_STOPPING_THEOREM.txt: one high owner per
pool may change from m to1 while m-1 unit backups stay fixed. Here B_g=m-1,
T_min=m, and the factor is2-1/m on the full Boolean cube (or even on every
profile with each high capacity at least1), matching the large-pool limit
of the finite star lower for this fixed m. Thus adding pairs, triples, or
all simultaneous high-owner changes to that lower family cannot beat2.

A lower-bound campaign must allow more than one independently changing
owner in some pools, mixed ownership, or another production structure.
This is a direct scoped mechanism and a stopping certificate, not an
improvement to the general all-owner/all-resource theorem. Fixed backup
owners truly have no variable reports in this domain; treating their
capacities as strategic would invalidate the constant-payment argument.

The longer symmetric Boolean allocation originally discovered on this
route is unnecessary: this pivot proof strictly enlarges its domain and
makes the stopping mechanism explicit. No priority claim is made for this
elementary construction.
