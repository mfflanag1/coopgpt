RANK-THREE FAILURE AND UNBOUNDED ENVELOPE COST FOR THE NATURAL HUBER EXTENSION
17 October 2026. Analytic result; pending independent audit.

Let r>=2. Activities consume one unit of every resource in a fixed hyperedge,
with between2 andr resources per activity, and earn one unit. Fixed actual
owners report arbitrary nonnegative resource bundles. Define
 h_r(t)=r t^2/2 for0<=t<=1/r; h_r(t)=t-1/(2r) for1/r<=t<=1.
Minimize sum_v c_v h_r(p_v) over0<=p<=1 and sum_{v in e}p_v>=1.
Pay raw y_i=sum_v b_iv p_v. Any fixed optimizer selection may be used below.

RAW COVERAGE AND FUNDING (NO INCENTIVE CLAIM)
Every selected price is dual feasible, so raw payments cover all coalitions.
At positive capacities, Slater holds (all prices3/4). KKT supplies edge
multipliers lambda_e>=0. At p_v=0, stationarity gives degree(lambda)_v=0;
at0<p_v<1 it gives degree(lambda)_v=c_v min(rp_v,1).
No p_v=1 can occur: every tight edge there has all other prices0, whose KKT
equations force its multiplier0; but stationarity at p_v=1 requires degree
at least c_v>0. This contradiction uses edge arity>=2.
Thus at ALL vertices degree(lambda)=c_v min(rp_v,1)<=c_v, so lambda is a
feasible original production plan. On every positive-lambda edge sum p=1.
Using p/min(rp,1)=max(1/r,p), with a harmless convention at p=0,
  sum_v c_v p_v
   =sum_e lambda_e sum_{v in e}max(1/r,p_v)
   <=(2-1/r) sum_e lambda_e <=(2-1/r)F(c).
The middle bound follows because an edge of size<=r and price sum1 has at
least one price>=1/r, leaving at most r-1 upward replacements by1/r.
Zero capacities are handled by the induced positive-vertex hypergraph,
deleting every activity incident to a zero vertex, and assigning zero vertices
price1. This preserves exactly the original positive-production opportunities.

The funding estimate alone is NOT an eligible mechanism theorem.

INCENTIVE FAILURE ALREADY ONE ACTIVITY, r=3
Take exactly r pure owners, one per input of a single r-input activity.
The technology is F(c)=min_j c_j. At capacities(a,1,...,1),0<=a<=1,
the unique positive-coordinate optimizer has
  p_1=1-(r-1)a/r,  p_j=a/r for j>=2.
Indeed lambda=a satisfies every KKT equality; at a<1 the latter r-1 prices
are in the strictly convex region, fixing them uniquely, and the first price
is fixed by the tight edge. The endpoint a=1 is uniquely all1/r by Jensen.
The zero endpoint agrees with the induced-positive convention.

The first owner's payment is
  y_1(a)=a-(r-1)a^2/r.
Its derivative is1-2(r-1)a/r, negative when a>r/[2(r-1)]. Thus every r>=3
fails binding-capacity truthfulness, independently of optimizer tie-breaking.
For r=3, true capacity1 pays1/3, but underreport3/4 pays3/8>1/3.

EXACT OWN-REPORT ENVELOPE CONSEQUENCE
Let E_i be the least own-report monotone majorant, namely the supremum of raw
y_i over own binding underreports with all other actual owners fixed. At the
balanced profile(1,...,1), each owner's one-dimensional optimization is exactly
the preceding quadratic. Hence
  E_i = r/[4(r-1)],
  sum_i E_i = r^2/[4(r-1)],  while F=1.
As r grows this is asymptotic to r/4. Therefore simply taking the monotone
envelope of this natural raw <2 funding extension cannot preserve any
rank-independent funding bound, even on one activity with pure ownership.
Each deviation used in the supremum is a unilateral change in the SAME fixed
game; simultaneous attainment of the different suprema is not assumed.

SCOPE
The graph theorem r=2 remains unaffected. This is a failure of a particular
regularizer and its own-report envelope, not of all hypergraph mechanisms.
For the single-activity pure-owner domain, paying every owner min_j c_j/r is
already truthful, efficient and covering. Thus the obstruction is expressly
architectural; it identifies why the graph proof cannot be extended by merely
moving the quadratic/linear threshold from1/2 to1/r.
