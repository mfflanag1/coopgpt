1. ORIGINAL CONE REPORTS AND OWNER IDENTITY
The cone is K={(t,x):t>=||x||}, with production t-||x|| and withdrawals D_i in
K such that retained b_i-D_i remain in K. The auxiliary u_i=Lx_i and their
positive/negative parts are evaluated per actual owner. No owner splitting,
merging, or change in strategic identity occurs. It would be wrong to assume
their coordinates change monotonically; the proof correctly avoids that.
For every coalition, the equality
 F(sum b_i)=sum(t_i-||x_i||)+2 int min(sum u_i^+,sum u_i^-)
is exact. Nonnegative auxiliary payments bounded by |u_i| are integrable
even if the embedding measure is not finite.

2. FINITE-CHANGE TCWM PROOF
For scalar z,w, |z^+-w^+|+|z^--w^-|=|z-w|. Summing over actual coalition
owners, applying the triangle inequality after aggregation, and integrating
gives int(|da|+|db|)<=sum||dx_i||<=dt. If 0<=W_a,W_b<=kappa, then moving
from (a,b) to (a+da,b+db) loses at most kappa times its total negative movement.
Combining this with df=dt-int(da+db) gives exactly
 Delta Y >= kappa[dt-int(|da|+|db|)]>=0.
The coefficient 2 on the auxiliary min game is necessary and is used
correctly; no factor of 2 is missing.

3. FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION PROOF
On straight cone restoration, let r=a'_S and v=b'_S. Pointwise r-v=L(dx_S),
where dx_S is the SUM of all withdrawn signed vectors. Hence the outside
option after integration is dt-||dx_S||, not sum_i(dt_i-||dx_i||).
For 0<=alpha,beta<=kappa and 1<=alpha+beta<=2kappa-1 the claimed inequality
 (kappa-1)(|r|+|v|)+(2alpha-kappa)r+(2beta-kappa)v+|r-v|>=0
is correct in all six ordering/sign regions. Its three printed expansions
are algebraically exact; swapping the two coordinates covers the rest.
After integrating this pointwise inequality,
 Y'>=kappa dt-(kappa-1)int(|r|+|v|)-int|r-v|
    >=dt-||dx_S||=F(D_S).
Integration in the restoration parameter proves the exact full coalition
outside option. No approximation, differentiability of the norm, or individual
outside-option substitution was used.

Seam justification clarification (not a counterexample): the explicit W is
piecewise smooth, with seams at zero totals, A=B, and B/A=t0 (or its symmetric
version). For each fixed omega, each u_i(s) is affine and changes sign at most
once, so a_S(s),b_S(s) are piecewise affine with finitely many pieces. On each
piece the seam equations are linear in s; a seam is crossed finitely often or
the path lies along it. In the latter case the two continuous smooth formulas
have the same tangential derivative, so either admissible one-sided gradient
gives the needed inequality. The displayed globally bounded derivative
estimates then justify Fubini and absolute continuity. This supplies the detail
behind the author's phrase that continuity handles seams. For an abstract
arbitrary locally Lipschitz W, one can instead use mollification or the convex
gradient constraints; the explicit mechanisms do not require that generality.

To remove any representative ambiguity, choose basis representatives of the
finite-dimensional map L once. Then u_i(s)=u_i(0)+s Ldx_i pointwise outside
one fixed null set. No uncountable family of exceptional sets is necessary.

4. EXPLICIT SCALAR INPUT CHECKS
Huber input (A>=B, r=B/A):
 alpha=(r/2)[1-(a-b)/A], beta=1-r/2+(a-b)/(2A).
The bounds 0<=a/A<=1 and 0<=b/A<=r give alpha in[0,1] and beta in[0,3/2],
as required for TCWM with kappa3/2. This input is not asserted to satisfy
the full-OUT derivative condition, and the audit makes no such inference.

Logarithmic input: c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1, t0=1-exp(-1/c)=1/c-1,
q=1-c-c(1-r)log(1-r)/r for r<=t0, q=c/r for r>=t0.
Both q and q' match at t0. On the first branch q'=c[log(1-r)+r]/r^2,
q+r q'=1+c log(1-r)>=0, and
cr+q+(1-r)r q'=1. These yield 0<=alpha,beta<=1+c and
1<=alpha+beta<=c+q<=1+c. On the second branch the extrema are
alpha<=c(1+r), beta<=c+c/r, and
c(1+r)<=alpha+beta<=c+c/r. Since r>=t0 and q<=1, these are again within
the stated bounds. In particular alpha+beta<=kappa<=2kappa-1 because
kappa=1+c>=1. Scalar coverage follows from nonnegative prices summing to
at least1; funding is B[c+q]<=kappa B. Boundary payments tend to zero when
the smaller total tends to zero, also following from this nonnegative total
funding bound. No endpoint r=0 or r=1 gap was found.

5. LOWER-BOUND TRANSFER
Fix a unit e and map (a_i,b_i) to (a_i+b_i,(a_i-b_i)e). This is a fixed
linear map on the original owners, with production exactly 2min(A,B).
Every coordinatewise underreport maps to a cone-feasible withdrawal. A
mechanism on the full cone domain restricts to this subdomain; dividing all
payments by2 preserves coverage, funding factor, TCWM, and full OUT.
Therefore the accepted scalar lower bounds transfer with exactly the same
constants, uniformly over owner counts. No regularity of the restricted
mechanism is assumed beyond the source lower-bound contracts.
