AN ELEMENTARY RANK-SENSITIVE FULL-OUTSIDE PSD RULE
17 October 2026. Routine structural baseline; novelty not asserted.

Let fixed actual owners report X_i in PSD_m, let T=lambda_min(sum_i X_i),
and allow every Loewner downward report X'_i=X_i-D_i with D_i>=0.
Set r_i=rank(X_i) and
                              y_i=(r_i/m)T.
This rule covers every coalition and satisfies full coalition outside-production
incentives. Its total payment is exactly (sum_i r_i/m)T.

Coverage: if coalition S has positive production, sum_(i in S)X_i is positive
definite and m=rank(sum_S X_i)<=sum_S r_i. Therefore y(S)>=T>=F(X_S).
Coalitions with zero production need no further payment.

Incentives: only S withdraws D_i. Write T'=F(sum_i X'_i), r'_i=rank(X'_i),
a=sum_S r_i and b=sum_S r'_i. Loewner order gives a>=b and T>=T', so
 y(S)-y'(S)=(aT-bT')/m >= (a/m)(T-T')>=0.
If F(D_S)=0 this is the desired inequality. If F(D_S)>0 then
 m=rank(D_S)<=sum_S rank(D_i)<=sum_S rank(X_i)=a.
Superadditivity of lambda_min gives T-T'>=F(D_S), and thus
                     y(S)-y'(S)>=F(D_S).
This includes singleton, pairwise, and arbitrary coalition deviations, without
changing owner identities or requiring a measurable choice of eigenvectors.

Consequence: with n=m owners and each report constrained to rank at most one,
every positive profile has all m owners active with rank one. Then sum_i y_i=T,
so the rule is efficient and full-OUT on the ENTIRE domain, including arbitrary
report-dependent orientations and all allowed Loewner underreports.
For n<m rank-one reports grand production is identically zero. For general
n rank-one owners the same rule has factor n/m whenever T>0.

This clarifies the capped-mask obstruction: its rotated-frame witness alone
lies in an easy efficient domain. The lower additionally uses higher-rank
junta owners and the requirement that the same retained-only architecture
handle all reports. No unrestricted mechanism lower follows from the frame.

Boundary: this is a total-output bound. Replacing r_i by the rank of
U_i=X_i-lambda_min(X_i)I does not preserve the proof, since U_i and its rank
need not increase under an own Loewner increase of X_i. A gain-normalized
rank-sensitive analogue would require new mathematics. The simple full-rank
case sum r_i=nm only gives nT, and is not a general PSD bound improvement.
