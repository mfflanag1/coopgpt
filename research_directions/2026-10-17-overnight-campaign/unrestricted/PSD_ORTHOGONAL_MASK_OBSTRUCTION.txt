A SQUARE-ROOT PSD-ORDER BARRIER FOR ARBITRARILY CORRELATED CAPPED MASKS
17 October 2026. Proposed theorem; independent review pending.
Authoring lane: unrestricted. Root owns integration and independent audit.

STATEMENT AND EXACT ARCHITECTURE
Let m>=4 and let the SAME n=m owners report matrices in PSD_m. Production is
F(X)=lambda_min(X). Consider a public, report-independent probability law rho
on subsets B of these m owner labels. Correlation and asymmetry are unrestricted.
On a profile U=(U_i) with lambda_min(U_i)=0 for every i, put
 G=lambda_min(sum_i U_i), U_B=sum_(i in B) U_i.
The architecture pays retained owners their deletion marginals
 y_i(B)=lambda_min(U_B)-lambda_min(U_B-U_i), i in B,
and pays a missing owner
 y_i(B)=min{Tr(P_B U_i),G}, i not in B.
Here P_B is a BOREL MEASURABLE, retained-only choice of a PSD trace-one matrix
supported in the minimum eigenspace of U_B. It may read B, owner labels, and
all individual retained reports, but no missing owner's report. The same rule
and law are used on all profiles. Additional public randomization is allowed
by conditioning on its report-independent seed. If the mask law depends on
that seed, rho denotes its marginal law and all expectations over B below
include BOTH seed and mask randomness. The measurable restriction
is used, rather than suppressed, in the rotation average below.

For EVERY such rule,
 sup_(profiles with G>0) E_B[sum_i y_i(B)]/G >= sqrt(m/10)-4/5.       (T)
Thus its worst-profile funding order is Omega(sqrt(m)) with exactly n=m owners.
Together with the previously established O(sqrt(m)) capped-mask upper this
settles the ORDER for this architecture, not its leading constant. It is not
an unrestricted truthful-mechanism lower bound and does not settle the
original owner/resource gap. All witness profiles have zero standalone values,
so the same statement applies to a gain-normalized extension of this rule.

The conclusion also holds if the closed cap G is replaced by the maximum
coalition marginal V_i=max_(A subset N\{i})[F(U_A+U_i)-F(U_A)]. In the frame
family below, every proper coalition has value zero and the grand coalition
has value 1, hence V_i=G=1 for all owners. The junta argument uses only open
payments and therefore is unaffected by this cap replacement. It does not
follow for an arbitrary different cap or a different coverage decomposition.

1. A WEIGHTED ORTHOGONAL FRAME
Set L=100m. In a fixed orthonormal basis let
 U_1=e_1 e_1^T,     U_i=L e_i e_i^T for 2<=i<=m.                  (1)
All standalone values are zero and sum_i U_i=diag(1,L,...,L), hence G=1.
Apply the SAME orthogonal transformation Q to every owner's matrix. The
rotated profiles keep m actual owners, the same report domain, and G=1.
For the averaging argument Q is Haar-uniform in O(m), independently of B
and of any public seed. An adverse Q is selected only after averaging.

Fix a proper retained set B and write l=m-|B|>=1. The kernel K_B of U_B is
the l-dimensional span of the missing rotated basis vectors. Its eigenvalue
zero is minimal, so the retained-only selector P_B is supported on K_B.
Condition on the ORDERED, ORIENTED vectors (Qe_i : i in B). This reveals at
least as much as the retained matrices, and therefore fixes the selector.
Conditional on these vectors the missing columns are a Haar orthonormal
basis of K_B. In particular, each individual missing Qe_i is uniform on its
unit sphere. Their mutual dependence is immaterial to the following sum.

There are at least (l-1)^+ missing high-weight owners i>=2. This count handles
either inclusion status of owner 1. The case B=all owners has no closed
payment, and will be included using the lower bound (l-1)^+ with l=0.

2. SPHERICAL CAP ESTIMATE, INCLUDING MIXED SELECTORS
If V is uniform on the unit sphere in R^l and 0<=t<=1/2, then
                 P(|V_1|<t)<=2t sqrt(l).                       (2)
For l=1 the left side is zero. For l=2 the arcsine density on [-1/2,1/2]
is at most 2/(pi sqrt(3))<sqrt(2). For l>=3 the coordinate density is maximal
at zero and equals Gamma(l/2)/(sqrt(pi)Gamma((l-1)/2))<sqrt(l).
The gamma-ratio bound follows from E sqrt(Y)<=sqrt(EY) for a
Gamma((l-1)/2,1) random variable. Integrating over an interval of length 2t
proves (2). No asymptotic sphere approximation is used.

For any fixed unit v in K_B and a missing high-weight owner i,
 t=1/sqrt(L)<=1/2 and
 P(L(v dot Qe_i)^2<1 | retained oriented vectors)
       <=2 sqrt(l/L)<=1/5.                                   (3)
Since min{L(v dot Qe_i)^2,1} equals 1 on the complementary event,
 its conditional expectation is at least 4/5.
Write the fixed conditional selector as P_B=sum_a alpha_a v_a v_a^T,
where alpha_a>=0 and sum alpha_a=1. Concavity of x -> min{x,1} gives
 min{Tr(P_B U_i),1} >= sum_a alpha_a min{L(v_a dot Qe_i)^2,1}.
The same 4/5 lower bound follows for an arbitrary mixed PSD selector.
One may diagonalize separately after conditioning: no measurable eigenvector
choice is required to establish this invariant conditional inequality.

Let delta=E_(seed,B)[m-|B|]/m be the marginal mean missing fraction. Summing only eligible
high-weight owners, then averaging Q and B, gives
 E_Q E_B[closed payment] >= (4/5) E_B[(m-|B|-1)^+]
                         >= (4/5)(m delta-1).                 (4)
Boundedness and Borel measurability justify Fubini/conditional expectation.
All other payments are nonnegative, so the supremum over this rotated family
is at least the right side of (4). There is no claim to supply a closed-form
adverse rotation for every arbitrary measurable selector.

3. THE SAME LABEL SET HAS A JUNTA WITNESS FOR SMALL delta
Choose any integer 2<=J<=m. For an active owner set S of size J, partition the
coordinate identity into J nonzero mutually orthogonal projections:
 J-1 rank-one coordinate projections and their complementary projection.
Assign these to the J owners in S, in any order, and assign zero to the other
m-J actual owners. Each standalone minimum eigenvalue is zero, the full sum
is I, and G=1. A retained sum has positive minimum eigenvalue if and only if
it retains ALL J active owners. When it does, every active owner's deletion
marginal is 1. Therefore the open payment is exactly
                             J * 1_{S subset B}.              (5)
This identity is independent of which active owner has the larger projection
and independent of the retained-only selector.

Average S uniformly among all J-subsets, independently of B. For each fixed
B, a union bound (or Markov applied to |S intersect B^c|) gives
 P_S(S not subset B) <= E_S|S intersect B^c|=J(m-|B|)/m.
Consequently E_S P_B(S subset B)>=1-J delta. Hence at least one fixed active
label set S gives expected open payment at least
                              J(1-J delta).                   (6)
No exchangeability or independence of the mask coordinates was assumed.
The zero owners are retained as actual owners, with their original labels.

4. MINIMAX: A FIXED LAW MUST HANDLE BOTH FAMILIES
If delta<=1/(2m), choose J=m in (6), obtaining at least m/2, which is at least
the right side of (T).
If delta>=1/4, (4) gives m/5-4/5 >= sqrt(m/10)-4/5 for m>=4.
Otherwise J=floor(1/(2delta)) satisfies 2<=J<=m, J delta<=1/2, and
J>=1/(4delta). Equation (6) is then at least 1/(8delta).
Together with (4), the worst profile costs at least
 max{1/(8delta), (4/5)m delta-4/5}
 >= max{1/(8delta), (4/5)m delta}-4/5
 >= sqrt(m/10)-4/5.
This proves (T). The two profiles used in this minimax argument can differ:
a report-independent architecture must satisfy its guarantee at both.

5. SCOPE: PROFILEWISE COMMUTING IS NOT A PUBLIC DIAGONAL DOMAIN
Every matrix in the orthogonal-frame profile (1), and in each rotated version,
commutes with every other matrix at that same profile. The diagonalizing
basis, however, varies with Q across profiles and is not supplied publicly.
The retained reports need not reveal the missing subspace directions.
Therefore the theorem holds even when adverse profiles are individually
commuting, but it does NOT claim the same obstruction in one fixed public
commutative algebra. In a fixed public basis lambda_min is a minimum of m
known linear forms, and the finite-form methods yield substantially smaller
asymptotic funding. That is consistent: a good fixed-basis rule need not meet
the guarantee on all rotated profiles in the full PSD report domain.

The obstruction exploits a rule's common retained-only support and global
closed cap. It does not itself obstruct owner-specific or missing-report-aware
supports, a new coverage decomposition, or an altogether different truthful
mechanism. Changing a payment formula requires a fresh coverage and incentive
proof; it is not justified merely by this lower bound.

ANTECEDENTS, STATUS, AND CHECKS
The canonical scalar architecture lower bounds already allow arbitrary
correlated subset laws, but their exact forced-support technology has
2^n-1 essential linear pieces. Its obvious diagonal PSD lift has that matrix
order. The present direct construction has matrix order m=n and is not the
same parameterization. See PSD_MASK_ANTECEDENT_COMPARISON.txt for a precise
linear-pencil obstruction to a smaller exact lift.
The initial root proof used an almost-dependent rotated frame and mixtures
of iid masks. This note replaces that frame by weighted orthogonal reports
and extends the second witness by averaging owner labels, allowing all fixed
subset laws. Haar conditioning, the spherical estimate, concavity, and the
union bound are established tools. Priority of this application is unconfirmed.
