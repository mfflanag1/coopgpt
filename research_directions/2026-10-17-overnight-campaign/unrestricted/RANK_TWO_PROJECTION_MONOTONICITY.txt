RANK-TWO GENERATING SYSTEMS: RAW PROJECTION IS OWN-MONOTONE
2026-10-17. Proposed structural lemma; priority unassessed.

Let A be any fixed finite 0/1 matrix with nonempty rows of cardinality at most2.
For nonnegative RHS b let q(b) minimize(1/2)||x||^2 over x>=0, Ax>=b.
Fix coordinate i. If only RHS rows containing i increase, then q_i cannot
decrease. Consequently its maximum over arbitrary decreases of those rows
occurs at the full RHS. There is no need for an own-report envelope in this
fixed rank-two GENERATING-SYSTEM problem, regardless of owner count.

PROOF BY ACTIVE COMPONENTS.
It suffices to increase one row r containing i along a bounded interval.
The unique quadratic projection is continuous and piecewise affine in this
one scalar. For continuity, moving any feasible point by |delta| times the
all-ones vector makes it feasible after a RHS perturbation of size |delta|;
compact sublevels and strict convexity then give continuity of minimizers.
For piecewise affinity, there are finitely many active sets. On any region
with fixed active face, choose independent active equalities Bx=c spanning
the face. Projection optimality makes q perpendicular to its tangent space,
so q=B^T(BB^T)^(-1)c. Candidate faces and their multiplier-feasibility regions
are finite polyhedral data, giving a finite subdivision of the parameter line.
Active nonnegativity equalities are singleton anchors x_j=0.

On one open affine interval, a changed row absent from the active face has
zero effect. If it is active, an independent basis can contain it; a dependent
changed row could not remain active while all other RHS values stay fixed.
Interpret two-coordinate basis rows as graph edges and singleton rows as
anchors. Every connected independent component is exactly one of:
(a) an unanchored tree;
(b) a tree with one singleton anchor;
(c) an odd unicyclic graph with no anchor.
An even cycle is row-dependent. Two cycles, or one cycle plus an anchor, or
two anchors in a connected tree exceed the component's column rank.
Isolated columns not in the active basis have projection coordinate0.

If the changed row is the own singleton, its derivative at i is1. If it is
an edge ij, compute the derivative of q_i as follows.
(a) Deleting ij splits the tree into components of sizes a (containing i)
and b (containing j). Take the particular response alternating +/-1 from i
on its side and0 on the other side. Its orthogonal projection off the tree's
alternating null vector subtracts a/(a+b) at i. Thus dq_i/db_r=b/(a+b)>0.
(b) If the anchor is on i's side, dq_i/db_r=0. Otherwise the unchanged anchored
side has zero derivative and the edge equality gives dq_i/db_r=1.
(c) For a branch edge the same0-or1 argument applies, with the cycle serving
as the anchor. For a cycle edge, the odd cycle equations give derivative1/2
at both of its endpoints.
Every derivative is nonnegative. Continuity across the finitely many affine
seams proves the one-row assertion; increasing the finitely many own RHS
coordinates sequentially proves the theorem.

STATIC FUNDING CONSEQUENCE.
More generally, for a fixed generating matrix of maximum row cardinality r,
its minimum-norm cover q has total payment at most rT, where
 T=min{sum_i x_i:x>=0,Ax>=b}.
Indeed KKT gives q=A^T lambda with lambda>=0 and positive lambda only on tight
rows. Explicitly the Lagrangian is (1/2)||q||^2-lambda*(Aq-b)-nu*q,
so stationarity is q=A^T lambda+nu. Nonnegativity multipliers vanish: at a
positive coordinate complementarity makes them0, and at a zero coordinate
this stationarity identity and A,lambda,nu>=0 force them0 as well.
For each positive-multiplier row S define omega_S=lambda_S/q(S); set it0
otherwise. Every denominator is positive: lambda_S>0 implies q_j>=lambda_S
for every j in S, hence q(S)>=|S|lambda_S>0. For q_i>0,
 sum_{S containing i}omega_S <=sum_{S containing i}lambda_S/q_i=1;
for q_i=0 the sum is0. Thus omega is feasible for the L1-cover dual and
 T>=sum_S b_S omega_S=sum_S lambda_S.
Therefore sum_i q_i=sum_S |S|lambda_S<=rT.
For rank2 this combines with own monotonicity to give a factor2 rule on any
local monotone report array whose cover systems have these fixed generators.
The factor2 cannot be improved uniformly for this raw-projection architecture:
a star with m leaves and unit edge demands hasT=1, q_center=m/(m+1), each
leaf1/(m+1), and total2m/(m+1)->2 as m grows.

SCOPE AND CONNECTION TO THE INTERIOR OBSTRUCTION.
The fixed generating system is essential. A full coalition game can have a
pair-generated cover polyhedron, but after lowering some pair demands its
previously redundant LARGER coalition rows may become binding. This theorem
does not license deleting those rows or assert monotonicity for that broader
local-relaxation problem. A production-model consequence requires proving
that the same rank-two generators remain sufficient at EVERY report profile,
with only incident generator demands changing under an own report.

PROJECTION_ENDPOINT_OBSTRUCTION.txt has rows of size3 and gives a strict
interior-relaxation improvement. Thus allowing row size3 is already enough
to invalidate the rank-two monotonicity/endpoint simplification. This sharp
arity boundary is a geometric stopping result, not a new general subsidy
bound for unrestricted production functions.

ESTABLISHED-INGREDIENT CAUTION.
A factor-r funding guarantee for fixed rank-r generating systems also follows
routinely from grand marginals: delete from an optimal L1-cover dual every row
incident to i to bound T-T_-i by their dual weight, then sum over i. Those
marginals dominate an efficient core and are own-monotone for a local report
array. Therefore factor2 alone is not the proposed contribution here. The
specific geometric fact proved above is monotonicity of the RAW quadratic
projection at rank2, with an explicit rank3 boundary obstruction.
