RADIUS-TWO UNIFORM THREE-OWNER POOLS DO NOT BEAT TWO
2026-10-17. Bounded-target stopping lemma, not a general theorem.

Fix k>=3 pure resource pools with three fixed owners per pool, F=min_s c_s.
At the hub every owner reports1. On the restricted domain at most two owners
report sigma instead, where0<sigma<1 is one fixed number. There is an explicit
covering individual-IC rule of factor at most5/3, uniformly in k. Thus this
natural radius-two family cannot supply a >2 unrestricted lower.

RULE.
At the hub pay every owner1/k; total3, equal to grand worth.
At a one-low profile, pay that owner0, its two same-pool high owners
 y=max{sigma,1-sigma}, and every outside high owner z=sigma/(k-1).
At a two-low profile in the SAME pool, pay that pool's two lows sigma each
and its remaining high owner1; pay all other owners0. This is efficient.
At a two-low profile in DIFFERENT pools, pay the two lows0, each of the four
high owners in those affected pools (1-sigma)/2, and every owner in any
unaffected pool sigma/(k-2). This is efficient.

COVERAGE OF THE TWO NONTRIVIAL CASES.
At one-low profiles let t>0 be a coalition's production. An outside pool must
supply ceil(t) unit owners, hence all outside pools together contribute at
least ceil(t)*sigma in payments. The affected pool chooses a in{0,1} low
and b in{0,1,2} high owners, with t<=a*sigma+b; its payment is b*y.
When y=1-sigma, direct checking of the five positive capacity breakpoints
sigma,1,1+sigma,2,2+sigma gives b*y+ceil(t)*sigma>=t.
Larger y preserves coverage.
At two different affected pools the same check gives for either affected
pool the relevant minimal high count b. Both affected pools together pay
b*(1-sigma), and the unaffected pools pay ceil(t)*sigma. The same five
breakpoint inequalities apply. If the two affected coalitions use different
choices, each separately has high count at least the common minimal b, so
the same lower bound applies. Grand total is
 4*(1-sigma)/2+3*sigma=2+sigma=T.

INCENTIVES.
The first owner lowering receives0 instead of its hub payment1/k.
When a second owner in the same pool lowers, its new sigma payment is no
larger than y at the one-low parent. When a second owner in a different pool
lowers, its new payment0 is no larger than its previous outside payment.
These exhaust own-report comparisons in the two-level radius-two domain.
No ownership or coalition is replaced along a comparison.

BUDGET.
Only one-low profiles can cost more than grand worth:
 (2*max{sigma,1-sigma}+3*sigma)/(2+sigma)
 =1 for sigma<=1/2, and5*sigma/(2+sigma)<=5/3 otherwise.
No claim about arbitrary report levels, mixed owners, or radius>=3 follows.
This result is a stopping certificate for the specific candidate only.

COROLLARY: ALL EQUAL POOL SIZES HAVE FACTOR AT MOST TWO ON RADIUS TWO.
Allow any common number m of owners per pool, all hub reports1 and the same
fixed low report sigma. For m=1 a single fixed pool owner is a veto owner,
so paying it grand worth is efficient. For m=2, pay just the two owners of
one fixed pool, each min{its reported capacity,T}; this covers and is own-
monotone, with total at most2T on every profile, even outside this domain.
For m>=4 use the same capped-capacity rule on one fixed m-owner pool.
Its total is at most m, while at most two altered owners imply
 T>=m-2+2*sigma.
Thus its factor is at most m/(m-2+2*sigma)<=2.
The case m=3,k>=3 is the construction above. For k=2 retain that construction,
except at the profile with lows in different pools use the average of the
two pool-capacity core covers: each low gets sigma/2, each high1/2. It is
covered and efficient, and each low's sigma/2 is at most its parent outside
payment sigma. For k=1 production is additive and capacity payments suffice.
This closes the entire homogeneous equal-pool two-level radius-two family,
with arbitrarily growing owner and pool counts, as a >2 lower-bound route.
