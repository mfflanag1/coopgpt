ONE-HUB UNILATERAL REPORT STARS HAVE SHARP FUNDING FACTOR TWO
2026-10-17. Original individual-IC model; structural stopping theorem.

SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS.
Fix actual owners N and a full report profile b for any monotone concave
homogeneous production function F. Consider any collection D consisting
of b and profiles obtained by decreasing the report of JUST ONE owner
from b, including arbitrary continuous decreases or multiple lower profiles
of the same owner. Only own-report incentive comparisons whose endpoints
belong to D are required. The theorem also holds for abstract nonnegative
monotone totally balanced games on this profile star, with coalition values
nondecreasing in reports and unchanged off the reporting owner.
There is a nonnegative covering rule on this ENTIRE domain D, own-monotone
on every admissible comparison, whose total is at most twice grand worth.
Thus no valid finite transition certificate confined to such a star can
prove a subsidy factor greater than2. This does not construct one global
all-profile mechanism: the selected pivot and core depend on the hub.

UPPER PROOF.
At the full profile choose an owner j minimizing
 t=v_b(N\{j}).
The restricted off-j game is balanced. Choose any nonnegative efficient
core c on N\{j}, so sum_(h!=j)c_h=t and c(S)>=v_b(S) for every S not
containing j. At EVERY profile p in D put
 y_j(p)=T_p,   y_h(p)=c_h for h!=j,
where T_p=v_p(N).
A coalition containing j is covered by y_j alone because v_p(S)<=T_p.
A coalition not containing j is covered by c because v_p(S)<=v_b(S).
For the full profile, T_p>=t. At a lower profile differing in owner i,
 T_p>=v_p(N\{i})=v_b(N\{i})>=t.
Hence total y(p)=T_p+t<=2T_p. This includes zero-worth profiles: if one
exists then t=0 and c=0, so total payments there are zero.
Every nonpivot owner's own payment is constant over D. The pivot's payment
is grand worth, which is nondecreasing in its own report. This verifies all
own-report comparisons, including chains of lower reports by one owner.
The case n=1 uses the empty restricted core and is efficient.

EXACT MATCHING LOWER FAMILY.
For integers k,m>=2 take k pools and m fixed owners per pool, with
 F(c)=min_s c_s. At the hub one owner H_s in each pool reports m, and its
m-1 low owners each report1. Hub grand worth is2m-1. There are k lower
profiles, the i-th reducing ONLY H_i to1; its grand worth is m.
Suppose a covering own-monotone rule on this star has funding at most kappa.
At the i-th low profile write h_i for H_i's payment and B_i for total
payment to low owners outside pool i.
The coalition containing all m owners of pool i and the high owner in every
other pool has worth m and is disjoint from B_i. Thus
 kappa*m >=m+B_i.
Averaging the worth1 coalitions consisting of H_i plus one low owner from
each other pool gives B_i>=(m-1)(1-h_i), hence
 h_i >=[2m-1-kappa*m]/(m-1).
This remains a valid inequality even if its right side is negative.
Own monotonicity carries these k lower bounds to the hub. Its all-low
coalition has worth m-1, so the hub budget implies
 kappa*(2m-1) >= k*[2m-1-kappa*m]/(m-1)+(m-1).
Rearranging yields the exact lower bound
 kappa >= [k*(2m-1)+(m-1)^2] / [(2m-1)*(m-1)+k*m].             (SL)
For fixed m, this tends to(2m-1)/m as k tends to infinity. Then m tending
to infinity proves that the uniform factor2 cannot be improved. For example,
choosing k=m^3 gives a single growing finite family with factor tending to2.
Only one common hub and k actual unilateral transitions are used.

RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE.
A star search cannot establish an unrestricted lower diverging with owner
or resource count, even if its games are general totally balanced production
games, its owner bundles are mixed, and its local underreports are continuous.
An unbounded lower must introduce profiles that are not all unilateral
underreports of one common hub, or impose strictly stronger incentives.
This is stronger in scope than the prior multi-glove single-sink certificate
barrier. It leaves the existing interacting-profile lower constructions open.

BOUNDED-RADIUS EXTENSION: ARBITRARY DIRECTIONS, NOT CLAIMED SHARP.
For any fixed hub b and 0<=r<=n, let D be ANY report domain in which at
most r actual owners' bundles differ from b. Changes may increase, decrease,
or be coordinatewise incomparable with the corresponding hub bundle. There
is a covering individual-IC rule on D with funding factor at most r+1.

First recall the downward construction. On an owner set L whose reports
are all <= their hub reports and at most s of which change, choose an s-set
J minimizing t=v_b(L\J), and an efficient nonnegative core c of this hub
subgame. Pay R=v_p(L) to each j in J, and c_h to h in L\J. Coalitions
meeting J are covered by one R; the others are covered by c. Padding the
actual changed set to size s gives R>=t. Budget is sR+t<=(s+1)R. Every
payment is at most R and is own-monotone. This includes s=0: J is empty
and the unaltered hub core is paid.

For the general profile p, define
 P(p)={i: b_i(p) is NOT componentwise <= b_i(hub)}.
Write p0=|P|<=r, L=N\P, and s=r-p0. For EACH possible label-set P once
and for all choose the downward construction on L with radius s: its pivot
set J_P and off-pivot core c_P depend only on P and the fixed hub, never
on current reports. Pay T_p to every i in P. To L apply that construction
using residual grand worth R_p=v_p(L). Since at most s remaining owners
changed and all their bundles are below the hub, its budget is at most
(s+1)R_p. Total payment therefore is
 p0*T_p+(r-p0+1)R_p <=(r+1)T_p.
Coverage holds for coalitions meeting P by one T_p payment, and for all
other coalitions by the downward construction.

Own incentives across label-set changes require checking. If i stays
outside P during an own increase, the label-set P is fixed, so the same
own-monotone downward rule applies. If i is already in P, it remains there
under an own increase and receives the nondecreasing full grand worth.
If i ENTERS P, its previous payment was at most R_old<=T_old<=T_new,
while its new payment is T_new. Thus the jump is monotone as well. These
are all cases, including bundles incomparable with the hub. Zero residual
or full production is covered by the same nonnegative bounds.

For r=1 the earlier matching lower proves the factor2 is sharp even on
downward stars. For r>=2 sharpness is unresolved. The result shows that
any family of valid certificates with diverging lower factor must have
unbounded owner-Hamming radius around EVERY possible fixed hub. In fact a
finite certificate lying in radius r cannot prove factor greater than r+1.
This is a restriction on the number of simultaneously changed ACTUAL owners,
not on resource dimension, number of forms, number of report levels, or
number of profiles. It does not assume that changed-owner identities are
fixed across profiles.
