UNBOUNDED COALITION-COVERAGE FAILURE OF SQUARE-ROOT SHAPLEY PAYMENTS
2026-10-17. Exact architecture obstruction, original individual-IC domain.

For a production game v_b(S)=F(sum_{i in S} b_i), let T=v_b(N) and phi_i its
Shapley value. The tempting rule y_i=sqrt(T phi_i) is nonnegative and individually
own-report monotone, and sum y_i <= sqrt(n)T. Nevertheless it fails coverage by
an arbitrarily large multiplicative factor, even on pure fixed-owner profiles
for finite monotone concave homogeneous production functions.

CONSTRUCTION.
Use one veto resource 0, k distinguished resources A, and m outside resources B.
For 1<=ell<=m, define
 F(c)=min{c_0, min_{i in A}c_i + L_{m-ell+1}(c_B)},
where L_h is the sum of the h smallest coordinates (equivalently the minimum
sum over all h-element subsets). This is finite, nonnegative, monotone, concave
and homogeneous. Each of the n=1+k+m actual fixed owners owns one distinct
resource and reports one unit of it. Thus T=1, and a coalition has value 1 iff
it contains the veto owner and either all k owners in A or at least ell in B.
All other coalition values are 0. Every subgame has an efficient nonnegative
core: allocate its entire value to the veto owner when present. The example is
therefore genuinely totally balanced and comes with an explicit production F.

EXACT SHAPLEY FORMULAS.
Assign independent uniform arrival times. Let Q be the ell-th order statistic
of the m outside arrival times, so Q~Beta(ell,m-ell+1), E Q=ell/(m+1), and
 D=E Q^(k+1)=product_{j=0}^k (ell+j)/(m+1+j).
For each i in A, it is pivotal exactly when all other A and the veto arrived
before its time u and the outside threshold has not yet arrived. Therefore
 phi_i=integral_0^1 u^k P(Q>=u)du=D/(k+1).
The veto's marginal is 1 when either all A or the outside threshold already
arrived, so
 phi_0=1-E Q+D/(k+1)=1-ell/(m+1)+D/(k+1).
The target coalition S={0} union A has value 1 but receives
 R(k,m,ell)=sqrt(1-ell/(m+1)+D/(k+1))+k sqrt(D/(k+1)).

SMALL EXACT FAILURE.
For k=2,m=40,ell=5, D=5/1763, phi_0=4649/5289, phi_i=5/5289 on A.
Thus R=sqrt(4649/5289)+2sqrt(5/5289) approximately 0.999040231535<1.
For a=phi_0,b=phi_i, d=1-a-4b=620/5289>0 and
 d^2-16ab=4160/9324507>0, an entirely rational certificate that R<1.

UNBOUNDED FAILURE.
For integer r>=5 set k=2^r, m=k^2, ell=k^2-4rk>0. Each factor in D is at most
 (ell+k)/(m+1+k)=1-(4rk+1)/(k^2+k+1).
Hence
 D <= exp(-(4rk+1)(k+1)/(k^2+k+1)) <= exp(-3r) < 2^(-3r)=k^(-3).
The elementary middle inequality follows after multiplying by the positive
denominator: r k^2+r k+k+1-3r>0. Also
 1-ell/(m+1)=(4rk+1)/(k^2+1) <= (4r+1)/k.
Consequently
 R <= sqrt((4r+1)/k+k^(-4))+1/k -> 0.
No universal constant multiplication of sqrt(T phi_i) repairs coalition
coverage in the unrestricted production class. This is an architecture
obstruction, not a lower bound on all truthful covering mechanisms. In this
particular pure-ownership veto family, paying the veto owner T already gives
an efficient truthful cover, underscoring the distinction.

A RELATED OVERSTRONG SUFFICIENT CLAIM ALSO FAILS.
In the separate family F(c)=min{c_0, sum_{g=1}^t min_{j in group g}c_j}, with t
leaf groups of size t and all unit pure owners, the minimum-norm covering
allocation gives the center x_0=1/2. Its Shapley value is
 phi_0=integral_0^1 [1-(1-u^t)^t]du ~ (log t)/t.
For t=16 the exact checker already gives phi_0<1/4=x_0^2/T. Therefore pointwise
projection-envelope domination M_i^2<=T phi_i is false as well (M_0>=x_0).

The exact checker verifies the 43-owner certificate, the rational moments and
bounds in several growing instances, and the separate pointwise obstruction.
The asymptotic proof is the displayed inequality, not finite sampling.
Novelty of this negative architecture result has not been assessed.
