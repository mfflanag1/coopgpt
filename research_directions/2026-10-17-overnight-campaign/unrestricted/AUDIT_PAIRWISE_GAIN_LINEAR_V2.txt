REQUIRED CLARIFICATION: CANCELLING MERGES.
Section4 currently says that when a merge removes two active coordinates one
may simply use the smaller previous active-owner error bound. That is not
literally justified: the merged owner is then NULL, and the inductive hypothesis
was stated only for active owners. The merged h-payment can be positive.

Correct case analysis:
* If z_i+z_j!=0, the merged coordinate is active, and the stated induction
  gives pair error at most E_(r-1)+epsilon.
* If z_i+z_j=0 and r=3, the only remaining active coordinate equals total2.
  The merged profile has G=0, so BOTH new null payments are exactly zero.
  The pair error is zero.
* If z_i+z_j=0 and r>=4, each of the two new null payments lies in[0,epsilon].
  The Banzhaf merged marginal is exactly0, so the pair error is at most
  2epsilon. Since E_(r-1)>=E3=3epsilon/2, this is at most E_(r-1)+epsilon.

Thus E2=0 and E_r=(3/2)(E_(r-1)+epsilon) are valid without changing342.
This is a repairable proof-detail issue, not a counterexample to the theorem.

TRANSLATION/SYMMETRY DEPENDENCY CHECK.
The compact feasible product consists of intervals[f_i,f_i+KG], not intervals
whose lengths grow under translation. Diagonal resource additions raise every
coalition's value and each owner's standalone value by the corresponding
added scalar, while leaving G unchanged. Hence the affine shift operators
preserve all finite constraints and have a common fixed point by finite
rectangular averages and compactness. No integration of a nonmeasurable rule
is used. Finite owner/global-resource symmetrization preserves the fixed-point
condition, gain budget, coverage, and protected deviations.

The signed-imbalance restoration argument really compares feasible reports
of the SAME n actual owners. It permits both signs of signed changes by adding
nonnegative physical resources on the appropriate side and cancelling the
diagonal translation. This is why mixed eligibility and the full unbounded
orthant are essential. It does not assume owners can transfer identities.

TWO-OWNER POTENTIAL CHECK.
For total2, pair neutrality compares(x,2-x) with(2,0), with all other reports
zero. The latter has G=0, so the active pair's h-sum is0. Owner symmetry gives
q(x)+q(2-x)=0, and no-gain profiles give q=0 on[0,2]. Null payments at other
profiles may be positive, but they do not enter this pair-sum equality.

independent of grand efficiency and independent of null payments.

FINITE MERGER CHECK.
Every merger preserves total2, cannot increase total negative mass, and cannot
increase active count. Hence all intermediary profiles stay in the class
r<=5, negative mass<=2. At pure residual profiles all standalone values vanish,
and anonymity plus the gain budget bounds each null payment by
epsilon=2K/(n-5). It is not assumed that outsiders' payments stay fixed.

The Banzhaf pair identity follows by expanding over subsets of outsiders;
the two intermediate q-terms cancel. The merged marginal is exactly zero when
the merged coordinate is zero. For r=2, complementarity and q(0)=q(2)=0 make
the Banzhaf coordinate exactly q(z_i). The preceding null-case clarification
therefore completes a uniform induction over all chosen profiles.

EXACT FIVE-OWNER CONTRADICTION.
For z=(2,-1,-1,1,1), the coalition's actual h-total must be nonnegative because
its signed total is0. Expanding the three Banzhaf marginals and collecting
formal q-coefficients, then using q=0 on[0,2] and q(-x)=-q(2+x), leaves
exactly -q(4)/8. Thus its potential total is at most-1/8. Three individual
errors of size E5 must cover that deficit. Since E5=57epsilon/8,
1/8<=171epsilon/8=(171/4)K/(n-5), giving K>=(n-5)/342.
This finite contradiction does not use the qualitative population limit.

QUALITATIVE COMPANION.
Its population-padding compactness construction is consistent with the finite
