EXACT FIVE-OWNER SEPARATION BETWEEN PAIRWISE AND THREE-WAY PRODUCTION
17 October 2026. Candidate finite lower certificate; independent audit pending.

CLAIM
For F(c)=min(c_A,c_B,c_C), FIVE fixed actual owners, all eligible to report
all three resources, every nonnegative, covering, TCWM rule has worst-case
funding factor at least103/64 >3/2. Only reports in {0,1}^3 are needed.
TCWM means every coalition's total payment is nondecreasing in any coordinate
of any coalition member. This is stronger than individual truthfulness.
The factor is NOT claimed for unrestricted individually truthful mechanisms.

Together with MIXED_MULTIGLOVE_TCWM.txt this places the optimum for this
three-resource technology and strong incentive class in [103/64,2].
In particular a factor3/2 theorem for all rank-three packing systems with
TCWM is impossible: the single-activity three-resource system already fails.
The graph theorem's factor3/2 therefore cannot extend in that same class.

FIXED OWNERS AND SYMMETRIZATION
Average a purported rule over all permutations of its five actual owner
labels. This finite report-independent average preserves all hypotheses,
including every TCWM comparison and the worst-case budget. It is therefore
enough to prove the lower for a permutation-invariant rule.
Encode an owner's binary report as t in{0,...,7}; its e-th coordinate is
(t>>e)&1. A profile is a histogram c=(c_0,...,c_7) with sum c_t=5. Let
Y(c,t) denote the payment to each actual owner of type t, when c_t>0.
The labels are fixed throughout each comparison. Histogram notation merely
uses symmetry; it does not create owners, merge owners, or transfer reports.
For a coalition histogram k, with0<=k_t<=c_t, define
 V(k)=min_e sum_{t:e-bit=1} k_t.
Every listed coalition can be selected among the actual owners at its profile.

THREE VALID ROW TYPES
All rows are expressed with their left-hand side <= right-hand side:
 Budget(c): sum_t c_t Y(c,t) - kappa V(c) <=0.
 Cover(c,k): -sum_t k_t Y(c,t) <=-V(k).
 Mono(c,t,e,k): sum_j k_j Y(c,j) -sum_j k'_j Y(c',j) <=0,
 where the changing actual owner has type t, t's e-bit is0, k_t>=1,
 c'=c-unit_t+unit_(t|2^e), k'=k-unit_t+unit_(t|2^e).
The coalition includes the changing owner and is otherwise identical.

CERTIFICATE
rank3_tcwm_5_exact_certificate.json lists49 such rows with exact nonnegative
rational weights. Multiplying and adding them gives coefficient -1 on kappa,
right-hand side -103/64, and nonnegative coefficients on every payment.
Nonnegative payments therefore imply kappa>=103/64. No numerical optimizer
is needed to verify this implication. The standard-library-only checker
 verify_tcwm_certificate.py rank3_tcwm_5_exact_certificate.json
reconstructs every row from the definitions, checks fixed owner counts and
coalition validity, adds all coefficients as Fraction, and verifies this
exact inequality. It also verifies a separate four-owner27-row lower3/2.

DISCOVERY STATUS
rank3_tcwm_lp.py generated a finite necessary-condition LP over all792
five-owner binary profile histograms (2641variables,54423rows). HiGHS returned
