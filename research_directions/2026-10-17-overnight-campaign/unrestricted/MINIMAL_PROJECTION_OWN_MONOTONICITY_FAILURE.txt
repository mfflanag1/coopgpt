A THREE-OWNER EXACT FAILURE OF RAW MINIMUM-NORM OWN MONOTONICITY
2026-10-17. Smallest possible owner count; architecture diagnostic.

Use four resources (c1,c2,c3,z) and production
 F=min(c1,c2+c3)+(1/2)min(z,c1,c2,c3).
It is nonnegative, monotone, concave and homogeneous, with a finite nonnegative
min-linear representation (distribute the sum over the two finite minima).
Owner1 reports (c1,z)=(1,t), while owner2 owns one unit of c2 and owner3 one
unit of c3. Compare t=0 and t=1; every other report is unchanged.

At t=0 the only positive coalition values are v12=v13=v123=1. The cover
polyhedron is x>=0, x1+x2>=1, x1+x3>=1 (the grand row is redundant).
Its minimum-Euclidean-norm point is x=(2/3,1/3,1/3): the two active row
multipliers are each1/3, so x=A^T lambda, with complementary slackness.

At t=1 the pair values remain1 and only the grand value rises, to3/2.
The new projection is (1/2,1/2,1/2). It is feasible; every feasible vector
has sum at least3/2, hence squared norm at least(3/2)^2/3=3/4, attained here.
Owner1's report increase therefore decreases its raw projected payment from
2/3 to1/2. This is a true fixed-owner production example, not an arbitrary
row perturbation. The lower report attains a point in the full profile's
own-report monotone envelope with coordinate at least2/3.

WHY THREE OWNERS IS MINIMAL.
For two owners write a=v({1}), b=v({2}), T=v({1,2}). Production gives
T>=a+b>=0. The cover projection's first coordinate is
 x1=max{a,min{T/2,T-b}}.
This follows by projecting onto x1>=a,x2>=b,x1+x2>=T. Under an own-report
increase by owner1, a and T cannot decrease and b is fixed; the formula is
therefore nondecreasing. The same holds for owner2, and one owner is trivial.
Thus no one- or two-owner production example can exhibit this failure.

This is not an unrestricted mechanism lower bound: both displayed profiles
have owner1 as a veto owner, and the veto-profile stopping lemma supplies an
efficient individually truthful cover throughout any all-veto profile domain.
