KEEPING THE GRAPH HUBER THRESHOLD ALSO FAILS AT ARITY THREE
17 October 2026. Exact incentive obstruction; independently check before use.

This uses the original graph regularizer h(p)=p^2 for p<=1/2 and p-1/4
above1/2. Thus it is distinct from moving the threshold to1/r. All optimizer
prices at both witness profiles are STRICTLY below1/2, so tie-breaking and
the quadratic/linear seam play no role.

Fix seven resource vertices0,...,6 and six unit-profit activities:
 e0={0,3,6}, e1={0,3,5}, e2={0,5,6},
 e3={0,1,2}, e4={1,4,5}, e5={1,3,6}.
Each activity consumes one unit at its three listed resources. Let A be the
6x7 edge-incidence matrix. There are exactly TWO actual owners, each allowed
its fixed listed resources:
 owner I: b=(0,0,1,3,1,3,3),
 owner II: a=(4,3,0,0,0,0,0).
Aggregate c=(4,3,1,3,1,3,3), equal to resource degrees.

At this profile the unique price minimizer is p=(1/3,...,1/3).
Every edge is tight, and edge multipliers lambda=(2/3,...,2/3) satisfy
 A^T lambda=2 diag(c)p. These are sufficient KKT conditions for the convex
program; all prices are interior in the strictly convex quadratic region.
Any other minimizer would contradict strict convexity at these coordinates.
Owner I's payment is11/3.

Now INCREASE ONLY owner I's resource2 report from1 to2. The new aggregate
is c'=(4,3,2,3,1,3,3). Its unique minimizer is
 p'=(13,13,9,11,11,11,11)/35.
Every edge again sums to1. The positive multipliers
 lambda'=(24,22,22,36,22,20)/35
satisfy A^T lambda'=2 diag(c')p'. All coordinates remain below1/2.
Owner I's new payment is
  (2*9+(3+1+3+3)*11)/35 =128/35 =11/3-1/105.
Increasing an actual owned capacity reduces that owner's payment. Equivalently,
underreporting capacity2 back to1 is strictly profitable. The other owner's
bundle, identities, eligibility, technology, and all other reports are fixed.

STRUCTURAL DIAGNOSIS
The active incidence matrix has one-dimensional kernel spanned by
 z=(-2,-2,4,1,1,1,1).
For c(s)=c+s e2 with s near0, the all-tight quadratic optimizer is
 p(s)=1/3*1+t(s)z,
 t(s)=-4s/[3(54+16s)].
Owner I's payment changes EXACTLY by -s/[3(27+8s)].
This comes from positive-part mass of the weighted tangent projection, not
from price ties or changing active components. At c the row for coordinate2
is (-16,-12,8,6,2,6,6)/27, whose positive part sums to28/27>1.
The graph proof succeeds because each active bipartite component has only
two opposite price directions; the rank-three component admits a direction
with unequal magnitudes and an excessive positive projection sum.

This rejects the graph Huber rule on arbitrary triple-resource production.
It is NOT an impossibility for all eligible rank-three mechanisms or an
unrestricted owner-count/dimension lower bound.
