OWN-ENVELOPE INFLATION IS UNBOUNDED COORDINATEWISE; SHARED WITNESSES DAMP IT
2026-10-17. Exact analytic obstruction, not an aggregate counterexample.

1. ONE FOCAL OWNER: INFLATION OF ORDER SQUARE ROOT OF OWNER COUNT.
Let t>=2, m=t^2, k=t, epsilon=1/m^2. Owners are focal i, center o, and
m leaves. Primitive full-cover constraints are
 x_o+x_l>=1 for each leaf l;
 x_i+x_o+sum_l x_l>=B;
 x_i+sum_(l in A)x_l>=b,
where A has k leaves. Put
 u=[1+(m-1)epsilon]/(m+1),
 B=epsilon+1+(m-1)u,  b=epsilon+k*u.
The full minimum-norm cover is
 q_i=epsilon, q_o=1-u, q_l=u.
A KKT certificate gives multiplier epsilon to the grand row, u-epsilon
(positive) to every pair row, and zero to the A-row. Every displayed row
is tight; q=A^T lambda follows from(m+1)u-(m-1)epsilon=1. In particular
q sums to B, proving the full minimum l1 cover value T=B.

Lower ONLY the focal grand-row demand B to0. Keep the A-row and every
pair row unchanged. The lower projection is
 s=q'_i=[m+1+k(m-1)]*epsilon/[k^2+m+1],
 v=q'_l=(b-s)/k, q'_o=1-v.
KKT multipliers are s on the A-row, v-s on selected pair rows, and v on
unselected pair rows. All are nonnegative for the specified t, and
 (m+1)v-k*s=1.
The grand row has zero multiplier and is redundant after its reduction.
Thus
 q'_i/q_i=[t^2+1+t(t^2-1)]/[2t^2+1] ~ t/2.
There is no dimension-independent constant C with M_i<=C*q_i(full).
The actual own envelope M_i is at least q'_i. The total conjecture is NOT
refuted: epsilon is small and these focal payments are small in absolute
terms. This only rules out controlling every envelope coordinate by a
constant multiple of its full-profile projection coordinate.

POSITIVE-MULTIPLIER CHECK.
Writing D=k^2+m+1, the equations give
 v=[1+k*s]/(m+1).
Hence v-s=[1-(m+1-k)s]/(m+1). For m=t^2,k=t and epsilon=t^-4,
 s< (t+1)/(2*t^4), and(m+1-k)*(t+1)<2*t^4 for t>=2.
Therefore v-s>0. Also u-epsilon=(1-2epsilon)/(m+1)>0.

2. GENUINE FIXED-OWNER PRODUCTION REALIZATION.
Let K_H and K_L be the nonnegative primitive full and lower cover
polyhedra above. Define F_H(c)=min_(x in K_H) x.c and likewise F_L.
These are finite minima of nonnegative linear forms (use their vertices).
Their indicator-coalition games are the totally balanced closures, with
exactly the same cover polyhedra and hence the same projections.
They coincide whenever c_i=0: the focal coordinate can satisfy the one
extra grand row at zero objective cost. Let
 F(c,z)=min{F_H(c), F_L(c)+B*z}.
Give owner i its base unit vector plus control z=1; all other owners get
their base unit vectors. At the high profile the indicator coalition game
is F_H: for coalitions containing i, B bounds every high coalition value;
for coalitions excluding i the two functions already coincide. Lowering
ONLY i's control to0 gives F_L. This is one fixed monotone concave
homogeneous production function on n+1 resources with n=m+2 actual owners.
No owner cloning, reassignment, or changing technology is used.

3. WHY A NAIVE SHARED-STAR REPLICATION DOES NOT AGGREGATE THE INFLATION.
Take M focal owners instead, each with its own row
 x_j+x_o+sum_l x_l>=G
and its own A-row x_j+sum_(l in A)x_l>=b. They share the same center/leaves.
For sufficiently small epsilon>0, the full KKT solution has all focal
coordinates epsilon, leaves u=[1+(m-1)M*epsilon]/(m+1), center1-u,
G=epsilon+1+(m-1)u, and b=epsilon+k*u.
Lower only focal i's first row. All OTHER focal rows are off-i constraints
and must remain full. In the exact positive-multiplier regime put
 D=k^2+(M-1)(m-1)^2+(m+1),
 delta=(m-1-k)*epsilon/D,
 s=epsilon+k*delta, a=epsilon+(m-1)*delta, v=u-delta.
The lowered focal receives s, every other focal a, leaves v, center1-v.
The changed focal's A-row is tight; other A-rows become slack. All other
focal first rows stay tight. KKT multipliers are s on the changed A-row,
a on each unchanged focal first row, and
 v-(M-1)*a-s on selected pair rows,
 v-(M-1)*a on unselected pair rows.
They are positive for sufficiently small epsilon, since at epsilon=0 the
pair multipliers equal1/(m+1), and all formulas are continuous.
Stationarity reduces exactly to
 (m+1)v-(M-1)(m-1)a-k*s=1.
When M>=2 and 0<=k<=m-1,
 s/epsilon=1+k(m-1-k)/D <=1+1/[4(M-1)].
Thus sharing the same off-owner star with even one other focal blocks the
unbounded amplification mechanism. This is a concrete instance of the
cross-owner locality that a general aggregate proof must capture. It is
only a theorem for this structured family, not a proof of the diagonal
conjecture or an assertion that all replication schemes fail.
