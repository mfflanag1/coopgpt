LINEAR COALITION-VALUE RULES CANNOT GIVE SUBLINEAR TCWM FUNDING
17 October 2026. Candidate structural theorem; independent audit required.

STATEMENT
For every n>=2 there is ONE finite-dimensional, nonnegative monotone concave
homogeneous production technology F such that every eligible TCWM mechanism
of the form
                 y_i(b)=sum_{empty!=S subset N} c_{i,S} v_b(S),
                 v_b(S)=F(sum_{j in S}b_j),
with real coefficients fixed across all reports, has worst-case total-funding
factor at least
                         (n-1)/2+1/n.                       (1)
All actual owners are fixed and fully eligible for all resources. The coefficients
may depend arbitrarily on F and n, may be signed, and need not be anonymous.
The statement is about a fixed linear transformation of the ENTIRE coalition-
worth vector; it therefore includes fixed semivalues and linear corrections.
It does not restrict nonlinear rules or mechanisms using reports beyond v_b.
Affine constants are also covered when finite-factor funding and nonnegative
payments are imposed: the zero profile forces each constant to be zero.

The routine TCWM rule [v_b(N)+v_b({i})]/2 is linear in these values and has
factor at most(n+1)/2. Thus the best uniform rate within this broad linear
architecture is Theta(n), with asymptotic coefficient1/2. A general o(n)
TCWM upper must be nonlinear in the coalition-worth vector, or use additional
report/ownership information. Historical priority is not yet established.

A REALIZABLE COALITION-VALUE PERTURBATION GADGET
Fix a nonempty target S subset N. This gadget has resources x_1,...,x_n,z.
For each nonempty U subset N define a nonnegative linear form
 L_U(x,z)= (|U|+1)/2 *sum_{j in U}x_j
            +M*sum_{j notin U}x_j + 1_{U=S}z,
 where M=n(n+1)+1. Define G_S=min_{empty!=U subset N}L_U.
This is a finite nonnegative min-linear technology, hence monotone, concave
and positively homogeneous. At x=1_T, z=0, for every nonempty T, the unique
minimizer is U=T and the value is |T|(|T|+1)/2:
 - If T subset U and U!=T, the excess is |T|(|U|-|T|)/2>=1/2.
 - If T is not a subset of U, the M term alone exceeds n(n+1)/2.
Therefore every competing form has margin at least1/2.
Give actual owner j one unit of x_j, and zero z to everyone. Let h belong to
S. Increase ONLY h's z-report from0 to epsilon=1/4. For a coalition T:
 - If h notin T, its resource bundle is unchanged.
 - If h in T and T!=S, its formerly unique minimizing form has z coefficient0,
   and every other form only increases or stays fixed, so its value is unchanged.
 - If T=S, its unique minimizing form increases by epsilon and retains its
   positive margin, so its value increases by exactly epsilon.
Thus one actual owner's coordinate increase changes v(S) alone among ALL
nonempty coalition values. This is an explicit realizable production transition,
not an assumption that arbitrary abstract-game changes are feasible.

ONE FIXED TECHNOLOGY FOR THE COMPLETE PROOF
Take the sum on disjoint resource blocks of:
 (a) G_{S_k} for k=1,...,n-1, where S_k={1,...,k};
 (b) one linear production resource;
 (c) a two-resource minimum;
 (d) an n-resource minimum.
The direct sum F is again finite, monotone, concave and homogeneous. It has
n^2+n+2 resources. At each test profile activate only the specified block;
all other resources report zero and contribute zero. Owners and eligibility
are unchanged across all tests. This construction freezes F before the rule
is selected, so it also covers coefficients chosen with full knowledge of F.

SYMMETRIZATION AND NORMALIZATION
Average an eligible linear rule over all permutations of actual owner labels.
The fully eligible domain, fixed F, each coalition-cover and TCWM inequality,
nonnegativity and funding factor are preserved. The averaged coefficients for
any k-element coalition S are alpha_k for i in S and beta_k for i notin S.
Set a_k=binom(n-1,k-1)alpha_k for1<=k<=n, and
b_k=binom(n-1,k)beta_k for1<=k<n.

TCWM COEFFICIENT INEQUALITIES
In gadget G_{S_k}, choose h=1. The changing owner belongs to the coalition
C={h} union (N\S_k). Its payment change is
 epsilon[alpha_k+(n-k)beta_k]. TCWM implies it is nonnegative. Equivalently,
                         a_k+k*b_k>=0, k<n.                (2)
No condition on nonrealizable game changes was used.

THREE COVERAGE/BUDGET TESTS
Activate the two-resource minimum, giving owner1 its first unit and owner2 its
second unit. This is the two-owner unanimity game with n fixed owners. Coverage
of {1,2} gives
 2 sum_{k=2}^n binom(n-2,k-2)alpha_k>=1,
 or equivalently
                  sum_{k=1}^n(k-1)a_k >=(n-1)/2.            (3)
Activate the n-resource minimum, giving owner j one unit of resource j. Only
N produces one unit, so grand coverage gives
                             a_n>=1/n.                     (4)
Finally activate the linear resource, giving only owner1 one unit. This is the
single-owner unanimity game, v(T)=1 iff1 in T. Total funding at this profile is
 K=sum_{k=1}^{n-1}k(a_k+b_k)+n*a_n.
Using (2),
 K >= sum_{k=1}^n(k-1)a_k+a_n
   >= (n-1)/2+1/n
by(3),(4). Its grand production is1, proving(1). QED.

EVIDENCE AND CONSEQUENCE
linear_tcwm_probe.py was a numerical discovery LP on the normalized coefficient
constraints, not a numerical proof. The explicit gadget and algebra above give
an all-n conventional proof. verify_linear_tcwm_obstruction.py independently
checks the gadget values, all changed coalition values and coefficient identities
with rational arithmetic. This theorem closes the fixed-linear-operator route
to a sublinear TCWM owner upper. It does not prove a linear unrestricted lower,
and does not constrain the original individual-incentive problem.
