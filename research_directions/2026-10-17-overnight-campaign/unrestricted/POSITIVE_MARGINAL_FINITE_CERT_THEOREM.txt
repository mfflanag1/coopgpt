STATEMENT
Let F(x)=min(x1,x2,x3). There are16 fixed actual owners, each eligible for all
three resources; reports in the unit cube[0,1]^3 per owner already suffice.
Consider any rule
 y_i(b)=sum_(R subset N\{i}) w_(i,R)[F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)]
with fixed nonnegative real weights independent of the reports. If the rule
covers every actual coalition and satisfies payment-only TCWM for all
coalitions of size at most4, its worst total-funding factor is at least5.
Weights need not be symmetric, normalized, or a Bernoulli mixture.

This is a finite architecture separation. The already accepted unrestricted
min3 TCWM rule has factor2, even protecting all coalition sizes and all finite
owner populations. Therefore the new lower is not a lower against arbitrary
mechanisms. No sharpness at5 or new asymptotic exponent is claimed.

SYMMETRIZATION AND VARIABLES
Finite owner-label averaging preserves every condition on the same fully
eligible16-owner report domain. The resulting weights depend only on
l=|R|. Let W_l=binom(15,l)w_l>=0, for l=0,...,15. Thus a payment is the weighted
sum, with weights W_l, of expected marginal contributions under uniform l-subsets
of the other15 actual owners.

At the balanced profile all16 owners hold(1,1,1). Every marginal contribution
equals1, total production equals16, and funding implies
                           sum_l W_l <= kappa.           (1)
At a second profile only three owners hold the three pure unit resources;
the others report zero. Each active owner has marginal1 exactly when the other
two pure owners are sampled. Coverage of those three owners gives
  sum_l c_l W_l >=1,  c_l=3*l(l-1)/(15*14).               (2)
Both profiles are in the same fixed-owner domain.

The protected coalition has q leaf owners. Focal owner0 initially reports

(0,0,1) and change nothing. Outside the coalition, k center owners each hold
(1/k,1/k,0); the remaining z actual owners report zero throughout.

Write epsilon=1/k. A restoration changes a sampled coalition's production by epsilon only
when the focal leaf is included, all other protected leaves are absent, and
at least t+1 centers are present. The exact TCWM increment, divided by epsilon,
is sum_l D_(q,k,t;l) W_l, where
 D_(q,k,t;l)= [sum_(j=t+1)^k binom(k,j)
                {binom(z,l-j)-(q-1)binom(z,l-1-j)}]/binom(15,l).             (3)
An out-of-range binomial coefficient is zero. The first term is the focal
owner's marginal increase; each of the q-1 unchanged coalition members gives

                         D_(q,k,t).W >=0.                 (4)
These are fixed finite report comparisons, not formal coefficient sign tests.
