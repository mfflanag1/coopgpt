NEXT-CAMPAIGN RANKING AFTER THE OVERNIGHT RUN
17 October 2026. Synthesis of accepted campaign evidence, not a new search.

RECOMMENDATION
Choose coalition-size dependence at activity arity three as the mathematical
mainline, and reserve-contract validation as the AI/energy application mainline.
Keep arbitrary-graph full outside production as the next mathematical alternate.
Consolidating the sharp coalition and norm theorems for specialist review should
continue independently; another research campaign is not a prerequisite for that.

The ranking measures prospective research value, not proof probability. Internal
audits have strengthened correctness evidence, but historical novelty, economic
importance and field-level significance remain unconfirmed. Strong Group
Monotonicity, semivalues, polymatroid decomposition and norm embeddings have
classical antecedents. The proposed contribution is the precise quantitative
fixed-owner production theorem, not invention of those ingredients.

1. MATHEMATICAL MAINLINE: HOW DOES FUNDING GROW WITH COLLUDING GROUP SIZE?

Define K_3(s) using nonnegative payments, coverage of ALL coalitions, and
payment-only monotonicity for incentive coalitions of at most s actual owners,
over all finite packing technologies whose activities use at most three owned
resource rows. Define K_3^OUT(s) with pooled-withdrawal outside protection for
those same incentive coalitions. Reports remain continuous binding underreports;
owner and resource counts may grow. The accepted interval is
  Omega(s^(1/4)) <= K_3(s) <= K_3^OUT(s) <= 3s^2.
The lower is an unrestricted mechanism lower. The 3s^2 upper uses a scaled
binomial semivalue. Late accepted root64 now closes ALL fixed nonnegative
whole-owner marginal-contribution weights, including asymmetric and n-dependent
weights: their best uniform funding order is Theta_r(s^(r-1)). A companion
internally checked exact certificate needs factor at least5 with16 owners and
protected coalitions of size4, already on min3. General mechanisms on that SAME
min3 technology attain2 regardless of s. These are strong architecture
separations; the unrestricted displayed frontier is unchanged. See
unrestricted/SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_GENERAL_FIXED_MARGINALS.txt and
unrestricted/POSITIVE_MARGINAL_FINITE_CERT_THEOREM.txt.
The earlier sqrt(log s) lower is superseded. See root adjudications20 and23,
energy/BOUNDED_COALITION_SPARSE_PRODUCTION.txt and
energy/FINITE_ARITY_THREE_LADDER_CERTIFICATE.txt.

Why first: this asks a broadly interpretable question about the cost of protecting
larger groups while retaining global coalition stability. There is a substantial
quantitative gap, a finite fixed-owner lower, and a general constructive upper.
It continues the strongest new program result rather than refining a failed
formula. It also distinguishes technological complementarity from coalition
size, which is useful for deciding what contractual protection is affordable.

Six-hour target: try for a general O(s) upper, with any proved O(s^(2-epsilon))
for fixed epsilon>0 qualifying; alternatively improve the unrestricted lower
exponent beyond1/4. Full OUT upper improvements automatically improve the
payment-only bound, whereas a payment-only lower applies to both contracts.
Retain all-coalition coverage and fixed identities throughout.

Gate: after90 minutes require a new restoration/packing inequality with a
demonstrated asymptotic consequence, or an exact growing transition certificate.
For an upper, start with profile-adaptive, fractional-resource or nonlinear
compensation. Check its actual mixed-owner restoration logic against min3 and
the finite16-owner witness before scaling it. These alternatives are live,
not already proved effective.
Do not continue merely because a larger finite grid looks promising. Spend the
next150 minutes proving the identified step and testing its adversarial cases;
reserve120 minutes for independent audit and a reproducible proof package.
Stop if progress merely reweights fixed nonnegative whole-owner marginal contributions,
including positive mixtures or owner-count-dependent coefficients. Larger bounded
examples and lowers requiring extra marginal-payment floors also fail this gate.
Record the precise missing inequality if the gate fails.

2. MATHEMATICAL ALTERNATE: FLOOR-FREE FULL OUTSIDE PRODUCTION ON GRAPHS

For arbitrary unit-profit resource graphs, current total-funding bounds are
  1+c <= kappa_OUT <= 2,  c(2-exp(-1/c))=1, c≈0.543063.
Complete multipartite components attain1+c. The factor2 rule additionally
enforces grand-marginal floors, so its optimality with those floors does not
settle the floor-free problem. TCWM alone is already sharp3/2. See root08,
root19, root50 and root/GRAPH_OUTSIDE_TWO_CANDIDATE.txt.

Why second: it is a sharply posed, reviewable problem adjacent to an accepted
strong theorem, with potential relevance to hardware-energy compatibility
graphs. Its significance ceiling is narrower than the coalition-size frontier,
but a new constant would clarify whether graph interaction itself is costly.
The existing positive binary lifts and component-local price gluing have exact
obstructions; those are not all-mechanism lower bounds.

Six-hour success: prove an all-graph full-OUT upper2-epsilon, or an unrestricted
fixed-owner lower strictly above1+c. A finite rational profile certificate can
establish the latter if it constrains arbitrary mechanisms, not just a price
ansatz. Start with unit profits and arbitrary mixed owners; treat weight transfer
as a separate proved step. At two hours require a new coupled compensation lemma
or exact dual certificate. Existing P4 price-grid scouts remain below1+c and
supply no improved unrestricted certificate; repeating them is not a new angle.
Stop positive-layer retuning and unstructured grids.
Always state whether an obstruction is against a representation, a mechanism
class, or every mechanism.

3. APPLICATION MAINLINE: DOES AN AI/ENERGY RESERVE CONTRACT FIT REAL CHOICES?

The sharp signed-L1 result gives a clean conditional Gaussian reserve model
F(t,x)=t-||x||_2, with factors3/2 for TCWM and1+c for full OUT. This is not yet
an empirical pooling result. Ordinary efficient norm-subgradient allocation
already covers coalitions; subsidy purchases the additional partial-withholding
guarantee. A total-output subsidy bound need not bound subsidy per incremental
cooperation gain. See root42 and root/RESERVE_APPLICATION_CORRECTED_GATE.txt.

The M100 intake is valuable measurement evidence:980 physical nodes, simultaneous
PSU-input observations, and a material distinction between input-power and
total_power fields. However, the April release contains only parts of four days,
sparse overlap and unknown state/ownership. Nodes are not automatically economic
owners. The Mac serving pilot does not validate this reserve institution. See
root61 and energy/M100_OBSERVED_PANEL_RESULT.txt.

Next bounded campaign: specify one institution with capacity entitlement,
actual owners, physically feasible joint load/capacity withdrawals, curtailment,
common reliability target, public value and a subsidy-paying sponsor. Then obtain
a longer simultaneous input-power/state panel, freeze chronological training and
holdout before evaluation, and test the model against an efficient baseline.
Keep temporal dependence, meter boundaries and common shocks intact.

Success is a defensible contract/data mapping and an honest out-of-sample test
of reliability, feasible deviations and sponsor economics; a documented failure
is also useful. Stop validation claims if ownership rights, delivery feasibility,
tail sample size or sponsor value cannot be established. Synthetic owner groups
can illustrate conditional theory but cannot validate multiowner behavior. Do
not extend to private costs or scarce-peak-power policy before this gate passes.

DEFERRED, NOT DISPROVED

Original individual-IC owner/resource gap: still the highest theoretical ceiling,
but this run did not improve its general asymptotic rate. The surviving diagonal
own-envelope conjecture sum_i E_i^2<=T^2 would improve the owner-bound constant
from roughly sqrt(2n) to sqrt(n), not close the logarithmic-versus-square-root
gap. Many shortcut inequalities are now exactly false. Reopen only with a new
aggregate normal-coupling argument or a true continuous-relaxation obstruction;
do not recycle coordinate counterexamples. See unrestricted/LANE_CLOSURE.txt.

Two-common/private polymatroids: one common/private-DR sharp3/2 and exact oracle
evaluation are strong new starting points. But positive fixed private-ray
averaging, independent three-block averaging and the natural logarithmic OUT
extension have certified failures. No general two-common impossibility follows.
Reopen when a genuinely joint common-resource compensation lemma is available;
its first test must include mixed owners holding common and private resources.
See root58-60. This remains a credible successor, not a presently justified
reason to delay review of the completed one-common theorem.
