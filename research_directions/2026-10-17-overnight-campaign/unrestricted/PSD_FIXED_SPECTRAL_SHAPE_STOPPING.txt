FIXED NORMALIZED SPECTRAL SHAPES CANNOT SAVE A DIMENSION-LINEAR FRACTION
Frozen 2026-10-17. Necessary individual-IC condition, not an all-rule lower.

RULE CLASS
For every matrix order m>=2, fix a scalar function f_m:[1,infinity)->(0,
infinity), continuous at1 and locally absolutely continuous, with f_m(1)=1.
The function may vary with m and with the fixed owner count, but not with
the reported profile. On positive-definite aggregate C, with
T=lambda_min(C), pay actual owner i
 y_i=Tr(P(C)X_i),   P(C)=f_m(C/T)/Tr(f_m(C/T)).                (1)
Assume this rule is individually own-report monotone for every fixed-owner
PSD report profile. Individual/full coalition outside-production incentives
would be stronger and hence also entail the necessary condition below.

THEOREM
The worst total-output funding factor of(1) is at least
       B_m=1+(m-1)*m^(-1/(m-1)) >= m-log(m).                 (2)
In particular B_m/m tends to1. No family of such rules can guarantee a
factor (1-epsilon)m for a fixed epsilon>0 in all growing dimensions.
This includes shapes that locally decay faster than the inverse and
therefore are not covered by the positive-resolvent order obstruction.

PROOF: A NECESSARY OWN-REPORT DIFFERENTIAL INEQUALITY
Fix m, write f=f_m and k=m-1. At the aggregate
C_s=diag(1,s,...,s), s>1, let one actual owner hold X=s E_jj for a high
coordinate j. A second owner holds C_s-X. All other owners, if any, may
be zero. Hold the second owner's report fixed and change only X to
x E_jj near x=s. The aggregate minimum eigenvalue remains1. The focal
owner's payment, as a function of x, is
                x*f(x)/(1+(m-2)*f(s)+f(x)).                 (3)
Monotonicity forces its derivative at x=s to be nonnegative at every
differentiability point of f. Since f>0, this is equivalent to

 f(s)[1+k f(s)] + s f'(s)[1+(k-1)f(s)] >=0.                (4)

The other owner stays fixed for this comparison; no identity changes or
cross-owner symmetry assumptions occur. Different choices of s may use
different profiles from the same unrestricted two-owner report domain.

INTEGRATING WITHOUT A MONOTONICITY ASSUMPTION ON f
Define
                  I(s)=s*f(s)/(1+k f(s))^(1/k).
On any finite interval[1,R], positivity and continuity bound f away from
zero. Local absolute continuity and the chain rule give, almost everywhere,
 (log I(s))'=1/s + f'(s)[1+(k-1)f(s)]/[f(s)(1+k f(s))].
By(4), this derivative is nonnegative. Hence I is nondecreasing and
 I(s)>=I(1)=m^(-1/k)=:c_m.
In particular q(s)=s*f(s)>=c_m. No sign restriction on f' was used.

At C_s, T=1 and total payment is
 A(s)=[1+k*s*f(s)]/[1+k*f(s)]=[1+k q(s)]/[1+k q(s)/s].
For fixed s>1 this expression is increasing in q. Therefore
 A(s)>=[1+k*c_m]/[1+k*c_m/s].
Let s tend to infinity to obtain the first bound in(2). Finally
 exp(-u)>=1-u with u=log(m)/(m-1) gives B_m>=m-log(m),
and B_m/m->1. This proves the theorem.

REGULARITY, BOUNDARIES, AND SCOPE
Local absolute continuity may be replaced by C1 regularity. Continuity
at1 is used only to identify I(1). The rule is tested entirely on strictly
positive aggregates, so no singular-profile convention can repair the
obstruction. Its scalar shape is positive by assumption; for finite
nonnegative shapes with f(1)=1, all-profile own monotonicity already forces
f(s)>0: if f(s)=0, hold the complementary report from(3) fixed and restore
the focal high coordinate from1 to s. Its payment falls from a positive
number to zero.

The theorem is a necessary architecture bound. It neither asserts that
B_m is attainable nor proves global incentives from(4). It does not cover
arbitrary profile-dependent f_C, owner-specific prices, a report-dependent
spectral allocation that is not a fixed scalar shape, or discontinuous
shape rules outside the stated regularity. Positive resolvent mixtures with
arbitrary profile-dependent weights are separately excluded by
PSD_POSITIVE_RESOLVENT_FUNDING_OBSTRUCTION.txt. The two classes overlap but
neither statement is being silently substituted for the other.

The known full coalition outside-production rule T*C^-1 has exactly mT
funding and is not trace-normalized as in(1). The present result says the
natural normalized separable spectral-shape route cannot save a fixed
fraction of that coefficient. It does not establish the unrestricted
general PSD_m optimum. The fixed-owner individual general funding bound
can be far smaller on two-owner domains; this obstruction is specific to
the spectral architecture, not the economics of two owners.

CHECKER
verify_psd_fixed_spectral_shape.py uses rational arithmetic to check(4),
the invariant derivative identity, the monotonicity of the total-payment
formula, and rational enclosures for c_m. The analytic proof, not these
finite checks, establishes the all-dimension statement. Priority review
has not been completed; this is a rigorous campaign stopping result.
