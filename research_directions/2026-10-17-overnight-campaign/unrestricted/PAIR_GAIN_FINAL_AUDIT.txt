1. TRANSLATION REDUCTION PRESERVES THE EXACT CONTRACT
Singleton coverage and Y<=S+KG imply, at every profile,
 0<=y_i-f_i<=KG, f_i=min(a_i,b_i).
These coordinate-dependent compact intervals bound the feasible mechanism
set; all covering, budget and finite incentive comparisons are closed linear
conditions in the product topology. No measurable or continuous starting
mechanism is needed.

For arbitrary t>=0, T_t y(b)=y(b+t)-t, where t_i is added to BOTH reports of
owner i. Standalone values and coalition production increase by the relevant
sum of t_i, while G stays fixed. Thus T_t preserves nonnegativity, all-coalition
coverage, the gain budget, and singleton/pair beta-OUT: the same translation
is applied to both compared profiles and the actual withheld bundle is unchanged.
This argument works for beta<1 as well as beta=1.

Finite rectangular averages of commuting translations have coordinate error
at most KG/L under each generating translation. Compactness gives a common
fixed point for any finite collection; the finite-intersection property then
gives one fixed by ALL translations. This is product compactness, not a claim
of a convergent sequence on an uncountable profile space. It does not integrate
an arbitrary mechanism over translations. Finite owner/resource symmetry
averaging preserves the resulting translation property and the full contract.
Consequently y_i=b_i+h_i(z), z_i=a_i-b_i, is justified without hidden anonymity,
homogeneity, regularity or null-payment assumptions on the starting mechanism.

2. FINITE PAIR TRANSFERS ARE FEASIBLE RESTORATIONS
For signed changes Delta_i inside a pair, put
 P=sum(Delta_i)_+, N=sum(-Delta_i)_+.
Starting at the pure residual profile, restore these two nonnegative parts.
The increase in actual payment is N+Delta H_pair. Beta-OUT and the reverse
restoration therefore give
 beta min(P,N)-N<=Delta H_pair<=P-beta min(P,N).
For a fixed pair sum, choose the matching of old/new owner types with minimal
transfer D. Then P=N=D and |Delta H_pair|<=(1-beta)D.
As h_i=pure_payment_i+min(z_i,0), this gives exactly each transfer row's
pay_old-pay_new+D beta<=D+sum min(new,0)-sum min(old,0).

Anonymity only permits a multiset notation for the profiles. Every row can
be realized by changing the SAME two actual owners and holding every outsider
fixed; sorting the displayed types is not owner entry or strategic relabeling.
Repeated types are different owners with equal payments under the averaged
rule. Padding uses n-5 existing null owners throughout, not a common mechanism
across different n. Payments and null prices may depend arbitrarily on n.

The unchanged first owner is legitimately a member of the protected pair;

Same-sign pure profiles have G=T=0 and all payments vanish.
