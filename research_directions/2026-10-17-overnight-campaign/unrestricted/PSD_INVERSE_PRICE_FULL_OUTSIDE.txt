A COMMON INVERSE PRICE GIVES FULL COALITION OUTSIDE PROTECTION ON PSD_m
17 October 2026. Elementary structural baseline; historical priority open.

Fixed actual owners report X_i>=0. Let C=sum_i X_i and T=lambda_min(C).
If T>0 define
                    y_i=T Tr(C^(-1) X_i).                    (1)
If T=0 set all payments to zero. Then (1) is nonnegative, covers every
coalition, and protects FULL coalition outside production under arbitrary
joint Loewner withdrawals of the same fixed owners. Its exact total is mT.
This is a TOTAL-output guarantee, not a gain-only S+constant*G guarantee.

Coverage. If T>0, then for any coalition S,
 Tr(C^(-1)X_S)>=lambda_min(X_S)Tr(C^(-1)).
Also T Tr(C^(-1))>=1. Multiplication proves y(S)>=F(X_S).
If T=0, every X_S<=C has zero minimum eigenvalue, so zero payments cover.
The total is T Tr(C^(-1)C)=mT in the positive case, and zero otherwise.

Full OUT. Restore coalition withdrawals D_i>=0 along the straight path,
with D=sum_S D_i, outside aggregate O fixed, C(s)=C(0)+sD and
X_S(s)=C(s)-O. Write h=lambda_min(D)>=0. On a positive-definite segment let
 a(s)=lambda_min(C(s)), L(s)=Tr(C(s)^(-1)X_S(s))
                         =m-Tr(C(s)^(-1)O).
The coalition payment is W=aL. For almost every s,
 a'>=h by superadditivity, and
 L'=Tr(C^(-1) D C^(-1)O)>=h Tr(C^(-2)O)>=0.
Therefore
 W'>=h[L+a Tr(C^(-2)O)]
    =h[m-Tr((C^(-1)-a C^(-2))O)].
The matrix A=C^(-1)-a C^(-2) is PSD. Since 0<=O<=C,
 Tr(AO)<=Tr(AC)=m-a Tr(C^(-1)). It follows that
                    W'>=h a Tr(C^(-1))>=h.                   (2)
Integrating gives the full coalition payment increase at least h=F(D).
This uses production of the entire pooled withdrawal, not just sum_i F(D_i).

Boundary justification. The minimum eigenvalue is Lipschitz on every bounded
matrix path, so its derivative exists almost everywhere and the displayed
bound follows from a(s+t)>=a(s)+t h. Matrix inverse is smooth on positive
definite segments. For C(0),D>=0, ker(C(0)+sD)=ker C(0) intersect ker D for
every s>0; thus the path is either positive definite for every s>0 or singular
throughout. In the latter case h=0 and all payments vanish. In the former,
0<=W(s)<=m a(s) implies continuity to0 whenever C(0) is singular; integrate
from a positive s and pass to the limit. No nonsmooth eigenvector choice is
needed. Owner identities and outsiders remain fixed along the entire path.

The factor m is not claimed sharp among mechanisms. The diagonal min-m
restriction has lower constants about m/2 for TCWM and about0.543m for full
OUT; the all-matrix optimal constants are not established here. In PSD_2 the
separate owner-aware L1/Lorentz construction is stronger. This baseline is
useful as a valid general full-OUT comparator to failed spectral shortcuts.

verify_psd_inverse_price.py checks exact diagonal coalition coverage and full
pooled withdrawals plus the derivative trace inequality. The proof above is
for all real PSD matrices, including noncommuting owner reports.
