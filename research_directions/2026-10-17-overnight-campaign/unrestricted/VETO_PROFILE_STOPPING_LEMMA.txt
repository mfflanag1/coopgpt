VETO-PROFILE STOPPING LEMMA FOR ORIGINAL INDIVIDUAL INCENTIVES
2026-10-17. Elementary structural check, no novelty claim.

Consider any collection of fixed-owner report profiles closed under the
comparisons being tested. Coalition values are monotone in each owner's report,
and changing owner i's report does not affect coalitions omitting i. At every
profile b with T_b=v_b(N)>0, suppose the veto set
 V_b={i: v_b(S)=0 for every S not containing i}
is nonempty. Allocate T_b equally among V_b and zero to everyone else; at T=0
allocate zero.

This is an efficient coalition cover: every positive-worth coalition contains
all veto owners, hence receives T_b>=v_b(S). It is individually own-monotone.
For an increase by owner i, if i was not a veto then its old payment was zero.
If i was a veto, all coalitions omitting i retain value zero, so i remains a
veto. Any owner j that was not a veto has a positive-worth coalition omitting
j; that witness remains positive after an increase. Thus the new veto set is
a subset of the old one, still containing i. Since T cannot decrease,
 T_new/|V_new| >= T_old/|V_old|.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS CAMPAIGN.
A finite report-transition LP supported entirely on profiles with at least one
veto owner cannot certify any original individual-IC funding lower bound above
1. This remains true if veto identities differ across unrelated profiles, and
if coalition worths take several positive values. In particular every
nonnegative totally balanced simple game has a veto player: an efficient core
of total1 must be supported on every winning coalition, so their intersection
is nonempty. Therefore an array of totally balanced simple-game snapshots
cannot yield a nontrivial original-domain lower certificate.

This closes the tempting attempt to repurpose the new veto/alternative-
threshold Shapley counterexample directly into an unrestricted lower bound.
An interacting-chain successor must include profiles whose positive coalitions
have empty common intersection. The production realization lemma still applies
to such richer totally balanced arrays, and can transfer a valid finite dual
to actual fixed owners and dimension at most2n.

This concerns INDIVIDUAL own-report incentives only. Equal-veto sharing may
violate coalition-total withdrawal monotonicity when another owner loses its
veto status, so no inference about the stronger TCWM frontier is intended.
