ORIGINAL-SOURCE COMPARISON FOR THE SHARP TCWM THEOREM
17 October 2026. Bounded comparison, NOT a novelty confirmation.

1. Branzei, Solymosi and Tijs (2007)
Type monotonic allocation schemes for a class of market games.
TOP15:78-88. DOI10.1007/s11750-007-0002-7.
Full published author-uploaded original read at:

Institutional metadata:
https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/type-monotonic-allocation-schemes-for-multi-glove-games-2/
Institutional PDF returned403; the author-uploaded published text was accessible.

Source comparison (Sections2,4; Definition1; Theorems5,6): their players have
one unit of exactly one fixed resource type. Allocation schemes select efficient
core points across subgames. Type monotonicity compares per-player payoffs after
one type's member exits: same-type survivors weakly gain; other types weakly
lose. Weighted rules, the nucleolus and tau-value provide such schemes.
Our lower uses all-profile bundle reports by fixed fully eligible owners and
coalition-total monotonicity under member resource increases. Its final profile
contains mixed owners. Their existence theorem neither covers those comparisons
nor supplies a subsidy-optimal mechanism under our requirement.

Independent distinguishing example: the equal-scarcity rule at pure A,pure B,
zero reports gives payments(1/2,1/2,0). Activating the zero owner to pure A gives
(0,1,0). Coalition{original A,activated owner} loses1/2, violating TCWM. This is
compatible with the classical rule's type monotonicity.

2. Moretti and Norde (2021)
A note on weighted multi-glove games. Social Choice and Welfare57:721-732.
DOI10.1007/s00355-021-01337-8.
Full published original read at:
https://d-nb.info/1240670206/34
Earlier author-institution original:
https://www.lamsade.dauphine.fr/CahiersLAMSADE/CahiersLamsade394.pdf

Definition3.1 partitions owners into types and gives each owner a scalar weight
of its single resource. Theorem3.2 establishes total balancedness; Theorems3.3
and3.4 classify supermodularity and population-monotonic allocation schemes.
These are fixed-game/core and subgame properties, not sharp funding bounds for
one rule on a fixed mixed-owner report domain. The introduction distinguishes
general glove-market games, which permit bundles, from its weighted multi-glove
subclass. Therefore our proposed theorem should preferably be described as
mixed-owner MINIMUM or glove-market production, with the broader domain explicit,
rather than silently using the pure-type definition of weighted multi-glove games.

3. Other scope
Root independently compared Dogan's absence-proofness original; its outside-
production term changes the incentive problem. Our payment-only TCWM theorem
does not establish that stronger outside-production property. Related population
monotonicity, collusion-proof cost-sharing and resource-monotonicity literature
may contain broader implications; these two source comparisons do not certify
historical priority. No outreach has been made.
