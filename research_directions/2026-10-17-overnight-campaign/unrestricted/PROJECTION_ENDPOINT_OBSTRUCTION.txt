INTERPRETATION.
One may not replace continuous own-report relaxation by choosing only full
or deleted coalition demands, even after retaining EVERY original coalition
row and repairing balancedness. An intermediate demand can tilt the projection
normal cone enough to produce a strictly larger focal payment. Any proof of
the live sqrt(n) envelope bound must control these interior changes.
The example does not show that an actual binary full/zero RESOURCE report
suffices or fails: endpoint here refers to coalition-demand rows, not resource
coordinates. The explicit realization uses a single actual resource withheld
from1 to0; that resource induces INTERMEDIATE coalition-demand changes.
