DUMMY-ZERO FINAL GATE: MIN2 CONSTRUCTIVE ESCAPE; MIN3 UNRESOLVED
17 October 2026. Corollary of accepted campaign results, not a novelty claim.

CONTRACT AND BASELINES
Fix actual owners with nonnegative resource reports. A game dummy i satisfies
F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)=0 for every subset R excluding i. Dummy-zero requires y_i=0.
Payments are nonnegative and cover every actual coalition. TCWM protects the
TOTAL payment of any coalition under restoration by any of its members; other
members may be unchanged. Full OUT additionally requires a coalition payment
increase at least F(sum of its withdrawn bundles).
For every fixed n, accepted scaled-Bernoulli rules already give finite
dummy-zero full-OUT funding r*n^(r-1) on min_r. Thus mere fixed-n infeasibility
is impossible. The real question concerns bounds uniform over growing owners.

1. UNIFORM MIN2 RULE WITH DUMMY-ZERO
For F(A,B)=min(A,B), let
 f_i=min(a_i,b_i), u_i=(a_i-b_i)^+, v_i=(b_i-a_i)^+,
 S=sum_i f_i, U=sum_i u_i, V=sum_i v_i.
Then T=F(sum b_i)=S+min(U,V). For U>=V and U>0 set
 p=V/(2U), q=1-p; use the symmetric formula for V>=U, V>0.
Define pi_i=u_i*p+v_i*q, with all pi_i=0 when U=V=0, and
                     y_i=(3/2)f_i+pi_i.                 (1)
This is the accepted signed-L1 construction under the linear cone isomorphism
t_i=(a_i+b_i)/2, x_i=(a_i-b_i)/2; here is a direct TCWM proof as well.

For a coalition with residual holdings (u,v), fixed outside residual holdings
(u_o,v_o), write W=u*p+v*q and U=u+u_o,V=v+v_o. On U>=V>0,
 W=v+(u-v)V/(2U),
 W_u=V(U-u+v)/(2U^2),
 W_v=1+(u-v-V)/(2U).
Because 0<=u<=U and 0<=v<=V<=U, both derivatives lie in [0,3/2].
The other half-plane is symmetric. W is continuous at U=V and at zero;
the bounded derivatives give the corresponding finite Lipschitz inequalities.
When one member restores a_i, either u_i increases at unit rate and f_i is
fixed, or f_i increases at unit rate and v_i decreases at unit rate. Thus the
coalition derivative is respectively W_u>=0 or 3/2-W_v>=0. Restoring b_i is
symmetric. Continuity handles sign/tie crossings. Sequential restorations
prove all-profile TCWM with the same actual owners.

Prices sum to one, so pi(S)>=min(u(S),v(S)). The identity
F(b(S))=sum_{i in S}f_i+min(u(S),v(S)) proves every coalition is covered.
For U>=V, total pi=V(3/2-V/(2U))<=3V/2. Consequently
                         sum_i y_i<=3T/2.

Dummy characterization: a nonzero min2 dummy must be pure on one side.
For i=(a_i,0), a_i>0, it is a dummy exactly when every other owner has
a_j>=b_j. Necessity follows by choosing a singleton j with b_j>a_j;
sufficiency follows by summing that inequality over every R. The other side
is symmetric. Therefore a pure-A dummy has f_i=0 and V=0, hence pi_i=0.
Zero-report owners also receive zero. This proves dummy-zero for (1).

The accepted signed-L1 theorem also supplies the stronger full-OUT version:
replace 3/2 by kappa=1+c and pi by the accepted logarithmic min2 rule,
where c[2-exp(-1/c)]=1. Its residual payments vanish on the abundant side
when the opposite residual total is zero, so the same dummy argument applies.
The full-OUT proof is the signed compensation proof in
../composition/CONE_L1_EMBEDDABLE_SHARP_THEOREM.txt, not a claim about the
Huber rule (1), which only has the TCWM proof above.

2. PRECISE ESCAPE FROM FIXED MARGINAL WEIGHTS
For any monotone production function, every nonnegative dummy-zero payment
rule has a POINTWISE report-adaptive positive whole-owner marginal
representation. If y_i>0, choose any R with marginal Delta_i,R>0 and set
w_i,R(b)=y_i(b)/Delta_i,R(b), with all other weights zero. If y_i=0 set all
weights zero. Dummy-zero guarantees the required positive marginal exists.
Choosing the first positive R in a fixed subset order makes this explicit.
No efficient computation, bounded coefficients, or extra regularity is asserted.

Thus (1) is a uniform bounded example in the report-adaptive positive
marginal-weight class. The accepted fixed-weight lower does not extend to
that class merely from positivity or dummy-zero. In particular min2 permits
TCWM factor3/2 even while every report-independent positive whole-owner
marginal-weight architecture has an unbounded uniform all-coalition factor.
This is a representation/scope corollary; it does not improve the already
accepted unrestricted min2 factors.

3. MIN3: EXACT STOP FOR SIMPLE NETTING, NOT FOR DUMMY-ZERO
Take fixed owners
 A=(1,0,0), B=(2,0,1), C=(2,1,0), F=min_3.
A is a dummy: the four R profiles excluding A have values 0,0,0,1 both
before and after A is added. Every owner's standalone minimum is zero, so
ownerwise balanced netting changes nothing. Aggregate totals are (5,1,1).
The accepted rule in ../root/GENERAL_FACE_AVERAGE_TCWM.txt defines
p_1(C)=C_1^-1 integral_[0,1]^2 F(C_1,u C_2,v C_3) du dv.
At C=(5,1,1), F(5,u,v)=min(u,v). Its expectation is
integral_0^1 Pr(u>=t,v>=t)dt=integral_0^1(1-t)^2dt.
Therefore the raw min3 resource1 price equals
             (1/5) integral_0^1 (1-t)^2 dt=1/15,
so A receives a positive payment. Therefore simply netting standalone minima
and then applying that integral rule does not establish dummy-zero for min3.

No scalable dummy-zero lower or owner-independent min3 dummy-zero mechanism
has been proved in this gate. The min2 positive result does not transfer by
assertion to min3. The original unrestricted individual-IC gap and the general
arity3 bounded-coalition gap remain unchanged.
