SCALED POSITIVE MARGINAL-CONTRIBUTION RULES HAVE A QUADRATIC SIZE BARRIER
17 October 2026. Extension of SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_OBSTRUCTION_GATE.txt.
Root proposed the compactness/exchangeability extension; this lane checked and
formalized it independently. Accepted after opposite-model, independent composition-lane and root review.

STATEMENT
Fix integers r,s>=2. Use the SINGLE public technology
                    F(x)=min(x1,...,xr).
For each n, all n actual owners are eligible for all r resources. Suppose
payments have the form
 y_i^(n)(b)=sum_(R subset N\{i}) w_(n,i,R)
                         [F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)],             (1)
where all weights are nonnegative, finite and independent of the report profile.
Weights may depend on n, i, R, r, s and this public technology. Assume coverage
for every coalition, TCWM for every coalition of at most s owners, and total
funding <=kappa_n F(b_N), all at every report profile.

Then
                 liminf_(n->infinity) kappa_n
                              >= s^(r-1)/r.              (2)
In particular, any such family with a uniform funding factor has factor at
least s^(r-1)/r. The accepted scaled binomial rule gives the complementary upper
r*s^(r-1), even with full OUT for the protected coalitions and coverage for all
coalitions, on every arity-at-most-r packing technology. Therefore this ENTIRE
fixed nonnegative marginal-weight architecture has worst funding order
Theta_r(s^(r-1)), uniformly over finite owner populations. At r=3 the exponent
is quadratic. For r other than3 the displayed general exponent governs.

This is an ARCHITECTURE lower, not an unrestricted-mechanism lower. On the
very same min_r technology, the accepted unrestricted all-coalition TCWM
mechanism has factor(r+1)/2 independent of s; at r=3 its factor is2. Thus(2)
is strong evidence of this architecture's inefficiency, not evidence that the
general arity-three size frontier must be quadratic. The class uses fixed
whole-owner marginal contributions; profile-adaptive, resource-level and
nonlinear ownership-aware mechanisms remain outside its scope.

Only reports in [0,1]^r per owner are needed for the lower proof: threshold
witnesses, pure-owner coverage, balanced-owner budget and null padding all lie
in that cube. Owner identities stay fixed in each comparison. Padding means
examining zero reports of additional fixed eligible owners, not owner creation
or strategic splitting. The finite-n weights need not be Bernoulli mixtures.

1. FINITE LABEL SYMMETRIZATION
Permute owner labels and average both payments and report labels over all n!
permutations of the SAME fully eligible owner model. Nonnegativity, funding,
all coalition coverage and size-at-most-s TCWM survive this finite convex
average. The symmetrized rule has one nonnegative weight w_(n,k) per subset
cardinality k, identical across focal owners. This uses symmetry of the public
domain; the original mechanism need not be anonymous.

Set W_(n,k)=binom(n-1,k)w_(n,k). The symmetrized payment is
 sum_(k=0)^(n-1) W_(n,k) E_[uniform k-subset R of N\{i}]
                                         [F(b_i+b_R)-F(b_R)].
At b_i=(1,...,1) for all owners, every marginal contribution is1. Funding
therefore gives sum_k W_(n,k)<=kappa_n. For n>=2 define the finite positive
measure
             mu_n=sum_k W_(n,k) delta_[k/(n-1)].          (3)
Its mass is uniformly bounded on every subsequence with bounded kappa_n.

2. UNIFORM FINITE-ACTIVE SAMPLING COMPARISON
Fix a profile with a active owner slots, including the focal slot, and pad the
remaining n-a owners with zero reports. The focal marginal depends only on
which q=a-1 other active slots belong to R. Under uniform k-subset sampling,
their inclusion indicators have the distribution of q draws WITHOUT replacement
from n-1 binary labels, k of them1. Under Bernoulli p=k/(n-1), the indicators
are q draws WITH replacement from those same labels.

Couple the draws to agree unless repeated population labels occur. Their total
variation distance is at most
                 delta_(n,q)=min(1,q(q-1)/(2(n-1))),      (4)
by the union bound for a collision among q independent uniform labels. One
explicit coupling keeps the with-replacement sequence when it is distinct;
on collision, replace it by an independent uniform distinct sequence. Both
conditional laws of the resulting distinct sequence are uniform, so it has
the required without-replacement law. This bound is uniform in k.

For a fixed profile, monotonicity bounds every focal marginal in [0,F(b_N)].
Consequently its expected value differs between the two sampling laws by at
most F(b_N)delta_(n,q). Multiplying by W and summing bounds the difference
between the symmetrized payment and its Bernoulli-mixture counterpart by
                  kappa_n F(b_N)delta_(n,q).              (5)
The finite-active Bernoulli marginal is a polynomial in p. Null owners have
zero marginal contribution and zero payment exactly in(1); none of their
payments has been discarded by assumption or deducted from a funding bound.

3. PASSAGE TO A COMMON LIMIT
If liminf kappa_n is infinite there is nothing to prove. Otherwise choose a
subsequence n_l->infinity on which kappa_(n_l) converges to its finite liminf L.
Positive measures(3) have bounded mass on the compact interval[0,1], so a
further subsequence converges weakly to a finite positive measure mu, with
mu([0,1])<=L. Every fixed finite-active marginal polynomial converges in
integral against these measures. Equation(5) tends to zero, so every fixed
threshold report inequality passes to the corresponding Bernoulli mixture
with the SAME limiting mu. This requires neither regularity of the original
payment map nor an infinite strategic population.

For the threshold witnesses of SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_OBSTRUCTION_GATE.txt, embed
the center amount x as(x,...,x,0) on the first r-1 rows, and a leaf amount l
as(0,...,0,l). Then F=min(center,leaf) at every coalition. Each finite witness
uses at most k+s active owner slots and can be null-padded whenever n_l is
large enough. Its protected coalition remains fixed and has size at most s.
Thus the limit mu satisfies all polynomial inequalities in that proof,
including the protected-pair tests excluding an atom at1. The proved support
lemma yields support(mu) subset[0,1/s]. No finite-n support assertion follows.

4. COVERAGE MOMENT AND CONCLUSION
Use r active pure owners, owner j holding the j-th unit vector, with all other
owners zero. For uniform k-subset sampling, each active owner's marginal is1
exactly when all other r-1 active owners are selected. Its expectation is
(k)_(r-1)/(n-1)_(r-1), with value0 if k<r-1. The same comparison(4), or this
explicit formula, shows uniform convergence to p^(r-1) on the atom grid in(3).
All r active owners together produce1; coverage and the limit therefore give
                        r integral p^(r-1) dmu >=1.
Since p<=1/s on the support,
  1/r <= integral p^(r-1) dmu <= mu([0,1])/s^(r-1)
                                    <= L/s^(r-1),
which is(2). This does not claim a quantitative finite owner threshold or
the optimal leading constant within the architecture.

SCOPE, TERMINOLOGY AND VERIFICATION
The proof includes n-dependent Bernoulli mixtures, owner-specific fixed
mixtures after label averaging, anonymous cardinality marginal rules, and
all nonnegative fixed weights(1). Classical normalized semivalues have unit
mass; arbitrary scaled positive marginal-contribution rules need not. The
Bernoulli semivalue is an ingredient, not terminology for every arbitrary-mass
rule in(1). Report-dependent coefficients and signed weights are excluded.

SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_CHECK.py checks the underlying finite fixed-owner threshold
identity independently. SEMIVALUE_MIXTURE_EXCHANGEABILITY_CHECK.py verifies
exact hypergeometric normalization, the uniform coupling bound(4), and the
pure-owner coverage moment on bounded grids. These checks corroborate the
analytic proof; weak compactness and the all-finite-population conclusion are
not established by enumerating those grids. Sampling without replacement,
finite permutation averaging and compactness are classical ingredients.
Historical novelty of this architecture theorem remains unconfirmed. The
Theta_r statement concerns order in s at fixed r; the lower/upper constants
differ by r^2 and are not claimed optimal. See root64 for the acceptance record.
