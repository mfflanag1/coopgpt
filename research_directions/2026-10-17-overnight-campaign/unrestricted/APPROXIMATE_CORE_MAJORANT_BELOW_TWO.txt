SUBLINEAR TRUTHFUL MAJORANTS CANNOT HAVE A BASE COVER FUNDED BELOW TWO
2026-10-17. Original individual-own-report monotonicity model.
Architecture obstruction; historical priority unconfirmed.

STATEMENT.
Fix 1<=beta<2. A nonnegative own-report-monotone rule y is called a beta-cover
majorant if, at every profile b, its coordinate box [0,y(b)] contains some
coalition cover z(b) with total at most beta*T(b), where T is grand worth.
For any integer m>=2 with m>1/(2-beta), put
 u_beta=(2m-1-beta*m)/(m-1)>0.
For every k>=2, one k-resource production function with n=mk fixed actual
owners forces every such rule to spend at least k*u_beta+(m-1) at one
specified profile of grand worth2m-1. Thus its worst funding factor is at
least [k*u_beta+(m-1)]/(2m-1), which is Omega_beta(n).
No symmetry, relabeling, selector consistency or continuity is required.
This extends the exact-core-majorant obstruction(beta=1). It does NOT prove
an unrestricted lower bound, nor claim that beta=2 is sufficient.

FIXED OWNERS AND PROFILES.
Let F(c)=min_{1<=s<=k}c_s. There are m permanently distinct actual owners
per pool. At the full profile one owner H_s per pool reports m, and the
other m-1 owners each report1. Thus T=2m-1. For each i the adjacent profile
lowers ONLY H_i from m to1, giving T_i=m. No owner changes identity.

LOW-PROFILE FORCING WITHOUT SYMMETRY.
At the i-th low profile let h=z_Hi, and let B be total base-cover payment
to the low owners in all other pools. The coalition consisting of all m
owners in pool i and the designated high owner in every other pool has
worth m. It is DISJOINT from those outside low owners. Consequently the
whole base-cover budget is at least m+B.
For every selection of one low owner from each other pool, the coalition
consisting of these selected owners and H_i has worth1. Average these
(m-1)^(k-1) inequalities to obtain
 h+B/(m-1)>=1.
Therefore
 beta*m >= sum z >=m+B>=2m-1-(m-1)h,
so h>=u_beta. Since y>=z, individual own monotonicity carries y_Hi>=u_beta
from its low profile to the specified full profile, separately for every i.
At that full profile the coalition of all low owners has worth m-1, so
coverage gives their total payment at least m-1. Adding proves the theorem.

CONSEQUENCE.
If a uniformly sublinear-funded truthful covering rule exists on the full
production class, then for every fixed beta<2 and all sufficiently large
owner counts there is a profile at which its payment box contains no cover
whose total is at most beta times grand worth. Extra spending is not merely
a coordinatewise enlargement of a nearly efficient cooperative allocation.
This is a necessary architecture condition, not a new eligible upper bound.

PROOF REVISION.
The first draft used finite owner-permutation averaging. That was unnecessary:
the disjoint coalition above forces the same inequality directly for the
changing owner's payment. The current result is stronger in presentation:
it applies at the one specified full profile of every asymmetric rule.

EXACT CHECK.
verify_approximate_core_majorant.py checks the rational forcing identity,
parameter positivity, and the forcing coalitions in finite examples.
The general theorem follows from the two displayed coalition inequalities.
