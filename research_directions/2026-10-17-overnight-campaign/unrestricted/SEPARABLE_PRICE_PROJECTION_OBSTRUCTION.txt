NO FIXED SMOOTH SEPARABLE PRICE REGULARIZER WORKS ON ALL TRIPLE-INPUT SYSTEMS
17 October 2026. Candidate structural theorem; independent audit required.

ARCHITECTURE AND QUANTIFIERS
Fix a convex nondecreasing h:[0,1]->R, C2 on a neighborhood of1/3, with
  0<h'(1/3)<infinity and0<h''(1/3)<infinity.
On each finite unit-profit, unit-consumption triple-resource hypergraph,
choose a minimizer of sum_v c_v h(p_v) over0<=p<=1 and sum_{v in e}p_v>=1,
and pay each actual owner i the linear bundle value b_i dot p(c).

For EVERY such FIXED h, there is a finite simple3-uniform resource hypergraph
and TWO fixed actual mixed owners on which this rule fails own-report
monotonicity. The failing optimizers are unique, have positive prices and
capacities, retain exactly the same tight edges, and have positive edge
multipliers. Thus neither tie-breaking, boundary handling, nor active-set
switching repairs the failure.

The theorem rules out a single fixed smooth regularizer used on all hypergraphs.
It does not rule out graph-dependent regularizers, nonlinear individual
payments, monotone envelopes with larger budgets, or unrestricted mechanisms.

LOCAL PROJECTION LEMMA
Let A be an (n-1) by n triple-edge incidence matrix of rank n-1,
whose kernel is spanned by a nonzero signed vector z. Let omega>0 be edge
weights, c=A^T omega>0, t0=1/3, and p0=t0*1. Because A p0=1,
lambda0=h'(t0)omega>0 satisfies stationarity c h'(p0)=A^T lambda0.
Convexity makes p0 an optimizer. Local strict convexity at every coordinate
makes it the unique global optimizer: any different optimum would contradict
strict convexity on the initial portion of their joining segment.

Fix a coordinate e with z_e>0 and increase c_e by s. The all-tight affine
space is p=p0+tau z. Define
 G(s,tau)=sum_v(c_v+s*1_{v=e}) h'(t0+tau z_v) z_v.
At(0,0), G=0 because sum_v c_v z_v=omega^T A z=0. Also
 G_tau(0,0)=h''(t0) Q>0, where Q=sum_v c_v z_v^2.
The implicit-function theorem gives
 tau'(0)=-[h'(t0)/h''(t0)] z_e/Q.
For small s, all prices remain in the strict convexity neighborhood. The
stationarity vector is orthogonal to z, hence belongs to range(A^T); the
unique multipliers vary continuously from strictly positive lambda0. This
all-tight candidate is therefore the unique true optimizer for small s.

Let actual owner I hold ALL capacities c_v at vertices with z_v>0, and none
at vertices with z_v<0. Owner II holds the rest. Both eligibility sets are
fixed. Owner I alone increases coordinate e. Writing
 W+=sum_{z_v>0} c_v z_v,
her payment derivative at0 is
  y_I'(0)=t0-[h'(t0)/h''(t0)] R_e,
  R_e=z_e W+/Q.                                      (P)
Equivalently R_e is the sum of the positive entries in row e of the weighted
tangent projection P=z z^T diag(c)/Q. Thus R_e>t0 h''(t0)/h'(t0) certifies
a strict incentive failure on an open interval of actual reports.

UNBOUNDED AMPLIFICATION WITH TRIPLE EDGES
Begin with four vertices and three edges
 {0,1,2}, {0,1,3}, {0,2,3}.
Their incidence kernel is exactly the span of(-2,1,1,1). Take current vertex
a0=1, of kernel value1, with defining edge{0,1,2}.
Repeat the following operation m times:
 - if the current vertex a_j has defining edge{u,v,a_j}, add a NEW vertex d_j
   and edge{u,v,d_j}; this forces z_{d_j}=z_{a_j};
 - add a NEW vertex a_{j+1} and edge{a_j,d_j,a_{j+1}}; this forces
   z_{a_{j+1}}=-2 z_{a_j}; use this last edge as its defining edge.
Every added row contains one newly introduced variable, so each step adds two
independent equations and vertices. The final incidence matrix has n=4+2m
vertices, n-1 edges, rank n-1, and one-dimensional kernel. All edges are distinct
triples. The final current value is(-2)^m.

Take even m>=2, let a=2^m=z_e>0 at the final vertex, and assign edge weight
omega=a^2 to the FIRST base edge{0,1,2}, and weight1 to every other edge.
These are auxiliary KKT weights defining actual capacities c=A^T omega;
ACTIVITY PROFITS REMAIN ONE. Every capacity is strictly positive.
The weighted low-amplitude base edge contributes2a^2 to W+, so W+>=2a^2.
A direct sum over the gadget gives
 Q=6a^2+(5/2)a^2+14=(17/2)a^2+14.
Indeed the two other base edges contribute12; the first added pair contributes
12; added pair j>=1 contributes(15/2)4^j. Their geometric sum gives the formula.
Consequently
 R_e >= 2a^3/[(17/2)a^2+14] >=(16/75)a >a/5.
This tends to infinity with m although every activity has arity THREE.
Choose even m with a/5>h''(1/3)/(3h'(1/3)). Equation(P) then gives y_I'(0)<0.
This proves the theorem.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE OVERNIGHT PROGRAM
The graph proof's scalar two-side derivative property does not extend to
bounded activity arity by changing a fixed smooth separable regularizer.
A rank-three constructive attack must alter the architecture (for example
owner-specific nonlinear charges or a nonseparable structural invariant), or
use a restricted hypergraph class with a proved bound on positive projection
mass. Merely increasing a fixed power or smoothing a different threshold
does not meet that gate.
