BOUNDED SUCCESSOR — HYPEREDGE BOUNDARY OF HUBER RESOURCE PRICING
17 October 2026,04:12UTC. Authorized by root after independent graph audit.

Question: Can the graph Huber dual-price construction extend to unit-profit
activities consuming one unit of each of at most r resource vertices, with
arbitrary fixed actual owners and all-profile underreport incentives?

Candidate regularizer h_r has derivative min(r t,1), i.e.
 h_r(t)=r t^2/2 for t<=1/r; t-1/(2r) for t>=1/r.
Minimize sum_v c_v h_r(p_v) over p>=0 and sum_{v in e}p_v>=1 for every edge,
with p<=1 without loss. Pay owner i its bundle dot the chosen price vector.

Gates: prove a general rank-sensitive eligible bound, OR identify a precise
incentive failure and demonstrate a general consequence (e.g. repaired envelope
funding must grow). A finite failed numerical optimizer alone is not enough.
Owner sets, technology and report domains remain fixed within each instance.
No conclusion about all mechanisms follows from a failed pricing architecture.

Initial structural fact: KKT alone may prove total raw payments <=(2-1/r)F,
because on a tight edge sum p=1 and sum max(p,1/r)<=2-1/r. This is a budget
statement, not an incentive proof. We will keep these implications separate.
