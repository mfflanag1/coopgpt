SHARP COMMON-PRICE TCWM FACTOR TWO ON THE SQUARE NORM CONE
17 October2026. Constructive proof candidate; historical priority unconfirmed.

THEOREM AND SCOPE
For K={(t,x,y):t>=max(|x|,|y|)} and F(t,x,y)=t-max(|x|,|y|),
there is an explicit common aggregate-price rule with coalition coverage,
TCWM for every coalition of fixed actual owners and every cone-feasible
restoration, and total funding at most2F. There is no restriction on the
number or ownership decomposition of reports. The common-price factor2 is
optimal, already if only individual monotonicity is imposed on two owners,
by NORM_PRICE_FRESH_COMMON_CONE_SEPARATION.txt.

This is a sharp result for aggregate-linear cone prices. Unrestricted
owner-aware mechanisms have smaller factors: the two-owner efficient
Shapley/core rule has full OUT, and the uniform-owner signed L1 transfer has
TCWM3/2 or full OUT1+c. The present rule asserts TCWM, not full OUT.
Here TCWM means the restoring coalition's total payment cannot decrease.
Full OUT would require its payment increase to be at least
F(sum_i D_i), the production of its aggregate restored/withheld bundle.

Indeed full OUT fails for this formula. Take owner1 A=(1,-1,-1), outsider
B=(2,2,2), and restore owner1 by D=(1,0,0). Its payment changes from3/2
to12/5, a gain9/10, while F(D)=1. All bundles and withdrawals are cone
feasible. Thus the distinction is witnessed by an exact own deviation.

1. FORMULA, COVERAGE AND FUNDING
By signed permutation, put an INTERIOR aggregate in the region
                             t>x>=y>=0.
Set
 H=t^2-y^2,  a=(t-x)/H,
 b=(t*x-y^2)/H=1-t*a,   c=(t-x)*y/H=y*a,
                             P(C)=(1,-b,-c).          (1)
Undo the signed permutation to evaluate other aggregates. At x=y the two
coefficients coincide at x/(t+x), so swapping the dominant coordinate
produces the same price. At y=0 its coefficient c vanishes, so sign changes
also match. At x=y=0 take P=(1,0,0). Thus P is continuous and locally
Lipschitz throughout the cone's interior, including these seams.

Here b,c>=0 and
                         b+c=1-(t-x)/(t+y)<=1.        (2)
For every coalition report B=(u,v,w) in K,
 P(C) dot B=u-bv-cw>=u-max(|v|,|w|)=F(B)>=0.
So all coalitions are covered. Total payment is
 P(C) dot C=(t-x)[1+(t*x-y^2)/(t^2-y^2)]<=2F(C),      (3)
because0<=t*x-y^2<=t^2-y^2. The same conclusions hold in every symmetry region.

At an aggregate on the boundary F(C)=0, choose any active supporting facet,
for example P=(1,-sign(x),0) if |x|=t>0 and P=(1,0,-sign(y)) otherwise.
At the origin choose P=(1,0,0). Since the facet is nonnegative on K and
P dot C=0, it pays zero to every owner in any decomposition of C into K.
Coalition values are also zero by superadditivity. This defines the rule at
all profiles. Although prices may have direction-dependent corner limits,
individual and coalition payments tend to zero as F(C) tends to zero:
nonnegativity and(3) bound each such payment by2F(C).

2. DERIVATIVE OF AN ARBITRARY COALITION'S PAYMENT
Write its aggregate report A=(u,v,w), with A in K and C-A in K. Holding the
outsiders fixed, its payment is
                     W=P(C) dot A=u-v+a(t*v-y*w).
Let Z=t*v-y*w. For a coalition restoration D, both A and C increase by D.
The derivative is g dot D, where
 g_t=1+a*v+Z*a_t,
 g_x=-1+a*t-Z/H,
 g_y=-a(y+w)+2*y*a*Z/H,
 a_t=(2*t*x-t^2-y^2)/H^2.                            (4)
We will prove g belongs to the dual cone: g_t>=|g_x|+|g_y|. Since K is
generated by the four rays(1,sigma,tau), sigma,tau in{+1,-1}, it suffices
to prove all four g_t+sigma*g_x+tau*g_y are nonnegative.
The proof allows arbitrary mixed A; it is not a pure-ray owner restriction.

3. THE TWO POSITIVE-x RESTORATION RAYS
For tau in{+1,-1}, algebra gives
 g_t+g_x+tau*g_y
   =a*(t-tau*y)/(t+tau*y)*[(t-v)+tau*(y-w)].          (5)
Because v<=u and C-A is in K,
                  t-v>=t-u>=|y-w|.
All factors in(5) are nonnegative. This proves the two cases sigma=+1.

4. THE TWO NEGATIVE-x RESTORATION RAYS
Set G_tau=g_t-g_x+tau*g_y. It is affine in(v,w), with coefficients
 L_v=a+2*t*(t-tau*y)*(x+tau*y)/H^2,
 L_w=-tau*a-2*y*(t-tau*y)*(x+tau*y)/H^2.              (6)
For tau=+1,
 L_v+L_w=2(x+y)/(t+y)^2>=0,
 L_v-L_w=2/(t-y)>0, and L_w<=0.
For tau=-1,
 L_v+L_w=2/(t+y)>0,
 L_v-L_w=2(x-y)/(t-y)^2>=0.
Thus always L_v>=|L_w|. Put h=(t-x)/2. The order-interval constraints imply
                  v>=max(-u,x-t+u),   |w|<=u.
The relaxed lower bound
 L_v*max(-u,x-t+u)-|L_w|u
drops the outsider constraint on w, so is no greater than the true minimum.
It is minimized at u=h, v=-h and
w=-sign(L_w)*h: for u<=h the lower bound decreases with u, and for u>=h
it increases with u. Both candidate reports(h,-h,+h) and(h,-h,-h) lie in
[0,C]_K when0<=y<=x<t: their outsider report is
((t+x)/2,(t+x)/2,y +/- h), whose last coordinate has magnitude
at most(t+x)/2. Thus the relaxed bound is attained in the original order
interval. It therefore suffices to evaluate these endpoints.

For tau=+1, L_w<=0 selects(h,-h,+h), and direct substitution gives
                     min G_+=2(x-y)/(t-y)>=0.         (7)
For tau=-1 the two possible endpoint values are
 G_- at(h,-h,-h)=2(x+y)/(t+y)>=0,
 G_- at(h,-h,+h)
   =2-(t-x)/(t+y)-(t-x)(x-y)/(t-y)^2.                (8)
The final expression is nonnegative: its second term is at most1, while
its third is at most1/4 since the two nonnegative factors t-x and x-y sum
to t-y. In fact this endpoint value is at least3/4. Equations(7)-(8) prove
the remaining two ray inequalities. Hence g is in the dual cone.

5. ALL-PROFILE TCWM, SEAMS AND BOUNDARIES
Restore an arbitrary coalition's individual bundles by D_i in K; their sum
D is in K. Along the straight restoration path the coalition and grand
aggregates are A(s)=A(0)+sD and C(s)=C(0)+sD, with outsiders fixed.
At every interior smooth point, Sections2–4 imply dW/ds=g dot D>=0.
The finitely many sign/permutation regions are separated by the hyperplanes
x=0,y=0,x=y,x=-y. A straight path crosses them finitely often or follows a
seam. The explicit prices agree on every interior seam; the payment is
locally Lipschitz, and the one-sided extensions have nonnegative path
derivatives on a followed seam as well. Thus integrating the nonnegative
derivative proves monotonicity on
an interior path, including all crossings and tangencies.

Production is nondecreasing under cone restoration. If the high endpoint has
F=0, the whole path has zero payments. If the high endpoint is interior and
the low endpoint is on the boundary, every positive path parameter is interior
(each previously zero facet slack acquires a positive increment). Continuity
of payments at the low endpoint, from the bound0<=W<=2F(C), passes the same
inequality to the boundary. This proves all-profile TCWM without changing
owners, restrictions on mixed holdings, or a hidden regularity assumption on
the reports. The formula itself is homogeneous, but no homogeneity assumption
was used in the matching lower theorem.
