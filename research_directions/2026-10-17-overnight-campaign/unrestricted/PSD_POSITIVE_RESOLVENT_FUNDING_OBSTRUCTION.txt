POSITIVE RESOLVENT MIXTURES CANNOT IMPROVE THE DIMENSION COEFFICIENT
Frozen 2026-10-17. Architecture theorem; no unrestricted mechanism lower.

MODEL AND STATEMENT
There are fixed actual owners with PSD reports X_i; C=sum_i X_i is positive
definite and T=lambda_min(C). A common spectral price pays
 y_i=Tr(P(C)X_i),       P(C)=f_C(C)/Tr(f_C(C)).
It is enough to define a positive number f_j on each eigenvalue lambda_j.
Assume the products h_j=lambda_j*f_j are nondecreasing with lambda_j.
Then the total output funding obeys

 sum_i y_i = (sum_j lambda_j f_j)/(sum_j f_j)
           >= m/(sum_j 1/lambda_j).                         (1)

Consequently any such architecture, on all positive-definite PSD_m
profiles, has worst-case total-output funding factor at least m. This
holds even before imposing any incentive condition. It is not an
impossibility theorem for arbitrary owner-aware mechanisms.

EXACT PAIRWISE PROOF
Order eigenvalues increasingly. Expanding and pairing terms gives
 (sum_j lambda_j f_j)(sum_j 1/lambda_j)-m sum_j f_j
   = sum_{i<j} [(lambda_j-lambda_i)/(lambda_i lambda_j)]
                *(lambda_j f_j-lambda_i f_i) >=0.
All denominators in(1) are positive. On C_L=diag(1,L,...,L), the right
side of(1) divided by T is m/[1+(m-1)/L], tending to m as L tends to
infinity. Each C_L is an admissible aggregate: it may be owned by one
actual owner with the other fixed owners reporting zero. Alternatively
split it as C_L/n among n positive-definite actual owners. The formula
for total payment is independent of this split.

POSITIVE RESOLVENT MIXTURES
In particular the assumption holds for
 f_C(lambda)=a_C + integral_[0,infinity) 1/(lambda+s) dmu_C(s),
where a_C>=0, mu_C is a nonnegative measure, evaluations are finite, and
the resulting prices are not all zero. Indeed lambda and
lambda/(lambda+s) are nondecreasing functions of lambda. The weights,
shifts and measure may depend arbitrarily on the entire reported profile,
on T, on m, on n, or on public labels; at a single profile the same scalar
function must be applied to all eigenvalues. Thus arbitrary adaptive
mixtures of positive shifts do not evade the bound.

Report-dependent convex mixtures of already normalized resolvent prices
also satisfy(1), either by linearity of the funding total or by absorbing
each component's normalization into its positive weight. Positive scalar
powers f(lambda)=lambda^(-p), 0<=p<=1, satisfy the same condition directly
because lambda*f(lambda)=lambda^(1-p). No integral representation theorem
is needed for this conclusion. Multiplying the trace-one price by a scalar
at least1 only increases the lower bound.

LIMITS AND RELATION TO PRIOR OBSTRUCTIONS
Pure normalized inverse price, f(lambda)=1/lambda, gives equality in(1)
at every profile and has worst funding factor m. Thus this lower bound is
sharp for the stated spectral-order architecture as a funding claim.
Its normalized inverse incentives are a separate issue; this note does
not infer them from(1). The already established unnormalized inverse rule
T*C^-1 gives a full coalition outside-production guarantee and exactly
mT funding; see PSD_INVERSE_PRICE_FULL_OUTSIDE.txt.

Negative shifts C-theta*T*I fall outside the positive-resolvent hypothesis:
their lambda*f(lambda) is decreasing. PSD_SHIFTED_HARMONIC_STOPPING.txt
separately bounds that family's possible leading-constant improvement by
a commuting individual-incentive obstruction. Spectral Huber prices also
escape this order condition but fail individual incentives on an exact
two-owner noncommuting example. None of these statements excludes a new
owner-aware construction or a genuinely different common-price class.

CHECKER
verify_psd_positive_resolvents.py checks the exact pairwise identity and
nonnegativity for rational positive resolvent mixtures and the limiting
funding arithmetic. The all-dimension theorem is the analytic identity.
External priority review has not been performed; this elementary identity
is used as a campaign stopping result, not claimed as a new matrix theorem.
