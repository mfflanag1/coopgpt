OWNER-COUNT FRONTIER UNDER COALITION-TOTAL WITHHOLDING MONOTONICITY
17 October 2026. Corollaries and successor contract; not the original weak-IC gap.

DEFINITION
Let K_TCWM(n) be the supremum over finite resource dimensions and finite-valued
nonnegative monotone concave homogeneous production functions F of the infimum
all-real-profile funding factor, for n fixed actual owners, under nonnegativity,
coalition coverage and TCWM. Owners have fixed eligibility sets and may hold
mixed resource bundles. Utilities are payments only. This stronger-incentive
quantity differs from the manuscript's individual-own-monotonicity quantity.

LOWER COROLLARY OF SHARP_TCWM_MULTIGLOVE_THEOREM.txt
For t>=2, use r=2t+1 resource types, s=t, and n_t=2t^2+1 actual owners.
The sharp theorem's finite lower becomes
 K_TCWM(n_t) >= 1+(t-1)(2t^2-1)/(4t^2-2t-1).
Therefore
 liminf_{n->infinity} K_TCWM(n)/sqrt(n) >= 1/(2sqrt(2)).
For n>=9, take t=floor(sqrt((n-1)/2)) and add n-n_t owners who
always report zero. Restrict any candidate n-owner rule to this report face,
and discard those extra owners' nonnegative payments. The resulting payments
for the n_t active owners preserve all their coalition-coverage and TCWM
inequalities and have no greater total. Thus the finite lower transfers.
One may instead give the extra owners empty fixed eligibility sets. For n<9
use only the trivial lower1; the displayed finite family requires t>=2.

OPTIMIZATION WITHIN THIS PARTICULAR LOWER FAMILY
Write m=(r-1)s, u=s-1, L=m-1=n-2. The excess lower factor is exactly
 beta=L*u/[2u^2+2u+L].
For fixed L>0 and real u>=0, its derivative has the sign of L-2u^2.
Hence its continuous maximum is attained at u=sqrt(L/2), and equals
 L/[2+2sqrt(2L)]. This proves that the asymptotic coefficient1/(2sqrt(2))
is optimal WITHIN THIS PARAMETER FAMILY. It says nothing about a sharp
unrestricted lower coefficient. Integer divisibility may reduce finite optima;
the sequence above asymptotically attains the continuous envelope.

ROUTINE UNIVERSAL UPPER
For every nonnegative monotone superadditive F with F(0)=0, set
 y_i(b)=[F(c)+F(b_i)]/2, c=sum_i b_i.
Every coalition's total is coordinatewise nondecreasing, since each term is.
A singleton receives at least its standalone worth. If |S|>=2, the global
term alone gives y(S)>=|S|F(c)/2>=F(b(S)). Nonnegativity is immediate.
Superadditivity yields sum_i F(b_i)<=F(c), hence total <=(n+1)F(c)/2.
Concavity and positive homogeneity imply the required superadditivity.
Thus, for the strong class, the currently established owner-count interval is
 (1/(2sqrt(2))+o(1))*sqrt(n) <= K_TCWM(n) <= (n+1)/2.
These are proved bounds, not a claim that this interval is historically novel.

FULL OUTSIDE-PRODUCTION EXTENSION OF THE UPPER (ROOT OBSERVATION)
Suppose coalition S withholds bundles d_i>=0, i in S, and can add outside
production F(d_S) to its new total payment; write c'=c-d_S and b_i'=b_i-d_i.
The loss of its payments under this rule equals
 |S|[F(c)-F(c')]/2 + sum_{i in S}[F(b_i)-F(b_i')]/2.
Superadditivity implies F(c)-F(c')>=F(d_S). For |S|>=2 this suffices.
For |S|=1 both bracketed differences separately dominate F(d_i), so their
half-sum also does. Thus y(S;b)>=y(S;b')+F(d_S) for every coalition.
This is a separate, stronger incentive statement with explicitly modeled
outside production. It does not turn the sharp (r+1)/2 TCWM upper into an
outside-production upper; that integral rule does not have this property.

NEXT BOUNDED TARGET
Seek a general o(n) TCWM upper in owner count, or an exact larger asymptotic
lower family. A new finite example alone fails the gate. An architecture
obstruction qualifies only if it identifies a necessary structural ingredient
for a genuinely different rule. Candidate route: compensate negative payment
externalities by a budget potential, exploiting concavity/superadditivity.
The original weak-IC owner/dimension gap remains live and is not resolved by
these stronger-incentive results.
