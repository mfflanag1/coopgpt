PROPER-DIMENSIONAL LOCAL COMPRESSION RULES CAN COST LINEARLY IN OWNERS
17 October 2026. Proposed structural composition obstruction; audit pending.

THE ARCHITECTURE AND QUANTIFIERS
Fix m>=2,n>=2, and one report-independent Borel probability law on matrices Q with
Q Q^T=I_r, where the possibly varying rank satisfies 1<=r<m almost surely.
Thus Q is an orthonormal compression to a proper public subspace, chosen by
the law before reports. The law may depend on m,n and owner labels. For each
realized Q, apply ANY nonnegative local rule z_i^Q to the compressed PSD_r
reports QX_iQ^T, with the SAME actual owners. Assume that this local rule covers
every local coalition pointwise. It may otherwise be owner-aware, nonlinear,
and arbitrarily strong; the sharp PSD_2/Lorentz rule is included when r=2.
All expected payments are assumed measurable. Extra local randomization is
allowed if the local coverage holds at each realization, or if its expectation
is taken to obtain z before the cap.

The global payment is one of
 y_i=E_Q min{z_i^Q,T},                                        (A)
 y_i=E_Q min{z_i^Q,V_i},                                      (B)
where T=lambda_min(sum X_i) and
 V_i=max_(R subset N\{i})[F(X_R+X_i)-F(X_R)].
The cap is applied per owner before averaging as displayed. The same lower
also applies if one averages z first and caps afterwards: its singleton
lower bounds remain positive on the family below.

For EVERY fixed law and EVERY such family of local covering rules, there is
a sequence of profiles, with those SAME n owners, all f_i=lambda_min(X_i)=0,
and positive grand gain G=T decreasing to0, for which
       total(A)/G -> n,       total(B)/G -> 1+n/2.             (1)
The second equality is for the specified witness sequence and architecture;
it is not a claim that 1+n/2 is its sharp worst-case factor. In particular,
both architectures have worst funding Theta(n), already for m=3,r=2 with
arbitrarily growing owner count. In (A), n is exactly the supremum factor
because the cap always gives total<=nT. Also V_i<=T: every coalition value
is nonnegative and bounded above by T, so every defining marginal is at
most T. Thus (B) likewise always has total<=nT, completing its stated
Theta(n) upper bound.

These conclusions use a profile chosen after the fixed law, which is the
correct order for a worst-profile lower bound. The law cannot depend on the
adversarial small parameter or on the reported matrices. For each fixed n
the limit in (1) is taken first; the conclusion then holds for every n>=2.

1. THESE ARE VALID GLOBAL INDIVIDUAL-OUT COMPOSITIONS IF THE LOCAL RULE IS
Compression preserves PSD order and satisfies
 lambda_min(QXQ^T)>=lambda_min(X).
Thus a locally covering vector also covers every original global coalition.
Capping such a vector at T preserves coverage: if a coalition contains a
capped member, that member alone supplies T; otherwise its payments are
unchanged. Capping instead at V_i also preserves coverage. Indeed, for the
capped members C of a coalition S, the uncapped remaining coalition R=S\C
already receives at least v(R), and adding the members of C one at a time
increases value by at most sum_(i in C)V_i.

If the local rule protects individual full outside production, then under
an own PSD restoration D each local payment increases by at least
lambda_min(QDQ^T)>=lambda_min(D). Both T and V_i also increase by at least
lambda_min(D), the latter termwise inside its maximum. The minimum of two
functions with that increment property preserves it. Averaging therefore
gives global individual full OUT. This is a genuine eligible composition
class, not a lower bound obtained by assuming an invalid incentive transfer.
General coalition OUT after ownerwise capping is not asserted.

2. ONE FIXED KERNEL DIRECTION MISSED ALMOST SURELY
For any proper subspace S of R^m, a uniformly distributed unit vector v lies
in S with probability zero. Apply Fubini to sphere measure and the law of Q:
there is a fixed unit v such that
                   P_Q(v in range(Q^T))=0.                   (2)
This handles varying ranks and atoms in the compression law.
The Fubini integrand is measurable: on each fixed-rank Stiefel piece,
v belongs to range(Q^T) exactly when Q^T Qv=v, a closed condition in (Q,v).
There are finitely many possible ranks, so their disjoint union is Borel.
Choose a unit
w orthogonal to v. Put v_epsilon=(v+epsilon*w)/sqrt(1+epsilon^2), epsilon>0.
For owners1,...,n-1 set X_i=I-vv^T; for owner n set
X_n=I-v_epsilon v_epsilon^T. All are rank m-1 and have standalone value zero.
Their kernels have trivial common intersection, so their sum is positive
definite and G_epsilon>0. Yet its Rayleigh quotient at v is
epsilon^2/(1+epsilon^2), hence G_epsilon tends to0.

For almost every Q in(2),
 Q(I-vv^T)Q^T=I_r-(Qv)(Qv)^T
has minimum eigenvalue gamma_Q=1-||Qv||^2>0. The last owner's compression
converges to this same positive-definite matrix. Local SINGLETON coverage
alone therefore forces every z_i^Q to stay bounded below by a positive
quantity while G_epsilon tends to0. No continuity of the local payment rule
is needed. Eventually every ownerwise cap equals its global cap.

For (A), min{z_i^Q,G_epsilon}/G_epsilon therefore tends to1 almost surely
and lies in[0,1]. Dominated convergence, then summation over the fixed n,
proves total(A)/G_epsilon->n. In particular the original standalone reserves
all vanish, so this is also an obstruction to a uniform gain-only bound.

3. THE MAXIMUM-MARGINAL CAP STILL HAS A LINEAR OBSTRUCTION
Write eta=epsilon^2/(1+epsilon^2). A coalition containing the special owner n
and k common-kernel owners has value
 g_k(eta)=[k+1-sqrt((k+1)^2-4k eta)]/2, k>=0.                  (3)
Here g_0=0. A coalition without n has zero value. Formula(3) is the smaller
eigenvalue in span{v,w}; all remaining eigenvalues equal k+1.
The function k -> lambda_min(k(I-vv^T)+(I-v_epsilon v_epsilon^T)) is concave
and nondecreasing on k>=0, so its successive increments are nonincreasing.
Consequently, with G=g_(n-1),
                V_n=G,        V_i=g_1 for i<n.                (4)
All V_i tend to zero. The same local singleton bound thus makes cap(B)
equal to V_i almost surely for each fixed Q eventually. Moreover V_i/G<=1,
so dominated convergence applies to normalized payments again. Rationalizing
(3) gives g_k(eta)/eta -> k/(k+1). Thus
 lim total(B)/G=1+(n-1)*[(1/2)/((n-1)/n)]=1+n/2,
which proves the second assertion of(1). The cap substitution cannot remove
the linear owner dependence.

4. IF THE LAW HAS FULL-DIMENSIONAL MASS
If the law places mass alpha on r=m, apply the same argument to its proper
part. Every full-dimensional realized capped vector still covers the grand
coalition and so has total at least G. Therefore the lower limits are at least
 (1-alpha)n+alpha for (A),
 (1-alpha)(1+n/2)+alpha for (B).
If alpha=1 the proper-compression obstruction says nothing: such a local
ingredient is already a mechanism on the original full PSD_m problem.

5. AN EXACT RATIONAL FIXED-COMPRESSION SPECIAL CASE
Take m=3 and Q projecting onto the first two coordinates. For n-1 owners let
X_i=diag(1,1,0). Let the last owner report
 X_n=[[16/25,0,-12/25],[0,1,0],[-12/25,0,9/25]].
It is the rank-two projector orthogonal to (3/5,0,4/5). All owners are
singular and every local standalone value is at least16/25. Grand value is
positive and at most9/25 by its third-coordinate Rayleigh quotient. Hence
every local payment in(A) is capped at G, giving total EXACTLY nG at this
one rational profile, for every local covering mechanism.
Here g_1=1/5 and G<=9/25, so(B) costs at least
                   [1+5(n-1)/9]G.
The exact checker verifies the PSD matrices, positive grand determinant,
compression minima, and these cap-forcing inequalities for growing n. The
general random-law theorem follows from the analytic Fubini/limit proof.

6. SUBTRACTING LOCAL STANDALONES LOSES GLOBAL COMPLEMENTARITY
One cannot repair the result by automatically discarding each local
standalone baseline. With m=3, the two original singular reports
 X_1=diag(1,1,0), X_2=diag(0,1,1)
have G=T=1. Project onto the first two coordinates. Local grand production
is1, local standalone sum is1, and local cooperation gain is0. A local
gain-only component with those local standalones removed pays zero, yet the
original coalition is productive. Any such subtraction needs a new global
coverage argument; the black-box local guarantee does not supply one.

7. ADAPTIVE COMPRESSION CAN LOSE ALMOST ALL OF THE ORIGINAL GAIN
There is a distinct obstruction even when Q is selected from the reports.
Let n=m and X_i=I-e_i e_i^T, i=1,...,m. Original standalone values vanish;
C=(m-1)I and the original gain is G=m-1. For ANY orthonormal rank-r
compression Q, with 1<=r<=m, write u_i=Qe_i. Then
 QX_iQ^T=I_r-u_i u_i^T,
 f_i^Q=1-||u_i||^2,
 sum_i f_i^Q=m-Tr(Q^TQ)=m-r.
The compressed grand value is m-1. Consequently its cooperation gain is
                         G_Q=r-1.                            (5)
This identity is independent of the orientation of Q and therefore remains
true for adaptive or random compressions. In particular, any rank-two
compression retains only 1/(m-1) of the original cooperation gain.

Suppose a composition transfers local components e_i^Q whose total is at
most kappa G_Q, with a nonnegative weighting measure of total mass W. If
all compressed ranks are at most r, its total is at most W*kappa*(r-1).
Grand-coalition coverage at this profile requires
                    W >= (m-1)/(kappa*(r-1))                 (6)
when r>1, and is impossible from these components alone when r=1. This
also applies algebraically to e_i^Q=z_i^Q-kappa*f_i^Q whenever the local
rule has total at most kappa times its local grand value. Such subtraction
does not automatically preserve componentwise nonnegativity or incentives;
neither property is asserted here.

Equation(6) is a scaling requirement, not a mechanism lower bound. Scaling
does not automatically produce a globally bounded mechanism: local gains
can also EXCEED the original gain. For example, the original matrices
diag(1,0,0) and diag(0,1,0) have global gain0, whereas compression onto the
first two coordinates has gain1. An adaptive composition therefore needs
both a coverage argument and an upper comparison to original gain.

CONCLUSION AND LIVE BOUNDARY
The sharp owner-aware PSD_2 mechanism remains valid locally. What fails is
report-independent proper-dimensional composition followed by ordinary caps:
local compression can turn globally singular ownership into positive local
standalone value. A report-dependent compression chosen to contain a global
minimum-eigenvalue direction escapes this lower bound but loses the automatic
own-report incentive argument. A decomposition that genuinely tracks global
complementarity, or a new owner-aware matrix construction, is still required.
This is an architecture obstruction, not an unrestricted PSD lower bound.
