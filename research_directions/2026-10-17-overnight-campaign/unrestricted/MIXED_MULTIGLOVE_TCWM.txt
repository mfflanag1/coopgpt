MIXED-OWNER MULTI-GLOVE PRICES SATISFY COALITION-TOTAL MONOTONICITY
17 October 2026. Proposed corollary of the manuscript's existing integral rule.
Independent review and historical priority comparison pending.

DOMAIN AND STRONGER INCENTIVES
Fix r resource types and any finite set of actual owners, each with a fixed
eligibility set and a nonnegative reported bundle b_i. Production is
 F(c)=min_{1<=j<=r}c_j, c=sum_i b_i.
Payments are payment-only utility; withheld capacity has no outside return.
TCWM means that every actual coalition's TOTAL payment is nondecreasing when
any one member increases any owned coordinate, holding all others fixed.
This implies immunity to joint coordinatewise underreports with transfers.
It is stronger than individual own-report monotonicity and weaker than
absence-proofness models that add outside production from withheld resources.

RULE
If T=min_j c_j=0, pay everyone zero. Otherwise use the existing prices
 p_j(c)=(1/c_j) int_0^T product_{l!=j}(1-t/c_l) dt,
 and pay y_i=sum_j b_ij p_j(c).
The existing manuscript proves pure-pool eligibility and total funding
 <=(r+1)T/2. Both coverage and this funding proof depend only on totals and
 therefore hold verbatim for arbitrary mixed owners. In detail,
 sum_j p_j=1 by differentiating product_j(1-t/c_j), so
 y(S)=sum_j b(S)_j p_j >=min_j b(S)_j=F(b(S)).
A bottleneck resource contributes at most T to total funding. Every other
resource contributes at most int_0^T(1-t/T)dt=T/2. Thus total <=(r+1)T/2.
This constant is the supremum for this rule at totals (1,M,...,M), M->infinity.

TCWM PROOF, INCLUDING MOVING BOTTLENECKS
Fix a coalition S, and increase resource e belonging to one member.
Write x=b(S)_e and a=c_e-x, so a is fixed throughout this comparison.
For each j!=e, the equivalent expression
 p_j(c)=(1/c_j) int_0^{c_j} product_{l!=j}(1-t/c_l)_+ dt
is nondecreasing in c_e: its integrand increases pointwise. Coalition S's
reported amounts of these other resources are fixed and nonnegative.
Its own resource-e contribution equals
 x p_e = [x/(x+a)] int_0^{x+a} g_e(t)dt,
 g_e(t)=product_{l!=e}(1-t/c_l)_+.
Both factors are nonnegative and nondecreasing in x, so this contribution
also cannot fall. This argument does not differentiate the minimum T and
covers all changes of bottleneck resource.
If a resource total is zero, all coalition payments and worths are zero;
changes leaving some total zero preserve this, and a change making all
positive starts from zero and ends nonnegative. Hence TCWM holds on the
entire nonnegative real report domain.

CONSEQUENCE AND LIMIT
For r=3 this gives TCWM funding factor2 for every fixed mixed ownership of
single three-way minimum production. More generally it gives (r+1)/2 for
unbounded r and owner counts. The theorem is a direct strengthening of the
already established integral-rule result, not a new unrestricted owner- or
dimension-growth theorem, and it does not extend to arbitrary rank-r packing
hypergraphs merely by summing edge mechanisms (resources may be shared).
The original formula is in supplement.tex, proposition hist:aggregate.
