CONCLUSION
The new direct PSD_m construction with n=m actual owners does not follow by
the usual exact linear lift of the canonical scalar architecture examples.
Those examples have exponentially many essential linear pieces, even when
their scalar resource dimension equals their owner count. Any exact symmetric
linear pencil representing that whole scalar technology needs at least as
many matrix rows as essential pieces. Thus scalar dimension and PSD matrix
order cannot be identified in this comparison.

AN EXACT LINEAR-PENCIL SIZE LEMMA
Let f(x)=min_(ell=1..L) a^ell dot x on R_+^d. Assume the coefficient vectors
a^ell are distinct, and each form is the unique minimizer at some point in
the strictly positive orthant. Suppose fixed REAL SYMMETRIC m by m matrices
A_j satisfy
              f(x)=lambda_min(sum_(j=1..d) x_j A_j)             (P)
for every x>=0. Then m>=L. Positive semidefiniteness of the individual A_j
is not needed for this lower bound.

Proof. Fix ell and choose a positive point x0 where that form uniquely wins.
It remains the unique minimizer on an open neighborhood U of x0. Let v be a
unit minimum eigenvector of M(x0)=sum x0_j A_j. For x in U, (P) implies
 D(x)=M(x)-(a^ell dot x)I is PSD and v^T D(x0)v=0.
The quantity v^T D(x)v is linear in x, nonnegative on U, and zero at its
interior point x0. Its coefficient vector is therefore zero. For every x in
U, v^T D(x)v=0, and PSD implies D(x)v=0. Varying x independently in each
coordinate within U now gives
                    A_j v=a^ell_j v for every j.
Thus each essential form supplies a nonzero common eigenvector for all A_j.
If ell differs from h, some j has a^ell_j!=a^h_j, and the corresponding
common eigenvectors are orthogonal because A_j is symmetric. There are L
nonzero mutually orthogonal vectors in R^m. Hence m>=L. QED.

For the canonical arch:tbarrier technology, L=2^n-1. The diagonal lift
A_j=diag(a^1_j,...,a^L_j) achieves order L, so this exact whole-technology
linear representation bound is sharp. Its old sqrt(n) lower gives only
Omega(sqrt(log(m))) when expressed in that lifted matrix order. The lemma
also prevents a noncommuting symmetric linear pencil of order m=n from
representing exactly the same scalar technology. It does NOT exclude a clever
smaller embedding of only finitely many certificate profiles, a nonlinear
report encoding, or an independent antecedent with fewer essential pieces.
No assertion that all conceivable prior reductions fail is made.

WHAT THE NEW DIRECT ARGUMENT ADDS
PSD_ORTHOGONAL_MASK_OBSTRUCTION.txt constructs matrix order m and n=m owners
directly inside the SINGLE technology lambda_min on PSD_m. A weighted
orthogonal frame, randomly rotated for the averaging argument, forces closed
cost >=(4/5)(m delta-1), where delta is the average missing fraction. An
averaged active-label junta forces open cost >=J(1-J delta), without any
independence of the mask bits. The resulting lower is sqrt(m/10)-4/5 for
either the global or maximum-marginal cap. This is a parameter-specific
architecture theorem, not an unrestricted mechanism lower bound.

Every individual adverse profile is commuting, but the diagonal basis varies
across profiles and is not public. One fixed public commutative algebra has
only m fixed linear forms. The canonical finite-K method (prod:upper and
its finite-support extensions) gives order log(K)/loglog(K) there. No
contradiction: the new rule must handle all rotated profiles in PSD_m with
one retained-only support-selection policy. Matrix order m is also distinct
from the ambient real vector-space dimension m(m+1)/2 of symmetric matrices.

WHAT REMAINS VIABLE BEYOND THIS ARCHITECTURE
Changing only the report-independent mask distribution, including arbitrary
correlation, or optimizing the retained-only common support cannot improve
the sqrt(m) order under the stated caps. Replacing G by V_i also does not help.
A useful next positive attempt must change the coverage decomposition, permit
a different information pattern for supports, or use a structurally restricted
matrix domain. For example, owner-specific supports depending only on other
owners retain a possible individual-incentive route, but do not automatically
give coalition coverage. A profile-dependent mask law likewise needs a new
incentive proof. Neither is claimed valid here.
