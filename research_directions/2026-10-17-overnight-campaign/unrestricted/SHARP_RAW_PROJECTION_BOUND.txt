SHARP STATIC UPPER-COVER PROJECTION FUNDING
2026-10-17. Elementary geometric observation; no novelty claim.

Let P={x>=0:x(S)>=v(S) for all S} and assume P contains a vector a>=0
with sum a=T. Let x be its minimum-Euclidean-norm point. Then
 sum_i x_i <= (1+sqrt(n)) T/2.
This is sharp for production games along every square owner count n=t^2.
The true own-report envelope is larger and is NOT proved to satisfy this bound.

PROOF.
First-order optimality with the feasible vector a gives
 ||x||^2 <= x dot a <= T u, where u=max_i x_i.
Thus 0<=u<=T. Cauchy–Schwarz on the remaining n-1 coordinates yields
 sum_i x_i <= u+sqrt((n-1)(Tu-u^2)).
Writing u/T=(1+s)/2 and using Cauchy–Schwarz on (s,sqrt(1-s^2)) gives
 (sum_i x_i)/T <= [1+s+sqrt(n-1)sqrt(1-s^2)]/2 <=(1+sqrt(n))/2.
For n=1 the same conclusion is immediate.

EXACT PRODUCTION SHARPNESS.
Fix integer t>=2, m=t+1, k=t-1, n=mk+1=t^2. There is one veto resource0
and m groups of k resources. Every resource has a distinct actual owner.
Use the monotone concave homogeneous production function
 F(c)=min{c_0, sum_{g=1}^m min_{j in g} c_j}.
At the unit profile, positive coalitions are exactly those containing owner0
and every member of at least one group. The only minimal cover constraints
are x_0+sum_{j in g}x_j>=1 for all groupsg. Symmetry and strict convexity give
 x_0=m/(m+k)=(t+1)/(2t), x_j=1/(m+k)=1/(2t),
with each constraint tight and nonnegative KKT multiplier1/(2t).
Consequently T=1 and sum x=(t+1)/2=(1+sqrt(n))/2.
The efficient core includes a=(1,0,...,0). At the maximizer,
 u=(1+1/sqrt(n))/2, exactly the equality condition in the geometric bound.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE LIVE ENVELOPE TARGET.
Any universal envelope bound must be at least(1+sqrt(n))/2 along squares.
The existing exact own-underreport computation for this veto/group example
shows that its envelope at the unit profile equals x; see the formula in
MIN_NORM_DELETION_OBSTRUCTION.txt. Thus it gives no envelope inflation.
The stronger conjecture sum_i M_i<=(1+sqrt(n))T/2 is plausible but unproved.
The simpler sufficient conjecture sum_i M_i^2<=T^2 would instead imply sqrt(n)T.
No new eligible payment theorem follows from the static calculation alone:
raw projection fails own-report monotonicity already with three owners.
