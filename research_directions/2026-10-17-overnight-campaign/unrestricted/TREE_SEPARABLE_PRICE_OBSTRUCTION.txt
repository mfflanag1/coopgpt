THEOREM (architecture-specific)
Fix any convex nondecreasing h:[0,1]->R which is C2 near t0=1/3, with
0<h'(t0)<infinity and0<h''(t0)<infinity. The rule that minimizes
  J_c(p)=sum_v c_v h(p_v)
over0<=p<=1 and sum_{v in e}p_v>=1, then pays each owner i sum_v b_iv p_v,
fails own-report monotonicity on some finite unit-profit triple-input
hypergraph whose incidence graph is a TREE and whose resource degree is<=2.
There are only TWO actual fixed owners. At the base profile every resource
capacity is1 or2. The profitable underreport changes only one actual owner's
root resource. All relevant minimizers are unique, interior, and all edges
retain positive KKT multipliers. No active-set switch or tie-breaking is used.

The quantifier is one FIXED regularizer used across all these hypergraphs.
The result does not exclude graph-dependent h or nonlinear owner-specific
mechanisms, and is not an unrestricted subsidy lower bound.

CONSTRUCTION
Take a complete rooted binary resource tree of depth D>=1. It has2^l resource
vertices at level l=0,...,D. For each nonleaf resource vertex, create one
activity consuming that parent and its two children, with unit profit.
Every activity has arity3. The incidence graph is connected and has
3(2^D-1) edges and3*2^D-2 vertices, hence is a tree. The root and leaves have
resource degree1; other resources have degree2.
Give each resource capacity equal to its degree:
 c_0=1, c_l=2 for1<=l<D, c_D=1 (constant within each level).
Owner I owns all resources at EVEN levels; owner II owns all ODD levels.
Their eligibility sets and identities are fixed throughout.

At the base profile all prices p_v=t0=1/3 are feasible and every activity is
tight. Giving every activity multiplier h'(t0)>0 satisfies stationarity
c_v h'(t0)=sum_{e incident v}lambda_e. Convexity proves global optimality.
Since h is strictly convex in a neighborhood of t0 and every capacity is
positive, any different minimizer would contradict strict convexity along the
initial part of the segment joining it to this point. Thus it is UNIQUE.

PERTURB THE ROOT
Increase only owner I's root capacity from1 to1+s, s>0 small. Seek a level-
symmetric all-tight solution
 p_l=t0+tau z_l,  z_l=(-1/2)^l.
Every activity constraint holds exactly, because z_l+2z_{l+1}=0.
The derivative of J along this one-dimensional family is
 G(s,tau)=sum_{l=0}^D (-1)^l(c_l+s*1_{l=0}) h'(t0+tau z_l).
At(0,0), G=0 because sum_l(-1)^l c_l=0. Its tau derivative is
 h''(t0) Q_D, where
 Q_D=sum_{l=0}^D c_l/2^l=3(1-2^{-D})>0.
Therefore the implicit-function theorem supplies tau(s) near0 with
 tau'(0)=-h'(t0)/[h''(t0) Q_D].                       (1)

This stationary all-tight candidate is the TRUE unique optimizer, not just
a restricted-family minimizer. To see it directly, let lambda_{D-1}=
c_D h'(p_D) at the leaf edges, and recurse upward
 lambda_{l-1}=c_l h'(p_l)-lambda_l  (1<=l<D).
At s=0 every lambda_l=h'(t0)>0; all remain positive by continuity.
The root equation lambda_0=(1+s)h'(p_0) is exactly G(s,tau)=0.
Thus full KKT stationarity and complementary slackness hold at every vertex
and activity. Prices stay strictly between0 and1 and in the local strict-
convexity region. Convexity gives global optimality and uniqueness as above.

OWNER PAYMENT
Owner I's aggregate payment is
 Y_I(s)=sum_{even l}2^l(c_l+s*1_{l=0})(t0+tau(s)z_l).
Since2^l z_l=1 on even levels,
 sum_{even l}c_l = D,
whether D is even or odd. Differentiating at0 and using(1),
  Y_I'(0)=1/3-[h'(1/3)/h''(1/3)] D/[3(1-2^{-D})].    (2)
For any fixed h this is negative when D is sufficiently large; it is enough
that D>h''(1/3)/h'(1/3). Hence increasing a genuine owned capacity lowers
that owner's payment for every sufficiently small positive s. The reverse
change is a profitable binding underreport in the SAME actual-owner model.

EXACT ORIGINAL-HUBER EXAMPLE
Use h(t)=t^2 below1/2 (the graph rule) and D=3. Then there are15 resource
vertices and7 activities. Level capacities are(1,2,2,1), and owner I holds
the root plus the four level2 resources. Initially all prices1/3, all edge
multipliers2/3, and owner I receives(1+4*2)/3=3.
Increase only root capacity1->2. The unique optimizer prices by level are
  (7,11,9,10)/29.
They are all below1/2. Activity multipliers by parent level are
  (28,16,20)/29.
The edge equations are7+2*11=29,11+2*9=29,9+2*10=29.
Stationarity is respectively
  root: 2*2*(7/29)=28/29;
  level1:2*2*(11/29)=(28+16)/29;
  level2:2*2*(9/29)=(16+20)/29;
  leaf:2*1*(10/29)=20/29.
Owner I now receives2*(7/29)+4*2*(9/29)=86/29=3-1/29.
No numerical optimization is used in this certificate.

SIGNIFICANCE AND LIMITS
The graph theorem's fixed Huber regularization genuinely exploits pairwise
activity incidence. Moving to three-way complementarity loses the derivative
invariant even when the activity-resource incidence remains acyclic, degrees
are bounded, capacities are tiny, and ownership consists of two fixed parties.
A rank-three extension therefore needs a different owner-payment architecture,
a graph-dependent regularizer with a new global funding argument, or additional
restrictions beyond incidence acyclicity. This theorem does NOT claim that
truthful, covering cooperation is impossible on these hypergraphs: with only
two actual owners an efficient all-profile rule already exists in the general
concave production model. It isolates the failure of the stated price rule.
