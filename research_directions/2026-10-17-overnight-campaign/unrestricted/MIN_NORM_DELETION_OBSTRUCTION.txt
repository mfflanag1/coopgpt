UNBOUNDED OBSTRUCTION TO THE MINIMUM-NORM DELETION BOUND
17 October 2026. Exact architecture obstruction; the own-envelope remains open.

PRODUCTION FAMILY
Fix integers m,k>=1. There are n=1+mk fixed actual owners, each owning one
resource: center0, and mk leaves partitioned into m disjoint groups of size k.
Technology is
 F(c)=min{c_0, sum_{g=1}^m min_{j in g} c_j}.
It is nonnegative, monotone, concave and homogeneous. Equivalently m activities
share the center resource; activity g consumes one unit of the center and of
every leaf resource in group g. All reported capacities are1. Grand production
is T=1. A coalition produces1 exactly when it contains the center and every
leaf of at least one group; all other coalitions produce0.

MINIMUM-NORM COVERS
The complete nonnegative coalition-cover polyhedron therefore reduces to
 x_0+sum_{j in g}x_j>=1 for g=1,...,m.
Strict convexity and group/leaf symmetry give x_0=a and every leaf b. Tightness
and minimization of a^2+mk*b^2 subject to a+kb=1 give
 a=m/(m+k), b=1/(m+k), R^2=m/(m+k).
This can also be certified by assigning dual multiplier1/(m+k) to every group
constraint: the resulting coordinate loads are exactly x and all rows are tight.
Deleting the center removes all positive coalition constraints, hence R_{-0}^2=0.
Deleting any leaf disables its group; the k-1 other leaves in that group become
null players. The same calculation with m-1 groups gives
 R_{-j}^2=(m-1)/(m+k-1),
 delta_0=R^2=m/(m+k),
 delta_j=R^2-R_{-j}^2=k/[(m+k)(m+k-1)].

FAILURE OF THE PROPOSED SQUARED-DELETION INEQUALITY
The deletion sum is
 D=m/(m+k)+mk^2/[(m+k)(m+k-1)].
For m=k=t it equals
 D=1/2+t^2/[2(2t-1)],
which tends to infinity, whereas T^2=1. Thus no constant multiple of T^2 can
bound this deletion sum on all production games. The smallest symmetric
instance t=2 has n=5 and D=7/6>1.

FAILURE OF THE DIRECT SQUARE-ROOT-DELETION PAYMENT RULE
Root observed that Y_i=sqrt(R^2-R_{-i}^2) is itself individually own-monotone,
nonnegative, and coalition covering. Own monotonicity follows because R^2
increases when own-report coalition constraints increase, while R_{-i} is fixed.
Coverage follows by setting coordinate i of x to zero, which is feasible for
the deletion polyhedron: R_{-i}^2<=R^2-x_i^2, hence Y_i>=x_i.
However on this family its funding is exactly
 sum_iY_i=sqrt[m/(m+k)]+mk*sqrt[k/((m+k)(m+k-1))].
For m=k=t this becomes
 1/sqrt(2)+t^2/sqrt[2(2t-1)] = (1/2+o(1))*n^(3/4).
So this otherwise valid rule cannot match the manuscript's O(sqrt(n)) bound.
This is an architecture obstruction, not an unrestricted mechanism lower bound.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT RULE OUT
The original own-report monotone envelope of the minimum-norm cover may be
much smaller than the square-root-deletion majorant. At the profile above it
actually equals x. To see this, reduce one leaf's sole capacity from1 to u.
Only its group constraint changes to x_0+sum_{j in g}x_j>=u. The payment to
that leaf is
 max{0, [(m+k-1)u-(m-1)]/[k(m+k)]},
which increases with u. Reducing center capacity scales all positive group
requirements and the entire minimum-norm cover linearly. Thus no owner gains
by underreporting from the all-unit profile, and the envelope total there is
 m(k+1)/(m+k), only(t+1)/2 when m=k=t.
A sqrt(n) upper for the ACTUAL own-envelope is therefore still a live question;
its proof must use more than the total decrease in minimum squared norm.

VERIFICATION
verify_min_norm_deletion_obstruction.py checks all displayed identities and
primal/dual stationarity with rational arithmetic for thousands of m,k pairs.
The formulas and symmetry/strict-convexity proof establish every integer pair.
The earlier five-owner three-edge-path numerical discovery in
qp_hypergraph_deletion_probe.json has D=31/30; the two disjoint groups here
are a strictly simpler and stronger counterexample.
