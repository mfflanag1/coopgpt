1. CORRECT THE QUANTIFIER IN THE FIXED-WEIGHT SUMMARY
CAMPAIGN_OUTCOMES.txt lines41–44 and NEXT_CAMPAIGN_GOAL.txt lines40–43 say
EVERY fixed nonnegative whole-owner marginal-weight rule has worst funding
order Theta_r(s^(r-1)). This is stronger than root64 and is literally false for
rules that overpay by an arbitrarily larger factor. Root64 supplies an Omega
lower for every eligible rule and an O upper from an explicit rule. Theta is
the optimal worst-case order within the architecture.

Suggested common replacement:
  "The optimal worst-case funding factor within report-independent nonnegative
  whole-owner marginal weighting is Theta_r(s^(r-1)). Every eligible rule obeys
  the lower bound, and the scaled-binomial rule supplies the matching order.
  Owner-specific, asymmetric and population-dependent fixed weights are included."

This correction does not change the next-goal conclusion: merely changing those
fixed weights cannot improve the quadratic exponent at arity three. The exact
16-owner factor5 versus unrestricted min3 factor2 comparison is correctly
identified as an architecture separation, without asserting sharpness of5.

in composition's note is also architecture-only and does not contradict the
