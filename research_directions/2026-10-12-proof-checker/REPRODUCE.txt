## Recount the saved capture

Create a fresh results directory so the archived capture stays unchanged:

```sh
proof_results=$(mktemp -d /tmp/coop-proof-recount.XXXXXX)
python3 -B score_capture.py . capture --output "$proof_results/SUMMARY.json"
python3 -B audit_runtime.py . capture --output "$proof_results/AUDIT.json"
python3 -B -O audit_runtime.py . capture --output "$proof_results/AUDIT-O.json"
cmp "$proof_results/AUDIT.json" "$proof_results/AUDIT-O.json"
python3 -B - "$proof_results" <<'PY'
import json, sys
from pathlib import Path
folder = Path(sys.argv[1])
summary = json.loads((folder / 'SUMMARY.json').read_text())
audit = json.loads((folder / 'AUDIT.json').read_text())
if summary != audit['quality_summary']:
    raise SystemExit('Primary and independent summaries differ')
print('Primary and independent summaries agree')
PY
```

`score_capture.py` reads only the 24 `collection/i_ID.json` inventory captures
and 24 `collection/p_ID.json` primary captures. It concatenates the exact
`requests[0].chunks[*].data.response` strings in recorded order, then supplies
both maps to `contract.summarize` using bare case IDs. It does not infer, repair,
parse-and-reserialize model answers, or treat prompts and cached summaries as
responses. Controls are deterministic offline proposals and require no model
calls. Warmup is unscored. Missing files remain missing and make
`input_complete` false; malformed raw containers raise an error.

The independent auditor checks frozen inputs, all 48 raw call bindings and their
chronology, transport, clocks, host gates, and cleanup before reporting
`data_complete_valid` and `qualified`. A scoring summary alone cannot establish
capture validity. Incomplete or aborted captures are not qualified subset runs.
The normal and `-O` audits must agree. The auditor recomputes quality independently
of `contract.summarize`.

To check deterministic construction, copy the study into a disposable directory
and run `python3 -B build_cases.py` followed by `python3 -B build_controls.py`
there. Compare `cases.json`, `BLIND_CASES.json`, and `controls.json` against the
frozen originals. Never rebuild or relabel the canonical study after outcomes.
Source URLs, commits, byte hashes, excerpt offsets, transformations, and licenses
are recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`, the cases, and `sources/`.

## A separate experimental rerun

A new collection is a new experiment, separate from saved-data reproduction.
Prepare a separately frozen study copy and choose a nonexistent output path;
do not overwrite or append to the canonical `capture` directory. For example,
from that prepared copy:

```sh
proof_replication=$(mktemp -d /tmp/coop-proof-replication.XXXXXX)
python3 -B collect.py --output "$proof_replication/capture"
```

Run once after host readiness is established. The collector verifies
`FREEZE.json`, the pinned Qwen3:32b model and Ollama 0.20.0 binary/manifest,
context 4096, single-slot operation on localhost port 11562, idle original port
11434, acceptable power state, and nominal thermal readings. Do not stop the
original server to make readiness pass. Readiness or runtime failure ends this
attempt; preserve its artifacts instead of retrying calls or filling gaps.

The fixed schedule is one unscored warmup, then all 24 inventories at cap 384,
then all 24 primaries at cap 256 in the same SHA256(`order:` + case ID) order.
Warmup cap is 32. All jobs exist before inference. The budget is 1800 seconds
with a 300-second reserve; scored calls retain the 90/95/100-second timeout and
owned-server watchdog limits. No answer-dependent scheduling, prompt changes,
repairs, replacements, or budget extensions are permitted.

This screen records execution diagnostics. It does not measure energy, serving
performance, or reviewer cost, and it does not authorize a follow-on runtime or
energy experiment. See `PROTOCOL.txt` for the frozen gates and claim limits.
