Independent design critique: blind per-component evidence inventory
12 October 2026
Scope: design review only. Read the completed evidence-checker REPORT.txt and
PROTOCOL.txt. No inference, canonical edits, or external claims were made.

RECOMMENDATION
Proceed to a separately frozen, fresh 24-claim engineering screen, conditional on
fixing the citation contract and the decomposition review described below. The
architecture is a defensible response to the observed errors, but it is an
independently generated answer plus deterministic comparison, not a verified
proof checker. Its explicit failure mode remains correlated semantic error by the
primary and inventory model. Do not describe a successful screen as proving the
mechanism, improving the underlying model, or establishing reliability.

1. SCOPE AND CLAIMS
The proposed 48 scored calls comprise 24 primary answers and 24 inventory calls.
The constructed proposals and JSON variants are local deterministic evaluations,
not additional model calls or independent replications. The experimental unit is
the claim/packet. There are 24 such units, with all proposals for a claim sharing
one inventory. Freeze those proposals before inventory or primary outcomes.

Reuse establishes presentation invariance of the parser/comparator for the tested
JSON equivalence class by construction. It does not demonstrate robustness of an
LLM exposed to differently serialized candidate answers, nor identify the cause
of the earlier discordant pair. Correctly state that the architecture removes
candidate presentation from the inventory model's input. Quote-free JSON parsing,
key order, whitespace, and citation-array order can be tested without inference;
invalid syntax and duplicate object keys should remain format referrals.

A comparison to the failed checker on a different fresh cohort is descriptive.
This screen does not identify the causal contribution of decomposition, blind
inventory generation, deterministic aggregation, or changed citation policy. A
same-case randomized ablation would be needed for that; it is outside this budget.
The reused source snapshots support packet-relative claims only. Same-model
stateless generation is input-blindness, not independence of errors or sources.

2. DECOMPOSITION AND ANNOTATION LEAKAGE
Supply both the unmodified claim and two neutrally worded components to the
inventory model. Components are an authored interface feature, not model-discovered
reasoning. If the primary also sees them, freeze that choice and acknowledge the
primary task changed; keeping the primary's original interface is the cleanest
way to isolate the proposed workflow operationally.

Before inference, a reviewer who does not see component gold, proof sets, controls,
or old outcomes should certify that the conjunction of the two components is
logically equivalent to the original claim. Preserve shared antecedents, scope,
quantifiers, temporal conditions, entities, and referents in each component.
Do not silently turn "if A, B and C" into unconditional B/C, split a disjunction,
or discard a qualifier. Components should not introduce a necessary premise as
though it were an extra conjunct, clarify an ambiguity using gold, or name an
excerpt/citation. Use neutral IDs, consistent ordering and formatting. Avoid
words such as "actually", "merely", or "fails" when absent from the claim.

Blind review must also certify that packets permit the three-valued aggregation
rule. Exclude or prospectively repair packet inconsistencies, unresolved referents,
and cases where packet-level implications refute the conjunction without refuting
either individual conjunct. For example, evidence "not both A and B" contradicts
A-and-B although neither A nor B is individually contradicted. Likewise, a packet
can make A and B jointly impossible through a shared constraint. Any-C aggregation
would incorrectly return insufficient in such cases. Restrict this bounded cohort
to independently evaluable factual conjuncts and explicitly state this limit; a
later general design needs a joint-consistency/whole-claim evidence channel.

Require that this restriction is checked from claim and packet, not inferred after
observing model errors. Balance the 24 claim labels if desired, but do not reveal
the balance or component-label distributions. Case/control/error labels must never
enter inventory requests. Freeze author disagreements and prospective repairs.

3. INVENTORY AND DETERMINISTIC CITATION CONTRACT
Do not require equality with one decisive citation list. That would reject
alternative valid proofs, repeating the previous contextual-citation concern in a
new form. Prefer this finite proof-family contract for packets with 3-4 excerpts:

For each component, inventory returns:
- supported, contradicted, or insufficient;
- for a decisive relation, every identified inclusion-minimal sufficient citation
  set establishing that relation, as a nonempty list of nonempty ID sets;
- for insufficient, an empty proof-family list.

The prompt must explicitly search the whole packet for support and contradiction
before choosing insufficient. A rationale or "searched all" flag is not evidence
that the search succeeded. If compact search notes are desired, keep them diagnostic
and never substitute them for proof-family accuracy.

For the runtime comparator, parse citations as a finite set C (while rejecting
syntactically duplicated IDs under the frozen schema). Reject unknown IDs, missing
or duplicate components, unsupported fields, empty decisive proof families,
nonempty insufficient families, and other schema errors to REVIEW. Validate
inventory structurally, independently of any candidate; never repair it from the
candidate or gold. Invalid inventory routes every associated candidate to REVIEW.

Aggregate: supported if both components supported; contradicted if at least one
is contradicted; otherwise insufficient. Compare the candidate verdict to this
aggregate before citation checks.

For aggregate supported, form E from every inventory support proof P with P a
subset of C. Accept citation coverage iff E contains a proof for each component
and the union of all proofs in E equals C.

For aggregate contradicted, form E from every inventory contradiction proof P
for a contradicted component with P a subset of C. Accept iff E is nonempty and
the union of all proofs in E equals C. Do not require decisive evidence for the
other component: one false conjunct refutes the entire conjunction.

For aggregate insufficient, require empty candidate citations. The inventory may
still contain a valid support proof for the known component. "Insufficient" does
not require both components to be insufficient. Candidate empty citations are
correct for the aggregate even though the inventory has component-level evidence.

This policy admits alternative proofs and extra complete corroborating proofs,
but rejects an arbitrary extra citation that participates in no sufficient proof.
It avoids conflating redundant evidence with irrelevant evidence. Freeze exactly
this interpretation in both the candidate rubric and independent scorer. If the
intended product instead requires a minimal proof, that is a different policy and
must be stated prospectively; "no irrelevant citations" alone does not mean minimal.

An inventory can still omit a legitimate alternative or invent a sufficient set.
Those are semantic inventory errors, not a reason to relax the comparator or amend
gold after outcomes. Gold authors can enumerate all 15 nonempty excerpt subsets
for each component when there are four excerpts, identify sufficient sets, and
reduce to the minimal antichain before collection. This modest exhaustive human
annotation makes alternative-proof scoring substantially more credible. The model
receives no such gold table. Return enough output tokens for the bounded proof
families; the old 64-token binary-checker cap is plainly unsuitable.

4. CHALLENGE DESIGN
Include the prior missed failure directly in fresh controls: an invalid
insufficient proposal where the packet decisively supports all components, and an
invalid insufficient proposal where one component is decisively contradicted.
Pair each with a valid proposal on the same claim. Otherwise the explicit new
insufficient-search requirement is not directly challenged.

Also include support missing one conjunct/premise, unsupported contradiction,
correct contradiction from only one false conjunct, alternative valid proof sets,
a context-dependent multi-excerpt proof, and irrelevant extra citations. Spread
these deliberately across the 12 paired claims before inference; categories may
overlap and need not be represented by disjoint groups of four. Preserve adequate
valid supported, contradicted, and insufficient controls. Do not select controls
from primary failures or from the resulting inventory.

Use JSON variants of exactly the same candidate object with key/whitespace changes;
if citations are formally unordered, include citation-order permutations. Freeze
the equivalence relation and canonicalization tests. Parser malformed-input tests
should include duplicate keys, duplicate IDs, unknown IDs, and extra prose.

5. GATES AND REPORTING
Keep the native zero-accepted-error, >=18-correct-retained, and >=90%-correct-
retention gates, along with 12/12 invalid rejected and >=11/12 valid retained.
All denominators and integer threshold formulas must be frozen. The prior
report's distinctions between per-offered completion and conditional accepted
quality remain essential. REVIEW is an unresolved referral, not a correction.

Require valid raw data, all 48 calls, inventory schema validity, and exact binding
of packet/component/input hashes to each inventory. Keep the existing runtime,
freeze, and lifecycle requirements. A primary format failure is not a semantic
error caught. If no schema-valid native error occurs, native sensitivity is
unobserved even if the screen passes.

Report inventory quality separately from downstream decisions: exact component
relations (48 slots), aggregate verdicts (24 claims), soundness/completeness of
proof families, omitted decisive evidence behind an insufficient relation,
invented decisive evidence, and schema failures. Do not treat 48 component slots
as independent observations. For every rejection, distinguish verdict mismatch,
citation-coverage failure, format failure, and invalid inventory mechanically.
Native and controlled tables remain separate; deterministic repeats add no sample
size. Report valid-control retention by verdict and alternative-proof status.

Do not silently add a new inventory-semantic gate to the main screen. If inventory
quality is intended to qualify a reusable evidence artifact, specify a separate
prospective threshold and report its result separately. Even with no such extra
gate, erroneous inventories forbid claims that the architecture verified every
part or faithfully identified evidence. Main-screen success would qualify only
the bounded accept-or-review workflow for planning a later test.

6. PRACTICAL ACCEPTANCE CHECKS BEFORE COLLECTION
- Freeze 24 fresh propositions, component-equivalence certificates, packet-level
  consistency checks, full gold proof families, controls, schemas, and comparator.
- Use synthetic unit cases to exercise all 3x3 component relation combinations,
  especially supported+insufficient and contradicted+insufficient.
- Test one-conjunct contradiction, cross-excerpt premises, multiple proof
  alternatives, redundant corroborating proofs, and arbitrary extra citations.
- Independently reconstruct comparator decisions from raw responses without
  calling the model or relying on production scoring helpers.
- Ensure the token/output/wall budgets can complete the richer 24 inventories;
  set caps prospectively and retain truncations as failures, with no retries.
- Keep native primary outputs immutable and emit only those originals on accept.

Overall: the strongest version is a blind, per-component answer inventory with
finite alternative proof families, a precise deterministic coverage comparator,
and explicit restrictions on what conjunctions its aggregation can represent.
It is useful because it removes candidate anchoring and presentation dependence
from one stage. Whether it catches omitted decisive evidence while retaining valid
answers remains the empirical question, not an architectural guarantee.
