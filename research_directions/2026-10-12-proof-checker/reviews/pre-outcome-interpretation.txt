Post-run interpretation and decision wording — prepared without outcomes
12 October 2026
Basis: frozen proof-checker PROTOCOL.txt and the repository README as read during
collection. No raw responses, intermediate results, or hardware were inspected.

Lead the final report with the frozen gate result and exact counts: primary joint
quality B/24; retained correct K/24; accepted errors E; referrals R; invalid controls
rejected X/12; valid controls retained Y/12; usable inventories U/24. Keep native,
controlled, and inventory-semantic results separate. Then state audit validity and
any inventory mistakes or omitted alternatives. A catch does not certify diagnosis.

If all gates pass:
"The blind component-inventory workflow passed its predeclared engineering screen
on these 24 authored documentation claims. It retained K correct original answers,
accepted zero observed errors, and referred R cases. This qualifies the restricted
workflow for planning a separately frozen downstream-serving study; performance
and energy measurements have not been activated."
If the primary made no schema-valid errors, append: "Catching naturally occurring
primary errors remains unobserved; the constructed challenges do not replace that
evidence." If inventory semantics are imperfect, state those counts immediately;
a workflow pass does not mean every component proof was correct or complete.

If valid collection fails a quality gate:
"The workflow failed its predeclared [name] gate. [Concrete failure counts.] This
attempt is closed with its frozen labels and all outcomes preserved. The selected
configuration is not qualified for downstream performance or energy testing."
A failure establishes nonqualification under this contract, not impossibility of
useful grounded inference, local serving, or pooling generally.

If capture, runtime, freeze, or cleanup validity fails:
"The attempt did not produce a qualifying valid collection because [specific
fault]. Available outcomes are retained descriptively; they do not establish the
planned qualification result." Separate that validity failure from any observed
semantic failure. Do not restart, repair the frozen study, or discard offers.

Limits that belong beside either result:
- Filtering cannot increase correctly completed original answers: K <= B. An
  increase in accuracy among accepted answers is a conditional denominator change,
  not demonstrated operator benefit. Referrals consume unknown human time.
- The 24 claims are authored and selected. Shared inventories make controls and
  JSON variants dependent. Forty-eight calls and up to96 local variant checks do
  not create independent samples or a population error bound.
- The architecture depends on human decomposition and component-separable claims.
  Same-model input blindness does not make semantic errors independent.
- Earlier cohorts differ in tasks, packets, decomposition, prompts and rules.
  Higher or lower scores do not demonstrate model improvement, deterioration, or
  the causal effect of this architecture. Preserve earlier results separately.
- Collection durations/load are diagnostics. No throughput, net useful-work,
  energy-efficiency, ownership-pooling, incentive, or economic-value result follows.

Recommended portfolio decision, regardless of pass or fail:
Consolidate this bounded result and stop automatic checker redesign on another
24-case cohort. Another microstudy needs an explicit project-level reason: what
remaining decision it would resolve, why existing evidence cannot resolve it, and
why that information is worth its cost. A sequence of increasingly tailored
qualification screens is not progress toward a measured production frontier.

The next planning decision should identify one actual infrastructure user task,
its acceptable error/referral burden, and the comparator against which useful
completion is valuable. Human decomposition/annotation, the extra inventory call,
and referral effort must be included in that workflow's cost. A passing screen
may support designing that comparison; a failing screen requires choosing whether
to change the workload/model materially or suspend this application, rather than
implicitly committing to another checker patch. No new experiment is proposed
for automatic execution here.

Keep the broader empirical target visible: quality-qualified useful completions
under declared deadlines and resource budgets; then compatible full-horizon energy
accounting, including idle and failed work. Two-hardware comparisons and actual
independent-owner pooling remain additional requirements. Local documentation
qualification, even if passed, supplies only one prerequisite and supplies no
validation of the portfolio's economic pooling mechanism or theoretical claims.
