MEASUREMENT CRITERION
Client SLO collection needs stable request identities, scheduled arrivals,
observed dispatch and first received content, finish/status for every offered
request, and synchronized full-window GPU board-power telemetry including idle.
Comparable configurations also need a repeatable workload and recorded model,
precision, software, serving settings, and hardware provenance. Failure and
rejection records must remain in the offered-request denominator.

Actual GPU execution-start and per-token timestamps are not prerequisites for
these client SLOs. First received content is a client-visible latency measure:
a streamed chunk may contain multiple tokens, and its receipt is not an exact
GPU first-token timestamp. Report chunk timing and server token counts separately;
do not infer token timestamps from chunk boundaries. No source below is rejected
solely for lacking GPU execution-start measurements.
