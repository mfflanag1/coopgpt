Mac-only resource model and next informative experiment

The user has one Mac. Represent it as one shared-memory machine and one GPU
execution service. GPU cores are execution units within that device, not resource
owners or independently reservable cards. Logical requests/tenants can share a
machine, but do not supply independent economic ownership or feasible withholding.

For a later request j, describe its operation, input dimensions, arrival a_j,
declared deadline d_j and verified output condition. Track queue delay and full
arrival-to-validated-result time. Count a job useful only if the answer passes
the predetermined correctness rule; a fast invalid answer is not useful throughput.
The present matrix products have an exact independent oracle. This gives a
controlled compute primitive, not a representative AI serving workload.

Memory has one system total U. An engineering admission policy needs conservative
application reservations b_j and headroom H for the operating system, background
activity and runtime allocations. A condition sum(b_j)<=U-H is meaningful only
after those quantities are justified. It cannot be obtained by adding physical
RAM to a fictitious second pool of VRAM, or by declaring all unused Metal buffer
bytes available. The current calibration records explicit buffer/reference bytes
and allocation samples; it does not estimate a validated H or worst-case b_j.

Timing t_j depends on warmup, dimension, competing operations, background desktop
load and scheduling. A three-repeat calibration is not a stationary service-time
model. Do not feed these medians to an optimizer and call its predictions measured
deadline performance. Independently freeze and measure an offered request stream.

Recommended next experiment, conditional on passing this calibration
Compare a serial FCFS queue with at most two operations in flight on identical
fixed mixed-size arrival streams. Include the same useful-output checks and count
all offered requests, including late, failed and unfinished requests. Measure
latency from offer to validated completion, including allocation/encoding and
validation. Device command duration remains a separate diagnostic.

Freeze the arrival rates and deadlines before those comparison outcomes. Keep
runtime/code/input values fixed, counterbalance configuration order, and retain
an explicit idle/warmup/drain interval. Record power source, thermal diagnostics,
memory observations and background interference. First ask whether concurrency
improves useful completion latency or only increases contention. A negative result
would reject that proposed local optimization for the tested workload.

No such policy comparison was run in this calibration. Before claiming an AI
infrastructure application, replace or supplement the artificial matrices with
one real application whose output correctness and resource profile are verifiable.
The earlier small-model documentation-answering configuration remains unqualified.

Energy follows useful-work validation. Attribute any measured whole-device energy
to the full concurrent interval once; do not sum overlapping per-job intervals.
Apple component-power estimates, if available, are not wall-plug energy. A causal
energy benefit needs a comparable control and the entire offered-work horizon.

Cooperative-game mechanism claims remain on their mathematical track. A one-Mac
queueing result cannot demonstrate heterogeneous-owner pooling, strategic
truthfulness, subsidies or the generality of the manuscript's theorems. Multiple
independent resources and economically meaningful rights/costs would be needed
for that empirical connection.
