Independent prospective critique: infrastructure-documentation claim verification

Recommendation
Proceed to one bounded, independently adjudicated 12-development / 36-confirmation case pack for a documentation-review assistant. Do not run the candidate until the source packets, labels, accepted evidence, scorer and gate are frozen. Keep one Qwen3:32b configuration and one prompt; no fallback, retries or response-informed case repair. This is a more credible operator-assistance proxy than KEEP/DROP, provided the cases require checking scope and conditions rather than finding a quoted string. It still does not establish real operator demand, time savings, operational safety, energy gains or pooling value.

Concrete service boundary
The prospective user is an engineer reviewing a proposed deployment/configuration statement against its cited, version-specific documentation. The system triages that statement and supplies inspectable sentence references; a person retains the decision. It neither retrieves an unspecified web corpus nor executes a configuration change. The claim is assessed only against the supplied packet and its explicit version/product/conditions. “Insufficient” means not established by that packet, not false in the world.

Use exactly two preselected official infrastructure documentation collections, pinned to immutable commits/releases and archived with hashes, licensing/provenance and source URLs. Partition documentation sections before drafting claims; no paragraph or closely repeated page/section may cross development and confirmation. Keep exact excerpt text, including qualifiers, tables converted by a frozen rule, and contextual definitions necessary to interpret pronouns or terms. Avoid fragments whose meaning depends on omitted warnings, headings, defaults or footnotes. Record version explicitly in the packet.

Case construction: six cells, fixed before responses
Cross three verdicts with two reasoning forms:
- Direct: one explicit statement determines a supported/contradicted verdict, or the necessary direct fact is absent for insufficient.
- Conditional/compositional: two statements must be combined, a scope/condition must be applied, or a necessary premise is missing. This half is necessary to test more than toy lookup.
Use 2 development cases and 6 confirmation cases per cell: 12 and 36 total. Each case has one atomic operator proposition, including its material condition/quantity if any. Give 6–10 numbered sentences drawn from 2–3 short, coherent excerpts, with plausible nearby distractors and a fixed packet-length envelope (approximately 150–350 words). Preserve sentence order within each excerpt. Do not pad every short packet mechanically. Match lengths and source/style distributions across verdicts so packet size, claim syntax, sentence IDs and negation words do not reveal labels.

Claims should resemble decisions an engineer would actually verify: whether a documented behavior holds under stated prerequisites, whether a value is a default or a hard constraint, or whether a claimed configuration combination is documented as supported. Require reviewers to record the concrete operator question served by each case. Do not fill the suite with spelling substitutions, arbitrary numeric perturbations or naked “not” flips. Some explicit negation/quantity contradictions are useful controls, but conditional scope and documentation terminology must carry real weight in the compositional half. Avoid multipart claims and opinions such as “best,” “safe” or “fast” unless the packet defines a testable meaning.

Three-way label contract
Supported: the supplied statements jointly entail the entire claim, with compatible scope and conditions.
Contradicted: the packet entails its negation or an incompatible value/condition. A stated maximum can contradict a greater claimed maximum; a recommended value does not automatically contradict another permissible value. A default is not a hard limit. A statement that a feature requires a condition does not contradict its use when that condition is unspecified; that is normally insufficient.
Insufficient: neither the claim nor its negation follows from the packet. Do not use failure to mention a feature as proof that the feature is unavailable. Distinguish intention from achieved effect—the precise ambiguity encountered in the preceding QA audit.
Exclude internally conflicting packets, materially ambiguous wording and disputed entailments during pre-response adjudication. Maintain an exclusion log and draw replacements from a predeclared ordered reserve before freezing. Selection/adjudication remains a constructed benchmark process, not sampling representative operator traffic.

Evidence scoring that avoids another exact-span trap
Output exactly {"verdict":"supported|contradicted|insufficient","evidence":["S1",...]}, with unique valid sentence IDs and at most three citations. Object whitespace/key order and evidence-list order are irrelevant. No free-text explanation is required.
For each supported/contradicted case freeze:
1. A family G of minimal sufficient sentence-ID sets, including every materially distinct acceptable proof found in review.
2. A set R of relevant corroborating IDs that may accompany a sufficient proof without being misleading.
Evidence passes when at least one G-set is contained in the returned IDs E, E is contained in R, and |E|<=3. Thus extra relevant corroboration is accepted; irrelevant citation dumping, incomplete two-sentence proofs and citations supporting a different claim are rejected. Construct packets so all reviewed valid proofs fit the three-citation limit; do not silently discard longer legitimate proofs merely to retain an item.
For insufficient require evidence=[] and explain in the public instructions that evidence IDs mean decisive support/contradiction, not merely topical context. Absence itself is not a sentence that can be cited. Reviewers privately record exactly which fact/premise is missing. If useful partial-context citations are desired, that is a different output contract and should not be improvised after responses.

Report schema validity, verdict correctness, evidence sufficiency, evidence relevance and joint success separately. Joint success requires the correct verdict and the applicable evidence rule; a correct verdict with invalid citations is not a useful verified completion. JSON failure counts as a task failure, while transport/host/clock failures are separately infrastructure-invalid. Do not translate an evidence-format failure into a factual-error claim.

Independent label/evidence review before inference
One author prepares each claim and packet. A second reviewer receives only the packet, claim and public label rules, independently labels it, proposes sufficient evidence sets and records scope assumptions. Reconcile discrepancies before model access; freeze both initial judgments and adjudication. For supported and contradicted cases, the reviewer should test whether removing any purportedly necessary evidence sentence destroys the proof. For insufficient, explicitly search the complete packet for a counter-witness; do not infer insufficiency from the author's intended label. A third adjudication is required for genuine disagreement; unresolved cases are excluded prospectively. Model-assisted review must be disclosed and is not equivalent to independent domain-expert validation.

Bounded qualification gate
Development: all 12 schemas valid; joint success >=11/12; >=3/4 within each verdict; >=5/6 within each reasoning form. No false-supported verdict on a gold contradicted or insufficient case. These criteria imply at least 1/2 per crossed cell. Run all 12 once; advance only after complete valid collection passes.
Confirmation: all 36 schemas valid; joint success >=33/36; >=10/12 per verdict; >=16/18 per reasoning form; >=5/6 in every crossed cell; again zero false-supported verdicts on non-supported claims. The additional zero false-support condition reflects the tool's purpose: claiming documentary support without it is the most consequential error. It is a finite engineering screen, not proof that its population false-support rate is zero.
A completed quality-gate failure ends the experiment. An infrastructure failure is an abort, not permission to revise or rerun the same cases. Preserve all raw responses, scored errors, partial/orphan captures and unattempted-stage status. Reuse the reviewed serial lifecycle, resource limits and host gates only after its owner confirms those prerequisites; this critique does not authorize or perform a launch.

Baselines and leakage limits
Always-insufficient is a frozen 1/3 verdict baseline and a weak floor only. Add one genuinely informative diagnostic before inference: a fixed lexical sentence ranker returning the top three sentences, evaluated for whether those citations contain an accepted minimal proof on supported/contradicted cases. It does not produce reliable three-way verdicts and must not be advertised as a competitive full-task baseline. If this retrieval-only method nearly saturates evidence coverage and the candidate's claims are mostly direct restatements, the suite has demonstrated lookup assistance rather than much reasoning value; report that honestly. Do not tune the ranker on candidate outputs.
Balanced synthetic labels and official-document familiarity are not representative workload frequencies. Pretraining exposure remains possible even with pinned sources and fresh claims. Confirmation prevents within-study tuning, not training contamination. Gold labels/proofs stay out of prompts and filenames/IDs visible to the model; balance language cues rather than giving all contradicted claims an obvious template. No correctness-based packet edits after first inference.

What passing would justify
Passing would justify a separately designed, human-reviewed service study on fresh real operator claims: assess whether the tool reduces review effort while preserving correctness, then measure arrivals/latency and full-horizon energy if warranted. It would not itself prove economic usefulness, superiority to a strong specialist/rule/retrieval workflow, deployable automation, general infrastructure expertise or pooling gains. The immediate next deliverable is the adjudicated frozen case pack and scorer, not a throughput sweep.
