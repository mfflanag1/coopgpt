Why this workload
The prior SQuAD qualification ended at 8/12 under its exact-span/abstention rule.
That result remains unchanged. This different task asks whether an engineering
claim is supported, contradicted, or not established by supplied documentation,
with excerpt citations. It is an informational assistant for reviewing GPU-serving
and scheduler claims, not a configuration agent. The operator/service use is a
plausible research proxy; real demand and time savings have not been established.

Fixed success gate
All 24 schemas valid, at least 22/24 correct verdict-plus-citation answers, at
least 7/8 supported and 7/8 contradicted cases, 8/8 insufficient cases, and zero
false-supported predictions on contradicted/insufficient claims. Run every case
once unless infrastructure fails; no retuning, relabeling, second model or retry.
Independent raw-response integrity checks must pass before reporting qualification.
The exact implementation is contract.py; the full design is PROTOCOL.txt.
