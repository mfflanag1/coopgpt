The one miss
Case 333f65d97eb66b804f84 asserts that the described GPU device plugin permits a
container to request half a physical GPU through its custom resource field.
The model returned {"verdict":"contradicted","evidence":["D2"]}.
The frozen answer is {"verdict":"insufficient","evidence":[]}.

D2 states the GPU request/limit placement, default and equality rules. Neither
D2 nor the other supplied excerpts establishes fractional allocation granularity
or permission. Equality of request and limit is compatible with equal fractional
values as a logical constraint; it does not by itself establish that such values
are admitted or forbidden. The operational behavior of the full platform is
outside this packet-relative task. The missing granularity premise makes the
frozen label defensible. This error is a false contradiction, not false support.
The response has no explanation, so outside-knowledge intrusion versus an invalid
inference from D2 cannot be distinguished from the saved output.

Limits and decision
The frozen lexical top-three ranker already locates complete proofs for all 16
decisive cases, though it includes irrelevant citations in every returned set.
It supplies no three-way verdict and is not a competitive full-task baseline.
Always-insufficient scores 8/24 on verdicts alone. The observed model score is a
narrow supplied-documentation result, not proof of broad reasoning or superiority
to operator review. Source snapshots can be development versions; public text
may be in pretraining. Actual operator demand, review time saved, operational
safety, energy efficiency and independent-owner pooling remain unmeasured.
The earlier SQuAD 8/12 outcome stays unchanged. Different tasks and rubrics do
not establish that the model improved between studies.

This study stops at the failed gate. A useful follow-up would test whether a
separately designed evidence-verification workflow catches unsupported decisive
verdicts, including false contradictions, on fresh independently adjudicated
cases. The present miss can become a development example, never a new held-out
success. Any follow-up needs its own frozen design; none was executed here.
Until a useful-work contract qualifies, more energy or concurrency sweeps would
not resolve the present semantic failure. Compatible measurements from two
hardware configurations and actual independent-owner counterfactuals remain
later requirements for the broader AI-infrastructure pooling project.
