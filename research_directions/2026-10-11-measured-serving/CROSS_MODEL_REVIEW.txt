**F2 (Low — research validity). Watt held-out "<10%" gate has near-zero discriminating power.**
watt_validation.py:125-132: 6 training runs per GPU at only two rate levels (0.017, 0.33 qps) fit a two-coefficient no-intercept model; held-out runs are repeats at the same two rates. With N ≈ rate×duration inside each group, the fit is effectively two group means and the hold-out tests repeat-to-repeat variance. REPORT.txt:62 states "all eight held-out errors under 10%" and line 63 correctly labels them conditional, but the pass should not be cited as model validation. Verification: refit on one rate, test on the other; expect failure.
