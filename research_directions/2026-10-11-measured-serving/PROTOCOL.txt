QUESTION AND DECISION
Can two fixed owners gain on-time completed requests from transferable metered
energy allowances after finite requests, online scheduling, and response deadlines?
Keep hardware fixed. Both arms share the same dispatch algorithm and demand queue.
Only the budget accounting changes. This is a conditional numerical diagnostic;
physical compatibility and independent full-horizon measurements are mandatory
before promoting a real inference consortium or extending to private costs/power.

REQUEST REPLAY
Each unique heldout job arrives once; order is lexicographic prompt SHA256.
Deterministic interarrival seconds:30,10,3. Absolute response deadline is
arrival +30,60,120 seconds. No batching; one serial job per configuration.
Physicalslot=API duration+4s: .5sprebuffer + .5spostbuffer +3scooldown.
Response endpoint=start+API duration+.5s, which excludes3.5s post-response tail.
This conservative experimental padding is an assumption, not new measured latency.
Source energy is the GPU request-window quantity including default +/- .5s
buffers. Do NOT subtract an invented baseline. Between-window idle/cooldown energy
is UNMEASURED and excluded. Therefore the energy allowances in this diagnostic
cover measured windows only, not a deployment electricity budget.
Queue: oldest eligible waiting job; among idle eligible configurations choose
smallest predicted GPU joules, tie by index. Eligibility requires forecast
response<=deadline and sufficient predicted energy reservation. Actual energy
settled on slotcompletion; budget overruns and actual late completions retained,
never retroactively rejected or clipped. Controller cannot inspect future events.
Autarky reserves each owner's allowance; pooling shares their sum. Demand, resource
inventory, admission information, predictions, and policy are identical. Pooling
means rights to energy on the same accounting boundary can move across nodes.
This is not necessarily a globally optimal online policy in either arm.
