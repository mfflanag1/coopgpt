Measured serving / energy study — reproduction

Python 3.10+ standard library only; no GPU, network, model weights or paid access
needed for the saved experiment. From this directory:

    python3 reproduce.py --output /tmp/coopgpt-measured-rebuild

Choose an output directory that does not yet exist. This runs 22 focused tests,
normally and with Python assertions disabled; regenerates calibration and all
162 request schedules; then byte-compares seven decisive artifacts to the saved
release. It verifies that the main protocol hash is unchanged. The full event
record is REPLAY_EVENTS.jsonl, one JSON object per sensitivity case with both arms.
REPLAY_TABLE.csv is the compact comparison. REPORT.txt supplies interpretation.

Optional upstream refresh, with internet access and a new output directory:

    python3 acquire_data.py --cache /tmp/coopgpt-source-cache --output /tmp/coopgpt-source-refresh

This retrieves two immutable MELODI CSVs (about 21 MB total) and the 22.3 MB
WattGPU source table, verifies full SHA-256 digests, then regenerates numeric
pairs and the 30-run selection. Downloads are free and ungated. Source refresh
does not overwrite committed data. Compare refreshed PAIRED_MEASUREMENTS.csv and
llama31_8b_a100_h100_runs.csv to the corresponding data subdirectories.

Source units: MELODI energy is kWh, multiplied by 3,600,000 for joules; durations
were Ollama nanoseconds, divided by 1e9. WattGPU energy is already joules, power
watts and time seconds. Do not combine their observations into one serving model.
