## 2. Trivialization check: universal indispensable power owner

**Yes, it trivializes (my derivation, elementary; verify by hand).** Let owner i* hold the entire row e* and A_{e*j}>0 for all j. Then any S∌i* has c_{S,e*}=0 ⇒ x=0 ⇒ v_b(S)=0. The rule U_{i*}=F(c), U_i=0 (i≠i*) is: coalition-covering (S∋i*: F(c) ≥ F(c_S) by monotonicity of F; S∌i*: 0 ≥ 0), own-report monotone for everyone (F nondecreasing in b_{i*}; constants otherwise), exact budget T, zero subsidy. This is the TU-LP version of P1 Thm 1 / P3's "maximum aggregator share". Consequences:
- The note's grand-marginal rule is **not budget-optimal** even here: F=min(G,P), GPU owners (1),(1), power owner (2): U=(1,1,2), total 2T, while all-to-veto pays T.
- Triviality breaks as soon as (i) power rights are additive across ≥2 holders (no veto), (ii) some columns avoid the power row, (iii) a non-veto positivity/fairness/outside-option requirement is imposed (cf. Perea et al. 2012 "EOwen" positivity, found only via abstract, https://riunet.upv.es/handle/10251/56504), or (iv) truthfulness must be strict (GPU owners are indifferent to withholding under the veto rule).
- Hence the designated-supplier variant should be scoped as "supplier holds a *share* of power or power is not universal"; otherwise the incentive premium is 0 by construction.

Also note: under fully additive ownership, Owen's core (P2 p.2, Thm 8 proof) is nonempty at **every** profile, so static CoS ≡ 0 and the entire separation *is* the incentive premium. Under common access (P2 optimistic rule / P1 §3.1 instance) static CoS can be n−1, so any premium must be measured per profile relative to v(N)+CoS(v_b).

## 5. Recommended single live gap
**Additive-rights sparse LP games, r=2:** define β(r)=inf over all payment rules U that are (i) own-report monotone under binding underreports and (ii) coalition-covering at every profile, of sup_b Σ_i U_i(b)/F(c). Known: β(1)=1 (dual prices are additive per row and truthful), β(r) ≤ r (note's rule). **Open: is β(2)>1, and what is β(2) on the two-essential-resource family with many owners per side** (the assignment/glove class of P4, where P4/Sprumont show population-monotone schemes already fail without veto structure)? This isolates the incentive premium with static CoS≡0, avoids the veto trivialization, and gives the base case for the general r-dependence. (The common-access premium should be posed only afterwards, relative to per-profile CoS(v_b).)
