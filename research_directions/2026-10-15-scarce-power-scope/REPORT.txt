Scarce-power cooperation: bounded scoping result
15 October 2026

Decision
The pivot produced a concrete, internally checked mathematical bridge. Continue
toward interacting hardware/power constraints; do not promote this scoping
corollary as a breakthrough or restart generic Mac benchmarking on its account.
The main submission manuscript was not edited.

What was proved
For a fixed set of actual owners, each allowed to hold both hardware and power,
consider continuous production
  F(g,q)=max {sum_j p_j x_j : 0<=x_j<=g_j, sum_j e_j x_j<=q},
with fixed positive public values p_j and power coefficients e_j.
There is an explicit payment rule on ALL nonnegative real report profiles that
covers every coalition, is nondecreasing in every owner's whole reported bundle,
and pays at most
  (1+1/(2e)) T ~= 1.183940 T.
Owner counts and hardware-type counts are unrestricted. The established generic
column-support-two grand-marginal bound is 2T. The theorem improves that baseline
for this star technology, not for all sparse production.

The new proof work is a direct mixed-owner incentive argument for the existing
two-pool exponential rule. It does not infer that arbitrary cloning/merging
preserves incentives. A standard fractional-knapsack identity then decomposes
heterogeneous hardware production into a fixed positive sum of two-input games.
Zeros, coefficient ties, changes of the binding input, and owners holding both
inputs are covered explicitly. PROOF.txt contains the full continuous argument.

What remains open
The existing lower witness gives total-payment factor at least 9/8=1.125 in
the worst case. Thus the sharp factor is between 1.125 and 1.183940. The star
class and mixed two-input class have the SAME optimal uniform factor, by a
two-way reduction. Increasing the number of hardware types alone therefore
does not create a new minimax frontier. These are theoretical subsidy factors,
not measured power savings or estimates of a real consortium's funding needs.

What the institution screen ruled out
- One indispensable power owner: paying that owner everything is truthful and
  efficient under the current payment-only weak incentive assumptions.
- Every coalition separately claims the entire public power pool: static core
  funding may grow with owner count while the additional incentive premium is
  zero. We prove a broader singleton-payment result for activities with one
  private input and fixed common inputs.
- Merely adding an additive power row to arbitrary sparse production does not
  simplify it: an owner-preserving embedding retains all original coalition
  values/report comparisons with one extra resource per activity.

These results explain why ownership must be part of the research question.
The trivial public-pool example is explicitly present in the 2009 Cost of
Stability paper. Veto energy-community games and common-pool production are
also established subjects. SOURCES.txt records exact scope and access limits.

Verification
An independently tasked Codex reviewer and a Fable 5.1 snapshot reviewer found
no consequential gap in the mixed-owner lemma, composition, 9/8 certificate,
static/incentive distinction or reduction in their reviewed scope. Minor
assumption and wording clarifications were incorporated. Internal AI review is
not human specialist review or historical novelty confirmation.

The standard-library verifier checked:
- 960 exact comparisons of the decomposition with independent primal vertices;
- 1,753 finite mixed-owner profiles, including zero holdings and pool ties;
- 15,048 coalition inequalities and 7,268 adjacent own-coordinate increases;
- 1,753 budget inequalities using certified rational exponential intervals;
- exact weighted-inequality cancellation of the existing 9/8 lower witness;
- all six fixed greedy orders on a three-profile interacting-resource example,
  with independent rational primal/dual certificates of optima 3,3,4.
Normal and optimized Python runs are required to agree. Finite checks supplement
the analytic theorem; they do not imply continuous coverage or novelty alone.

The independent literature delegate's proposed question 'is the r=2 factor
greater than one?' was rejected because the existing 9/8 witness already answers
it. Useful source leads were checked separately rather than treating the report
as authority. See REVIEW.txt for adjudication and reviewer scope.

Next precise target
Let activities form an arbitrary bipartite FOREST between hardware capacities
and independently owned power pools; each activity consumes one hardware input
and one power input. Allow owners, resource rows and activities all to grow.
Try for an all-profile coalition-covering bound strictly below the existing 2T
baseline, or establish a meaningful lower obstruction. Disjoint stars are already
covered by composition. The first interacting topology is a three-edge path.
We proved that even there no fixed report-independent greedy activity order is
optimal across all profiles. This blocks the direct merit-order route, not every
payment mechanism. NEXT_CAMPAIGN.txt gives a bounded attack and stopping gates.

Economic and empirical status
Additive power entitlements are a proposed closed-consortium contract. They must
refer to the same meter/time, avoid double counting, and respect actual concurrent
peak capacity after idle load. The theorem is continuous and payment-only; private
opportunity costs, integer scheduling, latency requirements and physical peak-power
implementation remain unproved. INSTITUTION.txt states these conditions precisely.

No experiment, source search or reviewer established a field-level breakthrough.
The useful outcome is a better-defined model, a checked unbounded-class corollary,
several eliminated routes, and a specific interaction problem worth considering.
