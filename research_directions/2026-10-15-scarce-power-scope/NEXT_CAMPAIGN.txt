Decision after the bounded scope
15 October 2026

GO for a further bounded mathematical attack on interacting hardware/power
constraints; NO-GO for treating the completed star corollary as a breakthrough
or for restarting generic Mac benchmarking without a model-specific question.

Completed base: one independently reservable hardware activity per type, one
additive power pool, arbitrary real mixed owner bundles. The factor is at most
1+1/(2e), with existing lower bound 9/8. This base is exactly reducible to the
mixed two-input minimax problem. Merely increasing hardware heterogeneity does
not create a new harder constant problem.

Recommended next exact target
Let G=(H union P,E) be a finite bipartite FOREST of hardware-capacity rows H and
power-entitlement rows P. Every edge jk is a feasible service activity with fixed
public positive value v_jk and power requirement e_jk. Define
  F(g,q)=max sum_(jk in E) v_jk x_jk
     subject to sum_k x_jk<=g_j for each hardware j,
                sum_j e_jk x_jk<=q_k for each power pool k,
                x>=0.
There are arbitrarily many fixed real owners; each may hold arbitrary bundles
across both H and P. Owners, hardware types, power pools and activities may all
grow. Coalition capacities add, and the one payment rule must work on every
nonnegative real report profile. Keep payment-only binding-underreport utility.

Find either:
(A) an explicit uniform factor C<2 with all-profile own monotonicity and every
coalition covered for the entire forest class; or
(B) a certified owner-preserving lower construction showing a substantive
barrier, ideally a family approaching 2; or
(C) a structural lemma that proves a genuinely larger class reducible to the
star/two-input result and isolates the first unsolved interaction.

The established grand-marginal upper is 2 for this class. A disjoint union of
stars is already solved by positive-sum composition even if owners span
components. The first topology to examine is the three-edge path, not another
isolated star. PROOF section 6 and verify.py give a three-profile certificate
that a fixed report-independent greedy activity order already fails there.
That certificate is an obstruction to one proof route, not to all mechanisms.

Before calling this an open problem, finish exact original-source comparisons
for capacitated assignment/production games and resource-withholding mechanisms.
Population monotonicity, demand/peak reporting and private valuation mechanisms
are different incentive notions and cannot be imported without a proof.

Proposed next attack budget: one six-hour pass, if authorized separately.
First hour: sources and precise forest frontier; second: exact finite-profile
LP/dual certificates on paths; next two: owner-preserving decomposition or leaf
elimination; final two: independent audit and conversion to a theorem or exact
stopping obstruction. No extension for accumulating small examples alone.

Success gate: theorem on an unbounded class improving the relevant bound, a
general lower obstruction, or a verified structural lemma that reduces a live
problem. Stop if the key lemma is merely the original problem in different
notation, if the model requires silently fixing strategic reports, or if no
advance beyond the star closure survives review. Record negative results.

Role of the Mac: exact symbolic/certificate work first. A later experiment is
justified only by a named physical assumption (for example, whether two feasible
concurrent configurations can share hardware while respecting an actual peak
quota). Pure batching speed is not the next scientific gate.
