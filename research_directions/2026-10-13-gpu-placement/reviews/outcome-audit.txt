Interpretation limits:
This is an audit of saved evidence, not a new proof of global optimality. The exact
plans are independently feasible and attain the saved oracle objectives. Optimality
continues to rely on the documented separate oracle/solver comparison. Altered
instance plans and separate oracle traces were not saved in RESULTS.json, so this
audit checks their recorded tuples and inequalities without independently verifying
those altered placements or recomputing optima. Hash agreement checks current bytes
against the saved freeze; it does not attest external timestamps or unseen runs.

The result supports a bounded synthetic implementation under the formal resource
model. Administrative priority is not measured economic value. Nothing here measures
live state accuracy, dispatch safety, software/network fit, serving latency, energy,
independent-owner pooling, or an LLM contribution. Timing is diagnostic. The discrete
placement model does not automatically validate continuous-production assumptions.
