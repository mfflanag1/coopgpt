Primary-source grounding, accessed 13 October 2026 UTC

These freely available sources motivate explicit resource and scheduling concepts.
No source supplies measured hardware data, a job trace, economic values or an
empirical observation for this experiment. SPEC.txt defines the actual model.

Kubernetes, Schedule GPUs
https://kubernetes.io/docs/tasks/manage-gpus/scheduling-gpus/
GPU scheduling requires vendor drivers/device plugins. Different GPU types can
be distinguished through node labels/selectors. This supports explicit type
compatibility; it does not establish our abstract family field as sufficient
runtime compatibility, nor provide a built-in per-device VRAM constraint.

Kubernetes, Device Plugins
https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/extend-kubernetes/compute-storage-net/device-plugins/
Standard extended resources use integer quantities, cannot be overcommitted,
and devices in that model are not shared between containers. Our whole-GPU
restriction is explicit; vendor-specific sharing, MIG and time-slicing are out
of scope. This is not a universal assertion that real GPUs cannot be partitioned.

Slurm, Generic Resource Scheduling (GRES)
https://slurm.schedmd.com/gres.html
GRES identifies typed resource counts and supports GPU job/node/task requests.
The prototype uses named devices and typed eligibility, but is not Slurm and
does not reproduce its selection, priority, reservation or preemption policies.

Slurm, sbatch
https://slurm.schedmd.com/sbatch.html
CPU and host-memory requests are separate resource dimensions. In particular,
--mem-per-gpu concerns allocated host memory; our min_vram_mib is an additional
formal device-profile requirement, not an interpretation of that Slurm flag.

Kueue, Topology Aware Scheduling
https://kueue.sigs.k8s.io/docs/concepts/topology_aware_scheduling/
Required topology can constrain a PodSet to a topology domain. Our single-node,
single-family multi-GPU job is a narrower modeling choice. Same-node placement
does not establish suitable interconnect, library, driver or execution behavior.

Google OR-Tools, Multiple Knapsacks
https://developers.google.com/optimization/pack/multiple_knapsack
Capacity-constrained assignment maximizing item values is established integer
optimization. This work adds a deliberately small GPU-specific formal interface,
independent verification and reproducible tests; it claims no new algorithm.
For larger instances compare an appropriate CP-SAT/MIP formulation and established
scheduling approaches before treating any learned policy as necessary or superior.
