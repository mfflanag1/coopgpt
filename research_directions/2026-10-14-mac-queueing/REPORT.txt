Interpretation
This short, artificial numerical workload supplies no material scheduling gain
under its fixed gate. Every serial job already met the deadline, so the useful
on-time count could not improve. All low-rate two-slot windows actually reached
only one active pipeline. At rate 40, just one two-slot window reached two active
pipelines; at rate 100 all four did. Across those four high-rate two-slot windows,
two pipelines were active for 0.11417 seconds, about 4.73% of their combined
measured window time. Occupancy is a post-collection descriptive recount, not
an additional promotion criterion. This workload offers limited overlap.

A secondary metric does improve: at rate 100, per-window p95 FIFO queue delay
was 2.54-3.10 ms with one slot and 0.023-0.027 ms with two. That did not translate
into the required improvement in full validated-completion latency. Queue time
alone is not the primary endpoint and cannot override the failed gate.

The per-window p95 was lower at higher offered rates for both configurations.
This is descriptive evidence; the study does not identify power management,
wakeup effects or any other cause. Do not infer that higher load generally
improves latency, or promote small low-rate differences as the primary result.
The timing streams overlap two complete pipelines, but the recorded GPU command
execution intervals never overlap. A shared Metal command queue and multiple
workers do not establish concurrent GPU kernels. CPU preparation, the exact
reference, full output comparison and hashing are part of each checked request.
This contract is more expensive than GPU-only completion and is not a serving
latency or end-user application-quality benchmark.

Design and hardware
The observed Mac is an Apple M3 Max with 30 GPU cores, 14 logical CPU cores and
96 GiB of unified RAM. Hardware totals are not unreserved placement capacity.
Each window offered dimensions 256,512,1024 repeated 20 times, independently of
worker availability. Admission was FIFO; slots remained active through resource
cleanup. The fixed rate/order scheme reversed configuration order in alternate
rounds. Nominal offers, actual enqueues and completion timestamps are all saved.
Four paired rounds on an active desktop do not support population confidence
intervals, causal generalization or claims about all arrival rates/workloads.

Every before/after power reading was Battery Power. All 12 paired comparisons
had consistent known power sources. No privileged power changes were made.
The largest window p95 producer delay was 7.202292 ms, within the prespecified
10 ms limit; this delay is INCLUDED in nominal-offer latency. Thermal diagnostics
are saved but are not temperatures or an outcome-based exclusion filter.

The maximum conservative active sum of tracked matrix/reference/hash storage
was 17,830,912 bytes (17.00488 MiB), below the 128 MiB two-slot cap.
This is an event-based explicit-storage bound, not a measured process peak.
Global Metal allocation samples are retained separately, never counted once per
CPU and again per GPU, and never treated as per-job deltas or free RAM. Metal/
Accelerate workspaces, allocator metadata and cached resources remain outside
the explicit cap. No energy sampler ran and no energy savings are established.
