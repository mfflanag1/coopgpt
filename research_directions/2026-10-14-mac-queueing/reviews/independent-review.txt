Design and identification
The treatment is one versus at most two complete CPU/GPU job pipelines, not two
independently allocatable GPUs or proof of simultaneous device kernels. A fixed
24-window sequence counterbalances within-pair order and varies rate order across
rounds. This reduces simple order drift but leaves background activity and short
single-machine sampling limitations. No population significance, general causal
effect, AI-serving, energy, economic or owner-pooling claim follows. The 10% effect,
100 ms deadline, 10 ms producer-jitter and low-load nonregression limits are
prespecified engineering gates; they are not confidence bounds or empirically
estimated universal thresholds. No production snapshot fields are required for
this explicitly constructed workload.

Coordinated omission and latency
The producer has its own thread, waits for absolute nominal offers, and records
enqueues while all worker slots are occupied. Queue locks are not held during
GPU work, CPU reference, hashing or cleanup. Nominal-offer-to-validated-completion
latency includes producer delay, queuing, allocation, preparation, GPU execution,
CPU reference and hashes. Actual-enqueue latency is reported separately. Slots
remain occupied through cleanup, while the completion endpoint is the documented
validated timestamp. All 60 planned offers remain in each on-time denominator;
missing, incorrect or late jobs cannot be counted as useful on-time completions.

Native concurrency and resource review
FIFO admission timestamps, ordinal and enqueue/admit/release events are assigned
under one condition lock. Exactly cap worker threads execute; a worker obtains
its next job only after finishing its previous one. Autoreleasepool cleanup occurs
before the post-cleanup sample and before FIFO.finish releases the slot. Worker
Job copies hold numeric data, not escaping Metal objects. Separate per-job MPS
objects and buffers use a shared thread-safe command queue. FIFO means admission
order; the review does not impose GPU submission or completion ordering on two
workers. Every measured row retains both raw hardware timestamps and its declared
host epoch without subtracting values across timebases. Warmups have a separate
origin and finish before the measured epoch.

A/B/C buffers, separate CPU C reference and hash scratch share one system-memory
pool. Both requested lengths and actual buffer allocatedSize plus reference/scratch
are checked. Per-active-slot payload is capped at 64 MiB, hence at most 128 MiB
for two active slots. Reconstructed sums of per-job payload bounds over active
slots are bounds for tracked explicit objects, not measured process peaks or
framework-private allocations. Global Metal allocation samples are not turned
into per-job deltas, free RAM, dedicated GPU memory or a production safety limit.

Correctness and independent analyzer
Small integer inputs and product sums justify finite exact equality for these
specific matrices. The native checker scans all output entries and records
independent GPU and CPU reference digests. The standalone analyzer imports no
collector/native bridge/BLAS/prior reducer. It independently constructs the exact
integer product using row-7/column-11 periodicity, expands full row-major Float32
little-endian content with positive zero, and compares both hashes. This verifies
content subject to ordinary SHA256 collision assumptions, not hardware attestation.

The analyzer replays all locked FIFO events, checks sequence/timestamps/counts,
whole-pipeline cap, row timelines, complete fixed dimensions/order, full comparison
counts, memory accounting, warmup separation, optional GPU timestamp consistency,
and source/plan/capture identities. It reports actual command-buffer interval
overlap without calling that simultaneous kernels. It reconstructs all twelve
paired contrasts, nearest-rank p95, ordinary median, planned and unrun denominators,
power-source comparability and producer jitter. Deadline/gate comparisons use
unrounded recorded arithmetic. Invalid comparisons cannot pass a promotion gate.

The root runner now invokes this independently authored window checker before
advancing, and the final analyzer reruns it from retained raw files. These are
repeated uses of the same checker, not two independent implementations. Independence
is from the native numerical implementation and collector, with a separately
constructed integer oracle. Results from rerunning unchanged records are not new
observations or a new physical replication.

Finding and repair

which could exceed the advertised campaign budget. Root repaired it to use one

ordinary failures retain their records and planned unrun identities. This closes
