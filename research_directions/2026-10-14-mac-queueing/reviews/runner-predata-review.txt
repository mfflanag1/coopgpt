Findings raised and verified repaired before data:
1. Post-native diagnostic interruption previously escaped before execution
   metadata was saved and could mislabel an executed window as unrun. The runner
   now preserves native stdout/stderr before post-diagnostics, catches diagnostic
   interruption, closes the window gate, and records an attempted window before
   fallible execution/postprocessing. Unexpected interruption therefore retains
   the attempted identity instead of adding it to the unrun denominator.
2. Native gate_passed alone previously permitted continuation. The runner now
   requires independent audit_window checks, with precomputed integer expected
   hashes, the fixed plan and inventory device names, before the next window.
   Final independent campaign auditing remains required and is a separate review.
3. The added inventory integration initially iterated devices=None on an expected
   unavailable-profiler path, losing the campaign record via uncaught TypeError.
   The runner now rejects non-list device inventory with a caught ValueError,
   preserves CAMPAIGN.json, retains all unrun identities, and launches no window.

Other checked properties:

- Native subprocess watchdog is at most 90 seconds and is recomputed after

- Invalid/failed/interrupted/timed-out windows stop subsequent collection.
