Saved-schedule fragility and measurement priorities — 11 October 2026

DECISION
The saved pooled primary schedule has substantial conditional timing headroom,
but the earlier evidence still does not establish a practical energy-saving or
economic pooling effect. The next decisive evidence is a paired full-window
measurement, including idle/cooldown draw and concurrent serving. Further tuning
on these same 92 selected requests cannot substitute for that evidence.

This analysis completes a useful offline step while hardware access is absent.
It audits 81 previously saved scenarios; no GPU experiment or new data acquisition
occurred. The new protocol was frozen before these calculations, AFTER original
results were known. Everything below is exploratory fixed-schedule sensitivity,
not a fresh holdout, a new controller, or an empirical promotion gate.

Keep admissions, GPU assignment, per-GPU job order and original start times as
release floors. Lengthen API service durations, propagating queue delays through
each GPU. Keep the declared four seconds of slot overhead unchanged. Rejected
requests remain rejected; all late completions remain visible.

response deadline, equal shares of 5,426.966 J of request-window allowance.

ENERGY: COMMON RESIDENT COST CANCELS, BUT MISSING FUNDING IS LARGE
Use the same two resident configurations and horizon for both alternatives.
Primary horizon H=970 seconds, including the last offered request's deadline.
Each recorded energy window spans API duration plus one second. The subsequent
three-second cooldown and other resident gaps were NOT measured by those windows.
Add hypothetical mean board power p_h only outside charged windows on owner h.
It includes cooldown; it is not independently measured idle power. The grid
{0,1,5,10,25,50} W is an illustrative axis, not a calibrated plausible range.

For arm a and owner h, let W_ah be observed window joules, L_ah measured-window
coverage, and I_ah=H-L_ah omitted time. Then E_ah=W_ah+p_h I_ah.
This neither subtracts a baseline from observed energy nor double-counts it.
Output also separates cooldown seconds from non-slot resident seconds, enabling
different rates later. This audit uses equal omitted-time mean rates across
those two phases on each owner, which is an assumption requiring measurement.

Primary omitted seconds:
                         Owner 0             Owner 1
 Separate               914.970219           509.022411

For the primary case the energy difference, in joules, is
 E_pool-E_separate = 97.295033 - 10.371934*p_0 + 81.529021*p_1.
The common p_h*H term cancels. If both owners have the same omitted-time rate p,
the difference is 97.295033 + 71.157087*p: pooling uses more energy for every
nonnegative p in THIS conditional model. With unequal unknown rates the ranking
can change. This is neither measured gross energy nor a clean GPU-only comparison.
More completions at higher energy could still be valuable; no economic objective
or valuation data has been supplied to decide that tradeoff.

The original budgets explicitly funded REQUEST WINDOWS ONLY. Reusing the same
numerical allowances as GROSS full-horizon budgets is a new hypothetical contract.
Under that reinterpretation, the primary separate plan is funded only up to a
uniform p=0.087930772 W and the pooled plan up to p=0.028382794 W. These small
frontiers reflect unbudgeted omitted time; they do not invalidate the old contract
and are not estimates of real GPU power. Across 81 cases the median respective
frontiers are 0.0698 and 0.0672 W; negative frontiers retain original budget debt.

These absolute funding numbers depend on the chosen H=970-second horizon.
Extending the shared resident period adds p_h times the extra seconds to each
owner's energy, while leaving the between-arm energy difference unchanged.

For illustration only, at p_0=p_1=10 W the unchanged schedules require:
                         Separate             Pooled
 Conditional gross J     19,527.161            20,336.027
 Additional funding J    14,100.195            14,909.061

Separate funding is sum_h max(0,E_h-B_h); pooled funding is max(0,sum E-sum B).
The original separate accounts cannot transfer their unused credits. These are
ex-post funding needs for fixed outcomes, not sufficient guarantees that a new
controller charging idle energy would make the same admissions or reservations.
No started request is deleted when its schedule becomes hypothetically unfunded.

EXACT TIMING CERTIFICATE
On one server, let t_j be saved start, d_j the positive API duration and A_j arrival.
The fixed plan executes S_j=max(t_j,C_{j-1}), C_j=S_j+4+r*d_j and responds at
R_j=C_j-3.5. Induction unrolls the recurrence to
 R_j(r)=max_{k<=j}[t_k+4(j-k)+0.5+r*sum_{l=k..j} d_l].
For D_j(q)=A_j+q*(D_j-A_j), it follows that the job is on time iff
 r <= min_{k<=j} [D_j(q)-t_k-4(j-k)-0.5] / sum_{l=k..j} d_l.
Every denominator is positive. The minimum is attained; a minimizing first job
and exact numerator/denominator are saved for every admitted job and deadline
multiplier. This is an elementary recurrence identity, not a novelty claim.
Negative/sub-unit limits preserve pre-existing lateness. Energy is NOT
extrapolated when service changes; the timing and energy studies stay separate.

WHAT TO MEASURE NEXT, AND WHAT TO STOP INFERRING
1. Full-run board energy, resident gaps and cooldown on a compatible hardware
   pair. The affine energy boundary shows why one source's fitted idle proxy
   cannot settle the ranking for these other configurations.
2. Actual queue/tail response under representative concurrent demand. Seven
   previously passing comparisons have only 1.9–3.2% separate-plan slowdown
   headroom, while the primary pooled plan has much more. Measure both arms;
   a larger difference caused by baseline lateness is not two-sided SLA success.
3. A budget/ownership contract grounded in a real scarce resource or expense.
   The earlier nonbinding-budget ablation served all 92 jobs in both arms;
   artificial account stranding remains a major mechanism in these replays.

These results support keeping the AI-infrastructure application as a measurement
candidate. They do not yet support advancing to private opportunity costs,
scarce peak-power incentives, causal energy-saving claims or a serving paper
presented as measured deployment validation. No expected-value-of-information
number is identified without measurement costs, parameter priors and an objective.
