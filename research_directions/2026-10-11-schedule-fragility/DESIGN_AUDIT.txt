Independent design audit: saved-schedule fragility and measurement priorities
2026-10-11. Source: saved measured-serving protocol, replay.py, event records,
report and primary diagnostic summary. No implementation or new replay outcomes.

VERDICT
Proceed with the proposed bounded, expressly post-result audit. The useful result
is a transparent boundary on what the saved schedules would require, not a newly
validated GPU consortium, fresh holdout, improved online scheduler, or formal
expected value of information. Keep baseline-energy completion and timing stress
separate. Do not extrapolate request energy when perturbing service durations.

A. Fix the objects before calculating additional outcomes
For each original case, preserve every admitted ID, assigned server, original start
time, per-server order, measured energy, and original rejections. Preserve existing
late jobs, negative pooling differences and debt cases. Each arm uses both resident
nodes through a COMMON horizon H. A suitable definition is
 H=max(last OFFERED arrival + that case's relative deadline,
       largest saved slot_end in either arm).
Use the complete offered manifest, not the last admitted/served request. Time starts
at zero. This horizon is part of the diagnostic and must not vary by arm. Timing
stress below has its own completion times; it does not silently redefine baseline
energy H. Report timing-stressed drain separately if useful, without costing it.

B. Correct missing-time energy without double counting
For a saved request j on node h, the existing event schema means:
 d_j=actual_service_seconds_j-4 is API duration;
 slot=[t_j,t_j+d_j+4]; response=t_j+d_j+0.5;
 already-charged measurement window=[t_j,t_j+d_j+1]
                                      =[start,slot_end-3].
The full API+1 window, including both half-second measurement buffers, already has
its entire reported GPU energy W_j charged. Do NOT add an idle baseline on top of
that same interval. Its source measurement and exact collector revision remain
uncertain; this is an audit conditional on the frozen window convention.

Let L_ah be the length of the union of those measurement windows in arm a on node
h, and W_ah the sum of its saved window energies. Check containment in [0,H],
nonoverlap and uniqueness before replacing union length with sum(d_j+1). Define
 I_ah=H-L_ah >=0.
At assumed constant nonnegative omitted-time mean board power p_h,
 E_ah(p_h)=W_ah+p_h I_ah,
 E_a(p)=sum_h W_ah + sum_h p_h I_ah.
Watts times seconds are joules. This is a conditional completion of missing data,
not measured energy, and not a per-request incremental-cost estimate.

The omitted time includes three seconds of modeled cooldown per admitted job;
cooldown may consume more power than resident idle. For precision, define
 C_ah=3*n_started,ah and Z_ah=H-sum_j(d_j+4).
Then E_ah=W_ah+p_idle,h Z_ah+p_cool,h C_ah. The simple one-rate analysis explicitly
sets p_idle,h=p_cool,h=p_h. Call p_h assumed mean power OUTSIDE measured windows,
not a measured resident-idle power estimate.

C. What cancels, and what cannot be concluded
For the two saved arms with the same nodes and H,
 E_pool-E_autarky = (W_pool-W_autarky)
                    -sum_h p_h(L_pool,h-L_autarky,h).
The common sum_h p_h H cancels. Higher omitted-time power need not erase a pooling
advantage or worsen relative total energy: extra measured-window coverage replaces
some unmeasured time. Compare energy and on-time requests separately; more requests
and more energy are neither an energy saving nor automatically a welfare improvement.
Fixed-schedule completion counts do not change merely because p is changed. Mark a
schedule budget-infeasible; do not silently delete served requests until it fits.

D. Funding interpretations and exact frontier
The original B_h allowances explicitly funded MEASURED WINDOWS ONLY. Treating the
same numerical B_h as full-horizon energy allowances is a NEW hypothetical budget
contract. It can expose a missing funding requirement but cannot refute the old
window-only result. Report at least these two interpretations separately:

1. Reuse original allowances as gross whole-horizon budgets. Autarky feasibility:
 W_aut,h+p_h I_aut,h <= B_h for each owner h.
For I>0, its exact owner threshold is p_h <= (B_h-W_aut,h)/I_aut,h. Negative slack
means already infeasible at p=0. If I=0, the condition is W<=B and there is no idle
power threshold. Pool feasibility is the halfspace
 sum_h p_h I_pool,h <= sum_h B_h-W_pool.
For a shared scalar p, the threshold is remaining total allowance / sum_h I_pool,h.
For independent p_0,p_1, preserve the halfspace rather than claiming a scalar bound.

2. Show additional funding required to preserve each saved outcome:
 autarky external topup=sum_h max(0,E_aut,h-B_h),
 pooled external topup=max(0,E_pool-sum_h B_h).
Also show per-owner requirements; pooling may legitimately use one owner's energy
allowance to fund execution on the other node. If existing unused credits are
transferable in the supposedly separate case, that changes its institution.

Final gross-energy feasibility is NECESSARY for the exact saved outcome. It does
not prove the old online controller would select the same requests after adding
idle charges. Reservations and eligibility would change. Do not call these bounds
an online-policy robustness result. With nonnegative continuous charges and no
borrowing, final actual energy bounds suffice for actual cumulative expenditure,
but not for every forecast reservation or reproducing the original dispatch path.

E. Unavoidable-idle language must be qualified
For a particular fixed schedule, p*I>B already rules it out even before charging
its nonnegative measured-window energy. For an empty always-resident system the
cost is sum_h p_h H. If this exceeds B, even leaving the nodes idle is unfunded.
Do not infer that EVERY possible active schedule is infeasible unless p_h is also
assumed/proved a lower bound on power during activity. A resident-idle reading is
not automatically such a bound. Under an explicit constant baseline floor valid
at all times, sum_h p_h H>B is a schedule-independent impossibility. Without that
extra assumption, label it an empty-resident-baseline funding failure instead.

F. Timing-only perturbation: correct causal propagation
Multiplying durations while leaving original starts fixed usually creates overlaps
as soon as a back-to-back slot is slowed. Never count those overlapping schedules.
The root's proposed fixed-assignment/order repair is mathematically correct:
 S_j(r)=max(t_j,C_{j-1}(r)),
 C_j(r)=S_j(r)+4+r*d_j,
 R_j(r)=C_j(r)-3.5=S_j(r)+0.5+r*d_j.
Use each server's original chronological job order independently. No rejected job
is newly admitted, no assignment changes, and no future output enters a dispatch
policy: this is post-hoc execution of a fixed saved plan, not a new online policy.
The release floors t_j prevent starts earlier than the saved trace.

Unrolling the recurrence gives, for each job j,
 R_j(r)=max_{k<=j}[t_k+4(j-k)+0.5+r*sum_{ell=k..j}d_ell].
Because every d_ell>0, the exact slowdown deadline threshold is
 r_j*=min_{k<=j}[(D_j-t_k-4(j-k)-0.5)/sum_{ell=k..j}d_ell].
Thus j is on time iff r<=r_j*. Save a minimizing k and numerator/denominator as a
witness. Values below one correctly retain jobs already late in the base trace.
Do not round critical ratios before classification.

For relative deadline tightening q, let A_j be arrival and L_j=D_j-A_j>0:
 D_j(q)=A_j+q L_j,
 r_j*(q)=min_k[(A_j+q L_j-t_k-4(j-k)-0.5)/sum d].
Equivalently, at fixed r, q_j*(r)=(R_j(r)-A_j)/L_j and the job is on time iff
q>=q_j*(r). Declared grids r in {1,1.05,1.10,1.25,1.5,2} and q in {1,.9,.8,.5}
are transparent deterministic stress cases, not probabilities or confidence bounds.
Uniform slowdown and deadline tightening make each arm's on-time count nonincreasing,
but their DIFFERENCE need not be monotone. No energy extrapolation accompanies them.

G. Independent verification and interpretation
Verify recurrence and closed-form response/threshold agreement on toy chains and
all saved jobs. At r=q=1 reconcile recorded starts, response times, outcomes and
counts. Inputs use serialized floats: exact decimal-rational arithmetic can certify
the stored numbers, or use a declared small absolute tolerance for reconstruction
without rounding threshold witnesses. Validate IDs/arrival/order/slot nonoverlap.
Check energy p=0 recovers original energies, added p charges only uncovered time,
common-H cancellation holds, and budget-boundary equality is accepted consistently.
Stress tests should include no jobs on a node, zero budget slack, negative slack,
zero uncovered time, consecutive jobs, idle gaps and already-late jobs.

The boundary indicates WHICH missing measurement could change a funding or timing
conclusion and the accuracy/resolution needed near that boundary. Without a prior
on unknown power/durations, a measurement cost, and an explicit decision objective,
this is a measurement-priority or sensitivity analysis—not a numerical expected
value-of-information calculation. It cannot choose the best real GPU deployment.
