Next local quality study: independent design recommendation
12 October 2026 UTC. Read-only review of prior REPORT, PROTOCOL and design.py;
no model calls, new data collection, downloads, or prior-file edits.

Recommendation
Move the PRIMARY qualification to a source-grounded support extraction task.
Do not spend another 9+27 calls qualifying KEEP/DROP as the useful workload.
A stronger model might solve that task, but a deterministic filter already solves
its entire stated business operation exactly. Faster or more energy-efficient LLM
execution of it would not establish an economically useful service. The prior
failure remains valuable as a measurement-pipeline and instruction-following
finding, not evidence that pooling cannot help a useful AI workload.

If a narrowly isolated model-change diagnostic is essential, permit exactly one
separately labeled 9-case fresh KEEP batch using frozen candidate A. Do not use its
result to select prompts, models or easy cells for the primary study, and do not
add its successes to primary development/confirmation counts. It is optional;
skipping it is my recommendation. Fresh tasks provide a comparable task-family
check, not a same-item causal comparison with the prior model. Qwen's tokenizer,
model template and other inherent serving differences are part of that new
configuration, so 'only the model changed' should not imply only one computational
mechanism changed.

Concrete useful task and baseline
Use a small, frozen corpus of public, redistributable support/policy documents and
natural support questions requiring selected facts from supplied evidence. Return
one JSON object containing a list of verbatim evidence spans, their document IDs,
and an explicit supported/not-found field. Questions should cover semantic mapping
from a customer's wording to policy evidence, not flags already announcing the
answer. Include unsupported requests to test abstention. Have two independent
human/model-assisted checks verify gold spans and ambiguity BEFORE testing the
candidate. Any unresolved item is removed by a documented pre-response rule, not
after its answer is observed. This is source-grounded extraction, not open-ended
customer-support automation or validated business value.

An economically meaningful baseline is retrieval plus a fixed non-LLM keyword/
rule selector with abstention. Freeze that baseline first and report its exact
accuracy, abstention and operating effort on the same heldout questions. A perfect
deterministic parser for a structured task is a reason to choose a different LLM
service, not a baseline to omit. Do not infer dollar savings without a real cost
of the displaced action or useful-job valuation. The later serving outcome is
correct supported answers meeting the latency requirement per unit of measured
energy, with all offered requests retained.

Bounded prospective gate
Use one pinned cached 32B candidate, one prompt, one generic schema, one documented
reasoning setting, fixed generous output limits and one development cohort. No
model/prompt retries within this campaign. Suggested compact composition: nine
fresh development questions, covering three input-length strata and three output
burdens (one fact, several facts, or explicit abstention), followed ONLY on 9/9 by
27 fresh confirmation questions, three per cell. A false answer, omitted required
span, extra unsupported span, or wrong abstention fails. Treat JSON object key
order and whitespace conventionally; array ordering should matter only where the
application needs it. Freeze exact admissible spans and normalization rules before
responses. A 27/27 result remains a small engineering gate, not a reliability claim.
If constructing sufficiently unambiguous tasks requires longer, finish the design
without collecting a convenient synthetic substitute and labeling it production.

Mixed lengths and holdout
Split by DOCUMENT/TOPIC family before writing development versus confirmation
questions; question-string disjointness alone is insufficient. Keep all paraphrases
and related questions together. A public corpus may have been in pretraining, so
claim only prospective question/answer separation, not proven unseen knowledge.
Newly authored evidence fixtures can check memorization dependence but are a
separate synthetic stratum. Keep scoring answers out of prompts and schemas;
allow only document evidence the service would actually receive.
Input-length bins must contain real relevant/distractor text, not repeated padding.
Output caps are safety bounds, not achieved length targets. Do not force 192/384
output tokens by asking for unnecessary prose. Classify future load by observed
serial service demand and naturally required answer size. If qualifying natural
outputs do not span a broad range, report that and revise the future workload
before its freeze; do not pretend token caps create mixed-length demand.

Operational and interpretive limits
Battery at 57% fails the current >=60% quality rule; wait for permitted power and
thermal conditions. Changing that gate now solely to run is unnecessary. Pin model
digest, quantization, Ollama version, actual context, request options and any
thinking/reasoning mode. Retain any distinct reasoning stream and account for all
its generation time/tokens when later measuring service. Do not silently discard
reasoning and call the exposed answer the full workload. Verify the chosen model's
loaded metadata and context diagnostics rather than inheriting the 3B model's
capacity assumptions. Quality collection carries no energy or latency-comparison
claim. A later concurrent sweep must use fresh requests, continue correctness
scoring under load, and have independently ready full-window energy capture.
