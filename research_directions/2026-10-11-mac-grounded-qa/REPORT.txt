Interpretation and recommended next step
This model/configuration has not met the declared accuracy gate. The data do not
show that it answered only eight questions sensibly, nor that it is broadly
unsuitable for useful serving. Exact span compliance, abstention and annotation
interpretation are distinct issues. This public Wikipedia proxy may be in pretraining;
it is not actual business traffic or the hidden benchmark test. Short answers
do not qualify long generation. The always-null baseline is a weak 50% floor.
Before another model or energy run, define an application-specific correctness
contract and independently validate answerability on fresh cases without inspecting
model predictions. A meaningful new study should distinguish factual support,
unsupported answers and required output shape, preserving a strict success gate
fixed before collection. This failed study remains closed and unmodified.
Only after a new configuration meets its prospective quality gate should we run
quality-aware arrival/latency tests with full-horizon energy. Independent-owner
pooling and compatible measurements from two hardware configurations remain
unestablished. There is no new theorem, causal efficiency result or breakthrough.
