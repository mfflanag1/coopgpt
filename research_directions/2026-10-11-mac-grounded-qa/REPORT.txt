Why this is the next step
The previous serial KEEP/DROP calibration obtained 1/9 and 0/9 exact answers,
despite 18 completed API responses. Those frozen results remain unchanged.
KEEP/DROP has an exact ordinary-code solution, so it is a poor workload for an
economic argument for LLM serving. This new, prospective study instead uses
passage-grounded QA with required abstention. It changes both task and model;
any future success would not isolate the cause of the earlier failure.

Prospective test
The pinned official SQuAD2.0 public development source yields 12 development
questions and 36 conditional confirmation questions. Article titles are split
before selection; article, paragraph text and question IDs do not cross phases.
Six cells cross answerable/unanswerable with three passage-character bands.
Gold annotations never enter the request. Empty string is not an abstention:
unanswerable success requires literal JSON null. Answerable exact match uses
the official normalization, with F1 retained as a diagnostic only.
Development requires 11/12 exact plus schema/subgroup conditions. Only a pass
activates confirmation, which requires 33/36 exact, 16/18 in each answerability
label, 10/12 in each length band, and 5/6 in each crossed cell. No alternate
prompt, fallback model, retry, energy sweep or post-response gate revision.
The single execution budget is 30 minutes, with request/cleanup headroom; it
can abort before all questions finish. Aborted or unattempted items are retained
or reported unobserved rather than replaced by a fresh run.

Interpretation and next gate
This is a Wikipedia benchmark service proxy, not real support traffic. Public
examples may have appeared in pretraining; the confirmation split is not the
hidden SQuAD test. A finite pass would qualify only this configuration for the
next bounded study, not certify population reliability or economic value.
Naturally short answers do not qualify long-output serving. The always-null
50% baseline is a weak floor, not a competitive economic alternative.
Once power permits, run this frozen gate exactly once and independently recount
it. Only after a valid pass should a new experiment use fresh inputs to measure
quality under arrival rates, latency and full-horizon energy on AC. Evidence of
independent-owner pooling and gains across two hardware configurations is still
missing. No mathematical novelty or field-level breakthrough is claimed here.
