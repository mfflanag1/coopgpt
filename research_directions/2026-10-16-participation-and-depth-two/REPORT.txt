2. NEW POSITIVE RESULT: SHARP PUBLIC PARTICIPATION/FLOOR CONTRACT
One actual owner A controls local energy allowances, one C controls shared grid
capacity P, and hardware owner H_i controls h_i at pod i. Local energy remains
local. Capacities are binding nonnegative reports and utility is payment only.
Let ell_i=min(h_i,L_i), L=sumell_i, r_i=h_i-ell_i, Q=sumr_i,
H=L+Q, T=L+min(P,Q), G=min(P,H), M=T-G.

Choose a fixed public contractual floor y_A>=theta M,0<=theta<=1. This is a
share of grand-coalition marginal contribution, not an estimate of a private
outside option. The sharp uniform total-payment factor is
  C_theta=1+theta/4.
The theorem holds on every real profile and arbitrarily many pods. All owners
and resource dimensions can grow; the matching even-pod family has every
resource and owner active. This is a proved general theorem on that stated
ownership class, not an inference from a finite grid.

The theta1 mechanism is particularly simple. For L>0 put
  x_i=ell_i clip((P-Q+r_i)/L), X=sumx_i,
  y_A=M, y_Hi=x_i, y_C=G-(X-L/4)_+.
At L0 pay C=G and everyone else0. It runs in O(m) arithmetic operations.
For other theta, mix this rule with the known efficient rule(0,min(P,Q),ell_i).
Total funding is exactly T+theta min(X,L/4). The lower combines coalition
coverage and the public floor by balanced counting. The central proof is the
grid-deficit envelope bound X+c_env-G<=L/4; it covers all hardware subsets.

Read application/continuous/CONTINUOUS_ATTACK.txt for the complete proof and
application/baseline/SOURCES_AND_CONTRACT.txt for economic interpretation.

PUBLIC HETEROGENEOUS VALUES EXTENSION
The same uniform sharp factor holds for arbitrary fixed public per-pod values,
provided a pod has the same value whether served with local or shared power.
Sort the public values and write F_v(S)=sum_k Delta v_k T_k(S), where T_k is
the unit-value game restricted to the first k pods with the SAME actual owners.
Greedy fractional knapsack simultaneously attains all prefix outputs for each
coalition. Summing the sharp prefix payment rules with fixed nonnegative
weights preserves incentives, coverage and the exact grand-marginal floor.
The direct implementation takes O(m^2) arithmetic operations after sorting.
This establishes robustness to public hardware-value heterogeneity; it does
not cover private values, different local/grid net values or different input
coefficients. Read application/weighted/WEIGHTED_VALUES.txt. The decomposition
ingredient is classical; priority of the combined guarantee is unconfirmed.

3. WHY A SEPARATE INCENTIVE PREMIUM STILL EXISTS
Define B_theta(b) as the least total funding satisfying coverage and the floor
at that ONE profile, without report comparisons. Let K be the worst ratio
of an all-profile mechanism's funding to B_theta(b), and C its worst ratio
to T. They optimize different objectives. Three fixed-owner profiles prove
  K_theta>=max{(8+2theta)/(8+theta),(4+2theta)/5}.
The new rule yields K_theta<=1+theta/4. Thus K_1 lies between6/5 and5/4,
and K_(1/2) between18/17 and9/8. The continuous K optimum remains open.

For any ONE rule on a fixed m>=2 domain (or a uniform family containing it),
the same two profiles give
  C+K>=2+theta/2.
The sharp rule attains C=K=1+theta/4. Therefore reducing the pointwise-static
premium below this value must sacrifice optimal worst production-normalized
funding. This resolves the apparent tension between a positive cross-profile
premium and equality of the optimal worst static/strategic funding constants.
For m=1 this tradeoff is false: (theta M,0,T-theta M) is efficient and
truthful, with C=K=1. This correction does not alter the uniform sharp theorem.
See application/weighted/PARETO_NOTE_v2.txt; coordinator and independent lane
checked this short corollary after the full-rule review snapshot.
