INDEPENDENT AUDIT: FIXED PUBLIC HETEROGENEOUS POD VALUES
16 October 2026. Read-only audit of institution/PUBLIC_VALUE_EXTENSION.txt.

VERDICT
The extension is valid as stated. It preserves the uniform sharp factor
1+theta/4, the exact floor theta times A's grand-coalition marginal, and all-
profile own-bundle monotonicity. No substantive mathematical correction found.
The crucial assumptions are fixed public nonnegative values, the SAME value
for local and grid service at a given pod, unit input consumption in both
modes, and the clean fixed ownership roles. The theorem is not for arbitrary
mixed ownership or route-specific values/costs.

1. LOCAL-FIRST REDUCTION IS EXACT FOR EVERY COALITION
Fix a coalition S. Its h_i,L_i,P are actual capacities if their owners belong
to S and zero otherwise. Let ell_i=min(h_i,L_i), r_i=h_i-ell_i. A feasible
allocation has local service a_i and grid service g_i satisfying
  0<=a_i<=ell_i, a_i+g_i<=h_i, sum g_i<=P.
Replace it by a'_i=ell_i and g'_i=min(g_i,h_i-ell_i). Grid use cannot rise.
Its service to pod i is
  a'_i+g'_i=min(ell_i+g_i,h_i)>=a_i+g_i.
Thus nonnegative same-route value v_i cannot decrease. This proves that
maximizing local output first is without loss, even if the initial allocation
used grid on a pod with available local energy. It also handles v_i=0.

After local service is fixed, grid allocation is the fractional-knapsack
problem max sum v_i g_i with0<=g_i<=r_i and sum g_i<=P. A fixed public
descending order v_1>=...>=v_m admits the feasible greedy allocation
  g_i=min(r_i,(P-sum_{j<i}r_j)_+).
For every prefix k simultaneously,
  sum_{i<=k}g_i=min(P,sum_{i<=k}r_i).
This simultaneous attainment is the load-bearing reason that the proposed
decomposition is an equality, rather than a sum of incompatible optima.

2. COALITIONWISE PREFIX IDENTITY
Set v_{m+1}=0 and Delta_k=v_k-v_{k+1}>=0. For any service vector s,
  sum_i v_i s_i=sum_k Delta_k sum_{i<=k}s_i.
Every residual allocation has prefix grid total at most min(P,r(prefix));
the greedy allocation attains all these bounds simultaneously. Therefore
the actual production value of EVERY coalition equals
  F_v(S)=sum_k Delta_k [ell(prefix_k,S)+min(P(S),r(prefix_k,S))].

Missing energy owners cause no exception:
* Without A, every ell_i is0 and r_i=h_i; the same greedy grid proof applies.
* Without C, P=0, no grid is allocated, and both sides equal sum_i v_i ell_i.
* Without both, both sides are0.
* An absent hardware owner makes h_i=ell_i=r_i=0 at that pod, regardless of
  A's retained local capacity. The greedy formula and identity still apply.
Thus the decomposition is valid coalitionwise on exactly the original game.
It does not introduce transferable power between independent physical copies.

3. FIXED OWNERS, DEVIATIONS, AND FLOOR
Every prefix component retains the full owner set A,C,H_1,...,H_m. It ignores
resource rows beyond its prefix; these hardware owners are null in that
component. This does not split, delete, or relabel an actual owner across
the mechanism. The public order and Delta weights are fixed before reports.

For A, a simultaneous underreport of multiple local rows is an allowed
whole-bundle deviation in each prefix; each component's own-payment theorem
already covers it. Ignored local coordinates have no effect in that component.
For H_i, prefixes containing i use the same one actual hardware report and
have own-report monotonicity; other prefixes pay that owner zero. For C,
each prefix sees the same actual P, and its component payment is monotone
in P. Therefore y_i=sum_k Delta_k y_i^k is nonnegative and own-bundle
monotone for every owner on every real nonnegative report profile.

Coalition coverage follows by summing the component coverage inequalities
with the nonnegative weights and using the exact coalitionwise identity.
In particular, since each component pays A exactly theta times its own
grand marginal,
  y_A=theta sum_k Delta_k[T_k(N)-T_k(N\{A})]
     =theta[F_v(N)-F_v(N\{A})].
No approximation or inequality is needed for the floor.

4. BUDGET, ZERO CASES, SHARPNESS, AND COMPLEXITY
Summing component budgets gives
  sum_i y_i <=(1+theta/4)sum_k Delta_k T_k(N)
            =(1+theta/4)F_v(N).
If F_v(N)=0, every component with positive weight has zero production and
zero payments; zero-weight components contribute nothing. If every value is
zero, all Delta weights and all aggregate payments vanish. The base rule's
zero-own-resources property also survives the weighted sum.

The all-unit-value even-m family is contained in the heterogeneous class.
It therefore proves the same best UNIVERSAL factor1+theta/4, with growing
active owner/resource counts. This is not a claim that every particular
fixed heterogeneous vector has that same optimal factor.

Sorting takes O(m log m). Evaluating prefix k and adding its active payments
takes O(k) arithmetic operations; summing over all k gives O(m^2), plus
sorting. This is a real/rational arithmetic-operation statement, not a new
bit-complexity theorem or a linear-time implementation claim.

ADDITIONAL CHECK: SAME-RULE PARETO INEQUALITY
Reviewed institution/PARETO_NOTE.txt. The algebra is valid for a fixed m>=2
(or for a uniform mechanism family whose domain includes the two-pod case):
  C+K>=2+theta/2.
Here C and K must describe the SAME mechanism. This is not an inequality
between the two infima obtained by optimizing each objective separately.

At O, sum of the two grid-plus-hardware constraints gives
2c_O+x_O+y_O>=2. With the floor a_O>=theta and total_O<=2C,
  c_O>=2+theta-2C.
Only the grid report increases from O to V, so c_V>=c_O. At V, local owner
plus both hardware owners produce2, and thus total_V>=2+c_V. Since B(V)=2,
  2K>=total_V>=4+theta-2C,
which proves the tradeoff. If m>2, give the other fixed hardware/local rows
zero capacities. Additional nonnegative payments only strengthen the same
inequalities, and the exact static B(V) remains2.

The new continuous rule has C=1+theta/4 at O. At V, its hardware payments
remain1 each, A receives0, and C receives theta/2, so K=1+theta/4 there.
The general upper gives no larger ratios. Thus this rule attains the tradeoff
line, and any rule with minimal C=1+theta/4 must have K>=1+theta/4. The stated
numerical implications C>=13/10 for K=6/5 at theta1 and C>=81/68 for K=18/17
at theta1/2 are arithmetically correct. They are necessary conditions, not
continuous attainability claims or a characterization of the whole frontier.

SCOPE FINDING -- SENT TO AUTHOR AND COORDINATOR
The Pareto note's initial unrestricted 'any mechanism' wording must explicitly
exclude the one-pod subclass or say it treats a family containing m=2. The
tradeoff is FALSE for m=1 and theta>0. A concrete counterexample is
  (y_A,y_C,y_H)=(theta M,0,T-theta M).
This vector is nonnegative and efficient. Its C+H payment is
T-theta M=G+(1-theta)M>=G, its A+H payment is T>=ell, and the grand coalition
is covered exactly. All remaining positive-worth coalitions are included in
those cases. A's payment is own-local monotone. The hardware payment equals
(1-theta)T+theta G, both summands monotone in its hardware report. C's payment
is constant zero. The floor is exact, and zero-production profiles pay zero.
Therefore the one-pod rule has C=K=1, with B=T at every profile. This does not
contradict uniform sharpness over all m or the m>=2 Pareto inequality.
