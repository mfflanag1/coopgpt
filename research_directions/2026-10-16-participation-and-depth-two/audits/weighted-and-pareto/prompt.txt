17:   L=sum ell_i, Q=sum r_i, H=L+Q,
18:   G=min(P,H), R=min(P,Q), T=L+R, M=T-G.

58: GRID-OWNER COALITIONS: THE LOAD-BEARING BOUND
59: Fix all h,L reports and regard hardware payments as functions x_i(p) of a
60: counterfactual grid report p. For hardware subset I write H_I=sum_I h_i and
61:   d_I(p)=min(p,H_I)-sum_I x_i(p).

63: ell(I)/L<=1. On p>=H_I it is nonincreasing. Consequently its own-past maximum
64: at current P is
65:   c_I=d_I(t), t=min(P,H_I).

68: We prove X+c_env-G<=L/4. Since x_i has saturated by P=H, replace current P by
69: p=G without changing X or any t=min(P,H_I). Fix a candidate I and set

72:   X+c_I-p=sum_{j notin I}x_j(p).
73: For each j outside I, Q-r_j>=sum_{i in I}r_i, so
74:   (p-Q+r_j)/L <= (p-r(I))/L <=ell(I)/L=s.
75: Therefore the preceding sum is at most L(1-s)s.

80: inside payments from t to p is at most s(p-t)=Ls delta, by the same Lipschitz
81: bound. Hence
82:   X+c_I-p

84:   <=L(1-s)[clip(s+delta)-delta]
