DIRECT LOCAL-RESIDUAL COMPOSITION: A SUB-TWO BUDGET WITH AN EXACT INCENTIVE OBSTRUCTION
16 October2026. New bounded attack; mathematical priority unassessed.

MODEL. D_d has activities p--h_j and h_j--l_j, unit profits/consumption. Actual owners have arbitrary fixed nonnegative bundles. Let H=sum_j h_j,M=sum_j min(h_j,l_j),R=H-M. Then
 F=M+min(p,R)=min(H,p+M).
At each local pair (h_j,l_j), run the proved mixed-owner two-input exponential rule, with total factor C=1+1/(2e). Let z_i be the sum of these local payments, and z_i^h the part paid against owner i's hardware accounts. For every coalition S, z(S)>=M(S), and z(N)<=C M. Each z_i is own-report nondecreasing.

CANDIDATE A. Define residual hardware account a_i=(H_i-z_i)_+, retain the owner's shared-power account p_i, run the mixed two-input rule on (a,p), obtaining t_i, and pay y_i=z_i+t_i.

COALITION COVERAGE IS VALID. Let z_S=sum_{i in S}z_i and a_S=sum_{i in S}(H_i-z_i)_+. If z_S>=H_S coverage is immediate. Otherwise a_S>=H_S-z_S and
 z_S+t(S)>=z_S+min(a_S,p_S)>=min(H_S,z_S+p_S)
          >=min(H_S,M(S)+p_S)=F(S).

BUDGET IS STRICTLY BELOW TWO. The local hardware part satisfies z_i^h<=H_i, and z_i>=z_i^h, so sum a_i<=H-Z_h with Z_h=sum_i z_i^h. For each local branch,
  min(h_j,l_j)-Z_h,j <= h_j/2.
If h_j<=l_j, hardware is the short pool and receives at least h_j/2. If h_j>=l_j, write eta=h_j/l_j>=1 (zero l_j is trivial) and rho=sum_i min(h_ij,l_j)/l_j<=eta. The hardware payment is (l_j/2)rho exp(1-rho)>= (h_j/2)exp(1-eta). The inequality eta[1+exp(1-eta)]>=2 for eta>=1 then proves the claim. Summing gives M-Z_h<=H/2.
Hence
 y(N)<=C M+C min(p,H-Z_h).
If p<=H-M, this is at most C F. If p>=H-M, F=H and the displayed bound is at most C(M+H-Z_h)<=3C H/2. Thus this candidate always covers and has total factor
  (3/2)(1+1/(2e)) approximately1.77591 <2.
It fails truthfulness, as the next exact example shows.

EXACT THREE-OWNER, TWO-BRANCH COUNTEREXAMPLE.
Fix owners I,A,B. Their allowed coordinates are fixed throughout:
 I owns h_2=1,p=2,and l_1=x, with0<x<1;
 A owns h_1=1;
 B owns l_1=1.
All other reports are0. The full production value is F=2. On local branch1, hardware total1 is short, capped local-power total1+x, and
 q=(1/2)exp(-x).
The local payments are z_I=qx,z_A=1-q,z_B=q. Branch2 has no local value.
Candidate A has residual accounts
 a_I=1-qx,a_A=q,a_B=0, so T_res=1+q(1-x)<2.
All shared power belongs to I and is capped at T_res; thus the residual rule's long-pool capped ratio is1 and its mixing coefficient is exactly1/2.
Consequently
 t_I=(a_I+T_res)/2=1+q/2-qx,
 y_I=z_I+t_I=1+q/2=1+(1/4)exp(-x).
This strictly DECREASES as I's own local-power report x increases. In particular reporting1/2 rather than3/4 raises I's payment. Owners remain fixed, and the deviation changes one own coordinate only. No owner is veto: each complement coalition has positive production.

CANDIDATE B, A NATURAL REPAIR, ALSO FAILS. Deduct only a fixed fraction beta of local hardware payments, using a_i=H_i-beta z_i^h with0<beta<=1, and again pay z_i+t_i. In the same profile, a_I=1 and a_A=1-beta+beta q; the residual total2-beta+beta q is strictly below p=2. Therefore
 y_I=1+(1-beta)/2+q(x+beta/2),
 dy_I/dx=q(1-x-beta/2).
For every beta>0 this is negative whenever1-beta/2<x<1. Thus no positive fixed residual-credit fraction repairs incentives within this architecture. This obstruction does not cover owner-specific adaptive corrections, different inner/outer mechanisms, or an unrestricted truthful rule.

LOAD-BEARING LESSON. Local subsidy credits have a nonlocal effect: a local-power report reduces another owner's residual hardware account, lowering the reporting owner's shared-power payment. Merely composing individually truthful primitives does not preserve truthfulness, even with three fixed owners, positive full production and no veto owner. A successful local-first rule needs an explicit payment correction for this spillover; the candidate's budget/coverage proofs alone are insufficient.
