NESTED LOCAL-TO-GLOBAL ENVELOPE: A VALID RESPONSE LEMMA AND A FAILED BUDGET REDUCTION
16 October 2026. Mathematical priority unassessed; partial result, not a truthful sub-two theorem.

MODEL/PRIMITIVE. The fixed-owner depth-two production model and local payments z_i are as in RESIDUAL_COMPOSITION.txt. Write C=1+1/(2e), H_i=sum_j h_ij, B_i=p_i+z_i. Apply the mixed-owner two-pool exponential rule to accounts (H_i,B_i), calling the result N_i.

VALID COVERAGE AND BASE BUDGET. Each real coalition has B(S)>=p(S)+M(S), so outer coverage gives N(S)>=min(H(S),B(S))>=F(S). Also sum z_i<=C M and therefore outer value T'<=min(H,p+C M)<=C min(H,p+M)=C F. Hence sum N_i<=C^2 F. This says nothing by itself about truthfulness: an owner's report changes OTHER owners' local payments z_k and hence their outer accounts B_k.

CROSS-INFLUENCE LEMMA. In the mixed two-pool exponential rule, fix owner i's accounts (a_i,b_i), all a accounts, and vary other owners' b accounts. Payment to i is coordinatewise nondecreasing in each b_k (k!=i) if a_i>=b_i, and nonincreasing if a_i<=b_i. It is globally 1/2-Lipschitz in their l1 distance. At a_i=b_i, its payment is always a_i, independently of other b accounts.

PROOF. Let A=sum a,B=sum b,T=min(A,B), q=(1/2)exp(1-rho), where rho is the capped long-pool total divided by T. Always rho>=1 and q rho<=1/2. All derivatives below are taken off cap/tie boundaries; continuity extends the result.
If B>=A>0, d_i=min(b_i,A), y_i=a_i+q(d_i-a_i). For an uncapped other b_k, derivative is -q(d_i-a_i)/A; capped others give zero. Its sign is that of a_i-b_i, and absolute value<=q<=1/2.
If 0<B<A, write rho=k+t where k counts capped a accounts and t is the sum of uncapped a accounts divided by B. Then dq/dB=q t/B. If a_i<B, dy_i/dB=q t(a_i-b_i)/B, with required sign and magnitude<=q t<=1/2. If a_i>B, dy_i/dB=q[1+t(1-b_i/B)]>=0; here a_i>B>=b_i and k>=1, so derivative<=q(1+t)<=q rho<=1/2. Limits cover zero totals. A line changing one external coordinate encounters finitely many cap/tie pieces, proving global monotonicity and the Lipschitz claim. Coordinate telescoping handles arbitrary changes.

ENVELOPE REDUCTION. Set E_i(b)=sup_{0<=b'_i<=b_i} N_i(b'_i,b_-i). These payments are own-report nondecreasing and cover every coalition because E_i>=N_i. (All ownership domains are fixed; the sup is over own feasible coordinatewise underreports.) Both own outer accounts H_i and p_i+z_i are own-report monotone. For any underreport, first raise just those two own outer accounts to their full values; the outer primitive's own-monotonicity can only increase payment. The cross-influence lemma then gives
 E_i <= N_i + I_i/2,
where, with the direction evaluated at the full profile,
 I_i=sup_{b'_i<=b_i} sum_{k!=i}(z_k(b'_i,b_-i)-z_k(b))_+ if H_i>B_i;
 I_i=sup_{b'_i<=b_i} sum_{k!=i}(z_k(b)-z_k(b'_i,b_-i))_+ if H_i<B_i;
 I_i=0 if H_i=B_i.
The sufficient bound sum I_i<=C F would imply total E<2F, since C^2+C/2 approximately1.99368. BUT THIS SUFFICIENT BOUND IS FALSE, as follows.

EXACT INFLUENCE OBSTRUCTION. One branch, four fixed owners: owners1,2 each have h=1 and shared p=1; owners3,4 each have l=1. Local full payments are z_i=1/2, H=2,M=2,p=2,F=2. Every owner's direction H_i-(p_i+z_i) is strictly negative. If any owner withdraws its one local coordinate, the opposite pool becomes long with rho=2 and q=1/(2e). Each of its two opposite-pool owners' local payments drops from1/2 to1/(2e). Thus I_i>=1-1/e for all four owners, and
 sum I_i/F >=2(1-1/e) approximately1.26424 >C.
It even exceeds 2(2-C^2) approximately1.19657, so replacing C by a slightly larger coefficient cannot close the gap through the same uniform charge. This is a counterexample to the influence-bound proof strategy, NOT to the envelope's subsidy bound. The proof relaxation discards loss from reduced own accounts and can overestimate actual gain.

EXACT FOUR-OWNER COUNTEREXAMPLE TO N ITSELF. Two local branches. Fixed owner I has h_2=5,p=3,h_1=x; owners A,B each have h_1=1; owner L has l_1=1; all remaining coordinates vanish. Let 3/4<=x<=7/8. All complement coalitions have positive value. Local branch1 has l=1 short, all three hardware accounts individually at most1, and
 q=(1/2)exp(-1-x),
 z_I=xq, z_A=z_B=q, z_L=1-q.
The outer short-pool total is T'=4+(1+x)q. Owner I's hardware account5+x exceeds T', while both other hardware accounts1 are uncapped. Thus the outer mixing coefficient is r=(1/2)exp(-2/T') and
 N_I=3+xq+r(1+q).
Since q'=-q, (T')'=-xq<0 and r'=2r(T')'/(T')^2<0,
 N_I'=q(1-x-r)+r'(1+q)<0.
Indeed 1-x<=1/4 and r>(1/2)exp(-1/2)>1/4; the last inequality follows from e<4. Therefore reporting x=3/4 instead of the true x=7/8 strictly increases payment. Grand production F=4 at both reports. This confirms that the envelope/own-loss correction addresses an actual failure, rather than a merely incomplete monotonicity proof.

JOINT BASE-PLUS-INFLUENCE CHARGE ALSO FAILS. One might retain actual base sum N rather than its universal C^2F bound and hope sum N_i+(1/2)sum I_i<2F. The following six-owner rational profile rules out that sufficient inequality as well. One local branch; four hardware owners each have h=1/2, and two local-power owners each have l=1/2; all shared power is0. Then F=1 and the local rule has q=1/(2e), Z=1+q=C. All hardware owners have positive direction H_i-z_i>0; both local-power owners have negative direction. Outer hardware accounts1/2 are all uncapped, so
 sum N_i=C+(1/2)exp(1-2/C)(2-C) approximately1.3887462656.
Each hardware owner may withdraw its hardware. The three other hardware payments increase by a total(3/4)[exp(-1/2)-exp(-1)], giving aggregate positive influence at least3[exp(-1/2)-exp(-1)]. Each local-power owner may withdraw its local power. The four hardware payments then decrease from total exp(-1) to exp(-3), giving aggregate negative influence at least2[exp(-1)-exp(-3)]. Consequently
 sum I_i >=3exp(-1/2)-exp(-1)-2exp(-3) approximately1.3521384012,
 sum N_i+(1/2)sum I_i >=2.0648154661>2F.
All owners and their coordinate domains remain fixed. Every complement coalition has positive production. This profile belongs to the depth-two model by setting unused branches/resources to0; the strict inequality also persists under sufficiently small positive perturbations by continuity. The failed sufficient charge again discards own-account loss; neither this example nor the earlier examples prove that the actual envelope E exceeds2F.
