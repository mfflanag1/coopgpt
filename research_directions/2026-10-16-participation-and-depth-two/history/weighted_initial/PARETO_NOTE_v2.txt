Two funding objectives cannot both improve: exact tradeoff at the sharp rule
16 October2026. Corrected v2: explicitly requires fixed m>=2, or a uniform
family containing all m. Supersedes PARETO_NOTE.txt; preserves its frozen hash.

Fix m>=2 and theta in[0,1]. For any SINGLE all-profile mechanism satisfying
the public marginal floor theta M_A, let C be its worst total funding divided
by production T, and K its worst total funding divided by pointwise static
need B_theta; both suprema range over T>0. This also holds for a uniform family
of mechanisms over all m, with the suprema additionally over m. The two
objectives refer to the SAME mechanism, not independently minimized infima. Then
  C+K >=2+theta/2.

Proof uses O=(1,1,1,1,1),V=(2,1,1,1,1), in capacity order(P,h1,h2,L1,L2),
and payment order(A,C,H1,H2). For m>2, set all other hardware and local
capacities to zero, preserving the full fixed owner set. Extra payments, if
any, only strengthen the total-funding inequalities below.
At O, floor a>=theta and the two grid-plus-single-hardware coalition constraints
give2c+x+y>=2. Since total_O>=a+c+x+y and total_O<=2C,
  c_O >=2+a_O-total_O >=2+theta-2C.
Own-grid monotonicity gives c_V>=c_O. At V, A+both hardware can produce2, so
  total_V>=2+c_V>=4+theta-2C.
Since B_theta(V)=2, total_V<=2K. Rearranging proves the inequality.

The continuous rule in CONTINUOUS_ATTACK.txt has
  C=K=1+theta/4.
Both upper bounds hold because total<=(1+theta/4)T and B_theta>=T. Equality for
C holds at O; equality for K holds at V, where hardware payments remain1 each,
the grid owner receives theta/2, and A receives0. It therefore lies exactly on
the lower tradeoff line. Any rule achieving the optimal C=1+theta/4 must have
K>=1+theta/4. Reducing K below that value necessarily sacrifices worst total
funding efficiency. This does NOT determine the remaining Pareto frontier or
the unconstrained infimum K_theta.

For theta1, the finite-grid lower K>=6/5 does not contradict this statement:
achieving K6/5 would require C>=13/10. For theta1/2, a K18/17 mechanism requires
C>=81/68. These are necessary tradeoffs, not claimed continuous attainability.

WHY m>=2 IS NECESSARY
For m=1 the rule(A,C,H)=(theta M,0,T-theta M) is efficient, nonnegative,
coalition-covering and own-monotone. Hardware payment equals
(1-theta)T+theta G, which is own-hardware monotone. A's theta M is own-local
monotone; C's payment is zero. Coalition A+H receives T>=ell, coalition C+H
receives T-theta M=G+(1-theta)M>=G, and singleton values are zero. Thus
B_theta=T and this rule has C=K=1. The earlier wording 'any mechanism' in
the m>=1 ambient model omitted this exception; that wording was incorrect.
This correction does not change the m>=2 proof or the universal-in-m result.
