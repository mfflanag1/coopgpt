SHARP CONTINUOUS FAIR-FLOOR RULE: C_theta=1+theta/4
16 October2026, follow-up after curated packet freeze. Candidate conventional
proof requiring independent audit. No historical novelty claim. This is the
PUBLIC MARGINAL-FLOOR extension, not arbitrary mixed ownership/floor-free work.

MODEL
Use the exact clean K2,m fixed-owner contract of PARTICIPATION_THEOREMS.txt,
m>=1, arbitrary nonnegative real reports, unit coefficients/values. Actual owner
A exclusively controls all local capacities L_i; C exclusively controls shared
grid capacity P; separate owner H_i controls hardware h_i at pod i. One output
unit uses one hardware unit and either one local unit at that same pod or one
shared grid unit. Coalition resources are exactly its members' reports. Owners
may withhold any coordinatewise smaller part of their fixed bundle, with no
additional report-dependent outside utility. Identity and roles never change.
Put
  ell_i=min(h_i,L_i), r_i=h_i-ell_i,
  L=sum ell_i, Q=sum r_i, H=L+Q,
  G=min(P,H), R=min(P,Q), T=L+R, M=T-G.
The public floor is y_A>=theta M,0<=theta<=1. No private-cost utility is added.
The static optimum satisfies sup B_theta/T=1+theta/4 by the frozen theorem.

THETA=1 RULE (linear time in number of pods)
If L=0, pay A0,C=G, all hardware0. Otherwise let clip(z)=min(1,max(0,z)) and
  x_i=ell_i clip((P-Q+r_i)/L), X=sum_i x_i,
  y_A=M,
  y_Hi=x_i,
  y_C=G-(X-L/4)_+ = min(G,G-X+L/4).
Then payments are nonnegative, coalition-covering, and own-bundle-monotone.
Their total is exactly T+min(X,L/4)<=5T/4. They vanish at T=0.
Each owner also receives zero when all her own resources are zero.

INCENTIVES AND NONNEGATIVITY
A: M=min(L,(H-P)_+) where H is fixed under A's reports; hence M is own-A
monotone in every L_i coordinate. For hardware i, the quantity
  p_i=(P-sum_{j!=i}r_j)_+
and L_-i=sum_{j!=i}ell_j are fixed under h_i changes. Its payment is
  min(ell_i, p_i ell_i/(L_-i+ell_i)),
with the natural zero value if the denominator vanishes. Both displayed terms
are nondecreasing in ell_i, hence in h_i. This includes crossing h_i=L_i and
the L=0 boundary. For C, H,L,Q,r_i are fixed. Each x_i(P) is nondecreasing and
ell_i/L-Lipschitz, so X(P) is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz. Also X saturates at
L by P=H because H-Q+r_i=L+r_i>=L. Therefore, for P'<=P,
  0<=X(P)-X(P')<=G(P)-G(P').
It follows that G-(X-L/4)_+ is nondecreasing. Since P-Q+r_i<=P, each x_i is
at most ell_i min(1,P/L), so X<=min(L,P)<=G. Thus y_C>=0. If P=0, X=G=0;
if h_i=0 then ell_i=x_i=0; if all L_i=0 then M=0. These claims also hold at
zero-production profiles. No strict truthfulness or coalition-strategyproofness
is claimed.

LOCAL-OWNER COALITIONS
Let D=G-R. Then M=L-D and0<=D<=L. Since r_i>=0,
  x_i>=ell_i clip((P-Q)/L)=ell_i D/L.
Thus for any hardware subset I,
  sum_I(ell_i-x_i)<=L-D=M,
and A plus I receives at least ell(I), its local-only worth. The L=0 case is
immediate.

GRID-OWNER COALITIONS: THE LOAD-BEARING BOUND
Fix all h,L reports and regard hardware payments as functions x_i(p) of a
counterfactual grid report p. For hardware subset I write H_I=sum_I h_i and
  d_I(p)=min(p,H_I)-sum_I x_i(p).
On p<=H_I this is nondecreasing: the subtracted sum has Lipschitz constant
ell(I)/L<=1. On p>=H_I it is nonincreasing. Consequently its own-past maximum
at current P is
  c_I=d_I(t), t=min(P,H_I).
Define c_env=max_I c_I (including the empty set). Clearly0<=c_env<=G.

We prove X+c_env-G<=L/4. Since x_i has saturated by P=H, replace current P by
p=G without changing X or any t=min(P,H_I). Fix a candidate I and set
  s=ell(I)/L in[0,1].
If H_I>=p, then t=p and
  X+c_I-p=sum_{j notin I}x_j(p).
For each j outside I, Q-r_j>=sum_{i in I}r_i, so
  (p-Q+r_j)/L <= (p-r(I))/L <=ell(I)/L=s.
Therefore the preceding sum is at most L(1-s)s.

If H_I<p, write delta=(p-H_I)/L>0 and t=H_I. For j outside I,
  (p-Q+r_j)/L <= s+delta,
so outside payment is at most L(1-s)clip(s+delta). The total increase of
inside payments from t to p is at most s(p-t)=Ls delta, by the same Lipschitz
bound. Hence
  X+c_I-p
  = sum_outside x_j(p) +sum_inside[x_i(p)-x_i(t)] -(p-t)
  <=L(1-s)[clip(s+delta)-delta]
  <=L s(1-s)<=L/4.
The middle inequality holds for delta<=1-s by equality, and for delta>=1-s
because1-delta<=s. Maximizing over I proves c_env<=G-X+L/4. Together with
c_env<=G this gives c_env<=min(G,G-X+L/4)=y_C. Since c_env>=d_I(P), every
grid-plus-hardware coalition is covered by y_C+sum_I x_i>=min(P,H_I).

BOTH ENERGY OWNERS
Its worth is v(I)=ell(I)+min(P,r(I)). Its incremental local contribution
M_I=v(I)-min(P,H_I)=min(ell(I),(H_I-P)_+) is at most
M=min(L,(H-P)_+). The grid-coalition inequality therefore gives
  y_A+y_C+sum_I x_i >=M+min(P,H_I)>=v(I).
Coalitions with neither energy owner have zero worth. All coalitions covered.

BUDGET AND ALL THETA
By definition,
  y_A+X+y_C=M+G+X-(X-L/4)_+=T+min(X,L/4)<=T+L/4<=5T/4.
Mix the theta=1 rule with the original efficient own-monotone rule
(0,R,ell_i), with fixed mixture weight theta. Fixed convex combinations preserve
nonnegativity, coverage, and own-report monotonicity. A receives exactlytheta M;
the total is T+theta min(X,L/4)<=(1+theta/4)T. The frozen O-profile static lower
requires factor>=1+theta/4. Thus the exact continuous WORST TOTAL funding factor
uniform over every finite m is C_theta=1+theta/4, even though the best
pointwise-static-normalized factor
K_theta is strictly greater than1 whenever theta>0. These are compatible facts.

ACTIVE UNBOUNDED SHARPNESS
For every even m take h_i=L_i=1 and P=m/2. All actual owners/resources are active.
Then T=m and M=m/2. A+all hardware requires a+sumx>=m. Average the grid-plus-I
coalition inequalities over all size-m/2 subsets I: c+(1/2)sumx>=m/2.
Add one half of (a+sumx>=m), one full copy of the averaged grid inequality,
and one half of (a>=theta m/2). The resulting left side is exactly total,
and therefore
  total>=m+theta m/4.
Thus sharpness holds with both owner and resource counts growing, not merely
through zero padding. The averaging ingredient is standard balanced counting.
The displayed rule is an upper bound for each fixed m; the uniform sharpness
claim does not assert this implementation is optimal on the one-pod subclass.
