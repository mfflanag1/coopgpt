Before numerical inspection, define a qualified snapshot as having all of:
1. One explicit decision instant and a complete relevant pending-job set, with
   semantics for scheduled/running/finished jobs and current reservations.
2. Identifiable whole devices with authoritative family and per-device VRAM;
   available CPU/RAM and unallocated devices on each selected node at that instant.
3. Ex-ante job CPU/RAM, GPU count, minimum per-device VRAM, allowed types and
   same-node gang semantics; no substitution of realized averages/peaks for requests.
4. Explicit owner identities and job placement permissions plus declared numerical
   priority scores. User/department labels are not resource-owner consent; a
   priority class is not a numerical value function.
5. An explicit bounded subproblem within 4 nodes/8 whole GPUs/8 jobs, with its
   selection and excluded workload/reservations accounted for, and a genuine
   observed placement/operator comparator if claiming improvement over practice.

Missing any item closes the full-contract gate. No default zero RAM/VRAM, synthetic
device IDs presented as observed identities, inferred consent, arbitrary numerical
priorities, cross-release ID joins or invented start/finish/placement records.
No optimizer is run on incomplete records. A reduced-resource simulation may be a
separate future study only with explicit assumptions; it is not this validation.
