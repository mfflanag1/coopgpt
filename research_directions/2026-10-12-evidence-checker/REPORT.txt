The native error/referral cases:
T-04: The primary called automatic shrinking/unmapping of expandable allocation
segments contradicted, citing growth behavior. The packet states growth but
neither establishes nor rules out the claimed shrink behavior. Frozen gold is
insufficient. The checker rejected this unsupported contradiction.
A-01: The primary returned supported with only D1. The frozen proof requires D1+D2:
D1 states the divisibility condition, while D2 identifies its matching-unit subject.
The checker rejected this incomplete citation proof. The verdict itself was right.
It also rejected the complete valid control, so rejection alone does not show
that it correctly diagnosed the missing citation.
A-03: The primary returned insufficient even though D1+D2 identify the parameter
and state startup validation with an error for offending sizes, contradicting
silent rounding. The checker accepted the incorrect insufficient verdict.
V-03: The primary correctly returned contradicted with D2: graph executables outside
PyTorch pools stay resident, contrary to removal from GPU memory. The checker
rejected this correct answer, creating the avoidable native referral.
These descriptions follow packet-relative frozen annotations; the checker returns
only a boolean, so they do not establish its internal reasoning or causal failure.

There are 24 distinct claim problems, not 36 or 72. The 24 constructed proposals use
12 of those claims; native and controlled results are deliberately separate and
are not independent confirmations. All 48 checker wire inputs differ exactly, but
there are eight pairs with equivalent proposal JSON after ignoring object-key
order and whitespace (40 unique inputs by that diagnostic, preserving array order).
Seven such pairs receive the same decision. The V-02 pair receives opposite decisions:
the native valid answer is accepted; its JSON-equivalent valid control is rejected.
This flags a robustness concern. A single unrandomized pair cannot isolate formatting,
order, caching, or backend variability as the cause. No calls were deduplicated,
excluded, rerun, or relabeled because of this observation.
