# Fresh claim pool — 24 proposed cases (read-only research report)

**Scope.** All assertions are about the supplied snapshot files only (developer snapshots, not product advice). Old cases (24) were read for exclusion; every proposition below targets a subsection not used as decisive evidence by any old case, and none is a paraphrase, polarity flip, or parameter swap of an old claim. No fractional-GPU example, no statistical/population/energy claims.

**Distribution.** 4 cases per source; totals 8 supported / 8 contradicted / 8 insufficient. Six decisive claims require two excerpts (K-01, T-01, R-01, L-01, A-02, V-01).

**Conventions.** Excerpts are verbatim, contiguous, identified by pool ID; anchors are exact start/end substrings with source line numbers. Slurm excerpts are given after `strip_html_and_collapse_whitespace` (same transformation as the old cases), with raw `.shtml` line refs. Char offsets / sha256 are **not** computed here (see Limitations). Each case lists its 3–4-excerpt packet, minimal sufficient sets (alternatives where they exist), relevant IDs, and rationale.
