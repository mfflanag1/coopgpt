EVIDENCE CHECKER: ONE BOUNDED PROSPECTIVE SCREEN (2026-10-12)

Question: Does a separate evidence-checking call reject unsupported answers on
fresh documentation claims without rejecting too many correct answers? This is a
small, deliberately balanced engineering screen, not a production accuracy,
performance, energy, pooling, model-ranking, or population-inference experiment.

Motivation: The prior frozen claim-qualification study completed 23/24 joint
verdict-and-citation decisions correctly. Its sole miss treated an unstated
fractional-GPU property as contradicted. That result, its inputs, and its scoring
remain unchanged. This study does not reuse that claim or collect more outcomes
under the old gate. It tests a new accept-or-review architecture on fresh claims.

Inputs and freshness: 24 newly authored claims, four per source section and eight
per gold verdict (supported/contradicted/insufficient). Reuse the six previously
pinned source snapshots and licenses; freshness applies to propositions and
assembled packets, not documentation versions. Exact-string exclusion is a mechanical check;
proposition freshness is source/author review judgment, not guaranteed by that check. The packets are 103-307 whitespace
words before any final pre-outcome adjudication; report final measured sizes.
Each packet contains three or four distinct excerpts. Cases include multi-excerpt proof obligations and unsupported negative as well as
positive conclusions. Sources are Kubernetes GPU/NFD, Ray resources, PyTorch CUDA
memory, Slurm GRES, vLLM APC, and vLLM sleep. Packet-only truth is the target; no
claim of current software behavior outside those snapshots. Source hashes,
character offsets, and declared HTML/whitespace transformations are preserved.
Author IDs are bookkeeping, never shown to the model. Freeze all amendments,
annotation decisions, exact cases, controls, prompts, schemas, code, and gates
before the first study response. Blind review uses claims and packets only.

Primary call: The same primary instruction and verdict/citation schema as the
previous claim study. Read the claim and packet; return supported, contradicted,
or insufficient plus up to three decisive citation IDs. Unsupported permission
is not prohibition; a requirement is not evidence of deployment compliance.
Supported requires the entire stated claim; contradicted requires its opposite
or incompatible conditions; insufficient requires neither and empty citations.
Score exact JSON with no prose, duplicate keys, repairs, or relaxed citations.
For a decisive verdict, citations must contain a declared sufficient proof and
no irrelevant citation. Alternatives are adjudicated before inference.

Checker: A fresh, stateless call on the same model receives the same packet and
claim plus the EXACT raw primary response, unparsed and unrepaired. Return only
{"accept":true} or {"accept":false}. No gold, prior outcomes, class balance,
control identity, error type, or other case context is sent. The checker audits
schema, verdict, full scope, missing premises, and citations from the packet.
A local schema check plus valid checker acceptance preserves the ORIGINAL answer.
Any primary format failure, checker format failure, or checker rejection routes
to human REVIEW. REVIEW is not a corrected answer, not an insufficient verdict,
and not a successful automated completion. Human review is not performed or costed
by this experiment. Same-model agreement is not independent source verification.

Controlled proposals: A fixed subset of 12 of those 24 claims, with one valid and
one invalid proposal each. Thus 24 distinct claims, 24 primary calls, and 48 checker
calls (24 native plus24 constructed). There are NOT36 distinct claims. Cases and
controls are fixed before any response; there is no outcome-conditioned selection.
Four missing-premise cases: K-03/T-04 false-supported, R-04/V-04 false-contradicted.
Four reversed verdict cases: A-01/T-02 supported-to-contradicted, K-02/V-02 reverse.
Four citation cases: R-01/L-01 omit one premise, A-03/L-03 cite a nondecisive excerpt.
Each invalid proposal is schema-valid and paired with a valid proposal for the
same claim; valid controls contain four of each verdict. Groups are local challenge
categories, not mutually exclusive scientific error taxonomies. This prevents a
schema-only checker or reject-everything policy from passing. Controls are a paired
stress test on reused claims; they are neither held out nor independent replications
of native answers. Never pool native and constructed accuracy. Keep all planned
calls even if a primary answer matches a controlled proposal; report duplicate
checker inputs separately without changing denominators or gate.

Order and runtime: 24 primary calls in SHA256('order:'+case_id) order, then all48
checker calls intermixed by SHA256('checker-order:'+request_id). Controlled proposal
serialization is json.dumps(candidate,sort_keys=True,separators=(',',':')). No
conversation context between calls. One warmup is unscored; max72 scored calls,
no retries, adaptive prompts, replacements, reruns, or post-outcome gate changes.
Qwen3:32b Q4_K_M on the inventoried M3 Max/96GB Mac with pinned Ollama0.20.0.
P1, context4096, temperature0, seed0, think=false and /no_think. Primary cap256,
checker64, warmup32. All other inherited runtime pins remain unchanged.
The owned localhost11562 server uses OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL=1; original11434 must be
idle and is never stopped. Verify model/binary/manifest pins, loaded context, no
thinking or truncation diagnostic in the server log. Require the observed quantity
4096-prompt_eval_count-output_cap>=128. This is an evaluation-count diagnostic, not
an independent tokenizer guarantee of full prompt length or absence of truncation;
we have not established how caching affects that API counter. Short packets and
server logs supply additional diagnostic evidence, not a formal token-count proof.
Total wall budget1800s from launch,300s reserve before another request,90s soft
request timeout,95s client hard bound,100s owned-server watchdog. Preserve failures
and partial records; incomplete data cannot pass. AC power or explicitly permitted
battery>=60%, nominal reported thermal state, and unchanged gates checked before
and after every scored call. Load is recorded but no new load cutoff is inferred.
Clean unload and owned-server stop required. This is serial correctness collection;
raw timing diagnoses execution only and is not a serving or energy benchmark.

Predeclared gates (all required):
1. Complete valid raw data, all24 primary and48 checker responses, exact prompt/
proposal binding, runtime/source freeze integrity, and clean lifecycle.
2. All48 checker responses are schema-valid. A malformed primary response is a
format referral, never counted as a semantic error caught by the checker.
3. Native: zero erroneous answers automatically accepted; at least18 correct
answers automatically retained; and at least90% of baseline-correct answers retained
(exact integer condition10*retained>=9*baseline_correct).
4. Controlled: all12 invalid proposals rejected; at least11 of12 valid proposals
accepted. Any failed criterion closes the engineering gate.
These thresholds are prospective engineering choices, not a powered hypothesis test.

Native sensitivity is observed only if at least one schema-valid baseline answer
is wrong. If none is wrong, the retention/control screen may pass, but catching
actual native errors remains unobserved. Accepted-correct count cannot exceed
baseline-correct count, because the checker never repairs answers. Report both
per-offered correct automated completion and conditional accepted quality, retaining
all24 cases in the principal denominator. Report baseline errors, errors caught,
errors accepted, correct answers referred, all referrals, and format referrals;
also report checker acceptance of malformed primary answers descriptively;
separate controlled rejection/retention by group and verdict. Report source-level
and case-level outcomes descriptively, not as independent samples or significance.

Decision: Complete this one experiment and audit it independently. Passing would
justify planning a later separately frozen downstream-serving test, not launching
performance/energy measurement automatically. Failure identifies a specific gate
and pauses that progression. Do not claim production reliability, measured energy
savings, economic pooling gains, or a field-level breakthrough from this screen.
