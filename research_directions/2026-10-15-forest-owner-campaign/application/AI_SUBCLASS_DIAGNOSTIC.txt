Interpretation

these domains. This is stronger evidence than merely failing to discover a
lower certificate: exact efficient finite-domain allocations were checked.
It is still not a mechanism on arbitrary real reports, nor a result for
unbounded m. An all-profile efficiency theorem, or a genuine counterexample
outside these grids, remains to be established. The ownership four-cycle by
itself must not be presented as proof of an economic incentive cost.

A useful exact production identity for a future analytic attempt is
 F(H,L,P)=sum_j min(H_j,L_j)+min(P,sum_j(H_j-L_j)^+).
It follows by using available local service first, then using grid power on
residual hardware, with a matching capacity upper bound. Apply the same
formula to coalition resources after zeroing absent owners. This identity
alone does not give own-monotone coalition-covering payments; in particular
its residual subtraction must not be treated as a positive composition of
known mechanisms without checking incentives.
