PROPOSED NEXT MATHEMATICAL CAMPAIGN -- NOT STARTED OR SCHEDULED

Objective: on depth-two subdivided stars p--h_j--l_j with an arbitrary number
of branches, prove a truthful coalition-covering rule whose total payment
is at most C*T for one absolute C<2. Actual owners may hold arbitrary mixed
bundles, both counts may grow, and every nonnegative real report is in scope.
Start with unit profits; the proved weighted-forest reduction handles prices
after a unit-profit theorem on an edge-deletion-closed class is established.

Public production is
 F(h,l,p)=sum_j min(h_j,l_j)+min(p,sum_j max(h_j-l_j,0)).
This O(n) value formula does not give a positive concave decomposition: local
power can decrease residual grid demand. A valid payment proof must control
that effect on the same owner's entire bundle.

Suggested fixed ceiling: six hours, with no automatic extension.
- First30minutes: freeze definitions, inherited results and exact gates.
- Next2hours: a direct allocation using the branch structure and actual owner
  bundles. Explore owner-specific supporting prices with an explicit new
  coalition-coverage coupling; neither coverage nor incentives may be inferred
  from numerical tests or strategic owner splitting.
- Parallel2-hour lane: compact owner-interaction-cycle lower searches with
  interacting whole-owner report changes; reconstruct exact certificates and
  use cycle-to-path transfer only within its proved scope.
- By hour4: either a concrete general lemma/theorem or an explicit obstruction
  to the proposed route. Do not spend the remaining time increasing a small
  finite grid without a new mathematical implication.
- Final2hours: independent proof audit, exact reproduction, source comparison,
  manuscript-ready statement or documented stopping result.

Positive success gate: a uniform C<2 theorem on the unbounded target, a stronger
unrestricted lower bound, or a structural lemma that demonstrably advances that
problem. A counterexample to a new restricted mechanism is a useful STOPPING
result, not satisfaction of the positive-progress gate.

Known filters: fixed-K positive concave decomposition is blocked; the target
has2^n exposed minimal covers. Fixed-q capped-support sampling is unbounded,
even with maximum-coalition-marginal caps and no veto owner. Fixed scalar-bias
tuning, grand-marginal/Shapley mixtures, and deterministic outside-scaling
marginal mixtures have explicit barriers. Another bounded-K primitive alone
does not meet the gate. Numerical promise without an all-profile proof does not.
The late Gibbs-cover test also blocks any report-independent single scalar
rate for the specified uniform-base-weight cover distribution; low rates
overpay and high rates violate incentives. Do not restart that candidate
without a material change to its architecture.

Broader parallel frontier: the unrestricted n/d gap now lies between logarithmic
lower bounds and square-root upper bounds. The fixed-mask class has matching
square-root growth, so an improvement must change a load-bearing mechanism
ingredient. Historical priority and human specialist review remain separate
gates from mathematical correctness.

Infrastructure interpretation: h_j is a fluid hardware reservation, l_j a local
energy allowance, and p a shared grid-energy allowance over one fixed period.
Ownership, reservation transfer, and binding withholding must be specified.
This is not yet a peak-power schedule, latency guarantee, private-cost mechanism,
or measured GPU pooling benefit.

Late applied refinement: the clean subclass with one owner of all local-energy
rights, one grid owner and separate hardware owners already has an efficient
fixed-order marginal rule when values and coefficients are1. The local-energy
owner receives0. Do not search for a positive subsidy lower bound for that
precise floor-free subclass. The full mixed-owner target remains open; for
the application, positive participation requirements or private outside
options must be specified before inferring a meaningful subsidy problem.
