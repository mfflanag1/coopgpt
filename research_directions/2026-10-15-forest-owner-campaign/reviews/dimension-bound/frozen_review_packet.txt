1: GENERAL RESOURCE-DIMENSION UPPER BOUND: (2+o(1))sqrt(d)
2: 15 October 2026. Candidate proof; independent review pending.
3: 
4: THEOREM
5: Let f:R_+^d ->R be finite, monotone, concave and homogeneous of degree one.
6: There are arbitrarily many but fixed n owners reporting whole bundles b_i>=0.
7: For every fixed 0<a<1, the existing relative-support/endpoint hybrid, averaged
8: uniformly over missing probability q in [0,a], is nonnegative, coalition-covering,
9: and nondecreasing in every owner's own bundle at every real profile. Its total
10: payment satisfies
11:  sum_i M_i(b) <= C_d(a) T(b),
12:  C_d(a)=[1+2d(-a-log(1-a))]/a.
13: In particular, taking a=1/(1+sqrt(d)) gives factor at most 1+2sqrt(d).
14: As d tends to infinity, a~1/sqrt(d) gives factor (2+o(1))sqrt(d).
15: Combining the existing dimension marginal bound and the new bound gives
16:  B_d^dim <= min{d, 1+2sqrt(d)},
17: and the same upper for arbitrary finite monotone concave homogeneous functions,
18: including boundary discontinuities. The bound is independent of n and the
19: number of supporting forms. It does not assert efficient oracle evaluation,
20: measurability of the full report map, or historical novelty.

22: THE UNCHANGED RULE
23: At each profile fix T=f(c), c=sum_i b_i. For a mask B, x_B=sum_i B_i b_i.
24: Choose the canonical relative support ell_(x_B) from the manuscript: it is
25: nonnegative linear on the coordinate face containing x_B, majorizes f there,
26: touches f at x_B, and equals +infinity outside that face. At x_B=0 it is zero
27: at0 and +infinity elsewhere. Put A_i(B)=min{ell_(x_B)(b_i),T}.
28: For independent B_i with Pr(B_i=0)=q, define
29:  p_i(q)=E[A_i(B)|B_i=0],
30:  m_i(q)=E[f(b_i+x_(B_-i))-f(x_(B_-i))],
31:  r_i(q)=q p_i(q)+(1-q)m_i(q),
32:  M_i=(1/a)int_0^a r_i(q)dq.
33: These are finite mask sums and integrals of finite polynomials; no random
34: sampling implementation or limit over technologies is needed. Eligibility is
35: the existing relative-support theorem: a closed support excludes its recipient,
36: both caps increase in the own report, endpoint marginals increase in the own
37: report, capped supports cover coalitions, and on-endpoint marginals dominate
38: on-support contributions. The proof works for every0<q<1.
39: 
40: SCALAR BERNOULLI RECIPROCAL LEMMA
41: Fix arbitrary u_1,...,u_n>=0 and let S_B=sum_i B_i u_i. Define
42:  R_i(B)=min{u_i/S_B,1}, taking R_i=0 if u_i=0 and R_i=1 if u_i>0,S_B=0.
43: Then, with p=1-q,
44:  E sum_(i:B_i=0) R_i(B) <= 2q/p.
45: Proof: for nonnegative u,S,
46:  min{u/S,1} <= 2u/(u+S),
47: with the same zero conventions. Conditioning on the own bit gives the exact
48: identity
49:  E sum_i (1-B_i) u_i/(u_i+S_B)
50:   = (q/p) E sum_i B_i u_i/S_B
51:   = (q/p) Pr(S_B>0) <=q/p.
52: The fractions with zero numerator and denominator are defined as zero.
53: The first equality holds because in either conditioned expression the ratio
54: is u_i/(u_i+sum_(j!=i) B_j u_j). Summing and applying the elementary inequality
55: proves the lemma. No lower bound on positive weights is used.
56: 
57: POINTWISE RESOURCE-COORDINATE DOMINATION
58: Assume T>0. For every mask and every owner i,
59:  A_i(B)/T <= sum_(e=1)^d min{b_ie/(x_B)_e,1},
60: with the scalar lemma's conventions coordinatewise.
61: If b_i has a positive coordinate outside the support of x_B, the right side
62: contains1 and the claim follows from A_i<=T. Otherwise the relative support
63: is an ordinary nonnegative linear form w on that face. Touching gives
64:  sum_e w_e (x_B)_e=f(x_B)<=T,
65: so for each positive coordinate (x_B)_e, w_e (x_B)_e<=T and
66:  w_e b_ie/T <= b_ie/(x_B)_e.
67: Use min{sum z_e,1}<=sum min{z_e,1} for nonnegative z_e to conclude. If x_B=0
68: and b_i=0, both sides are zero; the remaining zero-mask cases were handled
69: by the off-face argument. This also proves the same claim for any finite
70: nonnegative global support, regardless of minimizer ties.
71: 
72: EXPECTED CLOSED BUDGET
73: Apply the scalar lemma separately to each resource's weights u_i=b_ie.
74: The preceding pointwise inequality yields
75:  q sum_i p_i(q)=E sum_(i:B_i=0) A_i(B)
76:  <= [2d q/(1-q)]T.
77: Only d appears; owner count and support-form complexity have disappeared.
78: 
79: ENDPOINT BUDGET AND INTEGRATION
80: Let g(q)=E f(x_B), a finite polynomial. The elementary diagonal derivative
81: identity is sum_i m_i(q)=-g'(q). Therefore
82:  sum_i r_i(q) <= [2d q/(1-q)]T +(1-q)(-g'(q)).
83: Since g(0)=T and g>=0, integration by parts gives
84:  int_0^a (1-q)(-g'(q))dq
85:   =T-(1-a)g(a)-int_0^a g(q)dq <=T.
86: The integral of q/(1-q) is -a-log(1-a). Divide by a to obtain C_d(a).
87: At T=0 monotonicity makes all coalition worths, capped contributions and
88: endpoint marginals zero, so the same bound is immediate.

90: EXPLICIT PARAMETERS AND ASYMPTOTICS
91: For0<a<1,
92:  -a-log(1-a)=sum_(k>=2) a^k/k <=a^2/[2(1-a)].
93: Consequently C_d(a)<=1/a+d a/(1-a). The choice a=1/(1+sqrt(d)) gives
94: 1/a+d a/(1-a)=1+2sqrt(d). This a is at most1/2 for d>=1, so it stays inside
95: the old averaging interval range (although it need not be an inverse integer).
96: Alternatively choose j=ceil(sqrt(d))+1 and a=1/j to retain the literal old
97: integer parameter: C_d(1/j)<=j+d/(j-1)=(2+o(1))sqrt(d).
98: All parameters are chosen from the fixed resource dimension before reports;
99: there is no report-dependent retuning or deletion of zero owners/resources.
