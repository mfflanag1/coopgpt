1: All weighted double stars: positive decomposition into three-input components
2: 15 October 2026. Candidate structural lemma; historical novelty unconfirmed.
3: 
4: Fix centers u,v of capacities U,V, central edge profit a>0, leaves at u of
5: capacities s_j and edge profits b_j>0, and leaves at v of capacities t_k and
6: edge profits c_k>0. Endpoint consumption coefficients are initially one.
7: Define threshold capacities
8:   B(theta)=sum_{j:b_j>=theta}s_j,
9:   C(theta)=sum_{k:c_k>=theta}t_k.
10: For all nonnegative real capacities, the production value satisfies
11: 
12: F = integral_0^a min(U+C(a-theta), V+B(theta), U+V) dtheta
13:     + integral_a^infinity min(U,B(theta)) dtheta
14:     + integral_a^infinity min(V,C(theta)) dtheta.             (ADS)
15: 
16: Integrals are finite step-function sums. Their breakpoints depend only on the
17: fixed technology profits, never on capacity reports. There are O(J+K) terms.
18: All weights are nonnegative and every term is a minimum of at most three fixed
19: nonnegative linear combinations of actual resource capacities. No dominance
20: condition such as a>=max b+max c is imposed.
21: 
22: PROOF. Fix a feasible plan, with central quantity z and quantities on each
23: leaf edge. Integrating its selected-edge quantities over the three ranges in
24: (ADS) recovers exactly its weighted objective: the central edge is counted for
25: length a; a u-leaf for length min(b_j,a)+(b_j-a)_+=b_j, and a v-leaf similarly.
26: For a theta in (0,a), after aggregating the selected leaves the corresponding
27: unweighted three-edge path has optimum
28:   min(U+C(a-theta), V+B(theta), U+V).
29: This follows either from the three minimal vertex covers or from the direct
30: one-dimensional calculation below. The tail components are unweighted stars.
31: Thus every plan has value at most the RHS of (ADS).
32: 
33: We construct one plan attaining all component optima simultaneously. Put
34: m=min(U,V). For a given central quantity z in [0,m], allocate remaining center
35: capacity greedily to its incident leaves in descending original profit order.
36: For the central theta component, the value of this plan is
37:   z+min(U-z,B(theta))+min(V-z,C(a-theta)).
38: As a function of z, this is maximized exactly on the interval between
39:   L(theta)=clip(U-B(theta),0,m),
40:   R(theta)=clip(V-C(a-theta),0,m),
41: where clip(x,0,m)=max(0,min(x,m)). This follows from slope +1 before both
42: unclipped thresholds, 0 between them, and -1 after both, with endpoints clipped.
43: 
44: L is nondecreasing in theta, R is nonincreasing. All intervals I(theta) between
45: L(theta),R(theta) intersect pairwise: if theta<eta and I(theta) lay strictly
46: left of I(eta), this would force R(theta)<R(eta), a contradiction; if it lay
47: strictly right, this would force L(theta)>L(eta). A finite family of intervals
48: with pairwise intersections has common intersection. Only finitely many open
49: threshold cells occur, so choose
50:   z*=max_theta min(L(theta),R(theta)),
51: which lies in every I(theta). The greedy-leaf plan with this z* attains all
52: central components.
53: 
54: Let B_>a=sum_{b_j>a}s_j and C_>a=sum_{c_k>a}t_k. Every central theta includes
55: all these high-profit leaves. Consequently, for every theta,
56:   min(L(theta),R(theta)) <= clip(U-B_>a,0,m),
57:   min(L(theta),R(theta)) <= clip(V-C_>a,0,m),
58: and the same bounds hold for z*. For any theta>a, this implies
59:   min(U-z*,B(theta))=min(U,B(theta)),
60:   min(V-z*,C(theta))=min(V,C(theta)).
61: Indeed if high-profit leaf capacity exceeds its center capacity, z*=0;
62: otherwise the remaining center capacity suffices for every high-profit leaf.
63: The same greedy-leaf plan therefore attains every tail star too. This proves
64: (ADS). Zero capacities, absent leaf sides, tied values and m=0 are included;
65: changes on finitely many integration endpoints do not affect the formula.
66: 
67: PAYMENTS. Give each actual owner its contribution to the two or three linear
68: arguments in each term of (ADS). Apply the proved mixed-owner three-pool softmax
69: rule (using the mixed two-input rule or duplicating a form for two-form terms),
70: then sum with the positive interval lengths. Every real coalition obeys the
71: same decomposition. Increasing an owner's whole resource bundle raises each
72: of its derived accounts and leaves all other owners' accounts fixed. Thus the
73: result covers all coalitions and is truthful for binding underreports on ALL
74: nonnegative profiles, with total at most
75:   [1+W(2/e)] F, approximately 1.4630565 F.
76: The number of fixed owners and both leaf counts may grow without bound.
77: Owner identities do not change, and an owner may hold arbitrary resources on
78: both sides of the graph. Disjoint unions compose even when owners span them.
79: 
80: POSITIVE ENDPOINT COEFFICIENTS. On any forest with positive incident resource
81: coefficients A_re, choose a root in each component, assign positive row scale
82: s_root=1, and recursively choose s_neighbor so
83:   s_r A_re = s_neighbor A_neighbor,e =: lambda_e.
84: There is no consistency obstruction because the graph is acyclic. Set
85: z_e=lambda_e x_e. Multiplying each resource constraint by s_r converts it to
86:   sum_{e incident r} z_e <= s_r c_r,
87: and edge profit becomes p_e/lambda_e. Every owner bundle transforms by the
88: same fixed positive coordinate scalings. Thus arbitrary positive two-endpoint
89: coefficients, including the target's hardware coefficient 1 and power e_jk,
90: reduce to the unit-incidence model without affecting all-profile incentives,
91: coalition values or budget factors. The normalization does not solve a general
92: forest; it transfers (ADS) only to components with double-star topology.

FILE THREE_POOL_MERGE.txt
1: Mixed-owner three-pool softmax lemma
2: 15 October 2026. Candidate extension; historical novelty unconfirmed.
3: 
4: Fix arbitrary actual owners holding c_ij>=0 for j=0,1,2. Put T=min_j sum_i c_ij,
5: d_ij=min(c_ij,T), rho_j=sum_i d_ij/T, w_j=exp(1-rho_j), p_j=w_j/sum_h w_h.
6: For T>0 pay y_i=sum_j p_j d_ij; at T=0 pay zero. Coverage and total factor
7: 1+b, b exp(b)=2/e, follow from the existing exclusive-pool softmax proof:
8: every capped pool covers each coalition, some rho_j=1, and total/T is the
9: weighted mean of rho_j. This note supplies the missing ACTUAL-OWNER incentive
10: argument when one owner controls several accounts.
11: 
12: If an increased own coordinate belongs to a long pool, T is fixed. Its own
13: account payment has derivative p_j[1-(d_ij/T)(1-p_j)]>=0 before its cap and
14: zero thereafter; other p_h increase and their own-owner holdings stay fixed.
15: Hence the owner's whole payment increases.
16: 
17: Suppose instead the increased own coordinate x lies in a uniquely short pool 0.
18: T=x+a for fixed a>=0, w_0=1. Work off finitely many clipping/tie points.
19: For j=1,2 let k_j be the number of capped accounts and let t_j be the sum of
20: uncapped holdings divided by T. Thus rho_j=k_j+t_j and
21:   w_j' = w_j t_j/T.
22: Write A=w_1, B=w_2, W=1+A+B and M=(A t_1+B t_2)/W. We have M<1, because
23:   A(t_1-1)+B(t_2-1)
24:     <= sum_{j=1,2}(rho_j-1)exp(1-rho_j) <=2/e<1.
25: For this actual owner let a_j=d_ij/T. Its long account derivative delta_j is 1
26: when capped and 0 when uncapped. Also a_0=x/T<=1. Differentiating its SUM gives
27:   D=p_0(1-a_0 M)+sum_{j=1,2}p_j[delta_j+a_j(t_j-M)].
28: Capped terms are nonnegative since a_j=delta_j=1 and M<1. For uncapped terms,
29: a_j<=t_j. If t_j>=M their contribution is nonnegative; otherwise it is at
30: least p_j t_j(t_j-M). At most one of t_1,t_2 is less than M: if both were,
31: their weighted mean with the additional weight-one zero would be less than M.
32: If neither is, D>=p_0(1-M)>0. Otherwise relabel t_1=s<M and t_2=t. Then
33:   D W^2 >= 1+A(1-s+s^2)+B(1+A s^2)-Bt(1+A s).
34: Since B=exp(1-k_2-t)<=exp(1-t), we have Bt<=t exp(1-t)<=1. Consequently
35:   D W^2 >= A(1-s)^2+B(1+A s^2)>0.
36: Thus increasing a coordinate in a short pool also cannot lower actual-owner
37: payment. Formulae agree continuously at caps and ties. At zero output,
38: nonnegativity and the established total bound force continuity to zero.
39: Sequential coordinate increases prove whole-bundle underreport monotonicity.
40: 
41: COROLLARY. For F(c)=min{L_0(c),L_1(c),L_2(c)} with fixed nonnegative linear
42: forms L_j, assign actual owner i derived accounts L_j(b_i). The above rule
43: covers every coalition, is whole-owner truthful on all nonnegative profiles,
44: and pays at most [1+W(2/e)]F(c), approximately 1.4630565 F(c). The forms may
45: overlap in resource support; no owner splitting or population change occurs.
46: Positive sums of such technologies retain the same factor with arbitrary
47: mixed fixed owners. This does not prove the bound for every forest.

FILE RATIONAL_TWO_INPUT.txt
1: Exact rational implementations approaching the mixed two-input constant
2: 15 October 2026. Internal analytic lemma, not a historical novelty claim.
3: 
4: SETTING
5: Use the fixed-owner mixed two-input model from the scarce-power scope proof.
6: At T=min(A,B)>0 call s_i the short holding and d_i=min(l_i,T) the capped long
7: holding. Let rho=sum_i d_i/T>=1, u=rho-1. For integer k>=1 define
8:  E_k(u)=sum_{j=0}^k u^j/j!, p_k(rho)=1/(2 E_k(rho-1)),
9:  y_i=(1-p_k)s_i+p_k d_i.
10: At T=0 pay zero. Every arithmetic operation is rational on rational reports.
11: 
12: ELIGIBILITY
13: Every capped pool vector covers every coalition, so their convex combination
14: does also. Write R=E_{k-1}(u)/E_k(u); then 0<R<=1 and p_k'=-p_k R.
15: If an own long holding grows with T fixed, its own long-account derivative is
16: p_k(1-(d_i/T)R)>=0 before the cap, and zero after it. The own short-account
17: payment increases because p_k decreases. If the owner's short holding grows,
18: T grows at unit rate, rho' is in [-rho/T,0], and p_k grows. Its long-account
19: payment p_k min(l_i,T) therefore grows. For the short account x(1-p_k),
20: its derivative is at least 1-p_k-p_k R rho, using x/T<=1. This is nonnegative:
21:  p_k(1+rho R)<= (1+rho)/(2E_k(u))=(2+u)/(2E_k(u))<=1,
22: since E_k(u)>=1+u. At the aggregate tie every holding is uncapped and rho=1,
23: p_k=1/2; the formulas coincide. Clipping points are continuous. The bounded
24: budget below handles the zero-output boundary. Sequential coordinate changes
25: prove whole-bundle monotonicity for arbitrary mixed actual owners.
26: 
27: BUDGET
28: Total/T=1+u/(2E_k(u)). For k=1 the supremum is3/2. For k>=2, the derivative
29: of u/E_k has the sign of
30:  E_k-u E_k'=1-sum_{j=2}^k (j-1)u^j/j!.
31: It is strictly decreasing for u>0, crosses once, and its root is at mostsqrt(2).
32: Thus the exact worst-case excess beta_k=max_{u>=0}u/(2E_k(u)) is reached at
33: a unique u_k in(0,sqrt(2)]. In particular beta_2=(sqrt(2)-1)/2.
34: Every such rho=1+u is feasible with finitely many long-only owners and one
35: short-only owner, so this is a genuine uniform supremum for this rule family.
36: 
37: For a convenient explicit bound let delta_k=(3/2)^(k+1)/(k+1)!.
38: Taylor's remainder gives, for0<=u<=sqrt(2)<3/2,
39:  E_k(u)>=e^u[1-u^(k+1)/(k+1)!]>=e^u(1-delta_k).
40: Whenever delta_k<1,
41:  beta_k<=1/[2e(1-delta_k)].
42: Because E_k(u)<=e^u, beta_k>=1/(2e); hence beta_k converges downward to1/(2e).
43: The degree required for additive error epsilon is O(log(1/epsilon)/loglog(1/epsilon))
44: as epsilon tends tozero, by factorial growth. For rational reports of bitlengthL,
45: exact evaluation takes polynomial arithmetic/bit complexity in k,n,L; this claim
46: concerns this explicit two-input rule, not the general exponential-mask hybrid.
47: 
48: COMPOSITION
49: Every fixed positive sum of two-input technologies inherits this rational rule
50: and the same factor. This includes the previous single-power star theorem and
51: the dominant double-star decomposition. This is an implementability refinement,
52: not an improvement to the analytic factor or to the unrestricted9/8 lower bound.
