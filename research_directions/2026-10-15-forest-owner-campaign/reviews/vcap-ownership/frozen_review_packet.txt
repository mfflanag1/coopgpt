1: MAXIMUM-COALITION-MARGINAL TRUNCATION FOR THE REPORT-MASK HYBRID
2: Status: elementary structural lemma proved here; priority unassessed. No uniform forest-budget bound claimed.

4: SETUP. Fixed actual owner set N, monotone production f, v_b(S)=f(sum_{i in S}b_i), f(0)=0. Reports and binding deviations are coordinatewise nonnegative bundles; utility is payment only. Define
5:   V_i(b)=max_{S subset N\{i}} [v_b(S+i)-v_b(S)].
6: V_i is finite, nonnegative, and nondecreasing in owner i's whole bundle, with all other bundles fixed. It differs from the manuscript H_i=max core coordinate; V_i is at least the grand marginal, whereas H_i is at most the grand marginal.
7: 
8: LEMMA 1 (COALITION-COVERING TRUNCATION). If z>=0 covers every coalition, then the coordinatewise truncated vector min(z_i,V_i) also covers every coalition.
9: Proof: truncate coordinates sequentially. Suppose the current covering vector has coordinate i reduced to V_i. For S containing i, the new sum is z(S\i)+V_i>=v(S\i)+V_i>=v(S). Coalitions not containing i are unchanged. Finitely many such operations prove the claim. This proof is game-theoretic and does not use concavity, linear programming, or sparse production.

11: HYBRID. Suppose f=min_{w in W} w.c for a fixed finite collection of nonnegative forms. Independently retain each owner with probability p=1-q; let w_B be the first minimizing form for retained reports. Replace the existing global-T cap by
12:   a_i(w,b)=min(w.b_i,V_i(b)).
13: Define z_i=E a_i(w_B,b), p_i=E[a_i(w_B,b)|B_i=0],
14:   m_i=E_{B_-i}[ f(b_i+sum B_h b_h)-f(sum B_h b_h) ],
15:   r_i=q p_i+p m_i.
16: Then r is nonnegative, own-report monotone, and covers every coalition on every profile.
17: Proof: each full form covers, so Lemma 1 shows each capped form covers. Conditional on B_i=0, its selected w is independent of b_i and both terms in the min are own-monotone. The marginal m_i is own-monotone. When i is retained, the selected form satisfies
18:   w_B.b_i <= v(B)-v(B\i) <= V_i.
19: Hence its on contribution is unchanged by capping and is bounded by that same marginal. Thus r_i>=z_i, proving coverage.
20: 
21: EXACT BUDGET IDENTITY. With g(q)=E v(B), Z(q)=sum z_i, R(q)=sum r_i,
22:   R=Z-g-(1-q)g'.
23: Proof: sum_{i:B_i=1}a_i(w_B,b)=v(B), so q sum p_i=Z-g. Differentiating the finite mask polynomial gives sum m_i=-g'. All original hybrid integration identities survive unchanged.

37: STRONGER OBSTRUCTION: UNBOUNDED V-CAP HYBRID ON A DEPTH-TWO TREE.
38: This supersedes any numerical suggestion that a small fixed q might suffice.
39: For n>=2, take vertices p,h_1,...,h_n,l_1,...,l_n and edges p--h_j--l_j. Owner 0 holds capacity 1 at p and epsilon=1/n^2 at EVERY l_j. Owner j holds capacity 1 at h_j only. All full resource capacities are strictly positive, all owners remain fixed, and every activity consumes one unit at each of two endpoints. The full optimum is
40:   T=1+n epsilon=1+1/n.
41: Indeed the private activities use n epsilon and the hub activity uses 1; the h capacities admit this because n(1-epsilon)>=1.
42: For j>=1, V_j=1: retaining owner 0 plus j alone permits all of h_j's unit to be served, while without j that coalition has value0. No marginal can exceed h_j's unit capacity. Owner0 has V_0=T.
43: 
44: Fix any q in (0,1), p_ret=1-q. Condition on owner0 retained, and let K be the number of retained h owners. If K>=2, there are only two possible optimal-cover types:
45:   (a) exclude p, hence include all h_j, sampled cost K;
46:   (b) include p, then independently choose h_j or l_j on each private edge.
47: Type (b) uniquely chooses l_j for retained j (cost epsilon<1) and h_j for absent j (cost0<epsilon). Its sampled cost is 1+K epsilon<K for n>=2,K>=2 (strict also n=2,K=2). Thus the selected cover is unique: this argument is independent of any tie-breaking convention.
48: At this mask every absent j contributes min(1,V_j)=1 to Z. Therefore
49:   Z/T >= p_ret * E[(n-K) 1_{K>=2}]/(1+1/n)
50:        = p_ret * [nq - n q^n - n(n-1)p_ret q^(n-1)]/(1+1/n).
51: The right side tends to infinity linearly in n. Since R>=Z coordinatewise, the V-cap hybrid has no uniform finite budget factor on forests for ANY fixed retention parameter in (0,1).
52: 
53: The same obstruction holds for any fixed report-independent probability mixture over q with positive probability on (0,1): by Fatou,
54:   liminf_n E_q[R_n/T_n]/n >= E_q[q(1-q)]>0.
55: This statement allows integration measures with atoms at0 and1 but requires positive mass in the interior. It does NOT rule out mechanisms whose q distribution changes with owner count, a different dependence among owner masks, or mechanisms outside this hybrid architecture. Nor does it prove an impossibility for truthful forest mechanisms generally.

Its source is one real mixed owner supplying the scarce hub resource and tiny private complements, while many competing owners each retain maximum coalition marginal1. FILE NETTING_AND_THREE_OWNERS.txt
1: Netting closure and the three-owner boundary
2: 15 October2026. Internal derivations; no novelty claim.
3: 
4: 1. A general mixed-owner lifting lemma
5: For every assignment of a fixed finite owner set to two exclusive pools,
6: assume a scalar-capacity payment rule z that is nonnegative, covers the
7: min(total left,total right) game, spends at most C times that value (C>=1),
8: pays a zero-capacity owner zero, and is own-capacity nondecreasing and
9: 1-Lipschitz. No relation between rules for different role assignments is needed
10: except the same bound and these properties.
11: For actual mixed owner i with (a_i,b_i), put
12:   t_i=min(a_i,b_i), d_i=a_i-b_i.
13: Feed |d_i| to the exclusive-pool rule on the sign partition, assigning zero
14: net owners by any fixed convention, and set y_i=t_i+z_i.
15: Then y is own-bundle monotone, coalition-covering, and spends at most CT on
16: all mixed reports, with fixed actual owners.
17: 
18: Proof: for every coalition S,
19:  min(sum_S a_i,sum_S b_i)=sum_S t_i+min(sum_S(d_i)+,sum_S(-d_i)+).
20: Coverage and budget follow, since sum t_i+C*residual<=C*T.
21: When a_i increases while a_i<b_i, standalone value rises by delta and the
22: negative net capacity falls by delta. The 1-Lipschitz assumption prevents a
23: payment loss larger than delta. When a_i>b_i, standalone value is unchanged
24: and positive net capacity increases, so monotonicity applies. At a_i=b_i,
25: the net payment is zero on both sides. Other owners' signed reports stay fixed.
26: The b_i argument is symmetric. This proves direct incentive validity without
27: asserting a general strategic merger theorem.
28: 
29: The existing exclusive-pool exponential rule has own derivative in[0,1] in
30: the short region and in[0,1/2] in the long region, with continuous boundaries.
31: It therefore meets the lifting hypotheses. This is another simple route to
32: a mixed-owner exponential bound and should temper claims that mixed ownership
33: alone is a historically novel difficulty.
34: 
35: 2. Exact efficient rule for at most three mixed owners
36: Pad to three with zero owners. At a report, net each owner as above. If all
37: nonzero d_i have the same sign, pay each t_i. If one sign has a single owner
38: and the other sign has two, give the entire residual value to the sole owner
39: of the first sign, plus each owner's t_i. If there is exactly one positive
40: and one negative owner (third zero), divide the residual equally between those
41: two, plus t_i. This rule is efficient and covers coalitions because the residual
42: game has a veto owner, or two necessary owners receiving its value between them.
43: 
44: For a fully explicit own-monotonic formula, fix the OTHER two net capacities:
45: - Both strictly positive, sum D: pay i=min(b_i,a_i+D).
46: - One strictly positive D and the other zero:
47:     pay i=1/2 min(a_i,b_i)+1/2 min(b_i,a_i+D).
48: - Both strictly negative, absolute sum D: pay i=min(a_i,b_i+D).
49: - One strictly negative -D and the other zero:
50:     pay i=1/2 min(a_i,b_i)+1/2 min(a_i,b_i+D).
51: - Opposite signs or both zero: pay i=min(a_i,b_i).
52: These expressions are coordinatewise nondecreasing with the other reports
53: fixed and agree with the surplus description. Thus there is no incentive
54: subsidy for at most three actual mixed owners in the two-input class. The
55: existing four-owner9/8 lower witness is a sharp boundary in owner count for
56: the existence of a positive worst-case subsidy, not a determination of the
57: optimal subsidy once four or more owners are allowed.
58: 
59: This bounded-owner observation is not the campaign's unbounded-class gate.
60: It explains the exact finite-grid pattern and identifies an owner-dependent
61: mechanism feature absent from the scalar-bias family.

63: Independent seam audit, 15 October 2026.
64: The lift needs a family indexed by fixed role assignments, with the same C
65: and 1-Lipschitz bound. Other owners retain their role assignment throughout
66: an own deviation. At a zero net report, the selected sign convention can
67: change the rule used by others, but their payments are irrelevant to the
68: deviating owner's monotonicity; her own residual payment is exactly zero
69: in either role by hypothesis. On either open sign region, the applicable
70: role assignment is fixed. Integrate monotone/Lipschitz comparisons up to
71: zero, cross that zero seam, and continue on the other side. This proves
72: arbitrary finite coordinate changes, not only infinitesimal derivatives.
73: At a_i=b_i=0, payment is zero and both coordinate directions are covered.
