Efficient all-profile sharing in the clean local-energy/grid institution
16 October2026 UTC. Root construction, independently checked in the lower lane.
Scoped Fable review completed; root adjudications are in AUDIT.txt. No historical novelty claim.

Fix any number m of pods and actual owners A,C,H_1,...,H_m. Owner A controls
all local power coordinates L_j; C controls the shared grid coordinate P;
owner H_j controls only the hardware coordinate h_j. Capacities are arbitrary
nonnegative real reports. The unit-profit activities use one unit of hardware
and either one unit of grid capacity or one unit of local capacity. Coalition
resources include only their members' reports. Identity and roles remain fixed.

Put l_j=min(h_j,L_j), r_j=(h_j-L_j)^+, and R=min(P,sum_j r_j).
Pay A zero, pay C exactly R, and pay hardware owner j exactly l_j.
Then payments are nonnegative, exactly efficient, coalition-covering, and
own-bundle monotone for every real report profile and every finite m.

Efficiency. Production equals
 T=sum_j l_j+min(P,sum_j r_j).
Use all feasible local service first and then grid service on residual hardware.
This attains the displayed value. Any plan can serve at most sum_j h_j and
at most P+sum_j l_j: local service at j is at most l_j, and total grid service
is at most P. Their minimum equals the displayed expression. Payments sum toT.

Coverage. Let I be a coalition's hardware owners, l(I)=sum_I l_j and
r(I)=sum_I r_j. With neither energy owner it produces zero. With A but not C
it produces l(I), exactly the hardware payments it receives. With both A and C
it produces l(I)+min(P,r(I)), at most l(I)+R. With C but not A it produces
min(P,sum_I h_j). Its payments are l(I)+R, at least
 l(I)+min(P,r(I)) >= min(P,l(I)+r(I)) = min(P,sum_I h_j).
Thus all coalitions are covered, including arbitrary subsets of hardware owners.

Incentives. Hardware owner's own payment min(h_j,L_j) increases with h_j.
Grid owner's own payment min(P,sum_j r_j) increases with P. Local owner's
payment is identically zero as any subset of her L_j coordinates changes.
Consequently all allowed whole-owner coordinatewise underreports are weakly
unprofitable. This is WEAK truthfulness; A is indifferent across her reports,
so the theorem provides no strict disclosure incentive or unique equilibrium.

Established ingredient and scope. The payment vector is the fixed-order
marginal vector obtained by placing A first, all hardware owners next (in any
fixed order), and C last. Own-report monotonicity of fixed-order marginal
contributions under report locality is standard. The special property here is
that this order's vector covers every coalition for this production/ownership
subclass, proved above. No historical novelty is claimed. This resolves the
clean unit-profit committed-rights institution at arbitrary m; it does NOT
resolve arbitrary mixed ownership on depth-two resource trees. In particular,
do not merge energy/resource roles or introduce shared rights without proving
that the resulting owner's entire payment stays monotone in her entire bundle.

Economic gate. Zero net compensation to A is consistent with the stipulated
payment-only model and zero outside utility; A alone cannot produce. It becomes
inadequate if A has unreimbursed battery/degradation costs, private reservation
value or valuable outside use. Public operating reimbursements and net surplus
must be accounted for separately. The unit-profit/equal-route assumption is
also explicit. Unequal grid/local net margins require their own extension.
This is a mathematical efficiency theorem, not evidence of real pooling gains.

No group-strategyproofness. For two pods with h=L=(1,1), P=2, truthful
payments give C zero and both hardware owners1. If A reports both L as0,
C receives2 and hardware owners0. A is indifferent individually, but A and C
can both gain with a side payment. Coverage is at reported coalition values;
the theorem does not guarantee truthful equilibrium selection or collusion
resistance. This example is consistent with, and limits, weak individual truthfulness.
