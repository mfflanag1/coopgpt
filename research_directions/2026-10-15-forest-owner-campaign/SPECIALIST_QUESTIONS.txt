Topic: all-profile incentives in concave homogeneous resource-pooling games.
This packet is a new supplement, not a claim of historical novelty or an
already validated breakthrough. The main manuscript has not yet absorbed it.

MODEL
Fix n actual owners, d nonnegative resources, and a public finite monotone
concave degree-one homogeneous production function f. At binding reports b,
v_b(S)=f(sum_(i in S)b_i). Seek nonnegative payments y_i(b), covering every
coalition, with y_i coordinatewise nondecreasing in its own whole bundle.
Utility is payment only; strategic identities do not change. No extra fairness
floor or private cost is imposed. T=v_b(N); budget factor is sum y_i/T.

PROPOSED GENERAL RESULTS
A single all-profile rule exists with total payments at most
 min{d, n*sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2*sqrt(d)}*T.
Select the entire best rule using fixed public n,d before observing reports.
The owner and dimension bounds improve the project's prior n/log(n) and d
upper bounds. The sharper dimension parameterization is
 C_d(a)=[1+2d(-a-log(1-a))]/a, 0<a<1.

PROOF IN TWO STEPS
1. Fix a report-independent mask of open owners B. Choose a nonnegative
supporting form at their aggregate. Closed owners receive their support price
capped at T; open owners receive their grand marginal within B. The support
and cap give coalition coverage, and each payment is own-bundle monotone.
Relative-face supports handle downward discontinuities. See FOUNDATIONS.
2. With missing probability q, g(q)=E f(sum_open b_i). Open payments sum in
expectation to -(1-q)g'(q). Closed payments are bounded either by nqT or by
2dqT/(1-q), the latter via an exact Bernoulli reciprocal identity for each
coordinate. Uniform integration over q in[0,a] supplies the claimed bounds.
See portfolio/OWNER_SQRT_BOUND_v2 and DIMENSION_SQRT_BOUND_v1.

RESTRICTED LOWER BOUNDS
For the precisely defined fixed-mask/common-support/endpoint architecture,
sqrt(2n) is sharp to leading order, and dimension growth is Theta(sqrt(d)).
The lower constructions allow correlated/asymmetric masks and finite global
supports, with a separate extension to maximum-coalition-marginal caps.
They DO NOT establish unrestricted square-root lower bounds. Existing general
lower bounds remain logarithmic in n and d. See the three indexed obstruction
proofs and their scope clarifications.

QUESTIONS FOR AN EXPERT
1. Is an equivalent all-profile resource-withholding result already known,
particularly under resource monotonicity, co-investment or production-game
terminology? The closest supplied comparator is Amigo--Belzarena--Vaton2016;
scalar co-investment results and static subsidy results must be distinguished.
2. Does the supporting-face/mask construction preserve every required incentive
and coalition constraint at zero reports and boundary-discontinuous f?
3. Is there a sharper joint bound that uses support-coordinate compatibility
or the negative endpoint terms discarded by integration? The scalar factor2
alone is sharp even as q tends to0.
4. Can owner-specific supports or genuinely report-dependent sampling preserve
coverage and incentives while escaping the architecture lower bounds?
5. For the applied branch, can arbitrary depth-two resource trees admit C<2
with mixed actual ownership? Weighted double stars already admit about1.463057;
growing depth-two trees defeat the simple scalar-rate cover-Gibbs candidate.

PRIOR ART AND AUDIT BOUNDARIES
Bachrach et al.2009 Theorem3.5 already gives square-root static cost-of-stability
bounds for superadditive games. Here each individual profile has a nonempty
core; the cost concerns one payment map across different resource reports.
The hierarchy/core allocation on owner forests and the reasonable upper payoff
are classical ingredients. Source notes separate read originals, inherited
comparisons, and unresolved priority. Fourteen scoped model audits and exact
replays are preserved. Neither finite tests nor model review substitute for
human mathematical assessment or priority confirmation.
