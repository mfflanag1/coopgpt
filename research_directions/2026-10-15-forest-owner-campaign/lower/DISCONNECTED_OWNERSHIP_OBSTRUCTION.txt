Disconnected exclusive ownership can require an incentive subsidy on a path
15 October 2026. Exact internal counterexample; literature novelty unconfirmed.
This is an ownership-boundary result, not an improvement on general9/8.

A fixed unit-value production path has8 resource vertices0,...,7 and one
activity on each edge. Each activity consumes one unit at each endpoint.
Actual fixed owners are A={0,4}, B={1,5}, C={2,6}, D={3,7}. Resources have
exclusive ownership: no row is shared between owners. Reports of both
coordinates of one owner's bundle can independently decrease.

Consider three profiles, listed by resource order:
 P=(0,1,1,1,1,1,1,0),
 U=(0,1,1,2,1,1,1,2), increasing only D's bundle,
 V=(2,1,1,1,2,1,1,0), increasing only A's bundle.
The grand value at each profile is3. Explicit feasible matchings of value3:
 P,U use edges(1,2),(3,4),(5,6), one unit each;
 V uses edges(0,1),(2,3),(4,5), one unit each.
Upper bounds are the sum of capacities on the smaller bipartition side,
which is3 at each profile.

At P, coalition AD has value1, so y_A(P)+y_D(P)>=1.
At U, the coalition values AB=1, BC=2, ACD=3 imply
  2*sum_i y_i(U)-y_D(U)>=6.
At V, coalition values BC=2, ABD=3, CD=1 imply
  2*sum_i y_i(V)-y_A(V)>=6.
All these values are exact: the stated worths have disjoint unit matching
certificates, and resource-side capacity covers give matching upper bounds.
If the rule spends at most C times production, then
  y_D(U)<=6C-6,   y_A(V)<=6C-6.
Own-report monotonicity gives y_D(P)<=y_D(U), y_A(P)<=y_A(V). Therefore
  1 <= y_A(P)+y_D(P) <= 12C-12,
so C>=13/12.

This uses only three profiles and fixed actual owners. There is no entry,
identity splitting, common-access counterfactual, or outside-option change.
It shows the connected-block hypothesis of the classical owner-forest
argument cannot simply be dropped. It does not assert connected blocks
are necessary in every instance.

The bound is sharp on this three-profile subdomain: feasible payments in
owner order A,B,C,D are
 P=(1/2,1,1,1/2), U=(3/4,1/4,7/4,1/2), V=(1/2,7/4,1/4,3/4).
Every coalition is covered and the two required own-incentive comparisons
hold. P spends3, U and V spend13/4, yielding factor13/12.
The entire0,1,2 coordinate grid of6561 profiles also has reconstructed
rational primal factor13/12; its full independent verification is recorded
in DISCONNECTED_PRIMAL_REPLAY.jsonl. That remains a finite-domain upper
certificate, not an all-real-profile mechanism.
