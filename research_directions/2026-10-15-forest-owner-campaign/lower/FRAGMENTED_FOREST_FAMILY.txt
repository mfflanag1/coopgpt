An unbounded exclusive-ownership path family approaching the9/8 subsidy bound
15 October 2026. Exact internal result; external novelty unconfirmed.
This does not improve the unrestricted9/8 lower bound. It strengthens the
ownership-boundary interpretation without resource sharing or common access.

Technology and owners. For integer k>=2, take the path on4k vertices numbered
0,...,4k-1 with one unit-value activity on each edge. Each activity consumes
one capacity at each endpoint. Owners A,B,C,D exclusively own vertices whose
indices are respectively0,1,2,3 modulo4. No resource is shared. Each owner's
k capacities are independent report coordinates, all bounded by2.

Reports. Baseline P has capacity1 at every vertex except the first and last,
which have capacity0. At U, raise ALL D coordinates to2 and leave others at
P. At V, raise ALL A coordinates to2 and leave others atP. These are valid
single-owner bundle increases with fixed identities and fixed technology.
Let T=2k-1. The grand production at each report is T. AtP andU, the even-side
capacity sum is T; atV the odd-side sum is T. Matching certificates attainT:
 P,U: all k edges B_j-C_j and all k-1 edges D_j-A_{j+1};
 V: all k edges A_j-B_j and all k-1 edges C_j-D_j.
Here j indexes copies0,...,k-1.

Coalition worths. AtP, v(AD)=k-1. AtU,
 v(AB)=k-1, v(BC)=k, v(ACD)=2k-1.
AtV,
 v(BC)=k, v(ABD)=2k-1, v(CD)=k-1.
Each equality follows by matching the indicated available adjacent pairs,
with the capacity of the coalition's smaller relevant vertex side as upper
bound. For example atU, ACD uses every C_j-D_j and every D_j-A_{j+1}; D_j
has capacity2, while its neighboring A and C vertices have capacity1.

Lower bound. For any nonnegative all-profile own-bundle-monotone covering
rule with total payment at most C times grand value, sum the three U
coalition inequalities and separately the three V inequalities. This gives
  2 y_N(U)-y_D(U)>=2T,
  2 y_N(V)-y_A(V)>=2T.
Coalition AD atP and the two own-report comparisons give
  k-1 <= y_A(P)+y_D(P)
      <= y_A(V)+y_D(U)
      <= 4T(C-1).
Consequently
  C >= 1+(k-1)/(4(2k-1)) = (9k-5)/(8k-4),
which tends to9/8 as k tends to infinity. k=2 is13/12.

The three-profile finite LP is exactly sharp. Payments in order A,B,C,D:
 P=((k-1)/2, k/2, k/2, (k-1)/2),
 U=(3(k-1)/4, (k-1)/4, (3k+1)/4, (k-1)/2),
 V=((k-1)/2, (3k+1)/4, (k-1)/4, 3(k-1)/4).
They are nonnegative, satisfy the only required own comparisons, and cover
every coalition. For a direct check, the nonzero proper coalition worths at
P are AB=CD=AD=k-1, BC=k, ABC=BCD=k, ABD=ACD=k-1.
AtU they are AB=AD=ABD=k-1, BC=CD=ABC=BCD=k, ACD=T.
AtV the U list is reflected by A<->D and B<->C. All singleton and BD/AC
worths are zero. P spendsT and U,V each spendT+(k-1)/4.
This finite upper certificate does NOT construct a mechanism on all reports.

Both owner count and resource count can grow. Take m disjoint such paths,
with four distinct owners per path. Even if a mechanism's payments depend
on all components and allow cross-subsidies, the same factor lower applies.
For a fixed component r and arbitrary fixed reports of all others, the
preceding coalition inequalities and own transitions imply
  y_r(U,others)+y_r(V,others) >= 2T+(k-1)/2,
where y_r is total payment to its four owners. Average over independently
uniform U/V profiles of the other components. Equivalently,
  E[y_r]>=T+(k-1)/4
under the product-uniform U/V profile distribution of ALL components.
Sum over r. Some all-U/V profile then spends at least
m[T+(k-1)/4] while production is mT. Thus the factor lower survives arbitrary
cross-component dependence. At these U/V profiles every owner is active;
there are4m actual owners and4km exclusively owned resource vertices.
This is a valid fixed-owner argument, not strategic splitting or entry.

Interpretation. Classical hierarchical allocations ensure zero subsidy when
resource ownership blocks are connected on the physical forest. Fragmented
exclusive holdings force a worst-case factor at least (9k-5)/(8k-4), approaching9/8,
even on a collection of paths. Therefore overlapping/fractional ownership
is not necessary for the incentive obstruction. The established general9/8
bound remains numerically unchanged. No claim of all-forest sharpness or
external novelty is made.
