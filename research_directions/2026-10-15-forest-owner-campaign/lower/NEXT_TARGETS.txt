Three prioritized next mathematical targets
15 October2026. These are proposals, not established results.

1. Owner-sensitive two-input mechanism below1+1/(2e).
This remains the highest-value constructive target: a better all-profile
bound immediately transfers to the heterogeneous one-power-row star by its
positive two-input decomposition. Seek an exclusive-pool rule with uniform
C<1+1/(2e), ideally9/8, nonnegative own payments, own monotonicity and own
1-Lipschitz continuity; the netting lemma then handles actual mixed owners.
The design must use ownership/concentration information absent from a single
p(rho). The efficient three-owner rule shows why that matters: an owner who
alone supplies one residual pool can receive the residual surplus, whereas
a universal balanced scalar bias forces unnecessary long-side payments.
A useful attack is a piecewise rule based on the largest owner and the total
capacity supplied by the remaining owners on each side, with explicit checks
at ties, zero capacities and changes of the largest-owner identity. The
success gate is a proof on arbitrary real reports and unbounded populations.
A finite-grid9/8 fit or another bounded-owner formula does not pass.

2. Constant-factor allocation on an owner-interaction cycle with forest
attachments, preserving arbitrary mixed bundles and all report profiles.
The classical allocation completely resolves acyclic owner interaction; the
repeatedABCD path demonstrates that a four-cycle changes the incentive problem
even with exclusively owned resources. The next structural issue is whether
acyclic attachments can be removed/composed without increasing the incentive
factor of the cyclic part. Target a precise composition lemma, not a general
claim that graph structure alone solves incentives. It must keep actual
owners fixed, handle their joint resource reports, and bound subsidy without
dependence on the number of attached owners/resources. A successful lemma
would reduce a large sparse-production class to its cyclic interactions. If
composition fails, an exact counterexample identifying the joint-report term
that destroys monotonicity would be useful. Do not count another proof of the
classical owner-forest case as progress.

3. A lower certificate above9/8 on a genuinely interacting merged-owner cycle.
The new cycle-to-path transfer turns a compact finite cycle certificate into
an unbounded exclusively owned forest obstruction. Seven two-occurrence
four-owner patterns on an eight-cycle did not exceed9/8 on the tested grid;
repeating that grid search is low value. Instead, target a longer ownership
word with at least five owners, heterogeneous activity values, and a report
set containing interacting changes (commuting squares/chains), so that several
scarcity transitions compete for the same owner's payment. Use an adaptive
finite LP only to discover a small rational certificate, then replay it with
an independent production oracle and apply the transfer lemma. The success
gate is an exact lower bound strictly above9/8, or a proved obstruction to the
chosen certificate architecture. Failure of a finite search is neither.

Practical ordering: attack1 first because it improves the already established
unbounded star theorem directly; use2 as the main structural alternative; use3
as a bounded adversarial lane supporting2. The owner-forest boundary provides
a clean institution screen, not evidence that the remaining general problem
is easy or that a field-level breakthrough has already occurred.
