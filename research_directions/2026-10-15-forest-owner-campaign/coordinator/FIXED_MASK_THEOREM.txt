Fixed-mask hybrid: rational owner upper and sharp method obstruction
15 October 2026. Candidate theorem, independent review pending.

FIXED SETTING AND GENERAL MASK RULE
Fix n actual owners and finite monotone concave degree-one homogeneous f.
Use canonical extended relative supporting forms ell_x from the current supplement,
or fixed-priority finite minimizing forms when f is finite min-linear.
At a profile b let T=f(sum b_i), x_B=sum B_i b_i for B in{0,1}^n.
For any probability distribution nu on masks fixed independently of reports, set
 y_i^nu=E_nu[ (1-B_i) min(ell_xB(b_i),T)
              +B_i (f(x_B)-f(x_B-b_i)) ].                 (M)
Everything is finite after capping; no subtraction of infinite forms occurs.
Owners are never cloned or removed. The law may depend on the fixed technology
and n, but not on any report.

ELIGIBILITY. In an off term x_B is independent of b_i; both the selected support
at that aggregate and other reports are fixed. Its capped evaluation is therefore
coordinatewise nondecreasing in b_i. In an on term x_B-b_i is independent of b_i,
so its difference is nondecreasing. Each term is nonnegative. For every mask the
capped support vector covers every coalition. At an open owner, touching and
majorization give f(x_B)-f(x_B-b_i)>=ell_xB(b_i); this quantity is finite, uncapped,
and at mostT because b_i lies within the aggregate face. Consequently the vector
inside(M) dominates that capped covering vector, proving coverage. Averaging
preserves all these properties. Zero bundles and zero-value profiles receive zero.
This is a direct extension of the Bernoulli hybrid, not an invocation of an old
parameter restriction. It permits correlated masks.

EXACT OWNER UPPER
Let g_s be the mean of f(sum_{i in S}b_i) over all size-s subsets, so g_0=0,g_n=T.
For a uniform size-s mask, summing endpoint marginals over open owners gives
  E sum_{i:B_i=1}[f(x_B)-f(x_B-b_i)]=s(g_s-g_{s-1}).
(The average of all size-(s-1) deletions of size-s sets is g_{s-1}.) Off terms
number n-s and are individually at mostT. Thus
  sum_i y_i^(s) <= (n-s)T+s(g_s-g_{s-1}).             (1)

Fix an integer m in[1,n]. Put h=H_n-H_{n-m}=sum_{s=n-m+1}^n 1/s and choose
size s in{n-m+1,...,n} with probability (1/s)/h, then a uniform subset of that
size. This law is report-independent and rational. Multiplying(1) by those
probabilities and summing telescopes:
  total <= [n-m/h]T +[g_n-g_{n-m}]/h
        <= C_nm T,     C_nm=n-(m-1)/(H_n-H_{n-m}).     (2)
Choose m minimizing the finite rational list C_nm before reports. This supplies
an exact rational bound C_n=min_m C_nm for EVERY eligible f and dimension.
No efficient evaluation or measurable global support oracle is claimed.
For finite rational min-linear input, mask enumeration and payments use only
exact rational arithmetic (exponential mask count remains).

For comparison, choosing uniform weights over these m sizes gives
  total <= [(m-1)/2+n/m]T,
by summation by parts and nonnegativity of g. In particular m near sqrt(2n)
implies C_n<=(sqrt(2)+o(1))sqrt(n), retaining the new general owner-count rate.
For the harmonic law itself, when m is asymptotic to sqrt(2n),
h=m/n+m(m-1)/(2n^2)+O(m^3/n^3), so (2) equals
n/m+(m-1)^2/(2m)+O(m^2/n)=(sqrt(2)+o(1))sqrt(n).
Equation(2) is the advertised exact finite-n bound.
Existing sharper small-n or technology-specific rules may of course be chosen
in advance instead. These are total-payment factors; subsidy subtracts one.

SHARP LEADING ORDER FOR EVERY FIXED MASK LAW WITH CANONICAL FACE EXTENSION
For n>=3 choose the least m with m(m-1)>=2n; then m<=n. Use one fixed technology
  F(x)=sum_{k=1}^m h_k(x^(k)),
where each separate block has n coordinates and h_k is the sum of its k smallest
coordinates. This is finite nonnegative min-linear, monotone, concave, homogeneous.
At profile k, owner i holds coordinate i of block k at one, all other coordinates
zero. T=k. All these profiles share the same n owners and same technology.

For every mask with l=n-|B| closed owners, each closed owner's basis coordinate
lies outside the support of x_B, hence canonical ell_xB(b_i)=+infinity and its
capped payment is T. An open owner's endpoint marginal is1 exactly when l<k.
Therefore the exact normalized payment is
  l+(n-l)/k *1{l<=k-1}.                              (3)
This holds for each individual mask, without exchangeability or independence.
Let S=m(m+1)/2 and average normalized profile costs with weights k/S. Their
average at every mask is
  H(l)=l+(n-l)(m-l)_+/S.
If0<=l<=m,
  S H(l)=nm+(S-n-m)l+l^2>=nm,
since S-n-m=m(m-1)/2-n>=0. If l>=m, H(l)=l>=m>=nm/S because S>=n.
Thus H(l)>=2n/(m+1) for all integer l in[0,n]. Integrate over ANY fixed mask
law nu; some one of these fixed-technology profiles costs at least2n/(m+1).
Since m=sqrt(2n)+O(1), this lower is(sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n).

Combined with(2), sqrt(2) is the sharp leading constant for mechanism(M) with
canonical +infinity extensions off aggregate faces, even allowing arbitrary
correlated, asymmetric fixed mask distributions chosen for the technology.
The bound DOES NOT restrict finite-form selectors, other support extensions,
report-dependent laws with separately proved incentives, or arbitrary mechanisms.
It does not strengthen the project's unrestricted lower logn/loglogn.
