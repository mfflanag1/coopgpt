7. Bachrach et al. (2009), The Cost of Stability in Coalitional Games,
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.4385, Theorem3.5. Free original inspected by the
portfolio lane and coordinator. It gives a sqrt(n) total-payment bound for
STATIC superadditive games and asymptotic tightness via projective planes.
This is essential prior art for any phrase like 'square-root subsidy bound'.
Our concave homogeneous profile games are already balanced individually;
the additional cost is imposed by one payment rule's cross-profile incentives.
The static result does not establish such a rule, and its empty-core lower
examples are not lower certificates for our balanced production profile domain.
Novelty still requires a focused specialist assessment, not this distinction
alone. See portfolio/SOURCE_SCREEN.txt for the precise theorem comparison.
