SELF-CONTAINED FOUNDATIONS FOR THE NEW GENERAL BOUNDS
15 October 2026. Established ingredients from the existing truthful-production
manuscript, restated and checked here. No priority claim.

Fix n actual owners, d resources, and a finite-valued monotone concave degree-one
homogeneous f on the nonnegative orthant. Thus f(0)=0 and f>=0. Reports b_i>=0
are binding available bundles; utility is the payment, with no private operating
cost. Set c=sum b_i, T=f(c), and v(S)=f(sum_(i in S)b_i). Requirements are
nonnegative payments, sum_(i in S)y_i>=v(S) for every coalition, and coordinatewise
nondecrease of y_i in b_i while every other owner remains fixed. A budget factor
C means total payment <=CT; the external subsidy is at most (C-1)T.

RELATIVE SUPPORTS, INCLUDING DOWNWARD BOUNDARY DISCONTINUITIES
For x!=0, restrict f to its coordinate face F_x={z>=0:z_e=0 if x_e=0}.
The point x is in the relative interior. A finite concave function has a
supergradient u there. Monotonicity forces u>=0: f(x+t e)>=f(x), whereas the
support inequality gives f(x+t e)<=f(x)+t u_e. Homogeneity, applied to scalar
multiples of x both above and below x, gives u.x=f(x). The support inequality
on the whole face follows from the interior inequality by the segment from
x to any boundary point z and concavity; this does not require continuity at z.
Consequently u.z>=f(z) for every z in F_x. The set of such nonnegative u with
u.x=f(x) is nonempty, closed, and convex; its minimum-Euclidean-norm element
defines one fixed unique choice. Extend ell_x(z)=u.z on F_x and +infinity outside.
At x=0 use ell_0(0)=0 and ell_0(z)=+infinity otherwise.

For a finite min-linear technology one may instead select a fixed-priority
minimizing nonnegative global form, producing finite ordinary linear supports.
The arguments below apply to either selection. Each selection is fixed before
evaluating deviations and depends only on its indicated aggregate/report data.

MASK ELIGIBILITY
For any fixed subset B of owners let x_B=sum_(i in B)b_i and A_i=min{ell_xB(b_i),T}.
The vector A covers every coalition. If any member's raw price is >=T (including
+infinity), that member's capped payment alone covers its coalition. Otherwise
all relevant prices are finite, add on the support face, and majorize v(S).
For i in B, ell_xB(b_i) is finite and
 f(x_B)-f(x_B-b_i)>=ell_xB(b_i),
by touching at x_B and majorization at x_B-b_i. This marginal is <=T. Therefore
replace the open owners' prices by these marginals and retain A_i for closed
owners; the resulting vector Y(B) still covers every coalition.

For a closed i, ell_xB excludes b_i, so its raw price and cap T are separately
nondecreasing in b_i. For an open i, x_B-b_i excludes b_i and the marginal is
nondecreasing in b_i. Thus Y_i(B) is own-bundle monotone. Every report-independent
distribution over masks, including any fixed mixture of Bernoulli missing
probabilities, preserves these pointwise properties. The expected rule is a
deterministic payment map; no approximation by a finite sample is being claimed.

POLYNOMIAL ENDPOINT IDENTITY
For independent missing probability q, g(q)=E f(x_B) is a finite polynomial.
Conditioning on each owner's bit and differentiating the multilinear polynomial
along its diagonal yields -g'(q)=sum_i E[f(b_i+x_(B without i))-f(x_(B without i))].
This identity does not differentiate f, move owner identities, or assume report
continuity. Every q integral at a fixed profile is an integral of a polynomial.

THE ELEMENTARY d BOUND
The grand marginal U_i=f(c)-f(c-b_i) is own-bundle monotone and dominates the
selected support price at c, hence covers every coalition. If T>0, let
alpha_i=max_(e:c_e>0)b_ie/c_e. Then c-b_i>=(1-alpha_i)c coordinatewise, so
U_i<=alpha_i T by monotonicity and homogeneity. Finally
sum_i alpha_i<=sum_(e:c_e>0)sum_i b_ie/c_e<=d.
Thus total grand-marginal payment is <=dT. If T=0 every term vanishes. This
explains the prior linear dimension baseline without relying on a finite-form
approximation or on continuity at the boundary.

DEPENDENCIES AND LIMITS
Only the standard finite-dimensional supporting-hyperplane theorem for a finite
concave function on a relative interior is used above. General evaluation may
require exponentially many coalitions or masks; no polynomial-time theorem is
asserted. Taking the minimum of independently valid fixed bounds means selecting
one entire rule from known n,d,technology parameters before reports; it does not
mean taking pointwise minima of payments or tuning a rule after seeing reports.
