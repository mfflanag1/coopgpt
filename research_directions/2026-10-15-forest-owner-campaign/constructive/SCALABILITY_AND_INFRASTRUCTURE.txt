SCALABILITY, ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION, AND THE NEXT TARGET
This is model interpretation and a mathematical target, not measured evidence of AI-serving gains.

1. WHY THE DEPTH-TWO TREE IS A CREDIBLE NEXT MODEL
Let h_j denote divisible compute-time capacity in hardware pool j during one fixed planning period. Let p be a shared grid-energy allowance and l_j a local battery/onsite-energy allowance usable only by pool j. Each unit of throughput can consume h_j plus either p or l_j. The compatibility graph is exactly p--h_j--l_j, with arbitrarily many branches. Different jobs or hardware efficiencies can enter through fixed positive activity values and endpoint consumption coefficients; the forest normalization and threshold reduction handle these mathematically.

The resources must be genuinely separate entitlements. The shared grid allowance and local battery stock cannot both count the same electricity. A hardware pool must really be able to use either source. This is more concrete than treating physically fixed GPUs as freely movable between power zones. Owners may hold hardware, shared energy, local energy, or mixtures, and a coalition can use only the resources its members control. A binding underreport means verifiable withholding of those resources, not an unverifiable forecast.

The exact one-period unit-value production function is
 F(h,p,l)=min(sum h_j, p+sum min(h_j,l_j))
        =sum min(h_j,l_j)+min(p,sum(h_j-l_j)_+).
It is evaluable in O(number of branches). The second expression suggests local energy first and shared energy for residual demand, but those residuals decrease when local energy reports increase. That is precisely why a payment proof cannot simply compose separately truthful mechanisms for the displayed summands.

2. WHAT THIS MODEL DOES NOT YET SAY
The theorem uses a divisible fluid allocation in one period. An energy allowance is not automatically an instantaneous peak-power constraint. To interpret p as peak power times period length requires a constant or otherwise justified load profile. Request deadlines, queueing, integer GPU placement, model-memory fit, battery discharge-rate limits, time coupling, and private opportunity costs are not established by the current results.

Payment-only utility is economically cleanest for prepaid or otherwise committed entitlements. If actual operating costs matter, a truthful net-payment theorem does not by itself cover them. One possible later extension is known-cost reimbursement plus the proved net-surplus mechanism, with activity values adjusted by known operating costs; then the total-payment comparison must explicitly distinguish reimbursement from surplus subsidy. Private costs require a different mechanism contract.

3. WHAT SCALES ALGORITHMICALLY
The arbitrary double-star formula has O(number of edges) report-independent threshold terms. Sorting fixed profits and evaluating the two-/three-input rules yields a polynomial-size formula even when owner counts and leaf counts grow. Each actual owner can span multiple resources; ownership aggregation is linear and does not create new strategic identities.

The weighted-forest threshold reduction has at most |V|-1 nonzero intervals. Fixed endpoint rescaling and potentials are computed by a tree traversal. For positive rational coefficients, the bit length of products/ratios grows at most with the sum of input bit lengths along a path; there is no hidden exponential list of price supports in this reduction.

The three-/four-pool mechanisms use real exponentials. Their mathematical eligibility is proved, but a finite-precision implementation needs an explicit error/rounding policy before exact all-profile truthfulness can be claimed for executable payments. The separate rational two-input construction in the coordinator lane addresses only that two-input primitive. Do not infer a rational exact implementation of every softmax result from it.

For arbitrary-depth forests, the reduced unit-profit subproblems remain unsolved at factor<2. The threshold reduction removes fixed values and efficiency coefficients from the main difficulty; it does not supply the missing general mechanism. Nor does the exponential number of minimal covers imply that evaluating the production value is hard: ordinary flow algorithms, and especially the depth-two formula, are efficient.

4. A PRECISE NEXT CAMPAIGN CONTRACT
Keep fixed actual owners, arbitrary mixed bundles, binding coordinatewise underreports, nonnegative payments, all-profile coalition coverage, and payment-only utility. Work first on arbitrary-size depth-two subdivided stars with unit values and unit coefficients. Success must be either:
(a) a direct mechanism with a proved uniform factor C<2 and a rigorous account of the residual-demand incentive effects; or
(b) an exact sequence of finite report-transition certificates giving a new general-mechanism lower bound for this same unbounded class, with owners fixed within every certificate.

A bounded-family positive construction alone does not meet this gate. The current connected-value extreme-ray theorem rules out reducing the class to a positive sum of any fixed number-of-forms primitive. The reasonable-upper-payoff mask obstruction rules out every fixed Bernoulli parameter and every owner-count-independent interior mixture of those parameters. The fixed Shapley/grand-marginal and deterministic outside-scaling mixture obstructions remove two other simple routes. An owner-count-dependent mask schedule remains logically open, but must be assessed against the repository's separate mask-support obstructions before making it the main constructive bet.

The most defensible constructive question is therefore a coupled allocation using local-first dispatch with a payment correction whose derivative cancels the loss of shared-resource access. The principal lower-bound question is whether many branches create cumulative all-profile incentive pressure even though the production value has a simple O(n) formula. Neither question is resolved by the present campaign.

EXACT FORM-COUNT CHECK FOR THE NEXT TARGET. The depth-two subdivided star has exactly2^n minimal vertex covers: the all-h cover, and the2^n-1 covers containing p plus one vertex from each private branch with at least one leaf chosen. These covers are all exposed by positive capacity vectors. Thus its production value has an O(n) evaluation formula yet requires2^n distinct supporting linear forms, and the positive-concave-decomposition obstruction already applies at fixed tree depth2.
