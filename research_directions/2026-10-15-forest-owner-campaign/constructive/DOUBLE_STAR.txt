A positive decomposition for dominant-center double stars
15 October 2026. Constructive scoping result; historical novelty unconfirmed.

MODEL. The connected resource graph has central vertices u,v and an edge uv
of value a>0. Leaves attached to u have capacities s_j and edge values b_j>0;
leaves attached to v have capacities t_k and edge values c_k>0. The centers
have capacities U,V. Every edge consumes one unit at each incident endpoint.
All resource capacities are sums of arbitrary nonnegative bundles held by a
fixed real owner set. The owner count and both leaf counts are unrestricted.
Any owner can own resources on both sides and at several vertices.

Assume a >= b_max+c_max, with an absent side's maximum defined as zero.
Sort b_1>=...>=b_J>0 and c_1>=...>=c_K>0, put b_{J+1}=c_{K+1}=0,
B_j=sum_{h<=j}s_h, C_k=sum_{h<=k}t_h. Then

F = (a-b_1-c_1) min(U,V)
    + sum_j (b_j-b_{j+1}) min(U,V+B_j)
    + sum_k (c_k-c_{k+1}) min(V,U+C_k).                 (DS)

Missing-side sums and maxima are zero. All coefficients are nonnegative and
all arguments are nonnegative fixed linear combinations of resource capacities.

PROOF. Set m=min(U,V). Start with any optimal feasible edge quantities having
central quantity z<m. Increase z by delta<=m-z. At u, reduce incident leaf
quantities by max(0,delta-(U-z-L_u)), where L_u is their current total; this
amount is at most delta and at most L_u because z+delta<=U. Similarly at v.
This preserves all capacities (leaf capacities only become less used), while
lost leaf value is at most delta(b_1+c_1) and central gain is a delta. Hence
there is an optimum with z=m. The two leaf systems then independently maximize
fractional knapsack value with center budgets U-m and V-m. Layer decomposition
gives
F=a m + sum_j Delta b_j min((U-V)_+,B_j)
          + sum_k Delta c_k min((V-U)_+,C_k).
Use min((U-V)_+,B)=min(U,V+B)-min(U,V), and its symmetric version; telescoping
sum Delta b=b_1 and sum Delta c=c_1 yields (DS).

PAYMENTS. Apply the proved mixed-owner two-input exponential rule to each
minimum in (DS), using each real owner's contributions to its two aggregate
arguments. Sum with the fixed nonnegative coefficients. Every coalition obeys
(DS), so coverage adds. Own whole-bundle increases raise both derived accounts
coordinatewise without changing another owner's accounts, so all-profile
truthfulness follows from the mixed-owner lemma. The total is at most
(1+1/(2e))F. Zero capacities, zero-weight differences, ties, and zero output
are handled exactly as in the preceding star theorem. This is an unbounded
connected nonstar subclass of the forest model, not a bound for all forests.

SHARPNESS OF THE STRUCTURAL ASSUMPTION FOR THIS ROUTE. When both sides contain
a leaf and a<b_max+c_max, set U=V=1, set the maximum-valued leaf on each side
to capacity 1, and all other leaf capacities to zero. Saturating uv gives a,
while the two selected leaf activities give b_max+c_max>a. Thus the condition
is necessary and sufficient for central saturation to be optimal at every
capacity profile of this fixed double-star technology. This is only an exact
boundary for the saturation/decomposition route, not an optimal-payment lower
bound. In particular the existing values (3,2,2) lie outside this subclass.
