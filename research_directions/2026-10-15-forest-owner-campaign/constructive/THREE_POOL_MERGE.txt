Mixed-owner three-pool softmax lemma
15 October 2026. Candidate extension; historical novelty unconfirmed.

Fix arbitrary actual owners holding c_ij>=0 for j=0,1,2. Put T=min_j sum_i c_ij,
d_ij=min(c_ij,T), rho_j=sum_i d_ij/T, w_j=exp(1-rho_j), p_j=w_j/sum_h w_h.
For T>0 pay y_i=sum_j p_j d_ij; at T=0 pay zero. Coverage and total factor
1+b, b exp(b)=2/e, follow from the existing exclusive-pool softmax proof:
every capped pool covers each coalition, some rho_j=1, and total/T is the
weighted mean of rho_j. This note supplies the missing ACTUAL-OWNER incentive
argument when one owner controls several accounts.

If an increased own coordinate belongs to a long pool, T is fixed. Its own
account payment has derivative p_j[1-(d_ij/T)(1-p_j)]>=0 before its cap and
zero thereafter; other p_h increase and their own-owner holdings stay fixed.
Hence the owner's whole payment increases.

Suppose instead the increased own coordinate x lies in a uniquely short pool 0.
T=x+a for fixed a>=0, w_0=1. Work off finitely many clipping/tie points.
For j=1,2 let k_j be the number of capped accounts and let t_j be the sum of
uncapped holdings divided by T. Thus rho_j=k_j+t_j and
  w_j' = w_j t_j/T.
Write A=w_1, B=w_2, W=1+A+B and M=(A t_1+B t_2)/W. We have M<1, because
  A(t_1-1)+B(t_2-1)
    <= sum_{j=1,2}(rho_j-1)exp(1-rho_j) <=2/e<1.
For this actual owner let a_j=d_ij/T. Its long account derivative delta_j is 1
when capped and 0 when uncapped. Also a_0=x/T<=1. Differentiating its SUM gives
  D=p_0(1-a_0 M)+sum_{j=1,2}p_j[delta_j+a_j(t_j-M)].
Capped terms are nonnegative since a_j=delta_j=1 and M<1. For uncapped terms,
a_j<=t_j. If t_j>=M their contribution is nonnegative; otherwise it is at
least p_j t_j(t_j-M). At most one of t_1,t_2 is less than M: if both were,
their weighted mean with the additional weight-one zero would be less than M.
If neither is, D>=p_0(1-M)>0. Otherwise relabel t_1=s<M and t_2=t. Then
  D W^2 >= 1+A(1-s+s^2)+B(1+A s^2)-Bt(1+A s).
Since B=exp(1-k_2-t)<=exp(1-t), we have Bt<=t exp(1-t)<=1. Consequently
  D W^2 >= A(1-s)^2+B(1+A s^2)>0.
Thus increasing a coordinate in a short pool also cannot lower actual-owner
payment. Formulae agree continuously at caps and ties. At zero output,
nonnegativity and the established total bound force continuity to zero.
Sequential coordinate increases prove whole-bundle underreport monotonicity.

COROLLARY. For F(c)=min{L_0(c),L_1(c),L_2(c)} with fixed nonnegative linear
forms L_j, assign actual owner i derived accounts L_j(b_i). The above rule
covers every coalition, is whole-owner truthful on all nonnegative profiles,
and pays at most [1+W(2/e)]F(c), approximately 1.4630565 F(c). The forms may
overlap in resource support; no owner splitting or population change occurs.
The account-space proof holds on the full nonnegative orthant, so checking
coordinatewise changes may leave a particular physical-bundle image without
invalidating monotonicity between the image endpoints. Positive sums of such
technologies retain the same factor with arbitrary
mixed fixed owners. This does not prove the bound for every forest.
