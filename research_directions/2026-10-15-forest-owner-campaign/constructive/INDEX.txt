ACCEPTED MATHEMATICAL CORE (parent independently checked; cross-model review reported no substantive error)
1. THREE_POOL_MERGE.txt: direct all-profile mixed-owner extension of three-pool softmax, total factor 1+W(2/e) about1.46306. Actual owners remain fixed; no incentive claim is inferred from naive cloning.
2. ARBITRARY_DOUBLE_STAR.txt: positive report-independent threshold decomposition for every weighted double star, with arbitrary continuous capacities and unbounded leaf/owner counts. Combined with1 yields the same factor for mixed owners. Also proves positive endpoint-coefficient normalization on any forest. This supersedes the narrower DOUBLE_STAR.txt.
3. FOUR_POOL_MERGE.txt: direct all-profile mixed-owner four-pool rule, with optimal exponential-family rate 2-log3 and attained factor about1.66957. This covers any minimum of at most4fixed nonnegative linear forms, including unit four-edge paths. Optimality is only within that exponential family.
4. WEIGHTED_FOREST_REDUCTION.txt: exact positive threshold reduction of weighted forest matching/production to unit-profit subforests. Together with endpoint normalization, an edge-deletion-closed forest class has the same optimal uniform mechanism factor with arbitrary fixed positive weights/coefficients as with unit weights/coefficients. Prior-art comparison remains incomplete.
5. EXTREME_RAY_OBSTRUCTION.txt: any connected unit bipartite production value is an extreme ray among finite nonnegative concave homogeneous functions. Consequently no fixed-K positive concave decomposition into K-form primitives can cover all forests. FOUR_PATH_OBSTRUCTION.txt is an earlier special case, retained as a transparent small obstruction.

ADDITIONAL PROVED LEMMAS / ARCHITECTURE FILTERS (parent checked; include in final external review queue)
6. MAXIMUM_MARGINAL_CAP.txt: the classical reasonable upper payoff V_i=max_S[v(S+i)-v(S)] safely truncates every covering vector and preserves the report-mask hybrid's incentives and identities. But even this cap has unbounded fixed-q budget on a depth-two tree, robust to unique supporting covers, strictly positive capacities, and absence of veto owners. Any owner-count-independent mixture with interior q mass also fails. This is not a general mechanism lower bound and does not exclude owner-count-dependent q laws.
7. GRAND_MARGINAL_SHAPLEY_OBSTRUCTION.txt: direct corollary of an EXISTING repository asymptotic family. Fixed nonnegative mixtures alpha U+beta Shapley cannot uniformly beat factor2, already on one edge. It is not a newly discovered lower-bound family.

WHAT THE CAMPAIGN HAS NOT SOLVED
No uniform C<2 mechanism for arbitrary-depth forests is proved. No matching general lower bound2 is proved. Fixed actual mixed ownership and binding all-profile underreports remain essential. The results do not incorporate private costs, monetary operating costs, latency schedules, stochastic service, power-market trading, or real energy measurements.

RECOMMENDED NEXT MATHEMATICAL GATE
Target the unweighted depth-two subdivided star p--h_j--l_j, with unbounded branches and arbitrary mixed owners. It is the first simple unbounded class outside double stars where connected-value extreme-ray obstruction and the reasonable-cap mask obstruction both apply. Prove a uniform C<2 by a direct coupled allocation, or obtain an exact growing-owner lower certificate against unrestricted mechanisms. Do not count another fixed-K primitive, fixed marginal mixture, or fixed-q mask improvement as satisfying this gate. Its scalar value formula is
 F=min(sum_j h_j, p+sum_j min(h_j,l_j))
  =sum_j min(h_j,l_j)+min(p,sum_j(h_j-l_j)_+).
The second display is useful algebra but is NOT a positive concave decomposition; residual terms decrease when local power rises. Any recursive payment proof must explicitly control those incentive effects.
