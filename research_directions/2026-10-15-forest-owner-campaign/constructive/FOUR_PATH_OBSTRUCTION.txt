The four-edge path is not a positive sum of at-most-three-form technologies
15 October 2026. Exact obstruction to one decomposition route, not to mechanisms.

Take five vertices 0,1,2,3,4 in a path with four unit-profit, unit-consumption
edge activities and capacities c=(c0,...,c4)>=0. Its production function is
  F(c)=min{A(c),B(c),C(c),D(c)},
  A=c1+c3, B=c0+c2+c4, C=c0+c2+c3, D=c1+c2+c4.
These are precisely its four inclusion-minimal vertex covers. For completeness,
the bipartite vertex-cover LP has an integral optimum: clamp any feasible dual
vertex prices to [0,1], select even vertices with price>=theta and odd vertices
with price>=1-theta, and average theta uniformly over [0,1]. Every selected
set covers all edges; its expected capacity cost equals the fractional dual
objective. Strong duality gives the displayed formula.

CLAIM. F cannot be expressed, even only on the strictly positive orthant, as a
finite sum of concave piecewise-linear homogeneous functions each having at
most three linear pieces, with all sum coefficients strictly positive. In
particular no finite positive decomposition into minima of <=3 nonnegative
linear forms exists. Adding globally linear summands does not help.

PROOF. Absorb positive weights into summands. On an open chamber where one of
A,B,C,D uniquely minimizes, F is affine. Each concave summand must be affine
on that whole convex chamber: Jensen gaps are nonnegative and sum to zero.
Write a_s,b_s,c_s,d_s for its four chamber gradients.
All four chambers are nonempty, witnessed respectively by capacities
  (2,1,2,1,2), (1,3,1,3,1), (1,3,1,1,3), (3,1,1,3,1).

At c=(1,2,1,1,1), exactly A=B=C=3<D=4. The two independent equalities
A=B and B=C produce all three neighboring chambers and their pairwise facets
in a small positive neighborhood. Continuity across such a facet forces the
summand's gradient jump to be parallel to F's jump; concavity forces its
scalar to be nonnegative. Denote the scalars lambda_AB,lambda_BC,lambda_CA.
Summing the three gradient jumps around the triangle and using the linear
independence of A-B and B-C shows
  lambda_AB=lambda_BC=lambda_CA.
At c=(1,1,1,2,1), exactly A=B=D=3<C=4. The same argument gives
  lambda_AB=lambda_BD=lambda_DA.
The same A and B chamber gradients enter both neighborhoods, so lambda_AB is
the same scalar in both. Hence, for some lambda_s>=0 and fixed vector h_s,
  a_s=lambda_s A+h_s, b_s=lambda_s B+h_s,
  c_s=lambda_s C+h_s, d_s=lambda_s D+h_s,
where A,B,C,D on this line denote their coefficient vectors.
If lambda_s>0, these are four distinct gradients, impossible for a function
with at most three linear pieces. Thus every summand has lambda_s=0. Its
four gradients are equal, so the sum has no gradient jump between these
chambers, contradicting F's distinct gradients. QED.

The argument concerns positive CONCAVE sums with report-independent component
technologies. It does not preclude nonlinear mechanisms, signed identities,
minima of four or more forms, alternative nonconcave intermediate expressions,
or direct truthful coalition-covering rules with a factor below two.
