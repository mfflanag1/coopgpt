COROLLARY OF AN EXISTING REPOSITORY RESULT: FIXED GRAND-MARGINAL/SHAPLEY MIXTURES CANNOT BEAT FACTOR TWO
Not a new independent lower-bound family.

Let U_i=T-v(N\i), and let phi_i be the ordinary individual Shapley value. Consider y_i=alpha U_i+beta phi_i for fixed nonnegative coefficients alpha,beta, uniformly across all ownership profiles. Already on a single resource-pair activity, coalition coverage forces alpha+beta/2>=1.

Proof: use section5 of the existing HALF_FACTOR_THEOREM.txt (research_campaigns/2026-10-04-many-owner-subsidy). Put m=q^4,r=q^4-q^3. There are m unit left owners, r unit right fringe owners, and one right owner b with capacity m. Let S consist of all left owners plus b. Then v(S)=T=m, U(S)=m+(m-r), and the previously proved result gives phi(S)/m ->1/2. Dividing the required coalition inequality by m and letting q increase proves alpha+beta/2>=1.

At the balanced two-owner profile (one left unit, one right unit), sum U=2T and sum phi=T. Thus total payment is (2alpha+beta)T>=2T. No such fixed nonnegative mixture uniformly improves the factor2 baseline. This conclusion also rules out a fixed choice such as .5U+.6phi; it does not rule out nonlinear combinations, report-dependent weights with a separate incentive proof, or different payment rules.

An exact small counter to .5U+.6phi is already in section6 of the same existing note: capacities (1,1 | 1,1,2), coalition the two left owners plus the right capacity2 owner. Here v=2,U(S)=2,phi(S)=49/30. The proposed mixture pays1+(3/5)(49/30)=49/50+1=99/50<2. Thus the proposed1.6 factor fails even with5owners ononeedge.
