MODEL AND WHAT A BOUND MEANS
Owners report binding nonnegative bundles of available resources. The public
production function is monotone, concave and homogeneous of degree one. Each
coalition can pool only its members' reported resources. Payments must cover
every coalition's production value and must not decrease when an owner reports
more of its own bundle. Owners and technologies stay fixed throughout reports.
Utility is payment only: private costs, electricity tariffs, discrete jobs,
latency and empirical GPU performance are not established by these theorems.
The stated factors impose no additional original-core fairness floor.

T denotes grand production. A total-payment factor C means at most CT paid,
or at most (C-1)T external subsidy. It is not a measured return or welfare gain.
The general results include finite-valued concave homogeneous functions with
downward boundary discontinuities. They do not assert efficient evaluation.

1. GENERAL UPPER BOUNDS: THE MAIN NEW RESULTS

Owner count. For arbitrary fixed n owners and arbitrary resource dimension,
  total payment <= n sqrt(2/(n+1)) T = (sqrt(2)+o(1))sqrt(n) T.
This improves the project's previous O(n/log n) bound. A second construction
uses a rational distribution over fixed-cardinality masks and has explicit
factor min_m [n-(m-1)/(H_n-H_(n-m))], with the same leading constant.
Proofs: portfolio/OWNER_SQRT_BOUND_v2.txt and coordinator/FIXED_MASK_THEOREM.txt.

Resource dimension. For arbitrary numbers of owners and fixed d resources,
  total payment <= (1+2sqrt(d)) T.
Together with the elementary grand-marginal bound, use min{d,1+2sqrt(d)}.
The sharper parameterized expression is
  C_d(a)=[1+2d(-a-log(1-a))]/a, 0<a<1.
This improves the previous linear dimension upper bound to O(sqrt(d)). The
key step bounds the expected closed-owner support prices coordinate by
coordinate using an exact Bernoulli reciprocal identity. It is a new analysis
of the existing report-mask/endpoint construction, not a change of economic
model. Proof: portfolio/DIMENSION_SQRT_BOUND_v1.txt. The self-contained support,
coverage, incentive and d-baseline arguments are in coordinator/FOUNDATIONS.txt.

The earlier sharp bound in supporting-form count K remains available. Choose
the best entire rule from public n,d,K and technology information BEFORE
reports. Pointwise minima of payment vectors are not justified.

2. WHAT THE NEW LOWER BOUNDS DO AND DO NOT ESTABLISH

For fixed report-independent mask laws, common supporting prices chosen from
open reports, and endpoint payments to open owners, the owner-count growth
sqrt(2n) is sharp to leading order. This holds even for finite global supports,
arbitrary asymmetric/correlated mask laws, and randomized support selection.
Replacing the global T cap by the maximum-coalition-marginal cap V_i does not
escape that owner-count barrier.

The compressed constructions also show dimension-order barriers: global-T
caps require (sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(d) on an n=d family; even V_i caps require
(1/2-o(1))sqrt(d) on another growing family. These bounds establish the square-
root ORDER for these mechanism architectures, not the unrestricted problem.

The unrestricted lower bounds remain of order log n/log log n and
log d/log log d in their respective parameters. We have NOT proved a sharp
square-root law for all truthful mechanisms. Going below it requires a new
mechanism ingredient; the current proof does not exclude report-dependent
sampling with a separate incentive proof or incompatible owner-specific prices.
Proofs: portfolio/FINITE_SUPPORT_MASK_OBSTRUCTION_v2.txt,
portfolio/VCAP_MASK_OBSTRUCTION_v1.txt, and
portfolio/COMPRESSED_MASK_DIMENSION_BARRIER_v1.txt.

3. FOREST PROGRESS AND THE HARD BOUNDARY

Three and four input primitives now admit mixed-owner rules with factors
  C3=1+W(2/e) approximately 1.4630565,
  C4=1+W(3/e)/(2-log 3) approximately 1.669574.
The four-input rate is optimal within the specified exponential family.
These statements allow arbitrary fixed owners and all nonnegative real
bundles. They do not settle arbitrary input count.

Every weighted double star, with arbitrarily many leaves and owners, admits
the C3 bound. This removes the earlier dominant-central-edge restriction.
Weighted four-edge paths admit C4. More generally, an exact positive threshold
decomposition reduces weighted forest production to unit-profit subforests.
Fixed positive endpoint resource coefficients can also be normalized away.
Consequently the optimal uniform forest factor is the same for weighted and
unit-profit forests. Profit heterogeneity need not be a separate frontier.

However, connected unit-profit bipartite production functions are extreme
rays of the cone of nonnegative concave homogeneous functions. This prevents
a positive decomposition of every growing forest into bounded numbers of
supporting forms per component. Extending one more fixed input count will
not by itself overcome this obstruction.

The V_i cap preserves coalition coverage and incentives, but its Bernoulli
hybrid can overpay without limit on depth-two subdivided stars. The example
survives strictly positive full resource capacities and removal of veto owners.
Thus a fixed small missing probability or a better tie break is insufficient.

Proofs: constructive/THREE_POOL_MERGE.txt, FOUR_POOL_MERGE.txt,
ARBITRARY_DOUBLE_STAR.txt, WEIGHTED_FOREST_REDUCTION.txt,
EXTREME_RAY_OBSTRUCTION.txt, and MAXIMUM_MARGINAL_CAP.txt.

4. OWNERSHIP, NOT ONLY PHYSICAL TOPOLOGY

For exclusively owned resource rows with no additional shared constraint,
when production is component-additive on the fixed owner-interaction forest,
a classical hierarchical allocation gives efficient, coalition-covering,
own-bundle-monotone sharing.
Connected exclusive resource blocks on a physical forest suffice, but are
not necessary. The core/hierarchy ingredient is credited to existing work,
including Demange and Herings--van der Laan--Talman; it is not claimed as new.

Fragmented EXCLUSIVE ownership can nevertheless force subsidy on physical
paths. Repeating owners A-B-C-D over 4k resource vertices gives
  C >= (9k-5)/(8k-4), tending to 9/8.
The k=2 case is an exact 13/12 three-profile certificate. Product replication
allows owner and resource counts to grow without strategic identity changes.
This strengthens the interpretation of the existing 9/8 lower bound; it does
not raise that numerical frontier. A separate cycle-to-path transfer lets
finite cycle certificates guide future forest lower-bound searches.

Mixed two-input games with at most three actual owners admit efficient rules;
four owners can already require subsidy. A netting lemma explains how a
zero-at-zero, own-monotone, 1-Lipschitz exclusive-pool rule lifts to mixed
ownership. These are useful boundaries, not general-bound breakthroughs.
Proofs and exact certificates: lower/README.txt and the files it indexes.

5. FAILED ROUTES PRESERVED

- No forest/two-input finite-profile LP search found a lower bound exceeding
  9/8. Finite-grid upper
  certificates are not continuous-profile mechanisms; unfinished searches
  have no claimed optimum.
- The proposed sampled-support inequality Z<=T/(1-q) fails on a five-vertex
  path, with an exact rational witness.
- Fixed nonnegative mixtures of grand marginals and Shapley payments cannot
  uniformly beat factor 2, by an existing project family.
- Even owner-count-dependent, report-independent mixtures of deterministic
  outside-scaling marginals cannot uniformly beat 2 on a single-edge game.
- The natural Gibbs distribution over all minimal covers on depth-two trees
  also fails: every report-independent scalar rate either has budget at least
  d/36 on d-branch examples or violates an owner's underreport incentives.
  The two profiles use identical fixed owners and reporting domains. This is
  an exact obstruction to that architecture, not to all truthful mechanisms.
  See constructive/DEPTH_TWO_GIBBS_OBSTRUCTION.txt.

6. RECOMMENDATION

First consolidate the two general upper bounds and the clearly restricted
architecture barriers into a focused manuscript addendum and specialist
packet. These are the strongest outcomes of this campaign. The existing open
manuscript has deliberately remained unchanged during this research run.
Human assessment and a precise priority search remain necessary before any
field-level significance claim.

For the next mathematical campaign, use one bounded direct attack on arbitrary
depth-two subdivided stars p--h_j--l_j, with mixed owners and unbounded branches.
This is a concrete unbounded class that defeats the current capped
mask route. Require a uniform factor below 2, a stronger unrestricted lower bound, or a
structural lemma that advances that target; an exact obstruction ends the
proposed NEW mechanism attempt. A mechanism-specific obstruction is a stopping result,
not a resolution of the all-forest target. The weighted-forest reduction lets the first proof
work with unit profits. Avoid another sequence of fixed-K primitives.

In parallel, use compact owner-interaction cycles to search for an exact lower
certificate above 9/8, then apply cycle-to-path transfer. Insist on interacting
whole-owner report changes and exact certificate replay. A numerical hint is
not the stopping success gate.

For the broader general theory, the question is now whether the unrestricted
owner/dimension upper bounds can beat square-root growth. The current
architecture barriers identify what must change, but do not choose the new
mechanism for us. Owner-specific supports with a new coverage coupling are a
concrete research direction, not an established construction.

For AI infrastructure, build the next applied model around independently
controlled hardware reservations and power rights whose OWNER interaction
graph contains a cycle. Exclusive ownership with component-additive production
on an owner forest is an efficient classical baseline; a bare drawing of an
acyclic graph is insufficient when there are extra shared constraints.
A concrete candidate uses two fixed compute pods: one owner holds both local
battery reservations, two owners hold the hardware reservations, and a fourth
holds a shared deliverable grid reservation. The resource graph is a five-node
path, while the owner graph is a four-cycle. No GPU relocation is assumed.
This cycle does not itself prove a subsidy need; see AI_OWNERSHIP_INSTITUTION.txt.
Map shared electricity limits, reservations and feasible withholding
explicitly before claiming incentive costs. Latency, private opportunity costs
and measured energy remain separate empirical gates; the Mac experiments do
not currently establish a pooling benefit.

LATE APPLIED-MODEL RESULT
For the clean unit-profit depth-two institution, there is an explicit efficient
rule for every number of pods: let a_j=min(H_j,L_j), R=min(P,sum_j(H_j-L_j)_+).
Pay each hardware owner a_j, the shared-grid owner R, and the one owner of all
local-energy rights zero. These payments sum exactly to production. A coalition
with only local energy is covered exactly; one with only grid energy is covered
because sum_I a_j+min(P,sum_I residual_j)>=min(P,sum_I H_j); one with both is
covered by the same residual bound. Each actual owner's own-report incentive
is immediate. This is the classical fixed-order marginal vector for local-
energy owner first, hardware owners next, grid owner last; its core coverage
on this subclass is checked explicitly.

This proves that the concrete ownership cycle ALONE does not require subsidy.
It allows weak individual incentives and zero local-energy payment. It is
not group-strategyproof: a local-energy/grid-owner coalition can gain through
withholding and side payments. It does not satisfy a
positive fairness floor or private outside-option requirement. It does not
extend to arbitrary mixed ownership or unequal activity values as stated.
See AI_INSTITUTION_EFFICIENT_RULE.txt and the late completion/audit record.
Historical novelty is unassessed. A useful applied next question is which
additional economically necessary participation requirement changes this
zero-subsidy conclusion, rather than presuming that the cycle creates cost.
