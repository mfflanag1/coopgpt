PORTABLE REPRODUCTION

From this campaign directory run:
  python3 -B -O REPRODUCE.py

Only the Python standard library is required for the curated suite. It runs
14 top-level jobs, including the portfolio's five exact verifiers, at most
three in parallel. No optimizer, network, GPU, paid service, or LaTeX compiler
is used. Source hashes and output hashes are recorded in
REPRODUCTION_RESULT.json; actual output is saved in checks/.

The coordinator executed this suite from a clean staged copy of the curated
files. Every job passed. The three lower-bound finite grids each contain
6,561 profiles, and their certificate checker independently recomputes
coalition production and checks rational primal and dual data. Details and
additional selected certificates are in lower/README.txt.

Other key counts:
- Mixed three/four-input softmax: 4,720 profiles, 44,992 coalition inequalities,
  55,720 own-report increases, and 4,720 budget checks, using exact exponential
  polynomials and rational Taylor enclosures. The four-input tests use rate9/10;
  the sharper irrational rate is established analytically, not by this grid.
- Arbitrary weighted double stars:182,147 exact rational identity comparisons.
- Weighted forests:20,000 integer tree profiles and148,194 threshold optima.
- Dimension upper:576 scalar identities,61,632 coordinate-price comparisons,
  1,728 closed-budget and1,728 integrated-budget comparisons.
- Compressed dimension barriers:129,656 exact subset-score comparisons and
  332 unique-gradient/mask/error/budget comparisons.

These counts overlap within each test design and should not be summed into
an alleged number of independent experiments. Finite tests corroborate the
proofs and detect errors; they establish neither universal theorems nor novelty.

Discovery scripts named search_*, threshold_lp.py, bundle_threshold_lp.py,
projective_plane_discovery.py, and test_piecewise_weight.py are optional
exploration. Some require NumPy/SciPy. They are excluded from the curated exact
suite and must not be treated as accepted mechanism guarantees.

To verify archive integrity without changing files:
  python3 -B verify_manifest.py

SHA256.json covers every payload file except itself and the manifest checker.
Rerunning tests can update execution metadata; check the original manifest
before rerunning, or expect those reproducible metadata files to change.
