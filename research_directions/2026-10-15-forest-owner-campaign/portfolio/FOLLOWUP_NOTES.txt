FOLLOW-UP SCOPE AND DISCOVERY NOTES -- 15 October 2026

Upper-bound v2 adds only a clarification: a may exceed1/2 for small n,
so this is an explicit extension of the old j>=2 averaging parameter.
Pointwise eligibility was already proved throughout0<q<1.

The canonical-relative-support lower witnesses lie on proper coordinate faces.
Its full-T penalties use the prescribed +infinity extension off that face.
The lower is valid on the stated nonnegative whole-bundle domain, but may
vanish under strictly positive perturbations. It is not an obstruction to
finite global support selectors. This limitation is economically/materially
important and should accompany any presentation of the class-sharp result.

A preliminary follow-up searched stronger finite-form/unrestricted obstructions
using f(x)=min(sum_i x_i, A sum of k smallest x_i). The restricted report grid
{0,1,n^2}, n=4,6,8,12,16,20,24,32, k near sqrt(n), and A=1,n/k,n produced
numerical optimal total-payment factor1 in all24 symmetry-reduced LPs.
These are floating discovery solves only, not exact certificates or proofs of
efficiency on a full real report domain. They discourage promoting this natural
threshold family as a new unrestricted owner lower without an additional idea.
The discovery model uses scalar axis reports, not the whole-bundle domain;
a lower on that domain would transfer, but an upper there would not.
