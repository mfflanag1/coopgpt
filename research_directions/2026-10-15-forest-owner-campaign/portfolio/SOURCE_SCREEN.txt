6. Bachrach, Elkind, Meir, Pasechnik, Zuckerman, Rothe and Rosenschein,
The Cost of Stability in Coalitional Games, arXiv0907.4385 (24 July2009).
Full original Theorem3.5 and its proof read:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.4385
The theorem already gives a sharp square-root-n TOTAL-payment factor for static
superadditive games; the lower uses finite projective planes. This is essential
context: square-root subsidy growth itself is not new. Its static core-extension
problem differs from the present one, whose concave production profile games
already have efficient cores and whose extra cost enforces monotonicity across
reports. The static theorem does not supply an all-profile truthful mechanism
or transfer its projective-plane lower into balanced production games.
