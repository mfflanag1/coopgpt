STATEMENT
Even on productive depth-two networks with arbitrarily many branches and owners,
zero local funding slack delta=CM-Z does NOT imply that a full negative-gap
owner's raw nested payment is already its own-report envelope. The counterexample
uses only a local-resource underreport; no outer hardware is changed or restored.
The associated capped-total increase is first order while the deviator's own
local-payment loss is second order. Consequently no uniform response bound of
order D^beta, beta>1/2, can replace the square-root correction on this class.
