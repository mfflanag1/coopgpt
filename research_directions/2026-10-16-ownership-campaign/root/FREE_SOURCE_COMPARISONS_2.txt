EDMONDS (1965)
Minimum Partition of a Matroid Into Independent Subsets.
Free original: https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/69B/jresv69Bn1-2p67_A1b.pdf
Theorem1 on printed page69 states the rank-density criterion for partition into k independent sets. For a graph of fractional arboricity p/q, replicate every edge q times in parallel. Every edge-copy subset meets the p-times-rank criterion, so a partition into p forests gives a fractional forest cover of total p/q. This elementary consequence supplies the combinatorial ingredient of OWNER_FOREST_COVER.txt. Neither matroid partition nor fractional arboricity is a proposed new contribution. The density characterization, owner-report reduction and budget argument must be stated separately.

COMPARISON LIMIT
The sources establish that tree allocation and forest-cover machinery are old. They do not by themselves settle priority for this exact binding-capacity, shared-resource ownership formulation or its subsidy theorem. That combination may be routine. Treat the owner-forest/cactus result as a useful corollary and classification tool pending specialist comparison, not a field-level breakthrough.
