GENERAL 13/8 FUNDING FOR THE ORIGINAL MIXED-OWNER ACTUAL ENVELOPE
16 October 2026. Independent audit pending. No novelty or optimality claim.

THEOREM
Let the fixed actual owners have arbitrary nonnegative hardware, local-power,
and grid bundles, with any finite numbers of owners and productive branches.
At every report profile let H be total hardware, P total grid, and
M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), so physical production is F=min(H,P+M). The ORIGINAL
symmetric exponential nested rule's ACTUAL own-report monotone envelope obeys
                         sum_i E_i <= (13/8) F.
This is one all-profile mechanism with whole-owner coordinate underreport
incentives and all-coalition coverage. It does not merge owners, modify their
domains by regime, or assert efficient exact evaluation of the envelope.
It strengthens the earlier general 27/16 bound for this same mechanism.

1. EXHAUSTIVE REGIMES
F=0 gives zero payments. If 0<P+M<=H, the accepted power-bottleneck theorem gives
C^2 F<13F/8, where c=1/(2e), C=1+c. Otherwise H=F>0; normalize H=1.
Define pi_i=min(p_i,1), pi=sum_i pi_i. We divide at Pi=51/40.

If pi>=Pi, then P>=pi>1 and the accepted GRID_CAP_RANGE_BOUND applies:
 sum E_i <=1+(1/2)exp(1-pi)[pi+(1/2)exp(-exp(-CM))].       (1)
The right side increases in M<=1 and decreases in pi>=1. Its value at
M=1,pi=51/40 is less than 1.624053<13/8. The verifier checks this strictly
with rational signed Taylor bounds, not rounded decimal comparisons.
An independent short certificate is C<148/125,
 exp(-148/125)>153/500, exp(-153/500)<737/1000,
 exp(-11/40)<19/25. These imply (1)<162453/100000<13/8.

The remaining regime is pi<=Pi, allowing arbitrary raw P and cap overflow.
The following finite certificate covers its entire continuous moment region.

2. ACTUAL-OWNER MOMENTS AND EFFECTIVE OVERFLOW
Use full local payments z_i, Z=sum z_i, a_i=H_i, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), S=sum d_i.
Let J={i:d_i<a_i}, I={i:d_i>a_i},
 v=sum_J(a_i-d_i), zminus=sum_J z_i, delta=CM-Z.
The accepted low-grid moment proof gives
 M<=Z<=CM, 0<=M<=1, delta>=0,
 v+Z-M<=1, v+Z<=3/2, v+zminus<=1,
 (4/3)M+v-zminus<=4/3, 0<=zminus<=Z.                    (2)
These inequalities concern physical hardware/local-power roles and the same
actual negative-owner subset; they do not require a bound on raw P.

For any nonnegative local payment z, min(p_i+z,1)=min(pi_i+z,1). Define
 w_i=(pi_i+z_i-1)_+, Wplus=sum_I w_i.
Then 1<=S<=Pi+Z and 0<=Wplus<=Pi+Z-S. For a restored own comparison, local
payment loss D_i=z_i-z_i' and capped-account loss x_i=d_i-d_i' satisfy exactly
 D_i=x_i+w_i-w_i'<=x_i+w_i.
This is the only overflow identity needed in the reviewed positive-gap dual
proof. The identity holds also when raw p_i>=1, in which case x_i=0,w_i=z_i.
Thus the positive dual bound is valid with this EFFECTIVE overflow, even when
the raw overflow or P is unbounded. This is algebra inside the comparison;
the mechanism and feasible reports remain unchanged.

3. RETAINING THE LOCAL BUDGET DEFICIT
Choose any supporting parameter u in (0,1), and write
 q=exp(1-1/u)/2, A=1-q(1+1/u)>0, eta=q/S,
 lambda=A+eta, mu=(e-1)^2 eta^2/(4A).
The accepted positive dual inequality and negative-gap saving imply
 sum E_i <=1+lambda(S-1)+(lambda-b)v
       +min{(e-1)eta(Z-zminus),mu(Z-zminus)+A(Pi+Z-S)},  (3)
where b=(2/5)exp(1-S-delta). The saving uses
L((e-1)^2 z_i(a_i-d_i))>4/5 on every negative recipient,
since z_i+(a_i-d_i)<=1 and (e-1)^2<3. Tied recipients contribute at most a_i.
In contrast to the coarser previous certificate, delta is retained as a
separate interval parameter, coupled to M and Z by delta=CM-Z.

4. EXACT RATIONAL RECTANGLE CERTIFICATE
Let
 EL=sum_{k=0}^8 1/k!, EU=EL+(1/9!)(10/9),
 CL=1+1/(2EU), CU=1+1/(2EL), KU=EU-1,
 Cbar=148/125, Dbar=23/125.
Then EL<e<EU, CL<C<CU<Cbar and 0<=delta<=cM<Dbar.
Partition S in [1,Pi+Cbar] into 128 equal closed intervals [lo,hi], and
delta in [0,Dbar] into eight equal closed intervals [dl,dh]. All 1024
rectangles are either certified empty or receive a valid rational dual.
An empty rectangle satisfies lo>Pi+CU-dl, contradicting
S<=Pi+Z=Pi+CM-delta<=Pi+CU-dl.

On a nonempty rectangle, a saved rational u in (0,1) defines qlo,qhi by the
odd-degree25/even-degree24 signed Taylor bounds for exp(-(1/u-1))/2. Put
 Ahi=1-qlo(1+1/u), Alo=1-qhi(1+1/u)>0,
 lambdahi=1-qlo(1+1/u-1/lo), etahi=qhi/lo,
 muhi=KU^2 etahi^2/(4Alo), p=KU etahi,
 blo=(2/5) Taylor_25(exp(-(hi-1+dh))), theta=lambdahi-blo.
All comparison directions follow from qlo<=q<=qhi, lo<=S<=hi, delta<=dh.
In particular the multiplied quantities S-1,v,Z-zminus,Pi+Z-S are nonnegative.
The expression (3) is therefore at most
                 1+lambdahi(hi-1)+theta*v+t,            (4)
for a feasible nonnegative t in the following polytope. With variables
x=(M,Z,v,zminus,t), its 13 rows, in saved certificate order, are
 M-Z<=0;                  -CU*M+Z<=0;
 -Z<=Pi-lo;               -M+Z+v<=1;
 Z+v<=3/2;                v+zminus<=1;
 (4/3)M+v-zminus<=4/3;     -Z+zminus<=0;
 -pZ+p*zminus+t<=0;
 -(muhi+Ahi)Z+muhi*zminus+t<=Ahi(Pi-lo);
 M<=1;                    CL*M-Z<=dh;
 -CU*M+Z<=-dl.                                             (5)
The last two rows retain the deficit interval despite replacing the unknown
transcendental C by outer rational bounds. They do not assert delta equals
either rational expression. Every actual profile in the rectangle satisfies
them. The t-rows follow by increasing the right side of each minimum term;
the actual minimum in (3) can be used as t and is nonnegative.

For nonnegative rational dual weights y, exact arithmetic computes
 residual_j=max(0,objective_j-(matrix^T y)_j),
where objective=(0,0,theta,0,1). All five coordinates have valid upper bounds
                  (1,Cbar,1,Cbar,p*Cbar).
Consequently the weighted sum of (5), with these residual bounds, proves
 1+lambdahi(hi-1)+sum_l y_l*rhs_l+sum_j residual_j*cap_j.  (6)
This is direct weak duality. Numerical optimization only proposes the weights;
its convergence, exactness, or optimality is not used as evidence.

certificate.json contains 968 nonempty dual certificates and 56 provably
empty rectangles. verify_certificate.py reconstructs all rows and constants
independently, checks every endpoint, sign, residual, and exact strict target,
and imports neither the builder nor an optimizer. The largest exact bound is
stored as a rational; its approximate display is 1.620539847074376<13/8.
The high-pi and physical-power regimes are verified by the same standalone
script. Running with -I -O leaves the checks active because they use explicit
exceptions rather than assert. This is a continuous universal certificate,
not a finite list of ownership instances.
