A 7/4 FUNDING BOUND: SHARPENING OF THE RANGE ARGUMENT
16 October2026. Candidate conventional proof plus exact scalar certificate;
independently checked internally, external specialist/priority review unresolved.
Read mixed/RANGE_ROBUST_ENVELOPE_THEOREM.txt for model, primitive and valid
account-restoration argument. This note replaces only its final budget estimates.
It applies to EVERY owner's payment chosen from ANY opposing-account vector
b'>=p, b'_i<=B_i=p_i+z_i, sum b'<=K=P+CM, with full hardware H_i fixed.
Consequently it bounds both the actual report envelope and the directly
monotone range mechanism defined in RANGE_MECHANISM.txt. C=1+1/(2e).

1. LOW K: A ROLE-AWARE IMPROVEMENT TO 12/7.
Suppose 0<K<=H. Let d_i=min(H_i,K), D=sum d_i, rho=D/K>=1,
q=(1/2)exp(1-rho), and write z_i=z_i^h+z_i^l, Z=sum z_i, Zh=sum z_i^h.
Each local-power total is at most its local production: on the short side
(1-q)M<=M, on the long side (M/2)rho exp(1-rho)<=M/2. Hence
  Z>=M, Z-Zh<=M, Zh>=Z-M.
Also z_i^h<=H_i and z_i^h<=Z<=CM<=K, so z_i^h<=d_i.

At a restored vector with total T<=K, q(T) and d_i(T) are nondecreasing in T.
The payment is (1-q(T))b'_i+q(T)d_i(T). If d_i(T)<B_i it is at most B_i;
otherwise it is at most B_i+q(K)(d_i(K)-B_i). Therefore the universal individual
bound is
  payment_i<=B_i+q(d_i-B_i)_+<=B_i+q(d_i-z_i^h).
Summing gives P+Z+q(D-Zh). For t=Zh/K and rho>=1,
  (rho-t)exp(1-rho)<=exp(-t),
as the function increases until rho=1+t and decreases afterwards. Thus
  total<=P+Z+(K/2)exp(-Zh/K)
       <=P+Z+(K/2)exp(-(Z-M)/K)
       <=K[1+(1/2)exp(-(C-1)M/K)].
The final inequality follows by increasing Z to CM: the derivative in Z of
Z+(K/2)exp(-(Z-M)/K) is at least1/2 for Z>=M.
Here F=P+M, since F<=K<=H. Set t=(C-1)M/F in[0,C-1]. The ratio is at most
  r(t)=(1+t)[1+(1/2)exp(-t/(1+t))].
Its derivative is 1+(1/2)exp(-t/(1+t))*t/(1+t)>0. Since C<6/5 and
exp(-1/6)<=6/7 (from exp(1/6)>=7/6),
  r(t)<=r(1/5)<=(6/5)(1+3/7)=12/7<7/4.
K=0 has zero payments. This corrects the formerly coarse3C/2 bound.

2. HIGH P.
The frozen range proof already establishes total<=7H/4 when P>=H>0, and F=H.
No change is required. It uses only b'>=p, own ceiling B_i, and local-power<=H/2.

3. INTERMEDIATE P<H<K WITH F<H.
Use the frozen expression, with p=P/H,m=M/H,k=p+Cm,f=p+m<1,
q0=(1/2)exp(1-k),q1=(1/2)exp(1-1/p) (q1=0 at p=0):
  total/H <=3/2+p-q1(p+1/2)+q0(Cm-3/2).
C<6/5, k<C<6/5, q0>=2/5, q1>=(p-1/2)_+ imply
  total/H <=9/10+p+(12/25)m-g(p),
where g(p)=(p-1/2)_+(p+1/2).
Because 1<k and C<6/5, f>(5+p)/6. Substitution of m=f-p gives
  total/F <=12/25+6[9/10+(13/25)p-g(p)]/(5+p).
The bracket is positive on[0,1]. The last rational expression increases up to
p=1/2 (derivative numerator17/10 for its fractional term) and decreases after
p=1/2 (derivative numerator29/20-10p-p^2<0). Its maximum is
  96/55<7/4.
This is independent algebra; exact fractions corroborate it.

4. INTERMEDIATE P<H<K WITH F=H: FIVE QUADRATIC INTERVALS.
For fixed p, the frozen upper expression increases in m, since its m derivative
is C q0(5/2-Cm)>0. Replace m by1. It also increases in C, with derivative
q0(5/2-C)>0. Since e>sum_{k=0}^7 1/k!=685/252>125/46, we have C<148/125.
Thus the upper is at most
  W(p)=3/2+p -(p+1/2)exp(1-1/p)/2 -(79/500)exp(-p-23/125),
with exp(1-1/p)=0 at p=0. For p<=1/4, W(p)<=3/2+p<=7/4.
For the remainder use the following elementary continuous bounds.
 - f(p)=exp(1-1/p) has f''=f(p)(1-2p)/p^4. On[1/4,1/2] it is convex,
   and its tangent at1/2 is (4p-1)/e. On[1/2,1] it is concave, so chords
   between interval endpoints are lower bounds.
 - exp(-p-c)>=exp(-u-c)(1+u-p) for each fixed midpoint u and c=23/125.
 - For x>=0, the odd Taylor polynomial sum_{k=0}^{11}(-x)^k/k! is a lower
   bound for exp(-x), by the signed Lagrange remainder. Rounding down to six
   decimal places preserves that lower bound.
The tangent multiplier4p-1 and midpoint multiplier1+u-p are nonnegative
on their specified intervals, so substituting rounded lower values is safe.

Use intervals [1/4,1/2], [1/2,5/8], [5/8,3/4], [3/4,7/8], [7/8,1].
The first exponential has a linear lower bound A p+B in each. The second
has lower bound e_u(1+u-p). Therefore W is bounded by the exact quadratic
  -(A/2)p^2 +[1-B/2-A/4+(79/500)e_u]p
    +3/2-B/4-(79/500)e_u(1+u).
The finite rational certificate in verify_seven_fourths_scalar.py computes
its endpoints and interior vertex, if present. All five maxima are <7/4;
the largest is <1747/1000 (a display simplification, not a numerical proof).
seven_fourths_scalar.json records every rational coefficient, maximizer and gap.
An independently implemented512-interval rational-Taylor certificate in
../mixed/certify_seven_quarters_scalar.py provides a second continuous check.

5. CONCLUSION AND EVIDENCE.
The four exhaustive regimes have bounds12/7,7/4,96/55,and7/4. Hence total<=7F/4
uniformly over all finite numbers of actual mixed owners and branches, at every
nonnegative real report profile. This is not a sharpness claim. The proof uses
fixed actual ownership, not a cloning/merging invariance assumption. Rational
scalar certificates cover complete real intervals; separate finite profile
stress tests are only supplementary and do not establish the theorem alone.
