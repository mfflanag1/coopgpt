16 October 2026. Limited comparison, not confirmation of historical novelty.

Izquierdo and Rafels, The incentive core in co-investment problems,
UB Economics Working Papers2017/369.
Original institutional PDF:
https://diposit.ub.edu/bitstreams/d325b225-7dde-44ff-8a35-cd848434e593/download
The browser fetch failed; an ordinary download of this free institutional
copy succeeded. Root read the model in section2, Proposition1, Theorem1 and
its surrounding discussion. Their model has one scalar contributed input
and a technology with increasing average returns. They examine core
extensions under resource increases and stronger requirements protecting
other participants. The proportional allocation supplies an efficient
resource-monotone core rule on that domain. Our complementary multi-input
capacity model is outside those hypotheses. This is relevant prior art for
resource-monotonic core allocation, not evidence that the present quantitative
subsidy bound is already implied. Full texts are cached outside the campaign
directory and will not be included in the repository checkpoint.

Huang, Mei, Yang and Cao, original arXiv v1,2025:
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1
Root inspected the capacity-reporting model, Example1, the Shapley DSIC
argument and minimal-cut mechanism. This is a close institutional comparison:
binding capacity reports and payment incentives. Its efficient mechanisms
and core incompatibility questions do not by themselves establish our
all-coalition subsidized bound. The later Cao2026 original technical comparison
remains unresolved; do not treat this v1 comparison as completion of that task.

Guardiola, Meca and Puerto, production-inventory/Owen-point original:
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04328
Root read the PMAS definition and Theorems4.1,5.1,5.2. Population-monotonic
cost allocations under coalition expansion differ from own-capacity monotonicity
for a fixed owner set. The posting date must not be mistaken for the date of
the underlying research. This comparison supports a scope distinction only.

Solymosi, assignment-game population monotonicity,2023:
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00355-023-01477-z
Root read the relevant composition proposition and surrounding definitions.
Composition of population-monotonic schemes on independent player components
does not automatically address shared resources, report changes or fixed mixed
owners. Standard composition and core facts should be credited as ingredients.
