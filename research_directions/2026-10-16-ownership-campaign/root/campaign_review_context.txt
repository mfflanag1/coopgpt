MODEL AND PREVIOUSLY AUDITED DEPENDENCIES FOR TARGETED REVIEWS
16 October2026. Root clarification; not a new theorem.

Fixed actual owners report nonnegative capacities (h_ij,l_ij,p_i). Feasible
unilateral deviations reduce any or all of that SAME owner's coordinates.
Reports are binding; utility is its nonnegative payment. No identity changes,
private costs, withdrawals of other owners, or overreports are allowed.
H_i=sum_j h_ij,H=sum_i H_i,M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j),P=sum_i p_i,
physical value F=min(H,P+M). Branch coalition value is ALWAYS the minimum of
RAW coalition pool totals, not a minimum of individual capped quantities.

The original two-pool exponential primitive: orient the short total T<=long
total; short owner amount x_i, capped long amount d_i=min(y_i,T), D=sum_i d_i,
rho=D/T,q=exp(1-rho)/2; payment Phi_i=(1-q)x_i+q d_i. At T=0 all payments0.
At a pool tie both orientations give (x_i+y_i)/2, so the formula is continuous.
Each payment lies in[0,T], total lies in[T,C T],C=1+1/(2e).
Coalition coverage follows because both sum_A x_i and sum_A min(y_i,T) are
at least min(sum_A x_i,sum_A y_i). Their convex combination is too.

OWN MONOTONICITY: with short total held fixed, increasing own long uncapped
y_i has derivative q[1-(d_i-x_i)/T]>=0, then derivative0 above its cap.
Increasing own short x_i raises T. On a smooth cap cell, D' is the number of
active long caps and q'=q(rho-D')/T>=0. If d_i>=x_i, all derivative terms in
1-q+q d_i'+q'(d_i-x_i) are nonnegative. If d_i<x_i then its long cap is inactive,
d_i'=0, and derivative>=1-q-q rho>=0, since q(1+rho)<=1 forrho>=1.
By continuity across cap boundaries and the pool tie this proves both own
coordinate monotonicities. It also justifies restoring only OUTER own hardware
and grid after a local counterfactual has been computed. Such restoration is
an upper comparison, never a claim that the changed local profile is jointly
attainable with restored hardware.

At each local branch apply Phi, sum to z_i. Set raw nested payment
N_i=Phi_i((H_k)_k,(p_k+z_k)_k). The actual envelope E_i takes the supremum of
N_i over the same actual owner's entire feasible report box, holding all other
owners fixed. E_i>=N_i gives coverage; nested report boxes give truthfulness.
Independently optimizing each recipient does not require simultaneous
attainment. In hardware normalization H=1, let d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1),S=sum d_i,
a_i=H_i. Under physical hardware bottleneck S>=1.
