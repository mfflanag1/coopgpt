1. EXACT SUPPORT FUNCTIONS FOR THE TWO ROLE TOTALS
Consider one branch with hardware totalh, local-power totall, outputm=min(h,l),
original exponential primitive totalZ, and its local-power role totalL.
These are role components of actual-owner payments; no owner is split
strategically. For constantsB>=q>0 defineS=(B-q)L+qZ.
Then both bounds hold simultaneously:
  S<=h [q+(B/2)exp(-q/B)],                                (1)
  S<=m [B+(q/2)exp(-B/q)].                               (2)
They sum over arbitrary branches with the SAME coefficientsB,q.

Proof. Zero-output branches contribute zero. Let rho be the capped long-pool
ratio and w=exp(1-rho)/2, so rho>=1. If hardware is short thenh=m,
L=w rho m andZ=m[1+w(rho-1)]. Hence
  S/m=q+w(B rho-q).
Its maximum over rho>=1 occurs at rho=1+q/B and equals
S0=q+(B/2)exp(-q/B), proving(1) in this case.
If local power is short, letd=h/m>=rho. Then
  S/m=B+w(q rho-B).
The nonnegative numerator divided by d is at most its value divided by rho.
The derivative of that latter expression is
  B[w(rho+1)-1]/rho^2-qw<=0,
because w(rho+1)<=1. ThusS/h<=(B+q)/2<=S0. This proves(1).

For(2), the local-power-short expression is maximized at rho=1+B/q,
where it equalsS1=B+(q/2)exp(-B/q). The hardware-short boundS0 is no larger
thanS1. Indeed withx=B/q>=1 their difference divided byq is
  x-1+exp(-x)/2-x exp(-1/x)/2.
It is zero atx1; its derivative is at least(1-exp(-1))/2>0, since
(1+1/x)exp(-1/x)<=1. This proves(2). The extrema are scalar support-function
bounds, not a claim that both can be simultaneously attained on one profile.

2. RETAIN THE TRADEOFF IN THE INTERMEDIATE RANGE REGIME
Use the range mechanismR fromRANGE_MECHANISM.txt and normalize grand hardware
H=1. Assume0<=p=P/H<1<k=p+Cm, where m=M/H<=1 andC=1+1/(2e).
Putq0=exp(1-k)/2, q1=exp(1-1/p)/2 (q1=0 atp0), andB=1-q1.
ThenB>=1/2>=q0>0. LetL andZ denote normalized aggregate local-power payments
and total local payments, respectively.

The same individual short/long range inequalities used by the existing
7/4 proof imply, before replacing the two local totals independently,
  sum_i R_i <=1+Bp-q0+(B-q0)L+q0Z.                       (3)
For clarity, classify recipients by the sign ofB_i-H_i. A positive-gap
recipient has excess at mostB(B_i-H_i); a negative-gap recipient has deficit
at leastq0(H_i-B_i). The positive gap sum is at mostp+L, since the hardware
role payment is at mostH_i. The difference of positive and negative gap sums
is exactlyp+Z-1. BecauseB-q0>=0, these statements give(3). All bounds are
uniform before each recipient chooses its separate auxiliary vector.

IfF=H, applying(1) to(3) cancelsq0 and gives
  sumR_i <=1+B[p+exp(-q0/B)/2].                          (4)
For fixedp, the right side increases withm becauseq0 decreases withm.
Thus setm1. ReplacingC by148/125 only decreasesq0 and raises this upper.
The exact rational interval certificateverify_joint_role_scalar.py proves

The numerical maximum about1.72092 merely explains the available margin;

IfF<H, writef=p+m<1. Applying(2) instead gives
  sumR_i/F <= B+(1-q0)/f +(m/f)(q0/2)exp(-B/q0).          (5)
Sincek=p+Cm<=Cf andk>1, we havef>1/C and
  (1-q0)/f <=[1-exp(1-Cf)/2]/f <=C/2.
The last step follows by settingx=Cf>=1: the function
(1-exp(1-x)/2)/x is nonincreasing, because its derivative has numerator
exp(1-x)(x+1)/2-1<=0. AlsoB<=1,m/f<=1,q0<=1/2 andB/q0>=1.
Thus(5) is at most1+C/2+1/(4e)=3/2+1/(2e)<17/10.
