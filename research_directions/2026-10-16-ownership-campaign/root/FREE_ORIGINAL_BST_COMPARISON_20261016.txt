Section2,pp79-80 restricts each player to one unit of exactly one resource type;
type classes partition owners. Efficiency divides min_s n_s with no outside
funding. Section4 Definition1,p85 compares subcoalitions after one type member
leaves: remaining same-type owners gain, other types lose. Theorem5,pp86-87
constructs such schemes; Theorem6,p87 extends core allocations. Section5,
pp87-88 interprets this as a special production/flow model with one unit arc
per owner.

Bounded distinction: these are population/type-departure core schemes. They do
not establish our fixed-owner arbitrary-real mixed-capacity underreport incentive
property, or a simultaneous coalition-coverage/subsidy-factor theorem. The
production function overlaps in the unit, single-input-owner subcase. This
comparison resolves BST only; it does not establish novelty against other work.
