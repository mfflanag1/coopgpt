WHY THE IMPROVED WATER-PRODUCT PRIMITIVES DO NOT INHERIT THE OLD ROLE BOUND
16 October2026. Exact growing-family obstruction to a particular composition
proof. No impossibility claim for other mechanisms or actual nested funding.

For every fixed eta<1, the half-balanced water-product primitive, its quadratic
tilts, and the rational tilt all have a local branch whose total payment to
the local-power role exceeds eta times the branch's total hardware capacity.
Thus the old exponential bound L<=H/2 cannot be replaced by ANY universal
L<=eta H with eta<1 for these new primitives.

Fix integer k>=2 and n=k^2. Use n pure local-power owners, each with capacity
1/n, and n distinct pure hardware owners, each with capacity(1+1/k)/n.
All2n actual identities remain fixed during report comparisons. The local
power total is A=1, hardware total is H=1+1/k, and every hardware account is
below A. The short maximum M=1/n and the long maximum share t=1/n; s=1/n too.
Consequently all three tilt functions vanish here and h(s,t)=1/2.

The water equation is solved by lambda=1/n: the auxiliary cap contributes1/n
and the n hardware caps together contribute1. Therefore the water product is
  P=(1+1/k)^(-n),
and the total payment to the short local-power role is
  L=1-P/2.
The ratio is exactly
  L/H=[1-(1/2)(1+1/k)^(-k^2)]/(1+1/k).
The binomial theorem gives(1+1/k)^k>=2, so P<=2^(-k), and
  L/H >=[1-2^(-k-1)]/(1+1/k) ->1.
The universal elementary upper L<=H remains valid because L<=A<=H, so1
is the sharp uniform constant for this role inequality.

This is an unbounded sequence with both role populations growing. Its force
is a proved failure of the entire eta<1 role-bound approach, not another
isolated numerical case. The actual coalition game still has a good direct
truthful primitive. The obstruction explains why the improved two-input
funding factor cannot simply be inserted into the existing depth-two range
proof, whose high-P and intermediate-H estimates use L<=H/2 and its support
refinements. A different response/role argument or a different composition
would be needed; no funding lower for that composition is proved here.
