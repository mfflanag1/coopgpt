1. STATUS AND MATHEMATICAL SCOPE
For the original symmetric exponential nested rule's ACTUAL whole-owner
underreport envelope, arbitrary finite owner/branch counts, fixed permissions,
and arbitrary nonnegative mixed reports, this note proves the following
additional subregime result:

 If physical production is hardware-limited, H>0, and
 S=sum_i min(p_i+z_i,H)/H >=2, then sum_i E_i <(8/5)H.   (HS)

Here z_i denotes the full-profile local payment and each E_i is the exact
full-domain own-report envelope, not an independently optimized account box.
The proof uses the previously proved high-effective-grid bound if needed;
otherwise it uses the exact certificate below. No global C^2 assertion for
S>=2 is assumed. That previously repeated assertion was unsupported and is
explicitly withdrawn in ../positive_frontier/S2_SCOPE_CORRECTION.txt.

For 1<=S<2, a finite family of valid supporting inequalities, including the
new one-cap and inverse-deficit supports, still admits a rational moment
point with relaxed funding objective >8/5. This is a precise reason to stop
refining that particular linear relaxation. It is NOT an actual game,
an actual-envelope lower bound, an impossibility theorem for general 8/5,
or an obstruction to every possible support parameter or other proof method.
