A SHARPER DEFICIT-DEPENDENT INDIVIDUAL POSITIVE-GAP CEILING
17 October 2026. Original symmetric exponential local rule; fixed actual owners.
This is a structural full-profile inequality, not a new funding factor.

Normalize total hardware H=1. Put c=1/(2e), C=1+c, alpha=1-c,
M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), Z=sum_i z_i, delta=CM-Z. For a positive outer-gap
owner i, let a_i be its hardware, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), g_i=d_i-a_i>0.
For d in[0,c], define Q(d) as the UNIQUE q in[c,1/2] satisfying

                    c+q log(2q)=d.                    (1)

THEOREM. For every actual owner i and every full profile with M>0,

 z_i-alpha a_i <=1-M+M Q(delta/M).                     (2)

Consequently every positive-gap owner satisfies

 z_i+alpha g_i <=1+alpha-M+M Q(delta/M).                (3)

The quantities are well-defined because 0<=delta<=cM. At M=0, z_i=0 and
the limiting convention M Q(delta/M)=0 makes the same inequalities valid.
Counts, ownership overlaps, zero branches, grid overflows, and local branch
orientation are unrestricted. No owner report is optimized or restored here.

1. BRANCHWISE ROLE BOUND
On a productive branch let T=min(H_j,L_j), rho=sum capped long inputs/T,
q=exp(1-rho)/2, and alpha=1-c. Let h_i be i's actual branch hardware.
We claim

 z_ij-alpha h_i <=max(c,q)T+(H_j-T).                   (4)

If hardware is short, H_j=T and z_ij=(1-q)h_i+q ell_i, with
0<=h_i,ell_i<=T. Thus z_ij-alpha h_i=(c-q)h_i+q ell_i.
For q<=c this is at most cT, and for q>=c it is at most qT, proving (4).

If local resource is short, its own short report is at most T and its hardware
cap is at most h_i. Since q<=1/2<alpha,
 z_ij-alpha h_i <=(1-q)T+(q-alpha)h_i<=(1-q)T.
Also H_j/T>=rho. For 1<=rho<=2, q>=c and
 1-q-max(c,q)=1-exp(1-rho)<=rho-1.
For rho>=2, q<=c and 1-q-c<=1<=rho-1.
Both cases prove (4). A zero-output branch has z_ij=0 and satisfies (4)
with T=0 directly. Ties belong to either valid branch argument.

2. EXACT CONVEX DEFICIT AND JENSEN'S INEQUALITY
Write f(q)=c+q log(2q), with f(0)=c by continuity. For every productive
branch its exact local budget deficit is
 delta_j=CT-Z_j=T f(q),
because Z_j=T+q(rho-1)T and log(2q)=1-rho. The function f is convex,
has its unique minimum zero at q=c, and increases strictly on[c,1/2].
Let qbar_j=max(c,q_j). Then f(qbar_j)<=f(q_j). Weighted Jensen therefore gives

 f((sum_j T_j qbar_j)/M)
 <=(sum_j T_j f(qbar_j))/M<=delta/M.

The mean on the left belongs to[c,1/2]. Applying the increasing inverse Q
shows sum_j T_j qbar_j<=M Q(delta/M). Sum (4), use sum H_j=1 and sum T_j=M,
and obtain (2). Since g_i<=1-a_i, adding alpha a_i+alpha g_i<=alpha proves (3).

3. EQUIVALENT EXPLICIT SUPPORT FAMILY
For any t in(c,1/2], put s=1+log(2t)>0. The tangent inequality
 f(qbar)>=c-t+s qbar yields

 z_i+alpha g_i
 <=1+alpha-M+[delta+M(t-c)]/s.                        (5)

Taking the infimum over t gives (3), with endpoint limits at delta=0.
Thus the new bound can enter a rational certificate through directed
exponential/logarithm enclosures, without solving an inverse equation exactly.
Equation (3) can also be intersected with earlier valid bounds and z_i<=M,
g_i<=1-a_i, z_i<=a_i+g_i. No aggregation or merger of actual owners is implicit.

4. SHARPNESS ON THE FULL-UTILIZATION FACE M=H
For any q in[c,1/2], set rho=1-log(2q) in[1,2]. Use any number k of productive
branches with hardware T_j>0 summing to 1, all held by pure hardware owners.
One distinguished owner G holds local input T_j on every branch, no hardware,
and grid 1-q. If rho>1, one other actual local owner holds (rho-1)T_j on each
branch; at rho=1 that unused owner may be omitted. Hardware fragmentation and
branch splitting are arbitrary and can make the active count unbounded.

Every branch has coefficient q, M=H=1, z_G=q, a_G=0 and d_G=1, so g_G=1.
The total deficit is delta=f(q), and (3) holds with equality:
 z_G+alpha g_G=q+alpha=alpha+Q(delta).
Thus the bound is sharp on M=H for each deficit in[0,c], even though the
resulting instances need not maximize envelope funding. It concerns the
coefficient alpha=1-c; no claim is made that (3) is the best possible bound
after retaining more actual-owner moments.

5. CURRENT FUNDING CONSEQUENCE
This theorem supplies a proved owner-specific gap constraint beyond the
older square-root ceiling 2-M+K sqrt(cM delta), K=e-1. Intersect both bounds
when using them; neither comparison outside M=H is needed for correctness.
At the current diagnostic point M=1, delta=0.02627710294, the new right side
is about 1.10674, below the previous roughly 1.11946. That decimal is only an
illustration. A new universal funding constant still requires its own
all-profile certificate; the already frozen 323/200 theorem is unchanged.
