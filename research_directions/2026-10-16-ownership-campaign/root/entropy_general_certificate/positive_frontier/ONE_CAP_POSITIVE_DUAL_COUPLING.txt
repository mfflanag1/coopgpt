ONE CAPPED OWNER COUPLES THE POSITIVE DUAL TO AN ACTUAL PAYMENT CEILING
17 October 2026. Original actual whole-owner envelope; independent review pending.

Normalize H=1 and assume the full profile is a physical hardware bottleneck.
Use actual full values a_i,z_i,d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), S=sum d_i, and positive set
I={i:d_i>a_i}. Let G=sum_I(d_i-a_i), Zplus=sum_I z_i, and
Y=sum_I(E_i-a_i). Assume S<2. Fix any u in(0,1) and the positive-dual parameters

 q=exp(1-1/u)/2, A=1-q(1+1/u), eta=q/S,
 lambda=A+eta, p=(e-1)eta, mu=(e-1)^2 eta^2/(4A).

Suppose alpha>0 and B>=0 are valid full-profile constants such that every
positive capped owner satisfies z_i+alpha(d_i-a_i)<=B. Then, if mu<p,

 Y <=lambda G+mu Zplus
      +B (p-mu)(1-lambda)/(1-lambda+alpha p).            (OC1)

If mu>=p, the usual bound Y<=lambda G+p Zplus is available without any
correction. Both statements hold with Zplus replaced by the larger Z-zminus.
They remain upper bounds when the optimizing owner deviations cross cap
patterns, change local orientation, or change the outer bottleneck.

1. THE ACTUAL CAP COUNT AND PER-OWNER CEILING
Since S<2, at most ONE actual owner can have d_i=1. Every other positive
recipient has d_i<1 and therefore zero full effective overflow. The accepted
PD1 bound gives for those uncapped recipients
 E_i-a_i<=lambda g_i+min(p,mu)z_i, g_i=d_i-a_i.

For the possible capped positive owner j, PD1 always gives
 E_j-a_j<=lambda g_j+p z_j.
Independently, EVERY raw primitive payment is at most its short total. At any
whole-bundle underreport, the outer short total is at most the reported total
hardware, itself at most full H=1. Taking a supremum therefore yields E_j<=1.
Since d_j=1, this says E_j-a_j<=1-a_j=g_j. This concerns the actual envelope,
not an account-restoration comparison or a restricted report class.

For mu<p, combine the two individual bounds for j and sum the uncapped
recipients. This gives the exact intermediate bound

 Y<=lambda G+mu Zplus
       +min{(p-mu)z_j, (1-lambda)g_j-mu z_j}.           (OC2)

If there is no capped positive recipient, no correction is needed; the positive
correction in (OC1) remains safe. A capped tied owner cannot create a positive
overflow term. Nonpositive owners are handled by the separately proved saving
bounds and do not enter Y.

2. SHARP SCALAR MAXIMIZATION OF THE CORRECTION
One has 0<lambda<1, because
lambda=1-q(1+1/u-1/S) and S>=1. Write a=p-mu>0 and b=1-lambda>0.
For z,g>=0 satisfying z+alpha g<=B,

 min{a z,b g-mu z}
 <=min{a z,bB/alpha-(b/alpha+mu)z}.

The first line increases in z and the second decreases. Their intersection
is z*=bB/(b+alpha p), with g*=pB/(b+alpha p); it is feasible because
z*+alpha g*=B. The maximum equals
                      a b B/(b+alpha p).
This proves (OC1). The optimization is exact for this two-variable relaxation;
it does not claim the maximizing pair is attainable by the original mechanism.
For mu>=p use the first PD1 alternative for all positive recipients directly.

In the moment certificate G=S-1+v and Zplus<=Z-zminus. Thus (OC1) becomes

 Y-lambda v+mu zminus
 <=lambda(S-1)+mu Z+B(p-mu)(1-lambda)/(1-lambda+alpha p). (OC3)

All coefficients in its correction are nonnegative. A later rational interval

can cover that complementary regime in a separate exact funding certificate.
There is no established S>=2 C^2 theorem used by this note.

4. CONSEQUENCE AND LIMITATION
Unlike the older aggregate overflow bound Wplus<=Pi+Z-S, (OC2) forces the
entire positive overflow correction onto at most one actual owner and retains
that owner's true payment ceiling. It therefore prevents assigning that
overflow freely across positive recipients. Fixed actual identities and all
their feasible reports are preserved. This is a new necessary inequality for
the SAME mechanism's envelope, not a modified mechanism or a merger argument.
No improved universal funding factor beyond the frozen 323/200 is claimed in
this note without a separate exact certificate.
