GENERAL 323/200 FUNDING FOR THE ORIGINAL ACTUAL ENVELOPE
17 October 2026. Exact certificate candidate for independent review.
No new mechanism, universal lower bound, or novelty assertion is made.

THEOREM
For arbitrary finite actual-owner and branch counts, nonnegative mixed
hardware/local/grid reports, and fixed permissions throughout every report
domain, the ORIGINAL symmetric exponential nested rule's actual own-report
envelope E satisfies

                       sum_i E_i <= (323/200) F,         (1)

where F=min(H,P+M), H is total hardware, P is grid total, and
M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j). The same mechanism already has all-profile whole-owner
incentives and all-coalition coverage. The result strengthens its earlier
13/8 funding theorem. Exact envelope evaluation and optimality over all
mechanisms are separate questions.

1. EXHAUSTIVE PHYSICAL AND GRID REGIMES
The zero-production profile has zero envelope payments. For 0<P+M<=H the
accepted physical power-bottleneck theorem gives sum E_i<=C^2 F, with
c=1/(2e), C=1+c, and C^2<323/200.

Otherwise H=F>0; normalize H=1. Put pi_i=min(p_i,1), pi=sum pi_i and
Pi=11/8. For pi>=Pi, the accepted GRID_CAP_RANGE_BOUND gives

 sum E_i<=1+(1/2)exp(1-pi)[pi+(1/2)exp(-exp(-CM))].      (2)

The right side increases in M<=1 and decreases in pi>=1. Its value at
M=1,pi=11/8 is strictly below 8/5, and hence below 323/200. The standalone
verifier proves the required comparisons using exact rational exponential
enclosures, rather than rounded decimals. It remains to treat pi<=Pi.
Raw grid total P remains arbitrary in that regime.

2. FULL-PROFILE VARIABLES AND A COMMON POSITIVE-EXCESS VARIABLE
Write a_i=H_i, z_i for full local payments, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1),
Z=sum z_i, S=sum d_i, delta=CM-Z. Let I={i:d_i>a_i}, J={i:d_i<a_i},
v=sum_J(a_i-d_i), zminus=sum_J z_i, and

                        Y=sum_{i in I}(E_i-a_i)>=0.     (3)

All E_i in (3) are the actual full-domain envelopes of the SAME full profile.
The truthful report is feasible, so on I one has
E_i>=N_i=a_i+q0(d_i-a_i)>a_i, where q0=exp(1-S)/2>0; this proves Y>=0.
The reviewed negative-gap bound gives

 sum_i E_i <=1+Y-b v,  b=(2/5)exp(1-S-delta).            (4)

Indeed tied recipients have E_i<=a_i, and the exact negative-gap correction
is at least 4/5: K=e-1, z_i(a_i-d_i)<=1/4, K^2<3, and
L(3/4)=4/(3sqrt(e))>4/5. No independent attainment of owner deviations is
assumed by (4).

For EVERY u in(0,1), define
 q=exp(1-1/u)/2, A=1-q(1+1/u)>0,
 eta=q/S, lambda=A+eta, mu=K^2 eta^2/(4A), p=K eta.
The accepted positive dual and effective-overflow identity imply BOTH

 Y<=lambda(S-1+v)+p(Z-zminus),
 Y<=lambda(S-1+v)+mu(Z-zminus)+A(Pi+Z-S).               (5)

Thus all selected u rows constrain one common Y simultaneously. This is
stronger than selecting one u independently for each final scalar bound.
It is valid because each inequality directly upper-bounds the same actual
quantity (3). The substitutions Z_I<=Z-zminus and
W_I^effective<=pi+Z-S<=Pi+Z-S only enlarge nonnegative terms.

The earlier all-owner moment constraints are
 M<=Z<=CM, 0<=M<=1, 1<=S<=Pi+Z,
 -M+Z+v<=1, Z+v<=3/2, v+zminus<=1,
 (4/3)M+v-zminus<=4/3, 0<=zminus<=Z.                   (6)
They remain valid with arbitrary raw grid overflow.

3. ADDITIONAL PROVED ROLE AND ENTROPY CONSTRAINTS
For each t in[0,1), the established continuum hardware/local-role bounds give

 Z+(1-t)v<=1+exp(-t)/2,
 Z+(1-t)v<=(1-t)+M[1+(t/2)exp(-1/t)].                  (7)

At t=0 the second exponential contribution is zero. The subset hardware
deficit theorem gives
 2M+(1-c)v-c zminus<=2+K sqrt(c M delta).
For any alpha>0, Young's inequality sqrt(M delta)<=
(alpha M+delta/alpha)/2 supplies a valid linear upper relaxation.

The new subset entropy theorem states, for s in[0,1], t=exp(s-1)/2,
A=s+c-t and D=A+1/2,

          D M+A v+(c-t)zminus<=delta+D.                (8)

The independently proved deficit-weight-four endpoint additionally gives

 (65/64)M+(1/2+c)v+(c-1/2)zminus<=4delta+65/64.         (9)

These are necessary conditions on the full actual negative-owner subset.
They do not restrict hypothetical owner deviations or require actual joint
attainment of unrelated account extrema. This certificate uses only the
weight-four endpoint, not the newer parametric-weight extension.

4. RATIONAL OUTER RELAXATIONS ON A CONTINUOUS PARTITION
Set
 e_L=sum_{j=0}^8 1/j!, e_U=e_L+(1/9!)(10/9),
 c_L=1/(2e_U), c_U=1/(2e_L), C_L=1+c_L, C_U=1+c_U,
 K_U=e_U-1, Cbar=148/125, deltabar=23/125.
The exact inequalities e_L<e<e_U, C_U<Cbar, c_U<deltabar hold. Also
sqrt(c)<429/1000. For x>=0 let T_n(x)=sum_{j=0}^n(-x)^j/j!.
Signed Taylor remainders give T_odd(x)<=exp(-x)<=T_even(x).

Partition S in[1,Pi+Cbar] into 512 equal closed intervals [lo,hi] and
delta in[0,deltabar] into 64 equal closed intervals [dl,dh]. Their union
contains every low-pi full profile. All endpoints are rational. A cell with
lo>Pi+C_U-dl is empty, since S<=Pi+CM-delta<=Pi+C_U-dl.

The certificate's nonnegative vector is x=(M,Z,v,zminus,Y). Its first 12
rows, in order, are
 M-Z<=0; -C_U M+Z<=0; -Z<=Pi-lo;
 -M+Z+v<=1; Z+v<=3/2; v+zminus<=1;
 (4/3)M+v-zminus<=4/3; -Z+zminus<=0; M<=1;
 C_L M-Z<=dh; -C_U M+Z<=-dl; Y<=3.                    (10)

The cap Y<=3 is independently justified before using it in a certificate:
take u=1/2 in the first row of (5), use S-1+v<=S<=Pi+Cbar and Z-zminus<=Cbar.
With qlo=T_25(1)/2, qhi=T_24(1)/2, this gives
 Y<=(1-2qlo)(Pi+Cbar)+K_U qhi Cbar<3.
The final comparison is checked exactly. Thus no circular budget premise is
introduced by the Y cap. All vector coordinates have the valid upper bounds
                         (1,Cbar,1,Cbar,3).             (11)

The following additional rows are appended in the listed order:

* Twenty role rows from (7), t=0,1/10,...,9/10. Replace exp(-t) by T_24(t)
  and exp(-1/t) by T_48(1/t), which enlarge their right sides. At t=0 use zero
  for the coefficient t exp(-1/t).

* Eight Young rows, alpha in {1/16,1/8,1/4,1/2,3/4,1,3/2,2}:
  [2-r alpha]M+(1-c_U)v-c_U zminus<=2+r dh/alpha,
  where r=K_U(429/1000)/2. Lowering the left side via c_U and enlarging the
  right side via K_U, sqrt(c), and dh are all safe.

* Seventeen entropy rows, s=0,1/16,...,1. Let
  tlo=T_25(1-s)/2, thi=T_24(1-s)/2,
  Alo=s+c_L-thi, Ahi=s+c_U-tlo, Dlo=Alo+1/2, Dhi=Ahi+1/2.
  The rational row is
    Dlo M+Alo v+(c_L-thi)zminus<=dh+Dhi.                (12)
  Every variable multiplying an enclosed coefficient is nonnegative. Replacing
  left coefficients by lower bounds remains valid even if an endpoint Alo is
  slightly negative. The right side uses upper bounds.

* One weight-four row from (9), with c replaced by c_L on the left and delta
  by dh on the right. Both c coefficients multiply nonnegative variables.

* Sixty-four positive-excess rows, two for each
  u in {35/100,37/100,...,95/100} union {1/2}. Define
  qlo=T_25(1/u-1)/2, qhi=T_24(1/u-1)/2,
  Alo=1-qhi(1+1/u)>0, Ahi=1-qlo(1+1/u),
  lambdahi=1-qlo(1+1/u-1/lo), etahi=qhi/lo,
  p_hi=K_U etahi, mu_hi=K_U^2 etahi^2/(4Alo),
  phi_hi=(hi-1)[1-qlo(1+1/u-1/hi)].
  Then append
    Y-lambdahi v-p_hi Z+p_hi zminus<=phi_hi,
    Y-lambdahi v-(mu_hi+Ahi)Z+mu_hi zminus
          <=phi_hi+Ahi(Pi-lo).                        (13)
  To justify the sharper intercept, lambda(S)(S-1)=A(S-1)+q(1-1/S)
  increases in S; its coefficient of q in the equivalent formula is negative.
  Hence hi and qlo give phi_hi. The v coefficient decreases with S, so lo is
  used there. Other replaced factors multiply nonnegative Z-zminus and
  Pi+Z-S. A possibly negative Pi-lo after expansion creates no sign problem:
  the replacement A<=Ahi is made BEFORE expanding the nonnegative product.

There are exactly 122 rows. Finally b_lo=(2/5)T_25(hi-1+dh)>0 is a lower
bound for b in (4), so the funding objective is at most
                         1+Y-b_lo v.                  (14)

5. SPARSE RATIONAL WEAK DUALITY, INDEPENDENTLY REPLAYED
For a nonempty cell write its rational rows as R x<=h and objective
o=(0,0,-b_lo,0,1). The artifact supplies sparse nonnegative rational weights y.
Define residual_j=max(0,o_j-(R^T y)_j). Nonnegative x and caps (11) prove

 1+o*x <=1+y*h+sum_j residual_j cap_j.                  (15)

Every saved upper bound in (15) is strictly below 323/200. This artifact has
30,896 such certificates and 1,872 geometrically empty cells. The format also
allows a separate nonnegative dual combination with zero objective to certify
emptiness: y*h+sum_j max(0,-(R^T y)_j)cap_j<0, contradicting 0<=that bound.
No such additional empty-cell certificates were needed in this run.

The builder's numerical optimizer only proposes weights. Its success status,
optimality, and floating values do not establish (1). The standalone
verify_certificate.py imports neither the builder nor an optimizer. It checks
all 32,768 cell positions, every supporting parameter, constant sign, dual
weight, row, residual, strict target, empty-cell contradiction, and the two
analytic external regimes using fractions.Fraction. Explicit exceptions keep
every check active under python -O. The saved largest low-pi upper is
approximately 1.6141096740946201; its full rational value is in certificate.json.

This is a finite proof covering a continuous moment region with arbitrary
counts and all permitted actual-owner reports. It is not verification only
on a finite capacity grid. The exact supporting lemmas, rather than a claim
that each relaxed moment vector is physically attainable, justify coverage.
