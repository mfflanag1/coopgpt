A STRUCTURAL LIMIT OF CONTINUOUS CONCENTRATION-ONLY WATER-FILL PRICES
16 October2026. Root candidate for independent audit. No historical-priority claim.

CLASS AND CLAIM
Fix the water level and product P from WATERFILL_PRODUCT_THEOREM.txt. At a
positive profile orient the smaller pool as x, with total A, let M=max x_i,
d_i=min(z_i,A), D=sum d_i, L=max d_i, s=M/A and t=L/D. Consider ANY rule
  p=h(s,t)P, y_i=(1-p)x_i+p d_i,
where h is continuous on(0,1]^2 and the resulting payments are continuous
across aggregate pool ties. No differentiability, quadratic form, monotonicity
of h, or fixed balanced half-sharing is assumed. Require nonnegative payments
and own-report monotonicity for every fixed pure-owner domain, hence also if
the claimed theorem is for all mixed-owner domains.

Such a rule has worst funding ratio at least1097/972=1+125/972>9/8.
Thus a continuous concentration-only prefactor of this water product cannot
attain the unrestricted9/8 lower. Improving beyond this obstruction requires
changing the water product, adding state beyond(s,t), using owner-specific
coefficients, or leaving the stated continuity class. This is an architecture
obstruction, not an unrestricted mechanism lower bound.

1. BALANCED ORIENTATION IDENTITY
At A=B, D=A and P=1. For pure owners, the payment to an owner of the first
resource, approached from the side on which that resource is scarce, tends
to(1-h(s,t))a_i. Approached from the other side it tends to h(t,s)a_i.
Continuity of the allocation across the tie therefore forces
  h(s,t)+h(t,s)=1.                                      (1)
Any pair s,t in(0,1] is realized by finite positive short/long accounts with
those maxima and equal totals; owner counts are unrestricted. In particular
h(1/2,1/2)=1/2. One can equivalently impose(1) directly as orientation
consistency at balance. Continuity across balance is a material assumption.

2. OWN-REPORT INCENTIVES FORCE A FLAT INTERVAL
Fix FOUR actual pure owners: two short accounts of1/2 each and two long
accounts u,v with1/2<v<u<1. The short total is1, s=1/2, and the water level
is exactly1/2 throughout this open rectangle. Therefore
  P=1/(4uv), t=u/(u+v),
  y_u=h(1/2,t)/(4v), y_v=h(1/2,t)/(4u).                  (2)
Increasing only u increases t; monotonicity of y_u requires h(1/2,t) to be
locally nondecreasing in t. Increasing only v decreases t; monotonicity of
y_v requires the same function to be locally nonincreasing in t. Every
point t in(1/2,2/3) admits such a rectangle: choose
  t/[2(1-t)]<u<1, v=u(1-t)/t.
Both owner comparisons can realize every sufficiently small change of t
while retaining1/2<v<u<1. Thus h(1/2,t) is locally constant at every point
of this connected interval, and hence constant on the interval. This uses
finite own-report comparisons, not derivative or symmetry assumptions.
Continuity at its endpoints and(1) give
  h(1/2,t)=1/2 for1/2<=t<=2/3,
  h(t,1/2)=1/2 for1/2<=t<=2/3.                          (3)
The owners were never cloned, merged, added or removed along any comparison.

3. EXACT FUNDING OBSTRUCTION
Use the same four pure owners with short accounts(2/3,1/3) and long accounts
(1,1). Now s=2/3,t=1/2, and(3) forces h=1/2. The water equation is
  min(2/3,lambda)+2min(1,lambda)=5/3,
so lambda=5/9. Its product is
  P=(lambda/(2/3))*lambda^2=125/486,
and p=125/972. The total is A+p(D-A)=1+125/972=1097/972.
The gap above9/8 is exactly7/1944. This proof does not assert that the
four-owner institution itself needs this funding from a different rule.

4. RELATION TO THE CURRENT CONSTRUCTIONS
The accepted31/27 rule has h=1/2. The new quadratic balanced tilt has
h=1/2+a[(t-2/3)_+^2-(s-2/3)_+^2] and satisfies(1); it is subject to this
obstruction for every parameter, independently of its separate incentive
ceiling. This result covers arbitrary continuous antisymmetric concentration
prefactors, not merely that one-parameter family.

The exact active-long payment cancellation in(2) explains the limitation.
An owner-sensitive prefactor cannot vary freely with long concentration
where two actual long owners are water-active. The implication is global
through balanced orientation consistency. It does not imply that all
common-coefficient mechanisms or all truthful mechanisms face this bound.
