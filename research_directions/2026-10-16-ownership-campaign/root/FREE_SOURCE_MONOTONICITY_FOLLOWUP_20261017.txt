Di Luca, Izquierdo and Rafels(2013), Remarks on the proportional distribution
in increasing return to scale problems, Economics Bulletin33(4),2938--2947.
Original publisher PDF read, especially model Section2 and Theorem1/axiomsSection3:
https://www.accessecon.com/Pubs/EB/2013/Volume33/EB-13-V33-I4-P274.pdf
The single-input model assumes nondecreasing average production. Its proportional
allocation is efficient and in the core; Theorem1 characterizes it using core
selection, core invariance, and resource monotonicity. The latter requires that
increasing one contribution benefits that contributor without hurting others.
Thus compatibility of core allocations and incentives to contribute is already
established on suitable domains. Our proposed contribution cannot be that broad
idea. The relevant distinction is complementary multidimensional resources,
arbitrary fixed mixed ownership, and uniform external-funding guarantees for
all-profile own-report envelopes. This is a model comparison, not a priority proof.
