PURE HARDWARE AND LOCAL-ENERGY BUNDLES -- DIRECT EXTENSION CANDIDATE
16 October 2026. Depends on SPLIT_OPERATOR_RULE_v1.txt, awaiting audit.
No historical novelty or arbitrary mixed-role claim.

MODEL
Keep the same physical pods h_i--L_i and shared grid P, but partition the
hardware coordinates into arbitrary fixed nonempty blocks K_b. Actual owner
B_b controls all h_i for i in K_b. Independently partition the local-energy
coordinates into fixed blocks J_a, controlled by actual A_a. Grid C is a third
role. The A, B, C owner sets are pairwise disjoint. The two partitions need not
align. Each resource coordinate has exactly one actual owner. Every owner can
jointly underreport any of its own coordinates. Public floors remain theta times
each local operator's actual grand marginal. Hardware/grid floors are absent.

RULE
Use ell_i=min(h_i,L_i), r_i=h_i-ell_i and the same L,Q,H,G,R,T,M,D as v1.
For hardware owner b put e_b=sum_{i in K_b}ell_i and r_b=sum_{i in K_b}r_i.
For L>0 pay
 x_b=e_b clip((P-Q+r_b)/L), X=sum_b x_b,
 y_Bb=x_b, y_Aa=ell_a M/L, y_C=G-(X-L/4)_+.
At L=0 pay C=G and all others zero. Mix with the efficient baseline
(all A0, B_b=e_b, C=R) for general theta as in v1.
Total, nonnegativity and local floors are unchanged, yielding factor1+theta/4.

ACTUAL-OWNER INCENTIVES
For B_b under any whole-bundle increase, e_b is nondecreasing. The quantity
 p_b=(P-sum_{c!=b}r_c)_+
and e_-b=sum_{c!=b}e_c remain fixed. Thus
 x_b=min(e_b,p_b*e_b/(e_-b+e_b))
is nondecreasing, including zero and cap boundaries. Changes in r_b cancel
entirely in this formula; no assumption that only one pod report changes.
For each A_a, all hardware reports remain fixed and the v1 proof applies
unchanged. For C, sum_b e_b/L=1 gives exactly the same1-Lipschitz proof.

COALITION COVERAGE
A coalition's hardware set I is a union of full K_b blocks. Its selected local
rows K are the intersection of I with a union of J_a blocks, and may be a proper
part of individual K_b blocks. Coalitional worth is still ell(K) without grid,
or ell(K)+min(P,r(I)+ell(I\K)) with grid.
The total local payment is at least M*ell(K)/L, exactly as in v1.

For Q<P<H, normalize L=1 and set delta=P-Q. Now define
 z_b=e_b min(r_b,1-delta), Z=sum_b z_b.
For included hardware owners, sum x_b=delta*ell(I)+z(I), and for excluded
hardware owners, with o=ell(I^c), r=r(I^c),
 z(I^c)<=o min(r,1-delta).
These are precisely the identities used by the v1 scalar coverage lemma;
no step required K to be a union of hardware blocks. The proof transfers
word-for-word using s=ell(I\K), o<=1-s.

For P<=Q, the v1 grid-only proof also transfers: for every excluded hardware
owner b, Q-r_b>=r(I), so x_b<=e_b clip(P-r(I)) after normalizing L=1.
For P>=H, x_b=e_b and y_C>=Q. Local-only coverage follows x_b>=e_b D/L.
Zero cases and the baseline proof are identical to v1.

SHARPNESS AND COST
The previous one-local-operator/separate-hardware-owner lower family is a
subclass, so the uniform factor remains sharp. O(m+number of actual owners)
arithmetic operations suffice. No theorem of sharpness for each prescribed
partition or positive floor for a hardware/mixed owner is asserted.

WHY REAGGREGATION MATTERS
Merely summing the old per-pod hardware payments is not truthful. Give one
actual hardware owner both pods, take local capacities(1,1) and P=1. At h=(1,1),
the old per-pod formula pays each pod1/2, total1. At h=(3/2,1), L=2,Q=1/2 and
r=(1/2,0), so those payments are1/2 and1/4, total3/4. Raising an actual owner's
hardware decreases its summed old payment. The new owner-level formula pays
min(L,P)=1 at both profiles. This also illustrates why a general owner-merging
assertion would have been invalid.

SCOPE BOUNDARY
The proof relies on hardware owners having no local or grid report and local
owners having no hardware or grid report. It also uses disjoint coordinate
ownership within each role. Arbitrary overlapping contributions to one local
or hardware resource, or actual owners spanning roles, are not proved here.
