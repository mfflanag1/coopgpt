JOINT CAPACITY ACCOUNTING STILL CANNOT RESCUE THE CURRENT FIXED-COVER ROUTE
16 October 2026. Exact finite dual certificate; no historical novelty claim.
This strengthens the earlier obstruction that replaced each restricted value
F_r by F separately. It is still an obstruction to a stated BUDGET PROOF,
not a lower bound against arbitrary truthful mechanisms or their actual totals.

SCOPE
Let G be the complete ternary tree of height4:121 resource vertices,120 unit-
profit activities, each consuming one unit at each endpoint. The actual owners
are fixed; the argument concerns production values and does not split owners.
It holds for any fixed ownership that permits arbitrary nonnegative capacities,
for example one resource coordinate assigned to each of121 fixed owners.

Allow ANY finite collection of connected activity subtrees Q, each of diameter
at most4, with arbitrary fixed weights lambda_Q>=0. Every edge must be covered:
  sum_{Q containing e}lambda_Q>=1.                         (1)
A component may be charged using any class it belongs to, at the corresponding
uniform factor: stars C2=1+1/(2e), double-stars C3=1+W(2/e), and general depth-two
trees7/4. A star has diameter<=2; a double-star has diameter<=3. In a tree,
diameter<=4 is equivalent to having a center vertex of radius<=2. Isolated
vertices can be omitted. Collections of forests with mixed component types
are included by separating their connected components with the same weights.

For capacities c, define the exact JOINT surrogate budget
  B_lambda(c)=sum_Q lambda_Q C_Q F_Q(c),                    (2)
where F_Q is production with only Q's activities. This is the budget obtained
by summing the available component-factor guarantees, retaining their actual
joint production values rather than bounding them individually by F(c).

THEOREM
For every cover(1), some capacity profile c with F(c)=1 satisfies
  B_lambda(c)>=580203/257499 >2.253 >2.                    (3)
Moreover, even if star, double-star and depth-two charges were discounted to
7/6,4/3 and3/2 respectively, some profile would still satisfy
  B_lambda(c)>=2199/1060 >2.074 >2.                        (4)
Thus the present fixed positive-cover route cannot establish an all-tree
factor below2 merely by improving the joint estimate of the F_Q values. This
remains true after the stated substantial discounts to the component costs.

WHAT THE THEOREM DOES NOT SAY
Actual component payments can be strictly smaller than C_Q F_Q. A finer bound
using the actual payment formulas could escape this theorem, as could a new
lower-cost primitive, a larger solved component class, or report-dependent
covers accompanied by a valid incentive proof. No unrestricted subsidy lower
bound is asserted. The obstruction is already present on one finite tree;
it is not a new unrestricted asymptotic lower bound.

PROOF: STAR-CENTERED CAPACITY PROFILES
For every vertex v, let c^(v) put capacity1 on v and all its neighbors, and0
elsewhere. Only edges incident to v can produce, because a tree has no edges
between two neighbors of v. Thus F(c^(v))=1. For every connected activity
subtree Q with at least one edge,
  F_Q(c^(v))=1[v has an incident edge in Q]=1[v in V(Q)].   (5)

Choose nonnegative vertex weights g_v, normalized by D=sum_v g_v, and edge
weights a_e such that every allowed component Q obeys
  sum_{e in Q}a_e <= c_Q sum_{v in V(Q)}g_v,               (6)
where c_Q is a lower bound on its charged factor C_Q. Averaging(2) over the
profiles c^(v) with weights g_v/D, using(5),(6), and then the edge cover gives
  max_v B_lambda(c^(v))
   >=(1/D)sum_Q lambda_Q C_Q sum_{v in V(Q)}g_v
   >=(1/D)sum_Q lambda_Q sum_{e in Q}a_e
   >=(1/D)sum_e a_e.                                     (7)
This handles arbitrarily overlapping components and arbitrary nonnegative
weights. No LP strong-duality theorem or floating-point optimization is needed
to establish(7); optimization was used only to discover the explicit numbers.

CERTIFICATE A: CURRENT FACTORS
Each vertex at depth d receives g_d, and an edge whose child is at depth d
receives a_d. Use
  g=(24057,16038,10692,3300,0),
  a=(28431,18954,8112,1300),
  (c_star,c_double,c_depth)=(13/11,7/5,7/4).
There are3^d vertices at depth d and3^d edges with child depth d, so
  D=24057+3*16038+9*10692+27*3300=257499,
  sum_e a_e=3*28431+9*18954+27*8112+81*1300=580203.

The actual factors dominate these charges. Since e<11/4, C2>13/11.
For completeness, sum_{k=0}^4 1/k!=65/24 and the remaining tail is less than
(1/120)/(1-1/6)=1/100, so e<1631/600<11/4. Also C3>7/5: W(2/e)>2/5 because
e^(7/5)<5, which follows from e<3 and3^7=2187<3125=5^5. The depth factor is
the established7/4. Therefore(7) gives(3).

CERTIFICATE B: ROBUST DISCOUNTED FACTORS

  a=(5859,4221,1500,280),
  (c_star,c_double,c_depth)=(7/6,4/3,3/2).
Then

  sum_e a_e=3*5859+9*4221+27*1500+81*280=118746,

These discounts are smaller than the current component charges. In particular
C2>7/6 follows from e<3; C3>4/3 follows from e^(4/3)<6, since e^4<81<216.
Even the hypothetical3/2 depth-two charge therefore cannot make this proof
architecture succeed on all profiles.

COMPLETE EXACT VERIFICATION OF(6)
The following formulas check every connected component, without enumerating
all edge subsets or relying on the exploratory LP. Write w_uv=a_uv and let
t be the charge of the class under consideration.

For a star centered at v, the maximum of its edge weight minus t times its
vertex weight, allowing an empty selection of incident edges, is
  -t g_v + sum_{u adjacent v}(w_vu-t g_u)_+ .             (8)
Checking(8)<=0 for all121 vertices proves every star constraint.

For a double-star with central edge uv, that maximum is
  w_uv-t(g_u+g_v)
   +sum_{z adjacent u,z!=v}(w_uz-t g_z)_+
   +sum_{z adjacent v,z!=u}(w_vz-t g_z)_+.               (9)
Every nonempty diameter<=3 tree has such a central edge, allowing zero leaves
on either side. Checking(9)<=0 for all120 edges proves the whole class.

For a depth-two component centered at v, that maximum is
  -t g_v +sum_{u adjacent v}
       [w_vu-t g_u+sum_{z adjacent u,z!=v}(w_uz-t g_z)_+]_+ . (10)
Once the center is selected, child branches are independent because G is a
tree. A chosen child can retain precisely its profitable grandchild edges;
the entire child branch is retained exactly when its resulting profit is
positive. Thus(10) is an exact maximization over every connected subtree
within distance2 of v. Checking all121 possible centers covers every tree of
diameter<=4. Zero-profit choices and isolated vertices cause no issue.

The standard-library-only verifier verify_joint_cover_obstruction.py evaluates
(8)-(10) using Fraction/integer arithmetic for BOTH displayed certificates.
For each class the largest value is nonpositive, proving(6). All maxima are0
except the current-factor double-star maximum, which is -108/5. It also replays
the production identity(5) by exact vertex-cover dynamic programming on each
star-centered profile, for all single-edge, full-star and full-radius-two
components. The latter is a computational corroboration; the elementary
support argument already proves(5) for every component.

WHY MULTIPLE CAPACITY PROFILES ARE ESSENTIAL TO THIS CERTIFICATE
The offending profile may depend on the fixed cover. We do not assert that one
capacity vector makes all useful restricted values close to F simultaneously.
Indeed G contains a matching of size30. At any profile, the sum of the30
single-edge production values on that matching is at most F, so one is at
most F/30. In contrast the weighted family c^(v), together with the fixedness
of the cover, forces at least one large joint budget through(7). Each profile
can individually be handled cheaply by a cover tailored to that profile; such
tailoring is outside the fixed-cover scope and needs a new incentive argument.
