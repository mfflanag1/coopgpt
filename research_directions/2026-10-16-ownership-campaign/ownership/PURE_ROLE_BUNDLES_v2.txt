SHARP FUNDING WITH SEPARATE RESOURCE ROLES AND ARBITRARY OWNER BUNDLES
16 October 2026. Self-contained candidate proof, awaiting independent audit.
Supersedes no frozen file; v1 and SPLIT_OPERATOR_RULE_v1 remain preserved.
This note makes no historical-priority or arbitrary mixed-role claim.

THEOREM AND FIXED MODEL
Fix a finite set of m>=1 physical pods. Pod i has hardware h_i, local power L_i,
and access to shared grid P. An output unit uses one hardware unit and either
one unit of its pod's local power or one grid unit. Partition hardware rows into
nonempty fixed blocks K_b owned by actual B_b, and local rows independently into
nonempty fixed blocks J_a owned by actual A_a. One actual owner C owns P. All A,
B,C identities are distinct. Partitions may cross arbitrarily, but each resource
row has exactly one owner. Reports are all nonnegative real capacities; actual
owners may jointly underreport any part of their own coordinate bundle. Utility
is payment, and underreports are binding.

For any public theta in[0,1], a nonnegative all-profile payment rule covers every
coalition, is own-bundle monotone, and pays each A_a at least theta times its
grand-coalition marginal M_a. Its total is at most(1+theta/4)T, where T is the
actual grand production. Evaluation takes O(m+number of actual owners) arithmetic
operations. The uniform factor is sharp over all counts and partitions, by the
included one-local-owner lower family. This is not sharpness for each individual
fixed partition. Neither private costs nor strategic identity changes are allowed.

NOTATION AND GRAND MARGINALS
ell_i=min(h_i,L_i), r_i=h_i-ell_i,
L=sum ell_i, Q=sum r_i, H=L+Q,
R=min(P,Q), G=min(P,H), T=L+R,
D=G-R=min(L,(P-Q)_+), M=T-G=L-D=min(L,(H-P)_+).
For hardware owner b let e_b=sum_{K_b}ell_i, r_b=sum_{K_b}r_i.
For local owner a let ell_a=sum_{J_a}ell_i.
Removing A_a changes local total from L to L-ell_a and residual from Q to
Q+ell_a, while retaining all hardware. Therefore
 M_a=ell_a+min(P,Q)-min(P,Q+ell_a)
    =(ell_a-(P-Q)_+)_+=(ell_a-D)_+.

RULE AT THETA=1
For L>0, define clip(u)=min(1,max(0,u)) and
 x_b=e_b clip((P-Q+r_b)/L), X=sum_b x_b,
 y_Bb=x_b, y_Aa=ell_a M/L,
 y_C=G-(X-L/4)_+=min(G,G-X+L/4).
At L=0, pay C=G and every other owner zero. The A owners jointly receive M.

NONNEGATIVITY AND FLOORS
Since P-Q+r_b=P-sum_{c!=b}r_c<=P,
 0<=X<=L min(1,P/L)=min(L,P)<=G.
Hence y_C>=0. All other payments are nonnegative. As ell_a<=L and 0<=D<=L,
 y_Aa=ell_a-ell_a D/L>=max(0,ell_a-D)=M_a.
All payments vanish at T=0. An owner whose entire own resource bundle is zero
receives zero; for C this follows P=0=>X=G=0. All statements include L=0.

ACTUAL-OWNER INCENTIVES
A_a: all hardware, P and L_-a=sum_{i outside J_a}ell_i are fixed under its
reports. With K=(H-P)_+ fixed, its payment as a function of u=ell_a is
 u min(1,K/(u+L_-a))=min(u,K*u/(u+L_-a)),
using value zero when the denominator is zero. Both branches are nondecreasing
for u>=0 and have the same value at their junction. Every coordinatewise
increase of A_a's whole bundle increases u, proving its incentive claim.

B_b: under its whole bundle, p_b=(P-sum_{c!=b}r_c)_+ and e_-b=sum_{c!=b}e_c
remain fixed. Its payment equals
 min(e_b,p_b*e_b/(e_-b+e_b)),
with value zero if the denominator vanishes. This is nondecreasing in e_b;
e_b=sum_{K_b}min(h_i,L_i) increases with the entire hardware bundle. Changes
in r_b cancel out of p_b. Hardware/local-cap and zero-boundary cases are covered.

C: when L>0 all e_b,r_b are fixed; each x_b(P) is nondecreasing and
e_b/L-Lipschitz. Hence X is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz. For P>=H it is
constant at L. Consequently, for P'<=P,
 0<=X(P)-X(P')<=G(P)-G(P').
The positive-part function is nondecreasing and1-Lipschitz, so y_C is
nondecreasing. If L=0, y_C=min(P,H) has the same property.

ALL COALITIONS: BASIC REDUCTION
Given a coalition, let I be the union of its included hardware blocks K_b and
let J be the union of its included local blocks J_a. Write K=I intersect J.
With no C, its production is ell(K). With C, it is
 ell(K)+min(P,r(I)+ell(I\K)).
Its local owners receive (M/L)ell(J)>=(M/L)ell(K). Thus it suffices to prove
coverage for every hardware-block union I and every row subset K subset I with
the accounting contribution (M/L)ell(K). No fictitious owners are introduced.
Since x_b>=e_b D/L, coalitions without C receive at least
 (D/L)ell(I)+(M/L)ell(K)>=ell(K).
We handle coalitions with C next.

GRID-ONLY INEQUALITY, VALID FOR ALL P WHEN L>0
Normalize capacities and payments by L, so sum_b e_b=1. For hardware-block
union I set s=ell(I), r=r(I) and x_out=sum_{b excluded}x_b. For every excluded b,
 Q-r_b>=r, hence x_b<=e_b clip(P-r). Therefore
 x_out<= (1-s)clip(P-r).
Put a=P-r; then H_I=r+s. Elementary case analysis gives
 (1-s)clip(a)-(a-s)_+<=s(1-s)<=1/4:
 a<=0 gives0; 0<=a<=s gives(1-s)a<=s(1-s);
 s<=a<=1 gives s(1-a)<=s(1-s); a>=1 gives1-a<=0.
It follows x_out-(P-H_I)_+<=1/4, equivalently
 min(P,H_I)-x(I)<=P-X+1/4.
The left side is also<=P. When P<=H, G=P, so it is at most
 y_C=min(P,P-X+1/4). For P>=H, x_b=e_b and y_C=H-3/4=Q+1/4>=Q,
which directly covers min(P,H_I)<=ell(I)+Q. Restoring scale, we have
 y_C+x(I)>=min(P,H_I) for every hardware coalition.

CASE P<=Q
Here M=L. For included local rows K, the payment ell(K) is direct. The grid-only
inequality gives y_C+x(I)>=min(P,H_I)>=min(P,r(I)+ell(I\K)), completing coverage.

CASE Q<P<H
Normalize L=1 again and put delta=P-Q in(0,1),
 z_b=e_b min(r_b,1-delta), Z=sum_b z_b.
Then x_b=delta e_b+z_b, X=delta+Z, M=1-delta and
 y_C=min(P,Q+1/4-Z).
For the coalition put s=ell(I\K), r=r(I^c), o=ell(I^c)<=1-s.
Subtracting its local payment, the remaining grid deficit is
 d=min(P,r(I)+s)-delta*s-z(I).
This is<=P by nonnegativity. Moreover,
 d-Q+Z=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+z(I^c)
        <=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+o min(r,1-delta)
        <=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+(1-s)min(r,1-delta).
If r<=1-delta, set t=delta+r in[0,1]. The final expression is
 min(t,s)-s*t.
For t<=s it equals t(1-s)<=s(1-s); for t>=s it equals s(1-t)<=s(1-s).
If r>=1-delta, then s-r<=delta and the expression is1-delta-r<=0.
In every case the expression is<=s(1-s)<=1/4. Therefore
 d<=Q+1/4-Z as well as d<=P, hence d<=y_C. Coverage follows.

CASE P>=H AND ZERO CASES
For P>=H and L>0, M=0, x_b=e_b and y_C=Q+L/4>=Q. Every coalition with C
has worth at most H_I=ell(I)+r(I)<=ell(I)+Q, and is covered. Coalitions without
C were already covered by hardware payments. The cases agree at saturation.
If L=0, coalitions without C have value zero and those with C have worth at
most min(P,H_I)<=G, which C's payment covers. This finishes every real profile.

BUDGET AND ALL THETA
At theta1, total is exactly
 M+X+G-(X-L/4)_+=T+min(X,L/4)<=T+L/4<=5T/4.
Use the efficient baseline y_Aa=0, y_Bb=e_b, y_C=R. Its incentives follow
from constant local payments, own-hardware monotonicity of e_b, and own-grid
monotonicity of R. For coalition hardware I and local rows K, without C its
hardware payment covers ell(K). With C, put a=ell(I\K). Since Q>=r(I),
 ell(I)+R=ell(K)+a+min(P,Q)
          >=ell(K)+min(P,a+r(I)),
its coalition value. The baseline's total is T.
Mix the theta1 rule and baseline with fixed weights theta and1-theta. This
preserves all incentives, coverage and nonnegativity, pays each A_a at least
theta M_a, and has total T+theta min(X,L/4)<=(1+theta/4)T.

UNIFORM SHARPNESS (INCLUDED ONE-BLOCK FAMILY)
Take one local operator A, m even hardware owners, h_i=L_i=1, P=m/2.
Then T=m and M_A=m/2. Let a,c,x=sum_i y_Hi denote arbitrary eligible payments.
Coverage of A with all hardware gives a+x>=m. Averaging grid-plus-hardware
coverage over all m/2-element hardware subsets gives c+x/2>=m/2. The floor
gives a>=theta m/2. Multiply these three inequalities by1/2,1,1/2 respectively
and add: a+c+x>=m+theta m/4. Thus no smaller universal factor is possible.

EXACT FAILURE OF NAIVE HARDWARE MERGING
If one actual owner holds both hardware pods, local capacities are(1,1), and
P=1, the original PER-POD hardware payments sum to1 at h=(1,1), but only3/4
at h=(3/2,1). Specifically they change from(1/2,1/2) to(1/2,1/4). Increasing
an actual owner's report would lower its payment. The owner-block formula here
pays1 at both profiles. Thus the theorem uses a corrected account definition;
it does not rely on a false generic owner-merging principle.

LIMITS
Actual owners may not span hardware, local-power and grid roles. The proof also
does not cover overlapping ownership contributions to one physical resource
coordinate. The payoff floor concerns actual local-only operators. No measured
GPU gain, private-cost incentive, coalition strategyproofness, strict incentive,
or historical-priority claim follows.
