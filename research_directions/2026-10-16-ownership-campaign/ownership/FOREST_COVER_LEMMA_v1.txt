ACTIVITY-COVER COMPOSITION: UNBOUNDED PATHS AND A TREE OBSTRUCTION
16 October2026. Candidate consequences of the previously proved mixed two-input
primitive. No historical novelty claim. This is not an all-forest upper below2.

GENERAL FIXED ACTIVITY-COVER LEMMA
Fix finitely many resource coordinates, actual owners with arbitrary nonnegative
bundles, and productive activities. Activity e has a fixed nonnegative value w_e
and fixed positive consumption coefficients on its incident resource rows.
Production F(c) maximizes sum_e w_e x_e subject to nonnegative activity levels
and the capacity constraints. All ownership and technology data stay fixed.

Let E_r be finitely many fixed subsets of activities and F_r the same production
problem with only E_r allowed. Choose fixed lambda_r>=0 such that for every e,
  sum_{r:e in E_r}lambda_r>=1.
Suppose F_r has nonnegative all-profile coalition-covering, whole-owner-monotone
payments y^r with total<=C_r F_r on the SAME full actual-owner set. Null owners
are retained. Then
 y_i=sum_r lambda_r y_i^r
is nonnegative, whole-owner-monotone, coalition-covering for F, and has total
  <=sum_r lambda_r C_r F_r(N)<=(sum_r lambda_r C_r)F(N).

PROOF
For any actual coalition S, take an optimal production plan x for F(S). Its
restriction to E_r is feasible for F_r(S), because removing activities can only
reduce every resource use. Therefore
 sum_r lambda_r F_r(S)
 >=sum_e w_e x_e sum_{r:e in E_r}lambda_r
 >=sum_e w_e x_e=F(S).
Payment coverage follows. Since activities are merely restricted, F_r(N)<=F(N),
giving the budget. Every coefficient and component is fixed independently of
reports, so a positive sum of actual-owner own-bundle-monotone payments remains
own-bundle-monotone. No actual owner is split or merged. If F(N)=0 then each
component value and its payments are zero. This is an approximate covering
argument, not the exact positive decomposition blocked by the prior extreme-ray
theorem.

WEIGHTED STAR PRIMITIVE, INCLUDING POSITIVE INPUT COEFFICIENTS
Let a component be a star centered at resource u, with leaf v_e for each edge e.
The central consumption coefficient is a_e>0 and leaf coefficient b_e>0.
Set z_e=a_e x_e, S_e=(a_e/b_e)c_{v_e}, U=c_u and profit t_e=w_e/a_e.
Then sum z_e<=U, 0<=z_e<=S_e. In fixed descending public profit order, with
t_{k+1}=0 after the last leaf, fractional knapsack gives
  F_star=sum_k(t_k-t_{k+1}) min(U,sum_{j<=k}S_j).
Every term is a minimum of two fixed nonnegative linear resource forms. The
established mixed-owner two-input exponential rule therefore gives factor
C2=1+1/(2e) for the weighted star, preserving all actual owners and their whole
bundles. Fixed positive sums preserve this factor. Isolated vertices have zero
value and zero payments. The formula includes a single edge and zero profits.

ARBITRARY-LENGTH PATH THEOREM
Suppose the resource-activity graph is a path of ANY finite length, or a disjoint
union of such paths. Activities have arbitrary fixed nonnegative values and
positive endpoint consumption coefficients. Owners may hold any mixed bundles
across vertices and components. Then there is an all-profile truthful covering
rule with total factor
  (3/2)C2=3/2+3/(4e)<2.
Numerically this is about1.77591, but the theorem uses the exact constant.

PROOF
Order the edges of each path once and for all. For each offset r in{0,1,2}, let
E_r delete all edges whose index is r modulo3. Every remaining component has
at most two edges and hence is a star. Sum its star rules over its components,
retaining all actual owners; this gives factor C2 for F_r. Each original edge
belongs to exactly two of E_0,E_1,E_2. Apply the cover lemma with lambda_r=1/2.
Total is<=C2(F_0+F_1+F_2)/2<=3C2 F/2. Underreports may alter several components
and several resource roles, but each component's own-bundle proof and fixed sums
already cover that. All capacities and coalition comparisons are unrestricted.
The bound is uniform in path length, owner count, and number of components.
For an elementary rational margin, e>1+1+1/2+1/6=8/3 gives
  (3/2)C2<57/32<2.

This theorem does not identify the optimal path constant. It is an upper theorem
on unbounded-diameter paths, not a bounded-family construction or a proof for
all trees. The fixed coefficient normalization is only used inside stars, so
no consistency of scaling across the original full graph is required.

EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO STAR-FOREST COVERS ON ALL TREES
Take the subdivided star with m arms p--h_j--l_j, all2m edges of equal weight1.
A star forest is any edge subset whose connected components have diameter<=2.
Its maximum possible number of retained edges is m+1.
If it retains no root edge p--h_j, it has at most m leaf edges. If it retains
exactly one root edge, it can keep every leaf edge, totaling m+1; this is feasible.
If it retains k>=2 root edges, then no selected root arm may retain its own
leaf edge: that edge together with two root edges forms a length-three path
within one component, which is not a star. Thus at most m-k leaf edges remain,
and the total is at most k+(m-k)=m.

Consequently any distribution over star forests retaining every edge with
probability at least alpha has
  2m alpha<=E[number of retained edges]<=m+1,
so alpha<=1/2+1/(2m). There is no uniform alpha>1/2 over all finite trees.
In particular retention2/3,3/5 or any threshold>C2/2 fails for large enough m.
Equivalently any fixed fractional edge cover by star forests has total weight
at least2m/(m+1), approaching2. The crude cover-lemma budget using only the
star primitive therefore cannot prove a uniform factor below2 on all forests.
This is a route obstruction, not a lower bound against truthful mechanisms;
the new depth-two range mechanism works on exactly this obstruction family.

GENERALIZATION FRONTIER
Mixtures of solved component classes could evade the star-only obstruction.
For example, depth-two components cost7/4 while stars costC2. To prove an
all-tree improvement by the simple cover lemma, one needs a fixed fractional
activity cover with sum_r lambda_r C_r<2 uniformly over the tree, or a sharper
budget estimate exploiting F_r(N) jointly rather than replacing each byF(N).
Exact positive decomposition is separately ruled out by the earlier extreme-ray
theorem. Any separator-gluing alternative must address report-dependent residual
capacities and actual-owner incentive effects; a graph partition alone does not.
