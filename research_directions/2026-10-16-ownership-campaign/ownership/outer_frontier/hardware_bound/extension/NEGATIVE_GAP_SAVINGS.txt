NEGATIVE-GAP SAVINGS WITH ARBITRARY FULL GRID OVERFLOW
16 October 2026. Candidate; independent review pending.
Original exponential local primitive and outer primitive. Actual fixed owners,
all nonnegative real reports, arbitrary branches. This is an envelope inequality,
not by itself a complete C^2 funding theorem or a novelty claim.

Normalize full hardware H=1 and assume the physical hardware bottleneck P+M>=1.
Use a_i=H_i, z_i for full local payment, Z=sum_i z_i, M=sum_j T_j<=1,
C=1+1/(2e), K=e-1, delta=C M-Z>=0,
b_i=p_i+z_i, d_i=min(b_i,1), S=sum_i d_i, q0=.5 exp(1-S).
Every z_i<=M<=1 and S>=1: if any b_i>=1 this is immediate; otherwise S=P+Z
and Z>=M. For v in[0,K^2/4] put
 L(v)=2 exp(sqrt(1-v)-1)/(1+sqrt(1-v)).

THEOREM
Every full negative-gap owner i, meaning g_i=a_i-d_i>=0, satisfies
 E_i <=a_i-q0 exp(-delta) g_i L(K^2 z_i g_i).             (1)
Here E_i is the actual own-report monotone envelope of the raw nested rule.
The formula includes zero g_i or z_i by continuity. It follows that
 E_i <=a_i-q0 exp(-delta) L(K^2/4)g_i.                  (2)
The numerical constant L(K^2/4) is about .812; the symbolic expression is
load-bearing. Unlike a bound depending on sum_i min(p_i,1)+CM, (1) uses the full
capped total S and its owner-specific correction despite arbitrary full overflow.

PROOF: RESTORATION AND CAPPED TOTAL
Fix an arbitrary actual whole-owner underreport of i. Restore its OUTER hardware
and grid accounts after calculating the local payments at that underreport.
The outer primitive's own monotonicity only raises i's payment. This algebraic
restoration is not asserted to be an actual report. Other outer hardware accounts
stay fixed, so outer hardware total is now1. Write z'_k for the local payments,
D=z_i-z'_i in[0,z_i], and S'=sum_k min(p_k+z'_k,1).

If g_i>0, b_i=d_i<1. Thus its own full overflow is zero and its own restored
capped account is exactly d_i-D. Let W=sum_k(b_k-1)_+ and similarly W'. Then
 S'-S=(Z'-Z)+(W-W').
Every local report has decreased only in owner's i bundle. Therefore every local
output T'_j<=T_j and Z'<=C sum_j T'_j<=CM, so Z'-Z<=delta. Only opponents can
contribute positively to W-W', and each opponent's overflow decrease is at most
its positive local payment decrease. The sharp nonlinear outgoing theorem gives
 W-W' <=sum_{k!=i}(z_k-z'_k)_+ <=K sqrt(z_i D).
Consequently
 S'<=S+delta+K sqrt(z_i D).                            (3)
The same calculation works for g_i=0 unless d_i=a_i=1; this boundary case needs
only E_i<=a_i, shown below, and can be treated directly.

PROOF: PAYMENT AND OUTER POOL SWITCH
If S'>=1, the restored outer profile is hardware-short (or tied). Its coefficient
is q'=.5exp(1-S'), and its payment equals
 a_i-q'(g_i+D).
By(3), q'>=q0 exp(-delta-K sqrt(z_iD)).
If S'<1, every opposing account is uncapped and the opposing pool is short.
Writing q_h<=1/2 for the corresponding coefficient, i's payment is
 (1-q_h)(d_i-D)+q_h min(a_i,S')
 <=(a_i+d_i-D)/2
 =a_i-(g_i+D)/2.
As S>=1 and delta,D>=0, q0 exp(-delta-K sqrt(z_iD))<=1/2.
The same upper bound therefore holds on either side of the pool switch:
 E_i <=a_i-q0 exp(-delta) inf_{0<=D<=z_i}
                      (g_i+D)exp(-K sqrt(z_iD)).        (4)
This also proves E_i<=a_i when g_i=0, even if i's full account is capped.

PROOF: EXACT ONE-DIMENSIONAL MINIMUM
Suppose z=z_i>0 and g=g_i>0. Because b_i=d_i=p_i+z_i<=a_i<=1,
 z+g=a_i-p_i<=1, hence zg<=1/4 and K^2zg<1 (K<2).
Set A=K sqrt(z) and x=sqrt(D). The derivative of
 f(x)=(g+x^2)exp(-Ax), 0<=x<=sqrt(z),
has the sign of 2x-A(g+x^2). Its two roots are
 x_-=(1-sqrt(1-K^2zg))/(K sqrt(z)),
 x_+=(1+sqrt(1-K^2zg))/(K sqrt(z)).
The smaller root belongs to[0,sqrt(z)] and the larger exceeds sqrt(z).
For the first assertion, if Kz>=1 it is immediate; otherwise square the
nonnegative comparison sqrt(1-K^2zg)>=1-Kz, obtaining K(z+g)<=2.
For the second, if Kz<=1 it is immediate; otherwise squaring the nonnegative
comparison sqrt(1-K^2zg)>Kz-1 gives the same strict condition K(z+g)<2.
Thus f decreases to x_- and increases thereafter throughout the allowed domain.
Put r=sqrt(1-K^2zg). At x_-,
 x_-^2/g=(1-r)/(1+r), and A x_-=1-r.
The exact minimum is therefore
 g * [2/(1+r)] exp(r-1)=g L(K^2zg).
This proves(1). When z=0 the infimum in(4) is g and L(0)=1. When g=0 the
right side of(1) is a_i and the previously proved ceiling applies.

Finally L(v) decreases on[0,1): writing r=sqrt(1-v),
 d log[2 exp(r-1)/(1+r)]/dr=r/(1+r)>=0,
while r decreases with v. Since K^2zg<=K^2/4, (2) follows.

DEPENDENCIES AND SCOPE
Uses only eligibility/own monotonicity/local budget of the original exponential
primitive and SHARP_NONLINEAR_OUTGOING_LOSS.txt (including its stated short-stage
dependency). No arbitrary owner deletion, cloning, or changing roles is a feasible
deviation. The scalar minimization ranges over a larger set than feasible local
losses, which is valid for an upper bound and does not assert joint attainability.
Every owner may choose its own unrelated underreport in its envelope. The bound
can be summed over negative-gap owners; a complete joint positive-gap funding
argument remains open.
