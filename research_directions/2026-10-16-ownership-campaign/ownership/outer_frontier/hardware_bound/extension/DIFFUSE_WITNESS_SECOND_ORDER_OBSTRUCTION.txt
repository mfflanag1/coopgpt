5. UNIQUE OPTIMIZER AND SECOND-ORDER EXPANSION
OnT in[2e^2epsilon,1),

This provesT_*~1/L and quantifies the best choice of local production scale.

6. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SETTLE
The general leading coefficient is already matched by the main theorem. This
note explains the remainingloglog loss of its witness construction: maximizing
its local-production scale necessarily choosesT~1/log(1/epsilon). Nonuniform
local cap sizes or multiple hardware-heavy owners within this architecture do
not evade that tradeoff. A better unrestricted second-order lower construction
must leave this common-proportions parallel-branch class, or use a different
full outer ratio/profile structure. A general second-order upper bound for
W(epsilon) is NOT proved here.
