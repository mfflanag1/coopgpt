SIGNED OTHER-RECIPIENT LOCAL LOSS: THE STRONGER CONSTANT 4/e
17 October 2026. General theorem strengthening the preceding 3/2 note.
Original exponential primitive, actual fixed owners, arbitrary real mixed
hardware/local reports, arbitrary hardware fragmentation and branch counts.
The proof uses SIGNED_OTHER_LOCAL_LOSS_THREE_HALVES.txt Sections 2--4 for
its exact mixed-owner/cap reduction; all scalar bounds are certified below.

THEOREM
For every actual owner's LOCAL-ONLY whole-bundle underreport, with hardware
held fixed, let z_k,z'_k denote aggregate local payments. Then

        [sum_{k!=i}(z_k-z'_k)]_+ <= (4/e) z_i.           (1)

Here 4/e approximately 1.471517765 <3/2<e-1. The other recipients' gains are
counted negatively in this sum. No assertion of sharpness is made. In
particular, this is NOT a 4/e bound on the sum of OTHER positive losses.
It does not directly replace a capped opposing-account bound when caps hide
gains; that distinct accounting is not assumed in the theorem.

1. ORIGINAL-SHORT LOCAL CHANGES ALREADY HAVE CONSTANT 4/e
The exact derivative calculation in SHORT_STAGE_NONLINEAR_THREE_HALVES.txt
bounds total positive OTHER loss under a shorter-coordinate decrease of
physical size delta by (2/e)delta. For clarity, on any short-total cap cell
its bounding long-role derivative is
 q[rho^2-m(rho-1)]<=q rho^2<=2/e,
where m counts long coordinates above the current short total. This derivative
integrates over all cap cells; decreasing the short total cannot switch its
orientation. The owner has full short amount x>=delta, full coefficient q<=1/2,
and full short-role payment U=(1-q)x>=delta/2. Thus
 signed OTHER loss <=positive OTHER loss <=(2/e)delta<=(4/e)U<=(4/e)z_i.
Mixed own hardware only increases its full total payment. Persistent cap ties
and zero endpoints follow by the accepted primitive continuity convention.

2. ORIGINAL-LONG LOCAL CHANGES REDUCE TO TWO EXACT SCALARS
Before a pool switch, the prior note proves the signed ratio is at most
K/4, K=e-1, which is less than 4/e (for example e<3 gives K/4<1/2<4/e).

After a switch its actual cap comparison proves, for full other local mass
v in(0,1), own capped local d in[1-v,1], and arbitrary own hardware s,
that the ratio is at most
 f(d,v)={2exp(d+v-1)[1-v/2-exp(1-1/v)/2]-(1-v)}/d.
This is an upper reduction using the ACTUAL hardware caps, not a change of
owner identities. Its derivative has an increasing numerator as a function
of d, so its maximum is attained at one of the two endpoints

 f_0(v)=[1-exp(1-1/v)]/(1-v),
 f_1(v)=exp(v)[2-v-exp(1-1/v)]-1+v.                      (2)

The boundary v=0 has value one; the endpoint limits at v=1 are harmless.
We prove f_0<=7/5 and f_1<=1471/1000, both strictly smaller than 4/e.

3. EXACT FIRST-ENDPOINT BOUND
For v<=1/4, f_0<=1/(1-v)<=4/3. For v>=1/2, the exact Padé bound from the
prior proof gives f_0<=2/(1+v)<=4/3. On [1/4,1/2], the companion certificate
uses exact rational interval/Taylor bounds to verify f_0<7/5 on seven cells.
Its upper on a rational cell [l,u] is
 [1-L_17((1-l)/l)]/(1-u),
where L_17 is the odd degree-17 polynomial for exp(-x). Both numerator and
denominator are bounded in the direction displayed. Hence f_0<=7/5 everywhere.

4. EXACT SECOND-ENDPOINT BOUND
For v<=1/5, omit the nonnegative subtracted reciprocal exponential. The
remaining exp(v)(2-v)-1+v increases, so
 f_1(v)<=(9/5)exp(1/5)-4/5<1471/1000.
The final exponential inequality is checked with exact rational Taylor bounds.
For [1/5,1/4], the certificate uses 128 equal rational cells and the odd
negative-exponential Taylor polynomial of degree25, valid for x<=4.
For [1/4,1], it uses a deterministic adaptive rational partition, with degree17
sufficient because x<=3. Every accepted cell [l,u] verifies the strict rational
upper bound
 U_12(u)[2-l-L_N((1-l)/l)]-1+u <1471/1000,               (3)
with N=25 or17, and a nonnegative bracket. Here
 U_12(x)=sum_{k=0}^{12}x^k/k!+[x^13/13!]/[1-x/14]>=exp(x)
for 0<=x<=1, and L_N(x)=sum_{k=0}^N(-x)^k/k!<=exp(-x) for odd N and x>=0.
The first follows by a geometric bound on the positive Taylor tail; the
second follows from the positive Lagrange remainder after an odd degree.

The adaptive partition bisects a rational cell only when its current upper
exceeds the target. The script proves termination within its explicit depth
limit, then verifies exact adjacency, endpoints and total interval length.
The resulting 321 cells cover [1/4,1] with no gaps; the maximum accepted
rational upper is approximately1.470992672, below1471/1000. No floating value
controls acceptance. All checks use explicit exceptions and pass -I -O.

Finally the same exact positive Taylor tail gives
 e<=2222710781/817689600<87/32,
so 4/e>128/87>1471/1000>7/5. Thus both scalars in (2) are strictly below4/e,
which completes the original-long case for every mixed owner and cap pattern.

5. AGGREGATION AND LIMITS
Sum the signed OTHER losses over all independent branches changed by this
one actual owner. Each branch is bounded by 4/e times its full local payment;
branches with negative signed loss only reduce the sum. Taking a positive part
at the end proves (1). Counts are arbitrary, and the allowed underreports are
whole local bundles of the same owner. Grid reports do not enter this local
calculation; hardware remains fixed as explicitly required.

This theorem supplies a general signed-response constraint strictly belowK.
It does not establish a new funding constant without a separate cap/outer
comparison, and it does not claim the residual asymptotic coefficient is solved.
