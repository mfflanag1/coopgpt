SHARP SIGNED-RESPONSE PAIR: AN IMPROVED GENERAL DIFFUSE LOWER
17 October 2026. Candidate exact construction and asymptotic corollary.
Actual own-report envelope of the original symmetric exponential nested rule.
This uses the local sharp constant in SHARP_SIGNED_OTHER_LOCAL_LOSS.txt and
extends the independently checked earlier TWO_RESPONSE_BRANCH_DIFFUSE_LOWER.

Let c=1/(2e), C=1+c, K=e-1, and let v in(1/3,2/5) solve v exp(v)=1/2.
Then q=exp(-v)/2=v and Lambda=(1-v)^2/v is the exact signed local-response
constant. Let L=log(1/epsilon). W(epsilon) is the supremum of sum_i E_i/H over
all finite fixed-owner, arbitrary mixed-owner physical hardware-bottleneck
instances with max_i z_i/H<=epsilon. E is the actual own-report envelope of
the original nested primitive, not an arbitrary eligible mechanism.

THEOREM. For EVERY sufficiently small positive real epsilon there is a finite
fixed-owner profile with both productive branch count and active owner count
growing without bound, whose actual envelope funding is at least

 C+cK epsilon(L-log L)
      +c[2Lambda-2e-K log2]epsilon+o(epsilon).           (1)

Consequently the centered liminf for W is at least the coefficient in(1).
This improves the prior independent/shared response constructions but does
not establish the optimal residual coefficient. It is a lower bound on this
specified envelope, not an impossibility theorem for all truthful mechanisms.

1. EXACT RESPONSE MODULE WITH ALL ACTUAL LOCAL PAYMENTS <=epsilon
Use THREE productive branches, each with hardware
                         t=epsilon/(3v).
Distinguished actual owner J holds local t on each of these three branches
and no hardware. On branch k, a distinct mixed owner D_k holds BOTH hardware t
and local v t. It holds no other resource or grid. Every identity is fixed.
The full coefficient on each branch is q=exp(-v)/2=v. Thus
 z_J=3vt=epsilon,
 z_Dk=(1-v+v^2)t <t<epsilon,
 H_module=3t=epsilon/v=:tau,
 Z_module=tau+v epsilon.                               (2)
The strict inequality t<epsilon uses v>1/3. The solution belongs to(1/3,2/5):
(1/3)exp(1/3)<1/2 because e<27/8, while(2/5)exp(2/5)>1/2 by exp(2/5)>5/4.

If J deletes its entire local bundle on these three branches, each D_k is
its branch's sole productive owner and is paid exactly vt=epsilon/3.
Therefore all changed other accounts remain below epsilon, and
 sum_{k=1}^3(z_Dk-z'_Dk)=Lambda epsilon,
 Z_module-Z'_module=(1+Lambda)epsilon.                 (3)
These are signed OTHER and TOTAL identities, respectively. Every opponent
actually loses here; there is no hidden positive-gain cancellation.

2. UNBOUNDED CORE AND TWO RESPONSE MODULES
Take T=1/L and rho=1+log(T/(2epsilon))=L-log L+1-log2. For sufficiently small
epsilon, rho>=3. Choose k=floor L core branches, each hardware T/k. Take
finitely many actual local owners B_j with coefficients0<d_j<=1, sum_j d_j=rho,
and d_1=1. Floor(rho) full coefficients and, if needed, one fractional positive
coefficient realize this exactly. Each B_j holds local d_j T/k on EVERY core
branch and no grid. The same owner is retained across all these branches.

Fragment each core branch's hardware among
 m=max(2,ceil((1-epsilon/T)T/(k epsilon)))
pure hardware owners, all distinct from the local owners and other branches.
Every hardware fragment's full local payment is at most epsilon. At the full
profile q_core=epsilon/T, so
 z_Bj=d_j epsilon,   Z_core=T+epsilon(rho-1).           (4)
Adjoin two disjoint copies of Section1, distinguished owners named G and A.
Their six mixed opponents are distinct from all core owners and each other.
Give B_1 all remaining hardware
                         a=1-T-2tau                   (5)
on an additional branch with zero local supply. Thus H=1 and M=T+2tau.
This stranded local hardware is productive in the outer stage through grid.
For small epsilon a>0, and every listed owner is active at the full profile.
The maximum full actual local payment is exactly epsilon, attained by B_1,G,A.
Counts diverge since both k and the number of full core local owners diverge.

3. FULL GRID, CAPS, AND BASELINE
Let Z=Z_core+2Z_module. Give only G,A positive grid reports:
 p_G=1,     p_A=1-Z+epsilon.                           (6)
For all sufficiently small epsilon, p_A>0, and A's full opposing account
 b_A=p_A+epsilon=1-Z+2epsilon lies strictly between0 and1. Indeed Z tends to0
and Z>=T>2epsilon. G has opposing account1+epsilon, capped at1. Every other
account is its local payment at most epsilon and hence uncapped.
Thus the full capped opposing total is EXACTLY S=2, while P+Z=2+epsilon.
Moreover
 P+M=2-epsilon(rho+2v-2)>1                            (7)
for small epsilon. Hence physical F=H=1, full outer coefficient c, and full
raw budget is EXACTLY C. Only G has a full capped opposing account.

4. THREE SEPARATE ACTUAL OWN REPORTS
These are separate reports in three individual envelopes, not simultaneous
deviations. All other owners use their full reports for the lower bound.

(i) B_1 deletes its local bundle on all core branches and retains hardware a.
The core coefficient multiplies by e; every core branch remains hardware short
since rho-1>=2. All changed core accounts remain uncapped for small epsilon.
The exact outer capped-total increment is
 Delta=epsilon[K rho-(2e-1)]>0.                         (8)
B_1's exact raw-payment gain is
                  g_1=c[a(1-exp(-Delta))-epsilon].      (9)

(ii) G deletes its local bundle on its own response module, retains p_G=1,
and changes nothing else. Its own cap stays1. By(3), S becomes2-Lambda epsilon.
Its exact raw-payment gain is
                  g_G=c[exp(Lambda epsilon)-1].        (10)

(iii) A deletes its local bundle on its own response module, retains p_A,
and changes nothing else. Its own account falls by epsilon and stays uncapped.
Now S becomes2-(1+Lambda)epsilon, and its exact raw-payment gain is
 g_A=c[exp((1+Lambda)epsilon)(b_A-epsilon)-b_A].          (11)

All three changed outer profiles remain hardware short for sufficiently small
epsilon. Every report is a permitted local-only whole-bundle decrease by its
original actual owner. Thus, at the SAME fixed full profile,
                    sum_i E_i >= C+g_1+g_G+g_A.        (12)

5. EVERY-SMALL-EPSILON ASYMPTOTICS
By definition of rho,
 Delta=epsilon[K(L-log L-log2)-e],
 Delta^2=o(epsilon), and T Delta=K epsilon+o(epsilon).
The O(epsilon) response hardware removes only2tau=O(epsilon) from a, affecting
g_1 by O(epsilon^2 L)=o(epsilon). Expanding(9) gives
 g_1=cK epsilon(L-log L)-c[2e+K log2]epsilon+o(epsilon).
Since b_A tends to1, (10),(11) each equal c Lambda epsilon+o(epsilon).
Substituting into(12) proves(1) for every sufficiently small real epsilon;
the fractional core coefficient eliminates any integer phase restriction.

The local constant Lambda is sharp for signed local response, but this does
NOT prove two such modules exhaust all possible residual funding mechanisms.
It supplies a stronger exact general lower construction and a reusable local
response theorem. No global residual optimality or novelty claim is made.
