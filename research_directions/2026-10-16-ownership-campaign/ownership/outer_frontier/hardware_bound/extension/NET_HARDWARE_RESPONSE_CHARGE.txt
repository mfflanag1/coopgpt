NET HARDWARE RESPONSE CHARGE AND A FURTHER GENERAL RESIDUAL UPPER
17 October 2026. Candidate, separate from all frozen dependencies.
Depends on EFFECTIVE_HARDWARE_ENTROPY_MASS.txt, INCOMING_RETENTION_PENALTY.txt,
SHARP_LOCAL_ONLY_ENTROPY.txt, the accepted sharp outgoing theorem, and the
at-least-two-full-cap exclusion in TWO_CAPPED_ACCOUNTS_DIFFUSE_GAP.txt.
Same unit-coefficient, original-exponential, actual-own-envelope setting.
No historical priority or sharp residual-coefficient claim.

RESULT
For the physical hardware-bottleneck diffuse supremum W(epsilon), with
K=e-1, c=1/(2e), C=1+c and L=log(1/epsilon),

 limsup [W-C-cKepsilon(L-log L)]/epsilon
                    <= -c[2+K log2].                   (NHC)

The older one-response lower is -c[e+1+Klog2], giving a comparison interval
of width cK. Newer two-response and shared-response constructions improve
that lower, so cK is not the current best interval width. The new charge is the necessary OTHER hardware loss accompanying
entropy-producing local response. It is not a different mechanism.

1. EXACT BRANCH NET RESPONSE, INCLUDING A POOL SWITCH
Hold all inputs except one owner's original-LONG coordinate fixed. Normalize
full short total to T=1; d<=1 is its full capped long report, rho=d+v>=1,
q=exp(1-rho)/2, V=qd. Let R be its remaining original-long ROLE after the
underreport. This role is distinguished algebraically from its original-short
role, even when the same actual owner holds both. There is no owner splitting
or change in the feasible reports. Then 0<=R<=V and there is B>=0 such that

 B<=KV-R,
                   Delta Z<=B(v-1)-(V-R),              (1)

where Delta Z is the change in the TOTAL local payments to all actual owners.
Restoring T multiplies B,V,R,Delta Z by T and leaves v dimensionless.

BEFORE A SWITCH. Reducing a capped long coordinate by u<=d gives
B=q(e^u-1), R=q(d-u)e^u. Opponents' original-long roles gain exactly Bv;
the owner's original-long role changes by R-V; the total original-short role
loses exactly B. This gives equality in (1). Moreover
B+R=q[(1+d-u)e^u-1]<=q(e^d-1)<=K V.
The middle expression is increasing in u because its derivative is
q(d-u)e^u>=0. R<=V follows from its derivative -q(1-d+u)e^u<=0 as u rises.

AFTER A SWITCH. The other original-long raw total is v<1, and the new short
total t lies in [v,1]. Let q_new be its coefficient and set
B=1-q-q_new>=0, R=(1-q_new)(t-v).
Opponents' original-long roles gain Bv. The total original-short role loses
(1-q)-q_new sum_k min(s_k,t)>=1-q-q_new=B, since sum_k s_k=1. This proves
(1). The already-proved post-switch retention inequality is
(1-q_new)(1+t-v)<=q(1+Kd), exactly B+R<=KV.
The remaining long role is monotone in its own remaining coordinate: on a
short-side smooth cell its derivative is
1-q_new-(t-v)q_new' >=1-q_new(1+rho_new)>=0,
since q_new'=q_new(rho_new-m)/t, where m is the number of opposing long
hardware coordinates above the new short total t on the smooth cell, t-v<=t, and
q_new(1+rho_new)<=1 for rho_new>=1. It joins continuously to the pre-switch
formula, proving R<=V. Zero-output endpoints follow by continuity. The
persistent-cap convention is the same as in the accepted primitive proof.

2. LOW-PRICE BRANCHES AND A SINGLE FULL CAPPED ACCOUNT
Normalize total hardware H=1. By the accepted two-full-cap exclusion, profiles
with at least two full opposing accounts d_k=1 have only half the logarithmic
premium and cannot affect the limsup in (NHC). Thus it suffices to consider
profiles with zero or one full capped account, including equality b_k=1.
Hardware restoration also lets us restrict every actual envelope to local
reports in the relevant region S>=3/2+e epsilon. The complementary region is
already a fixed amount below C.

Put eta=L^{-2}, and for sufficiently small epsilon let J_low consist of the
full hardware-short branches with q_j<=eta. Their capped ratios obey
rho_j>=1-log(2eta)>2. Even deleting one whole long coordinate leaves other
local capped mass (rho_j-d_j)T_j>T_j. Therefore their orientation NEVER switches
under the owner's local report. All other branches form the remainder.

For an actual negative-gap recipient i and its one considered local report,
let V=sum_{j in J_low}V_j, R=sum R_j and B=sum B_j from (1). Thus
0<=R<=V<=z_i<=epsilon and 0<=B<=KV-R. Let A be the FULL effective hardware
mass from EFFECTIVE_HARDWARE_ENTROPY_MASS.txt, not just its low-price part.
Weighted log-sum with B_j/V_j<=K and that file's exact effective entropy lemma
gives, using (1),

 sum_low Delta Z_j
 <=B log[K(A+2V)/(2B)]-B-(V-R).                         (2)

The actual own loss on those branches is at least V-R, since its original-short
role weakly falls too. Its total loss D is therefore at least V-R.

On a remaining hardware-short branch, q_j>eta, so rho_j<rho_eta:=1-log(2eta).
The sharp long-coordinate incoming bound gives
I_j<=K V_j(rho_j-d_j)<=K rho_eta z_ij.
On a branch whose LOCAL pool is originally short, the accepted short-local
incoming estimate is at most z_ij. Hence the total local-payment increase over
all remaining branches is at most
C_eta(z_i-V), where C_eta=max{1,K rho_eta}=O(log L).      (3)
This safely includes the recipient's unused full hardware roles. It does not
claim all its remaining branch payments are exactly z_i-V.

3. THE ONLY HIDDEN CAP LOSS IS o(epsilon) AT THE OPTIMIZER
If every full account is uncapped, clipping can only reduce the opposing-total
increase, so S'-S<=Delta Z. If there is one full capped account k, then

 S'-S<=Delta Z+(z_k-z_k')_+.                            (4)

For the negative recipient i, k cannot equal i because its full d_i<a_i<=1.
Fixed grid reports make (4) immediate coordinate by coordinate: full uncapped
coordinates have capped change at most their raw change; a full capped
coordinate's discrepancy is at most its raw positive loss.

On a low-price branch, only k's original-short HARDWARE role can decrease;
its original-long role weakly increases. The cap-loss contribution is at most
B_j h_kj/T_j<=K q_j h_kj. Its full hardware role is (1-q_j)h_kj, so summing
all low-price branches yields

 hidden_low <=K eta/(1-eta) sum_low (1-q_j)h_kj
            <=K eta/(1-eta) z_k<=K eta epsilon/(1-eta). (5)

On the remaining branches, the accepted sharp outgoing theorem bounds the
total positive loss to OTHER actual recipients, and hence k's loss, by
K times i's full payment on those branches. This is at most K(z_i-V). Thus,
combining (2)--(5), and writing h_eta=K eta epsilon/(1-eta),

 S'-S <= B log[K(A+2V)/(2B)]-B-(V-R)
                         +(C_eta+K)(epsilon-V)+h_eta,
 D>=V-R.                                               (6)

For zero full caps the same upper is valid by adding nonnegative terms.
These are branchwise bounds on actual whole-bundle reports, not independent
choices of opponent losses. They remain valid with arbitrary full overflow.

4. SUM ALL NEGATIVE RECIPIENTS VIA THE ACTUAL NET CHANGE
Write g=a_i-d_i>0. The exact change of its payment is
 q0[g(1-exp(-(S'-S)))-exp(-(S'-S))D].
For any real x, 1-exp(-x)<=x. Also S'-S<=I<=Kepsilon(1+L), so
exp(-(S'-S))>=beta=exp[-Kepsilon(1+L)]. Consequently

 gain_i<=q0[g(S'-S)-beta D].                            (7)

Substitute (6), relax A+2V upward to A+2epsilon, and let J_A(g) be the
supremum over 0<=R<=V<=epsilon and 0<=B<=KV-R of

 g{B log[K(A+2epsilon)/(2B)]-B
                  +(C_eta+K)(epsilon-V)+h_eta}
                              -(g+beta)(V-R).          (8)

At B=0 the weighted logarithm is zero. This is a supremum of affine functions
of g with nonpositive intercepts. It is nonnegative, convex, and J_A(0)=0.
Therefore the convex chord inequality and G_-<=1-A show that the sum of ALL
negative recipients' gains is at most q0 J_A(1-A). No actual owner is merged.

For sqrt(epsilon)<=A<=1/4, put g=1-A>=3/4. At fixed V,R the B derivative of
(8) is g{log[K(A+2epsilon)/(2B)]-2}, uniformly positive for small epsilon.
Thus B=KV-R. Its R derivative after this substitution is
 -g{log[K(A+2epsilon)/(2B)]-2}+(g+beta),
uniformly negative. Thus R=0. Its V derivative is then
 K g{log[(A+2epsilon)/(2V)]-2}-(g+beta)-g(C_eta+K).
It is uniformly positive: the logarithm is at least L/2-log2, whereas
C_eta+K=O(log L). Hence V=epsilon at the scalar maximum. Substitution gives

 J_A(1-A)=epsilon{K(1-A)[log((A+2epsilon)/(2epsilon))-1]
                                    -(1-A)-beta}
                         +(1-A)h_eta.                  (9)

Both h_eta=o(epsilon) and replacing A+2epsilon by A inside the logarithm costs
o(epsilon), uniformly in this middle A region. This is the promised stability
argument: high-price and originally short-local responses carry only O(log L)
per unit remaining own payment, while low-price entropy carries order L.
It is an exact scalar optimization for all sufficiently small epsilon, not
an assumption that real extremizers happen to concentrate there.

5. FINAL OPTIMIZATION AND THE STILL-OPEN COEFFICIENT
The two noncompeting A regions are excluded by EFFECTIVE_HARDWARE_ENTROPY_MASS
using its weaker valid bounds; profiles with at least two full caps are excluded
by TWO_CAPPED_ACCOUNTS_DIFFUSE_GAP. Thus optimize (9) in the middle region.
Up to a uniform o(1), (9)/epsilon is

 K(1-A)(L+log A-log2)-(K+1)(1-A)-beta.

The unique interior maximizer A=1/x satisfies
 x+log x=L+1-log2-(K+1)/K.
At it the value is K(x-2+1/x)-beta, hence

 Q_epsilon=K(L-log L)-2K-2-Klog2+o(1).                  (10)

Add the accepted positive-gap charge q0(alpha-1)(r+1), alpha=exp(Kepsilon),
and maximize the outer ratio exactly as in the effective-mass proof. The
upper is 1+c alpha exp{[alpha-1+epsilon Q_epsilon]/alpha}. Its expansion is

 C+cKepsilon(L-log L)-c[2+Klog2]epsilon+o(epsilon).

This proves (NHC). Compared with the older one-response lower, the
order-epsilon interval has exact width cK; newer response constructions
shrink this interval from below. No claim of sharpness is made for
this final coefficient. In particular the existing positive-gap charge was
not strengthened here; coupling it to near-extremal entropy profiles is a
separate unresolved issue.
