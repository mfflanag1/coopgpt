ROLE-SENSITIVE COROLLARY OF THE SHARP SIGNED-RESPONSE THEOREM
17 October 2026. Same fixed-owner, arbitrary-branch, local-only scope as
SHARP_SIGNED_OTHER_LOCAL_LOSS.txt. No change to that frozen proof is required.

For a given owner i, let U_i be the sum of its full LOCAL-role payments on
branches where local supply is originally short, and V_i the sum of its full
LOCAL-role payments on branches where local supply is originally long. Tied
branches can be assigned to either orientation consistently. Then any own
local-only whole-bundle decrease satisfies

             sum_{k!=i}(z_k-z'_k) <= U_i+Lambda V_i.     (RC)

This sharp bound excludes all of i's hardware-role payments. It implies the
frozen theorem because U_i+V_i<=z_i and Lambda>1.

Proof. The original-short proof already bounds by its own short LOCAL role U.
For an original-long branch the before-switch proof gives
 L<=q(exp(u)-1)(1-v)<=K v(1-v) qd <=Lambda qd,
using the same u<=dv bound. The post-switch positive-loss case gives
 L<=(1-q)(1-v); division directly by qd, rather than by the complete mixed
payment, yields the identical endpoint expression in Section3 of the frozen
proof. When the other hardware total is below v the signed loss is nonpositive.
Hence the long branch is bounded by Lambda times its own full local role qd.
Restore branch scales and sum signed losses. No owner splitting is invoked.

The exact two-owner attaining example in the frozen theorem has a pure-local
changed owner and attains Lambda V_i. A balanced pure-local deletion attains
U_i, so neither separate coefficient can be reduced. This is a useful
structural refinement, not a new proof of an outer capped funding factor.
