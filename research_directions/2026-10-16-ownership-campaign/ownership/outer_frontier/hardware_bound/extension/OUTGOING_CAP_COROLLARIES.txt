CAP-DECREASE COROLLARIES OF THE SHARP OUTGOING-LOSS THEOREM
16 October 2026. Conditional on SHARP_OUTGOING_LOSS.txt; no full funding claim.
Use the fixed full hardware threshold H>0 and restored full grid p. These are
algebraic cap comparisons after local payments have been computed at an actual
own-bundle underreport. Let b_k=p_k+z_k, b_k^i=p_k+z_k^i,
 S=sum_k min(b_k,H), S^i=sum_k min(b_k^i,H),
 D_i^cap=min(b_i,H)-min(b_i^i,H)>=0.
The map x->min(p_k+x,H) is monotone and1-Lipschitz. Therefore the sharp outgoing
positive-loss theorem gives
                 S-S^i <=D_i^cap+(e-1)z_i.             (C1)
In particular (S-S^i)_+<=e z_i. This bound is tied to the actual deviating
owner's full local payment; it does not permit a recipient with z_i=0 to erase
other local accounts. If its own capped account does not change, the stronger
bound (S-S^i)_+<=(e-1)z_i holds directly.

For a concrete ownership specialization, assume a single owner g reports
p_g>=H and all other grid reports are zero. The restored outer pool remains
hardware-short. Every individual local account is at most M<=H, so
 S=H+Z-z_g, S^g=H+sum_{k!=g}z_k^g.
The grid owner's own cap stays H. Write u=(S-S^g)_+/H. Then
       u<=min{(Z-z_g)/H, (e-1)z_g/H},
       q0=(1/2)exp[-(Z-z_g)/H].
After restoring its outer hardware H_g and grid, its positive outer-payment
increase over the full raw payment is at most
 q0(H-H_g)[exp(min{(Z-z_g)/H,(e-1)z_g/H})-1].           (C2)
Indeed its restored payment is H_g+q(H-H_g), and q=q0 exp((S-S^g)/H).
For a cap-total increase the payment cannot rise, so the positive part is
covered. The actual underreport is bounded by the restored comparison through
primitive own-monotonicity. Taking the supremum over the actual owner g's
underreports preserves(C2).

The corollary is uniform over local mixed ownership and all owner/branch counts.
It does not bound the sum of other owners' envelope gains, and does not alone
prove a C^2 budget theorem for this or the unrestricted mixed-grid institution.
