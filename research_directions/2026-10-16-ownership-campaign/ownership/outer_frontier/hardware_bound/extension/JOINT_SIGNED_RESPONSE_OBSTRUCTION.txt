EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO THE PROPOSED TWO-RECIPIENT SIGNED-RESPONSE SUM
17 October 2026. Fixed actual owners, rational physical reports, local-only
underreports. Original symmetric exponential two-input primitive. This is a
counterexample to the SPECIFIC proposed inequality, not a funding lower bound
against all mechanisms and not a counterexample to the sharp signed theorem.

PROPOSED INEQUALITY (FALSE)
For two distinct actual owners A,G and two independently selected own local
underreports, with all hardware fixed, one might hope that

 sum_{k!=G}(z_k-z_k^{[G]})
       +sum_{k notin{A,G}}(z_k-z_k^{[A]})
                      <=Lambda(z_A+z_G),                (J)

where Lambda is the exact signed-other constant from
SHARP_SIGNED_OTHER_LOCAL_LOSS.txt. The second term excludes G, as occurs when
G's outer account stays capped and hides its gains during A's underreport.
The two reports are separate individual comparisons, not simultaneous changes.
Even taking positive parts of the two displayed sums does not repair(J).

EXACT THREE-OWNER INSTANCE
Owner B holds hardware1 and no local supply. Owner G holds local1 and no
hardware. Owner A holds local2/5 and no hardware. There are no other branch
resources. Both pools and all owner identities are fixed throughout.
Let q=exp(-2/5)/2. The full hardware-short profile has payments

 z_B=1-q,             z_G=q,             z_A=(2/5)q.     (1)

If G deletes its local supply, remaining local total2/5 is short. B's hardware
is capped at2/5, so its coefficient is1/2 and
 z_B^{[G]}=1/5,       z_A^{[G]}=1/5,      z_G^{[G]}=0.
Therefore the first signed sum in(J) is
             L_G=(3/5)(1-q).                            (2)

If A deletes its local supply, hardware and G's local supply balance at1.
The new payments to B,G are1/2 each and A is paid0. Hence the second sum is
             L_A=z_B-1/2=1/2-q.                         (3)
Both losses are strictly positive. Equations(1)--(3) give

 (L_G+L_A)/(z_G+z_A)=(11exp(2/5)-8)/7 >6/5>Lambda.      (4)

The first strict inequality is certified using the four-degree positive Taylor
lower bound for exp(2/5), which already exceeds82/55.
For the second, let w=45/128. An exact rational Taylor-tail upper gives
w exp(w)<1/2, hence v_*>w. Since(1-v)^2/v decreases on(0,1),
 Lambda<(1-w)^2/w=6889/5760<6/5.
The companion script verifies both rational inequalities with explicit errors
rather than assertions; it passes under python -I -O. No floating comparison
is used in the proof.

GROWING FAMILY AND DIFFUSE ACTUAL OWNERS
The obstruction is homogeneous and persists with arbitrary productive branch
counts. For any m>=3 and epsilon>0, take m branches of hardware
 t=epsilon/(m q), held by m DISTINCT actual owners B_j. The SAME G holds local t
on each, and the SAME A holds local(2/5)t on each. Then z_G=epsilon,
z_A=(2/5)epsilon, and each actual z_Bj=(1-q)epsilon/(m q)<epsilon, because
exp(2/5)<3/2 implies(1-q)/q=2exp(2/5)-1<2<m.
Both whole-bundle deletions give the same ratio(4), independently of m. Thus
arbitrary actual-owner fragmentation or a diffuse local-payment condition does
not restore(J). All actual owners and permitted reports are fixed per instance.

WHY THIS MATTERS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT SHOW
The sharp signed theorem counts G's GAIN when A deletes its local supply;
that gain equals1/2-q and cancels B's loss. In(J), G's gain is excluded because
an outer cap may hide it. Meanwhile G retains a different profitable response
for its own envelope. Thus independently sharp signed bounds cannot simply be
summed after deleting one recipient from the second signed sum. This identifies
a precise remaining joint cap-accounting constraint.

The example says nothing adverse about the validity of the original sharp
signed theorem, global diffuse asymptotics, or current funding bounds. It is
not a claimed counterexample to any mechanism-level subsidy conjecture.

OPTIONAL ALGEBRAIC CONSEQUENCE FOR A FUTURE LOWER INTEGRATION
If A's unused3/5 of an epsilon local-payment budget is allocated to an
independent sharp-response module, its extra signed response is(3/5)Lambda
in epsilon units. The combined response coefficient is then
 J=(11exp(2/5)-8)/5+(3/5)Lambda >2Lambda,
by(4). This is an exact response identity, but a standalone global asymptotic
lower needs the same grid/core/cap checks as the separately frozen pair lower.
No such extension is claimed as proved in this note; the prior frozen lower
remains unchanged for review.
