OWNER-SPECIFIC HARDWARE RESTORATION: A NON-ASYMPTOTIC COROLLARY
16 October 2026. Candidate corollary of GLOBAL_DIFFUSE_SECOND_ORDER.txt §3;
independent audit of that main theorem is in progress. No novelty claim.

Normalize a physical hardware-bottleneck full profile by H=1. Let z_i be the
full local payment of fixed actual owner i and S=sum_k min(p_k+z_k,1).
No diffuseness assumption on any OTHER owner is required.

If S>=3/2+e z_i, owner i's actual own-report envelope can be optimized while
reporting all its hardware and grid truthfully. It may still profit by changing
its local-resource bundle. Every such change preserves the same actual owners.

Proof. The main theorem's Section 3 uses the owner's own full z_i throughout:
after any of its mixed underreports and grid restoration, the current outer
capped ratio is at least S-e z_i>=3/2. Its exact hardware derivative is positive
in both outer gap signs, using the all-profile local derivative estimates. No
step uses any other owner's local-payment upper bound. Thus full hardware can
be restored without reducing the actual payment; grid restoration is always
harmless by outer primitive own monotonicity. The same proof handles all pool
switches before restoration, overflow, persistent cap equalities and zero ends.

For S>3/2, only owners in the fixed full-profile set
 J={i:z_i>(S-3/2)/e}
can fall outside this guarantee. Since Z<=C=1+1/(2e),
 |J|<eZ/(S-3/2)<=(e+1/2)/(S-3/2).
In particular, for S>=2 there are at most SIX such owners, since the right side
is at most 2e+1<7. For S>=5/2 there are at most three; for S>=3 at most two;
for S>=7/2 at most one. For S>=3/2+e every owner satisfies the guarantee because
z_i<=M<=1.

This is a restriction on which actual owners can need hardware-withholding
optimization; it does not replace their local-resource optimization by a finite
problem or prove an equivalent bounded-owner instance. Nor does it assert that
owners outside the guarantee in fact benefit from withholding hardware. It is
not by itself a general funding bound. Hardware below the owner-specific
threshold can be retained in every actual own-envelope computation, even when
other owners have large local payments and arbitrary mixed grid ownership.
