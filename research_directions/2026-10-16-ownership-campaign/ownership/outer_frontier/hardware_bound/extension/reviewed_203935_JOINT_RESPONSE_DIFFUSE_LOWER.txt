17 October 2026. Separate from the frozen sharp pair lower; no new scalar search.
Original exponential actual own-report envelope, fixed actual owners, all-real
reports. C=1+1/(2e), c=1/(2e), K=e-1, L=log(1/epsilon).

Let v_* exp(v_*)=1/2 and Lambda=(1-v_*)^2/v_*. Put w=2/5 and
 q_J=exp(-w)/2,
 D_G=(1-w)[2exp(w)-1],
 D_A=exp(w)-1+(1-w)Lambda,
 J=D_G+D_A=(11/5)exp(2/5)-8/5+(3/5)Lambda.              (1)
The exact certificate in JOINT_SIGNED_RESPONSE_OBSTRUCTION.txt proves

THEOREM. The same general diffuse supremum W(epsilon) as in
SHARP_SIGNED_RESPONSE_DIFFUSE_PAIR_LOWER.txt satisfies

 liminf [W-C-cKepsilon(L-log L)]/epsilon
                         >=c[J-2e-Klog2].              (2)

The lower is realized for every sufficiently small positive epsilon by finite
fixed-owner instances with productive branch and active owner counts tending
to infinity. It is a lower bound for the specified actual envelope, not for
all truthful mechanisms. The general residual coefficient remains OPEN.

1. TWO COUPLED RESPONSE MODULES AND ACTUAL OWNER BUDGETS
First create THREE joint branches with hardware
 t_J=epsilon/(3q_J). Each is held by its own pure hardware owner B_j.
The SAME owner G holds local t_J on all three; the SAME A holds local w t_J
on all three. Then
 z_G=epsilon, z_A,joint=w epsilon,
 each z_Bj=(1-q_J)t_J<epsilon,
 tau_J=3t_J=2exp(w)epsilon,
 Z_joint=tau_J+w epsilon.                              (3)
The hardware-payment inequality follows from exp(2/5)<3/2 and three copies.

Next create THREE independent residual branches with hardware
 t_R=(1-w)epsilon/(3v_*). On each branch a distinct mixed owner D_j holds
hardware t_R and local v_* t_R, while A holds local t_R. By the exact sharp
response module,
 z_A,residual=(1-w)epsilon, each z_Dj<t_R<epsilon,
 tau_R=3t_R=(1-w)epsilon/v_*,
 Z_residual=tau_R+v_*(1-w)epsilon.                      (4)
All six opposing owners are distinct; G,A remain the SAME actual owners
across their listed branches. Thus full z_G=z_A=epsilon and all other full
response local payments are strictly below epsilon.

2. UNBOUNDED CORE, GRID AND BASELINE
Use EXACTLY the core construction of Sections2--3 of the frozen sharp pair

Put all remaining hardware
                       a=1-T-tau_J-tau_R               (5)
on a zero-local-supply branch, held by the same core owner B_core,1. It does
not change its local payment epsilon. Total H=1 and M=T+tau_J+tau_R.
For small epsilon a>0. Let Z=Z_core+Z_joint+Z_residual and set
                       p_G=1, p_A=1-Z+epsilon.         (6)
All other grids are zero. For small epsilon p_A>0 and

Every actual full local payment is at most epsilon. The productive core
branches and its active local owner count both grow without bound.

3. EXACT THREE ACTUAL DEVIATIONS
Each comparison below is a SEPARATE own report, with all other reports full.
For the core owner, delete its full local bundle and retain hardware a. The
same exact core calculation gives
 Delta=epsilon[K rho-(2e-1)],
 g_core=c[a(1-exp(-Delta))-epsilon].                    (7)

For G, delete its joint local bundle only and retain grid1. Its own cap stays1.
On the joint module, A and the pure hardware owners share the remaining local
production w tau_J equally. Their SIGNED total loss is D_G epsilon by the
exact obstruction. A's counterreport account may increase by O(epsilon) but
remains uncapped: its full cap distance Z-2epsilon is asymptotic to1/L.
Thus S becomes2-D_G epsilon, and G's exact gain is
                       g_G=c[exp(D_G epsilon)-1].       (8)

For A, delete its local bundle on BOTH response modules and retain grid p_A.
On the joint module, G's local payment increases from epsilon to exp(w)epsilon;
its grid cap hides this gain completely. The joint pure hardware owners lose
[exp(w)-1]epsilon. On the residual module, the mixed opponents' total loss is
(1-w)Lambda epsilon. A's own local payment falls by epsilon and stays uncapped.
Therefore S becomes2-(1+D_A)epsilon, and its exact gain is
 g_A=c[exp((1+D_A)epsilon)(b_A-epsilon)-b_A].             (9)

All changed non-grid accounts remain O(epsilon), hence uncapped; G remains
capped, and A remains strictly positive and uncapped in every comparison.
All changed outer profiles remain hardware short. Thus at this SAME full
profile the actual envelope budget is at least C+g_core+g_G+g_A.

4. EVERY-SMALL-EPSILON ASYMPTOTICS
The core proof gives
 g_core=cKepsilon(L-log L)-c[2e+Klog2]epsilon+o(epsilon).
The response hardware tau_J+tau_R=O(epsilon) changes that expansion only by
O(epsilon^2 L)=o(epsilon). Since b_A tends to1, (8),(9) respectively equal
cD_Gepsilon+o(epsilon), cD_Aepsilon+o(epsilon). Their sum proves(2).
The fractional core coefficient allows every sufficiently small REAL epsilon;
there is no restriction to a subsequence or varying identities within an instance.

This lift is included because it demonstrates that the joint-sum obstruction
changes the actual asymptotic lower, rather than being a loose intermediate
inequality only. It still does not solve the global residual coefficient.
