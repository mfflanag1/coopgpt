HEAVY-OWNER / LIGHT-TAIL FUNDING REDUCTION
16 October 2026. Candidate consequence of SHARP_INCOMING_ENTROPY_v2.txt.
All owner identities remain fixed. The results bound contributions to the actual
own-report envelope; they do not merge owners, alter the technology, or claim
an equivalent finite-dimensional report space. Arbitrary full grid overflow,
real reports, mixed bundles, and unbounded owner/branch counts remain allowed.

1. NOTATION
At a physical hardware bottleneck normalize H=1. Let M<=1 be physical local
output, z_i local payments, a_i hardware shares, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), S=sum_i d_i,
r=S-1>=0, q0=exp(-r)/2. The full raw outer payment is
 N_i=a_i+q0(d_i-a_i).
Let E_i be the actual own-report envelope. Then E_i>=N_i, since truthful reporting
is among the feasible choices. Put K=e-1 and define
 f_M(z)=z[1+log(M/z)] for0<z<=M, f_M(0)=0,
 b_M(z)=exp(-K f_M(z)),
 psi_K(t)=(exp(Kt)-1)/t for t>0, psi_K(0)=K.
If M=0, all responses vanish and every E_i=N_i; assume M>0 below.

The sharp incoming/outgoing response proofs give the INDIVIDUAL bounds
 E_i-N_i<=q0(exp(Kz_i)-1)(d_i-a_i), ifd_i>a_i,             (P)
 E_i-N_i<=q0(1-b_M(z_i))(a_i-d_i), ifa_i>d_i.             (N)
Zero gaps haveE_i=N_i. These comparisons apply to every actual own underreport
before taking its supremum, including outer switches and arbitrary overflow.

2. A COUPLED TWO-MOMENT BOUND FOR ANY SUBSET OF OWNERS
Fix any subset L of actual owners; a useful choice is L={i:z_i<=epsilon}.
Let t=max_{i in L}z_i (zero for an empty subset), and define
 G_- =sum_{i in L,a_i>d_i}(a_i-d_i),
 W_- =sum_{i in L,a_i>d_i}z_i(a_i-d_i),
 W_+ =sum_{i in L,d_i>a_i}z_i(d_i-a_i).
Then

 sum_{i in L}(E_i-N_i)
 <=q0 { psi_K(t) W_+
       +G_-[1-b_M(W_-/G_-)] }.                          (T-moment)

IfG_-=0 the second term is zero; ifW_-=0 it also equals zero. The bound retains
both the owners' actual local payments and their actual outer gap weights.
In particular a tail of many owners with substantial grid but negligible local
payments produces a small correction; tail cardinality does not appear.

PROOF. Convexity ofexp(Kz) gives the upper chord
 exp(Kz)-1<=psi_K(t)z for0<=z<=t.
For0<z<=M, differentiating b=b_M gives
 b'=-K log(M/z)b,
 b''=[K/z+K^2 log^2(M/z)]b>0.
The continuous extensionb(0)=1 is convex on[0,M]. Weighted Jensen with weights
(a_i-d_i)/G_- therefore yields
 sum_{i in L,a_i>d_i}(a_i-d_i)b_M(z_i)>=G_- b_M(W_-/G_-).
Apply(P),(N) and sum. This proves(T-moment) without replacing any actual owner
or requiring its optimizing report to coincide with another owner's report.

3. AN EXPLICIT UNIFORM TAIL CHARGE COUPLED TO HEAVY ACCOUNTS
TakeL={i:z_i<=epsilon},0<epsilon<=1, andJ=N\L. Put
 A=exp(Kepsilon), B=exp(-Kepsilon[1+log(1/epsilon)]),
 a_J=sum_{i in J}a_i, d_J=sum_{i in J}d_i, Q_J=d_J-a_J.
AsM<=1, (P),(N) imply
 sum_{i in L}(E_i-N_i)
 <=q0[(A-1)G_+^L+(1-B)G_-^L].                            (4)
ButG_+^L-G_-^L=r-Q_J andG_-^L<=1-a_J, hence

 sum_{i in L}(E_i-N_i)
 <=(exp(-r)/2){(A-1)(r-Q_J)+(A-B)(1-a_J)}.               (T-fixed)

This is the requested coupling: heavy owners' full HARDWARE mass a_J and capped
OPPOSING mass d_J restrict the possible contribution of every remaining owner.
It is generally stronger than discarding the heavy accounts. The moment bound
in Section2 can be taken in minimum with(T-fixed), using the same actual data.

For a bound independent of the remaining grid total, put
 u=A-1>0, v=(A-B)(1-a_J)-uQ_J,
 r_min=max(0,d_J-1), r_hat=max(r_min,1-v/u).
Necessarilyr>=r_min, since S>=d_J. Elementary one-variable differentiation gives

 sum_{i in L}(E_i-N_i)
 <= T_epsilon(a_J,d_J)
 :=(exp(-r_hat)/2)(u r_hat+v).                            (T-heavy)

At every feasible r>=r_min the affine expressionu r+v is nonnegative:
ifQ_J>1-a_J use r>=Q_J-(1-a_J), and otherwise its value at zero is already
nonnegative. Its exponential product increases to1-v/u and decreases thereafter,
which proves the maximum formula. No geometric or strategic equivalence with
a problem involving onlyJ is claimed; the actual heavy-owner envelopes E_i
still depend on every report and branch of the original instance.

A simpler bound, valid without any heavy-account information, is

 sum_{i in L}(E_i-N_i)<= (A-B)/2.                         (T-simple)

Indeed G_-^L<=1 and G_+^L<=G_+<=r+1, so(4) is at most
(exp(-r)/2)[(A-1)r+A-B], whose maximum forr>=0 occurs at0 becauseB<=1.

4. FINITE CONCENTRATION OF ANY FUNDING FAILURE
For everyprofile,
 sum_i E_i<=sum_i N_i+sum_{i in J}(E_i-N_i)
                    +T_epsilon(a_J,d_J),               (R)
with the stronger moment correction available. Since sum_iN_i<=C=1+1/(2e)
and |J|epsilon<sum_i z_i<=CM<=C, every fixed excess above C can be localized
to a bounded number of actual heavy owners, with an explicit tail error:

 sum_i E_i>C+eta
 ==> sum_{i in J}(E_i-N_i)>eta-(A-B)/2,
     |J|<C/epsilon.                                     (L)

This has useful content whenever(A-B)/2<eta. The tail error(A-B)/2 tends tozero
as epsilon decreases tozero, uniformly over all owner counts, branch counts,
report magnitudes, and grid overflow. Thus an arbitrarily large population of
small-local-payment owners cannot conceal an order-one envelope funding loss.
The high-dimensional whole-bundle reports of heavy owners remain unrestricted.

5. SCOPE
The reduction neither proves nor disproves unrestrictedC^2 funding. It gives
an exact error budget and two coupled moments for treating many remaining
owners, while preserving actual whole-bundle incentives. The finite heavy set
is selected from one full profile only; it is not fixed across reports and
must not be used to redefine strategic identities or the mechanism. The
original rule and envelope remain fixed on the entire report domain.
