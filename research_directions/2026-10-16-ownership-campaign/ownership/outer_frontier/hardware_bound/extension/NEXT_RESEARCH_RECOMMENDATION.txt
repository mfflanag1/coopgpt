NEXT RESEARCH RECOMMENDATION -- A DEPTH-INDEPENDENT OWNERSHIP THEOREM
17 October 2026. Advisory handoff, not a new theorem or novelty claim.

RECOMMENDATION
Make the next main goal a uniform subsidy theorem for UNIT-PRODUCTION RESOURCE
FORESTS with arbitrary fixed mixed owners: a truthful coalition-covering rule
with funding kappa<2, independent of resource count, owner count AND tree depth.
The resource graph, not the owner-compatibility graph, is required to be a forest.
Allow one actual owner to hold any fixed bundle across branches and levels.
Precisely, graph vertices are genuine resource rows; each edge is an activity
consuming ONE unit at each of its TWO endpoints and earning ONE unit. Row
capacities are sums of actual-owner reports, the resource graph is acyclic,
and all owner eligibilities remain fixed. This excludes unbounded-arity serial
complementarity and general min-linear production, for which the manuscript
already proves unbounded funding; no constant conjecture is made for that class.
Treat the current actual-envelope C^2 question as a bounded secondary route.
Do not devote the main campaign to more order-epsilon response gadgets.

WHY THIS IS THE NEXT STEP IN SIGNIFICANCE
The accepted323/200 theorem already meets the depth-two campaign's initial
below-two gate. The exact diffuse law C+cKepsilon[L-log L]+O(epsilon) explains
this envelope's asymptotic behavior; sharp signed Lambda and the exact joint
cap-hiding obstruction explain why its final constant does not follow from
independent local bounds. Another small numerical improvement would refine an
already substantial candidate. A depth-independent theorem would instead
extend the ownership result to arbitrarily large production networks and
supply a reusable composition method. Economically it asks whether expanding
an acyclic network of complementary resource pools necessarily compounds the
funding cost of truthful capacity sharing. It remains a stylized production
model, not an empirical GPU/energy result or a model of private operating costs.

THE PRECISE FIRST LEMMA / NO-GO GATE
Use the existing exact separator state, not a new topology catalogue. For a
new shared resource of capacity P attached to disjoint child components with
boundary capacities h_j, let A_j be child output after removing the boundary
resource and M_j=F_j-A_j. Then
 F=A+min(H,P+M), A=sum A_j, H=sum h_j, M=sum M_j.
The obstruction is already known: M_j can DECREASE when the same owner's
internal substitute capacity increases, even though A_j+M_j increases.
For h--l--u, A=min(l,u), M=min(h,(l-u)_+). Thus feeding M into an individually
monotone primitive, or retaining an efficient vertex-core majorant, is invalid.

Target a COMPENSATED SEPARATOR LEMMA: construct payments from the coupled
baseline/residual states so that gains in an owner's baseline payment compensate
its own adverse residual change, with actual-coalition coverage and one SAME
funding constant kappa<2 before and after adjoining an arbitrary number of child
components. The invariant must permit arbitrary child depth; a product of
constants per level does not pass. Its report comparison must keep the actual
owner's entire bundle fixed as one strategic identity, including reports that
change both A_j and M_j on many children simultaneously. Cap hiding must be
accounted for jointly, not replaced by signed Lambda charges separately.

Run one bounded attack on this lemma. Accept either a genuinely recursive
construction with a depth-independent bound, or an exact actual-owner report
chain showing why the explicitly proposed coupled-state invariant cannot close.
A failure of one invariant is a method obstruction, not an unrestricted funding
lower. Do not repeat the already blocked fixed component-cover approach or the
virtual-vertex-core majorant: their growing-family obstructions are established.

KEEP THE TWO RESEARCH QUESTIONS SEPARATE
(A) Actual-envelope optimization: prove C^2 for the CURRENT depth-two envelope,
or find an exact admissible profile exceeding it. Either is useful; a failure
would rule out that numerical conjecture for ONE rule only.
(B) Unrestricted mechanism design: construct any eligible rule for all resource
forests, or derive an actual fixed-owner transition dual that lower-bounds EVERY
eligible rule. The present323/200, diffuse law and cap-response examples do not
settle(B). A lower-bound attempt must combine coalition constraints with genuine
cross-profile incentive inequalities, not assume the current exponential rule.
The single-pool ternary/binary population families already stop at9/8, so merely
adding owners or report-grid points in those families is not a new direction.

DECISION RULE
Prioritize the separator program if it yields a coupled invariant with a proved
one-step consequence. If it produces only another fixed-depth constant, a
sampling plot or an unproved recursively accumulating charge, stop and switch
to unrestricted transition-dual lower bounds. Do not rename that outcome a
breakthrough. Historical priority and specialist validation remain separate gates.
