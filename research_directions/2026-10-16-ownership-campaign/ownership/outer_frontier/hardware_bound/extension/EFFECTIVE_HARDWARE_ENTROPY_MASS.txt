EFFECTIVE HARDWARE MASS IN THE ALL-OWNER ENTROPY BOUND
17 October 2026. Candidate general lemma and asymptotic consequence.
Depends on accepted GLOBAL_DIFFUSE_SECOND_ORDER.txt, SHARP_LOCAL_ONLY_ENTROPY.txt,
and INCOMING_RETENTION_PENALTY.txt. No change to those frozen arguments.
Unit-coefficient depth-two model, original exponential primitive, fixed actual
owners, arbitrary real mixed reports, branch counts, and full grid overflow.
No historical-priority or optimal-mechanism claim.

RESULT
Let W(epsilon) denote the ACTUAL own-report-envelope funding supremum over
physical hardware-bottleneck profiles, normalized H=1 and max_i z_i<=epsilon.
Write K=e-1, c=1/(2e), C=1+c and L=log(1/epsilon). Then

 limsup_{epsilon->0} [W-C-cKepsilon(L-log L)]/epsilon
 <= c[K-2-K log2].                                      (EHM)

The earlier general upper was c(K-2). The new inequality recovers the precise
hardware expenditure on entropy-producing branches without counting unrelated
high-price branches as free entropy capacity. The accepted one-capped lower
is -c[e+1+K log2]; the order-epsilon gap remains nonzero (exact width 2cK).
This is a sharper general upper, not a claim that its coefficient is attained.

1. FULL-PROFILE HARDWARE ROLE AND THE NEGATIVE-GAP MASS
Let J consist of full-profile branches whose hardware pool is short or tied;
assign every full tie to this convention. On j write T_j=H_j>0 and q_j for its
full exponential coefficient. Set

 A_j=(1-q_j)T_j,   A=sum_{j in J}A_j.

Thus A is the TOTAL actual hardware-role payment on these branches. It is
not a report, a new owner, or a resource transferred between owners. If X is
their full physical production, then A=X-Q where Q=sum_j q_j T_j, and
X/2<=A<=X<=1. The hardware role of every actual owner on every branch is at
most its hardware holding. Let U_i be its total hardware-role payment across
ALL branches. Nonnegative grid and local roles imply d_i>=z_i>=U_i, so

 G_-:=sum_i(a_i-d_i)_+ <=sum_i(a_i-U_i)=1-sum_i U_i<=1-A. (1)

This proves the exact proposed refinement of the old 1-X/2 accounting. Zero
production branches contribute zero, and equality conventions cause no problem.

2. A POINTWISE EFFECTIVE-MASS ENTROPY INEQUALITY
On one original-long-coordinate branch normalize full short total to T>0.
Write d=min(own long report,T)/T in (0,1], rho>=1, q=exp(1-rho)/2,
V=q d T, v=rho-d, and A_b=(1-q)T. In the application the long coordinate
is LOCAL, so A_b is exactly the branch's hardware-role mass defined above.
Then, for every such branch,

              v<=log[(A_b+2V)/(2V)].                    (2)

PROOF. Put u=A_b/V=(1-q)/(qd)>=1, since q<=1/2 and d<=1. Solving for q gives
q=1/(1+ud), whence
 v=1-d+log[(1+ud)/2].
With w=1+ud, the elementary maximum of log w-w/u over w>0 is log u-1.
Consequently
 v=1+1/u+log(w/2)-w/u <= log(u/2)+1/u.
For x=1/u in [0,1], concavity gives
log(1+2x)>=x log3>=x, because e<3. Therefore
log(u/2)+1/u<=log[(u+2)/2], proving (2). This argument needs neither a lower
bound on q nor a positive lower bound on d. At V=0 the associated incoming
quantity is zero and the weighted formulas below use their continuous values.

Unlike the cruder log[T/(2V)], (2) counts the actual full hardware role A_b,
plus only 2V. The extra 2V is asymptotically negligible in the relevant regime;
no assertion that it can be dropped pointwise is needed.

3. ALL BRANCHES, ACTUAL OWN LOSS, AND POOL SWITCHES
Work first where S>=3/2+e epsilon, so the accepted exact restoration theorem
allows every actual owner to retain all hardware/grid and vary only its local
bundle. For a fixed owner and each j in J let V_j=q_j d_j T_j be its full
long-LOCAL role and D_j>=0 its actual own local-payment loss at the considered
local report. Set R_j=(V_j-D_j)_+ and W_j=K V_j-R_j.
The accepted sharp incoming and retention lemmas imply, INCLUDING AFTER A
POOL SWITCH,

 I_j<=W_j v_j,  0<=R_j<=V_j, (K-1)V_j<=W_j<=K V_j.       (3)

For D_j<V_j this is precisely retention. For D_j>=V_j before a switch,
I_j<=q_j T_j(e^{d_j}-1)v_j<=K V_j v_j. After a switch, retention equation (4)
gives (1-q_new)(1+t-v_j)<=q_j(1+K d_j); since t>=v_j, this implies
1-q_new<=q_j(1+K d_j), and hence
I_j<=T_j v_j(1-q_j-q_new)<=K V_j v_j. Thus (3) includes both sides of a
pool switch explicitly. All omitted zero roles have I_j=0.
Combining (2), weighted log-sum, and W_j/V_j<=K gives

 sum_j I_j
 <=sum_j W_j log[(A_j+2V_j)/(2V_j)]
 <=W log{[sum_j (W_j/V_j)(A_j+2V_j)]/(2W)}
 <=W log[K(A+2V)/(2W)],                                 (4)

where V=sum_j V_j, R=sum_j R_j and W=KV-R. Using ALL A_j, including branches
on which this owner has V_j=0, only increases the bound. Contributions from
original-short LOCAL coordinates are at most z_i-V, exactly as in the accepted
joint local-only entropy lemma. The actual total own local loss D is at least
V-R, because losses on all other branches are nonnegative. Therefore

 I<=z_i-V+W log[K(A+2V)/(2W)],
 D>=V-R, 0<=R<=V<=z_i<=epsilon.                         (5)

No branch was deleted and no actual owner was merged. The price q_j and A_j
refer to the fixed FULL profile; changing branch orientation later is already
covered by (3).

4. THE SAME CONVEX CHARGE, NOW WITH EFFECTIVE MASS A
For a full negative gap g=a_i-d_i>0, the accepted exact own-loss comparison is
 gain_i<=q0[g I-beta(g+1)D],
 beta=exp[-Kepsilon(1+L)], q0=exp(1-S)/2.
The sharp local-only incoming bound justifies beta for all sufficiently small
epsilon, as explicitly recorded in INCOMING_RETENTION_PENALTY.txt.

Relax A+2V in (5) upward to A+2epsilon. Define J_A(g) as the supremum over
0<=R<=V<=epsilon, W=KV-R of

 g{epsilon-V+W log[K(A+2epsilon)/(2W)]}
                   -beta(g+1)(V-R).                   (6)

This is a supremum of affine functions of g with nonpositive intercepts.
It is convex, nonnegative, J_A(0)=0, and nondecreasing for g>=0. Hence by the
convex chord inequality and (1), the SUM of ALL negative owners' gains is
at most q0 J_A(1-A). This is a scalar upper-bound argument; it is not an
implementation by merged strategic identities.

Suppose sqrt(epsilon)<=A<=1/4, and put g=1-A>=3/4. At fixed V, differentiating
(6) in R gives
 -g{log[K(A+2epsilon)/(2W)]-1}+beta(g+1),
which is uniformly negative for small epsilon because W<=Kepsilon and the
logarithm is at least L/2-log2. Thus R=0 at the maximum. The derivative in V
when R=0 is
 K g{log[(A+2epsilon)/(2V)]-1}-g-beta(g+1),
which is uniformly positive for small epsilon. Thus V=epsilon and

 J_A(1-A)=K(1-A)epsilon log[(A+2epsilon)/(2epsilon)]
                       -beta(2-A)epsilon.             (7)

Throughout this A range, replacing log(A+2epsilon) by log A makes an error
at most 2epsilon/A<=2sqrt(epsilon). Hence the error in (7) is o(epsilon),
uniformly over every finite instance, with no owner-count dependence.

5. EXCLUSION OF THE TWO NONCOMPETING A REGIONS
If A<=sqrt(epsilon), drop retention and use (5) with W=KV, or apply (2) directly.
This gives
 I<=epsilon+K V log[(A+2epsilon)/(2V)], 0<=V<=epsilon.
When A+2epsilon>=2e epsilon, the maximum of the logarithmic term occurs at
V=epsilon; otherwise its unconstrained maximum is (A+2epsilon)/(2e), times K,
and is O(epsilon). Thus uniformly in this region
 I<= (K/2)epsilon L+O(epsilon).
Negative-gap mass is at most one, so this region has funding at most
C+(cK/2)epsilon L+O(epsilon), after the accepted outer-ratio maximization.

If A>=1/4, (1) gives negative-gap mass at most 3/4, while the generic incoming
bound is at most Kepsilon L+O(epsilon). This region has funding at most
C+(3cK/4)epsilon L+O(epsilon). The positive-gap contribution is uniformly
O(epsilon) after outer-ratio maximization in both regions.
Both are eventually below the accepted lower with full leading coefficient
cK and the loglog correction. Profiles below the hardware-restoration threshold
also stay a fixed amount below C by the accepted generic diffuse bound. Thus
none affects the normalized limsup in (EHM).

6. UNIFORM ASYMPTOTIC OPTIMIZATION AND REMAINING GAP
In the middle A region, (7)/epsilon differs by o(1) uniformly from

 K(1-A)(L+log A-log2)-beta(2-A).                         (8)

Its second derivative is negative. For small epsilon the unique interior
maximizer A=1/x satisfies
 x+log x=L+1-log2-beta/K.
It obeys A~1/L, within the middle region. At this optimum (8) equals
 K(x-2+1/x)-beta
 =K(L-log L-1-log2)-2+o(1).                             (9)

Let alpha=exp(Kepsilon). Add the accepted positive-gap charge
q0(alpha-1)(r+1) to the raw total 1+q0 r, where r=S-1. The exact relaxed
outer-ratio maximum is
 1+c alpha exp{[alpha-1+epsilon Q_epsilon]/alpha},
where Q_epsilon is the maximum in (8), with the uniform o(1) included.
Using epsilon^2 L^2=o(epsilon) and (9), this becomes

 C+cKepsilon(L-log L)+c[K-2-Klog2]epsilon+o(epsilon).

This proves (EHM). Combined with the accepted one-capped lower, the current
order-epsilon interval is

 -c[e+1+Klog2] <= liminf residual
 <= limsup residual <= c[K-2-Klog2].                    (10)

Its width is exactly 2cK because K=e-1. The leading and loglog coefficients
remain sharp; the residual coefficient is still undetermined. In particular
this note does not claim that improving the incoming bound alone settles the
remaining positive-gap/negative-gap interaction.
