1. HARDWARE BUDGET RESTRICTS THE NEGATIVE-GAP ENTROPY MOMENT
Normalize full H=1 at a physical hardware bottleneck. Use the notation of the
heavy-tail theorem. LetL be a subset of actual owners withz_i<=epsilon, and let
a=sum_{i in L}a_i. SetA=exp(Kepsilon),K=e-1, and
 b(z)=exp(-K z[1+log(1/z)]) for0<z<=1, b(0)=1.
This b is a valid lower bound for the true b_M becauseM<=1.

For every negative-gap owner inL, g_i=a_i-d_i>0 implies its full account is
uncapped:d_i=p_i+z_i<=a_i<=1. Consequently
 z_i+g_i<=a_i.
WritingG=sum_{L,negative}g_i andW=sum_{L,negative}z_i g_i gives
 sum_{L,negative}z_i<=a-G,
 W/G<=min(epsilon,a-G) whenG>0.                          (1)
The second step uses both the weighted-average cap and the elementary bound
W/G<=sum_{L,negative}z_i; all quantities refer to the same actual full profile.
Sinceb is decreasing, convex Jensen from the prior theorem yields
 negative tail gain<=q0 G[1-b(min(epsilon,a-G))].          (2)

LetJ be the complementary heavy set,Q_J=d_J-a_J, andr=S-1. The light positive
and negative gap masses satisfyG_+^L=r-Q_J+G. Therefore positive chords and(2)
give the combined light gain at most
 q0{(A-1)(r-Q_J)+G[A-b(min(epsilon,a-G))]}.
IfG<=a-epsilon (a region present only fora>=epsilon), the final term is
increasing inG and hence bounded by its value atG=a-epsilon. In the remaining
region putz=a-G. Thus the following exact scalar upper bound is valid:

 sum_{i in L}(E_i-N_i)
 <=q0{(A-1)(r-Q_J)+V_epsilon(a)},                         (H-tail)
 V_epsilon(a)=max_{0<=z<=min(epsilon,a)}(a-z)[A-b(z)].

It can replace(A-B)(1-a_J) in(T-fixed) and(T-heavy) of the earlier note.
Together with the individual W_+,W_- moment bound, take whichever is smaller.
This is a coupling to actual remaining HARDWARE mass, not just owner count.

2. THE SCALAR MAXIMUM IS STRICTLY CONCAVE
F(z)=(a-z)[A-b(z)] has
 F'(z)=-A+b(z)-(a-z)b'(z),
 F''(z)=2b'(z)-(a-z)b''(z)<=0,
with strict negativity in the interior whena>0. Here
 b'=-K log(1/z)b,
 b''=[K/z+K^2 log^2(1/z)]b>0.
The endpointz=0 is continuous. For a>0,F'(z) tends topositive infinity asz
approaches0; atz=a, ifthis endpoint is available, F'=b(a)-A<0. Hence the
maximum is either the unique root ofF'=0 or the truncated endpointz=epsilon.
This is a controlled one-dimensional scalar problem; no reduction of actual
owner reports to a finite-dimensional mechanism is asserted.

For the r-independent heavy-tail bound setu=A-1, v=V_epsilon(a)-uQ_J and
r_min=max(0,d_J-1),r_hat=max(r_min,1-v/u). Then the same differentiation as in
the preceding note gives a uniform tail charge
 (exp(-r_hat)/2)(u r_hat+v).
Feasible nonnegativity follows fromV_epsilon(a)>=F(0)=ua.

3. AN EXACT GENERAL REGIME WITHepsilon<=1/8
Suppose EVERY actual owner's full local payment satisfiesz_i<=1/8. SetL=N,
a=1,epsilon=1/8, andA=exp(K/8). The original raw total is1+q0r. By(H-tail),
 sum_i E_i<=1+(exp(-r)/2)[A r+V_{1/8}(1)].                (3)

We show the scalar maximum is its endpointz=1/8. The elementary bounds
5/3<K<7/4 and2<log8<21/10 imply
 A<exp(7/32)<5/4,

Thus
 F'(1/8)=-A+b(1/8)[1+K(7/8)log8]
 >-5/4+(1/2)[1+(5/3)(7/8)2]=17/24>0.
By concavity F is increasing throughout[0,1/8], so
 V_{1/8}(1)=(7/8)[A-b(1/8)].                            (4)
This V is<A. Maximizing the right side of(3) overr>=0 therefore gives
 1+(A/2)exp[-1+V/A]
 =1+(A/2)exp[-1/8-(7/8)b(1/8)/A]

The substitution inA is increasing and inb decreasing, as is immediate from

and all displayed constants are checked by the accompanying Fraction script.
Restoring scale, (5) is funding timesH. There is no restriction on positive-gap
owner count or on full/changed grid overflow.

4. USE AND LIMIT
The result strengthens the convenient1/10 cutoff of the sharp incoming theorem
to1/8 by retaining hardware accounting. Its sharp diffuse limit remainsC, with
the same previously constructed growing family. More generally(H-tail) supplies
a sharper and concave tail subproblem for analyses of heavy recipients. It does
not establish unrestrictedC^2 funding, nor a matching lower bound for1/8 or the
numerical factor7081/5081.
