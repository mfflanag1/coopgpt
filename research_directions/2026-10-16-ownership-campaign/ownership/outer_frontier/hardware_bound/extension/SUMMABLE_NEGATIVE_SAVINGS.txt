SUMMABLE NEGATIVE-GAP SAVINGS AND HARDWARE FRAGMENTATION
16 October 2026. Corollary of NEGATIVE_GAP_SAVINGS.txt; review pending.
Same actual fixed-owner, all-real, arbitrary-branch domain. H=1, K=e-1,
q0=.5exp(1-S), delta=CM-Z. Let I={i:a_i>=d_i}, g_i=a_i-d_i,
G=sum_{i in I}g_i, Zminus=sum_{i in I}z_i, Aminus=sum_{i in I}a_i.
Use L(v)=2exp(sqrt(1-v)-1)/(1+sqrt(1-v)).

1. EXACT AGGREGATE CONSEQUENCE
The total envelope payment to these owners obeys
 sum_{i in I}E_i <=Aminus-q0 exp(-delta) G L(K^2 Zminus G).       (1)
Indeed z_i+g_i=a_i-p_i whenever g_i>0. When g_i=0, z_i<=d_i=a_i,
including capped owners. Thus Zminus+G<=Aminus<=1. For every i,
z_i g_i<=Zminus G.
Since L decreases, sum g_i L(K^2z_i g_i)>=G L(K^2Zminus G).
The resulting concentration bound is sharp as a scalar relaxation: put all
negative-gap hardware and local-payment mass in a single owner. This does not
assert such scalar states or the separately optimized deviations are attainable
by the actual production mechanism.

2. A SUMMABLE LOSS CORRECTION
For v in[0,1], L is concave, continuous at1, L(0)=1, L(1)=2/e. To verify
concavity, put r=sqrt(1-v). Then
 L'(v)=-L(v)/(2(1+r)),
 L''(v)=-L(v)(1-r)/(4r(1+r)^2)<=0 for0<v<1.
Thus L(v)>=1-(1-2/e)v. The loss relative to the hypothetical exact negative-gap
saving q0 exp(-delta)G is consequently at most
 q0 exp(-delta) K^2(1-2/e) sum_{i in I}z_i g_i^2.                 (2)
This hypothetical baseline is not asserted valid without the correction.
Parent's separate actual counterexample shows the correction can be needed.

3. VANISHING CORRECTION UNDER HARDWARE FRAGMENTATION
Let amax=max_{i:g_i>0}a_i, with amax=0 if no negative gap, and define
 vmax=K^2 amax^2/4<1,
 B(vmax)=(1-L(vmax))/vmax for vmax>0, with B(0)=1/4.
Concavity gives the stronger local chord L(v)>=1-B(vmax)v for0<=v<=vmax.
Every owner has z_i+g_i=a_i-p_i<=a_i, so z_i g_i<=a_i^2/4<=amax^2/4 and
 z_i g_i^2 <=(4/27)(z_i+g_i)^3 <=(4/27)a_i^3.
The first inequality is the exact maximum of z g^2 at fixed z+g, attained when
g=2(z+g)/3. Consequently the total lost saving in(2) is bounded more sharply by
 q0 exp(-delta) [4K^2 B(vmax)/27] amax^2 Aminus.                  (3)
Since B(vmax)->1/4 as amax->0, the leading constant is K^2/27.
In particular arbitrary grid overflow has at most a quadratic-in-amax effect
on this negative-gap savings estimate, uniformly in owner and branch counts.
The statement concerns the bound on the aggregate negative payments; it does
not claim the full funding factor converges to C^2 or control positive gaps.

SCOPE
All three inequalities preserve actual owners and independently chosen envelope
deviations. No role reassignment or cloning appears. The total hardware-role
slack Q=1+L_power-Z is NOT an additional useful per-owner cap here: already G<=Q.
An attempted refinement using min(G,Q) was discarded as redundant. The explicit
amax bound is instead a genuine condition on the fixed ownership profile.
