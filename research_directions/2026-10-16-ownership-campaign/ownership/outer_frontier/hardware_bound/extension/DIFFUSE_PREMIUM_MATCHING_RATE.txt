This concerns the actual own-report envelope E of the ORIGINAL nested exponential
primitive. It is not a lower bound against every truthful rule. All actual owners

report-dependent owner eligibility, or change of technology is permitted or used.

1. STATEMENT AND PREVIOUS UPPER BOUND
Let c=1/(2e),C=1+c,K=e-1. LetW(epsilon) be the supremum ofsum_iE_i/H over all
finite depth-two instances and physical hardware-bottleneck profiles with
max_i z_i/H<=epsilon, allowing arbitrary finite branch and owner counts,
nonnegative real reports, whole-bundle underreports, and arbitrary grid overflow.
The sharp incoming theorem gives
 W(epsilon)<=C+cK epsilon log(1/epsilon)+O(epsilon).
The following construction proves

 c(e-2) <= liminf_{epsilon->0+} (W(epsilon)-C)/(epsilon log(1/epsilon))
        <= limsup_{epsilon->0+} (W(epsilon)-C)/(epsilon log(1/epsilon))
        <= c(e-1).                                      (R)

Thus W(epsilon)-C=Theta(epsilon log(1/epsilon)). The logarithmic order of the
upper bound is necessary for the ACTUAL envelope. The leading constant remains
between the two displayed constants; it is not claimed sharp.
Moreover a positive lower coefficient already holds with physical local output
M/H fixed at1/4. Both productive-branch count and active owner count may tend to
infinity in the construction.

3. AN ACTUAL WHOLE-BUNDLE DEVIATION AND EXACT ENVELOPE GAIN
Fix oneB_i. Let it set all its productive-branch local reports tozero, retaining
its branch0 hardware and its zero grid. Every other owner's report and every
actual identity is unchanged. This is an ordinary feasible whole-bundle
underreport on the fixed report domain.

Every productive local branch now has capped ratiorho-1>=2, so its coefficient
becomeseq. The deviator's local payment becomeszero; all other full local

Consequently the new aggregate local payment is

 Delta:=Z'-Z=epsilon[K rho-(2e-1)].                       (2)
Forrho>=3, the bracket is at leaste-2>0. All changed opposing accounts remain

is unchanged at1 and the opposing total is exactly2+Delta. Thus its coefficient
isc exp(-Delta), with no outer cap or pool-switch ambiguity.

The deviator's full hardwarea=(1-T)/n is unchanged. Its full opposing account
wasepsilon and now iszero. Its ACTUAL raw-payment increase therefore equals

 gain_B=c[a(1-exp(-Delta))-epsilon].                      (3)

Each of the n fixedB_i owners has its own feasible deviation of this form.
The own-report envelope maximizes separately for each actual owner, so these
reports need not occur simultaneously. All other envelope payments are at
least their truthful raw payments. From(1)--(3),

 sum_i E_i >= C+c[(1-T)(1-exp(-Delta))-n epsilon].          (4)

This inequality is valid even at parameters where its added term is negative;
for the asymptotic parameters below the term is strictly positive. We are using
real feasible deviations of the actual rule, not a relaxed recipient-response
upper bound or a fictitious own/opponent decoupling.
