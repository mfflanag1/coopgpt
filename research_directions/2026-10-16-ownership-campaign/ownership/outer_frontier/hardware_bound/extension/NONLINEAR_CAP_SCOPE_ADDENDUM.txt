CAP-CONSEQUENCE CLARIFICATION FOR THE FIRST NONLINEAR THEOREM
16 October2026. Leaves the reviewed NONLINEAR_OUTGOING_LOSS.txt frozen.

In CAPPED_SUMMATION_OBSTRUCTION.txt the owner's full overflow is positive:
z_G=q=exp(-1/5)/2, w_G=q-2/5, and required other-cap loss is
R=1-4q/5. Thus the zero-overflow special case alone does not eliminate that
example's loss-free capped endpoint. The quantitative square-root bound does:
exp(1/5)>6/5 gives q<5/12, hence w_G<1/60 and z_G*w_G<1/144. The reviewed
B<11/4 therefore gives
                 B sqrt(z_G*w_G)<11/48<2/3<R.
So an unchanged own cap cannot be paired with the entire other-cap loss R
allowed by the older full-payment-only charge. This is an exact quantitative
comparison, not an inference from w_G=0, which would be false here.
It removes that particular relaxed endpoint. Optimizing cases with a positive
own cap loss and summing actual envelope payments remains a separate task.
