PROFILEWISE SHARPENING OF THE FIXED-OPPONENTS SURPLUS FLOOR
16 October2026. Candidate analytical corollary; review pending.
This sharpens sparse lane's complement-surplus bound M/2. Original exponential
primitive, actual fixed owners, all-real reports, arbitrary branches.

On branch j, let T_j be production, rho_j the full capped long/short ratio, and
q_j=.5exp(1-rho_j). Put
 Vlong=sum_j q_j rho_j T_j,
which is the TOTAL full payment allocated to original-long resource roles.
This is not the hardware/local-power role decomposition. For every actual owner i,
                    Z-z_i-M_{-i} <=Vlong.                         (1)
The same Vlong works simultaneously for every omitted actual owner. It satisfies
 Vlong<=min{M/2, 2cM-delta+(e-1)sqrt(c M delta)},                   (2)
where c=1/(2e), C=1+c, delta=CM-Z. At zero local slack, Vlong=2cM.

ONE-BRANCH PROOF
Normalize T=1. Let x<=1 be opponents' actual short total, y their actual long
total, and d their capped long total. Their full payments sum to(1-q)x+qd.
If y>=x, their coalition value is x, so the surplus is q(d-x)<=q rho.
If y<x, then y<1 and d=y. The omitted owner's own capped long amount is at most1,
so rho=d+d_i<=d+1, hence d>=rho-1. The surplus is
 (1-q)(x-d)<=(1-q)(2-rho).
This case necessarily has rho<2. Since
 q rho-(1-q)(2-rho)=2q+rho-2=exp(1-rho)+rho-2>=0,
the same upper bound q rho holds. Restoring T and summing proves(1).
For each prescribed rho>=1 the one-branch bound is attained by an actual owner
holding the entire short pool and none of the long pool: its opponents produce
zero alone, and receive exactly q rho T. This is sharpness of(1)'s profilewise
coefficient, not a lower bound on funding or on other mechanisms.

GLOBAL BOUNDS ON Vlong
The function rho exp(1-rho)/2 is at most1/2 for rho>=1, giving Vlong<=M/2.
Also write Q=sum_j q_j T_j. The full total payment identity is
 Z=M+sum_j q_j(rho_j-1)T_j, hence Vlong=Q+Z-M.
The exact scalar inequality proved analytically in MARGINAL_FLOOR_STABILITY.txt,
Section3, gives
 (q_j-c)_+ <=(e-1)sqrt(c delta_j/T_j),
with delta_j=CT_j-Z_j. It is also valid when rho_j>=2 because then q_j<=c.
Cauchy--Schwarz yields Q<=cM+(e-1)sqrt(c M delta). Therefore
 Vlong<=cM+Z-M+(e-1)sqrt(c M delta)
       =2cM-delta+(e-1)sqrt(c M delta),
proving(2). Zero-output branches are omitted without changing any quantities.
When delta=0, every productive branch has rho=2, so equality Vlong=2cM follows.

CAPPED OPPONENTS AFTER ANY OWN UNDERREPORT
The other owners' actual resource reports are unchanged. Coalition coverage
forces sum_{k!=i}z'_k>=M_{-i}, so their capped opposing total at full H=1 obeys
 sum_{k!=i}min(p_k+z'_k,1)
 >=min{1,P_{-i}+M_{-i}}
 >=min{1,(S-d_i-Vlong)_+},                              (3)
where S=sum_k min(p_k+z_k,1), d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1). The last step uses
P_{-i}+Z-z_i>=S-d_i. This remains valid with arbitrary full and deviated overflow.
It improves the M/2 coalition floor whenever full original-long-role payment is
smaller, especially near locally maximally funded profiles. Funding with several
positive-gap owners still requires a separate coupled summation argument.
