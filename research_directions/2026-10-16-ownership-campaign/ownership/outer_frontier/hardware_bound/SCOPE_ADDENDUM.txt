SCOPE CLARIFICATION FOR RECIPIENT_RESPONSE_HALF_v2.txt
16 October 2026. No change to the frozen theorem or proof.

Theorems B and C compare local-payment vectors using the fixed FULL hardware
threshold H and restored full grid reports. They are algebraic account
inequalities at that common threshold; they do not by themselves bound actual
outer payments at the deviating hardware total, or establish a payment bound
across an outer pool switch. Such a conclusion requires a separate outer
comparison argument. In particular, no additional grid-withholding improvement
for the capped-own-account inequality is asserted here.
