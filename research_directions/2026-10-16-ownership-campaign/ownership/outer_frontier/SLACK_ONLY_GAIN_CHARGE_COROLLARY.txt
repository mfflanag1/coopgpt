FAILURE OF CHARGING ALL OUTER GAIN ONLY TO UNUSED PRIMITIVE SLACK
16 October 2026. Corollary of CAPPED_RETENTION_OBSTRUCTION.txt.

At eta=0 in that example, every local slack is zero, and the full outer
primitive also has capped ratio2, so its own unused funding slack is zero:
 C*min(H,B)-sum_i N_i=0.
Nevertheless a genuine own underreport raises D's nested payment, and the
actual envelope satisfies E_i>=N_i for every owner. Therefore
 sum_i E_i>sum_i N_i
while both these local and outer unused-slack quantities vanish. No universal
inequality bounding the total envelope gain by a finite fixed combination of
only those two slack quantities can hold.

The failure persists if the claim is restricted to full outer ratios below2.
Let eta decrease to0 through positive values in the same family. The actual
own-payment gain converges to the strictly positive eta=0 gain, whereas
local slack stays zero and outer unused slack equals
 c[1-(1-eta)exp(eta)] ->0.
The previously specified eta=1/10000 gives one exact positive-gain instance;
the limit argument rules out every fixed finite coefficient on outer slack.

This does not refute C^2 funding: in this hardware-bottleneck family raw
funding is C*F at eta=0, leaving the separate margin(C^2-C)F. That margin,
grid capacity, or another physical-structure term must be used if the outer
hardware-short regime is to be closed by this route.
