EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO SLACK-ONLY CAPPED RETENTION
16 October 2026. Bounded outer-frontier result for the original exponential
primitive. No novelty or unrestricted mechanism impossibility claim.

CONCLUSION
The universal signed TOTAL-retention theorem cannot be replaced by a statement
for arbitrary weighted or outer-capped opponent totals with error bounded by
any finite constant times local slack. It fails on one saturated branch,
three fixed actual owners, and positive grid. The same example gives a TRUE
nested-rule incentive failure with full outer capped ratio strictly below2;
no restoration of the deviator's hardware is involved. This does not refute
C^2 funding of its own-report envelope.

1. FIXED ACTUAL-OWNER CONSTRUCTION
Write c=1/(2e), C=1+c. There is one local branch and three owners:
 owner A: hardware1, local power0, grid0;
 owner D: hardware0, local power1, grid0;
 owner O: hardware0, local power1, grid1-c-eta.
Take either eta=0 or eta=1/10000. Grid is positive. These are fixed roles;
no owner is split, merged, cloned, introduced or removed. All reports below
are ordinary coordinatewise withholding by owner D.

The branch has short hardware total1, capped long ratio2, local coefficient c,
and local payment vector
 z=(1-c,c,c), Z=C.
Its local slack is exactly delta=C*1-Z=0. Hardware total H=1. The full outer
opposing accounts are
 b=(1-c,c,1-eta).
Every account is at mostH, so their capped sum is S=2-eta. The full outer
coefficient is q_0=c exp(eta). The physical grand output is
 F=min(H,P+M)=1.
In particular eta>0 gives a full outer capped ratio strictly between1 and2.

2. A REAL OWN UNDERREPORT AND THE CAPPED SUM
Let D reduce its local power from1 to1-u, with0<u<1. Its hardware and grid
remain zero. Hardware and all other reports stay fixed, so the local short
pool stays hardware and its coefficient becomes c exp(u). The local payments
are exactly
 z_A'=1-c exp(u),
 z_D'=c(1-u)exp(u),
 z_O'=c exp(u).
If c(exp(u)-1)>eta, the outer opposing account of O exceedsH and is capped
at1. The other two accounts remain below1. Hence the new outer capped sum is
 S'=2-c u exp(u),
and D's own local loss is
 D_loss=c[1-(1-u)exp(u)]>=0.
The actual capped-retention defect at coefficient1/3 is
 (S-S')/3-D_loss
 =c[(1-2u/3)exp(u)-1]-eta/3.                            (1)
The uncapped TOTAL change does not have this defect: its reduction is exactly
D_loss. The obstruction is the disappearance, under the outer cap, of O's
payment increase that compensates A's payment decrease.

Take the rational withholding u=1/4. Since e<3, c>1/6, and exp(1/4)>1+1/4,
we have c(exp(1/4)-1)>1/24>eta, so the claimed cap status is strict.
Also exp(1/4)>1+1/4+1/32=41/32, so (1) is strictly greater than
 (1/6)[(5/6)(41/32)-1]-eta/3=13/1152-eta/3>0.
This violates any proposed bound by K*delta, because delta=0, for every
finite K. It remains a strict violation for eta=1/10000, where the full
outer ratio is strictly below2.

3. ACTUAL NESTED-OWN-PAYMENT INCREASE
Owner D has no hardware, before or after this underreport. Its original
nested payment is
 N_D=c^2 exp(eta).
Afterward the outer coefficient is c exp(c u exp(u)), giving
 N_D'=c^2(1-u)exp(u+c u exp(u)).
Thus its TRUE own-payment ratio is
 N_D'/N_D=(1-u)exp(u+c u exp(u)-eta).                    (2)
There is no hardware restoration or abstract opponent-account relaxation
in this identity. At u=1/4, c u exp(u)=exp(-3/4)/8. Since e<3,
exp(-3/4)>1/3. Therefore the exponent in(2) exceeds
 7/24-eta.
For eta=1/10000 put r=7/24-eta=8747/30000. The exact rational inequality
 1+r+r^2/2-4/3=1330009/1800000000>0
shows exp(r)>4/3. Hence(2)>1. The same holds for eta=0. In fact for the
specified positive eta, the payment ratio exceeds
 1+1330009/2400000000.
This proves a genuine nested-rule incentive violation on a continuous report
domain, at a full outer ratio below2, although every local branch is saturated.
Its own-report envelope correctly repairs this violation by paying D more.

4. ARBITRARY WEIGHTED RETENTION FAILS EVEN WITHOUT GRID
The same saturated local branch already refutes arbitrary weighted retention
before forming an outer stage. Choose weights1 on A and0 on both power owners.
The weighted total drops by c(exp(u)-1), while D's own loss is
c[1-(1-u)exp(u)]. Their ratio is
 [1-(1-u)exp(u)]/[exp(u)-1] ->0 as u->0.
Consequently every fixed positive retention coefficient lambda fails for
small enough u, with zero local slack. This says nothing against the proved
unweighted signed-total retention theorem.

5. LIMITS AND THE NEXT NECESSARY CHARGE
The example is a restricted response obstruction and an explicit incentive
failure of the raw nested rule. It is not a lower bound against all truthful
mechanisms, does not refute any proved funding bound for the envelope, and
does not prove that the envelope exceeds C^2. The full raw total is close to
C*F, leaving substantial room below C^2*F. A successful outer hardware-short
argument must use something beyond local slack: for example grid mass, the
physical bottleneck margin, or explicit cap-crossing compensation.
