TOTAL-INCREASE GAIN CONTROL ACROSS THE OUTER POOL SWITCH
16 October 2026. Structural lemma for the original exponential primitive.
Fixed actual owners; arbitrary mixed reports and positive grid allowed.

Assume the full physical profile has P+M<=H. Restore the selected owner's
outer hardware and grid reports to their full values after computing local
accounts. This can only increase its outer payment. Let full opposing total
and own account be B,b, and changed ones be B'>B,b'=b-D with D>=0.
Then, even if B and B' lie on opposite sides of H,
 Phi_i(Hvector,bvector')-Phi_i(Hvector,bvector)
 <=(1/2)[(B'-B)-D]_+.                                   (1)
No lower bound on the full outer ratio is assumed.

PROOF
By PHYSICAL_BOTTLENECK_CAP_SEPARATION.txt, every opposing coordinate at both
endpoints is at most P+M<=H. A linear interpolation between their account
vectors has this same property. Thus when the opposing pool is long its
capped total is its actual total; its payment depends only on total and
own account. When it is short, this is also true, with the capped hardware
vector fixed by the total.

Write a=H_i. Along the interpolating path let t be the opposing total and
w its own opposing account. Then0<=w<=t and0<=a<=H. If t<=H,
 phi(t,w)=(1-q(t))w+q(t)min(a,t),
 q(t)=(1/2)exp(1-r(t)), r(t)=sum_k min(H_k,t)/t.
On a cap cell with m capped hardware accounts,
 q'(t)=q(r-m)/t.
Therefore partial_w phi=1-q>=1/2. Also partial_t phi<=1/2: if a<=t,
 partial_t phi=q(r-m)(a-w)/t<=qr<=1/2;
if a>=t then m>=1 and
 partial_t phi=q+q(r-m)(1-w/t)<=q(1+r-m)<=qr<=1/2.
Here r>=1 and r exp(1-r)<=1. Negative derivatives cause no difficulty.

If t>=H, every opposing account is uncapped and
 phi(t,w)=a+q(t)(w-a), q(t)=(1/2)exp(1-t/H).
Thus partial_w phi=q<=1/2 and
 partial_t phi=q(a-w)/H<=q=partial_w phi.

Let k=D/(B'-B)>=0, so along the interpolation dw/dt=-k. If0<=k<=1,
then belowH the directional derivative is at most(1-k)/2, while aboveH
it is at most(1-k)q<=(1-k)/2. If k>=1, it is nonpositive in both regimes.
Integrate from B to B'. All cap cells and the pool tie are continuous, proving
(1). H=0 is the zero-output case; B=0 follows by continuity.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ACTUAL ENVELOPE
Choose a different feasible own underreport for each owner as usual. Restoring
its grid coordinate lets us set B'-B=Z^i-Z and D=z_i-z_i^i. The universal
local total-increase lemma gives
 sum_i[(Z^i-Z)-(z_i-z_i^i)]_+<=2Delta_+.
Hence the aggregate gain of all TOTAL-INCREASING deviations is at most Delta_+
throughout the physical power-bottleneck regime P+M<=H, without imposing the
previous full-outer-ratio>=2 hypothesis and without excluding outer switches.
This lemma does not handle total-decreasing deviations or the opposing-account
clipping that can occur when P+M>H.
