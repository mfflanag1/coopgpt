SHARP INCENTIVE CEILING FOR THE QUADRATIC CONCENTRATION TILT
16 October 2026. Separate candidate, leaving the 277/243 proof unchanged.
No historical-priority claim. Uses precisely the fixed actual-owner model,
water product P, normalized concentrations s,t, and notation of
../SMOOTH_TILT_277_243_THEOREM.txt. In this file the parameter is a>=0 and
 h_a=1/2+a[(t-2/3)_+^2-(s-2/3)_+^2], p=h_a P.

THEOREM. The rule is nonnegative, coalition covering, and own-bundle monotone
at every nonnegative real profile, for every finite fixed actual owner set,
for all 0<=a<=3/4. Conversely, every a>3/4 fails own-coordinate monotonicity
on a finite pure-owner profile. Thus 3/4 is the exact nonnegative parameter
ceiling for this class. Its best possible uniform funding factor is at least
5657/5000>9/8. This last number is a LOWER bound for this restricted class,
not an upper bound or an unrestricted mechanism lower bound.

1. REDUCTION AND BASIC BOUNDS
Payments are affine in a. It suffices to prove monotonicity at a=3/4 and at
a=0. The latter is the independently reviewed frozen water-product theorem.
The a=3/4 rule has 5/12<=h<=7/12, so coverage and nonnegativity follow from
the same capped-coordinate convex-combination argument. Continuity, orientation
at balance, zero reports, and finite cell partitions are exactly as before;
tilt threshold crossings are included among the cell boundaries.

Normalize each differentiated point to A=1, not the whole coordinate path.
Write U=(s-2/3)_+, V=(t-2/3)_+, e=M' in{0,1}, c=#long reports above A.
The old water derivative identities, dominant-long-cap lemma, and P bounds
remain unchanged. At general a, h'=2a[V*1_{c=1}*t(1-t)-U(e-s)] on short cells.
For a=3/4 this implies h'>=-1/24, h'_+<=11/18, and when e=1, h'_+<=1/9.
Use U(1-s)<=1/36 and V*t*(1-t)<=2/27. All below are cell statements and
extend across ties by continuity.

2. OWN LONG COORDINATE
The derivative is p[1+(d-x)(beta+eta)] with eta=h'/h.
For a strict maximum long account d=L,
 eta=2a*V*t*(1-t)/(h*d)>=0.
If active, eta*d<=4a/(27h)<=4/15<1. If inactive,
 eta=2a*V*(1-t)/(hD)<=2a/(9h)<=2/5<1/lambda,
using D>=1 and lambda<=1. Thus both the constant and x coefficient are
nonnegative in the same way as in the frozen proof.
For a nonmaximum long account,
 eta=-2a*V*t/(hD)<=0.
If V=0 use the old proof. Otherwise that account is inactive. It suffices to
check x=0. If L is active, d<=R=D-L, lambda-d>=3DV, while
 lambda*d*|eta|<= (2a/h)*V*t*(1-t)<= (4a/(9h))*V<=4V/5<3DV.
If L is inactive, d/lambda<(1-t)/t<1/2, whereas
 d|eta|<=2a*V*t*(1-t)/h<=4a/(27h)<=4/15<1/2.
Thus every long-coordinate derivative is nonnegative.

3. EASY SHORT-COORDINATE CASES
The exact derivative is
 1-p+p d'+p beta(d-x)+P h'(d-x).
If d>=x and beta>=0, the lower bound 1-h-1/24>=3/8 applies.
If d>=x and beta<0, the dummy is active; beta>=1-s/lambda and P<=lambda/s
again give this same bound. If d<x and beta<0, discard the favorable beta
term. For a maximum short owner use h'_+<=1/9, giving 1-h-1/9>0. For a
nonmaximum short owner x<=1/2, giving 1-h-(11/18)/2>=1/9.
Only d<x and beta>=0 remains. A sufficient bound is
 1-hP(1+beta*x)-P h'_+ x.                            (1)

4. NONMAXIMUM SHORT OWNER
Here x<=min(s,1-s). If s>2/3, P(1+beta*x)<=1/s<=3/2, and
 h'_+ x<=2a[(2/27)(1-s)+U*s*(1-s)]
         <=(3/2)(2/81+1/36)=17/216.
Consequently (1)>=1-(7/12)(3/2)-17/216=5/108>0.
If s<=2/3 then U=0. If V=0 use the old rule. If V>0, the maximum actual
long cap L is active. The original half-prefactor margin is >=3V/4 when
the dummy is inactive, and >=3V when it is active, exactly as in the frozen
277/243 proof. Increasing the prefactor costs at most
 2a V^2 <=(2a/3)V=V/2,
and the h' term costs at most
 2a V*t*(1-t)*x <=(2a/9)V=V/6.
Their total 2V/3 is strictly smaller than either available margin.

5. MAXIMUM SHORT OWNER
Here e=1,x=s. If V=0 then h<=1/2,h'<=0 and the original bound
P(1+beta*s)<=2 proves the result. Suppose V>0; at most one actual long
cap is active. If c=1, L=1 and P(1+beta*s)<=1, so (1)>=1-7/12-1/9>0.
It remains c=0. Then d'=0,h'=-2a U(1-s)<=0. The derivative is affine in
the own opposite account d in[0,s]. At d=s it equals 1-p>=0. Thus it is
sufficient to prove nonnegativity at d=0; this is an algebraic endpoint
bound at the current fixed global quantities, not a change of the profile.

If L is inactive, only the dummy is active, t<s, h<=1/2, and
P(1+beta*s)=2, so the derivative is nonnegative.
Suppose L is active and the dummy inactive. Then lambda>=s,
P=lambda/L, t<=L, and the derivative multiplied by L is
 L-h(lambda+s)+2a lambda U*s*(1-s).
Increasing psi(t) to psi(L) can only lower this expression. For a=3/4 its
resulting slope in lambda is
 -1/2-a[psi(L)-psi(s)]+2a U*s*(1-s)<=-1/2+1/24<0.
Thus the worst value occurs at lambda=L, and it suffices to prove
 (L-s)/2 >= a{[psi(L)-psi(s)](L+s)-2L U*s*(1-s)}.     (2)
If s<=2/3 and L>=2/3, dividing the right-brace expression by L-s gives
 (L-2/3)^2*(L+s)/(L-s).
For fixed L this increases in s, and at s=2/3 equals L^2-4/9<=5/9<2/3.
If L<=2/3 it vanishes. If s>2/3, the brace divided by L-s equals
 Q(L)=(L+s)(L+s-4/3)-2L U*s*(1-s)/(L-s).
For L>s this is increasing in L: both the first term and the negative of
L/(L-s) increase. Its value at L=1 is
 Q(1)=2/3-(1-s)^2<=2/3.
At L=s the undivided brace is nonpositive. Therefore in all cases the brace
is <=(2/3)(L-s), proving (2) for a=3/4.

Finally let both L and the dummy be active, so lambda<s. The d=0 derivative
multiplied by L is
 L-lambda-2a lambda[psi(t)-psi(s)]+2a lambda^2 U*(1-s).
Increase psi(t) to psi(L). The resulting expression decreases with lambda,
since its derivative is
 -1-2a[psi(L)-psi(s)]+4a lambda U*(1-s)<=-1+1/12<0
when L>=s. If L<s then t<=L<s and every term was already favorable. Thus
for the nontrivial L>=s case increase lambda to s. The resulting expression
is precisely the inactive-dummy expression already handled at lambda=s.
This proves every short derivative on D>A cells.

6. PERSISTENT D=A AND GLOBAL REPORTS
With A<B and D=A on an interval, exactly one positive long cap equals A.
Thus t=1,P=1 and h'=-2a U(e-s). For an owner without that long cap,
a maximum-short owner has derivative>=1-h. A nonmaximum-short owner has
x<=1-s and xh'<=2a U*s*(1-s)<=1/24, so derivative>=1-h-1/24>0.
The owner with that long cap has derivative 1+h'(1-x)>=1-1/24>0.
Long changes within the face are capped and irrelevant. All activation,
maximum, water, concentration-threshold and balance boundaries are continuous.
Finitely many cell intervals therefore prove every coordinate comparison;
compose coordinates of the SAME actual owner to obtain bundle monotonicity.

7. FAILURE FOR EVERY a>3/4
Use four pure owners. At the balanced profile, short reports are (s,1-s)
and long reports are (L,1-L), with 2/3<s<L<1. Increase the largest short
report toward s from below, keeping all other reports fixed. For sufficiently
small distance to balance, c=0, the dummy is inactive, only L is active,
and lambda tends to L. The left derivative of that owner's payment tends to
 J_a(s,L)=1-[1/2+a(psi(L)-psi(s))]*(1+s/L)
                +2a(s-2/3)(1-s)s.
As L tends to1 this tends to
 (1-s)[1/2-a(2/3-(1-s)^2)].
For any a>3/4 choose a rational s sufficiently close to1 so this is negative,
then a rational L<1 sufficiently close to1 while L>s. Continuity provides an
open interval immediately below balance on which the derivative is negative.
It contains a pair of rational reports, giving an exact own-report violation.
This argument is a conventional existence proof with strict inequalities,
not floating-point evidence. For illustration a=4/5,s=99/100,L=999999/1000000
has the exact limiting derivative
 -4191564020101/12626250000000000 <0.

8. A FUNDING OBSTRUCTION THAT SURVIVES THE SHARP PARAMETER CEILING
Fix two pure short owners with reports4/5,1/5 and two pure long owners with
reports1,1. Then s=4/5,t=1/2. The original product has excess
 E0=(1+s)^3/(54s)=27/200.
The tilted excess is
 E_a=[1-2a(4/5-2/3)^2]*(27/200).
For every truthful a in this class, 0<=a<=3/4, so
 E_a >= [1-(3/2)(2/15)^2]*(27/200)=657/5000.
Thus its uniform funding factor cannot be below5657/5000>9/8. This profile
also demonstrates why reducing the old s=1 extremizer is insufficient.
No claim that 5657/5000 is the exact optimal funding factor is made.
