EXACT FUNDING OF THE MAXIMAL TRUTHFUL QUADRATIC TILT
16 October 2026. Candidate proof, dependent on SHARP_PARAMETER_CEILING.txt
and the independently checked original-water concentration curve. No novelty
or unrestricted optimality claim. Fixed actual owners, arbitrary nonnegative
real mixed reports, every coalition and every own-bundle comparison.

THEOREM. Set a=3/4 in the quadratic tilt. Let s_* be the unique root in(2/3,1)
of
 -36s^3+27s^2+4s-2=0.
Define
 gamma=1+[1-(3/2)(s_*-2/3)^2]*(1+s_*)^3/(54s_*).
The rule's exact worst funding factor is gamma, approximately
 1.13140081712104235.
Together with the sharp incentive ceiling, this is also the best possible
uniform funding factor among all truthful members with parameter a>=0 of
this particular quadratic-tilt class. It is strictly above9/8. It is not a
lower bound for unrestricted mechanisms or general concentration prefactors.

1. NOTATION AND THE NONDOMINANT CASE
Normalize A=1. Put s=M, d_i=min(z_i,1), D=sum d_i, L=max d_i,
t=L/D, U=(s-2/3)_+, V=(t-2/3)_+, h=1/2+(3/4)(V^2-U^2).
Total payment is1+E, E=hP(D-1). If D=1 the excess is zero.
The original product satisfies
 (1/2)P(D-1)<=F(s),
 F(s)=1/8 for s<=1/2; F(s)=(1+s)^3/(54s) for s>=1/2.
This is the original-water concentration theorem, independently proved in
mixed/continuous_two_pool/concentration/CONCENTRATION_THEOREM.txt.
Define
 g(s)=[1-(3/2)(s-2/3)_+^2]F(s).
If t<=2/3 then V=0 and immediately E<=g(s).

2. REDUCE EVERY DOMINANT-LONG PROFILE TO ONE UNIT LONG CAP
Suppose t>2/3. The dominant-cap lemma says at most one actual long cap is
water-active. All transformations in this section compare a scalar funding
expression; they are not strategic deviations or assertions that a payment
is monotone in others' reports.

If an actual cap L is active, let R=D-L. All other actual caps are inactive.
Then R<L/2<=1/2. With s and R fixed, the water level is independent of L:
 lambda=1-R if the dummy is inactive;
 lambda=(1+s-R)/2 if the dummy is active.
The product is min(s,lambda)/s times lambda/L. Therefore
 E= h(s,L/(L+R)) * [min(s,lambda)/s]*lambda*(L+R-1)/L.
Since R<1, both h and (L+R-1)/L increase with L. Raising L to1 preserves
its activity, all other cap statuses and the dummy status, and only increases
E. All caps remain admissible capped reports. Hence L=1 suffices.

If no actual cap is active, the dummy alone is active. Hold D fixed and move
mass from other actual caps to a largest one until it reaches lambda. This
is feasible: D>1>lambda, each existing cap is at most lambda, and mass can
be removed from finitely many other caps without going below0. No cap becomes
active before the endpoint. The water level and product remain fixed, while
t and hence h only increase. The residual mass R=D-L decreases, and the
original t>2/3 ensures R<L/2 throughout. At L=lambda, treat this cap as the
single active cap at its boundary; its water formula matches that above.
Now increase L to1 at fixed R. This again increases the funding expression.
Thus this case reduces to the preceding one as well, with0<=R<1/2.

3. THE TWO SCALAR FUNDING EXPRESSIONS
At L=1 put t=1/(1+R), V=t-2/3, with0<=R<1/2.
If s<=1-R, the dummy is inactive and
 E=h R(1-R).
Writing q=1/2-R gives V=2q/[3(1+R)], so, after dropping the favorable -U^2,
 E <= [1/2+q^2/(3(1+R)^2)]*(1/4-q^2)
   <=1/8-(5/12)q^2<=1/8.
This is already at most max_s g(s).

If s>=1-R, the dummy is active and
 E_2(s,R)=h(s,1/(1+R))*R*(1+s-R)^2/(4s).             (1)
For fixed s, this expression increases in R on its feasible domain
1-s<=R<=1/2. To prove this, its derivative has the sign of
 K=h(1+s-3R)-(3/2)*V*R*(1+s-R)/(1+R)^2.
Use s>=1-R, h>=5/12, and V=(1-2R)/[3(1+R)] to get
 K >=(1-2R){5/6-R*(1+s-R)/[2(1+R)^3]} >=(1-2R)/3>=0.
The last inequality uses R<=1/2 and1+s-R<=2, so the subtracted fraction
is at most1/2. Therefore (1) is at most its value at R=1/2:
 E_2(s,1/2)=[1-(3/2)U^2]*(s+1/2)^2/(16s).
Here s>=1/2, and
 (s+1/2)^2/(16s) <= (1+s)^3/(54s),
because the difference after clearing positive denominators is
 32(1+s)^3-27(2s+1)^2=(2s-1)^2(8s+5)>=0.
Consequently E_2(s,R)<=g(s). This finishes all dominant-long profiles.
In all cases, E<=max_{0<s<=1} g(s).

4. EXACT MAXIMIZER AND ATTAINMENT
For s<=1/2, g(s)=1/8. For1/2<=s<=2/3, g=F increases. For s>=2/3,
 g(s)=(-9s^2+12s+2)*(1+s)^3/(324s),
 g'(s)=(1+s)^2[-36s^3+27s^2+4s-2]/(324s^2).
The cubic J(s) in brackets has J(2/3)=2,J(1)=-7, and
J'(s)=-108s^2+54s+4<0 throughout[2/3,1]. Thus there is exactly one root
s_* in that interval, g increases before it and decreases afterward, and
max g=g(s_*).

For any s>=1/2 take two pure short owners of reports s and1-s, and two pure
long owners each of report1. The fixed actual owner set has four members,
all positive when s<1. Then t=1/2, lambda=(1+s)/3, P=lambda^3/s and
E=g(s). Setting s=s_* proves exact attainment, not merely a limiting family.

5. OPTIMALITY WITHIN THE ONE-PARAMETER CLASS
The separate incentive theorem proves that any admissible a>=0 must satisfy
a<=3/4. At the fixed four-owner profile with short concentration s_* and two
unit long reports, the excess
 [1-2a(s_*-2/3)^2]F(s_*)
is decreasing in a. It is therefore at least g(s_*) for every truthful member
of this class. The a=3/4 rule attains the uniform bound just proved. This is
an exact optimization of the parameterized architecture only.

6. EXACT RATIONAL INTERPRETATION
The cubic and derivative signs define gamma without any floating-point
premise. Rational isolation of s_* and interval evaluation can certify a
printed decimal if needed; that is supplementary numerical presentation.
An elementary rational lower witness is g(4/5)=657/5000>1/8. In particular
this architecture cannot close the gap to9/8, even at its optimal parameter.
