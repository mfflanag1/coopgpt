THE CYCLIC BOX OBSTRUCTION HAS NO ACTUAL-ENVELOPE EXCESS
16 October2026. Analytic follow-on to the separately audited Rbox counterfamily.
No claim of a general3/2 theorem, mechanism impossibility, or historical novelty.

STATEMENT
Use exactly the fixed-owner cyclic family and two-input primitive Phi in
certificates/box_bound/CYCLIC_THREE_HALVES_OBSTRUCTION.txt. Let N be the raw
nested local-then-outer rule and let E_i be its true own-report monotone envelope:
  E_i(b)=sup_{0<=b'_i<=b_i}N_i(b'_i,b_-i).
For EVERY integer n>=3, at the displayed full cyclic profile,
  E_i=N_i for every actual owner i,
  sum_i E_i/F=z+(s-z)q0<89/60<3/2,                       (1)
where
  r=19/10, s=83/40, q=exp(1-r)/2,
  a=rq, b=1-q, z=a+b=1+(r-1)q,
  q0=exp(1-s/z)/2.
The numerical ratio is about1.3927843049338726. Only the exact inequality in(1)
is used. In contrast the recipient-box ratio is strictly above1.5001. Thus the
same family separates that relaxation from the true envelope; it supplies no
counterexample to an actual-envelope3/2 conjecture.

HARDWARE OWNERS
At the full profile every hardware owner has hardware s and local payment a.
Under one such owner's actual underreport, its local payment cannot exceed a:
the primitive is own-report-monotone, and the owner holds one hardware account
in each of its two affected branches. All other local-power reports stay1.
The total local payment remains <=nz. This last fact can also be checked
directly here: if a branch's reduced hardware total is <=1, its total primitive
payment is that hardware total. Otherwise its capped hardware ratio rho lies
in[1,r], and its total is1+(rho-1)exp(1-rho)/2<=z, since this scalar function is
increasing up to rho=2 and r<2. Sum over branches.

Restore the underreporting owner's outer hardware account to s while holding
the resulting local-account vector fixed. The established actual-owner
monotonicity of Phi can only increase that owner's outer payment. After this
restoration the hardware vector is(s,...,s,0), the owner's opposing account is
<=a, and its total is T<=nz<ns. As in the audited box proof, the payment is at
most a+(s-a)q0: for T<a it is <=T<=a, while for T>=a it is at most
  a+q(T)(min(s,T)-a),
which is nondecreasing in T and attained at the full T=nz. The full report is
itself feasible in the actual envelope, so
  E_i=N_i=a+(s-a)q0                                     (2)
for every hardware owner. This does not assume that arbitrary auxiliary
opponent vectors can be generated by real underreports.

LOCAL-POWER OWNER: ALL SIMULTANEOUS UNDERREPORTS
The sole local-power owner w can choose arbitrary reports t_j in[0,1], one
on each branch. It has no hardware or shared-power account. The hardware
reports on every branch remain47/40 and9/10. Put c=9/10 and q*=1/(2e).
Let B(t) be w's payment on one branch and Z(t) its TOTAL local payment.
For0<=t<=c,
  B(t)=(1-q*)t,             Z(t)=(1+q*)t.                (3)
For c<=t<=1, put x=c/t and q(t)=exp(-x)/2. Then
  B(t)=t[1-q(t)],           Z(t)=t+c q(t).               (4)
The formulas agree at c, are continuous at0, and Z increases. In the second
regime x is in[9/10,1]. The following two bounds hold over the whole interval:
  B(t)/Z(t)<5/7 for t>0,
  B'(t)>Z'(t)/2 on each smooth piece.                    (5)

Proof of the ratio bound: in(3), q*>1/6 because e<3, so
(1-q*)/(1+q*)<5/7. In(4), B/Z=(1-q)/(1+xq), and the desired inequality is
q(7+5x)>2. The function exp(-x)(7+5x)/2 is decreasing in x, since its derivative
is -q(2+5x); at x=1 its value is6/e>2. This proves the first bound.

For the derivative bound in(3), q*<1/4 because e>2, hence
B'-(1/2)Z'=1/2-(3/2)q*>0. In(4), direct differentiation gives
  B'=1-q(1+x),             Z'=1+x^2 q.
Thus B'-(1/2)Z'=1/2-q(1+x+x^2/2). The function
q(x)(1+x+x^2/2) has derivative -q(x)x^2/2 and is decreasing. Its maximum on
this interval is at x=9/10. The positive exponential series gives
  exp(9/10)>1+9/10+81/200+729/6000=4853/2000>12/5,
so q(9/10)<5/24. Therefore
  q(x)(1+x+x^2/2)<(5/24)(461/200)=461/960<1/2.
This proves both inequalities in(5), without numerical sign decisions.

Write B=sum_j B(t_j), T=sum_j Z(t_j). Then B/T<5/7 whenever T>0. Also T<=nz,
because each Z is increasing, and
  z=1+(9/10)q<1+(9/10)(5/24)=19/16,
  s/z>(83/40)/(19/16)=166/95>3/2.                        (6)
The outer hardware vector remains(s,...,s,0). Since T<=nz<ns, local payments
are the short opposing pool and w's raw nested payment is
  N_w=B[1-Q(T)],
where Q(T)=exp(1-n)/2 for0<T<=s and
  Q(T)=exp(1-ns/T)/2 for s<=T<=nz.                       (7)
These expressions agree at T=s; at T=0 the payment is0.

For T<=s, Q is constant, so N_w is nondecreasing in every coordinate t_j.
For T>=s put u=ns/T. By(6), u>=s/z>3/2. The function
  f(u)=uQ/(1-Q)=u/[2exp(u-1)-1]
is decreasing for u>=1, because its derivative numerator is
2exp(u-1)(1-u)-1<0. Since e>8/3>(8/5)^2,
  f(u)<f(3/2)=(3/2)/(2sqrt(e)-1)<15/22.                 (8)
On every smooth region, differentiation in any t_j yields
  dN_w/dt_j
   =(1-Q)Z'(t_j)[ B'(t_j)/Z'(t_j) -(B/T)f(u) ]
   >(1-Q)Z'(t_j)[1/2-(5/7)(15/22)]
    =(1-Q)Z'(t_j)/77>0.                                 (9)
The formulas are continuous at each t_j=c, at T=s, and at the zero profile.
Consequently sequential coordinate increases establish monotonicity on the
ENTIRE n-dimensional report box, not merely along equal-report paths. Therefore
the actual envelope of w is attained at its full report:
  E_w=N_w=nb(1-q0).                                     (10)

TOTAL AND EXACT GAP BELOW THREE HALVES
Sum(2),(10), divide by F=n, and simplify:
  sum_i E_i/F=a+(s-a)q0+b(1-q0)=z+(s-z)q0.
From s/z>3/2 and sqrt(e)>3/2, q0<1/3. Since z<s and z<19/16,
  z+(s-z)q0<(2/3)z+s/3
             <(2/3)(19/16)+(1/3)(83/40)
              =89/60<3/2.
All estimates are uniform in n>=3. No growing-family conclusion is inferred
from a finite set of examples.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMIT
On this family the raw nested rule already pays every owner at least as much
at the full profile as under any feasible own-bundle underreport. The excessive
box payment comes from keeping w's full B=nb while zeroing hardware responses.
Actual reports obey the joint relation(3)-(5), which excludes that combination.
This proves a separation on this family only. Elsewhere N is known to fail
truthfulness, and neither a general3/2 bound on E nor an implementable sharper
coupled mechanism is established here.
