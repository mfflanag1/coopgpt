EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO THE SIMPLE STAR / DEPTH-TWO COVER MIXTURE
16 October2026. Finite combinatorial certificate, not an unrestricted mechanism
lower bound. Candidate proof using an exhaustive tree-DP recurrence.

SCOPE
Consider fixed fractional activity covers as in FOREST_COVER_LEMMA_v1.txt. Each
subgraph is either a star forest, charged its known universal factor
C2=1+1/(2e), or a forest with every component of diameter<=4, charged7/4.
The simple proof bounds each component mechanism by C_r F_r(N)<=C_r F(N),
and therefore certifies the total factor sum_r lambda_r C_r.
There is a finite tree on which EVERY such fixed fractional activity cover has
sum_r lambda_r C_r>2. Thus merely mixing these two solved classes in that
particular proof cannot establish a sub-two factor for all forests.

EXPLICIT TREE AND AUXILIARY WEIGHTS
Use the complete ternary rooted tree of height4:121 vertices and120 edges.
Give each edge to a child at depth d weight3^(4-d), i.e.27,9,3,1 at depths
1,2,3,4. Each level has weight81; total auxiliary edge weight is324.
These weights define a dual counting argument for activity covers. They need
not be the economic profits of the production instance.

EXACT BOUNDED-DIAMETER OPTIMA
The maximum auxiliary weight of a star forest (component diameter<=2) is189.
The maximum auxiliary weight of a diameter<=4 forest is243.
Both optima include all choices of retained/deleted edges, with any number of
components and isolated vertices. They are certified by the following recurrence.

For a rooted subtree at v, state h stores the largest retained weight whose
component containing v has height h, with every component's diameter<=D.
At a leaf only state h=0,weight0 exists. Process children one at a time. For
current state(h,s) and a child state(t,u) across edge of weight w:
 - Delete that edge: keep height h and add the child's best score over all t.
 - Keep that edge: permit it iff t+1<=D and h+t+1<=D; update height to
   max(h,t+1) and add u+w.
Take the maximum score for each updated height. This is exhaustive because
the unique path between different child branches crosses v. Every retained
cross-branch distance is at most the sum of their branch heights, while all
within-child distances were checked already. The deletion case separates the
entire child forest. Conversely every valid forest determines exactly such
child choices, so induction proves both upper and attainability.

All vertices at a common depth have identical state profiles. The exact values
in this instance are:

 depth3: {0:0,1:3}
 depth2: {0:9,1:9,2:12}
 depth1: {0:36,1:54,2:42}
 depth0: {0:162,1:189,2:189}.

D=4

 depth1: {0:54,1:54,2:54,3:63}

The independent executable verify_mixed_cover_obstruction.py derives these
profiles on the explicit121-vertex tree using integer arithmetic, reconstructs
attaining edge sets, and checks their diameters by all-source graph distances.
It additionally compares the DP against all16,384 edge subsets of a binary
height3 tree, separately for D2 and D4; both comparisons pass. The full121-vertex
result is exhaustive via the proved recurrence, not a direct2^120 enumeration.

FRACTIONAL-COVER COST LOWER BOUND
For a star forest E_r, its auxiliary weight is at most189. For a diameter4
forest it is at most243. As C2<6/5<49/36,
 243/(7/4)=972/7<189/C2.
Thus in either class,
  weight(E_r)<=(189/C2) C_r.
If every original edge is covered with sum of fixed coefficients at least1,
then
 324<=sum_r lambda_r weight(E_r)
     <=(189/C2)sum_r lambda_r C_r.
Consequently
 sum_r lambda_r C_r>=324 C2/189=(12/7)C2>2,
where the last inequality follows from e<3, hence C2>7/6.
This is an exact strict obstruction to the simple charged-class cover argument.

WHAT IT DOES NOT EXCLUDE
It does not exclude a direct all-tree mechanism, a lower-budget primitive,
report-dependent covers with a separate incentive proof, or a sharper joint
estimate of sum_r lambda_r C_r F_r(N) instead of replacing each F_r(N) byF(N).
It also does not exclude new separator-gluing constructions or a different
class of solvable component technologies. The7/4 depth-two mechanism itself
is unaffected. No lower bound on the optimal truthful subsidy is asserted.
