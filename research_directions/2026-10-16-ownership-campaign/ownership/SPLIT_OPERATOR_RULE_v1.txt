SHARP SPLIT-OPERATOR RULE -- CANDIDATE COMPLETE PROOF
16 October 2026. Independent constructive lane. Awaiting independent audit.
No historical novelty claim. This is MODEL A of ../CONTRACT.txt, not mixed

STATEMENT
Fix any finite m>=1 and any partition of {1,...,m} into nonempty blocks J_a.
Owner A_a holds the local-capacity coordinates L_i for i in J_a, distinct H_i
holds hardware h_i, and C holds shared grid capacity P. All reports are arbitrary
nonnegative real numbers, with binding coordinatewise underreports and payment
utility. Every actual owner and block remains fixed across all report profiles.
For every public theta in [0,1] there is an O(m+g)-arithmetic payment rule that
is nonnegative, covers every coalition, is own-bundle monotone for every owner,
pays A_a at least theta times its grand marginal M_a, and has total at most
(1+theta/4)T. This factor is optimal uniformly over all m and partitions because
the one-block subclass contains the previously proved sharp even-pod family.
This last statement does not assert sharpness for every prescribed partition.

NOTATION
ell_i=min(h_i,L_i), r_i=h_i-ell_i, L=sum_i ell_i, Q=sum_i r_i, H=L+Q,
R=min(P,Q), G=min(P,H), T=L+R, M=T-G, D=G-R.
Thus 0<=D<=L, M=L-D=min(L,(H-P)_+), D=min(L,(P-Q)_+).
ell_a=sum_{i in J_a}ell_i. Deleting A_a removes ell_a locally and adds ell_a
to residual hardware. Hence
 M_a=ell_a+min(P,Q)-min(P,Q+ell_a)
    =(ell_a-(P-Q)_+)_+=(ell_a-D)_+.
The second equality with D uses ell_a<=L, including D=L.

THETA=1 RULE
For L>0 put
 x_i=ell_i clip((P-Q+r_i)/L), X=sum_i x_i,
 y_Hi=x_i, y_Aa=ell_a M/L,
 y_C=G-(X-L/4)_+=min(G,G-X+L/4).
Here clip(z)=min(1,max(0,z)). At L=0 pay C=G and all other owners zero.
Thus the total of all local-operator payments is exactly M, as in the old
merged-operator rule; only the distribution across actual local owners changes.

NONNEGATIVITY, FLOORS, OWNERS' ZERO REPORTS
For L>0, 0<=x_i<=ell_i, and because P-Q+r_i=P-sum_{j!=i}r_j<=P,
 X<=L min(1,P/L)=min(L,P)<=G.
Consequently y_C>=0. Also y_Aa>=0. Since ell_a<=L,
 ell_a M/L=ell_a-ell_a D/L >= ell_a-D,
which together with nonnegativity proves y_Aa>=M_a.
If T=0 then L=0 and G=0, so all payments vanish. If A_a reports a zero bundle
then ell_a=0 and its payment is zero. If h_i=0 then x_i=0. If P=0 then X=G=0
and C receives zero. At L=0 all definitions agree with these conclusions.

INCENTIVES -- WHOLE ACTUAL OWNERS
For A_a, hardware H and grid P are fixed, as is L_-a=sum_{i outside J_a}ell_i.
Let K=(H-P)_+. Its payment is the function
 u -> u min(1,K/(u+L_-a)), u=ell_a,
with value zero when u+L_-a=0. Both branches u and K*u/(u+L_-a) are
nondecreasing for u>=0, and match at the boundary. Equivalently the payment
is their minimum, with the natural zero convention. A coordinatewise increase
of the entire local bundle increases ell_a, so whole-bundle monotonicity follows.
This argument does not treat the block's coordinates as separate strategic owners.

For H_i, put p_i=(P-sum_{j!=i}r_j)_+ and L_-i=sum_{j!=i}ell_j. Both are fixed
under that actual hardware owner's report. Its payment is
 min(ell_i, p_i*ell_i/(L_-i+ell_i)),
with the continuous zero convention when the denominator vanishes. It is
nondecreasing in ell_i and hence h_i, including h_i=L_i and the L=0 boundary.

For C, all h,L reports are fixed. Each x_i(P) is nondecreasing and
ell_i/L-Lipschitz when L>0. Therefore X is nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz.
It has saturated at L by P=H. It follows for P'<=P that
 0<=X(P)-X(P')<=G(P)-G(P').
Since z -> (z-L/4)_+ is nondecreasing and 1-Lipschitz, y_C is nondecreasing.
At L=0 the same follows from y_C=min(P,H).

COALITION COVERAGE: REDUCTION TO ROW SUBSETS
For a coalition let I be its set of hardware pods, and let J be the union of
the blocks of its included local operators. Put K=I intersect J. Its available
local output is ell(K); it may only use grid when it includes C. Payments to
its local operators total (M/L)ell(J)>=(M/L)ell(K). Thus it suffices to prove
coverage for all I and all K subset I after assigning the accounting amount
(M/L)ell_i to each selected local row. This is a coalition accounting device,
not a change to ownership or strategic reports.

If C is absent, coalition worth is ell(K). Since x_i>=ell_i D/L,
 sum_{i in I}x_i+(M/L)ell(K)>=ell(K).

If C is present, coalition worth is
 ell(K)+min(P,r(I)+ell(I\K))=min(H_I,P+ell(K)).
It remains to show y_C+sum_I x_i+(M/L)ell(K) covers that expression.

CASE 1: P<=Q
Here D=0 and M=L, so all selected local output ell(K) is paid directly.
The original grid-only coalition inequality gives
 y_C+sum_I x_i>=min(P,H_I)>=min(P,r(I)+ell(I\K)).
For completeness the grid-only inequality also follows from the lemma below
at delta=0, or from the already proved single-operator rule, whose hardware
and grid payments are unchanged. The direct endpoint delta=0 proof is included
after the central case, avoiding any dependency on that prior result.

CASE 2: Q<P<H, with L>0
Normalize all capacities and payments by L, so sum ell_i=1. Set
 delta=P-Q in (0,1), z_i=ell_i min(r_i,1-delta), Z=sum z_i.
Then x_i=delta ell_i+z_i, X=delta+Z, M=1-delta, and
 y_C=min(P,Q+1/4-Z).
For I,K define s=ell(I\K), r=r(I^c), o=ell(I^c)<=1-s.
After subtracting the paid local amount, the required grid deficit is
 d=min(P,r(I)+s)-delta*s-z(I).
It is at most P because both subtracted terms are nonnegative. Furthermore
 d-Q+Z=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+z(I^c)
        <=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+o min(r,1-delta)
        <=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+(1-s)min(r,1-delta)
        <=1/4.
The last inequality is the elementary lemma proved below. Therefore d is at
most both P and Q+1/4-Z, hence at most y_C. This covers every coalition with C.

ELEMENTARY COVERAGE LEMMA
For 0<=delta<=1, 0<=s<=1, and r>=0,
 J=min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+(1-s)min(r,1-delta)<=1/4.
If s-r>=delta, then r<=s-delta and
 J=(1-s)[delta+min(r,1-delta)]<=s(1-s)<=1/4.
If s-r<=delta and r>=1-delta, then
 J=s-r-delta*s+(1-s)(1-delta)=1-delta-r<=0.
If s-r<=delta and r<=1-delta, then
 J=s(1-delta-r).
When s<=delta this is at most s(1-delta)<=delta(1-delta)<=1/4.
When s>=delta, the condition r>=s-delta makes it at most s(1-s)<=1/4.
Equalities and all boundaries are included.

DIRECT GRID-ONLY COVERAGE FOR P<=Q (L>0)
Normalize L=1. Write delta=P-Q<=0, x_i=ell_i clip(delta+r_i), X=sum x_i,
so G=P and y_C=min(P,P-X+1/4).
For hardware I let s=ell(I), r=r(I), and x_out=sum_{j outside I}x_j.
Need min(P,H_I)-x(I)<=P-X+1/4, equivalently
 x_out-min((P-H_I)_+,P)<=1/4; since H_I>=0 this is simply
 x_out-(P-H_I)_+<=1/4.
For j outside I, Q-r_j>=r, so delta+r_j=P-Q+r_j<=P-r.
Hence x_out<=(1-s)clip(P-r).
Put a=P-r. Since H_I=r+s, the left side is at most
 (1-s)clip(a)-(a-s)_+.
If a<=0 it is0; if 0<=a<=s it is at most s(1-s); if s<=a<=1
it equals s(1-a)<=s(1-s); if a>=1 it is1-s-a+s=1-a<=0.
Thus the bound is at most1/4. The deficit is also at most P, proving the
grid-only inequality. This proof in fact holds for any P, but the stated
case is all that is needed here.

CASE 3: P>=H (L>0)
Here D=L, M=0, x_i=ell_i and y_C=H-3L/4=Q+L/4>=Q.
A coalition without C has local worth at most ell(I), already paid. One with
C has worth at most H_I=ell(I)+r(I)<=ell(I)+Q, also paid. At P=H the cases
agree. For L=0, every coalition's worth is at most min(P,H_I)<=G=y_C when C
is included, and zero otherwise. This completes all-coalition coverage.

BUDGET, ALL THETA, AND SHARPNESS
Total theta=1 payment is
 M+X+G-(X-L/4)_+=T+min(X,L/4)<=T+L/4<=5T/4.
For theta in [0,1], mix this rule with fixed weight theta and the efficient
rule (A_a=0 for all a, C=R, H_i=ell_i) with weight1-theta.
That baseline is own-monotone: local payments are zero, R nondecreasing in P,
and ell_i nondecreasing in h_i. To verify its coverage, without C it pays all
available local output through hardware. With C, for hardware I and selected
local rows K subset I, put a=ell(I\K). Then
 ell(I)+R=ell(K)+a+min(P,Q)
          >=ell(K)+min(P,a+r(I)),
because Q>=r(I) and a+min(P,Q)>=min(P,a+r(I)). This is coalition worth.
Its total is T. Fixed mixtures preserve every incentive and coverage property,
and pay each A_a at least theta M_a. Total becomes
 T+theta min(X,L/4)<=(1+theta/4)T.

For the uniform lower, take a single local operator, any even m, h_i=L_i=1,
P=m/2. The original balanced counting with the local-operator floor forces
total >=m+theta m/4. This subclass is included here, so the upper is sharp
over arbitrary partitions and sizes. Stronger statements requiring multiple
active operators in every lower-bound family need a separate construction.

WHAT IS NEW IN THIS NOTE
Proportional redistribution preserves all actual local owners' whole-bundle
incentives and their separate marginal floors. The elementary coverage lemma
handles coalitions containing arbitrary subsets of local operators, which was
not supplied by merely merging the operators in the old theorem. The physical
model, public floor, old hardware/grid rule, and matching one-block lower are
unchanged. No mixed-role ownership result, new lower premium, measured pooling
gain, external expert endorsement or historical-priority claim is asserted.
