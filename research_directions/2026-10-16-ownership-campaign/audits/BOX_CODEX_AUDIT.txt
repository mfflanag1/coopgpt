1. Exact local-total envelope: derivation checked
------------------------------------------------
Fix the orientation in which a is short and its total t<=min(A,B)=T.
Every t in[0,T] is realizable by coordinatewise underreports of the actual
owners. The largest capped opposite total is D_b(t)=sum min(b_i,t), at least t.
Every capped total between t and D_b(t) can be attained by choosing accounts
inside the coordinate intervals[0,min(b_i,t)]. Thus the payment total is
maximized at capped ratio s=min(D_b(t)/t,2), because(rho-1)exp(1-rho) peaks
at rho=2. This is actual report attainment; no owner is cloned or split.

I independently differentiated t[1+(s-1)exp(1-s)/2] on each cap piece.
Writing D_b(t)=k t+r gives the stated bracket
 (k-1)+(rho-1)(rho-k).
For k=0 it is rho^2-rho-1>=-1 on[1,2], and the derivative is >=1/2.
For k=1 it is(rho-1)^2>=0. The remaining nonclipped case is the boundary
k=2,r=0,rho=2, consistent with the clipped derivative C. Continuity at cap
breakpoints and t=0 establishes monotonicity, so t=T is optimal.

The original short pool realizes total T already. Keep it unchanged, cap
the original long accounts at T, and scale those capped accounts uniformly
to total T min(rho,2). This independently constructs an attaining lower
report and is the witness used in the audit harness. The opposite orientation
at T has ratio1 and value T when the original pools were unequal; at a tie
both give T. This verifies the closed form and its least-majorant property.

Summing across branches is legitimate even for owners with multiple roles:
the report domain is the coordinatewise product of all their branch reports.
The branchwise attaining choices can therefore be combined into one actual
fixed-owner report vector. No shared capacity constraint couples these local
coordinates in the stated model. The conclusion would need reconsideration
if such a constraint were added to the model.

2. Recipient extrema: global cross-signs checked across pool switches
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix recipient k!=i. Its own a_k,b_k remain unchanged as i reports differently.
For a_k>=b_k, increasing another b coordinate increases its payment:
 - In B>=A, b_k<=a_k<=A, so the gap b_k-a_k is nonpositive; the capped
   b ratio rises and q falls, increasing a_k+q(b_k-a_k).
 - In B<A, B>=b_k and min(a_k,B)-b_k is nonnegative and nondecreasing;
   q(B) is also nondecreasing, so b_k+q(B)(min(a_k,B)-b_k) increases.
For a_k<=b_k the signs reverse: in B>=A the gap min(b_k,A)-a_k>=0;
in B<A, B>=b_k>=a_k and the gap a_k-b_k<=0 is constant.
Swapping the two pools establishes the signs for other a coordinates.

At A=B the two formulas agree; cap switches are continuous. At a zero pool,
all payments tend to zero with T. If a_k=b_k, both pools have total at least
that common own account, and the recipient receives exactly a_k throughout.
Hence the endpoint-deletion formulas hold globally, including equality and
zero cases. Branchwise minima/maxima add because i's branch coordinates are
independently bounded. Different recipients' attaining reports need not agree.

The example with fixed recipients(1,0),(0,1) and reporter(x,y) in[0,1]^2 is
correct: the two recipient payments always sum to1, while their individual
ranges are[1/(2e),1-1/(2e)]. Thus the product range is a strict joint relaxation.
It must not be called an exact representation of the attainable payment vector.

3. Actual-owner domain inclusion and the entire pointwise chain
--------------------------------------------------------------
For an own local-report increase, the set over which each opponent minimum
and maximum is taken expands. Therefore its minimum decreases and maximum
increases. The reporting owner's z_i, H_i and the exact total ceiling Lbar
are nondecreasing. Opponents' outer hardware coordinates remain fixed. Thus
each old feasible auxiliary box is contained in the new one, and at every old
vector the primitive's own-hardware payment is nondecreasing.

For an own shared-power increase delta, local payments and recipient extrema
do not change. Translating c to c+delta e_i shifts its own lower/upper bounds
and total ceiling by exactly delta, with all opposing intervals unchanged.
The primitive is monotone in its own opposing account. These two arguments
compose to whole-owner comparisons; no other owner's own payment is assumed
monotone under the reporter's change.

The full vector p+z belongs to every box, so Rbox>=N. For any actual own
underreport, every opponent z'_k lies between its exact recipientwise extrema.
Restore only the reporter's outer shared account and then hardware account.
Those are valid monotonicity comparisons in the primitive's account domain.
Its own opposing account is at most p_i+z_i, and total opposing mass is at
most P+Lbar. Thus the restored vector lies in the full-profile box and its
objective dominates the original nested own payment. Taking the supremum
gives E<=Rbox. This does not require the restored vector to be physically
attainable and does not establish equality with E.

Dbox is contained in the floor domain, which is contained in the Lbar range,
which is contained in the CM range. Maximization gives the remaining chain.
Actual nested coalition coverage then transfers coordinatewise, as does the
known7/4 total budget. Null owners have own hardware and own upper account0,
so receive0. If grand F=0, either H=0 or P+M=0; all resulting payments vanish.
For a grid-only owner, the box fixes every opposing account exactly at p+z,
while its own account is fixed at p_i, hence Rbox=N=E. This also follows for
Rfloor from the cross-sign argument in its source note.

4. Capped-box optimizer: feasibility and attainment checked
---------------------------------------------------------
Assume H>0,n>=2, lower_i<=beta, beta+Lminus<=K and lower_k<=upper_k.
At a short total T, feasibility requires
 sum lower<=T<=min(K,H,beta+Uminus).
The largest own coordinate is min(beta,T-Lminus). If this equals beta,
the residual T-beta lies in[Lminus,Uminus]; otherwise it equals T-Lminus
and all opponents can stay at their lower bounds. Thus the maximal own
coordinate is feasible, not merely an algebraic relaxation. Its coefficient
is1-q>0, so it is optimal. Before T0=beta+Lminus only its own account changes;
after T0 the sign of min(a,T)-beta gives exactly the claimed endpoint maximum.

The long region exists precisely when K>=H and beta+Uminus>=H. For a
nonnegative capped-own gap d-a>=0, the least capped total is
 max(H,d+sum min(lower_k,H)).
If the baseline total is below H, every baseline account is below H. Filling
opponents to total H is possible by the region criterion; no account needs
to exceed H before that total is reached. This attains the stated minimum.

For a strict negative gap d<a, beta=d<H. Therefore own overflow is zero.
The largest capped total is exactly
 min(beta+sum min(upper_k,H),K-sum(lower_k-H)_+).
Fill opponents from their lower bounds toward min(upper_k,H); the two terms
are exactly the available cap capacity and the budget net of unavoidable
overflow. This maximum is at least H: an opponent lower bound>=H already
ensures it; otherwise, if filling all useful caps still gave total<H, no
upper bound could exceed H, so beta+Uminus<H, contradicting feasibility.

Increasing a feasible own coordinate toward beta preserves long feasibility.
In the negative-gap region, the maximal capped ratio is nondecreasing and
a-c decreases, so its negative exponential penalty decreases. In the positive
region, the minimal capped-ratio derivative is0 or1/H, and the gap derivative
is nonnegative because0<=c-a<=H before the own cap. Above H the value is
constant. Equality joins continuously and is handled by the nonnegative branch.
These arguments establish the optimizer and its claimed witnesses on all
valid finite-dimensional domains, not only physical instances.

For n=1 the physical answer is F=min(H,beta); for H=0 the value is zero.
The input feasibility conditions are assumptions of the generic reference
optimizer, rather than fully validated runtime preconditions. That is acceptable
for this research reference but should be explicit if exposed as a public API.

5. Concrete findings, repairs, and remaining archival limitation
---------------------------------------------------------------
Finding A, repaired: tightened_range_reference.py returned all-zero witness
vectors at H=0 even when p>0. Example rows[[1,0,0],[0,0,0]] has required
lower accounts[1,0], so the returned zero vector was infeasible. The value0
was correct. The source owner changed the return to independent copies of p.
I reran the example and verified witnesses[[1,0],[1,0]].

Finding B, repaired: the original box note stated its negative-gap formula
for d<=a. At d=a=H<beta it omits own overflow and need not give an attainable
capped ratio. Example H=a=1,beta=K=2,opponent lower0/upper1 yields the stated
rho2, but fixing own account2 leaves actual capped ratio1. Payment remains1
because the gap is zero. The implementation already sent equality through
the positive branch. The source owner narrowed the text to d<a and explicitly
handled equality in d>=a. I checked the edited text and a long-only equality
case with own lower1.25,beta2,K2, obtaining payment1 at witness[2,0].

Historical limitation: mixed/verify_explicit_range_rule.py still deliberately
returns dummy zero vectors for zero-valued instances and its verification code
explicitly skips their attainment. This does not affect payment correctness,
but those historical dummy vectors must not be described as feasible maximizers.
Current tightened/floor/box implementations return valid baseline witnesses.
No remaining repair was applied by this audit lane.

  monotonicity; independent proportional capped witnesses attained every value.

 -2265 complete physical profiles from n2,m1 ternary; n3,m1 binary; n2,m2 binary.
 -96346 actual whole-owner underreports checked against Rbox and recipient bounds.
 -14506 whole-owner monotonicity/domain-inclusion checks.
 -11108 complete physical coalition inequalities.
 -2400 generic box domains, independent of the physical local-payment formulas.

funding ratio1.4434469107429617 is diagnostic only. The checks use binary floats,

proofs, or guarantees of truthfulness after finite-precision implementation.
