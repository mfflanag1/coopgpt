CHECKED DERIVATIONS
1. Fixed owners and growing family. For every n>=3 there are exactly n hardware
owners and one local-power owner, on n cyclic branches. Every hardware owner
holds47/40 on one branch and9/10 on the preceding branch, hence hardware total
s=83/40. All owners and branches are active; no null-owner padding supplies the
unbounded count. Underreports keep this one owner set and technology fixed.

2. Local formulas. Each branch has short local total1 and capped hardware ratio
r=19/10. Its two hardware payments are q and(9/10)q, and the power payment is
1-q. Hence each hardware owner's aggregate is a=rq and power's aggregate is nb.
The physical totals H=ns, M=F=n and local total nz follow. Because r<2, the
monotone local-total ceiling equals this current nz. The exact certificate
verifies z<s and s<3b, so all uncapped outer formulas hold for every n>=3.

3. Hardware recipients. Their own auxiliary ceiling is a<s and every box total
is <=K=nz<H. For fixed total T the outer payment increases in own opposing
account. For T>=a, the upper envelope a+q(T)(min(s,T)-a) increases with T;
for T<a the payment is at most T<=a. The full local vector attains the upper
bound at T=K, proving the displayed exact hardware Rbox formula. No joint
attainability of independent recipient extrema is assumed in this argument.

4. Power recipient. Suppressing all local power jointly makes ALL hardware
local payments zero, so those opponent lower endpoints are jointly attainable.
The vector retaining the recipient's full nb alongside those zero opponents
is nevertheless only an AUXILIARY box witness; its own account and opponents'
responses come from different reports. It satisfies every stated coordinate
bound and the total ceiling because nb<nz. The proof maximizing at T=nb is
correct: T(1-q(T)) is nondecreasing below nb, while nb(1-q(T)) is nonincreasing
above nb. The derivative inequality uses x=ns/T>=1, justified by T<=K<H.

5. Exact exponential enclosure. Odd/even Taylor polynomials S25,S24 have the
claimed lower/upper signs for exp(-x) on x>=0 by the Lagrange remainder. The
q interval is rounded outwards using exact floor/ceiling division. The signs
and monotonicities in the interval formula for G are correct, including the
negative term b exp(1-s/b)/2. All coefficient and argument positivity checks
are exact. The strict G>15001/10000 conclusion does not depend on floats or
assert statements. Independent replay confirms the reported enclosure.

6. Response coupling lemma. For a pure-power owner, hardware role payments are
entirely opponents' payments. In a power-short branch their ratio to the own

bounded hardware-support hypothesis; its vanishing constant for k growing
does not imply a uniform unrestricted bound.

7. Null-owner invariance is correctly profile-wise. Adding an all-zero account
changes neither primitive ratios nor recipient endpoints, and adds a forced
