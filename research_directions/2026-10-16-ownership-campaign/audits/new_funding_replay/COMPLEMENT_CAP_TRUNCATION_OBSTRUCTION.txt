FIXED-OPPONENT SURPLUS DOES NOT BOUND CAPPED OPPONENT LOSSES
16 October 2026. Exact actual-report obstruction; not a C^2 counterexample.

CLAIM BEING REFUTED
The fixed-opponent coalition theorem gives opposing capped mass at least
min{1,P_-i+M_-i}. It is not valid to remove that min when there are multiple
positive-gap recipients. In particular, C_i=Z-z_i-M_-i does NOT generally bound
the decrease of OTHER CAPPED opposing accounts under i's actual underreport.
Consequently E_i<=a_i+q0 exp(C_i)g_i need not hold even when the full profile
has no overflow, M=H, S<2, and its restored outer pool remains hardware-short.

AN UNBOUNDED FIXED-OWNER FAMILY
Let c=1/(2e), C=1+c, choose0<t<=1/10, and set epsilon=ct/2. Fix arbitrary
integers r,m>=1. There are r branches, hardware owners A_1,...,A_m, and two
local-resource owners i,B. Each A_k has hardware1/(mr) in each branch.
Each of i and B has local resource1/r in each branch. Owner B also has grid
p_B=1-c-epsilon>0. All other reports vanish. Every owner is active; both
owner and branch counts grow. All reports/deviations remain with these actual
owners and their fixed resource eligibility.

Every full branch is hardware-short with capped local long ratio2. Thus
H=M=1, Z=C, z_i=z_B=c, and total hardware-owner local payment is1-c.
The full outer accounts are
 d_i=c, d_B=1-epsilon, d_{A_k}=(1-c)/m.
Every full account is strictly below1; S=2-epsilon<2. Owners i and B are
positive-gap recipients. Every A_k has a negative gap c/m.
The opponents of i can still produce the full unit using B's local resource,
so M_-i=1 and
 C_i=Z-z_i-M_-i=(1+c)-c-1=0.                          (CT1)

ACTUAL PROFITABLE UNDERREPORT WITH NO OUTER RESTORATION NEEDED
Owner i reduces its local resource in every branch from1/r to(1-t)/r.
Its hardware and grid are already zero, so the actual outer hardware remains1.
The local primitive coefficient becomes q'=c exp(t). The aggregate local
payments are
 z'_i=q'(1-t), z'_B=q', sum_k z'_{A_k}=1-q'.
Owner B's opposing account is
 p_B+z'_B=1+c(exp(t)-1)-epsilon>1,
because exp(t)-1>=t and epsilon=ct/2. Thus B becomes capped although the full
profile had no overflow. Every other account remains uncapped. The actual
counterprofile opposing capped sum is
 S'=1+[1-q']+q'(1-t)=2-c t exp(t).
Hence S-S'=c t(exp(t)-1/2)>0.

The old and new ACTUAL outer payments to i have ratio
 N'_i/N_i=exp(t)(1-t) exp[c t(exp(t)-1/2)].             (CT2)
It is strictly greater than1 throughout the stated interval. Indeed the series
for-log(1-t) gives
 log(1-t)+t>=-t^2/[2(1-t)].
Also exp(t)-1/2>=1/2, so the logarithm of(CT2) is at least
 (t/2)[c-t/(1-t)]>0,
because t/(1-t)<=1/9<c (the elementary e<3 gives c>1/6).
Thus E_i>=N'_i>N_i, while(CT1) would make the claimed untruncated bound equal
to N_i=a_i+q0g_i. This is an exact violation of that bound.

WHY COALITION COVERAGE REMAINS CONSISTENT
The RAW aggregate opposing local payments to the opponents are unchanged:
 z'_B+sum_k z'_{A_k}=1=z_B+sum_k z_{A_k}.
The decrease to hardware owners is offset by B's local-payment increase.
The outer cap hides part of B's increase, so the capped opposing sum falls.
The valid grand-coalition floor only retains min{1,P_-i+M_-i}=1; its cap is
therefore load-bearing here. Weighted/partitioned actual-coalition floors or
the sharp nonlinear outgoing bound remain valid and may give a cap-safe repair.

This note does NOT show sum E_i>C^2, and does not rule out a sharper global
funding proof. It blocks only the stated use of an uncapped complement-surplus
charge as though it controlled other capped accounts.
