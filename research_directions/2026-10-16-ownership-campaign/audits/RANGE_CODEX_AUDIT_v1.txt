   Therefore power total<=[1-exp(1-eta)/2]l<=h/2. Zero branches are valid.
   Summing gives both z_i^h<=H_i and sum z_i^l<=H/2, without changing owners.

2. Restoring only shared p_i does not alter any local payment. Outer own-account
   monotonicity justifies that first increase. At the remaining local underreport,

   branch budget and production monotonicity. Restoring only H'_i to H_i in the
   OUTER account vector is legitimate even though the resulting account vector
   may not arise from a physical profile. The proof uses a payment inequality
   in account space, not a claim of feasible physical restoration. Every owner
   may have a different opposing vector, but each bound is individual and uniform.
   The common full-profile H_i,B_i and P,K constants are retained on summation.

3. D_+<=P+sum(z_i-H_i)_+<=P+sum z_i^l<=P+H/2 is valid, since z_i^h<=H_i.
   The cap inequality used later is also valid:
   min(p_i+z_i,H)<=min(p_i,H)+z_i.

4. Coverage: for any actual coalition S, local coverage gives z(S)>=M(S), and
   the physical depth-two production is exactly
   F(S)=min(H(S),p(S)+M(S)). Outer coverage therefore covers F(S), and E>=N.
   The supremum set expands under a whole-bundle report increase, proving E's

   If F=0, either H=0 or P=M=0; underreports preserve the corresponding zero

   for U>=1, giving7/4 at U=1. Owners with nonpositive excess require no bound
