1. ACTUAL OWNERS AND THE SHARP ROLE-SEPARATED RULE
The hardware-block rule is genuinely defined at the actual-owner level. For a hardware owner b,
 x_b=min(e_b,p_b e_b/(e_-b+e_b)),
 p_b=(P-sum_{c!=b}r_c)_+,
so its own residual r_b cancels from the coefficient. Holding all other owners' reports fixed, p_b and e_-b remain fixed even if the hardware owner simultaneously changes many coordinates, and e_b is coordinatewise nondecreasing. This proves whole-bundle monotonicity directly; it does not assume a generic owner-merging theorem. I independently checked the naive per-pod merging counterexample.

A local owner a's payout is min(u,K u/(u+L_-a)), with u=ell_a and K=(H-P)_+. Both functions are nondecreasing in u, including the zero-denominator extension. Arbitrary crossing of hardware/local ownership partitions does not change this own-report argument. The grid proof correctly uses that X(P) is1-Lipschitz until total hardware saturation and constant afterwards; this gives growth(X)<=growth(G), sufficient for monotonicity of G-(X-L/4)_+.

The split-owner floor is correct: ell_a M/L=ell_a-ell_a D/L >=(ell_a-D)_+, because0<=ell_a,D<=L. PURE_ROLE_BUNDLES uses the capped D=min(L,(P-Q)_+), whereas the static notes use the uncapped positive part. These are different conventions but yield the same positive-part marginal for every ell_a<=L; no contradiction results.

The coalition reduction retains actual owners. An included local block may contain rows outside the hardware coalition; its payment is therefore at least the claimed contribution(M/L)ell(K). The proof asks for coverage of the larger collection of all row subsets K within an actual hardware-block union I, which is a valid sufficient relaxation, not introduction of strategic subowners.

I rederived both1/4 deficit bounds. In the intermediate case, with L=1, delta=P-Q, outside residual r, unmatched inside local amount s, and outside local amount o<=1-s, the critical expression is
 min(delta,s-r)-delta*s+o*min(r,1-delta).
For r<=1-delta it is bounded by min(delta+r,s)-s(delta+r)<=s(1-s)<=1/4; for r>=1-delta it is bounded by1-delta-r<=0. The grid-only bound is likewise valid in all four intervals for P-r. The L=0, P<=Q and P>=H branches cover all omitted boundaries.

The theta mixture preserves incentives and covers coalitions with total T+theta min(X,L/4). The even-pod one-local-owner family proves the matching uniform1+theta/4 lower through a positive combination of actual coalition/floor inequalities. The sharpness is over the class of all admitted partitions, as stated; it is not claimed for every fixed partition. The model still excludes one owner spanning hardware/local/grid roles and overlapping ownership shares in a physical row.

2. STATIC VERSUS INCENTIVE FUNDING
The arbitrary-block static mixture is valid. Its report-dependent lambda is expressly not claimed truthful. Its extra funding is theta D(1-D/E)_+<=theta E/4<=theta T/4 in the only nontrivial range. The singleton-local static allocation has a_j+x_j=ell_j and totalT. Its grid coverage follows from
 sum_{j in J}min(ell_j,D)>=min(ell(J),D),
so the asserted all-profile static optimum B_theta=T is justified independently of finite LP evidence.

The three-profile certificate uses the same five actual owners throughout. The O->U and O->V comparisons change only one hardware owner's capacity. At O the three coalitions imply x+y>=6-4B; at the spokes the local floors and grid-plus-other-hardware coalition imply x_U,y_V<=3B-1-2theta. Combining gives B>=(4+2theta)/5, hence6/5 at theta1. I reconstructed its14-row cancellation independently with exact rational arithmetic: factor coefficient-1, all payment coefficients zero, and RHS-(4+2theta)/5. The general theta bound may be weaker than the elementary B>=1; the note does not claim otherwise.

The active-count extension is valid but is correctly described as padding: added mass z produces B>=(6+z)/(5+2z), approaching6/5 for z=1/k. Every added owner/resource is positive, but the aggregate added mass vanishes. This is not evidence of a growing-complexity obstruction, a lower above5/4, or an unrestricted mixed-owner impossibility.

The one-pod truthful efficient calibration, theta0 baseline and exact counterexample to the proposed singleton6/5 ansatz are all correct as written.

3. SINGLETON STRUCTURAL REDUCTION, STAR BARRIER AND RIGIDITY
The pair-excess characterization is an equivalence: e_j>=0 handles all no-grid coalitions, and deleting local-only members leaves exactly the disjoint J,K constraint
 c+x(J)+e(K)>=min(P,h(J)+r(K)).
Every disjoint pair itself represents an actual coalition. The minimal local payment formula and the identity for e_j when0<=x_j<=ell_j follow algebraically. These are static coverage reductions; they do not impose incentives automatically.

I independently checked the subset inequality(S) for the whole unrestricted-size single-star family. The omission of selected indices with a_i=ell_i-x_i=0 can only increase the deficit expression. After omitting them, selected x_i<P implies x_i=u_i>=(L+P)/5. The P>=2L/3, at-least-two-remaining-indices, and one-remaining-index branches are exhaustive, with the stated boundary inequalities valid. The resulting central/spoke payments satisfy floors, coverage, budgets and every unilateral comparison present in that restricted report domain.

The obstruction scope is accurate: one arbitrary-size star by itself has exact worst-case factor6/5, so merely increasing the number of its spokes cannot prove a larger lower. This does NOT provide compatible payments for unions of different stars or for the full report domain. The note explicitly preserves that distinction; it should remain prominent in consolidation.

The balanced two-pod rigidity statement follows without symmetry, continuity or homogeneity. At any scale lambda>0, upper bounds H1,H2<=3lambda/5 at the center combine with the central certificate to force equality. Its three coalitions then force A1=A2=C=2lambda/5. At each spoke the floors and grid-plus-unchanged-hardware sum bind exactly as stated. The individual spoke split between grid and unchanged hardware remains unfixed.
