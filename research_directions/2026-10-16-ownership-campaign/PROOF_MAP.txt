MODEL
Actual owner i reports h_ij,l_ij,p_i>=0; only coordinatewise underreports allowed.
H=sum h, M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), P=sum p, F=min(H,P+M).
Original primitive: short total T, long caps d_i=min(long_i,T),
rho=sum d_i/T, q=exp(1-rho)/2, Phi_i=(1-q)short_i+q d_i.
At T=0 all payments zero. Local z_i=sum_j Phi_i(h_j,l_j);
raw nested N_i=Phi_i((H_k)_k,(p_k+z_k)_k).
E_i is the supremum of N_i over i's ACTUAL whole-bundle underreport box,
opponents fixed. Nested boxes give own-report monotonicity; E>=N preserves
coalition coverage. Utilities are payments, without private costs or sybils.
C=1+1/(2e),K=e-1. Values/public coefficients require separate transfer proofs.
