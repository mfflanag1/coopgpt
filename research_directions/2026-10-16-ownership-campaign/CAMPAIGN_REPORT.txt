1. ECONOMIC MODEL AND INCENTIVES
Each fixed actual owner i may hold hardware h_ij and local power/resource l_ij
on many branches j, together with shared grid capacity p_i. Define
 H_j=sum_i h_ij, L_j=sum_i l_ij, H=sum_j H_j,
 M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), P=sum_i p_i, F=min(H,P+M).
Only binding componentwise underreports are feasible. Utility is payment.
Owners cannot split or merge identities; a hypothetical deviation holds all
other owners' reports fixed. Private operating costs, overreports, coordinated
deviations, and endogenous participation are outside this theorem.

The local two-pool primitive has short total T, capped long inputs d_i,
rho=(sum_i d_i)/T and q=exp(1-rho)/2. Its payment is
 Phi_i=(1-q)*short_i+q*d_i, with all payments zero when T=0.
Sum local payments to obtain z_i. The same primitive combines actual hardware
H_i with effective resource p_i+z_i to produce raw nested payment N_i.
The mechanism uses the ACTUAL own-report envelope
 E_i(b)=sup_{0<=b'_i<=b_i} N_i(b'_i,b_-i).
Nested report boxes make E_i own-bundle monotone; E_i>=N_i preserves the
proved raw rule's coverage of every actual coalition. The mathematical burden
is bounding the SUM of these separately optimized payments at one full profile.
No simultaneous attainment of distinct owners' deviations is assumed.

3. SHARP BEHAVIOR WHEN LOCAL PAYMENTS ARE DIFFUSE
Let c=1/(2e), C=1+c and K=e-1. Restrict to physical hardware bottlenecks and
full profiles satisfying max_i z_i/H<=epsilon. Let W(epsilon) be the worst
normalized envelope budget over all finite instances satisfying that condition.
With L=log(1/epsilon), we proved
 W(epsilon)=C+cK*epsilon*(L-log L)+O(epsilon).
The matching construction permits both owner and productive-branch counts to
grow, and works for every sufficiently small real epsilon. Hardware itself
need not be diffuse. The diffuse restriction is on the FULL profile; own
counterreports remain the original unrestricted underreport boxes.

The next residual is not solved. For
 R(epsilon)=[W-C-cK*epsilon*(L-log L)]/epsilon,
we have
 liminf R >=-0.777825046106238...,
 limsup R <=-c*(2+K log2) = approximately -0.58695573.
No existence of a residual limit is claimed. The improved lower is meaningful
as a consequence of the new cap-response obstruction, but further gadget tuning
is not recommended as the main research program.

4. NEW STRUCTURE AND A PRECISE FAILED ROUTE
For a local-only whole-bundle underreport retaining an owner's hardware, the
SIGNED total loss to other local recipients is at most Lambda*z_i, sharply,
where v_*exp(v_*)=1/2 and
 Lambda=(1-v_*)^2/v_*=1.194793583015429... .
A sharper role-sensitive statement charges only the local-resource payment
roles: coefficient1 when originally short, Lambda when originally long.
Own hardware-role payments are excluded from that sharper charge. An actual two-owner
instance attains the constant. This is distinct from the sharp positive-loss
bound K*sqrt(z_i D_i) proved earlier in the campaign.

Two owners' separate changes cannot simply be charged by adding these signed
bounds after caps. An exact three-owner profile gives ratio
 (11exp(2/5)-8)/7 >6/5>Lambda for the proposed joint inequality. An offsetting
local gain can be hidden by an outer cap. This obstruction has an unbounded
lift that improves the actual diffuse lower above. It refutes that particular
proof step, not the existence of a better mechanism.

A further sharp inverse-entropy ceiling retains actual owner compatibility:
for H=1, delta=CM-Z, alpha=1-c, a_i=H_i, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1),
and a positive gap g_i=d_i-a_i,
 z_i+alpha*g_i <=2-c-M+M*Q(delta/M),
where Q is the increasing inverse of c+q log(2q) on[c,1/2]. For M=0,
interpret M*Q(delta/M)=0. The inequality is attained
on the full-utilization face. Together with the true ceiling E_i<=1, it gives
a stronger positive-dual inequality when S=sum_i d_i<2, where at most one
owner can be fully capped. These are structural constraints, not claims that
every relaxed moment vector is physically realizable.
