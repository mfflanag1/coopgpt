WEIGHTED DEPTH-TWO PRODUCTION: COROLLARY WITH FIXED ACTUAL OWNERS
16 October2026. Depends on the current unit-incidence7/4 theorem and the earlier conventional weighted-forest reduction. Historical novelty not assessed.

STATEMENT. Let a finite production graph be a disjoint union of trees of radius at most2. Every activity edge e consumes fixed publicly known positive amounts A_ve at its two endpoint resources and earns a fixed publicly known positive profit w_e. Every actual owner may hold arbitrary capacities at arbitrary vertices, including across components. Owners and coordinatewise underreport domains are fixed. Then the unit-model7/4 truthful coalition-covering rule transfers to this model, uniformly in all owner, resource and activity counts.

PROOF. Apply dependency1. Apply dependency2 to the resulting weighted forest. An edge-deleted radius-two tree is a disjoint union of radius-two trees, stars and isolated vertices; choose a center of each nontrivial component. After choosing the center as the shared-resource side, aggregate all terminal leaves adjacent to each depth-one vertex into one local pool, giving each owner the sum of its own capacities at those leaves. Unit profits and incidence make this aggregation value-preserving for every coalition. A depth-one vertex lacking terminal leaves uses a local pool fixed to0. Each component is therefore a member of the unit depth-two model after fixed nonnegative account aggregation; isolated vertices contribute0. Apply dependency3 to each component and multiply by its fixed coefficient alpha_t. Sum all payments to each actual owner. Coverage follows by summing the component coverage inequalities and the exact identity for that coalition. Increasing an owner's original bundle increases each of its transformed component reports; each component payment is own-monotone. Total funding is at most(7/4)sum_t alpha_t F_{G_t}=(7/4)F_w.

OWNER-SET REQUIREMENT. Every component calculation uses the SAME full actual owner index set, including owners whose resources are zero in that component. Neither zero reports nor threshold deletion create/delete/reassign owner identities. The current range rule is not invariant under adding dummy owners, so silently dropping or adding strategic identities would describe a different mechanism. All positive row scalings and coordinate restrictions are public and report-independent.

SCOPE. Public strictly positive activity profits and strictly positive endpoint coefficients only. The statement does not cover private prices/costs, overreports, identity splitting, coalition deviations, negative-profit activities, cyclic weighted graphs, arbitrary-depth forests, or endogenous technology.
