EXACT9/8 ON THE FULL MIXED TERNARY TWO-POOL REPORT GRID
16 October2026. Root's pure-role rule plus checked actual-owner netting. This is a finite-grid theorem with unbounded owner count, not a continuous-report upper bound. No historical-priority claim.

MODEL. Fix any finite actual owner set. Owner i reports an arbitrary pair(a_i,b_i) in{0,1,2}^2 of binding resource amounts. Any fixed eligibility restrictions are allowed by setting the corresponding coordinate identically zero. Coalition value is
 v(S)=min(sum_{i in S}a_i,sum_{i in S}b_i).
Utility is payment. We require nonnegative payments, every actual coalition covered at every allowed profile, and own-coordinate monotonicity on this grid. Owner count is unrestricted. There are no grand-marginal floors, private costs, identity changes or physical consumption utility.

THEOREM. This entire class admits truthful coalition-covering payments with total at most(9/8)v(N). The uniform factor9/8 is sharp, already for two pure owners on each side. This sharp finite-grid result does not prove a9/8 upper for arbitrary real capacities.

1. PURE-ROLE GRID PRIMITIVE
First suppose each owner has at most one positive coordinate; zero accounts remain the SAME actual owners. Set A=sum a_i,B=sum b_i,T=min(A,B),d=|A-B|. Define phi as follows.

If T=0, pay every owner0.
If T>=2, orient A<=B and put q=1/(2*2^d). Pay a short-side owner with amount x the quantity(1-q)x, and a long-side owner with amount z the quantity qz. At A=B, q=1/2 and either orientation yields half each owner's amount. All owner amounts are at most2<=T, so no cap is needed.
If T=1, the short side has exactly one positive unit owner. If the long side has exactly one positive owner, pay1/2 to each of these two owners. If the long side has at least two positive owners, pay1 to the sole short owner and0 to all others. At A=B=1 there is one active owner on each side, so the first case is unambiguous. Zero-report owners always receive0.

COVERAGE AND BUDGET. For T>=2, a coalition with side sums A_S,B_S receives(1-q)A_S+qB_S, which is at least min(A_S,B_S); its total funding is
 T+q*d = T+d/(2*2^d).
For positive integer d, d<=2^(d-1), so the excess factor is at most1/(4T)<=1/8. At d0 there is no excess. At T1 with one active owner on each side the only productive coalitions contain both, who together receive1. With at least two active long owners, every productive coalition contains the unique short unit owner, who receives1. Both T1 cases are efficient. T0 is trivial.

2. OWN-REPORT MONOTONICITY AND DISCRETE1-LIPSCHITZ PROPERTY
Holding all other actual owners fixed and keeping a given pure owner's side fixed, its payment is nondecreasing in its report0,1,2, and every increase changes its payment by at most the resource increase. In particular its report1-to2 payment increment lies in[0,1]. Also0<=phi_i<=its report, so the claim for increases from0 follows immediately once1-to2 is checked.

If the opposite pool has total0, the payment remains0. Otherwise consider1-to2.
(a) The owner's pool is long or tied and T>=2. After the increase d rises by1, q halves, and its own amount doubles. Its payment is unchanged. If T=1 and its pool is long or tied, the short total and the number of active owners on its side stay fixed. The T1 rule gives the same payment before and after: either1/2 for a sole active owner or0 otherwise.
(b) The owner's pool is strictly short and T>=2. Integer totals imply it stays short or reaches a tie, with the gap d reduced by1. Thus the new q is2q, including at a tie. Its payment increment equals
 2(1-2q)-(1-q)=1-3q.
Here d>=1 and q<=1/4, so this increment lies in[1/4,1].
(c) The owner's pool is strictly short and T=1. It is the sole unit owner of that pool. If the other pool has one positive owner, that owner must report2; the old payment is1/2 and the new profile is a2-to2 tie, giving payment1 and increment1/2. If the other pool has at least two positive owners, the old payment is1. After the increase the new short total is2 and the new payment lies in[1,2], including a tie. The increment therefore lies in[0,1].
This exhausts all cases. The claim is a discrete Lipschitz property on the stated grid, not a derivative claim on unexamined real reports.

3. NETTING WITHOUT SPLITTING ACTUAL OWNERS
For arbitrary mixed reports define ownerwise
 t_i=min(a_i,b_i), u_i=(a_i-b_i)_+, w_i=(b_i-a_i)_+.
All u_i,w_i lie in{0,1,2}, at most one is positive, and the actual owner set is unchanged. Apply the preceding primitive to these residual accounts and pay
 y_i=t_i+phi_i(u,w).
No owner is cloned, and no independent strategic identities are assigned to the two original coordinates. The sign of a residual account is an accounting convention; incentives are proved below for the full actual owner's coordinates.

For every actual coalition S, the exact identity
 v(S)=sum_{i in S}t_i+min(sum_{i in S}u_i,sum_{i in S}w_i)
holds. Primitive coverage therefore gives actual coalition coverage. With T_res=min(sum u_i,sum w_i), total funding is at most
 sum_i t_i+(9/8)T_res <=(9/8)(sum_i t_i+T_res)=(9/8)v(N).

Fix owner i and increase a_i by one grid unit, holding b_i and all opponents fixed. If a_i>=b_i before the change, t_i stays fixed and its positive hardware residual increases by one; primitive monotonicity applies. If a_i<b_i, t_i rises by one and its power residual falls by one, possibly to zero. The primitive's discrete1-Lipschitz property bounds that residual payment loss by one. Hence the full payment cannot decrease. The integer step cannot jump across both sides of the zero residual; a longer increase is a sequence of unit increases. Increasing b_i is symmetric. Simultaneous own-coordinate increases are handled by such a sequence. This establishes whole-owner truthfulness on the FULL mixed ternary grid with fixed actual identities.

4. SHARPNESS AND EXACT LIMITS
Two pure owners per side with reports in{1,2} form a subdomain. The m2,H2 case of BINARY_POPULATION_STOPPING_LEMMA.txt proves the matching9/8 lower using fixed-owner report transitions. Thus9/8 is the exact uniform ternary-grid optimum, even though some eligibility patterns admit efficiency.

The theorem scales to a common public grid{0,s,2s} by multiplying capacities/payments by s>0. It does not extend by arbitrary rounding or independent cloning: rounding can lose coalition value, and merging strategic clones does not preserve incentives without a separate proof. In particular the result does not settle real-report two-pool funding, heterogeneous per-owner grids, additional positive report levels, or interacting depth-two production. The significance here is an exact stopping result: unrestricted owner growth and full mixed ownership on this common ternary two-pool grid cannot produce a lower exceeding9/8.
