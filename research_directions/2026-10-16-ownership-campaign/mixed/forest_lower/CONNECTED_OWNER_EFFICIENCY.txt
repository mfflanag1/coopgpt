CONNECTED EXCLUSIVE OWNER BUNDLES CANNOT AMPLIFY A FOREST LOWER BOUND
16 October 2026. Conventional structural lemma. Hierarchical tree allocations are an established style of core construction; no historical-priority claim is made here.

MODEL. Fix a finite resource forest. Each edge is an activity with fixed nonnegative profit and fixed strictly positive consumption coefficients at its two endpoints. Resource capacities are nonnegative reals. Every resource vertex belongs exclusively to one actual owner. Each owner's assigned vertices induce a connected set inside each physical tree in which that owner appears. An owner may appear in several distinct physical trees, but within each tree its full assigned bundle must be connected. This is a property of fixed resource eligibility, not of which reported capacities are positive. Zero reports do not change the partition or the root choice.

All actual owner identities are fixed. An owner may underreport any part of its entire assigned bundle, binding the available capacities. Utility is payment. There are no private costs, overreports, identity changes, coalition deviations or grand-marginal fairness floors in the theorem.

THEOREM. This entire class admits nonnegative, all-profile coalition-covering, whole-owner-monotone payments with total exactly the grand production value F. Counts of owners, vertices and activities, tree degree and tree depth are unrestricted. Hence its optimal uniform funding factor is1. No actual-owner report-transition family confined to this class can prove a subsidy lower bound above1.

CONSTRUCTION. Root every physical tree once and for all, independently of reports. Let T_v be the full descendant subtree rooted at vertex v, and let F_v(c) be production using only its resources and its internal activities. Set
 q_v(c)=F_v(c)-sum_{u child of v}F_u(c).
These q_v are intermediate accounting quantities, not separate strategic players. Pay each actual owner i
 y_i(c)=sum_{v assigned to i}q_v(c).
The proof below establishes actual-owner incentives directly; it does not invoke a generic owner-merging principle.

NONNEGATIVITY AND EFFICIENCY. Optimal plans in the child subtrees use disjoint resources and can be combined inside T_v while ignoring v and its incident edges. Thus F_v>=sum_child F_u and q_v>=0. Telescoping over each full physical tree gives sum_v q_v=F_root; summing trees proves sum_i y_i=F. This argument uses only feasible addition of plans on disjoint resources.

CONNECTED-SET COVERAGE. Let S be any nonempty connected set of vertices in one rooted tree. It has a unique highest vertex r. Let B(S) consist of children w outside S whose parent belongs to S. Because S is connected, it contains every path between its vertices; therefore the full subtree T_r partitions as
 S together with the disjoint full subtrees T_w, w in B(S).
The intermediate accounting sum telescopes exactly:
 sum_{v in S}q_v=F_r-sum_{w in B(S)}F_w.
An optimal production plan on S and optimal plans on all the T_w can be combined feasibly in T_r, ignoring crossing edges. Consequently
 sum_{v in S}q_v>=F(c restricted to S).
For an arbitrary vertex subset, apply this to each of its induced connected components. Edge activities with an absent endpoint have zero feasible level because endpoint coefficients are strictly positive; thus its production is the sum over those components. The q_v therefore cover every vertex subset. In particular, the union of the resources of any actual-owner coalition is covered, proving every required actual coalition inequality. The stronger intermediate vertex-subset coverage is proved here, not presumed to follow from actual coalition constraints.

ACTUAL-OWNER INCENTIVES. Fix one actual owner i and one physical tree. Its assigned vertices S_i are connected, so its payment contribution in this tree is
 F_{r_i}(c)-sum_{w in B(S_i)}F_w(c).
Under any coordinatewise increase of i's own whole report, every subtracted F_w stays fixed: T_w contains no vertex assigned to i, by connectedness of S_i. The first term is monotone in the reported capacities, since any previously feasible production plan remains feasible. Hence the payment contribution is nondecreasing. If the same actual owner appears in several physical trees, sum these nondecreasing contributions. The proof applies to all real reports, including zero capacities, and to simultaneous changes of all coordinates of the actual owner.

UNIT-PROFIT UNIT-CONSUMPTION EVALUATION. In the target unit model, all F_v and q_v can be computed in one postorder traversal. Set
 R_v=(c_v-sum_{u child of v}R_u)_+,
 q_v=min(c_v,sum_{u child of v}R_u),
 F_v=q_v+sum_{u child of v}F_u,
with empty child sums zero. These are the standard weighted-capacity vertex-cover dynamic states recorded in ownership/SEPARATOR_GLUE_OBSTRUCTIONS_v1.txt. Payment evaluation is O(|V|+|N|) arithmetic operations, after the fixed ownership/root structure is given. The broader weighted/coefficient statement can instead use the finitely many subtree production LPs; no linear-time claim is made there.

EXACT LIMITATIONS. Shared additive ownership of a resource vertex is excluded. Disconnected bundles within one physical tree are excluded. Either feature can destroy the own-payment telescoping argument: an allegedly fixed subtracted subtree may contain another resource of the same owner, or a changing capacity may be shared with an opponent. No claim is made that every disconnected partition needs subsidy; connectedness is sufficient for efficiency, not necessary. The known two-input9/8 lower has multiple genuine owners contributing to each resource and is outside this class. The earlier virtual-core2-1/m example has one actual owner on a disconnected alternating vertex set and is also outside it.

The theorem imposes no grand-marginal payout floors. It therefore does not contradict the singleton-local6/5 lower with theta1 floors, or the sharp role-separated1+theta/4 theorem. Some resource owners can receive zero payment despite a positive grand marginal; ordinary coalition coverage and payment-only incentives allow this.

RESEARCH CONSEQUENCE. A stronger unrestricted forest lower must use shared resource ownership, disconnected actual bundles, or another feature outside this theorem. Merely lengthening a path or branching a tree while keeping each owner's exclusive bundle connected cannot amplify9/8; it admits factor1 on the full continuous report domain. This is an exact structural stopping result for that proposed family, not an all-forest subsidy theorem and not a historical novelty claim.
