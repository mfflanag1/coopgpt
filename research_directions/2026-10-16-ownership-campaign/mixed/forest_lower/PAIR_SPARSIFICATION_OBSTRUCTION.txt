ALL-PROFILE OWNER-PAIR SPARSIFICATION IS FORCED TO RETAIN EVERY PRODUCTIVE EDGE
16 October2026. Elementary structural obstruction; no novelty claim.

SCOPE. Take the public rank-two production model and exact owner-row lift from OWNER_FOREST_COVER.txt. In this note every activity has EXACTLY two distinct genuine input rows, strictly positive coefficients on both rows, and nonnegative profit. Discard zero-profit activities before forming the productive actual-owner compatibility graph G. Actual eligibility and identities are fixed, and every owner may report independently any nonnegative capacity on its eligible coordinates, including zero. The same conclusion holds on fixed rectangular report domains with a positive upper bound on every eligible coordinate.

A FIXED PAIR-DELETION sparsifier chooses a report-independent subgraph H of G and retains only lifted cross-owner activity variants whose owner-pair edge belongs to H, plus arbitrary same-owner variants. It may retain any subset of the corresponding technologies; it does not add synthetic activities or new capacity constraints. Let F_H denote its production value.

THEOREM. If F_H(b,S)=F(b,S) for every actual coalition S and every allowed report profile b, then H=G. In fact equality of the GRAND value alone at every report profile already forces H=G. Thus positive all-report coalition-preserving sparsification cannot decrease fractional arboricity within this fixed owner-pair deletion architecture.

PROOF. Suppose productive compatibility edge ik is omitted. It is induced by some positive-profit activity e on rows r,s with r eligible to i and s eligible to k (after swapping endpoints if needed). Choose any delta>0 small enough to satisfy any fixed report upper bounds. Let the only positive coordinates in the entire fixed owner population be
 b_ir=A_re*delta,   b_ks=A_se*delta.
Every other coordinate, including all other resources of i and k, is zero. This is a profile in the SAME predetermined eligibility/report domain; no ownership or identity is changed. The original activity e can run at level delta, so
 F(b,{i,k})>=w_e*delta>0.
In the lifted restricted technology, every positive activity must use both nonzero accounting rows(i,r) and(k,s). Each activity has two distinct genuine inputs; a same-owner variant therefore cannot use both nonzero rows. A third-owner variant has a zero required capacity. The only remaining possible variants would have cross-owner pair ik, and all of these were deleted. Hence F_H(b,{i,k})=0. The same reasoning gives F_H(b,N)=0 while F(b,N)>0. This is a contradiction.

The claim is about deleting all cross-owner variants of an edge. It does not say every individual activity variant is indispensable: duplicate or dominated activities can be removed while retaining another route for the same productive pair. It also does not forbid a different nonlinear mechanism, a report-dependent construction with a separate incentive proof, capacity-preserving extended formulations, or a value-preserving decomposition that shares constraints differently.

EXACT LIMITATIONS. One-input activities are excluded from this obstruction because they can make a two-input activity economically redundant on its pair-only profile. Linked owner reports that forbid withholding one eligible coordinate while retaining another are also outside the proof. Neither limitation affects the target forest model, whose activities consume two genuine inputs and whose binding underreports are coordinatewise.

CONCRETE DEPTH-TWO EXAMPLE. A physical single-edge technology with n hardware-only owners and m power-only owners has productive owner compatibility K_{n,m}. Its pair-only report profiles force every one of the nm edges in every exact fixed pair-deletion sparsifier. Thus sparse PHYSICAL technology does not by itself imply sparse all-profile actual-owner compatibility. This does not establish any lower on arbitrary truthful funding: efficient or low-subsidy nonlinear rules may still exist on that same technology.
