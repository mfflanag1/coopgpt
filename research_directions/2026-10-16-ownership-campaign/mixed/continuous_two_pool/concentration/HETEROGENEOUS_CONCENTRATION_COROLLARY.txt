VALUE-WEIGHTED CONCENTRATION BOUND FOR HETEROGENEOUS HARDWARE
16 October 2026. Consequence of CONCENTRATION_THEOREM.txt and the fixed positive
decomposition in ../HETEROGENEOUS_STAR_COROLLARY.txt. The mechanism is unchanged.

Fix public positive values v_j and power intensities e_j. Order a_j=v_j/e_j
decreasingly, set a_{m+1}=0 and delta_k=a_k-a_{k+1}. With the fixed actual-owner
hardware reports g_ij and power reports q_i, put
  h_ik=sum_{j<=k}e_j g_ij, H_k=sum_i h_ik, Q=sum_i q_i,
  T_k=min(H_k,Q), F=sum_k delta_k T_k.
Omit terms with delta_k T_k=0. In every other term, define s_k as the largest
actual owner's short-pool account divided by T_k: max_i h_ik/H_k when H_k<Q,
or max_i q_i/Q when Q<H_k. At a tie either orientation is valid; the component
actually pays exactly T_k, so tied components may be bounded by that sharper
exact value or by the formula below.

The sum of the existing weighted water-fill payments satisfies
  total <= sum_k delta_k T_k[1+g(s_k)]
        = 9F/8 + sum_{k:s_k>1/2} delta_k T_k
                         (2s_k-1)^2(s_k+4)/(216s_k).      (1)
For tied positive components, use any one fixed tie orientation in this
display; choosing the smaller of the two concentrations also gives a valid
upper bound, although the exact efficient contribution is better.
This is a bound on the original globally truthful mechanism, not a new
piecewise mechanism selected according to the concentration reports.

For F>0 let theta be the fraction of decomposed production value carried by
majority-short-owner components:
  theta=sum_{k:s_k>1/2}delta_k T_k / F.
Since (2s-1)^2(s+4)/(216s)<=5/216 on [1/2,1], equation (1) gives
  total/F <=9/8+(5/216)theta.                             (2)
In particular, no majority owner in any positive-weight short-pool component
implies total<=9F/8. Contributions with ties can be omitted from the majority
penalty altogether if their exact efficient payments are used.

A SIMPLE SUFFICIENT REPORT CONDITION
Suppose each owner holds at most half the aggregate power, q_i<=Q/2, and at
most half of EACH hardware type, g_ij<=(sum_l g_lj)/2 for every i,j. Multiplying
by e_j>0 and summing over each prefix gives h_ik<=H_k/2. Thus every component
has s_k<=1/2 and the9/8 funding bound follows. The weaker exact test is just
the appropriate short-pool prefix concentration; the per-type condition is
sufficient, not necessary.

THE VALUE-SHARE BOUND IS ALSO SHARP
Fix three actual owners with (type1,type2,power) reports
  owner1=(1,1,0), owner2=(0,2,1), owner3=(0,0,1),
and both public energy intensities equal1. For any prescribed theta in (0,1),
fix public value ratios a1=1+theta and a2=1-theta. The positive layer weights
are delta1=2theta and delta2=1-theta. At prefix1 the short hardware total is1,
concentration is1, and the two long power accounts each equal1, so its exact
payment total is31/27. At prefix2 the short power total is2, concentration is
1/2, and the two long hardware accounts each equal2, so its exact payment total
is9/4. Thus F=2theta+2(1-theta)=2, the majority component's value share is
exactly theta, and
  total/F=[2theta*(31/27)+(1-theta)*(9/4)]/2
         =9/8+(5/216)theta.
All three owners and their eligible bundles remain fixed; owner2 owns both
hardware and power. Public coefficients are fixed within each instance, and
this family varies those public parameters only to display sharpness at every
theta. Endpoints theta=0 and1 are attained by the corresponding one-component
examples, so no zero-value activity is needed. This proves sharpness across
the stated model class, not within every preassigned set of public values.

This bound makes the relevant concentration observable from the same reported
resource accounts and public coefficients already used by the mechanism.
It does not identify empirical GPU ownership, production elasticities, private
power costs, or measured serving/latency behavior. The decomposition itself is
the established fractional-knapsack identity, and the result is a profilewise
corollary of the new water-fill analysis, without a priority claim.
