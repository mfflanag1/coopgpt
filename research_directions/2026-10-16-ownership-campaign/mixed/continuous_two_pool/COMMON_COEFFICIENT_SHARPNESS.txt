EXACT31/27 BARRIER FOR COMMON CAPPED COEFFICIENTS WITH HALF-PAYMENT AT BALANCE
16 October2026. Root's four-profile chain, formalized for independent review. This is a mechanism-class lower bound, not an unrestricted subsidy lower.

CLASS. Fix actual owners and the continuous binding two-input report domain. At every profile with positive short aggregate A<=B, let x_i be the owner's short account and d_i=min(z_i,A) its capped long account. The rule must have
 y_i=(1-p(b))x_i+p(b)d_i,
with ONE common coefficient p(b) in[0,1]. The coefficient may depend arbitrarily on the entire report profile, owner identities, population size and concentration; no scalar-rho restriction, differentiability or continuity is assumed. At every profile with equal positive aggregate totals, require p=1/2, so each actual owner receives half the sum of its two reports. Require ordinary actual-owner own-report monotonicity. Coalition coverage is not used in the obstruction, although it is automatically satisfied by this convex coefficient form.

THEOREM. Every rule in this class has worst-profile funding factor at least31/27. WATERFILL_PRODUCT_THEOREM.txt achieves31/27 on all nonnegative real reports and arbitrary mixed fixed owners. Thus the constant is exactly optimal WITHIN THIS CLASS.

FOUR PROFILES, THREE FIXED PURE OWNERS. Owner H holds only hardware; owners P1,P2 hold only power. Their identities, roles and eligible report coordinates stay fixed. Write a profile as(h;p1,p2):
 U=(1;2/3,2/3),
 V=(4/3;2/3,2/3),
 W=(1;1,2/3),
 Z=(1;1,1).
All capacities are strictly positive. The actual population is the same at every comparison.

At V the totals tie, so H receives(4/3)/2=2/3. Going from U to V changes only H's report from1 to4/3. Own-report monotonicity therefore gives
 1-p(U)<=2/3, hence p(U)>=1/3.                         (1)
Going from U to W changes only P1's report from2/3 to1. The short total stays1, so the old and new own capped reports are2/3 and1. Monotonicity gives
 p(W)>=(2/3)p(U).                                      (2)
Going from W to Z changes only P2's report from2/3 to1 and similarly gives
 p(Z)>=(2/3)p(W)>=(4/9)p(U)>=4/27.                      (3)
At Z, grand value is1 and total payment is
 (1-p(Z))+p(Z)+p(Z)=1+p(Z)>=31/27.
This proves the lower without limits, derivatives, virtual players or a numerical search.

The four profiles connect changes in BOTH resource pools. They are not all contained in a union of one-pool upward orthants from a single balanced hub, so this does not conflict with the earlier9/8 stopping lemma for that restricted transition architecture.

SCOPE. The example has a sole hardware owner and can be funded efficiently by the unrestricted truthful rule paying H the entire grand value and P1,P2 zero. Therefore31/27 cannot be presented as an intrinsic lower on this three-owner game, or as an unrestricted improvement over the existing9/8 lower. It forces any method beating31/27 to depart from the stated conjunction: either use owner-dependent coefficients or abandon half-payment on all balanced profiles (or otherwise leave the common capped convex-combination form). It does not say either departure is sufficient.

Zero-report extra owners can be included in a larger fixed population without changing the inequalities if the same class hypotheses are imposed. The positive water-fill theorem already handles unbounded active owner counts; the three-owner witness establishes its uniform class optimum. Historical priority of this elementary chain remains unassessed.
