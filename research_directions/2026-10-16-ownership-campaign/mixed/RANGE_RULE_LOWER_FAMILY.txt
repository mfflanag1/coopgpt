AN EXACT PERFORMANCE LOWER FAMILY FOR THE RANGE-OPTIMIZED RULE R
16 October2026. Conditional on the closed-form R characterization. This is an obstruction only for that specified mechanism, not a lower bound for all truthful mechanisms.

Fix one local branch. For each integer m>=1, use2m+1 fixed actual owners: m hardware-only owners with h=1/m each; m local-power-only owners with l=1/m each; and one shared-grid-only owner with p=1. All unlisted reports vanish. Then H=M=P=F=1, every local owner receives z_i=1/(2m), the grid owner receives z_G=0, and K=1+C with C=1+1/(2e).

The long-regime optimizer gives:
 - Grid owner: H_G=0,B_G=1,Sminus=0, so R_G=1/2.
 - Hardware owners: H_i=1/m,B_i=1/(2m), with no forced grid waste and enough other account cap room. Thus rho_max=K and
       R_i=1/m-exp(-C)/(4m).
 - Local-power owners: H_i=0,B_i=1/(2m), with capped grid baseline1. Thus rho_min=1+1/(2m), and
       R_i=exp(-1/(2m))/(4m).
The only possible short-regime total is T=H=P=1. Its payments are1/(2m) to each hardware owner,0 to each local-power owner and1/2 to the grid owner; these do not exceed the respective long-regime values.
Consequently
 sum_i R_i=3/2-exp(-C)/4+exp(-1/(2m))/4
          ->7/4-exp(-C)/4 approximately1.6734823681.
Therefore the sharp uniform funding factor of this explicit R rule is at least that limit. The currently proved7/4 upper factor leaves a real, bounded optimization gap. The lower family has unbounded owner count, but already uses one branch.

R IS A RELAXATION, NOT THE ORIGINAL OWN-REPORT ENVELOPE.
At the displayed full profile the original nested rule pays the grid owner N_G=1/(2e), since all local payments sum1 and the opposing capped total is2. The grid owner controls only p; the nested rule is monotone in that own account, so its actual own-report envelope satisfies E_G=N_G=1/(2e)<R_G=1/2. Thus exact evaluation of R does not secretly solve exact evaluation of E. R is itself truthful because its artificial feasible payment-account set expands appropriately under own reports; its economic report domains and actual owners remain fixed.

This example gives no obstruction to improving the actual envelope E below1.67348, and does not establish the sharpness of7/4 for R or for the model.
