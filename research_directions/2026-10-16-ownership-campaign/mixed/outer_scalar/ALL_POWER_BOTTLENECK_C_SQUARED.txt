C^2 FUNDING OF THE ACTUAL ENVELOPE ON EVERY PHYSICAL POWER BOTTLENECK
16 October 2026. New candidate theorem: independent audit REQUIRED.
Original exponential primitive only. No novelty or optimal-mechanism claim.

THEOREM
Use exactly the fixed-actual-owner model, nested raw rule N, and actual
whole-owner envelope E from
 certificates/box_bound/UNIVERSAL_RETENTION_AND_OUTER_DOMINANCE_THEOREM.txt.
Write c=1/(2e), C=1+c. At EVERY full profile satisfying
                 F=P+M<=H,
including arbitrary positive grid P, all local ratios, mixed actual owners,
and unbounded owner and branch counts,
                 sum_i E_i <= C^2 F.
Underreports may leave the displayed condition. E is the same globally
truthful envelope throughout. This is a funding theorem for that specified
rule, not a lower bound against all mechanisms. Sharpness follows from the
already proved saturation examples with P=0; it does not assert a positive-
grid extremizer or solve the hardware-bottleneck region P+M>H.

DEPENDENCIES
The original exponential primitive's own monotonicity, coalition coverage,
and total factor C; universal total-increase charge 2 Delta_+; universal
retention charge (20/21) Delta_-; and the already audited outer-dominance
funding theorem. Their exact hypotheses are those of the cited file and
UNIVERSAL_TOTAL_INCREASE_LEMMA.txt. The local role support function in section
4 is JOINT_ROLE_TRADEOFF.txt, with B=A+d and q=d. No water-fill primitive is
substituted. The new steps are the cap-preserving scalar reduction, hardware
role tangent, and analytical scalar certificate below.

1. NORMALIZATION AND ACTUAL COUNTERFACTUALS
If H=0 or F=0, all nested payments and their own underreport envelopes are
zero. Otherwise normalize H=1 and write
 p=P/H, m=M/H, f=p+m<=1,
 z_i=local payment/H, Z=sum_i z_i, b_i=p_i+z_i,
 beta=p+Z, Delta=Cm-Z=Delta_-+Delta_+.
Thus m<=Z<=Cm and 0<beta<=C. Let a_i=H_i/H, so sum_i a_i=1.
Let L be the normalized aggregate LOCAL-POWER ROLE payment, so each local
payment is its hardware-role plus power-role contribution. The hardware-role
payment of each actual owner is at most its hardware, hence
                  R=sum_i(b_i-a_i)_+ <= p+L.             (1)
No strategic owners are split by this accounting identity.

Choose an arbitrary, possibly different, whole-owner underreport for every
recipient i. Restore only its outer hardware coordinate to full a_i after
computing its counterfactual local payments; primitive own monotonicity
upper-bounds its actual payment. Every counterfactual opposing account is
at most P+M<=H: each local primitive recipient receives at most its local
output, so z_j^i<=M^i<=M, and p_j^i<=P. Therefore opposing accounts never cap
at H after hardware restoration, including straight interpolations of the
actual opposing vectors. This is special to the physical power bottleneck.

Let t be a restored opposing total, b its recipient's opposing account, and
W_i(t,b) its payment. For t<=1,
 W_i(t,b)=(1-q(t))b+q(t)min(a_i,t),
 q(t)=exp(1-sum_j min(a_j,t)/t)/2.
For t>=1, absence of opposing caps gives
 W_i(t,b)=a_i+exp(1-t)(b-a_i)/2.
These formulas agree at t=1. On every cap cell, W_b>=0, W_t<=W_b,
and W_t<=1/2. Indeed for t<=1, if r=sum_j min(a_j,t)/t and h is the number
of capped hardware accounts, q'=q(r-h)/t. The uncapped-own derivative is
q'(a_i-b)<=qr<=1/2. In the capped-own case h>=1 and the derivative is at
most q[1+r-h]<=qr<=1/2. Here W_b=1-q>=1/2. For t>=1,
W_b=q and W_t=q(a_i-b)<=q<=1/2.

Consequently a total-UP change G>0 with own-account loss D>=0 has gain at
most (G-D)_+/2. This follows by straight interpolation: if G<=D the derivative
is nonpositive; if G>D and W_t>=0 it is at most W_t(G-D), and if W_t<0 it
is already nonpositive. This explicitly covers the outer pool switch.
Writing grid withholding eta_i>=0,
 G-D=(Z^i-Z)-(z_i-z_i^i)-2 eta_i.
The universal local increase charge therefore bounds the sum of all total-UP
gains by Delta_+.

2. TOTAL-DOWN SCALAR REDUCTION, INCLUDING HARDWARE CAPS
Put hmax=max_j a_j. Fix full opposing account b0 and full total beta. Define
 b_*(t)=min(t,max(0,b0-(beta-t)/3)), 0<=t<=beta.
Whenever t<=hmax, at least one hardware account is capped, and
                  W_i(t,b_*(t)) is nondecreasing in t.   (2)
For completeness, on its lower clipping cell its derivative is nonnegative.
On its upper clipping cell b_*=t its derivative is at least1-qr>=1/2.
On a remaining cell b_*'=1/3; here b_*-a_i<=b_*<=t and r>=h.
These inequalities bound the possibly negative q' term, so its derivative
is at least
 (1-q)/3-q(r-h) >= [1-q(3r-2)]/3 >0.
The maximum of q(3r-2) on r>=1 is(3/2)exp(-2/3)<1. All clipping and cap
boundaries are continuous. The zero endpoint is continuous as well.

For a total-DOWN counterfactual t<=beta, set
 epsilon_i=[(beta-t)/3-(b0-b)]_+.
Then b<=b_*(t)+epsilon_i. Since W_b<=1, its gain is bounded by the gain
along b_* plus epsilon_i. For physical deviations,
 epsilon_i=[(Z-Z^i)/3-(z_i-z_i^i)-(2/3)eta_i]_+,
so universal retention yields sum_i epsilon_i<=(20/21)Delta_-.

If beta<=hmax, (2) bounds every down gain solely by epsilon_i. The raw outer
sum is at most C beta, giving
 sum E_i<=C beta+Delta_++(20/21)Delta_-
        =C^2f-Cc p-c Delta_+-(C-20/21)Delta_-<=C^2f.       (3)
This also proves the dominant-hardware case directly.

Henceforth beta>=hmax. For t>=hmax all hardware is uncapped when t<=1;
combined with the no-opposing-cap formula when t>=1, write
 W_i(t,b)=a_i+k(t)(b-a_i),
 k(t)=1-exp(1-1/t)/2 for0<t<=1,
 k(t)=exp(1-t)/2 for t>=1, and k(0)=1.
The function k is continuous, decreasing, and globally Lipschitz with
                         0<=-k'(t)<=4c.                (4)
The maximum derivative magnitude on(0,1] occurs at t=1/2; on[1,infinity)
it is at most1/2<4c.

For t>=hmax, except on the lower clipping cell, linearity in b gives the
retention-path gain at most
 [k(t)-k(beta)](b0-a_i)-k(t)(beta-t)/3.
Upper clipping only lowers this expression. On the lower clipping cell,
W_i(t,0)-W_i(beta,b0)
 =-a_i[k(t)-k(beta)]-k(beta)b0<=0.
For t<hmax, (2) bounds the gain by that at hmax, already covered. Thus,
with x_i=(b_i-a_i)_+,
 down gain_i <= g_beta(x_i)+epsilon_i,
 g_beta(x)=max_{0<=u<=beta}
       {[k(u)-k(beta)]x-k(u)(beta-u)/3}.
This maximum is nonnegative because u=beta is allowed. It is convex in x,
increasing, and g_beta(0)=0. Convexity gives
 sum_i g_beta(x_i)<=g_beta(sum_i x_i)<=g_beta(p+L).
This argument preserves actual owners and permits independently chosen
underreports; it is not a virtual-player incentive argument.

The full raw sum, when beta>=hmax, is exactly
                   raw(beta)=1+k(beta)(beta-1).
Using (20/21)Delta_-<=Delta_- and the total-UP charge gives
 sum E_i <= raw(beta)+g_beta(p+L)+Delta.                 (5)
Everything is uniform over separate actual underreports, so taking the
separate suprema is legitimate. A finite-owner epsilon approximation to
those suprema suffices; no simultaneous attaining deviation is claimed.

If beta<=1/2, the full uncapped hardware ratio is1/beta>=2, so the previously
proved outer-dominance theorem already gives C^2 f. It remains to prove (5)
is at most C^2 f for 1/2<=beta<=C.

3. A HARDWARE-CONSTRAINED LOCAL ROLE TANGENT
Set
                         a=(1-3c)/C.
For arbitrary local branches and actual mixed ownership,
                         L<=cH+aZ.                     (6)
This is sharp for local-power-short branches with ratio rho=2 and hardware
exactly twice their local output. In our normalization H=1.

Proof branchwise. Write T for branch output and q=exp(1-rho)/2. If local
power is short, L/T=1-q, Z/T=1+q(rho-1), H/T>=rho. The difference between
L/T and the proposed bound with H/T=rho is
 J(rho)=1-q-c rho-a[1+q(rho-1)].
It has J(2)=J'(2)=0 and
 J''(rho)=-q[1+a(rho-3)]<0 for rho>=1,
because0<a<1/2. Thus J<=0. If hardware is short, H=T,
L/T=q rho, and the difference is
 q[(1-a)rho+a]-(c+a).
Its derivative is negative on rho>=1. Its maximum is1/2-c-a<0, because
c+a=(1-c)^2/C>1/2. Zero-output branches contribute L=Z=0 and nonnegative
hardware. Summing proves (6). This is an accounting inequality, not a
claim of a new cooperative-game solution concept.

4. TWO SCALAR UPPER BOUNDS
Fix u in[0,beta] and put A=k(u)-k(beta)>=0 and t=beta-u. The excess of the
right side of(5) over C^2 f for this u is at most
 G=raw-beta-k(u)t/3+(1+A-C^2)p-Cc m+A L.                (7)

Using (6), m>=Z/C, Z=beta-p, and1-a=4c/C yields
 G<=S(beta,u)+c(4A/C-C)p,
 S(beta,u)=raw-C beta-k(u)t/3+A(c+a beta).               (8)
If A<=C^2/4 the p coefficient is nonpositive, so G<=S.
If A>C^2/4, use p<=beta to get
 G<=J(beta,u)
   =1-C^2 beta-C k(beta)+k(u)[c+(2beta+u)/3].            (9)

A second bound handles the small-beta, small-A corner. For any d>0 the
exact local-output support inequality is
 A L+dZ <=m[A+d+(d/2)exp(-1-A/d)].                      (10)
For 0<=A<=1/25 choose d=c-5A/7. This d is positive and the bracket in(10)
is at most Cc. Indeed exp(-x)<=1-x+x^2/2 implies it is at most
 Cc-A(12c-2)/7+c A^2/(2d).
Using2/11<c<3/16 gives d>=59/385 and
 (12c-2)/7>=2/77 > (1155/1888)/25 >= cA/(2d).
Consequently (7) and(10) imply
 G<=raw-C beta+(5/7)beta A-k(u)t/3,                    (11)
because the remaining p coefficient is1+d+A-C^2=-Cc+2A/7<0.

5. ANALYTICAL CERTIFICATE FOR THE REMAINING SCALARS
The finite rational checks used below are in
 verify_power_bottleneck_scalar.py and its .json output.
They use exact Fraction arithmetic and signed Taylor bounds for exp(-x),
not floating optimizers or report-grid enumeration. There are26 checks;
all pass with python3 -O. We give the interval reductions explicitly so
these checks certify full real ranges.

Useful constants:2/11<c<3/16,1/3<a<1/2,C<6/5. Let
 r(beta)=3c+(3a-1)beta.
For beta in[1/2,6/5], one has1/2<r(beta)<1. On0<u<=1,
 S_u=[k(u)-d(u)(u+r(beta))]/3,
 S_uu=-q(u)[r(beta)+(1-2r(beta))u]/(3u^4)<0,             (12)
where q(u)=exp(1-1/u)/2 and d(u)=-k'(u)=q(u)/u^2.
For u>=1,
 S_u=k(u)[(1-u)/3-c-(a-1/3)beta]<0.
Also, with v=1-a and w=a-1/3>0,
 S_beta=-C+v k(beta)+d(beta)[C-v beta]+w k(u).           (13)
Replacing k(u) by a constant upper bound makes the right side strictly
decreasing in beta>=1/2: below1 its derivative is
 -2v d+d'[C-v beta]<0; above1 it is
 -k(beta)[C+v(2-beta)]<0 throughout beta<=6/5.

CASE I: beta<=51/100 and A<=1/25.
By(4), A<=4c t. As k(u)>=k(beta)>=k(51/100), (11) gives
 G<=raw-C beta+t[(20c/7)(51/100)-k(51/100)/3]
   <=raw-C beta<=0.
The bracket is strictly negative by the exact certificate. The last bound
is the exponential primitive's established factor C at this uncapped profile.

CASE II: beta in[1/2,51/100] and A>1/25.
Here A<=1-k(51/100)<C^2/4, so(8) applies without a grid term.
The certified inequality k(51/100)+1/25>k(23/50) forces u<23/50.
Equation(12) and monotonicity in beta show
 S_u(beta,u)>=S_u(51/100,23/50)>0,
so S(beta,u)<=S(beta,23/50). Using(13) with k(u)<=1,
 S_beta<=1/80 throughout this beta interval. The certificate gives
 S(1/2,23/50)<-3/10000. Therefore
 S(beta,u)<-3/10000+(1/80)(1/100)<0.

CASE III: beta>=51/100 and A<=C^2/4.
Equation(12), monotonicity in beta, and the exact check
 S_u(6/5,2/5)>0 show that the u maximum is attained in[2/5,1].
For that interval k(u)<=k(2/5). We use S only as its displayed algebraic
function when enlarging this rectangle permits u>beta; such points are not
claimed to be actual retention comparisons. Formula(13) remains valid there.
The bound(13) with this value is decreasing in beta and is already strictly
negative at beta=51/100. Hence
 S(beta,u)<=max_{v in[2/5,1]} S(51/100,v).
The exact derivative signs at49/100 and1/2, together with(12), place the
last maximum in[49/100,1/2]. There q(u)>1/6 and
r+(1-2r)u>=1/2, so (12) yields S_uu<=-4/9.
The exact certificate also gives
 S(51/100,1/2)<-1/50000,
 -3/1000<S_u(51/100,1/2)<0.
The quadratic upper tangent therefore bounds the entire maximum by
 -1/50000+(9/8)(3/1000)^2=-79/8000000<0.

CASE IV: A>C^2/4.
The certified inequalities
 1-k(7/10)<C^2/4,
 k(3/5)-k(6/5)<C^2/4
force beta>7/10 and u<3/5. We may enlarge beta to[7/10,6/5].
For J from(9), its u derivative has the same formula as(12) with
r replaced by r_g=3c+2beta. On this rectangle1/2<r_g<3, so
r_g+(1-2r_g)u>0 for0<u<=3/5 and J is concave in u.
The certified J_u(7/10,7/20)<0, and this derivative decreases in beta.
Thus its u maximum is achieved at u<=7/20. There k(u)<=1, giving
 J(beta,u)<=U(beta)
 =1+c+7/60-(C^2-2/3)beta-C k(beta).
The function U is concave on beta>=7/10, including the tie at1, because
d(beta)=-k'(beta) is decreasing there. Exact checks give
 U(3/4)<-1/100 and0<U'(3/4)<1/50.
Its global tangent consequently satisfies
 U(beta)<-1/100+(1/50)(6/5-3/4)=-1/1000<0.

The four cases exhaust1/2<=beta<=C and0<=u<=beta. Thus every value defining
g_beta in(5) is funded by C^2 f. Together with(3), the established outer-
dominance case beta<=1/2, and the zero boundary, this proves the theorem.

SCOPE AND CURRENT STATUS
The candidate is a genuine extension from the previously separate outer-
dominance/dominant-owner regimes to ALL physical power bottlenecks. It does
not assert C^2 funding on the remaining physical hardware bottleneck. The
proof relies on previously audited universal retention/increase lemmas;
the new algebra and interval reduction need independent review before any
manuscript claim. Existing single-profile and finite-grid searches are not
used as proof. The file explore_role_support.py is explicitly diagnostic
and not a verification dependency.
