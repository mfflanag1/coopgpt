FULL UTILIZATION WITH GRID HEADROOM: AN OWNER-INDEPENDENT ENVELOPE BOUND
16 October2026. Candidate theorem; independent review pending.
Original exponential primitive, fixed actual mixed owners, real binding reports.
No novelty claim for the weighted-coalition or chord ingredients.

1. STRUCTURAL CLASS AND STATEMENT
Use the depth-two production model and actual own-report envelope E. Suppose
full local production equals hardware: M=H>0. Normalize H=M=1. Let z_i be full
local payment, Z=sum_i z_i, u_i=min(p_i,1), and choose fixed ceilings U_i such
that EVERY local counterprofile produced by ONE actual owner's underreport obeys
z'_i<=U_i for every recipient i. A fully explicit choice is
                   U_i=sum_j min(T_j,max(h_ij,l_ij)).
It works for all recipients, including the deviator, because each primitive
payment is bounded by both its branch output and its own larger resource account.
The trivial ceiling U_i=M also works, but gives a narrower class.

Assume the headroom condition
                     Gamma=sum_i (U_i+u_i-1)_+ =0.          (H1)
Then
                 sum_i E_i <=(14017/10000)H < C^2 H,        (H2)
where C=1+1/(2e). On the subregion S/H<=3/2 the stronger7H/5 bound holds.
The full opposing capped total S may grow without limit. Neither the number of
actual owners nor the number of branches is bounded. Ownership may be mixed:
an owner may hold hardware, local power, and grid at once.

Condition(H1) says that each grid/local owner has enough room below the outer
cap for its certified maximum counterprofile local payment. Pure-grid accounts
may exceed the cap arbitrarily: then u_i=1 and(H1) requires U_i=0, so that cap is
fixed. This is a sufficient structural condition, not a necessary one. The
actual envelope is truthful on the ENTIRE original real-report domain; only the
funding guarantee here is conditioned on the full profile satisfying M=H and(H1).
Deviations need not remain within this full-profile class.

2. EXACT CAP LINEARITY FROM THE WEIGHTED COALITION FLOOR
At every counterprofile after restoring the deviator's full OUTER grid account,
                       min(p_k+z'_k,1)=u_k+z'_k.            (H3)
If U_k>0 then p_k=u_k<1 and p_k+z'_k<=u_k+U_k<=1. If U_k=0 then z'_k=0,
and(H3) holds even for p_k>=1. This proves(H3) at every cap endpoint as well.

Thus the threshold-coalition floor of GRID_WEIGHTED_COALITION_FLOOR.txt has all
local weights equal1, and gives opponents' capped mass at least pi_{-i}+M_{-i},
where pi=sum_k u_k and M_{-i} is opponents' unchanged local coalition production.
There is no truncation at1. At full reports d_i=u_i+z_i, S=pi+Z. Put
 C_i=Z-z_i-M_{-i}>=0.
The complement-surplus proof in ONE_POSITIVE_HARDWARE_SEVEN_FIFTHS.txt gives
 C_i<=1/2. In addition the half-marginal bound gives M_{-i}>=1-2z_i, hence
                       C_i<=min(1/2,z_i+epsilon),          (H4)
where epsilon=Z-1 in[0,c], c=1/(2e).

For completeness, the half-marginal bound on a branch is phi_i>=(T-T_{-i})/2.
Normalize T=1 with short pool hardware. Put m=1-T_{-i}=max(h_i,1-L_{-i}).
If m=h_i, the short-role payment alone is at least m/2. Otherwise let
x=min(l_i,1)>=m. Other capped long mass is at most L_{-i}=1-m, so
q*x>=.5*x*exp(m-x)>=m/2, since x exp(-x) increases on[0,1]. Symmetry covers the
other orientation, and zero outputs are immediate. Summing proves the claim.

3. FULL-UTILIZATION ACCOUNTING
Let a_i=H_i and d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1). Let positive gaps be g_i=d_i-a_i>0,
negative total be v=sum_i(a_i-d_i)_+, and r=S-1. Then
 G=sum_positive g_i=r+v,      0<=v<=b:=1/2-epsilon.          (H5)
Every branch is hardware-short or tied. Its hardware-role payments are at least
half each owner's hardware and at most that hardware, while total long-role
payment is at most T_j/2. Therefore total hardware-role payment is at least
Z-1/2=1/2+epsilon, proving the v bound. A negative-gap owner is uncapped and
has a_i=p_i+z_i+(a_i-d_i). Since z_i>=a_i/2, it has z_i>=p_i+(a_i-d_i).
Consequently
                         Z_negative>=v.                  (H6)

The negative-gap savings theorem gives savings at least
 q0*(4/5)*exp(epsilon-c)*v,
where q0=.5exp(-r), because delta=C-Z=c-epsilon and its uniform savings
coefficient exceeds4/5. This bound permits arbitrary whole-owner reports and
different selected deviations for different recipients.

4. HIGH TOTAL: S>=3/2
Restore the positive recipient's own opposing account to d_i after its actual
local counterprofile. Opponents have capped mass at least pi_{-i}+M_{-i}, so
the total is at least S-C_i>=1. Thus the positive payment is at most
                         a_i+q0 exp(C_i)g_i.               (H7)
The load-bearing untruncated floor is justified by(H3); without it(H7) is false
in general, as the actual cap-truncation obstruction demonstrates.

Let A0=exp(1/2), B=(4/5)exp(epsilon-c), and
 A=(exp(1/2)-exp(epsilon))/b, with b=1/2-epsilon>0.
By(H4) and the exponential chord on[epsilon,1/2],
 exp(C_i)<=exp(epsilon)+A min(z_i,b).
Every g_i<=1, so(H5)--(H6) imply
 sum_positive g_i min(z_i,b)<=min{1+epsilon-v,b(r+v)}.
Therefore
 sum_i E_i <=1+.5exp(-r)*[
  exp(epsilon)(r+v)+A min{1+epsilon-v,b(r+v)}-Bv].           (H8)
This is valid for all r>=1/2, 0<=v<=b, and0<=epsilon<=c.

5. REDUCE ALL r AND v TO A ONE-DIMENSIONAL CERTIFICATE
For fixed epsilon,r, the bracket in(H8) is the minimum of two affine functions
of v. The first is
 L1(v)=A0*r+(A0-B)v,
which increases. The second decreases because its slope is exp(epsilon)-A-B<0;
the secant slope A is at least exp(epsilon). Their intersection is
                 v*=(1+epsilon-br)/(1+b).
Even if v* lies outside[0,b], the minimum of the two lines is at most their
intersection height, and it is also at most L1(b). Thus the bracket is bounded by
 min{A0*r+(A0-B)b, alpha*r+beta},
 alpha=(A0+B*b)/(1+b), beta=(A0-B)(1+epsilon)/(1+b).         (H9)
All slopes and intercepts in(H9) are positive. Each exp(-r) affine term decreases
for r>=1. Therefore their minimum decreases there too, and ALL r>=1/2 reduce to
r in[1/2,1]. This removes any owner-count or upper-S restriction.

The exact checker certify_headroom_high_total.py partitions only epsilon.
For an interval[ell,h], define b_lo=1/2-h, b_hi=1/2-ell and rational bounds
A0<=Au, B_lo<=B<=B_hi. Since alpha decreases with b when A0>B,
 L1(b)<=Au*r+b_hi(Au-B_lo),
 alpha*r+beta <=[(Au+B_hi*b_lo)/(1+b_lo)]r
                    +(Au-B_lo)(1+h)/(1+b_lo).
These are simultaneous upper bounds for every epsilon in the interval. The
minimum of the two upper lines is maximized after multiplication by exp(-r)
by splitting at their exact rational crossing. Each line's stationary point
is r=1-intercept/slope, clipped to its interval. Positive Taylor sums with a
geometric tail, or even-degree Taylor bounds at negative arguments, provide
exact upper bounds on the exponential evaluations.

6. LOW TOTAL: 1<=S<=3/2
For T=S-1/2 in[1/2,1], complement surplus alone gives every positive recipient
E_i-a_i<=g_i k(T), where k is the function in the one-positive theorem. Here
even the ordinary min(1,...) coalition floor would suffice, since T<=1.
The negative savings coefficient is at least(2/5)exp(1-S-c). Using v<=1/2,
 sum E_i<=1+T k(T)-(1/5)exp(1/2-T-c).
This increases on[1/2,1], since T k(T) is nondecreasing and the negative term
increases. At1 it equals3/2-(1/5)exp(-1/2-c)<7/5. The constant comparison is
verified in verify_one_positive_analytic_constants.py. Together with the high
case this proves(H2).

7. INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS
This is a demonstrated funding consequence of the exact threshold-coalition
floor, extending the separated-grid regime to arbitrary mixed ownership with
certified local-payment headroom. It includes unboundedly many positive gaps
and arbitrarily large capped opposing totals. It excludes variable cap overlap
measured by Gamma>0; that remaining case is not resolved here. The condition
uses resource-based ceilings if the displayed explicit U_i is chosen, so it
can be checked without evaluating an own-report envelope.

All original owner identities and all-profile incentives are retained. No new
mechanism, virtual-owner core constraint, empirical performance claim, or
historical novelty conclusion is asserted by this conditional funding theorem.
