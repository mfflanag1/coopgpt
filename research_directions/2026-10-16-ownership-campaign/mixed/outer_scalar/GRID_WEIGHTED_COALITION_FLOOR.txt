GRID-WEIGHTED ACTUAL-COALITION FLOORS FOR OUTER ACCOUNTS
16 October2026. Computable structural bound; no complete new funding constant.
The weighted sum of coalition inequalities is an established elementary step.

Normalize full hardware H=1. Fix an actual deviating owner i. All other owners'
local resources and grid reports are fixed. Let m(A)=sum_j min(H_j(A),L_j(A))
for coalitions A excluding i, and write u_k=min(p_k,1).
Suppose U_k>=0 is a report-independent upper bound on k's local payment z'_k
under EVERY underreport by i. The simple choice U_k=M always works. Define
 w_k=min{1,(1-u_k)/U_k} if U_k>0, and w_k=1 if U_k=0.
Then at every counterprofile,
             min(p_k+z'_k,1)>=u_k+w_k z'_k.                 (1)
For p_k>=1 this is equality with w_k=0 unless U_k=0 (then z'_k=0).
For p_k<1, use min(z'_k,1-p_k)>=(min{1,(1-p_k)/U_k})z'_k,
valid for0<=z'_k<=U_k.

Local all-profile coalition coverage and a finite layer-cake identity give
 sum_{k!=i} w_k z'_k
   =integral_0^1 sum_{k!=i:w_k>=t} z'_k dt
   >=integral_0^1 m({k!=i:w_k>=t})dt.
Consequently the opponents' capped outer total is uniformly at least
 R_i^weighted=sum_{k!=i}u_k
       +integral_0^1 m({k!=i:w_k>=t})dt.                  (2)
All sets are coalitions of the original actual owners. The threshold weights
are fixed while i underreports. No actual deviation is required to attain the
integral lower bound, and no virtual-player coverage inequality is introduced.

Unlike the single grand-opponents floor min{1,P_{-i}+M_{-i}}, expression(2) may
exceed1 and retains arbitrarily large fixed grid mass. It need not dominate that
other floor pointwise. Take the maximum of independently valid floors, including
(2), the grand-opponents floor, and fixed partition/fractional-packing floors.
For a full positive recipient gap d_i-a_i>0, every such floor R yields
                  E_i<=a_i+k(d_i+R)(d_i-a_i),
by the restoration argument in FIXED_OPPONENT_COALITION_FLOOR.txt. It can also
be retained inside the own-loss-dependent scalar bound through
 t_i(x)>=d_i-x+R. A general funding theorem still requires controlling the sum
of these individually valid bounds; no such conclusion is assumed here.

A MORE LOCAL EXPLICIT UPPER CEILING
For each opponent k, the choice
 U_k=sum_j min{T_j,max(h_kj,l_kj)}
also works, and is no larger than M. On any branch, its payment is a convex
combination of its short account and its capped long account. Both are bounded
by max(h_kj,l_kj) and by the COUNTERPROFILE branch output T'_j<=T_j. Summing gives
the stated upper ceiling. The opponent's h_kj,l_kj are unchanged when i deviates.
Any independently proved tighter ceiling may replace it.

FINITE EVALUATION
Sort the opponents' weights as0<=w_(1)<=...<=w_(n-1)<=1, put w_(0)=0, and let
A_l={k!=i:w_k>=w_(l)}. Then the integral equals
                   sum_l [w_(l)-w_(l-1)]m(A_l).
Ties give zero increments and cause no ambiguity. Suffix sums of resource
reports compute all these coalition values in O(n*m) arithmetic operations
after O(n log n) sorting, where m is the number of local branches. For rational
reports the weights, coalition values, and floor are rational. This note makes
no complexity claim for computing the actual envelope E itself.

EXACT STRUCTURAL SPECIALIZATION
If every opponent is either a pure grid owner (no local resources) or has p_k=0,
then w_k=1 for all local-resource owners, while grid-only owners contribute
u_k and no coalition output. Thus(2) becomes
                       R_i^weighted=pi_{-i}+M_{-i}.
There is no artificial cap at1. If M=0, every local counterfactual payment and
coalition value is0, all U_k=0, and(2) reduces exactly to pi_{-i}. Thus no
division by M or by a zero ceiling is required at this endpoint. This supplies a continuous ownership-aware
interpolation between separated grid ownership and mixed grid/local ownership.
It is a consequence of existing coverage and explicit payment ceilings, not a
claim that the underlying weighted-core inequality is novel.
