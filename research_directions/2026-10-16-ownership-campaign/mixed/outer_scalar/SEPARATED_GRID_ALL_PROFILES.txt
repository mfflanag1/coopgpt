C^2 FUNDING WITH SEPARATE GRID OWNERS AT ALL PHYSICAL PROFILES
16 October 2026. Candidate extension; independent audit required.
Original exponential primitive and its actual own-report envelope only.

MODEL AND STATEMENT
Use the fixed-owner continuous report model and N,E defined in
ALL_POWER_BOTTLENECK_C_SQUARED.txt. Thus hardware reports h_ij, local-resource
reports l_ij, and grid reports p_i are nonnegative; deviations are binding
coordinatewise whole-owner underreports. Put H_i=sum_j h_ij, H=sum_i H_i,
P=sum_i p_i, T_j=min(sum_i h_ij,sum_i l_ij), M=sum_j T_j,
F=min(H,P+M), c=1/(2e), C=1+c. The local exponential primitive gives z_i,
Z=sum_i z_i. The nested raw rule is N_i=Phi_i(Hvector,pvector+zvector), and
E_i is its supremum over owner i's actual own underreports.

Assume only the profilewise ownership condition
       p_i>0 implies h_ij=l_ij=0 for every j.                 (SG)
Other owners may own arbitrary mixed hardware/local bundles. Owner and branch
counts are unrestricted, and the underlying full-domain E is unchanged.
Then, conditional on the power-bottleneck theorem cited above,
                        sum_i E_i<=C^2 F.                    (1)
For the physical hardware bottleneck F=H<=P+M, the stronger strict estimate is
                  sum_i E_i < (C+c+1/32)H < C^2 H             (2)
when H>0. When P>=H, the earlier separated-grid result gives the stronger
constant 3/4+1/e+sqrt(e)/6 instead. This note extends to the previously missing
P<H<=P+M region; it does not improve that earlier bound where it applies.
No optimal-mechanism lower bound or priority claim is made.

1. NO VARIABLE OPPOSING CAPS, INCLUDING AN OUTER POOL SWITCH
It suffices to prove (2), since (1) on F=P+M<=H is precisely the cited power
bottleneck theorem, without requiring(SG). Normalize H=1. If H=0, E=F=0.
Let a_i=H_i, m=M<=1, pi=sum_i min(p_i,1), and beta=pi+Z.
If P<1, pi=P and beta>=P+M>=1. If P>=1, pi>=1, so again beta>=1.

Grid recipients have no hardware/local reports, hence no local payments at
any counterfactual. Their only own report is grid supply; primitive own
monotonicity gives E_i=N_i. For each nongrid recipient choose any actual own
underreport, independently across recipients, and restore only its OUTER
hardware coordinate after computing the counterfactual local accounts. This
increases its outer payment by primitive own monotonicity. Its own grid is0.
Every local account is at most M^i<=M<=1, so no local recipient caps at the
full hardware total. Grid accounts are fixed under these comparisons.

If P>=1, every outer comparison remains hardware-short and its capped
opposing total is exactly pi+Z^i>=1. If P<1, all grid accounts are below1,
and the same equality t=pi+Z^i=P+Z^i holds even when the outer bottleneck
switches. Thus the scalar W_i(t,b) and all cap-preserving reductions in
sections1--2 of ALL_POWER_BOTTLENECK_C_SQUARED.txt apply. Here b is the
nongrid owner's own local account. In the P>=1 case there is no claim that
t<pi is an actual comparison; allowing such t only enlarges an upper bound.

Let Delta_-,Delta_+ be the local slack sums on full branches with capped
ratio rho<=2 and rho>2, respectively; Delta=Delta_-+Delta_+=Cm-Z<=cm<=c.
The universal retention and positive-total response lemmas allow different
actual underreports for each recipient. The scalar reduction therefore gives
 total-UP gains <=Delta_+,
 total-DOWN gains <=sum_i g_beta((z_i-a_i)_+)+(20/21)Delta_-,  (3)
where the sums in(3) are over nongrid owners only, and
 g_beta(x)=max_{0<=u<=beta}{[k(u)-k(beta)]x-k(u)(beta-u)/3},
 k(u)=1-exp(1-1/u)/2 for0<u<=1,
 k(u)=exp(1-u)/2 foru>=1, and k(0)=1.
The function g_beta is nonnegative, convex, increasing, and zero at x=0.
The cap-cell comparison remains needed for u<max_i a_i; its proof is in the
cited scalar reduction, rather than an assumption that all hardware stays
uncapped along the whole comparison.

Each local payment splits into hardware-role u_i and local-power-role v_i,
with u_i<=a_i and sum_i v_i<=1/2. The latter bound is branchwise: if hardware
is short, the total local-power role is q rho T<=H_j/2; if power is short,
it is(1-q)T<=rho T/2<=H_j/2. Therefore
                 sum_nongrid (z_i-a_i)_+<=1/2.
Convex concentration yields sum_i g_beta((z_i-a_i)_+)<=g_beta(1/2).
The full raw outer total is at most C. Hence, uniformly over the separately
selected actual underreports,
                  sum_i E_i<=C+c+g_beta(1/2).                (4)
The following scalar estimate completes the proof.

2. UNIFORM SCALAR ESTIMATE
For every beta>=1,
                         g_beta(1/2)<1/32.                   (5)
Fix u in[0,beta]. Its expression is
 f(u,beta)=k(u)[1/2-(beta-u)/3]-k(beta)/2.
A negative square bracket already gives f<=0. Below assume it nonnegative.

If u>=1, put v=beta-u. Then
 f=k(beta)[exp(v)(1/2-v/3)-1/2]
 <=(1/2)[sqrt(e)/3-1/2] <1/32.
The bracket is maximized at v=1/2. The last inequality follows from
sqrt(e)<27/16, since e<11/4<729/256.

If0<=u<=1/2, put w=beta-1>=0. Then
 f=k(u)[(1+2u)/6-w/3]-exp(-w)/4.
Its w derivative is at most-(1-c)/3+1/4<0, so maximize at beta=1.
On[0,1/2], h(u)=k(u)(1+2u) is concave and increasing: k''<=0,k'<0 imply
h''<=0, and h'(1/2)=2-10c>0. The zero endpoint is interpreted by continuity.
Thus f<=(1-c)/3-1/4=1/12-c/3<1/44<1/32, using c>2/11.

It remains that1/2<=u<=1. Since k is convex here, it lies below its chord
k(u)<=A-Bu, with B=1-2c and A=B+1/2. Hence
 f<=Q(u,w)=(A-Bu)(u+1/2-w)/3-exp(-w)/4.                (6)
For fixed w, this is a concave quadratic in u. Its vertex is
 u*=1/4+1/(4B)+w/2.
Since1/2<B<2/3, the vertex starts inside[1/2,1] and reaches1 at
 w*=3/2-1/(2B), where0<w*<3/4. If w>=w*, Q is maximized on[1/2,1] at u=1,
already covered by the u>=1 case. If0<=w<=w*, the vertex value is
 V(w)=(T-Bw)^2/(12B)-exp(-w)/4, with T=2-3c.
The exact rational interval certificate verify_separated_grid_scalar.py gives
 V(0)<27/1000, 0<V'(0)<9/1000, and exp(-3/4)>15/32.
Since B<7/11, for0<=w<=3/4,
 V''(w)=B/6-exp(-w)/4 <7/66-15/128<-1/100.
Consequently the global quadratic upper tangent yields
 V(w)<27/1000+(9/1000)^2/(2/100)=621/20000<1/32.
This proves(5) on all u,beta, including their boundaries. Combining(4)--(5)
proves the first inequality in(2). The second follows from c^2>1/32:
e<11/4 implies e^2<121/16<8, hence1/(4e^2)>1/32.

3. CONSEQUENCE AND SCOPE
The original E is globally own-report monotone by the expanding own-report
box and coalition-covering because E>=N. The new budget result does not
change E or require counterfactuals to remain in the hardware-bottleneck
region. Under(SG), actual underreports do preserve the separation needed by
this proof. Thus, if(SG) is imposed by fixed eligibility, E is a uniform C^2
truthful covering mechanism on that full domain; if not, (1) is still a
profilewise funding guarantee for the globally defined E.
The previously established P=0 saturation examples satisfy(SG) vacuously,
so the C^2 constant for this particular envelope is sharp over the whole
separated-grid class. This is not a lower bound against other mechanisms.
The remaining open funding case is mixed grid/local or grid/hardware owners
at physical hardware bottlenecks. No algorithmic exact-evaluation claim for
the actual envelope is added here.
