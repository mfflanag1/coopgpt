OWN-LOSS-RETAINING SCALAR REDUCTION FOR POSITIVE OUTER GAPS
16 October2026. Candidate structural consequence; no global funding claim.
Uses the independently audited sharp nonlinear outgoing theorem with K=e-1.

Normalize full hardware H=1 and suppose the physical hardware bottleneck holds.
Use full local accounts z_i, a_i=H_i, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), S=sum_i d_i>=1,
w_i=(p_i+z_i-1)_+, and full positive gap g_i=d_i-a_i>0. Define
 J_i=min{K z_i, Z-z_i, U_{-i}+(2/e+1/2)a_i},
where U_{-i} is the full local hardware-role payment to OTHER owners. It may
be replaced by any smaller proved outgoing allowance, including branchwise
localized charges. For x>=0 let
 kappa_i(x)=min{J_i,K sqrt[z_i(x+w_i)]},
 t_i(x)=max{d_i-x,S-x-kappa_i(x)}.
Then the actual whole-owner envelope satisfies
 E_i <=a_i+max_{0<=x<=min(g_i,d_i-min(p_i,1))}
                k(t_i(x))*(g_i-x),                    (1)
where k(t)=1-.5exp(1-1/t) for0<t<=1, k(t)=.5exp(1-t) for t>=1,
and k(0)=1. The sharper short/long-role nonlinear coefficient may replace
K^2 z_i under the square root. A zero gap endpoint contributes0.

PROOF
Restore only the deviator's outer hardware and grid after computing its actual
local counterfactual. This raises its payment. Write D=z_i-z_i^i>=0 for its
actual local own loss and x=d_i-d_i^i>=0 for its capped outer own loss. Exact
clipping gives D<=x+w_i and
0<=x<=d_i-min(p_i,1). Sharp nonlinear outgoing loss and the other independently
proved bounds therefore imply that OTHER capped accounts lose at most
kappa_i(x). Their remaining total is at least S-d_i-kappa_i(x), and the own
capped account is d_i-x. If x>=g_i its payment is at most a_i, so only the
stated interval matters. Otherwise its own account exceeds a_i and the outer
payment decreases with the opposing capped total. That total is at least
t_i(x). Below1, hardware caps only increase q above .5exp(1-1/t), hence only
reduce the positive-gap payment relative to a_i+k(t)(g_i-x). Above1 the same
formula is exact. This proves(1) uniformly over actual own reports.

All quantities are taken at fixed actual owners and the fixed full hardware
threshold. The scalar max is a relaxation of actual reports, not a purported
simultaneous attainable deviation. It handles own cap crossing, caps on other
accounts, and switching the outer bottleneck. In particular, for a full
uncapped owner w_i=0, x equals its actual local own loss; the relaxation no
longer permits positive outgoing harm while holding that loss at zero.

2. QUANTITATIVE SAVINGS FROM FULL NEGATIVE GAPS
Let pi=sum_i min(p_i,1), M=sum_j T_j, C=1+1/(2e), and
 q_floor=.5exp(1-pi-CM).
Every restored counterfactual opposing capped total is at most pi+CM. Thus a
full nonpositive-gap owner d_i<=a_i satisfies
                       E_i<=a_i-q_floor(a_i-d_i).       (2)
If the counterfactual remains hardware-short, its coefficient is at least
q_floor and its own capped account is no larger than d_i. If it becomes
power-short, its payment is at most(a_i+d_i)/2, which is bounded by(2) because
q_floor<=1/2 in the physical hardware-bottleneck region: pi+CM>=S>=1.
When the full profile has no opposing overflow, S=P+Z and pi=P. Since Z>=M,
one may use the simpler smaller floor
                  q_floor_simple=.5exp(1-S-cM),
where c=1/(2e). This simple floor is NOT asserted for arbitrary full overflow.

Combining(1)--(2) bounds the complete envelope using only separately selected
one-dimensional own capped-loss variables, with no report grid or owner clone.
A uniform C^2 bound still requires a valid aggregate inequality for these terms.

3. EXPLICIT ONE-POSITIVE-GAP SCALAR GATE
Suppose there is exactly one positive-gap owner, and no owner has full overflow
(p_i+z_i<=1, with equality permitted). Put its a,z,d,D as above, and write S
for the full total and M for the local output. Every actual profile and its own
comparison satisfy
 0<=a<=d<=1, 0<=z<=min(d,M,a+1/2), 0<=M<=1,
 max(1,d+M-z)<=S<=1+d-a,
 0<=D<=min(z,d-a).
The first local-role restriction follows from own hardware role<=a and total
local-power role<=1/2. The lower S restriction follows from others' local
payment total Z-z>=M-z and their outer accounts having at least that total.
The upper S restriction follows because every other owner's d_k<=a_k.

Set eta=2/e+1/2,
 kappa=min{Kz,1-z+eta*a,K sqrt(zD)},
 t=max(d-D,S-D-kappa).
Summing(1)--(2) gives the rigorous scalar upper bound
 sum_i E_i <=1+k(t)(d-a-D)
              -.5exp(1-S-cM)*(1+d-a-S),               (3)
maximized over the displayed scalar domain. The directed charge used here is
U_{-i}+eta*a<=1-z+eta*a, by the individual hardware-role compatibility lemma.
This domain has no bound on the number of actual owners or local branches.

The diagnostic probe_one_positive_simple.py finds an approximate maximum1.379981
at a=0,d=1,M=1,z approximately.41111,S=2-z,D approximately.28571. These are
numerical optimization results only. Neither the maximum nor a C^2 theorem
for this entire scalar domain is certified by that search. Proving(3)<=C^2
by analytical reduction or a complete exact interval argument is a concrete
next gate. The more general many-positive-owner sum remains open separately.
