GENERAL HIGH-CAPPED-TOTAL FUNDING FOR THE ORIGINAL ACTUAL ENVELOPE
16 October 2026. Candidate proof, independent review requested.
All actual owners fixed; all real mixed reports; arbitrary grid overflow;
owner and branch counts unbounded. No novelty claim.

THEOREM
Use the original symmetric exponential primitive locally and at the outer pool,
and its ACTUAL own-report monotone envelope E. At a physical hardware bottleneck
H>0 normalize H=1 and put
 a_i=H_i, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), S=sum_i d_i, r=S-1,
 v=sum_i(a_i-d_i)_+, K=e-1.
Then the following bound holds for EVERY such full profile:

 sum_i E_i <= 1+(exp(-r)/2)[r+7/2-(1+exp(-K))v].         (HT1)

Consequently, if S>=3, then

 sum_i E_i <=1+11/(4e^2)<7/5<C^2.                       (HT2)

This removes the entire S>=3 tail from the unrestricted C^2 question for this
envelope. It does not settle the remaining region 1<=S<3. It neither assumes
hardware restoration nor imposes a restriction on underreports. The criterion
is a capped-account sum at the full profile, not raw grid supply or owner count.

1. FULL LOCAL POWER ROLES HAVE A GLOBAL RESOURCE BOUND
Let l_i denote owner i's FULL local POWER-role payments, summed over branches.
Hardware-role payments are nonnegative and at most its physical hardware, so
 z_i<=a_i+l_i. The usual individual local bound also gives z_i<=M<=1.
Moreover
                         sum_i l_i<=1/2.                (HT3)
For completeness, on a hardware-short branch of hardware total H_j, this sum
is q rho H_j<=H_j/2. On a power-short branch of production T and capped hardware
ratio rho>=1, it equals (1-q)T<=rho T/2<=H_j/2. The middle inequality is
2-exp(1-rho)<=rho, whose difference rho-2+exp(1-rho) is zero at rho=1 and has
nonnegative derivative thereafter. Branches of zero production contribute zero.
These are accounting roles inside the SAME actual owner's payment, not virtual
players whose incentive constraints are imposed separately.

2. AN EXACT TWO-RESOURCE EXPONENTIAL SUPPORT
For every 0<=a<=1 and 0<=l<=1/2,

 (1-a){exp[K min(1,a+l)]-1} <=2a+3l.                    (HT4)

Proof. Fix a and first consider 0<=l<=min(1/2,1-a). The difference between the
left side and 2a+3l is convex in l, so it suffices to check the interval endpoints.

At l=0, (1-a)(exp(Ka)-1)<=Ka<=2a. For 0<=a<1 the first inequality follows by
taking logarithms of exp(Ka)<=[1+(K-1)a]/(1-a): the derivative of the logarithmic
right side minus Ka is
 K{1/[(1+(K-1)a)(1-a)]-1}>=0,
since 1<K<2 and the denominator is at most one. Both sides agree at a=0;
the a=1 endpoint follows directly.

If a>=1/2, the other endpoint is l=1-a. It suffices that exp(K)<6, because
 (1-a)(exp(K)-1)<5(1-a)<=3-a=2a+3(1-a).
The second inequality is equivalent to a>=1/2. For l>1-a the capped left side
is constant and the right side increases, so this also handles the remaining
part of the original l interval.

If a<=1/2, the other endpoint is l=1/2. Put x=a+1/2 in [1/2,1]. The desired
inequality is equivalent to
              exp(Kx) <= (2+x)/(3/2-x).                 (HT5)
Use K<43/25 and define
 h(x)=log[(2+x)/(3/2-x)]-(43/25)x.
It is strictly convex because
 h''(x)=1/(3/2-x)^2-1/(2+x)^2>0.
Its tangent at x=3/4 has slope 56/33-43/25=-19/825<0. Thus throughout [1/2,1]

 h(x)>=h(3/4)+h'(3/4)(x-3/4)
      >=h(3/4)+h'(3/4)/4
       =log(11/3)-43/25+14/33
       =log(11/3)-1069/825>0.                            (HT6)

For an explicit finite rational bound, put x=1069/825. The Taylor terms through
degree18 and their geometric tail give
 exp(x)<=sum_{j=0}^{18} x^j/j! +[x^19/19!]/[1-x/20]
         <183/50<11/3.
The middle inequality is an exact rational comparison, replayed in the checker;
the tail ratio is bounded by x/20 after the first omitted term. This proves the
last strict inequality in (HT6). Also K<43/25 follows from e<68/25, and
exp(K)<exp(43/25)<6. These three elementary rational-exponential bounds are
replayed in the accompanying checker. This proves (HT5), all endpoints of the
convex l intervals, and hence (HT4) without a numerical optimization.

3. POSITIVE INFLATION IS CHARGED TO TWO ACTUAL RESOURCE TOTALS
For positive full gap g_i=d_i-a_i>0, we have g_i<=1-a_i. The accepted outgoing
comparison gives
 E_i<=a_i+q0 exp(K z_i)g_i, q0=exp(-r)/2.
By (HT3)-(HT4), its extra coefficient beyond q0 g_i satisfies
 g_i(exp(K z_i)-1)
 <=(1-a_i){exp[K min(1,a_i+l_i)]-1}<=2a_i+3l_i.
Let Pextra be the sum of these quantities over positive-gap owners. The hardware
held by those owners is at most 1-v, since the negative-gap owners' hardware is
at least their gap sum v. Consequently

                         Pextra<=7/2-2v.                (HT7)

This retains local-role information discarded by merely bounding every z_i by
one or the total local budget by C. Zero-gap owners may carry either resource;
omitting them can only weaken the upper bounds used here.

4. NEGATIVE SAVINGS AND SUMMATION
For a negative full gap g_i=a_i-d_i>0, the accepted sharp incoming comparison is
 E_i<=a_i-q0 exp[-K z_i(1+log(M/z_i))]g_i.
Since z_i<=M<=1, z_i[1+log(M/z_i)]<=M<=1, with its continuous zero convention.
Thus E_i<=a_i-q0 exp(-K)g_i. Zero full gaps have E_i<=a_i.
Using Gplus-Gminus=r and Gminus=v, sum the individual actual-envelope bounds:

 sum E_i<=1+q0[r+(1-exp(-K))v+Pextra]
         <=1+q0[r+7/2-(1+exp(-K))v].
This is (HT1). Every owner may choose a DIFFERENT whole-bundle underreport;
the compared roles, gaps and resource totals all belong to the same full profile.
The all-profile outgoing/incoming comparisons already handle own hardware loss,
pool switches, arbitrary opposing caps and full grid overflow.

For S>=3, r>=2. Drop the nonpositive v term. The derivative of
exp(-r)(r+7/2) is -exp(-r)(r+5/2)<0. Hence
 sum E_i<=1+(1/2)exp(-2)(2+7/2)=1+11/(4e^2).
Finally e>8/3 implies 11/(4e^2)<99/256<2/5, proving the first strict comparison
in (HT2). The accepted exact constant C^2>7/5 is also replayed below.

5. SCOPE AND REMAINING GATE
The proof uses the fixed actual owner's own local payment and accounting roles;
it does not clone owners or demand simultaneous attainment of their deviations.
It is a general structural consequence for an unbounded region of full profiles,
not a finite-family construction or an optimal-subsidy lower bound.
Combined with the earlier physical-power bound, this leaves the hardware case
with 1<=S<3 for the unrestricted C^2 target. Other independently accepted
criteria further remove diffuse and small directional-local-mass profiles.
