ENTROPY OF A HARDWARE SUBSET AND A SHARPER GENERAL SPILL CORRECTION
17 October2026. Candidate proof, independently derived with root.
Original symmetric exponential local primitive. Fixed actual owners, arbitrary
real mixed holdings, branch counts, reports, and nonnegative grid holdings.
No novelty claim. No new unrestricted funding constant is claimed here.

1. GENERAL TANGENT FAMILY
Let H be total hardware, M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), Z the total local payment,
c=1/(2e), C=1+c, and delta=CM-Z. For any fixed subset J of actual owners, let
a_J be its hardware and U_J its total physical hardware-role local payment.
For every t in[c,1/2], define

 s=1+log(2t),    A=s+c-t,    D=A+1/2.

Then 0<=s<=1, A>=s/2>=0, and

             delta+D(H-M) >=A a_J-s U_J.               (SE1)

In a full physical hardware-bottleneck profile normalize H=1, and let
J={i:d_i<a_i} be the STRICT negative outer-gap set, with
v=sum_J(a_i-d_i), Zminus=sum_J z_i. Zero-gap owners are excluded. Then

       delta+(A+1/2)(1-M) >=A v+(c-t)Zminus.            (SE2)

The spill coefficient A+1/2 improves the earlier valid coefficient A+1.
In particular t=1/2 gives the simple exact row

 delta+C(1-M)>=(1/2+c)v-(1/2-c)Zminus.                 (SE3)

These statements constrain full profiles; they impose no new strategic
condition on virtual accounts and do not alter the actual-report envelope.

2. BRANCH PROOF USING THE EXACT TOTAL CLIPPING LOSS
For a productive branch let T=min(h,l), rho=sum capped long reports/T,
q=exp(1-rho)/2. Its deficit is
 delta_j=T f(q),    f(q)=c+q log(2q)>=0.
The function f is convex on(0,1/2], with f'(t)=1+log(2t)=s. Hence
                 f(q)>=c-t+s q.                      (SE4)

The coefficient claim A>=s/2 follows from c-t+s/2>=0: its value at t=c is
zero and its derivative is -1+1/(2t)>=0 on[c,1/2]. In particular A-sq>=0.

If hardware is short, h=T and U_J=(1-q)a_J. Since a_J<=T and f(q)>=0,
 delta_j>=a_J f(q)>=A a_J-s U_J.

If power is short, h>=T. Crucially the total clipping loss of all hardware
accounts is EXACTLY h-rho T. Therefore
 sum_{i in J}min(h_i,T)>=a_J-(h-rho T),
 U_J>=q[a_J-(h-rho T)].
The latter remains valid when its right side is negative. Since A-sq>=0 and
a_J<=h, this gives
                A a_J-s U_J<=A h-s q rho T.           (SE5)

It remains to show delta_j+D(h-T)>=A h-s q rho T. Because D-A=1/2 and
h/T>=rho, the residual divided by T is bounded below by
 f(q)+(rho-1)/2-A+s q rho.
Put x=rho-1>=0, so q=exp(-x)/2. This expression is

 F(x)=t-s+x/2+q[s+(s-1)x].

At x=0, F(0)=t-s/2>=0, by log(2t)<=2t-1. Its derivative is
 F'(x)=1/2+q[-1+(1-s)x]>=1/2-q>=0.
Thus F(x)>=0, proving(SE1) branchwise. A zero-output branch has U_J=0,
delta_j=0 and satisfies D h>=A a_J. Summing yields(SE1).

For the full negative-gap set, d_i<a_i<=1 implies d_i=p_i+z_i, so
a_J>=Zminus+v, while U_J<=Zminus. Substitute these two inequalities into
(SE1) and use A-s=c-t to obtain(SE2). This covers arbitrary full overflow
among the other owners. No cap on an opposing coalition is replaced by an
uncapped total.

3. EXACT ENTROPY PERSPECTIVE WHEN M=H
If M=H, every productive branch has hardware short or tied. At H=1 let
n=v+Zminus. If n=0 the following bound is zero. Otherwise v<=Zminus, since
U_J>=a_J/2 and Zminus>=U_J while a_J>=v+Zminus. Thus r=v/n<=1/2.
Optimizing(SE2) over t gives

 delta>=0,                                      if r<=c,
 delta>=n[c+r log(2r)],                         if c<=r<=1/2. (SE6)

Indeed the tangent right side is n[c-t+r(1+log(2t))], whose derivative in t
is n(r/t-1). Its maximum is at t=c for r<=c and at t=r for r in[c,1/2].
Equivalently, this is the perspective of the convex function obtained by
setting f(q)=0 below its minimizer c. It is the exact optimization of the
proved tangent family, not merely a numerical curvature approximation.
At v=Zminus, it reduces to delta>=2c v. Consequently the former diagnostic
point M=1, v=Zminus approximately0.158, delta approximately0.0184 violates an
exact necessary condition: it would require delta at least approximately0.0581.
These decimals are explanatory; inequality(SE6) is exact.

4. SHARPNESS OF THE PERSPECTIVE CONSTRAINT
For any n in(0,1] and r in[c,1/2], an actual full profile attains
v=rn, Zminus=(1-r)n, delta=n f(r). On hardware mass n use hardware-short
branches with q=r, capped local ratio rho=1-log(2r), and give their hardware
to a pure-hardware negative-gap owner set J with no grid or local resource.
Each such owner receives (1-r) times its hardware, so its gap is r times it.
Split the local pool among finitely many pure-local owners with capped shares
at most one summing to rho. On the remaining hardware mass1-n use branches
with q=c and give each hardware owner grid c times its hardware, making its
outer gap zero. Pure-local owners have positive gaps and no hardware. All
reports are fixed actual-owner reports. Every individual opposing account
can be kept at most one; the total local payment is C-n f(r)>=1, so the
physical bottleneck is hardware. The full negative set is exactly J.
Splitting branches and the hardware within J allows arbitrarily many active
branches and actual owners without changing these totals. Thus(SE6) cannot
be strengthened using only the three numbers(v,Zminus,delta) on this face
for r in[c,1/2]. No sharpness assertion is made here for r<c.
This is sharpness of the structural constraint, not a lower bound against
truthful mechanisms or a funding extremizer.

5. CERTIFICATE USE
For any fixed t, (SE2) is linear in(M,delta,v,Zminus). Exact certificate rows
must enclose c and log(2t) with their correct signs, or use the endpoint t=1/2,
where the only transcendental constant is c. A floating LP using these rows
is a diagnostic only; it does not establish an improved global budget.
The stronger proposed spill D=c+s has not been proved in this note and must
not be substituted for D=A+1/2 in an accepted certificate.
