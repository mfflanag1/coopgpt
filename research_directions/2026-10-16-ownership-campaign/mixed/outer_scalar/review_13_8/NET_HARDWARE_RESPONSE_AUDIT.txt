1. BRANCH IDENTITY

Delta Z=B(v-1)-(V-R). Differentiating (1+d-u)e^u gives (d-u)e^u>=0,

After a switch, other original-long raw mass equals its capped mass v<1.
Its role gain is Bv with B=1-q-q_new. The original-short total payment falls
by at least B because its capped new mass is at most its original total1.
Thus the net inequality survives the switch. The quoted post-switch retention
inequality is precisely B+R<=KV. Own original-short role cannot rise: before
the switch q rises; afterward its payment is at most h_i/2, while its full
short-role payment was at least h_i/2. This independently justifies D>=V-R
even for an actual owner holding both coordinates.
