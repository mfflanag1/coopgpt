THEOREM
Let c=1/(2e), A=1/2+c, delta=CM-Z, and let J be any fixed actual-owner subset.
For EVERY real eta>=2,

 eta delta+[1+1/(16eta)](H-M) >=A a_J-U_J.              (EP1)

Thus in a full hardware-bottleneck profile with H=1, taking the STRICT negative
set J={i:d_i<a_i}, v=sum_J(a_i-d_i), Zminus=sum_J z_i,

 eta delta+[1+1/(16eta)](1-M)
                 >=(1/2+c)v-(1/2-c)Zminus.             (EP2)

The earlier eta=1 tangent family is available separately. The new family gives
eta2, D33/32; eta4, D65/64; eta8, D129/128; and arbitrarily large weights with
spill tending to one. The statements apply to actual subsets, not clones.

2. TAIL x>=1
Set b=1/2-2c>0. Then G(1)=0 and G'(x)=b+f(x)>0.
Consequently G(x)>=0 for x>=1, proving EP3 there.

3. THE INTERVAL2/3<=x<=1
Write u=1-x in[0,1/3] and
 phi(u)=1-(1-u)exp(u).
Then

 phi(u)>=u^2/2+u^3/3+u^4/8,

The latter follows from phi(v)=integral_0^v t exp(t)dt.

The polynomial
 (1/2+u/3+u^2/8)(1-u)

It follows that

where the final step is AM-GM. This proves EP3 on this interval for EVERY
eta>0, with no asymptotic interpretation.

This proves F2>0 on[0,2/3], completing EP3 and the theorem.
