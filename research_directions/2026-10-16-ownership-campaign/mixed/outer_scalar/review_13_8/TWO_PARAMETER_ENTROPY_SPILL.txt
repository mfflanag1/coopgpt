A TWO-PARAMETER ENTROPY TANGENT AND DEFICIT/SPILL FAMILY
17 October2026. Candidate analytic extension; independent review requested.
Original symmetric exponential local primitive and fixed actual-owner subsets.
No new funding constant or historical-priority claim.

STATEMENT
Put c=1/(2e). For any t in[c,1/2] and any real eta>=2, define
 s=1+log(2t), A=s+c-t, D0=2(A-sc).
For every actual-owner subset J,

 eta delta+[D0+1/(15eta)](H-M)>=A a_J-s U_J.             (TP1)

For the normalized STRICT negative outer-gap set J={i:d_i<a_i}, with
v=sum_J(a_i-d_i), Zminus=sum_J z_i, this gives
 eta delta+[D0+1/(15eta)](1-M)>=A v+(c-t)Zminus.          (TP2)

The endpoint t=1/2 has the stronger1/(16eta) correction proved separately in
PARAMETRIC_ENTROPY_DEFICIT_SPILL.txt; retain that row as well. The present
theorem supplies the whole tangent portfolio. The original eta1 rows also
remain available.

1. COEFFICIENT BOUNDS
View A as a function of s in[0,1]:
 A(s)=s+c-(1/2)exp(s-1).
It is concave, A(0)=0, A(1)=1/2+c. Consequently
 A>=s(1/2+c)>=3sc, since c<1/4.
In particular D0>=A, and b_t:=A-3sc>=0.

Uniformly in s, b_t<=27/200. To prove this put d=1-3c>0. The maximum of
ds+c-exp(s-1)/2 over all real s is c+d log(2d). Put y=6c-1 in(0,1), so
2d=1-y. The elementary series bound for the logarithm gives
 b_t<=c-d[y+y^2/2+y^3/3]<27/200.                        (TP3)
The last strict constant inequality is checked by directed rational bounds on c.

2. EXACT BRANCH REDUCTION
The hardware-short tangent proof and the exact total clipping loss are those
in SUBSET_ENTROPY_DEFICIT_COUPLING.txt. Since eta>=1, hardware-short branches
are covered. On a power-short branch, with x=rho-1>=0, q=exp(-x)/2,
f(x)=c-qx and h/T>=rho, the residual is minimized at h/T=rho because
D0+1/(15eta)>=A. It is enough to show

 eta f(x)-R_t(x)+x/(15eta)>=0,                          (TP4)
 R_t(x)=A(1+x)-s q(1+x)-D0 x.

At x=1, R_t(1)=0. Its derivative is
 R_t'(x)=A-D0+s qx<=A-D0+sc=-A+3sc<=0,
because qx<=c. Thus x>=1 is immediate. For0<=x<=1 put u=1-x,
phi(u)=1-(1-u)exp(u), Psi(u)=integral_0^u phi(v)dv. Direct substitution gives
 R_t(x)=(A-3sc)u+sc Psi(u)
       <=(27/200)u+c Psi(u)=:Rbar(x).                 (TP5)

3. REGION2/3<=x<=1, EVERY eta>0
Here u<=1/3 and the same positive-series bounds as in the endpoint proof give
 f(x)>=c u^2(1/2+u/3+u^2/8),
 Rbar(x)<=u[27/200+c exp(1/3)/54].
The decreasing polynomial in the first line gives x f(x)>=5c u^2/12.
The exact constant bound
 [27/200+c exp(1/3)/54]^2<c/9                           (TP6)
therefore implies Rbar(x)^2<=(4/15)x f(x).
AM-GM gives
 eta f(x)+x/(15eta)>=2sqrt[x f(x)/15]>=Rbar(x)>=R_t(x).
This proves TP4 in this region.

4. REGION0<=x<=2/3, EVERY eta>=2
Since f decreases here, the exact endpoint inequality
 f(2/3)=c-exp(-2/3)/3>1/90
implies f(x)>=x/60. Thus eta f(x)+x/(15eta) increases with eta>=2, and eta2
is enough. The reduced lower bound is
 Fbar2(x)=2f(x)-Rbar(x)+x/30
         =-c-27/200+(c+101/600)x+q(1-x).
It has
 Fbar2'(x)=c+101/600+q(x-2),
 Fbar2''(x)=q(3-x)>0.
The certified derivative signs at3/5 and2/3 are negative and positive,
respectively. Its minimum is therefore in[3/5,2/3]. The supporting tangent
at2/3, evaluated at3/5, equals
 -(2/5)c-17/500+(19/90)exp(-2/3)>0.                    (TP7)
This proves the entire compact interval and TP4.

5. SUMMATION AND SCOPE
Zero-output branches satisfy the required inequality because D>=A. Summing
the branch inequalities proves TP1, with no owner-count factor. For the
negative outer-gap subset use a_J>=Zminus+v and U_J<=Zminus, giving TP2.
All other owners' full caps and grid overflow are allowed.

If delta=0, letting eta tend to infinity gives the exact limiting spill D0.
This limiting spill is sharp for the subset statement: a power-short branch
with rho2, h=2T, q=c and J containing its hardware has
A a_J-s U_J=2(A-sc)T=D0(h-T). Thus the theorem moves toward a structurally
necessary boundary; it does not simply tune a numerical threshold.
For delta>0, the parameter family remains a sufficient necessary-condition
portfolio, with no claim that all its moment tuples are jointly attainable.
The exact checker corroborates only finite scalar constants; the analytic
argument establishes all real t,eta and all owner and branch counts.
