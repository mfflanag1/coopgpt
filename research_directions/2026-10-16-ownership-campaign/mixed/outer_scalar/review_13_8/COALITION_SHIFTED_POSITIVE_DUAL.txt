COALITION-FLOOR SHIFT OF THE POSITIVE-GAP DUAL
16 October 2026. Candidate structural strengthening; independent review pending.
Original symmetric exponential primitive and actual own-report envelope.
Fixed actual owners; all real mixed-bundle underreports; unrestricted counts.
No novelty claim for the scalar support/square-completion ingredients.

1. RETAIN THE OWNER'S HARDWARE AND FIXED OPPONENT FLOOR
Normalize full hardware to one at a physical hardware bottleneck. For a positive
full recipient i, use the established notation
 a=H_i, d=min(p_i+z_i,1), g=d-a>0, S=sum_k min(p_k+z_k,1)>=1,
 z=z_i, w=(min(p_i,1)+z_i-1)_+, K=e-1, c=1/(2e), C=1+c.
Let R>=0 be ANY certified lower bound on opponents' capped outer account total
at every actual i-underreport, after restoring the same outer hardware/grid
accounts used in POSITIVE_GAP_DUAL_AGGREGATION. It must hold uniformly for all
such reports. Weighted actual-coalition floors and the finite-cap LP floors
provide examples. They introduce no new players or report domains.

Put b=a+R and initially suppose b<1. Since the full report is allowed,
R<=S-d, so b+g<=S and b<S. At any comparison with own remaining positive gap
r=d'-a=g-x>0 and total capped account mass t, the stronger constraint is
              r<=min{1-a,(t-b)_+}.                      (CS1)
This follows from the own cap d'<=1 and t>=d'+R. The old dimension-free support
used only r<=min(t,1), discarding both a and R.

2. EXACT SHIFTED SUPPORT FUNCTION
Choose any u in(b,1], requiring b>0 if u=1. Set
 q=(1/2)exp(1-1/u),
 eta_b=q(u-b)^2/[u^2(S-b)],
 lambda_b=1-q[1+(u-b)(S-u)/(u^2(S-b))],
 A_b=lambda_b-eta_b=1-q[1+(u-b)/u^2]>0,
 mu_b=K^2 eta_b^2/(4A_b).                              (CS2)
Then for every0<=t<=S,
 min{1-a,(t-b)_+}[k(t)-lambda_b]_+ <=eta_b(S-t).         (CS3)
The coefficient eta_b is sharp for this scalar relaxation: the quotient is
maximized at t=u, or its endpoint limit if S=u=1. This does NOT claim that a
single physical counterprofile attains the relaxed scalar maximum.

For b<t<=1, the quotient before taking positive parts is
 F(t)=(t-b)[k(t)-lambda_b]/(S-t).
Its derivative has the sign of
 (S-b)(1-lambda_b)-q_h(t)D(t),
 D(t)=(S-b)+(t-b)(S-t)/t^2,
 q_h(t)=(1/2)exp(1-1/t).
The derivative of q_h D has the sign of the concave quadratic
 Q(t)=-(2b+1)t^2+(S+b+2bS)t-bS.
At its interval endpoints,
 Q(b)=2b^2(S-b)>=0, Q(1)=(1+b)(S-1)>=0.
Thus q_h D increases on[b,1], with the zero-boundary cases understood by
continuity. The definition of lambda_b makes F increase to u and decrease
after it. At u the derivative identity gives F(u)=eta_b.

For1<=t<=min(S,1+R), the cap in(CS1) is still t-b. Its quotient derivative
has the sign of J(t)-lambda_b, where
 J(t)=k(t)[1-(t-b)(S-t)/(S-b)].
Differentiation gives
 J'(t)=k(t)(S-t)(t-b-2)/(S-b)<=0
because t<=1+R=1+b-a and a>=0. Also J(1)=lambda_b(1), and monotonicity of
q_h D above gives lambda_b(u)>=lambda_b(1). Therefore the quotient decreases
through this region. Once t>=1+R, the cap is the constant1-a; its quotient
derivative has the sign of k(t)(1-S+t)-lambda_b. This is at most q0-lambda_b,
where q0=k(S), since exp(v)(1-v)<=1 for v=S-t>=0.
Finally
 lambda_b(u)>=lambda_b(1)>=1/(2S)>=q0.
The middle inequality follows by subtracting1/(2S): its difference is
 b(S-1)^2/[2S(S-b)]>=0. This proves(CS3) on the entire range. Positive parts
cause no problem because the right side is nonnegative. For t<=b the left
side is zero. The point t=S is interpreted directly or by a one-sided limit.

For u<1, A_b>=1-q(1+1/u)>0. At u=1, A_b=b/2>0 by the stated restriction.
The endpoint b=0,u<1 recovers the earlier support exactly.

3. CONSEQUENCE FOR EVERY ACTUAL POSITIVE RECIPIENT
The same exact own-cap identity and sharp outgoing theorem as before give
 B=S-t<=x+Kz,    B<=x+K sqrt(z(x+w)).
If t>=S the excess is at most q0*r<=lambda_b*g. If t<S, use(CS1)-(CS3):
 k(t)r-lambda_b*g <=eta_b B-lambda_b x.
The two outgoing inequalities and square completion therefore prove
 E_i<=a_i+lambda_b*g_i
       +min{K eta_b*z_i, mu_b*z_i+A_b*w_i}.              (CS4)
The coefficients can be chosen separately for each actual i from its actual
floor R_i. Different recipients retain different feasible own-report sets;
no common maximizing counterprofile is asserted. Summing the independently
valid upper bounds and retaining the negative-gap savings yields a legitimate
all-owner funding certificate, with no bound on owner or branch counts.

If b>=1, the direct restored-account floor already gives
 E_i<=a_i+k(d_i+R_i)g_i<=a_i+g_i/2.
This case can be combined with(CS4), rather than forcing it into the b<1 formula.
For ANY b, one may also take the minimum with independently valid old dual and
other bounds. The new formula does not unconditionally dominate every old
parameter choice because lambda_b and the overflow coefficient change.

4. A PRECISE COMPARISON AND A DEMONSTRATED FUNDING CONSEQUENCE
For fixed S>1, fixed lambda=1/2 and0<b<1, there is a unique u in(b,1) with
lambda_b(u)=1/2. The scalar support coefficient is strictly smaller than the
old b=0 support at that SAME lambda. Indeed the numerator is pointwise no
larger, and is strictly smaller at the unique old maximizer t<1; continuity
on the positive-support region makes the strict maximum comparison valid.
When w_i=0, both K eta z and K^2 eta^2 z/[4(lambda-eta)] strictly decrease
with eta. Thus(CS4) strictly improves that fixed-lambda old bound whenever
z_i>0 and a_i+R_i>0, uniformly over unrestricted owner/branch counts.
This is a pointwise certificate improvement, not a new asserted global constant.

At S=1 there is an additional exact consequence. If0<b<1, choose u=1:
 lambda_b=1/2, eta_b=(1-b)/2, A_b=b/2.
The old b=0 formula at this lambda has A=0 and supplies no finite quadratic
coefficient. The coalition shift removes that degeneration.

In particular, suppose S=1 and EVERY full positive-gap recipient has
 b_i=a_i+R_i>=1/2 and effective overflow w_i=0.
Recipients with b_i>=1 use the direct bound; the rest have
 eta_b<=1/4, A_b>=1/4, mu_b<=K^2/16<3/16.
Their total positive excess is at most Gplus/2+(3/16)Zplus.
At S=1, Gplus=Gminus=v<=1. For each negative recipient, z_i+g_i<=1,
so K^2 z_i g_i<3/4 and L(K^2 z_i g_i)>L(3/4)=4/(3sqrt(e))>4/5;
the last inequality follows from e<25/9. Since delta=CM-Z<=cM<=c<3/16,
the uniform negative saving exceeds (2/5)(1-c)v>(13/40)v, using
exp(-delta)>=1-delta. Also Zplus<=Z<=C<19/16. Consequently
 sum E_i <=1+(7/40)v+(3/16)Zplus
          <1+7/40+(3/16)(19/16)=1789/1280<7/5<C^2.     (CS5)
This statement allows unbounded owner and branch counts and arbitrary mixed
ownership. It is a sufficient full-profile class, not a restriction imposed
on the underreports. At S=1 with at least two positive recipients, their
zero-overflow condition is automatic: no single d_i can then equal1.
The conditional result is only an illustration of the general structural
support improvement; unrestricted C^2 funding remains open.
