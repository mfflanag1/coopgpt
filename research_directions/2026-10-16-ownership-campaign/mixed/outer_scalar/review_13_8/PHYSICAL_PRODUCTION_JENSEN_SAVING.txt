PHYSICAL-PRODUCTION JENSEN SAVING FOR NEGATIVE OUTER GAPS
16 October 2026. Independent implication of accepted sharp incoming entropy.
Fixed actual owners, arbitrary mixed reports/caps, original actual envelope.

Normalize a physical hardware-bottleneck profile by H=1. Let M be local physical
production, z_i full local payments, q0=exp(1-S)/2, and let J be the full
negative-gap set. Write g_i=a_i-d_i>0, v=sum_J g_i and Zminus=sum_J z_i.
For M>0 define
 f_M(x)=exp[-K x(1+log(M/x))], 0<x<=M, f_M(0)=1,
 K=e-1.
Then the TOTAL NEGATIVE-GAP SAVING is at least

           q0 v f_M(min{M,Zminus}).                    (PJ1)

More precisely one may retain the gap-weighted moment
 mu=(sum_J g_i z_i)/v and use q0 v f_M(mu), when v>0.
When v=0 the saving is zero. When M=0, all z_i=0 and the saving is exactly
q0 v, so (PJ1) has that natural zero-production convention.

PROOF
The all-profile sharp incoming comparison gives E_i<=a_i-q0 g_i f_M(z_i).
Each z_i<=M. On (0,M],
 f_M'(x)=K log(x/M) f_M(x)<=0,
 f_M''(x)=[K/x+K^2 log(x/M)^2]f_M(x)>0.
Thus the continuous extension at zero is convex and decreasing. Weighted
Jensen gives sum_J g_i f_M(z_i)>=v f_M(mu). Since g_i<=v,
mu<=sum_J z_i=Zminus, and also mu<=M. Decreasing monotonicity proves (PJ1).
The cap identity on a negative recipient also gives Zminus+v<=1, as in the
directional-mass theorem, but that extra weakening need not be made here.

COMBINATION WITH EXISTING CERTIFICATES
Let delta=CM-Z. The older accepted nonlinear saving is at least
(4/5)q0 exp(-delta) v. Both bounds apply to the SAME negative-gap sum, so one
may use
 q0 v max{(4/5)exp(-delta), f_M(min{M,Zminus})}.          (PJ2)
This is a maximum of two aggregate lower bounds; it does not claim that
different individual owners simultaneously attain an entropy extremizer.

For a rectangular certificate with M<=Mhi, Zminus<=zhi and delta<=dhi,
the valid constant saving coefficient is
 q0 min-bound = q0 max{(4/5)exp(-dhi),
                     f_Mhi(min{Mhi,zhi})}.
If Mhi=0 use the factor1. The comparison direction follows because the entropy
perspective x[1+log(M/x)] is nondecreasing in BOTH M and x on 0<=x<=M.
First enlarge M to Mhi, then enlarge x to min(Mhi,zhi); this order keeps each
argument in its valid domain. For an S interval use q0>=exp(1-Shi)/2.

The result is an analytical row refinement, not a completed new universal
funding certificate. In particular it is exact at Zminus=0, where every
negative recipient has zero local payment and cannot benefit from harmful
local incoming response. It can be weak when Zminus is large; retain the
maximum in (PJ2), not just the Jensen alternative.
