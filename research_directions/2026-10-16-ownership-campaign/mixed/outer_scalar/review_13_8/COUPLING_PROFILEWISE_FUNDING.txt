PROFILEWISE CONSEQUENCE OF THE INDIVIDUAL PAYMENT/GAP COUPLING
17 October2026. Dependent corollary; independent review requested.
Original symmetric actual envelope, physical hardware bottleneck, H=1.
This records a structural criterion, not a new universal funding constant.

Use c=1/(2e), C=1+c, K=e-1, M<=1, Z=CM-delta, delta>=0.
Define the following nonnegative full-profile quantities:

 t=K sqrt(c M delta),
 eta=1-M+t,
 B=[2(1-M)+t]/c,
 beta=[exp(K)-2]/(1-c),
 gamma=3/4+beta eta,
 J=gamma(Z+B).

Then the ORIGINAL actual-owner envelope satisfies

                  sum_i E_i <=1+exp(-1+J/2).          (PF1)

The inequality is useful only where its right side improves another available
bound. In particular, J<2[1+log(3/5)] is an explicit sufficient condition for
funding below8/5, and J<2[1+log(C^2-1)] suffices for C^2 funding. These are
profilewise sufficient conditions, not assertions that the thresholds always
hold. The8/5 condition contains a nonempty open neighborhood of the full
saturation face M=1,delta=0. No tiny numerical neighborhood is promoted as a
separate mathematical milestone here.

PROOF
For a positive gap g_i, INDIVIDUAL_PAYMENT_GAP_COUPLING.txt gives
z_i+(1-c)g_i<=1+eta, and z_i<=M<=1. Thus
 g_i <=(1-z_i)/(1-c)+eta/(1-c).
The exact scalar support lemma IG8 in that note, applied to the first term,
and convexity of exp(Kz) give

 g_i[exp(K z_i)-2]_+
 <=(3/4)z_i+eta[exp(K z_i)-2]_+/(1-c)
 <=[3/4+eta(exp(K)-2)/(1-c)]z_i =gamma z_i.

For the last step, on z in[0,1], the chord gives
exp(Kz)-2 <=(exp(K)-1)z-1 <=(exp(K)-2)z; its positive part obeys the same
upper bound because the right side is nonnegative.

Put S=sum_i min(p_i+z_i,1), r=S-1>=0, q0=exp(-r)/2, and let v,Zminus,
Zplus denote the negative gap mass and the corresponding negative/positive
local payment masses. The accepted individual bounds and zero-gap comparison
now give
 sum E_i <=1+q0[2r+(2-(4/5)exp(-delta))v+gamma Zplus].
The subset deficit coupling says
 Zminus >=(1-c)v/c-B.
Because Zplus<=Z-Zminus, substitute this into the preceding inequality:
 sum E_i <=1+q0[2r+gamma(Z+B)+
              {2-(4/5)exp(-delta)-gamma(1-c)/c}v].
The coefficient of v is strictly negative: gamma>=3/4 and c<3/16 give
gamma(1-c)/c>13/4>2. Discard that term.
Finally exp(-r)[r+J/2] <=exp(-1+J/2) for every r>=0 and J>=0, since
x<=exp(x-1) for x=r+J/2. This proves(PF1), including the case J/2>1
where the stationary point in r would lie outside the nonnegative domain.

This bound never sums one additive error per owner. All error terms are
charged to the TOTAL positive local-payment mass through gamma; the remaining
opponent deficit is charged through the single full-profile quantity B.
The original all-profile incentive and coalition-covering arguments are
unchanged. Every recipient may choose a different actual deviation.
