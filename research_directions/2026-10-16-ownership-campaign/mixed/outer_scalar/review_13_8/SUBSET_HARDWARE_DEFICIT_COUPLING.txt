SUBSET HARDWARE-ROLE COUPLING TO THE LOCAL BUDGET DEFICIT
16 October 2026. Candidate structural lemma, independent review requested.
Original symmetric exponential local primitive. All actual owners fixed.
No novelty claim. This note does not assert a new universal funding constant.

THEOREM
Let H be total physical hardware, M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), and let Z be the total
original local payment. Put c=1/(2e), C=1+c, K=e-1 and delta=CM-Z>=0.
For ANY fixed actual-owner subset J, let a_J be its hardware total and U_J the
sum of its physical HARDWARE-role local payments. Then

 U_J >=(1-c)a_J-2(H-M)-K sqrt(c M delta).                (SD1)

In particular, normalize H=1 and let J be the full negative outer-gap set,
v=sum_J(a_i-d_i), Zminus=sum_J z_i. Since z_J>=U_J and
a_J>=Zminus+v, one obtains

 (1-c)v-c Zminus <=2(1-M)+K sqrt(c M delta).             (SD2)

At M=1,delta=0, this forces v<=c Zminus/(1-c). It strictly improves the old
v-Zminus<=4(1-M)/3 on this saturation face. Both inequalities remain valid and
may be used together. This is a coupling of the same actual-owner subset to
the full branch deficit; it does not merge owners or impose virtual incentives.

1. ACCEPTED ONE-BRANCH COEFFICIENT STABILITY
On a productive branch with T=min(h,l)>0, capped long-pool ratio rho>=1 and
q=exp(1-rho)/2, its total local payment is T[1+q(rho-1)]. Its nonnegative
budget deficit is
 delta_j=T[c-q(rho-1)].
The accepted MARGINAL_FLOOR_STABILITY.txt, equation (4), proves

 (q-c)_+ <=K sqrt[c delta_j/T].                         (SD3)

This estimate is used only as a coefficient inequality. The new subset proof
below does not apply an individual marginal formula to a merged coalition.

2. HARDWARE-SHORT BRANCH
Here h=T and U_J=(1-q)a_J on this branch. Since a_J<=T,
 U_J >=(1-c)a_J-(q-c)_+ T
      >=(1-c)a_J-K sqrt(c T delta_j).
The term 2(h-T) is zero. This proves the desired branch version of (SD1).

3. POWER-SHORT BRANCH
Let h>=T be full hardware and let d_J=sum_{i in J}min(h_i,T). The total clipping
loss of all hardware accounts is at most h-T, so
 d_J>=a_J-(h-T), and U_J=q d_J>=q[a_J-(h-T)].
The lower bound remains valid when its bracket is negative.
Because 1-c-q>0 and a_J<=h, the deficit above this lower bound obeys

 (1-c)a_J-2(h-T)-U_J
 <=(1-c-q)a_J+(q-2)(h-T)
 <=(1-c)h-qT-2(h-T)
 =T[2-q-(1+c)h/T]
 <=T B(rho),  B(rho)=2-q-(1+c)rho,                     (SD4)

using h/T>=rho. We show B(rho)<=K sqrt[c delta_j/T].

At rho=1, B(1)=1/2-c=Kc. Also
B'(rho)=q-1-c<=-1/2-c<=-2Kc,
where the last inequality is equivalent to c>=1/6 and follows from e<3.
Therefore
                       B(rho)<=Kc(3-2rho).             (SD5)
For rho>=3/2 the right side is nonpositive and the claim is immediate.
For 1<=rho<=3/2, put u=2-rho in [1/2,1]. Then
 delta_j/(cT)=1-(1-u)exp(u),
 1-(1-u)exp(u)-(2u-1)^2=(1-u)[4u-exp(u)]>=0.
Indeed exp(u)/u decreases on [1/2,1], and its value at1/2 is2sqrt(e)<4.
Thus sqrt[c delta_j/T]>=c(2u-1)=c(3-2rho). Combine with (SD5) to obtain the
desired branch estimate. This proof includes the tie rho=1 and the rho=3/2
boundary without assuming uncapped actual owners.

4. SUM OVER BRANCHES
For a zero-output branch U_J=0 and
(1-c)a_J-2h<=0, so it contributes no error. Summing the productive-branch
inequalities and applying Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 sum_j sqrt(T_j delta_j)<=sqrt[(sum_j T_j)(sum_j delta_j)]
                         =sqrt(M delta).
This proves (SD1), including arbitrary mixed owners who occur in many branches.

For the negative outer-gap subset, d_i<a_i<=1 implies d_i=p_i+z_i, and hence
a_J=Zminus+v+sum_J p_i>=Zminus+v. Since Zminus>=U_J, substitute this into
(SD1) and rearrange to obtain (SD2). All quantities are from one full profile;
the lemma is intended to constrain a subsequent all-profile envelope proof.

5. CERTIFICATE FORMS AND EXACT COMPARISON DIRECTIONS
On a rectangle delta<=dhi, M<=mhi, the safe linear row is
 2M+(1-c)v-c Zminus <=2+K sqrt(c mhi dhi).
For any fixed alpha>0, another valid linear row follows from
 sqrt(c M delta)<=sqrt(c)(alpha M+delta/alpha)/2.
Neither form presumes the square-root upper bound is attainable by an actual
profile. If c is replaced by a rational interval [clo,chi], the left side
2M+v-c(v+Zminus) must use chi for an OUTER relaxation; the right side may use
an upper K and upper c. Signs matter because v,Zminus are nonnegative.

Two additional exact role rows, directly from JOINT_ROLE_TRADEOFF.txt, are
useful with (SD2). For every fixed t in [0,1),
 (1-t)v+Z <=1+(1/2)exp(-t),
 (1-t)v+Z <=1-t+M[1+(t/2)exp(-1/t)].                   (SD6)
At t=0 interpret the second exponential term as zero. To derive these, use
v<=1-Z+L and the joint support (1-t)L+tZ, taking B=1,q=t; the t=0 cases follow
by continuity. They interpolate the older simple hardware/production rows.

6. BOUNDED DIAGNOSTIC AND OPEN NUMERICAL GATE
Floating LP diagnostics (subset_role_probe.json) show these rows remove a
spurious M=1,delta=0,v=Zminus≈0.316 face of the previous certificate. They do
not yet certify an unrestricted8/5 bound: with pi bounded by11/8, some sampled
relaxations remain above1.6. In particular a v=0 face cannot be improved by
negative savings alone. These are diagnostics of a relaxation, not actual
report profiles or lower bounds. A new exact certificate is required before
claiming an improved universal funding factor.
