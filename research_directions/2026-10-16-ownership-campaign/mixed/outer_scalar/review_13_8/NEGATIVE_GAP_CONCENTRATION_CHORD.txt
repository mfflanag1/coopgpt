Let c=1/(2e), C=1+c, K=e-1, delta=CM-Z, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1),
g_i=a_i-d_i>0 on the full negative-gap set, v=sum g_i, Zminus=sum z_i there,
S=sum d_i and q0=exp(1-S)/2. The accepted individual theorem gives
 E_i<=a_i-q0 exp(-delta)g_i L(K^2 z_i g_i),
 L(y)=2exp(sqrt(1-y)-1)/(1+sqrt(1-y)).

Then the TOTAL retained negative saving is at least

 q0 exp(-delta)[v-(4K^2/15)sum_i z_i g_i^2]              (NS1)
 >=q0 exp(-delta)[v-(4K^2/15)v^2 Zminus].               (NS2)

The exact moment NS1 is preferable whenever it is retained. NS2 is a valid
aggregate reduction without an owner-count factor. It may be combined with,
or replaced pointwise by, the older uniform (4/5)q0 exp(-delta)v bound.

PROOF
For a full negative gap d_i<a_i<=1, the own account is uncapped and
d_i=p_i+z_i. Hence z_i+g_i=a_i-p_i<=1 and z_i g_i<=1/4. The exact bound
K^2<3 therefore implies 0<=K^2 z_i g_i<3/4.

Put s=sqrt(1-y). On[0,3/4],
 L'(y)=-exp(s-1)/(1+s)^2,
 L''(y)=-exp(s-1)(1-s)/[2s(1+s)^3]<=0.
Thus L is concave. Its endpoint values obey
 L(0)=1, L(3/4)=4/(3sqrt(e))>4/5,
where the strict comparison follows from e<25/9. The chord then gives
 L(y)>=1-(4/15)y on the entire required interval.

Substitute this into each individual negative saving and sum to obtain NS1.
Since 0<=g_i<=v, sum_i z_i g_i^2<=v^2 sum_i z_i=v^2 Zminus, proving NS2.
Every recipient may choose a DIFFERENT actual own underreport. The proof uses
only the full-profile gaps and local payments in the accepted individual bound,
so it neither merges owners nor assumes simultaneous attainment.

SCOPE
This improves the uniform4/5 coefficient when the aggregate correction is
small. It is a necessary contribution to an envelope upper bound, not an
attainability claim for the aggregate moment v^2 Zminus. Exact certificate
implementations must use an upper enclosure for4K^2/15 when subtracting the
correction; the retained positive prefactor exp(-delta) needs its lower bound.
