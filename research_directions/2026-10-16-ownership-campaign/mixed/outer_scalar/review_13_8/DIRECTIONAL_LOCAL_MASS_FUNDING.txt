DIRECTIONAL LOCAL-PAYMENT MASS: A GENERAL FUNDING CRITERION
16 October 2026. Candidate, internal independent review requested.
Original symmetric exponential nested primitive and its ACTUAL own-report
envelope. Fixed actual owners, all real mixed reports, arbitrary finite counts.
No novelty claim. Root supplied the positive-mass robustness extension.

THEOREM
Consider any physical hardware-bottleneck profile with H>0. Normalize H=1,
write a_i=H_i, z_i for full local payments, d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), and define
 Jplus={i:d_i>a_i},
 delta_plus=sum_{i in Jplus} z_i.
With K=e-1 and A=exp(K delta_plus), the ORIGINAL actual envelope satisfies

           sum_i E_i <= 1+(A/2)exp[-1/(4A)].             (DM1)

In particular, if delta_plus<=1/100, then
                         sum_i E_i <7/5<C^2.            (DM2)

The condition is on the AGGREGATE local-payment mass of positive outer-gap
owners, not on every owner's local payment. Negative-gap owners may have large
local payments, arbitrary mixed hardware/local/grid bundles, and arbitrary
feasible underreports. Full grid overflow is allowed. This is a condition on
the evaluated full profile, not a restriction imposed on subsequent reports.
All owner and branch counts remain unbounded.

At delta_plus=0, the simpler bound is
             sum E_i <=1+(1/2)exp(-1/4)<7/5.
This includes, but is not defined by, the case in which every positive-gap
recipient is grid-only. Grid ownership by negative-gap local owners is allowed.
The theorem is a sufficient profile class; it does not settle general C^2.

1. ACCEPTED ALL-PROFILE OUTER COMPARISONS
Set S=sum_i d_i>=1, r=S-1 and q0=exp(-r)/2. Accepted sharp outgoing/incoming
estimates, with full cap and outer-switch handling, give:
 for g_i=d_i-a_i>0,
  E_i<=a_i+q0 exp(K z_i)g_i;
 for g_i=a_i-d_i>0,
  E_i<=a_i-q0 f(z_i)g_i,
where
 f(z)=exp[-K z(1+log(1/z))], 0<z<=1, and f(0)=1.
The incoming theorem originally contains M instead of 1 inside the logarithm;
M<=H=1 permits the stated weakening. Also z_i<=M<=1.
For zero full gap E_i<=a_i. These comparisons optimize over the full actual
own-report domain and do not assume hardware can first be restored as an
actual report. Thus the current criterion is valid below the new hardware-
restoration threshold as well as above it.

2. NEGATIVE-GAP MASS AND LOCAL MASS CANNOT BOTH BE LARGE
Let Jminus={i:a_i>d_i}, g_i=a_i-d_i there, and v=sum_{Jminus}g_i.
Then 0<=v<=1. A negative gap implies d_i<a_i<=1, so that owner's full opposing
account is uncapped: d_i=p_i+z_i. Consequently

 Zminus+v=sum_{Jminus}(z_i+g_i)
          =sum_{Jminus}(a_i-p_i)<=1.                    (DM3)

This identity remains true if other owners have arbitrarily large grid overflow.
For 0<z<=1,
 f'(z)=K log(z)f(z)<=0,
 f''(z)=[K/z+K^2 log(z)^2]f(z)>0.
Hence f is decreasing and convex, with its continuous convex extension at zero.
For v>0, weighted Jensen and g_i<=v imply

 sum_{Jminus}g_i f(z_i)
 >=v f(sum_{Jminus}g_i z_i/v)
 >=v f(Zminus)
 >=v f(1-v).                                           (DM4)

When v=0 the same inequality is zero on both sides. At v=1, (DM3) forces
Zminus=0 and (DM4) gives the exact saving v. This is a coupling of full actual
owner payments and hardware accounts, not an independent cap relaxation.

3. A UNIFORM ELEMENTARY SCALAR ESTIMATE
For every v in [0,1],

                         v[1-f(1-v)]<=3/4.             (DM5)

For v<=3/4 this follows from f>=0. For v>3/4, monotonicity gives
f(1-v)>=f(1/4)>1/3. To check the last bound, use K<7/4 and log4<7/5:
 K(1/4)(1+log4)<21/20,
 exp(21/20)=e exp(1/20)<(11/4)(20/19)=55/19<3.
The inequality exp(x)<=1/(1-x), 0<=x<1, follows term by term from its Taylor
series; log4<7/5 follows from the exponential Taylor lower sum through degree6.
Thus v[1-f(1-v)]<(2/3)v<=2/3<3/4 in this second region.
No numerical optimization is needed for (DM5).

4. SUM THE ACTUAL ENVELOPES
Write Gplus=sum_{Jplus}(d_i-a_i)=r+v. Since z_i<=delta_plus on Jplus,
the positive comparison and (DM4) give the sharper profilewise inequality

 sum E_i<=1+q0{A r+[A-f(1-v)]v}.                         (DM6)

The quantities on the right are all from the same full profile, although each
individual envelope may choose a different actual underreport. By (DM5),
 [A-f(1-v)]v=(A-1)v+v[1-f(1-v)]<=A-1/4.
Therefore
 sum E_i<=1+(1/2)exp(-r)[A r+A-1/4].
The expression in square brackets times exp(-r), r>=0, has its unique maximum
at r=1/(4A)>0, of value A exp[-1/(4A)]. This proves (DM1).
Its derivative in A is positive, so a larger certified upper bound on
delta_plus may be substituted.

5. EXACT SMALL-MASS THRESHOLD
If delta_plus<=1/100, then
 K delta_plus<7/400,
 A<exp(7/400)<=400/393<51/50.
Thus
 (A/2)exp[-1/(4A)]<(51/100)exp(-25/102)<2/5,
where exp(25/102)>51/40 already follows from Taylor terms0--3.
This proves (DM2). The strict inequality 7/5<C^2 follows from e<87/32, hence
c>16/87 and (1+16/87)^2=10609/7569>7/5.

6. AN UNBOUNDED MIXED-GRID CLASS INCLUDED BY THE CRITERION
For each branch j=1,...,n, let owners A_j and B_j each hold hardware1/(2n),
local resource1/(4n), and grid1/(10n). Add a grid-only owner G_j with2/(5n).
All identities and permissions are fixed at every report. Every branch is
productive, H=1, M=1/2, P=3/5, so physical output is hardware-bottlenecked.
The local capped ratio is2 and c=1/(2e), giving
 z_Aj=z_Bj=(1+c)/(4n),
 d_Aj=d_Bj=[1/10+(1+c)/4]/n<1/(2n)=a_Aj=a_Bj.
The G_j are the only positive-gap owners and have z_Gj=0. Thus delta_plus=0
for every n. Both branch and actual-owner counts grow, and all local owners
also own grid: the class does not impose separated grid ownership.
Local underreports can change other local payments; the proof controls all
such reports rather than declaring their external responses absent.

The same construction can keep a negative owner's local payment bounded away
from zero while both counts grow. Use branch weights w_0=1/2 and
w_j=1/(2n), j=1,...,n, instead of equal 1/n weights, and multiply the displayed
per-unit branch bundles by each w_j. On branch0 each mixed local owner then
has z=(1+c)/8>1/8>1/10 for every n. Thus this example remains outside the
accepted max_i z_i<=1/10 diffuse criterion, but still has delta_plus=0.

This example illustrates the domain, not an extremizer or an unrestricted
lower bound. The theorem itself applies to every profile meeting (DM2),
including arbitrarily many positive and negative gaps and uneven ownership.
