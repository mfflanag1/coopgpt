OVERFLOW DECREASES ADMIT NO UNIFORM LINEAR GRID CHARGE
16 October 2026. Exact structural obstruction, not a funding lower bound.
Original exponential local primitive, fixed actual owners and real binding
underreports. This does not contradict the proved overflow-INCREASE bound.

CLAIM
There is no universal finite K such that
 sum_i sum_k (w_k-w_k^i)_+ <=K P,
where w_k=(p_k+z_k-H)_+, hardware and grid are restored for the outer comparison,
and each actual owner i chooses its own local underreport separately. Failure
already occurs on physical hardware bottlenecks. Both branch and owner counts
can grow.

ONE-BRANCH VERSION
Let n>=2. One actual owner A has hardware1 and grid p=2q, where
q=exp(1-n)/2. There are n other actual owners, each supplying local power1.
The local short total is1 and capped long ratio n. Its payments are
 z_A=1-q and z_i=q for each power owner.
Thus H=M=1, P=2q, and the sole outer overflow is w_A=q.
When any one power owner deletes its unit, the new local coefficient is e q,
so A's account becomes1-(e-2)q<1 and its overflow vanishes. Each separately
chosen deviation therefore decreases w_A by q. Their aggregate is
 nq=(n/2)P, with no finite universal constant. The deviating owners and their
eligibility are unchanged; no owner is cloned, replaced, or added by a deviation.

SIMULTANEOUSLY GROWING BRANCH AND OWNER COUNTS
Take m>=2 branches. The same owner A has hardware1 on every branch and grid
p=2m q. Each branch has its own n distinct unit-power owners. Full hardware
and output are H=M=m, and z_A=m(1-q), giving w_A=m q.
A unit deletion by one branch's power owner changes z_A by -(e-1)q. Since
m>=2>e-1, A stays strictly overflowing, and this deviation decreases w_A by
exactly(e-1)q. All mn power owners may choose these separately, giving
 sum_i sum_k(w_k-w_k^i)_+ =mn(e-1)q
                         =[n(e-1)/2]P.
Choose m=n and let n grow. Both counts grow and the ratio diverges. The full
physical profile has P+M>H. Its grid mass tends to zero relative to H, so
this also blocks a uniform Lipschitz perturbation argument in grid mass.

LIMIT
The proof only rules out a symmetric counterpart to the grid-funded
OVERFLOW-INCREASE lemma. It neither violates that lemma nor proves that
actual envelope funding exceeds C^2H. The local ratio n and local slack
remain available to a finer charge; signed capped changes and recipient
payoff weights may cancel these separate decreases.
