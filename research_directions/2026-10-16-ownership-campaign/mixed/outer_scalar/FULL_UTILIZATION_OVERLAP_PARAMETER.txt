FULL UTILIZATION WITH VARIABLE CAP OVERLAP: A QUANTITATIVE PARAMETER BOUND
16 October2026. Corollary of FULL_UTILIZATION_HEADROOM_FUNDING.txt's scalar
certificate and GRID_WEIGHTED_COALITION_FLOOR.txt. Candidate review pending.

THEOREM
Retain the original nested exponential rule and its actual own-report envelope,
with fixed actual mixed owners and all nonnegative real reports. Suppose M=H>0.
Choose ceilings U_i that BOTH bound every one-owner-counterprofile local
payment to i AND bound i's marginal contribution to every actual local
coalition. The explicit choice U_i=sum_j min(T_j,max(h_ij,l_ij)) has both
properties, as does U_i=M. Define the dimensional cap-overlap parameter
                  Gamma=sum_i (U_i+min(p_i,H)-H)_+.
Then, with gamma=Gamma/H,
             sum_i E_i <=[7/8+(5267/10000)exp(gamma)]H.       (1)
The bound is independent of owner and branch counts. It may be combined with
any better separately proved global bound; it is not claimed sharp.
In particular,
 Gamma=0        gives sum E_i<=1.4017H<C^2H;
 Gamma<=H/4     gives sum E_i<(25/16)H.                     (2)
Thus the theorem tolerates positive variable cap overlap; it does not simply
rename separated grid ownership. The actual envelope remains truthful on its
entire original report domain. The funding bound is conditioned on the full
profile's M=H and stated overlap parameter, not on restricting deviations.

PROOF
Normalize H=M=1 and use the notation of the headroom theorem: epsilon=Z-1,
r=S-1, negative gap total v<=1/2-epsilon, C_i=Z-z_i-M_{-i}<=min(1/2,z_i+epsilon).
No cap-linearity assumption is now made.

The weighted floor and its overlap addendum give opponents' capped total at
least pi_{-i}+M_{-i}-Gamma_{-i}, where pi=sum min(p_i,1) and
Gamma_{-i}=sum_{k!=i}(U_k+u_k-1)_+. Because S-d_i<=pi_{-i}+Z-z_i,
that total is at least S-d_i-C_i-Gamma_{-i}. After restoring the positive
recipient's own capped account to d_i, the opposing total is therefore at least
                  max{d_i,S-C_i-Gamma_{-i}}.
Restoring the account raises its payment; no actual report is asserted to
achieve the restored value or this lower floor.

The reciprocal-exponential inequality
                     k(t)<=.5exp(1-t),  t>=0,               (3)
is valid on BOTH sides of the outer switch. It is equality for t>=1. For
0<t<=1, it is equivalent to exp(1-t)+exp(1-1/t)>=2. The derivative of its
left side minus2 has the sign of
 exp[t-1/t-2log(t)]-1.
The exponent increases to0 on(0,1], since its derivative is (1-t)^2/t^2.
Thus the derivative is nonpositive, and the function decreases to0 at1.
The zero endpoint follows by continuity. This proves(3).

Consequently every positive-gap recipient, without any restriction on the
counterprofile's outer orientation, satisfies
 E_i<=a_i+exp(Gamma)*q0*exp(C_i)*g_i, q0=.5exp(-r).         (4)
This is the cap-safe replacement for the invalid untruncated Ci bound.

If S<=3/2, the general low-total proof in the headroom theorem never used Gamma0
and already gives sum E_i<7/5. Since the right side of(1) is at least1.4017,
this region is settled. Suppose S>=3/2. Let P_chord be the full positive chord
relaxation in(H8) of FULL_UTILIZATION_HEADROOM_FUNDING.txt, and let
              N=q0*(4/5)exp(epsilon-c)*v.
The exact high-total scalar certificate establishes
                  1+P_chord-N <=14017/10000               (5)
throughout the ENTIRE allowed epsilon,r,v domain. Its scalar statement is
independent of whether the physical profile has Gamma0; Gamma0 was only needed
to compare the ORIGINAL positive payments directly with P_chord. Here(4) gives
the corrected comparison with exp(Gamma)P_chord.

Also N<=.2exp(-1/2)<1/8: use r>=1/2, v<=1/2,
exp(epsilon-c)<=1, and sqrt(e)>8/5. Thus
 sum E_i<=1+exp(Gamma)P_chord-N
 <=1+(14017/10000-1)exp(Gamma)+(exp(Gamma)-1)/8
 =7/8+(5267/10000)exp(Gamma).
This proves(1), including all caps and outer switches.

For Gamma<=1/4, exp(Gamma)<=exp(1/4)<9/7; the fourth-power comparison follows
from e<68/25<(9/7)^4. Hence the right side of(1) is at most
              7/8+(5267/10000)(9/7)=108653/70000<25/16.
The exact comparisons are recorded in verify_headroom_overlap_constants.py.

SCOPE
The cap-overlap parameter uses ceilings valid for BOTH stated purposes.
A smaller ceiling improves this form only after proving both properties.
A payment ceiling alone is sufficient for Gamma=0, but not for the positive
Gamma deduction from the marginal-loss bound. It is not valid to
replace U_i by the owner's current local payment without proving that payment
cannot increase under another owner's underreport. Counts remain unbounded.
The case of arbitrarily large Gamma is not claimed to have a useful constant
from(1); the remaining general C^2 funding question is still open.

SEPARATE PAYMENT AND MARGINAL CEILINGS
More generally choose any payment ceilings U_i and any marginal-contribution
ceilings V_i. Put w_i=min(1,(H-min(p_i,H))/U_i) when U_i>0, and w_i=1 when
U_i=0. Define Gamma=sum_i (1-w_i)V_i. The same theorem and proof hold with this
Gamma: the exact threshold-coalition floor loses at most that quantity under
sequential deletion, because it is V_i that bounds each coalition loss.
This distinction allows improved payment ceilings without asserting they also
bound marginal production. The above explicit common-ceiling case follows by
setting V_i=U_i, when (1-w_i)U_i=(U_i+min(p_i,H)-H)_+.
