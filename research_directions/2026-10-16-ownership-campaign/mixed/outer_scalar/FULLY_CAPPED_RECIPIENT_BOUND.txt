FULLY CAPPED RECIPIENT: DIRECT OUTER ENVELOPE CONTROL
16 October2026. Candidate structural corollary of SHARP_OUTGOING_LOSS.txt.
Original exponential primitive; fixed actual owners and all-real underreports.

Normalize full hardware H=1 and assume the full physical hardware bottleneck
P+M>=1. Write a_i=H_i, b_i=p_i+z_i, d_i=min(b_i,1),
S=sum_i d_i>=1, q0=exp(1-S)/2, and N_i=a_i+q0(d_i-a_i).
Fix an actual recipient i with FULLY CAPPED opposing account d_i=1. Set
 g=1-a_i, K=e-1, and kappa_i=K z_i.
Then its actual own-report envelope obeys
 E_i <=a_i+q0*g*exp(min(kappa_i,S-1)).                  (1)
Equivalently its gain is at most
 q0(1-a_i)[exp(min((e-1)z_i,S-1))-1].                 (2)
The same statement holds with the sharper profilewise outgoing charge
 kappa_i=(4/e)U_i+(e-1)V_i,
where U_i,V_i sum the owner's full SHORT-role and LONG-role local payments
branchwise, using each branch's full bottleneck orientation. These roles need
not coincide with hardware and power roles across branches. The staged
outgoing proof explicitly proves this sharper charge.
No claim that summing(1) proves a uniform C^2 budget is made.

PROOF
Choose any actual own underreport. Restore only the owner's OUTER hardware
and grid coordinates after calculating the local payments. Primitive own
monotonicity increases its payment. The restored own local account cannot
increase, so the new capped account d_i'=1-D has D>=0. Other owners retain
their grid accounts. Their positive capped-account losses are no larger than
their positive local-payment losses, hence sum to at most kappa_i by the
sharp outgoing theorem. Thus, for the restored opposing capped total S',
                 S-S' <=D+kappa_i.                     (3)
This is an actual-owner statement; it does not apply a virtual-player constraint.

If S'>=S, its coefficient q'<=q0 and its own gap is at most g. If its new
gap is negative its payment is at most a_i<=N_i; otherwise it is also at most
N_i. Hence such a total-increasing comparison cannot raise its payment.

Suppose1<=S'<=S and put u=S-S'. Its gain is exactly
 q0[exp(u)(g-D)-g].
If g-D<0 there is no positive gain. Otherwise(3) gives
 D>=max(0,u-kappa_i). For0<=u<=kappa_i the upper expression increases in u.
Foru>=kappa_i, the derivative of exp(u)(g-u+kappa_i) is
 exp(u)(g-u+kappa_i-1)<=0, since0<=g<=1.
Since u<=S-1, its maximum occurs at min(kappa_i,S-1) with the corresponding
D lower bound0. This proves(1) for every comparison remaining hardware-short.

Suppose finally S'<1. Then EVERY restored opposing account is below1; in
particular its actual total t equals S'. Let b be the own account, so b<=t.
The outer payment is
 W(t,b)=(1-q(t))b+q(t)min(a_i,t),
 q(t)=.5 exp(1-rho(t)), rho(t)=sum_j min(a_j,t)/t.
On a hardware-cap cell q'=q(rho-h)/t, where h counts capped hardware accounts.
The path W(t,t-r), for fixed r>=0 and r<=t<=1, is nondecreasing. Indeed if
a_i<=t its derivative is
 1-q+q'[a_i-(t-r)] >=1-q-q(rho-h)>=1-q(rho+1)>=0;
the last inequality follows from q(rho+1)<=1 for rho>=1. If a_i>=t, its
payment is t-(1-q)r and its derivative is1+q'r>=0. Cap boundaries and the
zero endpoint are continuous.

If kappa_i>=S-1, use b<=t and the path with r=0 to obtain
 W(t,b)<=W(1,1)=(1+a_i)/2=a_i+q0*g*exp(S-1),
which is(1). If kappa_i<S-1, put r=S-1-kappa_i>0. The full other capped
mass is S-1, and its positive loss is at most kappa_i. Therefore the new
other mass is at least r, giving b<=t-r. Necessarily r<=t<1. Monotonicity
of W in b and the preceding path argument give
 W(t,b)<=W(1,1-r)=a_i+(g-r)/2.
Since g<=1 and r>=0,
 g-r<=g exp(-r): if g=0 this is immediate; otherwise
 1-r/g<=1-r<=exp(-r).
Thus W(t,b)<=a_i+.5g exp(-r)=a_i+q0*g exp(kappa_i), again(1).
All estimates are uniform over the actual own-report box, proving the envelope
bound. The degenerate g=0 case is included: such an owner cannot raise its
outer payment above its full hardware1.

RELATED SIGN OBSERVATION
For ANY recipient with full d_i<=a_i, a restored capped-total decrease cannot
increase its outer payment, even across the bottleneck. If S'>=1, its gap
is nonpositive and q'>=q0, while its own capped account weakly decreases.
If S'<1, b'<=d_i<=a_i and min(a_i,S')>=b'; sinceq<=1/2,
 W<= (a_i+d_i)/2 <=a_i+q0(d_i-a_i)=N_i.
This helps isolate which recipients require a decreasing-total gain charge.
