ONE POSITIVE GAP WITH SMALL TOTAL FULL OVERFLOW
16 October2026. Robustness corollary to ONE_POSITIVE_NO_OVERFLOW_SEVEN_FIFTHS.txt.
Candidate pending independent review; same original exponential primitive.

STATEMENT
In the same fixed-owner depth-two model, suppose F=H, at most one full outer gap
min(p_i+z_i,H)-H_i is positive, and
                 W=sum_i (p_i+z_i-H)_+ <= H/100.
Then the actual own-report envelope satisfies
                         sum_i E_i <=7H/5<C^2 H.
Both the actual owner count and branch count are unrestricted. The overflow
condition bounds total full overflow, not just the positive recipient's overflow.
The case W=0 is the previously certified theorem. No sharpness is claimed for
H/100 or7/5, and the result does not cover arbitrary large overflow.

PROOF MODIFICATIONS
Normalize H=1 and write d_i=min(p_i+z_i,1), S=sum_i d_i>=1. Let the sole
positive recipient have gap g=d-a; then 0<=g<=1 and1<=S<=1+g. Its full overflow
w=(p+z-1)_+ is at most W<=epsilon=1/100.

At any actual own underreport, let D_local=z-z' and x=d-d' be its LOCAL loss
and its CAPPED outer own-account loss after restoring outer hardware/grid.
Exact clipping gives D_local<=x+w<=x+epsilon. If x>=g, the recipient's payment
is at most a. Otherwise0<=x<=g, and the sharp nonlinear outgoing theorem and
directed hardware compatibility yield
 O<=min{K sqrt[z(x+epsilon)],1-z+eta*a}
  <=min{K sqrt[z(x+epsilon)],1+eta(1-g)-z}.
Maximizing over z gives the same kappa formula as the no-overflow theorem,
but with its first argument x+epsilon:
 kappa_e(x,g)=K/2*[sqrt(K^2(x+epsilon)^2
                     +4(x+epsilon)(1+eta(1-g)))-K(x+epsilon)].
The positive contribution above a is consequently at most
       (g-x)k(max{g-x,S-x-kappa_e(x,g)}).
Note that the total reduction subtracts x, not x+epsilon: x is the ACTUAL
capped own loss, whereas epsilon only relaxes the outgoing nonlinear allowance.

For all nonpositive-gap recipients, restored capped total is at most
 P+CM=S+W+CM-Z<=S+epsilon+c.
Their combined negative-gap saving is therefore at least
      (1/2)exp(1-S-c-epsilon)(1+g-S).
This reasoning is valid even when a full zero-gap owner has overflow: its gap
contributes zero. A strictly negative-gap owner's full account is uncapped.
If there is no positive gap, all gaps are zero and the usual E_i<=H_i ceiling
proves the result directly.

It remains to bound, for0<=x<=g<=1 and1<=S<=1+g,
 1+(g-x)k(max{g-x,S-x-kappa_e(x,g)})
     -(1/2)exp(1-S-c-epsilon)(1+g-S).
The exact rational checker certify_one_positive_small_overflow.py uses the same
box proof as the no-overflow certificate, with ONLY the following modifications:
 - compute the kappa upper bound using dh+epsilon instead of dh;
 - subtract only dh in the total lower bound;
 - use exp_lower(1-sh-c_upper-epsilon) for the negative saving;
 - retain epsilon=1/100 exactly.
It completes after1,985 evaluated boxes and993 accepted leaves, with no unresolved
boxes. The largest certified rational upper bound is approximately1.3999825709.
All numerical display is post-certificate; proof decisions use exact Fraction
arithmetic, integer square-root rounding, and Taylor remainder signs. Normal
and -O checks pass. The original certificate remains unchanged.
