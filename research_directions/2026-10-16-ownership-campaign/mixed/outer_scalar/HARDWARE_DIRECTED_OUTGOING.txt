HARDWARE-DIRECTED OUTGOING LOSS AND INDIVIDUAL ROLE COMPATIBILITY
16 October2026. Candidate structural lemmas; independent audit required.
Original exponential primitive, actual fixed owners, all-real mixed bundles.

For each branch j let U_kj,V_kj be the hardware-role and local-power-role
components of owner k's full local payment. Thus z_kj=U_kj+V_kj, U_kj<=h_kj.
Write H_j=sum_k h_kj, Z_j=sum_k z_kj, U_{-i,j}=sum_{k!=i}U_kj,
and let z_kj^i be payments after any actual whole-owner underreport by i.
Let eta=2/e+1/2. Then the following hold branchwise:
 z_ij+U_{-i,j}<=H_j,                                  (1)
 sum_{k!=i}(z_kj-z_kj^i)_+<=U_{-i,j}+eta*h_ij.          (2)
If only the own local-resource coordinate changes, the stronger bound is
 sum_{k!=i}(z_kj-z_kj^i)_+<=U_{-i,j}.                  (3)
In particular, after summing branches,
 z_i+U_{-i}<=H,
 outgoing_i<=U_{-i}+eta*H_i<=H-z_i+eta*H_i.            (4)
The roles in this note are HARDWARE/LOCAL-POWER roles, unlike the original
SHORT/LONG roles used in the sharper nonlinear outgoing accounting.

PROOF OF(1)
It suffices to prove V_ij+sum_k U_kj<=H_j. If hardware is short, T=H_j,
sum U=(1-q)H_j and V_ij=q min(l_ij,T)<=qH_j. If local power is short,
sum U=q D<=qH_j and V_ij=(1-q)l_ij<=(1-q)T<=(1-q)H_j.
Adding U_ij to V_ij then excluding it from the sum proves(1).
Summing also gives the useful aggregate constraint
 z_i-H_i <=H-Z+L,
where L=sum_k V_k and Z=sum_k z_k. This can strengthen z_i-H_i<=L when Z>H.

PROOF OF(2)--(3)
First reduce only i's hardware to its final report. On a hardware-short cap
cell the total positive cross derivative in the INCREASING hardware direction
is at most q*rho^2<=2/e, as in the sharp outgoing theorem. On a hardware-long
cell, an uncapped own hardware increase gives q'=-q/T, while others' short
reports and long caps stay fixed. Their positive payment derivatives sum to
at most q*sum_{k!=i}l_k/T<=q<=1/2. If own hardware is capped, their derivatives
are zero. Consequently the first stage's positive outgoing loss is at most
(2/e) times the amount of hardware withheld.

The OTHER hardware-role payments increase by at most one half times that same
withheld amount. To check this separately, in the hardware-short region
 U_{-i}=(1-q)sum_{k!=i}h_k,
so its derivative with respect to own hardware is
 -q' sum_{k!=i}h_k, whose negative magnitude is at most q(rho-m)<=q rho<=1/2.
In the hardware-long region an uncapped own increase gives derivative
 -(q/T)sum_{k!=i}min(h_k,T), again of magnitude <=q rho<=1/2; a capped own
increase gives zero. These derivatives are nonpositive, and all cap and pool
switches are continuous. Thus at the intermediate profile
 U_{-i}^*<=U_{-i}+(1/2)*(withheld hardware).

Now reduce only i's local-resource coordinate. For another recipient k, fix
its own hardware h_k and local amount l_k. The primitive's cross-sign property
shows that reducing opponents' local resource can hurt k only if h_k>l_k;
if h_k<=l_k this change cannot lower its payment, including pool switches.
When h_k>l_k, its payment is always at least l_k, and its initial excess above
l_k is at most its hardware-role payment U_k^*. Indeed V_k^*<=l_k, so
z_k^*-l_k=U_k^*+V_k^*-l_k<=U_k^*. Hence this entire second stage's positive
loss is at most U_{-i}^*. Combining stages and using positive-part subadditivity
gives(2). If there was no hardware change, this same reasoning gives(3).
The cross-sign property is proved on all cap/pool regions in
ownership/outer_frontier/hardware_bound/RECIPIENT_RESPONSE_HALF_v2.txt, section1.
The baseline l_k lower bound also follows directly from each primitive formula.

All paths use reports of the same fixed owner. Finitely many cell boundaries
and continuity justify integration and include zero endpoints. A zero-output
full branch has no initial payment to lose. Summing branchwise positive losses
bounds the positive loss of the aggregate payments and proves(4).

BRANCHWISE MINIMUM: A USEFUL LOCALIZATION
These bounds may be combined with the independently proved sharp and nonlinear
outgoing inequalities BEFORE summing branches. With D_ij=z_ij-z_ij^i>=0,
K=e-1 and B^2=(e-1)^2+32/e^2, one has
 outgoing_i <=sum_j min{
   K*z_ij,
   Z_j-z_ij,
   U_{-i,j}+eta*h_ij,
   B*sqrt(z_ij*D_ij)
 }.
Use the improved short/long-role coefficient in the last entry if available.
Branches where i's reports do not change contribute0. In particular branches
where both own reports are zero must be omitted rather than given a positive
hardware allowance. Taking a minimum only AFTER summing is valid but can lose
important information: a branch with high hardware payment but negligible own
local influence should not finance outgoing loss on an unrelated branch.

CONCRETE CONSEQUENCE
For a pure local/grid owner (H_i=0), outgoing_i<=min{(e-1)z_i,U_{-i}} and
U_{-i}<=H-z_i. Thus its positive-gap outer bound may use
 kappa_i=min{(e-1)z_i,Z-z_i,H-z_i},
and the corresponding nonlinear charge if the own loss is retained. The exact
outgoing-only cap-summation obstruction in the companion lane used an owner
whose full z_i=q=.5exp(-1/5); its hardware-role opponents total1-q. The present
bound replaces its earlier allowance S-1=1-(4/5)q by the strictly smaller1-q.
This specifically removes the artificial full half-hardware payment assigned
there. It does NOT by itself establish the complete mixed-grid C^2 funding
inequality, and no assertion of optimality for eta is made.
