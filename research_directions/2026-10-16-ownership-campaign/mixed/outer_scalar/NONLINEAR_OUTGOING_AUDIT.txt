I independently checked the short-stage own derivative, its integration, the
long-stage quotient inequality, the pool-switch accounting, all scale changes,
and actual-owner/branch aggregation. The following are the load-bearing checks.

For the short stage, m>=1 gives own derivative >=1-qr>=1/2. For m=0, the
unchanged original-long total is >=initial T=1, so r>=1/t, q<=t/2, and qr<=1/2.
These imply own derivative >=(1-t+v/t)/2>=(1-t+v)/2. Integrating yields exactly
v*epsilon/2+epsilon^2/4. Multiplying by A^2 U with A^2=32/e^2 and U>=x/2
gives(4/e^2)x*epsilon*(2v+epsilon); comparison with the squared outgoing bound
uses x(2v+epsilon)-epsilon=v(2x-epsilon)>=0. This covers the small-own-report
corner and does not assume a uniform positive lower derivative at a tie.

For a pre-switch long reduction u, the actual own loss formula is exact, and
omitting its nonnegative own-short term is legitimate. The remaining product
with the initial long role reduces to d[e^u u-(e^u-1)d], concave in d on[u,1].
At d=u this equals u^2. At d=1 it equals psi(u)=1-(1-u)e^u>0. The derivative
of (e^u-1)^2/psi(u) has precisely the stated sign2+u-(2-u)e^u, whose derivative
is psi(u)>=0. Thus K=e-1 is valid over the entire real interval, including
the u=0 limiting case.

At a crossing, the owner's new short-role payment is a/2, while its previous
long-role payment is d exp(-(d-a))/2. The inequality a<=d exp(-(d-a)) follows
from d<=1 and1-exp(-u)<=u. This compares actual accounting components of the
same owner. Applying the short-stage inequality after the switch and Cauchy
across the two losses yields B^2=K^2+A^2. Applying Cauchy again across the two
original coordinates, then across branches, is valid because primitive own
monotonicity makes every actual stage and branch own loss nonnegative. Those
losses sum exactly to the aggregate D_i. No favorable branchwise cancellation
or change in actual ownership is used.

The rational bound B<11/4 is correct:
(e-1)^2<49/16 and32/e^2<9/2 from8/3<e<11/4. The exact family with one unit
hardware owner and two unit power owners gives the claimed sqrt-scale limit,
and complete deletion forces B>=e-1. This establishes a gap between the proved
upper and known lower constants, not optimality of the upper.

Finally Dlocal<=Dcap+w is an exact consequence of own cap clipping with grid
restored. Combining it with the outgoing loss bound gives the displayed capped
