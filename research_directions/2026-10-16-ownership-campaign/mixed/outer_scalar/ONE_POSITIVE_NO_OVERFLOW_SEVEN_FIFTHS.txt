ONE POSITIVE OUTER GAP WITHOUT FULL OVERFLOW: A 7/5 ENVELOPE BOUND
16 October 2026. Candidate proved by analytical reduction and exact rational
certificate; independent review pending. Original exponential primitive only.
This is a structural class with unbounded numbers of actual owners and branches,
not a claim about all mixed-grid profiles, novelty, or the optimal constant.

1. MODEL AND STATEMENT
Use the fixed-owner depth-two production model and actual own-report envelope E
of the original nested exponential rule. Hardware H=sum_i H_i, local optimum
M=sum_j min(H_j,L_j), grid total P, and production F=min(H,P+M). The local
payment is z_i, with Z=sum_i z_i, and the full outer opposing account is p_i+z_i.
Let C=1+1/(2e), c=C-1, K=e-1, eta=1/2+2/e.

Suppose the full profile satisfies:
 (i) physical hardware bottleneck F=H<=P+M;
 (ii) no full outer overflow: p_i+z_i<=H for EVERY actual owner i;
 (iii) at most one owner has p_i+z_i>H_i.
Then
                         sum_i E_i <=(7/5)H < C^2 H.                 (1)
The no-overflow and one-positive-gap restrictions concern the full profile.
Own deviations may change either local bottleneck, create opposing overflow,
and change the outer bottleneck. All actual owners and their eligible resource
coordinates remain fixed. The theorem concerns payments, not welfare losses or
an explicitly polynomial algorithm for evaluating the actual envelope.

The zero-H case is immediate. Normalize H=1. Write a_i=H_i,
d_i=p_i+z_i<=1, S=sum_i d_i=P+Z>=1. Let r be the sole positive-gap owner and
put a=a_r, z=z_r, d=d_r, g=d-a. Since all other gaps are nonpositive,
                  0<=g<=1,                  1<=S<=1+g.             (2)
If no positive-gap owner exists, S=1 and the ordinary nonpositive-gap ceiling
E_i<=a_i gives total at most1. Hence assume g>0 below.

2. POSITIVE RECIPIENT: RETAIN OWN LOSS AND LOCAL HARDWARE
For any whole-owner underreport of r, first compute its local payments and then
restore only its OUTER hardware and grid coordinates. This algebraic restoration
raises its outer payment by own-coordinate monotonicity. Let
                  D=z-z_r' >=0,
and let O=sum_{i!=r}(z_i-z_i')_+ be outgoing positive local loss. The restored
own outer account is exactly d-D: the full account was at most1 and the local
own payment cannot increase. If D>=g, its payment is at most a, so it suffices
to consider 0<=D<=g. In fact D<=z, but that further restriction is not needed.

The sharp nonlinear outgoing theorem and directed hardware lemma give
 O<=K sqrt(zD),
 O<=U_{-r}+eta*a<=1-z+eta*a,                               (3)
where U_{-r} is full local HARDWARE-role payment to the other owners. The last
inequality is the hardware-role compatibility bound z+U_{-r}<=1. Since d=a+g<=1,
a<=1-g, and therefore
 O<=min{K sqrt(zD),w-z},             w=1+eta(1-g).           (4)
Maximizing this upper bound over the larger interval 0<=z<=w gives
 O<=kappa(D,g):=K/2*[sqrt(K^2 D^2+4Dw)-KD].                (5)
Indeed, the first term in(4) increases in z and the second decreases. Their
intersection solves kappa^2=K^2D(w-kappa). Formula(5) is also valid at D=0.
It is nondecreasing in K,D,w. No report or simultaneous attainment is claimed
at that maximizing artificial z.

Clipping opposing accounts at1 can only reduce their positive loss. Thus the
restored opposing capped total is at least both d-D and S-D-O. Define
 k(t)=1-(1/2)exp(1-1/t) for 0<t<=1,
 k(t)=(1/2)exp(1-t) for t>=1, and k(0)=1.
It is continuous and nonincreasing. When the restored total is at least1,
the positive-gap payment is exactly a+k(total)(g-D). Below1, the own hardware
account is uncapped whenever g-D>0, and the other hardware caps increase the
coefficient q above (1/2)exp(1-1/total); because the gap is positive, this only
LOWERS the payment. Hence
 E_r<=a+max_{0<=D<=g} (g-D) k(max{g-D,S-D-kappa(D,g)}).     (6)
Here we weakened d-D to g-D, another valid relaxation. Equation(6) includes
outer switching and opposing cap crossings.

3. NEGATIVE RECIPIENTS: FULL NO-OVERFLOW IS ESSENTIAL HERE
For any other owner i and any own underreport, restore its outer hardware/grid.
The resulting opposing capped total S' satisfies
 S'<=P+Z'<=P+CM = S+CM-Z <= S+cM <= S+c,
since every local production decreases and M<=H=1, while Z>=M. This uses the
FULL no-overflow identity S=P+Z; it is not asserted with arbitrary full overflow.
Thus, in the hardware-short outer regime, its coefficient is at least
                    q_*=(1/2)exp(1-S-c).
Its own opposing account cannot exceed d_i, so its payment is at most
                    a_i-q_*(a_i-d_i).
If the outer regime switches, its payment is at most(a_i+d_i)/2, which obeys
the same bound because q_*<=1/2. Sum over all i!=r and use
                    sum_{i!=r}(a_i-d_i)=1+g-S.
Combining with(6), the desired envelope budget follows from the scalar inequality

 1+(g-D) k(max{g-D,S-D-kappa(D,g)})
       -(1/2)exp(1-S-c)(1+g-S) <=7/5                    (7)

throughout 0<=D<=g<=1 and 1<=S<=1+g. Different owners may choose unrelated
underreports: the positive and negative bounds above are individually uniform.

All bound decisions and infeasible-box decisions are exact; there is no floating

For a rational box g in[gl,gh], S in[sl,sh], D in[dl,dh], intersect the bounds

 w_hi=1+(5/4)(1-gl),

The square-root upper bound rounds upward to a multiple of 2^-24 using integer
square roots and an exact rational comparison.

For fixed T, the map x -> x k(max{x,T}) is nondecreasing on0<=x<=1. Below T it
is linear; above T, its derivative is
 1-(1/2)exp(1-1/x)(1+1/x)>=0,
by (r+2)exp(-r)<=2 for r=1/x-1>=0. This matters: it permits the sharper positive
box bound x*k(max{x,T_lo}), rather than independently bounding its two factors.
An upper bound on(7)'s left side over the entire feasible part of the box is
 1+x*k_upper(max{x,T_lo})
   -(1/2)*exp_lower(1-sh-23/125)*G_lo.                  (8)
