FIXED OPPONENT COALITIONS GIVE A REPORT-INDEPENDENT OUTER FLOOR
16 October2026. Structural envelope bound. Original exponential nested mechanism;
uses established coalition coverage, not a newly claimed core theorem.

Normalize full hardware H=1. Fix an ACTUAL owner i and any whole-owner underreport.
Every other actual owner's local resource bundle and grid report remain fixed.
For an actual coalition A not containing i define
  m(A)=sum_j min{sum_{k in A}h_kj,sum_{k in A}l_kj},
  p(A)=sum_{k in A}p_k.
Local coalition coverage at the counterprofile implies
                         sum_{k in A} z'_k >=m(A).             (1)
After restoring i's outer hardware/grid, other opposing accounts remain p_k+z'_k.
Write c'_k=min(p_k+z'_k,1). For every such coalition,
                    sum_{k in A}c'_k >=min{1,p(A)+m(A)}.        (2)
Indeed sum min(b_k,1)>=min(1,sum b_k); then apply(1).
There is no assertion that different recipients' counterprofiles coincide.
Inequality(2) holds separately at EVERY underreport by i.

In particular the fixed opponents' grand coalition gives
 R_i=min{1,P_{-i}+M_{-i}},
 M_{-i}=sum_j min{H_j-h_ij,L_j-l_ij},
                   sum_{k!=i}c'_k >=R_i.                      (3)
This may be strengthened to any fixed disjoint partition of the opponents by
summing(2), or to the maximum over those partitions. More generally, any fixed
nonnegative packing weights lambda_A with sum_{A containing k}lambda_A<=1 for
each k!=i give the valid floor
 R_i(lambda)=sum_A lambda_A min{1,p(A)+m(A)}.                   (4)
This is a direct sum of actual-coalition inequalities. It does not impose
virtual-agent coalitions, change owners, or claim the maximizing LP is a new
mathematical ingredient. Finiteness of the actual owner set suffices.

POSITIVE-GAP ENVELOPE CONSEQUENCE
Let a=H_i, d=min(p_i+z_i,1), and suppose d>a. Let R be ANY of the above valid
floors. Restore i's full outer hardware/grid after its actual local underreport,
and then algebraically increase its own capped opposing account back to d.
Own-coordinate monotonicity raises its payment. With own account d>a, increasing
opponents' capped total lowers its payment on either side of the outer switch.
Therefore its envelope satisfies
                       E_i <=a+k(d+R)(d-a),                   (5)
where k(t)=1-.5exp(1-1/t) for0<t<=1 and k(t)=.5exp(1-t) for t>=1.
Below1, opposing hardware caps increase q and hence lower this positive-gap
payment, so the same upper bound applies. At or above1 the expression is exact
for the corresponding abstract opposing-account total. Formula(5) is an upper
bound, not an assertion that the abstract total or increased own account can be
attained by an actual report.

For a full-capped positive recipient d=1 this becomes
                        E_i <=a+(.5 exp(-R))(1-a).             (6)
It applies even with arbitrary full own/grid overflow. If own capped loss is x,
combine the report-independent floor with the sharper nonlinear bound by using
 t>=max{d-x+R,S-x-kappa_i(x)}
inside the positive-gap expression (g-x)k(t). This retains the unchanged
opponents' coalition value alongside the deviator's own-loss charge.

DEMONSTRATED EFFECT ON A PREVIOUS ACCOUNTING OBSTRUCTION
Consider any number of parallel local branches with total hardware1, full local
power supplied by owner i in amount1 and by opponents in amount1/5, and with i
owning no hardware. Give i enough grid capacity for its full opposing account
to be capped. The prior outgoing-only relaxation could assign E_i<=1/2.
Here the actual opponents can produce M_{-i}=1/5 without i, so R_i>=1/5 and
                       E_i <=(.5)exp(-1/5) <1/2.
The reduction holds under proportional replication across arbitrarily many
branches/owners. This removes that artificial half-payment allowance. It does
not alone prove the complete arbitrary-overflow envelope budget.

DEPENDENCIES
Only the fixed-actual-owner interpretation, local coalition coverage at all
reports, and outer primitive's own/cross monotonicity. These are existing
properties of the exponential primitive; no external priority claim is made.
