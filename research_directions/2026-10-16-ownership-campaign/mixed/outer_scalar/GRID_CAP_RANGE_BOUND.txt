GRID-CAP RANGE BOUND FOR THE ACTUAL NESTED ENVELOPE
16 October 2026. Candidate corollary; independent audit required.
Original exponential local and outer primitive, fixed actual mixed owners.
No novelty or optimality claim. This is separate from the half-response lemma.

THEOREM
Use the same physical model and actual envelope E as in the power-bottleneck
note. At any full profile with P>=H>0, put
 pi=sum_i min(p_i,H)/H >=1, m=M/H<=1,
 c=1/(2e), C=1+c, Q=exp(1-pi)/2.
Then
 sum_i E_i/H <=1+Q[pi+(1/2)exp(-exp(-Cm))].              (1)
Consequently all these profiles satisfy
 sum_i E_i <(27/16)H,
and if pi>=15/7, then sum_i E_i<C^2H.
This allows arbitrary owner/branch counts, local ratios, real mixed bundles,
and whole-owner underreports. The conditions are imposed only on the full
profile, not on the envelope's domain. No claim is made here for P<H.

PROOF
Normalize H=1. Write a_i=H_i, local payments z_i, total Z, local-power role
sum L, b_i=p_i+z_i, d_i=min(b_i,1), and S=sum_i d_i.
For any independently chosen actual own underreport, restore that owner's
outer hardware and grid to full while retaining the computed local payments.
Own monotonicity upper-bounds its actual outer payment. The full grid already
sums to at least1, so every restored outer profile is hardware-short.
The opposing capped sum satisfies
 pi <= S^i <= pi+Z^i <= pi+Cm.
Here min(p_k+z_k^i,1)<=min(p_k,1)+z_k^i, and the local total satisfies
Z^i<=CM^i<=CM. Hence its outer coefficient q_i lies in
                       [q_-,Q], q_-=Q exp(-Cm).
Local own monotonicity and restored grid give d_i^i<=d_i. Therefore the
recipient's restored payment is at most
 a_i+Q(d_i-a_i) if d_i>=a_i,
 a_i+q_-(d_i-a_i) if d_i<=a_i.
Let R=sum_i(d_i-a_i)_+. Since the local hardware role is at most its owner's
hardware and capping is subadditive,
 R<=pi+L, and S<=pi+Z.
Summing the preceding individual bounds gives
 sum E_i<=1+(Q-q_-)R+q_-(S-1)
        <=1+Q pi-q_-+(Q-q_-)L+q_-Z.
The exact hardware-side role support from JOINT_ROLE_TRADEOFF.txt, applied
with B=Q and q=q_-, says
 (Q-q_-)L+q_-Z <=q_-+(Q/2)exp(-q_-/Q).
Substitution proves(1). All comparisons hold uniformly before separate owner
suprema are taken; a simultaneous counterfactual profile is unnecessary.

EXACT CONSEQUENCES
The bracket's second term increases with m, so set m<=1. Since C<6/5 and
exp(-6/5)>3/10, we have exp(-Cm)>3/10. Also exp(-3/10)<3/4. Thus
 sum E_i/H <1+(1/2)exp(1-pi)(pi+3/8).                   (2)
The excess term decreases for pi>=1. At pi=1 it equals11/16, yielding27/16.
At pi=15/7 it equals(141/112)exp(-8/7), which is strictly smaller than
 C^2-1=exp(-1)+(1/4)exp(-2).
The accompanying exact rational certificate verifies all three exponential
comparisons by signed Taylor bounds, including the last positive margin.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMIT
The controlling grid statistic is its sum AFTER capping each actual owner's
amount by total hardware. A large grid report held by a single owner has pi=1;
therefore the stronger C^2 consequence is not implied merely by large P.
The range bound improves funding on the full P>=H region, but it leaves the
small-grid physical hardware bottleneck P<H<P+M and the concentrated-grid
C^2 question open. The half-response theorem may still help close those gaps.
