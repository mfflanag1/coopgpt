A COARSER CAP-OVERLAP COROLLARY OF THE WEIGHTED FLOOR
16 October2026. Companion to GRID_WEIGHTED_COALITION_FLOOR.txt.
Use its explicit ceiling U_k=sum_j min(T_j,max(h_kj,l_kj)), which also bounds
k's marginal contribution to EVERY actual coalition: deleting k from a coalition
can reduce a branch minimum by at most max(h_kj,l_kj), and by at most the full
branch output T_j. Sequentially deleting a set B therefore reduces its total
production by at most sum_{k in B}U_k.

For the opponent threshold coalition A_t={k!=i:w_k>=t},
 m(A_t)>=M_{-i}-sum_{k!=i:w_k<t}U_k.
Integrate and use (1-w_k)U_k=(U_k+u_k-1)_+. With
 Gamma_{-i}=sum_{k!=i}(U_k+u_k-1)_+,
the exact weighted floor implies the coarser bound
              R_i^weighted >=pi_{-i}+M_{-i}-Gamma_{-i}.       (1)
The right side can be negative without causing a problem; the exact weighted
floor and pi_{-i} remain nonnegative. In fact U_k<=M<=1 yields Gamma_{-i}<=pi_{-i}.

If V is any valid upper bound on the full opponents' local-payment surplus,
Z-z_i-M_{-i}<=V, then S-d_i<=pi_{-i}+Z-z_i implies
                    R_i^weighted >=S-d_i-V-Gamma_{-i}.       (2)
One may always use V=M/2 by the complement-surplus theorem, or the sharper
original-long-role total when independently established. Equation(2) explicitly
retains the cap-overlap charge; it does not justify dropping Gamma or silently
removing min(1,...) from the ordinary grand-coalition bound.

Exact reference: weighted_floor_reference.py computes the full threshold floor
and(1) for rational normalized reports. Its endpoint checks include M=0,
p>=1, zero U_k, and a mixed-grid instance with floor3/2 while the ordinary grand
floor is1. A separated-grid example has floor3, demonstrating that the new
floor is not artificially truncated at1. These calculations illustrate the
proved formulas; they are not empirical validation or a global subsidy proof.
