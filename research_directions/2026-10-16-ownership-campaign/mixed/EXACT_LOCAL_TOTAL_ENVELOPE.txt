THE EXACT MONOTONE ENVELOPE OF TOTAL TWO-POOL PAYMENTS
16 October2026. Conventional proof candidate awaiting independent audit. Priority unassessed.

LOCAL RULE. Fix n actual owners, each with two nonnegative account reports(a_i,b_i). Let Z(a,b) be the TOTAL payment under the mixed two-pool exponential primitive. Write A=sum a,B=sum b,T=min(A,B). If T=0 set Z=0. Otherwise let the long-pool capped ratio be rho=sum_i min(long_i,T)/T>=1. The total is
 Z=T[1+(1/2)(rho-1)exp(1-rho)].
At A=B, rho=1 and Z=T.

THEOREM. The least coordinatewise nondecreasing majorant of Z on the full nonnegative fixed-owner report domain is
 Lbar(a,b)=T[1+(1/2)(s-1)exp(1-s)], s=min(rho,2),
with Lbar=0 at T=0. Equivalently,
 Lbar(a,b)=max_{0<=a'<=a,0<=b'<=b} Z(a',b').                 (LT)
Thus Z<=Lbar<=C T, C=1+1/(2e), and Lbar is globally coordinatewise nondecreasing. The formula clips the long-pool ratio at the unique maximum2 of(rho-1)exp(1-rho); it does not clip the actual reports in the mechanism.

PROOF OF THE EXACT MAXIMUM. Fix an orientation for which the a pool is short at a candidate report and fix its total t,0<t<=T. Its own bounded coordinates can realize any such total. On the b side, the largest achievable capped total is D_b(t)=sum_i min(b_i,t); every capped total in[0,D_b(t)] is attainable by taking b'_i<=min(b_i,t). To remain long, its total must be at least t. Hence the maximum total payment with this orientation and t is
 G_b(t)=t[1+(1/2)(s_b(t)-1)exp(1-s_b(t))],
 s_b(t)=min(2,D_b(t)/t).
It is attained by choosing capped total t s_b(t). For the opposite orientation exchange a,b.

We show G_b is increasing in t on(0,T]. Between finitely many cap breakpoints b_i, write D_b(t)=k t+r with fixed integer k>=0 and r>=0. If D_b(t)/t>=2, then G_b(t)=C t. Otherwise put rho=k+r/t in[1,2]. Differentiation gives
 G_b'(t)=1+(1/2)exp(1-rho)[(k-1)+(rho-1)(rho-k)].
If k=0, the bracket is rho^2-rho-1>=-1, so the derivative is at least1/2. If k=1, the bracket is(rho-1)^2>=0. The only other possibility is k=2,r=0,rho=2, already covered by the clipped branch. Continuity covers cap and clipping boundaries; G_b(t)->0 as t->0. Thus the optimum of each orientation is at t=T.

If A<B, then D_a(T)=A=T. The opposite orientation therefore gives exactly T, while the original long b pool gives the displayed Lbar>=T. If A=B, both give T. The case B<A is symmetric. This proves(LT), including attainability by actual fixed owners' coordinatewise underreports. As a supremum over the coordinatewise lower box, the result is monotone. Any monotone majorant Q>=Z must satisfy Q(a,b)>=Z(a',b') at every point in that lower box, so it must dominate Lbar; this proves leastness.

GLOBAL CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CURRENT TECHNOLOGY. Sum Lbar_j over the independent local branch pairs to obtain Lbar_global. Branch reports are coordinatewise independent even when one actual owner spans many branches, so this sum is also the exact lower-box maximum of TOTAL local payment sum_i z_i. It is monotone, satisfies
 sum_i z_i<=Lbar_global<=C M,
and is explicit in O(nm) arithmetic/exponential operations on a dense report array.
Use L=Lbar_global in ABSTRACT_RANGE_MONOTONICITY.txt, replacing the old ceilingCM.
