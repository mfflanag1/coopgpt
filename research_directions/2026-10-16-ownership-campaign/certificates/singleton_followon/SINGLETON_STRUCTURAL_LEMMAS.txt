SINGLETON LOCAL OWNERS: EXACT COVERAGE REDUCTION AND A STAR-ROUTE BARRIER
16 October 2026. Independent conventional proofs. No novelty claim.

Scope
-----
The continuous6/5 upper for arbitrary singleton-local profiles remains open.
No larger profile grid was searched. This note isolates the exact coalition
constraints and proves that a natural unrestricted-size extension of the known
three-profile lower can never improve6/5. This is a limitation of that lower-
bound route, not a new all-profile mechanism theorem or a campaign victory.

1. Exact coalition coverage in terms of pair excess
--------------------------------------------------
For arbitrary pod count n, let ell_j=min(h_j,L_j), r_j=h_j-ell_j,
L=sum ell_j,Q=sum r_j. Singleton local owner A_j receives a_j, hardware
owner H_j receives x_j, and shared-grid owner C receives c; all are nonnegative.
Define pair excess e_j=a_j+x_j-ell_j.

Lemma. All coalition inequalities are equivalent to
 (i) e_j>=0 for every j;
 (ii) c+x(J)+e(K)>=min(P,h(J)+r(K)) for every disjoint pair J,K.

Proof. Condition(i) is the coalition {A_j,H_j}. Any coalition without C
produces ell(B intersection I), where I is its hardware set and B its local
set, so(i) suffices by adding the corresponding paired payments. For a coalition
with C, local owners outside I add payment and no production, so it suffices
to take B subset I. Put K=B and J=I minus B. Its production equals
 ell(K)+min(P,h(J)+r(K)),
whereas its payment equals
 c+x(J)+a(K)+x(K)=ell(K)+c+x(J)+e(K).
Subtract ell(K). Every disjoint J,K is itself a valid coalition, proving both
directions. Empty sets, zero capacities, and saturation are included.

In particular, the mandatory pair and full singleton floor constraints can
be imposed minimally for any chosen hardware x by setting
 a_j=max((ell_j-(P-Q)_+)_+,ell_j-x_j).
If0<=x_j<=ell_j, its pair excess is
 e_j=(x_j-min(ell_j,(P-Q)_+))_+.
Thus the total payment is exactly L+c+sum e_j, and the remaining grid coverage
problem is condition(ii). This is an exact general reduction; it does not
automatically make c or the hardware payments own-report monotone.

2. The natural star of reports
-----------------------------
Choose arbitrary n>=1, local loads ell_j>=0 and grid P>=0. Keep every actual
owner and its identity fixed. At the central profile O, set
 h_j=L_j=ell_j for every j.
For each i, form spoke U_i by increasing only owner H_i's hardware report
from ell_i to ell_i+P. All other reports remain fixed. Thus
 F(O)=L and F(U_i)=L+P.
At U_i the residual total is P, so every singleton local marginal floor is
exactly ell_j. The only nontrivial unilateral comparisons within this finite
report set are O<=U_i for owner H_i (unless P=0, when all profiles coincide).

The familiar two-pod witness ell1=ell2=P=1 belongs to this class and forces
factor6/5. We now prove that every member of the entire class, regardless of
pod count or real loads, has a feasible6/5 allocation on all its star profiles.
Therefore no lower-bound certificate using only these stars can exceed6/5.

3. An exact hardware-allocation inequality
------------------------------------------
Write
 q_i=min(P,L-ell_i),
 u_i=(L+6P)/5-q_i,
 x_i=min(ell_i,P,u_i).
Since q_i<=P, u_i>=(L+P)/5>=0. Then for every hardware subset J,

 min(P,ell(J))-x(J) <= min(P,L/5).                           (S)

Proof. The bound by P is immediate, so prove the bound by L/5. Put
 v=(L+P)/5; then u_i=v+(ell_i+P-L)_+.
If some selected x_i=P, the left side is nonpositive. Otherwise set
 a_i=ell_i-x_i and omit all selected indices with a_i=0. Such omission
cannot decrease the expression
 a(J)-(ell(J)-P)_+.
For every remaining selected index, x_i<P and a_i>0 imply x_i=u_i>=v.

If P>=2L/3, no such index exists. Indeed, if ell_i+P<=L, then
 ell_i<=L-P<=v<=u_i;
if ell_i+P>L, then
 u_i=ell_i+(6P-4L)/5>=ell_i.
Thus x_i=min(ell_i,P), and either x_i=P or a_i=0, both already resolved.

If P<=2L/3 and at least two indices remain, their total hardware payment
is at least2v>=P, so the expression is again nonpositive. The only remaining
case has a single selected index i. If P<=L/2, then
 min(P,ell_i)-u_i <=P-v=(4P-L)/5<=L/5.
If P>=L/2, use u_i>=ell_i+(6P-4L)/5 to obtain
 min(P,ell_i)-u_i <=ell_i-u_i<=(4L-6P)/5<=L/5.
This proves(S), including P=L/2, P=2L/3 and L=0.

4. Feasible6/5 payments on the entire star
------------------------------------------
At O pay
 H_i:x_i, A_i:ell_i-x_i, C:c0=min(P,L/5).
Pair coverage is exact, local floors hold because x_i<=P, and total payment
is L+c0<=6L/5. Here Q=0 and all pair excesses vanish. By Section1, coverage
reduces to c0+x(J)>=min(P,ell(J)); this is precisely(S).

At U_i pay
 A_j:ell_j for every j;
 H_i:x_i, all other hardware owners:0;
 C:c_i=max(q_i,P-x_i).

The floors hold with equality. Any coalition without C is covered by its
local owners. If C is present and H_i is included, its grid contribution is
at most P, covered by x_i+c_i>=P; its selected local contribution is covered
by its local owners. If H_i is absent, all selected hardware is drawn from
the other pods, and its grid contribution is at most
min(P,L-ell_i)=q_i<=c_i. Thus every coalition is covered.

The spoke's total payment is
 L+x_i+c_i=L+max(x_i+q_i,P).
Since x_i<=u_i=(L+6P)/5-q_i,
 L+x_i+c_i <= (6/5)(L+P).
The only changing owner H_i receives the same x_i at O and U_i, so every
own-report inequality within this domain is satisfied. When P=0, x_i=c_i=0,
all profiles and all assigned payments coincide. When L=0, the central
allocation is zero and each spoke pays its productive grid amount P to C.

Therefore the worst funding factor over this unrestricted-size class of
restricted report domains is exactly6/5: the preceding construction gives
the upper, and the existing balanced two-pod three-profile certificate gives
the matching lower. This does not define or certify payments away from the
star. In particular, independent star constructions need not agree when two
stars share a profile. A stronger lower must use different base structure,
interacting report changes, or constraints that link such stars globally.

5. Directional conclusion
------------------------
The pointwise static program is already efficient for singleton owners.
The6/5 obstruction is genuinely incentive-induced, but repeating its one-step
hardware spokes at arbitrary size does not strengthen it. Section1 identifies
the missing general coupling: hardware incentives must coexist with the
disjoint-set grid constraints c+x(J)+e(K), including the residual contribution
r(K) of paired owners. A general continuous rule must control those residual
terms while keeping the grid's own payment monotone. No such6/5 rule is claimed
here, and no further arbitrary piecewise ansatz was promoted.

6. Rigidity forced by any putative continuous6/5 rule
---------------------------------------------------
For every lambda>0, restrict an all-profile6/5 mechanism to the five-owner
two-pod star ell1=ell2=P=lambda. The existing14-row lower certificate is a
positive combination of inequalities with exact lower6/5. Consequently every
row in its support must be tight. This yields an exact necessary condition:

 At O:
  A1=A2=C=2lambda/5, H1=H2=3lambda/5.
 At U1:
  A1=A2=lambda, H1=3lambda/5, C+H2=lambda.
 At U2:
  A1=A2=lambda, H2=3lambda/5, C+H1=lambda.

Direct derivation: the spoke budgets, the two local floors, and the coalition
C+unchanged-hardware force each changing hardware payment <=3lambda/5.
Own-H monotonicity transfers those bounds to O. Its three pivotal coalition
constraints and its budget force their sum >=6lambda/5, hence both are equal
to3lambda/5 and every used budget/coalition inequality is tight. At O the
three coalition equalities imply A1=A2=C; its budget then gives2lambda/5.
At each spoke, equality forces both local floors to bind and the displayed
grid-plus-other-hardware sum to equal lambda.

This holds without assuming that the mechanism is symmetric, homogeneous,
continuous, piecewise linear, or representable by an optimization problem.
It is a genuine all-mechanism necessary restriction on the original real
report domain. It does not fix the individual C/other-hardware split at a
spoke and does not by itself construct or exclude a global6/5 mechanism.
