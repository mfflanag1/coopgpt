C^2 FUNDING WHEN A HARDWARE OWNER COVERS THE FULL OPPOSING POOL
16 October 2026. Candidate theorem for independent review.

Use the fixed-actual-owner physical model, exponential primitive Phi, nested
raw rule N, and actual whole-owner envelope E defined in
UNIVERSAL_RETENTION_AND_OUTER_DOMINANCE_THEOREM.txt. In particular
  H_i=sum_j h_ij, H=sum_i H_i, P=sum_i p_i,
  M=sum_j min(sum_i h_ij,sum_i l_ij), F=min(H,P+M),
  z_i=sum_j Phi_i(h_.j,l_.j), Z=sum_i z_i, B=P+Z,
  c=1/(2e), C=1+c, b_i=p_i+z_i,
  N_i=Phi_i(Hvector,bvector),
  E_i=sup_{whole-owner report_i'<=report_i}N_i(report_i',report_-i).
Split full local slack CM-Z into Delta_- from branches with capped long
ratio at most2 and Delta_+ from branches with ratio above2. All actual
owners, eligibility, and report domains remain fixed. Owner and branch
counts, grid capacity P, and local ratios are unrestricted.

THEOREM.
Suppose at the evaluated full profile there exists an actual owner k with
  H_k>=B>0.                                             (DH)
Then every whole-owner underreport is allowed, including reports violating
(DH) and reports that switch the outer short pool, and
  sum_i E_i<=CB+(20/21)Delta_-+Delta_+
    =C^2 F-Cc P-(C-20/21)Delta_--c Delta_+<=C^2 F.       (DH_bound)
The mechanism E is the same everywhere-defined truthful envelope, not a
rule whose domain is restricted to(DH). Condition(DH) is imposed only on
the profile at which its funding guarantee is evaluated.

This class is not contained in the earlier outer-ratio-at-least2 class:
the full capped hardware ratio can be anywhere in[1,n], including strictly
below2. The result needs neither H>=CB nor zero grid capacity. It is a
funding theorem for this specified nested envelope; it is not an optimal
subsidy lower bound against other mechanisms.

1. PHYSICAL POWER BOTTLENECK AND THE ABSENCE OF OPPOSING CAPS.
Local coverage gives Z>=M, so(DH) implies H>=B=P+Z>=P+M and hence F=P+M.
For any whole-owner underreport, write resulting local payments z_j^i and
local output M^i. Every primitive recipient payment is at most its local
output, so z_j^i<=M^i<=M for every actual owner j. Thus its opposing account
is bounded by
  p_j^i+z_j^i<=P+M<=H.                                  (DH_nocap)
To upper-bound the deviating owner's outer payment, restore only its outer
hardware argument to its original H_i, keeping all computed local accounts
unchanged. This can only increase its payment by primitive own monotonicity.
The restored full hardware vector is fixed throughout the comparison.
All opposing accounts in the full and counterfactual profiles are at most H.
This remains true on any straight interpolation between those account
vectors. Thus when the hardware pool is short, opposing caps never affect
the payment formula; it depends only on opposing total and own account.

2. TOTAL-UP CHANGES: A LEMMA VALID THROUGH THE OUTER POOL TIE.
Hold the full hardware vector fixed, let t denote the opposing total and b
the recipient's own opposing account, and write a=H_i. Assume every opposing
account is at most H. Let W(t,b) denote the primitive payment to this owner.
For t<=H,
  W(t,b)=(1-q(t))b+q(t)min(a,t),
  q(t)=exp(1-sum_j min(H_j,t)/t)/2.
On a cap cell put r=sum_j min(H_j,t)/t and m=# of capped hardware accounts.
Then q'=q(r-m)/t and W_b=1-q. If a<t,
  W_t=q'(a-b)<=q(r-m)<=qr<=1/2<=W_b.
If a>=t, at least one hardware account is capped and
  W_t=q+q'(t-b)<=q[1+r-m]<=qr<=1/2<=W_b.
Negative values of W_t cause no problem.

For t>=H, (DH_nocap) gives the hardware-short expression
  W(t,b)=a+q(t)(b-a), q(t)=exp(1-t/H)/2,
  W_b=q, W_t=q(a-b)/H<=q=W_b, W_t<=1/2.
At t=H the two expressions agree, including the endpoint value. Hence on
both sides of every tie/cap boundary,
  W_t<=W_b and W_t<=1/2, while W_b>=0.                   (DH_derivatives)

Suppose an underreport increases opposing total by G>0 and decreases its
own opposing account by D>=0. Interpolate the actual opposing vectors, so
t increases at rate G and own b decreases at rate D; their values remain
feasible and (DH_nocap) holds throughout. If G<=D, (DH_derivatives) gives
  dW/ds=W_t G-W_b D<=0.
If G>D and W_t>=0, the derivative is at most W_t(G-D)<=(G-D)/2; if W_t<0,
the derivative is already nonpositive. Therefore the TOTAL-UP gain is
  W(after)-W(full)<=(G-D)_+/2.                           (DH_up)
This proof expressly permits crossing t=H and does not assume W_b>=1/2
on the hardware-short side.

For a physical own underreport, let eta_i=p_i-p_i'>=0. Then
  G=(Z^i-Z)-eta_i,
  D=(z_i-z_i^i)+eta_i>=0,
so G-D=(Z^i-Z)-(z_i-z_i^i)-2eta_i. The universal total-increase lemma
gives the aggregate bound
  sum_i[(Z^i-Z)-(z_i-z_i^i)]_+<=2Delta_+.
Consequently all TOTAL-UP gains together cost at most Delta_+.

3. TOTAL-DOWN CHANGES WITH A DOMINANT HARDWARE OWNER.
Now t<=B. Condition(DH) guarantees at least one capped hardware account
for EVERY such total, so m>=1. Fix full own account b0 in[0,B] and define
the largest feasible retention-compatible comparison account
  b_*(t)=min(t,max(0,b0-(B-t)/3)), 0<=t<=B.
We claim W(t,b_*(t)) is nondecreasing in t. It is continuous and piecewise
smooth; a derivative check suffices on its cap and clipping cells.

On a cell where b_*=0, the derivative is q'min(a,t)+q*1_{a>=t}>=0.
On a cell where b_*=t, it is
  1-q+q'[min(a,t)-t]+q*1_{a>=t}
    >=1-q-q(r-m)>=1-qr>=1/2.
On its remaining cells b_*'=1/3 and 0<=b_*<=t, so the derivative is
  (1-q)/3+q'[min(a,t)-b_*]+q*1_{a>=t}
    >=(1-q)/3-q(r-m)
    >=[1-q(3r-2)]/3>0.
For the last strict inequality, q(r)(3r-2) is maximized on r>=1 at r=5/3,
with value(3/2)exp(-2/3)<1; exp(2/3)>1+2/3>3/2 proves this exactly.
Thus the claim holds, including all boundaries by continuity.

For an arbitrary total-down counterfactual with total t and own account b,
define epsilon=[(B-t)/3-(b0-b)]_+. Then
  b<=b_*(t)+epsilon.
Indeed the inequality before clipping is b<=b0-(B-t)/3+epsilon, while
0<=b<=t handles the two clipping cases. At fixed t the derivative W_b=1-q
is at most1, so
  W(t,b)<=W(t,b_*(t))+epsilon<=W(B,b0)+epsilon.           (DH_down)
This is an exact retention transfer with coefficient1; it requires no
quadratic account-excess term or outer-slack estimate.

For each physical own underreport,
  epsilon_i=[(Z-Z^i)/3-(z_i-z_i^i)-(2/3)eta_i]_+.
Universal local retention therefore yields
  sum_i epsilon_i<=(20/21)Delta_-.
All TOTAL-DOWN gains together cost at most this amount.

4. FUNDING AND SHARPNESS FOR THE FIXED RULE.
The full outer raw total is at most CB because H>=B. Sum(DH_up) over
total-up owners and(DH_down) over the remaining owners. The resulting bound
is uniform over the independently selected actual own underreports, and
taking their separate suprema proves the first inequality in(DH_bound).
Using Z=CM-Delta_--Delta_+ and F=P+M gives its displayed identity. All
saving coefficients are positive because C>1>20/21 and c>0.

Sharpness follows from the four-owner/two-branch saturation example:
owners A,B,D,W, with W local power1 on both branches and no grid;
branch1 hardware A=1+2c,B=1; branch2 hardware A=1,D=1+2c.
Both local ratios are2, Z=2C, H_A=2+2c=Z, and the other hardware totals
sum to Z. Thus(DH) holds, outer ratio is2, and raw total is C^2F. Since
E>=N and(DH_bound) gives the same upper bound, E=N in total and the bound
is sharp. This is sharpness of the particular envelope, not an impossibility
theorem for the underlying game, which has a veto power owner.

The remaining energy-short frontier has H_i<B for EVERY owner and full
capped hardware ratio below2. The hardware-short frontier H<B is also not
covered by(DH), and requires separate analysis of opposing-account caps.
