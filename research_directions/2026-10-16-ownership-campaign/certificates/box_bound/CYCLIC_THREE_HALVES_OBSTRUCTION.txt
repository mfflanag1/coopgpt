RECIPIENT BOXES DO NOT ADMIT A UNIFORM THREE-HALVES FUNDING BOUND
16 October 2026. Analytic mechanism obstruction, with exact rational certificate.

STATUS AND SCOPE
The proposed inequality sum_i Rbox_i <= (3/2) F is false. The family below
has arbitrarily many active actual owners and active branches. For every
integer n>=3, its ratio is the same explicitly defined number G and satisfies
  G > 15001/10000 > 3/2.
This is an obstruction to this particular box mechanism. It is NOT an
all-mechanism lower bound, an incentive impossibility, a claim about the actual
nested envelope E, or a novelty/priority claim. The existing uniform 7/4 bound
survives. The small strict margin is certified with rational arithmetic.

DEFINITIONS
For nonnegative vectors a,b, write A=sum a, B=sum b, T=min(A,B).
If T=0, Phi=0. If A<=B, put
  q=(1/2) exp(1-sum_i min(b_i,T)/T),
  Phi_i(a,b)=(1-q)a_i+q min(b_i,T).
For B<=A interchange a and b. The formulas agree at A=B.

At a physical profile, local payments are z_i=sum_j Phi_i(h_.j,l_.j).
Let H_i=sum_j h_ij, H=sum_i H_i, P=sum_i p_i,
M=sum_j min(sum_i h_ij,sum_i l_ij), F=min(H,P+M), beta_i=p_i+z_i.
The raw nested rule is N_i=Phi_i(Hvector,p+z).
For fixed i, the exact individual counterfactual opponent endpoints are
  m_k^i=min_{own-i underreports} z_k, M_k^i=max_{own-i underreports} z_k.
The owner-specific box consists of vectors c satisfying
  p_i<=c_i<=beta_i,
  p_k+m_k^i<=c_k<=p_k+M_k^i (k!=i),
  sum c<=K=P+Lbar.
Here Lbar is the exact monotone local-total ceiling. On a branch with short
total T and capped long-pool ratio rho, its contribution is
  T[1+(u-1) exp(1-u)/2], u=min(rho,2).
The box rule is Rbox_i=max_c Phi_i(Hvector,c) on this domain.
Only these definitions and the direct optimizer argument below are needed.

THE CYCLIC FAMILY
Choose
  r=19/10, s=83/40,
  q=exp(1-r)/2,
  a=r q, b=1-q, z=a+b=1+(r-1)q.
There are n hardware owners 0,...,n-1 and one local-power owner w, with
n branches indexed cyclically. All shared-power reports p_i are zero.
On branch j, hardware owner j reports
  h_jj=s-r+1=47/40,
and owner j+1 (indices modulo n) reports
  h_(j+1),j=r-1=9/10.
All other hardware accounts vanish. Owner w supplies exactly one unit of
local power on every branch; all other local-power accounts vanish.
All n+1 owners and all n branches are active. Ownership is fixed; no owner is
split, merged, introduced, or removed inside an incentive comparison.

Each branch has hardware total s and local-power total1. Its capped hardware
ratio is 1+(r-1)=r, because 47/40>1 and 9/10<1. Consequently the hardware
payments on the branch are q and (r-1)q, while w receives b. Each hardware
owner has total hardware s and total local payment a. Owner w has hardware0
and local payment nb. Therefore
  H=ns, M=F=n, z_vector=(a,...,a,nb), sum z_i=nz.
Since 1<r<2, the exact monotone local-total ceiling of each branch equals its
current total z. Hence K=nz.

The rational certificate below verifies
  0<a<s, 0<b<z<s, and s<3b.
Thus, for every n>=3, all hardware accounts s are below both nb and nz.
Put
  q0=exp(1-s/z)/2, q1=exp(1-s/b)/2.
The full-profile nested hardware payment is
  N_i=(1-q0)a+q0 s=a+(s-a)q0.

EXACT BOX PAYMENTS OF THE HARDWARE OWNERS
For a hardware owner i, its own opposing ceiling is beta_i=a<s. Every
feasible c has total T=sum c<=K=nz<H=ns, so only the short-opposing regime
is possible. Its payment is
  c_i+q(T)(min(s,T)-c_i),
where q(T)=exp(1-n min(s,T)/T)/2 for T>0 (zero total gives zero payment).
At fixed T this is increasing in c_i. If T<a, it is at most T<=a. If T>=a,
using c_i<=a gives an upper bound
  a+q(T)(min(s,T)-a).
Both q(T) and min(s,T)-a are nonnegative and nondecreasing on T>=a, so this
bound is at most a+q(K)(s-a). The full profile c=z_vector is feasible,
has c_i=a and T=K, and attains this value. Therefore, exactly,
  Rbox_i=a+(s-a)q0   (each hardware owner i).
This reasoning uses the actual recipient boxes only through feasibility of
the full vector and their own/total ceilings; it does not assume simultaneous
attainability of different recipients' extrema.

EXACT BOX PAYMENT OF THE LOCAL-POWER OWNER
Owner w can suppress all its local power. At that actual report all local
payments vanish, so every opponent lower endpoint m_i^w is zero. Thus the
auxiliary vector c*=(0,...,0,nb) is box-feasible: its own coordinate is beta_w,
its opponent coordinates are their exact lower endpoints, and nb<K=nz.
At this vector the short opposing total is nb; all hardware accounts satisfy
s<nb, so Phi_w(Hvector,c*)=nb(1-q1).

This value is the maximum. For total T<=beta_w, the best possible own
coordinate is at most T; its resulting payment is at most
  T(1-q(T)),
which is nondecreasing in T. Indeed it is linear while T<=s; for T>=s its
derivative is 1-exp(1-ns/T)(1+ns/T)/2, nonnegative because ns/T>=1 and
(1+x)exp(1-x)<=2 for x>=1. For T>=beta_w, the payment is at most
beta_w(1-q(T)), which is nonincreasing in T. Therefore T=beta_w is optimal,
and exactly
  Rbox_w=nb(1-q1).

SUM AND STRICT INEQUALITY
Dividing the sum by F=n gives, for every n>=3,
  G = z + (s-a) exp(1-s/z)/2 - b exp(1-s/b)/2.
The numerical value G approximately1.500118553069121 is descriptive only.
The strict assertion follows from the exact-rational enclosure procedure:

For x>=0 let S_d(x)=sum_(j=0)^d (-x)^j/j!.
Taylor's remainder gives S_25(x)<=exp(-x)<=S_24(x).
First enclose q using x=r-1=9/10 and divide these bounds by2. Round outward
only to rational multiples of10^(-15), obtaining
  203284829870299/10^15 <= q <= 203284829870300/10^15.
Let the resulting intervals be aL<=a<=aU, bL<=b<=bU, zL<=z<=zU,
using a=rq, b=1-q, z=1+(r-1)q. A rigorous lower bound for G is
  gL = zL +(s-aU) S_25(s/zL-1)/2 - bU S_24(s/bU-1)/2,
and an upper bound is
  gU = zU +(s-aL) S_24(s/zU-1)/2 - bL S_25(s/bL-1)/2.
All exponential arguments are nonnegative; all multiplying coefficients are
positive. The formula therefore follows directly by monotonicity and interval
arithmetic, without a floating-point sign decision. Exact Fraction arithmetic
verifies gL>15001/10000. Further outward rounding gives
  1.500118553069120 <= G <= 1.500118553069123.
The script check_cyclic_obstruction.py performs these rational inequalities
under Python -O, using explicit exceptions rather than assert statements.
Its output is cyclic-obstruction-certificate.json.

WHAT COUPLING IS MISSING?
The counterexample is not merely caused by combining incompatible OPPONENT
endpoints. For w, the all-zero opponent vector is jointly attainable: delete
all local power. The difficulty is retaining w's FULL local-payment ceiling
nb while selecting those zero opponent responses. This pairs its own response
from one report with opponents' responses from a different report.
Consequently imposing only exact joint opponent feasibility would not remove
this witness. One must also couple the own local account to the opponent
responses, or otherwise change the mechanism.

A GENERAL RESPONSE-COUPLING LEMMA
Let i have no hardware account. Suppose every branch on which i owns local
power has at most k positive hardware-owning recipients, where k>=1. At any
profile, and throughout i's whole local-report box, its actual local responses
obey
  sum_(t!=i) z_t >= kappa_k z_i,
  kappa_k = k/[2 exp(k-1)-1] >0.
There may be arbitrarily many branches and actual owners. Branches on which
i has no local power contribute only nonnegative opponent payments. Additional
opponent local-power ownership is allowed.

Proof. It suffices to prove the claim branchwise. A zero-production branch is
trivial. If local power is the short pool, let T be its total and rho the capped
hardware ratio. Then1<=rho<=k and q=exp(1-rho)/2. Owner i receives at most
(1-q)T; the opponents' hardware roles alone total rho q T. Thus their payments
are at least [rho q/(1-q)] times i's payment. The function
  f(x)=x/[2 exp(x-1)-1]
is decreasing on x>=1, since its derivative has numerator
2 exp(x-1)(1-x)-1<0. Hence f(rho)>=f(k)=kappa_k. If hardware is the short pool,
the opponents' hardware roles total (1-q)T, while i receives at most qT.
Because q<=1/2, the ratio is at least1>=kappa_k. Sum over branches. No claim
of simultaneous endpoint attainability is used. QED.

For the cyclic family k=2, so every actual report of w satisfies
  sum_hardware z_i >= [2/(2e-1)] z_w.
The box witness (0,...,0,nb) violates this strict coupling. It supplies a
concrete additional constraint needed to exclude this entire counterfamily.
The lemma alone does not prove a general3/2 funding theorem for a revised
mechanism, and its constant tends to zero with k. It therefore does not close
the unrestricted problem with growing hardware support per branch.

THE ACTUAL ENVELOPE IS SMALLER ON THIS FAMILY
The coupling has a quantitative consequence beyond excluding one witness.
Put kappa=2/(2e-1). Under any actual report of w, let c be its local payment
and T the total local payment. Then c<=nb and T>=(1+kappa)c. The outer
hardware accounts are unchanged, and q(T) is nondecreasing. Hence its actual
nested payment is at most c[1-q((1+kappa)c)]. This expression is nondecreasing
for 0<=c<=nb: before (1+kappa)c=s its exponential coefficient is constant;
afterwards the derivative is 1-exp(1-x)(1+x)/2>=0 with
x=ns/[(1+kappa)c]>1. The strict regime condition (1+kappa)b<s is verified
in the exact certificate. Therefore
  E_w <= nb[1-exp(1-s/((1+kappa)b))/2].
Each hardware envelope equals its full nested payment because
N_i<=E_i<=Rbox_i=N_i. Consequently, on every member of this cyclic family,
  sum E_i/F <= z+(s-a) exp(1-s/z)/2
                   -b exp(1-s/((1+kappa)b))/2 < 1401/1000.
The last strict inequality is also certified by Fraction/Taylor arithmetic
in check_cyclic_obstruction.py. This is a bound for this specific unbounded
family, not a general funding theorem. It proves the displayed >3/2 failure
belongs to the box relaxation rather than to the actual nested envelope.

AUXILIARY NULL-OWNER INVARIANCE
At an evaluated profile, adjoining an actual owner with every account zero
leaves Rbox payments of all existing owners unchanged and gives the new owner
zero. Every primitive local vector is extended by zero; its capped ratios,
local-total ceiling, and each existing recipient's underreport domain and
counterfactual endpoints are unchanged. The new opponent interval is[0,0],
so each old optimization domain and objective is exactly its original domain
with one forced-zero coordinate. For the new recipient its own hardware and
opposing ceiling are zero, hence its primitive payment is zero. This argument
also covers an originally single-owner profile using the defining optimization.
It is profile-wise null-owner invariance, not a merger or false-name claim.
