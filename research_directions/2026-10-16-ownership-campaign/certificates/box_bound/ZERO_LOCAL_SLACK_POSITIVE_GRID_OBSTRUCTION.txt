ZERO LOCAL SLACK DOES NOT ELIMINATE INCENTIVE GAIN WITH POSITIVE GRID
16 October 2026. Exact structural obstruction for the nested exponential rule.

This refutes the proposed proof principle that every gain from own withholding
can be charged only to local primitive budget slack. It does NOT refute the
C^2 funding conjecture, and it is NOT an all-mechanism subsidy lower bound.
The example has a veto owner; its purpose is to identify a necessary missing
term in a global proof. Both active owner and branch counts grow.

For every integer r>=1 take r branches and2r+1 FIXED actual owners. One
owner W has local power1 in every branch and grid capacity r. It has no
hardware. In branch j, two distinct hardware-only owners each have hardware1
in that branch and zero elsewhere. All coordinates not stated are zero.
Every owner is active; no owner is introduced, removed, or split across reports.
Put c=1/(2e), C=1+c.

Each full local branch has output1 and capped long ratio2, so its primitive
payments are1-c to W and c to each of its two hardware owners. Therefore
  M=r, Z=Cr, Delta_local=CM-Z=0,
  H=2r, P=r, B=P+Z=(1+C)r, F=min(H,P+M)=2r.
The outer hardware pool is short, and all opposing accounts are below H:
W's opposing account is(2-c)r<2r and each other account is c. Its outer
coefficient is
  q_0=(1/2)exp(1-B/H)=(1/2)exp(-c/2).
Thus the full raw payment to W is
  N_W(full)=(r/2)(2-c)exp(-c/2).                        (ZL_full)

Let the SAME owner W underreport its local power from1 to t in every branch,
retaining its full grid report r and its zero hardware. All other owners'
reports remain fixed. For0<t<=1, each local ratio remains exactly2 because
each of the two hardware capacities is capped at t. Hence
  z_W(t)=r(1-c)t, each hardware payment=ct,
  Z(t)=Crt, B(t)=r(1+Ct).
For t in[1/C,1], hardware remains the short outer pool. Every opposing
account stays below H, so the TRUE raw payment is exactly
  N_W(t)=(r/2)exp(1-(1+Ct)/2)[1+(1-c)t].                (ZL_t)
Its derivative divided by the positive factor
r exp(1-(1+Ct)/2)/2 is
  (1-c)-(C/2)[1+(1-c)t].
This is decreasing in t and equals -c at t=1/C. It is strictly negative
on the entire interval[1/C,1]. In particular, at the full report it is
  -c(3-c)/2<0.
Thus even arbitrarily small binding local underreports strictly increase
W's raw payment while the full local slack is identically zero.

At t=1/C the OUTER pools tie, the outer coefficient is1/2, and
  N_W(1/C)=r/C>N_W(full).                              (ZL_gain)
The exact positive gain is
  r[1/C-(2-c)exp(-c/2)/2].
Strict positivity follows from the derivative proof and needs no floating
approximation or special-function inequality. The primitive is continuous
at the pool tie, so the endpoint calculation agrees from both orientations.

CONSEQUENCE.
No finite coefficient A can make
  sum_i(E_i-N_i)<=A(CM-Z)
hold for every physical profile of this nested rule: the right side is zero
here and E_W-N_W>0. The same obstruction applies to any per-owner assertion
that zero local slack makes N own-report monotone. A successful global
C^2 funding proof must also use outer slack, physical-value slack, or an
equivalent coupling term. The previously proved outer-dominance theorem is
unaffected: this family has H/B=2/(1+C)<1, far outside its required full
outer capped ratio at least2. The actual-envelope conjecture itself remains
unresolved by this example.
