MODEL A: STATIC REDUCTIONS AND A SPLIT-OWNER INCENTIVE CERTIFICATE
16 October 2026. Independent exact/static lane; conventional arguments below.
No novelty or universal finite-LP upper claim.

1. UNIVERSAL STATIC BOUND FOR ARBITRARY BLOCKS
Use the contract's notation ell_j=min(h_j,L_j), ell_a=sum_{j in J_a}ell_j,
L=sum ell_a, Q=sum(h_j-ell_j), R=min(P,Q), T=L+R, D=(P-Q)_+.
Deleting local owner A_a changes its marginal by exactly M_a=(ell_a-D)_+.
Let E=max_a ell_a. If E=0 all local floors vanish; use the efficient baseline.
Otherwise set
 lambda=max_a theta M_a/ell_a = theta(1-D/E)_+,
where zero ell_a terms are assigned zero. This lies in[0,1].

Two allocations cover every coalition:
 (i) A_a=0, C=R, H_j=ell_j, the original efficient baseline.
 (ii) A_a=ell_a, C=P, H_j=0, the energy-only allocation.
For (ii), a coalition with local-owner subset B and grid indicator g has
production <=sum_{a in B}ell_a+gP; if no hardware is present it is zero.
Thus its designated energy payments cover it. For the baseline, write I for the
selected hardware and u<=ell(I) for its selected local contribution. With C its
worth u+min(P,h(I)-u) is nondecreasing in u, hence at most
ell(I)+min(P,h(I)-ell(I))<=ell(I)+R. Without C its worth is at most ell(I).

Their convex mixture with weight lambda on (ii) covers every coalition and
pays A_a=lambda ell_a>=theta M_a. Its total is
 T+lambda(P-R).
If P<=Q, the extra term is zero. If P>=Q+L, then D>=L>=E and lambda=0.
In the remaining range Q<P<Q+L, write D=P-Q. Then T=L+Q and extra funding is
 theta D(1-D/E)_+ <=theta E/4<=theta T/4.
Consequently the single-profile minimum B_theta always satisfies
 T<=B_theta<=(1+theta/4)T for arbitrary block and pod counts.
This construction is STATIC: report-dependent lambda can violate incentives.
The merged-owner balanced even-pod family from the original proof supplies
sharpness over the class permitting one local block. Thus any failure of the
5/4 target under splitting must come from cross-profile incentives, not a
higher worst-case static funding requirement.

2. EXACT STATIC EFFICIENCY WHEN EACH LOCAL OWNER HAS ONE POD
Suppose J_j={j}. At theta=1 define
 a_j=(ell_j-D)_+,
 x_j=min(ell_j,D),
 c=R=min(P,Q).
Then a_j+x_j=ell_j, a_j=M_j, and total is L+R=T.
We prove every coalition is covered, for arbitrary real capacities and any
number of pods. A coalition is described by local set B, hardware set I,
and presence/absence of C. Without C, its worth is ell(B intersection I),
covered because a_j+x_j=ell_j for j in B intersection I.

If C is present and P<=Q, then D=0, x=0, a_j=ell_j, c=P. Its payment is
at least ell(B intersection I)+P, which bounds its worth.
If C is present and P>Q, write J=I minus B and r(I)=sum_{i in I}(h_i-ell_i).
The coalition payment is at least
 Q+ell(B intersection I)+sum_{j in J}min(ell_j,D).
Its worth is
 ell(B intersection I)+min(P,r(I)+ell(J)).
Since Q>=r(I), P=Q+D and
 sum_{j in J}min(ell_j,D)>=min(ell(J),D),
we have
 Q+sum_J min(ell_j,D)>=min(Q+D,r(I)+ell(J)),
by considering ell(J)<=D or ell(J)>=D. This proves coverage.
All terms vanish at T=0. For 0<=theta<=1, mix this static allocation with
the efficient theta0 baseline; it pays a_j=theta M_j and remains efficient.
Therefore B_theta(b)=T(b) pointwise on the entire singleton-local domain.
This static allocation is not claimed own-H monotone; the next certificate
shows no globally efficient rule can satisfy the full theta1 requirements.

3. THREE-PROFILE, FIVE-FIXED-OWNER INCENTIVE LOWER
Two singleton local owners A1,A2, one shared grid owner C, and hardware owners
H1,H2. All resources are positive at the following profiles, in order
(P,h1,h2,L1,L2):
 O=(1,1,1,1,1), T_O=2;
 U=(1,2,1,1,1), T_U=3;
 V=(1,1,2,1,1), T_V=3.
Only H1 changes O->U and only H2 changes O->V. Owner identities stay fixed.
Let payments at O be (a,b,c,x,y), and let an eligible rule have total <=B*T
at all three profiles. At O the following three coalition constraints hold:
 a+b+x+y>=2                 [A1,A2,H1,H2],
 a+c+x+y>=2                 [A1,C,H1,H2],
 b+c+x+y>=2                 [A2,C,H1,H2].
Their sum is 2*total_O+x+y>=6. Thus
 x+y>=6-4B.
At U, both local marginal floors are theta, and C+H2 has worth1. Consequently
 x_U<=3B-1-2theta.
At V, the same floors and C+H1 give y_V<=3B-1-2theta.
Own-H monotonicity gives x<=x_U and y<=y_V. Combining,
 6-4B<=x+y<=6B-2-4theta,
so every all-profile rule satisfies
 B >= (4+2theta)/5.
At theta1 this is 6/5. By Section2, B_theta=T at every profile, so the same
6/5 is a genuine incentive premium over pointwise-static funding, without
any static instability or floor cost. This is a finite-profile lower bound
valid against unrestricted mechanisms on this ownership subclass, not a
continuous upper or a sharpness theorem for that subclass.

The exact 14-row nonnegative dual certificate found by the independent finite LP
is simply the preceding calculation divided by10:
 - budget O weight1/5;
 - each of its three coalition inequalities weight1/10;
 - the two own-H transition inequalities weight1/10 each;
 - budget U and budget V weight1/10 each;
 - C+other-hardware coalition at U/V weight1/10 each;
 - the two local floor inequalities at each U/V weight1/10 each.
In <= convention, summing these rows cancels every payment and gives
 -B <= -(4+2theta)/5.
The saved theta1 LP reports exact coefficient -1 and RHS -6/5.

4. ACTIVE-COUNT EXTENSION, WITH AN EXPLICIT LIMITATION
For any k>=1 append k new singleton local owners and k new hardware owners,
each reporting epsilon>0 at a private new pod in all three profiles.
Keep P=1 and the two original-pod reports O,U,V unchanged. Put z=k epsilon.
Every actual owner and resource is positive, and extra pods have positive local
production. Grand values become 2+z,3+z,3+z; Section2 still gives static funding
exactly equal to these values.
At O, the original three coalition constraints are unchanged. Since the original
five payments are <=the full budget, they give
 x+y>=6-2B(2+z).
At U and V, Q=1,D=0, so every local floor equals its ell value: their sum is
2+z. The grid+unchanged-original-hardware coalition still requires1, hence
 x_U,y_V <= B(3+z)-(3+z).
These yield
 B >= (6+z)/(5+2z).
Taking epsilon=k^(-2), so z=1/k, proves a family with unbounded active owner
and pod counts whose lower approaches6/5. This uses vanishing aggregate added
mass and is honestly an active-padding extension of the five-owner obstruction;
it is not a new growing-complexity lower construction or a bound above5/4.

5. ONE-POD AND THETA0 CALIBRATIONS
At one pod set ell=min(h,L), G=min(P,h), R=min(P,h-ell), T=ell+R,
M=T-G=min(L,(h-P)_+). The efficient truthful theta1 rule
 A=M, H=min(ell,G)=min(h,L,P), C=G-min(ell,G)=(G-ell)_+
has total M+G=T. Own-A monotonicity follows from M=min(L,(h-P)_+);
own-H from min(h,L,P); own-C from (min(P,h)-ell)_+ with h,L fixed.
C+H receives G. If G<=ell then A+H=M+G=T>=ell; if G>=ell then
A+H=M+ell>=ell. Other proper coalitions have zero worth. This proves all-profile
efficiency, not merely the finite calibration.
A fixed mixture with the theta0 baseline handles every theta in[0,1].
For arbitrary blocks theta0, baseline(A=0,C=R,H_j=ell_j) is efficient and
own-report monotone, as specified by the contract.

7. A FALSIFIED SINGLETON 6/5 CONSTRUCTIVE ANSATZ
This is an archival failure, not a theorem candidate. Let beta=6/5,
s_j=(P-Q+r_j)_+, and try
 x_j=min(ell_j,s_j,beta ell_j s_j/L),
 a_j=max((ell_j-D)_+,ell_j-x_j),
 c=min(G,beta T-sum_j(a_j+x_j)).
This enforces pair coverage and the intended budget but does NOT cover every
grid coalition. Exact finite falsification, recorded in
probe-singleton-six-fifths-m3.json and reproduced by
probe_singleton_six_fifths.py:

 Here L=T=5,Q=0,x=(12,24,24)/25,a=(13,26,26)/25,c=1.
 Coalition {C,H2} has worth2 but payment1+24/25=49/25, a deficit1/25.
Thus the hoped-for 6/5 rule needs shape-sensitive grid coverage; simply scaling
the merged hardware formula and charging the remaining budget to C fails.
