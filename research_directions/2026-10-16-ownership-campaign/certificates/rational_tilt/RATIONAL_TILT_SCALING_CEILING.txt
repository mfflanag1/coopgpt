EXACT SCALING CEILING FOR THE RATIONAL BALANCE TILT
16 October2026. Corollary of the independently proposed rational-tilt proof.

Let f be the function in RATIONAL_BALANCE_TILT_THEOREM.txt, and replace its
prefactor by h_a(s,t)=1/2+a[f(t)-f(s)] for a>=0, leaving the water product
and every actual owner unchanged. Then the mechanism is truthful and
coalition covering for0<=a<=1, and fails own-coordinate monotonicity for
every a>1. Its best uniform funding factor over truthful nonnegative a is
the gamma in the rational-tilt theorem, attained at a=1.

SUFFICIENCY. Payments are affine in a. At a=0 they are the reviewed
half-prefactor water rule, and at a=1 they are the rational-tilt rule.
Each intermediate mechanism is their convex combination, preserving
nonnegativity, every coalition inequality, and every whole-owner incentive.

NECESSITY. Use four pure actual owners at a balanced profile: two short
reports s,1-s and two long reports L,1-L, with2/3<s<L<1. Increase the
largest short owner's report toward s from below. On a sufficiently small
interval before balance, only L is an active actual long cap, the auxiliary
cap is inactive, and its water level tends to L. The limiting derivative
of the changing owner's payment is
  J_a(s,L)=1-[1/2+a(f(L)-f(s))](1+s/L)
                +a s(1-s)f'(s).
As L tends to1, the exact balance identity of f gives
  J_a(s,1)=(1-s)(1-a)/2.
For every a>1 choose any fixed rational s in(2/3,1), then rational L<1
sufficiently close to1 so that J_a(s,L)<0. By continuity an interval below
balance has negative derivative; two rational reports in that interval
give a binding own-report monotonicity violation on the same actual owner
set. The strict argument works even when a itself is not rational.
No finite numerical search is used in this necessity proof.

FUNDING OPTIMALITY WITHIN THIS SCALING FAMILY ONLY.
At the rational theorem's exact four-owner attainer s=s_*,t=1/2, the excess
for scale a is[1-2a f(s_*)]F(s_*), which decreases with a. Every truthful
nonnegative a is at most1, so its worst funding factor is at least the
a=1 value gamma. The a=1 rule attains the global upper bound gamma already
proved, establishing the claimed optimum for this one-dimensional family.
This does not optimize over different concentration functions or arbitrary
mechanisms, and does not narrow an unrestricted lower bound by itself.
