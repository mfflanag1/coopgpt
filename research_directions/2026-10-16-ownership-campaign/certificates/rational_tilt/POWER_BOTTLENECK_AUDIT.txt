Therefore z_j^i<=M^i<=M. Also p_j^i<=P, so every counterfactual opposing
account is at most P+M<=H at the FULL profile. Restoring only i's outer
hardware gives the full hardware vector: other owners' hardware was never
changed. Primitive own monotonicity makes this a valid upper bound on the
actual nested payment, even when the physical underreport changes bottlenecks.
Straight interpolations of these opposing vectors preserve the same per-account
cap bound. Hence the hardware-short formula with coefficient exp(1-t)/2 is
valid through every restored outer tie. No caps were dropped from profiles
where they could bind.

The full opposing accounts themselves satisfy the same claim. Thus when
beta>=hmax the full raw total is exactly1+k(beta)(beta-1), on both sides
of beta=1. The claim is an accounting consequence of the physical model,
not an assumption on arbitrary abstract opposing vectors outside that model.
