Source: mixed/outer_scalar/WEIGHTED_PRODUCTION_ROBUSTNESS_FUNDING.txt and

1. ETA AND THE ACTUAL-COALITION FLOOR
Each U_k is a ceiling on recipient k's local payment after EVERY actual owner's
underreport, evaluated relative to one full profile. It is not a marginal
contribution ceiling. The incoming half-gap bound supplies (1+z_k)/2; the
coordinate and total-output ceilings are independently valid. Taking their
minimum is safe. U_k=0 implies every counterfactual local payment is zero,
so assigning that recipient weight one causes no division or cap problem.

Every threshold set excludes the deviating actual owner. Its resources are
therefore unchanged during that underreport, and local coalition coverage gives
opponent capped mass >=pi-u_i+L_i. Because every threshold coalition is a subset
of the full owner set, 0<=L_i<=m. Thus eta=max_i(m-L_i) lies in [0,m]. No
simultaneous attainment of the weighted inequalities or independent local
technology responses is assumed.

2. CAP-SAFE POSITIVE COMPARISON
Restore the actual deviator's outer hardware, grid and own opposing coordinate
to their full values as monotonic upper comparisons. This need not be an actual
report; it is used only to upper-bound the actual envelope. Its full capped
coordinate d_i>=u_i implies total capped opposing mass at least
d_i+pi-u_i+L_i>=pi+m-eta>=S-(Z-m)-eta.
All other coordinates retain the uniform coalition floor. This retains actual
caps; it does not use the invalid uncapped complement-surplus shortcut.

For completeness I verified the global scalar majorant k(t)<=exp(1-t)/2.
It is equality for t>=1. For 0<t<1, set
h(t)=exp(1-t)+exp(1-1/t)-2. Its derivative has the sign of
exp(t-1/t)/t^2-1. The logarithm t-1/t-2log t increases to zero at one because
its derivative is (1-1/t)^2. Hence h decreases to h(1)=0, proving h>=0.
The t=0 case is the continuous limit. Even if the total lower bound is negative,
apply this majorant to the actual nonnegative total first. This gives R4 with
A=exp(epsilon+eta), where epsilon=Z-m, in the stated direction.

3. NEGATIVE SAVINGS AND ROLE BUDGET
The imported negative theorem supplies B=(4/5)exp(epsilon-cm), since its slack
is delta=cm-epsilon. Its arbitrary-overflow scope is exactly the needed one.
For the actual negative subset, a_i-d_i<=a_i-U_i^hw, and every omitted
a_i-U_i^hw is nonnegative. Thus v<=1-Z+L_power with
L_power<=min(m,1/2), yielding v<=b-epsilon, b=min(1,3/2-m).
The use of this role accounting does not strategically split an owner.
We have 0<=b-epsilon<=1 and A>=1>=B, so replacing v by b-epsilon increases
the funding upper bound. This step remains valid with full overflow.

4. FULL REAL SCALAR MAXIMIZATION
For theta=B/A=(4/5)exp(-cm-eta), direct differentiation gives the unique
maximum over r>=0 at r*=1-(1-theta)(b-epsilon)>=theta>0. Substitution yields
c*exp[eta+b-theta(b-epsilon)], including the cancellation of epsilon outside
the last term. This exponent increases with epsilon, and after epsilon=cm
it increases with eta because b-cm>=0. Setting eta=1/4 is a valid enlargement,
even when the actual eta<=m<1/4.

I rederived both m derivatives in R9. The first is positive on [0,1/2]; the
second is below -3/25 on [1/2,1]. Therefore the maximum is at m=1/2. With
x=c/2<3/32, monotonicity in x and the signed cubic exponential lower bound give
exp(-1/4-x)(1-x)>5/8. This makes the exponent strictly below 3/4 and funding
strictly below 1+exp(-1/4)/2<7/5<C^2. Equality eta=1/4, m=0, zero responses,
and arbitrarily large opposing total S are included.

5. GENERAL CERTIFIED FLOORS AND PRODUCTION RESERVE
The abstract-floor extension is valid: for any report-uniform R_i>=pi_-i,
eta=max(0,max_i[m+pi_-i-R_i]) lies in [0,m] and gives exactly the lower bound
used in Section 2. The finite cap-choice LP contains every actual counterprofile
payment vector and its objective is nonnegative above the grid baseline, so
it satisfies this premise. A maximum of independent valid floors remains valid.

Every weighted threshold set contains N\(J union {i}), since ordinary weights
equal one. Thus the stated production-reserve deletion condition is sufficient
for eta<=1/4. It is not necessary and does not merge the exceptional owners.
The at-most-five cap count in the remaining grid-tail cell is also valid using
2u_k+z_k>1 for each exceptional recipient. The floor reduction is an arithmetic
procedure over actual coalitions, not an exact envelope algorithm.
