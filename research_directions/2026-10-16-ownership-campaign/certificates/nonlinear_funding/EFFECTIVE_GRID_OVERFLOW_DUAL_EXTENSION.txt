EFFECTIVE GRID OVERFLOW IN THE POSITIVE-GAP DUAL BOUND
16 October 2026. Exact strengthening of the reviewed PD1-PD5 bounds.
Original exponential nested rule and actual whole-owner envelope only.

Normalize positive full hardware H=1, and assume physical hardware bottleneck
P+M>=1. Use z_i, a_i, d_i, S, q0 and the positive/negative/tied recipient sets
from POSITIVE_GAP_DUAL_AGGREGATION.txt. Define capped grid mass
 pi_i=min(p_i,1), pi=sum_i pi_i,
 w_i^eff=(pi_i+z_i-1)_+, W_+^eff=sum_{i:d_i>a_i}w_i^eff.
For every nonnegative z, min(p_i+z,1)=min(pi_i+z,1). In particular
                        sum_i w_i^eff=pi+Z-S.          (1)

CLAIM
The positive bounds PD1 and PD2 hold with w_i^eff and W_+^eff in place of the
raw overflow w_i=(p_i+z_i-1)_+ and W_+. The same replacement is valid in PD5,
without changing any negative-gap saving term. There is no assumption P<=1.

PROOF
Fix one actual owner's arbitrary whole-bundle underreport. Recompute the local
payments, and perform exactly the outer hardware/grid restoration used in PD1.
Let z_i' be the resulting own local payment, D_i=z_i-z_i'>=0, and
 d_i'=min(p_i+z_i',1)=min(pi_i+z_i',1), x_i=d_i-d_i'>=0,
 w_i^{eff,'}=(pi_i+z_i'-1)_+.
Applying the identity min(v,1)=v-(v-1)_+ to the two restored own accounts gives
           D_i=x_i+w_i^eff-w_i^{eff,'}<=x_i+w_i^eff.    (2)
Also D_i<=z_i as before. In particular when p_i>=1, d_i=d_i'=1 and x_i=0,
while w_i^eff=z_i, so (2) reduces to the valid D_i<=z_i. No size bound on the
original grid report is required. Equality, zero local payments, and zero
underreported local output are all included.

The sharp outgoing theorem therefore bounds the capped-total reduction B by
       B<=x_i+K z_i,
       B<=x_i+K sqrt(z_i(x_i+w_i^eff)), K=e-1.
These are exactly the two hypotheses used in the PD1 square completion.
For the same u,q,eta,lambda,A,mu as in that theorem, its unchanged argument gives
 E_i<=a_i+lambda g_i+min{K eta z_i,mu z_i+A w_i^eff}.    (3)
Sum over actual positive-gap recipients to obtain
 sum_I(E_i-a_i)<=lambda G+
                min{K eta Z_+,mu Z_++A W_+^eff}.       (4)
Nothing changes in the negative-gap proof. A strictly negative recipient has
d_i<a_i<=1, which forces p_i<1, so pi_i=p_i on that set. More fundamentally,
the negative saving lemma already permits arbitrary overflow by other owners.
Tied owners retain E_i<=a_i. This proves the asserted PD5 extension.

AGGREGATE CONSEQUENCE
For any chosen full-profile upper bound pi<=Pi_bar, (1) gives
       1<=S<=Pi_bar+Z,
       0<=W_+^eff<=Pi_bar+Z-S.                         (5)
All the other moment constraints in the low-grid 8/5 proof are unchanged:
they involve M,Z,the actual negative subset, and physical role payments rather
than the raw grid total. Thus its interval LP construction extends to a
capped-grid class pi<=Pi_bar, even when P is arbitrarily large. Specifically
replace the row -Z<=1-lo by -Z<=Pi_bar-lo and replace the remainder-row
right side Ahi*(1-lo) by Ahi*(Pi_bar-lo); cover S up to Pi_bar+Cbar. The
coefficient directions and dual residual corrections remain as before.
This note does not certify any new value of Pi_bar or resulting funding factor;
those require their own saved rational interval certificate.

ADDITIONAL ELEMENTARY INFORMATION
Since 0<=pi_i,z_i<=1,
        0<=w_i^eff<=min(pi_i,z_i),
        w_i^eff<=pi_i*z_i.
The product inequality follows from (1-pi_i)(1-z_i)>=0 when pi_i+z_i>=1,
and is trivial otherwise. These may be retained in a more detailed actual-owner
relaxation, but are not needed to establish (3)-(5). The clipping in this proof
is an algebraic identity inside the restored comparison, not a redefinition of
the globally truthful mechanism or of any owner's admissible report domain.
