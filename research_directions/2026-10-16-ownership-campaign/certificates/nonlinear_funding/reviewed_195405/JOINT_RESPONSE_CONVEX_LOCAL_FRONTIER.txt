AN EXACT JOINT-RESPONSE CONVEX REDUCTION FOR LOCAL-REPORT ENVELOPES
16 October 2026. Original exponential local and outer primitive. All actual
owners, their eligible coordinates, and all branch identities remain fixed.
No historical priority, polynomial bit-complexity, or new funding claim.

THEOREM AND SCOPE
Fix a finite full profile and an actual owner i. Let H_j be branch j's hardware
total, H=sum_j H_j>0, a_k=sum_j h_kj, and M its full physical local production.
Let z_k be the full aggregate local payments, b_k=p_k+z_k, d_k=min(b_k,H).
Assume:
 (A) the physical full profile is hardware limited: P+M>=H;
 (B) the exact envelope of i permits restoration of ALL its hardware and grid:
       E_i=max_{0<=t_j<=l_ij} N_i(h_i,t,p_i;b_-i);      (ER)
 (C) on every branch j with l_ij>0 and H_j>0,
                       sum_{k!=i} l_kj>=H_j;           (LB)
 (D) the full outer gap is nonnegative: d_i>=a_i.

Then if d_i=a_i, E_i=a_i. If d_i>a_i, its exact actual envelope is the maximum
of finitely many explicitly specified convex optimization problems. Each has
one branch-price variable per varying productive branch and one own-account
variable; the only nonlinear constraint is a concave entropy hypograph, and
the maximized objective is concave. The maximum ranges over the feasible cap
patterns of actual opponents. No independent payment-floor relaxation occurs.

Hypothesis (ER) is automatic for an owner with no hardware. It also follows
from the separate owner-specific restoration theorem where that theorem's
hypotheses hold, for example normalized S>=3/2+e*z_i. This note assumes (ER)
and does not re-prove that separate theorem. A monopoly hardware owner provides
another restoration case. Counts of branches and owners are unrestricted.

The result does NOT say that the six possible hardware-withholding owners
leave only six difficult optimizations. Conditions (LB), (D), and the number
of cap patterns are additional information; the remaining branch dimension
can grow without bound. Negative full gaps and branches whose pools can switch
under a local report are not covered by this convex theorem.

1. EVERY LOCAL COUNTERPROFILE KEEPS THE OUTER HARDWARE POOL SHORT
Let J consist of branches with l_ij>0 and H_j>0. On each j in J, (LB) says
opponents' local resources alone cover the complete fixed hardware H_j. Thus
its physical output stays H_j for every own local t_j in [0,l_ij]. Other
branches have no variable productive local report of i and retain their full
physical output. Consequently M remains unchanged throughout this optimization.
Local coalition coverage gives Z(t)>=M, so by (A)
                         P+Z(t)>=P+M>=H.               (2)
The outer hardware pool is therefore short or tied throughout. This argument
allows P=0; it does not require grid supply alone to cover hardware. It applies
after the exact hardware and grid restorations in (ER).

2. THE EXACT JOINT RESPONSE OF EACH VARIABLE BRANCH
Fix j in J and set T=H_j. Opponents' capped local accounts are fixed:
 c_kj=min(l_kj,T) for k!=i,
 R_j=sum_{k!=i}c_kj/T,    q0_j=exp(1-R_j)/2.
Since opponents' local total is at least T, R_j>=1. The own local cap
u_j=min(t_j,T) runs through precisely [0,u_max,j], where
u_max,j=min(l_ij,T). Reports above T give the same branch payment vector.
Define the branch-price coordinate
                q_j=q0_j exp(-u_j/T),
                q_min,j=q0_j exp(-u_max,j/T).
Every q_j in [q_min,j,q0_j] is feasible, with actual report recovery
                   t_j=-T log(q_j/q0_j).               (3)
This report lies in [0,u_max,j], hence is an allowed report by the SAME owner.
Both endpoints are strictly positive for every finite instance.

The exact payment to i on this branch is
 psi_j(q_j)=h_ij-h_ij*q_j-T*q_j*log(q_j/q0_j),           (4)
while every actual opponent k!=i receives
                 z_kj(q_j)=h_kj+q_j(c_kj-h_kj).         (5)
These are just the hardware-short primitive with the short vector equal to
the actual hardware holdings. They are valid even when some h_kj or l_kj is
zero, when u_j=T, and when a cap or pool equality persists. No owner cloning
is used. In particular
                psi_j''(q_j)=-T/q_j<0.                (6)
The own local payment is concave in this coordinate; every opponent response
is affine. This is the exact joint relation, not separate extrema of accounts.

Let z_k^fix be the sum of payments on branches outside J. Then
 beta_i(q)=p_i+z_i^fix+sum_{j in J}psi_j(q_j),
 beta_k(q)=p_k+z_k^fix+sum_{j in J}[h_kj+q_j(c_kj-h_kj)]
                 for k!=i.                            (7)
Thus beta_i is concave, and every beta_k for k!=i is affine, on the compact
price box Q=product_j[q_min,j,q0_j]. Formula (3) makes every point of Q a
simultaneously feasible whole local bundle. If J is empty, Q has one point.

At full own local supply each u_j is maximal, hence each q_j is minimal. The
derivative psi_j'=u_j-h_ij-T<=0 shows beta_i(q)<=b_i for every q in Q. This also
follows from the local primitive's own-report monotonicity.

3. NONPOSITIVE CANDIDATES CAN BE DISCARDED WHEN THE FULL GAP IS POSITIVE
For the actual local bundle associated with q, put x_*(q)=min(beta_i(q),H)
and C(q)=sum_{k!=i}min(beta_k(q),H). By (2), x_*(q)+C(q)>=H, and
 N_i(q)=a_i+(x_*(q)-a_i)
                     exp(1-[x_*(q)+C(q)]/H)/2.         (8)
If d_i=a_i, then x_*(q)<=d_i=a_i by the end of Section 2. Every value in (8)
is at most a_i, while the full report attains a_i. Equation (ER) gives E_i=a_i.

If d_i>a_i, the full report gives strictly more than a_i. Every candidate with
x_*(q)<=a_i gives at most a_i and cannot maximize. We may therefore restrict
to x_*(q)>a_i, and in particular a_i<H. This argument uses the FULL gap and
does not claim that the gap retains its sign under arbitrary local reports.

4. ONE EXACT CONVEX PROGRAM FOR EACH OPPONENT CAP PATTERN
Let A be a subset of the actual opponents, interpreted as their capped accounts.
Impose the affine constraints
             beta_k(q)>=H for k in A,
             beta_k(q)<=H for k not in A and k!=i.      (9)
Equality may be assigned to either pattern; this only duplicates boundary points.
On this region their exact capped total is the affine function
             C_A(q)=|A|H+sum_{k not in A, k!=i}beta_k(q).

Use an additional scalar x and the convex feasible set
 q in Q, (9),
 a_i<x<=H,
 x<=beta_i(q),
 x+C_A(q)>=H.                                         (10)
The own-account constraint is a hypograph of a concave function, and the last
constraint is affine. Maximize the concave objective
             L_A(q,x)=log(x-a_i)-x/H-C_A(q)/H.          (11)
Its own-account second derivative is -1/(x-a_i)^2; the remaining term is affine
in q. Therefore this is a convex optimization problem in the standard sense
of maximizing a concave function on a convex feasible set.

Let V_A be its optimum, with V_A=-infinity if the feasible set is empty. Then
                   E_i=a_i+(e/2)*max_A exp(V_A).         (12)
At least one pattern has a feasible positive-gap candidate, namely the full
profile's pattern. Every nonempty program with a positive candidate attains
its maximum: q and x are bounded, and the objective tends to -infinity as
x decreases to a_i. The other terms in (11) are bounded on Q.

EXACTNESS, INCLUDING THE AUXILIARY x VARIABLE.
For every actual positive-gap local report q, choose its actual cap pattern
and x=x_*(q). It satisfies (10), and (11) is exactly the logarithm of its gain
above a_i, apart from the constant log(e/2). Thus the right side of (12) is
at least the true envelope in (ER).
Conversely, at every feasible (q,x), increase x to x_*(q). This preserves all
constraints in (10), and cannot decrease the objective, since
        partial L_A/partial x=1/(x-a_i)-1/H>=0
for a_i<x<=H. At x=x_*(q), formula (3) is an actual simultaneous local report
and (8) realizes the program value. Hence the right side of (12) is at most
the true envelope. The auxiliary hypograph is therefore exact for this
optimization, even though an arbitrary lower x need not itself be realized
jointly with the same opponents. This monotonicity argument is essential.

5. REDUCING THE FINITE CAP ENUMERATION
Because each opponent beta_k is affine on Q, its minimum and maximum are given
explicitly by choosing the lower or upper endpoint of each q_j according to
the coefficient sign. If max_Q beta_k<=H, assign k permanently uncapped. If
min_Q beta_k>=H, assign k permanently capped. If it is identically H either
assignment is valid; use a fixed convention. Only opponents satisfying
                 min_Q beta_k<H<max_Q beta_k
need a binary cap choice. If their number is c_i, at most 2^(c_i) convex programs
are needed. Some can be empty. All actual-owner inequalities in (9) remain
present, and the branch-price dimension is |J|, not c_i or six.

The representation is finite for every finite input and has a globally concave
objective in each cap region. It is not a proof of polynomial bit complexity
for transcendental input constants, nor of fixed-parameter tractability of the
unrestricted envelope based on the hardware-exception count. It also does not
make a finite-precision numerical maximizer into an exactly truthful payment
implementation: certified approximation requires its own monotonicity argument,
such as the separately proved fixed-lattice padded construction.

6. WHAT THE RESTORATION THRESHOLD DOES AND DOES NOT GIVE
The owner-specific threshold can establish (ER) for all but a bounded number
of owners at high capped totals. For an owner additionally satisfying (LB)
and a nonnegative full gap, this theorem then supplies an EXACT joint-response
convex representation of its remaining optimization. If it owns no hardware,
only the local-supply and physical-bottleneck conditions need checking beyond
the automatic nonnegative gap.
However local pool-switch branches, negative-gap recipients, and many variable
cap patterns can remain for arbitrarily many owners. Those are genuine missing
conditions, rather than consequences of the six-owner restoration count.
The theorem establishes a tractable convex structure in a specified unbounded
class and retains every actual response, while leaving those broader obstacles
explicit.
