DIMENSION-FREE DUAL AGGREGATION OF ALL POSITIVE OUTER GAPS
16 October 2026. Candidate structural lemma for independent review.
Original exponential local and outer primitive, fixed actual owners. No
replacement mechanism, unrestricted optimality, or novelty claim is made.

1. SETUP AND STATEMENT
Normalize full hardware H=1 and assume P+M>=1. Let a_i=H_i, z_i be the
full local payment, b_i=p_i+z_i, d_i=min(b_i,1), S=sum_i d_i>=1, and
K=e-1. Let I={i:d_i>a_i}, with
 g_i=d_i-a_i, w_i=(b_i-1)_+,
 G=sum_I g_i, Z_+=sum_I z_i, W_+=sum_I w_i.
As usual E_i is the actual own-report envelope of the raw nested rule.
Define
 k(t)=1-(1/2)exp(1-1/t) for0<t<=1,
 k(t)=(1/2)exp(1-t) for t>=1,
 k(0)=1, q0=k(S).

For ANY scalar u in(0,1), put
 q=(1/2)exp(1-1/u),
 eta=q/S,
 lambda=1-q(1+1/u-1/S),
 A=lambda-eta=1-q(1+1/u)>0,
 mu=K^2 eta^2/(4A).
Then every full positive-gap recipient satisfies
 E_i <=a_i+lambda g_i+min{K eta z_i, mu z_i+A w_i}.       (PD1)
Consequently
 sum_{i in I}(E_i-a_i)
 <=lambda G+min{K eta Z_+, mu Z_+ +A W_+}.              (PD2)
No simultaneous attainment or common underreport is assumed. The scalar u
is common only to the upper bound; each owner retains its full actual report
domain, with arbitrary local/grid/hardware bundles and unrestricted counts.
In particular if the FULL profile has no overflow among positive recipients,
W_+=0 and the bound is lambda G+min(K eta,mu)Z_+.

2. THE EXACT SCALAR SUPPORT INEQUALITY
For these lambda,eta,
 min(t,1)[k(t)-lambda]_+ <=eta(S-t) for0<=t<=S.          (PD3)
The maximizer of the quotient on the positive part is exactly t=u; thus
eta is the sharp scalar coefficient for the chosen lambda. The endpoint
t=S is interpreted by continuity if its numerator also vanishes.

First lambda>=1/(2S)>=q0. To prove the first inequality, set y=1/u>=1;
exp(1-y)(1+y-1/S) decreases with y, since its derivative is
exp(1-y)(1/S-y)<=0. Its value at y=1 is2-1/S. The second inequality is
S exp(1-S)<=1. Also A>0 because exp(1-y)(1+y)<2 for y>1.

On1<=t<S, differentiation of [k(t)-lambda]/(S-t) has the sign of
 k(t)(1-S+t)-lambda.
Writing v=S-t>=0, this is q0 exp(v)(1-v)-lambda<=q0-lambda<=0.
Thus the largest quotient in this region occurs at t=1, when positive.

For0<t<=1, differentiate F(t)=t[k(t)-lambda]/(S-t). Its derivative has
the sign of
 S(1-lambda)-q_h(t)[S+S/t-1],
 q_h(t)=(1/2)exp(1-1/t).
The function q_h(t)(1+1/t-1/S) increases strictly with t on(0,1), so this
sign changes from positive to negative exactly at t=u by the definition of
lambda. At that point the stationarity identity gives
 k(u)-lambda=q_h(u)(S-u)/(Su), hence F(u)=q_h(u)/S=eta.
The endpoint t=1 cannot exceed this value, and the t>=1 argument handles
the remaining region. Taking positive parts proves(PD3), including t=0.
For S=1 the derivative argument is unchanged on(0,1), and k(1)<lambda.

3. APPLY THE SUPPORT TO ONE ACTUAL OWNER
Fix an arbitrary actual whole-owner underreport of i. Recompute local
payments, then restore only its outer hardware and grid to full. This weakly
raises its payment by own monotonicity of the outer primitive. Let d_i' be
its restored capped account, x=d_i-d_i'>=0, and t the resulting total
capped opposing account mass. The total t includes i's own restored capped
account d_i'; it is not an
opponents-only sum. If d_i'<=a_i, its restored payment is at most
a_i and(PD1) is immediate. Otherwise set r=d_i'-a_i=g_i-x>0.
Then r<=min(t,1). On t>=1 the restored payment is exactly a_i+k(t)r.
On t<1, a_i<d_i'<=t, so its hardware cap is inactive, and the full hardware
cap sum is at most1. Its outer coefficient is at least
(1/2)exp(1-1/t), giving payment at most a_i+k(t)r as well.

If t>=S, this excess is at mostq0 r<=lambda g_i, so assume t<S and set
B=S-t. The sharp outgoing inequalities and the elementary cap identity give
 B<=x+K z_i,
 B<=x+K sqrt(z_i(x+w_i)).                              (PD4)
Indeed own actual local loss D=z_i-z_i' satisfies0<=D<=z_i and D<=x+w_i;
other positive capped losses are at most the positive local outgoing loss,
which is at most both K z_i and K sqrt(z_iD). Increases of other accounts
can only decrease B. This argument permits arbitrary full overflow.

By(PD3),
 k(t)r-lambda g_i
 =[k(t)-lambda]r-lambda x
 <=eta B-lambda x.
Using the first bound in(PD4) and A=lambda-eta>0 gives
 <=K eta z_i-Ax<=K eta z_i.
Using the second, and writing y=x+w_i, gives
 <=K eta sqrt(z_i y)-Ay+A w_i
 <=K^2 eta^2 z_i/(4A)+A w_i,
where the last step is the exact square
 [sqrt(Ay)-(K eta/(2sqrt(A)))sqrt(z_i)]^2>=0.
The two bounds hold simultaneously for every underreport. Taking the actual
supremum proves(PD1). Summing and using
 sum_i min(s_i,t_i)<=min(sum_i s_i,sum_i t_i)
proves(PD2). This is a direct dual summation, not a merger of actual owners.

4. A COMPUTABLE ALL-PROFILE FUNDING CERTIFICATE
Let J={i:d_i<a_i}, g_i^-=a_i-d_i, G_-=sum_J g_i^-, Z_-=sum_J z_i,
Z=sum_i z_i, delta=CM-Z, C=1+1/(2e). Tied recipients have E_i<=a_i.
The separately proved negative-gap theorem uses
 L(v)=2exp(sqrt(1-v)-1)/(1+sqrt(1-v))
and gives E_i<=a_i-q0 exp(-delta)g_i^- L(K^2 z_i g_i^-).
Because z_i+g_i^-=a_i-p_i on J, one has Z_-+G_-<=1. Monotonicity of L
therefore yields the (possibly weaker) aggregate saving
 sum_J g_i^- L(K^2 z_i g_i^-)>=G_- L(K^2 Z_-G_-).
Consequently, for EVERY u in(0,1),
 sum_i E_i <=1+lambda G
   +min{K eta Z_+, mu Z_+ +A W_+}
   -q0 exp(-delta)G_- L(K^2 Z_-G_-).                   (PD5)
All quantities are from the full profile; G-G_-=S-1. Thus any profile
satisfying the right side <=C^2 is rigorously certified, regardless of owner
or branch counts and regardless of whether its optimizing deviations leave
that class. A stronger independently proved summed negative saving can be
substituted directly. Optimizing u is only a scalar minimization.

This note does not claim(PD5) is always <=C^2. Establishing that implication
requires further full-profile compatibility constraints or a stronger dual.
The subset hardware-role constraint in SUBSET_HARDWARE_ROLE_LOWER.txt is one
such compatibility constraint. Finite numerical evidence would not establish
the remaining global implication.
