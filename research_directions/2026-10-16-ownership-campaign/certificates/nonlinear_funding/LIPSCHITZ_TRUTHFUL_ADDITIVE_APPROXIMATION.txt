A FINITE ADDITIVE APPROXIMATION WITH EXACT OWN-REPORT MONOTONICITY
16 October 2026. Original symmetric exponential nested mechanism only.
Counts are arbitrary but finite; all actual identities and eligible coordinates
are fixed. A lattice and padding construction is an elementary approximation
method, not claimed historically novel. The explicit Lipschitz control below
is proved for this particular primitive and its composition.

RESULT AND LIMIT
For every fixed finite ownership instance and every fixed positive additive
accuracy epsilon, there is a payment rule A defined by a finite maximum at
every finite report profile, with
 * exact coordinatewise own-report monotonicity on the full nonnegative domain;
 * all-profile coalition coverage;
 * E_i<=A_i<=E_i+epsilon/n for each actual owner;
 * sum_i A_i<=sum_i E_i+epsilon.
Here E is the original actual own-report envelope and n is the FIXED owner count.
The real-valued formula has an implementable certified rational-output variant
for rational reports, with the same total additive error after splitting the
accuracy allowance. Evaluation is generally exponential in report dimension
and capacity magnitude. No polynomial-time or relative subsidy guarantee is
claimed. In particular the padded rule can pay positively when F=0.

1. THE EXPONENTIAL PRIMITIVE IS GLOBALLY 2-LIPSCHITZ IN l1
Let Phi(a,b) be its full vector of actual-owner payments. On a positive-output
cell, orient the short report vector as s, the long vector as ell, and write
 T=sum s_i, d_i=min(ell_i,T), D=sum d_i, rho=D/T>=1,
 q=exp(1-rho)/2,
                         Phi_i=(1-q)s_i+q*d_i.          (1)
The asserted global inequality is
 ||Phi(a,b)-Phi(a',b')||_1
              <=2(||a-a'||_1+||b-b'||_1).              (2)

First vary one short coordinate at unit speed while staying in one cell. Let
m be the number of capped long coordinates, so dT=1, dD=m and
 dq=q(rho-m)/T.
Here 0<=m<=rho. The l1 norm of the payment derivative is bounded by
 (1-q)+qm+(D+T)q(rho-m)/T
 =1+q[rho^2+rho-1-rho*m]
 <=1+q(rho^2+rho-1)<=1+5/(2e)<2.                       (3)
The last scalar maximum occurs at rho=2, since the derivative of
exp(1-rho)(rho^2+rho-1)/2 has the sign of (2-rho)(rho+1).

Next vary one uncapped long coordinate at unit speed. T is fixed, dD=1 and
dq=-q/T. Its vector derivative has l1 norm at most
                  q+(D+T)q/T=q(rho+2)<=3/2.            (4)
The last expression decreases on rho>=1. For a capped long coordinate the
derivative is zero. These estimates cover either physical pool after orientation.

Cap boundaries are continuous. At a pool tie all long coordinates are at most
T, rho=1 and Phi=(a+b)/2, so the two orientation formulas agree. At T=0 the
payment vector is zero, and its l1 norm at positive T is
 T[1+q(rho-1)]<=CT<2T,
which proves continuity at zero-output boundaries as well. A one-coordinate
path crosses only finitely many orientation/cap cells. Integrating the bounded
one-sided derivatives and then changing coordinates successively proves (2)
on the whole nonnegative domain. No lower bound on T or upper bound on reports
is required. The constant does not depend on owner count.

2. THE NESTED RAW PAYMENT IS 6-LIPSCHITZ IN ONE WHOLE BUNDLE
Let h_ij and l_ij be owner i's hardware and local resource on branch j; p_i is
its grid report. Local payments z are sums of the branch primitive outputs,
and the outer input vectors are H_i=sum_j h_ij and p_i+z_i.
Changing one local-resource coordinate by magnitude v changes the local payment
vector by at most 2v in l1, and hence the outer payment vector by at most 4v.
Changing one hardware coordinate also changes its owner's outer hardware by v,
so the outer input difference is at most 3v and its payment difference at most
6v. A grid coordinate gives at most 2v. Therefore for fixed opponents,
 |N_i(b_i,b_-i)-N_i(b_i',b_-i)|
 <=6 sum_j|h_ij-h_ij'|+4 sum_j|l_ij-l_ij'|+2|p_i-p_i'|
 <=6||b_i-b_i'||_1.                                    (5)
This includes arbitrary mixed ownership, ties, zeros, and underreports changing
all eligible coordinates together. The eligible dimension d_i is fixed by the
instance, NOT the number of currently nonzero reported coordinates.

The raw rule is consequently continuous. Its own underreport box [0,b_i] is
compact for every finite profile, so the supremum defining E_i is attained.
This existence statement is not an efficient optimization algorithm.

3. FIXED LATTICE PLUS A CERTIFIED PADDING
For each owner with d_i>0 choose a positive step h_i FIXED EX ANTE independently
of ALL reports. Let G_i={0,h_i,2h_i,...}^{d_i}. Define
 A_i(b)=6d_i h_i+
          max_{g in G_i, 0<=g<=b_i} N_i(g,b_-i).         (6)
If d_i=0 the owner has no eligible resource, N_i=E_i=0, and we set A_i=0.
Every feasible grid point is an actual underreport by the same original owner.

The candidate set in (6) is nonempty and finite at each finite profile, with
size product_{j=1}^{d_i}(1+floor(b_ij/h_i)). Its size may grow without bound
with reports; the overall report domain need not be bounded in advance.
If b_i increases coordinatewise while opponents remain fixed, the candidate
set only expands. Each candidate's value depends on g and b_-i, not on the
current full b_i. The padding is fixed. Hence A_i is EXACTLY own-report monotone.
The grid is not rescaled or selected by the owner's current report.

For every actual underreport x<=b_i, round every coordinate down to g in G_i.
Then g<=x<=b_i and ||x-g||_1<=d_i h_i. Equation (5) gives
 N_i(x,b_-i)<=N_i(g,b_-i)+6d_i h_i<=A_i(b).
Taking the supremum proves E_i<=A_i. Conversely every grid point is feasible,
so its value is at most E_i, and
                        E_i<=A_i<=E_i+6d_i h_i.         (7)
In particular A_i>=E_i>=N_i at every profile. The original raw rule covers every
actual coalition, so the new payments do too. This is exact coverage, not an
approximate incentive or coverage statement.

For a total additive tolerance epsilon>0, choose h_i=epsilon/(6n*d_i) when
d_i>0. Equations (6)-(7) give E_i<=A_i<=E_i+epsilon/n and sum A_i<=sum E_i+epsilon.
The owner count n, eligible dimensions d_i, tolerance epsilon and steps h_i are
all fixed independently of the reports. No virtual players are introduced.

4. CERTIFIED FINITE EVALUATION FOR RATIONALLY ENCODED REPORTS
For this implementation take a positive rational epsilon, so the prescribed
steps are rational. Exact comparison of arbitrary transcendental node values
is unnecessary. Fix
positive rational node accuracy xi_i ex ante, and compute a deterministic
rational upper value U_i(g,b_-i) satisfying
 N_i(g,b_-i)<=U_i(g,b_-i)<=N_i(g,b_-i)+xi_i.              (8)
Replace the node value in (6) by U_i. Candidate values still depend only on g,
the fixed opponents, and the prescribed accuracy. Thus own-report monotonicity
and coalition coverage remain EXACT, while the per-owner additive error becomes
6d_i h_i+xi_i. For example choose h_i=epsilon/(12n*d_i) and xi_i=epsilon/(2n).

Such node bounds can be obtained finitely with rational interval arithmetic.
For rational local inputs, pool/cap comparisons are rational; each nonzero
local coefficient is an exponential of a rational number, with computable
signed Taylor bounds. Compute nonnegative rational approximations to the full
local payment vector with total l1 error at most xi_i/8. Feed the resulting
rational opposing vector into the outer primitive. By (2), this changes its
recipient payment by at most xi_i/4. Evaluate the outer primitive at those
rational inputs within an interval of width at most xi_i/2, then add xi_i/4
to its upper endpoint. The result satisfies (8). This avoids needing to decide
an exact tie between sums of transcendental local payments: the outer primitive
is evaluated on rational approximations and its error is controlled by (2).
Zero-output branches are detected directly from the rational reports.

This is a finite certified algorithm for every rational report profile. Its
node enumeration can be exponentially large in d_i and in numeric input size;
there is no polynomial or fixed-parameter tractability claim based on the
number of cap-sensitive recipients. Fixed-ex-ante padding and precision are
essential for the stated exact monotonicity argument.

5. FUNDING LIMITATION AND THE OPEN ALGORITHMIC QUESTION
The guarantee is ADDITIVE. At the all-zero profile, if d_i>0 then (6) pays
6d_i h_i although F=0. Thus no finite multiplicative funding ratio follows
from this padded rule, and scaling epsilon using the current report can destroy
the nested-grid monotonicity proof. This defect is stated explicitly rather
than hidden by normalizing F to one inside the implemented mechanism.

The true envelope remains the limiting object as the fixed mesh tends to zero.
The theorem provides arbitrarily accurate finite, exactly truthful and covering
payments in absolute units, not an exact evaluator or an exact relative-funding
implementation of the newly proved envelope constants. A stronger algorithmic
result must retain joint attainable own/opponent response geometry or find a
report-consistent relative error scheme. The separate constant-gap obstruction
shows why the present independent coalition floors do not already supply one.
