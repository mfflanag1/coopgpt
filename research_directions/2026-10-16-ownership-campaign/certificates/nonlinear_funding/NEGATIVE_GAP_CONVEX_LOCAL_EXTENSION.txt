NEGATIVE GAPS ALSO ADMIT AN EXACT CONVEX LOCAL-RESPONSE PROGRAM
16 October 2026. Addendum to JOINT_RESPONSE_CONVEX_LOCAL_FRONTIER.txt and
CONVEX_LOCAL_FOUR_PROGRAM_BOUND.txt. All original actual identities remain fixed.
No historical priority, new subsidy bound, or numerical-truthfulness claim.

RESULT
The full-gap restriction (D) in the joint-response theorem can be REMOVED.
Keep its hypotheses (A) physical hardware bottleneck, (B) exact hardware/grid
restoration (ER), and (C) opponents' local supply (LB). Then every owner has
an exact finite convex-program representation, whatever its full gap sign:
 * positive full gap: the logarithmic gain program in the parent theorem;
 * zero full gap: E_i=a_i directly;
 * negative full gap: minimize the strictly convex loss given below on each
   actual opponent cap region.

The exact affine-coordinate ceilings and crossing count are unchanged. Thus
pi<=51/40 still gives AT MOST FOUR convex programs, with arbitrary owner and
productive-branch counts. The branch dimension and numerical-implementation
limitations in the parent theorem remain explicit.

1. EXACT NEGATIVE-GAP OBJECTIVE
Use the parent theorem's box Q and exact local-price responses beta_k(q).
For its variable branch j write T_j=H_j>0, h_ij for i's hardware there, and
u_j=-T_j log(q_j/q0_j), with 0<=u_j<=min(l_ij,T_j). Let
 R_j=sum_{k!=i}min(l_kj,T_j)/T_j>=1,
 q0_j=exp(1-R_j)/2,
 q_j in [q0_j exp(-u_max,j/T_j),q0_j].
The parent's formulas show beta_i is concave and maximal at its full report.

Suppose its full capped account d_i<a_i. Since a_i<=H, this implies its full
uncapped account b_i=d_i<a_i, and consequently
             beta_i(q)<=b_i<a_i<=H for every q in Q.    (1)
Define w(q)=a_i-beta_i(q)>0. In particular its OWN outer cap never binds during
this local-report optimization, and w is uniformly positive on the compact Q.

Fix any actual-opponent cap pattern A, with exactly the affine inequalities
in Section 4 of the parent theorem. Put
 C_A(q)=|A|H+sum_{k not in A, k!=i}beta_k(q),
 S_A(q)=beta_i(q)+C_A(q).
The true opposing total is at least H throughout, by the unchanged-physical-M
argument already proved there. The actual payment is exactly
 N_i(q)=a_i-(e/2)*L_A(q),
             L_A(q)=w(q) exp[-S_A(q)/H].               (2)
No auxiliary own-account variable is used. All accounts are the jointly
attainable actual responses recovered by t_j=u_j.

Therefore
 E_i=a_i-(e/2)*min_A min_{q in Q, cap pattern A}L_A(q).  (3)
Each region is closed, convex, and compact; empty regions are omitted. At least
the full profile's region is nonempty. We next prove its objective is STRICTLY
convex, so (3) is an exact convex-program representation.

2. THE LOSS HESSIAN
Let
 X=grad w, V=grad C_A, A_vec=grad S_A=V-X,
 W=Hessian w=diag_j(T_j/q_j).
Here W is positive definite for a nonempty variable-branch set. Explicitly,
 X_j=h_ij+T_j-u_j, and
 V_j=sum_{k not in A, k!=i}[min(l_kj,T_j)-h_kj].        (4)
Fixed branches and grid reports contribute constants to C_A. Direct
differentiation of (2), using Hessian S_A=-W, gives
 Hessian L_A / exp(-S_A/H)
   =(1+w/H)W -(X A_vec^T+A_vec X^T)/H
                      +(w/H^2)A_vec A_vec^T.           (5)
All terms include the actual opposing total, including i's actual account.

For any direction v, abbreviate
 D_v=v^T W v, x=X^T v, y=A_vec^T v.
Since x+y=V^T v, the elementary identity (x-y)^2>=0 gives
                  2xy<=(x+y)^2/2=(V^T v)^2/2.         (6)
The key is to bound the last scalar using the ORIGINAL local response geometry,
rather than allowing V and W to vary independently.

3. THE GEOMETRIC BOUND ON OPPONENT AFFINE SLOPES
On branch j, a sum over any subset of opponents' differences d_kj-h_kj lies
between minus their total hardware and their total capped local resources.
Hence, since R_j>=1,
 -T_j<=V_j<=R_j T_j, and therefore |V_j|<=R_j T_j.      (7)
Also q_j<=q0_j=exp(1-R_j)/2. The scalar function
 R^2 exp(1-R)/2 on R>=1 has maximum 2/e at R=2. Thus
 sum_j V_j^2/(T_j/q_j)
       <=sum_j q_j R_j^2 T_j
       <=(2/e)sum_variable_j T_j<=(2/e)H.              (8)
The last inequality uses that every variable branch is hardware short with
T_j=H_j, and the total hardware over ALL branches is H. It holds independently
of the number of branches, caps, and owners, including mixed ownership.

Weighted Cauchy-Schwarz applied to (8) yields
                   (V^T v)^2<=(2H/e)D_v.              (9)
Combining (5), (6), and (9) gives the explicit curvature lower bound
 v^T Hessian L_A v / exp(-S_A/H)
 >=(1+w/H)D_v-D_v/e+(w/H^2)y^2
 >=(1-1/e+w/H)D_v>0 for every nonzero v.              (10)
Thus L_A is strictly convex on Q, and in particular on every convex cap region.
The exponential factor is positive; no lower production or price bound beyond
the finite-instance positive q_j intervals is required for this sign statement.
If there are no variable branches, the feasible price set has one point and
the evaluation is immediate.

4. ATTAINMENT, EXACTNESS, AND THE NUMBER OF PROGRAMS
Every nonempty cap region has an attained minimum by continuity and compactness.
Strict convexity implies a UNIQUE minimizing price vector within that region,
even when it is attained on a cap boundary or price-box face. Different regions
can share that point. Reports above a local cap can be redundant, so this does
not assert a unique physical report beyond the canonical recovery t_j=u_j.

Conversely every q in a region recovers an actual whole-bundle local underreport
and exactly realizes (2). Every actual local underreport maps into at least one
such region. Together with (ER), these facts prove (3) with no relaxation gap.

The opponent affine accounts, their exact coordinate maxima, and the crossing
set are precisely those in CONVEX_LOCAL_FOUR_PROGRAM_BOUND.txt; none depend on
the sign of the recipient's outer gap. Its c_i<=2 conclusion therefore applies
unchanged at pi<=51/40, giving at most four exact convex minimizations for a
negative-gap owner, just as it gives four convex maximizations for a positive
one. At equality of the full gap, the parent proof gives E_i=a_i without any
optimization.

The result removes the negative-gap obstacle from this local-supply class. It
does not remove possible local pool switches or establish hardware restoration
where (ER) has not been justified. Nor does a numerical minimizer by itself
implement an exactly truthful finite-precision mechanism. Those distinctions,
and the unbounded branch dimension, remain as stated in the parent theorem.
