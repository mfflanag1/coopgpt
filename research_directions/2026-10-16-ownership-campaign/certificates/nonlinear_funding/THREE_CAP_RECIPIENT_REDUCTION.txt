THREE CAP-SENSITIVE RECIPIENTS BELOW CAPPED TOTAL TWO
16 October 2026. Structural reduction for the original exponential primitive.
All owners remain actual owners; all single-owner whole-bundle underreports are
allowed. No global C^2 funding theorem is asserted here.

SETUP AND DEPENDENCIES
Normalize positive full hardware H to one. Let M be full local output, z_k the
full aggregate local payment, and p_k the full grid report of actual owner k.
Thus 0<=z_k<=M<=1. Put pi_k=min(p_k,1), d_k=min(p_k+z_k,1), S=sum_k d_k.
For every actual deviator i, let z_k^i be recipient k's local payment following
an arbitrary coordinatewise whole-bundle underreport by i. The grid reports in
the following outer-account comparison are restored to their full values.

We use local own monotonicity, local coalition coverage, and the independently
audited half-gap incoming-response theorem
       sum_i (z_k^i-z_k)_+ <= (1-z_k)/2.                 (1)
The choices of the different owners' underreports in (1) are independent.
The weighted actual-coalition floor is the elementary layer-cake consequence
of coalition coverage, as set out in GRID_WEIGHTED_COALITION_FLOOR.txt. Neither
that standard argument nor the underlying coalition inequality is claimed new.

1. A COMMON CEILING FOR EVERY ACTUAL DEVIATOR
Write T_j=min(H_j,L_j) for full output on branch j and define
       U_k=min{M, (1+z_k)/2,
                  sum_j min(T_j,max(h_kj,l_kj))}.       (2)
Then z_k<=U_k and, simultaneously for every actual i and every i-underreport,
                         0<=z_k^i<=U_k.                (3)

Proof. Every local payment is at most the branch output, so z_k^i<=M.
Equation (1), or its single-deviator consequence, gives
z_k^i<=z_k+(1-z_k)/2=(1+z_k)/2. If i=k, own monotonicity gives the stronger
z_k^k<=z_k. On each branch the payment is a convex combination of the owner's
short-pool report and capped long-pool report. Both are at most the counterprofile
output T_j^i<=T_j and at most max(h_kj,l_kj): if k=i its reports only decreased,
and otherwise its reports stayed fixed. Summing proves the third ceiling.
The full report is an allowed underreport, proving z_k<=U_k. This also treats
zero-output branches and zero recipient payments without a limiting argument.

2. AT MOST THREE NONTRIVIAL WEIGHTS
Set
 w_k=min{1,(1-pi_k)/U_k} if U_k>0; set w_k=1 if U_k=0.
Let J={k:w_k<1}. This is a set of actual recipients, not new virtual players.
For every k in J,
                              d_k>1/2.                 (4)
If J is nonempty, consequently |J|<2S. In all cases, S<2 implies |J|<=3;
S<=3/2 implies |J|<=2. If S=0 then J is empty.

Proof. A nontrivial weight requires U_k>0 and pi_k+U_k>1. If p_k>=1 then
d_k=1>1/2. If p_k<1, (2) gives
 p_k>1-U_k>=(1-z_k)/2,
so p_k+z_k>(1+z_k)/2>=1/2. Clipping at one preserves the strict lower bound
d_k>1/2. This proof does not assert the false strict inequality
d_k>(1+z_k)/2 when z_k=1. Summing (4) proves the count.

For k outside J with U_k>0, p_k+U_k<=1, hence its outer account never caps at
any actual single-owner counterprofile. For U_k=0, every local payment is zero,
so its outer account is the constant pi_k even if p_k>1. Therefore only members
of J can have local payment changes hidden by the outer cap. The converse is
not asserted: some members of J may never actually cross a cap.

3. A FLOOR REQUIRING AT MOST FOUR COALITION VALUES
Assume S<2. Fix i and let A be any coalition not containing i. Its actual opponents'
resource reports are fixed; write
                    m(A)=sum_j min(H_j(A),L_j(A)).
At every i-underreport, (3) gives
        min(p_k+z_k^i,1)>=pi_k+w_k z_k^i  (k!=i).      (5)
Thus the opponents' capped total is uniformly at least
 R_i=sum_{k!=i}pi_k + integral_0^1 m({k!=i:w_k>=t})dt. (6)
Indeed, sum_k w_k z_k^i is the integral of payments to these fixed actual
coalitions, each at least its production. No counterprofile needs to attain
the lower bound and no reports of separate virtual roles are introduced.

Let the q<=3 exceptional opponents in J\{i} be ordered as
 0<=w_(1)<=...<=w_(q)<1,
and let A_ell be all opponents except the first ell exceptional opponents.
With w_(0)=0 and w_(q+1)=1, the integral in (6) equals
       sum_{ell=0}^q (w_(ell+1)-w_(ell)) m(A_ell).     (7)
Tied or zero weights merely produce zero-length intervals. Formula (7) uses
at most four original coalition values independently of the total owner count
and branch count. For an exceptional deviator i itself, it uses at most three.
For q=1, writing the sole exceptional opponent as k, (6) is explicitly
 R_i=pi_{-i}+w_k m(N\{i})+(1-w_k)m(N\{i,k}).          (8)

Precompute all branch resource totals in O(n*m) arithmetic operations, where
n is the number of actual owners and m the number of branches. Each recipient's
at most four coalition values then follows by deleting i and at most three
named owners from these totals in O(m) arithmetic operations. All recipient
floors therefore require O(n*m) arithmetic operations, once the local payments
and weights have been evaluated. This is an arithmetic-operation statement;
the exponential primitive makes the weights generally transcendental. No exact
rational bit-complexity or actual-envelope evaluation claim is made.

4. A VERIFIED DOMAIN WHERE THE UNTRUNCATED FLOOR IS VALID
If J is empty, (6) becomes
                         R_i=pi_{-i}+M_{-i},            (9)
where M_{-i}=m(N\{i}). All full outer accounts satisfy d_k=pi_k+z_k, including
U_k=0. Hence S=sum pi_k+Z and
                   d_i+R_i=S-C_i,
                   C_i=Z-z_i-M_{-i}.                  (10)
The positive-gap restoration bound therefore gives, for g_i=d_i-H_i>0,
                   E_i<=H_i+g_i k(S-C_i).              (11)
Here k(t)=1-(1/2)exp(1-1/t) for 0<t<=1, k(0)=1, and
k(t)=(1/2)exp(1-t) for t>=1. Any independent upper bound on C_i can safely
replace C_i in (11) because k is decreasing. This makes the previously unsafe
untruncated complement-surplus argument valid on this explicit class.

The ceiling in (2) can strictly enlarge a uniform-M headroom class. For example,
one balanced branch with n owners, each owning hardware and local resource 1/n,
has M=1 and z_k=1/n; the coordinate ceiling gives U_k=1/n. Arbitrarily many
positive grid reports with p_k<=1-1/n then satisfy J empty, although the crude
uniform-M condition would require all p_k=0. This example illustrates the
domain extension, not a sharp funding lower bound.

5. SCOPE AND REMAINING OBSTRUCTION
For S<2 there can still be arbitrarily many positive-gap owners. The conclusion
is that cap-sensitive recipients, and hence the coalition-floor threshold
states, have bounded count. It does not bound the number of deviators, merge
their resources, identify the exact envelope, or prove a global funding factor.
The exact family in COMPLEMENT_CAP_TRUNCATION_OBSTRUCTION.txt has just one
cap-sensitive recipient. It already invalidates deleting all cap corrections
in the general class. Equations (6)-(8) retain that recipient and its actual
coalition geometry instead of imposing the false untruncated floor.

If H=0 the physical output is zero, every local payment is zero, and all
funding and floor statements reduce to the zero-output case without normalization.
If H>0 but M=0, (2) makes every U_k=0, J is empty and (6) is simply pi_{-i}.
