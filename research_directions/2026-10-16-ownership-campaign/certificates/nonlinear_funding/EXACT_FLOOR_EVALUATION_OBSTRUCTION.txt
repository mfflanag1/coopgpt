AN UNBOUNDED CONSTANT-GAP OBSTRUCTION TO EXACT FLOOR EVALUATION
16 October 2026. Original exponential nested raw rule N and actual envelope E.
This rules out treating the current independent coalition-floor relaxation as
an exact evaluator. It does NOT rule out every finite-dimensional aggregate
frontier representation of the true continuous optimization problem.

1. FAMILY WITH NO CAP-SENSITIVE RECIPIENTS
For each integer r>=2, take r productive branches and r+1 fixed actual owners.
Owner A_j holds hardware 1/r on branch j and no other resource. One actual
owner B holds local resource 1/r on EVERY branch and no hardware. All grid
reports are zero. All owners are active and every branch has positive output.
The number of owners and productive branches both grows with r.

At the full profile, H=M=1. Every local branch is balanced, its coefficient is
1/2, z_Aj=1/(2r), and z_B=1/2. Since grid is zero and each common local payment
ceiling U_k<=M=1, all weights w_k are one, including zero-ceiling cases. Thus
the exceptional recipient set J is empty.

Every actual coalition of B's opponents has only hardware and no local
resources, so its local production is zero. The weighted opponent-production
floor, the direct cap-choice floor, the ceiling-aware floor, and the proposed
finite coverage LP floor all equal zero for B. In fact zero is the EXACT
minimum total payment to its opponents over B's actual underreports: B obtains
it by reporting zero local resource on all branches. The issue below therefore
persists even if the opponent minimum itself is evaluated exactly.

2. COMPLETE ACTUAL WHOLE-BUNDLE OPTIMIZATION
An arbitrary underreport by B is a vector x_j in [0,1/r]. It has no nonzero
hardware or grid coordinate to withhold. Since each branch has just one
hardware owner, its local coefficient remains exactly 1/2 whenever x_j>0;
zero-output branches give zero payments. Thus
 z_B'=t/2, z_Aj'=x_j/2, t=sum_j x_j in [0,1].            (1)
In particular B's own local payment and its opponents' TOTAL local payment
are equal at EVERY feasible whole-bundle report.

Outer hardware shares remain 1/r. For t>0 its capped long hardware ratio is
 rho(t)=r*min(1/r,t)/t>=1,
and its coefficient is q(t)=exp(1-rho(t))/2. B owns no hardware, so its actual
raw payment is
                         N_B(x)=t[1-q(t)]/2.            (2)
For 0<t<=1/r, rho=r and (2) is increasing linearly in t. For 1/r<=t<=1,
rho=1/t and
 dN_B/dt=[1-q(t)(1+rho(t))]/2>=0.
Indeed (1+rho)exp(1-rho)/2 is nonincreasing on rho>=1 and equals one at rho=1.
The two formulas agree at t=1/r, and t=0 has payment zero. Every t in [0,1]
is feasible (for example x_j=t/r). Thus (2) completely solves the original
continuous whole-bundle optimization, with
                              E_B=1/4.                 (3)

3. THE FLOOR RELAXATION HAS A FIXED POSITIVE ERROR
At the full profile a_B=0 and d_B=1/2. Combining this full own capped account
with the exact opponent floor zero gives the existing restored comparison
        d_B*k(d_B)=1/2-1/(4e),                          (4)
strictly greater than (3), by
                           (e-1)/(4e).                 (5)
This gap is independent of r; it does not vanish while the owner and branch
counts grow. The scalar outer comparison itself is exact at the relaxed state:
set every opposing owner's local payment to zero while restoring B's own
account to 1/2. All hardware shares 1/r are at most that opposing total for
r>=2, so the outer capped hardware sum is one and its coefficient is 1/(2e).
This relaxed state gives precisely (4).

Every opponent coalition-coverage inequality is satisfied by this zero vector,
as are the opponent payment ceilings. Hence adding ALL actual-opponent
coalition inequalities to that relaxation cannot remove the witness. The lost
constraint is (1): when opponents' total is zero, B's own local payment is also
zero. Restoring the latter to 1/2 is a valid upper comparison, but it breaks
the joint feasibility required for exact evaluation.

4. IMPLICATION AND LIMIT
Cap-sensitive recipient count alone does not make the present floor relaxation
an exact evaluator. The counterfamily has no cap-sensitive recipients at all,
and even an exact standalone opponent minimum is insufficient. An exact future
frontier must retain the joint attainable own-account/opponent-response relation.
For THIS family that frontier is the line (own,opponents)=(t/2,t/2), and has a
one-dimensional description; therefore this argument is not an impossibility
theorem against all constant-dimensional aggregate frontiers. It identifies
precisely which independence relaxation must be repaired.
