Decision
Do not use the current task/model configuration to compare useful-work energy or
infer pooling gains. The failure occurs even in this serial calibration. Under the frozen gate,
further concurrency testing is unwarranted without first qualifying accuracy. A future
bounded project should select a stronger task/model pairing and qualify it on fresh
held-out tasks before measuring scheduling. A more production-relevant workload
could be considered, but its economic value and correctness criterion must be
specified beforehand; lowering the present threshold or rescuing an easy subset
would not qualify this experiment. The economics direction remains open, while
these measurements provide no evidence of an infrastructure pooling benefit.
