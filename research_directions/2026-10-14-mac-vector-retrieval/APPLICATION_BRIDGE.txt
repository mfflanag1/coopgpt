What this workload can tell the AI-infrastructure program

Service unit: an exact top-ten answer under a specified squared-L2 corpus metric.
The corpus and query define a verifiable computation. This removes the previous
language-model accuracy bottleneck for this narrow numerical service. It does
not substitute numerical nearest neighbors for human semantic relevance.

Decision after correctness qualification
A future operator comparison can ask when batching independent tenants' retrieval
requests improves useful, deadline-compliant throughput, and what waiting time
or memory it costs. Larger matrix products may amortize launch and corpus-access
costs; waiting to fill a batch may instead violate request deadlines. These are
competing mechanisms with observable consequences, not an assumed gain.

The current cohort tests only implementation correctness and explicit storage.
It does not compare arrival scheduling, policies, sustained capacity or energy.
A pass would permit development calibration of single-request and small-batch
service on separate timed runs. Before a policy evaluation, freeze offered
arrivals independently of worker availability, deadlines, batching wait limits,
all-request denominators, configuration order and a meaningful improvement gate.
Report validation cost explicitly rather than comparing GPU-only intervals with
whole CPU work. Cached setup and shared corpus memory must be accounted once.

Economic and cooperative-game boundaries
This Mac supplies one unified resource pool. Query tenants can have different
priorities or deadlines, but those labels do not create independent hardware
owners. Measured scheduling gains would still need an explicit model of ownership,
capacity withholding, opportunity costs and coalition feasibility before any
truthful-payment or cooperative-welfare result applies. Our production theorems
must not be mapped to a nonconcave/discrete scheduler without proving the required
production assumptions. Public SIFT descriptors supply no empirical values,
private costs, prices or tenant utility estimates.

Energy gate
No energy is measured here. First establish a useful task and a sustained service
comparison. A later energy study must include full-horizon baseline/idle energy,
measurement provenance and uncertainty, the same useful-work correctness rule,
and equivalent workload/deadline coverage. Avoid energy-per-success claims that
omit late, failed, setup or unused-capacity costs.

This is a concrete measurement route within the broader program. It is not
novelty confirmation or evidence of a field-level breakthrough.
