WHAT POWERMETRICS CAN SUPPLY
The installed supported samplers include cpu_power, gpu_power, ane_power and
thermal. Its text labels identify CPU, GPU and ANE power in mW and a combined
CPU + GPU + ANE value. These are systemwide component estimates, not per-request
energy and not necessarily all SoC or machine consumption. Do not label them
GPU-board energy, wall-plug energy, electricity-meter readings, or complete host
energy. The combined value must not be added to its component values again.

Apple's help/man explicitly cautions that average power estimates may be
inaccurate and should not be compared between devices. They are useful for
same-device application optimization. A within-M3-Max scheduling comparison can
therefore report estimated component energy with this limitation. It does not
calibrate Apple estimates against NVIDIA NVML or MLPerf wall measurements.

The executable contains the requirement to invoke the program as superuser.
An authorized administrator launch is needed for the installed sampler; only
the sampler would require that privilege, not the request driver or analyzer.
No assumption is made that cached sudo credentials or user authentication are
available. No privileged launch is part of this audit.

FORMATS, TIMING AND UNITS
Default interval: 5000 ms. --sample-rate N sets milliseconds; a predeclared
1000 ms interval is a reasonable starting point for multi-minute workloads,
subject to a later overhead check. --sample-count N provides a finite stop.
--poweravg 0 disables the additional every-N-samples power summary, avoiding
double integration. Omit --show-initial-usage, which covers machine uptime, and
omit --show-usage-summary from the stream used for integration.

Supported formats are text and machine-readable plist; plist records are
NUL-separated. Executable strings expose timestamp and elapsed_ns plist fields,
plus cpu_power, gpu_power and ane_power names. This static inspection establishes
candidate fields, not a verified runtime schema or numeric-unit contract for
each plist field. The text format explicitly labels mW and elapsed milliseconds;
use that self-labeled output initially, or validate the plist schema and units
in a coordinated preflight before analysis. Preserve raw output either way.

For interval-average power P_k in mW over the actual sample duration d_k in ms,
  estimated energy in J = sum_k P_k * d_k * 1e-6.
If using a verified elapsed_ns duration, the conversion factor is 1e-12.
Use actual durations, not the requested interval or elapsed time between output
file reads. Integrate periodic interval averages once; do not trapezoid those
averages as if they were instantaneous endpoint power. If an analysis boundary
splits a sample, disclose any within-interval allocation assumption and provide
a boundary sensitivity check, or use full sample intervals bracketing the run.

The manual describes per-process energy impact as a weighted proxy. It is not
joules and cannot be substituted when privileged component telemetry is missing.
Battery readings can be out of phase with sampling, alias at short intervals,
and be distorted by charging/sleep events; they are not a replacement for a
calibrated power trace in this experiment.

UNPRIVILEGED ALTERNATIVE: NOT CURRENTLY INSTALLED
Official project documentation: https://github.com/vladkens/macmon
The project advertises rootless IOReport-based CPU/GPU/ANE estimates and JSON
output, with watt-valued component fields. It uses private macOS APIs. Current
documentation warns that unsupported counters can yield unavailable/stale or
zero values, including changes in newer macOS releases; zero is not by itself
evidence of zero consumption. Its additional sys_power field is not an external
wall-meter calibration. None of these capabilities was tested locally because
macmon is not installed. No IOReport helper was built, installed or run. A future
installation would need a pinned version and a local counter-validity audit;
switching tools does not remove Apple's estimation/scope limitations.

MINIMUM VALID FULL-HORIZON CAPTURE
1. Freeze exact model digest, quantization, Ollama version, context/output limits,
   seed/decoding settings, residency/cache policy, concurrency setting, request
   IDs and the mixed-length arrival trace. Predeclare service thresholds, finite
   offered duration, bounded drain/cancellation policy and denominator handling.
   Rates must be compared with the same workload contract. Keep failures and
   rejected requests in the offered denominator.
2. Coordinate a quiet measurement window. Record AC/battery state, power mode,
   thermal pressure and memory/swap state; do not silently change OS settings.
   Keep the request driver and measurement overhead consistent. Browser, Codex
   and other background work can affect these systemwide estimates; attribution
   to serving remains conditional on controlled background activity.
3. Start telemetry before the first measured request. Capture at least 30 valid
   one-second samples of loaded-model idle, then every offered request, all
   in-flight work through the predeclared drain, and at least 30 valid post-run
   idle samples after work has ceased. If studying loading, separately mark and
   retain the unloaded-to-loaded transition; do not mix cold and warm conditions.
4. Log wall-clock and monotonic anchors in the request trace and preserve sampler
   timestamps/durations. Record scheduled arrival, actual dispatch, first
   nonempty received content, completion and final status. Stream chunks must
   not be presented as individual tokens. Retain server token counts separately.
5. Ensure the finite sampler budget covers baseline, offered window, maximum
   drain and post-idle with margin. If it stops before work ends, coverage fails;
   do not extrapolate power into the missing tail. Keep canceled/timed-out work
   in the energy ledger until its termination is observed or explicitly mark
   the endpoint unverified. Separate baseline, offered, drain and post-idle
   phase totals while preserving one full-horizon total.
6. Primary energy accounting is the gross observed component estimate including
   idle within the declared comparison horizon. Pre/post baseline subtraction
   may be a labeled sensitivity analysis, with both baselines and drift shown;
   never silently clip a negative incremental estimate to zero. For fixed-time
   comparisons retain idle until the common horizon ends, even if requests
   finish sooner. Do not sum overlapping per-request allocations of device
   energy. Report energy and SLO attainment jointly.
7. Reject missing, invalid, nonfinite, stale, nonmonotone or unexplained-gap
   telemetry and mark absent channels unavailable. A zero ANE value can be real
   under GPU serving, but validity must come from the channel's functioning and
   observed workload, not the numeric zero alone. Counterbalance configuration/
   rate order and repeat runs; a single thermal trajectory is not replication.

long mixed workloads and several rates. Add coordinated component telemetry
before claiming even within-device estimated energy differences. If legitimate
sampler access is unavailable, keep energy unmeasured and continue the latency

independent physical owners. Pooling claims and cross-device calibrated energy
comparisons remain outside what this one-Mac experiment can establish.
