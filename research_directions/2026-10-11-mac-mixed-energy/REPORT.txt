Mixed-length Mac serving and component-energy study — measured, incomplete, quality gate failed
11 October 2026

Finding
The longer study produced 1,053 actual requests in 15 of 18 planned windows.
There were 183 validated API completions and 870 recorded timeouts. None of the
183 completed responses passed the frozen exact-answer test, so useful on-time
success was 0/1,053. There were 47 API completions within their latency deadlines,
but their answers were incorrect under the declared task contract. This does
not establish a useful-work scheduling gain, energy saving, or pooling benefit.
The study is incomplete: 81 planned requests in windows16–18 were never sent.

The negative result is not explained solely by answer formatting. An independent
raw audit reconstructed every gold answer from the input KEEP records. No completed
output contained the required Rdddd=ddd pair syntax. Loose posthoc extraction of
ID/value pairs matched the exact ordered gold content in only10/183 completed
outputs;173 still differed. Merely repairing the ID/value separator gives seven
exact answers; the other three loose matches retain extra labels or formatting.
These diagnostics are NOT replacement scores.
All183 remain quality failures under the original protocol. Examples and the
independent audit are retained. The model/task pair needed an accuracy-calibration
stage before this longer measurement campaign; generic warmups did not supply it.

Energy measurement
The successful segment contains2,346 raw powermetrics samples, no parser errors,
and a confirmed normal sampler shutdown. All75 measured phase intervals (five
per window) pass the frozen coverage and common-timeline consistency diagnostics.
For the15 full episodes, gross Combined CPU/GPU/ANE interval estimates sum to
25,881–26,971 joules, or about7.19–7.49Wh. This sum includes the separately labeled
serving-invalid window7; its telemetry interval itself passes energy validation.
It is a sum of loaded-idle-through-post-idle episodes, not the entire campaign:
startup, warmup, inter-window gaps and authentication time are outside that sum.

These are conditional whole-sample interval bounds under the stated complete,
continuous, nonoverlapping capture and +/-1-second timestamp alignment assumptions.
They do not bound physical calibration error; rounded timestamps alone cannot
prove absence of a short missing sample. Raw reported milliwatts are multiplied
by actual interval milliseconds, with duplicate GPU sections excluded. CPU/GPU/ANE
and Combined are shown separately in RESULTS.txt. Adjacent phase edge bounds may
overlap and must not be added to reconstruct a full-episode bound.

The estimates include other apps and OS activity, with no process attribution
or baseline subtraction. The Mac was charging and not an isolated inference
appliance. Brief synthetic verification checks also ran during collection and
are part of the background activity, not separately metered. Component telemetry
is not wall energy, battery energy, charger input, or data-center power. Since
useful on-time success is zero, joules per useful success is undefined in EVERY
observed window. A smaller gross episode total cannot be called an efficiency
gain when the useful-output gate fails. No power logs were joined across segments.

Clock validation finding
Window7 (P4,0.6,rep1) reports133.339326 seconds of monotonic client elapsed time
but133.318943 seconds between wall-clock workload phases, a20.383ms discrepancy.
This exceeds the frozen1ms tolerance. Collector code records the drain phase after
the client returns, so this disagreement does not by itself demonstrate premature
drain; a cross-clock issue is plausible. The cause is unproved. Window7 remains
invalid under the unchanged check. Future collection should use paired monotonic
phase clocks, with an explicit wall-clock bridge for telemetry, before freezing
a new protocol. We did not relax this check retrospectively.

Verification and provenance
All89 unit/integration tests pass in normal and optimized Python. The separate
independent checker passes33 synthetic assertion groups in both modes. The actual
combined analysis agrees with independent recounts on5,841 exact comparisons,
including counts, strata, conditional quantiles and matched differences, with zero
mismatches. An independent parse of all2,346 raw energy blocks also reproduces
all600 component lower/upper sums across the75 measured phase intervals exactly. Test success does not make the incomplete study complete or remove
window7's invalid label. Original FREEZE.json and CONTINUATION_FREEZE.json match
their files. COMBINED.json retains complete analysis, independent counts, errors,
source identities, both campaign records and completeness. ARTIFACT_MANIFEST.json
hashes delivered artifacts. REPRODUCE.txt describes stored-data checks.

Cleanup was checked directly on the host after the authorization timeout: no
owned serving/telemetry/collector process remained, original Ollama11434 was idle,
and the Mac reported AC charging. A stop sentinel prevents any late continuation
sampler from running on. No user Ollama server or unrelated app was stopped.

Decision and next gate
Do not extend these observations to resource pooling or choose a concurrency
winner. The next bounded experiment should first qualify a realistic, exactly
scorable workload/model pair at negligible queueing on fresh calibration tasks.
Require a predeclared high correctness rate and sufficient answer token budgets
across every intended input/output class; retain failures and freeze a separate
holdout before measuring any schedule. Repair and test the clock bridge and
power-authentication startup on a short capability run first. Then repeat a
complete, adequately budgeted rate sweep, bracketing saturation from below, with
request quality, latency and full-horizon energy measured together. Only after
that gate should we add a second compatible independently owned configuration
and test economically meaningful pooling/deviation/cost assumptions. No further
hardware run is automatically started by this report.
