Scientific limits and issues
No substantive raw-count, gold/scorer, or energy-arithmetic bug was found in this audit. The mixed UTC/monotonic drain check has a real measurement-consistency limitation illustrated by window7; preserve its prospective invalidation. Missing-window synthetic zero counts and unfinished proxies must never be narrated as observed server behavior. Conditional latency quantiles include only183 successful calls, excluding870 recorded errors and81 unrun offers; they cannot show overall latency reliability.
Energy bounds are conditional on uninterrupted, nonoverlapping, complete sampler capture and +/-1s endpoint alignment. They do not bound Apple calibration error. Gross CPU/GPU/ANE telemetry includes background apps, analysis/tooling, OS work and other activity; it is not process-attributed, whole-machine wall energy, battery draw, baseline-adjusted energy, or causal energy savings. Scheduled rate, concurrency, trace mix, wall time, timeout/cancellation policy and varying background state coexist; the data provide descriptive one-host finite-window comparisons, not steady-state capacity, population inference, independent request-level replications, cross-owner cooperation or economic pooling gains.
