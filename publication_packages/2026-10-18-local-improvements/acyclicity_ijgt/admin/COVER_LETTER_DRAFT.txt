Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Acyclicity and simultaneous protection of individual core maxima",
by Maxwell Flanagan.

The paper classifies simultaneous protection relative to individual maxima
of the entire original core in strict full-team pair-additive assignment markets.
For fixed connected sector graphs, trees have sharp guarantee1/K, while cyclic
graphs admit positive-core families whose best protection tends to zero as
sector size grows. The contribution is a quantitative graph classification;
the model, leximin, coordinate ideals and matching duality are credited as prior.

A companion paper on static subsidy asks the cost of protecting frozen H/3
floors with external payments and uses a different extremal family. The truthful
production companion instead imposes incentive constraints across reports.

The manuscript has not been publicly posted, published, or submitted elsewhere.
There is no funding/support, no competing interest, and no acknowledgment to add.
The manuscript reports Codex assistance and recorded later Claude internal
reviews. I have personally reviewed the manuscript, proofs, code and results.
Please consider the included
supplements as constructed-instance and computational-verification resources.
