SCOPE OF THIS SUBMISSION AND RELATED UNPUBLISHED WORK
Truthful production concerns a single payment rule across all reports by fixed
owners, with binding underreports, payment-only utility and coalition coverage.
Its primary sharp parameter is representation/minimal-cut complexity K.
Acyclicity concerns original-core protection without an external payment rule
across reports. Static Subsidies concerns payments above a fixed original core's
individual maxima in equal-sized three-sector pair-additive matching markets.
Neither companion's target is the cross-report sharp log K / log log K budget law.
The papers share established supporting-price/core ideas and research lineage;
that overlap is not presented as a separate contribution. No claim of independent
priority or separate provenance beyond these substantive scope differences is made.
