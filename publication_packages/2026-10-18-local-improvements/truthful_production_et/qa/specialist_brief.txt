What the new lower bounds restrict. The supplementary constructions force square-root growth for
report-independent mask laws using common supporting prices selected from open reports and endpoint
payments to open owners. The bounds allow asymmetric/correlated masks and randomized finite support
selection. Separate results cover the maximum-coalition-marginal cap. They do not apply to arbitrary
truthful payment maps or every report-dependent sampling scheme. The unrestricted lower bounds remain
logarithmic over logarithmic in owner count and dimension. No matching unrestricted square-root lower
bound has been established.

Closest original-source comparisons.
Production and stability. Owen (1975) and Samet–Zemel (1984) establish dual-price/core ingredients.
Moretti–Norde (2021) studies the weighted multi-glove game used as a special case. Fixed-game cost of
stability is established. Our issue links different reports even though each individual production game has
an efficient core.
Same disclosure model. Huang–Mei–Yang–Cao (2025 v1) uses binding capacity underreports and
payment utility in flow games. Its efficient mechanisms and core-selection obstruction are the nearest
strategic comparison. The present theorem keeps all coalition inequalities and allows subsidy; it does not
inherit their extra incentive guarantees.
Different monotonicity. Immorlica–Mahdian–Mirrokni compares an unchanged owner’s payment
when other owners join. Brânzei–Solymosi–Tijs (2007) compares participating subgames of unit owners
(final Definition 1, Theorems 5–6). Neither condition is silently identified with our same-owner bundle
comparison.
Same own-resource condition, different technology. Amigo–Belzarena–Vaton (2016 author version)
defines own-resource monotonicity and a strictly concave service model. Its Theorem 1 proves Shapley
own-resource monotonicity, while noting that Shapley can fail core membership. The supplement’s smooth
strictly concave example fits that inspected model and refutes a universal extension of the reported
projection simulations, not a proved theorem. The publisher final has not been compared in full. This
diagnostic is not homogeneous and is separate from the main theorem.
Established tools versus proposed contribution. Supporting prices, max-flow/min-cut, marginal
contributions, own-excluded selection, concentration, weighted counting, convex support theory and
uniform convergence are established. The proposed contribution is the joint sharp all-profile guarantee
and matching fixed-owner simple-network lower bound. The source search is bounded; historical priority
remains unconfirmed. The full technical comparison with Cao’s Superadditive Market Games (2026, DOI
10.1002/nav.70010) remains open; only its abstract and metadata have been inspected. Owen’s 1975 full
original and the ABV publisher-final text also remain unreviewed. These gaps do not establish nonoverlap.
What the result does not settle. K can be exponential in an encoded technology. The remaining
owner-count and resource-dimension gaps are now between logarithmic-over-logarithmic lower bounds and
square-root upper bounds. Exact enumeration and the supplementary certification procedure may require
exponential preprocessing. The paper asserts neither a recognized major open-problem resolution nor a
general theorem for private opportunity costs.
Review evidence. The main paper contains complete complexity, owner and dimension upper proofs
and the matching complexity lower. The supplement states the restricted architecture classes separately.
Source-hashed internal model audits and exact rational checks are preserved in the review-update packet.
These are not formal verification, specialist endorsement or priority confirmation. The 14 October PDFs
and archives are historical snapshots; the 17 October package exports the main paper, supplement and
this brief with their sources and scoped fresh checks. No outreach or submission has been performed.
Requested assessment. Check the cap/endpoint/deletion identities; check the reciprocal identity,
boundary faces and sharp scope of mask obstructions; check symmetrization, auxiliary budgets, weighted
transitions and cut inversion; identify any original theorem with the same fixed-owner, all-profile, fullcoverage guarantee.
