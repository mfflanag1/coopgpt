3    What the certificate does and does not prove
For k > 0, the first identity exhibits V ≤ 4T . The strict finite-market argument still requires the
main paper’s cases: if C2 > 0, the inequality is strict; if C2 = 0, then β = 0 and the residual bound
gives V ≤ 3T ; if k = 0, all residual weights vanish and W ≤ T . Compactness of the fractional
matching polytope lets strictness be applied at its maximizing point. Thus every fixed finite market
has s(M ) < T /3.
    The integral refinement uses C2 = β and an integer k ≥ 1. It bounds an integral obstruction
J(M, P ), not the full fractional optimum at a fixed market size. The main paper includes a fractional
packing with residual mass k = 1 and positive obstruction, ruling out that mistaken extension.
    The family with parameter ℓ ≥ 1 has n = 2ℓ + 1, T = 3(2ℓ + 1), all Hi = 3 and s(M ) = 2ℓ.
Consequently s(M )/T ↑ 1/3 without attaining it. It also attains the integral bound at k = ℓ + 1.
This is the current paper’s family, not the different triangle family used to bound protection within
the original core in the Acyclicity companion.
