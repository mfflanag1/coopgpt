DISPOSITION
Both packages are locally assembled and mathematically unchanged in their
model/proof sections. Static now cites the directly inspected extended-core
antecedent and compares the final Priority paper precisely. The old originals
remain material uncertainty for historical priority. Do not label either paper
fully source-cleared or silently replace these gaps with author approval.
The next evidence that can resolve them is lawfully obtained full originals
(e.g. the user's existing library access or supplied copies), followed by a
bounded exact statement/example comparison. No purchase or outreach is made.
An optional specialist review may help; it is not a compulsory submission gate.

SCIENTIFIC CLAIM BOUNDARIES TO RETAIN
Acyclicity: entire original strict-full-team core; tree guarantee1/K; cyclic
zero is a growing-size infimum, with positive finite cores. Nominal leximin,
duality, coordinate maxima and averaging are established tools.
Static: three equally sized finite sectors; nonnegative pair-additive full-team
worth; nonempty original core,T>0; frozen whole-core H_i/3 floors. Subsidy<T/3
in every finite market, supremum1/3; integral refinement is not a fixed-size
fractional optimum. Its family and objective differ from Acyclicity's.
