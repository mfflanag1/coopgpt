Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Sharp subsidies for protecting core maxima in three-sided assignment markets",
by Maxwell Flanagan.

The paper establishes a sharp subsidy bound for strict three-sided assignment
markets with nonnegative pair values, positive total worth and nonempty original
core. Requiring every coalition to be covered and every player to receive at least
one third of their maximum original-core payoff needs strictly less than T/3 in
each finite market; the supremum ratio is1/3. It also gives a sharp integral-
packing obstruction bound. Generic subsidy and extended-core ideas are prior.

a different extremal family. The truthful-production companion links payments
across resource-report profiles. This paper imposes no reporting incentives.
