1. ANTICHAIN: ORIGINAL PROOF ADJUDICATION
Read the complete ANTICHAIN_REDUCTION.txt and its integration in
/tmp/coop-noise-boundaries.tex, noise:antichain.

(a) Local graph and deletion value.
G_A is the structural union of paths meeting path-antichain A. Every path of G_A
meets A: before and after regions cannot overlap or cross outside A, because
that would concatenate a path meeting two A edges or create a directed cycle.
Deleting e in A therefore leaves precisely the through-(A minus e) flow problem:
F_{G_A}(c with e deleted)=R(A minus e). Setting capacities outside A-supported
paths to zero or omitting those edges makes the equivalence explicit.

(b) Why a minimum-capacity cut containing e forces c_e=delta_e.
This elementary statement holds in ANY fixed local graph, not just this reduction.
Let R=F_{G_A}(c) and R_-=F_{G_A}(c with e deleted). If a minimum-capacity cut
C of G_A contains e, then c(C)=R and deleting e gives a cut of capacity R-c_e.
Therefore R_-<=R-c_e. Conversely, remove all e-containing path flows from a
maximum flow of G_A. At most c_e units are removed, proving R_->=R-c_e.
Hence equality holds and delta_e=R-R_-=c_e.
Minimal counterexample assumptions give y_e>delta_e and y_e<=c_e, so no
minimum-capacity cut of G_A can contain e. Doing this for every e in A makes
any original local minimum-capacity cut disjoint from A.

Equivalent hinge check: a single-edge capacity change gives a minimum of affine
cut sums with slope zero or one. On 0<=t<=c_e it equals
min{R, R_-+t}. Thus reports t>delta_e preserve the through-A value R.
The hinge does not license treating an arbitrary inclusion-minimal cut as a
minimum-capacity one.

(c) The reviewer graph.
Edges m:s->u, e:u->t, uv:u->v, f,g:v->t all have capacity one. At e=1 the
four source-side vertex cuts have capacities:
  {m}:1; {m,f,g}:3; {e,uv}:2; {e,f,g}:3.
Thus R=1 and {e,f,g} is not a minimum-capacity cut. Deleting e keeps maxflow
one via m,uv,f, so delta_e=0; this is entirely consistent with no original
minimum-capacity cut containing e. In particular it does not refute (b).
I independently enumerated the four cuts at e=0 and e=1 with an inline exact
integer script. Both minima equal one. This is an exact finite check of the
reported graph, not the proof of the general claim.

(d) Simultaneous target profile.
For a cut C of G_A, let B=C intersection A. A flow realizing R(A minus B)
avoids B, so it is blocked by C minus B; hence its unchanged non-A capacity
is at least R(A minus B). At the joint profile c_e=y_e(c), the cut costs at
least Y(B)+R(A minus B). If B is nonempty, inclusion-minimality of the violating
A gives R(A minus B)>=Y(A minus B), so this is >=Y(A)>R(A). A cut disjoint
from A is unchanged. The original local minimum-capacity cut C* remains at R(A),
proving that the joint through-A value equals R(A). The empty complement case
uses R(empty)=Y(empty)=0 and is valid.
A through-A flow of value R(A) saturates every edge of C*: its total crossing
flow is at least R(A), the sum of capacities is exactly R(A), and nonnegative
flows cannot exceed their capacities. It is also a feasible full-network flow,
so each full-network effective cap on C* equals its unchanged original report.
The random barrier on C* is therefore fixed and independent of U_A.
This does NOT make C* a cut of the full graph; it only blocks every through-A
path and therefore every focal e-containing path.

(e) Local rival-flow estimate and unresolved step.
When the current focal effective cap exceeds R(A)-R(A minus e), every containing
cut of G_A has capacity greater than R(A). A local minimum-capacity cut excluding
e exists with effective-capacity sum at most R(A). For any full-network flow,
its e-path flow is bounded by that cut's random score; deleting those paths
bounds the endpoint maxflow marginal by the same score. Its expectation is at
most R(A)/2. This proves the local-rank bound without claiming that the local
cut bounds the full network's minimum-cut score.
The missing separate-witness-to-joint-payment comparison remains explicitly
unproved. Constant local rank and the shared barrier do not prove substitutes.
Thus no antichain theorem for arbitrary size is accidentally established.

Proof, replacing the paragraph beginning 'The own-report maxflow hinge':

The own-report maxflow hinge in the fixed local graph $G_A$ is
$F_{G_A}(c^t)=\min\{R(A),R(A\setminus\{e\})+t\}$.
Thus each witness with $z_e(c^t)>\delta_e$ preserves $R(A)$.
If a minimum-capacity cut $C$ of $G_A$ at the original profile contained $e$,
then deleting $e$ would give $R(A\setminus\{e\})\le R(A)-c_e$.
Removing the $e$-path flows from a local maximum flow gives the reverse
inequality, so $c_e=\delta_e$, contradicting $c_e\ge y_e>\delta_e$.
Consequently every original minimum-capacity cut $C^*$ of $G_A$ is disjoint
from $A$. All cuts in the following joint-profile argument are cuts of $G_A$;
no assertion that $C^*$ cuts the full graph is used.

Then retain the existing cut-sum argument, preferably writing 'a local
through-A maximum flow saturates C*' at its conclusion. Near-maximizing
witnesses can be chosen with the sum of their attained payoffs exceeding R(A)
if simultaneous witness profiles are discussed without attainment; the exact
joint y-target profile itself is well-defined even without attained suprema.
