## Primary sources and precise comparisons

### 1. Vazirani: established selectors and a prior implication for the half guarantee

Vijay V. Vazirani, *Fair Core Imputations for the Assignment Game: New Solution Concepts and Efficient Algorithms*. The [author's publication page](https://ics.uci.edu/~vazirani/) lists **ACM EC 2026**, verified on the assessment date. The latest [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11437) still identifies **v6, 20 November 2025**; that is the proof version inspected in the underlying source audit. Conference proceedings pagination was not verified.

Proof anchors in [v6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.11437v6): Definition 19, Algorithm 1, Remark 1, Lemmas 5 and 9, Theorem 8; printed pp. 17–18, 20, 22, 25, 27, 29–30. Full text and the algorithm page were inspected in the preserved research audit. Our deduction is as follows. Start from arbitrary rational core `y`; let `Omega_0` be the minimum initial essential payoff. Before permanent freezing at time `t_i`, coordinate motion is at most one per clock unit, and final `x_i >= t_i`. Thus `|x_i-y_i| <= t_i-Omega_0 <= x_i-Omega_0`, giving `2x_i >= y_i`. Initially fixed coordinates are unchanged; nonessential coordinates vanish. Maximizing over starting points gives the half guarantee. This is our inference from existing statements, not an explicit half theorem located in the paper.

The source algorithm is stated in a rational-weight setting. The direct proof in the manuscript treats real nonnegative weights and does not depend on extending the algorithm. The finite distance calculation requires the additional `T >= 2D+M` argument.

### 2. Older egalitarian and equality literature

F. Klijn, D. Vermeulen, H. Hamers, T. Solymosi, S. Tijs and J. Pere Villar, *Neighbour Games and the Leximax Solution*, MMOR 58 (2003), 191–208. The inspected [institutional text](https://pure.uvt.nl/ws/files/533861/110.pdf) is the **1999 working paper**, not the typeset 2003 article. Printed p. 6 defines descending lexicographic minimization; Theorem 3.1, p. 7, gives uniqueness for balanced games. Definition 3.7 and Theorem 3.9, pp. 8–9, give the alternating-adjustment characterization for neighbour games. This is substantial prior conceptual infrastructure; it does not state the arbitrary-graph finite distance formula.

Francesc Llerena, *The pairwise egalitarian solution for the assignment game*, ORL 40 (2012), 84–88, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.orl.2011.11.011). Only the publisher preview was inspected. Its introduction locates pairwise equal-split/core and Lorenz results in Section 3, Theorem 3.1, and stronger dominant-diagonal coincidences in Section 4, Theorem 4.1. Full hypotheses were not transcribed from a retrieved original. The manuscript proves its needed statements directly and does not claim this equality mechanism as new.

T. Solymosi and T. E. S. Raghavan, *Assignment games with stable core*, IJGT 30 (2001), 177–185, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s001820100072), is an established exact/dominant-diagonal source; complete original access remains limited. Solymosi's inspected [2023 full paper](https://unipub.lib.uni-corvinus.hu/9034/1/Sensitivity_of_fair_prices_in_assignment_market_ST_23.pdf), *Sensitivity of fair prices in assignment markets*, Theorem 3, p. 4, and Section 5, p. 9, give simultaneous side extrema and their midpoint. That midpoint is `(H_i+m_i)/2 >= H_i/2`. Hence half-of-coordinate-ideal protection for **some** selector long predates this work; midpoint and leximin need not coincide. Thompson (1981) and Núñez–Rafels' tau-value original remain citation-chain items rather than fully inspected sources here.

### 5. Representation theorems do not automatically preserve the selected payoffs

De Loera–Onn, [*All Rational Polytopes are Transportation Polytopes and all Polytopal Integer Sets are Contingency Tables*](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~deloera/RECENT_WORK/ipcouniversality.pdf), inspected author version, Theorem 1, PDF p. 2: a rational polytope is a bijective coordinate-erasing projection of a slim three-way transportation polytope with prescribed line sums. This concerns primal feasible polytopes. Our inference is that applying it here would require an additional standard integral matching-game realization of the **entire dual payoff core**, plus preservation of all added payoff coordinates, full sorted selectors and the `T` normalization. Those steps were not located.

Streekstra–Trudeau, [*Stable source connection and assignment problems as multi-period shortest path problems*](https://www.sdu.dk/-/media/files/om_sdu/institutter/ivoe/disc_papers/disc_2022/dpe8_2022.pdf), SDU DP 8/2022, 15 July 2022, was inspected in the underlying audit. Theorems 2–3, printed pp. 10–11, and Theorem 6, p. 21, give representations involving totally balanced games and strict multisided assignments as shortest-path games. The audited direction is not a theorem realizing arbitrary desired selector geometry as an entire standard three-sided assignment core.
