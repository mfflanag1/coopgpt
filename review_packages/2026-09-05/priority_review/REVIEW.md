## What was checked

Three independent lanes traced original papers, working versions, theses and citation chains; the root lane compared older generic fairness and optimization results. The important output is the theorem/hypothesis comparison, not the number of search hits. Searches included alternative terminology, permutations and translations, inverse binary matrices, weighted voting, named min-game classes and actual payoff-preserving assignment representations.

- [Stable-core report](stable_core/PRIORITY_REVIEW.md) verifies the elementary aspiration synthesis, original PES covariance discussion, and full final 2025 population paper. The latter access gap is now closed.
- [Asymptotic construction report](asymptotic/PRIORITY_REVIEW.md) derives the older voting-matrix substitution, including its exact selector formula and size bounds.
- [Assignment report](assignment/PRIORITY_REVIEW.md) compares canonical same-core constructions, sector replication, tree composition and recent 2026 work under their actual hypotheses.
- [Generic fairness report](root/PRIORITY_REVIEW.md) distinguishes aggregate welfare loss and sorted approximation error from opposing labeled-allocation distance. An exact affine control resolves an apparent overly broad coincidence theorem in a 2019 load-balancing source.

Primary locators include the [original PES paper](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/109504649/Dietzenbacher_2016_The_procedural_egalitarian_solution.pdf), [classical segment working paper](https://www.sdu.dk/-/media/files/om_sdu/institutter/ivoe/disc_papers/disc_2008/dpbe3_2008.pdf), [2014 matrix theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1404.2305), [2015 Fibonacci construction](https://air.uniud.it/retrieve/e27ce0c2-77a3-055e-e053-6605fe0a7873/10.5923.j.jgt.20150402.03.pdf), and [classical matching reduction](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-011-0273-y). ## Remaining uncertainty and recommended direction

The original multisided papers of Quint (1991), Lucas (1995), and the 2023 *Extreme Core Points in Multisided Assignment Problems* preprint remain uninspected in full. Rosenmüller's 2000 book is a potentially relevant finite MIN-game source that remains inaccessible; Isbell's original attribution was checked through later primary work, not through his original full text. Some recent thesis material was only indexed. These are explicit gaps, not evidence that the results are absent.

Proceed with the consolidated quantitative comparison as the main review target, and present the stable-core result as a modest note candidate. The next useful external questions concern the exact finite distance bound, the sparse full-core triple realization, the universal overlap inequality and their value to the literature. Broad questions about whether segment cores, Fibonacci matrices or general selector noncovariance are new have already received adverse answers.

Do not expand the research program on the strength of this audit. A specialist may still identify an earlier equivalent result, an immediate consequence we missed, or insufficient significance despite correctness. Outreach materials are revised to invite precisely those judgments and remain unsent.
