## Stable core

To: Bas Dietzenbacher <b.dietzenbacher@maastrichtuniversity.nl>

Subject: Priority question on stable cores and egalitarian stability

Dear Dr Dietzenbacher,

I have prepared a short draft addressing the stable-core question in your 2021 paper, *Monotonicity and Egalitarianism*. It gives a six-player game with a von Neumann–Morgenstern stable core whose procedural egalitarian claims are inefficient. The proposed contribution is the stable-core separation. The draft explicitly credits Mauri's earlier exact-game example and does not claim that half of the question as new.

Would you be willing to offer a brief priority assessment: has the stable-core implication been resolved elsewhere, and is there a convention or later result that changes this example's interpretation? Any pointer to a missing source would be valuable; a full referee report is not expected.

## Unrestricted asymptotic separation

To: Francesc Llerena Garrés <francesc.llerena@urv.cat>

Subject: Priority question on disagreement between egalitarian core selections

Dear Professor Llerena,

Given your work on egalitarian and Lorenz allocations, I would appreciate a brief priority assessment of the attached draft. For singleton-normalized exact TU games, let s_n be the supremum of the L1 distance between the full ascending-leximin and descending-lexicographic-minimum core allocations, divided by the grand-coalition worth. The draft proves n(2-s_n) tends to 2, using a convex-set overlap bound and explicit exact games with fully characterized cores.

The questions are whether this quantitative result, or its whole-core realization argument, is already known under different terminology, and whether our definitions match the relevant comparison in the egalitarian literature. Any missing source or concern about the claimed contribution would be especially helpful.

The work was developed with AI assistance and has undergone internal mathematical and exact-arithmetic checks, but has not received independent specialist review. The attached source package separates the conventional proof, calibration evidence, and unresolved priority questions. A full referee report is not expected.

Thank you for considering it,
Maxwell Flanagan
