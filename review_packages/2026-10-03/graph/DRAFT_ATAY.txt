Dear Professor Atay,

I would value a brief assessment of a 10-page draft, “Acyclicity and simultaneous protection of individual core maxima.”

In a strict multisided assignment model with equal sector sizes, nonnegative pair values, only full teams productive, and nonempty entire core, the draft proves a sharp 1/K protection guarantee on trees and vanishing best simultaneous protection on every cyclic allowed graph. The latter optimizes over all core allocations; the poorly protected player may vary with the allocation.

Your papers on multisided markets on m-partite graphs and supplier-firm-buyer games are the closest model precedents. I would particularly value your view on whether our strict-full-team formulation is correctly distinguished from those models and whether the all-selection cyclic impossibility is anticipated.

Could you point out an earlier equivalent result, a model mismatch, or a reason the proposed contribution is not substantial enough for a paper? If it appears worth pursuing, I would also welcome advice on the most important proof step to have independently checked. A short pointer would already be helpful.

The draft credits the bilateral half-optimal core and earlier tree/cycle results. Comparisons with the complete originals of Quint, Lucas and Stuart remain incomplete. It was developed with AI assistance and has internal exact checks, but no independent human validation or confirmed novelty is claimed.

I have attached the manuscript; the source and reproducible checks are available if useful.
