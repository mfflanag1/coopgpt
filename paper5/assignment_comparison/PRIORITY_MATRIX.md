## Evidence that changes the prior assessment

1. The two-sided source audit gives a complete derivation from prior algorithmic statements, with printed page anchors and independently checked interpretation. This supersedes the September 4 wording that treated the half guarantee as merely an unlocated possible new lemma.
2. The three-sided audit inspects the 2026 Martínez-de-Albéniz–Rafels–Ybern paper, absent from the first audit. Its sectorwise simultaneous-extrema property fails for the recurrence family, so it does not directly subsume the construction under any reordering of the sectors.
3. An explicit 2x2x2 cube has third mixed difference one. Any sum of pair-value functions has mixed difference zero. The construction therefore contains genuine three-way interaction, outside the inspected Quint/Stuart pair-additive classes.
4. The transportation-polytope universality and shortest-path representation results inspected do not supply a reverse, entire-core, selector-preserving realization. Their existence alone cannot establish priority against this claim.

## What remains unknown

Llerena (2012) and several older foundations were available only through primary previews or institutional records. The two detailed audits state access levels individually; no abstract or citation-chain description is represented as a fully inspected original.

The defensible priority verdict is **no equivalent main theorem located in the inspected literature**. It is not established first discovery. No expert has reviewed the package, and the work has not been formally verified in a proof assistant. The graph/triple comparison addresses a specific quantitative aspect of the location of fairness selections; it does not claim to solve Vazirani's open geometric-characterization question in full.

## Judgment on significance and direction

The combined result is more persuasive than three separate notes: it states one problem, identifies an exact pairwise bound, exhibits a protected three-sided class, and proves the unrestricted three-way extreme using small integer values and a low-dimensional core. Normalized L1 distance divided by two is the fraction of surplus that must change recipients between rules. Both outcomes remain efficient and core-feasible; this is neither lost welfare nor evidence that one rule is normatively wrong.

The current weakness is priority and economic scope. The extremal markets are deliberately constructed with fixed-zero dummies, exact binding constraints and replication. No evidence establishes prevalence in observed markets or stability of the extreme under general perturbations. I would advance this as a candidate theorem paper for specialist scrutiny, not describe it as a field-level breakthrough.

The next action with the highest value is a source-and-proof review focused on the sparse three-sided construction, the two inaccessible foundational originals, and the sharp finite distance argument. Another round of small numerical improvements has lower value. If new mathematics is subsequently authorized, a natural follow-up is whether economically motivated restrictions or perturbation tolerance retain a nontrivial protection; the exact finite-n coefficient is secondary.
