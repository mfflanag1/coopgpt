1. Moretti–Norde (2021), A note on weighted multi-glove games.
https://d-nb.info/1240670206/34
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00355-021-01337-8
Directly checked Definition 3.1 and Theorem 3.2 (PDF pp. 3–4), and final
weight-domain remark (p. 11). The minimum-of-pool-sums game and its core
existence are established, including real weights. Zero owners are null;
an all-zero pool gives the zero game. The manuscript proves its required
core construction directly, without importing the source's other zero-weight
characterizations. Private own-capacity reporting and the joint subsidized
payment guarantees are different questions. No new game model is claimed.

2. Kurz (2013), Measuring voting power in convex policy spaces.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.5936
https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5936
Directly checked arXiv v1, Definition 6.11, printed p. 17: the absolute
continuous Banzhaf endpoint-difference integral is established. Our identity
z_i=T BZI_i(f_d) is an application to a report-dependent capped minimum game.
The proposed contribution is its joint coverage, own-report monotonicity,
and budget analysis. Later continuous-value and resource-sharing literature
has not been exhausted. Versioned PDF retrieval failed, but the unversioned
original succeeded; there is no unresolved access issue for this definition.

3. Huang–Mei–Yang–Cao (2025), Mechanism Design in Max-Flows.
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1
Directly checked Sections 2.1–2.2, Example 1, Proposition 1, Definition 2,
Theorem 1, and Section 4.2. Binding underreports and payment utility without
use costs are established. Their efficient truthful mechanisms need not be
in the core. Serial parallel-edge layers give our special topology. Allowing
subsidy while requiring all coalition inequalities changes their optimization
question. Their capacity cross-monotonicity should not be confused with
population cross-monotonicity. No assertion is made that their impossibility
example alone implies our quantitative factor-two lower family.
