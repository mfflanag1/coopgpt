CURRENT CLAIMS AND REVIEW TARGETS -- 16 October 2026

COMMON MODEL
Finite fixed actual owners; arbitrary fixed nonnegative resource bundles;
only coordinatewise binding underreports; payment utility without usage
costs; nonnegative payments; every coalition covered at every real report
profile. T is grand-coalition production. No population change, private cost,
overreport, coalition incentive or measured-infrastructure claim.

1. SHARP REPRESENTATION/CUT COMPLEXITY -- EXISTING CENTRAL RESULT
Finite nonnegative min-linear technologies with at most K supporting forms:
worst optimal total-payment factor(1+o(1))log K/loglog K.
Same leading constant attained on simple DAGs with at most K structural cuts
and one fixed owner per edge. Main Sections2 and4 contain complete proofs.
These unchanged foundations are not freshly re-audited by the16Oct reviews;
the dated14Oct/7Oct audit records remain their provenance.

2. GENERAL OWNER/DIMENSION BOUNDS -- NEW MAIN SECTION3
Every finite-valued monotone concave degree-one homogeneous f on R_+^d,
including downward boundary discontinuities, admits total factor
min{d, n sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2sqrt(d)}.
No finite-form representation needed. Owner bound uniform in dimension;
dimension bound uniform in owner count. Rule selection uses fixed public
parameters before reports. No pointwise minimum of payment vectors and no
polynomial-time oracle claim. Full proofs in main and supplement.

3. ARCHITECTURE LOWER BOUNDS -- NEW SUPPLEMENT SECTION
Specified report-independent masks, common supports selected using only
open reports, and endpoint payments to open owners cannot improve
square-root owner growth; leading owner constant sqrt(2) is sharp within
the stated architecture. Dimension growth order sqrt(d) is also necessary.
Separate proofs handle T caps and maximum-coalition-marginal V_i caps.
Arbitrary correlated/asymmetric masks and randomized finite global support
selection are allowed; the empty-mask support law is fixed. A precise
canonical relative-support convention is addressed separately.
These are NOT unrestricted square-root lower bounds. Dimension leading
constant is not claimed sharp. No claim about all report-dependent masks.

4. STILL OPEN
Unrestricted owner and concave-dimension rates lie between
(1-o(1))log n/loglog n or log d/loglog d and the square-root upper bounds.
The supplement's Owner-count range and Concave dimension gap theorems
give the lower parameter translations. Historical nonconcave exact d bound
does not resolve the concave problem. Computational preprocessing can be
exponential. Novelty/significance remain specialist questions.

PRIORITY OF REVIEW
First check relative-face supports and closed-report independence, endpoint
integration, reciprocal scalar identity, and zero-profile conventions.
Then check lower-bound quantifiers, empty-mask anchors, and realization
of active supports under the same fixed technology and owner set.
The existing sharp K proof remains the central paper; new n,d bounds make
its dependence on economic instance size more informative.
