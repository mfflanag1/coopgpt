FREE ORIGINAL-SOURCE COMPARISON UPDATE -- 16 October 2026
Scope: new owner-count/dimension guarantees and closest report-monotonicity
precedents. This is a bounded comparison, not confirmation of novelty.

1. BACHRACH ET AL.2009 AND2018 -- STATIC FUNDING, FULL ORIGINALS READ
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.4385
https://jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/11270/26463/20899
2009 Theorem3.5 bounds additional subsidy by(sqrt(n)-1)v(N).
Published2018 Theorem7, printedp994, gives total funding factor sqrt(n),
asymptotically tight for superadditive games. The2009 statement is not a
strict improvement over this2018 theorem. Their subsidy is chosen for one
game, without a shared rule or incentives across capacity reports. Our
concave homogeneous games have an efficient core at every profile. The
proposed result links those profiles under own-bundle monotonicity.
The2018 static LP and balanced-weight dual are established ingredients.

2. HUANG--MEI--YANG--CAO2025v1 -- SAME BINDING-REPORT INCENTIVES
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1
Full original HTML inspected. The model uses fixed capacity owners, binding
underreports and payment utility. The paper proves efficient-core-selection
obstructions and positive truthful efficient mechanisms, including Shapley
(Proposition1) and minimal-cut mechanisms. These do not guarantee membership
in every profile's efficient core. Our positive result permits external
funding and retains every coalition inequality; it does not inherit their
additional split/merge or other-owner guarantees. Own-report monotonicity
alone is explicitly prior art, not the proposed contribution.

3. AMIGO--BELZARENA--VATON2016 -- POSITIVE SHAPLEY RESULT CREDITED
https://hal.science/hal-01400498v1
Inspected author manuscript, preserved locally from the earlier free-source
audit as ABV16-hal.pdf; printedp11 Theorem1 and the following discussion.
Theorem1 proves own-resource monotonicity of Shapley while noting that it
need not satisfy core stability. Projection into the efficient core is a
different mechanism, with monotonicity supported by simulations in that
version rather than a universal proof. The existing strictly concave
projection counterexample concerns extension of that computational evidence.
It does not refute Theorem1 and is not homogeneous. The publisher's final
text has not been compared in full. New main/brief text now credits the
positive Shapley theorem explicitly.

4. CAO2026 -- FULL COMPARISON UNRESOLVED

Superadditive Market Games. Publisher abstract/metadata inspected; no free

5. PRECISE PRIORITY QUESTION
Does earlier work establish a single nonnegative own-resource-monotone
payment rule on ALL nonnegative reports of FIXED actual owners, covering
every coalition with subsidy bounded independently of representation size
by O(sqrt(n)) or independently of owner count by O(sqrt(d))? Related terms
include endowment-monotone core selections, production allocation schemes,
binding capacity disclosure and subsidized stability. Population monotonicity
and static core existence are insufficient on their own. The restricted
mask/common-support lower bounds need their own priority check; they are
not unrestricted lower bounds. No recognized-major-open-problem claim.
