The paper asks how much external funding is needed to retain every coalition's
production guarantee while eliciting resources from fixed owners. For a
technology represented by K fixed nonnegative linear forms, it constructs a
deterministic rule on all nonnegative real report profiles with total payments
at most (1+o(1))T log K/log log K, where T is grand-coalition production. A
matching lower bound holds for the worst case with at most K structural cuts,
even on simple directed acyclic networks with one strategic owner per edge.
The sharp leading constant is one for this worst-case complexity parameter.

For every finite-valued monotone concave degree-one homogeneous technology,
the paper also gives total-payment factor
min{d, n sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2 sqrt(d)}, with n fixed owners and d resources.
The supplement's square-root lower barriers concern specified fixed-mask,
common-support payment architectures. The unrestricted owner-count and
resource-dimension rates remain open.

The economic question concerns one rule linking reports, although each
individual profile admits an efficient core. The incentive interpretation uses
binding componentwise underreports and payment-only utility. A total-payment
factor B means subsidy at most (B-1)T. K may be exponential in a compact
description; polynomial preprocessing is not claimed.

The prepared materials include the main proofs, an indexed supplement, and
scoped exact-check code with execution records and source hashes. Established
supporting-price, flow and marginal-contribution results are credited. Internal
Codex/Claude reviews and finite checks are not human peer review or formal
verification. The bounded literature comparison does not certify historical
priority; the full comparison with Cao's 2026 Superadditive Market Games and
the ABV publisher-final version remain unresolved; Owen's full 1975 original
remains unread.
