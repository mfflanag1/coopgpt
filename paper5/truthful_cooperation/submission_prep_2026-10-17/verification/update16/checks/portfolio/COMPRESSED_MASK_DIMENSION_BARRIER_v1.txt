RESOURCE-DIMENSION BARRIERS FOR FIXED-MASK SUPPORT/ENDPOINT HYBRIDS
15 October 2026. Candidate proof; independent review pending.

CLASS AND RESULTS
Fix owners, a finite nonnegative min-linear technology, and any report-independent
law on masks B. Select a common nonnegative global linear support at the open
aggregate x_B, using only B, technology and open reports, possibly at random.
Open owners receive their endpoint marginal f(x_B)-f(x_B-b_i). Closed owners
receive their support price capped either at T=f(sum b_i), or at
 V_i=max_{S subset[N]\{i}}[f(b_i+b(S))-f(b(S))].
Average the mask vectors. All caps and payments below are total-payment factors.

(A) For global-T caps, there are n-owner examples in dimension d=n on which
every such fixed-mask rule has worst-profile factor (sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(d).
(B) Even with the stronger V_i caps, there are n-owner examples in dimension
d_n=2n+ceil(n^(3/4)) on which every such rule has factor
(1/2-o(1))sqrt(d_n).
Thus the O(sqrt(d)) general dimension upper has the correct ORDER within both
fixed-mask architectures. This DOES NOT establish an unrestricted sqrt(d) lower.
The earlier sharp sqrt(2n) V-cap owner lower uses a different, larger technology.

COMMON FINITE SUPPORT CONSTRUCTION
Take n>=9 and a nonincreasing sequence delta_0,...,delta_(n-1)>=0, summing1.
Suppose delta_l=0 for l>=m, with 1<=m<n, and delta_0>=1/n+1/n^2.
Write r_l=n-l, v_l=sum_(j=l)^(n-1)delta_j, so v_0=1,v_n=0.
Then a_l=v_l/r_l decreases with l for1<=l<n: indeed
v_(l+1)<=(r_l-1)delta_l implies v_l/r_l>=v_(l+1)/(r_l-1).
It also follows that a_l<=a_1=(1-delta_0)/(n-1)<1/n.

Put epsilon=1/(100n^6), t=1+(n-1)epsilon, A=n^2. For every nonempty proper
subset S of[n], define w^S_j=A/|S|^2 for j in S and a_(|S|) otherwise.
Let
 f(x)=min{(sum x_j)/n, w^S.x:empty!=S!=[n]}.
It is finite, continuous, monotone, concave, and degree-one homogeneous.
At the regular profile P, owner i reports e_i+epsilon sum_(j!=i)e_j.
The full aggregate is t times the all-ones vector. The baseline is uniquely
minimal there, with T=t, since each subset form contributes at least At/|S|>t.

For a nonempty proper mask let C be its closed set, l=|C|, r=n-l.
Its aggregate equals r epsilon on C and1+(r-1)epsilon elsewhere. The unique
minimum is w^C. Here are all comparisons, so no tie priority is exploited:
1. Any S meeting an open coordinate costs at least A/(n-1)^2>1.
2. The C form costs v_l+epsilon E_l, where
   E_l=A r/l+(r-1)v_l, 0<=E_l<=2n^3.
   Its gap below the baseline r/n before the epsilon perturbation is
   r/n-v_l >=r(delta_0-1/n)/(n-1)>=1/n^3.
   Since2n^3 epsilon<1/n^3, it stays below r/n and hence below the actual
   baseline r t/n; in particular its value is below1.
3. If nonempty S is strictly contained in C, its first-coordinate contribution
   A r epsilon/|S| is strictly larger than A r epsilon/l. Its off-S coefficient
   a_(|S|)>=a_l multiplies all open coordinates and additional nonnegative
   closed coordinates. Hence it is strictly more expensive than C.
These exhaust all subset forms. The active aggregate is positive in every
coordinate, so ANY global supporting form has this unique gradient; randomized
support selection or redundant forms cannot change it.

Consequently every closed raw support price is at least
 A[1+(l-1)epsilon]/l^2 >=n^2/(n-1)^2 >t=T.
The masked production values are exactly
 f(x_B)=v_l+epsilon E_l for1<=l<n,
 f(x_N)=t=v_0+epsilon(n-1), and f(x_empty)=0.
Each open endpoint marginal, divided by T, is therefore at least
 delta_l-eta, where eta=1/n^3.
Indeed its unnormalized error is at most2n^3 epsilon, normalization adds at
most(n-1)epsilon since delta_l<=1, and (2n^3+n)epsilon<1/n^3.
All endpoint marginals are nonnegative by monotonicity, even when this lower
bound is negative. The normalized maximum coalition marginal V_i/T is at least
delta_0-eta for every owner, by using the grand coalition's endpoint marginal.

PART A: ONE REGULAR PROFILE, GLOBAL-T CAP, d=n
Let m be the largest integer with
 S_m=sum_(l=0)^(m-1) (m-l)/(n-l)<=1.
For n>=9, 2<=m<n/2. The latter follows from
S_m>=m(m+1)/(2n) and sqrt(2n)<n/2 for n>8.
Set delta_l=(m-l)/[(n-l)S_m] for0<=l<m, zero afterward.
These sum1 and decrease, because (m-l)/(n-l) decreases for m<n.
Also delta_0=m/(nS_m)>=m/n>=2/n, satisfying the common construction.

For a nonempty mask with l<m, its normalized budget is at least
 l+(n-l)(delta_l-eta)
 =l+(m-l)/S_m-(n-l)eta >=m-1/n^2=:C_n.
For m<=l<n, closed payments alone give at least l>=m. At the full mask l=0
the same endpoint bound applies, so EVERY nonempty mask has budget >=C_n.

To handle the empty mask in this SAME f and SAME n-owner domain, make n
cyclic anchors indexed by j. Let S_j={j,...,j+m-1} modulo n, and let z_j
have coordinate tau=m/(4n^3) on S_j and1 elsewhere. At Q_j all n owners
report the identical bundle z_j. Because a_m=0,
 T_j=n f(z_j)<=n A tau/m=1/4; it is strictly positive.
Every empty-mask global support w has total mass s=sum w_e>=f(1)=1.
Uniformly over the n cyclic windows, expected scarce weight is (m/n)s.
Thus at least a fraction1-2m/n of windows have scarce weight<=s/2, and on
those windows w.z_j>=s/2>=1/2>=T_j. All n closed owners are then capped
at T_j. Uniform anchor average normalized budget is at least A_n=n-2m>0.
This holds for every realization of the empty support and so also randomized
support selection. The anchor list is fixed independently of the selector.

Mix P with probability A_n/(A_n+C_n) and uniform anchors with probability
C_n/(A_n+C_n). Every mask, empty or nonempty, has weighted normalized budget
at least L_n=C_n A_n/(C_n+A_n). Average over any fixed mask/support law;
some one of these n+1 profiles attains at least L_n.
Since S_m=m(m+1)/(2n)+O(m^3/n^2) at m=O(sqrt n), maximality gives
m=(sqrt(2)+o(1))sqrt(n). Hence L_n=(sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n), with d=n.

PART B: V_i CAP, ONE REGULAR BLOCK AND ONE LEONTIEF BLOCK
Take m=floor(sqrt(2n)), put
 H=sum_(l=1)^(m-1) (m-l)/(n-l), a=1/(1+H),
 delta_0=a, delta_l=a(m-l)/(n-l) for1<=l<m, and zero afterward.
These sum1 and decrease. Since H<=m-1, a>=1/m and thus
a-1/n>=(n-m)/(mn)>=1/n^2, satisfying the common construction.
Let f be the corresponding regular technology above. Add a separate Leontief
block on D=n+R resources, R=ceil(n^(3/4)), and use the single fixed technology
 F(x,z)=f(x)+min_(1<=e<=D)z_e.
The resource dimension is d_n=n+D=2n+R. This sum is still finite min-linear.

At the regular profile activate only the f block and report zero on the
Leontief block. Every support restricts to the unique active-block gradient,
so the same raw-price and endpoint estimates hold. For each owner V_i/T>=a-eta
and V_i<=T; closed raw prices exceed T, hence their V_i caps pay at least
(a-eta)T. For1<=l<m, total normalized payment is therefore at least
 l(a-eta)+(n-l)(delta_l-eta)
 =am-n eta >=am-1/n^2.
For m<=l<n, closed payments alone give at least l(a-eta)>=am-1/n^2.
For l=0, endpoints give n(a-eta)>=am-1/n^2.
Thus EVERY nonempty mask has budget >=C'_n=am-2/n^2>0 (a harmless extra
error margin is retained), regardless of support randomization.

For the empty-mask anchors, index Leontief resources cyclically modulo D.
For each j take the n-resource scarce window S_j={j,...,j+n-1}. Give owner i
one unit of scarce resource j+i-1, zero on the other scarce resources, and
D units of EVERY abundant resource outside S_j. All f-block reports are zero.
Grand production is1. Removing any owner kills its unique scarce resource,
so grand production drops to0 and V_i=T=1 for all owners.
Every empty global support restricted to this block has sum at least1, hence
some coordinate e has weight at least1/D. This coordinate is abundant in
exactly R of the D cyclic anchors. On each of those anchors, every owner's
raw price is at least D*(1/D)=1, and each V_i cap is1. The uniform anchor
average normalized budget is at least A'_n=nR/D.
This argument is pointwise in the support: its maximum coordinate may depend
on the realization, but the anchor family and uniform weights do not.

Combine the regular profile and uniform anchors as before to obtain
 L'_n=C'_n A'_n/(C'_n+A'_n).
Here m~sqrt(2n), H~m^2/(2n)~1, a~1/2, so C'_n~sqrt(n/2).
Also A'_n~n^(3/4), hence L'_n~sqrt(n/2). Since d_n~2n,
 L'_n=(1/2-o(1))sqrt(d_n).
If desired this extends to all sufficiently large integer resource dimensions
by adjoining unused coordinates: d_(n+1)/d_n tends to1. This padding concerns
different fixed problems; the lower within each problem always uses one fixed
technology and the same n owners on every witness profile.

BOUNDARY AND DEPENDENCIES
The V_i truncation lemma and mask eligibility are recorded in
VCAP_MASK_OBSTRUCTION_v1.txt; the lower itself uses only the stated payments.
Finite min-linear concavity, supporting hyperplanes, Markov's inequality and
weighted averaging are established ingredients. The explicit compressed
construction and bounds are candidate project advances, with priority unconfirmed.
No report-dependent mask law, incompatible owner-specific supports, or other
truthful mechanism is excluded. In particular the unrestricted dimension gap
remains between log d/loglog d and O(sqrt d), not a proved Theta(sqrt d).
