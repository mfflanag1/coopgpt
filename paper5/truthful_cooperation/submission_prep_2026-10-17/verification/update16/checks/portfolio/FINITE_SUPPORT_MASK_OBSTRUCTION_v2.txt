SHARP sqrt(2n) BARRIER FOR FINITE-SUPPORT MASK HYBRIDS
15 October 2026. Version2; strengthened construction, independent review pending.
Earlier version remains unchanged. This is a class lower, not an unrestricted lower.

CLASS AND THEOREM
Use n fixed owners and one fixed finite nonnegative min-linear technology F.
Choose ANY report-independent law on masks B subset[N]. For each mask select a
common nonnegative global linear support ell_B at x_B=sum_(i in B)b_i. It may
depend on F, B and the open reports, and may randomize. No closed report enters
its selection. Pay
 Y_i(B)=min{ell_B(b_i),T} for i notin B;
 Y_i(B)=F(x_B)-F(x_B-b_i) for i in B,
then average, where T=F(sum_i b_i). This includes arbitrary fixed iid or
fixed-cardinality mixtures and any finite-form tie priorities. At the all-empty
mask the support law is independent of the entire report profile.

For n>=5 let m be the greatest positive integer satisfying
 m(m+7)/2 <= n-1.
Then there is ONE fixed continuous finite min-linear F on nm resources such
that every rule in the class has worst-profile factor at least
 L_n=m(n-1)/(n-1+m)=(sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n).
The general upper n sqrt(2/(n+1)) has the same leading constant. Thus sqrt(2)
is sharp for this architecture even when finite global supports are selected
optimally and mask bits are arbitrarily correlated. This does NOT prove a
sqrt(n) lower bound for unrestricted truthful coalition-covering mechanisms.

FIXED TECHNOLOGY
Put A=n^2. For k=1,...,m and 1<=s<n set
 a_(s,k)=(n-s)/n^2 if s<k, and a_(s,k)=0 if s>=k.
For S subset[N], 1<=|S|<n, define the nonnegative form
 w^(S,k)_j=A/|S|^2 for j in S, and a_(|S|,k) for j notin S.
On R_+^n let
 f_k(x)=min{(sum_j x_j)/n, w^(S,k).x: empty!=S!=N}.
On m separate n-coordinate blocks set F=sum_k f_k.
Expanding the finite product of form choices shows F is finite min-linear.
It is continuous, nonnegative, monotone, concave and degree-one homogeneous.
No free infrastructure or changing ownership is used.

REGULAR WITNESS PROFILES AND UNIQUE SUPPORTS
Let epsilon=1/(100n^6), t=1+(n-1)epsilon. At P_k owner i reports
 e_i+epsilon sum_(j!=i)e_j in block k and zero in all other blocks.
T_k=t: at the full aggregate, the baseline form is uniquely minimal since
each subset form contributes at least A t/|S|>n t>t.

For a proper nonempty mask let C be the closed set, l=|C|, r=n-l.
The active block aggregate is r epsilon on C and 1+(r-1)epsilon outside C.
The uniquely minimal form is w^(C,k), FOR EVERY k. To verify:
- Any S meeting an open coordinate costs >=A/(n-1)^2>1.
- The C form costs
  v_C=A r epsilon/l + a_(l,k) r[1+(r-1)epsilon].
  If l>=k this is <r/n. If l<k, its leading term is r^2/n^2,
  and its remaining term is <=epsilon(n^3+n).
  The gap r/n-r^2/n^2=rl/n^2>=1/(2n), whereas
  epsilon(n^3+n)<1/(2n); thus v_C<r/n<=baseline cost.
  Also v_C<1 (r/n<1). For l>=k that conclusion is immediate.
- If S is a strict nonempty subset of C, its first term A r epsilon/|S|
  strictly exceeds A r epsilon/l; its off-S coefficient a_(|S|,k) is
  at least a_(l,k), and it multiplies every open coordinate plus additional
  nonnegative closed coordinates. Hence its value is strictly larger.
These cases exhaust all S. At the full mask the unique form is the baseline.

At these positive active-block aggregates, EVERY global linear supporting form
restricts to that unique active-block gradient: its restriction majorizes f_k
and touches f_k at an interior point where the minimum is differentiable.
Inactive blocks contribute zero. Randomized ties or redundant forms cannot
alter the forced active-block gradient.

EXACT CLOSED PAYMENTS AND THRESHOLD ENDPOINTS
For every closed owner at a proper nonempty mask,
 ell_B(b_i)>=A[1+(l-1)epsilon]/l^2>=n^2/(n-1)^2>t=T_k.
Thus each closed owner receives EXACTLY T_k, irrespective of k.

If l=k-1, each open owner's on value is at least r^2/n^2 (including l=0,
where the on value is t>=1). Removing that owner makes k owners closed, so
its off value is A(r-1)epsilon/k. Therefore
 Delta >=r^2/n^2 -n^3 epsilon.
The normalized TOTAL endpoint payment to the r open owners is at least
 [r^3/n^2-r n^3 epsilon]/t.
Since r=n-l, r^3/n^2>=n-3l. Moreover t=1+(n-1)epsilon,
r n^3 epsilon<=n^4 epsilon, and r^3/n^2<=n. Thus
 [r^3/n^2-r n^3 epsilon]/t
 >=r^3/n^2 -n^2 epsilon-n^4 epsilon
 >=n-3l-(n^4+n^2)epsilon >=n-3m-1=:D.
The penultimate inequality uses 1/(1+u)>=1-u for the nonnegative leading
term and bounds the subtracted term by n^4 epsilon. Our epsilon gives
(n^4+n^2)epsilon<1. Other endpoint payments are nonnegative by monotonicity.
The removed-owner mask remains nonempty because k<=m<n.

TRIANGULAR PROFILE WEIGHTS
Let S0=m(m+1)/2 and pi_k=(m-k+1)/S0. These are positive and sum to1.
For every proper mask (including the full mask), average normalized budgets
across P_k using pi_k. Every closed owner contributes1 in every profile;
when l=0 there are no such terms. At k=l+1 the extra endpoint lower D applies.
Thus, if 0<=l<m, the average is at least
 l+D pi_(l+1)=l+D(m-l)/S0.
Our choice of m gives S0<=n-3m-1=D, so this is >=m.
If m<=l<n, the closed payments alone give at least l>=m.
Hence the weighted regular-profile budget is >=m for EVERY proper mask,
independently of which owners are open and of the selected support.

ALL-EMPTY-MASK ANCHORS
At B=empty let w be the selected global support, possibly random, restricted
to block1. It has nonnegative entries and s=sum_j w_j>=f_1(1)=1.
At most one index j has sum_(e!=j)w_e<1/2: two such indices would imply
w_j+w_h>2s-1>=s, contradicting nonnegativity of the other entries.

Set delta=1/(4n^3). At Q_j all n owners report the SAME bundle
 z_j=delta e_j+sum_(e!=j)e_e in block1, zero in other blocks.
Its positive grand production T_j=n f_1(z_j)<=n A delta=1/4.
For at least n-1 indices j, w.z_j>=1/2>=T_j, so all n owners receive T_j.
The uniform average normalized budget over Q_1,...,Q_n is therefore >=n-1.
This holds for every realization of w. Consequently randomizing supports,
including correlations with the mask law, cannot avoid the anchor bound.
The full anchor list and its weights are independent of the selector.

COMBINING INSIDE ONE FIXED DOMAIN
Give total weight (n-1)/(n-1+m) to the triangular regular-profile distribution,
and weight m/(n-1+m) to the uniform anchor distribution. All other payments
are nonnegative. For every proper mask the regular part contributes >=L_n;
for the empty mask the anchor part contributes >=L_n. Integrate over ANY fixed
mask and support law. The weighted average of normalized expected budgets
at this finite list of profiles is >=L_n. Some profile has factor>=L_n.
All profiles use the same F and the same n owners. Only their reports change.
Finally m=sqrt(2n)+O(1), so L_n=(sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n).

ELIGIBILITY AND SCOPE
For each fixed mask, closed prices exclude the recipient's report; open
endpoint marginals are own-bundle monotone. The open endpoint marginal
majorizes the selected linear support contribution, and capped support terms
cover every coalition. Thus every fixed-mask vector is eligible.
The lower applies only to the stated support/endpoint payment architecture.
It does not exclude report-dependent mask choices with separately proved
incentives, incompatible owner-specific supports, or a different mechanism.
Inactive resource blocks are zero in the witnesses, but every chosen support
is FINITE and active-block mask aggregates are interior. No +infinity extension
or arbitrary minimizer priority is exploited.

CONTRIBUTION BOUNDARY
This sharp leading-constant architecture lower complements the new general
O(sqrt n) upper. It supplies a precise reason that changing fixed mask weights,
mask correlations, or finite supporting-price selection alone cannot obtain
o(sqrt n). It leaves the unrestricted owner-count lower at log n/loglog n.
Min-linear concavity, support/core facts, and weighted averaging are established.
The construction is a candidate project-level advance; priority is unconfirmed.
