A. Sharp representation/cut-complexity funding
Current main Theorem 2.1 (prod:upper):
C_j(K)=j+[log K+2 log j+1]/log[j^3/(j^2+j-1)], j>=2;
an eligible deterministic rule on all nonnegative real bundle reports gives
the leading-one upper (1+o(1))log K/loglog K. Main Theorem 4.1 (dag:sharp)
matches it in the worst case over technologies/networks with AT MOST K forms/
minimal structural cuts, even on simple DAGs with one fixed strategic owner
per edge. K is representation complexity, not encoded input length. The lower
is not for every technology or an exact-count padding theorem. Total-payment
factor B means subsidy at most (B-1)T, not BT additional subsidy.

B. Owner-count and dimension upper bounds
Current main Theorem 3.1 (general:sqrt): for finite-valued monotone concave
degree-one homogeneous f on R_+^d, including downward boundary discontinuities,
there is an eligible all-profile rule with total factor
min{d, n sqrt(2/(n+1)), 1+2 sqrt(d)}. No finite-form representation is needed.
Each alternative is selected as a whole rule using fixed public parameters
before reports, not by a pointwise minimum of payments. The n bound is uniform
in d and the d bound uniform in n. No polynomial preprocessing guarantee.

Closest numerical antecedent: Bachrach et al. (2009), The cost of stability
in coalitional games, arXiv:0907.4385v1, Theorem 3.5:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.4385
Additional subsidy <=(sqrt(n)-1)v(N), equivalently total funding <=sqrt(n)v(N).
Published successor: Bachrach et al. (2018), Bounds on the Cost of Stabilizing
a Cooperative Game, JAIR 63:987–1023, DOI 10.1613/jair.1.11270,
Theorem 7, printed p.994/PDF page 8:
https://jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/11270/26463/20899
The full originals were directly checked in the coordinator's 16 October
adjudication. The published theorem gives multiplicative cost <=sqrt(n),
asymptotically tight for superadditive games. It is not sqrt(2n). Theorem 9's
2-2/(n+1) bound concerns anonymous superadditive games, not the present theorem.

Logical comparison: Bachrach's subsidy stabilizes one fixed game. Every
concave homogeneous profile here already has an efficient support allocation,
so its static additional cost is zero. The proposed bound controls a SINGLE
own-bundle-monotone rule across all reports of fixed actual owners. A static
existence theorem alone does not imply that cross-report selection theorem.
This distinction is an explanation of the target, not exclusion of every
deductive implication of prior work. Unrestricted optimal n,d rates remain
between log/loglog lower bounds and square-root uppers; do not label them sharp.

C. Restricted architecture barriers
Supplement Definition arch:def fixes a report-independent mask law, a common
finite global support selected using only open reports, closed payments capped
by either T or V_i=max_S[v(S+i)-v(S)], and open endpoint marginals. It allows
correlated/asymmetric masks and randomized support selection. The empty-mask
support law is fixed because no open reports are available.
Theorem arch:tbarrier: global-cap lower (sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n), already with d=n.
Theorem arch:vowner: the same leading owner lower with V_i caps (larger d).
Theorem arch:vdimension: V_i-cap lower (1/2-o(1))sqrt(d_n), d_n=2n+ceil(n^(3/4)).
Thus sqrt(2) is the sharp leading owner constant within these architectures;
dimension square-root ORDER is sharp there, not its leading constant. The
explicit minimum-norm relative-face convention has a separate extension.

Contribution boundary: these are proposed lower bounds for a named mechanism
class, not unrestricted lower bounds on all truthful payment maps. They do not
cover all report-dependent masks, arbitrary owner-specific supports or every
extended-valued selector. No inspected source in the supplied bounded record
has been shown to imply these exact statements; that is not a priority proof.
The classical static dual/support and subsidy ingredients do not alone yield
the same fixed-technology, same-owner, all-profile barrier. Population-based
cross-monotonic lower bounds cannot be silently substituted for these quantifiers.
