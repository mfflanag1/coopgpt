\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries Subsidies for truthful cooperation}\\[4pt]
{\large Sharp unrestricted bounds in weighted multi-glove games}\\[5pt]
Maxwell Flanagan --- Specialist review brief --- 6 October 2026
\end{center}

\textbf{Purpose.} Correctness, priority and significance review of an
unrestricted budget theorem. Written proofs and exact certificates have
internal model audits. Historical novelty and human specialist validation
remain unresolved.

\textbf{Model.} Fixed owners form $k\ge2$ nonempty complementary pools.
For nonnegative reports $c$, write
\[
 v_c(S)=\min_j\sum_{i\in S\cap N_j}c_i,\qquad T=v_c(N).
\]
Payments are deterministic, nonnegative, cover every coalition
($y(S)\ge v_c(S)$), and are nondecreasing in each owner's own report.
This is incentive compatibility for binding underreports when utility is
payment and there are no use costs. Factor $B$ means $y(N)\le BT$;
subsidy is $(B-1)T$. We impose no proportionality, anonymity, population,
group-report or split/merge condition. Zero-value profiles receive zero.

\textbf{Main theorem.} Let $B_k$ be the supremum over all finite fixed
$k$-pool ownerships of the best all-profile total-payment factor. Then
\[
 \boxed{\quad B_k=(1+o(1))\frac{\log k}{\log\log k}.\quad}
\]
Both owner and pool counts may grow. This rules out every universal constant
and establishes the sharp leading constant as one. Exact finite-$k$ factors
and lower-order terms are not determined.

\textbf{The lower mechanism.} Give each pool $m>d$ fixed owners with
reports in $\{1,m\}$. A type-$h$ pool has $h$ high owners. Two actual
coalitions force
\[
 A\le A\sum_{h<d}n_hx_h+n_d[(m-d)x_d+dz_d],
 \qquad A=(d+1)m-d^2.
\]
Average over all pool histograms with multinomial weights $\mu$ from
$p_h=\lambda^h/(h!Z)$, $0\le h\le d$, where $\lambda=A/\alpha$ and
$Z=\sum_{h=0}^d\lambda^h/h!$. For a unilateral increase
$n'=n-e_h+e_{h+1}$, the exact identity
\[
 \mu(n)n_h=(p_h/p_{h+1})\mu(n')n'_{h+1}
\]
transports only the changing owner's payment. Since
$A p_h/p_{h+1}=\alpha(h+1)$, the same destination budgets now control
all incoming types, giving $A\le(1+\alpha)B\,\mathbb E T$.
With $q=\lceil\log(d+1)\rceil$, $m=dq$, $\alpha=1/q$ and
$k\ge\lceil2qZ\rceil$, this forces $B\ge(1-o(1))d$ while
$\log k_d=(1+o(1))d\log d$. It improves the earlier single-base
hierarchy's coefficient $1/2$ to $1$. Symmetrization is a proof device;
no anonymity or strategic cloning is assumed.

\textbf{Upper bound and earlier class results.} Cap $d_i=\min(c_i,T)$.
The payment $d_i\Pr(j\text{ minimizes }\sum_{h\in N_j}d_hU_h)$ equals
$T$ times the established absolute continuous Banzhaf index. It covers
coalitions and is own-report monotone. Its factor is at most
$U(k)=R_k+1/\log R_k$, where $R_k\log(R_k/e)=\log k$;
a sharp pairwise probability estimate improves it strictly for finite $k$.
Proportional rules have sharp leading coefficient one. Nonlinear payments
escape their witnesses on bounded-altered-pool domains, but cannot avoid
the new unrestricted hierarchy. The optional original-core floor $H_i/k$
adds at most one to the factor and does not change the leading bounds.

\textbf{Established ingredients and nearby literature.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Moretti--Norde,
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00355-021-01337-8}{\emph{A note on weighted
multi-glove games}} (2021), Definition 3.1 and Theorem 3.2: the game and
total balancedness are established, including real-weight extensions.
\item Kurz, \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5936}{arXiv:1312.5936v1},
Definition 6.11: the absolute continuous Banzhaf functional is established.
Our proposed contribution is its joint payment guarantees and budget analysis.
\item Huang--Mei--Yang--Cao,
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1}{\emph{Mechanism Design in Max-Flows}}
(2025), Sections 2.1--2.2: binding capacity underreports and payment utility
without use costs; efficient core selection can fail truthfulness. Serial
parallel-edge layers realize our game. Our lower bound therefore also rules
out an absolute factor on all edge-owned max-flow games. The upper bound
here is only for layered games.
\item Fixed-game cost of stability addresses a different obstruction:
every fixed game here already has a nonempty core. Positive subsidy is forced
by allocations being linked across reports.
\item Immorlica--Mahdian--Mirrokni's cross-monotonic profit-sharing bounds
compare participating-player sets, including unchanged owners. The inspected
Br\^anzei--Solymosi--Tijs 2003 multi-glove working paper compares type
populations. Our own-report requirement makes neither comparison.
The 2007 final publication was not used for the latter theorem comparison.
\end{itemize}
These distinctions identify the proposed contribution; a bounded source
search does not confirm historical priority.

\textbf{Questions for specialist review.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Does an existing result imply the sharp unrestricted
$(1+o(1))\log k/\log\log k$ budget requirement under exactly these
fixed-owner, own-report assumptions?
\item Is there a gap in the multinomial changing-owner transport,
the shared destination-budget accounting, or the all-$k$ asymptotic inversion?
\item Does the capped-Banzhaf upper analysis follow from an established
continuous-value or resource-monotonicity theorem we should credit?
\item Is this sharp unrestricted theorem a useful contribution to truthful
cooperative allocation or max-flow mechanism design, and what comparison
would best establish its significance?
\end{enumerate}
