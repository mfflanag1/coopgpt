{\Large\bfseries Subsidies for truthful cooperation}\\[4pt]
{\large Sharp proportional bounds and nonlinear escape}\\[5pt]
Maxwell Flanagan --- Specialist review brief --- 6 October 2026
\end{center}

\textbf{Purpose.} A focused request for correctness, priority and significance
feedback on one manuscript. The results have written proofs, finite exact
checks and internal model review. Historical novelty and human specialist
validation remain unresolved.

\textbf{The model.} Fixed owners are partitioned into $k\ge2$ complementary
pools. Owner $i$ reports a nonnegative capacity $c_i$; write
\[
 v_c(S)=\min_{1\le j\le k}\sum_{i\in S\cap N_j}c_i,
 \qquad T=v_c(N).
\]
A deterministic nonnegative payment rule must satisfy
$y(S)\ge v_c(S)$ for every coalition and must be nondecreasing in each
owner's own report. This is incentive compatibility for binding underreports
when utility is payment and there are no use costs. A budget factor $B$ means
$y(N)\le BT$; the subsidy is $(B-1)T$. Owners cannot change identities or pool
memberships. Group, overreport and population-change guarantees are not assumed.

An optional floor is $y_i\ge H_i/k$, where $H_i$ is the largest payoff in the
\emph{original efficient core}. The floor costs at most one extra budget factor
via $\max\{y_i,[T-v_c(N\setminus\{i\})]/k\}$.

\textbf{Main results and scope.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{All profiles: a sublogarithmic upper bound.} Set
$d_i=\min(c_i,T)$ and let $p_j$ be the probability that pool $j$ has the smallest
sum $\sum_{i\in N_j}d_iU_i$, for independent uniform $U_i$.
The deterministic payment $d_ip_j$ covers all coalitions, is own-monotone,
and has factor at most $R_k+1/\log R_k$, where
$R_k\log(R_k/e)=\log k$. This is $(1+o(1))\log k/\log\log k$,
uniformly over finite owner counts. It equals the established absolute
continuous Banzhaf value of the normalized capped game, multiplied by $T$.
\item \textbf{Unrestricted necessary constant: two.} Any total-payment
constant uniform over both pool and owner counts must satisfy $B\ge2$.
This is a necessary bound, not a factor-two construction or a divergence result.
\item \textbf{Proportional class: matching first-order growth.} For final
payments $d_i\pi_j(c)$, with arbitrary nonnegative whole-profile pool prices,
the floor-free minimax factor is $(1+o(1))\log k/\log\log k$.
For $r$ owners per pool an explicit certificate gives
$B\ge kr/[k+(r-1)r^r]$.
\item \textbf{Nonlinear escape on the witnesses.} On all binary profiles in
$\{1/r,1\}$ with at most two altered pools, nonlinear payments have factor $2$
($3$ with the floor). On $[1/r,1]$ with at most $m$ altered pools, a different
continuous rule has factor $2m+3$ ($2m+4$ with the floor). These are
restricted-domain statements; the continuous formula's cost grows on an
explicit family with arbitrarily many altered pools.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What is established in the literature.}
The underlying game and its nonempty core are established in Moretti--Norde,
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00355-021-01337-8}{\emph{A note on weighted
multi-glove games}} (2021), Definition 3.1 and Theorem 3.2; the final remark
already covers real weights and discusses zeros. The integral functional is
Kurz's absolute continuous Banzhaf index,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5936}{arXiv:1312.5936v1}, Definition 6.11.
Neither ingredient is claimed as new.

The nearest strategic comparison is Huang--Mei--Yang--Cao,
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248v1}{\emph{Mechanism Design in Max-Flows}}
(2025), Sections 2.1--2.2 and 4.1--4.2. Serial parallel-edge layers realize our
model as a special network. Their efficient truthful mechanisms need not cover
every coalition. Our candidate results concern the cost of retaining that
coverage across reports after relaxing efficiency.

Fixed-game cost of stability and population-cross-monotonic sharing are
additional precedents, with different quantifiers. Our fixed games already
have nonempty cores; any positive subsidy requirement here comes from linking
allocations across reports. The manuscript and source ledger spell out those
comparisons. This bounded source check is not a novelty certification.

\textbf{The load-bearing distinction.}
The proportional lower proof transports a common pool price through report
changes made by different owners. A nonlinear rule need not preserve that
common price, and our witness family admits bounded nonlinear payments.
Accordingly, the lower certificate does not prove that every truthful covering
mechanism needs growing subsidy. The unresolved global question is
\[
 \text{Does one absolute }B<\infty\text{ work for all }k,\text{ owner counts,
 and nonnegative profiles?}
\]
No polynomial-time exact evaluation theorem is claimed for the Banzhaf rule.
The constants for the continuous restricted-domain rule are not claimed sharp.

\textbf{Questions for a specialist.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Is there an existing theorem combining report monotonicity, coalition
coverage and these budget guarantees, perhaps in continuous values, production
games, resource monotonicity or truthful max-flow mechanisms?
\item Does known prior work already imply the first-order proportional-class
bound, or the unrestricted necessary factor two?
\item Is the sharp class bound plus the explicit nonlinear escape a worthwhile
standalone contribution? Would a different central theorem or framing make the
paper more useful to the field?
\item Is there a specific gap in the capping/own-report argument, the linked
inequality certificate, or the two-pool extension that should be repaired before
wider circulation?
\end{enumerate}
