FREE ORIGINAL-SOURCE COMPARISON — 6 October 2026
This is bounded overlap checking, not a certification of historical novelty.
New claim under review: unrestricted truthful coalition-covering payments for
weighted multi-glove games require Theta(log k/log log k) worst-case factor.

1. Moretti–Norde, A note on weighted multi-glove games (2021).
https://d-nb.info/1240670206/34
Original PDF inspected: Definition3.1, Theorem3.2 and discussion of PMAS.
Exact same min-of-pool-sums game; core existence is established. Their allocation
comparisons across participating coalitions differ from own-capacity-report
comparisons. We do not claim the game or bottleneck-core construction as new.
No capacity-report subsidy asymptotic identified in these inspected results.

2. Huang–Mei–Yang–Cao, Mechanism Design in Max-Flows (2025).
https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08248
Original Sections2.1–2.2, Proposition1, Section4 and conclusion inspected.
Same binding-underreport/no-usage-cost convention; mechanisms there are efficient.
Their example refutes truthful core selection, and their Shapley/minimal-cut
mechanisms meet incentive properties without requiring all core inequalities.
The proposed hierarchy studies a subsidized coalition-covering relaxation and
proves a divergent budget bound already on serial parallel-edge layers. It does
not subsume their separate split/merge or other-owner monotonicity guarantees. We do not claim their incentive model as new.

3. Immorlica–Mahdian–Mirrokni, Limitations of cross-monotonic cost-sharing schemes.
https://immorlica.com/pubs/costshare.pdf
Original Definition3.3 and Theorem3.6/proof (printed pp.11–12) inspected.
They define approximate profit-sharing core and prove a linear-in-player-count
max-flow budget obstruction under cross-monotonicity across participant sets.
Their proof carries an unchanged owner's payoff when OTHER owners join. Our
hypothesis only compares the changing owner's own capacity reports, and imposes
no participation monotonicity. Their obstruction already uses two layers;
our general upper is bounded for fixed k=2, demonstrating that the hypotheses
cannot be silently identified. Their approximate-core/budget language and
monotonicity tradeoff are established; no reduction from their theorem to ours
has been proved. This is a close conceptual comparator requiring explicit credit.

4. Bachrach et al., The Cost of Stability in Coalitional Games (2009).
https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.4385
Original abstract/model record rechecked; established subsidy-to-stabilize concept.
Those characteristic functions are fixed, with subsidy addressing core emptiness.
Here each fixed weighted multi-glove game has a nonempty core; the additional
budget arises only from linking payments across privately reported capacities.
This difference is a model distinction, not evidence of priority by itself.

external stabilization payments are studied; the thresholded characteristic
function and fixed-capacity stabilization problem differ from our raw-flow,
report-linked lower bound. Broader follow-up literature remains to be assessed.
