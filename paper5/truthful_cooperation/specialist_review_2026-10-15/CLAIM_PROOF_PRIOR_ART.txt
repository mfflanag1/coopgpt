A. TRUTHFUL PRODUCTION — EXACT CONTRACT
Fixed finite owners and resource types; arbitrary overlapping whole-owner
bundles b_i≥0; fixed nonnegative forms V; f(c)=min_{w∈V}w·c; T=f(sum_i b_i).
One deterministic rule on all real profiles has nonnegative payments, covers
EVERY coalition, and is nondecreasing in its own owner's bundle with others
fixed. This is binding underreport truthfulness with payment-only utility.
It is not population/type/cross monotonicity, private-cost truthfulness,
false-name resistance, coordinated-deviation protection or endogenous entry.
No strategic cloning is used. B bounds TOTAL payment; subsidy is (B−1)T.
K counts forms (minimal structural cuts for networks), not encoded input size.

A1. General upper: main Theorem 2.1, PDF pp. 3–5, source label prod:upper.
For j≥2: C_j(K)=j+[log K+2log j+1]/log[j³/(j²+j−1)]. Optimizing j gives
(1+o(1))log K/loglog K, uniformly over finite owner counts/dimensions.
K=1 has an efficient rule; T=0 receives zero. Finite-K formula matters;
the asymptotic ratio is not a formula to evaluate at K=1.
Proof route: raw minimum form chosen after whole-owner deletions; capped
own-excluded contribution p_i^q; endpoint marginal m_i^q; mixture
r_i^q=qp_i^q+(1−q)m_i^q; M_i=j∫_0^{1/j}r_i^q dq.
Specific obligations: conditional selection independent of b_i; fixed priority
at ties; raw rather than capped selection; endpoint domination (5); selected
open terms unchanged by cap; deletion-polynomial derivative and exact minus-2
integration term (6); fixed-form Chernoff union (7); zero/boundary profiles.
Established ingredients: dual-price supports (Owen; Samet–Zemel), marginal
contributions, independent deletion and concentration. Proposed contribution:
the combined all-profile full-coverage mechanism and its sharp uniform budget.
No claim that the component tools themselves are new.
Evidence:14 October upper audit, integrated review and exact general-production
checks; current source recovers reviewed bytes via recorded bibliography-only
changes. Finite checks corroborate identities, not the universal proof.
Question: does any earlier rule already give these three guarantees under
this exact fixed-owner whole-bundle domain with this K-dependent budget?

A2. Production/network representation: Corollary 2.2, PDF p. 5, prod:lp.
Finite nonnegative resource LPs use fixed nonnegative dual vertices; directed
flow uses fixed inclusion-minimal source–sink edge cuts. Positive-profit
zero-resource activities are excluded. Capacity-independent offsets are outside
the homogeneous main model. Representation/core feasibility are prior tools.
K can be exponential. Exact enumeration of forms/masks is finite, not claimed
polynomial preprocessing. Supplementary certification has the same caveat.
Question: is the stated LP scope narrower/wider than any cited original in a
way that affects the strategic theorem, rather than just its representation?

A3. Matching lower: Theorem 3.1, PDF pp. 6–7, dag:sharp.
The optimum is a WORST CASE OVER technologies/networks with AT MOST K forms/cuts.
It is not a lower bound for each fixed technology or exact-count padding for
every integer K. Simple finite DAGs suffice; every edge has its own fixed
strategic owner, including all auxiliary fan edges with unrestricted domains.
Proof route: value-preserving fixed-owner permutations at arbitrary profiles;
actual fan-supported coalitions; only the changing owner's report transported;
multinomial identity; disjoint variable/auxiliary budgets Y,Q in (10), with
max{1+alpha,m/R}, not their sum; R=m; tail bound (11); structural-cut series
composition; log K~h log h; at-most-K inversion with 0<epsilon<1.
Established ingredients: max-flow/min-cut, averaging, weighted counting,
Stirling asymptotics. Proposed contribution: their fixed-owner report-transition
use with strategic auxiliary budgets and the matching leading constant one.
Evidence:14 October lower/integrated reviews, exact weighted transitions and
small graph/symmetry checks. No finite test proves asymptotic inversion.
Question: does a cited predecessor's incentive lower bound retain all these
owner/domain/coverage conditions and already imply this sharp minimax result?

A4. Guard against invalid reductions: Proposition 3.2, PDF pp. 7–8,
dag:subdivision. Full subdivision into separately owned edge pairs admits an
efficient eligible rule. It is an obstruction to a tempting proof reduction,
not a contradiction of A3, which uses shared fans and budgets. Proposition 2.3
(PDF p. 5) obstructs the UNCAPPED rule only; its two-owner efficient comparison
was repaired on14 October to the full-bundle Shapley formula. No new gap was
found in the central upper/lower. Ask a reader to identify actual counterexamples
or missing hypotheses, not to endorse a general claim of correctness.

B. ACYCLICITY — SEPARATE STATIC RESULT
Main Theorem 2.1 (PDF p. 3, source thm:main): on a fixed connected K-sector graph with
equal sector sizes, nonnegative pair values (including zero allowed edges),
only complete K-person teams productive, positive worth and nonempty ENTIRE
core, the sharp size-independent simultaneous ideal fraction is1/K for trees
and zero for cyclic graphs. Full nominal leximin attains the tree bound;
even the best core selection cannot avoid cyclic vanishing as market size grows.
H_i=max_{p∈core}p_i is held fixed when comparing allocations in one market.
Do not confuse static ideal protection here with resource-report incentives.
Proof map: Section 3 (PDF pp. 4–5) entire-core cut coordinates/clipping; Sections 4–5 (PDF pp. 5–8) explicit
triangle and simplex with exact 1/(2q+3); Section 6 (PDF pp. 9–10) selective subdivision to any
cyclic allowed graph while preserving nonempty positive cores.

productive connected partial-sector teams; their tree/cycle balancedness
classification is not silently identified with this strict full-team core.
Núñez–Rafels 2004 Definition 6p19/Example 7p20 already give the bilateral
half-optimal benchmark. Gangam et al. 2026p1621 supplies a vanishing example
with growing coalition size; our obstruction fixes sector/team size.
