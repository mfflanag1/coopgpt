1. MAIN PAPER MODEL AND QUANTIFIERS

The main paper states fixed finite owner/resource sets, a nonempty fixed
ordered finite family of nonnegative forms, the full nonnegative real bundle
domain, and componentwise own-report monotonicity with other reports fixed.
It explicitly restricts the interpretation to binding underreports and
payment-only utility. Costs, overreports, false names, population changes
and coordinated deviations are not silently covered.

The upper theorem correctly says that for every j>=2 there exists a rule;
the mechanism depends on j. Its displayed choice depends on technology
complexity K, not a profile-dependent active-form count. The finite bound,
zero-value convention and K=1 efficiency are consistent with the proof.
Natural logarithms are now specified explicitly.

The main worst-case quantities use at-most-K complexity and allow owner and
resource counts to grow through finite instances. The lower's fixed owners
are not changed by report transitions. Auxiliary owners remain strategic
on their full domains even though the finite witness keeps their reports
fixed. The paper distinguishes a sequence at exact construction cut counts
from an all-integer at-most-K worst-case conclusion. It does not claim an
exact-count padding theorem.

The economic statements match these quantifiers. An efficient core exists
at each fixed profile via any minimizing form, while the lower concerns
simultaneous incentives between profiles. Total-payment factor and subsidy
factor differ by one. The statements that complexity can be exponential,
owner/dimension rates remain unresolved, and polynomial preprocessing is
not established are consistent across introduction, conclusion and brief.

2. REPAIRED UNCAPPPED EXAMPLE

Both main and supplement explicitly set b1=(M,0), b2=(0,1). At the mask with
owner 1 off and owner 2 on, the form (1,0) uniquely minimizes the raw score;
the uncapped own contribution is M. The unchanged j=2 mixed-payment lower
bound M/6 and T=2-1/M follow as previously checked.

Both files replace the resource-owner-only efficient comparison by

 P_i=[f(b_i)+T-f(b_{-i})]/2, for the two owners.

This formula is valid on the full arbitrary-bundle domain. Summing gives T;
monotonicity of f gives own-report monotonicity and nonnegativity. The
min-linear function is superadditive, so T>=f(b1)+f(b2) and therefore
P_i>=f(b_i). These are all the nontrivial proper coalition constraints for
two owners. Thus the revised claim of an efficient eligible alternative
is correct. The correction is disclosed in the supplement and brief and
does not alter the upper theorem.

3. EPSILON RANGE

Both copies of the lower proof now use a fixed 0<epsilon<1 before setting
d0=floor((1-epsilon)log K/log log K). This ensures d0 is eventually positive
and tends to infinity. From log K_d0=(1+o(1))d0 log d0 one then obtains
log K_d0=(1-epsilon+o(1))log K<log K eventually. Letting epsilon decrease
to zero gives the claimed lower coefficient. The added range is appropriate
and is stated consistently in the correction disclosures.
