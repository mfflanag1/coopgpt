Dear Editors of Economic Theory,

Please consider “Sharp subsidies for truthful production and network
cooperation” for the Mechanism Design collection.

The paper asks how much external subsidy is needed to preserve every
coalition's production guarantee while eliciting capacity from fixed owners.
For technologies represented by K nonnegative supporting forms, it proves
a deterministic all-profile total-payment upper of (1+o(1))T log K/log log K
and a matching lower on simple directed acyclic flow networks with strategic
owners for every edge. The leading constant is one for the worst case with
at most K forms or structural cuts. The subsidy factor is the total-payment
factor minus one.

Each report profile already has an efficient core; the additional budget is
forced by linking payments across reports. The assumptions are binding
componentwise underreports, payment-only utility and fixed ownership. The
paper does not claim incentives for private operating costs, false names or
coordinated deviations. K can be exponential in a compact description.

The main paper contains complete central proofs. The indexed supplement
preserves supporting extensions and method obstructions; the accompanying
archive provides scoped exact checks, source hashes and execution records.
Established dual-core, flow, marginal and concentration ingredients are
credited. Related comparisons are version-specific. Internal AI-assisted
reviews and finite checks are not external peer review or formal verification.
