## Precise scope conclusion

Two separate precedents must not be collapsed:

- Bachrach et al. establish square-root total-payment bounds for stabilizing a **single static game**. They do not couple choices across report profiles.
- ABV Theorem 1 and Huang et al. Proposition 1/Theorem 1 establish genuine **cross-profile own-resource/capacity monotonicity** in narrower network models. Their monotone rules do not also provide the proposed general all-coalition subsidy guarantee.
- Cao may be the closest production-domain source, but its theorem-level overlap is unresolved without a freely accessible full text.

Thus, the proposed result’s narrow potentially distinguishing conjunction is:

> one payment rule defined on every profile of fixed owners’ \(d\)-dimensional bundles, own-whole-bundle monotonicity holding with other reports fixed, every coalition covered at every profile, and total payment bounded by an explicit function of \(n\) or \(d\).

None of the inspected theorem statements establishes that entire conjunction. That is a bounded comparison, not confirmation that nobody else has done so.
