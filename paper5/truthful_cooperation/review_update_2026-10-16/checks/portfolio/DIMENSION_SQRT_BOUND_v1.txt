GENERAL RESOURCE-DIMENSION UPPER BOUND: (2+o(1))sqrt(d)
15 October 2026. Candidate proof; independent review pending.

THEOREM
Let f:R_+^d ->R be finite, monotone, concave and homogeneous of degree one.
There are arbitrarily many but fixed n owners reporting whole bundles b_i>=0.
For every fixed 0<a<1, the existing relative-support/endpoint hybrid, averaged
uniformly over missing probability q in [0,a], is nonnegative, coalition-covering,
and nondecreasing in every owner's own bundle at every real profile. Its total
payment satisfies
 sum_i M_i(b) <= C_d(a) T(b),
 C_d(a)=[1+2d(-a-log(1-a))]/a.
In particular, taking a=1/(1+sqrt(d)) gives factor at most 1+2sqrt(d).
As d tends to infinity, a~1/sqrt(d) gives factor (2+o(1))sqrt(d).
Combining the existing dimension marginal bound and the new bound gives
 B_d^dim <= min{d, 1+2sqrt(d)},
and the same upper for arbitrary finite monotone concave homogeneous functions,
including boundary discontinuities. The bound is independent of n and the
number of supporting forms. It does not assert efficient oracle evaluation,
measurability of the full report map, or historical novelty.

THE UNCHANGED RULE
At each profile fix T=f(c), c=sum_i b_i. For a mask B, x_B=sum_i B_i b_i.
Choose the canonical relative support ell_(x_B) from the manuscript: it is
nonnegative linear on the coordinate face containing x_B, majorizes f there,
touches f at x_B, and equals +infinity outside that face. At x_B=0 it is zero
at0 and +infinity elsewhere. Put A_i(B)=min{ell_(x_B)(b_i),T}.
For independent B_i with Pr(B_i=0)=q, define
 p_i(q)=E[A_i(B)|B_i=0],
 m_i(q)=E[f(b_i+x_(B_-i))-f(x_(B_-i))],
 r_i(q)=q p_i(q)+(1-q)m_i(q),
 M_i=(1/a)int_0^a r_i(q)dq.
These are finite mask sums and integrals of finite polynomials; no random
sampling implementation or limit over technologies is needed. Eligibility is
the existing relative-support theorem: a closed support excludes its recipient,
both caps increase in the own report, endpoint marginals increase in the own
report, capped supports cover coalitions, and on-endpoint marginals dominate
on-support contributions. The proof works for every0<q<1.

SCALAR BERNOULLI RECIPROCAL LEMMA
Fix arbitrary u_1,...,u_n>=0 and let S_B=sum_i B_i u_i. Define
 R_i(B)=min{u_i/S_B,1}, taking R_i=0 if u_i=0 and R_i=1 if u_i>0,S_B=0.
Then, with p=1-q,
 E sum_(i:B_i=0) R_i(B) <= 2q/p.
Proof: for nonnegative u,S,
 min{u/S,1} <= 2u/(u+S),
with the same zero conventions. Conditioning on the own bit gives the exact
identity
 E sum_i (1-B_i) u_i/(u_i+S_B)
  = (q/p) E sum_i B_i u_i/S_B
  = (q/p) Pr(S_B>0) <=q/p.
The fractions with zero numerator and denominator are defined as zero.
The first equality holds because in either conditioned expression the ratio
is u_i/(u_i+sum_(j!=i) B_j u_j). Summing and applying the elementary inequality
proves the lemma. No lower bound on positive weights is used.

POINTWISE RESOURCE-COORDINATE DOMINATION
Assume T>0. For every mask and every owner i,
 A_i(B)/T <= sum_(e=1)^d min{b_ie/(x_B)_e,1},
with the scalar lemma's conventions coordinatewise.
If b_i has a positive coordinate outside the support of x_B, the right side
contains1 and the claim follows from A_i<=T. Otherwise the relative support
is an ordinary nonnegative linear form w on that face. Touching gives
 sum_e w_e (x_B)_e=f(x_B)<=T,
so for each positive coordinate (x_B)_e, w_e (x_B)_e<=T and
 w_e b_ie/T <= b_ie/(x_B)_e.
Use min{sum z_e,1}<=sum min{z_e,1} for nonnegative z_e to conclude. If x_B=0
and b_i=0, both sides are zero; the remaining zero-mask cases were handled
by the off-face argument. This also proves the same claim for any finite
nonnegative global support, regardless of minimizer ties.

EXPECTED CLOSED BUDGET
Apply the scalar lemma separately to each resource's weights u_i=b_ie.
The preceding pointwise inequality yields
 q sum_i p_i(q)=E sum_(i:B_i=0) A_i(B)
 <= [2d q/(1-q)]T.
Only d appears; owner count and support-form complexity have disappeared.

ENDPOINT BUDGET AND INTEGRATION
Let g(q)=E f(x_B), a finite polynomial. The elementary diagonal derivative
identity is sum_i m_i(q)=-g'(q). Therefore
 sum_i r_i(q) <= [2d q/(1-q)]T +(1-q)(-g'(q)).
Since g(0)=T and g>=0, integration by parts gives
 int_0^a (1-q)(-g'(q))dq
  =T-(1-a)g(a)-int_0^a g(q)dq <=T.
The integral of q/(1-q) is -a-log(1-a). Divide by a to obtain C_d(a).
At T=0 monotonicity makes all coalition worths, capped contributions and
endpoint marginals zero, so the same bound is immediate.

EXPLICIT PARAMETERS AND ASYMPTOTICS
For0<a<1,
 -a-log(1-a)=sum_(k>=2) a^k/k <=a^2/[2(1-a)].
Consequently C_d(a)<=1/a+d a/(1-a). The choice a=1/(1+sqrt(d)) gives
1/a+d a/(1-a)=1+2sqrt(d). This a is at most1/2 for d>=1, so it stays inside
the old averaging interval range (although it need not be an inverse integer).
Alternatively choose j=ceil(sqrt(d))+1 and a=1/j to retain the literal old
integer parameter: C_d(1/j)<=j+d/(j-1)=(2+o(1))sqrt(d).
All parameters are chosen from the fixed resource dimension before reports;
there is no report-dependent retuning or deletion of zero owners/resources.

CONTRIBUTION BOUNDARY
The production model, relative supports, hybrid eligibility, and derivative
identity are established earlier in this project. The new ingredient is the
coordinatewise capped-price domination plus the scalar Bernoulli reciprocal
identity, giving an owner-independent closed-budget estimate. This changes the
previous general dimension upper bound from d to O(sqrt d). The existing lower
bound of order log d/loglog d remains unchanged. It does not prove a sharp
dimension rate or an unrestricted sqrt(d) lower. Priority is unconfirmed.
