POST-REVIEW CLARIFICATIONS -- 15 October2026
These notes preserve the reviewed proof files and their snapshot hashes.

EMPTY-MASK SUPPORT LAW
In every anchor argument, conditional on B=empty the selected support law is
ONE fixed law for the fixed technology and owners: there are no open reports
on which it could depend. The anchor distribution is also fixed in advance.
The lower bound holds for every realized support after averaging the finite
anchor list. Integrating against that single support law commutes with the
finite anchor average. It would not justify choosing a different empty support
law separately for each anchor profile; that is excluded by the rule definition.
If the empty mask has probability zero, its conditional support law is irrelevant.

CANONICAL RELATIVE-SUPPORT EXTENSION
The architecture lower bounds also hold for the specific canonical relative
support used in the project. At every nonempty regular-mask profile, the active
resource block aggregate is interior and its selected finite gradient is unique.
All reported bundles lie inside that active face, so exactly the same forced
prices and endpoint estimates apply. On inactive blocks the bundles are zero.
At the empty mask, the canonical support gives infinity on every nonzero bundle;
therefore every anchor owner is paid the applicable cap. For global-T anchors
this is T, and for pivotal V-cap anchors it is V_i=T. The normalized empty-mask
budget is then n, which only strengthens the finite-support anchor lower bound.
This extends the class result to that explicitly defined canonical relative
support. It does not claim coverage of all unspecified extended-valued selectors.

WORDING AND UPPER-BOUND REFERENCE
Where FINITE_SUPPORT_MASK_OBSTRUCTION_v2 or VCAP_MASK_OBSTRUCTION_v1 says
'proper mask (including the full mask)', read 'nonempty mask'. The actual proofs
treat B=empty separately and include B=[n] in the regular-profile estimate.
