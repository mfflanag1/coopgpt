WHAT THE FRAGMENT PROVES
1. Both global-T-cap and maximum-coalition-marginal-cap mask vectors are
   eligible on every real nonnegative whole-bundle report profile.
2. An explicit fixed finite min-linear technology with d=n forces every
   global-T-cap fixed-mask common-support rule to have factor
   (sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n)=(sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(d).
3. A separate fixed technology with nm+n^2 resources forces the V-cap
   architecture to have factor (sqrt(2)-o(1))sqrt(n).
4. A two-block fixed technology with d=2n+ceil(n^(3/4)) forces the V-cap
   architecture to have factor (1/2-o(1))sqrt(d).
Every factor is TOTAL payment divided by grand production, not subsidy alone.

EXACT CLASS AND QUANTIFIERS
For each stated n, one fixed technology is chosen before the rule. Every rule
in the defined architecture is then defeated at some one profile from a fixed
finite list, using the same n owners and technology throughout that list.
The report-independent mask law may depend on technology/n/d and may be
arbitrary, asymmetric, correlated, or mixed; it cannot adapt to the reports.
At a mask, one common finite nonnegative GLOBAL supporting form is selected
using only the technology, mask and open reports. It may randomize and need
not belong to the displayed finite defining family. Closed reports cannot
enter support selection, even through grand production. The cap itself may
depend on the whole profile, exactly as prescribed in the architecture.
At the empty mask the law is ONE fixed support law for this technology and
owner set. Each anchor average is pointwise in its support realization before
integrating that same law. This is stated in the definition and averaging proof.
The explicit canonical relative-support convention is included in a separate
closing argument; no arbitrary extended-valued support class is claimed.
