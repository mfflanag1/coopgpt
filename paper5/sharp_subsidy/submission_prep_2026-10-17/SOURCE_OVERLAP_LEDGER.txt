3. COMPANION OVERLAP: SHARED MODEL/TOOLS, DIFFERENT THEOREMS AND FAMILIES

Acyclicity and simultaneous protection of individual core maxima:
- Same strict nonnegative pair-additive market/entire-original-core benchmark.
- Keeps total payment exactly T and maximizes simultaneous fraction rho(M),
  with nominal leximin examined separately. Classifies fixed connected sector
  graphs: tree guarantee 1/K, cyclic infimum zero as sector size grows.
- Its source triangular family has 3q+1 labels/sector, T=6q+1, weak-a ideals
  1/(q+1), strong-a ideals 4, b/c ideals 1, and rho=1/(2q+3). Selective
  subdivision transfers the obstruction to other cyclic allowed graphs.

This static subsidy paper:
- Fixes protection at H_i/3 and minimizes total payment ABOVE T while retaining
  all original coalition constraints; the benchmarks are frozen pre-subsidy.
- Family has 2ell+1 labels/sector, T=3(2ell+1), ALL ideals H_i=3, special-team
  worth 5 and exact subsidy 2ell. It uses separate coloring witnesses on a
  label/team incidence tree. Its pair alphabet {0,1,2} is shared, but these
  displayed family invariants and the target objective differ from Acyclicity.
- A label/team INCIDENCE tree is not the tree on SECTORS in Acyclicity. The
  subsidy family's sector support is triangular. Do not conflate the graphs.
- Acyclicity shows H/3 can be infeasible within a cyclic original core; it does
  not determine the sharp minimum subsidy or strict finite upper. Conversely,
  scaling a subsidized protected cover back to total T may violate original
  coalition inequalities, so this subsidy theorem does not yield Acyclicity's
  within-core ratio classification. No undocumented transformation is assumed.

Sharp subsidies for truthful production and network cooperation:
- Fixed owners report resource bundles to one fixed min-linear/concave
  production technology; one rule must cover coalitions at EVERY report and
  be nondecreasing in the reporting owner's own bundle.
- Its central theorem has worst total-payment factor
  (1+o(1))log K/loglog K, matching fixed-owner simple-DAG lower; later n,d
  bounds and architecture barriers remain separately qualified.
- It imposes no H_i/3 floor. This static paper imposes no reporting incentive
  condition. Both start from per-profile nonempty cores, but extra funding
  addresses different constraints. Static flow appendices do not become
  truthful mechanisms without a separate all-profile proof.

Editorial consequence: present a coherent shared research line, not three
invented models. State the reused definitions/tools and earlier-project lower
family. Preserve the genuinely distinct quantitative theorem statements and
cross-reference companions. Suggested paragraph:
"A companion study classifies simultaneous ideal protection within the strict
core by the sector graph. Here the fraction is fixed at one third and external
payments may raise the total above grand-coalition worth. A separate companion
studies resource reports and incentives across profiles; no such incentive
constraint is imposed here. The sharp subsidy family and the graph family's
ideal and matching formulas differ."
