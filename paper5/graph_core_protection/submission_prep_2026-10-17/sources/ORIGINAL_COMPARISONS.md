## Recovered: the distinct 2023 extreme-core preprint

F. Javier Martínez-de-Albéniz, Carlos Rafels and Neus Ybern, *Extreme core points in multisided assignment problems*, [SSRN4369524](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4369524), posted February 24, 2023. A normal browser visit completed its automatic verification and exposed the original download. The complete 13-page PDF was opened and downloaded through the supplied link. No login, payment or access-control bypass was used. The original is marked as a preprint, not peer reviewed. It is retained locally for reading and is not redistributed in the sharing archive.

Read scope: definitions and assumptions on pages 2–6; Theorems 1–2 and their proofs on pages 6–7; Examples 2–4 on pages 6–10; the strip-prone condition and Proposition 1 on page 10; Theorems 3–4 on page 11; Example 5 and bibliography on pages 12–13. Theorem 2 on page 7 was also visually inspected in the rendered PDF.

The paper fixes an optimal diagonal ordered by its worths. It defines auxiliary games on the sector set and concatenates their core allocations by cumulative sums. Theorem 1 gives an inclusion. Theorem 2 gives equality after imposing nonnegativity and the original inequalities outside the central strip. Theorem 3 removes outside-strip inequalities under its strip-prone condition. Theorem 4 further assumes nonnegative singleton values in the auxiliary games to identify the full core with the concatenation set. Its examples illustrate these conditions and their necessity; Example 4 reproduces a finite example attributed to Quint.

**Comparison:** Theorem 2 can redescribe a general input core, including ours after an optimal relabeling. We therefore do not claim this paper is excluded merely by non-supermodularity. The simplified theorems require conditions broader than those of the separate 2020 assortative paper, and no all-relabeling exclusion from their strip-prone class is asserted here. The source does not provide our growing fixed-alphabet family or a full-leximin-to-ideal conclusion. Converting a core representation into this theorem still requires the construction and selector analysis. No direct or worked deductive anticipation was found in its inspected statements, proofs or examples. This resolves the original-text comparison for this preprint at that scope; it is not a worldwide novelty certificate.
