Dear Editors,

Please consider “Acyclicity and simultaneous protection of individual core
maxima” for publication as an original research article.

The paper asks whether a stable allocation can protect every participant
relative to that participant's most favorable stable payoff. In equal-sized
multisided markets with nonnegative pair values, where only full teams produce,
it proves a sharp classification conditional on a nonempty core. On a tree of
K sectors, full nominal leximin simultaneously guarantees 1/K of every core
maximum. On any fixed cyclic allowed graph, the best attainable simultaneous
fraction tends to zero as market size grows, despite a strictly positive core.
An explicit triangular simplex gives the exact fraction 1/(2q+3).

The distinction from earlier graph-assignment results is economically material:
allowing partial teams to produce changes both the core and the individual
maxima. We credit earlier bilateral ideal protection, tree decomposition and
broader vanishing-protection examples. The paper's contribution is the precise
strict-full-team classification and constructive whole-core proof, rather than
a new fairness concept or a general claim that cycles preclude stability.

Complete proofs and a portable computational supplement are included. Finite
checks corroborate specified constructions and do not stand in for the universal
proof. The topic combines cooperative-game stability and distributional guarantees,
which motivates our choice of International Journal of Game Theory.

A companion manuscript on externally subsidized protection shares the strict
pair-additive market model and core-maxima benchmark, but its sharpness family
is different. It studies a different feasible-payment question. Earlier chain-cycle
