S1 REQUIREMENT CAPSULE
Required: editable source and compiled PDF; title, author names, affiliations (unaffiliated: city/country), corresponding-author designation/email; 150–250-word abstract without undefined abbreviations/unspecified references; 4–6 keywords; author–year citations; alphabetized, cited, published/accepted bibliography with available DOI links. Unpublished works belong in text. Footnotes cannot be bibliographic-only or contain full reference details. Acknowledgments belong on the title page. Use decimal headings, at most three levels.
Declarations: manuscript data availability, funding, competing interests, and substantive AI-use disclosure with human accountability; contributions, interests and funding also entered in the interface. Relevant declarations precede references. Human/animal ethics and consent are conditional. Obtain permissions if reusing protected material. Confirm originality, exclusive consideration and author/institutional approvals; disclose related/reused unpublished material in the cover letter.
Supplement: standard formats, identifying metadata in each file, manuscript “Online Resource” citation, descriptive caption, consecutive ESM filenames; ZIP permitted.
Recommended: Springer template, ORCID (if available), public data repository, manuscript contribution statement (title-page placement recommended).
Not established publicly: distinct title-page upload, mandatory cover-letter attachment, portal file slots/limits, additional form fields. No published page limit or mandatory JEL codes found. Reviewer suggestions are invited, not mandatory.
