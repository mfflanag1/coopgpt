CORE CLAIM AND NONNEGOTIABLE SCOPE

Fix owner identities, resource coordinates, and a production technology
f(c)=min_{w in V} w.c with K fixed nonnegative supporting forms. Each owner's
report is a whole nonnegative bundle. A single deterministic payment rule on
all real report profiles is componentwise own-report monotone, nonnegative,
and covers every original coalition. Its total payment is at most
(1+o(1)) T log K/log log K, uniformly in the numbers of owners and resources.
The matching worst-case lower bound already holds on simple DAGs with each
auxiliary edge owned by its own strategic player, for the class with AT MOST
K structural minimal cuts. That leading-one minimax statement is the center.

Truthfulness here means binding componentwise UNDERREPORTS and payment-only
utility, without operating costs. It does not establish arbitrary-report,
false-name, group-strategyproof, other-owner-monotone, or population-monotone
incentives. If total-payment factor is B, external subsidy factor is B-1.
K counts a supplied finite support representation, or structural minimal cuts
in the network specialization. It can be exponential in compact input size.
Do not confuse minimal cuts with capacity-dependent minimum cuts. Do not
describe the theorem as closing the owner-count or resource-dimension gaps.
