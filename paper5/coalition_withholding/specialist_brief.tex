\noindent\textbf{Review request.} Please assess the correctness and priority of
\emph{The cost of coalition withholding: resource dimension and coalition size}.
The new central result raises the recorded unrestricted arity-three lower from
order $s^{1/4}$ to order $s^{1/2}$. The older dimension frontiers and sparse
upper are consolidated in the same manuscript with full proofs. A new graph
section supplies a $1+1/\sqrt2$ full-OUT construction on complete P4 blow-ups
and disjoint unions. We seek
specific invalid steps, missing hypotheses, or antecedent theorems that imply
the bounds. No claim of confirmed historical novelty or a major-open-problem
resolution is being made.

\section*{1. Freeze this contract before comparing results}
An institution fixes finite actual owners, resource coordinates, public
technology $F$, and coordinatewise report boxes $0\le b_i\le\bar b_i$.
All real reports, including zero, are legal. Write $b_C=\sum_{i\in C}b_i$.
Nonnegative payments satisfy, at every profile,
\[
 y_C(b)\ge F(b_C)\quad\hbox{for every coalition }C,
 \qquad \sum_i y_i(b)\le\kappa F(b_N).
\]
For $|C|\le s$ and $0\le\delta_i\le b_i$ supported on $C$, the contracts are
\[
 \text{TCWM: } y_C(b)-y_C(b-\delta)\ge0;
 \qquad
 \text{full OUT: } y_C(b)-y_C(b-\delta)\ge F(\delta_C).
\]
Unchanged coalition members are allowed. The scalar $\kappa$ bounds
\emph{total funding}; the external supplement is $(\kappa-1)F(b_N)$.
There is no dummy-zero axiom, marginal floor, anonymity, continuity, linearity,
or efficient-price representation imposed on a lower-bound mechanism.
Owners are never cloned during a deviation.

The dimension class consists of finite-valued monotone concave homogeneous
$F$ on $d$ resource types, with every coalition protected. The sparse class is
finite packing production $F(c)=\max\{\pi\cdot x:Ax\le c,\ x\ge0\}$,
$A,\pi\ge0$, with at most $r$ positive resource entries per profitable column.
Both $n$ and $d$ may grow; only incentive coalitions have size at most $s$.

\section*{3. The new proof in one page}
Take nonzero vectors of $\mathbb F_2^{M+1}$ as resource rows, with basis
$v,e_1,\ldots,e_M$. Activities are all triples $\{a,b,a+b\}$, with unit
inputs and profits. There are $2M+1$ fully eligible actual owners, each with
real report box $[0,1]^{2^{M+1}-1}$. At profile $C_m$ the cap owner reports
$v$, every high owner reports $e_j$, and low owner $j$ reports $v+e_j$ only
when $j\le m$. Dormant high reports stay present. Exactly the active branch
triples are supported, so production is one.

Finite averaging over legal resource and owner permutations preserves every
contract inequality. The seed stabilizers exchange the cap with either private
owner at $C_1$, giving $q_1\ge1/3$ by coverage. Active private owners are
transitive at $C_m$, with payment $q_m$. Restoring low owner $m$ while
protecting it together with $k_m=\min\{s-1,2m-2\}$ old active private owners
gives
\[
 (k_m+1)q_m\ge k_m q_{m-1}.
\]
The old restorer's payment is discarded by nonnegativity, never assumed zero.
The cap and a branch satisfy $z+2q_M\ge1$, while total payment is at least
$z+2Mq_M$. Hence
\[
 \kappa\ge1+\frac{2(M-1)}3
       \prod_{m=2}^M\frac{k_m}{k_m+1}.
\]
The uncapped product is governed by
$Q_M=4^M/(2\binom{2M}{M})$; it proves the uniform square-root lower.
For fixed $s\ge2$, the capped certificate is maximized at $M=s,s+1$.
All coefficients can be cancelled with explicit nonnegative rational
multipliers. For an asymmetric original rule the expensive profile can be an
orbit image of $C_M$, rather than the displayed labeling.

The pure-owner version fixes one owner per resource, permits only that owner's
scalar report, and averages the \emph{joint} resource/owner automorphisms.
Its count is $n=d=2^{M+1}-1$, not $2M+1$. Higher arity replaces triples by
minimal binary circuits and retains the same logic.

\paragraph{Graph construction: a separate audit target.}
For the path $B$--$A$--$D$--$C$, form the directed bipartite double cover with
left capacities unchanged and right capacities scaled by $\alpha=1/\sqrt2$.
Among maximum flows, clamp each leaf's left use near half its capacity within
the feasible interval. Collapsing directed flow yields the funding bound
$(1+\alpha)F$. The difficult step is the selector: every coalition restoration
has nonnegative derivative coefficients whose sums across each graph edge
are at least one. A direct generic-perturbation segment proof treats all ties
and zero coordinates. Exact transportation within complete groups transfers
the result to blow-ups without changing actual owners. Full details are in
manuscript Section 6; the general-tree extension is not a theorem of this draft. Section6 also
proves an exact P6 obstruction to layerwise leaf clamping for every fixed
reserve fraction below one and every scaling $0<\alpha<1$. This is a failure
of that explicit recursion, not an impossibility for arbitrary tree rules.

\section*{4. Correctness questions with the highest consequence}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For the graph selector, do the two maximum-flow intervals and all
piecewise formulas agree, do the holding-cube inequalities cover every cell,
and does the boundary argument preserve full OUT for passive and mixed owners?
Does the complete-group transport preserve the withheld bundle's value as well
as current production?
\item Is the fixed-institution averaging lawful on the entire real report
box, including all dormant and zero-report owners? In the pure version, does
the joint action preserve every individual eligibility restriction?
\item Do the seed and private-owner stabilizers preserve the whole profile,
and are the stated activities the only supported columns of the full LP?
\item Does the coalition-size accounting allow every used transition without
silently imposing a dummy-zero or individual-incumbent condition?
\item Is the finite-owner compactness argument for the OUT dimension lower
valid without regularity assumptions on the original payments? In particular,
check inactive payments, dilation averaging and shifted abundant totals.
\item Do the face-average uppers handle activation and discontinuities at
support boundaries? For the sparse upper, check the coefficient signs for
$|C|\le s$ and coverage at the same-domain withheld profile.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{5. Priority questions and established ingredients}
Strong group monotonicity in Moulin--Sprumont's 2004 author version is a
close aggregate-sum antecedent; its Proposition 2(ii) additionally imposes
additivity, separability and exact balance. Do\u{g}an's 2013 absence-proofness
working paper is a close binary-participation analogue of OUT for efficient
subgames. Immorlica--Mahdian--Mirrokni's profit-sharing lower uses
per-incumbent cross-monotonicity. Those distinctions prevent a direct reading
of the inspected results as the present theorem, but do not establish priority.

Static cost of stability, including projective square-root constructions,
bounded-rank positive hypergraphs, and binomial marginal values are established.
Our claimed object is a quantitative cross-report funding obstruction despite
an efficient static cover at each packing profile. Please identify any theorem
that already yields it after a legitimate change of variables or normalization.
In particular:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Do any subsidized absence-proofness or strong-group-monotonicity results
already imply either dimension endpoint or the sparse coalition-size lower?
\item Is there a prior unrestricted lower with a better exponent for fixed
arity, under this same aggregate incentive contract and all-coalition coverage?
\item Do the unpublished-to-published comparisons change the contract or
conclusion? The packet leaves Sprumont's nearly-serial, serial-axiomatization
and discrete Aumann--Shapley comparisons, and Do\u{g}an's published version,
explicitly unresolved.
\item Are there applicable results in Schoppmann's small-coalition cost-sharing
work or Moulin--Sprumont's cross-subsidization paper? These are additional
leads, not claims of a theorem transfer.
\end{enumerate}
The graph comparison also needs scrutiny: Xiao--Fang's inspected 2021 preprint
uses efficient, per-incumbent population-monotonic matching allocations, whereas
our subsidized rule protects aggregate coalition payments under divisible
reports. Does any related result imply the quantitative selector bound under
this exact contract? The flow/cut and transportation ingredients are classical;
novelty is not assigned to them.
