A. EXACT CLAIMS TO COMPARE
P1. For all finite-valued monotone concave degree-one production functions
on d resources, and all fixed finite owner/endowment boxes, unrestricted
coalition TCWM has worst-instance factor (d+1)/2. Full OUT has factor
1+c(d-1), c(2-exp(-1/c))=1. Lower witnesses use min production. The
compactness lower is a contrapositive over finite bounded owner models.
P2. Finite nonnegative packing production of activity arity <=r admits
all-profile full OUT for coalitions <=s and coverage of EVERY coalition,
with factor r*s^(r-1), independent of n and d. This is an inherited campaign
upper, reconstructed and proved in manuscript Section4; classical marginal
values and bounded positive supports are ingredients.
P3. Same production class, arbitrary nonnegative payments: K3(s)>=sqrt(s/6).
Sharper B_s~sqrt(2*pi*s)/(3*sqrt(e)); B6=493/243. Fixed fully eligible boxes;
finite averaging only; inactive owners need not receive zero. Pure ownership
is a separate construction with a different actual-owner count.
P4. Fixed arity r>=3 admits Omega_r(s^((r-2)/(r-1))) by binary circuits.

K_r is a total-payment factor, not the outside supplement. Full OUT implies
TCWM. Lower on TCWM also lower-bounds OUT; an OUT upper upper-bounds TCWM.
Static coverage is never restricted by s. Full real report boxes, fixed actual
owners, unchanged protected members and no marginal/dummy floor are essential.

B. CHECKED ANTECEDENT COMPARISONS

1. Moulin and Sprumont, On Demand Responsiveness in Additive Cost Sharing.
Inspected: author version 24 May2004, definition pp4-6 and Proposition2 p12.
https://www.ruf.rice.edu/~econ/papers/2004papers/dmdrespr41.pdf
Published metadata verified from publisher: JET125(1),1-35,2005.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jet.2004.05.003
Strong Group Monotonicity really is the aggregate-sum inequality on its
scalar demand grid, including passive group members. The terminology/axiom
must not be presented as our invention. Proposition2(ii) additionally assumes
Additivity, Separability and exact balance. Its proof uses equality of total
shares to turn a protected-sum increase into a complementary decrease; that
step is not available merely from an overfunding inequality. The cost witness
C1(e1)=C1(e23)=C1(e123)=1 also violates production superadditivity; its lack
of homogeneity is an additional diagnostic. Neither reason proves that no
other theorem or reduction applies. Published theorem numbering/content
beyond the inspected author version remains unverified.

2. Immorlica, Mahdian and Mirrokni, Limitations of cross-monotonic
cost-sharing schemes. Author full version: Definition3.3 p11, flow theorem
and proof pp12-13. Published in ACM Transactions on Algorithms4(2),2008.
https://immorlica.com/pubs/costshare.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1145/1361192.1361201
Profit-budget signs are compatible: v(S)<=sum_i xi(i,S)<=alpha*v(S).
The alpha-core adds all-subcoalition coverage. The lower proof uses budget
balance and per-incumbent cross-monotonicity; do not add alpha-core as an
unstated theorem hypothesis. On binary participation with absent shares zero,
per-incumbent cross-monotonicity plus nonnegativity implies the coalition-sum
TCWM inequality by summing incumbent comparisons and adding entrants. The
reverse implication is not available. A lower for that stronger class does
not automatically lower-bound arbitrary TCWM rules. The two-layer flow
technology is min of two aggregate resources; the present dimension upper
already supplies constant-factor OUT rules there, so its linear incumbent
lower cannot transfer verbatim. A new valid coalition-sum argument remains
a legitimate specialist question; no assertion excludes all adaptations.

3. Emre Doğan, Absence-proofness: A new cooperative stability concept.
Inspected original: working paper11September2013, allocation-scheme model p5,
Definition2.1, Remark2.1 and Proposition2.1 p6; PDF/formula visually checked
in the sealed campaign and text reread during this consolidation.
https://econ.gatech.edu/sites/default/files/seminars/201402/absence-proofness.pdf
Published as Absence-proofness: Group stability beyond the core,
International Journal of Game Theory45,601-616 (2016; online2015).
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00182-015-0472-z
The working-paper condition is
 sum_(i in K\T) X_i(S\T)+v(T) <= sum_(i in K)X_i(S).
This is a close binary full-OUT antecedent. The inspected scheme is efficient
in each subgame; it has neither the present partial-report funding frontier
nor a checked quantitative arity/s theorem. Published text comparison remains
open. Missing originals are not negative evidence. Author is Emre, not an
unverified initial; working and published titles are distinct.

4. Bachrach, Elkind, Meir, Pasechnik, Zuckerman, Rothe and Rosenschein,
The Cost of Stability in Coalitional Games, SAGT2009,LNCS5814,122-134.
https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.4385
Campaign inspected original theorem on superadditive static games and
projective-plane examples (source map S10); fresh metadata verified here.
Static CoS already has square-root/projective ingredients. Our packing
profiles each have efficient static dual covers; the added cost arises only
from one rule having to satisfy cross-report inequalities. This is the
relevant distinction, not a claim that projective methods are new.

5. Meir, Rosenschein and Malizia, Subsidies, Stability, and Restricted
Cooperation in Coalitional Games, IJCAI2011,301-306.
https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/11/Papers/060.pdf
Original source in campaign; opposite-model QA reinspected it in this turn.
Its size parameter restricts blocking/coverage coalitions. Here all coverage
constraints remain and only protected report changes are size-restricted.
Substitution of k=s therefore does not produce the same optimization problem.

6. Feige, Feldman, Immorlica, Izsak, Lucier and Syrgkanis,
A Unifying Hierarchy of Valuations with Complements and Substitutes,2014.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.1211
Ezra, Feldman, Neyman, Talgam-Cohen and Weinberg,
Complement-Free Couples Must Communicate: A Hardness Result for Two-Player
Combinatorial Auctions,2018.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.09871
The positive-support hierarchy and bounded-rank loss bounds are established
antecedents. Metadata freshly inspected; campaign baseline-compatibility
record supplies the theorem comparison. The manuscript directly proves its
owner-supported decomposition and thinning inequalities, without assigning
novelty to these ingredients. The full-OUT coefficient argument and comparison
class must be assessed separately from value-hierarchy welfare results.

7. Casajus, Collusion, symmetry, and the Banzhaf value, author manuscript
31August2011. Original introduction/model inspected during consolidation.
https://home.uni-leipzig.de/casajus/texts/colluba.pdf
Classical marginal-value/collusion antecedent, particularly the p=1/2 value.
It is not a source for the projective Q_M certificate. General binomial
semivalues require their own priority comparison; Amer and Giménez,
Characterization of binomial semivalues through delegation games,
Naval Research Logistics54(6),702-708(2007), DOI10.1002/nav.20238, is a
metadata-only lead in the delegate's record, not a theorem inspected here.
The upper's binomial marginal mechanism and the lower's central-binomial
arithmetic identity are different objects. Do not conflate them.
