P4 PROTECTED-LEAF MAXIMUM-FLOW SELECTOR
24 October 2026. Consolidated proof after internal independent algebra audits.
Boundary integration is given directly below; human correctness and priority
review remain outstanding. This is a P4 theorem and its exact blow-up/disjoint-union transfer,
NOT an upper bound below 2 for all resource graphs. No priority claim is made.

THEOREM.
On the unit-profit path B--A--D--C, there is a nonnegative payment rule for
arbitrary fixed actual owners holding arbitrary nonnegative mixed resource
bundles, at every real report profile, satisfying every-coalition coverage
and full coalition outside-production protection (OUT), with total payment
at most (1+1/sqrt(2))*F(c). The rule is given explicitly below. The same bound
holds for complete blow-ups of this path and disjoint unions of these graphs.

1. DEFINITIONS AND FEASIBILITY.
For a resource graph with unit edge profits, F(c) is the maximum of sum_e x_e
subject to x_e>=0 and sum_{e incident to v} x_e<=c_v at every vertex.
On this path, F(c)=min(A+C,B+D,A+D). Each fixed owner i reports a vector b_i
of nonnegative resources and may only reduce its own coordinates. Owners may
hold any mixture of resources. Total capacity is c=sum_i b_i. For every
coalition T and coordinatewise reports b'_i<=b_i for i in T, with other
owners' reports fixed, full outside-production protection means
 sum_{i in T} y_i(b) - sum_{i in T} y_i(b'_T,b_{-T})
   >= F(sum_{i in T}(b_i-b'_i)).
All coalitions, including passive members whose report does not change, enter
this requirement. The budget benchmark is available production F(c), and the
funding bound is total payment, not merely the surplus over F(c).
Write capacities (A,B,C,D), alpha=1/sqrt(2), beta=alpha^2=1/2. Define
 V = min(A+C, alpha(B+D), A+alpha D),
 V'= min(B+D, alpha(A+C), D+alpha A).
These are maximum-flow values in the two orientations of the asymmetric
bipartite double cover: each left vertex has its original capacity and each
right vertex has alpha times that capacity. The two orientation components
are {A_L,C_L}->{B_R,D_R} and {B_L,D_L}->{A_R,C_R}.
For the first component, all possible maximum-flow left-use vectors satisfy
 w_A+w_C=V,
 max(0,V-A) <= w_C <= min(C,alpha D,V).
For the second, analogously
 w_B+w_D=V',
 max(0,V'-D) <= w_B <= min(B,alpha A,V').
The intervals are nonempty by the max-flow formula. Choose
 w_C=clamp(beta C; max(0,V-A), min(C,alpha D,V)),  w_A=V-w_C,
 w_B=clamp(beta B; max(0,V'-D), min(B,alpha A,V')), w_D=V'-w_B.
Here clamp(x;l,h)=min(h,max(l,x)). Thus the selected left-use vector is
continuous, nonnegative, homogeneous, and satisfies 0<=w_v<=c_v.

Equivalent useful formulas are
 w_A=min(A,alpha B+(alpha D-beta C)_+),
 w_D=min(D,alpha C+(alpha A-beta B)_+),
 w_C=min(C,alpha D,max(beta C,alpha(B+D)-A)),
 w_B=min(B,alpha A,max(beta B,alpha(A+C)-D)).
To verify, split the first orientation according as alpha D<=beta C; otherwise
compare alpha(B+D)-A with beta C and min(C,alpha D). This also verifies the
formula at ties by continuity. The other orientation is symmetric.

At c_v>0 let p_v=w_v/c_v; at c_v=0 set p_v=0. Pay actual owner i
 y_i(b)=sum_v b_iv p_v(sum_j b_j).
The choice of the price at a zero-capacity coordinate has no effect on any
payment. Prices and payments are nonnegative and prices lie in [0,1].

2. FUNDING.
The selected vector is realized by a feasible maximum double-cover flow.
Collapse its two directed copies of each original edge. At each vertex, the
resulting incident use is at most c_v+alpha c_v. Dividing all collapsed edge
flows by 1+alpha is therefore a feasible original fractional edge packing.
Consequently
 sum_i y_i=sum_v w_v=V+V' <= (1+alpha) F(c).
No integrality assumption enters this bound.

3. DIFFERENTIAL CERTIFICATE.
For a coalition with current aggregate holding a, write theta_v=a_v/c_v in
[0,1] at a strictly positive aggregate profile c. Its payment is
 H(c,a)=sum_v a_v p_v(c).
Under a restoration of a resource, outsiders stay fixed, so the restoration
coefficient is
 g_v=p_v+sum_j a_j partial_v p_j.
It suffices to show g_v>=0 for each vertex and g_u+g_v>=1 on every original
edge, at every smooth cell and every theta in [0,1]^4. Indeed these conditions
say g is a nonnegative fractional vertex cover, whence g dot d>=F(d).
Section 5 supplies a direct integration proof, including boundary profiles,
without invoking any inherited differential-certificate lemma.

Let u=A-alpha B and v=D-alpha C. The formulas split into the following cells.

4A. u<=0 OR v<=0.
If u<=0, then
 w_A=A, w_B=alpha A,
 w_C=min(C,alpha D), w_D=min(D,alpha C).
Thus A--B is the asymmetric single-edge rule on the branch A<=alpha B,
and C--D is the full asymmetric single-edge rule. For the first pair
 g_A=1+alpha theta_B>=1,
 g_B=(alpha A/B)(1-theta_B)>=0.
The other pair has nonnegative coefficients and its edge sum is at least 1,
as follows from the single-edge calculation below. The cross edge A--D is
safe because g_A>=1 and g_D>=0. The case v<=0 is symmetric.

Single-edge calculation: if X>=Y and t=Y/X, then
 w_X=alpha Y, w_Y=min(Y,alpha X).
For t<=alpha, the coefficient sum is at least 1 and both coefficients are
nonnegative. For t>=alpha,
 g_X=alpha t(1-theta_X)+alpha theta_Y,
 g_Y=(alpha/t)(1-theta_Y)+alpha theta_X,
and their minimum sum is alpha(1+t)>=alpha(1+alpha)>1.
This uses alpha=1/sqrt(2); in general the threshold is alpha(1+alpha)>=1.

4B. u>0 AND v>0: COMMON FORMULAS.
The selected use becomes
 w_A=alpha B+min(u,alpha v),
 w_D=alpha C+min(v,alpha u),
 w_B=min(B,beta B+(alpha u-v)_+),
 w_C=min(C,beta C+(alpha v-u)_+).
Because alpha<1, at most one of alpha u-v and alpha v-u is positive.

4C. MIDDLE CONE alpha v<=u AND alpha u<=v.
Here w_B=beta B, w_C=beta C,
 w_A=alpha(B+D)-beta C, w_D=alpha(A+C)-beta B.
Moreover p_A>=beta and p_D>=beta. For example u<=v/alpha gives
 alpha B+alpha v >= beta(alpha B+u)=beta A;
the other inequality is symmetric. The restoration coefficients are
 g_A=p_A(1-theta_A)+alpha theta_D,
 g_D=p_D(1-theta_D)+alpha theta_A,
 g_B=beta+alpha theta_A-beta theta_D,
 g_C=beta+alpha theta_D-beta theta_A.
They are nonnegative. The A--B and D--C edge minima are respectively
 beta+min(p_A,alpha) and beta+min(p_D,alpha), both >=2beta=1.
The A--D minimum is min(p_A,alpha)+min(p_D,alpha)>=2beta=1.

4D. OUTER CONE u<=alpha v (the other outer cone is symmetric).
Here
 w_A=A, w_B=beta B,
 w_D=alpha(A+C)-beta B,
 w_C=min(C,alpha(B+D)-A).
If w_C<C, then p_C>=beta. Also p_D>=beta: the unsaturated condition gives
 alpha v-u <= (1-beta)C, hence
 beta v <= alpha u+alpha(1-beta)C
because beta=alpha^2. Adding beta alpha C yields
 beta D <= alpha u+alpha C=w_D.
The coefficients are
 g_A=1-theta_C+alpha theta_D,
 g_B=beta+alpha theta_C-beta theta_D,
 g_C=p_C(1-theta_C)+alpha theta_D,
 g_D=p_D(1-theta_D)+alpha theta_C.
They are nonnegative. For the three edges, the respective minima are
 A--B: alpha+beta >=1,
 C--D: min(p_C,alpha)+min(p_D,alpha) >=2beta=1,
 A--D: alpha+min(p_D,alpha) >=alpha+beta>=1.
For the first displayed minimum, direct minimization yields alpha+beta
because alpha-beta>=0; it may be larger on lower-dimensional tie cells.

If instead w_C=C, then
 g_A=1+alpha theta_D,
 g_C=1+alpha theta_D,
 g_B=beta(1-theta_D),
 g_D=p_D(1-theta_D).
All coordinates are nonnegative. A--B has sum at least 1, and both A--D and
C--D have sum at least 1. Thus every smooth cell satisfies the certificate.

5. TIES, ZERO CAPACITIES, ACTUAL OWNERS, AND COVERAGE.
The use vector w is continuous and piecewise linear, with finitely many
polyhedral cells. On strictly positive capacities p=w/c is locally Lipschitz.
For every nonnegative outsider bundle o and coalition holding a, define
 H(o,a)=sum_v a_v p_v(o+a).
This payment map is jointly continuous on its whole nonnegative domain: a
term whose aggregate coordinate tends to zero lies between 0 and a_v and
therefore tends to zero; each positive-coordinate term is continuous by
continuity of w. Also H(o,0)=0.

Fix 0<=a'<=a and write d=a-a'. Choose a strictly positive generic vector z
and a sequence of positive epsilon tending to zero such that the segment
 c(t)=o+epsilon z+a'+td, 0<=t<=1,
is not contained in any proper hyperplane supporting a boundary of the
finitely many linear cells. Such a choice exists: choose z not orthogonal to
any of their finitely many nonzero normals, then omit the finitely many
positive epsilon values that cause containment for a boundary parallel to d.
The segment is strictly positive and meets each remaining boundary at most
once. Its coalition payment H(o+epsilon z,a'+td) is therefore continuous and
piecewise continuously differentiable, with derivative g dot d away from
finitely many breakpoints. At every such point the coalition fractions are
(a'_v+t d_v)/c_v(t) in [0,1], so Section 4 applies.

For any edge packing x feasible at d, the verified edge and coordinate
inequalities give
 sum_e x_e <= sum_{e=uv}(g_u+g_v)x_e
           = sum_v g_v sum_{e incident to v} x_e <= sum_v g_v d_v.
Taking the maximum over x gives g dot d>=F(d). Integrating over the finitely
many pieces and using continuity at their endpoints yields
 H(o+epsilon z,a)-H(o+epsilon z,a') >= F(d).
Joint continuity now lets epsilon tend to zero. Hence, at every real profile,
 H(o,a)-H(o,a') >= F(a-a').
The outsider perturbation is solely an analytic approximation of H; no owner
is added or reassigned in the asserted mechanism. This argument includes
segments lying on ties, zero capacities, the all-zero profile, and the grand
coalition. Every coalition's actual aggregate payment equals H(o,a), including
unchanged coalition members. Thus full OUT holds for the original fixed
mixed owners. Taking a'=0 proves coverage, since H(o,0)=0.

6. EXACT GRAPH TRANSFERS AND LIMITS.
Replace each of A,B,C,D by a nonempty independent vertex group, include every
edge between B--A, A--D, D--C groups, and no other edges. Aggregate capacities
within each group. The production value equals the P4 value: any original
packing gives an aggregate packing, and any aggregate plan can distribute
each group's incident use among its vertices and connect the assigned uses
across each complete bipartite group pair. Explicitly, for an aggregate edge
flow f_XY and group capacity C_X>0, assign its incidence to vertex v in X as
r_(v,XY)=c_v*f_XY/C_X. Summing over incident group pairs uses at most c_v.
If f_XY>0, put r_(v,XY)*r_(w,XY)/f_XY on edge vw; its two marginals are the
assigned incidences. Zero-capacity groups have zero incident aggregate flow;
zero-flow pairs need no division. This constructs the required packing. Applying the four aggregate prices
to every original resource in its group therefore preserves payments, OUT,
coverage, and funding for the same fixed mixed owners. Isolated resources can
be paid zero. For disjoint unions, sum the component payments; production and
withheld production are additive, so the same uniform constant applies even
when owners hold resources in several components.

The construction does not cover arbitrary resource graphs, arbitrary edge
profits, or non-complete connections between the four groups. It does not
prove optimality on P4. It does provide an all-real coupled-component price
construction below 2 on a class containing induced P4, outside the accepted
positive binary-min exact-decomposition class. The canonical general-graph
frontier remains [1+c_*,2].

7. RULE SHARPNESS AND REVIEW SCOPE.
At (A,B,C,D)=(4,1,1,1), F(c)=2, V=2alpha, V'=2. The rule therefore attains
its funding factor 1+alpha. This is sharpness of this explicit rule, not a
lower bound for arbitrary mechanisms on P4.
