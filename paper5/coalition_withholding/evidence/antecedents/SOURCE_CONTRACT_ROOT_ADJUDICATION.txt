CORRECTED REASONS FOR NONIMPLICATION
Moulin–Sprumont SGM really is the same aggregate incentive inequality on its
scalar grid, including passive coalition members. Its Proposition2(ii) is
an impossibility with additional additivity, separability and exact efficiency.
At its equal-cost profiles, exact efficiency converts the protected sum's
increase into the complementary agent's decrease. An overfunded cover lacks
that equality. Also, the stated cost witness has disjoint additive bundles
with values1+1 but union value1, so it is not a packing-production witness.
Absence of an s parameter is descriptive, NOT itself a nonimplication proof.

IMM has compatible profit-budget signs and an arity-two flow witness. Its
proof crucially bounds EACH incumbent's new payment by its old payment.
TCWM/full OUT instead constrain the protected sum including the entrant;
they do not imply that individual inequality. The all-real min2 factor2
rule separates the contracts. A theorem headline alone omits this crucial
restriction; use its section definition and actual proof.

ELEMENTARY NORMALIZATION WITNESS (CORRECTION SUPPORT ONLY)
Two actual owners each report b_i in[0,1] of one resource, F(b1+b2)=b1+b2.
Let g(t)=0 for t<1/2, and g(t)=t for t>=1/2. Define
  y1=b1, y2=b2+g(b1).
These payments are nonnegative, cover every coalition, and satisfy full OUT
for every actual coalition: its own coordinates pay their restored amounts,
and any g increment included is nonnegative. Total funding is at most2F.
Normalize both payments by the common positive factor F/(y1+y2) when F>0,
and use zero at F=0. At reports(2/5,1), the normalized owner1 payment is2/5.
After owner1 restores to(1/2,1), it becomes(1/2)*(3/2)/2=3/8.
Since3/8-2/5=-1/40<0, normalization breaks even singleton TCWM.

not a campaign progress claim or an antecedent impossibility theorem.
