Dependency: lower/PROJECTIVE_SQUARE_ROOT_LOWER.txt, including its fixed full-cube

FIXED s AND ARBITRARY LADDER LENGTH
Fix integers s>=2 and M>=1. Use the same projective technology and n=2M+1
actual owners. At each C_m, all 2m active private owners have payment q_m.
For m>=2 choose ANY k_m=min(s-1,2m-2) distinct old active owners, together
with the same actual restoring owner l_m. This coalition has k_m+1<=s
members. Whole pairs are not needed: every selected old active owner has the
same old payment q_(m-1) and the same new payment q_m. Zero-owner payment
of l_m at the old profile is nonnegative, not assumed zero. Thus TCWM gives

  (k_m+1)q_m >= k_m q_(m-1),
  q_M >= (1/3) P_(M,s),
  P_(M,s)=product_(m=2)^M k_m/(k_m+1).

At C_M, final coverage gives z+2q_M>=1, where z is the root payment.
The budget gives kappa>=z+2M q_M. Consequently

  kappa >= B_(M,s):=1+[2(M-1)/3] P_(M,s).                 (1)

All inequalities hold for arbitrary payments after the lawful finite average.
For the original unsymmetrized rule the expensive profile lies somewhere in
the symmetry orbit of C_M. Every profile and its transforms remain inside the
same fixed owner domains. As before this is a lower for TCWM and hence full OUT.

OPTIMAL LADDER LENGTH FOR THIS PARTICULAR CERTIFICATE
Put h=floor((s+1)/2). Up to m=h the unconstrained recurrence applies, so
  P_(h,s)=Q_h/h, Q_h=product_(j=2)^h 2j/(2j-1).
After h each factor is alpha=(s-1)/s. Therefore
  P_(M,s)=(Q_h/h) alpha^(M-h), M>=h.
Before the cap, (M-1)P_(M,s) strictly increases for M>=1. In the tail its
successive ratio, for M>=h and M>1, is M alpha/(M-1). This is greater than1 for M<s,
equal to1 for M=s, and less than1 for M>s. Hence (1) is maximized at the
two consecutive lengths M=s and M=s+1, with identical value

  B_s=1+[2(s-1)Q_h/(3h)] ((s-1)/s)^(s-h).               (2)

The M=s construction has n=2s+1 owners and d=2^(s+1)-1 rows. Nothing is
claimed about the best possible certificate or mechanism constant.

Using Q_h~sqrt(pi h)/2 and h~s/2, the increment B_s-1 is asymptotic to
  [sqrt(2*pi)/(3*sqrt(e))] sqrt(s).
This improves the constant, not the square-root exponent. For s=1, use the
ordinary coverage lower1; formula(2) is deliberately not evaluated at0^0.

EXACT SMALL EXHIBIT
At s=M=6, h=3, Q_h=8/5, alpha=5/6. Formula(2) gives
  B_6=1+(10/3)*(8/15)*(5/6)^3=493/243>2.
This uses thirteen fixed owners and 127 resource rows. The separate known
single-three-pool-minimum theorem has a factor2 TCWM rule for coalitions of
every size (see root/MIN_HUBER_CALIBRATION.txt, not only the s4 calibration).
Thus the two technology classes already differ at s6. This is not a lower
for the single-minimum class; it does not change that established rule.

EXACT NONNEGATIVE CERTIFICATE
Use seed 3q_1>=1; ladder rows (k_m+1)q_m-k_m q_(m-1)>=0; final coverage
z+2q_M>=1; and final budget kappa-z-2M q_M>=0. Give final rows weight1.
Starting with residual coefficient 2(M-1) at q_M, eliminate backwards:
  w_m=residual/(k_m+1), next residual=w_m*k_m.
Finally give the seed weight residual/3. All weights are nonnegative rational.
Every q and z coefficient cancels; the right side is exactly (1).
For M=1 all ladder/seed weights are zero and final coverage plus budget gives1.
