PURE FIXED-OWNER REALIZATION OF THE PROJECTIVE LOWER
Root derivation, 18 October 2026. Independently checked and opposite-model
reviewed (180432 and185516); proof-scope clarifications incorporated.
Historical priority remains unconfirmed.
Dependencies: lower/PROJECTIVE_SQUARE_ROOT_LOWER.txt and, for the sharper
bounded-s constant, root/PROJECTIVE_BOUNDED_COALITION_TAIL.txt.

The same lower admits two different fixed ownership institutions. The original
proof uses n=2M+1 fully eligible owners with all-ones endowments. This variant
uses n=d=2^(M+1)-1 pure owners, each owning exactly one resource unit. It does
not simultaneously obtain the small owner count and pure eligibility.

INSTITUTION AND GROUP ACTION
Keep the full binary projective triple technology on nonzero vectors r of
V=F_2^(M+1), with v,e_1,...,e_M an explicit BASIS of V. In particular v
is not in the span of the high vectors. Introduce one actual owner i_r
for EACH resource row r. Its fixed
endowment is the standard unit vector at r, eligibility is solely row r, and
its real binding report is t_r times that vector, with t_r in[0,1]. All d
actual owners exist throughout every profile, including unreported resources.

An invertible binary linear map g induces both a resource-index permutation
r->g(r) and the corresponding owner-label permutation i_r->i_(g(r)). Their
JOINT action preserves this fixed institution: the transformed report of owner
i_(g(r)) is t_r at its own row g(r). No unrestricted owner permutation is
used. The report box, coordinate order, feasible restorations, production,
coalition values, and coalition sizes are invariant. Conjugating any rule by
this joint action therefore preserves every contract inequality. Finite
averaging over GL(V) gives an equivariant rule with the same funding factor.

PROFILES AND STABILIZERS
At C_m activate exactly owners of rows
  {v} union {e_j:1<=j<=M} union {v+e_j:1<=j<=m}
at report1; every other owner reports0. The support and full-LP primal/dual
certificates are unchanged, so F(C_m)=1.

The seed maps v<->e_1 and v->v+e_1 fixing all e_j preserve the entire active
row set at C_1. Therefore their induced owner permutations preserve the whole
binary report profile, including all additional zero owners. They connect the
three seed owners, which receive equal payments a>=1/3 by coverage.

At C_m the active-index permutations and flips e_j->e_j+v preserve the active
row set and act transitively on its2m private owners. Thus they all receive
q_m. The transition C_(m-1)->C_m changes ONLY the same actual pure owner
i_(v+e_m) from0 to1. Protect that owner together with the specified unchanged
old active owners. Their fixed labels and eligibility never change inside a
comparison. Extra zero owners are excluded; their possibly positive payments
are dropped only through nonnegativity in the total budget.

All seed, recurrence, terminal coverage, and rational certificate steps of the
parent proofs now follow verbatim. Thus the square-root lower and bounded-s
tail B_s hold even for fixed PURE one-resource owners. At M=s, the pure
realization has n=d=2^(s+1)-1, whereas the fully eligible realization has
n=2s+1 with the same d. For s6 the bound493/243 uses127 pure owners or13
fully eligible owners; these counts must never be conflated.

For M>=2 no one owner is indispensable throughout the full report domain:
for any row r there is a projective triple avoiding r, and its three owners
can report1 while i_r reports0. The displayed C_m profiles nevertheless each
have a single reported veto owner at row v. Profilewise veto and global
institutional veto are different statements. No claim is made about bounded
resource dimension, minimal instance size, historical priority, or an improved
exponent beyond the parent theorem.
