# Orbit-level anatomy of the large six-player exchange cases

Date: 2026-08-10

The direct strict-bound proof contains three visually large retained menus:

```text
(p,m,e)=(3,2,1): 52 labeled directions,
(p,m,e)=(3,3,0): 71 labeled directions,
(p,m,e)=(4,2,0): 90 labeled directions.
```

Those 213 rows are not 213 unrelated mathematical ideas.  Quotienting first
by relabelings inside the sign classes `P={x>z}`, `M={z>x}`, and `E={x=z}`
leaves 44 role-placement orbits.  Forgetting the role placement and retaining
only the coefficient multiset leaves eight oriented templates, or six up to
reversing the direction.  The exact quotient is regenerated by
`audit_strict_exchange_large_case_templates.py`.

## 1. The common exchange lemma

Every retained row is an instance of one lemma.  Let `y` be `Lmin` or `Lmax`
and let `h(N)=0` be one of the templates below, assigned to labeled players.
If the first payoff stratum affected by `h` moves in the improving direction,
then lexicographic optimality forces a `y`-tight proper coalition `S` with
`h(S)<0`.  Exactness and core membership force that same `S` to satisfy the
opposite-selection comparison and all six singleton-witness comparisons.

Thus a template is not itself an inequality: it is a finite family of
negative-cut alternatives.  The proof objects show that, for the listed role
placements, no consistent choice of such alternatives exists when the
distance is greater than `2/3`.

### Extremal-level activation for every two-for-two exchange

The H7 activation is a single order statistic, not a large disjunction.  Write

```text
h=-e_a-e_b+e_r+e_s.
```

For Lmin, the first affected payoff stratum improves exactly when it contains
a recipient and no donor.  Equivalently,

```text
min(x_a,x_b) > min(x_r,x_s).                       (A1)
```

The raw first-stratum encoding chooses which recipient is lower and therefore
displays two alternatives.  Their combined failure has the four-term DNF

```text
(x_a<=x_r and x_a<=x_s) or (x_a<=x_r and x_b<=x_s)
or (x_b<=x_r and x_a<=x_s) or (x_b<=x_r and x_b<=x_s).   (A2)
```

The Lmax dual for `-h` is

```text
max(z_a,z_b) < max(z_r,z_s),                       (A3)
```

with the same four-term pattern after reversing the comparisons.  These
equivalences follow directly by conditioning on the lower recipient in (A1)
or the higher recipient in (A3); a solver-free audit also checks all 256
four-level assignments.

This lemma applies to all 95 labelled H7 rows in the large-case table and to
the order-refined packet relays.  It removes activation-disjunction bulk, but
does not classify the negative-cut blockers forced after activation.  In class
1 it reduces the exact proof object from 125 identities at 232 uses to 109 at
160 uses.  Class 2 now reuses both the minimum-level statement and its
maximum-level dual; classes 5 and 6 can reuse the same four-case interface.

```text

multiset of coefficients on M,
multiset of coefficients on E.
```

role lemma from assigning it between `P` and `E`, but relabeling two players

### Exact activation atlases for the 52/71/90-direction cases

The reduced `(3,2,1)` core contains 29 labelled directions, but their
activation conditions are eight elementary order-statistic statements.  Put
`E={e}`.  In the table, distinct symbols chosen from the same role class are

| schema | labelled direction `h` | side | exact activation condition | labelled rows |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| A1 | `-e_e+e_m` | Lmin | `x_m<x_e` | 2 |
| A2 | `-e_p+e_e` | Lmax | `z_p>z_e` | 3 |
| A3 | `-e_p+e_m` | Lmin | `x_m<x_p` | 6 |
| A4 | `e_p+e_e-e_{m_1}-e_{m_2}` | Lmax | `max(z_{m_1},z_{m_2})>max(z_p,z_e)` | 3 |
| A5 | `-e_p+e_e` | Lmin | `x_e<x_p` | 3 |

| A7 | `e_p+e_{p'}-e_{m_1}-e_{m_2}` | Lmax | `max(z_{m_1},z_{m_2})>max(z_p,z_{p'})` | 3 |

For a one-for-one transfer, the statement is immediate: the recipient must
lie strictly below the donor for Lmin, and the negative-coordinate player
must lie strictly above the positive-coordinate player for Lmax.  For a
two-for-two exchange, the Lmin condition is “the lowest recipient is below
the lowest donor,” while its Lmax dual is “the highest donor is above the
highest recipient.”  These are precisely the first-affected-level tests, so
ties make the corresponding strict comparison fail rather than create an
extra case.

The multiplicities sum to

```text
2+3+6+3+3+6+3+3 = 29.
```

Thus all activation logic in the reduced 52-direction case is humanized by
eight order-statistic comparisons and two exchange archetypes.  What remains
finite is the coexistence of the negative-cut blockers forced when these
conditions hold; this atlas does not claim to settle that layer by hand.

negative-cut blocker coexistence layer, not to lexicographic activation.

The eligible negative cuts themselves have a three-template atlas.  For a
one-for-one direction `h=-e_a+e_r`, negativity is exactly
`a in S, r not in S`; the other four players are free, so there are 16 cuts.
For `h=-e_a-e_b+e_r+e_s`, write `d=|S intersect {a,b}|` and
`u=|S intersect {r,s}|`.  Negativity is `d>u`: the `(d,u)=(1,0)` cases give
8 cuts after the two zero players are chosen freely, and `(2,0)` or `(2,1)`
give 12 more, for 20.

The asymmetric B4 direction

```text
h=e_p+2e_{p'}-e_{m_1}-e_{m_2}-e_{m_3}
```

has `h(S)<0` exactly when the number of selected `M`-players exceeds
`1[p in S]+2 1[p' in S]`.  With the remaining zero player optional, the
three possible positive-side loads contribute `14+8+2=24` cuts.  Its B5
reverse has the complementary `2+8+14=24` split.  Thus the 136 rows consist
of

```text
64 one-for-one rows       x 16 cuts
60 two-for-two rows       x 20 cuts
12 asymmetric rows        x 24 cuts
                         = 2512 eligible negative cuts,
```

each with the opposite-selection and six witness comparisons, or 17,584
individual comparison occurrences.  The atlas classifies the disjunctions;
it does not prove that the 2,512 alternatives cannot coexist.

### Exact projection of the blocker-coexistence layer

There is nevertheless an exact structural replacement for the witness
variables once one blocker cut has been chosen for every active direction.
Index the chosen blocker requirements by `j in J`.  Requirement `j` uses a
coalition `C_j` and a selected allocation `y^j` (either `x` or `z`), and asks

```text
q^i(C_j) >= y^j(C_j)             for every singleton witness i.
```

For fixed `i`, let `a^k in {0,1}^J` be the incidence column
`a^k_j=1[k in C_j]`.  The zero coordinate and simplex constraints give the
exact image

```text
{(q^i(C_j))_j : q^i>=0, q^i(N)=1, q^i_i=0}
  = conv{a^k : k != i}.                                  (W1)
```

Consequently the entire family of witness-`i` blocker requirements is
feasible if and only if, for every `lambda>=0`,

```text
sum_j lambda_j y^j(C_j)
  <= max_{k != i} sum_{j:k in C_j} lambda_j.             (W2)
```

This is the support-function theorem of
`S6_ZERO_ANCHOR_OVERCOVER_DUALITY.md`, now applied simultaneously to every
blocker choice and every anchor.  Since the six witnesses are otherwise
independent, (W2) for `i=0,...,5` eliminates all 36 witness coordinates
without weakening or strengthening the chosen-cut system.  The opposite-
selection half remains simply

```text
d(C_j)<=0 for an Lmin blocker,   d(C_j)>=0 for an Lmax blocker,
where d=x-z.                                             (W3)
```

Thus blocker coexistence has the exact projected form:

```text
choose one cut from the 16/20/24-cut template for each active schema;
enforce the corresponding sign condition (W3);
enforce the six five-column incidence-hull facet systems (W2).
```

For a fixed numerical `x,z` and a fixed cut choice, any infeasible witness
system has a small sub-obstruction.  The face
`{q>=0:q(N)=1,q_i=0}` has affine dimension four.  Applying Helly's theorem to
the blocker halfspaces on that affine space shows that at most five blocker
requirements, together with the simplex constraints, already make it empty.
If the minimal Helly subfamily includes the simplex as one of its convex sets,
only four blocker halfspaces are needed; without that extra condition the
uniform bound is five.  This pointwise support bound does not say that one
fixed five-requirement certificate works uniformly over an entire symbolic
payoff region, but it explains the observed support ceiling of five in the
class-5/class-6 affine certificates.

The projection is the sought exact-cone/facet theorem for the witness side
of all three large cases.  It converts the remaining task from continuous
witness feasibility to a finite transversal-and-facet problem.  It does not
yet prove that no global cut transversal satisfies (W2)--(W3), so the
six-player upper bound still relies on the certified finite formulas.

## 4. Independent coefficient-two control

The two strict sign cases that require the coefficient-two pool are separately
rebuilt by `independent_bound_two_reconstruction.py`.  That program imports no
production encoder, lex predicate, blocker, direction pool, minimizer, or SMT
serializer.  Only the frozen retained menus are shared as theorem data.  It
uses different variable names, writes the first-affected-level predicate
directly, rebuilds every negative coalition cut, and expresses the no-unique-
top anchor in the logically dual form.

For both `(2,3,1)` and `(3,3,0)`, the base system without exchange implications
is SAT.  The independently rebuilt full system is UNSAT in Z3 and cvc5, and a

## 5. A complete seven-menu packet orbit inside the 90-direction case

The anchor-localized `(4,2,0)` argument has eleven packet orbits.  Packet
class 6, representing four labelled packets, now has complete solver-free
arithmetic conditional on its frozen relay/use maps.  Its 49 direction nodes
do not require 49 cut lemmas: exact elimination leaves only seven direction
menus, and the weak `h11:C124` branch adds one candidate to each of the
`h7,h10,h15` menus.  Seven path-local transition rules generate all 47 relay
edges from the weak/strict regime, the preceding `h10` cut, and that same
one-bit refinement.

The complete accounting is

```text
924 eligible cut uses
  = 711 pure difference-cone exclusions
  +  11 packet/ancestor-witness context exclusions
  + 155 target-witness exclusions
  +  47 relay branches.
```

Nine rational identities certify the 11 context exclusions; 53 certify all
155 target-witness exclusions; 26 plus one direct identity certify every
activation failure not already structural.  Every identity is checked over
`Q` without a solver, and the cut verifier independently enforces the menu
atlas, transition grammar, and exhaustive partition.  What remains shared
with the encoder is the exclusion-use map itself.  This is therefore a
conceptual compression of one four-packet orbit, not independent external
validation of the full 90-direction theorem.

## 6. Remaining humanization boundary

This compression materially improves readability but is not yet an all-human
proof of the three large cases.  A complete hand replacement would have to
show, for each of the 19 selected sign-role schemas, why the eligible negative
cuts forced by the appropriate archetype cannot coexist.  The highest-value
next step is to derive common cut inequalities for A1--A8 and for the ten
anchor-localized `(4,2,0)` orbits, then handle the five-schema balanced core
B1--B5, whose last two schemas form the H4/H5 coefficient-two reversal pair.
