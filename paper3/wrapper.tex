\author{Maxwell Flanagan}
\date{July 2026 --- working fragment}
\begin{document}
\maketitle
\noindent\emph{Status: mathematical core of a paper in progress. Singleton
bumps are proved monotone even on balanced games.  Grand-coalition bumps remain
on an exact path but labeled resource monotonicity is disproved by an exact
five-player example.  Proper nonsingleton bumps are reduced to an exact
capacity dichotomy and one open path lemma; the capacity implication is proved
for every two-level failure dual, including all two-player coalitions.
Balanced nonexact counterexamples establish that
exactness is necessary for the remaining positive program.}
