## Obligation-by-obligation review

2. **Cover and integral worth, lines32–46 and93.** The displayed q local matchings partition all9q players and attain8q. Triple covers plus nonnegativity imply every coalition inequality; the proof does not impose productive pair coalitions or replace the entire core by a selected face.

3. **All27 local inequalities, lines52–65.** The sufficient local conditions are valid. Direct substitution into the raw tensor gives the nontrivial slacks listed in the note. 4. **Ordinary mixed triples, lines67–79.** With no bridge and not all three players in the same block, at most one pair contributes.
