# Cycle3 source and priority boundary

Checked September5,2026. This is a targeted follow-up to the existing source gate, not a new exhaustive priority review.

The exact27-player finite-attainment claim concerns anonymous FULL sorted leximin, true coordinate ideals over the FULL strict-triple core, and nonnegative pair-additive triple values. The proof and verifiers establish that claim internally. No inspected primary theorem in this follow-up identifies its exact example or a universal one-sixth bound; that is a bounded nonfinding only.

- [Vazirani, arXiv2402.11437v6](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.11437v6), revised November20,2025, studies fair core imputations for the bipartite assignment game. The version and model were rechecked against the current arXiv record. It provides the relevant sorted-selector precedent but does not license moving its bipartite results to complete strict triples. The v5 HTML was initially opened as an older control and was not mistaken for the latest revision. Earlier attribution of the bipartite half guarantee remains preserved in the main campaign.
- [Núñez–Solymosi, October15,2014 working paper](https://unipub.lib.uni-corvinus.hu/1722/1/cewp_201415.pdf), pp1–4, uses an externally fixed priority order of players in its lexicographic procedures. That differs from sorting the resulting payoff values as in our leximin. The paper's extreme-point results are therefore not a direct guarantee for our selector. The institutional working version was read; its later2017 journal record is not treated as an independently reread full text here.
- [Atay–Núñez2019](https://ataatay.github.io/webpage/Atay_Nu%CC%81n%CC%83ez_2019_ANOR.pdf), §4, Theorem1, gives bilateral-core composition under a cycle-free sector graph in its generalized model. Its discussion distinguishes strict models with no productive smaller teams. Those hypotheses remain essential. Our3/2 factorization lemma instead permits signed bilateral component payoffs and raises the total: it is a cover-feasibility statement, not their efficient core composition or a fairness theorem.

The elementary LP dual and difference-constraint tools, cube cancellation, and sector shifts are not claimed as new methods. The sharp3/2 cover-factorization consequence has not received a dedicated comprehensive priority audit. Its conventional proof can be retained as a tool without marketing it as a separate novel paper.

The earlier original-source gaps for Quint, Lucas, Stuart and the newer multisided extreme-core paper are not resolved by these searches. External specialist review remains pending. Neither finite attainment, its six-coordinate budget, nor a successful internal proof check supplies a global priority certificate or a field-level significance verdict.
