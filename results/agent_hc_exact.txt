LP-DUALITY REDUCTION of the Helly conjecture (Bondareva-Shapley):
M balanced <=> for every balanced collection (lambda_T>=0, sum lambda_T 1_T = 1_N):
  sum_k lambda_{S_k} d_k <= slack_b(lambda),  where slack_b(lambda)=v(N)-sum_T lambda_T b(T)>=0.
Each t_k balanced gives only the per-k bound lambda_{S_k} d_k <= slack_b(lambda). The
conjecture is the JOINT bound; it is NOT implied by the per-k bounds alone (a sum can
exceed the max), so it genuinely needs EXACTNESS (which constrains WHICH collections are
tight). The dangerous collections are those giving positive weight to >=2 bumped
coalitions S_j,S_k simultaneously -- i.e. S_j,S_k in a common minimal balanced collection
(e.g. a partition = DISJOINT arms). But: disjoint arms -> monotonicity DECOUPLES (proven
feasible regardless of M); overlapping arms (A∩B≠∅) -> M provably balanced. So the two
regimes that could break the conjecture are exactly the two regimes where feasibility is
independently guaranteed. This is why every exact 2-arm family is feasible, and strongly
suggests the m-arm join is always balanced.
